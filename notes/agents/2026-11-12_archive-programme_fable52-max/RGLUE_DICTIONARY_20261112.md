## Findings of this reading (all mine, all unchecked)

- **RG1. Corollaire 2 of scan 152 is false in dimension ≥ 3.** Take X = C³ and Θ the three coordinate planes. Then X^{*∞} is (C^*)³ near Θ, with fundamental group Z³. Each of the three components of P_1^*, the three of P_2^* and the six of P^*_{2,1} is a (C^*)³, and every map of the diagram Π̃_1 ← Π̃_{2,1} → Π̃_2 is an isomorphism on π₁; the diagram is a hexagon of copies of Z³, and its colimit has fundamental group Z³ × Z. With Π̃_3, Π̃_{3,1}, Π̃_{3,2} and Π̃_{3,2,1} added the hexagon is filled and the answer is Z³. In general the k-type of X^{*∞} is carried by the flags in codimensions ≤ k + 2, and C^{k+2} shows that k + 1 does not suffice. For the moduli stacks: the fundamental groupoid needs the strata of codimension 3 (triples of disjoint curves), which is the statement that the fundamental group of a complex of groups depends on its 2-skeleton.
- **RG2. Π̃_• is the nerve of the crossing category** (row 4), and the three structures of §11 together (flag category, multinormal torsors, generization) are the exit-path ∞-category of (X, Θ) up to level 1. H4 (i) should read: the faces of the real oriented blow-up are his P_d^*; the déploiement is the category that indexes them.
- **RG3. The MDT-structure is the exit category of the stable moduli in bimodule form** (row 17): for multicurves σ ⊇ τ, with stabilizers taken in the mapping class group, the group of ΠM^*_{G,G′} is Stab(σ, τ)/Δ_τ, an extension of Stab(σ, τ)/Δ_σ by Δ_σ/Δ_τ = Z^C; (105) is the composition of crossing arrows.
- **RG4. Forgetting at infinity** (row 19): the map of meridian lattices and the BZ of RESIDUE H.3.
- **RG5. In S2, the condition for the open stratum suffices** (rows 11, 15): if all strata are aspherical and Z^{I(x)} → π₁(U) is injective at every point, then for every pair of strata S ⊂ S̄′ the lattice Z^{d−d′} injects into π₁(S′), because the image of Z^d in the fundamental group of the link of S′ contains the fibre lattice Z^{d′} as a direct summand. Whether the conditions for steps of codimension one suffice is not decided.
- **RG6. The four pieces** (row 10) and the three nested strata A ⊂ B ⊂ R; the pieces with a disc factor are what X needs beyond X^*.
- **RG7. The maquette of the tower is the tropical moduli space** (row 16): π₀ of his 𝒟 for the stable moduli stack is the category of MD-graphs with generization morphisms; its classifying space is the boundary complex.
