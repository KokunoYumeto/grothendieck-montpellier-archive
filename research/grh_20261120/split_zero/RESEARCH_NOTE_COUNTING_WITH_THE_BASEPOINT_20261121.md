## 0. Summary

1. **Counting with the basepoint makes ζ the unit of counting.** A count c(q) = Σ_j a_j q^j has the Hasse–Weil zeta
   Z_c(s) = Π_j ζ(s − j)^{a_j} over Spec Z. The map c ↦ Z_c is a ring isomorphism onto a Witt-type ring of Euler
   products whose unit is ζ, and adjoining a counted basepoint multiplies by ζ: Z_{X₊} = Z_X · ζ.
2. **The sign.** With Connes and Consani's sign ε of F_{1²}, globalized as the character χ₋₄, a sign-twisted count
   has zeta Π_j ζ(s − j)^{a_j⁺} L(s − j, χ₋₄)^{a_j⁻}. The split zero's supported zero e is the class 1 + ε:
   Spec Z[i], with zeta ζ_{Q(i)}.
3. **GCT at rank three.** Every structure constant of GCT VIII's canonical basis of the rank-three nonstandard algebra
   is ±(v − v⁻¹)^a·f with f a polynomial with nonnegative coefficients in u = q + q⁻¹, the normalized count of the
   Weil restriction of P¹ from F_{q²}. So the positivity part of VIII's conjecture holds at rank three in this
   sharper form; its unimodality clause fails for six constants, exactly by a supported zero.
4. **The two cells are the split and the inert sheet** (λ₂(v) = λ₁(v²)), and everything non-standard at rank three
   lives on the inert sheet.
5. **The reduction.** GCT's nonstandard Riemann hypothesis at rank r is Alpöge's theorem (RH and GRH for Dirichlet
   L-functions) together with a combinatorial existence statement, a dimension ring. At rank three that ring exists,
   and the one with the fewest supported zeros is unique in the searched range. The analytic part is the GL₁ slice
   of the Grand Riemann Hypothesis; where GCT sits on the ladder above it is decided by the field of definition of the
   canonical basis.

## 1. Setting

- **The split zero** [proved: `H22_note/`, `H22_CHECK_20261121.md`]. For a commutative ring R, G(R) = R ⊔ {τ}, with τ
  the additive identity and a multiplicative absorber. The ring's own zero e = 0_R survives as a *supported zero*:
  idempotent, absorbing on R but not on τ. The map (value, support): G(R) → R × B is an injective semiring map onto
  {(x, b): b = 0 ⇒ x = 0}. Spec G(R) is Spec R with one extra Boolean generic point; G(R)-semimodules are Clifford
  monoids with R-linear fibres (support diagrams); the finitely generated projectives are the P(J, Q) over finite
  posets J. In Connes–Consani's spherical-algebra language the split zero is the refusal to identify a monoid's own
  zero with the basepoint [reading].
- **F_{1²}** [standard: Connes–Consani, arXiv:2609.00299]. F_{1²} = F₁[{0, 1, ε}], ε² = 1; every ring is an
  F_{1²}-algebra through ε ↦ −1; Frobenius exists only for odd n; a square root of the sign, J² = ε, gives their
  twistor line; globally the sign is the Gaussian character χ₋₄. In G(R) the fixed set of ε is {τ, 0} ≅ B when R has
  no 2-torsion: even powers of the sign land in the Boolean pair, odd powers keep ε [proved].
- **GCT** [standard: GCT IV, VII, VIII]. H_r is the Hecke algebra of S_r, normalized by (T_s − v)(T_s + v⁻¹) = 0;
  C′_s = T_s + v⁻¹, C_s = T_s − v; the nonstandard algebra Ȟ_r ⊂ H_r ⊗ H_r is generated by
  P_s = C′_s⊗C′_s + C_s⊗C_s. Write D = (v + v⁻¹)² = q + 2 + q⁻¹ and t = v − v⁻¹, so t² = D − 4. VIII's positivity
  conjecture says that the structure constants of its canonical basis have the form ±t^a f with f bar-invariant,
  positive and unimodal.
- **A** [standard; Alpöge, *Remark*, 20 Nov 2026, Lean-formalized]: RH, and GRH for every Dirichlet L-function, primitive
  or not. Nothing beyond Dirichlet is formalized (Main.lean / Statements.lean of his archive, read 21 Nov).

## 2. Counting with the basepoint

- **Based counts** [proved]. Counting a quotient with its basepoint adds one point: |X₊| = |X| + 1. For F_q this is
  q + 1 = |P¹(F_q)|, the counting function of Connes–Consani's absolute line, and products give (q + 1)^m, the tower of
  powers of P¹ whose weights are unbounded, which is what GCT's obstruction F42 (no single finite Weil object) demands.
- **ζ as the unit** [proved in `consults/CONSULT_a157422a_COUNTING_CONSTRUCTION.md`, Theorem 4; the Witt-ring statement
  standard, Ramachandran 2015, cited from memory]. c ↦ Z_c(s) = Π_j ζ(s − j)^{a_j} is a ring isomorphism: the sum of
  counts is the product of zetas, the product of counts is ζ(s − j) ⋆ ζ(s − k) = ζ(s − j − k), the unit is ζ, and
  bar-invariance of c is the functional equation s ↦ 1 − s of the completed product.
- **Sign-twisted counts** [proved, same file]. For ĉ = Σ_j (a_j⁺ + ε a_j⁻) q^j put
  Ẑ_ĉ(s) = Π_j ζ(s − j)^{a_j⁺} L(s − j, χ₋₄)^{a_j⁻}. At an odd prime p the local factor is the zeta of the count
  Σ_j (a_j⁺ + χ₋₄(p)^n a_j⁻) p^{nj}; the map is multiplicative over Z[1/2] and fails at 2. At split primes the counts
  are dimensions a⁺ + a⁻; at inert primes they are traces a⁺ − a⁻ for n odd and dimensions for n even.
- **The supported zero** [proved, same file, Proposition 4′]. N[ε]/(ε² − 1) → G(Z), a⁺ + ε a⁻ ↦ a⁺ − a⁻ (and 0 ↦ τ),
  is a surjective semiring map. The supported zero e is the image of the nonzero classes of trace 0, the multiples
  of 1 + ε: over F_p with p ≡ 3 mod 4 it is Spec F_{p²}, two conjugate points and no rational one; globally Spec Z[i],
  with zeta ζ(s)L(s, χ₋₄) = ζ_{Q(i)}(s). τ is the empty scheme, with zeta 1.
- **With A** [proved given A]. Every such zeta has its nontrivial zeros on the lines Re s = j + ½. Effectivity of ĉ is
  the absence of inverse factors; the pole orders give the a_j⁺ and the zero densities give the dimensions.
- **Caution** [reading]. "ζ is the unit" holds with Spec Z as the base. In Connes–Consani's absolute picture Spec Z is
  the curve over F₁ and ζ is its zeta; there the counted basepoint contributes the F₁-zeta of a point.

## 3. Rank three

- **Ground ring, every rank** [proved: counting consult, Theorem 1]. The Q(D)-span of the P-words is a Q(D)-form of
  Ȟ_r. In every basis of Q(D)-combinations of P-words every structure constant lies in Q(D) (in Z[D] when the basis
  and relations are integral, as at r = 3). Hence a is even and f is bar-invariant of Tate type with even weights.
- **Closed form** [proved, Theorem 2; computed: `counting_checks.py`, 132 of 132 against agent a8d75352's table].
  Ȟ₃ is presented by P_i² = D·P_i and one braid-type relation; its representations are θ (P_i ↦ D), the zero one, and
  ρ₁, ρ₂ of dimension two with P₁P₂P₁ = λ_i P₁, λ₁ = D, λ₂ = (D − 2)². The basis has a bottom element Σ, two cells
  γ¹, γ² of four elements, and μ = 1; the 132 nonzero constants are 19 + 1 + 16 + 32 + 32 + 32.
- **Positivity in a sharper form** [computed: `consults/E4_scripts/positivity_in_u.py`, 132 of 132]. Every f lies in
  N[u], u = q + q⁻¹. Indeed the factors that occur are D = u + 2, D − 1 = u + 1, D − 2 = u, 5D − 4 = 5u + 6 and
  2D² − 5D + 4 = 2u² + 3u + 2. Since u = |P¹(F_{q²})|/q is the normalized count of R = Res_{F_{q²}/F_q} P¹, every
  rank-three constant is ±t^a times an N-combination of products of R's.
- **The unimodality failures** [computed; found by consult a8d75352, P-F-E3-01, checked by us, not yet re-run
  independently]. Six constants, all equal to t²·[3]_q·u² with [3]_q = q + 1 + q⁻¹, fail VIII's unimodality: f has
  q-coefficients (1, 1, 3, 2, 3, 1, 1). The middle cohomology of R is two classes that Frobenius swaps, trace 0, the
  class 1 + ε: a supported zero. The traces dip; the graded dimensions of the realization R² × P², (1, 5, 11, 14, 11,
  5, 1), are unimodal.
- **Split and inert** [computed]. λ₂(v) = λ₁(v²): the second cell is the first at Frobenius squared, which is how an
  inert prime behaves; the opposite signs of the cells, δ₁ = −δ₂ = −(D − 1)(D − 4), read as the two values of χ₋₄
  [reading]. The six failures and the q = −1 symmetry below both live on the inert cell γ².
- **Special points** [computed: `gram_signpoint.py`, `cell_c3_check.py`, `cell_c3_both_sheets.py`].
  - q = 1 and q = ω: λ₁ = λ₂, the cells merge, and the trace form on the cell has rank one.
  - q = −1 (D = 0): λ₁ = 0 = θ(P_i); the trace form vanishes on the split cell and is monomial on the inert cell
    (γ₁ ↔ γ₂ with weight 64, γ₁₂ and γ₂₁ self-paired with weight 256). The cyclic rotation of the three slots of the
    trace is then an exact symmetry of order 3 of the inert cell's constants (all 64 triples): it fixes the two
    diagonal failures and turns two 3-cycles, each made of two failures and one of the two shortest products. With the
    Dynkin swap it generates C₆; the transpose reverses it, giving D₃.
  - q = i: λ₂ = 0, but no symmetry appears.
  - [standard] q = −1 is exactly where the Hecke algebra's trivial and sign characters coincide: T_s has eigenvalues
    v and −v⁻¹, equal when v² = −1.
  - At generic q the table's symmetries form (Z/2)³ (a8d75352), acting on the cell's labels as an index-3 subgroup of
    the symmetry group of a hexagon: the six failures, labelled by (first letter of x, last letter of x, last letter
    of y), are the cube {1,2}³ minus an antipodal pair [computed: `hexagon_labels.py`].

## 4. Composition: dimension rings

- **Criterion** [proved, counting consult, Theorem 5]. A monoidal category of sign-twisted Tate objects with
  Grothendieck ring free over Z[q^±][ε]/(ε² − 1) on the basis, specializing at ε = −1 to Ȟ₃ with its constants, exists
  iff there is a *dimension ring*: an associative structure on the same basis with constants d ∈ N[q^±], d ≥ |c|
  coefficientwise and d ≡ c mod 2.
- **Existence** [computed: `counting_scripts/`, Theorems 7 and 7′]. Such rings exist at rank three, including one with
  all 132 dimension polynomials unimodal.
- **Obstructions** [proved]. The constant-by-constant realizations do not compose (Proposition 5:
  (γ¹₁γ¹₁)Σ = 8D³(D − 1)²Σ but γ¹₁(γ¹₁Σ) = 16D²(D − 1)²Σ in their dimensions). Every composing ring carries cancelling
  pairs 1 + ε in the bottom cell (Proposition 6): supported zeros that no single constant shows.
- **Selection** [computed: `consults/dimension_ring_minimal_pairs.py`]. Over all 14,975 dimension rings of the searched
  range, the fewest cancelling pairs (7,952, of which 6,892 sit in the bottom cell) occur in exactly one ring, the
  consult's Theorem 7 ring (δ⁺ = D(D − 3) on both cells, the coefficientwise absolute value of δ₂); among the
  unimodal rings the fewest (11,764) occur in exactly one, the Theorem 7′ ring (δ⁺ = (D − 1)(D − 2)). Minimality
  outside the range is [open].

## 5. The reduction, and the ladder

- **Nonstandard RH at rank r** [proved given A, at the realizations above]. Every global zeta of a sign-twisted Tate
  count is pure by A, so purity is free; what is left at rank r is the existence of a dimension ring for the
  canonical basis. Purity was never the gate: GCT's nonstandard RH is an effectivity statement (consult a8d75352,
  finding Q2-d). At rank three the statement holds.
- **Grand RH** [reading, from the above]. The L-functions that occur are ζ(s − j) and L(s − j, χ₋₄): the GL₁ slice. The
  rung is decided by the canonical basis: a basis rational over Q(D) stays on GL₁ (A suffices); one that needs
  v = √q needs odd weights, realized by Gauss and Jacobi sums, that is Hecke characters of CM fields [standard: Weil],
  the ERH rung; pieces that are not CM need GL₂ and beyond. The complexity theorems that use GRH (Koiran's HN ∈ AM,
  Bürgisser's transfer) need Dedekind zeta functions, above A.

## 6. Alpöge's mechanism in these terms

- In the written-out sign-only proof of Deligne's bound (Hexagon 2611.00789), (−1)^{D−1}R_n = Tr(F^n | V^{⊗k}) +
  Tr(F^n | K), D = kd: the alternating trace formula becomes, up to one sign, a trace on an actual module, and the
  power-sum lemma bounds every eigenvalue [proved there]. The argument lives in the effective cone K₀⁺ and runs
  fibre by fibre with split-zero coefficients; the Boolean skeleton has weight 0 [proved: consult af2a18f6, Q3].
- **The sign is a parity** [reading, with a checked core]. For sign-twisted counts, even Frobenius powers give
  dimension counts, honest traces, and bound every eigenvalue; odd powers give traces, which can cancel. So purity
  comes from the even part (where the sign is absorbed, in split-zero terms into the Boolean pair) and positivity and
  unimodality of the odd part are a separate signed condition.
- The same Z/2 appears as the Möbius half-angle (a full turn of the Albert algebra's frame returns −1 on two of its
  three octonion blocks: the centre of Spin(8), cycled by triality, generating A₄ with the frame's 3-cycle) and as
  Connes–Consani's J² = ε [standard and reading].
- **Central against reflected** [computed core, reading]. Alpöge's sign is central. The complement involution of the
  Erdős–Straus divisor box reverses the 3-cycle of origins: a reflected coupling. The two-sheet group (C₃ × C₃) ⋊ C₂
  contains both a C₆ with central C₂ and a D₃; whether the mechanism runs after the lift is [open].
