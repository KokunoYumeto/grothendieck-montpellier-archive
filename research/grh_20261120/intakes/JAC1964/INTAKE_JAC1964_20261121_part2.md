## 1. Verdicts on the new findings

### H9-T17: after a finite cover, the height is not semipositive on the whole homologically trivial group. Targeted proof check, confirmed; I recomputed it.

**The structure.**
- **The orthogonal splitting.** z = z₀ + hD_z, with D_z = ψ_h^{−1} alb(hz). This rests on Kahn, *Refined height pairing*,
  Lemma 6.1 and Theorems 5.6, 6.2 and 6.6.
- **The Albanese-primitive part** is semipositive in characteristic 0 by S.-W. Zhang (arXiv 1001.4788, Theorem 1). That is
  cited, not new.
- **The Lefschetz part** H(hD, hD) = H(D, h²D) is negative definite modulo trace (Kahn 6.6).

**The example.** Take A = E⁴, a Lefschetz pencil of class d·Σa_i, and C = E → P¹ of degree 2 (or of degree N prime to p),
moved off the critical values. Let D come from the Poincaré divisor P = Δ − C×0 − 0×E₁.

What I recomputed:
- H(hD, hD) = ∫_{A×C} (h_A + b)³ P². Only 6d³ a₂a₃a₄ survives against P², and P² = −2 on E×E. So H(hD, hD) = −12d³.
- The positive direction (a₂ − a₃)D gives +4d, and the mixed pairing is 0.
- The norm is zero because [−1]^*P = P^{−1}.

**Bearing.** This refutes QH9-finite-sep without the primitive qualification, in every characteristic. Sound.
- The negative class is Lefschetz (alb(hz) ≠ 0), not primitive.
- It does not contradict H9-T7 over k(P¹), where Pic⁰ is constant.
- It does not contradict the primitive results of H9-T12.

**Novelty.** The phenomenon is classical: divisor heights are negative on Pic⁰ modulo trace. The explicit counterexample to
the unrestricted cover statement, and the split in every characteristic, are this run's.
