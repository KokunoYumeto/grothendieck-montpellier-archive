The Montpellier archive: the plan for all of it

The user, 10 Nov 2026: “download fucking everything”, “organize it on topic”, “Up contrast”; transcription in Pro mode in packages sized to 60-80 minutes of work; and for the plan: “assume we do everything. This is the way to do everything, and this is the order in which we probably want to do that”. What each section holds, how far Grothendieck took it and who has edited it: SECTIONS_STATE.md (10 Nov). Estimates below come from our own record; the first package corrects them.

Scope

  • 178 cotes, 16,074 site pages (versos included). About 11,300 pages have no edition, transcription or study.
  • Already ours: La Longue Marche (cote 140, 1,584 pp.): transcription done, second pass running. Les Dérivateurs (157-160): the Künzer-Malgoire-Maltsiniotis edition is complete; our companion covers 28 of its 32 chunks.
  • Complete outside editions: Pursuing Stacks (134-135; arXiv 2021, SMF vol. I) and the Esquisse (in print in French and English). These are transcribed last, and serve as checks meanwhile.

The stages, for every section

  1. Download, organize, enhance (running since 10 Nov): archive_tool.py download puts every cote in its topic folder (30 of 178 cotes, 1.2 GB on 10 Nov at 23:00 local; the server is slow). Packages use the Longue Marche image recipe.
  2. Transcription by GPT-6 Astra in ChatGPT Pro mode.
    • Each prompt gets one zip of about 45 scans, sized to 60-80 minutes; the Longue Marche’s 20-scan packages took 23-30 minutes.
    • Output: diplomatic LaTeX, a table of doubts, an inventory, pointers, one file per page and a time log (ARCHIVE_PROMPT_TEMPLATE.md).
    • The first package (MPL_1_002-050) sets the size.
    • About 260 prompts for everything, fewer once blank sheets drop out. Pro mode may be merged by the end of November; the user would buy a second Pro account for about 100 more prompts.
  3. Second pass by Claude subagents, as for the Longue Marche: each package checked against its scans, then the global view across each section. It is the largest subscription cost, so it runs in the Ultra weeks (the Fable half first: account plan on card plan-now).
  4. Editions on GitHub, published per book and chapter of the shape below, with the cote order as the second index (Fable, 10 Nov); per section: the diplomatic French and a reading text, anchored to cote and scan, with the rights statement and credit to every earlier edition used as a check.
  5. English translation with the method agreed on 10 Nov for the Longue Marche and Les Dérivateurs:
    • a glossary and notation table made by Fable;
    • scripts that lock the formulas and check every unit;
    • the model and route chosen on a small comparison, at the quality of Opus 5.5 at high effort or better.
    • At our transcription price as a ceiling ($0.065 a page), that is about $700 for 11,300 pages; the comparison sets the real figure.
  6. Development, for the sections left as research. Either a companion in the Dérivateurs style (commentary blocks, later work, examples, what we could push), or a book in the Longue Marche style (his text, then built out).
    • Candidates: Géométrie des formes, combinatorial geometry, the notes around the Longue Marche (into the LM book), De Rham, motives, stratified spaces.
    • EGA V and after. The user, 10 Nov: “It’s almost like write EGA5 then”; “whatever stuff would otherwise gone there, that isn’t already in any of the EGAs”; “there might be more basics implied by the working through of this that should be part of that concept”; “regardless, it should be done. What it ends up being called it’s not necessarily the” point.
      • Scope: his EGA drafts and plans (cotes 21-26), plus the foundational material scattered through the rest of the archive. What EGA I-IV, SGA or FGA already contain is cited, not redone.
      • Authorship as it is: his notes and plans, transcribed; the development AI-produced, with each part’s author and model named (“we don’t have to pretend that it was written by the original authors”).
      • Title open. First step, once the EGA drafts are transcribed: a map of what they contain against EGA I-IV, SGA and FGA.

Shape and order: Fable’s recommendation, adopted (10 Nov, late)

Source: ORGANIZATION_RECOMMENDATION.md (Claude Fable 5.2), asked for by the user: “how to organize the whole fucking blob of this shit in a way that makes it something that people click on and want to read eventually.”

  • Working title The Montpellier Manuscripts.
    • Front door: the annotated Esquisse, each section linking to the book behind it.
    • Six books: I. Elements, continued (“EGA V and after”); II. Cohomology and motives; III. The Long March; IV. Maps and polyhedra; V. Tame topology and the geometry of forms; VI. Stacks and derivators.
    • A workshop: early notes, documents, and the concordance from cote and scan to book and chapter.
  • Every page in three layers: what the folder is and what became of it; his text, French and English, linked to the scan; commentary and, where he left the work open, a development with full proofs. His text and ours never share a paragraph. The second pass also produces a register of every question and conjecture in the notes, with its status today.
  • Transcription order, after the pilot (cote 1):
    1. around the Long March: 147, 149, 146, 142;
    2. the 1960s test: 16, 47, 54, 44;
    3. the EGA drafts and plans: 21, 22, 23, 26, 35, 48, 102;
    4. the dated notes around the Long March: 144, 148, 143, 145, 141;
    5. stratified spaces and tame topology: 150-153, 155, 120, 121;
    6. motives: 14, 13, 12, 15, 18;
    7. crystals: 5-9, 4, 100;
    8. the 1976 de Rham notes: 137, 136;
    9. around Pursuing Stacks and the derivators: 158, 103-118, 135;
    10. maps and their arithmetic: 138, 76, 88, 70, 75, 161-6;
    11. the final state of the Géométrie des formes: 156-8, 156-1 to 156-3, 156-5, 156-9.
    These come to about 4,200 pages, the first tranche. The rest follows Fable’s file.
  • Clean typescripts (24; 25, “Nouveau EGA V”, early; 119, 134, 157, 162-5) go by the clean-scan route over the API, not Pro.
  • Before packaging, an inventory of the versos, so that unrelated pages (administrative circulars, other people’s typescripts) are left out and listed.
  • The download follows this order (DOWNLOAD_PRIORITY.txt).

Claude’s earlier order (the Géométrie des formes second, the combinatorial block whole) is superseded.
