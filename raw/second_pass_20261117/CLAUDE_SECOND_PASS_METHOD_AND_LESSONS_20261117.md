# The Claude second pass: method, what the first one taught, and how to run the next (17 Nov 2026)

The main session (Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max) wrote this on 17 Nov 2026, just before a compaction, at the user's
request ("check what the methodology was the first time we did the second pass ... if there's anything from that you
need to recall"). Sources:
- the RUNBOOK of 13 Nov, ~18:1x-23:0x UTC: the pilot, wave 2 and wave 3;
- the block prompts in `second_pass/prompts/`, generated by `make_second_pass.py`;
- the outputs in `second_pass/out/`;
- `SECOND_PASS_ROUTES_20261117.md` and `SECOND_PASS_PLAN_20261115.md`.

## 1. The method as built (it worked)

- **Blocks.** One agent per block of about 15-19 scans, one Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max each, in waves of ten.
- **Inputs per block:**
  - the first pass, one TeX file per scan;
  - the package's notes, doubts and inventory;
  - the prompt it ran on (its macros and conventions);
  - the edition matters of the cote (`edition_matters_20261117/`, added 17 Nov);
  - three images per scan: the whole page and two halves.

  The checker crops, enlarges and rotates with PIL where needed.
- **Method:**
  - check line by line against both halves;
  - settle every doubt mark the ink allows;
  - look for what was missed (conditions, symbols, margin notes, whole lines);
  - correct only where the image settles it;
  - keep the first pass's conventions;
  - never expand, modernise or complete;
  - do not guess.
- **Outputs per block:**
  - corrected `scanNNN.tex` files;
  - `*_changes.md`: each change with the old reading, the new one, what the image shows, and a confidence of high,
    medium or low;
  - `*_notes_checked.md`: Pro's research notes read against the corrected text.

  The final message gives the counts and the consequential corrections.

## 3. Lessons to apply next time

1. **Unresolved places pile up.** The pilot left 148 unresolved places in 51 scans, nearly three a scan. A second
   reader at the same effort does not settle the hardest ink. Those places need a different witness: Hua's
   transcription, a second Pro run, or the user's eye on a few. Count them per block, and route them, do not just
   record them.
2. **Conventions matter.** Corrections must follow the first pass's diplomatic conventions. Case: cote 147, scan 063,
   the struck hats of (78), handled by the convention of scan 030. Give every block the convention notes of its cote.
3. **The notes check is where Claude adds most per token.** It catches readings that change a research note, and so a
   prompt, an edition note or a claim. Those are what the field experts need. It needs the text, not the images.
4. **Consequential corrections go out the same day:** into the edition, the waiting prompts, and the experts' files.
   That loop worked on 13 Nov (LM5) and should be part of the procedure.
5. **Measure first.** Run two or three blocks, read the counts and the token use, then decide the scale. Do not
   launch waves before the pilot's numbers are read.

## 4. How to run the next one (the main session's recommendation, 17 Nov)

The full image check of every page by Claude costs about a week or more of the account. Split the work by what each
model does best and cheapest:
- **Pro does the text-against-scan grinding.** The 12 two-witness Pro second passes built on 15 Nov are kept in
  `second_pass_pro_20261114/_MPL_second_passes_for_the_claude_route_20261117/` and fit the transcription slots. They
  cost no money.
- **Claude does:**
  - the notes check, organized for the field experts: which notes bear on which runs, prompts and companions. Text
    only, so far cheaper than 26k a scan;
  - the places Pro's second pass leaves unsettled;
  - full image checks where they matter most: the Longue Marche neighbourhood (cotes 141-152) and anything a
    companion quotes.
- **Pilot first:** two packages, notes only. Measure the tokens per scan, then scale.

The user decides the route; this file records the facts and the method so the next cycle starts from them.
