  whose head can be seen is `¤longrightarrow`; a stroke without a head is `¤mathbin{-}` or a dash). I wrote an arrow
  where a crop shows the head (rows 23, 37, 38, 40, 41). Strokes I did not enlarge are left as they were: 007 (20),
  013 (9), and B_γ to Aut on 014.
- **Struck blocks.** The first pass's `¤deleted` is kept for the cancelled blocks: the top of 004, 009 L9-14, and 011
  (8) with its margin. The left-margin note on 011 is crossed by parallel diagonal hatching strokes (crop s_misc), so it
  stays deleted. Hua batch-01 p. 10 describes it instead as framed («encadrée d'un trait»).
- **Insertions.** As in the first pass, `¤inserted{...}` goes where the caret or connecting line ends (rows 13, 16, 21,
  54).
- **Pagination.** The first pass's `¤mspage` for both numeral series is kept; see own note O5 in the notes file.
