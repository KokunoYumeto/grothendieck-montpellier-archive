Massy, 3.11.1970\\
\end{flushright}
Dear Deligne,\\

Could you tell me whether you have finished looking at Rim's talk SGA 7 VI, and if so, send it to me with your comments (if not, tell me whether you intend to look at it and when)?

I have asked Miss Altazin, who typed your talk SGA 4 XVIII, to send it to you with the manuscript, so that you can correct it. Could you tell me whether you have received it and whether you intend to make the corrections? When they are done, or if you do not want to make them, send me the clean copy of the text please.

Have you had any news from Ferrand about SGA 6 XI?

Very cordially\\
