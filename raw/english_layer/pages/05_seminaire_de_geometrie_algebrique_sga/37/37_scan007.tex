Thus, I found myself working for three years in an institution while it was participating without my knowledge in a mode of financing that I consider profoundly immoral and extremely dangerous. Being now the only one to hold this opinion among my colleagues at the IHES, which has doomed to failure my efforts to obtain the removal of military subsidies from the IHES budget, I have taken the decision that was called for and am leaving the IHES on 30 September next, and am also suspending all scientific collaboration with this Institution, as long as it continues to accept such subsidies. Mr. L. Motchane, director of the IHES, has declared himself in agreement that, from 1\textsuperscript{st} October 1970, the IHES should no longer distribute the mathematical texts of which I am the author, or forming part of the Bois Marie Seminar in Algebraic Geometry. As was said above, the distribution of this Seminar is going to be handled by the firm Julius Springer, in the Lecture Notes series. I am happy to thank here the firm Springer and Mr. K. Peters for the effective and courteous help they have given me to make this publication possible, by taking on in particular the typing for photo-offset of the new lectures added to the earlier seminars, and of the missing lectures of the incomplete seminars.
