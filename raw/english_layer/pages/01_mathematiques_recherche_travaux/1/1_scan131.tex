Suppose first $p<1$, then we have, by
a classical upper-bound formula for the
sum of the $p$\textsuperscript{th} powers of the reciprocals of the
moduli of the zeros of an entire function
\[
f(z)=\det(1-zA)\qquad(0<p<1)
\]
\[
s_p(A)\leq p^2\int_0^\infty\frac{\log M_A(r)}{r^{p+1}}\,dr\tag*{\deleted{(10)}}
\]
now $M_A(\unsure{v|r})\leq M_{|A|}(\unsure{v|r})$ (\unsure{\ldots\ --\ --}) whence
\[
s_p(A)\leq p^2\int_0^\infty\frac{\log M_{|A|}(\unsure{v|r})}{{\unsure{v|r}}^{\unsure{p-1|p+1}}}\,dr
\]
\,; then applying formula
(9), we thus obtain the bound
\[
s_p(A)\leq\frac{p\pi}{\sin p\pi}S_p(A)\tag*{(10)}
\]
The factor $\frac{p\pi}{\sin p\pi}$ is $>1$, but tends to 1
as $p\to0$. Let then $0<p\unsure{\leq|<}+\infty$ be arbitrary,
and let $n$ be an integer $>0$ such that $\frac{p}{n}<1$.
We obviously have $s_p(A)=s_{p/n}(A^n)$, whence, by
virtue of (10) :
\[
s_p(A)\leq\frac{\frac{p}{n}\pi}{\sin\frac{p}{n}\pi}S_{p/n}(A^n)
\]
Now, we have \deleted{\unsure{$S_{p/n}(A^n)\leq S_p(A)$}}
\[
\|A^n\|_{p/n}\leq\underbrace{\|A\|_p\ldots\|A\|_p}_{n\ \text{factors}},
\]
as follows at once from formula (6), which will be.
proved below (then it is immediate
that this formula generalizes to the product of an
arbitrary finite number of operators), whence
\[
S_{p/n}(A^{\unsure{{-}|n}})=\left(\|A^n\|_{p/n}\right)^{p/n}
\leq\|A\|_p^p=S_p(A).
\]
