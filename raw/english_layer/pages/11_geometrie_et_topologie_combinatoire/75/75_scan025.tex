\uline{Orientations}

a) Real vector space $E$ of finite dimension $n$. The datum of $E$ is equivalent to that of the right torsor
\[
 P=\operatorname{Isom}(\mathbb R^n,E)\simeq\operatorname{bases}(E)
\]
under
\[
 G=\operatorname{Gl}(n,\mathbb R).
\]

We have a homomorphism
\diagram{\begin{tikzcd}[ampersand replacement=\&,column sep=small,row sep=small]
 G=\operatorname{Gl}(n,\deleted{\unsure{R|k}}\mathbb R)\arrow[rr,"s"]\arrow[dr,"\det"']
 \& {} \& \{\pm1\} \& u\longmapsto\deleted{\det}\,\operatorname{sgn}\det u\\
 {} \& \mathbb R^{\ast}\arrow[ur,"\operatorname{sgn}"'] \& {}
 \& \text{kernel }\operatorname{Gl}^+(n,\mathbb R)\,,
\end{tikzcd}}
and
\[
 \omega_E=P\times_G\{\pm1,s\}\simeq P/\operatorname{Gl}^+(n,R)
\]
is the set of orientations of $E$.

b) Free \inserted{\unsure{quadratic|quadratic}} module \deleted{\unsure{quadratic|point}} \inserted{$E$} over a commutative ring $k$ \unsure{(where|where} $2$ is invertible [$\exists$ orthonormal basis, $\deleted{\unsure{Q(e_i,e|Q(e_i,e_j)}} e_i^2=1$, $e_i e_j=0$ if $i\ne j$]

The datum of $E$ of rank $n$ is equivalent to that of the right torsor
\[
 P=\operatorname{Isom}_{k\text{-quadr.}}(k^n,E)\simeq\text{orthonormal bases}(E)
\]
under
\[
 G=\operatorname{O}(n,k)=\operatorname{Aut}_{k\text{-quadr}}(k^n,Q_n)\subset\operatorname{Gl}(n\unsure{.|,}k)
\]

\unsure{Now|We} here
\[
 G=\operatorname{O}(n,k)\xrightarrow{\substack{\det=s\\\unsure{\deleted{\operatorname{sgn}}}}}\{\pm1\}\qquad(\text{kernel }\operatorname{SO}(n,k))
\]
and the set of orientations of $E$ is (by definition)
\[
 \omega_E=P\times_G(\pm1,s)\simeq P/\operatorname{SO}(n,k).
\]

\uline{Compatibility} \uline{between a) and b)} Follows from the transitivity of the operation of extension of the group of operators in a torsor, and from the commutative diagram
\diagram{\begin{tikzcd}[ampersand replacement=\&,column sep=large]

\end{tikzcd}}
