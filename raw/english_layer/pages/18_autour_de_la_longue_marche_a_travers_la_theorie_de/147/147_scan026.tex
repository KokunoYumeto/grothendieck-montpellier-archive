Trivial MD-graph, having a single vertex of genus $g$, of marked weight $\nu$, and no edges, gives back the canonical open immersion
\[
(26)\qquadM_{g,\nu}\hookrightarrow\widehat{M}_{g,\nu}.
\]
Identifying the $M_{[G]}$ with sub-multiplicities of $\widehat{M}_{g,\nu}$, one sees that the $M_{[G]}$ (for all possible types of \unsure{\deleted{\unsure{MD}}|M\deleted{\unsure{D}}}-graphs, of numerical type $(g,\nu)$) form a \uline{partition} of $\widehat{M}_{g,\nu}$ into a finite number of sub-multiplicities.

\uline{Remark} Let us consider the closure of an $M_{[G]}$ in $\widehat{M}_{g,\nu}$, one finds that it is a finite union of\deleted{\unsure{s}} $M_{[G']}$. One says that $[G']$ is a specialization of $[G]$ if $M_{G'}\subset\overline{M_G}$ in $\widehat{M}_{g,\nu}$.
