2. The question of full faithfulness.

Let $K,K'$ be two finitely generated extensions of $\mathbb Q$ --- is it true that every $\Pi_{\mathbb Q}$-homomorphism $\Pi_K-\Pi_{K'}$ comes from a field homomorphism $K'\longrightarrow K$?
