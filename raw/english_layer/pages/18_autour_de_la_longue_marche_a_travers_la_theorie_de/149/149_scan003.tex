1. \uline{Faithfulness results}

To every field $K$, let us associate its étale topos $B_K$, which is a (pro\inserted{finite})Galois topos. The groupoid of points of $B_K$ is denoted by $\Pi_K$, it is canonically anti-equivalent to the category of separable algebraic closures of $K$. If $\bar K$ is such a closure, its group of \inserted{$K$-}automorphisms $\operatorname{Gal}(\bar K/K)$ or $E_{\bar K/K}$ is identified with the group of \deleted{$\unsure{K}$-}automorphisms of the $p^t$ \inserted{of $B_K$} associated with $\bar K/K$ (it is perhaps better to say: with the opposite of this group -- \deleted{$\bar K$} the variance of the algebraic\unsure{.|-} closures of $K$ is like that of the fiber functors, opposite to that of the points...)

Of course, $B_K$ is reconstructed from $\Pi_K$, as the topos of (continuous) local systems on $\Pi_K$ -- and in terms of $E_{\bar K/K}$, as the topos of discrete Sets with a continuous action of $E_{\bar K/K}$.
