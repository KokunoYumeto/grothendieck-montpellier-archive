How to lift the \inserted{Sylow} \inserted{2-}subgroups $\simeq\mathbb Z/2\times\mathbb Z/2$ of $\Gamma$, generated resp. by $\{\sigma_0,\tau\},\{\sigma_1,\tau\},\{\sigma_\infty,\tau\}$? Well precisely, they do \uline{not} lift --- it is well known that the local extensions of $\Gamma_0^+$ \inserted{(for example)} by $\mathbb Z_0$ (quotient of $\mathbb Z^{\{0,1,\infty\}}$ by $\mathbb Z^{\{1,\infty\}}$) is \uline{not} split, i.e. the generator $\dot\sigma_0$ of $\Gamma_0^+$ does \uline{not} lift to an element of order $2$ of $\mathcal E$ which centralizes (or what amounts to the same, normalizes) $L_0$.

One could amuse oneself by writing $S\Gamma$ and $S\mathcal E$ in terms of generators and relations --- I am not going to dwell on it. On the other hand, I am going to give formulas for the action of the $\operatorname{int}(\tilde\rho)$ and of the $\operatorname{int}(\tilde\sigma_i)$. One finds
\[
(15)\qquad(\operatorname{int}\tilde\rho)(p,\sigma,g_0,g_1,g_\infty)=(p,\dot\rho\sigma\dot\rho^{-1},\rho(g_\infty),\rho(g_0),\rho(g_1))
\]
\[
(16)\qquad\operatorname{int}(\tilde\sigma_0)(p,\sigma,g_0,g_1,g_\infty)=(p,\dot\sigma_0\sigma\dot\sigma_0^{-1},\sigma_0(g_0),\sigma_0(g_\infty),\sigma_0(g_1))
\]
One thus sees: a) For $\tilde u=(p,\unsure{\sigma|\tau},g_0,g_1,g_\infty)$ to commute with $\rho$, it is necessary and sufficient that \deleted{$g_0,g_1,g_\infty$ be} $\sigma$ commute with $\rho$ i.e. $\sigma\in\{1,\rho,\rho^2\}$, \uline{and} that one have
\[
(17)\qquad g_1=\rho(g_0),\quad g_\infty=\rho(g_1),\quad g_0=\rho(g_\infty)
\]
Which also means that $g_1,g_\infty$ are deduced from $g_0$ by $g_1=\rho(g_0),\ g_\infty=\rho(g_1)=\rho^2(g_0)=\rho^{-1}(g_0)$
