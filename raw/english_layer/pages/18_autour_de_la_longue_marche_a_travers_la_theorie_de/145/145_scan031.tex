One can also take as sole generators $\rho,\sigma_0,\tau_0$ (from which $\sigma_1,\tau_1$, \inserted{$\sigma_\infty,\tau_\infty$,} are deduced as $\rho\sigma_0\rho^{-1},\rho\tau_0\rho^{-1},\rho^2\sigma_0\rho^{-2},\rho^2\tau_0\rho^{-2}$) and the relations
\[
\left\{\begin{aligned}
\text{i)}\quad&\sigma_0^2=\tau_0^2=1\quad\tau_0\sigma_0=\sigma_0\tau_0\quad\text{(i.e. }(\overbrace{\sigma_0\tau_0}^{\tilde\sigma_0})^2=1\text{)}\\
\text{b)}\quad&\rho^3=1\\
&\sigma_0\underbrace{\rho\sigma_0^{-1}\rho^{-1}}_{\sigma_1}=\underbrace{\rho^2\cdot\tau_0\rho^{-2}}_{\tau_\infty}\underbrace{\rho\tau_0\rho^{-1}}_{\tau_1}\rho\\
&\text{i.e. }\sigma_0\rho\sigma_0^{-1}\rho^{-1}=\rho^{-1}\tau_0\rho\tau_0\\
&\text{or finally }\rho\sigma_0\rho^{-1}\sigma_0=\tau_0\rho^{-1}\tau_0\rho\quad\text{that is}\\
&\text{that is}\quad[\rho,\sigma_0][\rho^{-1},\tau_0]=1
\end{aligned}\right.
\]
\margin{\unsure{\deleted{i.e.}|i.e.} or again $\deleted{\operatorname{int}(\rho)\sigma_0}\quad[\rho,\sigma_0]=[\tau_0,\rho^{-1}]$}

It is therefore a quotient of the free product $(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)*\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z$, by the normal subgroup described by the relation $[\rho,\sigma_0][\rho^{-1},\tau_0]=1$.

\uline{NB} One finds a symmetry in the group, which exchanges $\sigma_0$ and $\tau_0$, and exchanges $\rho$ and $\rho^{-1}$. [thus which also exchanges $\sigma_1$ and $\tau_\infty$, $\sigma_\infty$ and $\tau_1$] --- but this symmetry is not compatible with the extension structure, as one sees at \deleted{\unsure{s}} at once (on\unsure{:|;} on passing to the quotient $\unsure{\mathfrak S_3|\mathcal S_3}\times\mathbb Z/2$, it should preserve the center!).
