Additional axiom
\[
 (M5)\quad \forall n,\quad \mathcal M_n\text{ stable under finite unions}
\]
\[
 (\Longleftrightarrow\ \varnothing\in\mathcal M_n,\ \deleted{\unsure{\delta}}\text{ and }A,B\in\mathcal M_n\Longrightarrow A\cup B\in\mathcal M_n)
\]
\uline{Proposition} Tame subsets of a tame space stable under finite unions.

\uline{Proposition} On $\varnothing$ there is a unique tame structure, which makes it an initial object of $\operatorname{Esp}_{\mathcal M_*}$.

\uline{Proposition} Let $X$ be a tame space, $(X_i)_{i\in I}$ a finite family of tame subsets of $X$. Then $\bigcup X_i$ is a tame subset of $X$. If $X=\bigcup_iX_i$, then $X$ is an amalgamated sum of the $X_i$ along the $X_i\cap X_j$ --- a map $f:X\longrightarrow Y$ \inserted{between tame spaces} is tame if and only if its restrictions to the $X_i$ are.

\uline{Question} Let us take
