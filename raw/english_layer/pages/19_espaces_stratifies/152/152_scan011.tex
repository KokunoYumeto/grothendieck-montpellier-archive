% L01
Returning to the case of a \inserted{general} circularized graph $\Gamma$,
% L02
Topologically, the topological graph $|\Gamma|$
% L03
is obtained from $|C|$ by \deleted{\unsure{the identifications}}
% L04
\unsure{a|i.e.} quotient by \unsure{the|C} following equivalence %
% L05
relation: one glues together
% L06
the edge $a$ and the other $\unsure{\sigma_C a|\sigma_C^a}$, \uline{reversing} the
% L07
orientations induced on these \unsure{edges|sides}.

% L08
In the case where $\Gamma$ is a
% L09
tree, one can interpret
% L10
it \inserted{topologically${}^{t}$} by considering
% L11
$C$ as the boundary
% L12
of an oriented disk $D$,
% L13
subdivided into \unsure{edges|sides}, and
% L14
by joining the ``centers''
% L15
of the \unsure{edges|sides} that correspond to one another
% L16
under $\sigma_C$, in such a way that the seg-\linebreak
% L17
ments obtained do not meet.
