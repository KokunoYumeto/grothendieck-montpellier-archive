The non-\uline{empty} $X_i^*$ then form a partition of $X$. The $X_i^*$ are the ``open strata''.

One sometimes introduces additional axioms:

(Strat 4) (faithfulness) $\forall i\in I$, $X_i^*\neq\phi$.

This axiom has the disadvantage of not being stable under \deleted{\unsure{the|r}} restriction to an open subset of $X$. It is sometimes supplemented by the stronger even less stable axiom

(Strat 4 bis) (Canonicity of $I$) the $X_i^*$ are $0$-connected i.e. connected nonempty,

(Strat 5) \inserted{(density) $\forall i\in I$,} $X_i^*$ dense in $X_i$, \inserted{i.e. $X_i=\overline{X_i^*}$;} i.e. $j<i\Rightarrow X_j$ \uline{nowhere dense} in $X_i$

This axiom has the advantage of being stable under restriction to an open subset. But we shall assume (Strat 4) (or even Strat 4 bis) or (Strat 5) only when expressly stated.
