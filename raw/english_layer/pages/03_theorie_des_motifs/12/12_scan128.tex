COROLLARY 7.- \uline{Let} $C$ \uline{be a chamber and} $x\in E$. \uline{There exists an element} $y$ \uline{of} $\overline{C}$ \uline{and only one and an element} $g\in G$ \uline{such that} $x=g(y)$.

Indeed the union of the chambers is the complement of $\displaystyle\handwritten{\bigcup}_{H\in\underline{H}}H$, hence is dense in $E$. Thus $x$ is adherent to a chamber $g(C)$ $(g\in G)$, say $x=g(y)$ with $y\in\overline{C}$. The uniqueness of $y$ then follows from corollary 5 to theorem 2, \S\ 2, number 3.

COROLLARY 8.- \uline{Let} $x\in E$ \uline{and} $G'$ \uline{be the subgroup of} $G$ \uline{consisting of the} $g\in G$ \uline{such that} $g(x)=x$. \uline{Then} $G'$ \uline{is generated by the reflections} $s_H$ $(H\in\underline{H})$ \uline{such that} $s_H(x)=x$.

We may suppose $G$ essential, and that $x$ is adherent to the chamber $C$. Then $x$ belongs to a face $C_J$ $(J\handwritten{\subset\{}1,2,\ldots,m\handwritten{\}})$ of $C$ (with the notation of \S\ 2, number 3). For $g\in G'$, we have $C_J\handwritten{\cap}g(C_J)\handwritten{\ne\varnothing}$, whence $gG_J=G_J$ (\S\ 2, number 3, theorem 2), and $G'\handwritten{\subset}G_J$. Since $G_J$ leaves $C_J$, and therefore $x$, invariant, we have $G'=G_J$. Thus $G'$ is generated by the reflections $s_i$ for $i\handwritten{\notin}J$.

2. \uline{Affine groups generated by reflections}.

\uline{In this number}, $G$ \uline{denotes a subgroup of the affine group of} $E$ (Algebra II, second edition, Appendix II, number 4), \uline{generated by reflections with respect to affine hyperplanes}. \uline{We assume that} $G$, \uline{equipped with the discrete topology}, \uline{acts properly on} $E$ (General Topology, III, \S\ 4).

To every affine map $s$, let us assign the linear map ${}^vs$ associated with $s$ (Algebra II, second edition, Appendix II, number 4). If $s$ is the reflection with respect to a hyperplane $H$, ${}^vs$ is the (linear) reflection with respect to the direction ${}^vH$ of $H$ (in the same place, number 3). Thus $s\handwritten{\rightsquigarrow}{}^vs$ is a homomorphism from $G$ onto a subgroup ${}^vG$ of the orthogonal group of $E$, generated by reflections.
