COROLLARY 2.- \uline{For every} $H\in\underline{H}$, \uline{let} $e_H$ \uline{be the unit vector orthogonal to} $H$ \uline{and lying on the same side of} $H$ \uline{as} $C$. \uline{Then} $e_H$ \uline{is a linear combination with positive coefficients of} $e_1,\ldots,e_m$.

Indeed, by proposition 3, we have $\displaystyle e_H=\handwritten{\sum}_{j\in I}b_j e_j$ with $b_j\in R$, whence $b_j=(e_H\handwritten{|}e'_j)$. Since $e'_j\in\overline{C}$, and since $e_H$ is on the same side of $H$ as $C$, we have $(e_H\handwritten{|}e'_j)\handwritten{\geq}0$.

COROLLARY 3.- \uline{With the same notation}, $s_i$ \uline{leaves invariant the set of} $e_H$ \uline{distinct from} $e_i$.

For $e_H\handwritten{\ne}e_i$, we have $\displaystyle e_H=\handwritten{\sum}_{j=1}^m a_j e_j$ where the $a_j$ are positive real numbers (corollary 2), and where one of the $a_j$, for $j\handwritten{\ne}i$, is $>0$. The vector $s_i(e_H)=e_H-2(e_H\handwritten{|}e_i)e_i$ is either one of the $e_{H'}$ or one of their negatives ; now, for $j\handwritten{\ne}i$, the coefficient of $e_j$ in the decomposition of $s_i(e_H)$ is $a_j$ ; hence $s_i(e_H)$ cannot be of the form $-e_{H'}$ since these $a_j$ are not all $\handwritten{\leq}0$ (corollary 2). Thus $s_i(e_H)$ is of the form $e_{H'}$. Which was to be proved.

COROLLARY 4.- \uline{The order of} $G$ \uline{is equal to the number of chambers defined by} $\underline{H}$.

This follows at once from b).

COROLLARY 5.- \uline{With the notation of theorem 1}, \uline{the set of reflections} $s_H$ $(H\in\underline{H})$ \uline{is necessarily the set of all reflections of} $G$.

Let $\underline{H}'\handwritten{\supset}\underline{H}$ be the set of hyperplanes $H$ such that $s_H\in G$. Every chamber relative to $\underline{H}'$ is contained in a chamber relative to $\underline{H}$ and only one. By corollary 4, the chambers relative to $\underline{H}'$ and to $\underline{H}$ are therefore the same. By lemma 3 of \S\ 1, number 1, every element of $\underline{H}'$ is a wall of a chamber, hence belongs to $\underline{H}$.

COROLLARY 6.- \uline{Let} $C$ \uline{and} $C'$ \uline{be two chambers}. \uline{Then} $\overline{C}\handwritten{\cap}\overline{C'}$ \uline{is the closure of a face} $F$ \uline{of} $C$. \uline{The element} $g\in G$ \uline{such that} $C'=g(C)$ \uline{is a product of reflections} $s_H$ $(H\in\underline{H})$ \uline{with respect to hyperplanes} $H$ \uline{containing} $F$.

This is a translation of lemma 7, \S\ 2, number 4.
