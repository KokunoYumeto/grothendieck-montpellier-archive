\subsection{The same low-degree cleanup for a family of free cells}

\begin{corollary}[AT25: Proved; mixed-cell extension of the carrier cleanup]
\label{AT25}
Work over $\mathbb Z_{(2)}$.
Let $X\to A,B$ have the source and target hypotheses
of AT24, now with a specified set of normalized
simplex-two free odd cells $x_i,y_i=dx_i$.
Their actual boundaries are $\partial x_i=dh_i$,
and their weights $b_i$ have a common positive lower
bound $b_*$. Suppose the bounds of AT20 hold uniformly:
one power $N=2^e$ is at least both row-zero cutoffs,
\[
 b_*\geq\max\{3,2N,\mu_A(1),\mu_B(1)\},\qquad
 2b_*+1\geq\max\{\lambda_A(4),\lambda_B(4)\}.
 \tag{AT.167}
\]
In simplex two require the actual presentation
\[
 X_2=C\otimes_R
       \bigotimes_i\bigl(\Lambda_R(x_i)\otimes_R\Gamma_R(y_i)\bigr),
 \tag{AT.168}
\]
where $C$ is $S$ tensored with the free PD algebra
on the other $R$-linear identity disks.
For every selected cell make the resolved
$I_1\langle2b_i\rangle$ extension for
$(\gamma_2(y_i),x_i y_i)$, and take the torsionfree
image of their simultaneous extension.

There are actual chosen target maps and an iterated
cleanup with every conclusion of AT24 in simplex two.
In particular it kills all mixed higher-arity
cohomology in forms one through three there, while
retaining the first skeleton, the rational coefficient
comparison, coefficient $H^0$, support and actual
maps to both targets.
The optional horizontal restoration of AT24 again
leaves simplex two unchanged.

Here cell arity counts every $x_i,y_i$ as one and
every original resolved generator as two.
No assertion about arbitrary later degree-four
repairs is included.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
For each $i$ perform the actual AT20 construction
using the same $N$ and its own elements
$q_i,\alpha_i,x_i',y_i'$.
The normalized $D(3)$ resolutions are free over
the same coefficient algebra and may be filled
independently. Their twists only involve the
corresponding $x_i,y_i$.
Consequently the free presentation (AT.168) and
the simultaneous relative PD universal property
give actual source maps and a single carrier
\[
                            F_A:Y_2\longrightarrow A_3.
 \tag{AT.169}
\]
On $C$ it is $s_0f_A$.
On each old pair it is (AT.158), and on that pair's
resolved module it has its corresponding correction
$-\varepsilon(t)(s_0q_i)\,da_{3,i}$ on $v_{0,i}(t)$.
All differential and coefficient-relation checks
are those already performed for each independent
summand in AT24. No orthogonality between the
target images of distinct cells is required:
their products are the products dictated by the
free PD source.

The first-face table (AT.160) holds for each $i$.
Every nonzero positive-arity reference image
still has form degree at least its total cell
arity. Thus (AT.161) holds for the common
grading, even on mixed polynomials.
Every positive-arity element has weight at least
its arity times $b_*$.

We check the low arities explicitly.
Arity one is the direct sum of the identity
complexes $x_i\mapsto y_i$, tensored with $C$.
For arity two the diagonal summand for each $i$
is exactly the combined original and resolved
complex of AT24, with injective connecting map
$I_1\langle2b_i\rangle/2\hookrightarrow
S\langle2b_i\rangle/2$.
The additional mixed summand for $i<j$ is
the actual three-term complex
\[
 \begin{split}
 S(x_i x_j)&\longrightarrow
 S(y_i x_j)\oplus S(x_i y_j)
                \longrightarrow S(y_i y_j),\\
 1&\longmapsto(1,-1),\qquad
 (a,b)\longmapsto a+b ,
 \end{split}
 \tag{AT.170}
\]
in forms two, three and four, with its evident
weight $b_i+b_j$.
The two displayed maps show exactness over $S$:
the first is injective, its image is the kernel
of the second, and the second is surjective.
Thus all arity-two cohomology in degrees at most
three vanishes. The tensor with $C$ is justified
by the same free $R$ complexes and finite-degree
$\operatorname{Tor}_1$ calculation in AT24.
The decomposition is a direct sum over the
diagonal and unordered pairs, so the proof
also covers an infinite set of cells.

One face argument must be changed from the
single-cell proof.
It is not asserted that every mixed element
of positive arity has zero faces.
Instead let a torsion pair $(z,b_0)$ to be
repaired have form degrees $q_*,q_*-1$, with
$q_*=2$ or $3$, common arity $a\geq3$ and
$db_0=mz$.
In the form $q_*-1<a$, a monomial using only
old selected $x_i,y_i$ and arity-zero coefficients
cannot occur: its form degree would be at
least $a$.
Every remaining monomial contains a resolved
or later generator with zero faces.
It follows that all faces of $b_0$ vanish.
The equation $db_0=mz$ and torsionfreeness
then prove that all faces of $z$ vanish too.
This gives the actual $K(2)$ parameter maps
needed for the repair.

In the target the identical argument uses
(AT.161): $F_A(b_0)$ has all positive faces
zero, and so does $F_A(z)$ by the equation
and torsionfreeness.
Their weight is at least $3b_*\geq2b_*+1$,
where $N_3(A)$ is vertically exact through
degree four by (AT.167).
The integral normalized primitives (AT.164)
therefore exist. Attach the actual $K(2)$
cells (AT.165) and extend the carrier with
these primitives. Their reference faces
are zero, so the common form/arity bound
is retained. The argument is independent
in the two targets but uses one source.

The arity-zero, one and two calculation above
also proves that saturation adds no element
in these arities at simplex two.
A positive-arity zero-form has a zero-face
factor, since every old selected generator
has positive form degree.
Thus its faces are zero, and the homogeneous
root decomposition and carrier extension
through A-SAT01 are exactly those proved
in AT24. They retain the form/arity bound.
In particular no assumption that all mixed
old products have zero faces is used in
the saturation step.

Finally iterate all nonzero homogeneous
degree-two and degree-three classes and
saturate after each stage.
The low-arity computation places every
such class in arity at least three and
weight at least $3b_*$.
The same single set of target cutoffs
therefore applies at every stage.
The compatible union and the optional
dimension-at-least-three horizontal
restoration are the actual constructions
of AT24. They give all the stated
properties, now including the mixed
classes.
\end{proof}
