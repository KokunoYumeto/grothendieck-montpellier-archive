\subsection{A genuine primary repair creates torsion at other primes}

\begin{proposition}[AT23: Proved; a limitation on prime-supported repair invariants]
\label{AT23}
Let $p$ be any prime and $w\geq3$ any integer.
There are actual torsionfree strict simplicial PD algebras
$X\hookrightarrow Y$ over $S_{\mathbb Z}$, with split
maps to the standard admissible theory $G$, having the
following properties.
Both have the coefficient $H^0$, the rational coefficient
comparison in every simplex and weight, unchanged first
two simplices $G_0,G_1$, and horizontal row deformation
retractions to $G$.
The extension is exactly an AT14 repair of a genuine
order-$p$ vertical three-class.
For every prime $r$, its simplex-two algebra has a
nonzero order-$r$ class in form degree $2r$ and weight
$rw$. In particular this repair creates order-two
cohomology in form four even when $p$ is an arbitrarily
large odd prime.
The degree-one based PD saturation leaves $Y$ unchanged.

Thus a rule asserting that a $p$-primary repair creates
only $p$-primary torsion is false even for these free
algebras and contractible parameters.
Taking $w$ arbitrarily large does not alter this conclusion.
The result does not refute an induction that repairs
the new prime torsion at its displayed weights.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Put $M=D(3)$ and take
\[
 X=G\otimes_{\mathbb Z}
       \operatorname{FreePD}_{\mathbb Z}(x_2M,y_3M),
       \qquad dx=p\,y,\quad dy=0,
       \qquad \operatorname{wt}(x)=\operatorname{wt}(y)=w.
 \tag{AT.145}
\]
Subscripts indicate form degrees. The actual projection
to $G$ kills both new parameter copies, and coefficient
inclusion is a strict section.
In simplex two, $M_2=\mathbb Z$.
The $S$-linear PD Poincare contraction of $G_2$ reduces
the vertical complex to
$S\otimes\Gamma(x)\otimes\Lambda(y)$.
Its form-two to form-three differential is
$x\mapsto p y$, with no other term in those form
degrees. Hence $[y]$ has exact order $p$ in degree
three and weight $w$.

Apply the actual two-cell repair with the same parameter:
\[
 Y=X\otimes\operatorname{FreePD}(u_2M,v_1M),
                    \qquad du=y,\quad dv=p u-x .
 \tag{AT.146}
\]
The old relation $dx=p y$ verifies $d^2v=0$.
Both new generators may be sent to zero in $G$, so the
specified target maps extend.
All displayed variables have weight $w$.

There is an actual integral PD change of even generators
\[
                         x'=x-pu,\qquad x=x'+pu.
 \tag{AT.147}
\]
The free PD universal property gives inverse maps.
They fix $G,u,v,y$, and their differentials give
\[
                    du=y,\quad dy=0,\qquad
                    dv=-x',\quad dx'=0.
 \tag{AT.148}
\]
Consequently (AT.147) identifies the actual algebra $Y$
with the tensor product of the even-to-odd identity
disk on $(u,y)$ and the odd-to-even identity disk on
$(v,x')$, over $G$ and with their same simplicial
parameter $M$.
The first disk has the integral identity pairs
$\gamma_n(u)\mapsto\gamma_{n-1}(u)y$ and an actual
weight-preserving contraction. It can be removed from
the cohomology calculation by that displayed
$S$-linear contraction, not by a quasi-isomorphism
inference about free PD functors.

In simplex two the remaining disk has
\[
 d\bigl(v\gamma_{n-1}(x')\bigr)
                  =-n\gamma_n(x'),\qquad n\geq1.
 \tag{AT.149}
\]
Its arity-$n$ complex is precisely this two-term map
between free coefficient modules.
Thus
\[
 H^{2n}(Y_2)\ \supset\
                (S/nS)\,[\gamma_n(x')]
\]
as the direct summand calculated by the actual
contractions of $G_2$ and the even disk.
There are no other generators in this remaining
simplex-two disk, so (AT.149) proves nonboundary
and exact order $r$ when $n=r$ is prime.
The class has weight $rw$.

Every underlying horizontal row of $X$ or $Y$ retracts
to that of $G$ by the simplicial contraction of $D(3)$.
Apply that contraction simultaneously to each indicated
parameter copy and use the free PD universal property;
the maps and homotopy preserve the horizontal weight
and form rows. This is the same actual generator
homotopy used in AT13.
The possibly twisted vertical differential in (AT.146)
is irrelevant to this row calculation.
Since $D(3)_0=D(3)_1=0$, the first two simplex algebras
are exactly those of $G$.
All terms are free abelian. The support $q\leq w$ is
preserved because every generator satisfies it.

Rationally the factors in (AT.145) and (AT.148) are
identity disks after the displayed scalar inversions,
and their elementary monomial contractions give the
rational coefficient comparison.
Their form-zero groups introduce no new variables,
so coefficient $H^0$ follows from that of $G$.
In every simplex, after contracting $G$ and the even
disk in (AT.148), a one-form is a coefficient-linear
combination of the distinct $v$ generators.
Its differential is the same combination of the
independent free even generators $x'$.
It is zero only when every coefficient is zero.
Thus $H^1(Y_n)=0$ in every weight.

For the last assertion, a rational zero-form whose
differential is integral has an integral primitive
for that differential by this $H^1$ vanishing.
Its difference from that primitive is a rational
coefficient; an integral vertex-zero value makes
that coefficient integral.
It therefore already belongs to $Y$.
No first saturation stage adds an element, so neither
does any later PD closure or stage.
This proves persistence through the full based
degree-one saturation.
\end{proof}

For a single object displaying unbounded new degree-four
weights, take one copy of (AT.145) for every odd prime $p$,
with strictly increasing weights $w_p\geq3$, and use their
graded tensor product over $G$.
Repair each displayed $p$-class by (AT.146).
Every element uses only finitely many factors, so the
explicit contractions and changes of variables above
apply in the filtered union.
Projection killing all factors except the $p$th is an
actual split strict PD map.
It detects the resulting order-two class in degree four
and weight $2w_p$.
The weights may be chosen to satisfy any prescribed
inequalities $w_p\geq pF(3)$.
This one repaired partial source therefore has degree-four
defects in unbounded weights, while retaining the stated
horizontal retractions, coefficient $H^0$, vanishing $H^1$,
rational comparison and first skeleton.
A further vertical repair is required; the example
does not rule out such a repair or a stronger weight
reserve that tracks its new primes.
