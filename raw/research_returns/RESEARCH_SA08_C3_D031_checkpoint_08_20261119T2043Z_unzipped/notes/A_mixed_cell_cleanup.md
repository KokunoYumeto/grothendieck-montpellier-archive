# AT25: mixed free cells have the same low-degree cleanup

The full proof is in sections/A_mixed_cell_cleanup.tex. It extends AT24 to any set of selected free simplex-two C21 odd cells satisfying one uniform lower weight bound.

Each actual AT20 carrier is prescribed on its own free old pair and coefficient-resolution module. All such assignments extend simultaneously over the free PD presentation; images of distinct cells need not be orthogonal. Every surviving first-face reference value has form degree at least its cell arity.

The low-arity calculation has one new summand for each pair of distinct old cells: the exact complex S → S² → S with matrices (1,−1) and (1,1). The diagonal resolved summands retain their injective I1/2 → S/2 connecting maps. Thus no low-arity H2 or H3 class is left.

Mixed old products need not have zero faces. The proof instead uses the actual null relation B of a torsion pair. Its form is strictly below its arity, so it cannot have a pure-old monomial; every term contains a resolved or later generator with zero faces. Hence B has zero faces, and dB=m z makes z have zero faces by torsionfreeness. The same argument applies to the target carrier. The normalized D3 primitives then give the actual K2 repair maps.

Homogeneous saturation and iteration proceed as in AT24. The result controls all mixed H1 through H3 in simplex two. It does not assert preservation of this reference bound after arbitrary later form-four repairs or a full admissible common-source construction.
