## Attempts and exact unresolved steps

1. **Alternating horizontal cells and vertical primitives.** Horizontal free differential cells preserve actual target maps using eventual row acyclicity. To kill a vertical class of degree q, one needs degree q-1 primitives with all S-linear and simplicial relations respected. Termwise exactness of a target does not supply such a natural S-linear lift. A primitive for each element of a coefficient tail introduces syzygies; their correction can enter lower form degrees. No convergence proof was obtained.

2. **Coefficient-tail killing.** For a fixed bad class z, one can seek primitives of sufficiently high coefficient multiples rather than of z itself. Over Z_(p), ordinary coefficient tails are generated by the infinitely many p-power basis elements. This observation does not produce coherent primitives: intersections of their principal S-ideals produce relations between chosen primitives. No finite-projective-dimension assertion for that tail module was proved or used.

3. **Strict splicing of a low truncation with a target.** The exact pullback and its cohomology are recorded as statement IC01 in `A_integral.tex`. The two junction cohomology groups are explicitly nonzero in general, even when both original complexes are contractible. Thus this specific construction supplies no completion theorem.

4. **Integral common targets.** Starting from A tensor_S B avoids prescribed receiving maps for later cells, but the pushout itself can have extra H^1 in unbounded weights (C19). Killing arbitrary positive cohomology by free primitives may create lower-degree classes through torsion relations. No admissible hull with coefficient H^0 exactly S was constructed.

5. **Tensor over the coefficient ring k with diagonal coefficients.** The algebra C=A tensor_k B has actual projections to A and B, using the other augmentation, when S maps by the divided-power coproduct T↦T_A+T_B. Its eventual vertical coefficient algebra has two independent coefficient directions. Adding the universal relative differential forms with total differential d_C+δ gives compatible projections which kill positive form degree. However the substitution δc↦δc+d_Cc conjugates the pure universal de Rham differential to that total differential. Consequently the original vertical quasi-isomorphism of C does not establish the required Poincaré assertion. Odd free generators already give the integral odd-to-even disk torsion. No functorial integral closure compatible with both projections was obtained.

6. **Spreading rational comparison over Z[1/D].** A rational map from a fixed elementary source involves finitely many denominators, but admissibility of that same elementary source does not descend by inverting finitely many primes: C11/D022 supply unbounded prime-power classes. Therefore finite denominator clearing of the map does not prove finite denominator clearing of the admissible source.

7. **Crystalline envelopes and derived de Rham.** We considered a simplicial ordinary polynomial resolution of S/I_N and the PD envelope of its augmentation kernel relative to the given coefficient PD structure. Simplexwise PD polynomial envelopes have the Poincare contraction, but the required eventual horizontal exactness of the envelope and all of its form rows was not established. For an envelope which is a cellular free PD resolution of S/I_N, C15 gives an explicit unbounded obstruction in the first form row. We opened Bhatt, arXiv:1204.6560v1, Definition 2.1 and Proposition 3.25/Theorem 3.27 to inspect the available comparison: its direct-sum totalization has degree equal to form degree minus simplicial degree, and its comparison is stated for E-infinity algebras. We produced no comparison map transferring this totalized conclusion to the two separate eventual conditions and strict maps required here. No conclusion of nonexistence of such a comparison is asserted.
