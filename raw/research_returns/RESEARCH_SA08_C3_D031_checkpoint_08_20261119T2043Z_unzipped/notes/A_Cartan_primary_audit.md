# Cartan 1976: exact primary comparison maps

Primary source: Henri Cartan, *Théories cohomologiques*, Inventiones Mathematicae35 (1976),261–271, DOI10.1007/BF01390140. The original scan was fetched from the Göttingen GDZ article PDF. Printed pages266,267,269,270 were directly inspected as images; OCR served only as a finding aid. The article PDF includes a preliminary catalogue page, so printed page266 is PDF page7.

Section4, printed page266, defines the morphisms used in that section as simplicial linear cochain maps. Theorem2 on the same page constructs the coefficient-identity map A*→C*⊗R(A) under vertical axiom(a) and the additional support A_p^n=0 for n>p. The displayed proof extends cycles successively by the cochain differential and the cochain test's representing property. Those are the exact maps examined here.

Theorem3, printed page267, assumes both cohomological theories' axioms, flat cycle modules, and multiplicativity of the coefficient map. It proves multiplicativity on induced cohomology by the displayed homotopy-commutative cycle-product square. We have not constructed a strict PD-algebra lift of the Theorem2 map for the arbitrary eventual theories of the present problem. The support q≤w in our variants is a weight inequality; the displayed hypothesis in Theorem2 uses simplex dimension. No transfer between those two inequalities is asserted.

Section7, printed pages269–270, constructs the standard divided-power forms, their simplicial face and degeneracy maps, and their row and cycle homotopy calculations. We read these as a primary check of the model and of the precise integration comparison available in this paper. No statement about all possible stronger comparisons is inferred from the scope of the cited results.
