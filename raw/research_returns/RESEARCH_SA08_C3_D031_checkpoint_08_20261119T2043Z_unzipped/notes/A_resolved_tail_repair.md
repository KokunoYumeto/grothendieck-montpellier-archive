# AT16: resolved coefficient-tail quadratic repair

The full proof is in `sections/A_resolved_tail_repair.tex`.

Input is actual S-linear K(2)-parameter data z in form four and η in form three with dη=mz, on L=I_N⟨s⟩. A free coefficient resolution Q of L is tensored with the two-term complex v₂→m u₃. Its signs are d(u_i)=−∂u_i and d(v_i)=m u_i+∂v_i. The finite generator complex is cut off in form zero by its last kernel, not by discarding the differential. Its only linear cohomology is L/mL in form three.

The twist δ(u₀)=zε, δ(v₀)=−ηε, zero elsewhere, is a degree-one cocycle. For an admissible target, Hom_s(K(2),A) has coefficient cohomology S in degree two in the required finite range. Therefore the actual target obstruction is Ext²(L/mL,S), which is zero by the proved nonnegative graded injective dimension bound. A total-Hom primitive factors through the bottom kernel by its terminal equation and gives an actual strict PD target map.

Over Q the explicit H(u_i)=v_i/m, H(v_i)=0 descends the truncation. Translation by a=−δH untwists the differential. Extending H as a derivation and dividing by positive arity contracts the rational free PD algebra. The torsionfree quotient thus preserves rational coefficient exactness, H0, both target maps, lower weights, and the entire first two simplices.

AT16-comparison gives an actual surjective strict PD map from this resolved algebra to the two-term nonfree tail algebra. It is the coefficient-resolution augmentation on u₀ and v₀ and zero on all other generators. It is a rational quasi-isomorphism and is an integral quasi-isomorphism on linear generator complexes.

The remaining question is the integral low cohomology of the nonlinear resolved algebra. No vanishing of H1, H2, or H3 after the S-balanced free PD construction has been asserted. A splitting of the underlying free abelian complex does not by itself give an S-linear contraction or a PD comparison. The other agents are checking this specific kernel rather than the already completed target lifting.
