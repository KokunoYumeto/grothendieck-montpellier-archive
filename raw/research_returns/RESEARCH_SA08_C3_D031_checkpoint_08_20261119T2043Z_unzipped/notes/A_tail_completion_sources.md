# Source and search record for coefficient-tail work

On 2026-11-19 we searched for `P-algebra union finite Hopf algebras bounded below free modules injective Margolis` and `divided power algebra self injective infinite graded injective dimension`. This led to Nagpal–Snowden, *The module theory of divided power algebras*, arXiv:1606.03431v2.

We fetched and opened the v2 LaTeX source (58 KB arXiv source archive, containing `dp_revised.tex` and `SSdefn.sty`) and the v2 PDF. The exact source used is:

- https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.03431v2
- https://arxiv.org/src/1606.03431v2
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/1606.03431v2

The v2 PDF has 55 pages. Theorem 7.9 is on printed page 43; Theorem 8.3 is on printed page 45 and proved on printed pages 46–48. In the fetched source, their labels are `thm:injdim2` and `thm:ext-torsion`, respectively; the latter statement begins at line 2078. Section 1.5 specifies the weight-graded Ext convention; our paper states its own shift convention explicitly and uses degree-preserving Ext.

Theorem 8.3 is used only with target a rank-one shifted free module over Γ_Zp(T), and source the bounded-weight quotient S/I_N. We do not assume the tail ideal is finitely presented. The completed product is a product in graded modules, where each coefficient weight is rank one. No result about ungraded products or ungraded Ext is substituted.

AT01–AT04 are complete derivations from the cited theorem plus explicit arithmetic and rational two-term resolutions. We make no priority claim. The search above sought a usable module theorem rather than a claim that the resulting special formulas had never appeared before.
