# AT21 and AT21b: explicit correction and comparison of the original cells

The complete proofs are in `sections/A_closed_boundary_cells.tex`.

AT21 starts from the actual free even-disk first skeleton. Every one-form boundary a is a finite sum f_j dx_j. Removing the coefficient part of each f_j and applying the displayed degeneracy product gives an actual normalized two-simplex t with ∂t=a−dh. The cell translations x↦x′+t and y↦y′+dt, with their subtraction inverses, are strict PD-DGA maps fixing the old source. Thus the closed-boundary presentation is proved by a concrete isomorphism.

AT21b records the additional quadratic comparison as its own proposition. The finite sums K_t and L_t satisfy dK_t=γ₂(dt) and dL_t=t dt−2K_t. The resulting D_t,E_t identify the two quadratic twists. The original relative resolved algebra uses exactly the edge values εD_t and εE_t; higher generators and W0 have zero edge. The explicit affine maps to the corrected K(2) algebra are inverse strict PD maps, including on the torsionfree quotients. No identification with a different contraction-based boundary choice from AT17 is asserted.

The source B and ζ expressions change by 2J E_t and d(J E_t). This gives actual inverse maps of the degree-three repair algebras, with U shifted by J E_t and V unchanged. Since J has zero edge, the correction is a genuine K(2) map. The same isomorphisms identify based PD saturation stages inside the rationalized algebras.

The general cell and resolved-algebra comparisons are integral. The displayed C,H,K,J and degree-three-repair formulas in this proposition use the actual AS22 resolution over Z_(2); no integral descent of that minimal resolution is asserted. Applying AT20 still requires its actual target maps, torsionfreeness, X0=S, coefficient H0 and rational comparison in every simplex and weight, and all stated cutoffs. Those assumptions have not been discarded. The independent finite certificate has609 exact checks; it supplements the full finite-sum and morphism proofs.
