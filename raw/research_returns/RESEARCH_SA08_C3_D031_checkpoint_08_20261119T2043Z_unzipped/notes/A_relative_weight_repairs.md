# Relative weight repairs and the remaining period

AT15 is proved in `sections/A_relative_weight_repairs.tex`.

The source assumptions are attained by the refined C21 construction: simplex zero is S and simplex one consists only of even-to-odd PD disks over S. The relative D(2) construction retains both simplex algebras. It kills a specified closed q-form parameter, preserves the rational coefficient resolution, and supports all new generators in the chosen weight and above.

For q=2, the essential arithmetic step is an actual target comparison. If db=mz and a is an old primitive of the bottom of z, then the source edge period of b_bottom/m−a equals the integral target period of U_bottom−f(a). This makes the source period divisible by m. After correcting a and b using row fillings, the boundary equations become b_bottom=ma, u_bottom=a, v_bottom=0. The target maps then extend. For q≥4, the same construction uses ordinary relative primitives in degrees at least two.

For q=3 the final bottom of V is a closed relative one-form. Its period modulo m is independent of all target primitive choices, and is exactly the obstruction to imposing v_bottom=0. The proof constructs every allowed change by m times an integral period and proves both directions of the criterion. It does not assume that this residue vanishes in arbitrary partial models.

Once both target maps have been constructed, the explicit C21 horizontal restoration preserves H0, H1, the rational coefficient comparison, and the simplex-one integral resolution. This supplies an actual operation with fixed target-dependent horizontal bounds, not a claimed completed iteration.

Shift clarification for the ongoing coefficient-tail variant: with (M⟨b⟩)^w=M^(w−b), AT04 gives RHom_grS(I_N⟨b⟩,S)=S^b in degree zero for b≥0, and zero positive Ext. In particular positive source weight does not annihilate the degree-zero Hom group. For a K(2) parameter the remaining coefficient-period group is in cochain degree two. No vanishing of that period is inferred from positive Ext vanishing.
