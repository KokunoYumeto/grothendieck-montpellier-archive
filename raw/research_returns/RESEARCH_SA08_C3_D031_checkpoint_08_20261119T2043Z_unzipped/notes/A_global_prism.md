# AT26: a full prism with an actual common reference

The complete proof is in sections/A_global_prism.tex. It uses the actual global relative free D2 presentation of the selected cells; a simplex-two presentation alone is not used to infer a global homotopy.

The reference algebra is P tensor G_N over S, followed by relative even zero-form D2 disks q_i with bottom h_i−h_i'. The map sends x_i to x_i'+dq_i, y_i to y_i', v0 to minus the augmentation times q_i y_i', and the remaining resolved generators to zero. Its maps to both targets use their actual coefficient-edge model and the q_i from AT20.

On the three top tetrahedra of each parameter prism the values are the full AT24 carrier, s1 of the reference value, and s2 of the reference value. All four faces are tabulated. The bottom edge prism is constant, and the base-algebra prism is the constant homotopy. The nonlinear differential and coefficient relations hold on each actual cochain algebra map.

The ordered interval orientation is explicit: endpoint zero is the reference and endpoint one is the originally prescribed map. No unproved reversal of the ordered interval is used.
