# AT24: a stable low-degree repair of one resolved cell

The complete proof is in sections/A_single_cell_cleanup.tex.

This construction uses an actual free C21 cell in normalized simplex degree two, after the explicit closed-boundary change. Its simplex-two algebra is free on the selected odd x and even y=dx over the other identity disks. That freeness permits a full PD cochain carrier into target simplex degree three; an arbitrary marked element with unspecified relations would not suffice.

The first face of the carrier sends x to x_ref+dq, y to y_ref, v0 to minus the coefficient augmentation times q y_ref, and every other resolved generator to zero. Give x,y arity one, the resolved generators arity two, and the other identity disks arity zero. Every surviving reference value has form degree at least arity. Thus every homogeneous form-two expression of arity at least three, and every form-one expression of arity at least two, has zero reference face.

An actual torsion pair in forms two or three and arity at least three consequently has a normalized D3 image. The target's vertical exactness supplies both integral normalized primitives. Their zero faces give the actual K2 two-cell maps, and their other faces are zero, so the carrier property is preserved. The fixed target cutoff suffices because arity a has weight at least a b.

The arity-zero identity-disk algebra, arity-one identity disk, and combined arity-two resolved complex all have zero cohomology in forms one through three. Saturation therefore adds no element in those arities. Every higher-arity rational zero-form root has zero faces and a homogeneous integral derivative; the target H1 and vertex condition make its rational carrier value integral. Thus the carrier also survives every saturation stage.

Iterating all remaining degree-two and degree-three classes kills them in simplex two, while saturation kills H1. The proof uses a filtered union of actual inclusions and compatible target maps. It does not infer that other simplex degrees have the same low cohomology, or that products of distinct selected old cells or subsequent degree-four repairs preserve the carrier bound. Optional C21 restoration uses dimensions at least three and literally leaves simplex two unchanged.
