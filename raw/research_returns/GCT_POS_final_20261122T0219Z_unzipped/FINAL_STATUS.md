## 1. The main conclusion and the remaining two gates

Let k_n(s)=g((s^n),(s^n),(s^n)) and M=2^(n-1). The joint proof K023/R-NEW004/K024/PNEW013 establishes an actual pole of the tensor Hilbert series at some root of order M·3^r, with r≥0, for every n≥2. Any representation with denominator a product of factors1-t^a must therefore have at least one exponent a≥M. This exceeds every polynomial in the unified rank parameter rank(x)=n²+5n, and also in the introductory parameter h=n⁴+O(n). It refutes numerical PH3 without requiring r=0.

The exact-M gate is more specific. For n≥4, K026–K031 show that the chain is the only cyclically stable irreducible fixed component and remove every stabilizer exception. The remaining contribution is an integral coherent moving class supported on closures of nonchain components meeting the nonzero higher mu_(3M) fixed locus. Actual collision components exist by K029 and K037. To cancel the known chain contribution, their specific simple Laurent contribution must cancel a unit modulo3. That contribution has not been evaluated. An arbitrary coherent class on the same support is not a substitute for it.

The strict-index gate is quantitative. PNEW016 proves dim X_n^(mu_M)≤n³/5 for n≥2000. The exact covariant constituent difference removes every wave of smaller two-primary order and has degree≤n³/5-1. For n≥79004, either p(k_n)>2^(n/16) and s(k_n)≥2^(n/16), or its positive coefficient mass is at least2^((17/240)n⁴-6n³-4). The needed upper bound is unproved. The old positive-part norm and the new difference norm have no proved ordering, and the Fourier addendum shows that the proposed subquartic bound would itself force the selected exact-M pole. Neither attained nonnegativity nor the smaller degree supplies this estimate.

## 3. Main routes attempted and where each ends

### Fixed-locus classification and the pole sum

1. **Small dimensions and a general finite cutoff.** Exact enumeration, lift coverage, full stabilizers and local sums settle n4 and n5 (K002–K014). K009 gives a finite all-dimensional cutoff. Its exponential size makes it a correctness reduction, not a uniform classification algorithm or a completed variable-n answer.
2. **The proposed sharp character-order ceiling.** The first free-support candidate was unstable; K017 records that failure. The positive amplifier of K018 produces actual critical tensors exceeding2^(n-1), with full-stabilizer refinements K019–K020. Thus the proposed ceiling itself fails. K021 shows higher target fixed loci can have growing dimension, so a curve-only extrapolation also fails.
3. **Regularity as a noncancellation principle.** K022 gives exact cancellation between two smooth fixed curves in a normal factorial Gorenstein ring with rational singularities. It has a different chosen grading, so it diagnoses the proposed general implication without claiming tensor cancellation.
4. **Cyclic trace and modular pole transfer.** The Scharf-based trace identity K023, the actual hook-chain analysis R-NEW004/K024, and PNEW013 succeed: numerical PH3 is refuted. The method allows an extra3-power and therefore stops short of exact-M noncancellation.
5. **Exclude all odd lifts.** K025 provides an actual critical odd-character example. K029 and K037 place odd inertia on actual collision components. A blanket exclusion is false.
6. **Remove stabilizer exceptions.** K026–K031 succeed in eliminating every such exception. The final collision support is real; no further removal follows merely from generic freeness or a finite stabilizer calculation.
7. **Normalize amplified collision components.** K032 identifies a normalization and exact graded coefficient formula for a family in dimensions2+56l. Its general density, conductor and lower moving Laurent terms are unevaluated. Knowing a normalization ring does not determine those lower contributions.
8. **Use the tractable rank-two component.** K033 computes the n10 normalization series and density507281. K034 confines residual issues to conductors/intersections, and K035 proves all-point grading inertia mu512 or mu1024. This component is not indexed in the remaining collision union. K036 extends the method, but its higher-order components can lie properly in larger target components; excluding their odd inertia does not remove the larger components.
9. **Infer3-integrality from a free cyclic orbit.** R-DIAG001 disproves that general inference. K037 then constructs a counterclass on the actual tensor collision support: its cyclic simple coefficient is4531295468/9. The specific moving Koszul class is still different and unevaluated.
10. **Compute actual local higher-root moving data.** K038 proves the displayed tensor's full stabilizer, the whole three-dimensional fixed component, normalization and top density9063377350. Its relative Euler factor is-omega²/3^5, giving local leading valuations-5 in1-t^(3D) and-8 in1-t/xi. This succeeds locally. The sum over all higher-root components and the target-dyadic class remain unknown.
