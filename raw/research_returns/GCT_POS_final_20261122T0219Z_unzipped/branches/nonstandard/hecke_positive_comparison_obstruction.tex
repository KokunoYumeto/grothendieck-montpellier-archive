\subsection{N-023: no effective comparison with the ordinary Hecke category}

Here the target is the \emph{ordinary split} Hecke Grothendieck ring,
with its nonnegative Kazhdan--Lusztig cone.  This is a stronger
requirement than an Euler-class comparison in a derived or Koszul
category.  The distinction is essential because the user explicitly
allows the latter framework.

Let $A=\mathbb Z[v,v^{-1}]$, $F=\mathbb Q(v)$,
$d=v+v^{-1}$ and $D=d^2$.  Write $b_w=C'_w$ for the positive
Kazhdan--Lusztig basis of the type-$A_2$ Hecke algebra $H_A$.
Let $\mathscr H_A=H_A\otimes_A H_A$, and call its cone
\[
 \mathscr H_A^+=\bigoplus_{a,b\in S_3}
       \mathbb N[v,v^{-1}](b_a\otimes b_b).
 \tag{N-140}
\]
The two actual nonstandard generators are
\[
 P_s=2b_s\otimes b_s-d(b_s\otimes1+1\otimes b_s)+D,
 \qquad s=1,2.
 \tag{N-141}
\]
The first cell of the fixed $E^{\rm opp}(3)$ basis contains
\[
 g_1=P_1P_2P_1-(D-2)^2P_1,\qquad
 g_2=P_2P_1P_2-(D-2)^2P_2.
 \tag{N-142}
\]
These formulas use the unambiguous first-cell coefficient of VIII;
they do not depend on the corrected sign in the second cell.

\paragraph{The precise positive-comparison theorem.}
Suppose $Y_1,Y_2\in\mathscr H_A^+$ satisfy, coefficient by coefficient
in the basis $b_a\otimes b_b$ and in powers of $v$,
\[
                  Y_i\ge |g_i|,\qquad Y_i\equiv g_i\pmod2.
                  \tag{N-143}
\]
Then the unital $F$-algebra generated by $Y_1,Y_2$ has dimension at
least11.  More precisely it has a quotient
\[
                   F^3\oplus M_2(F)\oplus M_2(F).
                   \tag{N-144}
\]
There is no bound on the degrees, sizes or supports of the positive
corrections in this theorem.  Neither bar invariance nor self-duality
nor a Dynkin symmetry is assumed.

\paragraph{A coefficient forcing strict character inequalities.}
The coefficient of $b_2\otimes1$ in $g_1$ is $-d^5$; by factor swap,
the same is true of $1\otimes b_2$.  Here is a direct derivation.
In a product with first-factor word $b_1$, then $1$ or $b_2$, then
$b_1$, the only way to obtain first-factor basis element $b_2$ is
to choose first factor1 from both outer factors.  Indeed
$b_1^2=db_1$ and $b_1b_2b_1=b_{121}+b_1$, so no other such word
contributes $b_2$.  The corresponding second-factor coefficient in
$P_1P_2P_1$ is
\[
       (D-db_1)(2b_2-d)(D-db_1).
\]
Its coefficient of the identity basis element is $-d^5$:
every other term is a multiple of $b_1,b_2,b_{12},b_{21}$ or
$b_{121}$.  The correction $(D-2)^2P_1$ has no $b_2\otimes1$
coordinate.  Consequently (N-143) implies
\[
         Y_1\ge d^5(b_2\otimes1+1\otimes b_2).
         \tag{N-145}
\]

Use the two scalar characters $T,S$ of $H_F$ and its standard
two-dimensional representation $\rho$.  Their values on the six
KL elements are
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrr}
 w&e&1&2&12&21&121\\\hline
 T(b_w)&1&d&d&d^2&d^2&d^3-d\\
 S(b_w)&1&0&0&0&0&0\\
 \operatorname{tr}\rho(b_w)&2&d&d&1&1&0.
\end{array}\tag{N-146}
\]
All displayed entries are nonnegative at every real $v>0$, and
$T(b_w)-S(b_w)$ is also nonnegative.  Thus positive additions cannot
decrease any of the following differences.  From (N-145),
\[
\begin{aligned}
 (T\otimes T)(Y_1)-(T\otimes S)(Y_1)&\ge d^6>0,\\
 (T\otimes S)(Y_1)-(S\otimes S)(Y_1)&\ge d^6>0,\\
 \operatorname{tr}(T\otimes\rho)(Y_1)
   -\operatorname{tr}(S\otimes\rho)(Y_1)&\ge2d^6>0.
\end{aligned}\tag{N-147}
\]
In particular the three restricted scalar characters are distinct.
The two matrix representations will be distinct once their
surjectivity is proved.  Inequalities at positive real $v$ are used
only to show that these rational-function differences are nonzero.

\paragraph{Surjectivity survives every positive correction.}
An integral model of $\rho$ is
\[
 B_1=\rho(b_1)=\begin{pmatrix}d&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 B_2=\rho(b_2)=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\1&d\end{pmatrix}.
 \tag{N-148}
\]
It satisfies $B_i^2=dB_i$, $B_1B_2B_1=B_1$ and
$B_2B_1B_2=B_2$, with $\rho(b_{121})=0$.
Set $\delta=(D-1)(D-4)$.  Direct substitution into (N-141)--(N-142)
gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 (T\otimes\rho)(g_i)&=-d\delta B_i,\\
 (S\otimes\rho)(g_i)&=-d\delta(dI-B_i).
 \end{aligned}\tag{N-149}
\]
For the second equality, the matrices $dI-B_i$ satisfy the same
quadratic and triple-product relations as $B_i$.

All matrices and projections are integral over $A$.  Thus (N-143)
implies that reducing the projected $Y_i$ modulo2 gives
$\alpha B_i$ or $\alpha(dI+B_i)$, respectively, where
\[
        \alpha=dD(D+1)=d^3(d^2+1)
        \quad\hbox{in }\mathbb F_2[v,v^{-1}].
\]
The determinant of the four flattened matrices
$I,B_1,B_2,B_1B_2$ is $d^2-1$.
Replacing $B_i$ by $dI-B_i$ leaves this determinant unchanged:
subtract suitable multiples of the first three rows from the fourth
and of the first row from the second and third.
It follows that the determinant of
$I,(T\otimes\rho)(Y_1),(T\otimes\rho)(Y_2),
 (T\otimes\rho)(Y_1Y_2)$, and likewise for $S\otimes\rho$,
reduces modulo2 to
\[
               \alpha^4(d^2+1)=d^{12}(d^2+1)^5\ne0.
               \tag{N-150}
\]
The substitution $d=v+v^{-1}$ does not annihilate this polynomial:
its highest Laurent coefficient is1.  Both determinants are
therefore nonzero over $F$, so both projected algebras are all of
$M_2(F)$.  This argument handles arbitrary correction terms in
(N-143); it is not a finite search over those terms.

\paragraph{The dimension obstruction.}
Let $B=F\langle1,Y_1,Y_2\rangle$.  The three scalar maps and two
matrix maps above are surjections to simple $F$-algebras.
Their five maximal two-sided kernels are distinct.  Distinctness
of the scalar kernels follows from (N-147); scalar and matrix kernels
cannot agree because their quotient dimensions differ.  If the two
matrix kernels agreed, the induced automorphism of $M_2(F)$ would
preserve trace, contradicting the last inequality of (N-147).
Trace invariance here is elementary: an automorphism preserves the
commutator subspace, which is the trace-zero subspace, and fixes the
identity, whose trace is2.  The Chinese remainder theorem for these
pairwise comaximal two-sided ideals now gives (N-144).
Consequently $\dim_F B\ge3+4+4=11$, proving the theorem.

\paragraph{Categorical consequence, with its exact hypothesis.}
Suppose a composing dimension ring on the ten fixed $E^{\rm opp}(3)$
labels admits a unital multiplicative comparison, after extending
scalars to $F$, to $\mathscr H_F$.  Suppose also that this comparison
comes from effective sign--Tate coefficient objects in the ordinary
split Hecke category: evaluation at $\varepsilon=-1$ gives the actual
ambient $E^{\rm opp}(3)$ classes, and evaluation at $\varepsilon=+1$
gives their ordinary effective KL classes.  For every coefficient,
an effective lift $a+\varepsilon b$ has trace $a-b$ and dimension
$a+b$, so (N-143) holds for the two first-cell images.  The image of
a ten-dimensional algebra, however, has dimension at most10 and
contains $B$.  This is impossible.

Thus \emph{no} such ordinary positive-Hecke comparison exists for
\emph{any} rank-three dimension table, including an unknown global
minimizer.  Faithfulness of the comparison is not needed.  Nor is an
assumption that the ten-label lattice contains the generators $P_s$;
the proof uses only two actual basis labels.

This does not exclude a comparison in a homotopy/derived Hecke
category in which evaluation at $\varepsilon=+1$ remains a virtual
Euler class with negative KL coefficients.  It does not exclude a
twisted trace whose coefficient action is not the stated evaluation,
or a different target category.  In particular it does not remove
the user's separate Koszul-duality possibility.  It proves an exact
obstruction for the ordinary effective target and leaves the more
general canonical-selection question open.

The positive split Hecke comparison is the standard categorification
theorem, credited to Elias--Williamson (2014), Theorem1.1 and the
preceding description of the split Grothendieck group, printed
p.~1093; see also Theorem3.5, pp.~1107--1108.  The algebraic
obstruction (N-140)--(N-150) is proved directly above.  The supplied
F59 results concern actual quadratic isomorphisms for an ordinary
Euler lift of $P_s$, with their stated coefficient objects; those
results are not being reproved or relabelled here.
