\section{K-030: removing every finite stabilizer from the symmetry exception}

This strengthens K-028.  Finite stabilizers of even order divisible by
three can also be handled.  The new ingredient is a finite-group
conjugacy-class argument, rather than a count of individual elements
modulo three.

\begin{lemma}[K-030a]
Let $H$ be a finite group, let $\chi:H\to\mathbb C^*$ be a
character, and let $\phi$ be an automorphism of three-power order
preserving $\chi$.  If a two-power root $\xi$ belongs to $\chi(H)$,
there is a two-primary element $h\in H$ with $\chi(h)=\xi$ whose
$H$-conjugacy class is fixed by $\phi$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Choose a Sylow two-subgroup $P\subset H$ and form
$E=H\rtimes\langle\phi\rangle$.  Since $H$ is normal, the
Frattini argument gives $E=H N_E(P)$: any conjugate of $P$ by
an element of $E$ is another Sylow two-subgroup of $H$, hence
is conjugate back by an element of $H$.  In particular the
normalizer maps onto $E/H$.

Choose $b\in N_E(P)$ projecting to $\phi$, and take its
three-primary part $b_3$.  This still normalizes $P$ and can be
chosen to have the same projection $\phi$.  Explicitly, if
$|b|=3^a c$ with $3\nmid c$, choose an exponent equal to one
modulo $3^a$ and zero modulo $c$.  Raising $b$ to that exponent
gives $b_3$ and preserves its image in the three-group $E/H$.

The image of $P$ under $\chi$ is the full two-primary subgroup
of $\chi(H)$.  Hence
\[
                  P_\xi=\{h\in P:\chi(h)=\xi\}
\]
is a nonempty coset of $P\cap\ker\chi$ and has two-power
cardinality.  Conjugation by $b_3$ preserves it and has
three-power order.  Its orbits have three-power sizes, so at
least one element is fixed.  Writing $b_3=(v,\phi)$ shows that
this fixed element satisfies $v\phi(h)v^{-1}=h$.  This is the
required invariance of its conjugacy class.  The assertion also
covers $\phi=1$, when the conclusion is immediate.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[K-030b: generic finite full-stabilizer type]
Let a connected complex reductive group $L$ act linearly on a
finite-dimensional space.  In a constructible family of points
whose full stabilizers are finite, a dense open subset of each
irreducible parameter locus has one ambient conjugacy type of
full stabilizer, whenever the stabilizer counts and the family
descend to that parameter locus.  This applies to full
$\overline G$ stabilizers above the nonzero stable quotient locus
of $X_n$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Finite stabilizer orders are constructible: the stabilizer
incidence morphism has finite reduced fibres, and the cardinality
of a fibre of a quasi-finite morphism is constructible.  After
shrinking the irreducible locus, fix this order as $a$.

There are finitely many abstract groups of order $a$.  For each
such group $\Gamma$, there are only finitely many $L$-conjugacy
classes of homomorphisms $\rho:\Gamma\to L$.  Here is a direct
rigidity proof.  The finite-type scheme $\operatorname{Hom}(\Gamma,L)$
has tangent space at $\rho$ equal to the one-cocycles of $\Gamma$
on $\mathfrak l$ via $\operatorname{Ad}\rho$; the tangent to the
conjugacy orbit consists of coboundaries.  For a cocycle $z$, put
$v=|\Gamma|^{-1}\sum_{h\in\Gamma}z(h)$.  Its cocycle relation gives
\[
             \operatorname{Ad}\rho(g)v=v-z(g),
             \qquad z(g)=v-\operatorname{Ad}\rho(g)v.
\]
Thus every tangent cocycle is a coboundary.  The tangent dimension
equals the orbit dimension, so the orbit is open at each of its
points in the homomorphism scheme.  All conjugacy orbits are
therefore open, and quasi-compactness makes their number finite.
In particular, there are finitely many conjugacy classes of
subgroups of order $a$.

For any one such subgroup, the condition that a stabilizer contain
an ambient conjugate is constructible by the fixed-point incidence
variety and Chevalley's theorem.  On the order-$a$ locus, containment
is equality.  This gives a finite constructible partition by
stabilizer type, and one part contains a dense open subset.

For the stated tensor application, the effective special-linear
stabilizer is finite on the stable locus.  At a nonzero point, a
homogeneous invariant nonzero on its closed orbit forces the
determinant image of the full stabilizer to be finite.  Thus the
full stabilizer in $\overline G$ is finite.  Stabilizer counts and
ambient types are unchanged along an effective special-linear
orbit and descend constructibly by Chevalley's theorem, exactly
as in K-028.  One may work on a homogeneous invariant principal
open, so all incidence varieties used are of finite type.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[K-030c]
Let $n\ge4$ and $M=2^{n-1}$.  If an irreducible locally closed
subvariety $W\subset (X_n^{\mu_M})_{\rm red}$ is preserved by
$\tau=(123)$ and has finite effective special-linear stabilizer
on a dense open subset, then $W$ is contained in the chain
curve $Y_n$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply K-030b and shrink to a dense open subset of $W$ on which
the full stabilizer modulo $S$ has one conjugacy type $H$.
The subset can be taken invariant under $\tau$.  Because
$\tau W=W$, the subgroup $\tau(H)$ is conjugate to $H$ in
$\overline G$.

Let $N$ be the normalizer of $H$ in
$\overline G\rtimes\langle\tau\rangle$.  The map
\[
 N\longrightarrow \operatorname{Aut}(H)\times\langle\tau\rangle
\]
given by conjugation and projection has kernel
$C_{\overline G}(H)$.  Its image $F$ is finite and maps onto
$\langle\tau\rangle$, since $\tau(H)$ is ambient-conjugate to
$H$.  A Sylow three-subgroup of $F$ also maps onto this cyclic
group.  Choose an element projecting to $\tau$; its induced
automorphism $\phi$ of $H$ has three-power order.  It preserves
$\chi$, which is invariant under both inner conjugation in
$\overline G$ and cyclic permutation of the factors.

Fix a primitive $M$th root $\xi$.  The fixed-point condition gives
$\xi\in\chi(H)$.  Lemma K-030a supplies a two-primary
$h\in H$ with $\chi(h)=\xi$ and $\phi(h)$ conjugate to $h$ in
$H$.  An element of $N$ representing the chosen member of $F$
has component $\tau$ and induces $\phi$.  Thus the
$\overline G$-conjugacy class of $h$ is fixed by $\tau$.

Let $h$ have order $L$, a power of two.  Take a lift to $GL_n^3$
and split its $L$th power in the scalar torus $S$, obtaining an
order-dividing-$L$ lift.  As in K-026, the cyclically stable
ambient class has three scalar-translated spectral multisets.
Since three is invertible modulo $L$, shifts of total zero make
these multisets equal.  Independent conjugation makes the lift
$(A,A,A)$ with $A^L=I$ and $(\det A)^3=\xi$.  Consequently
$\det A$ has exact order $M$.

For every point of the dense open subset of $W$, a conjugate of
this element fixes a closed representative.  The complete
support-folding and positive-margin argument of K-026 therefore
applies: P-F30 forces the cubic chain, all ordered tensor chain
coordinates are nonzero, and the diagonal torus is transitive
on their coefficients.  Its quotient point lies in $Y_n$.
Closedness of $Y_n$ extends the inclusion from the dense subset
to all of $W$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[K-030d]
Let $\Omega_{\rm fin}\subset X_n$ be the open quotient locus
of finite effective special-linear stabilizers.  In K-027 the
exceptional support can be reduced further to
\[
 Z_{\rm fin}=(Y_{\rm red}\setminus\Omega_{\rm fin})
 \ \cup\!
 \bigcup_{\substack{Y_i\ne Y_n\ \text{an irreducible component}\\
              Y_i\cap X_n^{\mu_{3M}}\not\subseteq\{0\}}}Y_i.
                                                        \tag{K-030a}
\]
An integral virtual coherent class supported there satisfies
the same Laurent congruence and all the necessary cancellation
conditions of K-027.  The only stabilizer exception now needed
is positive-dimensional effective special-linear stabilizer.
The separate exception for component closures meeting nonzero
$\mu_{3M}$ fixed points remains.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Openness of $\Omega_{\rm fin}$ follows from the subgroup
neighborhood assertion in Luna, Section III.1, Remark~$4^\circ$,
printed p.~98, already read for K-028.  Repeat the equivariant
coherent localization and finite dimension induction of K-028b,
replacing $\Omega$ by $\Omega_{\rm fin}$ and using K-030c at
each residual irreducible stratum.  No assertion that the coarse
quotient is smooth on $\Omega_{\rm fin}$ is used.  All coherent
extension closures remain inside original components with no
nonzero $\mu_{3M}$ fixed point, so the no-collision integrality
lemma of K-027 applies unchanged.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scope.}
K-029 shows that the collision component exception is populated
by actual high-dimensional amplified tensor components.  K-030
does not compute the associated exceptional Hilbert class or
remove that exception.  Its finite-group and rigidity arguments
do not apply to a positive-dimensional full stabilizer.
