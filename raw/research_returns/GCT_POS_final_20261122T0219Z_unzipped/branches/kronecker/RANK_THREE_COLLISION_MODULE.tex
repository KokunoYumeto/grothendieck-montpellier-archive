\section{K-037: an actual rank-three collision and a nonintegral coherent class}

Rank two in K-035--K-036 is a real restriction. An explicit
rank-three core supports odd grading inertia while retaining
the old frame data. In dimension eighteen its odd-root fixed
representation also gives an exactly evaluated coherent module
on the actual collision locus. This module is not identified
with the particular moving Koszul class of K-031.

\subsection*{A weighted ternary core}

For any real $\rho>1/3$, put
\[
\begin{aligned}
 Q_\rho={}&\sqrt{\rho-1/3}\,e_{000}
       +\frac{e_{012}+e_{021}}{\sqrt6}
       +\frac{e_{112}-e_{121}}{\sqrt2}\\
       &+\frac{e_{202}+e_{211}+e_{220}}{\sqrt3}
             \quad\in(\mathbb C^3)^{\otimes3}.
\end{aligned}                                             \tag{K-037a}
\]
Here $e_{ijk}=e_i\otimes e_j\otimes e_k$, and the boundary
coordinate in each factor is $e_0$.

\begin{lemma}[K-037a]
All three reduced Hermitian matrices of $Q_\rho$ are
$\operatorname{diag}(\rho,1,1)$. If $\omega$ is a primitive
cube root, the element with exponent lists
\[
                  (0,0,1),\quad(0,1,2),\quad(0,1,2)
                          \pmod3                         \tag{K-037b}
\]
fixes $Q_\rho$, fixes the three boundary vectors pointwise,
and has determinant-product character $\omega$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The first-factor flattening columns at positions $(1,2)$ and
$(2,1)$ are $(1/\sqrt6,1/\sqrt2,0)^t$ and
$(1/\sqrt6,-1/\sqrt2,0)^t$. Their off-diagonal contributions
cancel. Together with the boundary column and the three
third-coordinate terms, their reduced matrix is
$\operatorname{diag}(\rho,1,1)$. In the other two flattenings
the diagonal masses are respectively
$(\rho-1/3)+1/3$, $2/3+1/3$, and $2/3+1/3$.
No off-diagonal term survives. Every displayed tensor term
has total exponent zero modulo three under (K-037b).
The determinant exponent is $1+3+3=7\equiv1\pmod3$, and
each boundary exponent is zero.
\end{proof}

Replace a selected edge of critical mass $\rho$ in any
old tensor with free support by $Q_\rho$, adjoining two coordinates in each
factor and retaining all other old coefficients. Old and
core terms have no cross terms in any reduced matrix:
such a cross term would require an old edge to agree with
the selected edge in two coordinates. The result is
therefore critical. The order-three element (K-037b),
extended by the identity outside, fixes this tensor.
This construction permits a primitive odd determinant
character in just two added dimensions.

\subsection*{An actual target-order collision in dimension eighteen}

Use the K-020 tensor after two amplifications. Its dimension,
full diagonal period, and selected output mass are
\[
 m=16,\qquad D=2^{17}=131072,\qquad
                 \rho=\frac{76599}{131072}.              \tag{K-037c}
\]
The selected edge is $(15,13,13)$ in zero-based coordinates.
Its two first-factor frame masses are
\[
             \alpha_1=\frac{16189}{D},\qquad
             \alpha_2=\frac{38284}{D}.                   \tag{K-037d}
\]
The middle factor has one frame and the third has two.

Duplicate the selected eigenvalue twice in each factor of
a primitive order-$D$ generator. Let $h$ be the resulting
order-$D$ element on dimension eighteen and put $U=V_{18}^h$.
Its complete fixed representation is
\[
 U=(A_3\otimes B_3\otimes C_3)\oplus A_3^{\oplus2}
          \oplus B_3\oplus C_3^{\oplus2}\oplus\mathbb C^{40},
                                                            \tag{K-037e}
\]
with outside torus characters retained. Its dimension is $82$,
and the fixed special-linear Lie algebra has dimension $69$.

There is an effectively free critical tensor in this space.
Perform two successive edge doublings K-015 at the selected
edge and then at the new diagonal output. The original
selected mass has odd numerator and denominator $D$.
The first new diagonal mass is $1-\rho/2$, with odd
numerator and exact denominator $2D$; its doubling gives
exact diagonal period $4D$. All replacement masses are
positive. Coverage at each new diagonal persists: each
old selected coordinate has a nonzero remaining old frame,
and each new coordinate has its replacement edge avoiding
the other two new coordinates. Thus the general Lie-extension
lemma K-015 applies at both steps. The lifted spectra are
simple, their sole antipodal pairs are the selected old/new
pairs, and each has a nonzero one-paired-plane projection.
K-016 gives full stabilizer $S\times\mu_{4D}$, with faithful
determinant character. Reducing its weights
modulo $D$ collapses the three selected coordinates and gives
exactly (K-037e). The smooth-fixed-germ argument of K-029
therefore identifies the reduced closed image of $U$ with
a whole irreducible component
\[
               Y\subset X_{18}^{\mu_D},\qquad\dim Y=13.
                                                            \tag{K-037f}
\]
Here $D=2^{18-1}$ is the originally targeted order.

Replace the selected edge instead by $Q_\rho$, obtaining a
critical tensor $T\in U$. Extend (K-037b) by the identity
to an element $a$ of order three. It commutes with $h$,
and both fix $T$. The element
\[
                                  g=ha                    \tag{K-037g}
\]
has primitive determinant character of order $3D$:
$\chi(h)$ is primitive dyadic and $\chi(a)$ primitive cubic.
Criticality makes its quotient point nonzero. Hence
\[
                    Y\cap X_{18}^{\mu_{3D}}\ne\{0\}.       \tag{K-037h}
\]
This is incidence of the actual indexed component with the
higher odd-inertia locus.

\subsection*{A finite centralizer quotient on the collision locus}

Let
\[
 U_g=V_{18}^g,\qquad C_g=C_{SL_{18}^3}(g),\qquad
                       B=\mathbb C[U_g]^{C_g}.            \tag{K-037i}
\]
The first factor of $g$ has one double eigenvalue on the
first two selected coordinates; all other eigenvalues in
all factors are simple. Its complete fixed representation
has five binary vector groups and $46$ scalar groups.
The vectors are the three core positions
$(B_0,C_0)$, $(B_1,C_2)$, $(B_2,C_1)$ in the first two
first-factor coordinates, and the two old first-factor
frames. The three remaining core positions are scalar
groups, as are all remaining old groups. Consequently
\[
                 \dim U_g=56,\qquad
                 \dim\operatorname{Lie}C_g=53.            \tag{K-037j}
\]

Restriction makes $B$ a finite graded module over
$R_{18}=\mathbb C[V_{18}]^{SL_{18}^3}$. Indeed $g$ is
unitary, so the complete moment/nullcone argument and
graded finite-module induction of K-032 apply to $U_g$
and $C_g$. Its module support is the closed image $Z$ of
$U_g$. Since $U_g\subset U$ and $\chi(g)$ is primitive
of order $3D$,
\[
                         Z\subset Y\cap X_{18}^{\mu_{3D}}.
                                                            \tag{K-037k}
\]
No birationality assertion is needed. We do not identify
$\operatorname{Spec}B$ with the normalization of $Z$ or
assert that $Z$ is a whole $\mu_{3D}$-fixed component.

The $51$ compressed group-incidence columns are independent.
Deleting one outside row in the second and third factors
gives a maximal minor of absolute determinant
$3D=393216$. Their unique balance masses on the five binary
groups are
\[
                 \rho-\frac13,\quad\frac23,\quad\frac23,
                            \quad\alpha_1,\quad\alpha_2. \tag{K-037l}
\]
The three new scalar core masses are $1/3$; all other
old group masses are unchanged. The three scalar core
masses must be equal by the second- and third-factor
equations, and their sum is one at the last first-factor
coordinate. The full rank, minor, and group margins are
retained as exact certificates.

\subsection*{Exact Hilbert series of the supported module}

The primitive character of $g$ implies $B_s=0$ unless
$3D$ divides $s$ in the original tensor grading.
At $s=3Da$, the five forced binary degrees are $d_i=P_i a$,
where
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 (P_0,P_1,P_2,P_3,P_4)=(98725,262144,262144,48567,114852),\\
 S=3D=393216,\qquad\sum_iP_i=2S.
 \end{gathered}                                          \tag{K-037m}
\]
The scalar groups contribute unique monomials of their
forced degrees. The sole nonabelian factor of $C_g$
is $SL_2$ on the binary block; the block-center condition
is satisfied by $\sum_i d_i=2s$. Hence
\[
 \dim B_{3Da}
   =\dim\left(\bigotimes_{i=0}^4
                 \operatorname{Sym}^{P_i a}\mathbb C^2\right)^{SL_2}.
                                                            \tag{K-037n}
\]
This multiplicity requires no ternary-trilinear invariant
formula. Subtracting the weight-two multiplicity from the
weight-zero multiplicity gives
\[
 \dim B_{3Da}
     =[x^{Sa}](1-x)\prod_{i=0}^4(1+x+\cdots+x^{P_i a}).
                                                            \tag{K-037o}
\]
Expanding the numerator yields
\[
 \sum_{I\subseteq\{0,1,2,3,4\}}(-1)^{|I|}
       {\bigl(S-\sum_{i\in I}P_i\bigr)a-|I|+3\choose3},
                                                            \tag{K-037p}
\]
where a term is included only when its coefficient index
is nonnegative. No subset sum in (K-037m) equals $S$.
For every $a\ge1$, the included subsets are the empty set,
all singletons, every pair except $\{1,2\}$, and the one
triple $\{0,3,4\}$. The least positive slope is $16220$,
so no small-$a$ truncation changes this list. At $a=0$
the same polynomial sum gives the correct value one.
Expansion of these sixteen cubic polynomials gives
\[
                     \dim B_{3Da}
                       =4531688675a^2+131072a+1           \tag{K-037q}
\]
for all $a\ge0$. The verifier also checks (K-037o)
independently by direct weight-string multiplication at
$a=0,1,2$; the all-degree conclusion follows from the
finite symbolic identity (K-037p).

Putting $z=t^{3D}$, we obtain
\[
 H_B(t)=
       \frac{1+4531819745z+4531557604z^2}{(1-z)^3}.         \tag{K-037r}
\]
Its dimension is three and its density in this regrading
is $9063377350$, a unit at three. This is the density of
the finite quotient source and its pushforward module,
not an unproved density identification for $Z$.

\subsection*{The exact three-adic obstruction on the actual support}

At a primitive $D$th root set $u=1-t^D$, so $z=(1-u)^3$.
Substitution into (K-037r) gives
\[
 H_B(t)=\frac{9063377350}{27u^3}
           -\frac{1510519201}{3u^2}
           +\frac{4531295468}{27u}+O(1).                  \tag{K-037s}
\]
The leading and simple coefficients both have valuation $-3$
at three.

Let $\tau$ cyclically permute the tensor factors, and form
the actual graded coherent module
\[
                    \mathcal M=B\oplus\tau_*B\oplus\tau_*^2B
                                                            \tag{K-037t}
\]
on the tensor quotient. Cyclic permutation of its summands
gives a $C_3$ action. Its ordinary Hilbert series is $3H_B$,
and its nontrivial cyclic trace series is zero. Its support
lies in the union of the actual collision component
closures $Y,\tau Y,\tau^2Y$. The leading and simple
coefficients of $H_{\mathcal M}$ in (K-037s) have valuation
$-2$, so this orbit class is not three-integral.
Dividing by the moving-generator factor $D_M(t)$ of
K-027 cannot make its principal part integral: that
factor is a three-adic unit at the primitive dyadic
root, and the leading coefficient retains valuation $-2$.

This is an explicit coherent class on the actual tensor
collision locus, not on an unrelated affine variety.
A blanket three-integrality claim for arbitrary cyclic
orbit classes on the exception in K-031 is therefore
false. We do not assert that the particular exceptional
moving Koszul class equals, contains, or has the same
Laurent coefficient as (K-037t). Its value and possible
cancellation against the chain remain open. No pole
of the global tensor Hilbert series is deduced here.
