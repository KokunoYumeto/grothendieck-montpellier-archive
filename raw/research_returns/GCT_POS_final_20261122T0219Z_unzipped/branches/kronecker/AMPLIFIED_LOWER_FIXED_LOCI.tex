\section{K-021: cubic growth of the originally targeted fixed loci}

\begin{theorem}[K-021]
Let $T_k$ be the K-020 tensor, with
\[
 n_k=2+7k,\qquad D_k=2\cdot256^k,
\]
and let $x_k$ be its image in the $SL_{n_k}^3$ quotient.
For $1\le l<k$, the fixed scheme of the grading subgroup
$\mu_{D_{k-l}}$ is smooth at $x_k$ and has local dimension
\[
                         343l^3+14l+1.                 \tag{K-021a}
\]
Consequently, for $k\ge8$, the fixed scheme of
$\mu_{2^{n_k-1}}$ has local dimension at least
\[
          343\lfloor k/8\rfloor^3+14\lfloor k/8\rfloor+1.
                                                               \tag{K-021b}
\]
When $k=8l$, this is an equality.  In particular, in dimensions
$n=2+56l$ the originally targeted subgroup $\mu_{2^{n-1}}$
has a smooth fixed component of dimension $343l^3+14l+1$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
All quotient points in question are smooth by K-020.  Choose a
diagonal generator $g_k$ of the full finite stabilizer of $T_k$.
Its determinant character is primitive of order $D_k$.
The element $h=g_k^{256^l}$ therefore induces a primitive grading
automorphism of order $D_{k-l}$.

The spectral reduction in the proof of K-020 has the following
iterated form.  Reduce the factor exponents of $g_k$ modulo
$D_{k-l}$.  On the coordinates already present at stage $k-l$,
the reduced element is a primitive generator of that old
stabilizer, up to scalar factors whose product is one.  Every
coordinate added in the last $l$ gadgets has the same reduced
weight as the corresponding coordinate of the selected output
edge at stage $k-l$.

Indeed, at each gluing all eight coordinates of a gadget factor
are congruent modulo the previous period to its boundary
coordinate.  The next selected output is755, all three of whose
coordinates are new relative to boundary401.  Thus each subsequent
gluing refines a coordinate already in the preceding refined
block.  Successive reduction collapses precisely one nested
block in each factor.  The block size is
\[
                           r=1+7l.
\]
All other coordinates are unchanged.  The reduced old spectra
are simple, so there are no further equalities between factor
weights.  If $m=n_{k-l}$, each factor therefore has one weight
of multiplicity $r$ and $m-1$ other simple weights.

The complete old zero-weight support is exactly the selected
support, by K-020.  Hence a tensor coordinate is fixed by $h$
if and only if its collapsed triple is an old support edge.
The selected old edge expands to the whole $r\times r\times r$
block, giving $r^3$ fixed coordinates.  Any other old edge
shares at most one selected coordinate, by freeness.

At stage $k-l\ge1$, the selected output755 has coordinate degrees
$(3,2,3)$.  These are read directly from the22-edge gadget; all
three coordinates are new at the stage when that output is
selected, so there are no additional old incident edges.
There are consequently exactly
\[
                           3+2+3-3=5
\]
other old edges sharing one selected coordinate.  They each
expand to $r$ fixed coordinates.  The remaining $3m-8$ old edges
share none and each contribute one.  This is an exhaustive count:
\[
                       \dim V^h=r^3+5r+3m-8.             \tag{K-021c}
\]
The corresponding fixed Lie algebra has dimension
\[
 \dim(\mathfrak{sl}_{n_k}^3)^h
       =3\bigl(r^2+(m-1)-1\bigr)=3r^2+3m-6.             \tag{K-021d}
\]
The infinitesimal $SL_{n_k}^3$ action is injective, because the
effective stabilizer is trivial.  Taking fixed vectors is exact
for a finite-order complex linear operator.  The fixed tangent
space of the smooth quotient thus has dimension
\[
 r^3-3r^2+5r-2=(r-1)^3+2(r-1)+1=343l^3+14l+1.
\]
The fixed scheme of a finite-order automorphism of a smooth
complex variety is smooth, proving (K-021a).

Write $k=8l+s$, with $0\le s<8$.  The element inducing the
requested grading subgroup is $g_k^{2^k}$.  Since
\[
                 g_k^{2^k}=(g_k^{256^l})^{2^s},
\]
its fixed tangent space contains that of $g_k^{256^l}$.  This gives
(K-021b).  When $s=0$ the two elements coincide, giving equality.
\end{proof}

The theorem concerns actual smooth fixed schemes and supplies no
global noncancellation statement.  In particular, neither the
existence nor the dimension of these components alone proves a
pole of the global invariant Hilbert series at the stated roots.
