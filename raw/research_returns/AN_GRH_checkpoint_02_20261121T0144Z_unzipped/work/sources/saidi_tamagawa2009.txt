Abstract
In this paper, we prove a prime-to-p version of Grothendieck’s anabelian con-
jecture for hyperbolic curves over ﬁnite ﬁelds of characteristic p > 0, whose original
(full proﬁnite) version was proved by Tamagawa in the aﬃne case and by Mochizuki
in the proper case.
