## G-01 — PASS

embeds as a subring in the source polynomial ring, so is torsion-free as
a Z-module and hence flat. The characteristic-zero fiber claim is justified
by flatness of C over Z: tensoring the exact sequence at the kernel

## G-03 — PASS

The locus of reduced distinct length-r subschemes is open in Hilb^r(X):
it is the etale locus of the finite flat universal family. Linear
independence is the open surjectivity condition for the evaluation map
V* -> p_* O_Z(1), a rank-r locally free sheaf. Its dual defines the
claimed map to the Grassmannian. Nondegeneracy guarantees that this locus
is nonempty for r<=dim V.

Hilb^r(X) and the Grassmannian are projective; the graph closure is
projective. The projective tautological bundle has a proper map to P(V).
The preimage of the graph open is dense because a projective bundle
is an open surjective morphism (or by local triviality). The image of the
whole bundle is therefore exactly the closure of the image of this open,
using continuity in one direction and properness in the other.

For a fully expanded explanation of the remaining density assertion,
use the rank-r vector bundle direct sum of the r tautological lines over
X^r. Projectivize it and remove the locus where the sum in V is zero.
The sum map to P(V) on this open parameterizes the spans of all r-tuples.
The inverse image of the nonempty independent-tuples open is dense,
since X^r is integral and the projective bundle is locally trivial.
This proves that its image has the full secant closure. This is an
expansion of the argument in the notes, not a correction of a gap.

The boundary incidence Z contained in P(L) is also sound: the condition
that the universal linear forms annihilating L restrict to zero on Z
is closed in Hilb^r(X) times Gr(r,V). It contains the graph open, hence
the graph closure. It does not imply that L is exactly the span of Z,
as the notes correctly emphasize.

## G-07 — PASS

For finite free E, the dual of Sym^k(E*) is canonically Gamma^k E.
Therefore the bidegree-(k,n-k) component of the polynomial coproduct
indeed defines C_k(F):Gamma^k E -> Sym^(n-k)(E*), with columns the Hasse
derivatives; no factorial inversion occurs. For determinant and permanent,
squarefreeness means nonzero derivatives use distinct variables, rows,
and columns. A selected matching leaves exactly a complementary
determinant/permanent with the appropriate constant sign. Distinct pairs
of row and column subsets have disjoint monomial supports. Each resulting
polynomial contains coefficients 1 or -1, so is primitive over Z and
nonzero in every characteristic. The columns therefore give a direct sum
of binom(n,k)^2 rank-one free submodules; all fiber ranks are as stated,
including characteristic two. This also covers k=0,n, where there is
just one block.

For a pure power, the bidegree piece is exactly
binom(n,k) l(x)^k l(y)^(n-k). It is a decomposable tensor over Z,
possibly zero after reduction, so C_k(l^n) has rank at most one and
its relevant minors vanish universally. Sums of r such factorizations
factor through a free module of rank r, making the (r+1)-minor identity
valid over every commutative ring as well. In small characteristic this
power-map image may be a degenerate model of the intended symmetric
rank variety; the warning correctly keeps the polynomial-power and
complete-linear-system/divided-power models separate.

The normalized Hilbert tensor coefficient +/-1/n! follows because each
squarefree degree-n monomial has n! different ordered slots. The
normalized tensor is correctly placed over Z[1/n!]; n! times it is
integral. Characteristic-zero identification with ordinary catalecticants
uses nonzero factorial scalings. The metric singular-value and tensor-
norm conclusions are correctly restricted to archimedean Hilbert fibers.
