## G-B04 — Integral representation rings

Exact locators: Theorem 5, §3.7; §3.8 for GL_n. Author-hosted original scan inspected: https://www.college-de-france.fr/media/jean-pierre-serre/UPL8576975072934521334_Serre_Grpes_de_Gr.pdf .
