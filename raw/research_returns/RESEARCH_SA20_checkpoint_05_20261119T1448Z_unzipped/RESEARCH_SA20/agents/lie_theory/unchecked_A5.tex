\subsection*{Outstanding A.5 gates}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Definability of the maximal open local-triviality locus for a
general proper definable map with higher-dimensional base needs a
proof beyond the existence of some Hardt partition.  A5.008 proves
the dense-open and nowhere-dense-complement assertions in general,
and maximal-locus definability for curves and finitely triangulable
maps.  The remaining general definability statement is not inferred
from those restricted results.
\end{enumerate}
The strict-partition claim of 121/049 is no longer an open gate:
A5.009 and A5.010 refute it in all four named contexts, including
pure-dimensional complex algebraic spaces and a Zariski-open
witness.  The proposed proper-map recursion is independently
refuted by A5.002.

The exact maximal-axis TOP-B uniqueness gate is closed by A5.011.
The homeomorphism fixes the axis pointwise, while the two transverse
germs have different end obstructions.
