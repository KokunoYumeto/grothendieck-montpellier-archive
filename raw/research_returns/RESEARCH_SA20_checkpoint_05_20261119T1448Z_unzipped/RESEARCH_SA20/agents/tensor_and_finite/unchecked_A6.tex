\subsection*{A.6: conventions that are not suppressed}
The reconstruction theorem A6.005 uses conical control atlases with
specified radial trivializations, and the corner datum includes compatible
collars and simultaneous local triviality of the finite fiber diagram.
Over a general real closed field no assertion is made that arbitrary smooth
submersion data, without such a definable conical atlas, automatically
supply these extra data. The precise conical-atlas hypothesis is explicit
in the opened primary source ALMP2012, Definition 1(v).
A6.006 refutes the unrestricted morphism interpretation even in the
semialgebraic category. The names ``1-special'', ``2-special'', and
``admissible'' on 121/043 have no definitions on the supplied page;
A6.007--010 state exactly the ordinary fundamental-groupoid
interpretation treated here, its positive surface result and its
counterexamples. No claim identifies that interpretation uniquely with
Grothendieck's intended but unwritten definitions.

A6.010 supplies a complete compact dimension-two object classification
when the cyclic flag incidences and finite covering monodromies are
retained along with the peripheral groupoids. These extra data are
explicit, rather than inferred from the undefined word admissible.
