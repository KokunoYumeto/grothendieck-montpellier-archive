\subsection*{Part A: exact remaining gates after the first three hours}
The following are unresolved task scopes, not theorem labels. We do not
count a proved special case as a solution of its larger scope.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{A.1(b), the main concordance computation.}
A.113 identifies components for every compact definable manifold with
those of its finite PL model. A.103 computes them completely for tori
of dimension at least six, using the corrected Igusa sequence and the
unstable Burghelea--Lashof comparison. It does not compute the component
group for every possible manifold. A.115--A.116 make the PL and
semialgebraic homotopy groups independent of the real closed field.
For dimension at least five the PL-to-SA map is injective in every
degree and is an isomorphism on components and fundamental groups.
The first remaining semialgebraic comparison is surjectivity on
$\pi_2$ over $\R$, already for a finite closed PL model such as the
six-torus. A general o-minimal expansion retains the separate relative
two-parameter straightening gate; it need not have a real-field model.
Scalar straightening does not preserve a second parameter coordinate,
and total-space triangulation alone does not preserve the individual
fibres. The next approach is a relative parametric isotopy-extension
or homeomorphism approximation theorem with its exact projection
condition, or a class in the kernel of the map to TOP.
\item \textbf{A.5 and the stated A.6 scope.}
The separate A.5 list retains the general maximal-triviality-locus
definability gate. A5.011 refutes TOP axis-cancellation by an explicit
nonzero end obstruction and a homeomorphism fixing the axis. The strict
partition claim is refuted in PL, semialgebraic, complex algebraic and
complex analytic contexts. A.6 reconstruction is proved for the
explicit conical-control and compatible-corner data, with the exact
compact two-dimensional groupoid augmentation. No unrestricted
noncompact classification or identification with undefined manuscript
terms is inferred.
\end{enumerate}
The all-arity reconstruction, compact-axiom countermodels, prescribed
triangulation counterexample, intrinsic filtration repair and foliation
topos corrections are proved in their checked sections. Their earlier
working gates are not still pending merely because they occur in dated
checkpoint history.

A.1(d) is refuted in the categorical convention stipulated in the prompt
by A.111; A.117 treats the additional locally parameterized family model.
Hausdorff-only subset families are a separate optional comparison, and
A.118 proves that local joint parameterizations do not follow automatically.
A.209 now refutes unrestricted essential uniqueness in A.2 even when every
singular attachment is allowed. Its proof uses individual approximation
and uniform separation, without a parametric approximation assumption.
