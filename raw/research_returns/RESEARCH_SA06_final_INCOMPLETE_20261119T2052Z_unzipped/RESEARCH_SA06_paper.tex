\begin{abstract}
We distinguish the assertions established in this run from the tasks that
remain unsettled. We give a choice-independent geometric formula for the
Deuring determinant rule over the algebraic closure, explicit failures of
two enlarged descent formulations, and the positive norm and real-sign
normalizations. Root extraction followed by Honda--Tate realizes every
integral Weil number of weight $i$ on $H^i$ over the original finite field;
the weak exponent is $1$. We compute the norm-one divisor obstruction
including its unit-norm contribution, and distinguish it from this
unrestricted realization result. Further sections audit the cycle
conjectures, integral correspondences, rational slope parity, motivic
gerbe obstructions, effectivity, Weil-étale conventions, and pro-fibre
functors. A specified sixfold and elliptic family refute the complex
Albanese-family converse even rationally: the cohomological cylinder
vanishes while the full morphic Abel--Jacobi invariant survives.
We determine the complete dimension ranges for universal injectivity,
surjectivity, and bijectivity of ordinary local cycle restriction over
$\mathbb C$, integrally and rationally. We also determine ordinary local
divisor quotients and refute finiteness modulo ordinary algebraic
equivalence in every non-top positive codimension over $\mathbb C$.
In every positive characteristic, every algebraically closed coefficient
field $k$, and every local dimension $N\ge2$, explicit regular punctures
have integral quotients $B^r(X)\twoheadrightarrow(k,+)$ in every
nonzero codimension $1\le r\le N-1$. These exponent-$p$ targets leave the
unrestricted rational and complex top questions open.
Over specified transcendental coefficient fields in every positive
characteristic, ordinary restriction is not surjective for
$r+2\le N\le2r+1$, and is not injective for $r\ge2$ and
$r+1\le N\le2r$. The kernel witnesses restrict to zero integrally,
including at the critical boundary when $p$ divides $r$.
In every positive characteristic, a specified nonsplit quartic construction
gives an actual ordinary integral Picard restriction kernel containing
$(\mathbb Z/p)^{\min(3,N-1)}$ for every $N\ge3$, also over
$\overline{\mathbb F}_p$. In the stated excellent henselian
coefficient-field scope, integral ordinary divisor restriction is
universally injective exactly for $N=1$ and universally surjective
exactly for $N\le2$ or $N\ge4$. These finite kernels give no rational failure.
For every prime, every $r\ge2$, and every $N\ge2r$, products of
cubic surfaces give actual ordinary restriction kernels of exact
order $p$, already over $\overline{\mathbb F}_p$ and over every
algebraically closed extension. A primitive relative Milnor invariant
treats the entire exceptional image, including its Smith torsion,
in every characteristic. Together with the earlier examples, this
completes universal ordinary integral restriction injectivity over
the full class of algebraically closed coefficient fields in each
positive characteristic: for every $r\ge1$, it holds exactly for
$N\le r$. The lower-interval examples retain their specified
transcendental fields. Rational injectivity and higher-codimension
surjectivity beyond the previously proved ranges remain unsettled.
For smooth projective cones in positive characteristic with the stated
section-ring hypotheses, pro-descent and nilpotent $K$-theory compute
the rational Chow quotient and give a zero rational ordinary top quotient.
The top-degree result also holds for algebraizable germs with one smooth
exceptional divisor and ample conormal, without a retraction.
For every algebraizable normal strict-local threefold with regular puncture
over an arbitrary algebraically closed field of positive characteristic,
an actual proper-curve endpoint theorem reduces the rational ordinary top
quotient to an explicit degree-zero perfect-$K_0$ quotient on an SNC
exceptional surface. No ambient extension condition remains on its
perfect-family relations. A weighted exceptional intersection isogeny
also removes every component Albanese coordinate by actual proper
Picard families. Marked proper edge families remove the entire kernel
of restriction to component zero-cycles, without a dual-complex or
triple-period hypothesis. The remaining quotient is precisely the
direct sum of the component Albanese kernels modulo its induced
proper-family and exceptional-cycle relations; its general vanishing
remains undecided.
Under the displayed regular projective resolution and ample exceptional
conormal hypotheses, the endpoint comparison extends to every dimension
in positive characteristic, even for singular exceptional support without
normal crossings. For SNC support, mixed exceptional intersections also
remove all component degree and Albanese coordinates in every such
resolved dimension. This does not supply resolutions in arbitrary dimension
or evaluate the remaining intrinsic perfect-family quotient.
For every excellent local domain of dimension $N\ge2$
with residue field $\overline{\mathbb F}_p$, we prove
$\mathrm{CH}_1(\operatorname{Spec}R)_{\mathbb Q}=0$ and hence
$\mathrm{CH}^{N-1}$ of its puncture is zero rationally.
This includes mixed characteristic and requires neither henselianity,
ambient normality, nor a regular puncture. The determinant and normalized-surface
argument uses resolution only in dimension two. The corresponding
ordinary quotient vanishes wherever its field-family definition applies.
Characteristic-$p$ ordinary Picard restriction is proved for excellent
henselian coefficient-field germs in dimension at least four, with the
stated normality and regular-puncture hypotheses.
A status assigned to a proposition applies only to the precise
