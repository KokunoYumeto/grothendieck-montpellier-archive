# Proper abelian subgroups of the local Picard functor: remaining algebraization gate

## Status

We have not proved that an arbitrary morphism from an abelian variety to the local Picard functor is induced by an actual line-bundle family on the ordinary product of the punctured spectrum with that abelian variety. In particular, this converse must not be inferred from the already proved statement that every ordinary algebraic-equivalence class belongs to an abelian image in the local Picard group.

Boutot's local functor is constructed using henselized tensor products and étale sheafification. Carvajal-Rojas and Stäbler (2023), Appendix A, Theorem A.1 and Remark A.3, printed p.716, restate that precise definition and identify its values with actual Picard groups for strictly local parameter algebras. They do not make that identification for a general proper parameter variety. Their published primary PDF was opened at https://content.algebraicgeometry.nl/2023-6/2023-6-025.pdf (refs turn244762533view1 and turn643528107view3).

## Exact newer GAGA result and its limit

Devadas (2025), Theorem 4.3.1 and Corollary 4.3.3, proves algebraization of coherent subobjects and quotients of already algebraizable sheaves and full faithfulness for proper schemes over noetherian Henselian pairs. This is not an essential-surjectivity theorem. Section 4.4 explicitly leaves general coherent-sheaf algebraization in positive characteristic unresolved; Example 4.4.1 gives a rank-two counterexample in characteristic zero and does not settle whether rank one can fail. Corollary 4.3.4 does prove algebraization of maps between henselizations of proper finitely presented schemes. The primary full text was opened at https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-the-institute-of-mathematics-of-jussieu/article/gaga-for-henselian-schemes/9E0653261AD6CB0F0E38BAAC4ACE6979 (refs turn380450141view0 and turn232604211view1).

Thus a possible structured route is to convert a proper abelian subgroup into a morphism to a dual abelian scheme on a proper modification, and then apply algebraization of maps. Neither the conversion nor the passage back to an ordinary product-family has been proved in this note. Properness of the parameter space alone is insufficient as a cited justification.
