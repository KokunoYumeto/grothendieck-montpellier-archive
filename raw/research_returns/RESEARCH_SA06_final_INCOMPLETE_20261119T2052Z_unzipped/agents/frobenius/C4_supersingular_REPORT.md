# C.4u: supersingular threefolds away from characteristics two and three

The full proof is in C4_supersingular_addition.tex. It supplies an actual new affirmative family in Question 2: **all supersingular abelian threefolds over algebraically closed fields of characteristic at least five**, without assuming principal polarizability. We do not count the accompanying factorial reformulation as a separate gate closure.

The new primary input is the p-primary minimal-isogeny construction in Yu's Lemma 3.2 proof, author p. 7, using Lemma 2.4, p. 5. The full arXiv v2 was opened, saved, and extracted locally. Since the base field is perfect, the field extension in the statement disappears. The finite-index Dieudonne lattice quotient is killed by a power of p. Hence a power of p factors through a product of supersingular elliptic curves, which is a product of Jacobians. The already proved factorization of six for arbitrary threefolds now combines with it to give the displayed exact identity factorization.

No claim for all supersingular threefolds in characteristics two or three is made; the bound is reduced to the characteristic prime in each case. The literature question of principal polarizability in those two cases is being checked separately. No priority claim is made.
