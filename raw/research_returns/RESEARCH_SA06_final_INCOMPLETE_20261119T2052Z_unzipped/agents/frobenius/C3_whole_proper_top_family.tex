\paragraph{C.3bu. Whole proper top-family lifting (Proved).}
Retain the hypotheses of C.3bn.  Thus $k$ is algebraically closed of
characteristic $p>0$, $R$ is an excellent henselian algebraizable local
coefficient-$k$ algebra with residue field $k$, and
$Z\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ is projective, with $Z$ regular of pure
dimension $d+1$, $d\geq1$, and an isomorphism off the closed point.
An effective Cartier divisor $D\subset Z$ has the reduced closed fibre
$E$ as its full support, and $\mathcal O_D(-D)$ is ample.  Let $P/k$
be a fixed smooth connected projective curve and put $T=D\times_kP$.
Then
\begin{equation}
 F_\gamma^dK_0(T)_{\mathbf Q}\ \subseteq\
 \operatorname{im}\!\left(K_0(Z\times_kP)_{\mathbf Q}
                 \longrightarrow K_0(T)_{\mathbf Q}\right).
                                                        \tag{WP.1}
\end{equation}
This is equality with the prescribed perfect class on the entire
fixed exceptional product.  It does not assert extension of a
prescribed vector bundle as an object.  In exceptional dimensions
$d\leq2$, restriction on the whole rational $K_0$ group is surjective,
and
\begin{equation}
       K_{-1}(\operatorname{Spec}R\times_kP)_{\mathbf Q}=0.
                                                        \tag{WP.8}
\end{equation}
The latter assertion uses these geometric hypotheses; it is not an
application of henselian-local negative-$K$ vanishing to a product.

\emph{1. Exact lifting of finite perfect corrections.}
Call a finite scheme $F\subset T$ admissible if it is regularly
immersed with codimension $d+1$, its support lies in the smooth
top-dimensional strata of $E\times P$, and a defining regular
sequence has independent differentials on those strata.  The
nilpotent structure inherited from $D$ is retained.  We first prove
that
\begin{equation}
 [\mathcal O_F]\in\operatorname{im}\!\left(
           K_0(Z\times P)\longrightarrow K_0(T)\right)
       \qquad(F\text{ admissible}),                    \tag{WP.2}
\end{equation}
without taking a tensor power or rationalizing.

Choose $d$ sufficiently high relatively ample line bundles on
$Z\times P$, also ample in the $P$ direction, and sections on $T$
vanishing on $F$.  Serre vanishing gives surjectivity onto their
twisted conormal sections.  A general $(d+1)\times d$ conormal
matrix has full rank everywhere on $F$: rank drop has codimension
two and $F$ is finite.  The condition on $F_{\mathrm{red}}$ gives
a locally split injection on $F$ by Nakayama's lemma.
Away from $F$, impose transversality to a finite smooth stratification
of $E\times P$, for every prefix of the flag.  The finite first-jet
incidence argument in AE.5--AE.7 supplies all these open conditions.
Sufficiently high twists also have
\[
 H^1\bigl(Z\times P,\mathcal A_j(-D\times P)\bigr)=0,
\]
so the sections lift to the actual fixed product
\citep[Lemma 30.17.1]{frC3azStacksSerre}.

Let $j:W\hookrightarrow Z\times P$ be their intersection, and put
$H=W\cap T$.  Independent ambient differentials make $W$ regular
of pure dimension two along the full closed fibre.  Its nonregular
locus is closed by excellence; a nonempty proper image in the local
scheme $\operatorname{Spec}R$ would contain the closed point.
It follows that $W$ is regular everywhere.  The same argument
excludes components of an unintended dimension.  Thus $j$ is a
regular immersion of codimension $d$.  The flag meets the reduced
closed fibre in the expected dimension one, so $H$ is Cartier on
$W$ and has the full closed fibre as support.

The conormal condition identifies the flag equations with the
first $d$ members of a regular-sequence generating set of $I_F$.
The remaining generator cuts out $F$ as an effective Cartier divisor
on the possibly nonreduced Cohen--Macaulay curve $H$.
Set $Q_0=\mathcal O_H(-F)$ and $J=\mathcal O_W(-H)$.
For every $n\geq1$,
\[
                 H^2(H,J^n/J^{n+1})=0,
\]
because $H$ has dimension one
\citep[Proposition 20.20.7]{frWPStacksDimensionVanishing}.
The square-zero unit sequence therefore extends $Q_0$ through all
thickenings of $H$, without a power
\citep[Lemma 37.4.1]{frC3azStacksPicThickening}.

The powers of $J$ and $\mathfrak m\mathcal O_W$ are cofinal.
Proper Grothendieck existence algebraizes this formal line bundle
and its inverse on $W_{\widehat R}$; full faithfulness verifies
invertibility \citep[Lemma 30.24.3]{frC3azStacksExistence}.
Regularity of completion and Popescu express $\widehat R$ as a
filtered colimit of smooth $R$-algebras
\citep[Lemma 15.51.14; Theorem 16.12.1]
{frC3azStacksCompletion,frC3azStacksPopescu}.
The bundle descends to one such algebra
\citep[Lemma 32.10.3(2)]{frC3azStacksLimitBundles}.
For $\mathfrak m^M\mathcal O_W\subset J$, preserve the specified
$R/\mathfrak m^M$ point by henselian smooth lifting
\citep[Lemma 15.13.3]{frC3azStacksHenselSmooth}.
This gives a line bundle $Q$ on the fixed $W$ with $Q|_H\simeq Q_0$.
This is the actual finite-jet descent of AE.9, rather than a
retained formal or etale replacement of the product.

The square formed by $j$ and $T\hookrightarrow Z\times P$ is
Tor-independent, because $H$ is Cartier on $W$.
Writing $i:H\hookrightarrow T$, perfect pushforward and derived
base change give
\begin{equation}
 \bigl(j_*(1-[Q])\bigr)|_T
   =i_*\bigl(1-[\mathcal O_H(-F)]\bigr)
   =[\mathcal O_F]\quad\hbox{in }K_0(T).              \tag{WP.2a}
\end{equation}
The last equality is the Cartier exact sequence.  This proves
(WP.2) in perfect $K_0$, without a Cartan injectivity or gamma
pushforward assertion.

\emph{2. The top gamma tail.}
Apply the perfect determinantal construction AE.2--AE.6 with total
gamma degree $n=d+1=\dim T$.  Modulo $F_\gamma^{n+1}$, any element
of $F_\gamma^n$ is a sum of products of gamma operations of total
degree $n$ on sufficiently generated bundles.  The exact
Eagon--Northcott identity AE.3 replaces each factor by its perfect
maximal-minor class modulo the next filtration; its resolution
and grade statements are
\citet[Section 2.C; Theorems 2.1 and 2.16]
{D_BrunsVetter1988Determinantal}.
The resulting general intersection has dimension zero, misses all
lower-dimensional strata, and avoids every deeper rank locus:
for a factor of degree $i_a$, that incidence has codimension
\[
       2(i_a+1)+\sum_{b\ne a}i_b=n+i_a+2>n.
\]
Row and column reduction at the intersection gives $n$ defining
functions with independent differentials on its top smooth stratum.
Cohen--Macaulayness makes them a regular sequence.  The tensor
product of the perfect determinantal resolutions is therefore
exactly $[\mathcal O_F]$ for an admissible finite $F$.
The dimension theorem gives
$F_\gamma^{n+1}K_0(T)_{\mathbf Q}=0$
\citep[Chapter II, Remark 8.8.5]{D_Weibel2013KbookII}.
Consequently
\begin{equation}
 F_\gamma^{d+1}K_0(T)_{\mathbf Q}
   \text{ is spanned by admissible }[\mathcal O_F].    \tag{WP.3}
\end{equation}
Only the rational filtration bound is needed here.  By (WP.2)
the whole tail lifts.

\emph{3. Line-bundle differences on the curve representatives.}
Let $C\subset T$ be a curve produced in AE.5--AE.6.  It is a
projective Cohen--Macaulay curve, possibly nonreduced, regularly
immersed with codimension $d$.  It meets the lower-dimensional
strata of $E\times P$ only in a finite set.  Off that set, its
reduction is smooth and its $d$ defining differentials are independent
on the top stratum.
For every line bundle $M$ on $C$, choose an ample $A$ and an integer
$N$ so large that $M\otimes A^N$ and $A^N$ have the required generated
first jets.  General sections avoid the associated points and the
finite bad set, and have simple zeros on the smooth reduction.
Their zero schemes $F_+$ and $F_-$ are finite effective Cartier
divisors.  The composite immersions into $T$ are admissible: the
additional transverse section supplies the last independent
differential.  The Cartier sequences give
\[
 [M\otimes A^N]-[A^N]
   =[\mathcal O_{F_+}(F_+)]-[\mathcal O_{F_-}(F_-)].
\]
Every line bundle on a finite $k$-scheme is trivial.  Indeed its
ring is Artinian semilocal; choose simultaneous residue-field bases
by the Chinese remainder theorem and apply Nakayama at every maximal
ideal to obtain a single basis
\citep[Lemma 10.78.7 and proof hints]{frWPStacksSemilocal}.
Multiplication by $[A^{-N}]$ leaves both finite classes unchanged.
Hence
\begin{equation}
 [M]-1=[\mathcal O_{F_+}]-[\mathcal O_{F_-}]
                  \quad\hbox{in perfect }K_0(C).      \tag{WP.4}
\end{equation}
This argument retains the finite nonreduced schemes, rather than
replacing their perfect classes by cycles.

\emph{4. Correct the entire curve class.}
Perform the AE.7 flag for $C$.  Its $W$ is a regular threefold,
$H=W\cap T$ is Cartier on $W$, and $C$ is Cartier on $H$.
The conormal $\mathcal O_H(-H)$ is ample relative to $H\to P$.
Relative Serre vanishing and coherent cohomological dimension one
of $P$ give
\[
                    H^2(H,\mathcal O_H(-nH))=0
                         \qquad(n\gg0).
\]
The finitely many earlier Picard obstruction groups are killed by
$p$.  Successively taking $p$th tensor powers at those stages, then
using the finite-jet algebraization just recalled, gives a finite
$a=p^e$ and an actual line bundle $Q$ on $W$ satisfying
$Q|_H\simeq\mathcal O_H(-aC)$.  This is AE.8--AE.9.

Put $b_C=j_*(1-[Q])$ for $j:W\hookrightarrow Z\times P$.
The whole square with $T$, not just its endpoint squares, is
Tor-independent: the flag is regular and the equation of $D$
is a nonzerodivisor on $W$.  Successive Cartier sequences give
\begin{equation}
       b_C|_T=\sum_{r=0}^{a-1}
          [\mathcal O_C\otimes\mathcal O_H(-rC)|_C].   \tag{WP.5}
\end{equation}
Subtracting $a[\mathcal O_C]$ expresses the difference as perfect
pushforwards of line-bundle differences on $C$.
By (WP.4) it is a finite integral combination of admissible finite
classes on $T$.  Lift each by (WP.2), and let $c_C$ be their integral
sum on $Z\times P$.  We obtain
\begin{equation}
                   (b_C-c_C)|_T=a[\mathcal O_C].       \tag{WP.6}
\end{equation}
Thus the entire prescribed curve class lifts rationally.
AE.2--AE.6 express any class in $F_\gamma^d$ as a finite rational
sum of these curves modulo $F_\gamma^{d+1}$; (WP.3) lifts the
remainder.  This proves (WP.1).

\emph{5. Full rational $K_0$ in exceptional dimensions at most two.}
Nilpotent invariance in characteristic $p$ identifies
$K_0(T)_{\mathbf Q}$ with $K_0(E\times P)_{\mathbf Q}$ as rational
lambda rings, as in AE.13
\citep[Theorem A]{rootGeisserHesselholt2011}.
For $d=2$, start with a prescribed class on $T$.
Its componentwise ranks lift by the point isomorphism FF.4 and
pullback to the product.  After subtraction the class has rank zero.
Its determinant is a rational Picard class.
For each line-bundle representative $M$ on $T$, a finite power
$M^{p^e}$ lifts to $Z\times P$ by AE.8--AE.9 with $W=Z\times P$
and $H=T$: the conormal is ample relative to $P$, so the same eventual
$H^2$ vanishing applies.  If $Q$ is that lift, then
$p^{-e}([Q]-1)$ has the required determinant modulo
$F_\gamma^2$ after restriction.  The rank-and-determinant description
of $K_0/F_\gamma^2$ leaves precisely a class in $F_\gamma^2$, which
(WP.1) lifts.  Therefore
\begin{equation}
 K_0(Z\times P)_{\mathbf Q}\longrightarrow
                  K_0(E\times P)_{\mathbf Q}
       \quad\text{is surjective}\qquad(d\leq2).       \tag{WP.7}
\end{equation}
For $d=1$ ranks and (WP.1), applied to $F_\gamma^1$, suffice.
Finally FF.1 identifies the cokernel in (WP.7) with the group in
(WP.8).  This proves the negative-$K$ consequence by the exact
fixed-product blow-up sequence, not by a locality assertion.
\qed

\emph{Scope.}
Every flag and every parameter product above is proper over the
original local ring $R$.  After a transcendental scalar extension,
$R\otimes_k k(u)$ is generally not local or henselian, and these
arguments do not apply without further proof.  Localizing or
henselizing would change the prescribed product.  The result gives
no affine-parameter compactification and no valuation descent of
a proper $K_1$ family constructed only after that replacement.
It also does not construct a global exceptional family from
independent component families.  Thus the remaining component
Albanese-kernel quotient in C.3bt is unchanged.
