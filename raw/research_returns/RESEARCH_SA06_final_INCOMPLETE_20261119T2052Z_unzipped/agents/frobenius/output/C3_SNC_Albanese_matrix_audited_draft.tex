\paragraph{Weighted exceptional intersections remove the component
Albanese coordinates (proposed reduction).}
Retain the data and notation of C.3bm. Thus $k$ is algebraically
closed of characteristic $p>0$, $Z$ is the regular projective
threefold modification, $E=\bigcup_iE_i$ is its full SNC divisor,
and $D=\sum_i a_iE_i$ has $a_i>0$ and $\mathcal O_E(-D)$ ample.
Put $A_i=\operatorname{Pic}^0_{E_i/k,\mathrm{red}}$,
$B_i=\operatorname{Alb}(E_i)=A_i^\vee$, and $A=\prod_iA_i$.
The canonical Albanese class of a degree-zero cycle is independent
of a base point. Define
\begin{align}
 \alpha_E:S_0(E)&\longrightarrow\bigoplus_iB_i(k)\otimes\mathbf Q,
 \notag\\
 s&\longmapsto
 \bigl(\operatorname{alb}_{E_i}(\operatorname{ch}_2(s|_{E_i}))\bigr)_i,
 &S_{00}(E)&=\ker(\alpha_E). \tag{AM.1}
\end{align}
Write
$U=\theta(\bigoplus_i A_i(k))_{\mathbf Q}$, using the divisor-cycle
maps $A_i(k)\to\mathrm{CH}_1(E_i)$. Then
\begin{equation}
 U\subset J_K(E),\qquad
 \alpha_E(U)=\bigoplus_iB_i(k)\otimes\mathbf Q. \tag{AM.2}
\end{equation}
Consequently inclusion induces
\begin{align}
 \frac{S_0(E)}{J_K(E)}
 &\simeq\frac{S_{00}(E)}{J_K(E)\cap S_{00}(E)}, \tag{AM.3}\\
 B^2(X)_{\mathbf Q}
 &\simeq
 \frac{S_{00}(E)}
 {(J_K(E)\cap S_{00}(E))+
  (\theta\mathrm{CH}_1(E)_{\mathbf Q}\cap S_{00}(E))}. \tag{AM.4}
\end{align}
The component Albanese kernels and perfect-class compatibility
along SNC intersections remain in (AM.4). No vanishing is asserted.

\begin{proof}
\emph{1. Curve autoduality and intersection homomorphisms.}
For a smooth connected projective curve $C$, put
$J_C=\operatorname{Pic}^0(C)$ and let
$\lambda_C:J_C\to J_C^\vee$ be its principal polarization.
If $f:C\to J_C$ sends $x$ to $[x-o]$, then
\citet[Lemma 6.9 and Remark 6.10(c), author p.~23]
{D_MilneJacobians2021} gives
\[
                         f^*\lambda_C=-\operatorname{id}_{J_C}.
\]
Thus the canonical Albanese map into $J_C^\vee$, normalized
to induce the identity on Picard varieties, is $-\lambda_C f$.
This fixes the sign; Proposition~6.1 of that source,
pp.~19--20, gives the Albanese universal property. The statement
is valid in all characteristics.

For a smooth projective surface $V$ and a line bundle $H$, define
\[
 \kappa_H:\operatorname{Pic}^0_{V/k,\mathrm{red}}\to
              \operatorname{Alb}(V),\qquad
 L\longmapsto\operatorname{alb}_V(c_1(H)c_1(L)),\qquad
 \mu_H=-\kappa_H.
\]
One constructs the homomorphism by writing $H$ as a difference
of sufficiently ample smooth divisors and using their curve maps;
intersection of cycles proves independence of that choice.
These homomorphisms are additive in $H$.
For a smooth curve $i:C\hookrightarrow V$ and restriction
$r=i^*:\operatorname{Pic}^0_{V/k,\mathrm{red}}\to J_C$,
Picard--Albanese duality and the preceding sign give
\begin{equation}
                  \mu_{\mathcal O_V(C)}=r^\vee\lambda_Cr.
                                                        \tag{AM.5}
\end{equation}
In particular all $\mu_H$ are symmetric.

If $H$ is ample, take $n$ large and a smooth connected
$C\in|H^n|$. Serre duality and Serre vanishing give
$H^1(V,H^{-n})=0$. The divisor sequence therefore makes
$H^1(V,\mathcal O_V)\to H^1(C,\mathcal O_C)$ injective.
Hence the restriction homomorphism $r$ on the reduced Picard
varieties has zero tangent kernel and consequently finite kernel.
This does not assume smoothness of the full Picard scheme.
It follows that
\begin{equation}
                           n\mu_H=r^\vee\lambda_Cr       \tag{AM.6}
\end{equation}
is a polarization: $r$ is finite onto its image, and the theta
line bundle pulls back to an ample line bundle. We use the
finite-pullback implication of
\citet[Lemma 30.17.2 and its proof]{frC3beStacksFiniteAmple},
Serre vanishing
\citep[Lemma 30.17.1]{frC3azStacksSerre}, and sufficiently
ample smooth connected sections
\citep[Lemma 45.8.3]{frC3azStacksBertini}.

\emph{2. The weighted polarization identity.}
Treat each connected component $C_e$ of $E_i\cap E_j$,
$i\ne j$, as an edge $e=(i,j)$. This allows disconnected double
curves and multiple edges. Write
\[
 r_{ie}:A_i\to J_{C_e},\quad r_{je}:A_j\to J_{C_e},
 \qquad N_i=\mathcal O_Z(E_i)|_{E_i}.
\]
The Albanese images of exceptional divisor-cycle pushforwards
define a homomorphism $\Lambda:A\to A^\vee$.
Self-intersection and intersection of distinct components give
\begin{equation}
 \Lambda_{ii}=-\mu_{N_i},\qquad
 \Lambda_{ij}=-\sum_{e=(i,j)}
                  r_{ie}^\vee\lambda_{C_e}r_{je}\quad(i\ne j).
                                                        \tag{AM.7}
\end{equation}
For distinct components the cycle is the pushforward from $C_e$
of the restricted degree-zero divisor. Triple points do not
alter either formula.

Let $T=\operatorname{diag}([a_i]):A\to A$ and define
$d_e:A\to J_{C_e}$ by
$d_e(x)=r_{ie}(x_i)-r_{je}(x_j)$. Since
\[
 D|_{E_i}=a_iN_i+\sum_{j\ne i}\sum_{e=(i,j)}a_jC_e,
\]
additivity in (AM.5) gives the exact identity
\begin{equation}
 T^\vee\Lambda T
   =\operatorname{diag}\bigl(a_i\mu_{\mathcal O_{E_i}(-D)}\bigr)
       +\sum_{e=(i,j)}a_i a_j\,d_e^\vee\lambda_{C_e}d_e.
                                                        \tag{AM.8}
\end{equation}
On each diagonal, the edge contribution cancels the double-curve
term in $-D|_{E_i}$; off the diagonal it gives the minus sign in
(AM.7). This checks both the weights and the sign.

Each $\mathcal O_{E_i}(-D)$ is ample. Choose smooth curves
$C_i'\in|\mathcal O_{E_i}(-n_iD)|$ as in (AM.6), and a positive
integer $n$ divisible by all $n_i$. Multiplication of (AM.8)
by $n$ expresses it as the polarization associated with the
pullback of a product of positive powers of theta line bundles
under
\[
 A\longrightarrow\prod_iJ_{C_i'}\times\prod_eJ_{C_e},
 \qquad x\longmapsto((r_{C_i'}x_i)_i,(d_e(x))_e).
\]
The first factors already have finite kernel, so the whole map
has finite kernel and the pullback is ample. Thus
$nT^\vee\Lambda T$ is a polarization. It follows that $\Lambda$
is an isogeny. This uses finite maps and ample line bundles,
not Hodge theory; inseparability is harmless.

\emph{3. The classes already belong to the actual relation group.}
For $L_i\in A_i(k)$ choose a smooth connected projective curve
$P$ in $A_i$ through $0$ and $L_i$; the cases $\dim A_i\le1$
are immediate. Restrict a normalized Poincare bundle on
$E_i\times A_i$ to $E_i\times P$. Its divisor-class fibre
difference is $c_1(L_i)$. Proper pushforward along the fixed
$E_i\times P\hookrightarrow Z\times P$ gives an actual ordinary
codimension-two cycle family on the fixed resolution product.
EP.3 therefore gives
\begin{equation}
                           \theta(c_1(L_i))\in J_K(E). \tag{AM.9}
\end{equation}
Equivalently, take the pure weight-two perfect class of this
cycle on the regular product and restrict it to $E\times P$.
This does not assume a perfect pushforward from an individual
component into the singular SNC union.

An isogeny over an algebraically closed field is surjective on
points. The component Albanese images of (AM.9) are
$\Lambda(A(k))$, so (AM.7)--(AM.8) prove (AM.2). Their component
degrees are zero because the $L_i$ are algebraically trivial.
No passage from pointwise semiabelian surjectivity to a proper
parameter family is involved: the Poincare families above
are actual proper families before their ambient pushforward.

\emph{4. The residual denominator.}
Put $Q=\theta\mathrm{CH}_1(E)_{\mathbf Q}$.
We have $U\subset J_K(E)\cap Q\cap S_0(E)$ and $\alpha_E(U)$
is the full target of (AM.1). Subtracting an element of $U$
places any element of $S_0(E)$ in $S_{00}(E)$; this proves
(AM.3). If $j+q\in S_{00}(E)$ with $j\in J_K(E)$ and
$q\in Q\cap S_0(E)$, choose $u\in U$ with
$\alpha_E(u)=\alpha_E(q)$. Then
\[
 j+q=(j+u)+(q-u),\qquad
 j+u\in J_K(E)\cap S_{00}(E),\quad q-u\in Q\cap S_{00}(E).
\]
The reverse inclusion is immediate. Together with C.3bm.2,
this proves (AM.4).
\end{proof}

\emph{Scope.}
The weighted isogeny argument holds in every characteristic for
smooth SNC surface components with the stated negative ample
combination. Its application to the ordinary strict-local
quotient here retains the positive-characteristic algebraizable
threefold hypotheses of C.3bm and EP. Neither component
Albanese kernels nor the compatibility of perfect families
across double and triple strata has been removed.
