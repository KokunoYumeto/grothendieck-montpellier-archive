# Rational symbolic fields and the exact ambient endpoint

**Unregistered auxiliary proof; no main-gate movement.** Written 19 November 2026 by GPT-6 Astra Pro (OpenAI), `/root/frobenius_continuation`. This note removes a possible endpoint discrepancy after an actual symbolic family has been lifted. It does not supply that lift or cancel its supported boundary.

## RF.1. Hypotheses and conclusion

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic p>0. Let R be a noetherian local coefficient-k algebra with residue field k, and let g:Z→Spec R be a proper morphism from a regular noetherian scheme, an isomorphism away from the closed point. Suppose the reduced exceptional scheme E is the projective SNC surface in the already proved C.3bt scope. Normality is not needed for the coefficient calculation below. All groups are rational and are the nonconnective K-groups of perfect complexes.

For a new variable u, put F=k(u) and R_F=R tensor_k F. Then

    K1(R_F)_Q is generated by the image of R*_Q
        and the constant-field classes {u−a}, a∈k.         (RF.1)

Only finitely many such classes occur in any expression. Consequently

    psi^q = q on K1(R_F)_Q for every positive integer q.  (RF.2)

This concerns the actual nonlocal ring R_F. The proof never calls it local or henselian. It implies that ker(K1(Z_F)_Q→K1(E_F)_Q) has Adams weight one.

## RF.2. The natural rational blow-up square on smooth parameters

For every smooth separated finite-type k-scheme T, the FF proof applies to the actual product with T. Properness is required of g×1_T, which remains proper when T is not proper over k. The infinitesimal centres are Spec(R/m^n)×T and the exceptional thickenings are Z×_R Spec(R/m^n)×T. Their reductions are T and E×T. On finite affine covers, characteristic-p nilpotent invariance after inverting p identifies these terms with their reductions; finite-cover descent globalizes the comparison.

Thus the fixed-product KST/GH comparison gives the natural equivalence

    K(R×T)_Q ≃ K(Z×T)_Q ×^h_{K(E×T)_Q} K(T)_Q.          (RF.3)

Here K(R×T) means K(Spec R×T). To verify the spectrum assertion, use the relative comparison: KST gives an isomorphism of pro-homotopy groups for the two relative fibres; GH makes the infinitesimal relative systems constant after localization; the constant embedding reflects isomorphisms. The resulting map of actual rational relative fibres is a weak equivalence in every degree. No inverse limit of K-groups and no uniform p-exponent is used.

The construction is natural for parameter pullbacks and for the localization fibres below. This is the spectrum form of the already audited every-degree FF sequence, with nonproperness of T explicitly accounted for. No general cdh-descent theorem on arbitrary singular schemes is being imported.

## RF.3. Homotopy invariance on the coefficient scheme

The regular schemes Z and Spec k have homotopy-invariant K-theory. Rational K(E) is the actual coherent finite totalization of its smooth components, smooth double curves and individual triple points, by C.3bt. Every stratum is regular and has homotopy-invariant K-theory. Finite homotopy limits give K(E)_Q≃K(E×A1)_Q. Comparing RF.3 for a point and for A1 gives

    K(R)_Q ≃ K(R[u])_Q.                                 (RF.4)

In particular K1(R[u])_Q is the image of K1(R)_Q. This follows from this regular/SNC resolution; it is not an unsupported assertion of homotopy invariance for arbitrary singular rings. Since R is commutative local, K1(R)=R*.

## RF.4. Parameter-point support purity with its actual maps

Let B be a finite reduced subset of A1(k). For a∈B, the parameter equation u−a is a non-zero-divisor on every product in RF.3. Actual regular-immersion pushforward gives

    direct sum over a∈B of K(R)_Q
          → K(R×A1 on R×B)_Q.                           (RF.5)

This map is an equivalence. Take the fibres defining support of RF.3 for A1 and A1\B. Finite homotopy limits commute with these fibres, giving the corresponding supported homotopy-cartesian square.

For Z and Spec k, regular devissage identifies supported spectra with the direct sums of coefficient spectra. For E, use the PL.1 stratum-totalization proof with A1 in place of the proper ambient curve: regular devissage on every smooth stratum and disjoint-support excision give the direct sum of K(E). Parameter properness was not used in that purity argument.

All comparison maps are the actual Cartier pushforwards. They commute with g and the SNC restriction diagram by Tor-independent derived base change. Flatness of g is unnecessary: the one-element resolution by u−a stays exact after pullback, since the parameter equation remains a non-zero-divisor on Z[u], E[u] and every stratum. Thus these are maps of the actual coherent diagrams, with their geometric homotopies. Comparing the supported squares and their fibres proves RF.5 with its natural maps. This does not apply regular-ambient devissage directly to singular R×A1.

The Adams shift is one. Under RF.5, pushforward is exterior multiplication by the Koszul support class of a. That class is the localization boundary of the unit u−a, and psi^q[O_a]=q[O_a] in the support group. Raw-support localization and product compatibility give

    psi^q i_{a*}(x) = q i_{a*}(psi^q x).                (RF.6)

In particular the rank class in K0(R), of ordinary weight zero, has raw support weight one. No semisimplicity is used.

## RF.5. Localization and explicit residue lifts

Apply localization to A1\B and pass to the filtered system of all finite B. Perfect K-theory continuity identifies the limiting open with Spec R_F. Because k is algebraically closed, every nonzero polynomial in k[u] factors into the u−a; this is precisely localization of R[u] at k[u]\{0}. Exactness and RF.5 give

    K1(R[u])_Q → K1(R_F)_Q
                  → direct sum over a∈k of K0(R)_Q.     (RF.7)

Each residue vector has finite support. Locality gives K0(R)=Z by rank. The unit u−a, pulled from F*, has residue 1 at a and 0 at every other finite point, with positive-uniformizer convention. This follows from its two-term Koszul resolution, equivalently the localization module formula. There is no infinity condition because the ambient parameter is A1.

Given xi∈K1(R_F)_Q with residues (n_a), subtract the finite actual unit sum Σ_a n_a{u−a}. The difference has all residues zero and thus comes from K1(R[u])_Q. By RF.4 it comes from K1(R)_Q=R*_Q. This proves RF.1.

Both types of generator are unit classes, on which psi^q acts by q. Thus RF.2 is direct. Merely knowing the subquotients have weight one would not suffice without controlling extensions; the explicit residue lifts supply that control.

## RF.6. Exact endpoint after an actual lift

The abstract blow-up square over F has the FF exact sequence, without requiring R_F to be local. If a∈ker(K1(Z_F)_Q→K1(E_F)_Q), apply exactness to (a,0) in K1(Z_F)_Q plus K1(F)_Q. It puts a in the image of K1(R_F)_Q, so RF.2 proves

    psi^q = q on ker(K1(Z_F)_Q→K1(E_F)_Q).              (RF.8)

Let beta_Z∈K0(Z)_Q have weight two, with restriction beta on E. Suppose P/F is an actual smooth connected proper curve with F-rational points o,t, and an actual eta_Z∈K1(Z_F×_F P)_Q restricts to an exceptional family with endpoint exactly {u} beta_F. Then

    (eta_Z)_t − (eta_Z)_o − {u}(beta_Z)_F

belongs to RF.8. Applying (psi²−2)/6 to the actual eta_Z kills the discrepancy and fixes the desired product, whose psi² eigenvalue is8. This gives literal endpoint {u}(beta_Z)_F on the constant target. Make this correction over k(u) before any finite extension needed for the semistable parameter model in RS.

This is the endpoint part only. RF.6 assumes an actual lift on Z_F×P. NK does not supply that lift by replacing R with the nonlocal R_F. The compactified parameter-surface route still has its supported node obstruction; finite closure still has its trace boundary. No main quotient vanishing follows from this lemma alone.
