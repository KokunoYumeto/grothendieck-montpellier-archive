## Supersingular cycles: kernel information is not saturation

We opened Oliver Gregory's complete published HTML, *Torsion codimension 2 cycles on supersingular abelian varieties*, Canadian Mathematical Bulletin 66(2) (2023), 458–466, DOI 10.4153/S0008439522000431, at <https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/canadian-mathematical-bulletin/article/torsion-codimension-2-cycles-on-supersingular-abelian-varieties/9FA8E4E719B07441E78F9FF936C6FDC2>.

Theorems 1.1 and 4.1(1) prove that torsion codimension-two cycles on a supersingular abelian variety over any algebraically closed field of positive characteristic are algebraically trivial. Theorem 4.1(2) proves integral equality of homological and algebraic equivalence in codimension two over the algebraic closure of a finite field. These are precise results about the kernel of the cycle class map. They do not assert saturation of its image in the integral crystalline lattice. In §2 the displayed crystalline comparison explicitly tensors with `K=W(k)[1/p]`. Thus that comparison does not eliminate the remaining characteristic-prime coefficient in C.4u. We read the proof strategy in §4, including its use of a denominator-cleared diagonal decomposition and divisibility for the torsion Abel–Jacobi cokernel; this divisibility argument does not apply to the finite-index numerical cycle lattice in C.4r.

## Prym schemes in characteristic two

We independently opened the full 52-page author PDF of Achter and Casalaina-Martin, *Putting the P back in Prym*, dated 18 December 2024, at <https://www.math.colostate.edu/~achter/math/prym1224.pdf>. Theorem A (author p. 2), Theorem 5.12, Theorem 6.4 (author p. 46), and Lemma 5.17 (author pp. 44–45) retain characteristic two and the finite separable-cover hypotheses. The odd exponent-three case is an étale noncyclic degree-three cover of a genus-two curve and produces a **principally polarized two-dimensional** Prym. It is not an existence theorem for the generic nonprincipally polarized type-(1,2) surface or an arbitrary supersingular threefold. Theorem 5.3 and the relative Theorem 6.1 use numerical curve equivalence, consistent with the Matsusaka method already used in C.4. We do not infer a new existence result from their all-characteristic comparison theorem.

## Recent Weil-fourfold theorem

We opened the complete 24-page author PDF of Floccari and Fu, *The Hodge conjecture for Weil fourfolds with discriminant 1 via singular OG6-varieties*, at <https://irma.math.unistra.fr/~lfu/articles/HCWeilViaOG6.pdf>. Published metadata: Journal de Mathématiques Pures et Appliquées 210 (2026), article 103876, DOI 10.1016/j.matpur.2026.103876. Theorems 1.1–1.3, author pp. 2–3, give rational Hodge algebraicity and an isogeny description. Theorem 1.5, author p. 4, is explicitly formulated with rational l-adic coefficients. The footnote on author p. 5 states that the argument needs the Kuga–Satake construction only up to isogeny. These assertions do not supply an integral divided Poincare class on the residual product. We checked exact scope, not every proof in this paper. Its singular-locus/double-cover geometry remains a possible source of new cycles, but no odd mixed coefficient has been constructed from it here.

## Integral Tate counterexamples in characteristic zero

We opened the full primary HTML of J. S. Milne, *On the integral Tate conjecture for abelian varieties*, arXiv:2509.06707v1 (8 September 2025), at <https://arxiv.org/html/2509.06707v1>. Its unnumbered theorem transfers a failure of integral Hodge over C to a failure of integral Tate after a finite extension of a field finitely generated over Q. The field has characteristic zero. It neither proves a failure for the particular product of a type-(1,2) surface with its dual nor supplies a positive-characteristic counterexample by specialization. The distinction is retained.

## Other search leads not used as theorems

A primary arXiv abstract for Huang–Jiang–Yang, *Degree of irrationality of abelian surfaces*, arXiv:2609.02169, states a classification of the surfaces admitting a degree-three rational map to the plane. We did not audit its full proof or use this as a new manuscript theorem. A degree-three rational map alone does not furnish an odd scalar factorization through Jacobians. The explicitly associated Lagrangian Galois-closure construction is already covered by the all-divisor parity lemma in the separate report.

The remaining main cases are unchanged. This conclusion means that these particular checks did not resolve them; it is not a claim that their status in the entire literature has been proved open.
