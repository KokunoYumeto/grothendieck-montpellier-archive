## Explicit counterexample to the literal lemma

Over \(\mathbf C\), let
\[
Y=\mathbf P^4,\quad
X=(x_0=0),\quad
Z=(x_0=x_2=0),\quad
V=(x_0x_1-x_2x_3=0).
\]
Then \(X\) is a smooth ample hyperplane section, \(Z\simeq\mathbf P^2\subset X\), and \(V\) is an irreducible rank-four quadric cone. Its unique singular point is
\[
v=[0:0:0:0:1]\in Z.
\]
Thus \(V\) is smooth away from \(Z\), contains \(Z\), and meets \(X\) properly. The intersection \(V\cap X=(x_2x_3=0)\) is smooth away from \(Z\). These are the asserted open-complement hypotheses.

Moreover, the example is compatible with the indicated “general” Bertini degree. The ideal of \(Z\) is generated by \(x_0,x_2\), so its twist by 1 is globally generated, and Corollary 2.7 uses degree 2. A general quadric containing this plane has rank four: its quadratic form has a three-dimensional totally isotropic subspace, so rank is at most four, and the displayed example attains four. Its vertex lies on the contained plane. Therefore the degree-two example is not excluded merely by requiring a general member of that linear system.

On \(X\), the complement is the locus where both \(x_2\) and \(x_3\) are nonzero. Normalize \(x_2=1\). The remaining free coordinates give
\[
X-V\simeq\mathbf A^2\times\mathbf G_m,\qquad
H^1(X-V,\mathbf Q)\simeq\mathbf Q.
\]
Projection from \(v\) identifies \(Y-\{v\}\) with the total space of a line bundle over \(\mathbf P^3\), and \(V-\{v\}\) with its restriction over the smooth quadric \(Q\subset\mathbf P^3\). Therefore \(Y-V\) is an affine-line bundle over \(\mathbf P^3-Q\). The Gysin sequence contains
\[
0\longrightarrow H^1(\mathbf P^3-Q,\mathbf Q)
\longrightarrow H^0(Q,\mathbf Q)(-1)
\xrightarrow{\,[Q]\,}H^2(\mathbf P^3,\mathbf Q).
\]
The last map is multiplication by the nonzero degree 2, so
\[
H^1(Y-V,\mathbf Q)=0.
\]
The proposed isomorphism in degree \(1<\dim X=3\) is consequently false.

## Exact proof issue and retained limitation

The displayed sequence in the source mixes compact-supported cohomology of \((Y-X)-V\) with ordinary cohomology of the nonproper \(Y-V\) and \(X-V\). It is not the applicable localization long exact sequence for that nonproper ambient space. Artin vanishing therefore does not imply the claimed conclusion by that sequence.

This is a targeted mathematical and source check of Lemma 3.2. It is not an assessment of every possible repair to the main theorem, and it is not used to assert a counterexample to that theorem.
