## Exact proof audit

### Integral scalars and discriminant gluing

The stated Hodge-endomorphism hypotheses give scalar actions on H1 and T_Q. A scalar preserving their nonzero integral lattices is an integer. The Neron--Severi line has primitive generator ell, and there are no rational Hodge homomorphisms between that line and T_Q: a homomorphism in either direction would give a rational (1,1) class in T_Q, using the nondegenerate polarized pairing in the reverse direction. Thus H2 has precisely the two claimed scalar eigenspaces.

Unimodularity of H2(A,Z) and primitivity of ell give an integral w with (w,ell)=1. Since ell^2=4, its projector to Q ell is ell/4. The integral vector Z*w-s w is therefore (n-s)ell/4. Primitivity forces n-s divisible by4. This is a full-lattice gluing argument, not a conclusion from preservation of the two separate orthogonal sublattices. The expression s Delta22+(n-s)ell exterior ell/4 has exactly the required operator. For the convention using pushforward to the first factor, the component acting on H1 is Delta13, as stated.

### Integral Fourier construction and all Chern coefficients

The pushed structure sheaves of resolutions give an integral K0 class with ch0=ch1=0 and ch2=[Z]; lower terms vanish by support dimension and the leading term has coefficient1. A proper Fourier--Mukai pushforward produces an integral perfect K0 class. No WIT hypothesis is needed. Integral virtual Chern classes are available.

The Poincare exterior-algebra convention is consistent with the full diagonal identity. Integrating the four x generators gives F(Delta13)=P, F(Delta22)=P^2/2, and F(ell_x)=-ellhat. The bidegree map is (a,b)->(a,4-b). Thus c1(E) has only an ell1 component and tP; an ell2 component would require the vanished ch1(KZ). Consequently the (2,2) piece of c2(E), computed from (c1^2-2ch2)/2, is exactly

    (t^2-s)P^2/2 + (n-s)ell1 ell2/4.

The remaining pieces have other bidegrees and their products with P cannot enter H3(A) tensor H3(Ahat). The exterior identity P^3=-3ell1 ell2 P gives the normalized mixed coefficient

    m_Z = 3(t^2-s) - (n-s)/2.

This is the coefficient of the actual integral curve c2(E)P. The gluing condition makes its second summand even, and t^2=t modulo2 gives m_Z=t-s modulo2. There is no division of a Chow class. The normalization P^3/6 -> identity is the same previously certified Matsusaka convention as C4i/l/r; changing the global Poincare sign changes a harmless overall sign, not the parity criterion.

### Actual Jacobian splitting in both directions

Every normalized curve component of the integral curve c2(E)P gives an actual homomorphism factorization through its Jacobian. Hence an odd m_Z, together with the already proved 2 in I_J(A), yields1 by integral Bezout and gives a genuine splitting.

Conversely, use the universal degree-zero Picard bundle on J(C) times C, with J(C)=Pic0(C) and the chosen Abel map. Its H1-correspondence is the inverse of the Abel pullback. Therefore pulling by u and pushing along v composed with the Abel map gives u* v*=(v u)* on H1, with coefficient1. Summing gives t=1. The H2 action factors through H2(C), a Tate structure, so it vanishes on T(A), giving s=0. This argument uses neither a polarization inverse nor an isogeny denominator. It also handles signed integral sums of curves and morphisms that drop dimension under pushforward.

### Residual fibre-product method exclusion

A finite morphism between the two smooth surfaces is flat, and the fibre-product correspondence acts by gamma* gamma_*. Generic separability gives multiplicity1 for the diagonal component. Its residual cycle therefore acts by gamma* gamma_*-1. Since H1(Y)=0 and H2(Y) is generated by divisor classes, the first term vanishes on H1(S) and on the transcendental H2; the latter follows by the projection pairing with pulled-back divisors. Blowups preserve the relevant H1 and transcendental H2. Thus its pushforward has t=s=-1. Multiplication graphs have t=k,s=k^2, and divisor exterior products have t=s=0; each has even t-s. The method exclusion is consequently correct and does not exclude arbitrary codimension-two cycles.
