## 2. Why finite covers alone do not remove the remaining edge cycle

Let C and P be smooth projective curves, and let zeta belong to CH0(C times P)_Q. For a finite surjective morphism f:P'→P of degree d, smoothness of the curves makes f finite flat. Proper pushforward and flat pullback give

    (id_C times f)_* (id_C times f)^* zeta = d zeta.

Hence a nonzero rational zeta cannot become zero merely by this finite parameter change. This is a direct projection-formula calculation; it does not prove that a particular graph-family obstruction is nonzero. The marked edge construction of C.3bt does more than pull back a fixed cycle: it changes the rational unit, arranging that unit to equal one at each divisor-support point. That mechanism acts in K1 and does not itself provide a K0 correction of a component graph family.

For a curve A in a component S and a line-bundle family on A times P, the already recorded obstruction is a sum of products [q] exterior c1(N_q) on each double curve times P. Killing its two Albanese coordinates leaves an explicit Albanese-kernel product. No general injectivity or nonvanishing of that product map is claimed.

## 3. Why exceptional Picard images alone do not solve the problem

On a single smooth component S with Pic(S)_Q generated by an ample divisor H and normal class N a nonzero rational multiple of H, the map theta on Pic(S)_Q has nonzero degree on every nonzero divisor class. Consequently theta(Pic(S)_Q) intersect S00 is zero. Thus the proposed argument that exceptional divisor intersections alone must generate every component Albanese kernel has no general linear-algebra basis. No new example of a normal local threefold or new positive-characteristic K3 nonvanishing theorem is asserted here.

One tempting modification was checked explicitly. Blow up the regular ambient threefold along a smooth curve A in a component S. The new exceptional component R is a ruled surface over A. For L in Pic0(A), the theta image of its pullback to R has component restrictions of the form

    (i_{A,*} c1(L), -c1(L))

on S and R, up to the fixed normal/sign conventions and the other intersection coordinates. This is already an exceptional Poincare relation. It does not isolate the first coordinate: C.3bo's weighted Albanese map on the Poincare subgroup is an isogeny. Rationally that subgroup is a graph over the component Albanese coordinates, with zero intersection with T_E. The unique Albanese correction subtracts the same S contribution. A separate proper family on R with controlled restrictions to its boundary would still be necessary. This modification is therefore not a proved reduction.

## 4. Synchronized curve covers and framed-bundle routes

To glue actual graph correspondences across an edge, equality is required for the complete edge K0 classes on C times P. Prescribing equality at finitely many parameter endpoints is weaker. A proposed common finite cover of curves on adjacent components would have to synchronize the entire pullback divisors of the marked intersection points, including multiplicities. A map to P1 generally has additional points in every prescribed fibre; suppressing them would require rational functions with divisors constrained to the marked sets. No such general construction was proved.

A separate framed-bundle idea asks for complete families of bundles on a component with constant K-class along its boundary. The usual moduli spaces are quasi-projective, and a compactification by torsion-free sheaves does not automatically preserve the boundary restriction or yield a perfect family on the singular union. No general properness or rigidity theorem for these framed moduli was imported.

For an ample boundary D in a projective surface S, every closed curve in (S minus D) times a proper curve P which is proper over k has constant image in the affine variety S minus D. Thus literal zero-cycle families whose support avoids D on the whole proper parameter cannot move points in S minus D. This elementary observation does not classify relative perfect K0 families: a relative K-class on S times P need not be represented by codimension-two cycles disjoint from D times P. Equating these two notions would be an additional theorem, and is not assumed.

## 8. A limited relative-Picard proper-family construction

Let S be one smooth component and D its whole intersection with the union F of the other components. Suppose a line bundle M on S is equipped with an actual trivialization along D. The pair gives a relative K0 class m on (S,D) whose absolute image is [M] minus one. Let L belong to Pic0(S). Choose a smooth proper connected curve P through the origin and L in Pic0(S), and pull back a normalized Poincare line bundle L_P to S times P. The product

    m times ([L_P] minus one)

is an actual relative K0 class on (S times P,D times P). The closed-cover fibre sequence for E=S union F sends it to an actual K0 class on E times P, with zero restriction to F times P. Taking its finite weight-two projector gives a defining proper family. Its component endpoint on S is c1(M)c1(L), and every other component endpoint is zero. This proof uses the supplied boundary trivialization, not only equality of endpoint restrictions.

If M itself belongs to Pic0(S), the product has zero Albanese class by the already audited product-of-algebraically-trivial-classes statement. Thus products from ker(Pic0(S)→Pic(D)) times Pic0(S) are explicitly removed by proper families. This does not remove arbitrary component Albanese kernels; it may give nothing when the boundary restriction on Pic0 is injective up to isogeny. It is recorded as an auxiliary family mechanism, not a new main reduction.

## 9. Actual framed bundles: a precise rigidity statement and its limit

**Nevins, T. A. (2002). Representability for some moduli stacks of framed sheaves. Manuscripta Mathematica, 109(1), 85–91. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00229-002-0290-z**

The entire six-page primary v2 PDF at https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0008190, dated10May2002, was opened and read, including the complete proof of Proposition3 and the complete proof of Theorem1. The author's institutional publication record at https://experts.illinois.edu/en/publications/representability-for-some-moduli-stacks-of-framed-sheaves/ was separately opened to verify the journal metadata; the seven-page published layout was not read. The proof's external Cartan–Eilenberg spectral sequence and Sernesi representability references were not newly opened. An institutional route to a different framed-moduli paper returned403; no mathematical claim was taken from that unavailable file.

Nevins's Proposition3 constructs an affine-bundle tower A_n for vector bundles on the infinitesimal neighborhoods D(n), with a fixed framing F on a smooth curve D in a complex projective surface S, provided H0(D,End(F) tensor N_{D/S}^{-n})=0 for every positive n. For a fixed F the base of the tower is a point, so every A_n is affine.

This gives the following direct auxiliary consequence. Assume additionally that D is ample. A vector bundle V on S times a smooth connected proper curve P, with an actual isomorphism V restricted to D times P equal to the pullback of F, has pairwise isomorphic fibres on S. Indeed its maps P→A_n are constant because P is proper connected and A_n affine. The bundles V_t and V_o therefore have isomorphic restrictions to D(n) for every n. For fixed t, write H=Hom(V_t,V_o), a vector bundle on S. For sufficiently large n, Serre duality followed by Serre vanishing gives H1(S,H(-(n+1)D))=0. The exact divisor sequence then lifts an isomorphism on D(n) to a global homomorphism V_t→V_o. Its determinant is nonzero along D. Its zero divisor would be an effective divisor disjoint from ample D, which is impossible. The global homomorphism is therefore an isomorphism.

This consequence is used only in the complex, smooth-divisor scope just stated; no unproved extension of the source's moduli theorem to arbitrary characteristic is imported. It is a restriction on **actually framed vector bundles**. A class in relative K0 or an equality of virtual K0 classes on D times P does not provide such an actual fixed framing of any chosen vector-bundle representative. Equality in the Grothendieck group includes exact-sequence relations and does not mean the bundles are isomorphic. No compatible stabilization/presentation theorem bridging this distinction has been proved. Thus the rigidity statement supplies neither nonvanishing of the present quotient nor a counterexample to any C.3bt conclusion.
