## Independent reconstruction

1. **Resolution and common model.** The affine-cover clause of Cossart–Piltant Theorem 1.1 gives projectivity, and clause (ii) gives the isomorphism over the regular puncture. The torsion-free pullback of the generically invertible dual sheaf embeds in its invertible reflexive hull on the regular resolution. Its ideal is already principal in codimension one and on the puncture. Proposition 4.4 principalizes precisely by regular centres contained in the nonprincipal locus. Thus the resulting regular projective common model maps to the second Proj model without changing the puncture. The hypotheses of Kollár–Witaszek Lemma 8 really hold on that common model; no unsupported conjunction of separate existence results is used.

2. **SNC support and ample exceptional divisor.** Cossart–Jannsen–Saito Corollary 0.4 applies to the reduced closed fibre, whose dimension is at most two, in the regular excellent ambient threefold. Its proof supplies a regular projective modification, unchanged off the closed fibre. Kollár–Witaszek Lemma 6 applies because the previous ambient model is regular and hence locally factorial. Relative ampleness of the sum of a sufficiently large pulled-back ample divisor and an ample divisor for the intervening morphism gives the required exceptional ample Cartier divisor.

3. **Effectivity and full support.** An eventual globally generated power has an ideal of sections inside the normal local ring, by Hartogs on the puncture and the fixed rational trivialization. Its evaluation ideal is the corresponding invertible subsheaf of the structure sheaf. The ideal cannot be the unit ideal: otherwise the trivial relatively ample bundle would make the projective birational morphism finite, hence an isomorphism to the normal base, contradicting singularity. Thus it is primary for the maximal ideal and has positive order on every component of the full exceptional fibre. The negative ample divisor is therefore an effective Cartier divisor with exactly the required support. Ampleness passes from its reduced support to the nilpotent thickening.

4. **Exact ordinary quotient.** The previously proved EL and EP hypotheses now hold for every ring in the stated class. Finite data descend to a pointed étale finite-type model; regularity near the proper exceptional fibre permits the smooth rational Chern-character comparison. EP identifies the image of ordinary fixed-product curve-family relations with all perfect gamma-two endpoint relations on the exceptional surface. Chow localization therefore gives (C.3bm.1). This uses only endpoint realizability, not extension of the original whole perfect family.

5. **Degrees and exceptional cycles.** Degrees of the codimension-two Chern classes on each smooth projective exceptional component are constant in a connected proper curve-family. Hence the relation subgroup is contained in the componentwise degree-zero subgroup. For the matrix built using a single ample line bundle on the entire exceptional surface, off-diagonal entries are the nonnegative degrees of its restrictions to double curves. The matrix is symmetric even though this line bundle need not extend to the resolution: both entries restrict the same global line bundle on the same double curve. The positive exceptional coefficients turn the matrix into a symmetric matrix with nonnegative off-diagonal entries and strictly negative row sums. The displayed energy identity proves negative definiteness. Consequently the exceptional pushforwards span every degree coordinate over the rationals, giving exactly (C.3bm.2).
