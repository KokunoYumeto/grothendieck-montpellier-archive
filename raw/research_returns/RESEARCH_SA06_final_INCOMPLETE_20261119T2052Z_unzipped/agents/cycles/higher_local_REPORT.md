# C.3ae: ordinary codimension-two local restriction

The proof is finalized and independently passed by root and frobenius. It has been integrated immediately after C.3ad. There is no novelty claim.

For every N≥7, the displayed four integer coefficient matrices specify an excellent normal strictly henselian local complex ring R of dimension N, with regular puncture X and regular punctured principal divisor Y. The ordinary integral restriction cokernel CH²(X)_alg→CH²(Y)_alg surjects onto Pic⁰(C)(C), where C is the Fermat plane quartic. Thus the ordinary rational J² restriction map is not surjective. The explicit target cycle is the pullback of a displayed line on the Fermat K3 surface times the divisor of a geometric generic point minus a displayed algebraic point on C.

The cycle invariant first lifts a class from the puncture to the regular proper divisor model and restricts it to the exceptional projective bundle. Its ambiguity is multiplication by the normal-bundle class. In codimension two, the projective-bundle relation has degree at least three, so quotienting by this normal class is precisely evaluation h=−a. Intersection with τ=4D−H on the K3 surface and pushforward then gives an integral Pic(C) invariant. The target family maps by multiplication by −9.

For a source ordinary family, its fixed proper parameter curve permits closure on the fixed regular model. The rational Chern-character isomorphism lifts that closure class to a virtual vector bundle. The rank-one conormal exterior sequence forces contraction of its degree-two Hodge class with the first-neighbourhood deformation class to vanish. The exact quartic Jacobian-ring calculation makes the primitive part of the relevant mixed tensor vanish. Boundary corrections contribute only the hyperplane factor or constant classes, which the detector removes. The resulting Picard-family morphism is constant. This is why the invariant kills every ordinary source family difference, integrally, despite using rational Chern characters in the Hodge argument.

The two finite certificates use the same fixed coefficient tuple. The 64×57 matrix has rank 57 modulo 101, and its first 57 rows have determinant 47 modulo 101. All twelve projective charts of the common-zero scheme of the four sections have unit ideal modulo 101. Projectivity over Z proves base point freeness in characteristic zero. Root and frobenius independently reproduced these calculations with different algorithms. The complete group-theoretic and deformation argument is in `higher_local_surjectivity.tex`; the finite computations alone are not the proof of the Chow statement.

The unrestricted-CH spread route was rejected before integration. A cycle on a strict local puncture can descend to a pointed etale neighbourhood varying with the parameter, and a class on an open first neighbourhood need not extend over the compactified parameter. The proof makes no such extension assumption: it starts from an actual ordinary family on the fixed product and closes it before applying the obstruction. This distinction is retained in `higher_local_CHECK_EVENTS.tsv`.

The exact remaining universal ordinary J² restriction-surjectivity dimensions over C are N=4 and N=6. N≤3 has no codimension-two target cycles; N=5 was already refuted in C.3r; N≥7 is refuted here. No assertion for arbitrary CH² preimages, characteristic p, or other codimensions follows without an additional proof.
