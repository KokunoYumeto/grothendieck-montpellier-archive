## A concrete obstruction to a semiabelian shortcut

Let E be an elliptic curve over an algebraically closed field, and let a be a nontorsion point. Form a nodal projective curve C by gluing E to P1 at 0_E~0_P1 and a~infinity. Its degree-zero Picard group maps surjectively on points to Pic0(E), with kernel k*. Nevertheless, any line-bundle family on C×P, with P a smooth connected proper curve, has constant induced map to Pic0(E). Indeed, after normalization and removing line bundles pulled back from P, gluing the two nodes forces f*O_E(a−0) to be trivial. If f:P→E were nonconstant, it would be finite locally free; taking its norm would make O_E(a−0) torsion. This contradicts the choice of a. The same obstruction applies to determinant families of perfect classes. It illustrates why killing a torus subgroup pointwise does not supply proper families lifting every point of the abelian quotient. It is not a counterexample for the exceptional-surface quotient.

Freshly opened exact primary inputs for this elementary warning are Stacks Project Proposition 44.6.6, Tag 0B9Z (rigidified Picard representability, entire proof), and Lemma 31.18.6, Tag 0BD2 (finite locally free norm, entire proof). The norm line-bundle identity is the usual determinant norm calculation and can also be checked directly on transition functions. No finite computation was used or claimed for this note.

## Remaining edge/triple test, not a counterexample

For an edge curve C meeting triple strata, a pointwise class of the form u·c1(L) in weight-two K1(C) has zero weight-two restriction to K1 of a residue field. Its actual Poincare family on C×P can nevertheless restrict at a triple point to u·M in K1(P), where M is a nontrivial degree-zero line bundle on P. Thus pointwise compatibility does not automatically give a compatible whole perfect family. Over Fpbar the constant unit u is torsion and this specific rational expression vanishes; over a larger algebraically closed field the same argument is unavailable. No proof has been obtained that this obstruction survives all endpoint-zero family corrections or exceptional pushforwards. It is recorded as a precise remaining test, not as a nonvanishing theorem.
