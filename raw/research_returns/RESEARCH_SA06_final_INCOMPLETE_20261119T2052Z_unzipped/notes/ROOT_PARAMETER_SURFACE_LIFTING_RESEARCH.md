## PS.1. Exact setup and claim

Let k be algebraically closed, R a noetherian local coefficient-k algebra with residue field k, and S a smooth connected projective surface over k. Put B=SpecR×_kS. The claim is integral:

`F_gamma^3 K0(B)=0`.

Neither characteristic, regularity, normality nor henselianity of R is needed for this bound. K0 means perfect K-theory; B has the resolution property because it is projective with an ample line bundle over the affine base. Its vector-bundle and perfect K0 descriptions agree. The following proof uses finite semilocal supports, not a bound by the larger total dimension of B.

## PS.2. Relative sections and finite supports

Fix an ample line bundle H pulled back from S. For every vector bundle V on B, relative Serre vanishing gives surjectivity of global sections onto the closed-fibre sections after a sufficiently high twist: apply it to the coherent sheaf mV. On the smooth projective closed fibre, sufficiently high twists generate first jets. Thus the finite-dimensional first-jet incidence argument used in AE permits general sections with the following properties.

If rankV≥3, one such section of V⊗H^n has no zero on the closed surface: vanishing has codimension rankV>2 in the section incidence. Its zero scheme on B is proper over SpecR and misses the closed fibre, hence is empty. The section therefore splits off a line subbundle with locally free quotient. Iterating expresses [V] as a sum of line-bundle classes and one rank-two bundle class. Ranks zero and one are handled separately.

If V has rank two, a general section of V⊗H^n has finite transverse zero scheme on the closed surface. Its actual zero scheme F on B is etale over R near every point of that fibre: locally the two section equations have invertible relative Jacobian on the smooth relative surface B/R. The non-etale locus is a closed subset of the proper F and misses the closed fibre, so it is empty. Properness and quasi-finiteness make F finite etale over R. The same reasoning applied to two transverse high-twist Cartier sections gives finite etale complete intersections. Relative effective-Cartier flatness near the closed fibre is the exact Stacks062Y(3) criterion; properness extends it to all of B. Finite support is also directly covered by Stacks02UP and02LS, whose full displayed proofs were freshly read in the NK audit.

Every such finite R-scheme is semilocal. Every invertible module on it is trivial, by the Chinese-remainder/Nakayama proof already supplied in WP.4. More generally, every vector-bundle class of constant rank zero restricts to zero in K0(F), since finite projective modules over a semilocal ring are classified by their locally constant rank. The finite etale immersions used here are regular of codimension two, so their structure sheaves and pushforwards are perfect even when R is singular.

Let G be the subgroup of K0(B) generated by the classes [O_F] of the finite zero schemes just constructed, including the transverse intersections of two line-bundle sections. If I is the rank-zero subgroup, the perfect projection formula gives

`I G=0`,

because x·[O_F]=j_*(j^*x)=0 for every x∈I. Tensoring any such class by a line bundle leaves it unchanged.

## PS.3. Exact generation in gamma degree two

First, for any two line bundles M,N on B,

`([M]−1)([N]−1) ∈ G`.

Choose high twists M H^a,H^a,N H^b,H^b and four sections whose closed-fibre zero curves are smooth and pairwise transverse wherever a pair is used. Such simultaneous choices are a finite collection of open first-jet conditions. Write their relative Cartier divisors D_M+,D_M−,D_N+,D_N−. The Cartier exact sequences give the exact identity

`[M]−1 = [M][O_D_M+] − [O_D_M−]`,

and its N analogue. Multiplying expands the product into four perfect classes of the transverse intersections, with line-bundle twists. Each intersection is finite etale over R, and its twists are trivial. This proves the assertion integrally. It also applies to a square of a line difference by using two independently chosen divisor representations of the same line bundle.

For a rank-two bundle V, set

`c(V)=gamma^2([V]−2)=[det V]−[V]+1`, and `d=[det V]−1`.

Choose h=[H^n] as in PS.2 and W=V⊗H^n. The Koszul resolution of its transverse section gives

`[O_F]=1−[W^*]+[det W^*]`.

Multiplying by detW converts the right side to c(W), because W^*⊗detW≅W. The left side is unchanged since detW restricts trivially on finite semilocal F. Therefore c(W)=[O_F]. Direct expansion, with no discarded higher term, gives

`c(W)=h c(V)+h(h−1)d+(h−1)^2`.

Consequently

`c(V)=h^−1[O_F]−(h−1)d−h^−1(h−1)^2 ∈ G`,

using the line-product calculation and invariance of G under line twists.

Every rank-zero class is now a sum of line differences and elements of G: reduce bundles to rank two by PS.2 and use [V]−2=d−c(V). It follows that I²⊂G and I³=0. Conversely each generator of G is a rank-two Koszul gamma class, so G⊂F_gamma²K0(B).

For completeness this proves the gamma bound, not just the augmentation bound. For a line element, gamma_z([M]−1)=1+([M]−1)z. For a rank-two bundle, gamma_z([V]−2)=1+([V]−2)z+c(V)z². Gamma series of arbitrary virtual rank-zero classes are finite products and inverses of these expressions, because the preceding bundle reductions generate K0. Every coefficient of total degree at least three belongs to I³+IG and vanishes. Products of gamma operations of total degree at least three vanish for the same reason. This proves PS.1 integrally and also shows F_gamma²K0(B)=G.

## PS.4. Polynomial form and proper-surface lifting

On the rank quotient, psi² acts as1; on I/G it acts as2; on G it acts as4. The last equality can be checked on c(V) directly: psi²c(V)=4c(V)+c(V)(d+[V]−2)=4c(V), since IG=0. Thus

`A(psi²)=0 on K0(B)`, where `A(X)=(X−1)(X−2)(X−4)`.

The same polynomial annihilates K0(S), by taking R=k. This statement requires no Adams semisimplicity.

Now assume characteristic p>0 and retain the fixed-product KST/GH/TT blow-up hypotheses of FF: g:Z→SpecR is proper, Z regular, an isomorphism away from the closed point, and E is its reduced closed fibre. The every-degree FF exact sequence on the fixed proper parameter S gives

`K1(Z×S)_Q ⊕ K1(S)_Q → K1(E×S)_Q → K0(B)_Q → K0(Z×S)_Q ⊕ K0(S)_Q`.

For a class eta with psi²eta=8eta, its boundary is killed by A(psi²) on K0(B), while A(8)=168. Hence that boundary is zero rationally. The K1(S) summand can be absorbed by its actual pullback to Z×S. Every such weight-three class on E×S therefore lifts to an actual K1 class on the same Z×S. No localization or henselization of R tensor_k k(u) occurs.

Similarly, any class of weight three in the kernel of K0(Z×S)_Q→K0(E×S)_Q is zero: exactness puts the pair (x,0) in the image of K0(B)_Q, so A(psi²)x=0; weight three gives168x=0. This is injectivity on the indicated eigenspace, not on the entire K0 group. Polynomial corrections can be used throughout in place of a global singular-K1 splitting.

## PS.5. The still unresolved boundary step

Suppose a varying proper parameter curve over k(u) is spread over an open of a smooth projective surface S with SNC boundary D. Localization puts the obstruction to extending its exceptional K1 class in K0(E×S on E×D). Contributions coming from the smooth boundary components can be lifted by WP (for the top weight in exceptional dimension two); the proper-surface statements above can then remove the corresponding forget-support obstruction and correct an extendable remainder.

But the supported boundary need not decompose into those contributions. At intersections of boundary components, support-union exactness involves negative K-groups of E; in the relevant shifted weight it includes K_-1(E) of weight one. These groups can be nonzero. Replacing D by its normalization or using the full K0 lift over a nodal parameter curve does not supply supported devissage in the singular ambient product. No theorem in this note kills the node term for the actual HK/VE class. The compactification route therefore remains incomplete and UK.7 is unchanged.
