## Complete proof checks

1. **Projective singular perfect K0.** A power of the maximal ideal annihilates O_D. The quotient of the local k-algebra by that power is finite-dimensional over its residue coefficient field k. Thus D and D×P are projective over k and have the resolution property. The gamma dimension theorem applies to their vector-bundle K0, including their singular and nonreduced structures. In particular gamma-three vanishes on E, a projective surface. No Cartan map to coherent G0 is used to discard classes.

2. **Generated-bundle reduction.** Dualizing a generating surjection for V-dual tensor L^m and then twisting by L^m gives an exact sequence from V to a sum of L^m with quotient Q tensor L^m, where Q is generated. Products of generating Q-sections and first-jet-generating line sections generate the required first jets. Hence a rank-zero virtual bundle is a difference of equally ranked sufficiently generated bundles; a common positive summand arranges rank at least two and positive determinants. Expanding gamma_t(A−B) gives the stated gamma-two identity, including both determinant cross terms. Products of rank-zero classes reduce modulo gamma-three to products of line differences, and then to differences of positive lines.

3. **Perfect lci curves.** For a generic map O^(n−1)→A, the deeper rank-drop locus has codimension six and misses the threefold. An invertible minor leaves a two-entry column. Its two entries are a regular sequence on the Cohen–Macaulay Cartier divisor D×P. The local Koszul verification yields the global Hilbert–Burch sequence and its exact perfect K0 identity. The flag splitting principle identifies its degree-two term with gamma-two of A−n. This represents every gamma-two class modulo gamma-three by a finite rational combination of perfect lci curve classes. First-jet incidence on the finitely many reduced SNC strata and two endpoint fibres gives transverse component curves, finite double-stratum intersections, avoidance of triple strata, and finite endpoint lci schemes. The bad incidence images need not be closed; their closures are proper by the dimension estimate. The revised proof states this correctly.

4. **Actual regular hypersurface family.** After a high twist, the conormal rank-two bundle on the curve has generated sections. A general section is nowhere zero on its one-dimensional reduction. Its hypersurface H contains C as a Cartier divisor. At C the selected combination has nonzero ambient differential because the two normal equations were transverse along the relevant SNC stratum; away from C the ordinary first-jet incidence argument supplies the same condition. The extra requirement excluding every entire surface E_i×{v} is legitimate: the restriction space has dimension at least two uniformly in v, using a generated twisted ideal and a further very ample factor, while v ranges in dimension one. C cannot contain a whole such surface. H is flat over P by the fibrewise Cartier criterion.

5. **Lifting the equation and regularity.** Relative Serre vanishing on the fixed actual Z×P lifts the equation defining H. The resulting Cartier hypersurface W is regular along the closed fibre. Its nonregular locus is closed by excellence; if nonempty, its proper image in the local base would contain the closed point, a contradiction. The equation is a nonzerodivisor on every Z-fibre over P: every regular component meets the exceptional fibre by properness, where the relevant surface is not identically annihilated. Thus W is also flat over P. No parameter-dependent henselian neighbourhood has replaced the fixed product.

6. **One finite p-power, not an unbounded sequence.** For J=O_W(−D) and L=J|H, relative ampleness over the curve P gives eventual higher-direct-image vanishing. Leray therefore gives H²(H,L^n)=0 for all sufficiently large n. H¹ need not vanish. At each of the finitely many earlier steps the square-zero Picard obstruction is killed by p. Raising the current partial line bundle, together with every previous restriction, to its pth power preserves compatibility and permits the next extension. After the vanishing threshold the exponent stays fixed. There is one compatible formal line bundle restricting to O_H(−C)^(p^e).

7. **Algebraization retaining the whole curve.** The exceptional and maximal-ideal filtrations have cofinal powers. Projective existence over the completion algebraizes the line bundle; algebraizing the inverse and using full faithfulness verifies invertibility. Regular completion and Popescu descend the finite presentation to one smooth R-algebra. Henselian smooth lifting is performed modulo a fixed power m^M contained in J, rather than merely modulo m. It therefore preserves the whole H, including its entire proper parameter curve, and gives an actual invertible sheaf on the fixed W.

8. **Exact endpoint identity.** The Cartier pushforward j_*(1−Q) is perfect. Flatness and the regular sequences give the required Tor-independent endpoint and divisor base changes. Each C_u is finite Artinian, so every line bundle on it is trivial. The geometric-series identity gives exactly p^e[O_Cu] in perfect K0(D). This does not require a singular gamma-filtered pushforward theorem. Division by p^e, summation of the finite generators, and gamma-three vanishing on E prove the endpoint assertion.

9. **Tetrahedral consequence.** The natural kappa K₂(P) class has rank zero. Nilpotent invariance gives a rational lambda-compatible lift to D×P. Its endpoint values are square-zero of Adams weight two, so gamma-two of an integral multiple is the negative of that multiple. Applying EL to this correction yields the exact endpoint classes on Z. The pointwise FF isomorphism identifies the unique lifts; ch₂ on smooth finite-type models gives ordinary algebraic equivalence on the fixed product. The retained proper-curve symbol lemma, including the characteristic-two specialization, supplies every K₂(k) coefficient relation. Together with the fully audited TC exceptional-cycle quotient, this proves B²(X)_Q=0 for the specified ordered tetrahedral germs in every positive characteristic. It does not prove the universal threefold assertion.
