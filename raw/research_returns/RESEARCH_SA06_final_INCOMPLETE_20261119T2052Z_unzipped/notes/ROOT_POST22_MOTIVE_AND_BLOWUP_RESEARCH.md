## Explicit boundary point-blowup attempt

Choose a sufficiently high global H on E and a section s transverse to all positive-genus double curves and nonzero at all vertices. Blowing up the ambient regular threefold at their finite intersection Σ makes each old component a point blow-up. For the strict positive-genus boundary D_i', the bundle L_i=π_i*H−Σe_x has trivial restriction to D_i' via s. If H−D_i is sufficiently positive, the sections s and the equation of D_i times sections of H−D_i generate the transformed bundle. They recover the original surface away from D_i, so they define a big birational linear system contracting D_i'. This supplies proper curves through moved finite supports which avoid the positive-genus boundary. No unsupported theorem that every negative-definite divisor contracts is used.

However the required corrections along the newly created rational edges must retain equal restrictions on the old edge. Cycles derived the exact necessary identity BP.4, which the root checked independently: writing the exceptional coefficients a_x,b_x and plane coefficients c_x, the new-edge defects are −a_x−c_x and −b_x−c_x; their difference satisfies Σ(r_S,x−r_T,x)[x]=(M_S−M_T)|C in Pic(C)_Q. The single high section supplies only the sum divisor H|C, not each marked point class. Hence unrestricted rational-edge degree surjectivity does not supply the simultaneous corrections. This does not rule out arbitrary perfect families; the immediate divisor-correction route is incomplete.
