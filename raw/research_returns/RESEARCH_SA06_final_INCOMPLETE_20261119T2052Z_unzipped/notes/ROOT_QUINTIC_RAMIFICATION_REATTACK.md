# Generic quintic: the twisted ramification gate remains

19 November 2026, 15:54–16:00 UTC. Research only. No numbered result or main-gate movement.

Root reread the entire final proof of C.3au in `agents/cycles/fermat_quintic_top.tex`. Its actual algebraically trivial products and proper first-jet trace are retained exactly; its specified Fermat cover is not replaced by an arbitrary quintic cover.

For a smooth projective surface V with L=K_V and a generically finite separable morphism f:T→V from a smooth projective surface, the ramification section identifies K_T with f*K_V tensor O_T(R_f). The traced coherent product

    H1(T,f*K_V) × H1(T,O_T) → H2(T,f*K_V) → H2(V,K_V)

is Serre-dual to multiplication by the ramification section

    H1(T,O_T) → H1(T,O_T(R_f)).

This identifies the precise bilinear map to test; it does not prove its vanishing or nonvanishing for arbitrary covers. The Fermat cover in C.3au has the needed nonzero map.

An attempted obstruction via the absence of an abelian-type summand in the transcendental H2 of a very general quintic does not establish this vanishing. The ordinary untwisted cup uses two H1(O_T) factors. Here the first factor is H1(T,f*K_V), and there is no proved assertion that it is generated by products of H0(T,f*K_V) with H1(T,O_T). The cycles owner independently identified this precise gap after rereading `notes/quintic_cover_method_limits.md`.

For elementary covers T inside V×P cut by M exterior B, with P a curve and M a sufficiently positive power of O_V(1), the exact sequence and the quintic's H1(O_V(j)) vanishings give only the parameter H1 contribution to H1(T,f*L). Products with parameter Pic0 then have zero trace for dimension reasons. This repeats a limitation of that particular construction; it gives no statement about all irregular covers.

There is a second independent gap: an arbitrary actual codimension-two ordinary family on the strict-local resolution has not been proved to be a sum of proper pushforwards of the Picard products used in C.3au. Even a universal vanishing result for the displayed coherent map would therefore not, by itself, produce an ordinary-quotient invariant. Both gates remain explicit.

The proposed generic-quintic counterexample is not registered. No new computation was run.
