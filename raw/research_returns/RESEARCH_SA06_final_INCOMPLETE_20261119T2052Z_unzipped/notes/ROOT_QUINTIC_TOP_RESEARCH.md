# Unresolved characteristic-zero top quotient: current research record

Date: 19 November 2026, 10:45--10:50 UTC. This note records an unsuccessful route and source checks, not a new theorem or a smaller main gate.

## Test case and exact missing step

Let V be a smooth quintic surface over Qbar, extended to C, with L=O_V(1)=K_V. Its positive twists have H1(V,L^j)=0; H2(V,L)=C and H2(V,L^j)=0 for j>=2. The first absolute infinitesimal K2 term has the candidate component H2(V,L) tensor Omega^1_(C/Q). A local transverse-curve residue can see scalar multiples of db. This alone does not determine the ordinary algebraic-equivalence quotient.

The missing question is the image of actual families on a fixed product X times Q, where Q is a proper smooth parameter curve. In particular, a possible H2(V,L) tensor H0(Q,Omega^1_Q) contribution cannot be discarded merely because q(V)=0. The product-of-curves proof C.3ar uses actual Picard deformations in H1(V,L^j) and therefore does not cover this example.

We considered presenting an ordinary family through a correspondence on a nonhomogeneous Cartier surface and its relative Jacobian. Its constant abelian part might constrain the desired component, but neither the required vanishing nor a construction attaining it has been proved. Generic absence of an isotrivial curve family is not assumed. Formal deformation classes are not silently substituted for actual fixed-product families.

## Searches and primary sources actually inspected

Searches included “Srinivas local Chow groups algebraic equivalence cone surface absolute differentials quintic”, “Chow group punctured spectrum henselian local ring algebraic equivalence top cycles characteristic zero”, exact phrases “local Chow groups” with “algebraic equivalence”, and “henselian” with “algebraic equivalence” and “cone”. Broad search results about other meanings of local Chow groups were discarded.

- Cortinas, Haesemeyer, Walker, and Weibel, *K-theory of cones of smooth varieties*, arXiv:0905.4642v2, https://arxiv.org/pdf/0905.4642: full primary PDF opened. We read the introductory formulas, Proposition 3.5 and Example 3.6 (author pp. 14--15), including the twisted arithmetic connection and the number-field-model splitting. These calculate homogeneous affine-cone K-theory; they do not identify the ordinary algebraic-equivalence subgroup on its punctured strict henselization. No such transfer is inferred.
- Devadas, *GAGA for Henselian schemes*, JIMJ 24(4) (2025), 1547--1589, DOI 10.1017/S147474802500009X: full publisher PDF opened and Theorem 4.3.1, its proof, and Corollaries 4.3.2--4.3.4 read at pp. 1577--1581. A finitely presented henselian ideal inside an algebraizable sheaf can be algebraized. This does not provide a henselian ideal from an arbitrary formal one. The cohomology comparison in Theorem 3.6.1 assumes positive characteristic; Example 4.4.1 explicitly obstructs an unrestricted characteristic-zero coherent-sheaf conclusion. The paper is already in the bibliography under frDevadas2025HenselianGAGA.
