\section{From the finite dihedral type to its exact local parameter}
\label{sec:cubic-finite-type-local}

Retain $p,q,\theta,C_\theta,\tau_\theta$ from
AC-G72-character.  Put $F=\mathbb Q_q$, let $F_2/F$ be the
unramified quadratic extension, and set
\[
 K_q=\operatorname{GL}_3(\mathbb Z_q),\qquad
 K_q(1)=1+qM_3(\mathbb Z_q),\qquad
 J=\{g\in K_q:\overline g\in P_{2,1}(\mathbb F_q)\}.
                                                        \tag{G72L.1}
\]
Inflate $C_\theta\boxtimes1$ to $J$ through its reductive
quotient $\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb F_q)\times\mathbb F_q^\times$,
and denote the resulting representation by $\sigma$.

\begin{proposition}[AC-G72-local: the finite type forces the tame local block]
\label{AC-G72-local}
For an irreducible admissible characteristic-zero smooth
representation $\pi$ of $\operatorname{GL}_3(F)$, the following
are equivalent:
\[
\begin{split}
 &\operatorname{Hom}_{\operatorname{GL}_3(\mathbb F_q)}
       (\tau_\theta,\pi^{K_q(1)})\ne0;\\
 &\pi=\operatorname{Ind}_{P_{2,1}(F)}^{\operatorname{GL}_3(F)}
                  (\pi_\theta\xi\boxtimes\eta),
\end{split}                                                   \tag{G72L.2}
\]
where induction is normalized, $\xi$ is an unramified character
of $\operatorname{GL}_2(F)$, $\eta$ is an unramified character
of $F^\times$, and $\pi_\theta$ is any one depth-zero
supercuspidal representation whose maximal-compact type is
$C_\theta$.  The induction in (G72L.2) is irreducible.

Under the usual characteristic-zero local Langlands
correspondence its Weil--Deligne parameter has the form
\[
       \left(\operatorname{Ind}_{W_{F_2}}^{W_F}
                       \widetilde\theta\right)\oplus\eta_W,
                         \qquad N=0,                        \tag{G72L.3}
\]
where $\eta_W$ is unramified and $\widetilde\theta$ is tame,
with restriction to $\mathcal O_{F_2}^{\times}$ equal to the
inflation of $\theta$ from
$\mathbb F_{q^2}^{\times}/\mathbb F_q^{\times}$, up to
interchanging $\theta$ and $\theta^{-1}$.  Unramified
normalizations and the unramified depth-zero rectifier are
absorbed in $\widetilde\theta$.  In particular its inertia
characters are exactly $\theta,\theta^{-1},1$, its inertia
image is finite of $p$-power order, and the rank-two Weil
summand is irreducible.  This statement concerns an actual
finite type vector; it makes no assertion that the positive
multiplicity condition of AC-G72 has been met.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The inflation to $K_q$ of $\tau_\theta$ is
$\operatorname{Ind}_{J}^{K_q}\sigma$.  Compact Frobenius
reciprocity gives the exact equality
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}_{\operatorname{GL}_3(\mathbb F_q)}
            (\tau_\theta,\pi^{K_q(1)})
              =\operatorname{Hom}_{J}(\sigma,\pi).           \tag{G72L.4}
\]
The subgroup $K_q(1)$ is in the kernel of $\sigma$, so the
image of any $J$-intertwiner is already fixed by $K_q(1)$.
No averaging in residue characteristic $p$ is used: this is
an identity of characteristic-zero intertwining spaces.

The subgroup $J$ is the parahoric for the lattice-chain facet
of block sizes $(2,1)$.  The representation $\sigma$ is
cuspidal on its reductive quotient, since $C_\theta$ is
cuspidal and every irreducible character of the rank-one
factor is cuspidal.  Apply Morris, \emph{Level Zero
G-Types}, Compositio Math. \textbf{118} (1999), Theorem~4.8
and its complete proof, printed pp.~154--155.  The proof
identifies the inertial support with the classes of
supercuspidal representations of the Levi
$L=\operatorname{GL}_2(F)\times F^\times$ containing
$C_\theta\boxtimes1$ on
$Q=\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb Z_q)\times\mathbb Z_q^\times$.
The required cover is precisely his Lemma~3.6 and the summary
in~3.8, printed pp.~148--149.  His definition of an
$\mathfrak S$-type in~4.3 is an ``if and only if'', so it
applies in both directions of (G72L.4).

In this case the possible Levi classes form one inertial
class.  Indeed the normalizer of
$\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb Z_q)$ in $\operatorname{GL}_2(F)$
is $F^\times\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb Z_q)$, as follows
by its action on the homothety class of the standard lattice.
Morris's Proposition~4.1 and the proof of Theorem~4.5,
printed pp.~151 and 153, identify every supercuspidal
representation containing $C_\theta$ with compact induction
of an extension of that type to this normalizer.  Because the
extra subgroup is central, such an extension is determined by
the scalar assigned to $qI_2$; the scalar action on
$\mathbb Z_q^\times I_2$ is already fixed by $C_\theta$.
Any two choices differ by an unramified determinant twist:
over an algebraically closed coefficient field, choose a
square root of their ratio for the value of that twist at
$q$.  The rank-one factor containing the trivial
$\mathbb Z_q^\times$-character is simply an unramified
character.  This proves the claimed unique Levi inertial
class.

An irreducible representation in this class is a subquotient
of the induction in (G72L.2).  The two singleton supercuspidal
segments have different degrees, two and one.  They cannot
be linked, since unramified twisting never changes the
degree of a supercuspidal representation.  Zelevinsky,
\emph{Induced representations of reductive $p$-adic groups.
II}, Theorem~4.2, printed p.~184, therefore makes that
induction irreducible.  It follows that the subquotient is
the whole induction, proving (G72L.2).

For clarity the Langlands input here is the established
characteristic-zero depth-zero correspondence, not a new
proof of that correspondence.  An exact primary research
restatement is Collacciani, arXiv:2501.09085, Section~1:
(2)--(3) give the depth-zero-supercuspidal/finite-cuspidal
bijection; (4)--(8) and Theorem~1.1 specify its parahoric
head; Theorem~1.5, equation~(13), gives compatibility with
Macdonald's inertia-and-monodromy parameter.  The latter
imports Silberger--Zink, \emph{Explicit Shintani base change
and the Macdonald correspondence}, Appendix~A.2,
Proposition~2.  In our finite cuspidal support the two distinct
factors $C_\theta$ and $1$ each occur once.  Their partitions
are consequently both $(1)$; there is no larger partition
with the same multiplicities.  The corresponding parameter
is the tame orbit $\{\theta,\theta^q\}$ of length two and
the trivial orbit of length one, each with zero nilpotent
operator.  As $\theta^q=\theta^{-1}\ne\theta$, the first
orbit gives the irreducible induction in (G72L.3).
The unramified factors in (G72L.2) affect Frobenius but not
these inertia characters.  This proves all the stated
parameter consequences.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[AC-G72-stable: residual placement excludes a global line]
\label{AC-G72-stable}
Let $K$ be an imaginary quadratic field and let $R$ be a
semisimple three-dimensional $p$-adic representation of
$G_K$, with $R^c\simeq R^\vee$.  Suppose its semisimplified
reduction is
\[
       \overline R^{\mathrm{ss}}
           =\delta_+\oplus1\oplus\delta_-,\qquad
       \delta_+^{\,c,\vee}=\delta_-,                         \tag{G72L.5}
\]
where these three characters are distinct.  In particular
$1$ is the only individually conjugate-self-dual character
in this list.  Suppose $q$ splits in $K$, that
$\overline R^{\mathrm{ss}}$ is unramified at a chosen
place $v\mid q$, and that
\[
       \overline R^{\mathrm{ss}}(\operatorname{Frob}_v)
                    =\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,-1).          \tag{G72L.6}
\]
If the actual local parameter of $R|_{G_{K_v}}$ is (G72L.3),
then $R$ is irreducible.  In particular, an actual finite
$\tau_\theta$ automorphic eigensystem with these residual
properties has irreducible attached $R$, provided the
retained local--global compatibility identifies its actual
Weil--Deligne parameter with that in AC-G72-local.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Frobenius exchanges the two inertia eigenspaces of the
rank-two summand in (G72L.3).  Its characteristic polynomial
on that summand therefore has the form $X^2-a$, while its
polynomial on the unique one-dimensional summand is $X-b$.
A stable lattice in $R$ makes the relevant eigenvalues
integral units.  Reducing the factorization and using
(G72L.6) gives
\[
              (X^2-\overline a)(X-\overline b)
                         =(X+1)^2(X-1).                    \tag{G72L.7}
\]
If $\overline b=1$, the remaining quadratic would be
$(X+1)^2$, whose linear coefficient is $2\ne0$ because
$p>3$.  This is impossible for $X^2-\overline a$.
Thus $\overline b=-1$ and $\overline a=1$.

If $R$ were a sum of three global characters, its local
Weil representation would also be a sum of characters,
contradicting the irreducible rank-two summand in (G72L.3).
These are all possibilities: a semisimple representation of
dimension three decomposes into irreducible summands with
dimensions either $1+1+1$, $2+1$, or $3$, with multiplicities
counted.  The only remaining reducible possibility is thus a
global irreducible rank-two summand and a global character
$\lambda$.
Conjugate duality must preserve this unique rank-one
summand, so $\lambda^c=\lambda^{-1}$.  Its reduction is one
of the characters in (G72L.5) and has the same property;
therefore $\overline\lambda=1$.  Locally $\lambda$ must be
the unique rank-one constituent of (G72L.3): its rank-two
summand is irreducible and has nontrivial inertia characters,
whereas the inertia-invariant subspace is exactly the displayed
one-dimensional summand.  Its Frobenius
reduction is consequently $\overline b=-1$, contradicting
$\overline\lambda(\operatorname{Frob}_v)=1$.  This proves
irreducibility.  Arithmetic versus geometric Frobenius does
not change this contradiction, since $1$ and $-1$ equal
their own inverses.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The remaining existence and propagation gates]
AC-G72-local and AC-G72-stable convert an actual finite type
vector into the required finite-inertia irreducible local/global
shape.  They do not supply the strict inequality in AC-G72,
a correctly weighted ordinary starting eigensystem, or the
actual per-curve support condition $J\subseteq\ker f$.
Nor do they assert that (G72L.6) is available for every literal
source datum: for example, when $\psi=1$ and $e$ is even,
$q\equiv-1\pmod p$ gives $\psi(q)q^e=1$, not $-1$.
The exact old \texttt{IsSeedAlt} and ordered local-component
conditions remain to be matched after any proposed global
construction.
\end{remark}
