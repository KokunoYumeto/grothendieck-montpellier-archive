\section{A bounded even dual and eventual killing of the mixed obstruction}
\label{sec:cubic-cyclotomic-mixed-obstruction}

This section kills the mixed-cup obstruction after passage up a
cyclotomic tower and constructs fresh native ordinary support on
each higher host.  The source unramifiedness condition concerns
the auxiliary place, not the coefficient prime.  These results
still do not instantiate the old single-character frame or prove
containment of a prescribed curve in the new support ideal.

Put $p=269$ and retain the actual host $H$ of AC-G83/G87.
Write $F=H^+$, so that $H=KF$, $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})$, and
$F=TR$ with $T/E$ cyclic of $p$-power degree, $T/\mathbb Q$
real dihedral, $E/\mathbb Q$ real quadratic, and $R/\mathbb Q$
real multiquadratic.  All completions of $F$ at $p$ are unramified
over $\mathbb Q_p$.  Let $F_n$ be the degree-$p^n$ layer of its
cyclotomic $\mathbb Z_p$-extension and put $H_n=HF_n$.
Let $k$ be one finite coefficient field containing the residual
characters.  Put
\[
 \gamma=\bar\psi\bar\epsilon^e,\qquad
 \omega=\gamma^{-2},\qquad
 W=k(\omega\eta_K),\qquad
 V=W^\vee(1)=k(\lambda),\quad
 \lambda=\eta_K\bar\psi^2\bar\epsilon^{2e+1}.
 \tag{G91.1}
\]
Here the characters extending from $\mathbb Q$ are restricted to
the indicated groups.  On $G_{F_n}$ the character $\eta_K$ is
the CM quadratic character.  All character orders are prime to $p$.

Choose a finite Galois extension $L/F$ of degree prime to $p$
splitting the full cubic residual datum, the CM quadratic character,
and $\bar\epsilon$.  Put $L_n=LF_n$.
Let $N$ be the closed normal subgroup generated by all
away-$p$ inertia groups intersected with $G_L$, and put
\[
 \Gamma_n=G_{F_n}/N.
 \tag{G91.2}
\]
The complete set of killed inertia groups is stable under
$G_F$; because the cyclotomic tower is unramified away from $p$,
this is the same subgroup $N$ at every layer.  In particular
$[\Gamma_n:\Gamma_{n+1}]=p$.

For the actual full local maps at the $p$-places set
\[
 C_n(W)=\operatorname{Cone}\left(
 C^\bullet(\Gamma_n,W)\longrightarrow
 \bigoplus_{v\mid p}C^\bullet(G_{F_{n,v}},W)
 \right)[-1].
 \tag{G91.3}
\]
The arithmetic comparison proved in AC-G90 identifies this
complex with the anti part of the doubled CM strict complex
for $k(\omega)$.  Its proof uses the finite prime-to-$p$
splitting extension and exact invariants, not an asserted
injectivity of inflation on $H^2$.

\begin{lemma}[AC-G91-bound: the even dual is uniformly bounded]
\label{lem:G91-bound}
The character $\lambda$ is even over $\mathbb Q$, and remains
nontrivial on every $G_{F_n}$.  There is a finite constant $C$,
depending on the fixed host and character but not on $n$, with
\[
 \dim_k H^1(\Gamma_n,V)\leq C.
 \tag{G91.4}
\]
No $\mu$-vanishing theorem for the cubic coefficient splitting
field is required.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Complex conjugation has values $-1$ on $\eta_K$ and
$\bar\epsilon$, whereas $\bar\psi^2$ has value $1$.
Thus $\lambda$ is even.  At $p$, the original condition
$\operatorname{comp}_p(\psi)^2=1$ makes $\bar\psi^2$
unramified, and $\eta_K$ is unramified as well.
On the inertia of any initial unramified $F_v/\mathbb Q_p$,
the character $\lambda$ is therefore
$\bar\epsilon^{2e+1}$.
The cyclotomic character there has order $268$.
An odd exponent is not divisible by $268$, so this restriction
is nontrivial.  A subgroup of $p$-power index cannot reduce
the image of a character of order prime to $p$; the same
nontriviality holds on every layer.

Let $B_\lambda/\mathbb Q$ be the finite abelian field cut out
by $\lambda$, and let $M_n=F_nB_\lambda$.
The field $B_\lambda$ is totally real, and $[M_n:F_n]$ is
prime to $p$.  Restriction identifies $H^1(\Gamma_n,V)$
with a character sector of full, unramified-away-$p$
cohomology over $M_n$, and in particular injects it into
\[
                    H^1(G_{M_n,S_p},k).
 \tag{G91.5}
\]
Here is the required inertia check.  Inflate a cocycle to
$G_{F_n}$ and restrict to $G_{M_n}$, where its coefficient
action is trivial.  The restriction is additive.  It kills
each away-$p$ inertia intersection with $G_{L_n}$ by (G91.2).
That intersection has index prime to $p$ in the corresponding
inertia group of $M_n$.  An additive homomorphism to $k$ which
kills a subgroup of such index is zero.  Hence the restriction
is unramified away from $p$.  Restriction is injective on
cohomology because $[M_n:F_n]$ is prime to $p$.

The argument of AC-G76-host applies to the totally real field
$M_0=TRB_\lambda$ with an arbitrary abelian factor in place
of its multiquadratic factor.  We check that extension here.
The field
\[
 A=ERB_\lambda(\mu_p)
\]
is abelian over $\mathbb Q$, while
$M_0(\mu_p)/A$ is cyclic of $p$-power degree, possibly smaller
than $[T:E]$.  Ferrero--Washington gives cyclotomic
$\mu(A)=0$.  The finite-$p$ ascent proved and sourced in
AC-G76-host gives $\mu(M_0(\mu_p))=0$.
Its unramified Iwasawa module and its $p$-split quotient
are consequently finitely generated over $\mathbb Z_p$.

By the exact coinvariant-to-class-group map of AC-G74,
the dimensions modulo $p$ of the $p$-split class groups of
$M_n(\mu_p)$ are uniformly bounded after eventual total
ramification.  The number of primes above $p$ is then fixed.
For the totally real field $M_n$, the exact Euler-duality
formula AC-G76.2 gives
\[
 \dim_k H^1(G_{M_n,S_p},k)
       =1+a_{M_n}(\bar\epsilon)+u_{M_n}.
 \tag{G91.6}
\]
Both terms on the right other than $1$ are uniformly bounded
by the preceding class bound and the fixed number of primes.
Enlarge the constant for the finitely many initial layers.
This proves (G91.4).  The larger field containing the cubic
character was used to define $\Gamma_n$, but is not used
as the field to which Ferrero--Washington is applied.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[AC-G91-kill: eventual zero restriction on the strict obstruction]
\label{thm:G91-kill}
For all sufficiently large $n$, restriction is the zero map
\[
                H^2(C_n(W))\longrightarrow H^2(C_{n+1}(W)).
 \tag{G91.7}
\]
Consequently any nonzero strict adjacent class at a fixed
layer stays nonzero after restriction, and its mixed
obstruction of AC-G90 vanishes at some later layer.
There it admits the polarized long-entry filling and the
strict local fillings of AC-G90.
This conclusion requires the initial nonzero adjacent class;
it does not prove its existence.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The nontriviality in AC-G91-bound implies
$V^{\Gamma_{n+1}}=0$.  Inflation--restriction for the
index-$p$ subgroup therefore makes
\[
 r_n:H^1(\Gamma_n,V)\longrightarrow H^1(\Gamma_{n+1},V)
 \tag{G91.8}
\]
injective.  Their finite dimensions form a nondecreasing
bounded sequence, so $r_n$ is an isomorphism for every
sufficiently large $n$.  The restriction--corestriction
identity is
\[
       \operatorname{cor}_{n+1,n}\,r_n=p=0
                        \quad\hbox{on }H^1(\Gamma_n,V).
 \tag{G91.9}
\]
Surjectivity of $r_n$ then proves
$\operatorname{cor}_{n+1,n}=0$ on the whole next-layer group.

The exact arithmetic compact-support duality in AC-G90 is
\[
 H^2(C_n(W))\ \times\ H^1(\Gamma_n,W^\vee(1))
                     \longrightarrow k,
 \tag{G91.10}
\]
a perfect pairing.  It is obtained from duality over $L_n$
by taking exact invariants of the group of order prime to $p$.
The global trace, defined by the local Brauer invariants,
is invariant under field automorphisms.  Thus the CM anti
part pairs with the anti part of the dual CM coefficient;
after descent to $F_n$ this dual coefficient is exactly
$V=k(\lambda)$ in (G91.1).  There is no extra sign in the
trace pairing.

Restriction in the first variable is adjoint to
corestriction in the second: this is the cup-product
projection formula followed by the field trace.
For sufficiently large $n$ the second map is zero by
(G91.9).  Perfectness of the next-layer pairing proves
(G91.7).

The adjacent character is
$\alpha=\bar\chi^{-j}\gamma^{-1}$ on $G_{H_n}$.
It has nontrivial image of order prime to $p$ by the
retained separated cubic residual datum.  Its restriction
to any $p$-power-index subgroup remains nontrivial.
At every actual coefficient-prime local map, the local separation
witness of AC-G90 makes $k(\alpha)^{G_v}=0$, and this remains
true after restriction of $p$-power index.  Thus AC-G90.4 identifies
the relative degree-one group with the strict global kernel,
including at every higher layer.
Inflation--restriction consequently preserves any nonzero
strict adjacent class.  Its strict local zero conditions
are preserved as well.  The mixed relative obstruction
commutes with restriction by its explicit cocycle formula
in AC-G90.  Restrict the class to a layer beyond the
stabilization index above and then one further layer.
Its obstruction is zero by (G91.7), while the adjacent
class is still nonzero.  The exact filling criterion of
AC-G90 gives the asserted polarized long entry and local
primitives.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[AC-G91-host: fresh native support on every cyclotomic layer]
\label{thm:G91-host}
Retain the four explicit source inputs of AC-G89-support:
$\mathrm{LevEx}$, $\mathrm{ProModularRingExists}$,
$\mathrm{LamEx}$ and $\mathrm{ModU}$, together with the
already used cyclic automorphic base-change theorem.
For every $n$, a fixed weight $w\geq2$ of AC-G87 supplies
an actual absolutely irreducible ordinary representation on
$H_n$ with the same restricted local types.
A fresh auxiliary place and coefficient DVR then give a native
ordinary determinant ring $B_n$ and a proper support ideal $J_n$.
The exact finite, torsionfree, dimension, $\mathrm{Unr0}$,
$\mathrm{N4}$, $\mathrm{P1core}$ and $\mathrm{P3core}$ conclusions
of AC-G89 hold there, with weight dimension $3[F_n:\mathbb Q]$.

This is a fresh support construction on $H_n$.  For $n>0$
there is no cyclotomic-character-preserving map of the existing
base-$H$ group onto its cyclotomic subgroup satisfying the
unchanged $\mathrm{TwLink.\pi\_eps}$ clause.  No comparison
of $J_n$ with the earlier ideal $J$ is inferred.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\emph{The higher host and actual representation.}
The initial field $H$ is unramified at $p$, whereas every
nontrivial layer of the cyclotomic $\mathbb Z_p$-extension
of $\mathbb Q$ is ramified at $p$.
Hence $H$ has trivial intersection with that tower.
In particular $H_n/H$ is cyclic of degree $p^n$ and
\[
 \operatorname{Gal}(H_n/\mathbb Q)
      =\operatorname{Gal}(H/\mathbb Q)\times C_{p^n}.
 \tag{G91.11}
\]
The extension $H_n/F_n$ is the base change of the CM quadratic
extension $H/F$, so it remains unramified at finite places.
Every coefficient-prime or old ramified-automorphic place
which was relatively split remains relatively split.
The actual representation restricted from $H$ gains no new
ramification away from the old finite set.

Its rank-three absolute irreducibility survives restriction
through the cyclic degree-$p$ steps.  Indeed, a nontrivial
character constituent of its enlarged commutant would give
a character self-twist of order $p$; comparison of determinants
would force that character cubed to be $1$, impossible for
$p=269$.  Cyclic automorphic base change, at the same checked
scope used in AC-G83, supplies the actual cuspidal realization;
its conjugate duality and parallel regular algebraic weights
are preserved.  Restrict the actual local ordinary flags.
All the residual local character images have order prime to
$p$, so their images and distinctness are unchanged on a
subgroup of $p$-power index.  The local Hodge weights remain
$(1-w,1,1+w)$ at every new embedding.  The exact finite
away types and zero monodromy remain valid under restriction.

\emph{A fresh scalar auxiliary place.}
Use the old prime-to-$p$ field $\Omega$ of AC-G89.6.
Every prime-to-$p$ quotient of the group in (G91.11) kills
both its cyclotomic $p$-factor and its dihedral $p$-rotation
subgroup.  Thus $H_n\cap\Omega$ is contained in $H$ and
\[
                         H_n\cap\Omega=K.
 \tag{G91.12}
\]
The same element of $\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/\mathbb Q)$
used in AC-G89-v0 is therefore compatible with the identity
on $H_n$.  Chebotarev provides a fresh rational auxiliary
prime splitting completely in $H_n$, with residual
cyclotomic value $-1$, $\gamma$-value $1$, and cubic value $1$.
Exclude all finitely many ramified places and the coefficient
prime.  Its place in $F_n$ has residue degree one and is
relatively split, and satisfies the literal $\mathrm{V0ok}$.
The auxiliary rational prime is unramified in $H_n$.

It is this auxiliary prime, called $p$ in the source record,
which appears in $\mathrm{HidaSetting.p\_unr}$.
The coefficient prime is called $l$ there; the record includes
$p\ne l$ and $\operatorname{Norm}(v_0)=p$.
Consequently ramification of $H_n$ at our coefficient prime
$269$ does not violate that source field.

The maximal abelian subextension in (G91.11) has Galois
group of exponent dividing $2p^n$.  It has no quotient of
order $67$, so $H_n$ still does not contain $\mu_{269}$.
Choose the coefficient DVR from the outset to contain the
actual representation and every $p$-adic embedding of $H_n$.
The native $\mathrm{Coef}$ and $\mathrm{Large}$ conditions
permit this choice; it is not an assertion of the old
pointwise counting restriction $e_{\mathcal O}=1$.

\emph{Native support at that layer.}
Every field, place, representation, ordered flag and old
inertia condition used in the direct $\mathrm{Pair}$ proof
of AC-G89 has now been verified for $H_n$.
Define its local $\mathrm{th}$ from the actual characters,
and use the new coefficient DVR itself as the native test
object.  The same ordered product and prime-to-$p$ inertia
arguments give every $\mathrm{Pair}$ field.
The same weights with $w\geq2$ verify all the literal
arithmetic $\mathrm{PT}$ conditions.
The retained source propositions therefore give $B_n$ and
$J_n\subset\ker x_n$, hence $J_n$ proper, with all the
claimed ring and propagation properties.
Its inertia-weight ring has $3[F_n:\mathbb Q]$ variables
by the literal $\mathrm{LamEx}$.

\emph{The unchanged-base map is a different assertion.}
The pro-$p$ cyclotomic image of the base group is
$1+p\mathbb Z_p$, whereas that of the cyclotomic subgroup
at layer $n$ is $(1+p\mathbb Z_p)^{p^n}$.
The away-inertia quotients do not change these images.
A homomorphism preserving the cyclotomic character would
put the first image inside the second, which is impossible
for $n>0$.  This proves precisely the stated obstruction
to that unchanged-base interface.  It has no bearing on
the freshly constructed $B_n,J_n$.
\end{proof}
