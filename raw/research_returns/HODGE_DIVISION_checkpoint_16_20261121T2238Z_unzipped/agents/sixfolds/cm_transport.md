**Status.** The transport and full-character arguments below are new elementary deductions. The source sheaf and its semiregularity are explicitly conditional on Markman's unrefereed arXiv:2502.03415v2. No published status is claimed. The construction reaches determinant class `[-1]` and produces one nonzero Weil direction, with a chosen compatible polarization on the balanced CM variety. ## SF-CM01: pulling semiregularity across a fixed isogeny

**Proposition.** Let f:A→Y be an isogeny of complex abelian varieties, G=ker(f), and let Fbar be an ordinary semiregular coherent sheaf on Y. Then F=f*Fbar is a perfect coherent sheaf, has no negative self-Ext, and is G-semiregular for the canonical coherent action of the actual finite subgroup G⊂A×{0} by translations. Moreover

\[
\exp(-c_1(F)/r)\operatorname{ch}(F)
=f^*\bigl(\exp(-c_1(\overline F)/r)\operatorname{ch}(\overline F)\bigr),
\qquad r=\operatorname{rank}\overline F\ne0.
\]

**Proof.** Over C the isogeny is finite étale. Its deck translations satisfy f∘t_g=f. Pullback therefore gives canonical isomorphisms t_g*f*Fbar≅f*Fbar, and their cocycle identity is the functorial identity for pullback of the equality f∘t_g=f. This is an honest coherent translation action, not a projective action or a central extension with an invisible kernel. Finite étale descent gives an equivalence Coh(Y)≅Coh(A)^G, and the corresponding derived equivalence identifies

\[
\operatorname{Ext}^2_A(F,F)^G
\simeq\operatorname{Ext}^2_Y(\overline F,\overline F).
\]

In characteristic zero taking invariants is exact, so no positive group-cohomology terms intervene. The full Atiyah class and its trace commute with étale pullback. Under the displayed identification, the restriction of the full semiregularity map is thus f*∘σ_Fbar. The map f* on ⊕_q H^{q+2}(Y,Ω_Y^q) is injective: f_*f*=(deg f)id on complex cohomology and respects Hodge summands. Consequently this restriction is injective. This is strong G-semiregularity and hence the weak condition used in DEG_L S-14 (Perry Definition2.6/Lemma2.7). Every coherent sheaf on the smooth A is perfect, and self-Ext in negative degrees vanishes because both arguments are sheaves in degree zero. The normalized-character identity follows from naturality of rank, first Chern class and Chern character. ∎

This proposition itself has no conjectural or preprint input. Its use of semiregularity means the ordinary full Atiyah-trace map, with all components; it is not a middle-component criterion.

## SF-CM02: the entire invariant character

**Proposition.** Let (A,K,h) be of Weil type, dim A=6, signature(3,3), and let a rational even cohomology class κ remain of Hodge type under all local deformations in its full nine-dimensional polarized Weil period domain. Then

\[
\kappa=P(h)+t,\qquad P\in\mathbf Q[x],\quad t\in W_K\subset H^6(A,\mathbf Q).
\]

If the middle component of κ is not proportional to h³, then t≠0. The statement concerns the entire even cohomology, not merely H6.

**Proof.** The Hodge circle of a balanced Weil point lies in SU(3,3): its determinant on either six-dimensional K-eigenspace is z³z^(-3)=1. As the point varies through the period domain these circles are conjugate by SU(3,3). Their infinitesimal generators generate a nonzero ideal in the simple real Lie algebra su(3,3), hence all of that Lie algebra. A class of weight zero and type(p,p) for every such circle is therefore SU(3,3)-invariant. Conversely the polarization and the two determinant tensors are invariant.

After extending scalars to C, write H¹=U⊕U*, with U the standard six-dimensional representation of SL6. Then

\[
\bigwedge^k(U\oplus U^*)
=\bigoplus_{a+b=k}\bigwedge^a U\otimes\bigwedge^b U^*.
\]

For 0≤a,b≤6 an invariant occurs precisely when a=b, or when (a,b)=(6,0),(0,6). Indeed the exterior powers of the standard representation are irreducible; their highest weights are distinct except that ∧0U and ∧6U are both trivial. When a=b the invariant has dimension one and is generated by the a-th power of the invariant pairing h. The two exceptional invariant lines are ∧6U and ∧6U*, constituting the Weil plane in degree6. Thus the full invariant subspace is Q[h]/(h7)⊕W_K (with the degree6 h³ line already included in the polynomial summand). The decomposition is rational because h and the sum of the two determinant lines are rational. This proves the formula and the last assertion. ∎

Equivalently, one may use Markman's explicit Spin(V)_P-invariance from Lemma6.2.3/Remark6.2.4, pp34–35; the representation calculation above supplies the exact full-character conclusion that S-14 requires.

## SF-CM03: a conditional seed on E6 with balanced K action

**Conditional theorem.** Assume the sheaf construction and semiregularity assertions in Markman arXiv:2502.03415v2: Theorem1.4.1(2)–(4), Lemmas9.1.4,9.3.1,9.3.5,9.3.11 and Remark9.3.7. Let K=Q(√−m), m>0 squarefree, and let E be any elliptic curve with End_Q(E)=K. There exist:

- the actual abelian variety A=E6 equipped with the standard balanced action diag(a,a,a,abar,abar,abar);
- a compatible integral ample polarization θ of determinant class [-1];
- a reflexive coherent sheaf F of rank r=32m on A;
- a finite translation subgroup G⊂A×{0}, with its canonical coherent action and an equivariant structure on F;
- an algebraic rational B0=−c1(F)/r;
- P∈Q[x] and 0≠t∈W_K;

such that F is G-semiregular, Ext^{<0}(F,F)=0, and

\[
\exp(B_0)\operatorname{ch}(F)=P(\theta)+t
\]

in the full even cohomology. Multiplying θ by an explicit positive integer once it has been chosen makes it very ample and only rescales P. The conclusion holds for every imaginary quadratic K; class number one is unnecessary for this existence statement.

**Proof, including the finite choices.**

1. **Choose an admissible radicand.** Set d=4m. Then d≥4 is even, K=Q(√−d), and Markman's source rank is r=8d=32m. Put N=d+1. In particular gcd(r,N²)=1. This avoids the inadmissible d=1 specialization for Q(i) and permits the untwisted descent of Lemma9.3.5. The finite cyclic groups in that construction have order N.

2. **Choose a good smooth CM Jacobian.** Let τ0 be a Siegel period matrix of the product principally polarized E³. The orbit Sp6(Q)·τ0 is analytically dense in Siegel upper half-space H3. One elementary proof is that the Cayley transform X↦(1−X)(1+X)^(-1), on the symplectic Lie algebra near zero, is defined over Q and parametrizes a neighborhood of the identity; rational Lie-algebra points are dense there. Since Sp6(R) is connected, rational points are dense throughout the group. Its action on H3 is transitive. Every rational symplectic transform of τ0 represents a principally polarized abelian variety isogenous to E³: the transformed marking changes the rational symplectic lattice by a commensurable one.

The Jacobian locus of smooth genus-three curves contains a nonempty open subset of A3, and the nonhyperelliptic Jacobian locus is open. For completeness, nonhyperelliptic genus-three curves are smooth plane quartics; their period differential is dual to Sym²H0(K_C)→H0(K_C²). Both spaces have dimension6 and the map is an isomorphism because a plane quartic has no quadratic equation. Hence the image contains an analytic neighborhood. The generic orbit and incidence hypotheses in Markman Lemmas9.1.4 and9.3.1 define a nonempty open subset of the finite moduli cover that also records the N-torsion groups. The image of a finite étale cover is open, so there is a nonempty analytic open subset of A3 consisting of good Jacobians. Density supplies q∈Sp6(Q) with q·τ0 in this open subset. Choose one such q, a corresponding smooth nonhyperelliptic C and good cyclic groups G1,G2 as in those lemmas. Then X=J(C) is isogenous to E³.

This is an existence choice in a nonempty open subset; no specific q, plane quartic equation or torsion coordinates have been evaluated in this run. Therefore this step must not be described as a completed numerical certificate or explicit equation-level CM example.

3. **Take the ordinary semiregular quotient sheaf.** Markman constructs the simple reflexive sheaf mathcalE on M=X×Xhat, of rank r, and a finite group of translation/tensor symmetries. Lemma9.3.5, p84, uses even d and gcd(r,N²)=1 to cancel the tensor part by a determinant power. For example, an integer a with ar≡−1 modulo N² supplies the needed cancellation; the precise sign follows the convention for the intermediate derived object. The result descends along the finite translation quotient q_M:M→Y to an ordinary reflexive sheaf Fbar of rank r. Remark9.3.7, p86, identifies the normalized twisted sheaf mathcalB with the normalization of this ordinary Fbar. Lemma9.3.11, p88, says mathcalB is semiregular.

The ordinary Fbar is also semiregular. Its normalized Atiyah class is at_Fbar−(c1(Fbar)/r)id. Replacing the ordinary Atiyah class by this normalized one multiplies the entire Atiyah-trace map by exp(−c1(Fbar)/r). On ⊕_qH^{q+2}(Ω^q) this is an invertible triangular operator. The twisted normalization has the same self-Ext space: its local scalar twists cancel in the endomorphism complex. Therefore injectivity of the full normalized semiregularity map is equivalent to injectivity of the ordinary one. This proves the required hypothesis on Fbar. Moreover q_M*κ(Fbar)=κ(mathcalE).

4. **Put the action on the standard balanced E6.** The variety Y is isogenous to X×Xhat and therefore to E6. Choose a rational isogeny identification End_Q(Y)≅M6(K), where this K initially denotes the central scalar CM action inherited from E. Markman's Weil action η:K→M6(K) commutes with that central K. After identifying the two copies of K, the polynomial of η(√−d) factors as (T−√−d)(T+√−d) over the central K. The corresponding eigenspaces each have K-dimension3, because their holomorphic dimensions are the Weil multiplicities(3,3). A matrix in GL6(K) therefore conjugates η to diag(√−d I3,−√−d I3). Choosing such a matrix and clearing its denominators yields a K-linear isogeny f:E6_balanced→Y. This argument uses the equality of the elliptic CM field and the secant field; it would not work with an unrelated CM elliptic field.

5. **Pull back and normalize.** Set F=f*Fbar and G=ker f. SF-CM01 proves strong G-semiregularity, with an honest translation action and no gerbe or projective-coherence issue, and proves negative-Ext vanishing. The Weil polarization hY on Y pulls back to a compatible positive rational polarization. Choose c>0 such that θ=c f*hY is the first Chern class of an ample line bundle. Markman Lemma3.1.3, pp23–24, gives determinant[-1] on M, hence on Y and its K-linear pullback. Scaling the polarization by c changes its rank-six Hermitian determinant by c6=Nm_K/Q(c³), so the determinant class remains[-1].

Set B0=−c1(F)/r. Theorem1.4.1(3) says the entire κ(mathcalE) stays Hodge on the full Weil period domain. The finite isogenies induce local isomorphisms of the corresponding period domains, so κ(F)=f*κ(Fbar) has the same property. SF-CM02 gives κ(F)=P(θ)+t in the full even cohomology. Theorem1.4.1(4) says κ3(mathcalE) has nonzero Weil projection (its K-translates together with h³ span three dimensions). Isogeny pullbacks are injective and preserve the Weil plane; consequently t≠0. This finishes the conditional construction. ∎

## What this moves and what it does not

Under Markman's stated preprint inputs, the request for a seed on a balanced CM E6 can be met with a genuine coherent sheaf and an effective finite translation action. The first-order assertion of Theorem1.4.1 alone is insufficient; the finite quotient and Lemma9.3.11 are essential. Choosing even d removes the separate gerbe-coherence gate. SF-CM02 removes the separate whole-character gate. The construction supplies one nonzero Weil direction. Its K-translates span the plane; applying the supplied division/reduction machinery to those algebraic directions gives the whole plane, but does not magically preserve semiregularity under arbitrary direct sums representing a prescribed direction.

Applying DEG_L S-14 (using its Perry Theorem1.2 input) to the resulting object yields bounded division on the associated determinant[-1] family, conditionally on the Markman source assertions. That bound is existential until the CM Jacobian, sheaf presentation and degree data are made effective. This note does **not** label it an explicit numerical bound and does not replace the unconditional Gaussian and Eisenstein constants already obtained from cyclic covers.

The seven other class-number-one fields are reached by this conditional non-curve construction in determinant[-1]. Nonsplit determinants are not reached: the obstruction is Markman's explicit determinant computation, not a deficit of source dimension. A compatible polarization on E6 need not be the product principal one, and no such identification is asserted.

In the project language, this constructs the conditional K-theory input to DEG_L S-14 rather than dividing a virtual class without an object. Semiregularity supplies deformation; the full λ-ring Chern character gives the flat class. The passage to uniform integral cycles still uses the authorized HECKE_DIV/GHOSTS reduction, the uniform form of condition A (cote16 scan050). No cycle is extracted from condition I alone (cote16 scan049/cote44 Th4), and no bound is inferred from the size of the CM isogeny or core index J.

## SF-CM04: every secant in the fixed hyperbolic spin model has split determinant

**Proposition.** Let (V,q) be a hyperbolic rational quadratic space of dimension4n. Suppose f∈End_Q(V) satisfies f²=−d, d>0, and q(fx,y)=−q(x,fy). Give V its K=Q(√−d) structure through f. The nondegenerate Hermitian form

\[
 H(x,y)=d\,q(x,y)+\sqrt{-d}\,q(fx,y)
\]

(up to the harmless convention interchanging its linear argument) is split of K-rank2n. In particular det(H)=[(−1)^n] in Q*/Nm(K*).

**Proof.** The skew-adjointness implies q(fx,x)=0, so H(x,x)=d q(x,x). Since q is hyperbolic, choose a nonzero rational isotropic vector x; this is a K-isotropic vector for H. Nondegeneracy of H permits splitting off a Hermitian hyperbolic plane U_K containing Kx. The rational quadratic form Re(H|U_K)/d is hyperbolic of rank4: in a K-hyperbolic basis, the two K-lines are isotropic rational planes and pair perfectly. The H-orthogonal complement is K-stable and also q-orthogonal. Since q and the removed rank-four quadratic form are both hyperbolic, rational Witt cancellation shows that q on this complement is hyperbolic. Repeat until V is the orthogonal sum of n Hermitian hyperbolic planes. Their determinant is(−1)^n modulo norms. ∎

**Application and scope.** Markman's ambient quadratic lattice is always H¹(X)⊕H¹(Xhat), with evaluation pairing; it is the sum of2n rational hyperbolic planes. For an oriented rational K-secant, his f and H have exactly the displayed properties (arXiv:2502.03415v2, §3.1, Equation3.1.2 and Lemma3.1.2, pp22–23). The elementary proposition therefore shows that the split determinant is not limited to his explicit ample secants exp(±√−dΘ). An arbitrary K-secant in that same hyperbolic spin model still cannot reach nonsplit determinants. The linear-algebra proposition is unconditional; any geometric use of the unrefereed secant construction remains explicitly conditional. This reduces a proposed repair of the determinant gap to a genuinely different quadratic/Hermitian source, rather than a different secant in the same source.

## SF-CM05: the literal elliptic-chain specialization is not a semiregular seed

This is a counterexample to transferring the smooth-curve semiregularity calculation verbatim to the elliptic-chain boundary used in Markman's openness argument. It is not an obstruction to smooth CM Jacobians or arbitrary complexes on E6.

Let A3=E1×E2×E3 and, for b∈E2\{0}, put

\[
 C_b=(E_1\times\{0\}\times\{0\})\cup
 (\{0\}\times E_2\times\{0\})\cup
 (\{0\}\times\{b\}\times E_3).
\]

This is a connected genus-three curve with two ordinary nodes. At either node its ideal in the smooth threefold has the local form(z,xy), so C_b is a local complete intersection. Its normal sheaf has at least four independent global sections: three from translating A3, and one from varying b. To see independence, restrict away from the nodes to the three components. A translation vector(v1,v2,v3) has normal components(v2,v3),(v1,v3),(v1,v2), respectively. The b-variation is zero on the first two components and nonzero only in the E2-normal direction of the third. Vanishing of a matching translation on the first two components forces v1=v2=v3=0, which cannot match that variation. The translation tangent map itself is injective by the same restriction argument. The family C_b is flat locally in b: its nodal local equations and component genera are constant, or equivalently its Hilbert polynomial and its two transverse incidence relations are constant. Hence these really are Hilbert-scheme tangent vectors.

Write I=I_Cb. Applying Hom(I,−) to0→I→O_A3→O_Cb→0 shows that Hom(I,O_Cb)=H0(N_Cb/A3) injects into Ext¹(I,I), because Hom(I,I)→Hom(I,O_A3) is the isomorphism C→C. Tensoring by Pic0(A3) supplies a three-dimensional subspace H¹(O_A3)⊂Ext¹(I,I); its trace is the identity since I has rankone, while the embedded Hilbert deformations have trivial determinant. The two subspaces are independent. Thus ext¹(I,I)≥4+3=7, and Serre duality on the abelian threefold gives ext²(I,I)≥7. The full semiregularity target has dimension

\[
 h^2(O_{A_3})+h^3(\Omega^1_{A_3})=3+3=6.
\]

Consequently I is not semiregular.

The same defect survives the natural cyclic-orbit repair. Suppose H⊂A3 is a finite translation subgroup such that the translates C_b+h are pairwise disjoint. For b in a small neighborhood they remain disjoint. Let Z_b be their union and let I_Zb have its canonical H-linearization. The H-invariant normal deformations contain the four-dimensional diagonal copy of the normal deformations above, and H¹(O_A3) contributes three invariant trace directions. Equivariant Serre duality is valid with trivial volume character because H consists of translations. Therefore dim Ext²(I_Zb,I_Zb)^H≥7. More decisively, finite étale descent identifies this invariant object with the ideal of the embedded image of C_b in A3/H, an abelian threefold. Its ordinary semiregularity target still has dimension6, so the descended ideal is not semiregular. Thus the canonical orbit ideal is not weakly H-semiregular. Since its automorphisms are scalars, changing the linearization only twists the descent by a torsion line bundle and does not remove the kernel.

For the natural product action on the exterior product of two such orbit ideals, the failure persists: a nonzero factor kernel in Ext² tensored with the identity of the other factor maps to zero under the product full semiregularity map. This element remains nonzero in the Künneth direct summand. Twisting by line bundles and applying a derived equivalence preserve this categorical kernel. Accordingly, Markman's elliptic-chain example proves openness/nonemptiness of certain incidence conditions; it cannot itself be substituted as the required semiregular object at a product CM point by this naive specialization.
