# Initial exact local-symbol result (draft; not independently checked yet)

On H=ker(lambda), choose n in {1,5,7,11} representing CRT u. Define L2(h)=(n,3)_2 and L3(h)=(n,3)_3. Standard Hilbert formulas give L2=(-1)^a2 and L3=(-1)^a3. Their product is (-1)^Delta. E58 has n=5, hence L2=+1,L3=-1; its global symbol (5,3)_5=-1 compensates. No failure of Hilbert reciprocity.

Ramification table: n=1: empty; n=5: {3,5}; n=7: {2,7}; n=11: {2,3}. At infinity all are +1. Global rational numbers congruent n modulo12 can introduce additional primes; local symbols at2,3 remain unchanged.

A rational Hilbert realization of this pair with no ramification outside {2,3} exists iff Delta=0. If bits00 choose a=1; bits11 choose a=-1. The latter quaternion algebra (-1,3) is isomorphic to (2,3): their ratio class (-2,3) splits since -2=N_{Q(sqrt3)/Q}(1+sqrt3). Thus discriminant6 corresponds bits11, NOT an unequal-bit obstruction.
