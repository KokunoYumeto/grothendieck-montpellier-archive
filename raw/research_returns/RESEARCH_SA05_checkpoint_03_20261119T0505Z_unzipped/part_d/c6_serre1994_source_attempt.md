## Honest outcome

The full 1994 chapter remains an explicitly documented source gap.
The already opened 1993 résumé's section 2.4 assumes good reduction
when formulating the motivic Frobenius statement. That observation
does not settle what the complete 1994 chapter says elsewhere.

No exact named conjecture equivalent to the requested weak
unramifiedness independence was verified in this attempt. Fontaine's
opened C_WD and Serre's opened 1970 conjectures about conductors or
local factors remain sufficient statements; this search supplies no
converse. It would be incorrect to label them exact equivalents.
