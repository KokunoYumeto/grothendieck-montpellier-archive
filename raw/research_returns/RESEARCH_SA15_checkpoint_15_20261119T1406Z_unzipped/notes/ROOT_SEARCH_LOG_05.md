# Additional source and approach record for checkpoint 05

## A.1: why the new coherent counterexample answers a stronger question

The continuing searches concerned smooth very ample Kodaira counterexamples, restriction on the Picard scheme, Frobenius-killed coherent classes, and integral crystalline weak Lefschetz. Original full texts inspected include Lauritzen–Rao (1997), Section2 and its claim on pp3–5; Mukai's RIMS1736 (December2011), Theorems1–3; Totaro (2019), introduction and Theorem3.1; Ekedahl (1988), Proposition2.14 p120; Biswas–Holla, arXiv math/0603299v2, Section2 and Remark2.4; and Cotner–Zavyalov's Examples3.3.6/3.4.6. The exact endpoints and limitations are in the bibliography and census. These searches did not by themselves produce a verified smooth very ample surface with the required nonzero map. In particular negative line-bundle cohomology on a rational homogeneous variety cannot alone produce a kernel in its vanishing structure-sheaf cohomology.

The successful next approach was to change the ambient variety. A finite cyclic cover supplies a second summand in its structure-sheaf cohomology, and multiplication by the new homogeneous coordinate maps onto that summand identically. The explicit cone embedding verifies very ampleness. The all-prime cohomology of the sixfold was re-proved by an apolar functional, rather than delegated to a numerical sample or an unstated representation-theory result. This construction is credited as a derivation in this dossier using the opened Lauritzen–Rao example, without a priority claim. The original surface and full integral questions are retained.

Nakkajima–Yobuko arXiv1902.09110v3 was downloaded and read at Theorem6.10, hypotheses6.5 and Remark6.11, printed pp52–53. It supplies a precisely qualified integral weak-Lefschetz statement and a corrected large-degree proof. The lift/log hypotheses and the min(dim,p) range prevent using it to close the remaining arbitrary low-degree crystalline gate. The report does not silently remove those hypotheses.

## A.3–A.4: fixed covectors and a finite exact algorithm

Root reopened Sannai–Tanaka arXiv1411.5294v5 Lemma4.5 p11 and checked its Frobenius decomposition for ordinary abelian varieties. The product proof separately verifies ordinary SNC degeneration, all-degree normal generation, extension of invariant covectors across parameter divisors, and the exact character relations. The attempted assertion that taking a Cartier root preserves a Frobenius character space was rejected during audit. The revised proof establishes preservation by comparing a pth-power identity at an eigenvector and its first-order variation. This repair is essential to the exact invariant-space count.

The full-image algorithm was audited as an actual terminating procedure. No source was substituted for an unspecified Galois-group oracle. Its finite-field factorization step is bounded enumeration; its finitely generated field step is a shifted norm and square-free resultant; its absolute-factor step is a bounded coefficient ansatz followed by a proved terminating search in finite separable extensions. Root opened Stacks Tags037P,037Q,030W,030X, namely Lemmas10.47.8,10.47.13,10.44.2,10.44.3, and checked the needed finiteness, separability and geometric-integrality assertions. The finite field E is recovered from normalized factor coefficients, and its embedding in the selected frame component is computed by linear algebra.

The exact quartic-family test is a genuine smooth family, with three affine singularity ideals checked. The six-dimensional frame algebra has all six GL2(F2) actions, and a simple Artin–Schreier pole proves that it stays a field over algebraically closed constants. The algorithm gives an exact effective answer; a structural identification of all general-ambient groups is a distinct remaining objective. The product examples are now a concrete next test of that objective.

## B.3–B.4 and E.2

Anema–Top–Tuijp (2018), original SIGMA paper, Theorem1.1 p2 and Proposition5.1 pp4–5, was reopened. The former identifies the symplectic full-three-torsion Hesse family, and the latter gives the cube ratio of discriminants. Those exact statements support the invariant-differential step in the wild valuation argument. Root and the crystals agent independently checked the ramification calculation. The height-two level-four characteristic-two counterexample also passed an independent exhaustive group-law certificate. The reduction agent's log records its primary potential-good and supersingular-automorphism locators.

For E.2, Ben-Zvi–Nevins, original arXiv math/0212094v3, was reopened at Remark2.7 (pro-category tensor conventions), Proposition2.11, Theorem3.11, Theorem4.3 and Remark4.5. These identify ordinary pullback for the left-module/stratification equivalence; the claim is not inferred from their separate !-crystal result. Stacks Tag0CNF, Lemma29.47.9, was reopened for finite universal homeomorphisms and Frobenius powers. The arbitrary-flat characteristic-zero extension was independently checked against the previously opened Gaitsgory–Rozenblyum Corollary2.2.2 and Proposition3.4.3. A nilpotent filtration proves the ordinary flatness of every resulting value without a finite-generation assumption.

The full relative E.2 assertion is still under investigation. Ordinary tensor products cannot be replaced by completed tensor products in a descent argument without justification. In particular completion along the diagonal need not be faithfully flat over the uncompleted tensor product, so the tempting completed-nerve shortcut has not been used to claim a general theorem.
