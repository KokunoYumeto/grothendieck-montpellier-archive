## Main formulas

For a finite type T, let Gamma_T be its graded-cell permutation group. For each unpinned C, let K_C and I_C be the kernel and image of Aut(C) on its graded cells. Then N_T = |Gamma_T| sum_C |K_C|/|Aut(C)| = sum_C |Gamma_T|/|I_C|. The manuscript's multiplication holds iff every I_C is trivial, while rigidity means every K_C is trivial.

For h=z^3(z-1)^2(z-c), let delta=25c^2-32c+16 and p=25c^3-12c^2-24c-16. The remainder upon division by z^2-(5c+4)z/6+c/2 is Pz+Q with P=-delta*p/7776 and Q=c(5c-8)(25c^3-6c^2+8)/2592. Thus P itself is quintic. On p=0, Q=(32-52c-c^2)/1500 and beta=h/Q. Three roots c_j=(4+18r_j+6r_j^2)/25, r_j^3=2, give all maps; each has moduli field Q(r_j), which is also a definition field.

For a connected clean compact holomorphic map, the complete cellwise group is Aff+(R) for the one-loop sphere; R>0 for the sphere dipole of every order n>=1; C2 for opposite-paired one-vertex one-face bouquets with 2g edges (g>=1); and trivial otherwise. The positive-genus model is y^2=x^(2g+1)-x, beta=x^(2g). Only the continuous sphere families cause nonuniqueness of the Belyi function; the C2 preserves beta already.
