\begin{abstract}
We distinguish the manuscript's polynomial-law morphisms from the
divided powers of Hom modules used to define strict polynomial functors.
The proposed identification is false. We prove the precise bridge,
the polynomial Hom-law for standard divided powers over arbitrary
rings, independence of the omitted unit axiom, and a necessary and
sufficient mixed-degree coherence repair. We determine the exact
essential image of the walking-complex construction over all
commutative rings and distinguish its nonnegative source from its
Frobenius enlargement. We reconstruct integral cochains and their
homotopy information from the full natural divided-power action on
the test functor, and separate that action from cohomology divided
powers and from binomial operations. We also prove the corrected
fibre-rank criterion, including nonregular base rings.

For cote 138 we give the orbit-size count for pinned maps, determine
all three sextic trees and their cubic fields of moduli, classify
the cellwise holomorphic automorphism exceptions, and compute the
complete Kummer images and every finite-level index for the stated
torsors. The Fermat fibres have dependent rank-two coordinates and
therefore an infinite adelic index in the rank-two affine ambient
group. Every finite counterexample has an attached exact certificate.
All claims are scoped and labelled; no priority is claimed.
\end{abstract}
