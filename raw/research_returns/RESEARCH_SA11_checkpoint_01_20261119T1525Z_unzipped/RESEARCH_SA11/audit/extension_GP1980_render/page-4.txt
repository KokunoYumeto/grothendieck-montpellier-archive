in counterclockwise order. Since in order to get from position | to position n

right, it is clear that each extreme point moves monotonically left-to-right

makes sense to talk about the direction that the extreme point is moving in at
some stage when it is not at either end of a permutation: by convention we
shall also say it is moving to the /eft when it occupies position 1 or position
n. Because of the monotonic motion in each direction, it is clear that no two

other only when two are moving in opposite directions. (Note: This

must make p — I moves to the left and n — p moves to the right by the end of
