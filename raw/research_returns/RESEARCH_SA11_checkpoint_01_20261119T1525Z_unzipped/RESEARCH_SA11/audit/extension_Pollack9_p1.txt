On the Number of Cells Defined by a
Family of Polynomials on a Variety

Saugata Basu}

Abstract

Let R be a real closed field and V a variety of real dimension k’
which is the zero set of a polynomial Q € R[Xj,...,Xx] of degree
at most d. Given a family of s polynomials P = {Pi,...,Ps} C
R[X1,..., X4] where each polynomial in P has degree at most d, we
prove that the number of cells defined by P over V is (5)(O(d))*. Note
that the combinatorial part of the bound depends on the dimension
of the variety rather than on the dimension of the ambient space.
