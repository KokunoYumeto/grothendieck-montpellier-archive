    For a degree bound b, a primary table through b consists of a generator of the full
2-primary subgroup of F∗p , when b ≥ 2, and, for each odd prime q ≤ b, the field Kq /Fp , the
element ζq of order q, and the full q-primary generator ωq constructed in Proposition 3.2.
This is precisely the data consumed by the splitting procedures. All calls in this section are
over Fp , even when the splitting procedure itself uses an extension field.
