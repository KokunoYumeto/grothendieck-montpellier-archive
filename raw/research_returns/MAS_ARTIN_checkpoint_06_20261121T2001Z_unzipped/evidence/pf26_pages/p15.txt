Lemma 4.1. The module Tt is killed by λt . The map

                             Tt −→ Tt+1 ,         (d, W ) 7−→ (d, λW )

is injective, and its image is Tt+1 [λt ]. Consequently, inside any Tm the successive modules
are precisely Tj = Tm [λj ] for 1 ≤ j ≤ m.

Proof. For a qualifying pair, multiplication by λt gives ([W ], λt W ), which is one of the
quotient relations. The displayed map respects both the qualifying equation and the relations.
If its value is zero, then
                                  (d, λW ) = ([W ′ ], λt+1 W ′ )
for some W ′ ∈ Λ. Injectivity of λ on the lattice gives W = λt W ′ , so the original pair was
already zero in Tt . Conversely, suppose that the class of (d, W ) in Tt+1 is killed by λt . There
is then a W ′ ∈ Λ such that

                               λt d = [W ′ ],      λt W = λt+1 W ′ .

The second equation gives W = λW ′ , and (d, W ) is the image of (d, W ′ ) ∈ Tt . Iteration
proves the last assertion.
