Its bit complexity, and the degrees of the rational coefficients of h in the basis 1, Y, . . . , Y q−1 ,

    The characteristic bound is a convenient sufficient condition for the scalar searches in
Lemma 2.2; the main algorithm satisfies it with considerable room. The roots of F need
not be supplied, and the norm solver does not require F to split over k. The function
automorphism ρ is the inverse of the action σ used in the divisor sections. Their powers run
over the same cyclic group, so they define the same norm.
    We first turn the norm equation into the problem of constructing a rank-one idempotent
in an algebra. We then obtain that idempotent from spaces of matrices regular at the places
of the rational function field. This use of intersections of maximal orders is closely related
to the explicit matrix-algebra algorithms of Ivanyos–Kutas–Rónyai [14]. Their general
algorithm permits a finite-field factorization oracle. Here the cyclic presentation allows the
local orders to be written explicitly, and a fiber calculation supplies the idempotent by linear
algebra. We give the construction and its bounds in full.
