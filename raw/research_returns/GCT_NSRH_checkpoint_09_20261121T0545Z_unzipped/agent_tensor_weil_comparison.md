## 2. Exact pure tensor realization of all actual degrees

Let C_pol be the E-linear category of finite-dimensional B-comodules, or the tensor subcategory generated by the actual polynomial tensor objects and their subquotients. Let W_E denote finite-dimensional E-vector spaces with an invertible linear operator, with the usual tensor-product operator.

**Proposition T2.** There is an exact faithful E-linear strong monoidal functor
\[
\mathcal R_0:C_{\rm pol}\longrightarrow W_E,\qquad
N\longmapsto (\omega N,1),
\tag{T2}
\]
where omega is the ordinary comodule forgetful functor. In the IV Hopf setting it also preserves all existing rigid duals, evaluation, coevaluation, and determinant inversion. At an arithmetic coefficient embedding every image is pure of weight zero and extends to a continuous Galois representation on a point.

**Proof.** Forgetting the coaction is exact and faithful because kernels and cokernels of comodule maps are computed on the underlying vector spaces. The bialgebra multiplication defines tensor-product coactions, and their underlying vector spaces are exactly the ordinary tensor products. The identity operators obey 1 tensor 1=1, so the ordinary associativity and unit comparisons are Weil isomorphisms satisfying the same coherence identities. Every source morphism is an intertwiner because it commutes with identity operators. In a rigid Hopf category the underlying dual is the ordinary linear dual, with its dual evaluation and coevaluation, so the same reasoning applies to both. The identity eigenvalues have modulus one in every complex embedding and are units at every finite place. QED.

This retains the entire actual degree-changing action, not merely dimensions. On the actual module X to tensor degree r the right generators are the original D P_i, and tensoring two consecutive blocks gives precisely the original block inclusion of named actions. All defining relations continue to be equalities of their original matrices. Every ordinary left operator, including those in F43 that are not themselves comodule intertwiners, is also a Weil morphism because its Frobenius commutator is zero. Every specified linear evaluation, multiplication, determinant or comodule map remains the original map. No claim that every linear map becomes a comodule map is being made.

For a fixed pure unit scalar c one may instead set F_X=c and F on a homogeneous tensor degree r equal to c^r, with inverse on dual degree. This preserves all homogeneous source maps; to include arbitrary sums and arbitrary comodule morphisms uniformly without an extra grading hypothesis, the identity construction (T2) is the unconditional version.

This result uses no general semisimplicity theorem at an algebraic parameter. It works for the actual specialized bialgebra and tensor maps whenever those coefficient maps are defined. ## 3. A nontrivial coherent pure tensor Frobenius

The identity construction can be supplemented by a genuine tensor character without retuning the deformation parameter.

Fix roots of unity zeta and eta in E. Evaluation at diagonal matrices
\[
A=\operatorname{diag}(\zeta,\zeta^{-1}),\qquad
B'=\operatorname{diag}(\eta,\eta^{-1})
\tag{T3}
\]
defines characters of the ordinary quantum GL_2 coordinate algebras. Indeed their off-diagonal matrix coefficients go to zero; the remaining defining relations are satisfied by commuting diagonal scalars, and the quantum determinants map to one. Composing their tensor product with IV Proposition 10.5 gives an algebra character chi of the actual nonstandard Hopf algebra. On the fundamental X its matrix is A tensor B'. The same construction is available directly on the FRT bialgebra because (A tensor B') to tensor degree two commutes with (T1).

For a right comodule with coaction rho, define
\[
F_N=(1\otimes\chi)\rho_N .
\tag{T4}
\]
This is a natural transformation of the underlying forgetful functor. It is monoidal because chi is multiplicative:
\[
F_{N\otimes N'}=F_N\otimes F_{N'} .
\tag{T5}
\]
In the Hopf setting its inverse uses chi composed with the antipode, and the action on a dual is the inverse transpose. On X the four eigenvalues are zeta to the plus or minus one times eta to the plus or minus one.

On the tensor category generated by X, its dual, and their subquotients, every eigenvalue is a root of unity. To check this without a generic semisimplicity assumption, choose a common order L for zeta and eta. Then F_X to power L is identity, and so is F on every tensor product, dual, stable subspace and quotient under consideration. In characteristic zero these operators are semisimple with root-of-unity eigenvalues. The resulting tensor fiber functor is therefore pure of weight zero under every embedding and gives continuous Galois representations at every coefficient prime.

The actual right nonstandard generators and all source comodule intertwiners commute with these Frobenii by naturality. The ordinary left raising and lowering operators need not commute with them. In the defining bases their exact covariance is
\[
F_XE_VF_X^{-1}=\zeta^2E_V,\quad
F_XF_VF_X^{-1}=\zeta^{-2}F_V,\quad
F_XE_WF_X^{-1}=\eta^2E_W,\quad
F_XF_WF_X^{-1}=\eta^{-2}F_W .
\tag{T6}
\]
Here E and F on a two-dimensional factor are the elementary upper and lower matrices; tensoring the actions and their coproduct preserves the same formulas. Thus, if desired, E_V is a Weil map L_{\zeta^2} tensor X to X, where L_a is the pure rank-one coefficient object with Frobenius a. The equality follows directly from F_X E_V=(zeta squared) E_V F_X. The other operators have the corresponding coefficient lines. The identity choice of (T2) retains these operators as ordinary invariant endomorphisms instead.

No claim is made that such a character is the Cayley operator of F46, the arithmetic Frobenius of a finite-field geometric source, or the formal grading substitution v=Q to an extension-dependent power. It is a coherent pure tensor structure on the actual fixed-parameter family.

## 4. The full-centralizer comparison already fails in degrees one and two

First verify the actual low-degree endomorphisms, rather than assume an abstract example.

**Lemma T7.** For the polynomial FRT comodule category,
\[
\operatorname{End}_{B}(X)=E,\qquad
\operatorname{End}_{B}(X^{\otimes2})=E\,1\oplus E\,P.
\tag{T7}
\]

**Proof.** There are no relations in coordinate degree one. Its dual acts on X as the full algebra End_E(X), whose commutant is E. In degree two the only relations are precisely the linear entries of P U-U P, where U denotes the matrix of the universal tensor-square corepresentation. Taking the degree-two annihilator identifies the acting coordinate dual with
\[
\{a\in\operatorname{End}_E(X^{\otimes2}):aP=Pa\}
 =\operatorname{End}(\operatorname{im}P)
   \oplus\operatorname{End}(\ker P).
\tag{T8}
\]
This is also the degree-two instance of the source proof of Theorem 12.1. Taking its commutant gives independent scalars on the two nonzero blocks of ranks 10 and 6, which is exactly the right-hand side of (T7). QED.

For the actual IV Hopf category the simpler facts needed below still hold: End_H(X)=E and End_H(X tensor X) contains independent 1 and P. The first follows from the ordinary quantum-product restriction in Proposition 10.5: the elementary raising and lowering matrices on each fundamental factor generate all four-by-four matrix units on X, so a comodule endomorphism commuting with that action is scalar. The second is the defining FRT intertwining relation, which remains true after determinant inversion. The obstruction below needs only these facts and does not silently assume that arbitrary Hopf localization preserves every polynomial Hom space. Exact dimension two in (T7) is proved for the polynomial bialgebra category.

**Theorem T9.** Neither of these actual categories admits a fully faithful E-linear strong monoidal functor to finite-dimensional Weil representations on a point. More narrowly, no strong monoidal assignment X to V can identify both End(X) and End(X tensor X) with their full Frobenius centralizers.

**Proof.** Let F be the invertible operator on the nonzero image V of X. Fullness in degree one says End_F(V)=E. Since F itself belongs to this endomorphism algebra, F=c times the identity for some nonzero c. Its full centralizer is then End_E(V), so dim(V)=1. Strong monoidality makes the image of X tensor X the one-dimensional V tensor V, whose centralizer also has dimension one. But the source endomorphism algebra contains the linearly independent operators 1 and P, contradicting faithfulness and fullness in degree two. QED.

There is a corresponding statement directly for auxiliary centralizer objects H_r: if H_{r+s} is isomorphic as a Weil object to H_r tensor H_s, if the degree-one algebra is E, and if each algebra is required to be the entire End_F(H_r), the degree-two nonstandard algebra cannot have dimension two. F46 succeeds because its rank-six H is an auxiliary degree-three presentation object, not the third tensor power of a one-dimensional full-centralizer degree-one object.

This is an obstruction to full recovery from finite point-Weil centralizers. It does not exclude faithful tensor actions, subalgebras of centralizers, additional algebra or comodule decorations, geometric objects with more morphisms, or other target categories. In particular it does not conflict with (T2)–(T6), which are faithful and preserve all specified source maps without being full.

## 5. Exact rational extension-law classification

The following is a sharp specialization of the rational dilation result in F26; it is not being presented as an unrelated general theorem.

**Theorem T10.** Let b be a fixed real number greater than one. Let f be a nonzero rational function over C, assume f is defined and nonzero at b, and suppose
\[
f(b^n)=f(b)^n
\tag{T9}
\]
for every sufficiently large integer n, at which the left side is defined. Then
\[
f(x)=x^m
\tag{T10}
\]
for an integer m. Conversely every such monomial satisfies (T9).

**Proof.** The Laurent expansion at infinity of a nonzero rational function has the form
\[
f(x)=a x^m(1+O(x^{-1})),\qquad a\ne0,\quad m\in\mathbf Z.
\]
Consequently f(b^{n+1})/f(b^n) tends to b^m. By (T9) the same ratio is identically f(b) for sufficiently large n. Thus f(b)=b^m. Substituting back gives f(b^n)=b^{mn} for infinitely many distinct positive values of b^n. The rational function f(x)-x^m has infinitely many zeros and is identically zero. The converse is direct. QED.

The theorem requires only one fixed b, and eventual equality is enough. No positivity, integrality or reality assumption on the coefficients of f was used. In particular no extra nontrivial constant phase is allowed by the all-extension law: a constant zeta would have to equal zeta^n for all sufficiently large n, forcing zeta=1.

Set b=Q^{1/2}, with Q a fixed prime power. A rational eigenvalue family compatible with retuning the parameter under every finite extension is therefore v^m. Under the normalization v=Q^{1/2}, its eigenvalue has weight m. If it is required to have weight zero, m=0 and f=1. Under the inverse convention v=Q^{-1/2}, apply the theorem to g(x)=f(x^{-1}); the same conclusion holds for weight zero and the weight sign is reversed for general monomials.

The bar-reciprocal relation f(v^{-1})=f(v)^{-1} imposes no further restriction on these monomials; they already satisfy it. A nonconstant rational Cayley phase family cannot satisfy both the extension retuning law and weight zero. This excludes any rational replacement of that form, rather than only the particular F46 parameter Delta.

This result concerns a rationally labeled eigenvalue family with the stated branch equality. It does not assume, or prove, that every eigenvalue of a larger geometric realization has such a label. In particular it does not classify multivalued algebraic branches without a specified continuation, and it does not prohibit fixed roots of unity when no parameter-retuning rule is imposed.

## 6. What gate remains after these constructions

The existence of a coherent finite pure tensor family carrying the actual fixed-parameter action is now explicit, including all tensor degrees and the IV rigid dual maps. It is therefore too strong to describe the remaining problem as absence of any pure tensor realization.

The stronger full-centralizer dictionary is impossible in the stated point-Weil target, by the actual degree-one/degree-two calculation. A usable comparison must instead retain the nonstandard algebra as a specified subalgebra of an endomorphism algebra, or carry extra geometric or categorical structure that distinguishes the relevant morphisms. Fullness after forgetting that extra structure cannot be demanded.

For a sign-to-weight route one still needs the all-extension trace comparison with the specified quantum grading and the opposite-sign output required by F24. The identity and root-of-unity tensor structures do not supply it. The rational classification proves that a nonconstant rational pure-phase retuning cannot repair this particular comparison. VIII's prescribed integral and infinity lattices, bar globalization and basis selection are also not determined by the existence of these fiber functors; no part of (T2)–(T10) changes that previously established distinction.
