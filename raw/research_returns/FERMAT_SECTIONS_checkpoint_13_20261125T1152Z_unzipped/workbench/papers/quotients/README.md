# Fermat Selmer sections in solvable quotients

This editable standalone LaTeX project contains the companion paper, exact certificates and their outputs. Compile using BUILD.md; main.tex is the entry point and main.pdf is the current rendered paper. DEPOSIT.md records scope and provenance.

The all-prime finite-derived/metabelian/prosolvable theorem covers odd exponents divisible by 3, 5, 7, 11, 13 or 19. The unrestricted affine pro-three theorem covers 3^r, 5·3^r and 7·3^r for r≥1. Compact localization has additional base and regular-prime cases; at base 5/7 the entire compact pro-three section is the image of a rational cusp. A hypothetical noncuspidal prosolvable section at any odd n≥5 has jointly open two-adic and three-adic Frey images. The remaining affine boundary and simultaneous-open-image problems are explicitly unresolved.

The manuscript gives full proofs of its deductions, exact primary citations, and distinguishes independent statement-only model reconstructions from exposed audits. No human verification or priority claim is made. Original text and programs are CC0 1.0; cited literature is not redistributed.

## Regular-prime extension

The current source proves integral nonabelian localization for every odd regular prime and classifies the geometric pro-17 Selmer sections for exponents 17^r, including the stated stable, finite-derived and ordinary lower-central quotients. It proves no new all-prime classification at seventeen and no additional pro-3 family. The new proof has producer and whole targeted checks; its statement-only reconstruction is pending.
