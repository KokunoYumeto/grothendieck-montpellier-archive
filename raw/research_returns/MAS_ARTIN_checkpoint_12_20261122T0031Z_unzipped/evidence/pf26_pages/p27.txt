6.2   Why one fiber contains a rank-one matrix
Define the finite-dimensional k-algebra of global sections and its evaluation image by

                S = {a ∈ E : a ∈ Av for every v},          B = ev∞ (S) ⊆ Mq (k).           (6.9)

We will compute both spaces by linear equations. First we establish the feature of B that
will make this useful: up to change of basis it is the algebra of all block upper triangular
matrices. Its first block therefore contains the image of a rank-one idempotent.
