3     Auxiliary primes and primary generators
The splitting procedure needs explicit generators of small-prime parts of multiplicative
groups. This section constructs those generators from the auxiliary primes supplied in
Theorem 1.2. The construction uses linear algebra in cyclotomic algebras and calls the
factorization procedure only in strictly smaller degrees. Existence of small auxiliary primes
is a separate number-theoretic question; no size bound for them is assumed in this section.
    We use the notation vq (a) and the full q-primary subgroup introduced in Section 2.
    Throughout the construction, p > n ≥ 2. For each prime q ≤ n, recall that the supplied
