# MO014–015: evaluation with the actual ideal projection retained

## Results and scope

`projected_ideal_gram.tex` proves actual S1 maps between every pair of polynomial-weight ideal completions in the MO012 regimes. Bounded inclusions and a common nuclear pairing prove equality of the resulting traces and Fredholm determinants. This is not an identification of the orthogonal projectors.

For the two CGP character families, the exact sum-metric closed theta map gives a finite-band normal-equation transport of the completed-ideal trace. The unregularized source map is not asserted bounded. Compact output localization supplies the explicit finite kernel MO221 and bounded K; only the regularized polar maps are used in the cyclic trace.

The finite error certificate MO226 uses actual theta Gram projections and orthogonal finite retained-jet Riesz projections. It is valid with any finite set of certified jets, without assuming no further zeros. Exhaustive spans prove convergence. The Hermite seminorm majorant MO227 bounds the nuclear tail. Generic-width existence does not itself give an effective distance constant for an arbitrary chosen numerical width.

`projected_ideal_blaschke.tex` retains the actual Hardy projector as U_B Pi_diag U_B*. The two diagonal blocks of Pi_diag are logistic multipliers with the exact inherited sum norm. Their positive Fourier transform gives the plus i/(8a) odd-difference correction, and the zero-frequency delta gives half of each unrestricted one-sided trace. Boundary Blaschke derivatives have polynomial bounds in precisely the MO012 regimes, so the correction is absolutely integrable.

## Limits

These formulas evaluate the actual completed-ideal endomorphism trace. They do not by themselves identify it with Meyer's canonical positive-source trace or the full smooth zero-quotient trace. The later MA-MO-017 theorem now proves that genuine nuclear return with its actual source topology and domains, and evaluates the completed-ideal trace. MA-MO-019 then proves equality with both actual one-sided traces and cancellation of the explicit MO015 correction. The projector is retained: the assembled unrestricted two-block trace is twice the ideal trace, and a compact Gevrey test makes the value nonzero. The FT002 omitted-divisor condition remains unchanged; these are positive-source trace comparisons, not a full zero-quotient identity.
