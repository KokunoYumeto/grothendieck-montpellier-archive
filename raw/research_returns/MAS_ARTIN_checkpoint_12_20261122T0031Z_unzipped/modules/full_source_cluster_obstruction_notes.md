## Actual source construction

## Conditional cluster and limitations

A cluster of k_n zeros in the disk of radius d_n/2 centered at a−d_n+iT_n forces failure when (k_n−p)log(1/d_n)/log(2+|T_n|) tends to infinity. The disk Blaschke upper estimate is proved directly. Fixed k>p and superpolynomially small d_n suffice; growing k_n and suitably small polynomial d_n also suffice. Such a configuration is not asserted for any actual Hecke function. It is compatible with the retention of those individual interior factors, but no theorem here decides whether the arithmetic hypotheses can occur.
