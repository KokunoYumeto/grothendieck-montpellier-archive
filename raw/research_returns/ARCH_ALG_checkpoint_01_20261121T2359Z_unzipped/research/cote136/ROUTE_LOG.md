# Integral route log

All entries below distinguish a checked obstruction to a named construction from an answer to connectedness.

## Established improvements in this run

1. **Homotopy calculus.** Finite oriented path cotensors preserve admissibility. The cospan theorem supplies Ore squares, and a path pullback supplies cancellation after passing to the congruence generated by strict simplicial homotopies. This proves the specified equalization property over semilocal rings and identifies the exact integral fundamental-group gate.
2. **Arithmetic finite gluing.** Replace the tensor of all rational local comparison models by one rational B_N. This removes the sum of N_i, leaves (q+1)max N_i in horizontal cutoffs, and makes the fixed-degree vertical cutoff independent of the prime list. The local standard models still obstruct passing to all primes with one zero-form cutoff.
3. **Pure horizontal cells.** Omitting the inherited relative H1 repairs yields simplexwise tensor products of free vertical unit disks. ARCH-136-DISK-SUPPORT proves a uniform annihilator L_d and exactness through degree3. This is a strictly stronger partial source than the previously unspecified higher torsion statement.
4. **Inverse refinement.** ARCH-136-LOW-REFINEMENT excludes a map from an admissible refinement that lifts the degree-one generator in a specified free unit-disk partial source. It does not exclude forward repair or a replacement changing low generators.

## Routes examined without a completed theorem

- **Naive vertical truncation gluing.** Pulling a low-degree replacement back along a brutal quotient C/C^{>d} retains the original high terms, but the seam can create H^d and H^{d+1}; a compatible differential-image condition is missing. No prime-uniform completion follows.
- **Ordinary or derived tensor target.** The inherited AQ-136-TENSOR obstruction is horizontal Tor over Gamma(T), even for flat examples. Replacing module tensor by derived tensor does not remove it; no new proof of this accepted result is claimed.
- **High-form cell killing.** Adjoining a primitive in degree d-1 kills a torsion degree-d class but can create lower rational cohomology. Simultaneously imposing its torsion relation introduces coherent primitive choices. AQ-136-HIGHER-COMPLETION shows that an unrestricted relative-completion lemma is false. The pure unit-disk source is a stricter case under investigation.
- **Degreewise prime repair.** Only primes at most d/2 occur in degree d in the free source. This suggests repairing one bounded degree at a time. A compatible tower cannot simply be assumed: inverse refinement preserving low generators is explicitly obstructed. The forward direction and changes in low-degree maps remain active.
- **Naive eta_p lattice.** A candidate requiring dx divisible by p on zero forms threatens the coefficient boundary in the zero-form horizontal row. An exact admissibility proof has not been supplied, and this candidate is not used as a result.
- **Product invariants.** The derived coefficient comparison is uniform in modulus in each fixed degree. Packaging linear classes at infinitely many primes does not by itself produce a component invariant. Unbounded-arity multiplicative defining systems are being examined separately.
