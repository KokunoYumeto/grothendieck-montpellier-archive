\label{sec:gl2-minus7-higher4}

\tag{G7H4.1}

Every displayed residue has gcd one with its modulus.
The full dimensions are \(101\) and \(109\), respectively.
No primality or squarefreeness assertion about a modulus is used.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The uniform full integral cusp basis of
Proposition~\ref{prop:minus7-gl2-generic} has size \(d=4e-3\):
\[
 DA^{m-j}B^j\ (0\le j\le m),\qquad
 DB^{m-j}C^j\ (1\le j\le m),\qquad m=2e-2.
\]
The first \(d\) positive Fourier coefficients form a unit lower
triangular matrix. Thus they determine the exact integral coordinates
of every Hecke image; no independent-family approximation is involved.
The coefficient tables of the three integral weight-one generators,
the relation \(AC=B^2\), the integral eta-product \(D\), and the
complete diamond action are precisely those in the generic proposition.

The new wrapper
\path{hecke_minus7/higher4/gl2_twist_residual_certificate.py}
uses exactly the same arithmetic function as the earlier five-case
certificate. Equality of the complete function's abstract syntax trees
has been checked. Its new wrapper reads the exact residual integers
from the support certificate and records their source hash.
For each of the four new pairs it reconstructs all series, the entire
basis, all diamond images, and the full Hecke matrix over
\(\mathbf Z/M_{e,i}\mathbf Z\). It retains the Hecke correction
\(2^{2e}[Q^{n/2}]\langle2\rangle F\) when \(n\) is even.
The series precisions are \(218\) and \(234\), and every coefficient
through \(109\) and \(117\), respectively, is checked after the
coordinates are reconstructed.

Exact matrix multiplication produces \(T_2^3\). Determinant
elimination uses only pivots whose gcd with the modulus is one,
with every modular inverse independently checked by multiplication.
Every pivot in all four calculations is a unit; direct integer gcds
of the final residues with their moduli are one. The two degree-specific
JSON reports preserve the complete identifying matrices, raw Hecke
coefficient columns, full Hecke matrices, every determinant pivot,
and the residues (G7H4.2). These are actual full executions of the
new cases. The proof of the generic proposition then gives unit
trace differences for every eigenform and every cubic-root twist
at every prime divisor of the corresponding modulus.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scope.}
This proposition concerns the complete classical spaces only.
Its arithmetic use also requires the separately checked full unitary
comparison and the generic source and conductor interface.
