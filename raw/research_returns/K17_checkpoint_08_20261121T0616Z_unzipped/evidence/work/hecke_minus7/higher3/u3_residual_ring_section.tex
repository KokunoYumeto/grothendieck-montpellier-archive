\subsection{Unitary comparisons modulo residual integers}
\label{sec:u3-residual-ring}

\begin{lemma}[K17-U3-RESIDUAL-RING]\label{lem:u3-residual-ring}
Retain the complete two-class group and level over
$E=\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-7})$ in Theorem~\ref{thm:u3-minus7-unit}.
Let $k\ge1$, $e=k+1$, and $M>1$ satisfy
\[
 \gcd\bigl(M,336(2k+1)!\bigr)=1,
 \qquad \gcd(M,E_e)=1,
 \qquad E_e=1+2^e+2^{2e}.
 \tag{UR.1}
\]
Work in the finite quadratic algebra
\[
 B_M=(\mathbf Z/M\mathbf Z)[\omega]/(\omega^2-\omega+2).
 \tag{UR.2}
\]
Write $\mathcal A_k$ for the entire algebraic modular-form space
of highest weight $(k,0,-k)$ at this group and level.
Use the full harmonic kernel of the signed-permutation orbit sums
in bidegree $(k,k)$, and the same six norm-two and seven norm-four
frames as in (U7.7)--(U7.8). Let $A_0$ be an identifying evaluation
matrix for a basis of this kernel, and let $B_2,B_4$ be the sums of
the substituted evaluations $f(U^*z,U^tw)$ and $f(V^*z,V^tw)$.
If
\[
 \det A_0\in B_M^\times,
 \qquad
 \det\bigl(E_e^2A_0-E_eB_2-B_4\bigr)\in B_M^\times,
 \tag{UR.3}
\]
then, for every prime $\ell\mid M$ and every coefficient place
above $\ell$, the full two-class comparison
\[
 \det\bigl(E_e I-2^{e-1}T_2^+
       \mid\mathcal A_{e-1}\bigr)
 \tag{UR.4}
\]
is a unit. The same holds in the opposite minuscule direction.
Neither primality nor squarefreeness of $M$ is required.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The adelic class set, both stabilizers, their inclusion, and every
actual coset are independent of $k$ and $M$. In particular the full
operator remains
\[
 T_2^+=\begin{pmatrix}S_{11}&\operatorname{inc}\\S_{21}&0\end{pmatrix}
 \quad\text{on }\mathcal H_k^{\Gamma_1}
                    \oplus\mathcal H_k^{\Gamma_2}.
 \tag{UR.5}
\]
All matrices have only powers of $2$ in their denominators. The
right inverse (U7.11) for the harmonic contraction has denominator
primes at most $2k+1$. Therefore it is defined over $B_M$ and over
the localization of $O_E$ at every coefficient place above a prime
dividing $M$. The group orders $48$ and $336$ are units there.
The contraction kernel and both finite-group invariant modules
thus commute with residue-field reduction, exactly as in the
proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:u3-minus7-unit}.

We make explicit why a saved basis modulo a possibly composite
integer is sufficient. The full invariant source and target have
the disjoint-support orbit bases (U7.14), of ranks $n_k,n_{k-1}$.
The contraction is split surjective. Suppose $d=n_k-n_{k-1}$
saved columns are killed by it and have a $d$ by $d$ identity
minor on distinct coordinates. At every maximal ideal of $B_M$
their reductions are independent; the residue-field kernel has
dimension $d$, so those reductions form its entire basis. The
identity-coordinate projection splits the inclusion of their
span into the kernel. The complementary finitely generated
module has zero residue at every maximal ideal and is therefore
zero by Nakayama. Hence the saved columns form the whole kernel
over $B_M$, and their images at every coefficient place are the
whole reduced characteristic-zero invariant space. This argument
does not treat $B_M$ as a field.

The norm of $a+b\omega$ is $a^2+ab+2b^2$. In the finite algebra
(UR.2),
\[
 a+b\omega\text{ is a unit}
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \gcd(a^2+ab+2b^2,M)=1.
 \tag{UR.6}
\]
For the forward implication apply conjugation and multiply.
If this scalar norm is a unit in $B_M$, the constant coefficient
of its inverse equation makes it a unit in $\mathbf Z/M\mathbf Z$.
For the converse multiply the conjugate by the inverse of the norm.
This proves the unit test simultaneously at all prime divisors,
including both factors when $\ell$ splits in $E$.

By (UR.1), the lower-right scalar pivot $E_e$ in the full
comparison is a unit. Block elimination gives the equivalent
endomorphism
\[
 D_e=E_e^2I-E_e\,2^kS_{11}
                  -2^{2k}\operatorname{inc}S_{21}
 \tag{UR.7}
\]
on the first invariant module. The denominator cancellation
(U7.16) remains valid in every weight, so its evaluation matrix
is precisely $E_e^2A_0-E_eB_2-B_4$. The first condition in
(UR.3) identifies the entire endomorphism; the second makes it
invertible. Reduction at each coefficient place and the integral
lattice argument prove (UR.4). The full-space conjugation
involution from Theorem~\ref{thm:u3-minus7-unit} identifies the
opposite direction integrally at these primes as well.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[K17-U3-MINUS7-HIGHER3]\label{prop:u3-minus7-higher3}
The unit conclusion of Lemma~\ref{lem:u3-residual-ring} holds for
the following pairs $(e,M)$:
\[
 \begin{array}{c|l}
 e&M\\ \hline
 20&9496386083\\
 20&552972895573825165951583191\\
 22&80348736972167\\
 22&2789260013036301774436972122687974510453\\
 24&3369865772726176603506574274451733385689247.
 \end{array}
 \tag{UR.8}
\]
Each assertion covers every prime divisor of the indicated
integer. No claim that the larger integers are prime is used.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Here are the complete invariant-space counts:
\[
 \begin{array}{c|r|r|r}
 k&n_k&n_{k-1}&d\\ \hline
 19&1875&1542&333\\
 21&2713&2269&444\\
 23&3804&3228&576.
 \end{array}
 \tag{UR.9}
\]
The enumeration runs over all pairs of three-part
compositions, with coordinate differences even and simultaneous
permutation orbits. It includes every signed-permutation
invariant monomial.

For the five rows of (UR.8), in their displayed order, the exact
residues of $\det D_e$ in $B_M$ have zero $\omega$-coefficient
and constant coefficient
\[
 \begin{array}{c|r}
 \text{row}&\det D_e\bmod M\\ \hline
 1&1026900898\\
 2&514586621306233221634475202\\
 3&38390168543202\\
 4&875682305527210214142517183562570591024\\
 5&445305974135918810894106170147365896254386.
 \end{array}
 \tag{UR.10}
\]
In each case the norm is coprime to $M$, and the evaluation
determinant is a unit. The exact unreduced comparison point is
$E_e=1+2^e+2^{2e}$; the computation uses its residue modulo $M$.
All the coprimality conditions in (UR.1) are checked exactly.

For reproducibility, the files are under
\path{work/hecke_minus7/higher3/}. The preparation program
\path{prepare_ring_model.py} produces a separate
\path{e20_m...}, \path{e22_m...}, or \path{e24_m...} directory for each row,
containing \path{model.json} and \path{model.txt}. Its row
reduction uses only pivots coprime to $M$. Every resulting
column is checked against the full contraction. The producers
\path{compute_ring_schur.cpp} and its optimized version
\path{compute_ring_schur_fast.cpp} evaluate the complete polynomial
space and compute the two determinants, using the same exact
quadratic-algebra arithmetic. Their larger-integer
versions use exact GMP integers; the smaller cases use
64-bit residues and 128-bit products. It uses extended-Euclidean
inversion and checks every pivot norm is coprime to $M$.
A final nonunit diagonal entry is multiplied without inversion;
an earlier failure to find a unit pivot is reported as
inconclusive, never as a field-rank assertion.

The independent standard-library program
\path{verify_ring_schur.py} does not import the producer or its
row reduction. For each case it reconstructs all thirteen frame
classes, every invariant orbit, the entire contraction kernel,
and its identity-coordinate minor, rejecting duplicate sparse
indices. It checks the complete text--JSON agreement and
recomputes every polynomial evaluation at all $d$ identifying
points for all thirteen frames. It then computes both determinants
by column elimination, using only norm-unit pivots and exact
integer arithmetic. Agreement with the producer and the norm
gcds are recorded in the case's \path{verify_ring_schur.json}.
The model stores two additional points for possible diagnostics;
this higher-weight certificate uses exactly the $d$ identifying
points and makes no additional-point verification claim.
Lemma~\ref{lem:u3-residual-ring} now applies to every listed row.
\end{proof}
