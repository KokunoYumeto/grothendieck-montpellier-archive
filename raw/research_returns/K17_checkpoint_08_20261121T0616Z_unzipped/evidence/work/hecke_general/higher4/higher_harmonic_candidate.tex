\subsection{The next complete level-three Hecke models}
\label{sec:u3-higher4}

\begin{proposition}[K17-HECKE-HIGHER4]
\label{prop:hecke-higher4}
For \(e=26,28\) and \(p=7,13\), use the full models of
Theorem~\ref{thm:u3-general} and Proposition~\ref{prop:gl2-general}.
Let \(P_{e,p}\) be the characteristic polynomial of
\(p^{e-1}T_p^+\) on the entire compact-unitary coefficient space
of highest weight \((e-1,0,1-e)\), and let \(Q_{e,p}\) be the
characteristic polynomial of the usual \(T_p\) on the entire
\(S_{2e+1}(\Gamma_0(3),\chi_{-3})\). The dimensions are
\[
\begin{array}{c|r|r|r|c|r}
e&\dim V_{e-1}^{\Gamma}&\dim V_{e-2}^{\Gamma}&
\dim\mathcal H_{e-1}^{\Gamma}&\text{swap }(+,-)&\dim S_{2e+1}\\\hline
26&613&529&84&(44,40)&16\\
28&815&710&105&(55,50)&18.
\end{array}
\tag{HH4.1}
\]
Put
\[
 N_{e,p}=P_{e,p}(1+p^e+p^{2e})\,Q_{e,p}(1+p^{2e}).
 \tag{HH4.2}
\]
For the exact residual integers \(M_{e,i}\) in (G7H4.1),
\[
\begin{aligned}
\gcd(N_{26,7},N_{26,13})
 &=2^{450}3^{456}5^{42}11^8\,79^2\,3461\,16759\,
       M_{26,1}M_{26,2}^3,\\
\gcd(N_{28,7},N_{28,13})
 &=2^{487}3^{566}5^{12}19^4\,23^3\,29\,53\,15083\,
       M_{28,1}M_{28,2}^3.
\end{aligned}
\tag{HH4.3}
\]
Every other displayed factor is a proved prime below \(2^{31}\).
No primality or squarefreeness of the \(M_{e,i}\) is asserted.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The full harmonic model is uniform in \(k=e-1\).
A simultaneous permutation orbit of bidegree-\((k,k)\) monomials
is invariant under the diagonal sixth roots of unity precisely when
\[
 |\alpha|=|\beta|=k,\qquad
 \alpha_i-\beta_i\equiv0\pmod6\quad(i=1,2,3).
 \tag{HH4.4}
\]
Every such orbit is included. In these degrees this includes
all differences \(0,\pm6,\pm12,\pm18,\pm24\).
Direct exact enumeration gives the source and target counts in
(HH4.1). The complete contraction matrix is reconstructed by
applying \(\sum_i\partial_{z_i}\partial_{w_i}\) to every orbit sum.
The verifier proves its full target rank, verifies every saved
harmonic column is in the kernel, and proves independence of
those columns. The dimension difference proves that this is the
entire harmonic space. It also checks the action exchanging the
two variable triples, giving the recorded swap dimensions.

The neighbor sets are the complete \(57\) and \(183\) cosets
already constructed at \(7\) and \(13\). Their replay checks every
Hermitian Gram matrix and every residue line, including exact
equality of the distinct lines with \(\mathbf P^2(\mathbf F_p)\).
For every basis vector and every neighbor \(U/\pi\), the scaled
action is exactly
\[
 p^{e-1}\rho(U/\pi)h(z,w)=h(U^*z,U^tw).
 \tag{HH4.5}
\]
The full integer evaluation matrix is independently reconstructed
and its exact determinant proved nonzero. Every transformed value
at every neighbor is recomputed, and both component identities
\(AM_0=B_0\) and \(AM_1=B_1\) are checked over \(\mathbf Q\).
The full operator is therefore identified, column by column.
The coefficient equations are also checked at two additional
point pairs for each auxiliary prime.

The same variable swap exchanges the two minuscule directions.
The exact change multiplying the swap-minus basis by \(1+2\omega\)
is verified entry by entry, giving the full rational form of the
operator. The scaled action preserves the integral invariant
harmonic lattice, so its rational characteristic polynomial has
integer coefficients. Independently the complete polynomial is
recomputed by exact Faddeev--LeVerrier recursion, retaining the
terminal Cayley--Hamilton zero matrix. The verified factor degree
patterns at both primes are \(4+8+72\) for \(e=26\) and
\(4+9+92\) for \(e=28\). Only exact multiplication of those
factors is used; their irreducibility is not needed.

The full classical basis is
\[
 Da^{2e-5-3j}b^j,
 \qquad 0\le j\le\lfloor(2e-5)/3\rfloor,
 \tag{HH4.6}
\]
by Proposition~\ref{prop:gl2-general}. The successive leading
Fourier orders give a unit triangular identifying matrix and the
dimensions \(16,18\). The full certificate reconstructs the series
to precision \(260\), which exceeds all required identifying
indices. It uses the complete Hecke coefficient formula, including
\(\chi_{-3}(p)p^{2e}a_{n/p}\) at indices divisible by \(p\).
After reconstruction it checks every coefficient through \(37\)
at \(7\) and through \(20\) at \(13\). The whole characteristic
polynomial is verified by exact Faddeev recursion and by separate
fraction-free determinant evaluations at \(0,1,1+p^{2e}\).

The individual complete unitary data are
\path{hecke_general/higher4/harmonic_hecke_e26_exact_data.json}
and \path{hecke_general/higher4/harmonic_hecke_e28_exact_data.json}.
The full classical data are
\path{hecke_general/higher4/gl2_higher4_certificate.json}.
The unchanged standard-library unitary verifier and its isolated
exact optimization wrappers retain every check described above.
The algebraic equivalence of those wrappers is established in
Section~\ref{sec:higher4-replay-equivalence}.

Finally, the collector evaluates the complete two-branch products
at both primes. An independent support verifier evaluates the
polynomials by direct power sums, computes the gcd of the whole
products, proves every declared small prime by trial division,
and checks exact multiplication of all the factors in (HH4.3).
It also verifies the separate repeated-gcd support certificate of
Lemma~\ref{lem:comparison-support-gcd}. Thus the prime support is
complete even without factoring either residual integer. The
files are \path{hecke_general/higher4/collect_higher.py} and
\path{hecke_general/higher4/verify_residual_support.py}, with the
individual support JSON and full replay reports.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Arithmetic scope.}
Under the generic source and trace interface, a coefficient prime
greater than \(2^{31}\) outside these residual supports is
eliminated by at least one of the two auxiliary primes. The gcd
uses the product of both complete branches, so that the same
auxiliary prime gives units for both. Primes dividing a residual
integer require the separate complete level-seven comparisons.
