\begin{proposition}[K17-HECKE-HIGHER2-GL2]
\label{prop:hecke-higher2-gl2-derivation}
For $e=14,16,18$, the full classical spaces
$S_{2e+1}(\Gamma_0(3),\chi_{-3})$ have dimensions $8,10,11$,
respectively. In each case the integral basis is
\[
 Da^{\,2e-5-3j}b^j,\qquad 0\le j\le\lfloor(2e-5)/3\rfloor.
\]
Let $N_{e,p}$ be the combined comparison integer defined in (G6),
using the entire compact coefficient space and the entire classical
space. The exact characteristic polynomials at $p=7,13$ in the
separate full-model certificates give the following gcds:
\begin{align*}
\gcd(N_{14,7},N_{14,13})
 &=2^{106}3^{101}5^7\,53^3\,419\,16519\\
 &\quad{}\cdot15083^3\,7547263^3\,42800473^3,\\
\gcd(N_{16,7},N_{16,13})
 &=2^{123}3^{147}5^4\,11^4\,23\,401\,13687\\
 &\quad{}\cdot36293^3\,38177^3\,25078104767246023^3,\\
\gcd(N_{18,7},N_{18,13})
 &=2^{177}3^{180}5^{17}\,7\,13^8\,19^2\,37^2\,73\\
 &\quad{}\cdot3343^3\,7691\,8609\,18541^3\,151871^3
                  \,8361790201^3.
\end{align*}
Every displayed factor is prime. The only factors at least $2^{31}$
are $25078104767246023$ in the middle row and $8361790201$ in the
last row.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The complete space and integral-basis assertion is the uniform
Proposition~\ref{prop:gl2-general}; substituting $e$ gives the stated
dimensions. The exact extension program
\texttt{higher2/gl2\_higher2\_certificate.py} reuses only the audited
integer series and matrix routines, not a stored operator.
It expands the basis modulo $q^{261}$ and applies (G4)--(G5)
to every basis vector.
The largest identifying coefficient index is $13\cdot11=143$,
below the chosen precision $260$.
All identifying correction terms at $n=7$ are included;
additional comparisons run through $n=37$ for $p=7$ and $n=20$
for $p=13$, including their nonzero correction terms.
The lower triangular coefficient matrix has diagonal one, so these
are the full Hecke operators. The complete matrices, raw coefficient
rows, monic integral characteristic polynomials, and evaluations are
stored in the accompanying JSON.

For each matrix the program checks the exact
Faddeev--LeVerrier divisions and terminal zero matrix, and verifies
the characteristic polynomial independently by fraction-free
determinants at $0$, $1$, and $1+p^{2e}$.
The separate compact-space certificate reconstructs the entire
scaled $U(3)$ models, of dimensions $14,21,27$, at the same two
primes. The combination program
\texttt{higher2/combined\_higher2\_certificate.py} reads the full
polynomials of both models, evaluates them at
$1+p^e+p^{2e}$ and $1+p^{2e}$, checks agreement with the model
outputs, and applies the integer Euclidean algorithm to the two
products. Exact multiplication checks the displayed factorizations.

All factors except the largest in the middle row are proved prime
by trial division. For that remaining integer $r$, the certificate
verifies
\[
 r=25078104767246023,\qquad
 r-1=2\cdot3^3\cdot10487\cdot44284289839,
\]
proves each displayed factor of $r-1$ prime by trial division, and
checks
\[
 3^{r-1}=1\pmod r,\qquad
 3^{(r-1)/q}\ne1\pmod r
 \quad(q=2,3,10487,44284289839).
\]
The element $3$ consequently has order exactly $r-1$ in
$(\mathbf Z/r\mathbf Z)^\times$. Thus $\varphi(r)\ge r-1$,
which forces $r$ to be prime.
This is a complete order certificate, not a probable-prime test.
The output records hashes of both frozen full-model inputs.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scope of this computation.}
The first row has no prime outside the published small-prime range.
The second and third rows each isolate one further prime; they do not
show a nonzero Selmer group or algebraic $K$-group there.
By Lemma~\ref{lem:isolated-reflection}, a sufficient additional
condition at the middle-row prime $r$ is
$B_{r-17}\not\equiv0\pmod r$.
The separate calculation below treats the last-row prime.
The source admissibility and unit criterion are the uniform
Corollaries~\ref{cor:u3-general-coverage} and
\ref{cor:general-hecke-unit}, not an identification with the source's
larger characteristic product.
