# The actual cubic companion as a specified triangular lifting obstruction

Status: complete algebraic construction and author proof; independent audit requested. **No arithmetic nonvanishing theorem is asserted.** This note identifies the exact object a higher-reciprocity theorem would have to detect. It does not replace it by an arbitrary ordinary triple Massey product.

## 1. Actual modules and coefficient field

Use the actual weighted logarithmic construction of K17-CUBIC-ODD. The weights of the compact free basis are 1 on Q, 2 on T^+, and 3 on T^-. Let R=Z_p[[Gamma]][1/p], and write D for the gamma operator. The assigned-degree-two module is the actual extension

\[
0\longrightarrow N=\widehat{\bigwedge}^{2}Q[1/p]
\longrightarrow E_2\longrightarrow T^+[1/p]\longrightarrow0.
\]

It is the even extension already constructed in K17-UNIVERSAL-PAIR. In degree three the actual source has

\[
0\longrightarrow W_3\longrightarrow E_3\longrightarrow T^-[1/p]\longrightarrow0,
\qquad W_3=L_3(Q[1/p])\oplus[Q[1/p],T^+[1/p]]
\]

as completed vector spaces. The direct sum description is not asserted to split the Gamma action. More intrinsically, W_3 is generated by brackets [Q,E_2], subject to antisymmetry and the Jacobi relations on three Q inputs. The Gamma action on E_2, including its quadratic extension class, determines its action on W_3.

Fix an odd character a and a root alpha of its cyclic companion polynomial q_a. Choose three odd characters a_1+a_2+a_3=a. Pass to a finite splitting field L/Q_p, with integers O, and put

\[
z_1=1+T_1,\quad z_2=1+T_2,\quad
z_3=\alpha/(z_1z_2),\qquad
K=\operatorname{Frac}(O[[T_1,T_2]][1/p]).
\]

Every root alpha is in 1+m_O. Thus substituting z_3-1 defines the same integral formal specialization as in Triple-NV. This K is the function field of the full triple divisor; it is not obtained by replacing a bounded power-series ring by Q_p[[T]].

Choose the rank-one R-bases of Q in its odd characters. Let f_i:Q_L->K be evaluation at D=z_i on character a_i and zero on the other characters. Then

\[
f_i(Dx)=z_i f_i(x).
\]

These are the three first-superdiagonal coordinates of a proposed strictly upper triangular 4-by-4 matrix map.

## 2. The two proper-pair coordinates exist uniquely

Define alternating functionals on N by

\[
n_{12}(x\wedge y)=f_1(x)f_2(y)-f_1(y)f_2(x),
\]
\[
n_{23}(x\wedge y)=f_2(x)f_3(y)-f_2(y)f_3(x).
\]

Their Gamma eigenvalues are z_1z_2 and z_2z_3. Let q_+(z) be any nonzero polynomial annihilating all of T^+[1/p], for example the characteristic polynomial of D on its finite integral lattice. No cyclicity of T^+ is needed here.

Both q_+(z_1z_2) and q_+(z_2z_3)=q_+(alpha/z_1) are nonzero elements of K, because the displayed monomials are nonconstant functions of algebraically independent formal variables. They are therefore units in K. Define, for y in E_2,

\[
b_{12}(y)=\frac{n_{12}(q_+(D)y)}{q_+(z_1z_2)},\qquad
b_{23}(y)=\frac{n_{23}(q_+(D)y)}{q_+(z_2z_3)}.
\tag{UT.1}
\]

The numerators are defined because q_+(D)y lies in N. Each b extends its n, is Gamma-equivariant for its displayed eigenvalue, and has only proper-pair denominators. Any two such extensions differ by an equivariant map from T^+ to K with eigenvalue z_iz_j; applying its annihilator shows that difference is zero. Hence (UT.1) is unique and independent of the chosen annihilator polynomial.

On a cyclic even companion this is exactly its previously constructed rational pair kernel. Thus the proper-pair defining data can be specified canonically after passing to the triple-divisor field. Pair-NV is not needed to construct this defining data; it is a separate assertion that some of the pair residues are nonzero.

## 3. The central functional is the literal matrix bracket

Let e_ij be the standard matrix units, and give the strictly upper triangular Lie algebra n_4(K) its length grading. Send

\[
x\in Q\longmapsto f_1(x)e_{12}+f_2(x)e_{23}+f_3(x)e_{34},
\]
\[
y\in E_2\longmapsto b_{12}(y)e_{13}+b_{23}(y)e_{24}.
\]

Compatibility on [Q,Q] is exactly the definition of n_12,n_23. Consequently there is a well-defined functional psi:W_3->K characterized by

\[
\psi([x,y])=f_1(x)b_{23}(y)-b_{12}(y)f_3(x).
\tag{UT.2}
\]

This is the e_14 coefficient of the matrix commutator. It respects the Jacobi relations because matrix commutators do. On three Q inputs it is the usual Lie word evaluation, not an arbitrary cubic tensor. The formula is Gamma-equivariant:

\[
\psi(Dw)=z_1z_2z_3\psi(w)=\alpha\psi(w).
\]

It has Delta character a_1+a_2+a_3=a. Both terms in (UT.2) include the actual pair extension data, so this construction retains the mixing of [Q,T^+] into L_3(Q) under Gamma. Dropping those terms would give the wrong cubic obstruction.

## 4. Pushout and the exact Triple-NV residue

Restrict E_3 to its odd character a and push it out along psi. Write K_alpha for K as an R_L=O[[Gamma]][1/p]-module on which gamma acts as alpha. This gives

\[
0\longrightarrow K_\alpha\longrightarrow E_{a,\psi}
\longrightarrow T_a[1/p]\longrightarrow0.
\tag{UT.3}
\]

Assume T_a[1/p] is cyclic, as in the cubic criterion, and identify it with R_L/(q_a). For a cyclic vector v and an actual degree-three lift y, put w=q_a(D)y in W_3. The elementary two-term free resolution of R_L/(q_a) gives

\[
\operatorname{Ext}^1_{R_L}(R_L/(q_a),K_\alpha)
=K/q_a(\alpha)K=K.
\]

Under this identification the extension class (UT.3) is psi(w). This follows by lifting the free generator of the resolution to y: its relation q_a maps to q_a(D)y, and pushout applies psi. Changing y changes psi(w) by q_a(alpha) times a scalar, hence not at all. Repeated roots are retained; neither a derivative nor a simple-root hypothesis enters this computation.

By (UT.1)--(UT.2), psi(w) is exactly the e_14 coefficient obtained by substituting the already solved pair kernels into the actual odd companion error. This is the function called C_{a;a_1,a_2,a_3} in K17-CUBIC-ODD, restricted to z_1z_2z_3=alpha. Thus:

**For this ordered character triple, Triple-NV is exactly nonvanishing of the specified pushout extension (UT.3).**

This statement is proved in the module category. It does not assume that continuous cohomology commutes with localization to K.

There is an equivalent triangular lifting interpretation within the actual rational weighted Lie quotient. Modulo its center, n_4(K) is the two-step Lie algebra whose first and second layers were just specified. A Gamma-equivariant lift over the odd torsion component exists precisely when (UT.3) splits. The source's remaining odd degree-one quotient Q_L is free over R_L, so any Gamma-module extension over that quotient splits. A section is chosen by finitely many R_L-basis lifts, giving a bounded denominator. Changing these lifts by degree-three terms does not change any bracket of weight at most three. Hence the Q part creates no additional obstruction. Conversely a compatible triangular lift restricts on E_3 to an equivariant extension of psi and therefore splits (UT.3). This formulation concerns the specified rational Lie quotient; no additional assertion about a topological cohomology group over the function field is needed.

## 5. Norm-compatible cyclotomic inputs do not vanish generically

The three linear evaluations can be replaced by those supplied by the norm-compatible cyclotomic family (1-zeta_{p^{n+1}}), character by character. Here is the nonvanishing input, with its actual proof.

For an odd a_i, set theta_i=omega^(1-a_i), an even character. At F_0, the theta_i projection of the cyclotomic p-unit 1-zeta_p is nonzero in the rational p-unit space. For nontrivial theta_i, its conjugates generate the cyclotomic unit component, which has full rank by Hasse's cyclotomic-unit index theorem. For the trivial theta_i, its norm is p, giving a nonzero valuation direction. The Kummer class is therefore non-torsion.

The kernel of restriction from H^1(P_0,Z_p(1)) to H^1(H,Z_p(1)) is the inflation of H^1(Gamma,Z_p(1)). Delta commutes with Gamma, so this inflated group has only the odd cyclotomic Delta character. It cannot kill the stated even Kummer component. Its restriction to H is nonzero. The explicit norm-transfer map Phi of K17-NORM-TORSION is consequently nonzero on Q_{a_i}; it kills all Lambda torsion by that proved lemma.

After the involution gamma->u gamma^-1, this is a nonzero map between rank-one R modules, hence a nonzero one-variable multiplier h_i times the chosen evaluation f_i. Here h_i(z_i) means evaluation of its bounded power series in z_i-1 after that involution. Replacing f_i by h_i(z_i) f_i multiplies b_12 by h_1(z_1)h_2(z_2), b_23 by h_2(z_2)h_3(z_3), and psi by their product h_1(z_1)h_2(z_2)h_3(z_3). Each factor remains a nonzero function on the triple divisor, so it is a unit in K. For the third factor, injectivity follows by the integral change of variables V=z_3-1, W=T_1, whose inverse is z_2=alpha/(z_1(1+V)); the constants lie in the maximal ideal. Thus replacing the abstract rank-one evaluations by the actual universal cyclotomic inputs does not change whether (UT.3) is zero.

## 6. Exact remaining arithmetic requirement

Writing the universal cohomological variables as w_i=u/z_i converts the triple divisor to

\[
w_1w_2w_3=u^3/\alpha.
\]

The dual cohomological character is omega^(3-a). This records all three Tate twists and the inversion; a theorem about another character or the repeated cyclotomic direction would not establish this assertion.

A useful higher-reciprocity source must do both of the following, with its actual hypotheses checked:

1. Identify its specified three-input obstruction, after the proper-pair localizations, with (UT.3), or with its nonzero dual quotient, including its defining systems and indeterminacy.
2. Prove that this obstruction is nonzero at every root alpha needed by the actual odd cyclic companion (some ordered matching triple may be chosen for each root).

The calculations above supply the exact algebraic object and show that the norm-compatible linear inputs are available. They do not prove item2. A Bockstein-cokernel identity without a vanishing/nonzero-support theorem for its complementary augmentation-graded H^2 module is not enough. Likewise, passing from an infinite source to a finite quotient, or commuting a completed cohomology group with this localization, still requires an explicit comparison theorem if that route is used. The module-theoretic construction itself avoids those unproved operations.
