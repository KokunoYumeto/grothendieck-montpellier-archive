6. The maximal-pair and componentwise-maximal pole-order argument isolates precisely one multivariate Laurent coefficient. Crossing denominators have nonzero base-field constant term. Every selected numerator restriction is merely nonzero, which suffices in the fraction field. Repeated roots are fully retained.
7. Arithmetic finite generation is now proved directly. Kummer gives finite H1(P,Fp), hence finitely generated P and second-countable H. Adjusted generators normally generate H because their quotient is procyclic and maps isomorphically to the given Gamma quotient. Their classes generate M over Lambda.
