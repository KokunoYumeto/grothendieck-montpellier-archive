## 1. Fixed input and notation

Let H be the compact cyclotomic kernel in the checked pro-2 presentation P=C_2*Z_2. Write c for complex conjugation, gamma for the generator with chi(gamma)=u=5, and h=c gamma c gamma^(-1). The conjugates D^x(h), x in Z_2, topologically generate H, where D is conjugation by gamma.

In the checked rational normal form,

    h=exp(a), a=sum_(m>=3 odd) s_m,
    gamma=exp(-a/2)u,
    D=Ad(exp(-a/2)) composed with tau,
    tau(s_m)=u^m s_m.

The s_m freely generate the rational weight-completed Lie algebra. Complex conjugation acts on weight n by (-1)^n. Let F^n H be the inverse image of rational weight at least n. Thus H=F^3 H, the filtration is separated, and

    [F^a H,F^b H] is contained in F^(a+b) H.

Write H_(<=N)=H/F^(N+1)H for the actual compact image through weight N. Both c and D preserve the filtration. D acts on its rational weight-n graded space by the scalar u^n. More specifically, if an element starts in weight m, then D on its logarithm through weight m+1 is just tau, because the inner factor has logarithm of weight at least3.

Identify H_ab=Lambda=Z_2[[gamma-1]], with [h]=1. Complex conjugation acts on ALL of this abelianization by minus identity. Indeed c(h)=h^(-1), and c gamma c=h gamma. Conjugation by the extra H factor is trivial in H_ab, so c takes each gamma^x[h] to its negative; these classes generate Lambda. This exact minus action is essential.

Set

    m_i=2i+3, a_i=u^(m_i),
    P_i(gamma)=product_(j<i)(gamma-a_j), P_0=1.

We use closed generated subgroups and closed commutator subgroups throughout.

## 2. Inductive claim

There exist elements g_i in H and corrections w_i such that, for every i>=0:

1. g_i lies in F^(m_i)H and [g_i]=P_i(gamma) in H_ab.
2. Put S_i=the closed subgroup generated by g_0,...,g_i. Its projection onto H_(<=m_i) is surjective.
3. For every already constructed j<i,

       D(g_j)=g_(j+1) w_j g_j^(a_j),

   where w_j belongs to the closed commutator subgroup of S_j and also to F^(m_j+1)H.

The power in this exact identity is an ordinary integer power for u=5. The proof below would also allow the corresponding Z_2 powers.

The initial case is g_0=h. Its abelian class is1 and its first weight is3. Through weight3, D(h)=h^(u^3); the subgroup generated by h is invariant under both D and its inverse because u^3 is a Z_2 unit. It therefore contains the entire orbit and equals H_(<=3). No earlier weight exists.

## 3. The parity correction step

Assume the claim through i and put m=m_i, g=g_i, S=S_i. Write the two lowest possible logarithmic terms as

    log(g)=A+B+(terms of weight at least m+2),

where A has the odd weight m and B has the even weight m+1. Since c acts by parity and 2m>m+1,

    c(g)g lies in F^(m+1)H,
    log(c(g)g)_(m+1)=2B.                                  (A1)

All BCH commutators start above the weight in this formula. The exact minus action on H_ab also gives c(g)g in H'.

We now lift this commutator from S without assuming the desired generation at weight m+1. Work in H_(<=m+1). Surjectivity of S through weight m gives

    H_(<=m+1)=image(S) K,
    K=F^(m+1)H/F^(m+2)H.

The subgroup K is central: its commutator with H lies in F^(m+4)H and is zero in this quotient. Hence the closed commutator subgroup of H_(<=m+1) is exactly that of image(S). Continuous maps of compact groups carry the closed commutator subgroup onto the closed commutator subgroup of the image. We can therefore choose

    w0 in S'

whose image through weight m+1 equals c(g)g. In particular w0 lies in F^(m+1)H, and its leading weight-m+1 logarithm is 2B. This does not require any claim about the integral lattice in that even weight.

Put

    q=D(g) g^(-u^m).

The weight-m term cancels. The inner part of D contributes first in weight m+3; BCH cross terms also contribute only above m+1. Thus

    q lies in F^(m+1)H,
    log(q)_(m+1)=u^m(u-1)B.                               (A2)

The scalar

    t=u^m(u-1)/2=2*5^m

is an integer. Define w_i=w0^t and

    g_(i+1)=q w_i^(-1).

Equations (A1)--(A2) cancel the full weight-m+1 term, so g_(i+1) lies in F^(m+2)H. Its abelian class is

    [g_(i+1)]=(gamma-u^m)[g_i]=P_(i+1)(gamma),

since w_i is in S'. The exact recursive identity is

    D(g_i)=g_(i+1) w_i g_i^(u^m),

as required. The new element has EXACT first weight m+2: its linear coordinate there is P_(i+1)(u^(m+2)), a product of nonzero differences of distinct powers of5. Its odd primitive coefficient is consequently the exact Newton value, not a further multiple of2.

## 4. Propagating generation through the new odd weight

Put N=m+2=m_(i+1), and let G be the image of S_(i+1) in H_(<=N). For j<=i the exact recursive identity puts D(g_j) in S_(i+1), since w_j belongs to S_j'. For the last generator, scalar action on its first weight gives

    D(g_(i+1))=g_(i+1)^(u^N) modulo F^(N+1)H.

Thus D restricts to an endomorphism of the finitely generated compact pro-2 group G. On its Frattini quotient the displayed generators transform by

    g_j -> a_j g_j + g_(j+1),  j<=i,
    g_(i+1) -> a_(i+1) g_(i+1),

because all w_j are commutators in these generators. The coefficient matrix is triangular with odd diagonal. Its reduction modulo2 is invertible. Therefore the images of the generators span G/Phi(G), even without assuming that the listed generators are independent. The closed subgroup D(G) is Frattini-surjective, and hence D(G)=G.

D is an automorphism of the ambient H_(<=N), so equality also gives invariance under D^(-1). The compact subgroup G contains h=g_0, hence all its integer D-conjugates, and by closure all its Z_2-conjugates. Those conjugates generate H. Therefore G=H_(<=N). This establishes the induction hypothesis for i+1.

No assertion that S_i is c-stable was used. No integral Hall-generation hypothesis in the next even weight was used. The only use of that even layer was its centrality and the exact parity formula (A1).

## 5. Consequence via the checked Newton-basis theorem

The induction constructs, for every i, an element g_i of actual weight m_i with abelian class exactly P_i(gamma). Equivalently, the primitive odd image ideal attains v_2(delta_i)=3i+v_2(i!) in every odd weight.

All hypotheses of the independently checked K17-2-NEWTON-BASIS proposition are now satisfied, with b_i=1. Hence the g_i form a compact free pro-2 basis, and their leading odd classes freely generate the entire integral weight-graded Lie algebra over Z_2. The independently checked exact outer-filtration comparison identifies this weight filtration with the actual integral tripod filtration.

The deduction depends on the specific inputs in section1, particularly c=-1 on the entire H_ab and the absence of weights1 and2 in the rational unipotent action. It does not transfer automatically to an odd irregular prime, whose even abelian components violate the first of these inputs. It also does not identify these primitive generators with a differently normalized prescribed family without comparing that normalization.
