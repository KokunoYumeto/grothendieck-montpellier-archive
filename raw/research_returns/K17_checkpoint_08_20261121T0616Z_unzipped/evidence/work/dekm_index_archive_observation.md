# DEKM: an independently verified cone/projective index conflict

21 November 2026 UTC. Reviewer: GPT-6 Astra Pro, `/root/archive_kummer`; effort not exposed. This note distinguishes a forced inconsistency in dimension labels from a theorem whose truth is not decided here.

## Exact observed definitions

In the current author version `work/k12_source_data/dekm_author_current.pdf`, §1.1, printed p3, X_N* is the cone C_N* modulo positive real homotheties. In §1.2.1, p4, a Voronoi cell is the image of the positive cone generated by the rank-one forms vv^t. The set Sigma_n* is explicitly defined there using cells of dimension n in X_N*. Definition2.1, p6, defines the perfection rank r as the real dimension of the span of those same rank-one forms.

These definitions force a projective cell of perfection rank r to have dimension r−1. Here is a direct proof. The cone lies in its r-dimensional linear span and has nonempty relative interior there. The trace functional is strictly positive on every nonzero positive semidefinite form, so every positive homothety orbit intersects the hyperplane trace=1 exactly once. This cross-section has affine dimension r−1. It is precisely the projective cell.

By contrast, Remark3.2, p14, calls a well-rounded configuration with s=n an associated cell of dimension n in X_n*. In the range at issue Theorem2.5 identifies its perfection rank with s, so the dimensions from the definitions are n−1. Table4, p15, copies the cone-rank counts of Table3 into columns labelled as the projective cell dimension. For example, the GL8 row begins with the 13 full-rank perfection-rank-eight configurations labelled by n=8, although their projective cells have dimension seven.

Thus the displayed definitions and these dimension labels differ by one. This observation is mathematically forced; it does not depend on a visual guess about the table or a rerun of its enumeration.

## What does and does not follow

Enumeration and orientation reversal for cones of perfection rank at most twelve directly show that the corresponding oriented projective chain groups in degrees at most eleven vanish after the stated localization. They do not, by that fact alone, establish vanishing of the degree-twelve projective chain group or its homology. The orientation convention does not remove the dimensional shift: the radial direction has a canonical positive orientation, so orientation on the linear cone span and on its trace-one cross-section differ by that fixed oriented one-dimensional factor.

Theorem4.5, p16, explicitly asserts vanishing of H_k(Vor_GL8) through k=12. Table5 then shows the corresponding Steinberg degree-five zero, using Proposition1.5's shift k↦k−N+1. The actual statement might be true by further calculations or another argument. I have not proved it false and do not label the theorem false. The enumerated passages in §§3–4 that I read do not supply the extra projective degree after correcting the dimension convention.

The finite double-building reduction in `work/k12_double_building_gate.md` uses rank eight only through Steinberg degree four, which corresponds to projective Voronoi degree eleven. Its two conditional group vanishings are therefore unaffected by this specific concern. A final reduction of K12 to only the rank-nine and rank-ten groups additionally invokes the source's rank-eight degree-five assertion; that source dependence should remain explicit until the degree-twelve justification has been settled.
