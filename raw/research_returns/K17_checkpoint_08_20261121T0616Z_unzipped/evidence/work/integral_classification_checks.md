## Filtration proof and finiteness

Theorem K17-VANDIVER-KERNEL and its faithful rational realization identify H with the actual image; the checked outer-filtration saturation supplies the precise integral filtration. For M=H/Hclosed-derived, put M^n=image(F^nH). These are closed compact Zp submodules. For each x in all their images, the nested fibers F^nH intersect pi^-1(x) are nonempty compact sets. Their intersection gives an element in the separated intersection of F^nH, so x=0. This explicitly proves separatedness of the quotient filtration, a property that should not be assumed without compactness.

The map grH→grM is onto and kills every bracket. It factors through Lie abelianization degreewise. Complex conjugation acts by (−1)^n on each actual weight n, and the idempotents (1±c)/2 act integrally for odd p. Therefore the plus filtration has no odd quotients. Each g_n is finite free by the outer Johnson embedding; the bracket submodule in weight n is a finite sum of images of finite tensor products, so is compact, closed and finitely generated. Tensoring the resulting quotient with Qp commutes with this finite sum. Brown's rational free odd Lie algebra has zero even abelianization; hence every even integral abelianization piece is a finite p-group. The same holds for the plus quotients of grM.

If only finitely many of those plus quotients were nonzero, all later steps would be equal, because the odd quotients already vanish. The separated intersection forces the stationary submodule to be zero. Since F^1H=H, the finite chain then expresses the entire Mplus as an extension of finitely many finite groups. This contradicts its positive Zp rank. Thus infinitely many even quotients of grM, and consequently of the actual Lie abelianization, are nonzero. A Lie algebra generated by homogeneous odd classes has zero even abelianization, proving the negative classification.

## Precise scope
