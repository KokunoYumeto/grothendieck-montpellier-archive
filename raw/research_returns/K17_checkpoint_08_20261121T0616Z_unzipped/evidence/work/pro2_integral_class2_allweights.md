# K17-2-CLASS2: all-weight Newton correction in the class-two quotient

Researcher: /root/vandiver_k, GPT-6 Astra Pro; effort not exposed. Date: 2026-11-21 UTC. Status: complete paper proof, independently whole-read and passed by root and /root/tripod_integral. The detailed independent audit is work/pro2_integral_class2_tripod_audit.md. This theorem concerns the image in the quotient by rational generator length at least three. The separate parity induction in work/pro2_integral_allweights_candidate.md subsequently resolves the higher-length lift; that conclusion is not inferred from this quotient theorem alone.

## 1. Statement

Use the checked rational normal form h=exp(a), gamma=exp(-a/2)u with u=5 and a=sum_(m>=3 odd)s_m. Pass to the rational class-two quotient, keeping generator lengths one and two, and let H^(2) be the actual compact image there. Give it the induced arithmetic-weight filtration.

**Theorem.** In every arithmetic weight, the integral graded Lie algebra of H^(2) is the free class-two Z_2 Lie algebra on one primitive generator in each odd weight m_i=2i+3. Its primitive coefficient in the canonical rational coordinate s_(m_i) is

    delta_i=product_(j<i)(5^(m_i)-5^(m_j)),

up to a Z_2 unit; v_2(delta_i)=3i+v_2(i!). More precisely, the ith Newton word can always be corrected by actual integral commutators to have class-two weight at least m_i.

This is a theorem about the filtered compact class-two image. Passing to this quotient need not be strict for the full arithmetic filtration: higher-generator-length terms of a lifted word might have smaller arithmetic weight. The theorem therefore leaves exactly that higher-length lifting issue unresolved.

## 2. The finite-cutoff commutator lattice

Fix r>=1 and the even cutoff W=2r+2. Put a_i=u^(2i+3), 0<=i<r, and

    P_i(X)=product_(j<i)(X-a_j),
    P_r(X)=sum_(t=0)^r c_t X^t.

The linear image of H at this cutoff is the image A of Lambda under evaluation at the r nodes a_i (using gamma as the variable). Its kernel is P_r Lambda by successive monic division. A is free over Z_2, with bases either 1,gamma,...,gamma^(r-1) or P_0,...,P_(r-1). The basis change is unit triangular. Gamma and its inverse preserve A, since P_r has unit constant coefficient.

The image C of the closed commutator subgroup in the class-two quotient is central, and is the image of the exterior square of A. Its basis vectors in the coordinates [s_(2k+3),s_(2l+3)] are

    P_i(a_k)P_j(a_l)-P_j(a_k)P_i(a_l),   i<j,

for the pairs i+j<=r-2. These vectors are triangular in the order of increasing i+j, with diagonal delta_i delta_j. Indeed P_i(a_k)=0 for k<i. A nonzero evaluation at a target pair k<l must have k+l>=i+j; equality forces the same pair. Pairs with i+j>r-2 vanish after the weight cutoff. Thus the displayed vectors are a full integral basis of C and have leading weight 2i+2j+6. They are actual commutators of lifts of the Newton linear basis. C is stable under gamma and its inverse, by the exterior-square description.

Define the actual ordered Newton relation

    R_r=product_(t=0)^r h_t^(c_t),   h_t=gamma^t h gamma^(-t),

with increasing t. Its linear image vanishes at this cutoff. Write E_r for its central logarithm there. The kernel of the linear projection of H^(2) at this cutoff is generated by C and the gamma orbit of E_r. To see this, before applying the representation the relevant abelian classes form P_r Lambda; the gamma conjugates of R_r generate this ideal modulo the closed commutator subgroup. Compactness supplies every element of the closed ideal. The image of that commutator subgroup is exactly C. Consequently it is enough to prove E_r belongs to C. Once this is true, its whole gamma orbit belongs to C as well.

The word “central” in this argument refers to the length-two derived subspace. The whole center of a weight-truncated class-two Lie algebra can also contain high-weight length-one generators; they play no role in this kernel calculation.

## 3. A polynomial formula for E_r

For a nonnegative integer t, the linear coefficients of log(h_t) at weights m,n are X^t,Y^t, with X=u^m,Y=u^n. Its central coefficient is the polynomial

    b_t(X,Y) = -1/2 [Y^t S_t(X)-X^t S_t(Y)],
    S_t(X)=1+X+...+X^(t-1), S_0=0.

This follows either by iterating D=Ad(exp(-a/2))tau in class two or by summing its geometric series. Thus the central coefficient of R_r is the evaluation at X=a_k,Y=a_l of the alternating polynomial

    Z_r(X,Y)=sum_t c_t b_t(X,Y)
       +1/2 sum_(x<y) c_x c_y (X^x Y^y-X^y Y^x).             (C2.1)

Every coefficient of Z_r is in (1/2)Z. The formula includes all class-two BCH terms; it is not the linear span of the individual logarithms.

Expand this alternating polynomial in the tensor Newton basis:

    Z_r(X,Y)=sum_(i<j) z_(i,j)
       [P_i(X)P_j(Y)-P_j(X)P_i(Y)].                           (C2.2)

Only indices at most r are needed because the degree in each variable is at most r. Monic polynomial division gives z_(i,j) in (1/2)Z. It remains to show z_(i,j) is integral whenever i+j<=r-2, since all other terms vanish at the target coordinates of cutoff W.

## 4. The parity identity

All nodes a_i are odd. Reduce the integral polynomial 2Z_r modulo 2 and put

    A=X-1, B=Y-1, Q=A+B+AB=XY-1.

Then P_r(X)=A^r, P_r(Y)=B^r, and P_r(XY)=Q^r modulo2. The linear b_t contribution to twice (C2.1) is

    (Q^r+A^r)/B + (Q^r+B^r)/A.

These are polynomial quotients: their numerators vanish when the relevant denominator variable is zero. The cross term is

    P_r(X)P_r(Y)+P_r(XY)=A^r B^r+Q^r,

because c_t^2=c_t over F_2. Therefore the exact identity over F_2 is

    2Z_r = (Q^r+A^r)/B + (Q^r+B^r)/A + A^r B^r + Q^r.        (C2.3)

Each displayed term has total (A,B)-degree at least r-1. This proves

    2Z_r in (A,B)^(r-1) over F_2.

On the other hand P_i(X)P_j(Y) reduces to A^i B^j. Reducing twice the tensor Newton expansion shows that 2z_(i,j) is even whenever i+j<r-1. Since z_(i,j) was already a half-integer, it is an integer for every i+j<=r-2. This is exactly the required integrality. No estimate of enormous rational coefficients and no cancellation assumption is needed.

## 5. Consequences in the class-two quotient

By (C2.2), E_r is an integral combination of the actual Newton commutator basis vectors at cutoff W. Hence E_r belongs to C. The finite-cutoff argument proves that the entire kernel of the linear projection is C.

Choose an actual product of commutators with central image E_r and divide R_r by it. The resulting word has unchanged abelian class P_r and has no class-two components through weight 2r+2. Its next odd linear coefficient is delta_r. This constructs the Newton-minimal primitive at every odd weight in H^(2).

At even weight, the full filtered kernel is C, whose triangular Newton basis has precisely the leading vectors delta_i delta_j[s_(m_i),s_(m_j)]. At odd weight, the length-two part is absent, and the just constructed primitive attains the exact Newton lower bound. These observations prove the stated free class-two graded Lie algebra in all weights. The proof does not assert that the chosen words have no higher-length components below their desired odd weight in the full image. QED.

## 6. Reproducible checks and proof status

It then performs exact rational elimination in the triangular Newton-wedge basis. Completed exploratory runs r=3,4,5,6,7,8,9,10,12,16,20 all give integral coefficients.
