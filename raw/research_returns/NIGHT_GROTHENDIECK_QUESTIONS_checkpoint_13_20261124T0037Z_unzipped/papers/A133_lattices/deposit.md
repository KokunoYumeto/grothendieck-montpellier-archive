**Abstract:** Grothendieck asks which local rings make symmetric-group lattice induction polynomial in the trivial representations. Regular and coherent regular characteristic-zero rings, local rational algebras, filtered local colimits and rational nil thickenings give positive cases; positive characteristic has Newton relations. Distinguished monic algebras over every unramified characteristic-zero DVR are positive exactly when regular. Cohen coefficient rings extend this criterion to complete p-torsion-free rings with Artinian principal special fibre and to finite-free local algebras over every mixed-characteristic DVR with that p-special fibre. In particular all local monogenic algebras over unramified DVRs are classified, including inseparable residue extensions. Singular members have countably many split exact-order N=min(n,pr) generic classes; N is the special-fibre length in the completed-field case. Every nontrivial DVR principal truncation is negative. Its vertical class has exact order p when p divides n; an explicit relation to the standard mesh proves vanishings, nonsplitting of that particular order-p subgroup, and larger split primary cyclic groups under precise ramification hypotheses. Completion injects full lattice K0 and identifies generic kernels over every characteristic-zero DVR. Arbitrary square-zero extensions of Z_(p), quadratic arithmetic orders, the split Witt–Green failure and the integral tensor-power formula are also treated. The full classification remains open.
