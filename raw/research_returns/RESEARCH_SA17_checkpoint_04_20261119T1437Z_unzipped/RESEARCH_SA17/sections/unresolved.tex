\section{Exact remaining gates}
\status{Open; this document is an incomplete research return.}
For A.1 and L1, our completed gluing and local-to-global propositions require
actual representing schemes over the local bases. A uniform Artin--Rees
argument has now passed a targeted proof check: conditional on those local
representations, condition~8 on finite-type tests yields openness of the
\emph{\'etaleness} locus, hence an algebraic space. The separated \'etale
relation has finite fibres over a Noetherian scheme and is quasi-compact;
valuative uniqueness then gives a separated, locally quasi-finite scheme.
The remaining main gate is deriving the needed local schemes from the literal
actual-modularity premise of condition~7, rather than a stronger
pro-modularity premise. We do not identify these premises.

L2 is now refuted by Theorem~\ref{A:L2-counterexample}, which verifies
every other axiom, including all noetherian adic limits, for the supported
subgroup of $\alpha_p$. The dimension-one independence example settles
the other question of A.2. A different, henselized infinitesimal-group
candidate is still being tested against the main theorem: all of its
axioms except arbitrary fpqc descent, and its nonrepresentability, are
proved in Proposition~\ref{A:hensel-remaining-gate}. It is not yet a
counterexample to the main theorem. It also fails the strengthened
adic axiom, as Proposition~\ref{A:hensel-adic-failure} proves.

B.3 has explicit failures on reduced, non-reduced, and non-Noetherian bases
and exact positive classes from the opened sources. The nonvanishing detector now has an independent all-prime proof and
exhaustive certificates at two and three. The requested literature
answer is the documented union, not an absolute maximality theorem;
finite direct-product and filtered-colimit closure of the raw union
are each equivalent to the precise inclusion isolated in
Proposition~\ref{B:closure-reduction}. That inclusion remains under
investigation. The \'etale-local envelope and all negative-base
exclusions are treated separately.

C.1 is reduced beyond regular Noetherian rings to rings whose modular fibres
can be singular. The arbitrary-ring obstruction sequence and explicit
normal-domain and torsion counterexamples are stated in Part~I. A necessary
and sufficient intrinsic classification for every singular mixed-characteristic
ring remains open in this run. The complete degree-two group and tensor
ring over flat dual numbers are now determined in
Theorem~\ref{C:dualnumber_C2_ring}; this is a full computation for that
family, not an all-ring classification. For C.3, the representation-theoretic formula
over fields is proved; explicit unrestricted modular indecomposable
classification and the corresponding non-field computation remain.
The special-$\lambda$ criterion is now complete over every affine base
with two invertible, and the rings are determined in ranks at most two
and in all ranks over rational algebras.
For C.4, both requested continuations now have complete proofs and
independent targeted audits: the noncommutative unipotent alternative
for connected finite-type formal groups over perfect fields, and the
baseline-free criterion for ordinary groups and their constant group
schemes. The exact categories are stated in those theorems. No further
representability assertion for arbitrary group-scheme data is inferred
from the ordinary-group construction. The proposed universal smooth
splitting statements and the identification with Hall isoclinism remain
refuted in the explicit scopes already recorded.
