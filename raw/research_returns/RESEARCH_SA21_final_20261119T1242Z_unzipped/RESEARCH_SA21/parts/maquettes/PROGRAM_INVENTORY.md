All paths in this inventory are relative to the `RESEARCH_SA21` package root. Reproduction commands are run from that root with Python 3.10 or later. The mathematical scripts use the standard library only. Their output records exact finite computations; no enumeration is used as a proof for infinite objects or for unbounded finite cardinality. Durations below are the recorded durations of the retained run, rather than estimates; internal timings and whole-process wall timings are distinguished. ### Part A: support calculations and order censuses

**`code/check_supports.py`.** Reproduce with `python code/check_supports.py > code/outputs/check_supports.json`. This verifies the full finite Part A countermodels and cross-checks the Dacey basis criterion, the Boolean neighbourhood criterion, the atom criterion, and the equivalence between the global clique condition and the figure support law. It enumerates **1,100 labelled simple graphs** on zero through five vertices, with counts `1,1,2,8,64,1024`. Orthomodular counts by size are `1,1,2,8,52,532`; Boolean counts are `1,1,2,8,49,462`; the Sumner-normal counts in this range equal the orthomodular counts. This finite coincidence is not asserted beyond the enumerated range; the separately checked eight-vertex counterexample separates them.

The individual models include the eight-symbol crown interval magasin (256 subsets,20 supports,400 closed-set pairs, actual interval relations and all its figures), the four-vertex path (6 supports), the five-cycle (12 supports), two disjoint edges (6 supports), the two twins (2 supports), the five-vertex Boolean noncograph (8 supports), the weak spatial five-point model, the reconstructed ten-element lattice (all 100 operation pairs and 1,000 associativity triples), the old eight-stratum model (20 figures,18 supports,324 closed-set pairs), the external support-function example, and both correspondence examples. The extended final run additionally checks equality of the scan87 and scan90 open-set definitions for all 8 strata, equality with the open-lieu polars for all 8 strata, and the incidence-coverage/part(d) identity for all 20 figures of the older countermodel. Recorded aggregate internal runtime: **0.2201011680008378 seconds**. The complete witnesses, lattice tables and axiom flags are in the JSON output. The preceding run, before these part(d) checks, is retained as `code/outputs/check_supports_before_part_d.json`, with runtime **0.1883168230051524 seconds**; it is an earlier run on the same main census population, not another 1,100 graphs.

## Operational scripts and source/build records

The following programs handle retrieval, metadata, packaging or typesetting rather than mathematical verification. Their success is not used as a mathematical certificate.

- `parts/maquettes/code/extract_remote_pages.py`: strict PDF parsing with 64KiB HTTP blocks. Successful recorded commands extracted 156-8 scans 4,6 into the external source area (`extract_156-8.log`), scan 31 (`fetch_scan31.log`), and the selected 156-9 pages used by the root (`logs/156-9_selected_fetch.log`). - `code/fetch_tex_dependencies.py`: retrieval of missing official TeX dependencies; attempts and outcomes are in `logs/tex_dependency_fetch.log` and `logs/tex_dependency_fetch_mirror.log`. It is an environment operation. The first logged attempt records a lualibs HTTP402 failure after 13.513223410001956s and a 1,993,888-byte luaotfload retrieval after 15.339996163995238s. The mirror run records lualibs 282,788 bytes in 6.461892783001531s and luaotfload 1,993,888 bytes in 7.122167740002624s. These are per-package elapsed times, not a summed or aggregate runtime.
- `code/build_support_metadata.py`, `code/build_references.py` and `code/merge_metadata.py`: generation and consolidation of statement, event and bibliography records. They do not perform mathematical checking. Aggregate runtimes are not recorded.
- `code/build_checkpoint.py`: scheduled immutable checkpoint ZIP creation, excluding attached sources, earlier ZIPs and transient build products. Its retained JSON/tool result and checkpoint manifest report files, bytes and creation time; no mathematical runtime is attached.
- `code/render_pdf_qa.py`: operational Poppler/Pillow rendering of each of the 59-page QA baseline and final stable PDF at 125 dpi, with 59 page PNGs and 7 labelled contact sheets per version. Recorded render times are **26.15894421900157 seconds** for the baseline and **26.86010785899998 seconds** for the final version. These times measure rendering, not the full visual-review workflow, and this is visual QA rather than a mathematical test.
- LuaLaTeX/BibTeX builds and PDF rendering are documented in the root `logs/` and final typesetting report. The Part C preflight and isolated Lua font-map check are documented in `parts/maquettes/BUILD_REPORT.md`; transient harness outputs remain outside the packaged mathematical certificates.
