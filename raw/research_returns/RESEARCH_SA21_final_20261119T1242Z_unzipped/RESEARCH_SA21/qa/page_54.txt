For the final equivalent repair, in a finite distributive lattice the condition on lower covers
means that every nonzero join-irreducible is an atom. Every element is a join of join-irreducibles,
so the lattice is atomistic. The map to subsets of atoms is then a Boolean isomorphism:
distributivity makes distinct atoms disjoint, and every subset has its join. Conversely finite
Boolean lattices satisfy both requirements. The preceding theorem now applies to the whole
maquette. This also independently checks SA21-R7-06.
