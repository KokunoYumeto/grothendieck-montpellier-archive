Theorem 1.30 (Proved: the exact finite ordered-set class) . For every finite poset L,
ΣM (L) is orthomodular ⇐ ⇒ L is a conditional lattice .
The conclusion does not require L to be bounded or connected.
Proof. First, S(L) is a conditional lattice exactly when L is one. For copies of comparable base
elements, their meet and join are their minimum and maximum. For incomparable x, y with
a common lower bound, the meet of any two copies is (x ∧ y)+; dually, with a common upper
bound the join is (x ∨ y)−. These formulas prove sufficiency of the condition on L. Conversely,
projecting a meet or join of two copies to its base element gives the corresponding greatest lower
or least upper bound in L. Consequently the forward construction in Lemma 1.28, followed by
Theorem 1.27, proves the required sufficient direction.
