2.8 The Cont and primitive-subdivision systems: exact bridges
Proposition 2.23 (Proved: the final Cont bridge) . The final Cont 1–5 plus Fig 1–4 of 156-
4/34–40, with the geometric-dimension clause retained and with transitivity of internal refinement
added, are equivalent to the prime At 1–7 plus L1, D 0 and that same dimension clause. The
optional finite-principal clause Cont 3′ can be added on both sides. The transitivity addition
cannot be omitted, even if Cont 6 and Cond 7 are imposed.
Proof. The figure clauses are exactly the prime downset incidence system. Cont 1 identifies
incidence with compatible refinement, giving At 6; refinement of figures is defined by the stratum
test At 4. Cont 4(a) is L1, and Cont 5 is D 0. It remains to obtain the least carrier. For X ≪ F ,
choose Y ∈ ~F refined by X. Finite height below Y gives a minimal A ≤ Y refined by X, so XρA.
For any other Z ∈ ~F refined by X, choose similarly B ≤ Z with XρB . A place pρX exists by
L1. Internal transitivity gives p ∈ UA ∩ UB. The compatible strata A, B must coincide by Cont
4(b). Thus A ≤ Z for every candidate Z, proving At 5. Figure refinement is a partial order by
the carrier proof in Theorem 2.8, which does not otherwise use internal disjointness. Conversely,
in the prime core all figure clauses and Cont 1–2 hold. Carrier uniqueness makes open shadows
of distinct compatible strata setwise disjoint, supplying Cont 4(b). The remaining clauses are
exactly the stated added hypotheses.
