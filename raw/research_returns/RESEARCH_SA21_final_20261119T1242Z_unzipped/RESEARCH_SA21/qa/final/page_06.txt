Theorem 1.9 (Proved: the precise meaning of Sumner’s condition) . For an orthoset in which
all cliques are admitted as figures, the following are equivalent:
1. For every clique F and A ⊆ F , cl F ∩ N (A) = cl(F \ A).
2. For every maximal clique D and every distinct u, v, D ⊆ N (u) ∪ N (v) implies u ⊥ v.
The equivalence holds also for infinite cliques, using choice. On finite graphs (2) is precisely
Sumner (1974, p. 492), and is normality in the terminology of Paseka and Vetterlein (2022,
Definition 2.4 and Lemma 2.5). On an infinite orthoset their normality tests finite orthogonal
families; we do not identify that convention with an unrestricted infinite-family assertion.
The finite condition is strictly weaker than the basis criterion of Theorem 1.3, even for finite
comparability graphs.
