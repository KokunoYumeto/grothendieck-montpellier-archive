Abstract
We distinguish the support ortholattice, the Boolean calculus within one figure, and the
incidence and refinement axioms needed to construct those figures. We give necessary and
sufficient order-neighbourhood tests for orthomodularity and Booleanity. For a finite ordered
set, its interval magasin has orthomodular supports exactly when every pair bounded above
has a join and every pair bounded below has a meet. For an arbitrary ordered set, its interval
magasin has Boolean supports exactly when the nonisolated elements form a chain. We give
explicit finite counterexamples to several proposed equivalences. The interval magasin of
a four-point poset has a nonorthomodular twenty-element support lattice although every
finite figure has the prescribed Boolean support calculus. We reconstruct the ten-element
ortholattice on scan 156-9/113, audit the successive axiom systems, correct the event-structure
and o-minimal comparisons, and characterize the oriented maquettes arising from regular
CW and simplicial complexes. Every finite counterexample is accompanied by executable
verification.
