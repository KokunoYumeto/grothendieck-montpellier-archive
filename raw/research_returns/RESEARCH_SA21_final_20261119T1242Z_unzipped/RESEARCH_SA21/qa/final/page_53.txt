6.5 Polyhedral and simplicial conditions
Theorem 6.9 (Known, with the exact application proved) . For a poset P of nonempty cells, let
P 0 = P ∪ {0} with a new minimum. The following are equivalent:
1. P is the nonempty face poset of an abstract simplicial complex;
2. every interval [0, x] is a finite Boolean lattice and P 0 is a meet-semilattice;
3. every interval [0, x] is finite Boolean and the map x ↦→ Vx, where Vx is the set of atoms
below x, is injective.
The equivalence of the first two conditions is stated by Björner (1984, §2.3, pp. 8–9).
