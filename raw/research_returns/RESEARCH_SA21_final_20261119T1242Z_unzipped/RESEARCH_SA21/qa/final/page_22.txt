156-4/26–31,
Cont 1–4
Set-theoretic figures, with the empty figure and point figures, the
preliminary admissibility clauses, closure under closed subfamilies, and
compatibility tested on elementary figures. This is a different numbering
and is not an abstract finite-dimension axiom system.
156-4/34–44,
Cont 1–5
The incidence order and hereditary compatibility; Fig 1–4 specify
principal figures, clique downsets, closed subfigures and compatible
unions; Cont 3 is finite geometric dimension, with optional Cont 3 ′
finite principal deployments; Cont 4 is nonempty open lieu-shadows
and their setwise separation; Cont 5 is disjointness hereditary under
refinement. Optional Cont 6 detects disjointness on lieux; Cond 7 is
their orthogonality across distinct compatible open strata; Cond 8 is
induced refinement.
156-4/53–83,
C1–C80
C1 bounded joins; C2 irreducible generation; C3 compatibility by strata;
C4 compatibility across distinct incidence components; C5 subdivisions
preserve cosupport; C6 induced subdivisions, with uniqueness, the
stratum formula and join preservation; C7 0 extension of subdivisions;
C80 excludes an incidence and subdivision cycle through an elementary
object. C4 bis asks for componentwise assembly. Final C8, at scan 87,
is a separate gluing axiom.
156-6, At 1–7 Formula (1.20) on scan 12 explicitly defines strata by bounded-join
primality, before scan 17 renames C1–C3. With that definition the
incidence presentation is prime; the later clauses are refinement by strata,
least carrier, compatible refinement implies incidence, and internal
transitivity. Importing only 156-4’s equality-based irreducibility gives a
weaker presentation.
156-7, At 1–7 Same intended core, but At 5 and At 6 exchange numbers. The written
hypothesis X ◁ F in At 6 must be X ≪ F , as in its own M -translation.
The term “strictement irréductibles” inherits the prime meaning of 156-6
if that earlier definition is retained.
156-8, Mag 1–4 Two orders with ≤⊆≪; least carriers in principal deployments;
symmetric irreflexive internal disjointness hereditary under internal
refinement; all distinct strata under one top are internally disjoint.
156-8/30–32,
primed and div
Internal refinement becomes primitive. The strong primed version is
equivalent to Mag 1–4. The final written Mag 4 ′, and later Mag div 3,
require only comparable pairs and are strictly weaker. Divisibility and
the two-strata clause repair the local formulation.
156-8/20–21,
atlas At 1–5
Bounded joins, generation, compatibility and refinement detected on
strata, and the primitive internal disjointness formula, together with
Mag 1–4. Add (B.P) for the claimed equivalence with an actual atlas of
clique downsets.
156-9/41, At 1–4 The change of names does not change the strong relational theory. A
comparable-only reading inherits the earlier weakening.
156-9/47–50, At
spat 1–3
An incidence order, internal disjointness, the comparable-pair clause,
and an injective deployment into ordered spatial figures. The target’s
pairwise orthogonality condition is essential; with that typed target the
strong two-strata axiom is restored. Ordinary disjoint-set labels do not
suffice.
