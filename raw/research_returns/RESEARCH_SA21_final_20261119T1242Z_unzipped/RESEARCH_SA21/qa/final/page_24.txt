2.3 Core calculus and exact implications among the optional criteria
Lemma 2.6 (Proved: carrier calculus) . In a prime atelier with At 4–At 7, carriers compose. If
P ≪ F and G ≤ F , the displayed formula for P |G is a figure and is inf≪(P, G). Restrictions
commute with incidence unions and with incidence intersections inside a fixed parent. If F, G are
compatible, every common refinement refines F ∩ G. These conclusions require no lieux and no
finite-dimension hypothesis.
Proof. If Z ≪ P ≪ F , let A be its carrier in P , and let B be the carrier of A in F . Then ZρAρB ,
so At 7 gives ZρB . Thus B is the carrier of Z in F . The inverse image of a downward-closed
deployment under a carrier is downward closed and is an admitted subfigure. It has exactly the
strata refining G, and it refines G by At 4. If H ≪ P, G, a stratum Z of H has carrier A in
P ; its carrier in F lies in G. Composition makes that same stratum the carrier of A in F , so
A ≪ G and Z ≪ P |G. This proves the infimum. Union and intersection formulas are ordinary
inverse-image formulas. Finally, take the carrier of a common-refining stratum in F ∨ G. Its
minimality puts it below a stratum of F and a stratum of G, hence in both deployments. At 4
completes the claim.
