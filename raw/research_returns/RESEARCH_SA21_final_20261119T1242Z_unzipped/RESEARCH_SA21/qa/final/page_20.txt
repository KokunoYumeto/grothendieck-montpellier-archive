2.1 The literal incidence axiom and its exact repair
Theorem 2.2 (Proved: exact bounded-join representation criterion) . Suppose every bounded
family in the nonempty poset F has a join, and every figure is the join of its completely
join-irreducible strata. Then F ↦→ ~F is an order embedding and preserves all nonempty meets.
The following are equivalent:
1. it preserves every bounded join as a union;
2. all strata satisfy (B.P);
3. its image contains every downward-closed subset of every one of its members, with joins
there given by unions.
Under these equivalent conditions, compatibility by strata is precisely the condition that two
admitted downsets have an admitted union whenever all their cross-pairs of principal ideals have
admitted unions. If every figure has finitely many strata, the prime condition is equivalent to
distributivity of every principal figure ideal.
Proof. Generation gives F ≤ G exactly when every stratum below F is below G; hence the order
embedding. Every nonempty family has a meet: take the bounded join of its common lower
bounds. A stratum lies below that meet exactly when it lies below each family member. The
equality ~⋁i Fi = ⋃i ~Fi is precisely (B.P). If D ⊆ ~F is a downset, H = ⋁X∈D X exists; primality
gives ~H = ⋃X∈D ~X = D. Conversely, an image consisting of downsets and closed under bounded
unions has those unions as its joins, because the deployment map is an order embedding. This
proves the equivalences and the compatibility translation.
For the final assertion, every interval [0, F ] is finite. A finite downset lattice is distributive.
