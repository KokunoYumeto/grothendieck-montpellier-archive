1.6 A faithful support function that is not fully faithful
Counterexample 1.22 (Proved: answer to A.5) . Let M = {a, b}, with identity incidence,
interior refinement aρb in addition to identities, and no internal disjunction. The figures are
∅, {a}, {b}. Let P = {p, q} and define the external support function
|a| = {p}, |b| = {p, q}.
It is faithful and normalized, but not fully faithful in the exact sense of 156-8, scan 86.
Proof. Interior refinement is a partial order. Incidence lifting is trivial; orthogonality monotonicity
and the strong incidence axiom are vacuous. Both principal interior lower sets contain a, so the
magasin itself is faithful. Since incidence is identity, external open and full supports coincide.
They are nonempty, monotone under interior refinement, and their intersections are always
nonempty. Thus they detect exactly the prescribed empty disjunction relation. They cover P
and distinguish its two points, satisfying the usual normalization and separation conventions as
well. Full faithfulness requires, for a figure F ,

|Z|◦ = P \ |F |.
For F = {a} the left side is empty, while the right side is {q}. Hence full faithfulness fails. This
property is not equivalent to reflecting refinement, and a constant singleton support function
would not provide the required counterexample.
