1.4 Faithfulness, lieux, and general magasins
Proposition 1.13 (Proved: the faithful case, R1 rechecked) . Let (M, ρ, ⊥) be a magasin whose
interior refinement ρ is a preorder and whose faithfulness means
x ⊥ y ⇐ ⇒ there is no z with zρx and zρy.
In the Alexandrov topology of ρ-lower sets, Σ(M ) is exactly the complete Boolean algebra of
regular open sets. In particular every family of mutually orthogonal supports satisfies the
relative-complement and nonempty-meet conclusions of the support theorem.
