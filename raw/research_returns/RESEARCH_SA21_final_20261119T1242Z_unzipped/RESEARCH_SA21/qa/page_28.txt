Counterexample 2.13 (Refuted: finite gluing or D 0 need not give D) . Take M = {x, y, q},
incidence x < y , refinement x ≪ y, q ≪ y, and compatibility consisting of equality and the pair
x, y. The figures are 0, {x}, {q}, {x, y}. The lieux are x, q, and Uy = {q}. The prime core, L1,
L2, CL(spac), At supp and D 0 hold. No two nonempty figures are disjoint, so L2 and D 0 have
exactly their stated, mostly vacuous content. Every incidence cover of {x, y} contains {x, y} itself;
the other nonzero figures are elementary singletons. Consequently all compatible local refinement
data glue uniquely. This checks At 11, including arbitrary covers after deleting repetitions.
For parents x ≤ y, the refinements x ≪ x and q ≪ y restrict respectively to x and 0 on their
intersection. Those restrictions are disjoint, while x, q are not. Thus D and L3 fail, and so does
C. The exhaustive code verifies every finite cover and all its refinement assignments. This also
shows why CL(spac) alone must not be confused with the relative C axiom.
Counterexample 2.14 (Refuted: At Fil for a restricted atlas) . Let M = {an, xn : n ∈ N}, with
discrete incidence, refinement generated by an ≪ xn, and internal disjointness between exactly
those pairs having different indices. This is a strong local magasin: the open-lieu set of either
member of the n-th pair is {an}. A full figure chooses at most one member of each pair. Restrict
the atlas to those figures containing only finitely many an, allowing arbitrarily many xn. It
contains all principal figures and is closed under subfigures, bounded unions within the atlas and
finite compatible unions. It therefore satisfies all the prime core, local and finite-gluing axioms
proved above.
Let F = {xn : n ∈ N} and Gk = {a0, . . . , ak}. These form an increasing family of refinements
of F ; their restriction to each stratum xn is eventually constant. Their union {an : n ∈ N} is
omitted, and no admitted figure can bound it. Thus At Fil, At 11 bis and arbitrary gluing fail. For
the last assertion use the disjoint cover of F by the singleton figures xn, with local refinements
an. This identifies the exact extra atlas closure needed by the otherwise valid gluing theorem.
Proposition 2.15 (Proved: extension after the two missing hypotheses) . For the full figure
family of a strong magasin satisfying L1 and At supp, subdivision is equivalent to incidence-
maximal refinement, and At 14 follows. This assertion uses the usual axiom of choice through
Zorn ’s lemma. It does not give uniqueness in At 14 bis or ter.
