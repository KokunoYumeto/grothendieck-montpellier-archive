Theorem 1.10 (Proved: every finite flag is admissible) . Every finite prefigure, and hence every
finite figure, of M (L) satisfies Axiome supp 1 for every poset L. This conclusion does not assert
orthomodularity of the whole support lattice, nor does it extend to arbitrary infinite figures.
Proof. The endpoints of a finite prefigure form a finite chain t0 < · · · < t n. Inserting all points
Pti and all intervals Ititi+1 yields an alternating flag H containing the prefigure. An original
interval has consecutive endpoints in this chain: another endpoint strictly between them would
be incompatible with that interval. The empty prefigure is immediate; a one-point flag causes
no difficulty. For a symbol Z let TZ = {h ∈ H : Z ⁄⊥ h}. If nonempty, TZ is a contiguous block
in H: all members before it are positionally below Z, and all members after it are above Z, by
transitivity of the positional order. Suppose TZ, TW are nonempty and disjoint, with TZ before
TW . Choose consecutive hi, hi+1 at a cut between these blocks. Then Z < p hi+1 and hi <p W .
The consecutive symbols are either Pt, Itu or Ist, Pt. In either case the two inequalities say that
every endpoint of Z is at most t and every endpoint of W is at least t. Thus Z < p W , except
possibly equality of the same point symbol, which the disjoint nonempty traces exclude. Now
let Z ∈ cl H and H ⊆ N (Z) ∪ N (W ). The first trace is nonempty, since cl H ∩ N (H) = ∅. The
traces are disjoint. If TW is empty, W ∈ N (H) directly gives Z ⊥ W ; otherwise the preceding
argument does. Theorem 1.17 proves admissibility of H. Its Boolean family of subjoins is closed
under intersections and relative complements, so restricting that family to any subflag proves the
assertion for the original prefigure. This supplies the missing global comparison in the finite-flag
computation of 156-9, scans 21–22.
Counterexample 1.11 (Refuted: an unrestricted infinite extension) . Let L = Q ∐ {a, b}, with
the usual order on Q, with a < q exactly for q ≥ 0, and with q < b exactly for q < 0. There are
no other comparisons involving a, b. The countable figure F = {Pq : q ∈ Q} of M (L) violates
Axiome supp 1. Thus a statement for finite flags cannot silently be upgraded to all figures of the
maximal, infinitary magasin.
