7 What the computations certify
The attached programs enumerate the finite relations and all the quantified finite cases stated in
their output. They check each finite counterexample independently of the prose proof. A finite
enumeration does not prove a universally quantified result about infinite objects; those assertions
have separate mathematical proofs. Search counts are counts of the precisely stated labelled or
naturally labelled objects, not automatically counts of isomorphism classes.
8 Sources searched and remaining source-access limitations
We searched the exact manuscript title and combinations of “Grothendieck”, “cote 156”,
“orthomodular”, “Dacey graphs”, “orthogonality spaces associated with posets”, “regular CW
posets”, and “prime event structures” . We found the official notice of Jean Malgoire’s 3 October
2002 introductory seminar on this manuscript ( Malgoire, 2002). Thus absence of prior attention
must not be claimed. This notice alone does not establish a published proof of the specific
statements treated here. Michel Hua’s project was opened as a contemporary transcription and
reading resource; any mathematical point adopted from it would be attributed explicitly. The
mathematical proofs here were checked against the specified manuscript passages and the primary
sources listed below.
