The positional relation is a strict partial order. Transitivity follows by comparing all endpoints
through the nonempty endpoint set of the middle symbol. A putative cycle would force all
these endpoints equal, so all its symbols would be the same point, contradicting strictness. Its
comparability relation is therefore symmetric and irreflexive. Suppose U < p V and U ′ρU , V ′ρV .
Every endpoint of U ′ is at most every endpoint of V ′, by the endpoint containment inequalities.
Equality U ′ = V ′ could only be a common point at an endpoint of one of the original intervals;
such a point cannot internally refine that interval. The alternative that both originals are that
point contradicts U < p V . Hence U ′ <p V ′, proving heredity of disjunction. Finally the principal
incidence ideal of Iab consists of Pa, Iab, Pb, in that positional order. All three distinct pairs are
internally disjoint. Point ideals are singletons. This is the full two-strata condition, not merely
its comparable-incidence weakening, and completes strong Mag 1–4.
