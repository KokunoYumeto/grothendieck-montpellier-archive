Thus At 12 follows exactly when these common-refinement deployments remain in Σ. In particular

it follows if elementary refinements restrict to empty or elementary figures on every elementary

Proof. Put A = X ′|K, B = Y ′|K, and K′ = X ′ ∩ Y ′. The common-refinement lemma gives

whereas the carrier of q lies in ~Y ; they are distinct, and At 10 again applies. This proves the
automatic first clause and hence (B.Gate).
For the final assertion, compatibility of the parents makes K empty or elementary. The added
restriction hypothesis makes A, B empty or elementary. If either is empty, so is K′. Otherwise
compatibility of the elementary A, B, through (B.Sigma), puts their common deployment in
Σ.
