3 B.2. What replaces the false global connectedness axiom
The global incidence graph of M , the incidence graph of a single figure, and the global refinement
graph are three different objects. We give their exact relationships. In particular, replacing the
first by either of the latter two is a change of statement that must be made explicit.
Theorem 3.1 (Proved: all incidence components of M (Fig(L))). Let L be an arbitrary set.
A multistrate in Fig(L) is an elementary admissible family of subsets of L, ordered by closed-
subfamily incidence. If |L| ≥ 3, the connected components of its comparability graph are exactly:
1. the isolated multistrate {L};
2. for each p ∈ L, the two-object component
{L \ {p}} < {L \ {p}, L};
3. one component containing every remaining multistrate.
