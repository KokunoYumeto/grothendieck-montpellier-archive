Refinement of figures is defined by At 4. Its antisymmetry for arbitrary, possibly infinite
figures follows from carriers: if F ≪ G ≪ F , the successive carriers of X ∈ F are Y ∈ G, Z ∈ F ,
with XρY ρZ . But the carrier of X in F is X, so Z = X; antisymmetry on M gives Y = X.
Thus F ⊆ G, and symmetrically equality. Generation and primality are union statements for

To prove C, let F, G be compatible parents and let P ≪ F, Q ≪ G have compatible restrictions
to K = F ∩G. For distinct A ∈ P, B ∈ Q, take their carriers in the parent union. Distinct carriers
are internally disjoint, hence AδB. If the carriers agree, they lie in K, so both strata occur in
the compatible restrictions and are again internally disjoint. Thus the union is a clique downset,
and the atlas’s finite-union condition admits it. D, D 0 and gluing follow from Proposition 2.6.

Theorem 2.9 (Proved: the precise local equivalence) . For a strong magasin, Mog L2 is
XδY ⇐ ⇒(∀p ∈ UX , q ∈ UY ) pδq. (B.Loc)
It implies Mog L1, UX ⁄= ∅, by irreflexivity. Under the prime incidence convention, strong
magasins with (B.Loc) and their chosen atlases are equivalent to prime ateliers satisfying At 1–7,
L1, L2, L3 and CL(spac). The inverse definition of δ is the right-hand side of (B.Loc).
In the earlier formulation using ≤, ≪, Mog 1, Mog 2, Mog L1 and a symmetric irreflexive
graph on the lieux, together with the all-pairs div 4 clause, produce a strong magasin by this
definition if and only if the resulting internal refinement is transitive . It suffices to impose
transitivity when the first object is a lieu; under these hypotheses that restricted condition forces
full transitivity. In the later ≤, ρ formulation, the exact repair is to require nonempty UX and
replace comparable-only div 3 by the all-pairs condition (B.M4) tested on open lieux.
Proof. The forward half of (B.Loc) is Mag 3; its converse is Mog L2. If UX = ∅, that converse
would give XδX . The strong magasin already supplies L3. Its disjointness formula is
F ‖ G ⇐ ⇒(∀X ∈ ~F , Y ∈ ~G) XδY. (B.MD)
Indeed the right side ensures compatibility by (B.MC) and rules out a shared stratum; the
converse uses (B.M4) in the compatible union. Decompose each lieu by its carrier and use (B.Loc)

cross-pair in (B.MC), proving compatibility.
