Lemma 5.2 (Proved: carriers, compatibility and restrictions) . The relations ≤, ≪ are partial
orders with ≤⊆≪. Refinement has a unique monotone carrier
cG,F : Str(G) − →Str(F ), C B ⊆ CcG,F (B).
For m ≪ n, the least substratum of n refined by m is its top-cell carrier. Consequently
m ≪◦ n ⇐ ⇒ m ≪ n and m◦ ⊆ n◦.
Figures are compatible exactly when, after uniting their elementary subfigures, all distinct
multistrates have disjoint top cells; the join is then that union. They are disjoint exactly when
their supports are disjoint. For G ≪ F , H ≤ F , induced refinement is
G|H = {B ∈ G : cG,F (B) ∈ H}, |G|H| = |G| ∩ |H|.
Proof. Reflexivity and transitivity follow from containment. If F ≪ G ≪ F , an F -cell C lies
in a G-cell D, which lies in an F -cell C′. Nonempty intersection forces C = C′, hence C = D.
Repeating proves equality of figures. A downward-closed subfamily retains its cells, so incidence
implies refinement. Carriers are unique since target cells are disjoint. If B′ ⊆ B, then
CB′ ⊆ B′ ⊆ B ⊆ Cc(B) X = c(B).
The target cell Cc(B′) meets c(B); frontier compatibility gives c(B′) ⊆ c(B). All carriers of an
elementary figure therefore lie below its top-cell carrier, which must occur in any target subfigure
containing the refinement. This proves the carrier assertions.
