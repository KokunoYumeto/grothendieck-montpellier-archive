\subsection{A class-specific two-row quotient on at most five states}

All cohomology in this subsection has rational coefficients.

\begin{lemma}[Proved: relative classes in a cofiber annihilate extending classes]
\label{CP2:A-cofiber-relative-annihilator}
Let $Y$ be the homotopy cofiber of a map $E\to A$.  If
$z\in H^2(Y)$ restricts to zero in $H^2(A)$, then
\[
 z\smile\beta=0\qquad\text{for every }\beta\in H^2(Y).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Replace the map by its mapping-cylinder inclusion $E\subset A'$.
Excision identifies reduced cohomology of the cofiber, with its
products, with relative cohomology of $(A',E)$ in positive degrees.
The relative exact sequence expresses $z$ as $\delta u$ for a
cocycle $u$ on $E$.  Extend $u$ to a cochain $\widetilde u$ on
$A'$, so $z$ is represented by the relative cocycle
$d\widetilde u$.  Represent $\beta$ by a relative cocycle $b$,
which vanishes on $E$.  The cup-product differential gives
\[
 (d\widetilde u)\smile b=d(\widetilde u\smile b).
\]
The cochain $\widetilde u\smile b$ also vanishes on $E$, so this
is an exact relative cochain.  This proves the claim, including
the case $\beta=z$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Proved: descent of a selected corner restriction]
\label{CP2:A-selected-corner-class-descent}
Let $q:M\twoheadrightarrow H$ be a surjective finite-monoid map
and let $e\in M$ be idempotent such that
$\mathbb QM e\to\mathbb QHq(e)$ is an isomorphism of left
$\mathbb QM$ modules.  Let $S$ be a finite left-$N$, right-$M$
biset, with $BN$ rationally acyclic, and put $U=S\otimes_M H$.
Write $X$ and $X'$ for the actual source and target collages.
If the source-corner restriction of $x\in H^2(X)$ equals
$q^*a$ for some $a\in H^2(BH)$, then there is $y\in H^2(X')$
whose restriction to $BH$ is $a$, and
\[
 x^2=q_X^*(y^2).
\]
Here $q_X:X\to X'$ is the actual induced collage functor.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual induced set $U$ is a quotient of $S$: surjectivity of
$q$ writes every $[s,h]$ as $[sm,1]$.  Its left $N$ action is the
canonical commuting action.  By
Lemma~\ref{CP2:A-idempotent-quotient-bridge-connectivity}, the map
of actual bridges $E\to E'$ is surjective on $H_2$ and therefore
injective on $H^2$ by pullback.

The restriction of $x$ to the source corner pulls back to zero
on $E$, since its other corner restriction belongs to
$H^2(BN)=0$.  The commuting square and the bridge injection imply
that $a$ pulls back to zero on $E'$.  The target two-block exact
sequence therefore supplies an extending class $y\in H^2(X')$.
The difference $z=x-q_X^*y$ restricts to zero on $BM$ and on $BN$.

Collapsing the actual rationally acyclic corner $BN$ gives a
rational homology equivalence, hence a ring isomorphism, to the
actual homotopy cofiber of $E\to BM$.  Under this comparison $z$
is a relative class.  Lemma~\ref{CP2:A-cofiber-relative-annihilator}
shows that $z$ annihilates both $q_X^*y$ and itself.  Expanding
$(q_X^*y+z)^2$ gives the assertion.  No higher-homology comparison
between $X$ and $X'$ has been asserted or used.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Proved: arbitrary two-row sources on at most five states]
\label{CP2:A-two-row-band-five-state-right-zero}
Let $M$ be any finite monoid whose proper minimal ideal is a
rectangular band with two rows and any positive number of columns.
For every finite left-$R_3$, right-$M$ biset $S$ with $|S|\le5$,
all rational degree-two products on its collage vanish.
There is no restriction on the outside monoid elements or on
higher homology.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First assume $S$ is nonempty, and fix $x\in H^2(X)$.  If its
restriction to $BM$ is zero, the relative-annihilator lemma already
gives $x^2=0$.  Otherwise represent that restriction by its balanced
column-value function $\lambda:J\to\mathbb Q$, modulo constants.
This uses the natural band--Tor description with the one-dimensional
row-augmentation module.  The function is nonconstant.

For $m\in M$, let $\sigma_m$ and $\tau_m$ be its row and column
actors.  The row-augmentation scalar is
\[
 \chi(m)=\begin{cases}
 1&\text{if }\sigma_m\text{ is the identity},\\
 -1&\text{if }\sigma_m\text{ is the transposition},\\
 0&\text{if }\sigma_m\text{ is constant}.
 \end{cases}
\]
Balancing says, for a scalar $b_m$ independent of $j$, that
\[
 \lambda(\tau_m(j))=\chi(m)\lambda(j)+b_m.
\]
Thus equality of column values is preserved by every column actor.
Collapse the columns to the finite value set
$\Lambda=\lambda(J)$ and take the actual image $H$ of the row and
collapsed-column actor homomorphism.  Let $t=|\Lambda|\ge2$.
The $2t$ constant actor pairs form its rectangular minimal band.

An affine map of the finite ordered set $\Lambda$ with slope one
is the identity.  A slope-zero map is constant.  A slope-minus-one
map, if present, is the unique reflection exchanging the least and
greatest values.  Consequently $H$ is precisely either the pure
$2\times t$ band with an identity, or that monoid with one extra
unit acting by the row transposition and this column reflection.
Its square is the identity.  No other actor pair can occur.

The resulting homomorphism $q:M\twoheadrightarrow H$ preserves
both rows and is bijective on $Me$ for any band idempotent $e$.
The collapsed value function defines a balanced class
$a\in H^2(BH)$ whose pullback is the original corner restriction.
This is naturality of the two augmentation sequences in the
band--Tor formula: the row module map is the identity, and the
column module map is induced by $J\to\Lambda$.

The actual induced biset $U=S\otimes_M H$ has at most five states.
The pure-band and one-extra-actor theorem
\ref{CP2:A-two-row-one-extra-five-states} says that every degree-two
product on the target collage is zero.  Apply the selected-class
descent lemma to obtain $x^2=q_X^*(y^2)=0$.
This proves that every rational degree-two square on $X$ is zero;
polarization then gives every degree-two product as zero as well.

For completeness, if $S$ is empty the collage is
$BM\sqcup BR_3$.  Every balanced form for a two-row band has rank
at most one, so Lemma~\ref{A:rank-one-square} kills its second
squares, and polarization again kills all products.  The other
component is rationally acyclic.  This also covers a one-column
band, whose source second cohomology is zero.
\end{proof}
