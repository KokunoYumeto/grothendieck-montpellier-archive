\subsection{The proper-image branch is complete}

\begin{theorem}[Proved: all fifty-one proper faithful images are excluded]
\label{CP2:A-eleven-proper-faithful-images-complete}
In the first branch of
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-eleven-three-five-faithful-image-split}, the original
$(11,3,5)$ collage cannot have the homotopy type of $\mathbb CP^2$.
This assertion covers every induced state size from one through five
and uses no boundedness condition on the quotient collage.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The one-state case and every source-fixed state are covered by the
unconditional fixed-state lemma.  For each of the fifty-one faithful
source hulls of orders seven through ten, enumerate all remaining
band acts on each of two, three, four, and five states.  This gives
exactly sixteen complete source-order/state-size profiles.  The
independent scope checker reconstructs every right and opposite
action, with their complete stabilizers and orbit weights.  No
whole-source, right-action, proper-retract, or higher-homology
omission is made.

The integral comparison forces $b_1(\mathbb F_2)=0$ and
$b_2(\mathbb F_2)=1$ on any induced quotient of a hypothetical
$\mathbb CP^2$.  After these two necessary filters, the complete
streams retain $17\,365$ literal collages.  For $17\,159$ of them,
the normalized-bar generator $u$ has an explicitly checked
three-cochain $w$ with $\delta w=u\smile u$ over $\mathbb F_2$.
The other $206$ records are preserved as nonzero mod-two squares;
their nonzero-square status is also checked by the independent
augmented-system rank calculation.

These $206$ records have precisely two source hulls: the order-nine
source indexed $8040$ in the original positive catalog contributes
$43$, and the order-ten source indexed $156766$ contributes $163$.
Both have projective full row-augmentation modules over the
contracted rational algebra.  The exact rational sections were
previously checked, and the new join independently reconstructs
both sections from their literal tables.  It verifies
\[
 PX=I_2,\qquad \operatorname{diag}(L_a,L_a)X=XV_a
\]
for every contracted-algebra basis element, together with explicit
monoid isomorphisms from the newly enumerated hull labels to those
literal source tables.  Thus
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-projective-row-two-column-all-states} gives zero
rational degree-two products for all their collages, unconditionally.
It applies to the actual quotient targets and requires no transfer
of bounded homology.

For a record with the displayed mod-two primitive, naturality and
the integral comparison contradict the nonzero mod-two square on
$\mathbb CP^2$.  For either projective source hull, the same
comparison over $\mathbb Q$ contradicts its nonzero rational square.
Every possible quotient is therefore excluded.  The complete
sixty-four stage exits, input/build bindings, full ordered literal
streams, the exact $43+163$ frontier partition, and the two newly
reconstructed projectivity witnesses are joined in
\texttt{proper51\_complete\_mixed\_field\_join.json}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark*}[The stronger mod-two statement is false]
The $206$ frontier records show that the assertion that all
fifty-one target hulls always have zero mod-two squares is false.
Their rational product-zero proof is a separate valid obstruction.
The original square-only streams and their nonzero-square
certificates remain unchanged; no positive third-homology test is
substituted for them in this proper-image branch.
\end{remark*}
