\begin{lemma}[Proved: the fourteen proper remaining actor images]
\label{CP2:A-eleven-proper-remaining-hulls-complete}
Let $H$ be one of the fourteen proper faithful targets in
Corollary~\ref{CP2:A-remaining-faithful-hull-comparison}, and let
$U$ be a nonempty left-$R_3$, right-$H$ biset with at most five
states.  If the associated collage $Y$ has
\[
 H_1(Y;\mathbb F_2)=0,\qquad H_2(Y;\mathbb F_2)=\mathbb F_2,
\]
then its degree-two generator has zero square over $\mathbb F_2$.
Consequently no hypothetical projective-plane collage can have
any of these fourteen faithful images.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The fourteen targets consist of the unique order-nine
four-by-two hull, twelve order-ten four-by-two hulls, and the
pure order-ten three-by-three hull.  A right-source fixed state
gives the unconditional square obstruction of
Lemma~\ref{CP2:A-hull-square-fixed-state}; this also handles
every one-state image.  All other induced state sizes two
through five are covered by the complete literal core forms of
Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-remaining-hulls-small-band-forms}.

The frozen unpruned square-only engines enumerate every extension
of each such band action to its source, and every commuting
left action of the opposite corner.  The independent fixed-order
checker reconstructs the same complete state orbits and their
weights.  No higher-homology, proper-retract, or projective-row
omission is used in this run.  The exact completed scopes are
\[
\begin{array}{c|r|r|r}
 (|H|,|U|)&\text{right-act orbits}&\text{biset orbits}&
 \text{retained square primitives}\\\hline
 (9,2)&1&1&0\\
 (9,3)&1&5&0\\
 (9,4)&2&22&0\\
 (9,5)&9&153&32\\
 (10,2)&15&15&3\\
 (10,3)&27&88&11\\

\]
The low-homology tests exclude 144 bisets in degree one and

the producer supplies a nonzero normalized two-cocycle $u$

$\delta w=u\smile u$.  The separate literal checker verifies

The final join reconstructs every retained literal composition

scope and certificate streams through their checked ends.
It binds all source lines, normal-form inputs, executable builds,

For a hypothetical original projective plane, the actual
band-bijective quotient comparison preserves integral first

its boundedness are not used.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Proved: the 151 equal-order remaining actor images]
\label{CP2:A-eleven-actual-remaining-hulls-complete}
No collage with opposite corner $R_3$, five connecting states,
and source one of the 151 equal-order targets in
Corollary~\ref{CP2:A-remaining-faithful-hull-comparison}
has the homotopy type of $\mathbb CP^2$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The complete source list contains 140 four-by-two hulls and
eleven three-by-three hulls.  The same unpruned square-only
engines, unchanged inputs and fixed-order independent checker
give 6047 right-act state orbits and 75,044 biset state orbits.
Of these, 1564 fail the necessary first-homology test and
53,047 fail the second-homology test.  The remaining 20,433
literal categories divide exactly as follows:
\[
 20433=20357\ \text{verified mod-two square primitives}
          +76\ \text{preserved square frontiers}.
\]
All four execution stages have observed zero exits.  The literal
checker verifies 44,400,909 associativity, 33,264,924 cocycle,
and 330,923,392 primitive equations.  It does not mislabel the
76 frontier records as mod-two zero squares.

Every frontier belongs to one of exactly two literal source
hulls, with 38 records in each: order-eleven input indices 96
and 127, respectively global hull indices 109 and 140.  Both
have verified projective full three-dimensional row modules
among the 109 certificates of
Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-remaining-four-row-projective-hulls}.
Their unconditional rational zero-product theorem excludes
all 76 original collages.  A projective plane would have a
nonzero rational square, irrespective of the mod-two square
record.  No reported higher-homology number is used as a
premise, and the separately prepared higher-homology adapter
is not executed for this completion.

The final source join records every frontier's original
certificate ordinal and full line hash, reconstructs its
entire typed multiplication table from the action streams,
and binds its literal source table to the corresponding
projective-module section.  The square-zero and rationally
excluded lists are disjoint and exhaust the 20,433 records
through the actual ends of all files.  The frozen finite
receipt is
\path{remaining_faithful_hull_square11/actual_root_reviewed1/complete_verified.json}.
When the faithful quotient of an original eleven-element
source has order eleven, it is a monoid isomorphism and the
coend map $[s,h]\mapsto s q^{-1}(h)$ is an actual biset
isomorphism.  Thus the present five-state exclusion applies
to the original collage, with no transfer of higher
boundedness through a proper quotient.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Proved: the eleven--three--five two-object budget]
\label{CP2:A-oriented-nineteen-eleven-three-five}
No two-object triangular category with corner orders eleven
and three and five arrows in the indicated source-to-target
direction has classifying-space homotopy type $\mathbb CP^2$.
Its opposite is excluded as well.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The order-three corners other than $R_3$ have projective
trivial right rational modules.  Exact opposite-corner orbit
comparison reduces their collages to ordinary acts over the
eleven-element source without changing rational homology or
its ring.  Boundedness is then inherited through that actual
rational equivalence, and
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-bounded-eleven-acts} excludes the result.
This is the same small-opposite-corner gate used in the initial
nineteen-arrow scope.  It remains to take the opposite corner
to be $R_3$.

Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-eleven-three-five-remaining-band-shapes}
is exhaustive for a hypothetical projective plane: the
nonzero rational corner restriction forces positive source
second homology; all two-row minimal ideals, including their
nontrivial local groups, are excluded by the five-state
theorem; three-by-two sources are excluded by
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-eleven-three-five-three-by-two-complete};
and the pure five-by-two band is excluded by its projective
row module.  The only remaining source shapes are therefore
four-by-two and three-by-three.

Their positive-second-homology faithful images have exactly
the complete 165-target cover.  The fourteen proper images
are excluded by
Lemma~\ref{CP2:A-eleven-proper-remaining-hulls-complete}, using
only the preserved low homology and pullback of the square.
The other 151 are actual equal-order images and are excluded
by Lemma~\ref{CP2:A-eleven-actual-remaining-hulls-complete}.
These disjoint cases exhaust the budget.  Opposition preserves
the classifying-space homotopy type, giving the dual assertion.
\end{proof}
