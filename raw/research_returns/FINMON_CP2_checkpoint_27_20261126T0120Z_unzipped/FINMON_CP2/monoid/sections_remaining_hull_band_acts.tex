\subsection{Small acts for the two remaining faithful covers}

\begin{lemma}[Proved: a complete core description of rectangular-band acts]
\label{CP2:A-small-rectangular-band-acts-by-partitions}
Let $K=I\times J$ be a rectangular band and let $S$ be a
finite right $K$-act with no point fixed by all of $K$.
Its core $U=SK$ is a disjoint union of orbits indexed by a
finite set $A$.  Each orbit is the block set of a partition
$\pi_a$ of $J$ with at least two blocks.  On that orbit,
$(i,j)$ sends every point to the block $\pi_a(j)$.
For each transient point $x\in S\setminus U$ and each
row $i\in I$, choose an orbit $a(x,i)\in A$; then
\[
                  x(i,j)=\pi_{a(x,i)}(j).
\]
Conversely all such data define a right $K$-act without a
fixed point, and every such act occurs this way.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For a point $u=x(i,j)$ in the core, one has
$u(k,l)=x(i,l)$.  Hence its orbit is independent of $j$
and its action is independent of $k$.  The surjection
$J\to uK$, given by $l\mapsto u(k,l)$, defines its
partition.  If two such orbits meet, the same identity
shows that all their labeled images agree, so the orbits
coincide.  A one-point orbit is exactly a common fixed
point and is excluded by hypothesis.

For a transient point $x$, its images along each fixed
row lie in one of these orbits, and their column labels
are forced.  This yields the stated choices.  Conversely,
the formula makes $(x(i,j))(k,l)=x(i,l)$, on both core
and transient points, which is precisely the action
law.  Every core orbit is attained from its own points,
so the specified core is exactly $SK$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Proved: the complete small input forms]
\label{CP2:A-remaining-hulls-small-band-forms}
The numbers of no-fixed-point right band acts on two,
three, four, and five states, up to state relabeling,
are respectively
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrr}
K&2&3&4&5\\\hline
4\times2&1&1&2&9\\
3\times3&3&4&10&43.
\end{array}
\]
These forms, together with the unconditional fixed-state
argument, cover every induced state size at most five
needed for the two faithful actor covers.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Enumerate the partitions of the labeled column set with
at least two blocks.  Choose their multisets with total
block count at most the prescribed state count, and then
enumerate every transient row-choice tuple from the
lemma.  Comparing the complete lists of maps under every
state permutation removes exactly the isomorphic acts.

For two columns there is one eligible partition.  A core
has one or two two-point orbits.  The latter uses four
states; on five states its remaining point has a
four-row choice tuple modulo interchange of the two
orbits, giving $2^4/2=8$ possibilities.  Together with
the one-orbit form this gives nine.

For three columns there are three two-block partitions
and one three-block partition.  On five states, the
one-orbit possibilities contribute four.  Two two-point
orbits of equal partition type give $3\cdot2^3/2=12$
possibilities; distinct types give
$\binom32 2^3=24$.  A two-point and a three-point orbit
with no transient point contribute three more.  Thus
the total is $4+12+24+3=43$.  The smaller state counts
follow from the same cases.

The separately authored input constructor checks every
literal band-action equation and compares the complete
canonical map sets at sizes four and five with the
earlier frozen input families.  It writes all forms and
their exact source-hull identifications in
\texttt{remaining\_faithful\_hull\_square11/inputs\_verified.json}.
No action of a larger source or opposite corner has yet
been omitted or classified by this input calculation.
\end{proof}
