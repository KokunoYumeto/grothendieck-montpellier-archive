\subsection{The fifty-five-source six-state frontier}

\begin{proposition}[Proved: complete source reduction for $(10,3,6)$]
\label{CP2:A-nineteen-ten-three-six-source-reduction}
In the jointly oriented nineteen-arrow scope of
Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-two-object-nineteen-initial-scope}, a hypothetical
projective-plane category with corner and cross-hom sizes $(10,3,6)$
must have opposite corner $R_3$ and one of the fifty-five source
tables in the projection described below.  Forty-four have minimal
band $3\times2$ and eleven have minimal band $2\times4$.
All fourteen positive-second-homology source classes with
nontrivial minimal local group are excluded for every state count.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The complete positive source census has $157145$ ordinary
isomorphism classes, with $157131$ rectangular-band minimal
ideals and fourteen nontrivial minimal local groups.
The complete opposite catalog has seven ordinary classes.
For every opposite corner except $R_3$, the trivial right
rational module is projective.  The actual exact-orbit comparison
gives a rational homology equivalence to an ordinary act cone
over the original source, with at most six states.  Its bounded
homology and the order-ten instance of
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-bounded-eleven-acts} exclude the required square.

Each line is applied after the preceding lines.  The full earlier

For the present state count, its forty-four finite rows and all

The supplied source-proof flags are all zero.

The all-state actor-image gates have unconditional zero-product

The actual proper-retract and corner-reduction gates now use the

Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-global-lower-nineteen}.
Thus no transfer of bounded homology through a mere
second-cohomology isomorphism is needed.

For the fourteen nontrivial-group sources, apply

Their minimal ideals have two rows, two columns and local

The target has zero degree-two products for every state count

Naturality therefore excludes all fourteen sources.

For these specific tables, the fresh certificate

source associativity equations, $1400$ quotient-product equations,
$3024$ target associativity equations and $280$ averaged-column

This is an all-state algebraic comparison.
\end{proof}
