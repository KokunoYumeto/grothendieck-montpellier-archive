\subsection{Analytic completion of the $(7,3,8)$ budget}

\begin{proposition}[Proved: the $(7,3,8)$ oriented piece]
\label{CP2:A-oriented-eighteen-seven-three-eight}
The $(7,3,8)$ piece of the joint twenty-two-budget orientation is
completely excluded by the source classification and the proved
all-state obstructions.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The complete positive seven-element source catalog has $43$ classes.
There are no nontrivial-minimal-group source classes at this order:
a minimal ideal with at least two rows and two columns and nontrivial
local group already has at least eight elements, before adjoining the
external identity.  Of the seven ordinary three-element opposite
monoids, exactly one has more than one minimal column, namely $R_3$.
The remaining opposite cases have already been removed by the joint
orientation's proved small-corner reductions.

The exact disjoint source partition, in the stated priority order, is
\[
 43=28+1+5+1+6+1+1.
\]
The $28$ first sources have the previously verified local or projective
row-character graph witnesses.  The next source has an actual
semicentral idempotent retraction, and the next five have the integral
idempotent-pair quotient.  Those actual reductions would give a
smaller-arrow $\mathbb{CP}^2$ model and contradict the already proved
unrestricted seventeen-arrow exclusion.
The remaining source $20$ has the independently certified stable first
syzygy.  The six sources $3,15,19,28,34,39$ are covered by
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-seven-triangular-family-right-zero}.
Finally source $42$ is the identity-adjoined $3\times2$ rectangular band
and is covered by Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-two-column-band-graph-source};
source $41$ is the identity-adjoined $2\times3$ band and is covered by
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-two-by-three-band-right-zero-all-states}.
These last two theorems apply to every finite nonempty connecting
biset, so no eight-state action enumeration is needed.

The independent source join
\path{monoid/certificates/seven_three_eight_analytic_complete.json}
reconstructs all $43$ literal multiplication tables and their $14749$
associativity equations, verifies the minimal rectangular coordinates,
and canonically relabels every table under all identity-fixing
permutations.  The resulting set is exactly the positive rational
second-homology subset of the complete ordinary seven-element census.
It recomputes both actual-reduction predicates, checks all six
triangular-family table identifications, and binds every remaining
source to the appropriate reviewed theorem or algebra certificate.
It also independently reconstructs the unique required opposite
three-element class.  Every source is assigned once and the remainder
is empty.  Earlier failed or unfinished action runs are preserved but
supply no premise of this analytic closure.
\end{proof}
