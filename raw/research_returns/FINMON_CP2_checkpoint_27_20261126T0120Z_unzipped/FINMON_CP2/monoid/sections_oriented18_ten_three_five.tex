\subsection{The final jointly oriented eighteen-arrow budget}

\begin{theorem}[Proved: the complete $(10,3,5)$ budget]
\label{CP2:A-oriented-eighteen-ten-three-five}
There is no $\mathbb CP^2$ category in the jointly oriented
$(|M|,|N|,|S|)=(10,3,5)$ scope of
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-two-object-eighteen-oriented-twenty-two}.
The scope includes all $157145$ ordinary source classes with positive
rational second homology: $157131$ with trivial minimal local group
and $14$ with nontrivial minimal local group.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The complete source census is
\path{monoid/certificates/source10_complete_manifest.json}.
The target input is the complete catalog of seven ordinary
three-element monoids.  After the already proved small-corner
reductions, its unique required opposite corner is $R_3$, the
two-element right-zero band with an identity adjoined.

The exact source partition is as follows.  The counts are disjoint:
each line is applied after the preceding lines.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lr}

\hline
Previously checked graph, retract, pair and stable-syzygy gates & $127156$\\
General $2\times2$ actor-quotient obstruction & $27437$\\
General $2\times3$ actor-quotient obstruction & $1088$\\

Pure-band fourth Betti-number divisibility & $1$\\

\hline
Total with trivial minimal local group & $157131$\\

\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The complete partition, all literal-source links, and the explicit
local-to-full index bijection for the last line are recorded in
\path{monoid/certificates/final_m10_projection_verified.json}.
Its independent root and referee reconstructions check all $157131$
assignments.  The $44$ projected input rows change only their source
indices; their multiplication tables, minimal-band coordinates and
actor fields are unchanged.  All have a $3\times2$ minimal band.
The source proof flags on this projection are all zero.
In particular no new unverified algebraic omission is used inside
the final finite calculation.

Each actual actor-image transfer uses a theorem valid without a
boundedness assumption on the target.  The two-column bounded
obstruction is applied to the original hypothetical $\mathbb CP^2$
collage.  The actual arrow-reducing gates use the independently
proved exclusion through seventeen arrows.  Thus the partition does
not infer bounded target homology from a mere second-cohomology
isomorphism.

The finite producer and independent fixed-order scope checker are
one-guard derivatives of the previously reviewed engines: the sole
change admits exactly these $44$ sources at $(10,3,4)$ or $(10,3,5)$.
Four fresh builds, two of each source, exited successfully and gave
byte-identical pairs.  The closed four-state control checked $1550$
right-act orbits and $2715$ biset orbits; all $490$ retained literal
categories had zero square.  The five-state production then gave
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{right-act orbits}&=11344,\\
\text{left-action scopes}&=9026,\\
\text{biset orbits}&=32450
   =19786+9190+3474.
\end{aligned}
\]
The three summands are, respectively, proper equivariant biset

literal categories.  Every one of the $3474$ retained categories
has an independently verified primitive over $\mathbb F_2$ for its degree-two square.
There are no unexcluded records and no proof-omitted left records.
All right-scope and left-scope orbit weights, full stream counts,
literal table reconstructions, and gzip end markers are joined;
all four production stages have observed exit code zero.

The nontrivial-local-group supplement contains all fourteen required
source classes.  It has $4324$ biset orbits and $1563$ retained

third homology over $\mathbb F_2$.  Thus the complete budget has $5037$ retained
literal checks, comprising $4715$ zero-square certificates and

The complete source, proof, numerical and group-case join is
\path{monoid/certificates/ten_three_five_complete.json}.
No interrupted or historical incomplete stream is a premise.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Proved: all twenty-two jointly oriented budgets]
\label{CP2:A-all-two-object-eighteen-budgets}
Every one of the twenty-two jointly oriented two-object
eighteen-arrow budgets is excluded.  Together with the proved
orientation and strong-support reductions, there is no two-object
category with eighteen arrows whose classifying space is
$\mathbb CP^2$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The immutable manifest
\path{monoid/certificates/two_object18_22_complete_snapshot.json}
adds only the preceding theorem to the already checked twenty-one
budget snapshot.  Its exact additive-branch derivation preserves
all twenty-one earlier rows, all historical snapshots, and the
complete budget inventory.  The resulting counts are twenty-two
closed budgets and zero pending budgets.  The scope remains the
proved joint orientation; it is not a claim that either ordered
corner profile is excluded independently of that reduction.
\end{proof}
