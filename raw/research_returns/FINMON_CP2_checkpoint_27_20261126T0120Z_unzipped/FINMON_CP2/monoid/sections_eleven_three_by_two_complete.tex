\subsection{The minimal three-by-two family in the eleven--three--five budget}

\begin{theorem}[Proved: the complete three-by-two source family is excluded]
\label{CP2:A-eleven-three-five-three-by-two-complete}
A collage with source monoid of order eleven having a
$3\times2$ rectangular-band minimal ideal, opposite corner
$R_3$, and five connecting arrows cannot have the homotopy
type of $\mathbb CP^2$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Suppose that the collage is a projective plane.  Its nonzero
rational degree-two square forces a nonzero restriction to
the source corner: the opposite corner is rationally
acyclic, and the two-block relative products vanish.
Thus the source has positive rational second homology.
Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-three-by-two-faithful-hulls-through11}
then gives its complete faithful actor-image cover, and
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-eleven-three-five-faithful-image-split}
partitions that cover into exactly fifty-one proper images
of orders seven through ten and fifty equal-order images.

Every proper-image case is excluded by
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-eleven-proper-faithful-images-complete}.
This branch includes all induced state sizes from one to
five.  Its $17\,365$ retained literal collages are covered
by $17\,159$ mod-two square primitives and $206$
unconditional rational product-zero instances.  Neither
higher homology nor boundedness of an induced target is
used in that branch.

For an equal-order image $q:M\to H$, the surjection is a
monoid isomorphism.  The coend map
\[
        S\otimes_M H\longrightarrow S,
        \qquad [s,h]\longmapsto s q^{-1}(h)
\]
is an isomorphism of bisets.  Consequently this target is
the actual five-state collage, and its positive third
homology is a valid projective-plane obstruction.

The fifty equal-order sources were enumerated on all
five-state band acts.  Source-fixed states are excluded by
the unconditional fixed-state lemma.  For the others, the
independent fixed-order scope computation verifies all
$33\,070$ right-action orbits and $84\,032$ biset orbits,
including complete stabilizers and weights.  No
whole-source, proper-retract, or higher-homology pruning
is used in this enumeration.

After the necessary tests $b_1(\mathbb F_2)=0$ and
$b_2(\mathbb F_2)=1$, exactly $25\,730$ literal collages
remain.  For $25\,255$, the independent normalized-bar
checker verifies an explicit equation
$\delta w=u\smile u$ over $\mathbb F_2$.  The remaining
$475$ have nonzero mod-two squares and were retained in
the original stream.  A separate checker subsequently
verifies positive mod-two third homology for each of
these $475$ actual collages.  Its selection hint is not
used as a homology certificate: the full literal bar
rank calculation is repeated independently.

The exact original record indices and hashes partition
the $25\,730$ records into those two disjoint sets.  The
adapted third-homology stream changes only the reason
field, and every multiplication table and cochain remains
identical.  Thus every equal-order possibility is excluded
by a checked square or a checked third-homology obstruction.
The four observed zero stage exits, original full scope,
literal category links, and exact subsequent partition
are joined with the completed proper branch in
\texttt{eleven\_three\_by\_two\_complete.json}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark*}[Exact remaining scope]
This theorem excludes the minimal-$3\times2$ source
family, not the entire $(11,3,5)$ budget.  The larger
$4\times2$ and $3\times3$ minimal bands have separate
faithful covers and still require their own square
arguments or complete finite checks.  The preceding
homology argument is confined to equal-order images;
it is not applied to a proper actor quotient.
\end{remark*}
