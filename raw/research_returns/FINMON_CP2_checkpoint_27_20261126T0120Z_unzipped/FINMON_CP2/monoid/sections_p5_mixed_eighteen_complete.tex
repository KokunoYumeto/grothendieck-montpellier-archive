\subsection{Completion of the five--five--eight profile}

\begin{theorem}[Proved: the complete $(5,5,8)$ exclusion]
\label{CP2:A-five-five-eight-complete}
No two-object collage with two five-element endomorphism
monoids and eight connecting arrows has classifying space
of type $\CP^2$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If neither corner is $P_5$, both have zero rational second
cohomology, so the relative-cup lemma applies.  If both
corners are $P_5$, the complete eight-state double-$P_5$
certificate theorem applies.  It remains to treat precisely
one $P_5$ corner, which may be put at the source by
opposition and the self-opposition isomorphism of $P_5$.

Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-P5-mixed-eight-scope} reduces this
case to the twenty-nine right forms $1,\ldots,29$ against
the forty-two non-$P_5$ opposite monoids specified there.
The literal normal forms satisfy
\[
 R_{00}=R_{10},\qquad R_{01}=R_{11}
\]
exactly at indices $1,4,10,20,23$ among those twenty-nine.
Lemma~\ref{CP2:A-P5-row-independent} excludes these five
forms for every one of the forty-two opposite corners:
their orders are at most seven, as required by that lemma.
This removes $210$ entire input scopes by proof, leaving
exactly $24\cdot42=1008$ finite scopes.

The separately named fixed-order checker exhausts those
$1008$ scopes and reports
\[
\begin{array}{c|r}
\text{raw compatible left actions}&24\,369\,241\\
\text{state-isomorphism orbits}&8\,626\,346\\
\text{wrong first homology}&17\,635\\
\text{wrong second homology}&7\,069\,558\\
\text{retained zero-square certificates}&1\,539\,135\\
\text{retained positive-third-homology certificates}&18.
\end{array}
\]
The four orbit exclusions sum to the stated complete orbit
count.  Every retained literal table and its square primitive
or third-homology obstruction is independently checked.
The exact action-to-category linker checks all compositions
and reasons in the preserved full certificate stream.
Thus every required finite scope is excluded.

The closed receipt is
\path{p5_mixed_s8.row_complete_verified.json} under the
mapped \path{monoid/certificates/two18_p5_runs} archive root.
Its join program is \path{monoid/code/join_mixed8_row_scope.py}.
It checks the exact Cartesian scope partition, derives
the five omitted right indices from their literal maps,
reconstructs the forty-two opposite indices from the full
$228$-table catalog, and consumes every full action record.
In particular, it distinguishes the $8\,626\,346$ independently
enumerated finite orbits from the $2\,035\,161$ additional
records in analytically omitted scopes.  Their sum is the
$10\,661\,507$ records in the preserved unpruned stream.
The $233\,320$ retained records among the latter also pass
the literal checker, but their enumeration is not needed
as a premise of the proved omission.

The earlier unpruned independent scope process was stopped
with observed exit status $130$ after the complete producer,
literal and exact-link checks had finished.  Its incomplete
scope output is explicitly unused.  The separately reviewed
refined checker consumes the entire same closed stream,
including every omitted record, and checks its gzip end.
The new proof therefore uses neither an interrupted census
nor an assumed equality of refined and unpruned counts.
\end{proof}
