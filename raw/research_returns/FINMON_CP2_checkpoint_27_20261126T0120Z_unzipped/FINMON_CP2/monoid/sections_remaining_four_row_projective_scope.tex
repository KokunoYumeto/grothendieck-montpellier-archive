\begin{corollary}[Proved: the remaining proper and equal-order target scopes]
\label{CP2:A-remaining-hull-projectivity-scope}
In the 165-hull cover for the remaining minimal-band shapes of
the $(11,3,5)$ budget, Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-remaining-four-row-projective-hulls}
unconditionally excludes 11 of the 14 proper target hulls and
98 of the 151 equal-order target hulls.  The proper targets not
settled by that proposition are exactly the following three:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
 \text{order}&\text{minimal band}&\text{global hull index}&
 \text{index in the frozen order-ten input}\\\hline
 10&4\times2&1&0\\
 10&4\times2&4&3\\
 10&3\times3&153&12
\end{array}
\]
The corresponding equal-order remainder consists of 42
four-by-two hulls and all 11 three-by-three hulls.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The independently checked section file is joined to every literal
input line, preserving its original local index and its full
table hash, by
\path{monoid/code/join_remaining_row_projectivity_scope_v2.py}.
The resulting receipt
\path{remaining_faithful_hulls11/four_row_projectivity_input_scope_join.json}
checks all 165 distinct global indices, all three original source
files and their unchanged all-zero proof flags.  Its exact
partition has sizes $11+3$ in the proper branch and $98+53$ in
the equal-order branch.  In particular, this is a literal
source-table join, not a comparison of aggregate counts.

For a proper target, the actual quotient comparison of
Corollary~\ref{CP2:A-remaining-faithful-hull-comparison} is an
isomorphism on rational second cohomology.  An unconditional
zero-product theorem for that target therefore pulls back to
the original collage, regardless of its induced state size.
For an equal-order image the quotient and its coend biset are
actual isomorphisms, so the same obstruction applies directly.
No higher target homology or target boundedness enters either
use of the projective-row theorem.
\end{proof}
