\subsection{Faithful actor covers for the remaining larger bands}

\begin{proposition}[Proved: finite covers for the four-by-two and three-by-three shapes]
\label{CP2:A-remaining-faithful-hulls-through11}
The faithful actor-image monoids of order at most eleven with
positive rational second homology and rectangular-band minimal
ideal of the indicated shape have the following ordinary
isomorphism counts:
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrr|r}
\text{minimal band}&|H|=9&|H|=10&|H|=11&\text{total}\\\hline
4\times2&1&12&140&153\\
3\times3&0&1&11&12.
\end{array}
\]
These $165$ literal tables cover the faithful images of every
source of order at most eleven in those two shapes having
positive rational second homology.  They do not
constitute a census of all original order-eleven monoids.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Write the minimal band as $K=I\times J$, with
$(i,j)(i',j')=(i,j')$, and put $r=|I|$, $c=|J|$.
Every element acts by a pair of transformations $(\sigma,\tau)$,
with product
\[
 (\sigma,\tau)(\sigma',\tau')
       =(\sigma\circ\sigma',\tau'\circ\tau).
\]
The $rc$ pairs of constant transformations and the identity
pair are mandatory.  They are distinct because $r,c\geq2$.
Any set containing these pairs automatically has all products
involving a band element; therefore closure needs to be tested
only on its additional actors.

For $(r,c)=(4,2)$ the full transformation-pair monoid has
$4^4 2^2=1024$ elements.  Its mandatory subset has nine
elements.  The order bound permits at most two of the remaining
$1015$ actors, so the complete search examines
\[
 1+1015+\binom{1015}{2}=515621
\]
subsets.  For $(r,c)=(3,3)$ there are $3^3 3^3=729$
actors, ten mandatory elements, and at most one of the
remaining $719$ actors: exactly $720$ subsets are examined.

The rational second-homology filter is exact linear algebra,
not a modular-rank approximation.  Normalize a bilinear
functional by $L(0,i)=L(j,0)=0$.  It is balanced precisely when
every actor satisfies
\[
 L(\tau(j),i)-L(\tau(0),i)
       -L(j,\sigma(i))+L(j,\sigma(0))=0
       \qquad(1\leq i<r,\ 1\leq j<c).
\]
The dimension of this solution space is
$\dim_{\mathbb Q}H_2(BH;\mathbb Q)$, by the natural
band--Tor formula.  Gaussian elimination over exact fractions
therefore discards exactly the zero-second-homology hulls.

In the four-by-two search, the closed-subset counts at orders
nine, ten, and eleven are $1,205,6449$; the positive-second-
homology counts are $1,198,4900$.  In the three-by-three
search, the closed-subset counts at orders ten and eleven
are $1,177$, of which $1,171$ have positive second homology.
The program compares complete actor sets under all
$r!c!$ row and column relabelings.  These are exactly the
ordinary isomorphisms: every isomorphism preserves the
characteristic minimal band, its row and column permutations
conjugate the actors, and faithfulness then determines each
element.  Conversely a conjugacy of complete actor sets
preserves the displayed multiplication.  No identification
with an opposite monoid is imposed.

The program
\path{monoid/code/enumerate_remaining_faithful_hulls11.py}
reconstructs every canonical multiplication table, re-extracts
its actors, and verifies all $19652$ product entries and
$214710$ associativity equations.  All fourteen faithful
classes through order ten agree exactly with the corresponding
classes extracted from the earlier complete source catalogs.
Full tables, balanced ranks, closed-subset multiplicities,
canonical actor sets and catalog identifications are stored
with the separately bound
\texttt{remaining\_faithful\_hulls11/scope\_verified.json}.
The resulting canonical counts are those in the statement.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Proved: the comparison required to use these covers]
\label{CP2:A-remaining-faithful-hull-comparison}
Let an eleven-element source in a hypothetical $(11,3,5)$
projective-plane collage have minimal band $4\times2$ or
$3\times3$.  Its faithful actor quotient belongs to the
corresponding cover above.  If its order is smaller than
eleven, the induced collage has at most five states and the
same integral first and second homology as the original.
If its order is eleven, the quotient is an isomorphism and
the induced collage is the original five-state collage up
to isomorphism.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The nonzero global degree-two square forces a nonzero source
restriction, because the opposite corner $R_3$ is rationally
acyclic and the product of two relative classes from the
two-block decomposition vanishes.  Hence the original
source has positive rational second homology.  The faithful
actor quotient is bijective on the minimal band, so
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-band-quotient-integral-low-degree} preserves
the corner second homology and the collage first and second
homology.  The induced state set is the quotient coend
$S\otimes_M H$, of cardinality at most $|S|=5$.
When $|H|=|M|=11$, the surjective monoid map is bijective;
the map $[s,h]\mapsto s q^{-1}(h)$ is an isomorphism of the
induced biset with $S$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark*}[Coverage does not settle the remaining square questions]
No collage in these two covers is excluded by the census
alone.  A proper-image target requires an unconditional
square obstruction or a necessary first- or second-homology
obstruction.  Its higher homology and its proper retracts
cannot be used as projective-plane obstructions for the
original source without an additional comparison theorem.
In particular, the two-column graph formula is not being
applied to the three-by-three shape.
\end{remark*}
