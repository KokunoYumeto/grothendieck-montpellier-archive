\subsection{Projective full row modules in the four-by-two cover}

\begin{proposition}[Proved: 109 unconditional projective-row targets]
\label{CP2:A-remaining-four-row-projective-hulls}
Among the 153 positive-second-homology faithful $4\times2$ actor hulls
of Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-remaining-faithful-hulls-through11}, the
certificates described below verify projectivity of the full
three-dimensional row augmentation module for 109 hulls.  Their
distribution by monoid order $9,10,11$ is respectively $1,10,98$.
For every such hull $H$, every finite left-$R_3$, right-$H$ biset,
and its associated two-object collage, all rational products of
degree-two cohomology classes are zero.  This assertion has no
bounded-homology or state-count hypothesis.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Put $A=\mathbb QH/\mathbb QK$, where $K$ is the minimal band.
Let $q=\dim A$ and let
\[
 V=\ker(\mathbb Q\{0,1,2,3\}\longrightarrow\mathbb Q)
\]
be the full row augmentation module, with basis
$v_j=[j]-[0]$ for $j=1,2,3$.  The band acts by constant row maps,
so annihilates $V$.  The literal outside elements give a basis of
$A$.  There is a canonical surjective $A$-linear map
\[
 P:A^{\oplus3}\longrightarrow V,\qquad
 (a_1,a_2,a_3)\longmapsto\sum_{j=1}^{3}a_jv_j.
\]
Surjectivity follows from the identity element.

For each of the 109 displayed positive certificates, the supplied
rational matrix $X:V\to A^{\oplus3}$ satisfies
\[
 PX=I_3,\qquad
 \operatorname{diag}(L_a,L_a,L_a)X=XV_a
 \quad(a\text{ an outside basis element}),
\]
where $L_a$ is left multiplication on the contracted algebra and
$V_a$ is the full row action on $V$.  These equations make $X$
an $A$-linear section, hence make $V$ a direct summand of a finite
free module.  The projective-row theorem
\ref{CP2:A-projective-row-two-column-all-states} therefore applies.

The producer \path{monoid/code/certify_remaining_four_row_projectivity.py}
uses exact rational elimination only to find candidate sections.
The independent checker
\path{monoid/code/check_remaining_four_row_projectivity.py}
does not import that solver or its action matrices.  From each
literal multiplication table it reconstructs the full four-row
permutation-column images
$a([j]-[0])=[\sigma_a(j)]-[\sigma_a(0)]$, checks that every
band element annihilates them, and verifies both section identities
coefficient by coefficient.  The checker verifies 20,079 rational
coefficient equations and joins each certificate to its exact
literal source table.  The complete section and verification
receipts are
\path{remaining_faithful_hulls11/four_row_projectivity_sections.json}
and
\path{remaining_faithful_hulls11/four_row_projectivity_verified.json}
under the registered nineteen-arrow working directory.
\end{proof}
