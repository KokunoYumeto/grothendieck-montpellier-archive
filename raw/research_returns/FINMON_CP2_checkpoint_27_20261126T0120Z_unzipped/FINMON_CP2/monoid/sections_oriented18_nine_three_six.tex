\subsection{Complete closure of the $(9,3,6)$ budget}

\begin{theorem}[Proved: the jointly oriented $(9,3,6)$ piece]
\label{CP2:A-oriented-eighteen-nine-three-six}
The budget $(|M|,|N|,|S|)=(9,3,6)$ contributes no
$\mathbb CP^2$ category in the joint orientation of
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-two-object-eighteen-oriented-twenty-two}.
Its entire positive-second-homology source scope is covered,
including the two sources with a nontrivial minimal local group.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-positive-corners-nine} gives exactly
$8046$ ordinary source classes: $8044$ with rectangular minimal
band and two with local group $C_2$.  The complete ordinary
order-three opposite catalog leaves only $R_3$ after the
proved small-corner reductions.

Among the band sources, all $7784$ two-by-two classes are
covered by Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-two-by-two-band-right-zero-all-states},
and all $129$ two-by-three classes are covered by
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-two-by-three-band-general-right-zero}.
Both theorems use actual collage maps and are uniform in
the state count and homology.

There are $129$ three-by-two source classes.  For $125$ of them,
the faithful simultaneous row-and-column actor image has a
projective full row-augmentation module.  The exact section
identities in the preceding subsection are checked on the
literal target algebra, and the actual quotient is bijective
on the minimal band.  Corollary~\ref{CP2:A-projective-actor-image-cup-transfer}
therefore kills all products.  This uses an unconditional
target theorem; it does not infer bounded target homology
from an $H^2$ isomorphism.

Three remaining original source tables, with indices
$7956,8034,8037$, have contracted algebra
\[
 A=\mathbb Q1\oplus J,\qquad \dim J=2,\qquad J^2=0.
\]
The literal outside elements are $1,a,b$ and every product
among $a,b$ lies in the band.  Thus the character
$(1,a,b)\mapsto(1,0,0)$ has nilpotent kernel.
Their full row modules have dimension two.
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-local-three-outside-row-graph} applies
directly to each hypothetical original $\mathbb CP^2$
collage, whose rational homology is bounded.
The final three-by-two source, index $8038$, is covered
without any boundedness hypothesis by
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-nine-source8038-all-states}.
This gives the exact three-by-two partition $129=125+3+1$.

The two remaining band shapes are pure bands with an identity.
The $4\times2$ source is covered by
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-two-column-band-graph-source}.
For the $2\times4$ source there are six states and a hypothetical
model has fourth Betti number one, so
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-two-by-four-band-six-states} gives the
required zero products.  The band scope is therefore exhausted:
\[
 8044=7784+129+125+3+1+1+1.
\]

Finally the independently complete $C_2$-source supplement
covers both nontrivial-group sources on six states opposite
$R_3$.  It has $13\,229$ raw compatible actions and $1913$
state-isomorphism orbits.  The latter split exactly as
\[
1913=563\ \text{wrong }H_1
     +804\ \text{wrong }H_2
     +406\ \text{zero square}
     +140\ \text{nonzero }H_3.
\]
The full independent scope, literal bar certificates, ordered
stream joins and closed execution receipts verify all $546$
retained categories.  Hence neither of these last two source
classes supplies a model, completing the whole budget.
\end{proof}
