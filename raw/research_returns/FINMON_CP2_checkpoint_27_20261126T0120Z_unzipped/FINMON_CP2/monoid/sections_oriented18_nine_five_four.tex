\begin{proposition}[Proved: the $(9,5,4)$ oriented piece]
\label{CP2:A-oriented-eighteen-nine-five-four}
The $(9,5,4)$ piece of the joint twenty-two-budget orientation is
completely excluded, including the opposite monoid $P_5$ and the two
sources with nontrivial maximal group in their minimal ideal.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Certificate proof]
The complete positive source catalog has $8044$ classes with a minimal
rectangular band and two classes with local group $C_2$.
For all $8044$ band-source classes, the finite scope includes every one
of the $43$ required five-element opposite monoids.  The independent
right-action census has $349013$ raw extensions and $199316$
state-isomorphism classes.  After the previously proved gates,
$3448127$ opposite-action scopes yield $15911359$ raw compatible
actions and $14400712$ biset state orbits, partitioned by
\[
 14400712=9339373+16657+3725883+(1318685+114).
\]
The terms are proper retractions, wrong first homology, wrong second
homology, and zero-square or positive-third-homology literal tables.
All $1318799$ retained tables pass the independent full literal checker
and exact ordered table linker.  The original fixed-order scope checker
and every pipeline stage finish with observed exit zero.  Source,
normal-form, target, executable, count and ordered-stream bindings agree
in \path{m9_n5_s4_band.complete_verified.json} under
\path{monoid/certificates/two18_oriented_runs}.

The two remaining local-$C_2$ source classes are covered by
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-C2-source-eighteen-complete}: their corresponding
closed piece has $1904$ zero-square and $412$
positive-third-homology literal certificates.  The exact current scope
join binds those independent receipts to this same $(9,5,4)$ budget.
Together the two source types exhaust the positive catalog, as required
by the joint orientation.  No later row-sign omission or graph-chain
omission is used in the band-source computation here.
\end{proof}
