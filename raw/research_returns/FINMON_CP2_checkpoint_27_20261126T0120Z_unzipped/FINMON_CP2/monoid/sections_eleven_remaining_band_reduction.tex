\subsection{The exact remaining minimal bands in the eleven--three--five family}

\begin{proposition}[Proved: only two source shapes remain]
\label{CP2:A-eleven-three-five-remaining-band-shapes}
If a collage with an eleven-element source, opposite corner $R_3$,
and five connecting states has the homotopy type of $\mathbb CP^2$,
then its source minimal ideal is a rectangular band of shape
$4\times2$ or $3\times3$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The rationally acyclic opposite corner and the relative-product
argument force nonzero rational second homology at the source.
The minimal-ideal coordinates and natural band--Tor formula
therefore require a proper minimal ideal with at least two rows
and two columns.  A group source or a minimal ideal with only
one row or column has zero rational second homology.

Every two-row case, including a nontrivial local group, is
excluded on five states by
Corollary~\ref{CP2:A-Rees-two-row-right-zero-gates}.
For at least three rows and two columns, a nontrivial local
group would give at least $3\cdot2\cdot2+1=13$ elements,
which exceeds eleven.  Thus the remaining ideal is a
rectangular band, and its complete size list is
\[
 (r,c)=(3,2),\quad(4,2),\quad(5,2),\quad(3,3).
\]
The three-by-two family is excluded by
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-eleven-three-five-three-by-two-complete}.

For a five-by-two band, the ten ideal elements leave only
the identity outside the ideal.  The source is exactly
$(I_5\times J_2)^1$.  Its contracted rational algebra is
$\mathbb Q$, and its full four-dimensional row-augmentation
module is projective.  Hence
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-projective-row-two-column-all-states}
kills every rational degree-two product, with no state-count
or boundedness assumption.  Only the two stated shapes remain.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark*}[The residual covers are finite but unexcluded]
The remaining $4\times2$ and $3\times3$ source shapes have the
$165$ faithful hulls of
Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-remaining-faithful-hulls-through11}.
Fourteen hulls have order below eleven and require all induced
state sizes at most five with unconditional obstructions.
The other $151$ have order eleven, so the quotient is an actual
isomorphism and retains the five-state collage.  A faithful-hull
cover, an input preparation, or a successful control run does
not itself exclude either branch.
\end{remark*}
