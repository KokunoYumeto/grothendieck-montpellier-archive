\subsection{The new jointly oriented band-source computation}

The twenty-two ordered budgets in
Corollary~\ref{CP2:A-two-object-eighteen-oriented-twenty-two}
are the scope of the new search.  All four reverse $P_5$
target budgets are retained.  The computational engines
and receipts in this subsection concern the specified
oriented pieces; a single such receipt does not assert
an unrestricted exclusion of its ordered profile.

\begin{lemma}[Proved: the omitted projective opposite corners]
\label{CP2:A-oriented-eighteen-projective-targets}
For all twenty-two budgets, opposite corners whose minimal
ideal has one column may be omitted.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The trivial right rational module of such a corner $N$
is projective, by averaging its minimal local group.
The actual orbit functor from the collage to the ordinary
$M$-act cone on $N\backslash S$ induces a rational
homology equivalence, by the exact derived-orbit comparison
of the bridge.  It consequently induces a rational ring
isomorphism.  If the original collage had projective-plane
type, this ordinary cone would have bounded rational
homology and the same nonzero second square.  Here
$|M|\leq10$, so Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-bounded-eleven-acts}
gives a contradiction.  Boundedness is transferred along
the actual comparison; it is not inferred merely from
an isomorphism in degree two.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Precise input partition.}
The new general band engine accepts $5\leq m\leq10$,
$3\leq n\leq7$, $4\leq s\leq8$, and $m+n+s=18$.
It therefore covers band-source pieces of twenty-one
of the twenty-two budgets.  The budget $(5,4,9)$ needs
a separate nine-state $P_5$ engine.  At source order
nine, two positive classes have nontrivial minimal local
group; at source order ten, fourteen do.  These classes
are separate obligations and are not covered by a
rectangular-band input file.

The required opposite tables are taken from the complete
ordinary catalogs through order seven.  After the preceding
lemma, their counts at orders $3,4,5,6,7$ are respectively
\[
 1,\quad6,\quad43,\quad445,\quad5852.
\]
The count $43$ includes $P_5$.  The exact table indices,
source-family multiplicities, normal-form files and
implementation costs are recorded by
\path{monoid/code/oriented18_cost_inventory.py}.

\paragraph{Right acts and source extensions.}
The complete rectangular-band normal forms are restricted
to those having no fixed state.  The standard core-orbit
description is unchanged: a core orbit is a quotient of
the column set, and every transient state is labelled by
one compatible core-orbit choice for each row.  The new
constructor checks all literal band laws, reconstructs
canonical core and transient signatures, and compares each
stratum with its independent Burnside coefficient count.
There are twenty-four required $(r,c,s)$ input families.
Twenty-three also agree exactly with the no-fixed-state
subsets of earlier complete catalogs; the new family is
the nine-state $2\times2$ band input.

For a fixed band action, the domain of an outside source
actor $a$ is determined by every equation involving
$ka$ and $ak$, $k$ in the minimal band.  The producer
caches that exact ordered pair of product lists.  The
cache includes empty domains and does not identify two
distinct actor keys.  All remaining outside products
are imposed by the associative assignment search.  The
independent checker scans every transformation of the
state set for the same literal equations and assigns
actors in fixed order, without product propagation.
Both computations use complete state automorphism groups.

\paragraph{Commuting left actions and exclusions.}
Every required opposite table is represented in the full
centralizer of each right action, modulo its full
stabilizer.  The reviewed two-constant, central-retract
and row-independent $P_5$ lemmas are applied only with
their stated hypotheses.  A proper equivariant biset
retract has its literal idempotent witness checked on
both sides.  The actual cut-retraction theorem then
produces a smaller projective-plane category, contradicting
the completed seventeen-arrow lower bound.

All other action orbits are subjected to independent
normalized-bar checks over $\mathbb F_2$.  Incorrect
$H_1$ or $H_2$ dimensions exclude the table.  Retained
tables have either positive $H_3$, or a literal degree-two
cocycle generator whose Alexander--Whitney square is
the coboundary of the supplied degree-three cochain.
The literal checker reconstructs all typed compositions,
identity and associativity equations, and all cochain
equations independently.  A separate streaming linker
reconstructs the entire category table from its two
actions and compares every retained table and reason
with the literal-certificate stream in exact order.
Every input stream is consumed through its gzip trailer.

For these budgets the largest number of normalized pairs is
\[
 (m-1)^2+(n-1)^2+s(m+n-2)=140,
\]
attained at $(10,3,5)$.  The new independent checker uses
four machine words, hence $256$ bits, for degree-two
vectors.  Its degree-four word equations still check
every possible fourth arrow, since there are at most
eighteen arrow labels.  No degree-two component beyond
bit $127$ is discarded.

\begin{proposition}[Proved: complete old-scope and wide-cochain controls]
\label{CP2:A-oriented-eighteen-engine-control}
The new producer, independent scope checker, literal
checker and exact stream linker reproduce the complete
$(9,4,4)$ seventeen-arrow band-source control.  The wide
literal checker also reproduces all $8406$ completed
eight-state $P_5/P_5$ certificates and passes an actual
$140$-pair positive control with six rejecting corruptions.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Certificate proof]
The closed receipt
\path{engine_control_complete_verified.json}, under the
mapped \path{monoid/certificates/two18_oriented_runs}
archive root, binds all four exact source derivations,
their inputs, the independent outputs and the compared
old streams.  The producer and fixed-order checker agree
on $349013$ raw right actions, $199316$ right-action
orbits, $1768990$ raw left actions and $1619622$ biset
orbits.  The $170706$ retained tables consist of $170692$
zero-square certificates and $14$ positive-$H_3$ certificates.
All decompressed right-action, left-action, left-scope
and literal-certificate bytes agree exactly with the
previous complete control.  The $124914$ removed old
right-scope rows all have zero active scope and zero
search counts.

The high-pair control adjoins a new source identity to a
previously checked seventeen-arrow category.  Its normalized
dimensions in degrees one through four are
$(16,140,1252,11252)$.  An independent Python boundary
calculation verifies its cocycle and square primitive.
The C++ checker accepts the control and rejects, for the
specified literal reason, a noncocycle corruption at bit

primitive corruption, a primitive range overflow, and
an identity-law corruption.  The exact bytes and observed
exit statuses are bound in
\path{wide_controls/controls_verified.json}.
\end{proof}

\end{remark*}
