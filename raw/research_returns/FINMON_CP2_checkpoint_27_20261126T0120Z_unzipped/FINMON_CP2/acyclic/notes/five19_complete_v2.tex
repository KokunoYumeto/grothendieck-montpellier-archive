\subsection{Complete five-object acyclic-support exclusion at nineteen arrows}

\begin{theorem}[Proved: five objects with acyclic support through nineteen]
\label{CP2:A-five-object-acyclic-nineteen-complete}
A finite category with five objects, acyclic support, and at most nineteen
arrows cannot have classifying space homotopy equivalent to $\CP^2$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The complete eighteen-arrow exclusion
(Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-global-lower-nineteen}) handles all smaller cardinalities.
At nineteen arrows a projective-plane category must have connected support.
The fresh cardinality enumeration, modulo object relabelling and opposition,
has exactly $445562$ connected profiles. The original profile rules
(Lemma~\ref{CP2:A-nineteen-profile-H2-rules} and Proposition~
\ref{CP2:A-nineteen-actual-profile-reductions}) exclude $444413$ of these.
The graph-block reductions, including
Corollary~\ref{CP2:A-nineteen-graph-gluing-gates}, exclude a further $205$.
Thus the disjoint partition is
\[
445562=444618+944.
\]
An independent program reconstructs every support orbit, cardinality
profile, original reason, recursive second-cohomology test, and graph
witness, and checks the exact ordered residual ledger. Every residual
corner has at most seven arrows.

For each of the $944$ remaining profiles, the finite engine enumerates
corner monoids from the independently checked catalogues, connecting
bisets up to state relabelling, and all remaining composition maps subject
to every associativity equation. The action and composition algorithms
are unchanged from the reviewed eighteen-arrow engine; the separately
checked derivative changes the cardinality guards and capacities only.
All three closed eighteen-arrow controls reproduce their complete
ordered certificate streams and independent checker outputs exactly.

The complete nineteen-arrow run examines $53793527$ weighted associative
tables ($8212458$ normalized tables) and emits $1167390$ retained literal
records. An independent checker reconstructs each literal category and
its normalized bar chains over $\mathbb F_2$. It verifies $1144996$
records by an explicit degree-two generator and a primitive for its
Alexander--Whitney square, and $22394$ records by positive third
homology. No retained record remains unexcluded. Each obstruction is
incompatible with the mod-two cohomology of $\CP^2$.

The separate complete join rehashes all source, catalogue, scope, build,
control, and production dependencies; checks every actual child exit
and complete-profile footer; rereads every compressed literal stream;
reconstructs every source--target histogram; and matches every ordered
uncompressed digest, reason count, and independent checker output.
It verifies all $944$ required profiles with no missing profile or
unexcluded record. The complete receipt is
\texttt{acyclic/outputs/five19\_complete\_independent\_join.json},
with SHA-256
\texttt{0368498a7e9b05769b2d9de84295583cab4844ba1b6af593f8dc64a346d40114}.
Together with the structural partition this proves the assertion.
\end{proof}
