\subsection{The complete checked first-syzygy source supplement}

\begin{theorem}[Proved: exact positive first-syzygy source scope]
\label{CP2:A-stable-syzygy-source-scope}
In the frozen positive rectangular-band source catalogs through
order ten, apply the previously checked graph-character,
extreme-idempotent and integral idempotent-pair reductions.
The following additional source classes have checked first-syzygy
certificates for
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-stable-syzygy-row-graph-obstruction}:
\[
\begin{array}{c|r|r|r|r}
 |M|&\text{catalog size}&\text{previously retained}
      &\text{new certified sources}&\text{remaining}\\\hline
 7&43&9&1&8\\
 8&510&138&69&69\\
 9&8044&2195&691&1504\\
 10&157131&41332&11357&29975
\end{array}
\]
The order-five and order-six catalogs, of sizes one and five,
respectively, already have no retained source under those preceding
reductions and contribute no new rows here.  The new order-seven
source is exactly index $20$.

Each newly certified source is excluded opposite $R_3$ for every
finite nonempty connecting biset with bounded rational homology.
It may also be excluded against every target in the six-target
nonprojective order-four scope when the connecting set has at most
four states.  The latter statement uses $|M|\le10$.
These are source exclusions; they assert no completion of a
remaining finite action search.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
There are $43674$ retained sources before this supplement.  Of
these, $40976$ have a $2\times2$ minimal band and are represented
by $9565$ recorded contracted-algebra and row-character forms.
Every source-to-form association carries a literal permutation of
the contracted monoid basis.  The remaining $2698$ sources have
other band shapes and are kept without any new exclusion.

Exactly $2219$ of the recorded algebra-character forms have
positive certificates.  Each gives an integer $r\ge1$, rational
idempotents $e_i$, bases of the principal projectives $Ae_i$,
and an invertible rational intertwiner
\[
 V^{\oplus r}\oplus\bigoplus_i Ae_i
       \xrightarrow{\cong}\ker(A\xrightarrow{\chi}V).
\]
Thus the canonical free cover $A\to V$ supplies precisely the
$d=1$ exact return required by
Lemma~\ref{CP2:A-stable-syzygy-two-term-amplitude}.

The separate checker
\path{code/check_stable_syzygy_witnesses_root.py}
imports neither the grouping program nor the witness search.
It uses rational arithmetic with independently implemented
elimination.  It checks the algebra and character identities of
all $9565$ forms, all $2219$ positive intertwiners, and every basis
of the $4772$ displayed principal projectives.  The check includes
$319380$ scalar intertwining equations and nonzero determinants.
It then reconstructs all $40976$ source associations directly from
the original monoid tables, checking $1449897$ contracted products
and $243535$ row-character entries.  Every other band shape and
every record without a positive witness remains in scope.

The complete receipt
\path{outputs/stable_syzygy_sources_root_review.json}
records the exact removed and remaining source indices, all input
digests, and the complete witness-stream digest.  The separately
reviewed source and compact scope join is
\path{acyclic/outputs/stable_syzygy_scope_complete.json}.
The disjoint sums are
\[
 12118=1+69+691+11357,\qquad
 31556=8+69+1504+29975,\qquad
 43674=12118+31556.
\]
Only positively checked module data enter these sums; failure of
the witness search has no mathematical consequence.

For a left $R_3$ corner the graph comparison has no state bound,
so Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-stable-syzygy-row-graph-obstruction} applies
directly.  In the six-target order-four scope,
Lemma~\ref{CP2:A-four-corner-graph-chain} supplies the same natural
graph chains for five targets, again without a state bound.
The sole remaining target is the nilpotent four-element corner.
When the connecting set has at most four states and $|M|\le10$,
Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-nilpotent-four-corner-four-states} excludes
that target separately.  This proves exactly the two stated
applications, without extending the nilpotent-target result to
larger connecting sets.
\end{proof}
