\label{CP2:A-eighteen-three-two-object-budgets}

has exactly two objects. In the joint orientation of
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-two-object-eighteen-oriented-scope}, its budget

\[

\end{corollary}

\ref{CP2:A-oriented-eighteen-seven-three-eight} and
\ref{CP2:A-oriented-eighteen-ten-four-four}.

\path{monoid/certificates/two_object18_19_complete_snapshot.json}.

checks all file bindings, the unchanged twenty-two-budget inventory,
and each successive one-budget change. All other profile rows agree
exactly with their preceding snapshots. The separately complete
nontrivial-minimal-group source pieces are included wherever required.
The resulting complement is precisely the three displayed budgets.
This uses the proved unrestricted seventeen-arrow lower bound only
where an actual arrow-reducing comparison is part of the corresponding
proof. Neither incomplete streams nor failed checks supply a
finite-completeness premise.
\end{proof}
