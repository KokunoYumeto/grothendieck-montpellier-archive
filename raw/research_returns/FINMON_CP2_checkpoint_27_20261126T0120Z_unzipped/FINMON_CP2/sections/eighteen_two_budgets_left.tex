\subsection{The twenty-budget join and its remaining sources}

\begin{corollary}[Proved: the complete twenty-budget reduction]
\label{CP2:A-eighteen-two-two-object-budgets}
Every eighteen-arrow category of type $\mathbb{CP}^2$, if one exists,
has exactly two objects and, in the established joint orientation,
budget $(9,3,6)$ or $(10,3,5)$. Identities are included in every count.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The preceding nineteen-budget reduction is joined with the complete
$(8,3,7)$ exclusion. The root checker independently reconstructs all
$510$ positive source tables, checks their $261120$ associativity
equations and the sole required three-element opposite corner.
Its source partition is
\[
 510=492+6+9+1+2,
\]
comprising the general two-by-two actor theorem, six two-by-three
sources, nine two-column sources, one integral-pair reduction, and
two semicentral reductions. The separate twenty-complete snapshot
changes precisely this one budget; every other row and the full
twenty-two-budget inventory are unchanged. The final root receipt is
\path{outputs/eight_three_seven_complete_root_review.json}.
Together with the previous complete exclusions this leaves exactly
the two displayed budgets as the next decision gate.
\end{proof}
