\begin{proposition}[Proved: the additional constant-actor source classes]
\label{CP2:A-constant-actor-source-scope}
In the complete positive band-source catalogs of orders eight, nine
and ten, respectively $27$, $220$ and $2298$ sources satisfy the
hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-constant-actor-left-identity-quotient}.

\begin{proof}[Proof and complete literal scope check]
The source catalogs and their order and completeness are already
independently verified. We scan every literal table, requiring a
$2\times2$ minimal band, one element $e$ of that band giving both
constant actions of every outside element, and one outside element
$q$ satisfying $qv=v$ for every outside element $v$.
Every successful row records the actual $e$ and $q$ and checks
all multiplication equations of the stated quotient to $P_5$.
The complete check is
\path{code/check_constant_actor_source_scope_root.py}, with its
source tables, full witness lists, exact counts and hashes in
\path{outputs/constant_actor_source_scope_root_review.json}.
The intersection with the separately verified frozen source
complements gives the second set of counts. At order eight the
new source indices are exactly
\[

\]
The theorem supplies the actual collage quotient and the required
cohomology isomorphism in degree two. No action prefix or finite
state restriction is used in this cut.
\end{proof}
