\begin{remark*}[Opened comparison for the returning-syzygy argument]
The projective self-extension characterization in
\cite[Proposition~2.9, printed page~8]{CP2-BennisMahdou2006}
is related background.  Its standing hypotheses are commutative,
and its characterization includes an additional Ext-vanishing
condition.  We use neither that characterization nor an assertion
that our characters are strongly Gorenstein projective.
The returning-syzygy lemma above is proved directly for the stated
possibly noncommutative algebra and two-term complex.  The searches,
opened version and exact scope of this comparison are recorded in
\path{notes/post17_periodic_literature_search.md}; we claim no priority.
\end{remark*}
