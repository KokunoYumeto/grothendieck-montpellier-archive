\begin{proposition}[Proved: two universal three-state coned star profiles]
\label{CP2:A-star-eighteen-universal-three-scope}
The original frozen star18 indices $36$ and $56$, with profiles
\[
 (6,1,7,1,3),\qquad (7,1,6,1,3),
\]
are excluded by Corollary~\ref{CP2:A-coned-three-leaf-bounds}.
Together with the preceding star proof partitions, the
$1204$ canonical profiles therefore split into $1162$
proved profiles and $42$ profiles requiring finite checks.
No completion of those forty-two checks is asserted here.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The separately frozen program
\path{converse/scope18/star18_coned_three_supplement.py}
tests all forty-four preceding residual matrices in both
leaf orders. It requires a trivial leaf on a singleton
stalk and at most three arrows to the other leaf, whose
corner order is at most seventeen. These are exactly the
actual-type conclusion of the cited corollary, without
any hypothesis on the center order. The only qualifying
original rows are $36$ and $56$. The old $61$-, $46$- and
$44$-row ledgers and their finite records remain unchanged;
the new ledger contains precisely the other forty-two rows.
Thus $1160+2=1162$ and $44-2=42$.
The independent program
\path{acyclic/code/check_star18_coned_three_supplement.py}
reconstructs all eighty-eight leaf-order tests, both
literal witnesses, and every residual ledger byte without
importing the producer. Its complete receipt is
\path{acyclic/outputs/star18_coned_three_independent_audit.json}.
The separately named
\path{converse/scope18/check_star18_coned_three_join.py}
retains all earlier finite-stream checks and selects only
these forty-two required original indices.
\end{proof}
