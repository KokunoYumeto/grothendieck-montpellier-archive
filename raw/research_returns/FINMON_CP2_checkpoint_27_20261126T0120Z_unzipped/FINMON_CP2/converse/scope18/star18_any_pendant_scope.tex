\begin{proposition}[Proved: the arbitrary-pendant star supplement]
\label{CP2:A-star-eighteen-any-pendant-scope}
Among the $42$ star profiles of
Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-star-eighteen-universal-three-scope},
exactly one further profile is excluded by the arbitrary
singleton-pendant theorem: the original profile $51$, with
center order $6$, leaf orders $5,5$, and both stalks singletons.
Consequently the complete $1,204$-profile star scope is
partitioned into $1,163$ profiles excluded by proofs and
$41$ profiles still requiring their finite certificates.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Each leaf is a singleton pendant with nontrivial corner.
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-singleton-pendant-any-corner} replaces
either leaf corner by its identity and, under the
projective-plane hypothesis, preserves the actual homotopy
type. The resulting literal category has fourteen arrows,
contradicting Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-global-lower-eighteen}.

The producer \path{acyclic/code/star18_any_pendant_corner_scope.py}
tests both leaves of every preceding profile. The independent
checker \path{converse/scope18/check_any_pendant_scopes.py}
reconstructs all $84$ tests, both smaller hom-matrices, the
single excluded original index, and the exact bytes of the
$41$-row ledger. The latter check is recorded in
\path{converse/scope18/any_pendant_scopes_independent.json}.
The separate finalizer
\path{converse/scope18/check_star18_any_pendant_join.py}
retains all previous source, scope, certificate and observed-exit
checks, and selects precisely those $41$ finite premises.
This partition does not assert that an unfinished finite
search has completed.
\end{proof}
