\subsection{Completion of the full-linear eighteen-arrow case}

\begin{theorem}[Proved: full-linear three-object categories through eighteen]
\label{CP2:A-linear-eighteen-complete}
No three-object category with full linear acyclic support
and at most eighteen arrows has classifying space of type
$\CP^2$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-global-lower-eighteen} excludes budgets
at most seventeen.  At eighteen arrows, write the six
positive cardinalities as $(a,b,c;x,y,z)$, with successive
corner orders $a,b,c$ and stalk sizes $x,y,z$ on $01,12,02$.
There are $\binom{17}{5}=6188$ labelled cardinality tuples.
Opposition sends the tuple to $(c,b,a;y,x,z)$ and fixes
exactly $140$ of them.  Hence there are $3164$ opposition
orbits.  Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-linear-eighteen-scope}
and its independent complete predicate reconstruction
give the exact partition
\[
 3164=2791\text{ proved profile exclusions}
          +373\text{ finite profiles}.
\]
Every finite profile has corner orders at most seven.
The full ordinary monoid catalogs therefore cover every
required corner table; both opposition classes are
included whenever they are distinct.

For fixed corner tables the category is exactly three
connecting bisets $S,T,U$ and an outer-equivariant map
$T\otimes_{M_1}S\to U$.  The canonical action enumeration
and subsequent balanced-map enumeration are exhaustive
by Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-linear-biset-enumeration}.
They divide actions by their state permutations and
composition maps by the long biset's automorphisms,
with the full orbit--stabilizer weights of that
proposition.  No corner automorphism is divided out.
The resulting workload count refers to state-labelled
tables on the fixed corner representatives, rather
than category-isomorphism classes.

The original eighteen-arrow engine uses only the general
corner-table cuts audited in
\path{converse/scope18/linear18_engine_scope.md}.
The second version additionally uses the actual
extreme-minimal-group reduction of
Corollary~\ref{CP2:A-extreme-minimal-group-eighteen}.
The third additionally uses the adjacent-edge and fixed-arrow
cuts of Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-linear-adjacent-edge-homology-cuts}
and the actual trivial-extreme-action reduction of
Corollary~\ref{CP2:A-linear-trivial-extreme-actions-eighteen}.
All these extra eighteen-arrow branches have explicit
arrow-count guards.  The separate source derivations
preserve the prior versions.  Their validation on
seventeen-arrow controls gives byte-identical ordered
streams and unchanged mathematical counters.  For
eighteen-arrow controls, separately written literal
predicates remove exactly the records removed by the
new proof gates and reproduce the new ordered streams.
The unchanged independent literal checker accepts
those entire retained control streams.

The adopted partition consists of $53$ original profiles,
$87$ second-version profiles and $233$ third-version
profiles.  Each profile has a closed complete producer
stream and a successful independent literal check.
Together they cover
\[
 207\,856\,864\,600
\]
orbit-weighted tables after the proved cuts.  Their
retained literal records split as
\[
 53\,334\,624
   =53\,125\,287+209\,337.
\]
The first summand has a verified nonboundary binary
two-cocycle and a three-cochain primitive for its
square.  The second has verified positive third
homology over $\mathbb F_2$.  The independent checker
also verifies the typed multiplication tables and
$23\,138\,898\,425$ associativity equations over these
records.  The initial exact first- and second-homology
filters and these two certificate outcomes exclude
every table by Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-cup-certificate}.
There is no surviving record.

The separate finalizer
\path{converse/scope18/check_linear18_three_version_join.py}
imports no producer, runner or scope-generator module.
It reconstructs all $3164$ cardinality orbits, the exact
$2791+373$ partition, and the disjoint $53+87+233$
selection.  It rehashes the complete source, proof,
catalog, binary, control and predecessor-stop bindings.
For every selected stream it checks the literal
source-target matrix, compressed and uncompressed
digests and counts, complete producer footer, and
independent-checker outcomes.  Original completion
receipts retain their frozen publication rule requiring
successful producer and checker exits; both subsequent
versions additionally bind separate immutable observed
exit records.  Every selected compressed stream is read
to its closed end.  No partial or unselected stream is
adopted.

The full independent join exited successfully with
all $373$ profiles present and no survivor.  Its compact
receipt is
\path{converse/scope18/linear18_three_version_complete_independent.json}.
The full join has SHA256
\path{54964894ca40dac5791e227db83cc268c28c880903cd80955f7f5ec5cca4e79f}.
The separate observed execution receipt is
\path{converse/scope18/linear18_full_join_observed_exit.json}.
This proves the theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Proved: eighteen arrows leave only two objects]
\label{CP2:A-eighteen-only-two-objects}
Every eighteen-arrow category with classifying space
of type $\CP^2$ has exactly two objects.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Corollary~\ref{CP2:A-eighteen-two-or-linear-three}
leaves only two objects or full-linear three-object
support.  The preceding theorem excludes the latter.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Proved: six remaining jointly oriented eighteen-arrow budgets]
\label{CP2:A-eighteen-six-two-object-budgets}
If an eighteen-arrow category has classifying space
of type $\CP^2$, then, after the positive-source
orientation of Lemma~\ref{CP2:A-two-object-positive-orientation},
it is a two-object collage with corner orders $m,n$
and connecting hom-set size $s$ satisfying
\[
\begin{gathered}
 (m,n,s)\in\{(6,4,8),(7,3,8),(8,3,7),\\
             (9,3,6),(10,3,5),(10,4,4)\}.
\end{gathered}
\]
In this orientation its degree-two generator restricts
nontrivially to the source corner, and the connecting
right source act has no global fixed state.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The preceding corollary forces two objects.  Theorem
\ref{CP2:A-strong-two-eighteen} gives acyclic collage support, and
Lemma~\ref{CP2:A-two-object-positive-orientation} chooses
the stated orientation before any ordered profile is
discarded.  Corollary
\ref{CP2:A-two-object-eighteen-oriented-twenty-two}
gives the complete twenty-two-budget scope under this
orientation.

The separately reviewed sixteen-budget completion
manifest
\path{monoid/certificates/two_object18_current_manifest.json},
with SHA256
\path{0e5758586780df06faa3c0d42bf6abf7f01078d77ae74e5a15991d17ef6bca40},
joins sixteen entire oriented budgets, including every
required opposite-corner table and the separate
nontrivial-minimal-group source pieces.  Its exact
complement in the twenty-two-budget inventory is the
six displayed triples.  The independent constituent
source, scope, stream and literal checks are retained
in that manifest.  This is a joint orientation
argument: the nonzero source restriction is chosen
first and is preserved as the hypothesis of each
adopted exclusion.  The six remaining finite obligations
are not asserted complete by this corollary.
\end{proof}
