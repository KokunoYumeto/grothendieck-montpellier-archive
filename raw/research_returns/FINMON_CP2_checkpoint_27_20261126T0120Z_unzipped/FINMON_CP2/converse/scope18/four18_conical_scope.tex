\begin{proposition}[Proved: the conical-block four-object scope supplement]
\label{CP2:A-four-eighteen-conical-scope}
Among the $1235$ remaining four-object eighteen-arrow
profiles, Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-conical-block-cut}
excludes exactly $329$ additional profiles.  The full
$61420$-profile scope is therefore partitioned into
$60514$ profiles excluded by proved reductions and

\begin{proof}
For each of the $1235$ literal matrices, test every
ordered pair $(p,q)$ of distinct objects.  The predicate
requires $p$ to be extreme with corner order at most
four and $q$ to be an initial or terminal object of
the full deletion.  The latter condition is precisely
that its deletion row or column consists entirely of
ones, including its diagonal entry.

The scope generator
\path{acyclic/code/four18_conical_block_supplement.py}
and the independently written checker
\path{converse/scope18/check_four18_conical_block_scope.py}
agree on all $1235\cdot12=14820$ tests, every literal
witness, and the complete $906$-row complement.  The
independent receipt is
\path{converse/scope18/four18_conical_block_scope_independent.json}.
The separate finalizer
\path{converse/scope18/check_four18_conical_join.py}
preserves every predecessor proof, schedule, source,
stream, exit, and literal-count check.  The split
$446+460$ is obtained by intersecting the exact residual
indices with the frozen original successful-receipt set.
This scope proposition does not assert completion of
any missing finite profile.
\end{proof}
