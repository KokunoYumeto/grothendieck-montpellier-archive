\begin{proposition}[Proved: the pendant-eleven scope supplement]
\label{CP2:A-four-eighteen-pendant-eleven-scope}
Among the $710$ profiles of
Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-four-eighteen-singleton-deletion-scope},
Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-pendant-eleven-permitted-triangle}
excludes exactly $68$ further profiles.  The full
$61420$-profile scope is therefore partitioned into
$60778$ proved exclusions and $642$ finite obligations,
selecting $311$ original and $331$ second-version receipts.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Test every object of each remaining profile as the
pendant.  Its unique strict neighbor must have corner
order at most two; the pendant corner must have order
at most eleven; and the retained three-object category
must satisfy at least one of the eight permitted-triangle
conditions.  No bound on the pendant stalk is imposed.
The programs
\path{acyclic/code/four18_pendant_eleven_supplement.py}
and
\path{converse/scope18/check_four18_pendant_eleven_scope.py}
agree on all $710\cdot4=2840$ tests, all $68$ complete
witnesses, and every byte of the $642$-row complement.
The latter program independently reconstructs strict
adjacency from the individual arrows and joins the
selected indices to the frozen original receipt set.
Its receipt is
\path{converse/scope18/four18_pendant_eleven_scope_independent.json}.

The separate finalizer
\path{converse/scope18/check_four18_pendant_eleven_join.py}
retains every predecessor proof, source, schedule,
closed-stream, observed-exit and literal-count check.
This arithmetic reduction does not assert completion
of the remaining finite obligations.
\end{proof}
