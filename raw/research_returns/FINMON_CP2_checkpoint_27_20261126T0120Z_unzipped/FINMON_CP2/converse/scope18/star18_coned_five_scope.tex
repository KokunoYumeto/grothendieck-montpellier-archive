\begin{proposition}[Proved: two coned five-element leaf profiles]
\label{CP2:A-star-eighteen-coned-five-scope}
The original frozen star18 indices $31$ and $54$, with profiles
\[
 (6,1,5,1,5),\qquad(7,1,5,1,4),
\]
are excluded by Corollary~\ref{CP2:A-star-coned-five-leaf}.
Together with Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-star-eighteen-small-leaf-scope},
this partitions the $1204$ canonical star profiles into $1160$
proved profiles and $44$ profiles requiring finite composition
checks. No completion of those forty-four checks is asserted here.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The separately frozen supplement
\path{converse/scope18/star18_coned_five_supplement.json}
tests the forty-six retained original matrices in both leaf
orders. The center bound is eight, the coning leaf and its
stalk both have size one, and the other leaf and stalk have
size at most five. These are exactly the preceding corollary's
hypotheses. The only two qualifying matrices have the displayed
profiles and original indices. Removing them leaves forty-four
rows, with the old sixty-one-row and forty-six-row records
preserved separately. Thus $1158+2=1160$ and $46-2=44$.
The independent program
\path{acyclic/code/check_star18_coned_five_supplement.py}
imports none of the scope or producer programs. It reconstructs
all ninety-two leaf-order tests, the two literal witnesses,
and every byte of the new forty-four-row ledger. Its complete
receipt is
\path{acyclic/outputs/star18_coned_five_independent_audit.json}.
The separately named finalizer
\path{converse/scope18/check_star18_coned_five_join.py}
retains the original finite-stream checks and requires every
remaining row; its input-only pass asserts no finite completion.
\end{proof}
