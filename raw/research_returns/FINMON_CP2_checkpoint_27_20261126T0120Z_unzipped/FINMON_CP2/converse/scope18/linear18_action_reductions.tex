\subsection{Exact edge-homology cuts in a linear table}

\begin{proposition}[Proved: adjacent-edge and fixed-arrow tests]
\label{CP2:A-linear-adjacent-edge-homology-cuts}
Let $C$ have three objects with full linear acyclic
support $0<1<2$.  Write $C_{01},C_{12}$ for the full
adjacent two-object subcategories, and $M_i=C(i,i)$.
Each of the following conditions forces all rational
degree-two products of $BC$ to vanish:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $H_2(BC_{01};\mathbb F_2)=0$ and
      $H^2(BM_2;\mathbb Q)=0$;
\item $H_2(BC_{12};\mathbb F_2)=0$ and
      $H^2(BM_0;\mathbb Q)=0$;
\item $H_2(BC_{01};\mathbb F_2)=0$ and some arrow
      into object $2$ from object $0$ or $1$ is fixed
      by every left $M_2$ actor;
\item $H_2(BC_{12};\mathbb F_2)=0$ and some arrow
      out of object $0$ into object $1$ or $2$ is fixed
      by every right $M_0$ actor.
\end{enumerate}
These tests depend only on the corner tables and their
actions on the strict hom-sets; they do not depend on
the products of two strict arrows.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Each nerve has a finite number of simplices in every
degree, so its integral homology is finitely generated
in every degree.  The universal coefficient theorem
shows that zero binary second homology implies zero
rational second homology and cohomology.  Apply
Lemma~\ref{CP2:A-two-block} to the partition
$C_{01},M_2$, or to the partition $M_0,C_{12}$, for
the first two assertions.  For the last two, use
Corollary~\ref{CP2:A-fixed-arrow} and its opposite
with those same full-subcategory partitions.
The rational corner condition may be certified either
by the same binary test or by semisimplicity of its
rational algebra, which makes all positive rational
homology groups zero.
The adjacent subcategories contain no composable pair
of strict arrows.  Their tables consist precisely of
their two corner tables and the commuting actions on
their single stalk, proving the last assertion.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Proved: trivial extreme actions at the eighteen-arrow budget]
\label{CP2:A-linear-trivial-extreme-actions-eighteen}
An eighteen-arrow category with full linear three-object
support cannot have type $\CP^2$ if either $M_0$ is
nontrivial and acts trivially on both outgoing stalks,
or $M_2$ is nontrivial and acts trivially on both
incoming stalks.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-trivial-extreme-corner-retract}
would give an actual projective-plane model with at
most seventeen arrows, contrary to
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-global-lower-eighteen}.
\end{proof}
