\begin{proposition}[Proved: the exact extreme-corner scope at eighteen arrows]
\label{CP2:A-four-eighteen-trivial-extreme-scope}
Among the $1,426$ four-object profiles remaining after the
singleton-pendant retractions, precisely $191$ have a nontrivial
source or sink corner with nonempty, exclusively singleton strict
crossings. Thus the complete $61,420$-profile scope is partitioned
into $60,185$ profiles excluded by proved reductions and $1,235$
remaining finite-search profiles. This is a scope partition;
completion of those finite searches is a separate requirement.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Corollary~\ref{CP2:A-singleton-extreme-eighteen} excludes each
new profile. The frozen scan tests all four objects in each of the
$1,426$ literal hom-matrices, checking both source and sink
conditions and requiring at least one crossing. It records the
smaller matrix obtained by changing only the chosen diagonal
entry to one. The $191$ recorded witnesses have corner orders
$2,3,4,5,7$ with multiplicities $106,65,17,2,1$, respectively;
every smaller matrix has at most seventeen arrows.

The producer
\path{converse/scope18/four18_trivial_extreme_supplement.py}
and the separately written checker
\path{acyclic/code/check_four18_singleton_extreme_scope.py}
agree on all $5,704$ object tests, every witness, the original
profile indices, and the exact bytes of the remaining ledger.
Their receipts are
\path{converse/scope18/four18_trivial_extreme_supplement.json}
and
\path{acyclic/outputs/four18_singleton_extreme_independent_audit.json}.
The original ledgers and finite-search records are retained.
\end{proof}
