\subsection{Completion of the eighteen-arrow star scope}

\begin{theorem}[Proved: every three-object star through eighteen]
\label{CP2:A-star-eighteen-complete}
A three-object category with star-shaped acyclic support
and at most eighteen arrows cannot have classifying space
homotopy equivalent to $\CP^2$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Disconnected categories are excluded by connectedness, and
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-global-lower-eighteen} excludes every
category with at most seventeen arrows.  It remains to
consider exactly eighteen arrows.  Opposition makes the
center a source.  Write its profile as $(a,b,c,s,t)$,
where $a,b,c$ are the corner orders and $s,t$ are the
two nonempty stalk sizes.  There are
$\binom{17}{4}=2380$ positive profiles.  The leaf swap
$(a,b,c,s,t)\mapsto(a,c,b,t,s)$ fixes exactly $28$:
these satisfy $a+2b+2s=18$ and $2\le b+s\le8$.
Thus there are $(2380+28)/2=1204$ profile orbits.

The independently checked original scope reduces these
to $61$ finite obligations.  The successive, separately
frozen supplements remove $15$, $2$, $2$, $1$, and $1$
profiles.  Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-star-eighteen-five-center-four-scope}
records the resulting exact partition
\[
  1204=1164+40.
\]
All forty remaining profiles have corner orders at most
seven, so the complete ordinary monoid catalogs through
order seven cover every corner choice.

For fixed corner tables, a source-star category is
exactly a pair of commuting left/right actions on its
two stalks: no pair of strict arrows is composable.
The canonical-biset enumeration therefore lists every
table up to independent state permutations of the two
stalks.  If those stalks have sizes $s,t$ and their
stabilizers have orders $A,B$, the weight
$s!t!/(AB)$ recovers every state-labelled table with
the fixed corner representatives.  No corner
automorphism is divided out.  The enabled corner cuts
are the proved projective-orbit and relative-cup cuts
listed in \path{converse/scope18/star18_engine_scope.md}.
Their hypotheses do not require a seventeen-arrow bound.

Thirty-eight profiles use their complete original
eighteen-arrow streams.  The remaining two are original
indices $52=(7,1,3,2,5)$ and $53=(7,1,4,2,4)$.
Corollary~\ref{CP2:A-star-three-binary-center-scope}
restricts their center to the single monoid
$(\{0,1\}\times\{0,1,2\})^1$.  A separate producer
recognizes this monoid by its literal rectangular-band
table and retains the unchanged action enumeration and
certificate routines.  Its validation-mode output on
the fixed seventeen-arrow control is byte-identical to
the original output.  On the completed eighteen-arrow
profile $60$, an independent test over all $6!$ band
relabelings removes exactly $124990$ of the original
$125968$ records and matches the new ordered stream of
$978$ records exactly.  The same literal checker
accepts that entire retained stream.  These controls,
the proof, both binaries, and the full source derivation
are bound by the two new production receipts.

The adopted forty streams cover $4\,100\,653\,251$
orbit-weighted tables after the proved corner cuts.
They contain exactly
\[
  9\,930\,935
  =9\,802\,502+128\,433
\]
literal certificates: the first summand has a verified
primitive for the square of its nonzero binary second
class, and the second has verified positive binary
third homology.  The independent literal checker also
verifies all typed compositions and associativity,
including $7\,876\,756\,666$ associativity equations
over the adopted streams.  The preceding binary
homology filters and these two certificate outcomes
exclude every table by Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-cup-certificate}.
These counts measure the verified computation, rather
than category-isomorphism classes.

The finalizer
\path{converse/scope18/check_star18_binary_center_join_v2.py}
imports no producer or runner.  It rechecks every proof
partition and exact residual ledger, then binds all
forty required receipts to their literal matrices,
source and binary digests, complete producer footers,
independent-checker outcomes, and compressed and
decompressed stream digests and counts.  It reads every
adopted compressed stream to its closed end.  The two
new receipts additionally contain immutable prelaunch
bindings and separate observed producer/checker exit
records; the original receipts retain their frozen
publication rules requiring both successful exits.
The five-entry original star profile is checked against
the literal nine-entry matrix in its exact stored order.
The full join has no missing profile and no survivor.
Its digest and totals are recorded in
\path{converse/scope18/star18_binary_center_complete_independent_v2.json}.
All incomplete and proof-redundant original streams
remain outside the adopted finite count.
\end{proof}
