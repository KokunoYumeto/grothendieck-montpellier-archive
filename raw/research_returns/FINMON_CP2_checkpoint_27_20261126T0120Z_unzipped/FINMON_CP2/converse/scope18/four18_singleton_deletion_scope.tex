\begin{proposition}[Proved: the singleton-deletion block scope supplement]
\label{CP2:A-four-eighteen-singleton-deletion-scope}
Among the $906$ profiles of
Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-four-eighteen-conical-scope},
the singleton-deletion block theorem excludes exactly
$196$ further profiles.  The full $61420$-profile
scope is therefore partitioned into $60710$ proved
exclusions and $710$ finite obligations, selecting
$311$ original and $399$ second-version receipts.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Apply Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-singleton-deletion-block-cut}
to every ordered pair $(p,v)$ of distinct objects in
each remaining profile.  The exact cardinality test
requires a small extreme corner at $p$, a unique
singleton incoming or outgoing arrow at $v$ within
the deletion of $p$, a trivial corner at that arrow's
other endpoint, and the two retained corner orders
at most two.  The two separately written programs
\path{acyclic/code/four18_singleton_deletion_block_supplement.py}
and
\path{converse/scope18/check_four18_singleton_deletion_scope.py}
agree on all $906\cdot12=10872$ tests, all $196$
complete witnesses, and every byte of the $710$-row
complement.  The latter checker reconstructs the
incoming and outgoing conditions by enumerating the
individual arrows, and separately joins the indices
to the frozen original successful-receipt set.

Its receipt is
\path{converse/scope18/four18_singleton_deletion_scope_independent.json}.
The separate finalizer
\path{converse/scope18/check_four18_singleton_deletion_join.py}
retains all predecessor proof, schedule, source,
closed-stream, observed-exit and literal-count checks.
The partition establishes the reduced obligations;
completion of missing finite profiles is not asserted
by this proposition.
\end{proof}
