\subsection{Completion of the four-object eighteen-arrow case}

\begin{theorem}[Proved: four objects are excluded through eighteen arrows]
\label{CP2:A-four-eighteen-complete}
No finite category on four objects with at most eighteen
arrows has classifying space of type $\CP^2$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-global-lower-eighteen} excludes every
smaller budget. At eighteen arrows,
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-strong-four-eighteen} excludes cyclic
support. Connected acyclic support has exactly $61420$
canonical cardinality profiles by
Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-four-five-eighteen-scopes} and
its independent full-scope reconstruction. The separately
proved supplements, ending with
Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-four-eighteen-final-binary-conical-scope},
give the exhaustive partition
\[
61420=60854\text{ structurally excluded profiles}
       +566\text{ finite profiles}.
\]

The finite enumeration ranges over every corner monoid
in the complete ordinary-isomorphism catalogs and
every commuting pair of endpoint actions on each
strict hom-set, up to independent state permutations.
It then enumerates every typed product of two strict
arrows satisfying associativity, without quotienting
the composition maps further. Thus every category on
the prescribed corner representatives is represented;
the action-orbit weights recover the corresponding
labelled-table workload. The original engine uses
the proved semisimple-corner height obstruction. The
second engine additionally uses only the separately
reviewed exact eighteen-arrow extreme-corner reduction.
Its full source derivation, exact seventeen-arrow
regressions and independently filtered eighteen-arrow
controls are bound in the final verification.

All $566$ selected profiles completed: $306$ use the
original engine and $260$ the second engine. They cover
$596432253$ weighted tables and $2859139$ retained
literal obstruction records. The independent scalar
checker verifies $2849824$ nonzero second cocycles
with primitives for their zero squares, and $9315$
positive-third-homology obstructions. There is no
unexcluded record. These obstructions contradict
the mod-two cohomology ring or homology of $\CP^2$.
The retained tables satisfy $1386326963$ independently
checked typed associativity equations, in addition to
the normalized bar and cochain equations.

The separate finalizer
\path{converse/scope18/check_four18_final_binary_conical_join.py}
imports no producer, runner or scope-generator module.
It reconstructs the complete cardinality partition and
each later proof supplement, rehashes every bound
source, proof, catalog, binary and control receipt,
and requires every selected completion. For every
literal stream it checks the compressed and uncompressed
hashes, source-target hom-set matrix, exact record counts,
producer footer and observed producer/checker exits.
The full run exited successfully with no missing profile
and no survivor. Its compact receipt is
\path{outputs/four18_final_binary_conical_complete_independent.json};
the full join has SHA256
\path{18c1f8b858bd60156a288c455aa7122bf2d2312a8afbe9c46012728e65575db0}.
This closes both acyclic and cyclic support and proves
the theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Proved: the remaining eighteen-arrow object scope]
\label{CP2:A-eighteen-two-or-linear-three}
Any eighteen-arrow category with classifying space of
type $\CP^2$ has either two objects or three objects
with full linear acyclic support.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Corollary~\ref{CP2:A-eighteen-at-most-four-objects}
leaves only two, three or four objects. The preceding
theorem removes four. For three objects,
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-strong-three-eighteen} removes cyclic
support and Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-star-eighteen-complete}
removes the source and sink stars. These exhaust the
connected three-object supports other than the full
linear support.
\end{proof}
