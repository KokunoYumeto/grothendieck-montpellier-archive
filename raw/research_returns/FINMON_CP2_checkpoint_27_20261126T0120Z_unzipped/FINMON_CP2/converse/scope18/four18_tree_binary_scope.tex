\begin{proposition}[Proved: the small binary tree and pendant-circle scope]
\label{CP2:A-four-eighteen-tree-binary-scope}
Among the $642$ profiles of
Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-four-eighteen-pendant-eleven-scope},
the small binary tree theorem excludes $25$ profiles
and the trivial parallel pendant lemma excludes $36$.
Exactly four profiles satisfy both tests. Thus $57$
new exclusions leave $585$ finite obligations, selecting
$306$ original and $279$ second-version receipts.
The full scope is $61420=60835+585$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For every retained profile, test each of its four
objects as the distinguished corner in
Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-tree-small-binary-corners}, and
as the pendant in
Lemma~\ref{CP2:A-trivial-pendant-parallel-circle}.
The programs
\path{acyclic/code/four18_tree_binary_supplement.py}
and
\path{converse/scope18/check_four18_tree_binary_scope.py}
agree on all $642\cdot8=5136$ predicates, all complete
witnesses, the $25$ and $36$ profile sets and their
four-profile intersection, and every byte of the
$585$-row complement. The independent program builds
strict adjacency and stalk multiplicities from the
individual arrows and computes connected components
separately. Its receipt is
\path{converse/scope18/four18_tree_binary_scope_independent.json}.

The separate finalizer
\path{converse/scope18/check_four18_tree_binary_join.py}
retains every earlier proof, source, schedule,
closed-stream, observed-exit and literal-count check.
This proposition establishes the reduced obligations;
it does not certify completion of any missing profile.
\end{proof}
