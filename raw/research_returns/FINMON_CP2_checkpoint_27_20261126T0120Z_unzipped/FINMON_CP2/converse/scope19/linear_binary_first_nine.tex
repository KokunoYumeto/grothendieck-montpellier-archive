\subsection{A six-state graph at the order-nine endpoint}

\begin{proposition}[Proved: two initial parallel arrows and six graph states]
\label{CP2:A-binary-first-six-graph-states-nine}
Let a full-linear three-object category have trivial first two
corners, final corner $N$ of order at most nine, and
\[
 |C(0,1)|=2,\qquad |C(1,2)|+|C(0,2)|\leq6.
\]
If its rational homology is bounded and its fourth rational Betti
number is at most one, all rational degree-two products vanish.
The same holds after opposition.  In particular the nineteen-arrow
profile $(1,1,9;2,3,3)$ is excluded.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Put $D=C|_{\{0,1\}}$, $Y=C(1,2)$ and $Z=C(0,2)$.
Name the two arrows of $C(0,1)$ as $x_0,x_1$.
The category of elements of the connecting $D$-act has objects
$Y\sqcup Z$ and, for each $y\in Y$, two arrows
\[
                y\longrightarrow yx_0,\qquad
                y\longrightarrow yx_1.
\]
There are no nonidentity endomorphisms or further composable
nonidentity arrows.  The two arrows remain distinct when their
endpoints coincide.  Its classifying space is the subdivided
finite graph $\Gamma$ with vertex set $Z$ and one oriented edge
for each $y\in Y$, directed from $yx_0$ to $yx_1$.

The left $N$ action preserves the chosen endpoint labels:
$n(yx_i)=(ny)x_i$.  Identifying the two half-edges at each
bivalent $y$ with a single parametrized edge is therefore a
natural equivariant homeomorphism to this graph.  In particular
it does not replace the graph by its homology or discard loops
or parallel edges.  The bridge for the cut $D\to N$ is thus
the actual graph homotopy-orbit space, with its projection to
$BN$.

Collapse $BD$ in the actual two-block pushout and call the
resulting cofiber $Q$.  Since $BD$ is a finite graph, this map
is an isomorphism on fourth rational homology, preserves bounded
rational homology, and induces a surjection
\[
                  H^2(Q;\mathbb Q)\longrightarrow H^2(BC;\mathbb Q).
\]
The space $Q$ is the graph cofiber just described.

Apply the actual right-zero realization of
Lemma~\ref{CP2:A-directed-graph-right-zero-realization}, using
opposition for the left $N$ action.  The resulting collage has
source $N^{\mathrm{op}}$, opposite corner $R_3$, and exactly
$|Z|+|Y|\leq6$ connecting states: the vertices and the unsplit
edges of $\Gamma$.  Its classifying space is homotopy equivalent
to $Q$.  Lemma~\ref{CP2:A-nine-right-zero-six-bounded-top}
therefore kills its rational second products.  The displayed
surjection pulls these zero products back to every product on
$BC$.  Opposition proves the dual assertion.
\end{proof}
