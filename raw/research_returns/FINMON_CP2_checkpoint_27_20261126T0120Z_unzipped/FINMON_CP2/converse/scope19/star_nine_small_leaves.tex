\subsection{Small leaves at a nine-element central corner}

\begin{lemma}[Proved: a bounded order-nine source with at most six states]
\label{CP2:A-nine-right-zero-six-bounded-top}
Let $M$ be a monoid of order at most nine, let $N=R_3$, and let
$S$ be a nonempty left-$N$, right-$M$ biset with at most six states.
Suppose its collage $X$ has bounded rational homology and
$\dim H_4(X;\mathbb Q)\leq1$.  Then every rational degree-two
product on $X$ vanishes.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For $|M|\leq8$, use
Proposition~\ref{CP2:A-star-eight-three-leaves} with one leaf.
Assume $|M|=9$.  If $H^2(BM;\mathbb Q)=0$, both corner
restrictions vanish, and the two-block relative-product lemma
annihilates all second products.  Otherwise use the complete
source-bound classification in the proof of
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-oriented-eighteen-nine-three-six}.
We recall its precise scope, to distinguish the boundedness
hypothesis here from any finite-state computation.

Of the $8044$ rectangular-band source classes, $7784$ have minimal
band $2\times2$, and $129$ have minimal band $2\times3$.
Their products vanish for every state count by the unconditional
actor-quotient theorems.  Among the $129$ classes with minimal
band $3\times2$, $125$ have a projective row actor image, the
three original source tables $7956,8034,8037$ satisfy the local
three-dimensional-algebra obstruction, and source $8038$ satisfies
its all-state obstruction.  The local theorem is applied directly
to the present bounded collage, with its original corner $M$;
boundedness of an actor-image collage is not inferred.
The single pure $4\times2$ band is covered by
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-two-column-band-graph-source}.
All these statements give zero products, with no state bound.

The sole remaining band source is the identity-adjoined $2\times4$
band, at index $8042$.  If $H_4(X;\mathbb Q)=0$, the conclusion
is immediate.  Otherwise its fourth Betti number is one, and
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-two-by-four-band-six-states} states explicitly
that all second products vanish under precisely this top-rank
and six-state hypothesis.  It does not require the other homology
groups to equal those of a projective plane.

The two remaining positive-second-homology source classes have
minimal ideal $2\times C_2\times2$.  The actual comparison in
Theorem~\ref{CP2:A-minimal-group-collapse-collage-H2} induces
an isomorphism in rational second cohomology to a $P_5$ collage.
The latter has zero second products for every state count by the
unconditional two-by-two theorem, so pullback proves the claim.
The exact source partition and original-table bindings are in
\path{monoid/certificates/nine_three_six_complete.json}; no finite
action stream from that budget is used in this argument.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Proved: merging small leaf corners in positive degrees]
\label{CP2:A-small-leaf-right-zero-merge}
Let $C$ have source-star support, with central monoid $M$, nonempty
stalks $S_i$, and leaf monoids $N_i$ of orders at most three.
There is an actual functor from $C$ to a two-object collage with
source $M$, opposite corner $R_3$, and a connecting set $T$ with
\[
             0<|T|\leq\sum_i |S_i|,
\]
which induces rational homology isomorphisms in all degrees at
least two.  Its cohomology pullback preserves products and is an
isomorphism in those degrees.  In particular it preserves bounded
rational homology and the fourth Betti number.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
If the trivial right $\mathbb QN_i$ module is projective, replace
that leaf by the trivial monoid and $S_i$ by the left orbit set
$N_i\backslash S_i$.  The actual orbit functor is a rational
homology equivalence: on the corresponding bridge it is the
natural exact-coinvariant comparison, and the corner $BN_i$ is
rationally acyclic.  This remains true after the commuting right
$M$ bar and after gluing all the other leaves.  If the trivial
right module is not projective, the order-three minimal-ideal
classification leaves exactly $N_i=R_3$, which is retained.

Next replace each trivial leaf by $R_3$ acting trivially on its
orbit set.  The unit inclusion defines an actual functor.
The bridge inclusion is
\[
 B(T_i,M)\longrightarrow BR_3\times B(T_i,M),
\]
which is a homotopy equivalence, since $BR_3$ is contractible.
The same holds at the leaf corner.  Thus this replacement also
preserves rational homology and products, without increasing any
stalk size.

All leaf monoids are now copies of $R_3$.  Fold these objects to
one copy of $R_3$ and retain the disjoint union $T=\bigsqcup_i T_i$
as its connecting set.  This is an actual category functor: both
actions preserve every summand, and there are no arrows between
distinct leaves before folding.  In the two-block homotopy-pushout
description, the source corner $BM$ and the disjoint union of
bridges are unchanged.  The map on the leaf block is
\(\bigsqcup_i BR_3\to BR_3\).  Both leaf blocks have zero
positive rational homology.  Comparing the two Mayer--Vietoris
sequences therefore gives isomorphisms on $H_j$ for every
$j\geq2$.  Only the degree-zero leaf-block term can differ,
and it enters the comparison in degree one.  Naturality of this
actual functor gives the claimed cohomology-ring comparison.
All groups in degree one are finite dimensional, so the same
comparison also preserves boundedness.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Proved: nine-element centers with six small-leaf states]
\label{CP2:A-star-nine-three-leaves-six-states}
A source or sink star with central corner of order at most nine,
every leaf corner of order at most three, and total stalk size at
most six cannot have classifying space of type $\CP^2$.
More generally, bounded rational homology and fourth Betti number
at most one force all its rational degree-two products to vanish.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
For a source star, apply the actual merging functor of the
preceding proposition.  Its target satisfies every hypothesis
of Lemma~\ref{CP2:A-nine-right-zero-six-bounded-top}.  The
cohomology isomorphisms in degrees two and four pull the zero
products back to the star.  Opposition proves the sink case.
\end{proof}
