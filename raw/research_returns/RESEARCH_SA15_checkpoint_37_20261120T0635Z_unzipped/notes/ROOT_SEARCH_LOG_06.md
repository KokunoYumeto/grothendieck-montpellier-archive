Its cyclic-cover surface has a geometric cuspidal fibre F with affine ring k[u^ell,u^p], and the ramification section meets the smooth point at infinity. The ample line bundle Z_(a,b) restricts to O_F(a infinity). The theorem's nonvanishing range has1<=a<=ell-1 and ell dividing p+1. If ell=p+1, then a<=p; all a<p still give constants, and a=p gives only the two-dimensional space spanned by1 andu^p. None embeds this cuspidal fibre. Therefore these particular nonvanishing polarizations are not very ample and do not supply the surface version of the cyclic-cover restriction counterexample. This argument does not rule out other polarizations or surface constructions. It is a recorded failed route, not claimed movement in A.1.
