\subsection{Twisted Cartier blocks for arbitrary odd cubic pullbacks}

All fields in this subsection are algebraically closed of characteristic
two, and all Cartier operators are relative, with fixed invariant volume
forms. For a line bundle \(P\) of odd order, \(P^{1/2}\) denotes its
unique half in the group of odd-order torsion line bundles. An occurrence
of \(P^{(2)}\) instead denotes its actual Frobenius twist on the twisted
variety; these operations are distinct.

\begin{lemma}[Twisted blocks on an arbitrary odd cubic pullback]
\label{lem:DE-general-odd-source-twisted-blocks}
\status{Proved; full independent proof audits passed.}
Let \((C,\Theta_C)\) be a nonordinary principally polarized abelian
surface and set \(N=\mathcal O_C(3\Theta_C)\). Let
\(\eta:B\to C\) be an etale isogeny of positive odd degree \(a\),
and put \(M=\eta^*N\), \(h=9a\). The surface \(B\) need not
carry a principal polarization. For every nontrivial odd-order
\(P\in\operatorname{Pic}^0(B)\),
the following two determinants are nonzero polynomials:
\begin{enumerate}
\item multiplication by \(s\in H^0(B,M)\), followed by relative
Cartier,
\[
 H^0(B,MP)\longrightarrow
 H^0(B^{(2)},M^{(2)}(P^{1/2})^{(2)});
 \tag{DE-GOS1}
\]
\item the contraction by \(\lambda\in H^0(B^{(2)},M^{(2)})^*\)
of the relative Cartier pairing
\[
 H^0(B,MP)\otimes H^0(B,MP^{-1})
                 \longrightarrow H^0(B^{(2)},M^{(2)}).
 \tag{DE-GOS2}
\]
Both are square matrices of size \(h\).

More precisely, for any finite collection of such \(P\), all the
determinants in
(DE-GOS1) and (DE-GOS2) are simultaneously nonzero on a nonempty
open of the same Fermat cubic in \(\mathbf P(H^0(C,N))\), with
\(s=\eta^*s_C\) and \(\lambda\) the functional supported on the
trivial \(\eta\)-character with coordinates \(s_C\).
This open can also be chosen so that \(s\) lies on the actual common
maximal-rank wall for \(|M|\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the isogeny and cubic principal line bundle in the hypotheses.
Write
\[
 \eta_*\mathcal O_B=\bigoplus_{\chi\in H}R_\chi,
 \qquad H=\ker\bigl(\eta^*: \operatorname{Pic}^0(C)
                               \to\operatorname{Pic}^0(B)\bigr),
 \tag{DE-GOS3}
\]
where this finite group has odd order \(a\).
An odd-order lift \(Q\) of \(P\) exists. Indeed, an arbitrary lift
under the dual isogeny is annihilated by an odd integer: an odd order
of \(P\) takes it into the odd kernel of that isogeny. Therefore
\(\eta^*Q=P\), and none of the lines \(Q R_\chi\) is trivial,
since its pullback would otherwise make \(P\) trivial.
Projection formula gives
\[
 H^0(B,MP)=\bigoplus_{\chi\in H}H^0(C,NQ R_\chi).
 \tag{DE-GOS4}
\]
Every summand has dimension nine.

For \(s=\eta^*s_C\), finite etale Frobenius base change and the
relative Cartier projection formula identify the summand map in
(DE-GOS1) with
\[
 H^0(C,NQ R_\chi)\longrightarrow
 H^0(C^{(2)},N^{(2)}
                    ((Q R_\chi)^{1/2})^{(2)}).
 \tag{DE-GOS5}
\]
The target is correct because its Frobenius pullback is
\(N^2Q R_\chi\). These targets are exactly the character summands
of the displayed target in (DE-GOS1): taking halves is an automorphism
of the odd torsion group, and
\(\eta^*(Q^{1/2})=P^{1/2}\). In particular this is a permutation
of character blocks, with no untyped identification of a variety
with its Frobenius twist. The proof of (DE-CI5), applied to this
character coset, is the same finite etale comparison.
Each block (DE-GOS5) has nonzero determinant by
Lemma~\ref{lem:DE-common-incidence-multiplication}, since
\(Q R_\chi\ne\mathcal O_C\). Their product proves the first claim.

For (DE-GOS2), push both inputs to \(C\) and choose \(\lambda\)
supported on the trivial target character. A summand
\(NQ R_\chi\) pairs only with \(NQ^{-1}R_{\chi^{-1}}\).
Thus the full matrix is, up to a permutation of rows and columns,
the direct sum of the nine-by-nine balanced cross matrices for
the nontrivial odd lines \(Q R_\chi\). Their determinants are
nonzero by Theorem~\ref{thm:ppav-non-two-torsion-cross-Cartier}.
This supplies an explicit nonzero restriction of the full determinant,
and proves the second claim.

For the simultaneous wall assertion, keep one fixed symmetric
theta basis on \(H^0(C,N)\). By
Lemma~\ref{lem:odd-cover-common-Fermat-coordinates}, every one of
the block determinants just described is nonzero on its integral
Fermat cubic when the functional on the trivial target has the
same coordinate vector \(s_C\). Multiplication changes only the
indices \(s_i\mapsto s_{-i}\); it does not make a general linear
change in these Fermat variables. This statement also applies to
the nontrivial lines \(R_\chi\) needed for the untwisted common
maximal-rank condition on \(|M|\).
Intersect these finitely many nonempty determinant opens on the
integral Fermat cubic with the nonvanishing of the central Pfaffian
cubic. Proposition~\ref{prop:odd-cover-common-rank-wall-nonempty}
then identifies the resulting sections with actual common-wall
points for \(|M|\): the central rank-eight block is the only
noninvertible multiplication block, and the actual left kernel
is the stated trivial-character functional. The corresponding
dual form has rank \(h-1\). This proves the final assertion,
without a genericity inference from the larger section space.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[A full odd source controls the residual quotient]
\label{prop:DE-general-odd-source-quotient-bound}
\status{Proved; full independent proof audits passed.}
In the notation of the lemma, suppose the relative geometric image
on the complete system \(|M|\) is
\(\operatorname{GL}_{h-1}(\mathbf F_2)\).
Let \(\pi:A\to B\) be any etale isogeny of odd degree \(n\),
put \(L=\pi^*M\), and let \(V\) be the rank-\((hn-1)\) generic
relative Lang space for \(|L|\). Let \(K\) be its canonical
proper submodule from the full-radical structure theorem.
On a nonempty open of the source slice \(\mathbf P(H^0(B,M))\),
the actual deck decomposition satisfies
\[
 V=V_0\oplus V_{\mathrm{nt}},\qquad
 \dim V_0=h-1,\qquad
 K\subseteq V_{\mathrm{nt}}.
 \tag{DE-GOS6}
\]
Consequently \(\dim(V/K)\geq h-1\).
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Decompose \(\pi_*\mathcal O_A\) into its odd character lines
\(P_\chi\). Each nontrivial character is a nontrivial odd-order
line on \(B\). The first part of the lemma makes all its Cartier
multiplication blocks invertible on a common nonempty open of the
source section space. Intersect this open with the source smooth,
sharp open. The pullback divisor is smooth and connected, the latter
by ample connectedness as in the proof of
Proposition~\ref{prop:DE-common-Cartier-incidence}. The actual
relative Frobenius is the direct sum of the source part and the
bijective nontrivial character cycles. Averaging the odd deck group
over \(\mathbf F_2\) gives exactly the local-system splitting
in (DE-GOS6).

Removal of these extra determinant loci is a dense-open restriction
of a normal integral source base. Its fundamental group still
surjects onto the source geometric image, by the connected finite
etale cover argument in
Proposition~\ref{prop:DE-Lang-deck-slice-quotient-eight}.
Thus the source slice image projects to the full
\(\operatorname{GL}(V_0)\). No independent action on the second
summand is required.

Apply the last assertion of the lemma simultaneously to the finite
set of outer character lines \(P_\chi\). It gives an actual source
common-wall point where all outer multiplication and cross matrices
are invertible. The composite \(A\to B\to C\) is an odd etale
cover of a cubic principal system. Accordingly the ambient point
lies on the full common maximal-rank wall for \(|L|\), by the
same character computation, or directly by
Theorem~\ref{thm:odd-cover-full-hyperplane-inertia}.
The source relative stable rank is \(h-2\) at that point; the outer
character cycles remain bijective. Hence its regular Lang hyperplane
is \(H_0\oplus V_{\mathrm{nt}}\), with \(H_0\) a hyperplane of
\(V_0\).

Use exactly the strictly henselian regular-point lifting argument
in Proposition~\ref{prop:DE-Lang-deck-slice-quotient-eight}.
The fixed hyperplane of the ambient full radical is identified with
these regular source-plus-character vectors. Only the constant-rank
regular part is compared; the full generic fibre is not specialized
through the rank drop. Lemma~\ref{lem:DE-Lang-block-slice-bound}
now gives \(K\subseteq V_{\mathrm{nt}}\) and the asserted bound.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The bound from a known odd divisor degree]
\label{cor:DE-general-source-principal-divisor-bound}
\status{Proved; full independent proof audits passed.}
Let \((A,\Theta_A)\) be a fixed nonordinary principally polarized
abelian surface, and write \(d=em\) with \(e,m\) positive odd integers. Choose an
outgoing maximal-isotropic quotient \(\pi:A\to B\) of degree
\(m^2\), with principal descent \(\Theta_B\).
If the geometric image in degree \(e\) on \(B\) is full, then the
canonical exceptional submodule in degree \(d\) on \(A\) satisfies
\[
 \dim K\leq 9(d^2-e^2),\qquad
 \dim(V/K)\geq9e^2-1.
 \tag{DE-GOS7}
\]
Its main Lang component therefore has degree at least
\[
                   2^{9d^2-1}-2^{9(d^2-e^2)}.
 \tag{DE-GOS8}
\]
In particular the hypothesis holds whenever the separately proved
fixed-surface degree-\(e\) theorem holds for every nonordinary
principally polarized target. The case \(e=1\) recovers the
unconditional eight-dimensional bound.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The exact descent line can be chosen so that
\(\pi^*\mathcal O_B(3e\Theta_B)
  =\mathcal O_A(3em\Theta_A)\), with the same harmless symmetric
odd-character adjustment as in the outgoing quotient theorem.
Apply the outgoing quotient theorem also to the positive odd
source degree \(e\) on \(B\): it writes
\(\mathcal O_B(3e\Theta_B)=\eta^*N\) with \(N\) cubic principal
and \(\deg\eta=e^2\). The preceding proposition then applies
with \(a=e^2\), \(h=9e^2\), and \(n=m^2\), proving (DE-GOS7). The main-orbit statement is used only in this
fixed-principal-system specialization: Lemma~\ref{lem:DE-Lang-main-orbit}
gives its exact degree \(2^{9d^2-1}-2^{\dim K}\), yielding (DE-GOS8).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Limits of the source restriction]
\label{rem:DE-general-odd-source-scope}
\status{Proved; full independent proof audits passed.}
The explicit nine-dimensional witnesses prove the required twisted
determinants and the actual wall comparison; neither is inferred
merely from generic rank in a larger complete system. The source
full-image hypothesis is kept explicit. Distinct deck slices are not
compared inside one generic fibre, and these bounds do not prove
\(K=0\) at a new prime degree. All arguments use previously typed
relative Cartier and odd-isogeny statements. No numerical experiment
or new external theorem is needed or claimed.
\end{remark}
