tensor to every odd degree has not been justified. The naive Pfaffian
degree and Heisenberg-character compatibility give a warning against that
extension; they are not asserted as a proved counterexample to every
possible tensor construction. The suggestion was explicitly withdrawn as
an available proof input. The abstract alternative of irreducible
rank-drop inertia plus purity remains a gate, without a proved
irreducibility theorem.
