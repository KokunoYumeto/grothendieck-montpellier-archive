\subsection{Two uniform graph witnesses for the even mixed-product comparison}
\begin{lemma}\label{lem:root-even-mixed-grid-witnesses-candidate}
\status{Candidate assembly; graph proof complete, geometric inputs separately required.}
Let $d\ge2$ be even, $n=3d$, and let $Z$ be the elliptic grid
with graph $K_{n,n}$. Partition its row and column indices first
by $I_0=J_0=\{0,1,2\}$ and their complements, and secondly by
$I_1=J_1=\{0,1,3\}$ and their complements.
Each cross-edge set has $N=18(d-1)$ edges. Its sum-zero
cochain space maps to an $(N-2)$-dimensional heart in
$H^1(K_{n,n},\mathbf F_2)$.
The second heart contains vectors with nonzero projection
respectively to the internal graph cohomologies of each
block of the first partition, and zero projection to the other.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $e_{ij}$ for the edge cochain. A vertex coboundary
supported on the cross-edge set is constant on each internal
connected bipartite block. Modulo the global constant it is
therefore a multiple of the sum of all cross edges. This sum
has even support $N$ and is in the sum-zero cochain hyperplane.
Consequently the heart dimension is $(N-1)-1=N-2$.

Take
\[
 v_D=e_{02}+e_{12},\qquad v_C=e_{34}+e_{35}.
\]
Both have even support in the cross-edge set of the second
partition. The first is supported on the small internal
block of the first partition, and the second on its large
internal block. They evaluate to one on the respective
four-cycles
\[
 e_{01}+e_{02}+e_{21}+e_{22},\qquad
 e_{33}+e_{34}+e_{43}+e_{44}.
\]
Thus neither restriction is a coboundary. The other internal
restriction is zero. All displayed indices exist for $n\ge6$.
This proves the assertion for every even $d$, without relying
on the finite certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}\label{rem:root-even-mixed-grid-assembly-candidate}
\status{Conditional; not a full monodromy theorem.}
In the proposed geometric application the large block has
relative rank $9(d-1)^2-1$, the small block has rank eight,
and the cross-heart has rank $18(d-1)-2$. Their sum is
$9d^2-4$, the rank of the common kernel of the two proved
invariant covectors. The grid relative rank is instead
$9d^2-3d$, so the comparison must use the finite Lang part;
it must not identify full generic and special fibres.

If the full lifted-intersection wreath and actual gluing
translation lemmas hold for these unequal odd-degree
components, every nonzero invariant subspace contains the
cross-heart. The two witnesses then give nonzero projections
to both simple natural blocks. Actual block-diagonal groups
put both entire blocks in the invariant subspace. The dimension
identity above proves irreducibility of the common covector
kernel. These geometric and group hypotheses still require
their own complete proofs; this graph lemma does not assert them.
The retained program \path{code/mixed_even_degree_common_grid.py}
checks degrees $2,4,\ldots,20$ only as supporting exact arithmetic.
\end{remark}
