# Discarded refined-weight lead before B71

This was a private proof outline, never a candidate theorem or an executed numerical check.
For the later small pair b=(2,p+1), a common finite twist gives special point-action
weights (1,0) for the two original formal groups. The quotient by the level-three
graph has point-action weights (-1,0). I initially considered using coefficient
congruences with these point-action weights directly in the ring-action formula
of B64, which would incorrectly give large positive residues.

The conventions must be converted. For point action
T(sigma P)=zeta^c sigma(T(P)), the corresponding semilinear ring parameter has
weight -c. Thus a coefficient of T_j^p in coordinate i has valuation congruent
to p c_j-c_i in the point-action notation. The level-three quotient with
point weights (-1,0) permits degree-p cross coefficients of valuation 1,
so the proposed two-step logarithm improvement does not follow.
The complete B64 coefficient proof was reread to check this sign.

No assertion was sent for promotion, and no program was executed for this
discarded route. The subsequent B71 argument instead uses actual marked
quotients and exact point-field ramification, avoiding a thickness claim.
