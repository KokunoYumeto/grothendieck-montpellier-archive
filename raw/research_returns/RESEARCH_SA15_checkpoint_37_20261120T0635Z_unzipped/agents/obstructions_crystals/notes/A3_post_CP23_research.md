# Post-CP23 positive-index research — unproved routes retained

## Exact remaining scope
The fixed-surface primitive mixed case has ordinary polarization index a>0 and local index b=0. The proved common-power theorem treats 0<a<=b. The positive-index determinant-variation reduction remains a common-radical/nonvanishing problem; none of the routes below is promoted.

## Odd polarization factor
If chi(M)=2^a D with D odd, the canonical etale cyclic quotient of degree 2^a leaves chi(H)=D, not necessarily a principal polarization. Any principal-source argument must retain this factor. A possible odd-degree norm reduction uses N=2^a D lambda_M^{-1} on A-dual, an odd isotropic quotient psi:A-dual->C of degree D with psi^*H=N, then R=2^a lambda_H^{-1} on C-dual and phi=psi-dual. Formally phi^*M=D R and Nm_phi R=M in NS. Actual nodal interpolation and all integral descent hypotheses have not been established for this route, so no transfer theorem is claimed.

## Possible local-factor inseparable reduction
A quotient by the local alpha_2 factor might give phi:B->A and phi^*M=N^2 with chi(N)=chi(M)/2 and ordinary index lowered by one. Norm images of smooth curves would be cuspidal, not nodal. Independent p-rank gains under smoothing the cusps are not proved; ordinary local jet interpolation alone does not supply them. This route is unproved.

## The boundary pairing that actually occurs
The already proved dual exact sequence 0->Q-dual->g-dual->i_*Rs->0, with g acyclic, identifies U=H1(Q-dual) with H0(D,Rs). Thus the surface cup-wedge map is a pairing of boundary classes from this residual pencil. It is not the ordinary A tensor Rs multiplication map. A derived exterior-square resolution could identify this boundary pairing more explicitly, but no such formula or nonvanishing theorem has yet been proved.

## Koszul route to investigate
For the nowhere-zero combined section (s0,s1) of W=V direct-sum VP, the Koszul resolution, twisted by L=det(V), has the schematic shape
0 -> L^-1 -> W-dual -> wedge^2(W-dual) tensor L -> W -> L ->0.
For this L twist the middle bundle splits as O^2 direct-sum End(V)P, but this is the same-weight contraction, NOT the actual determinant-direction map. The actual crossed determinant map uses the LP twist and has middle bundle P^2 direct-sum End(V). Thus the acyclicity of End(V)P cannot be silently used for the actual determinant directions. This correction was made before any theorem or audit claim. Typed differentials and a genuine lower bound remain to be established.

## Shortcuts explicitly rejected
- Replacing the actual determinant directions Delta_s by all H0(LP) ignores H1(KP).
- The general smooth-curve pencil trick controls a different multiplication map.
- A claimed uniform h0(KP)=1 is not available; descended alpha_2 examples have additional kernel pairs.
- A torus action on the pencil acts through squares in characteristic two, so orbit dimension does not prove differential rank.
- The odd-part quotient is not automatically principal.
