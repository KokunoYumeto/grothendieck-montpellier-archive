\subsection{The unipotent quotient bundle in the primitive mixed case}

\begin{lemma}[An explicit two-step quotient]
\label{lem:DE-A3-primitive-unipotent}
\status{Proved; complete independent root and companion audits passed.}
Work over an algebraically closed field of characteristic two. Keep the
primitive mixed hypotheses, with local polarization index zero,
and write the relative Frobenius factorization as
\(B\xrightarrow{\eta}C\xrightarrow{\nu}T\), where \(\eta\) is a
\(\mu _2\)-torsor and \(\nu\) an \(\alpha _2\)-torsor. Let
\(H=\eta^*J\), \(V=\nu_*J\), and \(L=\operatorname{Nm}_\nu J\).
Then the rank-two bundle
\[
 U=\operatorname{End}^0(V)/\mathcal O_T
   \simeq F_{\mathrm{abs},T}^*V\otimes L^{-1}
\]
has an exact filtration \(0\to\mathcal O_T\to U\to\mathcal O_T\to0\).
All absolute-Frobenius notation in this display is semilinear. In relative
notation its right-hand side is \(F_T^*(V^{(2)})\otimes L^{-1}\), where
\(F_T:T\to T^{(2)}\).
In local torsor coordinates \(z_i^2=h_i\), \(z_j=z_i+c_{ij}\), and
transition functions \(a_{ij}+b_{ij}z_i\) for \(J\), its extension class
is represented by
\[
 \gamma_{ij}=\frac{b_{ij}^{2}}{a_{ij}^{2}+b_{ij}^{2}h_i}.
 \tag{DE-UB1}
\]
This class is nonzero in the primitive local-index-zero case.
Consequently the previously acyclic bundle \(\mathfrak g=UP\) is an
extension of \(P\) by \(P\) with nonzero extension class.
More generally, the same construction with positive local index gives
\(U\simeq\mathcal O_T^{\oplus2}\) and
\(\mathfrak g\simeq P^{\oplus2}\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The characteristic-two conjugation representation on trace-zero
two-by-two matrices modulo scalars is the Frobenius-twisted standard
representation divided by the determinant. This proves the displayed
bundle identification by direct change of frame.

The assertion about the filtration can also be verified without any
classification of homogeneous bundles. On the indicated overlap a
transition matrix for \(V\) is
\[
 M_{ij}=\begin{pmatrix}
 a&ac+bh_i\\ b&bc+a
 \end{pmatrix},\qquad
 N_{ij}=\det M_{ij}=a^2+b^2h_i,
 \quad h_j=h_i+c^2.
\]
The normalized squared matrix \(M_{ij}^{[2]}/N_{ij}\) sends
\((h_j,1)^t\) to \((h_i,1)^t\). In the frames with ordered columns
\((h_i,1)^t,(1,0)^t\), its matrix is
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}1&b^2/N_{ij}\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
This gives the actual global subline and quotient line, together with
(DE-UB1). In particular the filtration is not being descended from an
unmarked splitting after a purely inseparable cover.

For nonzeroness, let \(\delta\) be the invariant vector field generating
the \(\alpha _2\)-action on \(C\); locally \(\delta(z_i)=1\).
The class
\(\kappa_{ij}=\delta(a+bz_i)/(a+bz_i)=b/(a+bz_i)\)
is the value \(d\lambda_J(\delta)\) in
\(\operatorname{Lie}(C^\vee)=H^1(C,\mathcal O_C)\).
Indeed infinitesimal translation by \(\epsilon\delta\) changes the
transition function by the factor \(1+\epsilon\kappa_{ij}\), which
is the defining differential of the polarization homomorphism.

Let \(\nu^c:T\to C^{(2)}\) denote the complementary isogeny, with
\(\nu^c\nu=F_C\) and \(\nu^{(2)}\nu^c=F_T\).
Its local pullback sends the twisted transition of \(a+bz_i\) to
\(a^2+b^2h_i\). Its kernel is the remaining \(\mu _2\) factor of
\(\ker F_C=\mu _2\times\alpha _2\), and
\((\nu^c)^*J^{(2)}=L\). Its dual isogeny has etale kernel, so
\[
 (\nu^c)^*:H^1(C^{(2)},\mathcal O_{C^{(2)}})
     \longrightarrow H^1(T,\mathcal O_T)
\]
is an isomorphism, by the exact dual-kernel theorem
\cite[Theorem~7.5 and proof, p.~100]{EdixhovenVanDerGeerMoonenAV}.
Directly
\((\nu^c)^*\kappa_{ij}^{(2)}=b^2/N_{ij}=\gamma_{ij}\).

Since \(\eta\) is an isomorphism on the local-local divisible group,
the local index of \(J\) remains zero. The local-local block of
\(\lambda_J\) is therefore an isomorphism. It does not kill the
nonzero local \(\alpha _2\) tangent \(\delta\), so \(\kappa\ne0\),
and the displayed isomorphism gives \(\gamma\ne0\).
If the local index is positive instead, the local-local block of
\(\lambda_J\) is divisible by \([2]\), so its differential kills
\(\delta\). Then \(\kappa=0\), hence \(\gamma=0\), and the displayed
extension splits. The local index here is that of the actual line
bundle \(H=\eta^*J\), which agrees with that of \(J\); it is not
silently replaced by the index of a primitive root of \(H\).
Here preservation of the local index also follows directly from
\(\lambda_H=\eta^\vee\lambda_J\eta\), the pullback-polarization formula
\cite[Proposition~7.6 and proof, p.~100]{EdixhovenVanDerGeerMoonenAV}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[What this calculation does not prove]
\label{rem:DE-A3-primitive-unipotent-scope}
\status{Proved; complete independent root and companion audits passed.}
The formula also gives \(d\gamma_{ij}=\gamma_{ij}^2\,dh_i\), with
\(dh_i\) the global differential of the \(\alpha _2\)-torsor.
Lemma~\ref{lem:DE-A3-unipotent-Frobenius} below proves that
\([\gamma]\) lies in the Frobenius-nilpotent line of
\(H^1(\mathcal O_T)\). That additional identification is not needed
for the filtration above.
Neither the filtration nor nonzeroness of its extension class proves
the required nonvanishing of the residual two-step boundary after
restriction to \(\Delta_s\). The actual missing assertion remains
\(\dim\operatorname{im}\Psi_s\ge2\).
In particular an even exponent may raise the local index of the actual
section bundle, placing it in the split case rather than the primitive
nonsplit case. No all-degree nonvanishing is inferred by identifying
these two situations.
