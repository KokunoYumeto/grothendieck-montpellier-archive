\subsection*{Candidate norm-thirteen cubic reduction}
No geometric or monodromy conclusion is asserted in this note.
On $C_z:y^2+y=ax^5+bx^3$, put $a=z^2$, $b=z^2+z+1$,
$\delta=z+1\ne0$, and use the established order-four automorphism
$g(x,y)=(x+1,y+G(x))$, with
$G(x)=zx^2+\delta x+\beta$, $\beta^2+\beta=\delta$.
Then $\psi=3+2g$ has Rosati norm $13$.

For $P=(p,y_0)$, put $d_1=ap^4+bp^2$, $d_2=d_1^2+bp$,
$F=G(p)+d_1$, $Z=\delta+G(p)+d_1+d_2$.
The general section of $H^0(7W)$ with order three at $P$, after imposing
the value at $gP$, has its remaining derivative equation
$\delta l+Zc=0$. Thus set $l=Z,c=\delta$.
The cubic coefficient simplifies to
$A_3=Z(F+\delta)+\delta^2$, and $F^2=Z+ap$.
The proposed norm factorization is
\[
 X^3(X+1)^2\left(aZ^2X^2+(A_3^2+apZ^2)X
                         +\delta Z^2+a\delta^2\right).
\]
Accordingly the residual global coefficients would satisfy
\[
 aL=Z+\delta^2+\delta^4/Z^2,\qquad
 p^2+Lp=M+\delta/a+\delta^2/Z^2.
\]
Set $j=aL+\delta^2$, $k=\delta^4$, $e=\delta^{-2}$.
Then $C(Z)=Z^3+jZ^2+k$ has distinct nonzero roots.
Also
\[
 Z=a^2p^8+(a^2+1)p^4+(a+1)p^2+ap+\beta+\delta.
\]
Reducing by $p^2+Lp=u_0+eZ$ gives
\[
 A(L)p=b_2Z^2+b_1Z+b_0,
\]
where
\[
\begin{gathered}
 A=a^2L^7+(a^2+1)L^3+(a+1)L+a,\\
 H=a^2L^6+(a^2+1)L^2+(a+1),\quad J=a^2L^4+(a^2+1),\\
 b_2=Je^2+a^2e^4j^2,\qquad b_1=1+He+a^2e^4k.
\end{gathered}
\]
The expected generic triple obstruction is
\[
 P_2=b_2^2j^2+b_1^2+LA b_2=0,\qquad
 P_1=b_2^2k+LA b_1+A^2e=0.
\]
At $A=0$, repeated values of $b_2Z^2+b_1Z$ are detected by
\[
 T=b_1^3+j^2b_1b_2^2+kb_2^3=0.
\]
These identities require exact verification, special-fibre analysis,
and a uniform good-parameter proof before any theorem is asserted.
