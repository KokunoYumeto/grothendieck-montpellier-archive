\subsection{An arbitrary odd norm step from any known odd degree at least three}
\label{subsec:cubic-component-norm-transfer-candidate}
\status{Candidate; complete symbolic extension submitted for audit.}

Work over an algebraically closed field of characteristic two. Let
$\phi:(B,\Theta_B)\to(A,\Theta_A)$ be an etale polarized isogeny
of odd multiplier $n>1$, with $B$ nonordinary and indecomposable.
Put $K=\ker\phi$, $k=n^2$, and $\mathcal H=(K\setminus0)/\pm$.
Fix an odd integer $e\ge3$ and assume the relative image of
$|3e\Theta_B|$ is the full group $\mathrm{GL}_r(\mathbf F_2)$,
where $r=9e^2-1$. Write $R=9ke^2-1$, $t=(k-1)/2$, and
$\delta=9e^2(k-1)$.

\begin{lemma}[Clean cubic-component boundaries and all node types]
\label{lem:cubic-component-clean-node-product-candidate}
\textup{[Candidate.]}
There is a clean common target boundary formed by the norms of
$e$ smooth sharp members $C_i\in|3\Theta_B|$, simultaneously for
every source partition $C_i+r_i$ with $r_i\in K$ and $\sum r_i=0$.
Every individual norm is nodal, all mutual intersections are
transverse, and no target branch packets coincide. One can instead
impose exactly one simple contact of any prescribed shifted
component pair, retaining all other conditions.

For the generic norm of a smooth sharp source in $|3e\Theta_B|$,
the complete additional-node permutation image is
\[
                         \prod_{h\in\mathcal H}S_{18e^2}.
\]
It has $\delta$ additional nodes and lies in target relative rank $R$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Start with the clean three-cubic configuration, or the one prescribed
simple contact, of
Lemma~\ref{lem:nine-theta-clean-cubic-contacts-candidate}.
Choose the remaining cubics one at a time. Each avoids the finite
singular and pair-intersection sets already chosen. A two-point
norm packet of the new component meeting any fixed old component
has a one-dimensional point parameter and two independent value
conditions. Its pair tangencies with an old component are proper
by the same joint-jet calculation. A new component through a fixed
old pair intersection is a single nonzero value condition. Thus
all new triple packets and contacts are proper incidences.
The individual smooth/sharp/nodal-norm conditions remain dense
opens. Finitely many component choices and kernel shifts impose
only finitely many such proper exclusions. This gives all the
clean assertions, including a single isolated shifted contact.
Translating by a sum-zero tuple preserves the actual source
line bundle $\mathcal O_B(3e\Theta_B)$ and leaves the target fixed.

The generic nodality and ordered-two-node incidence proof of
Lemma~\ref{lem:nine-theta-node-product-candidate} applies with
coefficient dimension $r$. Four values are independent: multiply
the three cubic separating sections used there by a section of
$3(e-3)\Theta_B$ nonzero at the four points, omitting this factor
when $e=3$. Generic translated-pair first jets follow similarly
from the cubic jets by a local-unit factor. The irreducible main
ordered-two-node incidence and lower-dimensional overlap strata
give two-transitivity in each type. The isolated shifted contact
constructed above gives one genuine characteristic-two quadratic
transposition in that type alone. The larger normal node-cover
open and its dense smooth sharp restriction justify this inertia
exactly as in the nine-theta proof, with no stable-fibre comparison.
Hence the product image is as stated. Each type has
$(3e\Theta_B)^2=18e^2$ nodes, so their total is $\delta$.
Normalization adds this many unit graph directions to the source
relative rank $r$, giving $r+\delta=R$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The weighted complete graph and all gluing hearts]
\label{prop:cubic-component-weighted-graph-gluing-candidate}
\textup{[Candidate.]}
The norm-family image contains an actual natural
$\mathrm{GL}_r$ fixing a complement and every translation in
$\operatorname{Hom}(U,T_h^0)$, where $\dim U=r$ and
$T_h=\mathbf F_2^{18e^2}$ is the coordinate node module of type $h$.
For the common target graph $G$, all translated-partition hearts
span its total-edge-sum hyperplane. A type-permutation-invariant
subspace of one $T_r$ contained in all partition kernels is zero.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Use $e$ vertices, eighteen loops per vertex and nonzero unordered
type, and eighteen cross edges per vertex pair and label $g\in K$.
Select label $r_j-r_i$ on pair $i,j$ for a tuple with $\sum r_i=0$.
There are
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 E&=18e\frac{k-1}{2}+18k\binom e2=9ke^2-9e,\\
 \dim G&=E-e+1=9ke^2-10e+1,\\
 \dim G_{\rm selected}&=18\binom e2-e+1=9e^2-10e+1.
 \end{aligned}                                                    \tag{EC.1}
\]
The selected graph is connected, so unselected cochains embed
in graph cohomology and form the kernel $T_r$ of dimension
$\delta$. Each type consists of $18e$ loops and
$36\binom e2$ cross edges, hence has dimension $18e^2$.

Here is the uniform graph argument, without restricting to a finite
list of groups or numbers of components. A dual cycle annihilating
all coordinate hearts is constant on every type. Loop anchors make
each such constant independent of the partition. For any fixed
pair $i,j$, its selected difference can be every element of $K$:
put the required shift on $j$ and its negative on a third vertex.
Varying this difference makes every cross-edge value equal to
every nonzero type constant. Thus all edge values are a single
constant. The all-ones vector is indeed a cycle, since each vertex
has $18k(e-1)$ incident cross edges. The span of the hearts is
therefore exactly the total-sum hyperplane $G^0$.

If a vector in a common invariant subspace is nonconstant in one
type, transposition differences and the full symmetric factor
give every pair vector in that type. Choose a loop and a cross
edge, then a partition selecting that cross edge. Its restriction
has just one nonzero coordinate among eighteen parallel selected
edges, so cannot be a vertex coboundary. This is impossible.
All vectors are consequently type-constant. Write their constants
as an even function $a:K\to\mathbf F_2$, with $a(0)=0$.
Use a partition $(-u,0,u,0,\ldots,0)$. The selected triangle on
the first three vertices has coboundary condition
$a(u)+a(u)+a(2u)=0$. Multiplication by two is bijective on $K$,
so all constants vanish. This proves the common-kernel assertion.

For the representation, the simple group $\mathrm{GL}_r$ cannot
occur in a symmetric factor $S_{2r+2}$, since
\[
 v_2|\mathrm{GL}_r|=r(r-1)/2>2r+1
                         \ge v_2|(S_{2r+2})|.
\]
Goursat and the proved natural-module first-cohomology vanishing
give the actual source block and a split quotient
$(T/T')\oplus U$, with actual translation kernel $U^*\otimes T'$.
The full Lang projection is a finite etale $T/T'$-torsor over the
normal source Lang scheme, so the splitting extends across the
reducible source by the exact finite-birational argument of
Proposition~\ref{prop:nine-theta-full-gluing-candidate}.

For each component pair its selected eighteen intersection points
have full $S_{18}$ monodromy. The cubic-intersection proof applies
to arbitrary two Picard twists, not just opposite twists: the
restriction kernel is $H^0(\alpha_j\alpha_i^{-1})$, still of
dimension zero or one, and all the surface/curve jet and tangency
counts are unchanged. The other components impose only the clean
dense open conditions just verified. Thus the isolated selected
contact and every $S_{18}$ conjugate force the splitting to lift
each selected pair difference to its exact target cochain modulo
$T'$. Summing nine disjoint pair differences gives the entire
eighteen-edge packet. At a vertex all incident selected packet
totals sum to a source coboundary; their target sum is the
unselected vertex star modulo the full target coboundary. In
every type this star has
\[
              36(e-1)\text{ ones},\qquad
              18e^2-36(e-1)\text{ zeros}.                         \tag{EC.2}
\]
Both numbers are positive. An independent type transposition
and its conjugates therefore put the whole heart $T_h^0$ in $T'$.
This proves every claimed gluing column. The component torsors
are trivial on strict henselian local branch neighbourhoods, so
the selected inertia image is exactly the pure pair line, as in
the full nine-theta proof; no extra component column is assumed
away in this comparison.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Arbitrary odd norm transfer from a known degree]
\label{thm:cubic-component-arbitrary-odd-norm-transfer-candidate}
\textup{[Candidate.]}
Under the opening hypotheses the relative geometric group of
$|3en\Theta_A|$ is $\mathrm{GL}_{9e^2n^2-1}(\mathbf F_2)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Correct the fixed norm line by one target translation. A nonzero
proper global invariant subspace $S$ projects either to zero or
onto the simple $U$ on the connected maximal-rank norm family.
All partitions of the common boundary lie in that same source
complete system. The graph embeds relatively because ambient
cohomology injects into each smooth ample cubic normalization.
For $f=f(B)$, the source and target boundary relative ranks are
\[
 r-(e-1)(2-f),\qquad R-(e-1)(2-f),                              \tag{EC.3}
\]
respectively. Indeed each component contributes $8+f$, the graph
dimensions are (EC.1), and the ambient quotient removes $f$.

Apply only the proved clopen closure in the full normal relative
Lang scheme. In the zero-projection case, all of $S$ survives
inside every finite etale partition kernel; the common invariant
kernel assertion makes it zero. In the surjective case, the
gluing maps put every heart in $S$. At the common boundary they
span $G^0$, and $\dim(G^0\cap T_r)=\delta-1$.
Clopen intersection with the finite etale $T_r$ transports this
lower bound back to the generic family. Thus
$\dim S\ge\delta-1+r=R-1$. A proper hyperplane would give a
fixed quotient covector, excluded by the exact odd-degree
nonordinary covector theorem. This proves irreducibility without
identifying any full stable fibres across the rank drop (EC.3).

The normal transvection subgroup has semisimple representation
by the normal-socle argument. Its actual natural $\mathrm{GL}_r$
block has multiplicity one and belongs to a simple summand; all
type hearts join that summand by the gluing transvections.
Its dimension is at least
\[
                r+(18e^2-1)t=R-t>R/2.
\]
Every conjugate intersects it, so this summand is invariant under
the full irreducible image and is all of $V$. The previously
proved transvection classification and actual-block no-alternating-
form argument give $\mathrm{GL}_R$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[All odd multiples of three, five, or seven in two-rank zero]
\label{cor:f0-odd-multiples-three-five-seven-candidate}
\textup{[Candidate.]}
On every fixed two-rank-zero principally polarized surface over
a perfect field of characteristic two, every positive odd degree
divisible by at least one of $3,5,7$ has geometric and arithmetic
relative image $\mathrm{GL}_{9d^2-1}(\mathbf F_2)$. Every even
degree has the already proved full one-covector stabilizer.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Write $d=en$ with $e\in\{3,5,7\}$ dividing $d$. For a nonproduct
target, Theorem~\ref{thm:nonordinary-arbitrary-odd-neighbor-candidate}
supplies a nonproduct incoming source of multiplier $n$ and the
same two-rank. Each of these three source degrees has already
been proved on every fixed two-rank-zero principal surface.
For $n>1$ use the preceding theorem, and for $n=1$ use that known
degree directly. Products were previously classified in every
degree. Full geometric general linear image forces the same
arithmetic image. The all-even assertion is
Corollary~\ref{cor:f0-all-even-nine-theta-transfer-candidate}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Scope and verification]
\label{rem:cubic-component-norm-transfer-scope-candidate}
\textup{[Candidate.]}
The transfer requires an already known full source degree
$e\ge3$ odd. It does not give the missing source degree one
transfer, and does not prove all odd degrees. The first remaining
fixed two-rank-zero odd degree is eleven; fixed two-rank-one
degrees five and eight also remain separate. The proof is a
uniform graph and geometric extension of the fully audited
nine-theta argument. No further finite group census or numerical
extrapolation is used; all new dimensions and the star formula
are derived in (EC.1)--(EC.3). Complete independent audits are
required before promotion.
\end{remark}
