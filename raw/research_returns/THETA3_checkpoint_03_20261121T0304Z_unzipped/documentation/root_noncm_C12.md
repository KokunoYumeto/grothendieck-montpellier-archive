# THETA3-C12: a purely non-CM four-form period of normalized value 500

Source input: Schoen, *An arithmetic ball quotient surface whose Albanese variety is not of CM type* (2014), pp133–134. Let Y be the smooth compact arithmetic ball surface obtained by resolving the complete intersection

    y01^5-y03^5+y13^5=0,
    y02^5-y03^5+y23^5=0,
    y12^5-y13^5+y23^5=0

in P5. Schoen proves this resolution is a ball quotient and that each Fermat-curve projection extends to a morphism. Let C5 be the Fermat quintic, C0 its degree5 quotient with equation y²=1-x^5, and C→C0 the etale double cover obtained by normalizing the fibre product with the elliptic curve F: s²=1+x+x²+x³+x⁴. Schoen proves j(F)=2048 and F non-CM. The map C→F has degree2.

**Statement.** There is a connected finite etale cover X→Y of degree4 with a generically finite morphism (a1,a2):X→F×F of degree500. For an invariant holomorphic form alpha on F normalized by integral_F i alpha∧bar(alpha)=1, the holomorphic2-form xi=a1*alpha∧a2*alpha satisfies

    integral_X xi∧bar(xi)=500.

All four degree-one sources in this period are the non-CM Hodge structure H1(F,Q). The map (a1,a2,a1,a2):X→F⁴ has pushforward cycle

    500 [Delta_13 × Delta_24],

where the notation means the surface defined by x1=x3 and x2=x4. Thus the construction reaches a purely non-CM four-factor period, while its Hodge class is a product of divisor classes.

**Proof.** The two Fermat projections (y01:y03:y13) and (y02:y03:y23) give a dominant morphism Y→C5×C5. On the affine patch y03=1 put u=y13 and v=y23. The function field of C5×C5 is generated by u,v,y01,y02 with y01^5=1-u^5 and y02^5=1-v^5. The function field of Y adjoins y12 satisfying y12^5=u^5-v^5. Along the diagonal of C5×C5, at its generic point where u is a local parameter and u!=0, the function u^5-v^5 has valuation1: it is (u-v) times a unit. Consequently it is not a fifth power, and the Kummer extension has degree5. Resolution does not change function fields. Composing with the two degree5 maps C5→C0 gives a morphism f:Y→C0×C0 of generic degree125.

The product C×C→C0×C0 is a connected Galois etale cover of degree4 with group (Z/2)^2. Its degree is coprime to125, so its function field is linearly disjoint from that of Y over the base field: their intersection has degree dividing both4 and125, and the degree4 extension is Galois. Therefore

    X=Y×_(C0×C0)(C×C)

is connected, finite etale of degree4 overY, and has generic degree125 overC×C. It is smooth projective and its analytic universal cover remains the complex ball. Its lattice has finite index in the arithmetic lattice of Y, so remains arithmetic (not asserted congruence). The two degree2 maps C→F give a generically finite map X→F×F of degree125*2*2=500.

Set eta=pr1*alpha∧pr2*alpha on F×F. Since integral_F alpha∧bar(alpha)=−i, the Koszul sign from moving bar(alpha) across the second alpha gives integral_(F×F) eta∧bar(eta)=−(−i)^2=1. Integration of a top-degree form under a proper generically finite degree500 map multiplies by500. Equivalently its pushforward fundamental class is500[F×F]. This proves the period formula. Postcompose with the closed immersion (p,q)↦(p,q,p,q) to obtain the stated pushforward class in F⁴. That closed image is the transverse intersection of the two diagonal divisors; hence its class is their product. QED.

**Limit and next gate.** The new non-CM forms come from the noncongruence covering curves, not from the adelic U(1) theta lift of the original congruence machine. The positive degree500 period does not supply an exceptional Hodge tensor; all its algebraicity is already expressed by diagonal divisors. A source whose maps do not factor through this product-of-curves construction, together with C04's projector/rank condition, is still required for that further step. The result sharpens the scope of the non-CM escape rather than claiming a new general Hodge-conjecture case.
