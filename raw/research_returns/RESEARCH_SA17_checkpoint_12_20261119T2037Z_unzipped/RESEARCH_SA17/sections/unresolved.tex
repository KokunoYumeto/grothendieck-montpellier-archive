Theorem~\ref{A:main-counterexample}. The henselized functor $P$
satisfies all eight intended conditions, including arbitrary fpqc

Theorem~\ref{A:pro7-representability} gives the proved correction
using pro-modularity; the unramified corollary, quasi-modular charts,

L1 is now proved independently of actual local representability:
Theorem~\ref{A:L1-general} establishes openness of every actual
modularity locus on a locally finite-type test. Its proof propagates
Artinian formal \'etaleness from an actual modular point, uses one
uniform thickening along a boundary stratum, and constructs the
actual open chart only after that infinitesimal argument.
Thus no A.1 gate remains. The openness assertion does not supply
the missing existence of actual local models in the false main
criterion.

L2 is refuted by Theorem~\ref{A:L2-counterexample}, which verifies
every other axiom, including all noetherian adic limits, for the
supported subgroup of $\alpha_p$. The dimension-one independence

Proposition~\ref{A:hensel-adic-failure} proves.

exhaustive certificates at two and three. The requested literature

refuted by Proposition~\ref{B:PD-not-QSyn}: a partially perfected fibre
product is reduced, $F$-finite and PD torsion free but not quasisyntomic.

filtered-colimit stability. Its \'etale-local envelope is stable under
finite products and \'etale descent but still fails filtered-colimit

C.1 now has the exact criterion for every regular coherent ring:
characteristic zero and the unit isomorphism $\mathbb Z\simeq K_0(k)$

Theorem~\ref{C:coherent-regular-modular-locus} requires local regular

obstruction sequence and explicit

\emph{Odd} torsion also occurs in this comparison kernel over the
dyadic cubic thickening: Theorem~\ref{C:cubic_three_torsion} constructs
independent classes of exact order three for the group of order two.
This prevents an attempted reduction which would discard all torsion
prime to the acting group order.
Theorem~\ref{C:squarezero_intrinsic_criterion} supplies an exact
intrinsic criterion for every $A\ltimes J$, with $A$ a
characteristic-zero DVR of maximal ideal $(2)$ and $J$ any module:
the full polynomial answer holds if and only if $2:J\to J$ is
bijective. Theorem~\ref{C:ramified_squarezero_criterion} extends this to
every characteristic-zero DVR with residue characteristic two,
with arbitrary ramification and arbitrary $J$. This includes every
square-zero thickening of $\mathbb Z_{(2)}$, with no finiteness
restriction. Theorem~\ref{C:allprime_squarezero_criterion}
also gives the exact criterion for every square-zero module over
an unramified characteristic-zero DVR of arbitrary residue
characteristic $p$: multiplication by $p$ must be bijective.
The primary curvature-length invariant detects every nonzero
$p$-torsion direction, including those in divisible modules, by
passing through finite submodules with arbitrarily large exponents.
Theorem~\ref{C:ramified_torsion_infinite} proves infinite order modulo
the polynomial image for every full-ramification-height torsion witness,
at every prime. Every unramified torsion witness therefore has infinite
order. The cyclotomic cancellation in
Proposition~\ref{C:cyclotomic_torsion_cancellation} shows why this argument
cannot simply be extended to lower-height ramified torsion.
Theorem~\ref{C:cyclotomic_flat_criterion} also settles every flat
module at minimal cyclotomic ramification, using a nonsplit integral
lattice. Theorem~\ref{C:dyadic_normal_node_criterion} classifies
all local polynomial and formal nodes $xy=2^n u$ over every dyadic DVR
with maximal ideal $(2)$: polynomiality holds exactly for $n=1$,
equivalently exactly when the node is regular.
Theorem~\ref{C:ramified_exponent_two_node} additionally settles exponent

Theorem~\ref{C:normal_trivialK0_kernel} shows that even normal local

Proposition~\ref{C:shallow_commuting_trace} removes all commuting

Theorem~\ref{C:S3_isotypic_shallow_cancellation} further removes every

\begin{remark}[Open: the remaining modular orthogonal classification]
\label{C:BO_general_gate}
The explicit canonical template and operation theorems now determine
C.3 over algebraically closed odd-characteristic fields and their
finite-dimensional commutative coefficient algebras.
Theorem~\ref{C:Om-finite-base-algorithm} determines every finite
commutative ring with two invertible; Theorem~\ref{C:finite-ring-powers}
extends this to arbitrary powers of any one such ring, with exact
finite-image-function coordinates.
Theorem~\ref{C:all_algebraic_fields_WG} gives the full answer over
every algebraic extension of a finite field: finite Frobenius orbits,
Schur index one, integer coordinates and a possible determinant bit.
Its finite operation procedures state the precise subfield-degree access.

Theorem~\ref{C:artinian_effective_imperfect_C1} gives complete recursive
indices and all operations over every finite-dimensional algebra over
an effective countable $C_1$ field, including imperfect fields when
factorization over finite towers is supplied. Projectivity is decided
by a linear splitting test, and the dual throughout is $\operatorname{Hom}_R(-,R)$.
The stated arithmetic access is not deduced from the abstract $C_1$ property.
Theorem~\ref{C:function-field-WG} removes the factorization hypothesis
for every explicitly presented finite tower over $\overline{\mathbb F}_p(t)$:
the preceding three lemmas prove the needed ordinary terminating algorithms,
including inseparable extensions.
For every perfect field and every finite-dimensional coefficient algebra
over it, Theorems~\ref{C:perfect_Galois_indices} and
\ref{C:artinian_perfect_Galois_indices} classify all indecomposables
by finite geometric Galois orbits, with explicit Schur-index data.
Corollary~\ref{C:perfect-residue-Artin-reduction} includes every Artin
ring of prime characteristic with perfect residue fields.
Their residual hermitian groups now have explicit scalar presentations
and finite operation-extraction formulas in
Theorem~\ref{C:hermitian_scalar_presentation} and
Corollary~\ref{C:WG_hermitian_scalar_operations}.
The separate division-index and primitive-idempotent arithmetic remains
explicitly supplied input; an arbitrary-field word-problem algorithm
is not asserted.

Theorems~\ref{C:complete-finite-canonical}--\ref{C:complete-finite-products}
give exact coordinates for all complete Noetherian local rings with finite
odd residue field and arbitrary products of such rings.
Their image in the solved finite-ring coordinates is characterized by
one uniform bound on the ranks and coefficient degrees of representing
pairs of forms. The fixed finite-coordinate tests terminate; the theorem
does not assert a finite test for the unbounded quantifiers.
Theorem~\ref{C:profinite-WG} removes both locality and Noetherianity:
it gives the exact bounded finite-coordinate answer for every profinite
commutative ring with two invertible. The rank bound is replaced by a
uniform size for an ambient projective idempotent matrix. Directed-system
compactness proves the image and injectivity without assuming cancellation
over the limiting ring.
Theorems~\ref{C:complete_algclosed_indices} and
\ref{C:complete_algclosed_WG} settle complete Noetherian local algebras
with a specified countable algebraically closed odd-characteristic
coefficient field and effective finite quotient tables.
Their canonical indices are compatible infinite arrays, and their
integer operation coefficients are object-dependent eventual constants.
Proposition~\ref{C:complete_unbounded_detection} proves that no fixed
rank-and-degree truncation cutoff can replace this qualification,
and that coefficient streams alone do not decide equality.

Theorems~\ref{C:finite-field-henselization-WG} and
\ref{C:arithmetic-henselization-WG} give ordinary terminating
classification and operation algorithms for every henselization of a
finitely presented integer algebra at a supplied finite odd-residue point.
This includes singular, nonreduced and mixed-characteristic local rings,
without a factorization or stabilization oracle.
Theorem~\ref{C:arithmetic-strict-henselization-WG} supplies the full
ordinary answer for their strict henselizations as well, with a
specified computable algebraic closure of the finite residue field.
Theorem~\ref{C:effective-henselian-flat-unions} removes Noetherianity
for computable flat local unions with unchanged residue field.
Corollary~\ref{C:infinite-variable-henselian-WG} includes the
henselizations and strict henselizations of local polynomial rings
in countably many variables over finite fields and over the integers
at a finite odd-residue point; these have infinite Krull dimension.
Theorem~\ref{C:henselian-products-WG} gives the exact answer for
arbitrary products of these solved local factors. Its image is specified
by the three explicit minimal positive-rank, negative-rank and
coefficient-degree bounds of
Proposition~\ref{C:henselian-minimal-form-bounds}.

Theorems~\ref{C:C1-henselization-WG} and
\ref{C:function-field-henselization-WG} extend the ordinary answer to
the stated effective, possibly imperfect, $C_1$ residue fields and
all explicitly supplied finite towers over $\overline{\mathbf F}_p(t)$.
Theorem~\ref{C:effective-coherent-henselization-WG} uses finite matrix
kernels instead of Noetherianity. Its lexicographic valuation examples
have nonzero infinitely divisible elements and cannot be flat local
unions of Noetherian local stages, so they remove a further family.
Theorem~\ref{C:C1-coherent-products-WG} gives exact bounded product
coordinates with the full square-class coordinate at symmetric factors.

Theorems~\ref{C:global-function-field-arithmetic}--
\ref{C:global-function-hermitian-isometry} now compute factorization,
all places, actual division algebras, reduced norms, and every hermitian
isometry problem over explicit global function fields of odd
characteristic, including inseparable towers and wild division degree.
The quaternion skew-hermitian argument retains the rank-one norm
obstruction, rather than invoking false local-global isometry.
Theorems~\ref{C:global-function-coefficient-WG} and
\ref{C:global-function-henselization-WG} consequently give complete
ordinary canonical representation and form coordinates, equality,
tensor products and exterior operations over every finite-dimensional
coefficient algebra over those fields, all stated effective local
henselizations, and effective valuation henselizations. No division-index
or hermitian-isometry oracle remains in this family.

Theorems~\ref{C:henselian-global-field-arithmetic} and
\ref{C:henselian-global-coefficient-WG} further give complete ordinary
answers over all supplied finite extensions of henselized global-place
fraction fields, their finite coefficient algebras and the stated effective
local henselizations. The projective-point and regular-completion arguments
decide every required division index and hermitian isometry.
Corollary~\ref{C:henselian-global-new-families} explicitly separates
these non-$C_1$, nonglobal positive-characteristic fields from the earlier
completed families.

Theorems~\ref{C:separably-closed-effective-arithmetic} and
\ref{C:separably-closed-coefficient-WG} remove the explicit finite-imperfection
separably closed fields and their stated coefficient algebras and
coherent local henselizations. Computable $p$-components and Haran's
quantifier elimination give actual arithmetic; the computed division
fields may be purely inseparable, and the form blocks have free integer
coordinates. For imperfection degree at least two, the anisotropic
quasilinear form proves these fields are not $C_1$.
Theorems~\ref{C:infinite-residue-henselian-arithmetic} and
\ref{C:infinite-residue-henselian-WG} further settle all finite towers over
the explicit one-variable henselian fields with these infinite residue
fields. A certified whole residue-field table and an actual integral
basis give each finite quotient; Haran and
Lemma~\ref{C:Greenberg-congruence-decisions} decide the required
congruence, division-index and hermitian-isometry questions.
Corollary~\ref{C:infinite-residue-new-families} proves their separation
from the earlier global and finite-residue families. No unproved
iteration over these new henselian residue fields is asserted.

Thus the remaining task concerns coefficient bases outside the completed
families and residual division-index arithmetic or word-problem access
not supplied by the stated effective hypotheses. This is not made into a
uniform ordinary equality algorithm by a formal matrix presentation:
Theorem~\ref{C:locally-finite-equality-obstruction} proves such an
algorithm impossible from arbitrary computable field arithmetic alone.
The special-$\lambda$ criterion remains complete over every affine
base with two invertible; low ranks and all rational algebras were
already determined. The infinite generation in
Proposition~\ref{C:BO3_not_finitely_generated} is compatible with
all these infinite canonical index sets.
