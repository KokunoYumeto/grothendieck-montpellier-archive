# The tame A2 cover and a precise MCM-comparison boundary

AI-produced research note, GPT-6 Astra Pro (OpenAI), RESEARCH_SA17 / c1_mixed_s3, 2026-11-19. Uncompiled research. This note does not decide polynomiality of the ramified exponent-three node.

## The proposed tame cover

For the node R=A[[x,y]]/(xy-pi^3), adjoining cube roots of unity to A permits the tame cyclic cover S=A[[u,v]]/(uv-pi), with x=u^3 and y=v^3 and the order-three action (u,v) -> (zeta*u,zeta^(-1)*v). The ambient ring S is regular: pi=uv eliminates the class of pi from its cotangent space. The fixed ring is R by the weight decomposition of monomials.

As an R-module, S has one free summand and two rank-one MCM summands isomorphic, in some order, to I1=(x,pi) and I2=(x,pi^2). For example the weight-one part is generated by u and v^2; multiplication by u^2 identifies it with (x,pi^2). The weight-two part is generated by u^2 and v; multiplication by u identifies it with (x,pi). These are nonfree two-generated rank-one modules. Thus restriction of an S-free representation need not be R-projective, already for the free rank-one S module with trivial group action.

The two ideal exact sequences in C:ramified-A2-MF-cancellation give [I1]+[I2]=2[R] and 2[I1]=[R]+[I2] in the MCM Grothendieck group, so [S]=3[R] there. They do not make S perfect over R. A nonfree MCM module over a local Cohen--Macaulay ring cannot have finite projective dimension, by Auslander--Buchsbaum. In particular neither ordinary restriction of scalars nor a finite-projective-dimension transfer is available for this cover.

## Every ordinary MF transfer class disappears after enlarging to MCM representations

Let S0 be regular noetherian local, let g be a nonzero nonunit, and let R=S0/(g). Write E for the exact category of R-free C2 representations and F for the exact category of C2 representations whose underlying finitely generated R-module is MCM; F has all short exact sequences of such representations. The inclusion E -> F is exact.

Take an r by r matrix factorization Phi*Psi=Psi*Phi=g*I and its involution

    T = [[I,Phi],[Psi,-I]] over R.

Let X=coker Phi, Y=coker Psi, and M=(R^(2r),T). The map constructed in C:MF-Swan-transfer has value delta=[M]-r([1]+[sgn]). Then delta maps to zero in K0(F).

Here is a direct exact-sequence proof. The Tate complex of M is acyclic, so there is a short exact sequence in F

    0 -> (Lplus, trivial action) -> M -> (Lminus, sign action) -> 0,

where Lplus=coker(T-I), Lminus=coker(T+I), and the first map identifies Lplus with im(T+I)=ker(T-I). The quotient map is T-I, with image im(T-I)=ker(T+I). All these modules are MCM because T-I and T+I lift to a matrix factorization of g over S0.

Projection onto the first or second block gives, respectively,

    0 -> Y -> Lplus -> X -> 0,
    0 -> X -> Lminus -> Y -> 0.

One can check injectivity over S0, where Phi and Psi are injective, or over R using matrix-factorization exactness. Also the periodic presentation gives 0 -> Y -> R^r -> X -> 0. Hence [Lplus]=[Lminus]=r[R] in K0(MCM R). Applying the exact constant-action functors with signs plus and minus to these relations proves [M]=r([1]+[sgn]) in K0(F).

Therefore

    image Delta_g is contained in ker(K0(E) -> K0(F)).

This supplies a precise reason that passing to MCM representations can lose the very classes under investigation.

## An actual failure of general injectivity

The comparison K0(E) -> K0(F) is not injective for arbitrary hypersurfaces. Take S0=A[epsilon]_(2,epsilon), g=epsilon^3, where A is an unramified characteristic-zero dyadic DVR. Then R=A[epsilon]/epsilon^3 is local. The rank-one factorization Phi=epsilon, Psi=epsilon^2 gives

    T = [[1,epsilon],[epsilon^2,-1]].

This is exactly q=u-1, B=1, d=1 in the already compiled theorem C:cubic_three_torsion (sections/C_finish_cubic.tex). Its relative Swan class has order exactly three, detected by the fully proved first-epsilon-Bockstein invariant. The preceding exact-sequence argument sends that nonzero class to zero in K0(F).

This is an application of the existing audited theorem, not a new finite search or a new detector claim. It also prohibits assuming that the generic Swan kernel over a dyadic ring is two-primary: three is a unit in this coefficient ring, yet the displayed class has order three.

## What would be needed for the A2 node

For the A2 node, the newly audited C:ramified-A2-MF-cancellation proves that the particular class Delta_g(I1), and hence Delta_g(I2), is zero. That removes the above explicit MF source of a comparison kernel for those two ideals. It does not show that every kernel element of K0(E) -> K0(F) is generated by ordinary nonequivariant MF transfer classes. Such a kernel-generation theorem, or another genuine transfer landing in R-projective representations, remains necessary before the tame cover and [S]=3[R] can establish full polynomiality.

No claim that this comparison fails or is injective for the A2 node itself is made here. No finite detector calculation is used in this note.
