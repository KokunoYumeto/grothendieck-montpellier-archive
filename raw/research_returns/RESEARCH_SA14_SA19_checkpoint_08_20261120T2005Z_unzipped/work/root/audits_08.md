## A2Z — separable degree-p construction exclusion

PASS. Read the entire proof and checked the integral mu-p torsor of a nontrivial order-p line bundle, constant rescaling of its pulled-back trivialization, and the finite birational normalization factorization. The torsor is Gorenstein even when nonnormal. Its tautological root trivializes the pulled-back torsion line, so its dualizing sheaf is the pullback of that of the base divisor. Finite duality identifies the normalization conductor with the canonical-sheaf difference; invertibility of both canonical sheaves makes this an effective Cartier divisor. The ramification determinant of the ambient separable map has an effective divisor containing the full inverse-image divisor, and divisor adjunction gives the opposite canonical difference. Intersecting with the ample normal polarization yields a strict contradiction. Opened Stacks Lemma47.15.8 in tag0A7A and Lemma48.14.1 tag0B4B. Degree p, smoothness, and scheme-theoretic equality of the full inverse image are essential retained hypotheses. This excludes the tested construction only.

## A2P — relative Frobenius multisection exclusion

PASS, independently by DGJ after the root derivation. In the quotient projectivization convention, the section class on P(F*E) is H'-p*detE+N; relative Frobenius gives pH-p*detE+N on P(E). On Y, K^p=rho^*N. Substitution into the projective-bundle canonical formula yields normal line Q^p and relative canonical Q^(p-1), where Q=K²*rho^*detE^-1. A killed nontrivial p-torsion class makes Y the normalization of its integral degree-p mu-p torsor, and A2Z's effective conductor gives Q^(p-1)=O(-D). This contradicts an ample normal line. No disjoint-section hypothesis is needed. This is a failed-construction exclusion, not formal-review movement for A2.

## F13c/d — bounded cyclic cover reduction

PASS. For every finite etale resolution cover, proper henselian specialization (Stacks58.9.1, tag0A48, reopened) reduces finite characters to a proper nodal curve. Artin–Schreier and surjectivity of the etale additive polynomial F−1 on a finite-dimensional vector group give H2(Fp)=0, hence p-divisibility of its Qp/Zp characters. Combining this with the already audited fixed Swan bound and Witt-restriction step removes an unramified twist and leaves one uniformly bounded p-power order. The cohomology reduction uses an injection after a cover of degree invertible in the coefficient field. A tame rank-one coefficient and its degree-one affine cocycles both kill wild inertia, since a pro-p group has no nonzero homomorphism to an additive ell-group when ell differs from p. Monomiality and Shapiro convert a degree-linear bound on these bounded-degree cyclic covers into a rank-linear bound. The geometric generator estimate F13.6 is expressly still missing; neither the general local Betti bound nor the derived-length-three case is marked proved.

## F13e — refined-Swan zero length

PASS. Saturation of the nonzero refined-Swan section on each smooth proper exceptional component gives a line bundle with an effective zero divisor. The two line terms in the residue sequence bound its degree. Finite etale covers scale curve genus and marked-node count by the component degree. Components above one original component are disjoint, so projection formula gives the exact self-intersection and off-diagonal sums. Substituting the conductor cutoff and k+Bt=0 yields d_a(-B_ii)(t_i-s_a), with the claimed sign. The sum of the component cover degrees is the total cover degree. This estimate controls zeros on the original resolution only; it does not prove a quantitative cleanup theorem.

## F13f — strict subunit cutoff subclass

PASS for its explicit numerical hypothesis. Integral rank-one Swan coefficients below one vanish. Their p-primary characters are unramified and their remaining characters tame. Induction through finite etale resolution covers preserves tameness, as do extensions because wild inertia has prime-to-coefficient order. Tame cocycles factor through the full tame quotient. F9 and the single fixed-cover inflation bound give the coefficient-prime-not-p estimate; F12 and composition series give coefficient p. The statement does not assert that the cutoff can always be made subunit.

## I7b — strict dense whole-column quotients

PASS. Read work/bce/I_strict_dense_columns.tex. Once the codomain is the actual differential image, a preimage already exists; the finite-Ext correction used in I7 therefore does not require completeness of A. Enlarging a finite cyclic input module by one denominator kills the relevant localized Ext class. Injectivity of the finite target hull summands extends the correction. This proves openness onto the image in the subspace topology. I6 supplies density in the cycle group. The finite topology on maps from a direct sum is the product of the source-column finite topologies, so products preserve openness of these quotient maps and density of their images. Descent to a complete additive target remains conditional on a continuous map defined on the entire preceding-degree family. This does not solve construction or continuity of that map for the broad target category; I remains undecided.

## F13g/h — Swan residue gap and improved exponent

PASS. Independently opened Yatagawa arXiv1712.09234v1 Definition2.1 p8 and the refined-Swan formula (2.5) p9, whose local field setup is an arbitrary complete DVR of characteristic p. Reopened Krishna–Majumder arXiv2601.12177v2 Corollaries9.6–9.7 pp55–56 and Lemma9.11 p57: stalk compatibility alone is supplemented by the latter lemma's identification with Kato's conductor. If a positive Swan coefficient n is prime to p, all higher-weight Witt coordinates contribute only lower pole order; the weight-one coordinate has pole n and nonzero logarithmic residue n times its leading unit. The global residue is therefore a nonzero section of O(S)|C, forcing S.C≥0. A nonzero Swan divisor has negative square, so some component of negative intersection must instead have positive p-divisible coefficient, at least p. Combining this gap with the previously checked Witt contraction improves the uniform unramified exponent to any e≥0 with p^(e+1)>max(t_i), and improves the sufficient numerical hypothesis in F13f from t_i<1 to t_i<p. The general cleanup and derived-length-three gates remain unresolved.
