For each positive exact logarithmic conductor coefficient s_i, the levelwise refined Swan injection gives a nonzero global section of Omega1_X(logD)(S) restricted to C_i. The conormal/residue extension has line degrees 2g_i-2+nu_i and zero, so the degree inequality is S.C_i >= -max(0,2g_i-2+nu_i). If s_i=0 it holds because all off-diagonal intersections are nonnegative. For C=-B positive definite with nonpositive off-diagonal entries, the negative-part quadratic-form argument proves C inverse entrywise nonnegative, so Cs<=k implies s<=C inverse k. The signs are consistent.

Witt restriction in the source's reversed coordinate convention lowers the conductor by floor(n/p); under Z/p^m embedded in Qp/Zp it represents multiplication by p. One fixed p^e therefore makes all p-primary characters unramified along the exceptional divisor. Conductor zero for these characters has an integral Artin–Schreier–Witt representative and is unramified. The prime-to-p intersection/adjugate argument gives the factor |detB|. Purity extends each resulting character over the regular resolution. Finite characters separate points of the abelian profinite group, so its inertia kernel is killed by the fixed product. Its ambient abelian group is finitely generated by the already audited prime-to-p and pro-p results; its subgroup killed by that integer is finite.

Choosing an open abelian subgroup disjoint from that finite inertia gives one fixed cover. For every exceptional DVR on a fixed resolution upstairs, properness maps that DVR back to the original resolution. Its inertia image is therefore both in the original finite inertia kernel and in the chosen open subgroup, so it is zero. This proves simultaneous extension of every pulled-back abelian coefficient system, independently of its rank and coefficient prime.

For coefficient prime ell=p, use F7. For ell different from p, a cohomology class is an affine cocycle extension of the unramified system; its inertia is an additive ell-group, so its wild pro-p inertia vanishes. It factors through the full tame quotient bounded in F9. Inflation–restriction across the fixed finite cover bounds the additional term without assuming that its degree is prime to ell. This proves the all-prime, rank-linear H1 estimate.

Finally, a minimal nonzero normal subgroup in the derived group of a finite metabelian quotient is an elementary abelian ell-group; its conjugation action factors through the abelianization. Thus precisely the proved all-prime abelian-monodromy estimate applies. Nongenerating lifts are complements. In the split case the End_H(M) summand in H1 makes the complement count strictly less than the number of possible lifts, including the equality case in the ceiling bound. Induction and compactness give the uniform metabelian generator bound. Higher derived length and nonsolvable wild quotients remain outside the theorem.
