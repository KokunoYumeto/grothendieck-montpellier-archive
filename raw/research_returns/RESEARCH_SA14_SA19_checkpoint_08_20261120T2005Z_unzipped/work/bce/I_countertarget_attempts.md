## Convergence in measure

A target of measurable functions with convergence in measure need not have open module ideals. Thus completeness and joint continuity alone do not automatically imply the category used in I8. This is a real distinction.

Nevertheless, taking L0 of countable discrete Cousin modules does not immediately give a discontinuous endomorphism action. For each random input, finitely many values capture probability arbitrarily close to one. The finite topology controls those finitely many values, giving continuity in measure. A proposed nonatomic distribution over infinitely many source or intermediate primes instead conflicts with continuity of the prescribed product degree-zero algebra: idempotents outside any finite set converge to zero there, whereas their action remains nonzero on a set of full measure. No valid countertarget resulted.

## A rank-metric model on an abstract graph

For an abstract graph with disjoint finite n-by-n complete bipartite blocks, let the arrow module consist of matrix sequences M_n with rank(M_n)/n tending to zero, equipped with the supremum normalized-rank metric. Diagonal row and column rings act continuously. The all-ones matrices T_n have rank one and lie in this complete module. Each bTc has rank at most one in every block. Identity matrices supported on one block have normalized rank one although they tend to zero in the coordinatewise finite topology as the block moves out. This suggests a genuine obstruction for an unrestricted abstract incidence graph.

It is not a counterexample for the residual complex. The central A-action and the local diagonal rings are additional requirements. Arbitrarily large adjacent-prime bicliques can be obtained in a regular three-dimensional local ring by using p_i=(x+b_i P(y,z)) and q_j=(x,y-a_jz), with P the product of y-a_jz. But local coefficient actions then depend on both row and column and need not preserve the proposed rank norm.

More decisively, semilocal Chinese remainder interpolation allows any finite adjacent-prime coefficient matrix to be approximated by one bTc after clearing a denominator outside all source primes. Extra factors in this denominator make b satisfy any prescribed finite collection of target-adic conditions. A rank topology forbidding these approximations therefore violates the required local-ring actions or their joint continuity. The degree-one candidate I9 makes this obstruction to the countertarget precise.
