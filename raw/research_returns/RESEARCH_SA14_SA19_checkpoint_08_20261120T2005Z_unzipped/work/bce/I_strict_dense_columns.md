# Part I: strict dense column quotients over an arbitrary regular local ring

GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI), 20 November 2026. This is a refinement of FHI's proved I6–I7, not a proof of the full broad-topology presentation. Parent's all-column observation is included explicitly. No independent human verification or priority is claimed.

## Precise statement

Let A be a regular noetherian local ring, K its residual resolution normalized by heights, and E_p the injective hull at a prime p of height h. Give every Hom its finite topology. Set C_p^j = Hom_A(E_p,K^j), with differential by postcomposition. For every j > h+1,

    d : C_p^{j-1} -> im(d) ⊂ C_p^j

is an open surjection, where the image has its subspace finite topology. **Completeness of A is unnecessary for this assertion.** Its image is dense in Z_p^j = ker(d:C_p^j -> C_p^{j+1}), by I6.

For a fixed source height h, set C_h^j = Hom_A(K^h,K^j). Then

    C_h^j = product_{ht(p)=h} C_p^j

as topological A-modules. Consequently d:C_h^{j-1}->im(d) is again open for j>h+1, and its image is dense in the full column-cycle group Z_h^j.

## Proof of openness

A basic source neighborhood is the annihilator of finitely many inputs. These inputs lie in a cyclic A-submodule N=Ae⊂E_p with p-primary annihilator I and with I A_p generated by powers of a regular system of parameters of A_p. The finite module Ext_A^{j-1}(A/I,A) localizes to zero at p, since j−1>h and the localized quotient has a length-h Koszul resolution. Choose v∉p killing that Ext module, and put N′=A(e/v). Multiplication by v on E_p is an automorphism; hence Ann(e/v)=I and N⊂N′.

Take f in im(d) which vanishes on N′. By the definition of the image there exists g∈C_p^{j-1} with dg=f. Its restriction to N′ is a cycle. Restriction from N′ to N acts as multiplication by v under both identifications with A/I. Thus the cohomology class of g|N vanishes. There is b:N->K^{j-2} with db=g|N. Since N is cyclic, b has finitely many nonzero target components. Extend each of those components to E_p by injectivity, giving b-tilde. Then g−d(b-tilde) vanishes on N and still maps to f.

It follows that

    im(d) ∩ Ann(N′) ⊂ d(Ann(N)).

The right side is a subgroup containing a neighborhood of zero in im(d), hence is open. This is the required quotient openness. The only change from I7 is that a preimage is supplied by membership in im(d), so no abstract exactness or completeness of A is used.

For the full source height, every finite set in K^h is supported at finitely many primes. The direct-sum universal property and this observation give the asserted product decomposition, including its topology. Products of open surjections are open: check basic products restricting finitely many coordinates. The image is the product of the coordinate images, and products of dense subgroups are dense. This proves the all-column statements.

## Conditional extension consequence

Let Q be a separated complete topological abelian group. Suppose a continuous additive map ψ:C_h^{j-1}->Q kills d(C_h^{j-2}). Since j−1>h, I6 says that this latter image is dense in ker(d:C_h^{j-1}->C_h^j); continuity therefore makes ψ kill the kernel. Quotient openness gives a continuous map im(d)->Q. It extends uniquely to Z_h^j by uniform continuity of group homomorphisms and completeness of Q.

Thus **once the preceding-degree map on entire source columns is continuous**, higher column descent causes no further local quotient-topology problem. Localizing at an endpoint q makes the last residual term E_q, so this also supplies the conditional extension for each individual interval p⊂q of height gap at least two.

## What this does not establish

It does not construct the preceding-degree map for arbitrary adjacent-arrow families, or prove that the sums of its individual endpoint values converge in an arbitrary complete target. A target without open left ideals supplies no immediate uniform estimate on all source-column tails. The first-arrow case j=h+1 is expressly excluded: its topology is the mixed topology of I4, not simply the local adic topology.

The whole-source-column differential has cycles as its target. An arbitrary endomorphism K^h->K^j is not a cycle; reducing separately to endpoint localizations introduces families indexed by those endpoints. Product openness alone does not identify or control that assembly in the target. The full Part I broad-topology gate remains **Undecided**.
