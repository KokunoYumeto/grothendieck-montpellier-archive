1. The generic stabilizer of the reduced component is canonically mu_N, with a mu_N-gerbe over the root curve at the crossings. Each full stabilizer has exponentN, including mu_N² at crossings, so the Nth power of a line bundle descends to the ordinary coarse curve. Dividing its degree byN gives precisely the normalization asserted. In particular O(mathfrakC_j)|mathfrakC_i has degreeB_ij/N, and J has degree d_i. This differs by a generic-stabilizer factor from the usual stack degree; finite etale pullback still scales it by a positive rational factor, so all sign and semistability arguments are valid. A finite etale trivializing cover of V may remain a stack if its inertia representation has kernel, but the determinant argument remains valid on that tame root/gerbe cover; no scheme-cover assertion is required.

2. Generic-character decomposition is canonical because the gerbe is banded by the root's mu_N. Taking invariants kills the nonzero characters and is exact. A zero-character bundle descends to the effective root curve. In a graded local basis its coarse pushforward is free and the adjunction matrix is diagonal with root-parameter exponents from0 toN−1. Its determinant degree loss is therefore the sum of those exponents divided byN. The asserted Riemann--Roch bounds follow. **Minor correction:** in F8.3 use the non-strict upper bound <=nu_i rank(W0), since the displayed strict bound fails vacuously when nu_i=0 or rank(W0)=0. The later estimates only require <=.

3. The coarse etale pushforward assertion for F_p coefficients is also valid. Over a strict henselian local coarse neighborhood, the finite root cover is a finite union of strictly henselian local schemes, and its etale global-section functor is exact: every etale cover has a member with a section. The quotient's remaining higher direct images are finite-group cohomology, killed by averaging because N is prime to p. This argument covers nonreduced closed fibres as well, since the etale topos is insensitive to nilpotents. It supplies the claimed acyclicity and closed-fibre restriction compatibility. Coherent exactness is the same invariant calculation after finite affine pushforward.

4. The negative-layer count in step5 is exact and does not depend on the ordering chosen in Suzuki's negative filtration. Each addition of mathfrakC_i increases its coefficient once through1,...,mNc_i. The generic character of its layer changes by one moduloN. Every other component divisor is canonically trivial on a neighborhood of the generic point of mathfrakC_i, so it contributes zero generic character. Each fixed character summand V_i,chi is therefore selected exactlym c_i times. The surviving-rank total is m c_i sum_chi r_i,chi=m c_i r. This is enough to cancel all N-dependence in the low-pole H1 bound; no assumption that the finite monodromy group is abelian is made.

5. Each generic-character summand of V has slopezero. It is a direct summand of a semistable degree-zero bundle; every summand has nonpositive degree and their degrees sum tozero. Tensoring with each negative layer gives the claimed negative slope, and the absolute layer-degree bound mL_i follows directly from coefficients between0 andmNc_j and the normalized B_ij/N. The resulting H1 estimate is uniform inN.
