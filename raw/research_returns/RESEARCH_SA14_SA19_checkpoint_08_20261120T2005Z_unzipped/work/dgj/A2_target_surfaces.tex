\subsection{Surface targets and the leading vanishing order}
\paragraph{A2S (Deduction from A2V and the existing A3s surface criterion).}
Suppose $X$ is a smooth projective threefold and $Y\subset X$ is a
smooth very ample divisor in characteristic $p>0$. If
$\operatorname{Pic}(X)\to\operatorname{Pic}(Y)$ has a nonzero point
kernel, then $Y$ is of general type. Let $Y_0$ be its smooth minimal
model and put $L=\mathcal O_Y(Y)$. The integer $a\geq1$ supplied by
A2V, whose leading vanishing order is $m=pa$, satisfies
\[
 a^2L^2\leq B_p(Y):=
 \begin{cases}
 K_{Y_0}^2,&p\geq3,\\
 \max\{K_{Y_0}^2,K_{Y_0}^2-3\chi(\mathcal O_Y)+2\},&p=2.
 \end{cases}
\]
Indeed, A2V gives $H^1(Y,L^{-a})\ne0$, and $L^a$ is very ample.
Apply Proposition~\ref{a:generaltype}, already established as A3s,
to this line bundle. In particular the quasi-elliptic alternative
in a criterion using ampleness alone does not survive here: on its
integral arithmetic-genus-one general fibre, a very ample bundle
has degree at least three, contradicting the corrected vanishing
bounds of \cite[Theorem 3.1, pp.~4--5]{Zhang2022}.

These are necessary conditions only. They do not construct a
point-kernel example or classify the ambient threefold. This paragraph
records a consequence of the existing A3s result and does not close a
new case beyond that result combined with A2V.
