# A3 root-cover transfer: independent verification

Status: proof checked. The construction works over an algebraically closed field of characteristic p. It uses a full general basis of H⁰(Q^m), not an arbitrary small embedding subspace.

## Transfer lemma

Let S be a smooth connected projective variety and Q an ample globally generated invertible sheaf. Choose m prime to p with Q^m very ample. Choose a basis s₁,…,s_N of H⁰(S,Q^m) whose zero divisors are smooth and have simple normal crossings (in dimension two this means pairwise transverse and no triple intersections). Such bases form a nonempty open subset of the space of bases: apply Bertini and the finite collection of pair/triple avoidance conditions. Let

\[
Y=\operatorname{Spec}_S\bigotimes_{j=1}^N
\left(\bigoplus_{i=0}^{m-1}Q^{-i}\right),\qquad t_j^m=s_j.
\]

Then π:Y→S is finite flat of degree m^N. At a point where r branch divisors meet, local étale coordinates identify the ramified part with adjoining mth roots of r independent regular parameters. The remaining factors are étale, since m is invertible and their defining sections are units. Thus Y is smooth.

With the branch divisors chosen irreducible and distinct, Y is integral. Inductively at the generic point of the next branch divisor, all earlier root covers are étale. The new root polynomial has valuation one at any prolongation of that divisor, so it is Eisenstein and has degree m over the previous function field. Hence the total generic algebra is a field of degree m^N; flatness then excludes additional vertical components.

The sheaf π*Q is very ample. Use its sections consisting of the pullbacks of H⁰(Q) together with t₁,…,t_N. If s∈H⁰(Q) is nonzero, D(s)=D(s^m) is affine. Because the complete Q^m embedding contains s^m in the linear span of its chosen basis, the algebra O_S(D(s)) is generated by s_j/s^m. Over this affine open the covering algebra is generated by z_j=t_j/s, with z_j^m=s_j/s^m. Thus the z_j generate the whole covering algebra over k. The morphism given by the displayed sections is a closed immersion on each target affine chart where an old section s is nonzero. Those charts cover Y by global generation of Q. Properness then makes the morphism a global closed immersion.

Finally (m^N)⁻¹Tr splits O_S→π*O_Y. By the projection formula, every H^i(S,Q⁻¹) is a direct summand of H^i(Y,(π*Q)⁻¹). In particular nonzero negative first cohomology survives.

## Explicit Raynaud input in characteristic two

Take k algebraically closed of characteristic two and C:y²−y=x¹⁵, with its unique infinity. Then g(C)=7, div(dx)=12∞, t=y/x⁸ is a uniformizer, and the Frobenius-killed Tango class t⁻¹ lies in H¹(O_C(−6∞)). Set N=O_C(2∞), L=N³=O_C(6∞), and use the nonsplit extension E from this class. Let P=P(E) in the quotient convention, H=O_P(1), and take the cyclic degree-three cover ψ:S→P with eigensheaf M=H⁻¹⊗N², branched over E_section+C'', as in the opened Zheng v1 construction. Put φ=πψ and Q=O_S(2Etilde)⊗φ*N.

The bundle N is globally generated by 1,x. The positive cyclic pushforward gives φ*Q=N⊕N³. The first eigentype has zero divisor 2Etilde and is nonzero away from Etilde; the second has nonzero evaluation on Etilde. As N and N³ are globally generated, Q is globally generated. It is ample by the stated Raynaud numerical criterion, or directly by Nakai: Etilde has positive self-intersection and meets every horizontal curve, while φ*N has positive intersection with horizontal curves and Etilde meets every fibre positively.

The negative pushforward gives

\[
H^1(S,Q^{-1})=H^0(C,N^{-2})\oplus H^0(C,O_C)=k.
\]

This is the a=2,b=1,p=2,ell=3 case; it does not use the discrepant a=1 boundary in Zheng's claimed range.

The odd exponent m=9 is explicit and sufficient for very ampleness. One has

\[
\phi_*Q^9=\operatorname{Sym}^6E\otimes N^9
\oplus\operatorname{Sym}^5E\otimes N^{11}
\oplus\operatorname{Sym}^4E\otimes N^{13}.
\]

This has a filtration with line quotients N^{9+2i+3j}, for 0≤i≤2 and 0≤j≤6−i. All have degree at least18. Twisting by the ideal of any length-two subscheme of C leaves degree at least16>2g−2=12, so H¹ of every quotient and hence of the whole vector bundle vanishes. Global sections therefore give arbitrary sections over any such base subscheme.

Every geometric fibre is the cuspidal genus-one curve whose affine ring is k[u²,u³] and whose remaining point ∞ is smooth. Q^9 restricts to O(18∞), which is very ample directly: 1,u²,u³ generate its affine coordinate ring off∞, and u¹⁷/u¹⁸ is a local coordinate at∞. Since H¹ of a positive degree line bundle on this fibre vanishes, fibre sections come from φ*Q^9. Separating length-two schemes on a fibre, on two different fibres, and over a doubled base point now proves that Q^9 is very ample on S.

For c≥9, h⁰(C,N^c)=2c−6. Thus h⁰(S,Q^9)=210+186+160=556. Choose a general basis of that space and take all556 ninth-root covers. The resulting smooth integral surface Y has a very ample line bundle π*Q and H¹(Y,(π*Q)⁻¹)≠0. The degree of the cover is9^556, so the trace splitting is available in characteristic two.

For a completely specified field choice, start over F₂bar and choose the basis using a556×556 matrix of algebraically independent coefficients; pass to an algebraic closure of the resulting rational function field. The general-basis Bertini conditions hold at this generic matrix. This identifies a definite example without choosing numerical points outside a discriminant locus.
