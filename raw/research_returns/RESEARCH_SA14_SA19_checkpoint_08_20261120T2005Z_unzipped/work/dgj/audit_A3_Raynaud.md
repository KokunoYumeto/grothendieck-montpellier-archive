## Frobenius-connection vanishing lemma

Let C be a smooth projective curve over a perfect field of characteristic p, let L have positive degree, and let

\[
0\longrightarrow O_C\longrightarrow E\longrightarrow L\longrightarrow0
\]

be nonsplit with split Frobenius pullback. Then, for 0≤n<p and deg M<n deg L,

\[
\operatorname{Hom}(\operatorname{Sym}^{n}E,M)=0.
\]

For n=0 this is the negative-degree line-bundle assertion. For n>0 the splitting F*E=O⊕L^p is unique because Hom(L^p,O)=0. The canonical connection on F*E preserves O and has the canonical connection on its quotient L^p. Its off-diagonal entry β:L^p→Ω_C is nonzero. Indeed, if β were zero the splitting would be horizontal and descend, contradicting the nonsplit extension. This last descent can be checked directly: in local Frobenius frames a horizontal homomorphism has coefficients with zero differential; over the function field these are pth powers, and the roots remain regular wherever the coefficients are regular, by their valuations. The local roots glue to a homomorphism before Frobenius.

For φ:Sym^n E→M, its Frobenius pullback has components φ_j:L^{pj}→M^p. Degree forces φ_n=0. Horizontality on the jth summand, when φ_j=0, gives jβ·φ_{j−1}=0. Descending through j=n,…,1, all coefficients j are nonzero and β is nonzero. Consequently all φ_j vanish, so φ vanishes by faithful flatness of Frobenius on a smooth curve.

In the Zheng/Raynaud construction with ell|(p+1), d=(p+1)/ell, L=N^ell, and Z_{a,b}=O(a Etilde)⊗φ*N^b, the a=ell direct-image summands are

\[
\operatorname{Sym}^{id-1}(E)^\vee\otimes N^{ip-b-\ell},\qquad 1\leq i\leq\ell-1.
\]

Here 0≤id−1≤p−d<p and deg M−(id−1)deg L=−(i+b)deg N<0. The lemma annihilates all global sections; the ordinary direct image is zero because all the corresponding O_P coefficients have negative fibre degree. Thus H¹(X,Z_{ell,b}^{−1})=0 for every b>0.

Since Etilde²=deg L/ell=deg N>0, the restriction of Z_{a,b}^{−1} to Etilde has degree −(a+b)deg N. The exact sequence for adding Etilde therefore gives injections H¹(X,Z_{a+1,b}^{−1})→H¹(X,Z_{a,b}^{−1}). Consequently the vanishing holds for every a≥ell,b>0. This proves that the two-parameter family supplies no counterexample in that entire range, without extrapolating a finite computation.

## Boundary calculation p=3, ell=4, a=1, b=3

Take C:y³−y=x⁸, with its unique point at infinity, and let L=O(4∞), N=O(∞). The uniformizer t=y/x³ gives w=t³x satisfying w=1+t¹⁶w^{−5}; hence x=t^{−3}+O(t¹³). The class η=t^{−1} in H¹(O(−4∞)) is nonzero because the pole semigroup is generated by 3 and 8. Its Frobenius pullback is zero because η³−x∈t¹³k[[t]]⊂t¹²k[[t]]. The extension has local frame v₁=t^{−4}(e₁−t^{−1}e₀).

The opened source uses the quotient projective-bundle convention: π*O_P(1)=E and ω_π=O_P(−2)⊗π*L. Consequently R¹π*O_P(−m)=Sym^{m−2}(E)^∨⊗L^{−1}. Proposition 3.3, with M=O_P(−1)⊗N³, gives four summands of ψ*Z_{1,3}^{−1}:

\[
O_P(-1)N^{-3},\quad O_P(-1),\quad O_P(-2)N^3,\quad O_P(-3)N^6.
\]

Thus H¹(X,Z_{1,3}^{−1})=H⁰(N^{−1})⊕H⁰(E^∨⊗N²). The first summand vanishes. A map E→O(2∞) has coefficients a₀,a₁ regular away from infinity, with ord∞a₀≥−2 and ord∞(a₁−t^{−1}a₀)≥2. The first condition forces a₀ constant. The second then forces a₁ to have pole at most 1, hence to be constant too, and its constant and t^{−1} coefficients must both vanish. Therefore H¹(X,Z_{1,3}^{−1})=0.

The sign of η is irrelevant to this conclusion. The projective-bundle dual, determinant twist, cyclic-cover exponents, and formal-extension transition are mutually consistent.
