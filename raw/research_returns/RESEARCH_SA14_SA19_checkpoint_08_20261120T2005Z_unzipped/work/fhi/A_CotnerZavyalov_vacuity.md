# Cotner–Zavyalov Example 3.4.6(ii): vacuous added hypothesis

The published article was independently opened at
https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/8556201EDB51D7F2F76F5950A800CD64/S0010437X24007231a.pdf/lefschetz_theorems_in_flat_cohomology_and_applications.pdf .
Exact locator: Example 3.4.6(ii), printed pp.1867–1868; its proof explicitly uses noninjectivity of H1(Y,alpha_p)→H1(X,alpha_p) from Example 3.3.6, while additionally assuming both schemes smooth and H0(Y,Omega1)→H0(X,Omega1) an isomorphism. Footnote6 says the authors do not know whether such examples exist.

For every smooth scheme Z over a perfect field there is a natural injective additive map H1_fppf(Z,alpha_p)→H0(Z,Omega1). Write a torsor locally as z^p=a_i and send it to the glued differentials da_i. Differences on overlaps are pth powers. If all da_i vanish, local pth roots exist by the kernel-of-differentiation calculation and normality; the torsor is locally trivial. Since alpha_p has no sections on reduced Zariski opens, local trivializations glue uniquely. The map is Frobenius semilinear under the standard coefficient scaling, but its naturality and injectivity as an additive map are enough.

Therefore injectivity on global ordinary one-forms forces injectivity on H1(alpha_p). The extra hypothesis in Example3.4.6(ii) is impossible for the pairs under discussion. This is a vacuity check of a conditional auxiliary example; it does not contradict the paper's main theorems or part(i), which concerns Picard schemes and their tangent spaces.
