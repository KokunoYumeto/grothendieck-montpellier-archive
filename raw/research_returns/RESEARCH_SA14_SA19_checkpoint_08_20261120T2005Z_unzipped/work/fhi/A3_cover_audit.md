# Independent A3 root-cover audit

For the parent's construction, assume S is a smooth connected projective surface over an algebraically closed field k of characteristic 2, Q is ample and globally generated, and Q^9 is very ample. Choose a general basis s_1,...,s_N of H0(S,Q^9). The finite set of Bertini conditions can be imposed simultaneously: every divisor (s_j=0) is smooth; every two meet transversely; every three have empty intersection. Let Y be the simultaneous ninth-root cover, with algebra tensor_j (direct sum_{i=0}^8 Q^{-i}), multiplication via s_j.

1. Finite flatness: the displayed algebra is finite locally free of rank 9^N. Its trace sends 1 to 9^N, which is invertible in k.

2. Smoothness: étale locally the roots of all unit sections split, since 9 is invertible. There are at most two nonunit branch equations, and their vanishing parameters are part of a regular system of parameters by transversality. Adjoining their ninth roots gives a regular local ring. Thus Y is smooth.

3. Connectedness: as O_S-module, the algebra is a direct sum of Q^{-sum i_j}, with exactly one degree-zero summand. For m>0, H0(S,Q^{-m})=0 by ampleness; hence H0(Y,O_Y)=k. A disconnected proper scheme would have a nontrivial idempotent in its global sections. Therefore Y is connected. Together with smoothness, this also gives integrality.

4. Very ampleness: put L=pi^*Q. Its sections include the pullbacks of H0(S,Q) and the tautological roots t_j. For u in H0(S,Q), let S_u be its nonvanishing locus and Y_u its inverse image. Since Q^9 is very ample and u^9 is in the full span of the s_j, S_u is affine and O(S_u) is generated by s_j/u^9. On Y_u write z_j=t_j/u. Its algebra is O(S_u)[z_1,...,z_N]/(z_j^9-s_j/u^9), and hence is generated over k by the z_j. The projective map from the displayed sections of L restricts to a closed immersion on every chart u nonzero. The old Q sections cover Y. The map is therefore an immersion; properness upgrades it to a closed immersion. Consequently L is very ample. Use of the full basis matters for the affine-coordinate-ring generation.

5. Cohomology: projection formula and normalized trace split Q^{-1} into pi_*pi^*Q^{-1}. Thus pullback H1(S,Q^{-1}) to H1(Y,L^{-1}) is injective. The nonvanishing survives.

No remaining obstruction was found under the stated Q hypotheses. This checks the finite-cover step, not the independent construction of the original Raynaud surface and its Q.
