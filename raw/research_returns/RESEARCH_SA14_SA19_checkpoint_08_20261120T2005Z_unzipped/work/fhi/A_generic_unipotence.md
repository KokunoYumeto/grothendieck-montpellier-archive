# Strong unipotence of the generic Picard restriction kernel

The complete proposition and proof are in A_generic_unipotence.tex. It strengthens root Proposition R3g to normal X and to finite connected unipotent kernel, thereby checking the stronger wording of SA14 H1.

The opened primary input is Michel Raynaud, Specialisation du foncteur de Picard, PMIHES 38 (1970), 27–76, Proposition 6.2.1 p.50 and Proposition 6.2.3(1) p.51. The p.50 original scan was visually checked. For multiplicative M and its Cartier dual M', it identifies H1_et(Z,M') with Hom(M,Pic_Z/k) over algebraically closed k. Set M=mu_p and M'=Z/p, and use naturality under restriction plus the ample-divisor fundamental-group surjection. This rules out every mu_p subgroup of the kernel.

The remaining algebra is proved directly: the maximal etale quotient E of the Cartier dual has connected diagonalizable dual inside the connected kernel, so E is a p-group. If nonzero, it gives a mu_p subgroup. Hence the Cartier dual is connected, its coordinate algebra is local, and the faithful regular representation has a filtration by trivial one-dimensional factors. The kernel is therefore unipotent.

For normal X, use the rational logarithmic differential and the smooth locus. Zero rational dlog gives pth roots of local units in k(X); normality makes these local units, hence trivializes the line bundle. The incidence argument uses only the smooth locus and the locally free relative cotangent sheaf there. The tangent-space computation uses the exact sequence of a Cartier hyperplane and geometric integrality, not smoothness of all X.
