## Katz–Pandharipande: geometric generic axes

Nicholas M. Katz and Rahul Pandharipande, *Inequalities related to Lefschetz pencils and integrals of Chern classes*, author-hosted PDF https://web.math.princeton.edu/~nmk/Inequalities15.pdf . The original author PDF pp1–3 were visually read. Introductionp1 works over an arbitrary algebraically closed field with an invertible prime ell and defines the variable quotient local system. Theorem0 p2, citing Katz, Larsen's alternative, moments, and the monodromy of Lefschetz pencils, Theorem2.2.4, gives full orthogonal monodromy for even fibre dimension≥2, d≥3 and variable rank>8. Corollary2 p3 establishes the rank bound except for linearly embedded P3 with d=3. Corollary3 p3 therefore gives full O(Nd) for every odd ambient dimension≥3 and d≥3 outside that sole exception. Corollary3 has no characteristic-two exclusion; the characteristic qualification later in the separate Theorem4 must not be imported into it.

This is the input for JAC-P09. The new deduction is the finite-orbit argument for algebraic cycles on the geometric generic fibre and its identification with the axis summand in H9-T7; Katz's monodromy theorem itself is cited background, not independently reproved.
