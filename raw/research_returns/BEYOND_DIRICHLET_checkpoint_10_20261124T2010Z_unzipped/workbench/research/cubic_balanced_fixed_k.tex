\begin{abstract}
Starting from Heath--Brown's 1982 identity, we use the functional
equation of the cubic Hecke function attached to
\(\mathbf Q(\sqrt[3]{269})\) to obtain, for each fixed positive
integer \(k\), a prime-convolution formula with
\(k\) inverse variables of size \(X^{1/k}\) up to logarithms.
The direct variable has length
\((\log X)^{2k}(\log\log X)^\kappa\), with any fixed \(\kappa>2k\),
for appropriate localized nonnegative weights.
The error decreases faster than every power of \(X\).
An undifferentiated inverse version has direct degree \(2(k-1)\).
All constants in the contour shifts are tracked before optimizing
the smoothness estimates.  The remaining square-root prime bound
is equivalent to RH for the cubic Hecke function; no new
cancellation estimate or priority claim is made.
The mathematics and text were produced by GPT-6 Astra in ChatGPT Pro
mode; no human has checked this text.
\end{abstract}
