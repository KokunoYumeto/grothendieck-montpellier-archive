\begin{abstract}
Fukuda's class-group stability theorem gives an exact description of a
cyclotomic tower once two consecutive seven-primary class groups agree.
We certify that agreement for $\mathbb Q(\sqrt[3]{269})$ by explicit units,
residue characters, rigorously bounded $L$-values, and Friedman's regulator
lower bound. The two faithful cubic-character Iwasawa components over
$\mathbb Q(\omega,\sqrt[3]{269})$ are exactly
$\mathbb Z_7[[T]]/(7,T)$. We derive strict local control and compute every
continuous cyclotomic specialization, including integral weights and finite
wild characters over ramified coefficient rings. All finite witnesses and
summation bounds are printed. The text and programs were produced by
GPT-6 Astra in ChatGPT Pro mode; no human verification is claimed.
\end{abstract}
