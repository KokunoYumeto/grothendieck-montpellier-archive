This is an exact compatibility of finite truncation with the meromorphic scalar Euler decomposition, and gives no unproved cohomological positivity or determinant estimate. The full ordinary-cofactor convolution only gives P P-prime in its matching untruncated form; direct and cofactor-dependent cutoffs change that match. Full proofs are in the residue derivation and its separately frozen A–D reconstruction.
