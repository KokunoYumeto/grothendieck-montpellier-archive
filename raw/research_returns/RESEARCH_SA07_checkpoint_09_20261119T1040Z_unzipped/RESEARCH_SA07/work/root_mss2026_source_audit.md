## Balanced single-line reconstruction

Root independently checked the tensor argument supplied by period_triangle. For a special derivation, T=sum_i(a_i tensor D(b_i)-b_i tensor D(a_i)) belongs to the cyclically invariant tensors: the associative expansion of specialness is T minus its one-step cyclic rotation. In a balanced multidegree in which a,b each occur once, write D(a)=[A,a]; then A contains only complementary letters. The unique b-leading part is -bAa+baA. Its rotations to the unique a-leading positions give a(Ab-bA), so D(b)=[A,b]. The entire cyclic tensor is determined by its b-leading part because b occurs once. This proves the paired tangency in that block with the same sign convention.
