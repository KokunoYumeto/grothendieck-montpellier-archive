## G3.2: exact maps

At each finite Malcev level the Zariski closure R of the discrete
mapping-class action is an extension of Sp by a unipotent group.
The relative-completion universal property gives a surjection to R:
its algebraic image is closed and contains the Zariski-dense discrete
image. Hain3.4 makes the Torelli Malcev completion surject onto the
prounipotent radical of relative completion, so its derivation image
is precisely Lie R_u.

For a tangential base point on a once-punctured surface, Hain14.4
explicitly constructs the limit MHS and the action map into derivations
annihilating the full boundary logarithm. Hain4.10 says the weight
filtration on the Torelli Malcev Lie algebra is its lower central
series; 4.11 and14.4 give a strict MHS action map. Hence the associated
graded image is generated as a Lie algebra by the degree-one image.
Here H1 of the once-punctured surface is pure of weight minus one;
the Malcev weight filtration is the lower central/length filtration,
so these are the actual special derivation degrees in the work text.
The degree-one image is the rational Johnson image Lambda^3 V.
Enomoto--Satoh3.5/3.6 identifies the entire rational Johnson image
with the Lie algebra generated by exactly that degree-one image.
Thus the two subalgebras of the same derivation algebra agree. No
individual discrete filtration-kernel closure was identified by density.

The scalar-extension assertion is also valid at every finite level:
the coefficient equations of vanishing polynomials on the rational
discrete image are rational linear equations in a finite-dimensional
coefficient space. Their kernel commutes with scalar extension.
The actual profinite geometric image lies in R(Q_l) by continuity
of these equations and the dense discrete subgroup.

## G3.3: actual normalization and the boundary adjustment

The marked-tangent universal action is an actual action on the
punctured group fixing the boundary geometrically and taking it to
its cyclotomic power arithmetically. Its geometric image is normal.
Consequently every arithmetic representative normalizes R(Q_l).
When the homology action is trivial, the cyclotomic multiplier is one.

An element whose outer filtration begins in degree6 has only the
possible degree2 inner term c*omega below that degree; the boundary
equation and the free-Lie centralizer calculation remove degrees1,3,4,5.
The correction exp(-c ad Z), with Z the full boundary logarithm,
belongs to R(Q_l): the actual boundary Dehn twist gives integral powers
of this one-parameter unipotent subgroup, and those powers are
Zariski dense at each finite nilpotent level. Multiplying by this
correction preserves normalization. The corrected automorphism has
leading special derivation D of degree6.

For an element r of Lie R_u of filtration degree1 and leading term
delta in J1, the actual difference Ad(phi')r-r remains in Lie R_u,
has filtration degree at least7, and has leading term [D,delta].
The strict image identification in G3.2 therefore places that term
in J7. Linearity extends this to the universal arithmetic graded image.

## Stable-range map and the final representation-theoretic use

The boundary-fixed stabilization from genus3 to genus9 extends each
old automorphism/derivation by identity/zero on the new generators.
It respects the free-group lower central filtration and the Johnson
maps. The contraction defining ES on an extended derivation has no
new-letter contribution, so it commutes with stabilization. Projection
killing the new letters commutes with cyclic permutation and gives
a left inverse on cyclic tensor coinvariants. Thus the source's
genus9 stable vanishing implies the needed genus3 vanishing.

The final use of complete reducibility is correctly included. An
Sp-invariant map defined only on U6 can first be extended to h6 by
an Sp-equivariant projection. Handle permutations then annihilate
T2-T1 and T3-T1 even if the individual Ti have not been shown to lie
in U6. This avoids an unproved extension of q6 to those individual
vectors. The claimed finite matrix kernel itself remains covered by
