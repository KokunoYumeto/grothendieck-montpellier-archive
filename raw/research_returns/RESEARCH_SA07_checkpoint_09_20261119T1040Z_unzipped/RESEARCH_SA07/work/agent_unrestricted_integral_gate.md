## UF-1. The proposed open derived containment already contains the full arithmetic identification

Fix an arbitrary finite family of abelian varieties over a number field
K and let A be its product. Enlarge K so that all arithmetic algebraic
monodromy groups are connected and the relevant absolute Hodge tensors
are defined. Write

    V = H_1(A(C),Q),
    M = MT(V), H = M^der,
    G_l = Zariski closure of rho_l(Gamma_K) in GL(V tensor Q_l).

The inclusion G_l -> M_Q_l is the actual inclusion under the fixed
Betti--etale comparison. Commelin Lemma4.18, pp658--659, gives the
equality of their connected centers under that same inclusion.

An open subgroup U of H(A_f) contained in the actual Galois image
rho(Gamma_K intersect Gamma_Fcm) has, for every l, a projection
containing an open neighborhood in H(Q_l). Taking tangent spaces of
these actual matrix groups gives

    Lie(H)_Q_l subset Lie(G_l) subset Lie(M)_Q_l.

The center equality also gives

    Lie Z(M)_Q_l = Lie Z(G_l) subset Lie(G_l).

Since M is reductive, its Lie algebra is the direct sum of its center
and its derived Lie algebra. The two inclusions therefore force
Lie(G_l)=Lie(M)_Q_l. Connectedness in characteristic zero gives
G_l=M_Q_l. This is the full arithmetic Mumford--Tate equality, with
its prescribed embedding, at each prime. Thus obtaining this exact
open H(A_f) containment for every arbitrary finite family would prove
that equality for every abelian variety. This is a necessary
consequence of the proposed map, not an assumed conjecture or a
conditional extension theorem.

The same conclusion follows from an open subgroup in the simply
connected cover of H whose actual image in GL(V tensor A_f) is
contained in the Galois image: a central isogeny has an isomorphism
on Lie algebras in characteristic zero. Merely replacing H by its
simply connected cover does not remove this identification step.

## UF-2. The unconditional adelic result and its precise image

There is a substantial unconditional result for every A, and its
actual map must be retained. Let

    pi_l:G_l^sc -> G_l^der,
    q_l:G_l -> G_l^ss=G_l/Z(G_l)^0.

The source defines Gamma_l^sc as the preimage of q_l(rho_l Gamma_K)
in G_l^sc(Q_l). It is a preimage under the semisimple quotient, not
an assertion that every element is the image of a Galois element in
the fixed Betti representation.

Hui--Larsen, *Maximality of Galois actions for abelian and hyper-Kahler
varieties*, Duke169(6)(2020),1163--1207, Theorem1.3(a), printed1166,
proves that Gamma_l^sc is hyperspecial maximal compact for every
sufficiently large l. The definitions are printed1164--1165. The
original journal PDF was downloaded from NSF and printed1166 was
visually read. The theorem applies to all degrees of abelian
cohomology, and in particular to this actual H_1 representation by
inverse duality. Remark1.4, pp1166--1167, expressly specifies the
remaining common-Q-group issue in its adelic formulation.

The lifted commutator is an actual morphism

    kappa_l:G_l x G_l -> G_l^sc,
    pi_l kappa_l(g,h) = ghg^-1h^-1.

It is obtained by lifting to the central product of the connected
center and simply connected derived cover. The commutator is
unchanged by the central choices, so it descends. Kappa is a
morphism of varieties; its image is not treated as a subgroup.

Serre's2013 independence theorem gives a finite definition extension
for which the actual adelic image contains a product of compact
open local images. The dimensions of G_l^sc are bounded by dim GL(V).
Combining the just-opened all-abelian maximality theorem with
Hui--Larsen2015, Theorem3.8, authorp12, proves that the subgroup
generated by

    product_l kappa_l(rho Gamma_K, rho Gamma_K)

is a special adelic compact subgroup of product_l G_l^sc(Q_l), and
is a fixed finite product of that lifted-commutator set. Here the
notation means the simultaneous componentwise kappa applied to two
elements of the one actual adelic image, as in their theorem.
After pi=product pi_l its image lies in the actual commutator image
of rho Gamma_K. This is an unconditional all-abelian statement.

Its ambient groups are the actual local G_l^sc. It does not provide
a rational subgroup of GL(V) carrying all these actual embeddings,
nor an equality with the derived Mumford--Tate group. The next
section supplies an explicit matrix example showing why local
isomorphisms alone cannot be used as the missing map.

Restricting the source further to Gamma_Fcm also requires a separate
actual image argument. In E.2.26 that argument used c-commutators on
every factor of a rationally defined derived group. For an arbitrary
G_l, the same formal first step has the following exact tangent:

    X |-> (1-Ad C)X in Lie(G_l),

where C is the actual conjugation-isogeny intertwiner on a finite
G_Q-stable family. Conjugates of these vectors generate an ideal
I_l of Lie(G_l). The double-commutator construction reaches
[I_l,I_l]. E.2.26 proves that this is the entire derived Lie algebra
by its rational-factor and centroid maps. No such equality has yet
been proved here for an arbitrary family. The all-abelian
Hui--Larsen theorem over Gamma_K supplies no replacement for this
specific restricted-source calculation.

## UF-3. An exact matrix example for the common-form map

This example concerns the group-theoretic inference, not a claimed
geometric Galois representation.

Put a=sum_(n>=1)5^(n!) in Z_5. This a is transcendental over Q.
Indeed, for a nonzero P in Z[X] of degree d, write
a_N=sum_(n=1)^N 5^(n!). For large N, P(a_N) is a nonzero integer,
since the distinct integers a_N cannot all be among finitely many
roots. Its ordinary size gives

    v_5(P(a_N)) <= log_5 |P(a_N)| <= d N! + O_P(1).

If P(a)=0, the divisibility of P(a_N)-P(a) by a_N-a in Z_5 gives
v_5(P(a_N)) >= (N+1)!, a contradiction for N>d. In particular
1,a,a^2 are Q-linearly independent.

Let J=SL_2/Q, let i:J->GL_3 be i(g)=diag(1,g), and set

    b_5 = [[1,0,0],[a,1,0],[a^2,0,1]] in GL_3(Z_5),
    b_l = 1 for l unequal to5,
    J_l = b_l i(J_Q_l)b_l^-1.

Every J_l is split simply connected, and the isomorphisms
phi_l=Ad(b_l) compose the same rational representation i with
integral local changes of basis. The compact groups
b_l i(SL_2(Z_l))b_l^-1 are hyperspecial, giving exactly a
common abstract Q-form and an integral adelic conjugacy.

However, every element of J_5 fixes the vector (1,a,a^2)^t
pointwise. If T is a rational3-by3 matrix in J_5(Q_5), the three
row equations T(1,a,a^2)^t=(1,a,a^2)^t and the Q-linear
independence of1,a,a^2 give T=1. Therefore

    GL_3(Q) intersect product_l J_l(Q_l) = {1}.

The rational group J has strong approximation, but its diagonal
rational points map under product phi_l to matrices which need
not be rational in the fixed three-dimensional space. The only
rational matrix in their entire ambient product here is1. Thus
the map from a common Q-form to the fixed Betti coordinates is
an additional exact obligation; it cannot be suppressed by
renaming locally conjugate groups.

## UF-5. The smallest remaining finite-family input and retained scope

The first single-object input outside E.2.26 is an arbitrary
geometrically simple non-CM abelian fourfold. The sharp unresolved
arithmetic identification in the opened low-dimensional source
is the End_Qbar(A)=Z case: the actual semisimple group can be
the Sp8 group or a form of the tensor-product SL2^3 group.
The first case has the required equality; in the latter case
the exact remaining comparison is the inclusion

    G_l(A)^der -> MT(A)^der_Q_l

with its fixed H_1 action. Lombardo2015 Theorem2.19,p6 records
this dichotomy. Its following Remark2.20 records a2013 announced
preprint; this audit does not import that announcement as a
proved equality. Commelin2019 Remark10.4,p676 explicitly retains
the fourfold End=Z problem. The original author-hosted final
2025 Memoirs paper of Fite--Kedlaya--Sutherland, Section1.10,p8,
still states that arbitrary fourfold arithmetic MT is unresolved.
The current web check found no opened primary theorem settling
this exact fixed-comparison case. No individual numerical
fourfold is claimed to have a disproved arithmetic equality.

The unconditional result UF-2 is valid for this fourfold as
well; it provides maximality in its actual local groups. The
remaining rational embedding and transporter equations inUF-4
have not been solved for that arbitrary input. No additional
dimension-scoped extension is pursued here. The full abelian
criterion remains E.2.21--E.2.22, and the additional full-pure
extension remains part of the original task.
