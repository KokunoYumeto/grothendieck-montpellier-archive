## Final 2026 family-period theorem

Opened the primary final Bakker–Pila–Tsimerman article, JIMJ25(6)(2026),3187–3206, DOI10.1017/S1474748026101972, at the publisher. Theorem1.1 pp3189–3190 concerns fields of meromorphic germs over the complex function field; Section4.2 pp3202–3203 gives the elliptic derivative calculation. The exact specialization is the local-ring map ev_s:O_{S^an,s}→C. At a smooth positive-dimensional base, a local parameter t−t(s) is nonzero and belongs to its kernel. Hence this map does not extend to the fraction field: the inverse of that parameter would have to map to an inverse of zero. The differential identity ev_s(d(t−t(s))/dt)=1 also shows that evaluation does not commute with derivation followed by constant evaluation. These typed maps recover the specific specialization obstruction already recorded in TG-8; the final edition supplies no missing injective arithmetic specialization. No status changes. The original cited preprint is retained, with the final-edition audit added to its bibliography note.
