\subsection*{First coarse end jets after a toric power cover}

Let $Y_h$ be the minimal resolution of
$A_h=k[[u_h,v_h,w_h]]/(u_hv_h-w_h^N)$, $N\ge2$.
The subscript $h\ge1$ records the later toric power map; this resolution
is an ordinary $A_{N-1}$ resolution. Let $B'_-,B'_+$ be the two coordinate
end branches. Write the first chart as
\[
 u_h=U,\quad w_h=UY,\quad v_h=U^{N-1}Y^N
\]
and the last chart as
\[
 v_h=V,\quad w_h=XV,\quad u_h=X^NV^{N-1}.
\]
Thus $hB'_-$ has completed ring $k[[U]][Y]/(Y^h)$ and $hB'_+$ has
completed ring $k[[V]][X]/(X^h)$.

Let $\widetilde F,\widetilde G$ be actual full sheaves of reflexive
$A_h$-modules. The global Hom restriction to the two disjoint fat branches
contains
\[
 U^h H^0(hB'_-,\mathcal Hom(\widetilde F,\widetilde G))
 \ \oplus\ 
 V^h H^0(hB'_+,\mathcal Hom(\widetilde F,\widetilde G)).       \qquad(1)
\]
Neither $h$ nor $N$ is suppressed in this claim: the exponent $h$ is
exactly the pullback of a first-order coarse end ideal.

\paragraph{Complete rank-one calculation.}
The existing all-characteristic full-module classification reduces the
proof to $F_a,F_b$, associated to $I_0=A_h$ and
$I_a=(u_h,w_h^a)$, $0<a<N$. Put
\[
 d=b-a,\qquad \delta=\mathbf1_{b>0}-\mathbf1_{a>0}.
\]
The local Hom frames are $u_h^\delta$ on the first chart and $w_h^d$ on
the last. The global fractional Hom ideals are the three cases in
(D.HF.6): $A_h$ for $a=b$, $(u_h,w_h^d)$ for $d>0$, and
$(1,v_hw_h^d)$ for $d<0$.

For a left target monomial $U^{h+n}Y^j$, with $n\ge0$ and $0\le j<h$,
use the global fractional-Hom element
\[
                       u_h^{h+n+\delta-j}w_h^j.         \qquad(2)
\]
Its first-chart coefficient relative to $u_h^\delta$ is the required
monomial. The exponent $h+n+\delta-j$ is nonnegative. If $d>0$ it is
at least one, so (2) belongs to $(u_h,w_h^d)$; in the other two cases it
belongs to the displayed Hom ideal as well. Its last-chart coefficient
has $X$-order
\[
                  N(h+n+\delta-j)+j-d.
\]
This is at least $h$. Indeed its minimum over $j$ is attained at $h-1$
and equals $h+N-1+Nn+N\delta-d$; for $\delta=0$ use
$-(N-2)\le d\le N-2$, for $\delta=1$ use $1\le d\le N-1$, and for
$\delta=-1$ use $-(N-1)\le d\le-1$. Hence the element vanishes on
the entire opposite fat branch.

For a right target monomial $V^{h+n}X^j$, use instead
\[
                       v_h^{h+n-j}w_h^{j+d}.           \qquad(3)
\]
The right coefficient relative to $w_h^d$ is the prescribed one.
If $d\ge0$, (3) lies in the relevant Hom ideal immediately; if $d<0$,
write it as $v_h^{h+n-j-1}w_h^j(v_hw_h^d)$, whose prefactor is regular.
On the left its $Y$-order is $N(h+n-j)+j+d$, with minimum
$h+N-1+Nn+d\ge h$. Thus it vanishes on the opposite fat branch.
For each fixed $j$ all coefficients are supplied by an arbitrary
$k[[u_h]]$, respectively $k[[v_h]]$, series. Summing over $j<h$ proves
(1), then the individual character direct sums prove it in all ranks.
This proof also allows the characteristic to divide $N$.

\paragraph{Exact descent to a toric twisted full category.}
Assume now that $h$ is prime to the characteristic and that
$q:Y_h\to Y$ is the toric $h$-power cover, with group
$G=\mu_h^2$, discussed in the isolated toric model note. On the end
charts the coarse parameters satisfy $u=u_h^h$ and $v=v_h^h$, and
$q^*B_\pm=hB'_\pm$. Let $\widetilde F,\widetilde G$ carry projective
$G$-equivariant structures with the same fixed scalar cocycle. Their Hom
has an honest $G$-action. Suppose that both underlying ordinary bundles
are full, as required above. Put
\[
             H=(q_*\mathcal Hom(\widetilde F,\widetilde G))^G.
\]
This is the ordinary coarse Hom bundle of their twisted bundles on
$[Y_h/G]$; its rank is $rs$ if their ranks are $r,s$.
Exact invariants, finite pushforward and the projection formula identify
its restriction to $B_\pm$ with the invariant restriction upstairs.
The image in (1) is $G$-stable, so exactness of invariants gives
\[
 \operatorname{im}\bigl[H^0(Y,H)\to H^0(B_-\amalg B_+,H)\bigr]
             \supset u H^0(B_-,H)\oplus v H^0(B_+,H).    \qquad(4)
\]
The old full-Hom vanishing upstairs gives $H^1(Y,H)=0$. Therefore
\[
       h^1\bigl(Y,H(-B_--B_+)\bigr)\le 2rs.             \qquad(5)
\]
This bound has no factor $h$, no chain-length factor, and no principal
chain-energy term. Membership in the full boundary restriction image is
exactly membership of the two first coarse jets in the finite image.

\paragraph{Specified Artinian families within this category.}
Let $T$ be a local Artinian $k$-algebra. For actual projectively
$G$-equivariant vector bundles on $Y_h\times\operatorname{Spec}T$ whose
closed fibres are the full bundles above, the existing RDP Hom vanishing
and the elementary small-extension argument give ordinary
trivializations with specified closed-fibre marking. Apply the exact
weight-zero projection to such a lifted isomorphism in its honest Hom
representation. It remains an isomorphism because its reduction is one,
and becomes equivariant. Thus these actual families are equivariantly
isomorphic to the constant closed-fibre bundles. Tensoring (4) with $T$
then proves the same exact first-coarse-jet criterion over $T$.
This is a statement about actual bundle families in the specified
category, not an arbitrary coefficient-flat reflexive hull construction.

\paragraph{What this does and does not settle.}
The calculation is an actual block-framing estimate once the toric
coefficient gerbe and the full objects upstairs have been identified.
It permits charging boundary defect per attachment rather than charging
$-Z_{\mathrm{chain}}^2$ after deleting core cross terms. In a global
argument the existing inverse-Cartan/Schur attachment bound is the
appropriate numerical input.

Neither identification is proved by this note. In particular, an
arbitrary lattice on the root resolution need not pull back to a full
ordinary bundle on the contracted rational double point. Replacing it
by that full bundle preserves the bundle away from the chain, but can
change its framing near the attached core and its comparison with
one-step Frobenius. A uniform bound in the original fixed Cartier cycle
for those changes must be proved before invoking (4) in the hereditary
unit atlas. Generic generation alone does not imply the required
fullness. The note contains no new assertion about arbitrary global
ramified orders, object effectivity, or an unbounded-period count.
