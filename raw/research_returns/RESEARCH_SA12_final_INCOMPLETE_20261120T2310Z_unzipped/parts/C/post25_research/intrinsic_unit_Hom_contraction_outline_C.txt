Unnumbered proof route: intrinsic finite jets for actual bounded-pole unit Hom
Status: research derivation sent to D/root, not promoted or independently audited.
Recorded UTC: 2026-11-20T18:51:35.078579+00:00

Scope. Work on a fixed proper regular resolution Y of a complete local surface, with full-support effective Cartier cycle C and I=O_Y(-C). Coefficients T are finite local Artinian k-algebras. The vector bundles F_a,G_a and their actual cyclic absolute-p-Frobenius arrows already exist on Y_T. Assume the arrows are integral and their inverses have a fixed pole bound cC (or choose one common c bounding the total loss in the induced Hom operator). This is not a realization argument for arbitrary finite-cycle quotient data.

Define the cyclic semilinear Hom operator
  (P eta)_(a+1)=phi_(G,a) Frob^*(eta_a) phi_(F,a)^(-1).
For an integral map, the result is a section of H_(a+1)(cC). A difference in I^v H is sent into I^(pv-c) H. Choose a fixed N with pN-c>N.

The equation on a single retained NC jet is intrinsic. Pullback of eta_a|NC is defined on pNC. Thus P eta is defined as a section of H_(a+1)(cC) on (N+c)C because pN>=N+c. The original eta_(a+1)|NC has a canonical image in H_(a+1)(cC)|((N+c)C): the inclusion H->H(cC) sends I^N H into I^(N+c)H(cC). Equate these two sections on (N+c)C. This avoids interpreting a denominator on a nonreduced cycle. The equation is functorial in T and polynomial in the finite jet, with Frobenius degree p after choosing any actual finite-cycle Hom presentation.

Existence from the finite equation uses local formal lifts only. On affine formal charts trivializing the relevant bundles and C, lift the retained eta arbitrarily to an integral formal map eta_0. The finite equation says P eta_0-eta_0 lies in I^N H, so P eta_0 is integral. Iterate eta_(j+1)=P eta_j. Differences have orders v_(j+1)>=p v_j-c, starting at v_0>=N; these tend to infinity. The sequence converges I-adically to an integral formal solution. The same estimate proves uniqueness among solutions agreeing modulo I^N. Since local initial lifts on overlaps differ in I^N, their limits agree and glue. No initial global Hom lift and no image of global sections is required.

For a cyclic f-stage system apply the operator to the product of all stages. It rotates them and applies one Frobenius at each step. The same minimum-order estimate applies, with no f-dependent precision. Its unique fixed point solves all stage equations simultaneously; there is no separate expanded q=p^f equation.

The resulting formal map algebraizes by the already used full faithfulness of proper formal completion for coherent Hom over the complete Noetherian coefficient base. For Artinian T this base is A tensor_k T and is complete. If its retained jet is an isomorphism, the limit is invertible along the closed fibre; properness over the local base makes it an isomorphism everywhere. Conversely any actual unit map restricts to the stated finite equation. Hence the conditional unit-Hom/Isom functor on actual bounded-pole families should be identified with this finite intrinsic fixed-jet functor, not with a raw image of a finite-section map.

Finite presentation is a separate standard finite-cycle step, not a consequence that arbitrary image functors are fp. For actual bundle families on a fixed proper curve, relative Hom is represented by the usual proper-flat Hom construction. In the already proved fixed-determinant quotient setting, it also follows from the finite free RHom complex: H^{-1}=0 and H^2=0 after all coefficient changes allow the two outer terms to be split off locally, leaving a two-term complex in degrees0,1. Its degree-zero kernel is represented by linear equations. The fixed-jet equality above is then an equalizer of morphisms between such finite-presentation spaces; invertibility is open. Any claim over finite-type coefficient bases must explicitly use completed tensor bases and their Noetherian formal-effectivity hypotheses, rather than silently applying GE to arbitrary rings.

Remaining issues. The existing bounded-degree witness complex can avoid choosing moving cohomology bases, but converting this conditional fp statement into an O(t r^2), bounded-degree full object atlas still requires the coflat/split/full-realization locus and uniform bounded-pole Frobenius arrows for every admitted family. This note supplies neither. It makes no hereditary representation-count or finite-generation claim. D owns the eventual conditional unit-Isom statement and its audit requests.
