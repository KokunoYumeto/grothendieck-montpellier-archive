\subsection{Boundary of the positive moduli results}

The former field-descent gate in C.1(iii) is closed by
Theorem~\ref{C:all-constant-classification}: its criterion is necessary
and sufficient for every connected reduced noetherian base with the
manuscript's geometric marking.  We do not replace that intrinsic
criterion by a condition on the global-functions field alone;
Proposition~\ref{C:field-descent} shows why that replacement would
lose an actual descent obstruction.

The universal representability assertions asked in C.2 are refuted by
the checked examples.  Our positive algebraicity statements assume
finite presentation, and the stated quasi-finiteness result also
assumes a locally noetherian base.  We have not asserted corresponding
positive theorems for arbitrary nonnoetherian bases and proper flat
morphisms without finite presentation.  This is a limitation of those
additional positive refinements, not an unresolved truth value of
the original universal claims.
