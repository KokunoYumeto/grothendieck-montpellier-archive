Rank-period anchors: exact remaining quantitative issue
2026-11-20. Unnumbered research diagnostic; no promoted theorem or movement.

1. What the new qualitative statement supplies.

The Candidate D_rank_period_generating_root_anchor.tex combines the
known CT--Ojanguren--Parimala local period-index theorem with one
subdivision of each ramification node. The new node has orders
(m_i,h), where h is the old secondary-residue order, and commutator h.
Every rank-M twisted bundle then contains every inertia simple. For
a fixed such anchor A and a rank-r twisted bundle F, r=M s,

  B = q_*End(A) has ordinary rank M^2,
  E = q_*Hom(A,F) is locally free as a right B-module of rank s.

All-ring Morita in this assertion uses an already fixed geometric
anchor and actual locally free modules. It uses no relative
reflexive hull, matrix saturation, or replacement by a canonical
full pullback.

Applying the already proved stable-anchor construction before
coordinates gives an additional useful arrangement: since that
operation preserves rank M, the forced inertia types still make
the result a generator. Thus the old character-saturation rank
M^2 multiplication is unnecessary, and the comparison can remain

  P subset B subset P(C),   Q subset E subset Q(C),

where P and Q are the canonical full ordinary order and Hom
bundles on the original comparable-cycle model, actually pulled
back to the refinement. This is still a sandwich, not a generation
or cohomology theorem for B and E.

2. Why the ordinary presentation still loses the period.

The current proved BH atlas presents E as an ordinary bundle of
rank M r. Its top Hom block alone has multiplicity (M r)^2.
Replacing its former rank-M^2 anchor by a rank-M anchor therefore
improves that naive loss but still does not give O(t r^2) variables.
Using the actual rank-r unit as its own anchor has the same issue:
the ordinary coarse Hom has rank r^2.

An intrinsic B-module presentation could instead use a quotient
B^(s+1) -> E and a B-line kernel, followed by a fixed number of
small B-projective syzygies. If all fixed B/line complexes had
cohomology O(t M^2), the top/top, mixed, and bottom terms would
have costs O(t M^2 s^2), O(t M^2 s^2), and O(t M^2 s), respectively.
Those are O(t r^2). This is only a numerical conditional reduction.
The following actual statements are still needed on a fixed proper
cycle of precision independent of M,t,r:

  (a) a controlled B-generating source for E;
  (b) syzygies remaining locally free B-modules with controlled
      positive twists;
  (c) fixed finite Hom complexes and all-ring equations for their
      objects, products, inverses and Frobenius phases;
  (d) the whole-cycle H1 tails/pole bounds needed for effectivity.

The full-bundle theorem for P and Q proves none of (a)--(d) for
an arbitrary intermediate B/E just from the displayed sandwich.
The existing nonflat-saturation diagnostic still applies to any
attempt to manufacture boundary families by saturating raw order
modules, even though it is not an example of this optimized order.

3. Minimal rank plus inertia generation alone does not bound H0.

Here is a precise geometric diagnostic, deliberately separate from
the stronger P-stable-anchor requirement. It can be used on any
fixed root-gerbe resolution having an unramified exceptional P1
component B0, with only a fixed finite set S of attaching points.
Such a component is produced by an ordinary blowup of a smooth
point of a ramified exceptional component: locally the residue
character is unramified at that point, so the new exceptional
generic residue is zero. A single root of order m occurs at its
intersection with the old component.

For the simplest case take m=M>=2 and one attaching point.
On B0 the spectral coarse curve is P1 itself. Its residual root
curve has one root point of order M. Its geometric Gm-gerbe is
neutral: the coarse P1 Brauer group vanishes and the possible
root-point H1 residue over algebraically closed k is zero. Choose
one neutralizing weight-one line Lambda. The rank-M anchor type
requires all M root characters once.

Choose line summands with these same root characters and coarse
degrees N,-N,0,...,0. Their direct sum V_N, tensored with Lambda,
has exactly the same node inertia representation as a fixed
rank-M anchor, for every N. Identify those node fibres and retain
the fixed anchor on the other residual components. The previously
proved residual Milnor pasting constructs a rank-M residual
twisted bundle. Coherent H2 on the proper tame residual curve is
zero, so it lifts through all formal thickenings; the existing
algebraic generator/Grothendieck-existence argument algebraizes
it. Denote a resulting anchor by A_N. Its generic class and rank
remain fixed. The same forced inertia argument makes it generating.
This construction concerns qualitative anchors, which were never
required to be Frobenius unit objects.

The Hom from the degree -N summand to the degree N summand has
coarse line O_P1(2N-epsilon), where epsilon is 0 or 1, the carry
between the two normalized root weights. Requiring its section to
vanish at the coarse attaching point gives

  O_P1(2N-epsilon-1),   h0 >= 2N-1  (N>=1).

Vanishing at that coarse point means zero in the entire ordinary
coarse End fibre there, including the root-monomial terms. Thus
these sections glue by zero on the other components of E_red.
Consequently

  h0(E_red, q_*End(A_N)|E_red) >= 2N-1.

With b fixed attaching points/root marks, the same argument gives
2N-O(b) instead. The residual determinant may be kept fixed:
the two varying ordinary degrees cancel. The local node
representations are also fixed.

Therefore neither rank M nor the fact of being an inertia
generator gives a uniform bound O(t M^2) for this H0 space,
even on a fixed model and at fixed M. This is not asserted for
anchors already made stable under the canonical full order P.
The existing stable-hull construction can alter these lattices.
Nor is any counterexample to a representation count or to the
existence of a better normalized anchor claimed.

5. A precise normal-layer bookkeeping warning (research only).

For a rational (-2) chain D of length L, take Z=hD. Then
-Z^2=2h^2 while the graph has L vertices. Charging one positive
cohomology cost per component-normal layer gives Lh, which is
not O(L+h^2). This does not disprove the desired whole-cycle
bound: the actual scalar layers O_D(-jD) have multidegrees
(j,0,...,0,j), H1=0 and h0=2j+1, so h0(O_(hD))=h^2.
Suitable component orderings exhibit degree-minus-one acyclic
terms. The required cancellation must remain in the coefficient
complex; an absolute rank-based sum does not establish it.

After the rank-period subdivision a connected cyclic degree-mi
cover of an old P1 with only two marked points has full index
mi at both ends. Thus hi=mi and the bridges between interior
rational valence-two vertices have residual root orders1. Their
balanced spectral bundles are ordinary trivial summands. This
is a precise candidate locus for chain contraction, but neither
whole-thick-cycle compatibility nor fixed Frobenius poles have
been proved by this graph calculation.
