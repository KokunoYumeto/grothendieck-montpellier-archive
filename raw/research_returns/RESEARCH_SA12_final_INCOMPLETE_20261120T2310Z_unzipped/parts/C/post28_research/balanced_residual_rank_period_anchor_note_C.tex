\subsection*{Balanced residual anchors and the remaining comparison estimate}

Work in the exact setup of Proposition~\ref{D:rank-period-generating-root-anchor}.
Let the original SNC graph have $v$ vertices and $e$ edges, and let
$g_i=g(E_i)$. Write $\mathcal D$ for the \emph{reduced} inverse image
of the exceptional divisor in the subdivided mixed-root stack, and
$\mathcal G_{\mathcal D}$ for the restricted actual coefficient gerbe.
This is a nodal proper tame curve. The statements below concern this
reduced stack curve. They do not concern restriction of the coarse
order $q_*\mathcal End(\mathcal A)$ to a coarse thick cycle.

\paragraph{Exact spectral geometry after subdivision.}
At an old component $E_i$, the adjacent new root order is precisely
its secondary order $h_x$. Thus the residual root stabilizer on the
spectral cover is $h_x/h_x=1$. Its spectral curve $R_i$ is an ordinary
smooth connected curve, finite of degree $m_i$ over $E_i$, ramified
with index $h_x$ at the old node $x$. The checked mixed-node residue
calculation and tame Riemann--Hurwitz give
\[
  2g(R_i)-2=m_i(2g_i-2)+\sum_{x\in E_i}m_i(1-h_x^{-1}).
\]
There are $m_i/h_x$ points above each marked node. Consequently the
\emph{logarithmic} degree is exactly
\[
  2g(R_i)-2+\#\{\text{points above nodes}\}
       =m_i(2g_i-2+\operatorname{val}_i).
\]
The spectral cover of a new exceptional component is the degree-$h_x$
cover of $\mathbf P^1$ ramified fully at its two ends. Its coarse curve
is $\mathbf P^1$. Its two residual root orders are
$a=m_i/h_x$ and $b=m_j/h_x$. In particular its logarithmic degree
with the two endpoints marked is zero. Orders equal to one are omitted.

\paragraph{A balanced residual choice exists.}
Use the exact residual spectral equivalence already proved in
\ref{D:SNC-general-residual-category}. Choose one neutralizing
weight-one line on each spectral gerbe. On an old $R_i$ choose the
ordinary spectral bundle
\[
                    W_i=\mathcal O_{R_i}^{M/m_i}.
\]
On a new two-point root curve write $D_0,D_\infty$ for the two root
divisors and put $k_x=M/h_x$. This integer is divisible by both
$a$ and $b$. One explicit choice of spectral bundle is
\[
 W_x=\bigoplus_{\ell=0}^{k_x-1}
  \mathcal O\bigl((\ell\bmod a)D_0+(\ell\bmod b)D_\infty\bigr).
\]
If an order equals one its exponent is zero. Each character at either
end occurs with the required uniform multiplicity. Shifts caused by
neutralizing-line choices only permute these uniform lists. The exact
mixed $(m,h)$ calculation says that these marginal lists uniquely
specify the full scalar-weight-one node representation. Hence the
component bundles admit actual fibre isomorphisms at every node.
The already checked Milnor kernel/normalization construction glues
them to a rank-$M$ twisted bundle $A_0$ on $\mathcal G_{\mathcal D}$.

There is no obstruction to extending this one bundle through successive
nilpotent neighbourhoods of $\mathcal D$: the obstruction groups are
$H^2$ of coherent sheaves on a proper tame curve, hence zero. One may
use the already established fixed qualitative algebraic generator to
apply ordinary proper Grothendieck existence to the corresponding
coarse modules. This gives an actual rank-$M$ bundle $\mathcal A$ on
the full gerbe with residual bundle $A_0$. This is a ground-field
existence construction. It asserts neither a flat relative reflexive
hull nor a uniform bound for its formal coefficients or poles.

\paragraph{The residual endomorphism budget is degree-linear.}
On an old component the endomorphism cohomology is that of
$\mathcal End(W_i)$, since the neutralizing line cancels. Thus
\[
 h^0+h^1=(M/m_i)^2(1+g(R_i))
       \le M^2(2+g_i+\operatorname{val}_i/2).
\]
On a new root $\mathbf P^1$, every summand of
$\mathcal Hom(W_x,W_x)$ has coarse degree in $\{-2,-1,0\}$:
the difference of two normalized exponents at one root point has
floor either $-1$ or $0$. Exact tame pushforward therefore gives
$h^0+h^1\le k_x^2\le M^2$.

At an old--new node of type $(m_i,h_x)$, the inertia-equivariant
endomorphism fibre has dimension
\[
       (m_i/h_x)(M/m_i)^2=M^2/(m_i h_x)\le M^2.
\]
The normalization sequence for the endomorphism sheaf is exact,
including on the coefficient gerbe by tame invariants. Its cohomology
sequence bounds the sum $h^0+h^1$ by the sum for the component
normalizations plus twice the sum of the node dimensions. There are
$e$ new components and $2e$ new nodes. Consequently
\[
 h^0(\mathcal G_{\mathcal D},\mathcal End A_0)
 +h^1(\mathcal G_{\mathcal D},\mathcal End A_0)
     \le M^2\bigl(2v+\sum_i g_i+6e\bigr).
\]
The previously checked geometric budget makes this $O_R(tM^2)$ on
our degree-$t$ cover models. This bound concerns one explicitly
chosen balanced residual anchor, not arbitrary anchors of rank $M$.

\paragraph{Why this does not yet give the required order estimate.}
The reduced inverse image $\mathcal D$ is smaller than the inverse
image of the coarse reduced exceptional divisor: a coarse equation
$x=0$ pulls back as $z^{m_i}=0$. Therefore the preceding bound is
\emph{not} a bound for
$H^\bullet(E_{\mathrm{red}},(q_*\mathcal End\mathcal A)|_{E_{\mathrm{red}}})$,
let alone for its restriction to the proper comparison cycle $bZ$.
The intervening root-normal layers and their gluing are real data.

Separately, the canonical full order
$P=\operatorname{full}(\operatorname{End}(A_U))$ admits a stable
rank-$M$ root anchor with
$P\subset B\subset P(C)$. That stable hull is still generating by
the proved rank-period proposition. However, the hull can change the
balanced residual spectral bundle just chosen. Conversely the
arbitrary formal lift of $A_0$ above has no proved uniform pole
comparison with this canonical full order. These two constructions
cannot presently be combined without a new argument.

A sufficient missing geometric estimate would be a simultaneous
choice of $A$ with the canonical $O_R(C)$ order/comparison bounds
\emph{and} a uniform whole-cycle bound
$h^0+h^1(bZ,B\otimes L)=O_R(tM^2)$ for the bounded set of positive
and inverse twists used by the intrinsic order-module complexes.
One also still needs a controlled short $B$-projective presentation
of every counted object; residual rank $r/M$ and the sandwich
$Q\subset E\subset Q(C)$ do not supply it. This note establishes no
finite object atlas, class count, or new main-gate movement.

\paragraph{Source scope.}
The spectral equivalence, mixed-node simple calculation, tame
normalization sequence, coherent $H^2$ vanishing, and qualitative
algebraization are the already fully audited inputs of the cited
rank-period and general-SNC packets. The new calculations here are
the direct logarithmic Riemann--Hurwitz identity and the elementary
endomorphism dimension estimate. No new primary-source proof audit
or independent four-party verification of this unnumbered note is
claimed.
