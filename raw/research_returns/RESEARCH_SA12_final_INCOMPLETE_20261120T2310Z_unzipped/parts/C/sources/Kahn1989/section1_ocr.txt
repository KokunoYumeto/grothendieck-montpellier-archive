Introduction

In 1979 J. McKay observed that for a finite subgroup GcSL(2,C) and the
associated rational double point singularity C?/G there is a bijective cor-
respondence between non-trivial irreducible representations of G, up to conjugacy,
and irreducible components in the exceptional set of the minimal resolution of
C?/G. Since then, efforts by Herzog, Gonzalez-Sprinberg, Verdier, Artin, Esnault,
KnGrrer, Auslander and Reiten and others aimed at explaining McKay’s cor-
respondence have led to a good understanding of indecomposable reflexive modules
over local rings of rational double points. Recently, Wunram was able to extend
most of the results to two-dimensional quotient singularities. See the bibliography
for references.

While it is known that quotient singularities are distinguished among the normal
surface singularities by the fact that they admit—up to isomorphism—only finitely
many indecomposable reflexive modules [8], surprisingly little is known about the
set of isomorphism classes of indecomposable reflexive modules in general. The
aim of the present paper is to shed some light on this problem by relating the task
of classification of reflexive modules to that of locally free sheaves over certain
projective curves.
