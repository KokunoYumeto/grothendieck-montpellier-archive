\subsection*{The actual full category of a specified quantum toric A-chain}

Let $k$ be algebraically closed of characteristic $p>0$, let $N\ge2$, and
let $h\ge1$ be prime to $p$.  No invertibility of $N$ is assumed.  Put
\[
 S=k[[x,y]],\qquad
 T=S^{\mu_N}=k[[U,V,W]]/(UV-W^N),\qquad
 U=x^N,\quad V=y^N,\quad W=xy,
\]
where $\mu_N$ has weights $(1,-1)$.  Put
\[
 A=k[[u,v,w]]/(uv-w^N),\qquad
 u=U^h,\quad v=V^h,\quad w=W^h.
\]
The finite map $\operatorname{Spec}T\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ has toric
group $G_0\simeq\mu_h^2$.  The map is not asserted to be flat at the
singular vertex.  On the minimal resolutions $Y_T\to Y_A$ it is finite
flat, and in every regular toric chart it is the map taking both
parameters to their $h$th powers.

\paragraph{The group and the specified coefficient gerbe.}
Define the diagonalizable group $G$ by its character group
\[
 X^*(G)=\mathbb Z^2/
          \langle(Nh,0),(0,Nh),(h,h)\rangle.
\]
It acts diagonally on $S$.  The invariant ring is $A$, and there is an
exact sequence
\[
             1\longrightarrow\mu_N\longrightarrow G
                         \longrightarrow G_0\longrightarrow1. \tag{B.QA.1}
\]
Indeed the two characters $N(1,0)$ and $(1,1)$ generate a subgroup
isomorphic to $(\mathbb Z/h)^2$; its quotient is $\mathbb Z/N$, with
$(1,0)$ mapping to $1$ and $(0,1)$ to $-1$.  Their corresponding
functions are $U$ and $W$.  This verifies the sequence without assuming
that $N$ and $h$ are relatively prime.

Choose the Heisenberg central extension
\[
 1\longrightarrow\mu_h\longrightarrow H_h
                  \longrightarrow G_0\longrightarrow1
\]
with primitive commutator pairing, and set
$H=G\times_{G_0}H_h$.  The shift and clock representation $V_0$ of
$H_h$ has dimension $h$ and central weight one.  The specified gerbe
on the root resolution is
\[
                  \mathcal G=[Y_T/H_h]\longrightarrow[Y_T/G_0].
                                                               \tag{B.QA.2}
\]
Here the central $\mu_h$ acts trivially on $Y_T$.  Its restriction away
from the exceptional chain is equivalently
$[(\operatorname{Spec}S\setminus\{0\})/H]$.
To see this, the map
$\operatorname{Spec}S\setminus\{0\}\to
 \operatorname{Spec}T\setminus\{0\}$
is the actual fppf $\mu_N$-torsor of the existing A-chain calculation;
quotienting it by the extension (B.QA.1) gives the indicated quotient
by $H_h$.  The base stack $[Y_T/G_0]$ is the root stack with order $h$
along every exceptional and both nonexceptional toric boundary curves.

These formulas specify a particular gerbe.  They do not identify an
arbitrary Brauer class or an arbitrary pair of attachment branches with
this model.  A larger prime-to-$p$ scalar band may be retained by
pushout of the central extension, with the same central weight-one
calculation; no such identification is silently made below.

\paragraph{All geometric punctured objects in this model.}
Every weight-one vector bundle on the displayed punctured gerbe pulls
back to an $H$-equivariant bundle on the punctured regular surface
$\operatorname{Spec}S\setminus\{0\}$.  Its reflexive extension $M$ to
$S$ is free.  The equivariance extends: after each finite flat
coefficient extension by $k[H]$, the parameters $x,y$ remain a regular
sequence, so Hartogs extends the matrices and their inverse.  Applying
the same argument after a second such coefficient extension preserves
the action identity.

The group $H$ is linearly reductive also when $p\mid N$.  Its normal
subgroup $\mu_N$ is diagonalizable, so taking a specified character
is the exact projection onto one grading summand.  The quotient $H_h$
has order $h^3$ prime to $p$, so invariants there are exact by the
ordinary averaging operator.  Their composite gives exact invariants
for $H$.  Consequently an equivariant linear section of
$M\to M/(x,y)M$ exists.  Equivariant Nakayama gives
\[
                       M\simeq S\otimes_k V           \tag{B.QA.3}
\]
for the finite-dimensional representation $V=M/(x,y)M$.
This is an assertion about actual geometric objects; it is not a
relative reflexive-hull operation over arbitrary coefficient rings.

There are exactly $N$ simple weight-one $H$-representations, all of
dimension $h$.  Here is the direct argument.  Decompose first by the
central $\mu_N$-character $a\in\mathbb Z/N$.  This character extends
to $G$, since $X^*(G)\to X^*(\mu_N)$ is surjective in (B.QA.1).
Twisting by its inverse makes $\mu_N$ act trivially and hence descends
the representation to $H_h$.  Its weight-one representation category
has one simple object $V_0$: the $h^2$ shift-clock matrices span
$\operatorname{End}(V_0)$, so its twisted group algebra is
$\operatorname{Mat}_h(k)$.  Untwisting gives one simple $V_a$ for
each $a$.  Different $a$ are distinguished by their $\mu_N$-characters.
Thus
\[
 V\simeq\bigoplus_{a=0}^{N-1}V_a^{n_a},\qquad
                \operatorname{rk}V=h\sum_a n_a.        \tag{B.QA.4}
\]
This proof uses neither semisimplicity of a constant group of order
$N$ nor an ordinary etale $\mu_N$-cover.

\paragraph{The full extension and its actual Hom.}
Take $\mu_N$ invariants of (B.QA.3).  They form a reflexive $T$-module
with its descended $H_h$-action.  As an ordinary $T$-module, the
$a$-summand is $h$ copies of the rank-one character module, identified
with
\[
 I_0=T,\qquad I_a=(U,W^a)\quad(0<a<N).
\]
Taking its ordinary full pullback on $Y_T$ is functorial for this
equivariance and gives an actual weight-one bundle on (B.QA.2).
Its underlying bundle on $Y_T$ is
\[
                    \bigoplus_a F_a^{h n_a},            \tag{B.QA.5}
\]
where $F_a$ is the ordinary full line bundle of $I_a$.  In particular
it is an ordinary full bundle.  Every geometric punctured object in
this specified gerbe has such a full extension, and the multiplicities
$n_a$ are intrinsic to its punctured isomorphism class.

Punctured maps also extend between these full extensions.  This follows
either from the free equivariant $S$-models and Hartogs, or from the
existing functorial full pullback of reflexive $T$-modules.  Invariants
are exact and full sheaves have the original reflexive modules as
their global sections.  Consequently this full extension functor is
fully faithful.  It is not asserted to preserve every short exact
sequence of reflexive modules.

The ordinary full-Hom vanishing on the fixed RDP gives
\[
 H^1\bigl(\mathcal G,
          \mathcal Hom(F(V),F(V'))\bigr)=0,             \tag{B.QA.6}
\]
because the central character cancels and $G_0$ invariants are exact.
The coarse Hom on the regular root quotient is locally free: on a
regular root chart each homogeneous matrix basis vector has one
invariant monomial in its prescribed pair of residue classes.
Its ordinary rank is $rr'$ for ranks $r,r'$.

For actual vector-bundle families on this fixed gerbe over a local
Artinian $k$-algebra, with a specified full closed fibre, the same
vanishing gives rigidity and specified-isomorphism lifting.  More
explicitly, first lift an ordinary isomorphism on $Y_T$ using the
already proved RDP Artinian argument, and then apply the $H_h$-invariant
projection to its honest Hom representation.  The projection retains
the specified reduction, so the resulting equivariant map is still
invertible.  Arbitrary maps lift in the same way.  This involves actual
bundles on the fixed resolution, not a claim that a varying reflexive
module has a flat full pullback.

The fat-end calculation in the separate C note now applies to these
full extensions without an additional ordinary-fullness hypothesis.
After exact invariants its boundary image contains the first coarse
$u$ and $v$ end ideals, and its defect has dimension at most $2rr'$.
The same statement holds for the actual Artinian bundle families just
described.  This concerns the two reduced coarse end branches; arbitrary
large coarse normal thickenings or a global object atlas are not
silently substituted for them.

\paragraph{An explicit one-step Frobenius discrepancy.}
The canonical reflexive comparison can also be computed without a
chain-length loss hidden in a rank assertion.  For $0\le a<N$, write
\[
                 pa=kN+c,\qquad0\le c<N,
                 \quad k=\lfloor pa/N\rfloor.
\]
The reflexive hull of the one-step Frobenius image of $I_a$ is the
actual fractional module $U^k I_c$.  The factor $U^k$ is retained in
this statement: the specified punctured map is not replaced by an
unrecorded boundary gauge.  On the exceptional curve $E_j$, the source
$F_a^{\otimes p}$ and target $U^kF_c$ differ by the effective coefficient
\[
       d_j=p\min(j,a)-kj-\min(j,c),\qquad1\le j<N.      \tag{B.QA.7}
\]
Effectivity follows directly from the inclusion of the Frobenius image
into its reflexive hull and its generating full pullback; it also
follows by the four intervals cut out by $a,c$.  The continuous
piecewise-linear function in (B.QA.7) is zero at $j=0,N$ and all its
slopes have absolute value at most $p$.  Therefore
\[
                         0\le d_j\le p\min(j,N-j).     \tag{B.QA.8}
\]
The representation $V_0$ is replaced by its actual Frobenius twist;
its dimension remains $h$ since $p$ is prime to $h$.  The central band
and its specified comparison are retained.  Direct sums give the same
divisorial loss for every full object, independently of its rank.

Suppose this particular A-chain has been contracted inside a model
with inherited Cartier cycle $C=\sum C_jE_j+\cdots$ and actual Jacobian
bound $0\le R\le\lambda C$.  The local determinant calculation used
in (D.LR.8) gives at every internal vertex, not only the middle one,
\[
        \min(j,N-j)\le \operatorname{ord}_{E_j}R
                                      \le\lambda C_j. \tag{B.QA.9}
\]
The toric $h$-power pullback has coefficient $hC_j$ on $Y_T$.
Thus (B.QA.8) is bounded by $p\lambda$ times this pulled-back cycle,
with no factor depending on $N,h$, or the rank.  Formula (B.QA.9) is
invoked only when the stated actual Jacobian estimate is available on
the chosen model.  No assertion is made for an arbitrary additional
sequence of free blowups.

\paragraph{Remaining global conditions.}
The note establishes an exact category and comparison for the specified
Heisenberg model.  An application still requires an actual identification
of the geometric gerbe on a contracted pair, with its coefficient-line
and boundary data, with this model.  A chosen full replacement must be
matched to the neighboring core and to the prescribed global order
action.  Large normal-thickness core overlaps cannot be charged as
independent rectangles if their total dimension is superlinear.
Finally, a small all-coefficient object presentation on the core and
its compatibility with these full block choices remain necessary.
The local discrete count
$\binom{r/h+N-1}{N-1}\le(r/h+1)^N$
and the rank-independent boundary defect do not themselves produce
that global atlas or the unbounded-period hereditary count.
