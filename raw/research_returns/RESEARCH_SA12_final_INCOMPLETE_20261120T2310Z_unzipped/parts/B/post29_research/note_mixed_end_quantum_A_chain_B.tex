\subsection*{Mixed end orders in the full quantum A-chain model}

Retain the specified toric construction of the preceding B research note,
but let the common root order be $L$, prime to the characteristic, and
let the commutator order be $h\mid L$.  Let $m_-,m_+\mid L$ be the
desired end orders, with $h\mid m_-,m_+$.  In the intended application
one may take $L=M$, the global geometric period, while $h$ is the
secondary residue order of this particular two-ended chain.  This note
does not identify an arbitrary gerbe with the model.

Put $S=k[[x,y]]$, and define $G_L$ by
\[
 X^*(G_L)=\mathbb Z^2/
             \langle(NL,0),(0,NL),(L,L)\rangle.
\]
Then $S^{\mu_N}=T=k[[U,V,W]]/(UV-W^N)$, and
$G_L/\mu_N\simeq\mu_L^2$.  Compose with the two power maps
$\mu_L^2\to\mu_h^2$, and pull back the nondegenerate Heisenberg
extension $H_h$ of $\mu_h^2$.  Denote the resulting central extension
of $G_L$ by $\widehat G$.  The actual kernel of
$\widehat G\to H_h$ is diagonalizable of order
\[
                              N(L/h)^2.                \tag{B.ME.1}
\]
Its characters extend to $G_L$.  Decomposing by such a character and
twisting by an extension reduces each central weight-one block to the
unique $h$-dimensional Heisenberg simple.  Hence there are exactly
$N(L/h)^2$ simple types, all of dimension $h$.  Exact grading and
prime-to-$p$ averaging prove this also when $p\mid N$.

As in the preceding note, every actual geometric punctured object
extends to a free equivariant $S$-module with constant representation.
Taking $\mu_N$ invariants and then ordinary full pullback gives a bundle
on the toric $L$-power resolution whose ordinary summands are
$F_a^h$, $0\le a<N$.  Additional characters distinguish the simple
types but do not change this ordinary full-bundle statement.
The full-Hom vanishing, actual Artinian bundle rigidity, and the
explicit one-step Frobenius coefficient (B.QA.7) therefore remain
valid.  No relative reflexive-hull operation is invoked.

\paragraph{End descent without deleting the interior sectors.}
First work on the root stack of order $L$ along the chain and both
ends.  The model gerbe descends to the stack with end orders
$m_-,m_+$ and chain order $L$: the redundant end groups
$\mu_{L/m_-}$ and $\mu_{L/m_+}$ map trivially to $\mu_h^2$, since
$h\mid m_-,m_+$.  Thus the central extension is the pullback of the
corresponding mixed-root central extension.

Suppose the initial punctured twisted bundle descends to these mixed
end orders.  The full replacement is identical to it away from the
exceptional chain, so the redundant end inertia acts trivially on a
dense open of each end divisor.  On the root chart the restriction to
that connected end is a vector bundle with an exact character
decomposition for the redundant diagonalizable group.  The nontrivial
character summands have rank zero on the dense open and hence are
zero on the entire end, including its intersection with the chain.

This gives actual descent, not merely equality of generic characters.
Locally take a homogeneous basis of the bundle modulo the normal
parameter of the end and lift it equivariantly.  Nakayama gives a free
homogeneous basis upstairs.  All its redundant-end characters are
zero; invariants in that basis are free over the ring with the normal
parameter replaced by its $(L/m_\pm)$th power.  Pullback recovers the
original module, and the multiplication and gluing identities descend
as well.  The same argument applies to actual Artinian bundle families:
the character summands are projective and those with zero closed fibre
vanish by Nakayama.

No descent is imposed along the interior chain components.  Their
extra sectors are retained as part of the discrete full type.  In
particular a rank-$M$ anchor need not generate every node type after
raising the chain root order to $L=M$.  A hybrid core/block atlas
would use the full block types directly; it cannot regard all of them
as free modules of one fixed rank over that anchor's coarse order.

\paragraph{The coarse boundary defect and discrete budget.}
Use C's exact fat-end calculation with the fatness parameter $L$.
The pullbacks of the two reduced coarse end divisors are $LB'_-$ and
$LB'_+$.  The global full-Hom image contains $U^L$ and $V^L$ times
every respective fat-end section.  Taking exact $\mu_L^2$ invariants
therefore contains the first coarse $u$ and $v$ ideals.  The coarse
Hom has rank $rr'$, so its two-end restriction cokernel has dimension
at most $2rr'$, independently of $L,h,N$.  The same finite image
criterion holds for actual Artinian families in the specified full
category.  The end descent just established is compatible with these
coarse Hom and image calculations.

For a rank-$r$ object put $s=r/h$ and $K=N(L/h)^2$.  Its possible
geometric full types number
\[
             \binom{s+K-1}{s}\le(s+K)^s.                \tag{B.ME.2}
\]
If $L\le r$, then
$\log(s+K)\le\log N+3\log(r+1)+O(1)$.
For disjoint blocks with total chain length and number of blocks
$O_R(t)$, summing this bound gives
$\log(\#\text{type lists})=O_R(tr\log(r+1))$;
over $f$ phases it is $O_R(ftr^2)$.
All actual end-descent and unit compatibility conditions select a
subset of these lists.  They are not charged as new continuous
coordinates by this discrete estimate.

The first coarse end estimate is not a statement for arbitrary
high normal thickenings of the core.  Already for the ordinary
structure sheaf, thick-end restriction defects can grow quadratically
with the thickness.  An actual coupled core/block construction is
still required to avoid that cost, and a global small object atlas
has not been supplied by (B.ME.2).
