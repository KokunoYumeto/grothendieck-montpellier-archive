QUALIFIED ARTINIAN RECOVERY — RESEARCH OUTLINE, NOT A PROMOTED STATEMENT

Date: 2026-11-20, after the checked pointwise two-level proof.
Owner: B. No finite-family atlas or representation count is asserted.

Fix one cover A/R and its regular resolution Y. Let T be a finite local
Artinian k-algebra with residue k. Suppose M_T is a finite A_T-module,
T-flat with MCM special fibre, and F_T is an actual vector bundle on Y_T
with a map pi* M_T -> F_T, equal to M_T on the puncture and reducing to
the canonical full pullback of M_0. The existence of such F_T is an
assumption: arbitrary cycle-chart objects are not known to satisfy it.

1. Relative Hartogs gives H0(Y_T,F_T)=M_T. Indeed x,y are regular on
M_T because it is free over S_T; punctured sections are M_T, and the
given pullback map supplies the reverse inclusion.

2. The relative Noether-different factorization of z Id_(M_T) pulls
back to F_T -> O^a -> F_T. At a generic exceptional stalk the evaluation
pi*M_T -> F_T is surjective by nilpotent Nakayama. Its kernel is killed
by a power of a nonzero underlying function; a map to a free target
kills it. Thus the first factor extends there. Hartogs for vector
bundles on the Cohen--Macaulay scheme Y_T extends across the remaining
codimension-two set. Composition is z Id because it is so on the
puncture and the target has no such torsion.

3. Lift the r closed-fibre generic generating sections from M_0 to M_T.
Their determinant is relatively regular, by the Artinian filtration.
The cokernel is T-flat and has support finite over A_T, since its
underlying support misses every exceptional component. Hence
H1(O_Y)^r tensor T -> H1(F_T) is onto, and J H1(F_T)=0.

4. The scalar trace-clearing inclusion is an inclusion of fixed
k-vector spaces. Flat k-base change gives
H0(I_b O_(Y_T))=H0(I_b O_Y) tensor T, so z clears these sections into
q^b A_T. The z-factorization and c0^2-colon argument therefore give
K_b(T) subset q^(b-2c) M_T. Here M_T is S_T-free and the colon identity
base-changes from the fixed scalar c0 in S; its leading form over k
remains a nonzerodivisor in T[X,Y].

5. The gcd-divisor two-generator syzygy base-changes from Y and remains
exact after tensoring with F_T. As before, diag(x,y) on its middle
term and multiplication xy on its first term make the transition
H1(I_(b+1)F_T) -> H1(I_b F_T) zero. Consequently

  im[H0(F_T|T_(b+1)) -> H0(F_T|T_b)] / I_l
       is canonically M_T/I_l M_T.

For a further Artinian base change T->T', the recovered quotient
commutes with base change by its identification with M_T/I_l M_T,
although the individual H0 spaces or raw images need not commute.
The same reasoning is functorial for genuine module maps.

REMAINING GATE: prove that a bounded-degree finite diagram on the
cycle/parameter thickenings produces an actual pair (M_T,F_T) with the
listed hypotheses, and encode arrows without an O(n^2 r^2) matrix
ambient. The outline does not establish this effectivity, nor a
uniform presentation of the varying determinant parameter space.
