Research note: failure of a symmetric framing-loss energy inference
Author B; independently checked algebraically by C.
Date: 20 November 2026, after checkpoint17.

Status and scope
----------------
This is an exact linear-algebra diagnostic for a proposed proof step.
It is not a new numbered theorem, an actual finite covering, or a
counterexample to finite generation.  No assertion is made that this
whole family occurs as resolutions of covers of one fixed surface germ.
Additional contraction-specific conditions could exclude some members.

The proposed inference was that the already checked bounds for the
canonical energy and the total finite framing-jet length imply a linear
bound for the energy of the framing-loss vector.  The following negative
definite intersection matrices disprove that inference from these
numerical bounds alone.

The graph
---------
Fix integers h >= 2 and N >= 3.  Take a rational (-2) chain of length
N-1.  Attach one core curve of genus1 and square -1 once to the left
endpoint.  Attach a second, rational core curve of square -(h^2+1) at
h distinct transverse points of the right endpoint.  The core curves
do not otherwise intersect.  Multiple distinct transverse intersections
between a pair of curves are allowed by the SNC graph convention.

Let C_N be the positive A_(N-1) Cartan matrix and G=C_N^{-1}.  Its
endpoint entries are

  G_11 = G_(N-1,N-1) = (N-1)/N,   G_(1,N-1) = 1/N.

Let the two rows of the attachment matrix T be e_1 and h e_(N-1),
and let N_core=diag(1,h^2+1).  The effective core Schur complement is

  S = N_core - T G T^t
    = [[1/N,       -h/N],
       [-h/N,  1+h^2/N]].

Both diagonal entries are positive and det(S)=1/N>0.  Thus S is
positive definite, and so is the full negative intersection matrix,
by the Schur-complement criterion.  Its inverse is exactly

  S^{-1} = [[N+h^2, h],
            [h,     1]].

Canonical energy
----------------
For a component of genus g and square -b, the canonical intersection
is kappa=2g-2+b.  It is zero on the entire (-2) chain and its core
vector is kappa=(1,h^2-1).  Hence the full canonical energy equals

  kappa^t S^{-1} kappa
    = N+h^4+2h^3-h^2-2h+1
    = O(N+h^4).

The total number of vertices is N+1 and the total sum of the positive
self-intersection magnitudes is 2N+h^2.  Thus, for N=h^6 and n=N,
all these quantities and the canonical energy are O(n).

Symmetric branch-isolation loss
--------------------------------
Every attachment occurs at one of the two endpoint components, so
each individual distance d_i=min(j_i,N-j_i) is1.  The symmetric
finite-jet construction assigns to each branch

  ell_i = sum_over_all_branches min(d_i,d_j) = h+1.

There are one left branch and h right branches.  After summing the
losses by core component, the forcing vector is

  ell = (h+1, h(h+1)) = (h+1)(1,h).

Its total length is (h+1)^2, which is at most
2 sum_core b_i = 2(h^2+2), in agreement with the proved Schur bound.
Nevertheless its effective energy is

  ell^t S^{-1} ell = (h+1)^2 (N+4h^2).

For N=h^6 the ratio of this energy to the canonical energy is
asymptotic to h^2.  In terms of n=N, the framing energy grows as
n^(4/3), even though the canonical energy, number of vertices,
sum of self-intersection magnitudes, and total framing length
all satisfy linear upper bounds in n.

Consequences for the attempted argument
---------------------------------------
One cannot feed the symmetric branch-isolation vector into the
integral-cycle minimization lemma and infer a linear energy bound
from the existing linear budgets.  The Schur complement S, rather
than the unreduced core matrix N_core, controls the relevant energy.
The useful Loewner inequality

  0 <= T(G+G R_chain)T^t <= 2 TGT^t < 2 N_core

does not repair this problem, because it gives no such uniform
bound after insertion of S^{-1}.

Possible repair, still unresolved
---------------------------------
The symmetric isolators needlessly force every branch independently.
In this endpoint example, the coordinate u has order1 on the left
branch and order N-1 on the right branches.  Thus it can lift a left
section with very high-order leakage into the right cluster, whose
larger core degree can absorb interpolation conditions.  This
suggests simultaneous or block-triangular framing, with thresholds
adapted to the available core degrees, rather than independent
vanishing at all other branches.

No proof of that repair for arbitrary attachments, general full
bundles, or nonreduced thickenings is supplied here.  It remains a
separate step before any hereditary atlas or global cover count.
