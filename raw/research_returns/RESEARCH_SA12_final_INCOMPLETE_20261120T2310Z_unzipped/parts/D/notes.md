## D.8: Hom-finiteness lead L10 is false

Use the category of orbit-finite nominal sets for the group of finitely
supported permutations of a countably infinite set. Objects have finite
support and finitely many orbits. Each transitive object is A^{(n)}/H for
H≤S_n; finite products have finitely many orbits (equality patterns), finite
coproducts and invariant quotients remain orbit finite, and all surjections
are effective descent morphisms within this category. The terminal object
is connected. Hom sets are finite because an equivariant map from a transitive
object is determined by one image fixed by a finite-set stabilizer, and such
a fixed-point set is finite in every orbit-finite nominal set.
Nevertheless X=A→1 has unbounded splitting multiplicity after the base
changes Y'=A^{(n)}: the n coordinate maps are disjoint sections. Their union
is a summand, with complement the tuples whose extra coordinate is new.
So G0–G4 plus finite Hom sets does not imply G5.

Useful exact repair: require connected probes for every nonempty object
(e.g. each object is a coproduct of connected objects), and for each map
X→Y between connected objects require a *uniform* bound on
|Hom_Y(T,X)| as T→Y ranges over connected probes. This is equivalent to G5
under the stated connected-probe condition. Distinct maps from connected T
have disjoint graphs because equalizers are complemented subobjects by G3.

## 2026-11-20 03:03 UTC: why the pronilpotent route cannot finish the remaining group argument

We tested the attempted implication from smallness, prime-to-p finite generation, and finite generation of the pronilpotent quotients of all open subgroups. It is false as an abstract profinite-group implication. Let S_n=A_n for n>=5, m_n=|A_n|^n+1, and G=product_{n>=5} A_n^{m_n}. The classical simplicity and even order of A_n show that G has trivial maximal prime-to-2 quotient. It is small: any map to a given finite group kills every A_n with order larger than that target, and so factors through only finitely many factors, each itself finite. Every open subgroup contains a tail product; every finite nilpotent image of that tail is trivial because each A_n is perfect. Thus the maximal pronilpotent quotient of each open subgroup is finite.

Nevertheless G is not topologically finitely generated. If d elements generate S^m, their coordinate d-tuples must all be different, since two identical coordinate tuples force the generated subgroup into the equality diagonal between those coordinates. Consequently m<=|S|^d. Our m_n>|A_n|^n forces more than n generators for the finite quotient A_n^{m_n}. The finite numerical certificates for n=5..15 are checked by code/check_small_group_obstruction.py. The abstract simplicity of alternating groups and the infinite-product argument are separate mathematical inputs, not claims certified by that computation. This construction is not claimed to be a geometric fundamental group and is not a counterexample to D1 or D2. It isolates why the Suzuki pronilpotent theorem, even coupled with strong finiteness for individual finite targets, leaves a genuinely nonabelian uniform-generation question.
