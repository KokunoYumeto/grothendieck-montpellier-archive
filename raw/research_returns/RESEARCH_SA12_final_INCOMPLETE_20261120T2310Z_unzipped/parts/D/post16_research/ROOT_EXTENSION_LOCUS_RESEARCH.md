## Fixed line-twist calculations sent to D

For a proposed Brauer-zero unit projective object represented by an ordinary bundle E with F_q^*E tensor M isomorphic to E, the Chern identities on its actual vector-bundle locus imply c1(E)/r=−c1(M)/(q−1) and ch2(E)=r(c1(M)/(q−1))²/2. A representative M with normalized class in a fixed lattice box therefore gives rank-linear Chern character there. This does not by itself prove the resolution extension or Artinian presentation. D is checking the fixed one-step tensor digits and elementary-transformation corrections.

For one fixed rank-one reflexive module P over a normal local surface R, let I_P be an m-primary ideal contained in the evaluation image P tensor P-dual→R. If M and N=(M tensor P)** are reflexive, then I_P N lies in the image of M tensor P→N: for x=sum(p_i phi_i), the morphisms phi_i tensor id on the puncture extend N→M by reflexivity, and their evaluation identity extends as well. This gives a rank-independent saturation loss for fixed P. It is a proof at the indicated ordinary-ring level, not a theorem that reflexive hull commutes with arbitrary Artinian base change. That relative step remains to be checked before use in a parameter atlas.
