\subsection*{The exact cost of thick coarse ends even for the trivial full line}

Let $T=k[[u,v,w]]/(uv-w^N)$, $N\ge2$, and let $Y$ be its
minimal resolution.  Let $B_-,B_+$ be the disjoint strict transforms
of the two coordinate branches.  The ordinary full bundle in this
calculation is just $\mathcal O_Y$, of rank one; there is no root
cover or nontrivial coefficient gerbe.

Fix $1\le a,b\le N$.  The exact framing defect is
\[
 \dim_k H^1\bigl(Y,\mathcal O_Y(-aB_--bB_+)\bigr)
       =\frac{a(a-1)}2+\frac{b(b-1)}2+\min(a,b).
\]
In particular equal thickness $a=b=m\le N$ gives $m^2$, whereas
the first-order case gives one.  Thus the first-coarse-end estimate
does not, by itself, give a linear bound in arbitrary coarse
normal thickness.

\paragraph{Complete calculation.}
On the two end charts use
\[
 u=U,\quad w=UY,\quad v=U^{N-1}Y^N;
 \qquad
 v=V,\quad w=XV,\quad u=X^NV^{N-1}.
\]
Consequently the two fat-branch rings are
$k[[U]][Y]/(Y^a)$ and $k[[V]][X]/(X^b)$.
Since $a,b\le N$, $v$ restricts to zero on the first and $u$
restricts to zero on the second.  The hypersurface ring $T$ is
free over $k[[u,v]]$ with basis $1,w,\ldots,w^{N-1}$.
For each $0\le j<\min(a,b)$ the coefficient of normal degree $j$
in its restriction is therefore precisely
\[
       \bigl(U^j f(U),\,V^j g(V)\bigr),
                  \qquad f(0)=g(0).
\]
Conversely every such pair is realized: choose a power series in
$u,v$ whose restrictions to the two coordinate axes are $f,g$.
The common constant is exactly the compatibility condition for
that choice.  In this normal degree, the missing coefficients
have total dimension $2j+1$: $j$ low powers on each side and
one equality between the two leading coefficients.

If $\min(a,b)\le j<\max(a,b)$, only the thicker branch remains.
Its image is precisely $U^j k[[U]]$ or $V^j k[[V]]$, so the
missing dimension is $j$.  All these normal-degree pieces are
independent, because the expansions in the displayed free basis
are independent.  Hence the cokernel of
\[
 T\longrightarrow H^0(aB_-,\mathcal O)\oplus
                         H^0(bB_+,\mathcal O)
\]
has dimension
\[
 \sum_{j=0}^{a-1}j+\sum_{j=0}^{b-1}j+\min(a,b),
\]
as asserted.  The divisor sequence identifies this cokernel with
the stated $H^1$, because $H^1(Y,\mathcal O_Y)=0$ for the full
trivial bundle on this rational double point.  The calculation
is valid in every characteristic, including when $p\mid N$.

\paragraph{Exact scope.}
This is a calculation for the actual coordinate branches and
their indicated normal thickenings.  It does not contradict the
proved first-coarse-jet framing or the fixed finite-subscheme
bounds.  It does not show that the thicknesses which arise in a
particular cover family evade the global energy, Jacobian, or
Schur-complement budgets.  It does show that a proof replacing
full thick-core overlap data by first coarse jets needs an
additional comparison, cancellation, or weighted estimate.
No global small atlas, arbitrary-gerbe identification, or
representation-count obstruction is inferred.  The only reused
input is the already fully audited full-RDP $H^1$ vanishing;
all new algebra is the calculation above.
