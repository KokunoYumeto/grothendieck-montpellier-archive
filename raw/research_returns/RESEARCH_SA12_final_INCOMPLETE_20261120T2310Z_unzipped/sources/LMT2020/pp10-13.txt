           T HEOREM 2.7. Let G be a finite almost simple group with socle G 0 . Fix a
           maximal subgroup H of G and a non-Frattini chief factor A of H .

            (i) If A is nonabelian, then δ H (A) ⩽ 2.

           (ii) If A is abelian but noncentral, then δ H (A) ⩽ 2.

          (iii) If A is central, then δ H (A) ⩽ 3, unless |A| = 2 and (G, H ) is one of the pairs
