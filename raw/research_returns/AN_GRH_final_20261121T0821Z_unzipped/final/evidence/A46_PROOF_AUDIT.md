The integral endomorphism p_n=n*e_n is invariant under every automorphism sigma -> sigma^u, so it descends even when the geometric deck group is nonconstant. Its actual image P_n is defined over the original field. The operator A is invertible on the faithful factor and zero on every proper conductor; hence im(A)=im(p_n) geometrically and A*p_n=n*A in the integral group ring.

2. **Arithmetic collision step.** The cover is a valid geometric prime-to-p neighbourhood; the geometric normal index-n subgroup has the induced pro-prime-to-p topology, by taking normal cores and products of prime-to-p finite quotients. A section collision gives rational lifts with delta=[Q'-P'] p-primary. The rational condition implies p_n*delta=0. Since n is a p-unit, n*A*delta=0 implies A*delta=0. The notation e_n*delta is legitimate on the p-primary module using the inverse of multiplication by n. No constant extension is used to pretend that rational vanishing persists.

3. **Integral divisor.** Independent prime-primary deck factors make all subset products in A distinct. Its d=2^omega(n) coefficients are exactly plus or minus one, and half have each sign. Two distinct base points have disjoint free deck orbits, so A(Q'-P') is nonzero, principal, and has d simple zeros and d simple poles. The resulting function is separable in every characteristic. The signs are in the integer divisor group and are not reduced modulo p.

4. **Field bound and free action.** The field generated by all conjugate degree-d functions has index e dividing d and genus at most (d/e-1)^2. It is free because an odd-prime stabilizer would partition a fibre divisor of degree e into equally weighted orbits of that odd-prime length.
