## Exact statement and scope

Every ordinary genus-two curve in characteristic two with an integral Rosati-symmetric T satisfying `T^2+T-1=0` is a reflection quotient of a smooth genus-six dihedral curve whose cyclic degree-five quotient is an unramified genus-two cover. This is proved for each given curve, not only a generic moduli point. Over finite fields all data descend after finite constants; every geometric target point can then be realized as the image of a rational lift in a constant split fibre.

Applied to A38, this proves actual quintic-transfer image membership for its canonical-detector failures. It does not prescribe the rank of the source, its Weierstrass pointing, simultaneous failure at both reflection images, or failure for every cover. It is not a point-section counterexample and it has no GRH input.

The difference map on E times E is finite of degree three and generically separable; miracle flatness supplies the pullback intersection formula.
2. The fixed vertical divisor occurs with multiplicity exactly one. Principal-polarization translation stabilizers exclude any other vertical component; the unequal theta classes exclude horizontal components. Removing the fixed vertical component gives bidegree (2,2), self-intersection two, and arithmetic genus six.
3. Riemann--Roch identifies the exact fibre divisor as `Q+R ~ K+T(P-t)`, including the positive sign. The Abel symmetric-square map is an isomorphism away from the canonical class, so this is a scheme-level divisor identification, not merely a set of possible points.
4. Any reducible or nonreduced residual divisor would be the sum of two automorphism graphs, possibly equal. Their induced sum T would force a quotient automorphism w with Phi5(w)=0. Faithfulness on the Jacobian would give an order-five curve automorphism. This is impossible because the ordinary genus-two hyperelliptic branch set has three points and the automorphism group modulo its involution embeds in S3.
5. The critical Gauss fibre product has separable nonconstant projections on every component. In characteristic two the derivative of `[2]` vanishes; hence the auxiliary map `h=TzP-2zQ` has nonzero differential. If its image were contained in TTheta, tangent equality would force its inverse parameter to be P or its hyperelliptic conjugate. These alternatives respectively make Q constant or make TTheta a theta translate, both contradictions. Thus all off-vertical singularities occur for only finitely many parameters.
