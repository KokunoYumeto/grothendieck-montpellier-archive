# Further character-family and unit-L-function scope probe

Date: 2026-11-21. Reader: GPT-6 Astra Pro; effort not exposed; AN_GRH /root. Outcome: no applicable uniform determinant or exponent theorem obtained from the inspected passages. This is a bounded source-scope check, not an exhaustive absence claim.

The question was whether a cohomological family construction supplies the fixed characteristic-p rational determinant missing in A51, for a geometric ell-power tower with ell different from p.

1. H. Esnault and M. Kerz, *Etale cohomology of rank one ell-adic local systems in positive characteristic*, Selecta Mathematica27 (2021), article58, DOI10.1007/s00029-021-00681-y. Primary author text: https://page.mi.fu-berlin.de/esnault/preprints/helene/134_esn_ker_hl.pdf . I read the introductory hypotheses and statements of Theorems1.1,1.3,1.5,1.7, and the generalized Fourier-Mellin setup in section4.1, including Definition4.4 and the displayed coefficient-ring description. These results use a coefficient prime different from the geometric characteristic, with a completed group ring over that same coefficient prime and the corresponding adic character tower. They do not state a characteristic-p primitive-torsion bound for the cross-prime tower considered here. No whole proof of these theorems was audited in this probe.

2. M. Emerton and M. Kisin, *Unit L-functions and a conjecture of Katz*, primary author manuscript https://www.math.uchicago.edu/~emerton/pdffiles/katz.pdf . I read the introduction, Theorems0.1-0.2, the coefficient setup in section1, and the complete statement of Corollary1.8 with its preceding inverse-limit explanation (manuscript pages1-6). The corollary places the ratio of the Euler L-function and its compactly supported cohomological L-function in 1+m*T*Lambda<T>, for a complete local coefficient algebra with finite residue field and the indicated constructibility hypotheses. It is not a uniform bound on the valuation of the cohomological determinant at T=1 across unrelated conductor factors. Applying a result separately at each finite coefficient ring does not establish one fixed rational determinant across the cross-prime inverse-limit algebra of A51. The long proof of Corollary1.8 was not read in this probe.

The variable T in the second source is the L-function variable, not the finite-order character parameter of A51's fixed-rational-function lemma. Rationality or a unit correction in the former variable therefore does not supply the latter missing premise. The search also surfaced generic nonvanishing results for prime-to-characteristic twists; their characteristic-zero nonvanishing statements were not promoted to p-adic-unit assertions or to prescribed unramified covers. No uninspected theorem is used as a new input.
