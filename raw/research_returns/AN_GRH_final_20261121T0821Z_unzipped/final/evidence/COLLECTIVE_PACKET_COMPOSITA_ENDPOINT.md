# Collective good packets on an actual common cover: bounded endpoint

**Outcome:** no new geometric witness or small integral annihilator was obtained. The precise positive step is that finitely many previously obtained good packets can be preserved during further A49 supply. ## 1. Preserving finitely many actual good packets

Fix a finite field k of characteristic p and finitely many constant, connected, P-split odd cyclic covers D_i of the original genus-two C. Suppose A49's character criterion has provided a good signed p-orbit on each cover. The finite geometric etale p-torsion V_i=J_(D_i)[p](kbar) is an F_p-vector space; arithmetic Frobenius is an invertible linear map of finite order T_i. Its action commutes with the constant deck group. Let T be a common multiple of those orders.

If k_j/k has degree j congruent to1 modulo T, then Frobenius on every V_i over k_j is exactly the old Frobenius, since F_i^j=F_i. The invariant subspace on every deck-character packet is unchanged. A finite p-primary group is zero if and only if its p-torsion is zero; equivalently a nonzero Frobenius-invariant p-primary element would yield one of order p. Thus all the old character-unit packets persist under this extension. This argument uses only finite geometric etale p-torsion and is valid even if T has a p-factor. It does not require a rational abelian subvariety for a single p-adic packet.

For a new application of the already proved A49 variable-quotient note, enlarge its finite excluded-prime set S as required and let M be its A42 modulus. The progression j congruent to1 modulo lcm(M,T) is infinite and satisfies gcd(j,M)=1. Therefore the Weil estimates, fixed-kernel counting and nonuniformity argument of that note still supply another good signed packet over arbitrarily large such extensions. Choosing S to contain the old conductor primes gives a new conductor coprime to the old ones. This proves preservation and finite accumulation of actual packets. It does not say that all faithful packets of any one cyclic quotient become good.

No new proof of the Lang or character formula is claimed here: the exact inputs are A49 and its variable-quotient note, together with the previously checked relation between the character unit test and actual rational etale p-torsion.

## 2. Actual composita and their quotient operators

After the preceding permitted constant extensions, take a connected component of the fibre product of finitely many constant cyclic covers through the selected rational P-lifts. This is an actual connected etale Galois cover D of C with constant abelian group H, a subgroup of the product of the original cyclic groups. A hypothetical point-section collision supplies the compatible rational Q-lift on this same component. Let delta=[Q'-P'] in J_D(k)[p^infinity]. For a quotient h_i:D -> D_i with kernel K_i one has the literal divisor identities

    (h_i)_* delta = delta_i,
    h_i^*(h_i)_* = N_(K_i),    N_(K_i)=sum_(g in K_i) g.

Let S_p be a finite unramified extension of Z_p containing all roots of unity of order dividing exp(H). Since p does not divide |H|, S_p[H] splits into the actual character idempotents. On the eta-character summand,

    eta(N_(K_i)) = |K_i| if eta is trivial on K_i,
                  0 otherwise.

These are identities of the actual norm/pullback endomorphisms. Consequently, if B_i is an integral annihilator supplied on D_i and a lift to Z[H] is chosen, then N_(K_i) B_i annihilates delta. Every integral combination of these lifted relations still has zero character value on any eta that factors through none of the selected quotients. No assertion that an arbitrary point of that undetected summand occurs as delta is made.

For pairwise coprime cyclic conductors, the compositum is cyclic of product degree and its faithful product-conductor characters factor through none of the old quotients. This is exactly the already established A51 proper-norm limitation, now applied to a family of preserved actual packets.

## 3. The genuinely noncyclic case does not merge signed packets

The more informative test is H=(Z/l)^2, l an odd prime different from p, with all l+1 cyclic line quotients included. Every nontrivial character eta has kernel one line K_eta. It is trivial on another line K only when K=K_eta. Thus

    eta(N_K)=l for K=K_eta, and eta(N_K)=0 for every other line.

All characters eta^u, u in (Z/l)^times, have the same kernel, and form the rational cyclotomic block attached to this one line quotient. The signed p-orbits subdivide this block. Norm/pullback relations from the other line quotients contribute zero on the entire block; they cannot replace the missing conditions on its other signed orbits. Relabelling a cyclic quotient by a unit only relabels its physical characters and does not change which L-values are units. Deck translation in H acts within a character summand and does not permute these line quotients or their signed packets.

This remains an exact statement about actual correspondences, without assuming an arbitrary module can be geometrically realized. Integral sums of lifted relations may cancel coefficients; no general lower bound against all such cancellations is asserted here. What the calculation establishes is that the available constraints are blockwise, and the known supply has not supplied the uncontrolled conditions needed to turn such cancellation into a successful small annihilator. A new argument exploiting the point-supported nature of delta beyond the norm identities could in principle add information; none was proved in this bounded attempt.

## 4. Adding a genuinely nonabelian cover leaves new representations to control

The compositum of cyclic Galois covers of C is abelian. To obtain a nonabelian group G one must add further covers. For any actual cyclic quotient D -> D_i of such a G-cover, its normal kernel K_i makes N_(K_i) central. On an absolutely irreducible characteristic-p representation rho (p does not divide |G|), the K_i-invariant subspace is G-stable. It is therefore either zero, in which case N_(K_i) acts by zero, or the whole representation, in which case rho factors through the cyclic quotient. Every irreducible representation of dimension greater than one is in the first case for every cyclic quotient.

Thus the old cyclic packet conditions do not control those new representation components through these actual norm maps. This does not claim that such components necessarily have nonzero rational p-torsion, or that the particular point-supported class can occupy them. A new geometric theorem forcing their stable part to vanish, or giving a small integral annihilator on the actual class, is still needed.
