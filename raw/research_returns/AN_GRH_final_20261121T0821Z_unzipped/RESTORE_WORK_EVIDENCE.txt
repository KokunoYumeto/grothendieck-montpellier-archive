AN_GRH final package

The requested paper, PDF and ledgers are at the ZIP root. Their identical working copies and all certificates and reproduction scripts are under final/. Supplied and recovered sources retain their recorded paths.

Restore the retained work/ and final/evidence/build/ trees after unzipping, from the ZIP root, with:

    tar -xJf work_and_build_evidence.tar.xz

For reproduction, use the retained final/ directory, whose scripts expect source/ and work/ as siblings. Every retained file is recorded by exact path, size and SHA256 in FINAL_CONTENTS_MANIFEST.json. No earlier checkpoint ZIP is included. Complete external primary-source caches are omitted; locators, source hashes and exact reading records are retained.
