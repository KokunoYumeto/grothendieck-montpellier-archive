# GHOSTS research package

This checkpoint contains all current authored deliverables and research documentation. It is an in-progress research record, not the final package. The supplied source ZIP remains the source of the original attachments; retrieved source URLs and exact locators are recorded in the paper and proof memos. Third-party source PDFs and disposable rendering outputs are not duplicated here.

Compile GHOSTS.tex from this directory with pdflatex twice. The main text inputs all section files. GHOSTS.pdf is a reading copy; final pagination and visual review are still in progress. For the current mathematical status, read QUESTIONS.tsv and LEDGER.md. Proof memos retain the history of candidate arguments; later check events and the current paper govern their accepted status.

The audit distinguishes complete proof readings, independent mathematical reconstructions, exact computations, and cited background. No Lean rebuild or formal verification is claimed. Exact exterior-algebra scripts and their outputs are included as supplementary checks. Rejected candidates remain documented with their failure.

At this checkpoint the exact minimum16 is proved for both t_star and its primitive half-class at every geometric good reduction. Explicit bounds for each fixed Eisenstein Weil fourfold family, and the finite-prime gate, are proved. The alternative rational class w and further computational-input questions remain under active investigation.
