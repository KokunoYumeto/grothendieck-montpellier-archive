## 1. The cyclotomic-kernel group is already free pro-p

For an odd prime p, put

`F=Q(mu_p)`, `K=Q(mu_(p^infinity))`,

`Omega=maximal pro-p extension of F unramified outside p`,

`P_p=Gal(Omega/F)`, `H_p=Gal(Omega/K)`,

`Gamma=Gal(K/F)~=Z_p`, `Lambda=Z_p[[Gamma]]`.

The maximal p-extension of K unramified outside p is the same Omega. A finite p-extension of K descends to a sufficiently large cyclotomic layer; taking its normal closure over F still gives a p-extension because the intervening cyclotomic groups are p-groups. Thus Iwasawa's theorem applies to this exact H_p.

**Original primary theorem.** K. Iwasawa, *Riemann–Hurwitz formula and p-adic Galois representations for number fields*, Tohoku Math. J.33(1981),263–288, **Theorem2 pp273–274**. For a cyclotomic Z_p-field K and a finite set S of places containing all p-places, the Galois group of the maximal p-extension of K unramified outside S is free pro-p if and only if `mu_K=0`. His definition of cyclotomic Z_p-field in §4 includes all p-power roots of unity. K above meets it.

Iwasawa's immediately preceding paragraph on p273 expressly recalls Ferrero–Washington's theorem that mu vanishes when K/Q is abelian. The original Ferrero–Washington reference is *The Iwasawa invariant mu_p vanishes for abelian number fields*, Annals109(1979),377–395, DOI10.2307/1971116. Therefore

`H_p is free pro-p for every odd prime p, regular or irregular.`       (I1)

This is established literature, not a new result of this run. The whole short proof of Iwasawa's Theorem2 was read. It relates H^2(H_p,F_p) to the p-divisibility of the direct-limit class group; mu=0 makes that group p-divisible and gives vanishing H^2, the freeness criterion. The original PDF was downloaded from

https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/tmj1949/33/2/33_2_263/_pdf/-char/en

and saved as `work/iwasawa_riemann_hurwitz.pdf`; the theorem page was rendered and read in `work/iwasawa_thm2_p273.png`. The web search tool did not itself open this PDF; the exact URL was obtained from the journal issue page and retrieved over HTTP. Journal metadata retrieval: `turn885034855view1`. A separate primary exposition, Yamagishi1994, Introduction item(4), independently cites the same Theorem2: https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~kyodo/kokyuroku/contents/pdf/0884-19.pdf, retrieval `turn301647671view1`.

Do not confuse (I1) with freeness of P_p over the finite field F. The latter is free of rank `(p+1)/2` precisely at regular primes; at irregular primes its arithmetic generators and relations differ. The normal subgroup H_p can be free even though P_p is not.

Weak Leopoldt alone gives the Lambda rank `(p-1)/2` of H_p^ab; it does not kill its Lambda-torsion. Ferrero–Washington kills the mu component, but it need not kill the lambda component.

## 2. A concrete nonzero part of H_p^ab is invisible to all odd linear Soulé characters

Let

`M=H_p^ab`,

and let `A_infinity=colim_n Cl(Q(mu_(p^(n+1))))[p^infinity]`, with transition maps given by extension of ideals. Let `X_infinity=lim_n Cl(Q(mu_(p^(n+1))))[p^infinity]`, with norm maps. The direct and inverse limits are different objects; the formulas below keep them separate. Superscripts plus/minus denote the eigenspaces for complex conjugation.

Kummer duality gives

`M^+ ~= (A_infinity^−)^vee(1)`.                                  (I2)

A precise primary-author exposition is Romyar Sharifi, *Iwasawa Theory*, Chapter3, **Corollary3.3.14** (the Kummer exact sequence), **Proposition3.4.7** (the plus/minus decomposition), and **Corollary3.4.8** (pseudo-isomorphism to `(X_infinity^−)^iota(1)`):

https://www.math.ucla.edu/~sharifi/notes/iwasawa-ch03.html .

Retrieval `turn885034855view0` contains the exact sequence and decomposition. Here `vee` is Pontryagin dual and iota is inversion of Gamma. In the cyclotomic tower there is a single prime above p, generated by the cyclotomic uniformizer at every finite level, so the p-split class-group variant does not introduce a class-group correction in this setting.

At an irregular prime the minus part of the p-class group of F is nonzero. Moreover the finite-level minus class groups inject into A_infinity^− (Sharifi **Proposition3.4.1**, with its proof read). Therefore

`M^+ != 0` for every irregular p.                               (I3)

The lambda contribution survives mu=0: Iwasawa §5 pp272–273 gives the divisible-group structure `A_infinity~=(Q_p/Z_p)^lambda` when mu=0. In particular `M^+` is a nonzero finite-rank free Z_p-module, while it is a **torsion Lambda-module**. These are compatible statements. Lambda-torsion here does not mean p-torsion. For irregular p, its Z_p-rank lambda-minus is positive.

Now let

`ell_m:H_p -> Q_p(m)`, odd `m>=3`,

be any of the rational abelian coordinates in the actual tripod image, equivalently the corresponding nonzero rational multiple of a Soulé character. It factors through M and is equivariant for complex conjugation. On M^+ conjugation acts as +1, while on Q_p(m) it acts as -1. Hence

`ell_m|_(M^+)=0` for every odd m>=3.                             (I4)

**Whole-proof check of (I3)–(I4).** The only arithmetic input is nonzero minus class group at an irregular prime and its injectivity into the direct limit; (I2) then transfers nontriviality. For (I4), equivariance gives `ell_m(x)=-ell_m(x)` for x in M^+, and multiplication by2 is injective in Q_p. Thus the linear map from M to the abelianization of the rational tripod image has a nonzero kernel. This deduction was read in full; it makes no claim that the original nonabelian representation has a kernel.

Vandiver controls the plus class group of F. It does not remove the minus class groups in (I2). Thus even the strongest all-k assertion `K_(4k)(Z) tensor Z_p=0`, via Vandiver, leaves this blind part in place. The example p=37 is concrete: Vandiver37 holds, but the irregular minus eigenspace at index32 survives.

## 3. Why the p=2 moment proof does not transfer verbatim

At2 the arithmetic group presentation gives a compact Gamma-orbit basis whose degree-one Magnus coefficient is an arbitrary bounded measure on Z_2. The odd linear coordinates evaluate its Amice transform on a grid with an interior accumulation point. This separates every first nonzero degree, and then every higher tensor degree.

For irregular p, (I1) gives an abstract free pro-p group, but (I2)–(I4) rule out the required linear separation. Take a nonzero x in M^+. Choose a lift h in H_p. Its Magnus expansion relative to an abstract free basis has a nonzero degree-one term, while the generator-length-one part of its rational tripod image is zero. The key implication

`first nonzero source Magnus degree remains detectable in target generator length`

is therefore false for that method already in degree one. The image of h may still be nontrivial in higher generator length, so this is an obstruction to the argument, not to Ihara's kernel equality.

The obstacle is also stronger than a missing choice of an integral basis. The nonzero Lambda-torsion summand M^+ cannot be a free orbit module `Lambda^r`; changing a basis of the underlying free pro-p group cannot remove its Gamma-module structure. A proof must use the nonlinear action of Gamma and detect these extra directions through commutator coordinates.

## 4. A sharper next gate: nonlinear lifts of the Iwasawa relations

A finite presentation of P_p supplies generators in the odd character directions and extra even directions, with relations whose abelianized Gamma-polynomials encode M^+. The moment proof would extend only after controlling how those polynomial relations lift into the completed noncommutative algebra. Their first nonlinear terms are arithmetic cup products; higher terms are related to higher cohomological operations. Merely knowing the characteristic ideal or a class-group order does not specify those terms.

The verified example p=37 supplies genuine positive information here. McCallum–Sharifi, *A cup product in the Galois cohomology of number fields*, author's preprint pp20,23,28, Theorem7.5, Corollary8.3, Theorem9.7, shows a nonzero coupling involving the even degree32 direction and the bracket of degrees3 and29. Their degree32 conclusion proves that an extra integral direction is detected by the tripod image. It does not prove that all such directions and all their iterated commutators are detected in every weight.

One concrete formulation for the next stage is therefore:

> Starting from the actual minimal pro-p presentation of P_p and its Gamma action, prove that every nontrivial source Magnus series has a nonzero coefficient after eliminating the even arithmetic generators by their full nonlinear relations in the rational weight completion.

At a regular prime there are no extra even directions of this kind. At2 the C_2*Z_2 presentation permits the direct compact-orbit proof. At an irregular prime the nonzero module (I2) makes a proof using only the first linear moments impossible; a nonlinear separation theorem is needed. Neither the retrieved step-17 K-group bound nor Vandiver supplies that theorem.

This is the precise unresolved route after the successful p2 argument. No assertion that the field equality fails at37, or at any irregular prime, is justified by these calculations.

## 5. Supplement: the actual integral weight Lie algebra at2

Nonzero integral elements in every odd weight already exist. Indeed each actual graded lattice `g_(2,m)` is a finitely generated torsion-free Z_2-module, and its rationalization contains Brown's nonzero odd generator. Multiplying that generator by a sufficiently large power of2 places it in the actual lattice; choose a representative in `F^mH_2`. This proves existence, independently of K-group vanishing and independently of the new kernel theorem. Choosing a generator relative to the actual image ideal of a character is still different from fixing its value to1 in a prescribed target normalization.

The kernel proof does not prove surjectivity of

`FreeLie_Z2(s_3,s_5,...) -> g_2`.

The remaining question is equality, weight by weight, between the actual lattice and the Lie lattice generated by primitive odd classes. Brown gives rational equality and therefore injectivity of a free integral source for any odd classes whose rational images form the standard free generating system. Surjectivity is an integral saturation problem. No checked proof or counterexample for this surjectivity at2 was obtained in this run.

The involution gives a useful rational normal form, but does not solve saturation. With c represented by -1 in a Levi subgroup, `h=c gamma c gamma^(-1)` satisfies `c h c=h^(-1)`, so `a=log h` has only odd total weights. Writing gamma as `v u`, the element `exp(a/2)v` is c-fixed. A further conjugation by an even-weight unipotent element can remove that factor, because `u^w-1` is nonzero in Q_2 for every positive weight w. One obtains a rational form `gamma=exp(-a/2)u`. Both division by2 and inversion of `u^w-1` can enlarge the integral lattice, so this normal form must not be advertised as an integral free basis. It is a possible starting point for finite-weight lattice calculations, not an established all-weight result.
