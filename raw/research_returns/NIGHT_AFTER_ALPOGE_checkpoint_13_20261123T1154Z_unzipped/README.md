# Research papers after Alpöge

Four standalone LaTeX projects and PDFs cover CM Galois orbits and effective André–Oort, theta-chain size, K-groups of the integers, and Ihara’s kernel question. Read LEDGER.md and QUESTIONS.tsv for the current proved results and exact remaining gates. Every project contains BUILD.md; deposit sheets accompany the papers.

Only original or substantively revised work is included. The attached archive and external literature are needed to reproduce source-level audits and are referenced by path, citation, URL and hash. No external source repository, third-party paper, TeX runtime, Lean runtime or earlier checkpoint is included.

Permanent workbench numbers and public commits are assigned by the depositor. Local identifiers do not reserve numbers in the public registry.
