## Title

Effective and ineffective lifts of Mulmuley's rank-three algebra, after Forsberg

Author line: deliberately empty.

## Abstract

Faul, Goswami, Joubert, and Manuell's zero adjunction separates an absent coordinate from a present coordinate of value zero, while Mulmuley's nonstandard canonical-basis programme asks for positive and unimodal structure constants. We construct an associative inverse-semiring lift of the rank-three table with no supported zero in any bottom coefficient, and prove that preserving the bottom ideal makes this impossible. We also describe the restricted adelic semiring and its units. Five effective associative lifts are reconstructed by matrix multiplication; two have unimodal dimension polynomials. One lift has only 38 occupied zero-trace weights. The zero-trace weights are counted separately from cancelling multiplicities. A displayed coefficient in Mulmuley's rank-three table has a nonunimodal positive factor, contrary to the verification claimed there. Applying Forsberg's obstruction, we construct a second associative support lift that admits no effective dimension lift. At every rank we also specify a finite torsion-free integral word order over Z[D], with faithful Laurent base change and a localization certificate. Global minimization and the least positive-basis coefficient ring at rank four remain open.
