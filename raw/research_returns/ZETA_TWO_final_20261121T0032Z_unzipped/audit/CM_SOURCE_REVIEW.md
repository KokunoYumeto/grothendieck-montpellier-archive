## Main result and decisive source

For a pure CM motive M of weight w in the absolute-Hodge category generated by Artin and potentially CM abelian motives, Anderson's **Corollary 5.7.2 and Remark 5.7.3** (printed p.181, PDF p.30) give an integer factorization into algebraic Hecke characters, **all of weight w**. This same-weight refinement was the substantive normalization gate. The source is:

G. W. Anderson, *Cyclotomy and an extension of the Taniyama group*, Compositio Mathematica 57 (1986),153–217, https://www.numdam.org/item/CM_1986__57_2_153_0.pdf.

The relevant equation and remark were visually checked. The printed remark internally references “Corollary 5.8.2” although the factorization is the displayed preceding Corollary 5.7.2. This is a source cross-reference typo, **not a Grothendieck-archive transcription correction**. It should not be put in TRANSCRIPTION_NOTES.tsv as an archive correction.

Write ψ_a(p)=χ_a(p)Np^(w/2), with χ_a unitary. Then

L(M,s)=∏_a L_Fa(χ_a,s−w/2)^m_a.

Assumed RH for all continuous unitary Hecke characters makes each factor holomorphic and nonzero off center in 0<Re(s−w/2)<1. Integer exponents preserve this. Thus **all zeros and poles in the shifted open unitary strip are central**. No Artin holomorphy conclusion follows. The Tate convention M(n) changes weight to w−2n and shifts the line to (w+1)/2−n. V(−i) has weight2i and L(V(−i),s)=L(V,s−i).

For pure algebraic characters a norm-only unitary factor |.|^(it) can occur only with t=0 because the infinity type has no imaginary norm exponent. Its completed poles are consequently at s=w/2 and s=w/2+1, outside the open strip. General continuous unitary norm characters have boundary poles with imaginary translation; this also causes no interior issue.

Fargues, *Motives and automorphic forms: the (potentially) abelian case*, Theorem7.1 pp24–25 verifies equality of motivic and Weil-representation L-functions **including the archimedean factors**; Proposition7.8 and Corollary7.9 p26 explicitly supply virtual Hecke induction. URL in sources TSV. This handles CM descent; requiring CM endomorphisms already defined over Q would be an unnecessary restriction. We do not assume all relevant projectors algebraic: existing Chow motives whose realizations belong to this CM category inherit the L-function statement, while general absolute-Hodge realizations remain in the category in which the literature proves the theorem.
