# Implications of the archive audit

The constructions in cote 133 supply useful algebraic operations and virtual formulas. They do not, by themselves, furnish a positive formula for irreducible tensor-product multiplicities or a saturation theorem. This conclusion identifies what the formulas actually prove; it does not exclude a future positive geometric rule.

The first obstruction is already visible on the free two-point C2-set. The subset operation differs from the exterior operation after linearization. The inverse-symmetric Burnside operation repairs linearization, but is virtual and is not special; neither interpretation supplies the missing positivity (A-001–A-002). A nontrivial irreducible has no effective finite-set permutation lift at all (A-003). A signed Young-subgroup expression can therefore be exact without turning into an effective counting rule.

Scan028's Cauchy formula survives integrally in exact K0, for finite local ranks (A-010 and A-017). Its monomial coefficients can nevertheless have negative irreducible coefficients: psi2 of the standard S3 representation is 1+standard−sign (A-004). This explains why characteristic-zero Schur-Weyl decompositions and the manuscript's monomial expansion have different effectiveness properties.

The quadratic pages require an equally consequential distinction. Their WG is the split isometry-group completion. It need not be a special lambda-ring even with two invertible (A-005), unlike the modern Grothendieck-Witt group with metabolic relations. In characteristic two even the proposed exterior pre-structure fails (A-006), and the general-topos stable-hyperbolic criterion fails (A-008). Exterior powers do not descend to the Witt quotient even over ordinary fields of characteristic different from two (A-007). The etale Stiefel-Whitney formulas are valid in their precise setting, and the hyperbolic formula was already proved by Esnault–Kahn–Viehweg in1993 (A-009).

The reported polynomial induction-ring question has distinct answers according to the exact category. Characteristic-zero fields, local Q-algebras, and exact Zp-lattice categories have the polynomial ring. F2 does not. Characteristic zero alone is insufficient for a singular local base (A-013). These are statements about formal induction generators, not claims about nonnegative Kronecker support.

Borger's special lambda-scheme framework presupposes an actual geometric object carrying the required structure. The Burnside pre-structure alone does not supply it, and the supplied cohomological theorem does not identify any graded Kronecker multiplicity spaces (A-016). Purity, Tate type, and special lambda operations require a separate argument connecting their grading to a saturated support monoid. Root and sibling findings address that distinct geometric-complexity gate.

For the programme on Grothendieck's texts, the audit closes the literal split-WG question on scan006 negatively, repairs the group-action error on scan019, answers scan028's effective-summand question negatively while proving its K0 identity, and closes the study's Zp-lattice subquestion. No claim of authenticity of manuscript ink is made from the supplied transcriptions.

## Further positive constructions and actual multiplicity gates

A-018 supplies an explicit integral matrix whose rational kernel is every fixed-height Kronecker multiplicity space. This gives a constructive positive vector-space model and exact nonvanishing criterion; its polynomial bound is in the magnitude of N for fixed heights, not a polynomial-time solution when rectangle heights grow.

A-019 states the missing algebraic hypotheses sharply. A domain with one-dimensional lattice-graded pieces has a multiplicative monomial basis; if it is normal, its support is saturated in its generated lattice. Primitivity of that lattice gives saturation in the ambient weight lattice. A saturated support can still acquire holes after projecting the grading, so a proposed toric model must verify the relevant projection too.

The verified 2026 Foulkes–Howe proposition implies that the determinant closure has its full ambient degree-d module when n>=d(d−1). A-020 and its stronger adaptive refinement A-021 retain all copies in further shape ranges. A-021 proves full multiplicity if n>=B_d(lambda_2), with B_d(L)=L+sum_{j=2}^{d-1}min(j,L). In particular n>=(d−1)lambda_2 suffices. Thus a surviving actual multiplicity obstruction must have d(d−1)>n and B_d(lambda_2)>n, and hence (d−1)lambda_2>n. These are concrete new restrictions on the actual GCT comparison; they do not assert global Kronecker saturation.

A-022 makes the latter bound uniform over every shape that can occur on the padded permanent. Such a shape has lambda_2<=dm, proved by the padding morphism and horizontal Pieri. An optimized recurrence N_d(L), using p>min(q,L) in the differential kernel test, therefore excludes every actual multiplicity obstruction of degree d if n>=N_d(dm). Its exact maximum is binom(d,2)+max(0,max_{2<=j<=d-1}(min(j²−1,dm)−binom(j,2))); just two indices around sqrt(dm+1) need evaluation. The simpler sufficient condition n>=dm+binom(d,2)−1 already follows from A021. This strictly advances the general remaining degree gate beyond the uniform square-root scale from the cited preprint. The exact formulas, proof, source dictionary, and reproducible integer checks are included.

A-023 strengthens A022 by computing the kernel of each full power D^s, rather than requiring every intermediate homogeneous space to have injective derivative. The resulting exact threshold P_d(dm) is bounded by floor(d(d+m−1)/2); above this sufficient threshold all positive permanent-side types retain every ambient determinant-side copy. Its exact triangular-number maximum may be smaller. The same argument improves the uniform Chow equation exclusion to inner degree at least(d−1)^2. These reductions rely on actual equivariant maps and their kernels, identifying extra categorical data that a mere effective or special lambda-ring class does not provide.

A-024 makes A019 concrete: the rectangle-square family has a polynomial highest-weight algebra with an explicit monomial basis. Its generated lattice has index4 in the ordinary even-total weight lattice. Thus this actual positive normal model has relative saturation and precisely the parity failures of ordinary saturation recorded in F019. A-025 uses two nested row constraints at once to strengthen the BIP joint gate to lambda3>=420 OR lambda52>=3; its coefficient cover retains all ideal multiplicity copies.

The total-degree-pruned refinement in A-025 improves its joint gate further to lambda3>=539 OR lambda52>=3. The uniform proof combines142 exact integer cases with a decreasing infinite tail, retaining the same all-copy support-cover argument.

A-026 gives an additional positive unbounded family: three equal height-three rectangles. The actual invariant algebra is a known polynomial algebra on degrees6,9,12, so the coefficient counts2a+3b+4c=h. Its h-support is {0,2,3,...}, with primitive generated lattice Z and a saturation hole at1. Thus even a genuine polynomial Kronecker invariant ring and an explicit positive monomial basis do not imply saturation after the scalar grading projection. This contrasts constructively with A024, where graded pieces were one-dimensional but the ambient weight lattice was nonprimitive.
