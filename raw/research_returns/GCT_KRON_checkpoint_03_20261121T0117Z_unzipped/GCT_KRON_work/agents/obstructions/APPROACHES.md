## Routes tested that do not yet improve the surviving gate

- **Normality, positive basis and purity.** O003 is a concrete countermodel: normal polynomial invariant ring, nonnegative monomial multiplication, pure affine-space cohomology, but support `{0,2,3,...}` in its own groupZ. Therefore these properties alone cannot remove the hole. This is not a claim that additional geometry could never help.
- **General K-theoretic alternating positivity.** Brion concerns multiplication in coherent K-theory of flag varieties. No basis-preserving map identifying it with the fixed-degree Kronecker product and preserving stretching was found or asserted. The structural distinction and actual representation-ring holes block a formal inference, not every possible future construction.
- **Universal all-form projection beyond the fixed row threshold.** O010 proves the necessary dimension bound `binom(m+r−1,r−1)<=(r−2)n²+2`. At n=m^c, fixedr>=2c+2 fails for all sufficiently large m. This leaves permanent-specific projections and shape-sensitive evaluations available; M009 deliberately uses the latter and is not blocked by this dimension count.
- **Small parameter count of permanent projections.** An r-variable permanent pencil has at most r*m² coefficient parameters, much smaller than all degree-m forms. This does not by itself put its image inside the determinantal image or compare their multiplicities. F010's rank formulation specifies the missing datum; dimensions of parameter spaces do not determine ranks of the evaluation maps.
- **Direct split-variable Laplace expansion of the permanent.** For matrices A,B, expanding each selected edge gives `per(A+B)=sum_{I,J,|I|=|J|} per(A[I,J]) per(B[I^c,J^c])`. Grouping terms only this way produces `sum_k binom(m,k)^2=binom(2m,m)` separated summands. This bound is exponential in m and does not improve the polynomial-in-m two-trie bound for a fixed number of variables. No lower bound against a better compression is claimed.
- **Subset-state dynamic programming.** The standard subset lattice gives size2^m−1 (Grenet), now recorded by F009. It improves the finite-size gate to m>=19/IP and m>=190/BIP but does not beat a fixed polynomial m^c for arbitrarily large m.
- **Partial derivative/minor spaces.** A k-th derivative of a permanent is a combination of complementary minors. Counting all such minors gives up to binom(m,k)^2 states; merely restricting to a fixed r-variable subspace does not supply a proved small simultaneous basis that beats the existing universal bound. This route has no proved gate improvement in the surviving regime.
