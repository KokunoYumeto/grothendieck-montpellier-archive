## CX-G01 — The exact representation-ring transfer of the analytic hypotheses

Let U=Spec Z[1/Bℓ] and let π:Y→U be a finite étale cover obtained by spreading out a finite number field E/Q. Its geometric fiber is the finite continuous G_Q-set Hom_Q(E,Qbar), and π_*Q_l is the corresponding permutation local system. In K_0 of finite-dimensional characteristic-zero representations its class is [Ind_{G_E}^{G_Q}1]. At unramified p its local reciprocal factor is det(1−Frob_p p^(−s)|π_*Q_l); the determinant is a product over Frobenius cycles, one factor 1−p^(−fs) for each residue degree f. Consequently its Euler product is the Dedekind zeta function with the finitely many excluded factors removed. This is an exact dictionary for CX-R01/R02 and CX-K01–K04/K08, rather than an additional analytic theorem.

Proof. The fiber consists of embeddings of E, with G_Q acting by postcomposition. The stalk of the pushforward of the constant sheaf is the vector space of functions on this fiber, with its permutation action. A cycle of length f has characteristic polynomial T^f−1, so det(1−tFrob) on this cycle equals 1−t^f. Multiplying over cycles gives exactly the prime-ideal Euler factors. Trace counts fixed points, hence degree-one prime ideals, which are the specializations used by Koiran. Finite étale covers and finite fundamental-group sets are equivalent by SGA1 V, §4 Theorem1 and §7 (with the geometric fiber functor). This proves the dictionary. For an abelian cover, character decomposition yields precisely the primitive Dirichlet factorization proved in CX-R01. For the cubic cover of CX-R02, the commutator trace obstruction proves that its class is not a virtual sum of rank-one characters. Thus replacing representation categories by their K_0 groups does not erase the exact missing input in the nonabelian case. The analytic transfer is identical, and the arbitrary-number-field hypothesis remains conditional on M.

For a number field K, a finite-order ray-class character defines a rank-one local system on an open of Spec O_K by class field theory. Its pushforward to an open of Spec Z is generally of higher rank, even when K/Q is abelian. This is the geometric form of the class-field and nonresidue hypotheses in CX-N05–N08 and the auxiliary-field constructions. The corresponding Hecke Euler product is the L-function of that rank-one arithmetic local system. The finite-order cases are Artin motives with coefficient field containing the character values; no assertion concerning general motives is needed.

## CX-G02 — Multiplicative algorithms as group-scheme and torsor computations

The statements CX-N01–N06 retain their exact algorithms under the following dictionary. Residues of an integer modulo N are functions on Spec Z/NZ; the units form G_m(Z/NZ). Miller's powers and gcd checks are explicit computations in this ring and its unit group. Over F_q, a power nonresidue a for r|q−1 is a rational point of G_m whose class in F_q^×/(F_q^×)^r is nonzero. Equivalently the finite étale μ_r-torsor Spec F_q[t]/(t^r−a) has no F_q-section. A primitive root generates the cyclic group G_m(F_q); a generating set and a single generator are different outputs.

Proof. G_m=Spec Z[t,t^(−1)] represents units, so multiplication and exponentiation of points are literally the original modular operations. When r is invertible in F_q, differentiation gives r t^(r−1), a unit in F_q[t]/(t^r−a) for a≠0. This algebra is therefore finite étale; μ_r acts by multiplying t, and it is a torsor because the quotient of any two roots is an r-th root of unity after an étale cover. A rational section is exactly an r-th root in F_q. Thus the nonresidue test a^((q−1)/r)≠1 used earlier detects the nonzero torsor class, by cyclicity. Order tests against the prime factors of q−1 and the assembly of primary components remain the identical group operations. No algebraic-geometric dictionary supplies the missing factorization of the integer q−1.

For a squarefree polynomial f over F_q, A=F_q[t]/(f) is finite étale, and its product decomposition is the decomposition into connected components Spec F_(q^d_i). Factoring f is equivalent to computing the primitive orthogonal idempotents of A together with the corresponding minimal polynomials. Indeed the Chinese remainder theorem sends each factor to the projector onto its component; conversely an idempotent e defines the factor ideal (1−e)A, and its inverse image in F_q[t] is generated by the corresponding monic divisor of f. This equivalence is algorithmic by polynomial arithmetic and linear algebra. Repeated factors are separated first by squarefree decomposition, with Frobenius-root extraction in the perfect base field. The finite-étale dictionary does not supply the computational cost of finding idempotents; that cost is precisely the factoring theorem being transferred.

Locators: SGA3 VIII1.1–1.2 (diagonalizable group schemes, including G_m and μ_r); SGA4 IX3.2 (Kummer exact sequence); SGA1 I and V §4 Theorem1, §7 (finite étale covers). Archive cote28, scan3, sheet2 (group actions G×X→X and their axioms); cote98, scan12, sheet11 (finite G-sets). The Miller test is an algorithm on these points, not a new structural theorem in SGA3.

## CX-G03 — Class-group computation is Picard-group computation

For X=Spec O_K, the ideal class group is Pic(X). Under this identification CX-N07 is a bound on the finite set of prime-divisor line bundles needed to generate Pic(X), conditional on M. CX-N08 certifies that computed principal-divisor relations and units generate the full relation lattice; its remaining smoothness gate is unchanged.

Proof/dictionary. A nonzero fractional ideal I of the Dedekind domain O_K is an invertible O_K-module, hence a line bundle on X. Tensor product corresponds to ideal multiplication. Every rank-one locally free module embeds in K after choosing a basis of its generic fiber; clearing denominators realizes it as a fractional ideal. Two such modules are isomorphic exactly when their ideals differ by multiplication by an element of K^×: an isomorphism extends to multiplication by that scalar on the one-dimensional generic fiber. Thus fractional ideals modulo principal fractional ideals identify with Pic(X). The generator [𝔭] corresponds to the invertible sheaf associated to the prime ideal 𝔭; with the effective-divisor convention it is O_X(−𝔭), and reversing signs does not affect generation. The factor-base map from the free abelian group on selected primes to Pic(X) is the divisor-to-line-bundle map. Its kernel consists of principal-divisor relations supported there. The units are the kernel of the principal-divisor map from K^×, which is exactly the kernel used in the class-number/regulator identity. Therefore every assertion and every remaining search cost in CX-N07/N08 is transported without loss.

Finite class/ray characters are the rank-one arithmetic local systems described in CX-G01. Spec O_(H_K)→Spec O_K is the finite étale Hilbert-class-field cover; a ray class field is étale after deleting its modulus primes, with the real-place condition retained in the arithmetic character data. The zeta factorization of CX-N07 supplies the same hypothesis, not merely ζ_K.

Locators: SGA4 IX3.3 (H^1_et(X,G_m)=Pic(X)); SGA6 V3.10 and VI3.2–3.3 for the associated rank-one classes and γ-filtration. Archive cote2, scan5, sheet4: rank augmentation and Picard comparison for schemes of dimension at most one. This is an inspected companion, not a claim that the manuscript proves a class-group algorithm.

## CX-G04 — Integral orbit-closure models and the precise role of FGA

Let G=GL_(m²),Z and V be the free Z-module of degree-m forms in m² variables, with its change-of-variables action. The characteristic-zero determinant orbit closure has a canonical schematic closure X_Z inside the affine space V_Z, defined by I_Z=I_Q∩Z[V], where I_Q is its characteristic-zero ideal. This model is flat over Z. Its projectivization gives a flat projective family and hence a point of the appropriate Hilbert scheme. These statements provide integral objects for CX-E06/E08 and CX-K05–K08; they do not assert that the fiber in every characteristic equals the orbit closure formed in that characteristic.

Proof. By construction Z[V]/I_Z injects into Q[V]/I_Q, so it is torsion free as a Z-module. A torsion-free module over the PID Z is flat: it is the filtered union of its finitely generated torsion-free submodules, each free, and filtered colimits of flat modules are flat. This proves flatness of the affine model. The determinant orbit closure is a cone over Q: a scalar multiple of det_m is obtained over Qbar by scaling the matrix variables, and the orbit closure is invariant under all scalar multiplications; the homogeneous ideal consequently descends to Q and contracts to a homogeneous ideal over Z. Every graded piece of the quotient is a finitely generated torsion-free abelian group, hence free. Localizing at a homogeneous element and taking the degree-zero part preserves flatness: localization preserves flatness and the degree-zero summand is a direct summand as a Z-module. The standard affine charts of Proj therefore prove flatness of the projective family. It is of finite presentation, since Z[V] is noetherian. Its fiber Hilbert functions agree in every degree by freeness of the graded pieces, so the Hilbert polynomial P is constant. FGA Exp221, Theorem3.2, now gives the classifying morphism Spec Z→Hilb^P(P(V)_Z/Z).

The original G-action also spreads to this model. The coaction on Q[V]/I_Q is defined by the integral change-of-variables formula. For f∈I_Z its coaction maps to zero after tensoring with Q. The target Z[G]⊗(Z[V]/I_Z) is torsion free over Z (Z[G] is a localization of a polynomial ring, and the other factor is flat), so it was already zero. Thus I_Z is a G-stable ideal. This proves that X_Z is a G-scheme, without a claim that formation of a scheme-theoretic image commutes with every special-fiber base change.

Finite presentation and Hilbert/Quot representability give parameter spaces, not an efficient list of defining equations. No step above bounds the degrees or bit lengths of generators of I_Z or computes its Hilbert polynomial in the succinct input size. Therefore the independent equation-access premise in CX-K06 is preserved. The finite-morphism and open-parameter-locus dictionary in CX-K08 is the relevant effective normalization statement; it can be placed in Hilbert/Quot language but is not supplied by representability alone.

## CX-G05 — λ-operations, Schur functors, and why rank data do not give obstructions

All the representation objects above may be put in their exact-category Grothendieck rings, with tensor product as multiplication and exterior powers λ^i. For locally free sheaves, the same operations live in K_0(X). With the rank augmentation ε, write γ_t(x)=λ_(t/(1−t))(x) for x of rank zero; F^j_γ is generated by products γ^(i_1)(x_1)…γ^(i_s)(x_s) with Σi_a≥j. These are the structures of SGA6, not additional consequences of GRH or fast SAT.

The exact transport of CX-E07 is [p_*O_(X_F)]=N[O], N=#F. Hence λ_t(N[O])=(1+t)^N. Its reduced class [p_*O_(X_F)]−N[O] is zero, so every positive γ-operation on that reduced class vanishes. These statements hold functorially after base change. Proof: CX-E07 identifies the sheaf with a direct sum of N copies of O. Exterior powers of that free sheaf have ranks binomial(N,i), giving the displayed polynomial; the reduced class is literally zero. Thus the counting algorithm computes one rank, and this exact K_0 restatement contains no nontrivial reduced γ-class.

For a locally free E on a Q-scheme, or on a base where d! is invertible, Schur functors are images of the usual Young idempotents acting on E^(⊗d); idempotents split, so their images are locally free. Their K_0 classes are the corresponding universal Schur operations. Equivalently one can define the Schur classes integrally by the Jacobi–Trudi polynomial in symmetric powers, where Σ h_i t^i=λ_(−t)([E])^(−1). The Young-idempotent construction requires the stated inversion of denominators; the mere assertion that a base ring has characteristic zero does not suffice, and no integral semisimplicity assertion is made. Applied to the graded coordinate representations of CX-E08 over a characteristic-zero field, the irreducible Schur multiplicities are the quantities needed for occurrence or multiplicity obstructions.

The rank obtained by CX-E07 does not determine these representation classes. For a concrete proof, in Rep_Q(C_2) the trivial character and sign character both have dimension one but their traces at the nonidentity element are 1 and −1. Equality in K_0 would force equality of traces, so the two classes differ although their ranks agree. Thus the rank homomorphism is not injective. Passing from a SAT count to its rank-class dictionary alone cannot recover the equivariant coordinate-ring class required by CX-E08. This is a precise failure of that proposed dictionary, not a complexity separation or a prohibition on an additional reduction.

Locators: SGA6 V2.4, V3.10 (equation3.10.1), VI3.2–3.3; SGA3 I4.7.1–4.7.2. For the standard Schur-operation notation, Macdonald, Symmetric Functions and Hall Polynomials, 2nd ed., I§3, equation(3.4), p41, is an ancillary reference to the Jacobi–Trudi definition. ## CX-G06 — The exact purity and motive boundary

The arithmetic finite-étale permutation sheaves in CX-G01, and their finite-order character constituents, have finite monodromy. At a good prime every Frobenius eigenvalue is a root of unity and thus has complex absolute value one. Tensor powers, exterior powers, and direct summands in characteristic zero preserve this statement. This is pointwise weight zero. It does not add a new global zero-location theorem for their Euler products: the latter theorem is exactly the A_D or M input identified in PartI.

Proof. A finite-order matrix has eigenvalues roots of unity, since it is annihilated by T^a−1 for some a. Tensor eigenvalues are products and exterior-power eigenvalues are products over subsets. Eigenvalues of a stable direct summand occur among those of the whole representation. All have absolute value one. By contrast the assertion that the global Euler product has no zeros away from Re(s)=1/2 involves its analytic continuation in the complex variable s and the entire infinite set of primes. No step in this eigenvalue argument supplies such a continuation or zero estimate; the number-field analytic gate remains explicitly in the statement of CX-G01. This is an audit of the proved statements, not a theorem of logical independence.

For the Boolean schemes X_F of CX-E07 the stronger description is immediate: each finite-field fiber is a disjoint union of N rational points, its geometric étale cohomology H^0_et((X_F)_(Fbar_q),Q_l) has N trivial Frobenius eigenvalues, and its higher geometric étale cohomology vanishes. Its zeta function is (1−t)^(−N); its Artin motive is N copies of the unit motive. The fast count computes this multiplicity from a succinct presentation. It neither constructs a nontrivial motive nor changes a purity theorem.
