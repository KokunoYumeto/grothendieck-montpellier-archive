\subsection{A polarization realizing the actual generator adjoints}

\begin{proposition}[P-F74: polarized positivity of the actual quadratic source block]
\label{prop:nonstandardpolarization}
Retain the data of P-F70 at $q=3^e$, including the ordinary surface
$\mathcal A/\mathbb F_Q$, the fields $K\subset L$, the actual
specialized image $M_2(K)$, and the generator matrices
\[
 A_1=\begin{pmatrix}f&\gamma\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 A_2=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\1&f\end{pmatrix}.
                                                               \tag{P-F74a}
\]
Choose an ample line bundle on $\mathcal A$ over $\mathbb F_Q$,
and let $\lambda_0$ be the product polarization on $\mathcal A^2$.
Put
\[
 H=\begin{pmatrix}f&\gamma\\\gamma&f\gamma\end{pmatrix}
          \in M_2(K).
                                                               \tag{P-F74b}
\]
There exist positive integers $N_+,N_-$ such that
\[
 \lambda_+=N_+\lambda_0H,\qquad
 \lambda_-=N_-\lambda_0H^{-1}
                                                               \tag{P-F74c}
\]
are polarizations induced by ample line bundles defined over
$\mathbb F_Q$.  In particular their Rosati involutions are
\[
 X^{\dagger_+}=H^{-1}\overline X^{\mathsf T}H,
 \qquad
 X^{\dagger_-}=H\overline X^{\mathsf T}H^{-1}
                    \quad (X\in M_2(L)),
                                                               \tag{P-F74d}
\]
where the bar denotes CM conjugation on $L$ and fixes $K$.

The actual quasi-endomorphisms $A_1,A_2$, and their prescribed
rescalings $FA_1,FA_2$, are self-adjoint for $\lambda_+$.
They are positive semidefinite in the precise reduced-trace sense
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}_{L/\mathbb Q}\operatorname{tr}_2
               (X^{\dagger_+}A_iX)\ge0
                       \qquad (X\in M_2(L),\ i=1,2),
                                                               \tag{P-F74e}
\]
and the same assertion holds with $A_i$ replaced by $FA_i$.
The normalized operators $A_i/f$ and their complements
$I-A_i/f$ are self-adjoint positive-semidefinite idempotents.

For the actual left cohomological intertwiner of P-F70, which
uses $\rho_\ell(X)=\jmath(X^{\mathsf T})^*$, use $\lambda_-$:
the opposite generator images $A_i^{\mathsf T}$ are self-adjoint
and positive semidefinite for that polarization.  Thus the
generator-fixing involution on the actual source image is
compatible with the polarized correspondence action, including
the transpose convention and the prescribed scalar $F$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{The exact positive form.}
P-F52bk--bl proves from the actual source coefficients that the
two real conjugates $g_1,g_2$ of $\gamma$ satisfy
\[
                       0<g_i<f^2\qquad(q>0,\ q\ne1).
                                                               \tag{P-F74f}
\]
Here $q=3^e>1$, and $f=q^5+q+q^{-1}+q^{-5}>0$ is rational.
At each real embedding of $K$, the leading entry of $H$ is $f$
and its determinant is $\gamma(f^2-\gamma)>0$.
Consequently both embedded real matrices $H$ are positive definite.
Direct multiplication gives the complete adjoint identities
\[
 HA_1=A_1^{\mathsf T}H
       =\begin{pmatrix}f^2&f\gamma\\f\gamma&\gamma^2\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 HA_2=A_2^{\mathsf T}H
       =\begin{pmatrix}\gamma&f\gamma\\f\gamma&f^2\gamma\end{pmatrix}.
                                                               \tag{P-F74g}
\]
The issue is to obtain a genuine polarization from this form,
not merely a positive numerical matrix.  We give a direct
construction by pullbacks of ample line bundles.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{A positive rational decomposition over $K$.}
Write $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt a)$ with $a=A(q)>0$ nonsquare.
Every totally positive element $k=u+v\sqrt a\in K$ can be
written
\[
                         k=s^2+c,
              \qquad s\in K,\quad c\in\mathbb Q_{>0}.
                                                               \tag{P-F74h}
\]
If $v=0$, take $s=0,c=u>0$.  Otherwise total positivity gives
$u>|v|\sqrt a$.  The continuous function
$x\mapsto x^2+av^2/(4x^2)$ on $x>0$ has minimum
$|v|\sqrt a$, attained at $x^2=|v|\sqrt a/2$.
There is therefore an open interval of positive real $x$
on which this function is less than $u$.  Choose a rational
$r$ in that interval and set
\[
       s=r+\frac{v}{2r}\sqrt a,\qquad
       c=u-r^2-\frac{av^2}{4r^2}>0.
\]
The number $c$ is rational, and direct expansion proves
(P-F74h).  This proves the decomposition with no appeal
to a positivity-cone theorem for polarizations.

Apply it to the totally positive element
$k=\gamma(f^2-\gamma)/f$.  For the row matrices
\[
 p_1=(1,\gamma/f),\qquad p_2=(0,s),\qquad p_3=(0,1),
\]
one then has the exact identity
\[
       H=f\,p_1^{\mathsf T}p_1+
                    p_2^{\mathsf T}p_2+c\,p_3^{\mathsf T}p_3.
                                                               \tag{P-F74i}
\]
Indeed the lower-right entry on the right is
$\gamma^2/f+s^2+c=\gamma^2/f+k=f\gamma$;
the other three entries agree immediately.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{Clearing denominators and proving ampleness.}
Each row $p_j$ represents an element of
$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb F_Q}(\mathcal A^2,\mathcal A)
\otimes\mathbb Q$.
Choose a positive integer $m$ clearing all three denominators,
so that $P_j=mp_j$ are actual homomorphisms over $\mathbb F_Q$.
Choose a positive integer $N_+$ so that
\[
        a_1=N_+f/m^2,\qquad a_2=N_+/m^2,\qquad
        a_3=N_+c/m^2
                                                               \tag{P-F74j}
\]
are positive integers.  Let $\mathcal L$ be the chosen ample
line bundle on $\mathcal A$ and define
\[
 \mathcal M=P_1^*\mathcal L^{\otimes a_1}
             \otimes P_2^*\mathcal L^{\otimes a_2}
             \otimes P_3^*\mathcal L^{\otimes a_3}.
                                                               \tag{P-F74k}
\]
Every bundle and map in this expression is defined over
$\mathbb F_Q$.

The combined map $P=(P_1,P_2,P_3):\mathcal A^2\to\mathcal A^3$
has finite kernel.  In fact its first and third rows form
the actual endomorphism with rational matrix
$m\begin{pmatrix}1&\gamma/f\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ of $\mathcal A^2$,
which is invertible in the quasi-endomorphism algebra.
After clearing the denominator of its inverse, its kernel
is contained in a multiplication-by-an-integer kernel,
which is finite.  The map $P$ is therefore an isogeny
onto its image followed by a closed immersion, and is finite.
For the equivalence of finite kernel and an isogeny, and
finiteness of that isogeny, see Milne, \emph{Abelian Varieties},
Proposition~8.1, printed p.~12, in
\url{https://www.jmilne.org/math/xnotes/AVs.pdf}.

The external tensor product of the three positive powers
of $\mathcal L$ is ample on $\mathcal A^3$: after taking a
common positive power, the three projective embeddings
and the Segre embedding make it very ample.  Its pullback
by the finite map $P$ is $\mathcal M$, hence is ample.
The exact general pullback statement is Stacks Project,
Lemma~29.38.7(2), Tag~0892:
\url{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0892}.
A finite morphism is affine and therefore quasi-affine,
so its stated hypothesis applies.

For an actual homomorphism $P_j$, the polarization of its
line-bundle pullback is $P_j^\vee\lambda_{\mathcal L}P_j$;
polarizations of tensor products add.  These formulas are
Milne, Lemma~16.2(c)--(e) and its proof, printed p.~30.
The product Rosati involution fixes $K$ by P-F70, so
(P-F74i)--(P-F74k) give exactly
\[
                    \phi_{\mathcal M}=N_+\lambda_0H.
                                                               \tag{P-F74l}
\]
This proves the first assertion of (P-F74c) by an actual
ample line bundle, with all denominators and coefficients
cleared.

For the second, choose a positive integer $t$ clearing the entries of
$H^{-1}$, so $T=tH^{-1}$ is an actual isogeny of
$\mathcal A^2$.  Pull back $\mathcal M$ by $T$.
Since $H$ is symmetric and CM conjugation fixes its entries,
\[
 T^\vee(N_+\lambda_0H)T
                       =N_+t^2\lambda_0H^{-1}.
\]
Its line bundle is again ample by finite pullback.
Taking $N_-=N_+t^2$ gives $\lambda_-$.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{Rosati, operator positivity and word order.}
From the definition $X^\dagger=\lambda^{-1}X^\vee\lambda$
(Milne, Section~17, printed p.~35), scaling a polarization
by a positive integer does not change its involution.
The product involution is CM conjugate transpose, so
(P-F74c) gives (P-F74d).  Equation (P-F74g) now proves
$A_i^{\dagger_+}=A_i$.  Since the rational scalar $F(q)>0$,
the same holds for the prescribed $FA_i$.

To verify the stated positivity rather than only
self-adjointness, fix a complex embedding $\sigma:L\to\mathbb C$.
Conjugating by the positive square root of $H_\sigma$ sends
$\dagger_+$ to Hermitian adjoint.  It sends $A_i$ to a
Hermitian matrix $E_i$ satisfying $E_i^2=fE_i$.
Thus its eigenvalues belong to $\{0,f\}$ and it is
positive semidefinite.  For
$Y=H_\sigma^{1/2}X_\sigma H_\sigma^{-1/2}$, cyclicity of
matrix trace gives
\[
 \sigma\!\left(\operatorname{tr}_2(X^{\dagger_+}A_iX)\right)
                         =\operatorname{tr}_2(Y^*E_iY)\ge0.
                                                               \tag{P-F74m}
\]
Summing over all embeddings proves (P-F74e).
Multiplication by $F>0$ preserves it.  Dividing by $f>0$
gives eigenvalues $0,1$; their complements also have
eigenvalues $0,1$, proving the normalized projector statement.

For the exact cohomological convention, write
$X^\star=H^{-1}X^{\mathsf T}H$ on the actual source image
$M_2(K)$.  This is its generator-fixing anti-involution,
uniquely determined because $A_1,A_2$ generate that algebra.
The second involution in (P-F74d) satisfies
\[
               (X^{\mathsf T})^{\dagger_-}
                      =H XH^{-1}=(X^\star)^{\mathsf T}.
                                                               \tag{P-F74n}
\]
Thus the opposite-algebra embedding used before pullback
in P-F70 preserves the specified involutions.  In particular
$A_i^{\mathsf T}$ are self-adjoint for $\lambda_-$, and the
same embedding-wise argument gives their positive
semidefiniteness.  The compatibility with the polarized
Tate pairing is the defining adjoint identity for Rosati,
written explicitly in Milne, Section~17, p.~35; passage to
its dual $H^1$ uses Theorem~15.1(a), p.~27.
This retains the actual ordered-word intertwiner of P-F70
and resolves its transpose issue without changing generators.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[P-F74o: polarized positivity on the full source algebra]
Retain the full actual specialized source algebra and the
faithful correspondence realization of P-F73 on
$\mathcal X=\mathcal A^2\times(\mathcal E^2)^3\times\mathcal E$.
There are product polarizations $\Lambda_+$ and $\Lambda_-$,
induced by line bundles over $\mathbb F_Q$, with the following
properties.  For the direct geometric embedding
$\jmath\Phi:\mathcal B_q\hookrightarrow\operatorname{End}^0(\mathcal X)$,
the actual $Q_i,FQ_i$ are Rosati self-adjoint for $\Lambda_+$.
For the opposite embedding $\jmath\tau\Phi$ used in the
actual left $H^1$ action, they are Rosati self-adjoint for
$\Lambda_-$.  In either case the normalized $Q_i/f$ and
their complements are self-adjoint idempotents, and all
these operators, as well as $Q_i,FQ_i$, are positive
semidefinite for the corresponding Rosati trace form.
This assertion applies to the full $21$-dimensional actual
source image, including all its ordered products; it does
not assert that every product is self-adjoint or positive.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The quadratic block is the proposition.  For the three
rational matrix blocks of (P-F73b), set
\[
 H_j=\begin{pmatrix}f&g_j\\g_j&fg_j\end{pmatrix},
              \qquad j=3,4,5.
                                                               \tag{P-F74p}
\]
The checked inequalities (P-F73i) give $0<g_j<f^2$.
Thus each $H_j$ is a positive definite rational matrix.
The same pullback construction works on $\mathcal E^2$,
starting with any base-field ample line bundle on $\mathcal E$:
now $k_j=g_j(f^2-g_j)/f$ is positive rational, so in
(P-F74h) take $s=0,c=k_j$.  All rows are rational
quasi-homomorphisms of elliptic powers.  Clearing their
denominators and the positive rational coefficients exactly
as in (P-F74j)--(P-F74l) gives genuine polarizations with
Gram matrices a positive integer multiple of $H_j$ and of
$H_j^{-1}$.  No assumption about commutativity of the full
endomorphism algebra of $\mathcal E$ is used: its Rosati
involution fixes rational scalars, which are the only entries
of these rows and matrices.

On the final scalar factor choose any polarization of
$\mathcal E$; both actual source generators act there as zero.
Take the product of this polarization and the four
constructed polarizations to obtain $\Lambda_+$; take the
inverse-Gram choices to obtain $\Lambda_-$.  External tensor
products of the ample factor bundles are ample by the
Segre argument already used above, so these are actual
base-field polarizations.  On the block diagonal source
image their Rosati involutions restrict to the respective
blockwise Gram adjoints.  Equations (P-F74g) and (P-F74n),
with $\gamma$ replaced by $g_j$, prove the two self-adjointness
claims.  The scalar block is unchanged.  The generator-fixing
source anti-involution and the actual opposite-pullback word
order of (P-F73o) are therefore compatible on all components.

There is also a positivity argument in the full
quasi-endomorphism algebra, not only in the block diagonal
subalgebra.  Let $D_i$ denote either geometric image of
$Q_i$, using its corresponding Rosati involution $\dagger$.
The exact source relation and the adjoint identity give
$P_i=D_i/f=P_i^\dagger=P_i^2$.  Consequently for every
$Y\in\operatorname{End}^0(\mathcal X)$,
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}(Y^\dagger D_iY)
       =f\operatorname{Tr}\bigl((P_iY)^\dagger(P_iY)\bigr)\ge0.
                                                               \tag{P-F74q}
\]
Here $\operatorname{Tr}$ is the endomorphism trace in Milne,
Theorem~17.3, printed p.~35, which states positive definiteness
of the Rosati trace form.  Trace is cyclic, so its stated
$\operatorname{Tr}(ZZ^\dagger)$ form also applies to
$\operatorname{Tr}(Z^\dagger Z)$.  The positive rational
scalars $f,F$ preserve the inequality.  Replacing $P_i$ by
$I-P_i$ gives the complement assertion.  This argument
allows all cross-factor homomorphisms in
$\operatorname{End}^0(\mathcal X)$; it requires no claim
that the full geometric endomorphism algebra is block
diagonal.  Every source product remains the actual
correspondence product by P-F73, but positivity of the
individual generators does not assert positivity of such
products.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Precise remaining comparison.}
This is a fiberwise polarized correspondence realization
of the actual specialized image algebra and its
generator adjoints.  It supplies no ordinary graded
semisimple category and no natural adjunction of source
functors, so the formal-parameter obstruction of P-F52
is unchanged.  Positivity here is positivity of real
embedded operators and the trace form (P-F74e), not
nonnegative coordinates in the displayed candidate
canonical basis, effective algebraic cycles, or a model
of its individual coefficient spaces.  The polarization
and its clearing integers can depend on $q$; no uniform
family, degree bound or supplied iterated-theta
identification is asserted.
