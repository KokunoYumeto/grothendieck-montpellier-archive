The complete definition context is IV §2.4, “Cells,” printed pp.18–19, equation (17). Supplied labels are “ss cells” and “e preorder,” lines 1567–1591.

The preorder on a module basis is generated by a nonzero coefficient of one basis element in the action of an algebra element on another. Equivalence classes are cells. A cellular submodule is explicitly a submodule spanned by a subset of the designated basis. A cellular subquotient is the quotient of two such coordinate submodules, with the difference of their basis subsets as its basis. The right-action variant is explicitly stated on p.19.

Thus “cellular” in the relevant source premise supplies the coordinate-subset hypothesis. It does not merely mean that an abstract invariant subspace exists.

Conjecture 19.1, printed pp.130–131, source label “cj canonical basis X^r,” supplies:

- Clause (ii): the generic nonstandard Hecke cells \(\check\Gamma_{\alpha,P_V,P_W}\) carry the designated generic simple modules and their integral forms.
- Clause (iii): after \(v=1\), the basis is partitioned into symmetric-group cells called quasi-cells, indexed by \(\nu,\zeta\) inside each fixed \(\alpha,P_V,P_W\), with the stated Specht cell modules and dominance-refined order.

This is not a source assertion that all \(P_i\) entries are nonnegative Laurent polynomials. Adding that stronger requirement is the auxiliary hypothesis under test.

## 4. Why the all-algebra cell definition matches generator reachability

Equation (17) quantifies over every algebra element, not only the named generators. For an algebra generated by the \(P_i\), its preorder still equals reachability in the directed graph of the generator matrices.

Indeed a generator edge is an allowed algebra-action relation. Conversely, a nonzero entry of a word matrix is a sum over generator paths. If it is nonzero, at least one contributing path has nonzero factors, so its endpoints are connected by generator edges. A nonzero entry of a linear combination of words implies a nonzero entry of at least one word. Taking transitive closure proves equality of the two preorders. This argument does not need positivity; cancellations can remove a word coefficient but cannot create a path where none exists.

At \(v=1\), the \(P_i\) and the symmetric-group generators in (C2) generate the same image algebra. Thus the specialized graph computes exactly the quasi-cell notion used in clause (iii).

## 5. Exact comparison scope

The new positivity gate can therefore be stated for an arbitrary regular basis satisfying the relevant generic cell and specialized coordinate-submodule requirements, not only diagonal rescalings of Figure 21. Positivity on a full tensor basis passes to the matrix block of any cellular subquotient because that subquotient has a subset basis and inherits the corresponding entries.

This leaves a different question open: an arbitrary positive basis that does not preserve the source's designated cell/quasi-cell subset structure. The negative-trace F57 result concerns an extra full-algebra block; the present cell-compatibility premise concerns an actual two-row block. They have distinct hypotheses and should not be conflated.
