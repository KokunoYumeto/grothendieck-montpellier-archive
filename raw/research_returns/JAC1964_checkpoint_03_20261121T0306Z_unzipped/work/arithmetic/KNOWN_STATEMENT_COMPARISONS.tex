\subsection{Explicit unresolved
assertions}\label{explicit-unresolved-assertions}

The proof notes establish a split positive Picard block, exact height
orthogonality, a nonzero complementary Fermat class, and an
unconditional positive central derivative for its rank-two CM motive.
Root's AR-10 constructs the corresponding canonical adelic height line.
AR-13 now proves the specified Fermat class has height greater than
1/100, through a local Hodge-lattice bound and a finite enclosed theta
computation. They do not establish positivity on the entire
complementary Chow group; do not supply c₂ or an automorphic height
identity; do not assert a full Hecke-GRH theorem from the Dirichlet
interface; and do not turn a regulator determinant into a positive
quadratic form.
