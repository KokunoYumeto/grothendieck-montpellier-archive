# HODGE_SIGN final package

Start with **HODGE_SIGN.pdf** for the readable report or **HODGE_SIGN.tex** for the complete, self-contained LaTeX source. The latter contains the whole paper and does not depend on working fragments.

## Required deliverables

- HODGE_SIGN.tex: checked findings and cited background in Part I; unestablished stronger claims and source boundaries in Part II.
- QUESTIONS.tsv: every treated question, state, finding IDs and scope/gate, including every SA06 C.1 row.
- LEDGER.md: dated gate changes, approaches, corrections and timing rule.
- CHECK_EVENTS.tsv: full proof readings, independent re-proofs, source inspections, computations and refutations, with actual model and effort-availability information.
- IMPLICATIONS.md: consequences for the archive, BRIDGES, SA06, Fable suggestions, H8/H9 and conditional literature.
- TRANSCRIPTION_NOTES.tsv: mathematical candidates for rereading the archive, with confidence and missing-image qualifications.

## Supporting material

The package retains the compiled PDF, independent check notes, original supplied sources, selected cited Lean files and provenance, reproducible integer/rational computations, tables, separate hunch corrections, verification notes and file hashes. External literature PDFs are cited by exact statement and URL rather than repackaged.

The Wilson and step10 scripts reproduce the stated numerical models. The normalized-order script reproduces exact HNF closure; its TSV and JSON certificates contain the full matrix data. These computations are not a Lean kernel build.

Selected retrieved Lean sources are under evidence/lean_source; paths in the paper are relative to that root. Additional targeted-search matches are under evidence/lean_search_matches. The complete upstream archive is identified by URL, size and SHA256 and is not duplicated. The supplied archive files are retained under sources.

The preliminary root_sign_analysis.md and LEDGER_initial.md preserve work history. Their former geometric-gap statements are superseded by SG-08–SG-11. Use the final paper and current ledger for accepted conclusions. Assembly fragments and the register-assembly script are retained for transparency; the single-file paper remains self-contained.

## Main result and boundary

The generic Gysin kernel supplies the effective sign needed by Parseval and tensor amplification, proving the finite-field absolute-value assertions without identifying parity with Hodge-index positivity. The raw cote44 height formula and literal ambient-group openness claim are refuted, with precise corrections where available. Integral comparisons specify their lattices and normalizations.

A separately specified normalized integral intertwiner for the intended theta chain is not supplied or asserted. The m2 local-contribution table is not an exact nonzero period valuation. No general standard conjecture is claimed solved, and no proof uses an unspecified Masullo theorem.
