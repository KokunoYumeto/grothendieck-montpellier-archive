\begin{abstract}
This final return is incomplete. It distinguishes checked findings,
inherited accepted inputs, and unresolved questions. Research stopped
under the requested rule after two consecutive scheduled reviews
without qualifying movement. Integral
connectedness of the category of admissible divided-power de Rham theories
is not claimed. New checked reductions give a precise simplicial-homotopy
interpretation of the quoted equalization conjecture, remove the sum of
prime-dependent bounds from finite arithmetic gluing, and bound the
vertical torsion of a common free horizontal source by a number depending
only on form degree. A further free construction satisfies every
admissibility condition except eventual vertical exactness in degree two;
its remaining simultaneous-lifting obstruction is an explicit coefficient
residue. The associative localization is terminal, and all degree-one
determinant products also have a prime-uniform comparison. A proposed
strict PD totalization retains the integral tensor obstruction.
An alternative partial source fixes the actual first skeleton and has
only degree-three vertical cohomology left to repair. A further construction
leaves only degree four, with controlled local factors throughout the
other repairs. Coefficient-tail
duality and coherent strict realizations now control all linear
primitive relations in the indicated range. Their nonlinear divided-power
cohomology remains unresolved; the nonflat-disk calculations distinguish
raw defects from those surviving the prescribed low-degree repair.
The native diagonal now has a proved nonnilpotent coefficient-tail
action even after that low repair. This refutes a proposed annihilator
argument, without producing a nonzero actual graph residue.
A separate relative unit-disk factorization proves residue vanishing
when the original native weight meets a fixed target comparison bound.
For independent graph-zero native blocks, chosen coherent path images
remove that extra weight hypothesis and control every low cone residue
through the specified low-degree repair. For a complete native cell under
an old algebra with its coherent boundary data already present, the
relative path obstruction is confined to one specified critical simplex
dimension. This does not supply unrestricted horizontal continuation.
Guarded native probes are now classified up to finite-path homotopy;
their critical prescribed endpoints cannot retain a suitable admissible
intermediate. A finite-skeleton replacement theorem validates a
replacement once the required truncated theory has been constructed.
The degree-four linking obstruction can be repaired on the two-truncated
category, but the repeated low-degree repairs needed to construct that
truncated theory remain open.
The complete low-degree filtration and explicit mixed residue formula
now show why the weaker graph invariant is not preserved. One fixed
source diagram has no admissible intermediate retaining its maps.
A relative free construction upgrades it to a partial source satisfying
every admissibility condition except the degree-two tail. These are
failures of prescribed choices; their identical admissible targets
still possess another common source.
The full tensor and interval hypotheses in cote
134--8 do not force higher coproduct surjectivity; the explicit example
nevertheless has the predicted localization. The motive sections give
geometric Chow constructions, exact mixed Tate criteria, a published-only
ordinary target, and a complete Honda--Tate check. The letter companion
includes a complete verification of the type-A normal form and a
finite orbit count. No result conditional on an outside preprint is
presented as unconditional.

\section{The integral question remains open}
\paragraph{Stopping review.}

was the second consecutive review without qualifying movement.

\paragraph{ARCH-136-OPEN.}
The results in the first part do not prove connectedness of
$\mathcal A_{\mathbf Z}$, terminality of its all-arrow localization,
or the integral homotopy equalization property. The strongest new
constructive reduction is ARCH-136-DEGREE-TWO-FREE: every finite family
has a strict common partial source, free as an abelian group in every
bidegree and simplex, with all required horizontal tails, coefficient
cohomology in degree zero, zero degree-one cohomology in every weight,
and eventual vertical exactness in every degree at least three.
Only degree two remains. Its prescribed Tate-pair repair is governed
exactly by ARCH-136-DEGREE-TWO-RESIDUE. No uniform annihilator or
finite-prime bound is retained for this remaining degree.

The complementary ARCH-136-DEGREE-THREE-COMMON-SKELETON constructs
a degreewise free partial source with literally the common first skeleton,
coefficient cohomology in degree zero, zero cohomology in degrees one and
two in every weight, all horizontal tails, and all vertical tails in
degrees at least four. Its degree-three repair has both a graph-residue
condition and a coefficient-kernel condition. This is not asserted to
be a strictly smaller gate than the degree-two construction.

ARCH-136-DEGREE-FOUR-CONCENTRATION gives a further complementary
source with the same actual first skeleton, all horizontal tails,
all vertical tails except degree four, and controlled local factors
whose cohomology vanishes in degrees one through three.
LOW-FACTOR-PRESERVATION proves this control through horizontal and
RH1 steps and Tate pairs in degrees two, three and at least five.
The omitted degree-four operation is the exact compatibility step
identified in that proof. This is not counted as a strictly smaller
main gate merely because the remaining form degree has changed.

ARCH-136-DEGREE-FOUR-FACTOR-OBSTRUCTION shows, with the exact
chosen first skeleton, why unrestricted degree-four attachment cannot
preserve the controlled local-factor condition. Both targets in that
example still have an evident common admissible source.
ARCH-136-TWO-TRUNCATED-LINKING-REPAIR corrects the new degree-three
linking homomorphism after a degree-four attachment on simplicial
degrees zero through two. It does not prove compatibility with the
next lower-degree repairs. ARCH-136-FINITE-SKELETON-REPLACEMENT
shows that an admissible truncated theory, once constructed, can
replace the corresponding skeleton in each target with uniform bounds.
The missing construction of that truncated theory remains essential.

ARCH-136-Q3-LOW-FILTRATION computes the complete low arity
filtration for a degree-three Tate stage. The explicit mixed
period formula shows that its mixed residue is already determined
by the old maps. ARCH-136-Q2-Q3-COMPATIBILITY-OBSTRUCTION now
refutes preservation from old graph compatibility alone for
both degree-two and degree-three attachments, even after the
immediate RH1 repair and in the two-truncated setting. The
example retains prescribed old maps to two copies of the same
admissible target; it is not a counterexample against all sources.
The separate coefficient test proves that a proposed Hom-vanishing
shortcut fails, while its specified standard-target residue is zero.

ARCH-136-GUARDED-NATIVE-PROBE, together with its
admissible-intermediate counter-bridge, rules out the proposed
critical native probes as an all-source obstruction with the prescribed
admissible intermediate. They do not assemble paths under arbitrary
old boundary data. ARCH-136-FREE-COMPLEX-LIFT-CAUTION records a direct
counterexample to an external unrestricted lifting statement; no
positive result in this paper uses that statement.

An alternative partial source, ARCH-136-DISK-SUPPORT, retains a
different useful control: its positive degree-$d$ cohomology is killed
by $L_d=\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,\lfloor d/2\rfloor)$ uniformly
in weight and simplex. These are two proved reductions, not two
simultaneously established properties of one completed source.
The inverse-refinement and finite-skeleton counterexamples exclude
specified repairs, not every possible common source. Even the genuine
pullback of two identical theories over a common finite skeleton need
not admit forward completion extending both projections. Its diagonal
admissible source shows why that direction of obstruction is insufficient.

The one-disk Moore tower now gives an exact $p$-local completion,
but ARCH-136-NATIVE-LIFT proves that its native primitives need not
lift along a prescribed guarded target map. ARCH-136-GUARDED-PAIR
does supply every individual sufficiently high coefficient-tail
pair. ARCH-136-RELATIVE-COHERENT-MOORE now supplies all finite
derived coefficient relations, and ARCH-136-COHERENT-PD-REALIZATION
realizes them by an actual strict PD extension. Its explicit rational
unit-disk coordinates justify a subsequent integral repair through
degree one. This does not control the new higher classes: the
construction reaches form zero for every prime, and arbitrary cycles
need not have the native divided-power derivative form. The nonflat
even-disk examples prove that weightwise freeness of its linear
parameters does not supply an integral Poincare lemma.
ARCH-136-TAIL-EXT-VANISHING identifies the derived graded dual of
every positive coefficient tail with the coefficient module itself.
ARCH-136-SKELETON-COHERENT-MOORE consequently makes the coherent
realization preserve the common graph in degrees at least four;
its native degree-four calculation kills the displayed linear
degree-three relation classes. This does not exhaust the nonlinear
classes. ARCH-136-ACTUAL-TAIL-SYZYGY proves a raw degree-zero defect
for every permitted free resolution of a prime-power tail. The
positive-form disk examples are killed by the extra-closed-zeroform
ideal, and ARCH-136-CYCLIC-DISK-QUOTIENT proves a complete contraction
after that quotient for sums of cyclic modules. The subsequent
ARCH-136-ACTUAL-TAIL-CONSTANT-QUOTIENT proves that the standalone
even disk on the actual noncyclic bottom lattice still has nonzero
degree-one cohomology after the full quotient, at every odd prime.
The complete RH1 repair can kill those classes; its higher cohomology
and its interaction with the other coherent generators remain unresolved.
The actual native quadratic component is identified by
ARCH-136-DIAGONAL-REDUCTION. Its degree-three cohomology is not
annihilated by any power of any positive coefficient tail, by
ARCH-136-QUADRATIC-TAIL-ACTION, even after the specified low-degree
repair. Thus the successful mixed-class annihilator test does not
extend to the diagonal. This still supplies no nonzero actual target
graph residue. ARCH-136-TORSION-GRAPH-RESIDUE removes the ordinary
torsion degree-two contribution from that cone obstruction; the
degree-three torsion quotient still requires a separate argument.
ARCH-136-RELATIVE-DISK-FACTORIZATION and HIGH-NATIVE-RESIDUE give
one such argument when the original native weight is already above
the target comparison range. The sufficient bound may grow with
the prime, so it is not the required prime-uniform construction.
The full homogeneous-block extension is
ARCH-136-NATIVE-ALL-ARITIES. For independent graph-zero inputs,
ARCH-136-CHOSEN-NATIVE-PATHS and CHOSEN-PATH-LOW-REPAIR allow
coherent images to be chosen so that every low cone residue vanishes,
uniformly across their native primes, even after the complete low
repair. These statements exclude boundary-constrained attachments
under an old algebra and arbitrary later horizontal continuations.
For independent graph-zero native operations of degree at least six,
HIGH-DEGREE-PRESCRIBED-PATHS also allows the coherent images to be
prescribed: the relative path-extension obstruction is supported
only in degree three. The exceptional native degree-four obstruction
and the general old-boundary step are kept distinct.
ARCH-136-RELATIVE-CELL-PATHS treats a complete native cell under an old
algebra with its entire coherent boundary already installed: its
fixed-input path obstruction is concentrated in simplex dimension
$n=D-2$. ARCH-136-PRIME-INTERPOLATION-INVARIANCE makes that
obstruction independent of the native interpolation when the integer
is prime. CRITICAL-NATIVE-PATH-COMPONENT realizes a nonzero prescribed
path component on a nonadmissible source, without an admissible-domain
conclusion. ARCH-136-FIRST-CELL-RESIDUES controls all low graph-difference
residues for a genuine first native two-cell under the free even-disk
envelope, through RH1, with the stated original-native-weight guard.
ARCH-136-DEGREE-THREE-LINKING-REPAIR uses graded injective linking
coefficients to preserve degree-two vertex compatibility for full-boundary
Tate cells with already installed boundary data. For the reduced
representables used in the main construction, ARCH-136-LINKING-CHAIN-GATE
requires an additional vanishing on the intersection with new simplicial
boundaries. None of these hypotheses is silently supplied by the
degree-two source construction.
ARCH-136-LINKING-TRIANGLE-OBSTRUCTION realizes a nonzero such
intersection value in the actual reduced degree-three repair of the
checked finite-skeleton example, for every extension of its fixed old
maps and in unbounded weights. Both targets are the standard theory;
this is still a prescribed-repair obstruction, not a component invariant.
The associative all-arrow localization is
terminal by ARCH-136-ASSOCIATIVE-TERMINAL, and all degree-one
Massey sets are rigid under the comparison. The separate strict
commutative argument ARCH-136-DETERMINANT-RIGIDITY also identifies
all higher degree-one determinant sets by integral local systems,
with one cutoff independent of arity. Those results do not
reflect terminality back into the strict commutative PD category.
The strict PD end tested in ARCH-136-TW-TOR fails the horizontal
condition even on $B$, with nonzero degree-one row homology in
every prime-power weight. Its exact vertical comparison is
insufficient to make that proposed common target admissible.
The scaled bar envelope is an exact positive-form construction, but
does not supply all indecomposable coefficient tails. Its unscaled
radial version fails mixed target divisibility. A relative bar over
the standard zero-form algebra also has persistent vertical torsion.
Every admissible target under the standard theory contains its image
injectively, and contains it integrally saturated when the target is
torsion-free. None of these constraints yet excludes torsionful targets.

The homotopy calculation reduces the integral quoted equalization
property exactly to vanishing of the fundamental group of every
component. Together with connectedness this is terminality. These
two integral assertions remain separate. Over the semilocal rings
specified in AQ-136-SEMILOCAL they both hold.
