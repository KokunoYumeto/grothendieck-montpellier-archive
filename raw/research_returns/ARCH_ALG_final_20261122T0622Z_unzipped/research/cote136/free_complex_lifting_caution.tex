\section{A source audit: degreewise freeness does not lift closed generators}
\label{sec:136-free-complex-lifting-caution}

\paragraph{Exact source and scope.}
While looking for a replacement theorem for the remaining
nonnegative-cochain PD repair, we checked Antoine Caradot and
Zongzhu Lin, \emph{Differential graded algebras with divided
powers and homotopy Lie algebras},
\url{https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.22614v4}, version 4 of
20 June 2026. Their definition of \(\operatorname{Cplx}(A)^{\rm free}\)
in Section 2.1, p.~6, means free in each degree. Proposition 3.10,
pp.~19--20, claims a lift from its free PD algebra through any
PD-ideal surjection. The proof tries to lift the underlying
chain map from degreewise freeness. The following checks that
precise claim. It concerns the inspected version, not any
uninspected later correction. This source is not an input to
any positive theorem in this paper.

\paragraph{ARCH-136-FREE-COMPLEX-LIFT-CAUTION.}
Degreewise freeness of a cochain complex does not imply the
lifting assertion of that Proposition 3.10, even over
\(\mathbb Z\) and for a free complex supported in one
positive cochain degree.

\begin{proof}
Use cohomological gradings and the actual strict differential
PD algebras
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 D'&=\Gamma_{\mathbb Z}(u)\otimes\Lambda_{\mathbb Z}(v),
       &|u|&=2,\quad |v|=3,\quad du=v,\quad dv=0,\\
 D&=\Gamma_{\mathbb Z}(\bar u),
       &|\bar u|&=2,\quad d=0.
 \end{aligned}
                                                        \tag{136.FL1}
\]
The differential on \(D'\) is the special derivation
\(d\gamma_n(u)=\gamma_{n-1}(u)v\). Its square is zero:
the next differential contains \(v^2=0\). Thus the
augmentation ideals \(I'\subset D'\), \(I\subset D\)
are differential PD ideals. The assignment
\[
                       \pi(u)=\bar u,\qquad\pi(v)=0
                                                        \tag{136.FL2}
\]
is a strict PD dg algebra morphism, and \(I'\to I\)
is surjective in every degree.

Let \(X\) have \(\mathbb Z x\) in cochain degree two,
zero elsewhere, and zero differential. It is degreewise free.
The map \(\rho:X\to I\), \(x\mapsto\bar u\), is
a chain map, and extends to the free PD algebra on \(X\).
If the asserted PD dg lift to \(D'\) existed, its value
on \(x\) would be a closed element of \((D')^2\)
mapping to \(\bar u\). But
\[
                   (D')^2=\mathbb Z u,
                   \qquad d:(D')^2\to(D')^3,
                                      nu\longmapsto nv
                                                        \tag{136.FL3}
\]
is injective. There is no such closed element. This contradicts
the asserted lift with all its displayed hypotheses satisfied.
The failure already occurs for the underlying chain map, before
any nonlinear divided-power relation is considered.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Consequence for the archive route.}
This elementary obstruction gives no counterexample to
Grothendieck's common-source question. It prevents an invalid
shortening of the coherent parameter arguments on cote 136,
physical pp.~117, 119, 121, scans 117, 119, 121: one must actually
solve the chain equations, including the bottom relations.
A lift of the graded generators is insufficient.
The valid free-PD extension of a \emph{given chain map} is a
different assertion and is not challenged here.
