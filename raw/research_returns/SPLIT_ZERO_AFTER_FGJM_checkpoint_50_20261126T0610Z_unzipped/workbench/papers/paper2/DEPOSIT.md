## Title

Quotient counts and sign-Tate zeta functions after Faul, Goswami, Joubert, Manuell, and Ramachandran

Author line: deliberately empty.

## Abstract

Faul, Goswami, Joubert, and Manuell define the zero-adjoined inverse semiring, while Ramachandran describes finite-field zeta functions as Witt vectors. We extend the known quotient count of the zero-adjoined integers to a weighted family and separate it from the Euler products associated with effective sign-Tate classes. Their common coefficient quotient remembers each occupied weight and its signed trace, but loses the multiplicities needed for an effective zeta function. We give exact descent criteria, the correction at the ramified prime, and a finite-type obstruction to an earlier proposed monoid zeta function. We also describe the Boolean sheaves attached to a discrete divisor. Along the Hurwitz path joining the two quotient counts, interval arithmetic certifies five simple-zero trajectories; two end to the right of the line of absolute convergence. Alpöge's Dirichlet Riemann hypothesis is used only as an external input for explicitly specified degree-one factors.
