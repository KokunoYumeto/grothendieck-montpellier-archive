\section{Nested coefficient projections and the remaining four-row gates}

\subsection{OBS-001. Exact nested-jet dominance at sizes nine and twenty-three}
Use coefficient coordinates $c_\alpha$ for
$F=\sum_{|\alpha|=n}c_\alpha x^\alpha$ in four variables, indexed from zero.
For $0\le e\le c\le b\le n$, put
\[
 J_n(b,c,e)=\{\alpha:\alpha_1+\alpha_2+\alpha_3\le b,
       \ \alpha_2+\alpha_3\le c,\ \alpha_3\le e\},
 \qquad N_n(b,c,e)=|J_n(b,c,e)|.
\]
The value of the count is, explicitly,
\[
 N_n(b,c,e)=\sum_{v=0}^{e}\sum_{u=v}^{c}(b-u+1).
\]
The restriction $b\le n$ explains why no further degree-$n$ bound occurs
in this sum.

\paragraph{Statement.}
For $n=9$ and $n=23$, the projection of $D_{n,4}$ to the coefficient
space indexed by $J_n(b,c,e)$ is dominant if and only if
$N_n(b,c,e)\le 2n^2+2$.
Consequently, for every degree $d$ and every partition
$\lambda\vdash nd$ of length at most four, put
\[
 (b,c,e)=(\min(n,\lambda_2),\min(n,\lambda_3),\min(n,\lambda_4)).
\]
If $N_n(b,c,e)\le 2n^2+2$, then $K_n(\lambda)=a_\lambda$.
In particular no padded-permanent multiplicity obstruction of that
weight is possible, for any choice of $m<n$.

\begin{proof}
The dimension bound $\dim D_{n,4}\le 2n^2+2$ is CUE\_GCT G-06.
It excludes dominance whenever the number of projected coordinates
exceeds that bound. For the converse we give exact, independently
reconstructed Jacobian certificates.

All computations use $p=101$. Start the linear congruential generator at
$s=20261122$, replace $s$ successively by
$1664525s+1013904223\pmod{2^{32}}$, and extract
$\lfloor s/2^{16}\rfloor\pmod {101}$.
Take $A_0=I_n$, $A_1=\operatorname{diag}(1,\ldots,n)$, and fill
$A_2,A_3$ in row order by these residues. Differentiate the coefficient
map of $\det(\sum_{i=0}^3x_iA_i)$ with respect to all $4n^2$ matrix
entries, including those of $A_0$ and $A_1$.

The checker constructs the adjugate without evaluating or interpolating
the determinant. Write $B=x_1A_1+x_2A_2+x_3A_3$, $C_0=I_n$ and
\[
 e_k=\frac{\operatorname{tr}(BC_{k-1})}{k},\qquad
 C_k=e_kI_n-BC_{k-1}\quad(1\le k<n).
\]
Then
$\operatorname{adj}(x_0I+B)=\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}x_0^{n-1-k}C_k$,
and the derivative in the $(u,v)$ entry of $A_i$ is
$x_i\operatorname{adj}(x_0I+B)_{vu}$.
These formulas hold in the finite field since $p>n$.
They also show that every entry reconstructed by the checker is the
reduction of the actual integral coefficient Jacobian.

There are $\binom{n+3}{3}$ nested profiles. It suffices to check those
maximal under coordinatewise increase among the profiles with
$N_n\le2n^2+2$: every other such coordinate set is contained in a maximal
one. The checker enumerates all profiles and all these maximal members.
For $n=9$ they are exactly
\[
\begin{array}{c|r|r}
 (b,c,e)&N_9&\text{selected determinant modulo }101\\\hline
 (8,8,7)&164&76\\
 (9,6,5)&164&74\\
 (9,9,3)&164&39
\end{array}
\]
For $n=23$ the 31 maximal profiles and their nonzero maximal minors are
listed in \texttt{results/det23\_nested\_profiles.json}; the complete
set is
\[
\begin{gathered}
(16,16,16),(17,14,14),(17,15,11),(17,17,10),\\
(18,12,12),(18,13,10),(18,15,8),(18,18,7),\\
(19,11,11),(19,12,9),(19,14,7),(19,16,6),(19,19,5),\\
(20,11,10),(20,12,7),(20,13,6),(20,17,5),\\
(21,10,10),(21,11,7),(21,12,6),(21,14,5),(21,21,4),\\
(22,10,8),(22,11,6),(22,12,5),(22,16,4),\\
(23,9,9),(23,10,7),(23,11,5),(23,14,4),(23,23,3).
\end{gathered}
\]
Every listed submatrix has full row rank. The first three size-nine
calculations were also performed using ordinary scalar Gaussian
elimination, independently of packed elimination. The finite-field
nonzero minors lift to nonzero integral minors, hence prove dominance
over $\mathbb C$.

Finally S-001 (the full support lemma F-016) puts every vector in the
highest-weight multiplicity space of weight $\lambda$ in the polynomial
algebra on the indicated coordinate set. A dominant coefficient
projection makes restriction injective on this entire polynomial
algebra, hence on the entire highest-weight space. Its image therefore
has dimension $a_\lambda$, proving $K_n(\lambda)=a_\lambda$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Certificate scope and arithmetic.}
The two standalone programs are \texttt{check\_binary\_jets.py} and
\texttt{check\_nested\_profiles.py}. They read no inherited coefficient
matrices or minors. Each output records the seed, selected rows and
columns, the matrix hash and the minor residue. Packed elimination is
ordinary Gaussian elimination with base-$2^{32}$ digits: a row update
forms $a+10100-vb$ digitwise, with all digits of $a,b$ and the pivot
multiplier $v$ in $\{0,\ldots,100\}$. Each intermediate digit lies
between 100 and 10200, so no carry or borrow passes between entries.
For digitwise modular reduction the reciprocal product is also below
$2^{32}$; the quotient estimate differs from the true quotient by at
most one and the final mask corrects that difference.
Row swaps and pivot products are recorded
with their signs. This is an exact algorithm, not floating-point rank.
The results are characteristic-zero statements; the prime $101$ is a
certificate prime, not a claim of uniform dominance in all
characteristics.

\subsection{OBS-002. Exact early gates on a partial-Hessian direction}
Consider the four-row direction
$\nu_n=(5n-7,3,2,2)$ in degree five and its stretches
$s\nu_n$ in degree $5s$. For $n=9$, OBS-001 excludes $s=1,2$ and
first ceases to exclude at
\[
 s=3,\quad d=15,\quad \lambda=(114,9,6,6),\quad a_\lambda=1250.
\]
For $n=23$, it excludes $s=1,\ldots,6$ and first ceases to exclude at
\[
 s=7,\quad d=35,\quad \lambda=(756,21,14,14),
 \quad a_\lambda=2859327863.
\]
These are the first remaining tests under this exclusion criterion;
they are not assertions that gaps occur at these stretches.

\begin{proof}
The nested profiles are $(\min(n,3s),\min(n,2s),\min(n,2s))$.
Substitution into the finite sum for $N_n$ gives the asserted thresholds.
For example at $n=23,s=6$ the profile $(18,12,12)$ has 1001 coordinates
and is in the checked frontier. At $s=7$ the profile has 1520 coordinates,
exceeding 1060. The multiplicities are computed by the exact character
coefficient method described below. No permanent or determinant
evaluation rank is inferred from a positive ambient multiplicity.
\end{proof}

\subsection{OBS-003. A complete low-degree candidate sieve, with its limitations}
In degrees $6,7,8,9,10$ for nonics in four variables, the number of
positive-ambient-multiplicity partitions not excluded by OBS-001 is,
respectively,
\[
                 446,\quad846,\quad1426,\quad2205,\quad3228.
\]
Among these, the only partition of ambient multiplicity one is
\[
                   d=6,\qquad\lambda=(15,13,13,13).
\]
There are none in degrees seven through ten. This is a sieve for one
kind of potentially easy witness, not a computation of determinant
ideals in these degrees.
For $n=23,d=6$ the same complete sieve leaves 12214 positive-multiplicity
labels after OBS-001, and none has ambient multiplicity one. The total
four-row ambient multiplicity sum before that exclusion is 9500810962;
exactly thirteen four-row labels have zero ambient multiplicity.

\begin{proof}[Finite exact character calculation]
Expand
\[
 \prod_{\alpha_0+\alpha_1+\alpha_2+\alpha_3=n}
       (1-tx^\alpha)^{-1}.
\]
Its $t^d x^\gamma$ coefficient is the weight multiplicity in
$\operatorname{Sym}^d(\operatorname{Sym}^n\mathbb C^4)$.
The implementation stores the total number of nonconstant coefficient
factors and the last three weight coordinates; factors corresponding
to $(n,0,0,0)$ fill out the remaining outer degree. It successively
performs the geometric-series update for every other monomial of degree
$n$, truncating only outside the needed degree and weight box. This
computes integer weight multiplicities exactly. Multiplying the
character by $\prod_{i<j}(1-x_j/x_i)$ and extracting $x^\lambda$
gives its highest-weight multiplicity as the alternating sum of 24
weight multiplicities, by the Weyl character formula. The program
enumerates every partition of $nd$ into exactly four positive parts, computes
this alternating sum, and applies the exact integer coordinate-count
test of OBS-001. The complete output includes every surviving partition
and its ambient multiplicity. Hence the asserted finite list and counts
are independently reproducible.
Partitions with fewer than four parts are already excluded by the
dominance theorem for ternary determinant images (CUE\_GCT G-05).
\end{proof}

\paragraph{An upper bound that does not decide the candidate.}
For this same label, an exact Murnaghan--Nakayama calculation gives
\[
\begin{split}
g((15,13,13,13),(9^6),(9^6))&=25792324,\\
\operatorname{sg}((15,13,13,13),(9^6))&=12860921.
\end{split}
\]
The trace of factor interchange is $-70482$, and the alternating-square
multiplicity is $12931403$. The two rectangular factors may be transposed
simultaneously without changing their tensor square or its interchange
action, so $(9^6)$ is equivalent here to $(6^9)$.
The checker sums over all 386155 cycle types of $S_{54}$, using
\[
g=\sum_{\rho\vdash54}\frac{\chi_\lambda(\rho)\chi_{(9^6)}(\rho)^2}{z_\rho},
\qquad
\operatorname{sg}=\frac12\sum_{\rho\vdash54}
 \frac{\chi_\lambda(\rho)(\chi_{(9^6)}(\rho)^2+
                  \chi_{(9^6)}(\rho^2))}{z_\rho}.
\]
Thus the stabilizer-based symmetric-Kronecker upper bound supplies no
useful restriction beyond $K_9(\lambda)\le a_\lambda=1$.
It neither proves determinant nonvanishing nor determinant vanishing.

\subsection{Coherent-ideal interpretation}
For each dominant nested projection, restriction on the corresponding
coefficient algebra is injective. Its intersection with the homogeneous
ideal of $D_{n,4}$ is zero, and therefore every highest-weight
multiplicity space supported in that algebra maps injectively to the
coordinate ring. This is the concrete equivariant coherent-ideal
statement behind OBS-001. It compares images of homogeneous sections;
it requires no projective normality or vanishing of higher cohomology.
The Cauchy/Schur passage in the support proof is related to the supplied
transcription of Grothendieck cote 133, scan 028; the numerical ranks
are additional data, not consequences of identities in a Grothendieck
group. No assertion about an uninspected archive scan is made.
