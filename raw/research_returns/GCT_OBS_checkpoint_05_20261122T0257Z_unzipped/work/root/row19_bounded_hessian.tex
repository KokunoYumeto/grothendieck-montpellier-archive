\subsection*{OBS-010. The size-23 exclusion through second row nineteen}
For every four-row partition $\lambda\vdash23d$ with
$\lambda_2\le19$, in every coefficient degree $d$,
\[
                         K_{23}(\lambda)=a_\lambda.
\tag{OBS-010.1}
\]
Consequently any actual multiplicity obstruction at $(17,23)$ has
$\lambda_2\ge20$. Padded equality at second rows18 or19 is not asserted.

\begin{proof}
Apply the complete bounded-Hessian criterion OBS-008 with ordinary
jets1 through16 as the lower variables and jets17 through19 as the
high variables. Their dimensions are968 and571. On the chart $F_0=1$
the determinant parameter map has generic rank1059: the inherited
dimension bound gives the upper bound, and the independently reconstructed
Jacobian minor gives the matching lower bound. The normal dimension
is therefore480, and the relative tangent dimension over the dominant
lower projection is91.

Take the sum of all polynomial $\mathrm{GL}_3$ types whose first
row is at most19. Equivariant comultiplication and
Littlewood--Richardson containment give both closure under centered
high partial derivatives and the complete list of possible high
quadratic Taylor components. In the notation of the generic checker,
the list is
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|r}
(j,k,r)&\text{Schur type}&\text{dimension}\\\hline
(17,17,16)&(18,16)&510\\
(17,18,16)&(19,16)&714\\
(17,18,17)&(18,17)&360\\
(17,19,17)&(19,17)&567\\
(18,18,18)&(18,18)&190\\
(18,19,18)&(19,18)&399
\end{array}
\]
These are precisely the two-row summands $(j+k-r,r)$ with
$j+k-r\le19$; when $j=k$ only even $r$ occur. Their total dimension
is2740. The integral highest symbols are
$(a_0b_1-a_1b_0)^r a_0^{j-r}b_0^{k-r}$, and every retained basis
vector is specified by an explicit word in the two simple lowering
operators.

The normalized coordinate change is the same triangular centering as
in OBS-008--OBS-009. With $\ell_i=\operatorname{tr}(A_i)/23$ and
$T_{j,\alpha}=(23-j)!\alpha!F_{j,\alpha}/23!$, its differential
on a lower-kernel direction is
\[
 dC_{j,\alpha}=
 \sum_{i=17}^{j}\ \sum_{\substack{\beta\le\alpha\\|\beta|=i}}
   \prod_{k=1}^3\binom{\alpha_k}{\beta_k}
   (-\ell)^{\alpha-\beta}\,dT_{i,\beta},\qquad17\le j\le19.
\]
It is invertible. The complete combined test thus has
$480+2740=3220$ rows and $91\cdot92/2=4186$ quadratic columns.

\end{proof}
