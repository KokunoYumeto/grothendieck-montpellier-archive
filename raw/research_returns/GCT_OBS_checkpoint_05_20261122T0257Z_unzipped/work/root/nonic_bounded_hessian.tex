\paragraph{Proof of the all-copy and all-degree conclusion.}
OBS-008's bounded-Hessian criterion applies to the full space
$\mathcal B$: the lower map is dominant, and the combined forms are
independent. If an element of $\mathcal B$ vanished on the determinant
image, the quadratic Taylor identity would first force its entire
gradient to vanish on a dense open. Every centered high partial then
vanishes on the image and still belongs to $\mathcal B$. Induction
on high degree, with lower dominance for the degree-zero case, makes
the polynomial identically zero.

By S-001, a highest-weight vector of four-row weight $\lambda$ uses
only jets through $\min(9,\lambda_2)$. Centering on $F_0\ne0$ and
specializing $F_0=1$ injects its fixed-outer-degree highest-weight
space into the centered space of tail weight
$(\lambda_2,\lambda_3,\lambda_4)$, as established in S-007.
When $\lambda_2\leq11$, this space lies in $\mathcal B$.
A nonzero ideal highest-weight vector would contradict the zero
kernel just proved. The total tail weight is at most33, so high
degree can be four; the general derivative induction applies without
a quadratic or cubic truncation assumption. This applies to the entire multiplicity space,
so $K_9=a$ in every degree. Since always $q\leq a$, the claimed
necessary condition for a padded obstruction follows.
