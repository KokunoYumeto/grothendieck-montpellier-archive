\subsection*{Prospective OBS-011. Third relative jets for a bounded tail box}
\textbf{Status: the criterion and its accelerated application are under
review. No new multiplicity equality is asserted in this provisional
section until an independent numerical certificate has passed.}

Let $f=(l,g)$ map a nonempty open subset of an affine space to
$L\oplus G$, with dominant lower projection $l$ and constant generic
rank $r$ on a pivot open, and put $c=\dim L+\dim G-r$.
Let $W$ be a rational frame for the left
kernel of $df$, and let $R$ be a rational right inverse to $dl$.
As in OBS-008 allow centered high coordinates $y=B(l)g+b(l)$ with
$B$ invertible, and dominant centered lower coordinates $z=z(l)$.
Write $H=\tfrac12d^2f$ and $T=\tfrac16d^3f$ for the symmetric
Taylor forms at a source point.

Let $\mathcal B\subset k[z,y]$ be closed under all high partials.
Suppose every high quadratic Taylor part of a member lies in a
fixed space $Q_2$, and every high cubic Taylor part lies in $Q_3$.
For a direction $v\in\ker dl$, put
\[
 U(v)=-R H_l(v,v),\quad
 u(v)=B\,dg(v),\quad
 h(v)=B\bigl(dg(U(v))+H_g(v,v)\bigr).
\]
The source curve $p+t v+t^2U(v)$ has lower output constant through
order two. Its third output coefficient is
$T(v,v,v)+2H(v,U(v))$.

For bases $q_a$ of $Q_2$ and $r_b$ of $Q_3$, consider the following
two systems of scalar polynomial forms in the relative direction $v$:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
 &\text{quadratic constraints}&\text{cubic constraints}\\\hline
\text{normal row }\nu&W_\nu H(v,v)&
 W_\nu\bigl(T(v,v,v)+2H(v,U(v))\bigr)\\
\text{quadratic row }a&q_a(u(v))&2q_a(u(v),h(v))\\
\text{cubic row }b&0&r_b(u(v)).
\end{array}                                                    \tag{TR-1}
\]
Here $q_a(\ ,\ )$ is the symmetric bilinear polarization. If these
$c+\dim Q_2+\dim Q_3$ rows are independent, using
any finite set of quadratic coefficient columns and cubic evaluation
columns, then no nonzero member of $\mathcal B$ vanishes on the image.

\begin{proof}
All entries vary rationally on the pivot open, so a nonzero maximal
minor persists on a nonempty open set. Let $P$ vanish on the image.
Its gradient in ordinary output coordinates has the form $\eta W$.
Write its high quadratic and cubic Taylor parts as
$\sum_a c_aq_a$ and $\sum_b e_br_b$, at the given output point.
The coefficients of $t^2$ and $t^3$ in
$P(f(p+t v+t^2U(v)))=0$ are exactly the linear combination of
(TR-1) with coefficients $(\eta,c,e)$. Lower first and second output
coefficients are zero; this is why no low/high mixed derivative of
$P$ enters. The ordinary gradient kills $df(U)$ in the second
coefficient. It also kills any added term $df(Z)$ from an optional
third-order source correction, so such a correction is unnecessary.
The lower dependence of $B$ and $b$ contributes to the ordinary
gradient term already included in $W$, and contributes no extra high
quadratic or cubic term because the lower series starts in order three.

Independence forces $\eta=0$. Invertibility of $B$ then shows that
every centered high partial of $P$ also vanishes on the image. These
partials belong to $\mathcal B$. Induction on the high degree, with
lower dominance as its degree-zero case, gives $P=0$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The proposed nonic application.}
Take lower jets1 through6 and high jets7 through9, and let
$\beta=(12,8,8)$. Let $\mathcal B$ be the intersection of the sum
of Schur types $\nu\subseteq\beta$ with the sum of polynomial torus
weight spaces $\alpha\le\beta$ coordinatewise. Both conditions
survive high partial differentiation: Schur containment follows from
equivariant comultiplication and Littlewood--Richardson containment,
and weights decrease by a nonnegative coefficient weight. The same
argument puts the high second and third derivatives in the similarly
trimmed quadratic and cubic spaces $Q_2,Q_3$.

The dimension inventory gives $\dim Q_2=3327$ and $\dim Q_3=2705$.
The cubic space consists of94 copies in66 profile/type pairs, before
the weight cut, and has full uncut dimension7175. The exploratory
builder constructs each highest vector from three-letter flag brackets,
selects the required highest-weight multiplicity, and records integral
lowering words. Its character count and Gelfand--Tsetlin weight count
are included in the source. These statements still need the separate
whole-source and independent numerical checks for the proposed application.

The ordinary nonic normal space has dimension56 and the relative
tangent dimension is80. Thus the second constraint block has
$56+3327=3383$ rows and $80\cdot81/2=3240$ columns. If its columns
are independent, its left kernel has dimension143. The full
independence in (TR-1) is then equivalent to full row rank of a
$143+2705=2848$ row matrix formed from cubic evaluations after
this elimination. Indeed any row relation must lie in the left
kernel of the quadratic block, and the reduced cubic block must
then annihilate that remaining relation together with its cubic
coefficients.

A successful completed certificate would exclude every tail contained
in $(12,8,8)$, in every outer degree, including the fourth
partial-Hessian stretch $(152,12,8,8)$ in degree20. At present this
is the precise proposed next gate, not a proved conclusion.
