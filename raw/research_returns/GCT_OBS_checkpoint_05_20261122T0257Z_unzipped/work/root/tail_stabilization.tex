\subsection{OBS-005. Two further all-degree multiplicity families are excluded}

\paragraph{General stabilization statement.}
Fix $n\ge2$ and a partition $\beta=(b,c,e)$ with $b\ge c\ge e\ge0$.
Put $w=b+c+e$ and
\[
 H_d=\operatorname{HW}_{(nd-w,b,c,e)}
        \operatorname{Sym}^d(\operatorname{Sym}^n\mathbb C^4),
\]
whenever the indicated weight is a partition. Let $B_\beta$ be the
highest-weight space of weight $\beta$ in the polynomial algebra on
the ternary symmetric tensors $C_2,\ldots,C_n$, where $C_j$ is assigned
weight $j$ under simultaneous scalar multiplication of its three slots.
Then $H_w\simeq B_\beta$, multiplication by $F_0^{d-w}$ identifies
$H_w$ with $H_d$ for $d\ge w$, and every earlier $H_d$ embeds into
$H_w$ by multiplication by $F_0^{w-d}$.

Consequently, if restriction to the determinant locus is injective on
$H_w$, it is injective on every admissible $H_d$.

\begin{proof}
Use normalized polars
$F_j(v)=T(e_0^{n-j},v^j)$ for a degree-$n$ form with symmetric tensor
$T$, and set $F_0=T(e_0^n)$. On $F_0\ne0$ define
\[
 C_j(v)=T\left(e_0^{n-j},
               \left(v-\frac{F_1(v)}{F_0}e_0\right)^j\right).
\]
The three-dimensional unipotent radical acts by translating the first
polar by a multiple of $F_0$. The displayed change of coordinates
sets that polar to zero. Its invariant algebra is therefore
\[
 \mathbb C[F_0,F_0^{-1},C_2,\ldots,C_n].
\]
This is an explicit polynomial coordinate change on the localized
algebra; it uses no general finite-generation assertion for unipotent
invariants. The remaining upper triangular subgroup is that of
$\mathrm{GL}_3$ on the last three coordinates.

Every $C_j$ has coefficient degree one and has slot weight $j$ in the
last three variables. Thus a localized highest-weight vector of
coefficient degree $d$ and last-three weight $\beta$ has the unique form
\[
              \sum_k F_0^{d-k}I_k(C_2,\ldots,C_n),
\]
where $I_k$ has $k$ tensor factors, slot weight $w$ and highest weight
$\beta$ for $\mathrm{GL}_3$. Setting $F_0=1$ identifies the localized
space with $B_\beta$. This substitution is injective on the indicated
homogeneous weight space.

Expanding the centering formula shows that each $C_j$ has denominator
dividing $F_0^{j-1}$. A monomial of slot weight $w$ with $k$ factors
therefore has denominator dividing $F_0^{w-k}$. Consequently
$F_0^{w-k}I_k(C)$ is a polynomial of coefficient degree $w$ and
highest weight $((n-1)w,b,c,e)$. Every element of $B_\beta$ has such
a lift, proving $H_w\simeq B_\beta$. Multiplication by the indicated
powers of $F_0$ gives both the higher-degree isomorphism and the
earlier-degree injection.

If a nonzero earlier vector vanishes on the determinant locus,
multiplication by $F_0^{w-d}$ would give a nonzero vanishing vector
in $H_w$, which is impossible under the hypothesis. For $d>w$, a
vector in $H_d$ is $F_0^{d-w}$ times a vector in $H_w$. The determinant
locus is irreducible and $F_0$ is nonzero on it, as witnessed by the
constant pencil $x_0I_n$. Hence multiplication by $F_0$ is injective
on its coordinate ring. The higher-degree assertion follows as well.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Two exact nonic comparisons.}
For every admissible $d\ge4$ and every padded permanent with $m<9$,
\[
\begin{split}
 K_9(9d-21,9,6,6)&=a_{(9d-21,9,6,6)},\\
 K_9(9d-20,9,7,4)&=a_{(9d-20,9,7,4)}.
\end{split}
\]
Thus $q_\lambda\le K_9(\lambda)$ throughout both families.
The stabilized ambient multiplicities are1250 for the first family
and2030 for the second, attained respectively for every $d\ge21$
and every $d\ge20$.

\begin{proof}
The polynomial character
\[
 \prod_{2\le|\alpha|\le9}(1-z^\alpha)^{-1}
\]
and the six-term $\mathrm{GL}_3$ Weyl extraction give dimensions1250
and2030 for weights $(9,6,6)$ and $(9,7,4)$. The independent bounded
knapsack calculation is in \texttt{check\_minimal\_tail\_gates.py}.
A second calculation keeps the number of tensor factors of each order
as separate profile degrees. It gives103 nonzero profiles for the
first weight and90 for the second. Their summed dimensions agree
with the independent total counts.

For weight $(9,6,6)$ use six alternating triples and three free
highest-weight slots. For weight $(9,7,4)$ use four alternating triples,
three alternating pairs on coordinates0 and1, and two free
highest-weight slots. Attach the slots to symmetric-tensor vertices
of orders2 through9. These flag-bracket contractions have the indicated
weights and are killed by both positive simple root operators.
The construction expands all $6^6$ or $6^4 2^3$ color assignments;
it does not use the full color symmetry special to S-006.

The actual integral polynomials are supplied in
\texttt{root/results/tail\_9\_6\_6/} and
\texttt{root/results/tail\_9\_7\_4/}. Their coefficient tables,
graphs, profile degrees and pivot monomials specify every function
without a basis-choice ambiguity. The standalone
\texttt{check\_tail\_basis.py} reads the tables directly, checks
every monomial's weight, applies both raising operators over
$\mathbb Z$, and checks a nonzero coefficient minor modulo101 in
each profile. Thus the1250 and2030 polynomials are independent
highest-weight vectors, regardless of the exploratory graph builder.
The character upper bounds make them complete bases.

Apply the stabilization construction at degrees21 and20 respectively,
and evaluate at the explicitly generated centered nonic determinant
pencils of QC-007, now retaining tensor order9 as well. QC-008
reconstructs every matrix entry using only the standard library and
then performs ordinary scalar Gaussian elimination. The results are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|r|r}
\beta&\text{complete evaluation matrix}&\text{rank}&
      \text{selected maximal minor modulo }101\\\hline
(9,6,6)&1250\times2060&1250&88\\
(9,7,4)&2030\times2060&2030&9.
\end{array}
\]
The first scalar elimination uses the first1280 sample columns,
which already suffice; the second uses all2060. A nonzero modular
minor is a nonzero rational minor at genuine determinant pencils,
so restriction is injective on the full stabilized highest-weight
space. The preceding theorem propagates injectivity to every
admissible degree. Finally $q_\lambda\le a_\lambda$ for every
quotient of the ambient coordinate ring, proving the asserted
inequalities without assuming equality on the padded side.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Effect on the stretched partial-Hessian search.}
At $n=9$, the third stretch of $(38,3,2,2)$ has degree15 and weight
$(114,9,6,6)$. OBS-005 now excludes that stretch, so OBS-002's
previous first unexcluded stretch has been decided. The fourth
stretch, of degree20 and weight $(152,12,8,8)$, is the next case
not decided by these results. No conclusion about that fourth
stretch follows from a nonzero function in the third.

\paragraph{Geometric interpretation and the ratio question.}
The theorem computes zero kernels of actual Schur-isotypic restriction
maps, then transports them by a nonzero homogeneous section on an
integral scheme. It is the coherent-ideal and localization setting
of GD-1--GD-3. In particular the displayed comparisons also apply
when $m\le6$, so that $9/m>\sqrt2$. They are fixed-tail families:
the last three parts stay fixed while the first grows. They do not
decide all stretches of a fixed four-row direction with four positive
normalized parts, which remains a separate part of question6.
