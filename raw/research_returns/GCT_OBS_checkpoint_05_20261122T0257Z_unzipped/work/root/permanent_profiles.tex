\subsection{OBS-004. Exact padded multiplicities from permanent coefficient projections}

Let $J_n(b,c,e)$ and $N_n(b,c,e)$ have the meanings in OBS-001.
The following statement concerns the coefficient projection itself,
rather than only evaluations of a form at selected variable values.

\paragraph{Statement.}
For $0\le e\le c\le b\le17$, the projection of
$P_{17,4}$ onto the coordinates $J_{17}(b,c,e)$ is dominant if and
only if $N_{17}(b,c,e)\le1124$.
Consequently, for $(m,n)=(17,23)$, every highest weight
$\lambda\vdash23d$ satisfying
\[
 \lambda_2\le17,\qquad
 N_{23}(\lambda_2,\lambda_3,\lambda_4)\le1124
\]
has $q_\lambda=a_\lambda$. This is an all-copy equality in every
degree in which the displayed tuple is a partition.

In particular the following degree-six labels have fully determined
padded multiplicities:
\[
\begin{array}{c|r|r}
\lambda&N_{23}(\lambda_2,\lambda_3,\lambda_4)&q_\lambda=a_\lambda\\\hline
(89,17,16,16)&1122&1359\\
(91,17,17,13)&1120&4719\\
(94,17,15,12)&1066&9474\\
(94,17,16,11)&1072&10464
\end{array}
\]
The determinant multiplicities of these four labels have not been
computed by this certificate; the first column gives candidates, not
obstructions.

\begin{proof}
QC-01 establishes $\dim P_{17,4}=1124$ with the independently
reconstructed finite certificate. This excludes a dominant projection
to more than1124 coordinates. There are just two maximal nested
profiles below that count:
\[
\begin{array}{c|r|r}
(b,c,e)&N_{17}&\text{coefficient-Jacobian minor modulo }101\\\hline
(17,16,16)&1122&22\\
(17,17,13)&1120&61.
\end{array}
\]
Indeed, every profile with $c\le16$ lies below the first. For $c=17$
one has $b=17$, and direct substitution in the count formula shows that
the count is at most1124 exactly when $e\le13$.

Here is a reconstruction of the coefficient Jacobian, including the
interpolation that distinguishes this certificate from an evaluation
rank. Use the same integral pencil and prime as QC-01 at $m=17$:
the linear congruential generator has initial state20278274 and update
$s\mapsto1664525s+1013904223\pmod{2^{32}}$, extracting
$\lfloor s/2^{16}\rfloor\pmod{101}$. Fill the four coefficients of
each matrix entry in row order. The forward subset recurrence and its
reverse differentiation give all $4\cdot17^2=1156$ partial derivatives
of the permanent at each grid point
\[
 (17-t_1-t_2-t_3,t_1,t_2,t_3),\qquad
 t_i\ge0,\quad t_1+t_2+t_3\le17.
\]
Each differential column is a homogeneous form $F$ of degree17.
Put $g(t)=F(17-|t|,t)$. The finite Newton identity gives
\[
 g(t)=\sum_{|\beta|\le17}
      \Delta^\beta g(0)\prod_{i=1}^{3}\binom{t_i}{\beta_i}.
\]
Successive forward differences along the three axes reconstruct these
coefficients exactly. Expanding each binomial polynomial and then using
\[
 F(x)=\left(\frac{x_0+x_1+x_2+x_3}{17}\right)^{17}
  g\left(\frac{17x_1}{x_0+x_1+x_2+x_3},
         \frac{17x_2}{x_0+x_1+x_2+x_3},
         \frac{17x_3}{x_0+x_1+x_2+x_3}\right)
\]
recovers its ordinary homogeneous coefficient vector. The expression is
a polynomial after expansion. Every factorial and the integer17 are
invertible modulo101. These operations are an exact linear change of
basis, applied simultaneously to all1156 columns.

The supplied checker performs precisely these differences, binomial
expansions, rescaling and three translations of the homogenizing
variable. Its two selected coefficient submatrices have the full row
ranks and nonzero minors displayed above. The output records all row
labels, all selected columns and the hashes of the reconstructed matrices.
It also substitutes six original grid points back into the complete
coefficient matrix and verifies every differential column at each point.
Nonzero residues certify nonzero rational Jacobian minors and hence
dominance in characteristic zero. Containment of nested coordinate sets
then proves the assertion for every smaller profile.

For the padded statement fix the linear factor to be $x_0$.
Multiplication $Q\mapsto x_0^6Q$ identifies the coordinates indexed by
$J_{17}(b,c,e)$ with those indexed by $J_{23}(b,c,e)$ whenever $b\le17$.
Thus the chosen projection of this subset of the padded image is already
dominant. By S-001, every element of the entire highest-weight
multiplicity space is a polynomial in those coordinates. Restriction is
therefore injective on that entire space, giving $q_\lambda=a_\lambda$.
The four numerical ambient multiplicities are entries in the complete
exact degree-six plethysm calculation of OBS-003; they use the same
24-term Weyl extraction, not an inference from dimension alone.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Reproducibility and geometric form.}
Run \texttt{check\_permanent\_nested\_profiles.py}; it imports only the
new permanent-differentiation module shipped in the packet, and reads no
inherited matrices. The output is
\texttt{results/per17\_nested\_profiles.json}.
Geometrically, the dominant coefficient projections induce injections of
their polynomial coordinate algebras into the padded coordinate ring.
The stated Schur-isotypic kernels are zero. This is the SGA~3 weight and
EGA~II homogeneous-ideal dictionary of OBS-001, with the permanent
parameter morphism in place of the determinant one. The arithmetic
certificate proves a characteristic-zero assertion and supplies no
semisimplicity claim in characteristic101.
