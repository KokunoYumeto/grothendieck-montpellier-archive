\subsection*{BR-011: exact projective reductions inside the permanent flag search}
\textbf{Status: proved as a conservative refinement, using only completed
per-case exclusions.  Root whole-proof and both new search-source reviews
passed on 2026-11-22 at approximately 02:28--02:30 UTC.  No fresh root
search or rank rerun is claimed.  The structural/hash merger is separately
identified and records its exact current scope.}

This refinement starts only with the explicit $381$ remaining coordinate
orbits of BR-010.  It uses the same independently certified connected
solvable subgroup, the same proper incidence, and the same complete
invariant-flag necessity proved there.  Its new feature is to follow the
entire surviving linear parameter spaces, rather than leaving every
positive-dimensional first-wedge arrangement open.

\paragraph{Exact first-wedge arrangement cover at every flag step.}
Let $F$ be an invariant subspace of $Q=(A\otimes B)/T(C^*)$, and let
$K$ be the joint kernel in one weight space of $Q/F$ of every retained
positive root.  Every invariant one-dimensional successor in this
weight space is represented by a nonzero point of $K$.  The source
enumerator constructs all such kernels, in all weights.  Its coordinates
are chosen in a complement of $F$, so a nonzero parameter gives a genuine
one-dimensional successor.

Apply the exact first-wedge arrangement cover from BR-010 to $K$.
The output is a finite list of rational linear spaces $K_i\subseteq K$
whose projectivizations cover every successor satisfying both first
Koszul rank inequalities.  Keeping maximal such spaces does not remove
a parameter: it removes only spaces contained in another retained space.
Each one-dimensional $K_i$ supplies one rigid successor, which is returned
to the ordinary flag search.  Larger $K_i$ are passed in full to the next
procedure.  No generic sample replaces a surviving parameter space.

\paragraph{Homogeneous determinantal hypersurface covers.}
Write the proposed extension vector as
\[
 v(x)=x_0v_0+\cdots+x_{d-1}v_{d-1},\qquad [x]\in\mathbb P^{d-1},
\]
where all $v_i$ have the same torus weight and their classes form a basis
of one retained parameter space.  Fix a higher Koszul map of exterior
degree $p$ along a factor of dimension $n$.  Its pure-tensor rank cost is
$c=\binom{n-1}{p}$.  Let $E_0=T(C^*)+F$.

The checker first computes the image of the map on $E_0$ over
$\mathbb Q$, separately in every output weight.  It reduces the new
columns modulo that fixed image.  The residual matrix in each weight
block $w$ is a matrix $L_w(x)$ of homogeneous linear forms.  Hence
\[
 \operatorname{rank}K_p(E_0+\langle v(x)\rangle)
 =b_0+\sum_w\operatorname{rank}L_w(x),
\]
where $b_0$ is the already computed fixed-image rank.  Equal output
weights are grouped together; no assumption that the higher-Koszul
columns have distinct weights is made.

In each block select a square minor $D_w(x)$ of size $r_w$ that is not
identically zero.  Integer evaluation points are permitted only to
\emph{select} its rows and columns.  The determinant itself is then
recomputed exactly in $\mathbb Q[x_0,\ldots,x_{d-1}]$, and is asserted
nonzero and homogeneous of degree $r_w$.  Thus these are explicit
polynomial minors, not an extrapolation of sampled rank.

If
\[
 b_0+\sum_w r_w>17c,
\]
then every admissible parameter lies in
\[
 \bigcup_{w:r_w>0}V(D_w)\subset\mathbb P^{d-1}.
\]
Indeed, outside this union every chosen minor is nonzero, so the displayed
rank is too large.  Factoring the minors into irreducible polynomials over $\mathbb Q$
gives an exact cover by the zero loci of these factors, including after
base change to the algebraic closure.  An individual factor need not
remain geometrically irreducible; retaining its whole zero locus is
what makes this cover valid.  A rational linear factor gives a complete hyperplane parameter
space, computed by exact nullspace elimination and mapped back to the
original quotient coordinates.  Its dimension is strictly smaller.
Repeatedly applying this step therefore terminates along every linear
branch.  Any nonlinear factor remains explicitly OPEN as its entire
hypersurface; its sampled points are never substituted for the variety.

The script tries exterior degrees $p=2,3,4$ in both factors.  Each
successful map supplies a complete necessary cover, so choosing any one
of these covers is valid.  A cover consisting entirely of hyperplanes
is preferred.  If only a cover with a nonlinear remainder is found, its
linear branches may still be studied, while the complete nonlinear
remainder is retained OPEN.  If no certified generic lower bound exceeds
the threshold, the full current family stays OPEN.

\paragraph{Exact projective lines and their special points.}
When the parameter dimension reaches two, the reviewed exact pencil
engine is applied to the full projective line.  It recomputes all selected
univariate minors over $\mathbb Q[t]$, keeps every irreducible exceptional
factor and the point at infinity, and checks the exceptional ranks in the
corresponding exact number fields.  The projected-Koszul pencil engine
is available if these tests leave the line open.  An excluded pencil
contributes no successor.  Every remaining rational special point is
inserted as a rigid flag and returned to the same arranged search.
Every unhandled nonrational special point is retained OPEN.  Thus a
finite exceptional locus is never silently discarded.

\paragraph{Rigid tests and the terminal $(2,2)$ condition.}
Every constructed rigid extension is checked by the same exact or
certified-modular Koszul tests used in BR-010.  At a full seventeen-plane
$E$, the driver may additionally use the previously reviewed
$P_{2,2}(E)$ prolongation computation.  The necessary condition is
\[
 \dim P_{2,2}(E)\ge17.
\]
For completeness, if $E$ is spanned by seventeen independent rank-one
tensors $a_i\otimes b_i$, the vectors $a_i^2\otimes b_i^2$ lie in
$P_{2,2}(E)$ and are independent.  To see independence, choose linear
forms $\ell_j$ on $A\otimes B$ with
$\ell_j(a_i\otimes b_i)=\delta_{ij}$ and evaluate the bidegree-$(2,2)$
forms $\ell_j^2$.  Their evaluation matrix on these squares is the
identity.  The prolongation is the kernel of linear maps whose entries
vary algebraically with $E$ in local Grassmannian charts; kernel dimension
is upper semicontinuous.  Hence the same lower bound holds on every limit
of such seventeen-planes.  The proper border-rank incidence used in
BR-004/010 supplies precisely such an $E$ whenever the tensor has border
rank at most seventeen.  This terminal test is not applied to smaller
partial extensions.

\paragraph{Coverage and conservative completion.}
Suppose a border-rank-seventeen tensor survived the starting coordinate
case.  The fixed-point argument gives an invariant full extension, and
Lie's theorem gives a complete invariant flag inside it.  Induct on the
flag dimension.  The raw joint-kernel enumeration lists its next line;
the exact first-wedge arrangement retains it; every chosen polynomial
minor cover retains it; and the projective-line exceptional analysis
retains it unless a necessary rank bound fails.  A retained rational
point is continued, and all other retained possibilities are recorded
OPEN.  Therefore a complete run with neither a terminal rigid survivor
nor an OPEN family contradicts the assumed flag.  Such a case has border
rank at least eighteen.

The search deduplicates equal rational subspaces by canonical row
reduction.  It does not identify distinct parameter loci by a sampled
normal form.  Every timeout, assertion failure, unsupported number-field
continuation, or other exception leaves the entire coordinate case OPEN.
The final flag lists are meaningful only when \texttt{search\_complete}
is true; no unfinished record can produce EXCLUDED.  Partial processing
of the $381$ starting cases leaves all unprocessed cases in the gate.

\paragraph{Files and computation scope.}
The two new source modules are
\texttt{scripts/refine\_per10\_arranged\_flags.py} and
\texttt{scripts/projective\_koszul\_cover.py}.
They import the already reviewed exact linear algebra, tensor module,
first-wedge arrangement, pencil, projection, and finite-point insertion
modules.  SymPy is used for exact polynomial determinants, factorization,
and rational number-field arithmetic; no floating-point rank enters an
exclusion.  Per-case records are saved in
\texttt{certificates/per10\_arranged\_flags/}.  Each hypersurface record
stores the actual selected minor polynomials, their row and column
indices, the fixed and generic rank bounds, the chosen factor cover,
and all nonlinear remainders.  The checked baseline remains
\texttt{certificates/per10\_combined\_gate.json}; it is not overwritten.

\paragraph{Conservative merge and current result.}
The separate structural checker \texttt{merge\_per10\_projective\_refinements.py}
checks the identity of every completed case, the complete rigid exclusion
counts at every level, the absence of unhandled projective branches, and
the strict scalar inequalities in each claimed failed test.  It hashes
every included exclusion record and all three new source modules before
removing their masks from the checked BR-010 baseline.  It explicitly
records that the ranks are not recomputed during the merge.  All
unprocessed and OPEN cases remain.  Its current result is
\texttt{certificates/per10\_projective\_gate.json}; a PARTIAL phase label
does not mean that the conservative remaining gate omits unfinished cases.
