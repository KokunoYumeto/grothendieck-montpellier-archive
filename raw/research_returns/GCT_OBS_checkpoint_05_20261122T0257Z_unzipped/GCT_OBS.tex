\begin{abstract}
This continuing research package distinguishes proved comparisons from
uncomputed multiplicity gates. It reconstructs the twenty-five exceptional
seventh-minimum prolongation computations, gives an arithmetic proof of
CP border rank at least 43 for the integral determinant and permanent
tensors of size four in every characteristic, independently verifies the
quaternary permanent dimension certificates and their uniform insertion,
and supplies a full support-lemma proof and an explicit recomputation of
the degree-twelve LMR comparison $6>5$.
For the main padded problem, nested coefficient projections of determinant
pencils of sizes nine and twenty-three are completely classified. Complete
determinant restriction matrices exclude four all-degree fixed-tail
families, of stabilized multiplicities804,1250,2030 and2386. New polynomial
arguments determine the full padded multiplicity in larger regions,
including every four-row label with second row at most 9 at $(8,9)$,
and every four-row label in coefficient degrees at most nine. A relative
second-derivative criterion proves $K=q=a$ for all four-row labels with
second row at most17 at $(17,23)$, in every degree. The bounded-Hessian
extension then proves $K=a$ through second row19. The same criterion
proves $K_9=a$ for all nonic four-row labels with second row at most11.
Thus the main necessary second-row bounds are12 and20, respectively. The equal-tail-nine nonic comparison has2386 copies and its complete
graph, entry and scalar-rank certificates have all passed. An explicit nineteen-term degeneration improves a
determinant ten-plane upper bound. The complete characteristic-zero
ten-plane exclusion also proves CP border rank at least44 for the
determinant coefficient tensor. Its exact tenth minimum remains18 or19.
No uncomputed restriction rank is described as a multiplicity obstruction.
\end{abstract}

or SymPy. Accelerated exploratory basis builders are distinguished
from these verifiers. Supplementary exact JavaScript BigInt checks,

Generated numerical matrices are
reconstructed from formulas and recorded seeds. Archived representative
lists used by the orbit cover are independently validated, including
their disjointness and exhaustive coverage.

images of restriction maps. Cote 133, scan 028, is cited through the
supplied transcription for the tensor/Schur-operation framework;
cote 133, scan 019, distinguishes an equivariant class from its rank.

and diagonalizable-group grading language, as cited and checked in the
inherited runs. Proper Grassmannian incidence is the setting for the

modern complexity bound by itself, and no purity, motive, GRH, or
