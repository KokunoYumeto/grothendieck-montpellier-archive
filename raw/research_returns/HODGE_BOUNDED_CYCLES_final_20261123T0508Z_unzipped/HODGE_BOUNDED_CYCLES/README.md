# Hodge bounded cycles

This final archive contains one standalone, editable research paper and its proof, source and computation records. The paper is **Hodge’s conjecture for abelian varieties as a uniform bound over finite fields, after Brünjes and Serpé**. It has 120 pages, with all 27 local TeX inputs and its bibliography included.

## Main result

For special families induced by morphisms of Shimura data, the paper proves the equivalence between rational Hodge algebraicity on every fibre, a uniform cycle-complexity bound on a dense set, and a uniform bound on effective numerator degrees at infinitely many good reductions of each CM fibre. Globally this is equivalent to the rational Hodge conjecture for all complex abelian varieties. The auxiliary prime may vary; the integer clearing denominators is initially unrestricted and is controlled by the geometric positivity estimate. The proof of this equivalence uses the identified published geometric foundations, not the recent CM Hodge-conjecture preprints.

The paper also proves effective extraction of the Weil plane from one detector, the 48/192/36 fourfold bounds, general-field polarization and stabilization results, the stated conditional quantitative higher-dimensional bounds, and the eventual minimum-degree alternative under reduction. It studies explicit construction obstructions and sufficient correspondence criteria for exceptional classes on Mumford squares.

## Research outcome and limits

The Hodge conjecture is not proved here. Uniform split Weil detectors in every field and dimension, a first split eightfold detector outside the Eisenstein field, and the first required exceptional Mumford-family correspondence remain open. Section 33 and `QUESTIONS.tsv` state their exact remaining conditions.

A positive Gaussian network is constructed with a nonzero Weil character, positive multiplicities and an exact solution of all matrix-valued vertex-diagonal trace equations. Its certificate verifies all 6272 coordinates. This does not solve the off-vertex equations, full Atiyah obstruction or strong semiregularity, and no deforming eightfold detector is inferred. The paper also proves a generic obstruction for the specified coordinate-sign networks and a tree-gluing obstruction. These exclude specified constructions, not arbitrary algebraic cycles.
