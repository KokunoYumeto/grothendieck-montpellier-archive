\paragraph{A current rank theorem does not remove the regulator hypothesis.}
Longo--Vigni, \emph{The Tamagawa number conjecture and Kolyvagin's
conjecture for motives of modular forms}, Research in the Mathematical
Sciences 13, article 64 (2026),
\url{https://doi.org/10.1007/s40687-026-00646-7}, was checked directly
in the publisher's primary full text.  Theorem B (Theorem 5.42),
with the assumptions in \S1.2.1, assumes an integral regulator
isomorphism and specified injectivity statements for Heegner
Abel--Jacobi maps.  Its rank conclusion cannot establish the missing
injectivity in AR-16a.  Moreover, \S1.2.1 explicitly says that the
squarefree-level assumption excludes CM; \S1.3.1 also imposes
$k\equiv2\pmod{2(p-1)}$, which fails for $k=4,p=5$.
Conjecture 2.50 is the scalar-extended motivic regulator isomorphism,
and Remark 2.51 distinguishes the paper's arithmetic Chow domain
from the whole homologically trivial Chow group, with their equality
conjectural in that general setting.  These are independent reasons
not to apply the theorem to the Fermat full-Chow gate.  This source
audit does not modify the already proved Selmer dimensions or
surjectivity of the Fermat regulators in AR-19 and AR-23.
