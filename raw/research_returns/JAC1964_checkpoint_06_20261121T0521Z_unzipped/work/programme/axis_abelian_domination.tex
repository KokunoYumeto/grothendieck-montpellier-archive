\subsection*{JAC-P19. An ample axis has no abelian rational domination}

\begin{proposition}[JAC-P19]
Let $A$ be an abelian variety over a field $k$, and let
$W\subset A$ be a smooth geometrically integral complete
intersection of ample effective Cartier divisors
$H_1,\ldots,H_c$, with $c\ge1$ and $\dim W>0$.
After any extension of the ground field, there is no
dominant rational map from an abelian variety to $W$.
In particular this holds for a smooth ample
codimension-two axis in the geometric height problem,
in every characteristic. Products of abelian varieties
do not give an exception.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
It is enough to work over an algebraic closure of the
extended field. Suppose an abelian variety $B$ admits
a dominant rational map $u:B\dashrightarrow W$.
Compose with the closed immersion $j:W\hookrightarrow A$.
A rational map from a smooth variety to an abelian
variety extends everywhere, and a map from an abelian
variety to an abelian variety is a translation of a
homomorphism. The exact source is Milne,
\emph{Abelian Varieties}, version~2.00 (2008), Chapter~I,
Theorem~3.2, printed p.~17, and Corollary~3.7,
printed p.~19. These assertions have no separability
restriction. Thus the image of $j\circ u$ is a
translate $a+B'$ of an abelian subvariety $B'\subset A$.
Dominance and properness of $B$ give
$j(W)=a+B'$ as reduced closed subvarieties. Consequently
$W$ is isomorphic to an abelian variety, and
$\omega_W\simeq\mathcal O_W$.

On the other hand, the conormal exact sequence for the
smooth complete intersection and $\omega_A\simeq\mathcal O_A$
give the adjunction identity
\[
       \omega_W\simeq
       \mathcal O_A(H_1+\cdots+H_c)|_W.
\]
The right side is ample, while the trivial line bundle
is not ample on a positive-dimensional projective
variety. This contradiction proves the claim.
A product of abelian varieties is itself abelian,
so the same proof applies to that source.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact implication for the remaining axis gate.}
For the axis $W=H_1\cap H_2$ in H9-T7, the unresolved
positive-characteristic form is
\[
       (-1)^{m-1}\deg_W(ww'),\qquad
       w,w'\in\ker\bigl(\kappa_*:
       \operatorname{Alg}^{m-1}(W)\longrightarrow
       H^{2m+2}(A)(m+1)\bigr).
\]
The proposition excludes transfer of 032 through an
actual dominant rational map from an abelian source.
It does not exclude a correspondence from an abelian
variety, an abelian-type motive, or domination by a
product of curves of higher genus. Those are different
conditions. In particular it neither refutes the
Hodge-standard sign nor proves it for a new fixed axis.

The role of an actual geometric transfer can be made
precise. If a finite surjective map $v:V\to W$ from a
smooth projective variety were supplied, then $v^*h_W$
would be ample, $v^*$ would inject rational cohomology
because $v_*v^*=\deg(v)$, and a primitive class would
remain primitive. The projection formula would identify
the actual primitive forms by
\[
       (-1)^{m-1}\deg_V(v^*w\,v^*w')
          =\deg(v)(-1)^{m-1}\deg_W(ww').
\]
Thus Hodge-standard positivity on $V$ for this specific
ample pullback would suffice. No such finite source is
constructed here; the proposition proves that $V$
cannot be abelian. A mere split motive, as in JAC-P16,
does not supply this intersection identity or its sign.
