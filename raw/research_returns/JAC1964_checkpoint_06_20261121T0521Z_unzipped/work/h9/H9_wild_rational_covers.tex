\subsection*{H9-T21. Wild Artin--Schreier scaling closes every generic rational-cover degree}

\begin{theorem}[H9-T21]
Let $A/k$, $n=2m\ge4$, and
$f:\widetilde A\to\mathbf P^1_t$ be as in H9-T19, with
$\infty$ a smooth pencil value.

In characteristic $p>0$, take $s$ transcendental over $k$,
$F=\overline{k(s)}$, and the rational Artin--Schreier cover
\[
        K=F(t)\subset L=F(y),\qquad
                     t=\frac{y^p-y}{s}.
                                                               \tag{H9.109}
\]
Every homologically trivial codimension-$m$ cycle is
admissible, and
\[
 h_L(z,w)=\frac1p h_K(\operatorname{cor}z,
                            \operatorname{cor}w),\qquad
 \operatorname{rad}h_L
       =\operatorname{cor}^{-1}(\operatorname{rad}h_K).
                                                               \tag{H9.110}
\]

More generally, in every characteristic and for every
integer $N\ge2$, let $T_N$ be the open parameter space of
degree-$N$ maps $\mathbf P^1\to\mathbf P^1$ unramified
above the old critical set $\Sigma$. With constants
$\Omega=\overline{k(T_N)}$, use its geometric generic map
to define $K=\Omega(t)\subset L=\Omega(y)$. Then
\[
 h_L(z,w)=\frac1N h_K(\operatorname{cor}z,
                            \operatorname{cor}w),\qquad
 \operatorname{rad}h_L
       =\operatorname{cor}^{-1}(\operatorname{rad}h_K).
                                                               \tag{H9.111}
\]
Thus the geometric generic rational cover of every degree
adds no height directions. In abelian dimension four,
accepted032 and H9-T8 make the full homologically trivial
height group positive semidefinite. In higher even
dimension the exact formula transfers the original axis
form, without asserting a new sign for that form.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\paragraph{The wild rational family is smooth over its parameter.}
Assume first that $\operatorname{char}k=p>0$. On
$S=\mathbf G_{m,s}$ the map (H9.109) is the finite flat
degree-$p$ morphism
\[
 [y_0:y_1]\longmapsto
      [y_0^p-y_0y_1^{p-1}:s y_1^p]
\]
from $\mathbf P^1_y\times S$ to
$\mathbf P^1_t\times S$. Its deck group is the constant
group $(\mathbf F_p,+)$, acting by $y\mapsto y+c$.
On the affine line its derivative is $-1/s$, so it is
\'etale at every finite value. Its only branch value is
$\infty$, where the old pencil is smooth. Hence the
pullback $\rho:\mathscr X\to S$ is smooth and proper, and its
finite map to $\widetilde A\times S$ is flat of degree
$p$. In particular it is unramified above every critical
pencil value, so H9-T12(iii) gives admissibility for all
homologically trivial generic cycles. Wild ramification
over this smooth fibre introduces no local height
correction.

\paragraph{The nontrivial deck cohomology is an actual Fourier transform.}
Write $q=|\Sigma|$, and let $V$ be the variable pencil
local system of rank $r>0$. By JAC-P07 and SGA~7~II,
XVIII~6.7.2, it is absolutely irreducible, tame, and has
one rank-one transvection at every point of $\Sigma$.
It is unramified at infinity, and $r\ge2$ because its
nondegenerate middle pairing is alternating.

Fix an invertible prime $\ell$ and a nontrivial additive
character $\psi:\mathbf F_p\to
\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell^\times$. For $a\in\mathbf F_p^\times$,
the corresponding character part of the covering direct
image on $\mathbf A^1_t$ is
$\mathcal L_\psi(a s t)$, up to inversion of the character.
Let $\mathcal V$ be the middle extension of $V$ to
$\mathbf A^1_t$. Condition~A and proper base change exclude
additional stalks at the old singular fibres, exactly as
in H9-T19. At infinity the nontrivial character is wild
with Swan conductor one and has no inertia invariants.

The outer terms of the middle-degree Leray filtration
are deck invariant, and the fixed odd ambient contribution
has $H^1(\mathbf P^1_y)=0$. Thus the same permanent
$(1,n-1)$ term calculation as in H9-T19 gives
\[
 \begin{split}
 W_{a,s}
   &=H^1\bigl(\mathbf P^1_t,
       j_*(V\otimes\mathcal L_\psi(a s t))\bigr)\\
   &=H^1_c\bigl(\mathbf A^1_t,
                    \mathcal V\otimes\mathcal L_\psi(a s t)\bigr).
 \end{split}                                                  \tag{H9.112}
\]
In the second line the extension at infinity is by zero
because its invariants vanish. At the old critical values
$\mathcal V$ already means the middle extension. These
facts justify the compact-support identification without
replacing a middle extension by extension by zero at a
transvection point.

Katz defines the naive Fourier transform by
\[
 \operatorname{NFT}_\psi(\mathcal V)
       =R^1\operatorname{pr}_{2!}
          (\operatorname{pr}_1^*\mathcal V
                         \otimes\mathcal L_\psi(tz)).
\]
Equation (H9.112), functorially in $s$, identifies the
character summand of the actual lisse sheaf
$R^n\rho_*\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ with the
pullback of $\operatorname{NFT}_\psi(\mathcal V)$ by
$s\mapsto a s$.

\paragraph{The Fourier sheaf is irreducible with infinite image.}
The sheaf $\mathcal V$ is an irreducible Fourier sheaf in
Katz's sense: it is an irreducible middle extension, and
its rank $r\ge2$ prevents it from being an additive
character sheaf $\mathcal L_\psi(bt)$. Katz,
\emph{Exponential Sums and Differential Equations},
\S7.3.5--7.3.6 and Theorem~7.3.8(3), therefore make
\[
                     W=\operatorname{NFT}_\psi(\mathcal V)
\]
an irreducible Fourier sheaf. Lemma~7.3.9(2) gives its
generic rank $q$: the old finite critical points have
drop one and Swan zero, and the original infinity has
only slope zero. Equivalently, for $s\ne0$ the
Grothendieck--Ogg--Shafarevich formula gives
$\chi(\mathbf P^1,j_*(V\otimes\mathcal L_\psi(a s t)))=-q$,
while $H^0$ and $H^2$ vanish because of the wild character
at infinity.

The local formula at the Fourier parameter $0$ is equally
precise. Katz, Corollary~7.4.3.1, gives
\[
 W(0)/W_0
      =\operatorname{FT}^{\mathrm{loc}}_\psi(\infty,0)
                                                    (V_\infty).
\]
Since $W$ is a middle extension, its stalk $W_0$ is
$W(0)^{I_0}$. Theorem~7.4.4(2)--(3) sends the trivial
rank-$r$ inertia representation to the trivial rank-$r$
representation. Thus
\[
                W(0)/W(0)^{I_0}=\mathbf1^{\oplus r}.
\]
Jordan normal form, or Theorem~7.5.4(5)--(7), now gives
\[
             W(0)=J(1,2)^{\oplus r}
                         \oplus J(1,1)^{\oplus(q-2r)}.
                                                               \tag{H9.113}
\]
There are no nontrivial tame characters or wild pieces.
In particular $q\ge2r$, and inertia has nontrivial
unipotent image. Because the coefficient field has
characteristic zero, that image is infinite. By the
finite-index-normal-core argument of H9-T19, the
irreducible sheaf $W$ has no nonzero finite-orbit vector.
The same is true after $s\mapsto a s$.

Every nontrivial deck summand in (H9.112) therefore has
no finite-orbit vector. An algebraic class on the
geometric generic fibre, defined over a finite extension
of $k(s)$, has a finite orbit. It follows that all its
nontrivial deck projections vanish. The actual Chow
projection
\[
                  Z-\frac1p Q^*Q_*Z
\]
is globally homologically trivial for every algebraic
middle cycle $Z$. The wild cyclic identity
$Q^*Q_*Z=\sum_{c\in\mathbf F_p}c^*Z$ holds on rational
Chow groups just as in the tame case: the generic fibre
product is the reduced union of the deck graphs, whose
top-dimensional cycle gives the correspondence identity.
Ramification does not add a top-dimensional component.
Admissibility and the height projection formula now
prove (H9.110), including its exact radical.

\paragraph{The same Fourier argument applies at every rational-cover step.}
The proof just given uses only the following properties
of the current pencil over its rational base: constant
$R^n$ and $R^{n-2}$; middle sheaf equal to a constant
fixed part plus the middle extension of a positive-rank
irreducible $V$; condition~A; and tame rank-one
transvections at the critical values with smooth infinity.
They persist after any finite rational base cover
unramified over the old critical values. Indeed proper
base change preserves the sheaf description and the
nonzero local vanishing cycles. The new variable system
is a pullback of the original $V$ and stays absolutely
irreducible: on the complement of the branch points the
cover is finite \'etale, so its monodromy image has finite
index in that of the original punctured base; apply
SGA~7~II, XVIII~6.7.2. Removing additional smooth points
does not change the representation's image. Thus the
Fourier calculation may be repeated on the resulting
rational base even though its total space is no longer
the original blowup.

\paragraph{A genuine cover family of every degree.}
In characteristic $p>0$, factor
$N=M p^a$ with $(M,p)=1$. If $M>1$, start with the
cyclic family $t=x_0^M+s_0$ of H9-T19, on
$S_0=\mathbf A^1-\Sigma$. If $M=1$, start with the
identity cover $t=x_0$ and $S_0=\operatorname{Spec}k$.
Then append $a$ rational covers
\[
              x_{j-1}=\frac{x_j^p-x_j}{s_j},
                \qquad s_j\in\mathbf G_m,\quad 1\le j\le a.
                                                               \tag{H9.114}
\]
Each new cover is \'etale at every finite value
and sends infinity to infinity. Therefore all old
critical values remain unramified and infinity remains
smooth. The parameter spaces are products
$S_j=S_{j-1}\times\mathbf G_m$, and the composition is
an actual degree-$N$ rational map family.

Let $\mathscr H_j$ denote its relative middle cohomology,
and let $Q_j$ be the last finite cover. Pullback and
trace give the lisse orthogonal direct sum
\[
 \mathscr H_j=Q_j^*\mathscr H_{j-1}\oplus\mathscr E_j,
      \qquad \mathscr E_j=\ker Q_{j*}.
                                                               \tag{H9.115}
\]
Restricting $\mathscr E_j$ to the geometric generic
$\mathbf G_m$ fibre over $S_{j-1}$ gives exactly the
Artin--Schreier Fourier kernel just proved, for the
pulled-back pencil. It has no finite-orbit vector.
Hence $\mathscr E_j$ has none under the full parameter
monodromy. The old composite kernel has none by
induction; pulling it back from $S_{j-1}$ preserves that
property because the projection $S_j\to S_{j-1}$ has
a section and is therefore surjective on fundamental
groups. The direct sum (H9.115) shows inductively that
the entire kernel of pushforward from the composite
cover to $\widetilde A$ has no finite-orbit vector.

This argument is on lisse sheaves over the actual tower
parameter spaces. It does not assume that arbitrary
Chow classes descend in a tower of constant fields, nor
that cycles specialize surjectively. In characteristic
zero one simply uses $a=0$, so H9-T19 supplies the same
property for every $N$.

\paragraph{Passage to the full degree-$N$ map space.}
The open $T_N\subset\operatorname{Hom}_N(\mathbf P^1,
\mathbf P^1)$ is nonempty because it contains the
composite family (H9.114). It is smooth and irreducible
as an open subset of the pairs-of-binary-forms parameter
space. The universal map is finite flat of degree $N$,
and its pullback pencil is smooth and proper, precisely
as in H9-T19a. Those assertions do not require
$N$ to be prime to the characteristic. The unramified
condition over the nonempty $\Sigma$ excludes
inseparable maps from this parameter open.

Its new middle cohomology is the genuine lisse kernel
\[
  \mathscr E_{T_N}
        =\operatorname{im}\left(1-\frac1N Q^*Q_*\right).
                                                               \tag{H9.116}
\]
Restriction to the tower family has no finite-orbit
vector by (H9.115). Therefore $\mathscr E_{T_N}$ has
none; no surjectivity from the tower's fundamental
group to that of $T_N$ is needed. An algebraic kernel
class on the geometric generic fibre is fixed after a
finite extension of $k(T_N)$ and must consequently be
zero. The actual cycle
$Z-N^{-1}Q^*Q_*Z$ is then globally homologically
trivial. The same global height argument proves
(H9.111). Corestriction is surjective over $\mathbf Q$,
so testing against all cycles proves the stated radical
equality. H9-T8 gives semipositivity in abelian dimension
four under 032.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Primary source and scope.}
The Fourier facts above were read directly in Katz's
corrected author manuscript of \emph{Exponential Sums
and Differential Equations}: \S7.3.3--7.3.6, chapter~7
pp.~10--12 (PDF pp.~210--212); Theorem~7.3.8 and
Lemma~7.3.9, pp.~12--13 (PDF pp.~212--213);
Theorem~7.4.3, Corollary~7.4.3.1 and Theorem~7.4.4,
pp.~16--18 (PDF pp.~216--218); Lemma~7.5.1 and
Theorem~7.5.4, pp.~19--22 (PDF pp.~219--222).
These give the needed local quotient by inertia
invariants and preserve its tame Jordan sizes. No
claim of full orthogonal monodromy is used.

This scaling family is not the isotrivial translation
$y^p-y+s$: its cohomological variation is the Fourier
transform with the nontrivial unipotence (H9.113).
The conclusions concern geometric generic covers in
the indicated parameter spaces. They do not assert
positivity after every specialized rational cover or
vanishing of the Chow norm kernel. The norm kernel is
proved height-radical. There is no arithmetic
Abel--Jacobi injectivity inference.
