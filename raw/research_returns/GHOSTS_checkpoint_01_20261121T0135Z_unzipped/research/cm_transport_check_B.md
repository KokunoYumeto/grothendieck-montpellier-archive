## Delicate points checked

1. **Uniform Hilbert list across characteristics.** FGA Exposé 221, Lemma 2.4 concerns reduced equidimensional subschemes in geometric fibers of the given family, not just subvarieties over one fixed field. Integral supports qualify. Boundedness gives finitely many Hilbert polynomials. The finite union of products used in B01 is therefore legitimate over the arithmetic base.
2. **Hilbert boundaries.** A fixed Hilbert polynomial may allow lower-dimensional components and nilpotents. Its leading term still fixes the degree of the top-dimensional fundamental cycle. SGA 4 1/2 *Cycle* §2.3.1 assumes flat finite type and fibers of dimension **at most** d, not that every fiber is reduced or pure. The trace construction therefore applies to the whole universal flat subscheme and gives precisely the top-dimensional class; discarded lower-dimensional components do not invalidate compatibility.
3. **Singular or nonflat parameter base.** The ambient X is smooth after arbitrary base change to the Hilbert scheme. The universal closed subscheme is flat over that scheme. The relative class construction does not require the Hilbert scheme to be smooth or flat over the arithmetic base. Thus the class difference is a genuine global section of a pulled-back lisse sheaf.
4. **Class locus.** Evaluation of a lisse section at a geometric point of a connected component is injective on global sections. Its vanishing is consequently constant on that component. The zero-class locus is a finite union of open-and-closed connected components and is proper over U.
5. **Infinitely many primes and fixed multiplier.** Properness makes the image closed, not merely constructible. Infinite closed support on the one-dimensional integral arithmetic base contains its generic point. A closed point in the nonempty generic Hilbert fiber gives cycles over a finite extension, preserving N and the separate numerator degree bounds. The rational/integral counterexample B02a is correctly separated.
6. **Single-prime gate B04.** After removing the finitely many nonflat primes of the full H_D, flat going-down supplies a generic-fiber generization of every special point. The class condition, being open-and-closed, contains this generization. This proves the same-(N,D) conclusion without identifying the class-correct components or bounding N. The exceptional integer can be chosen independently of t and N because flatness is imposed on all of H_D.
7. **Torsion construction of b_D.** Over a Dedekind domain, torsion-free is flat even for modules not finitely generated over the base. The torsion ideal is finitely generated over each finitely presented affine algebra. A finite product of annihilators kills it, and localization gives torsion-free algebras. For literal integral norms, first clear denominators of the annihilators using only units on U; equivalently use the numerator of the norm after clearing those denominators.
8. **Automatic multipliers B06.** Since t_l is nonzero on connected U and lisse, every pullback stalk is nonzero. On one connected component of H_D, equality alpha=N beta can hold for at most one N. There are finitely many components, so the possible multipliers are finite. This is valid only because the degrees count integer multiplicities in the two numerators. The zero class and rationally normalized degrees are explicitly excluded where necessary.
9. **Exact eventual constancy B03.** The finite closed threshold sets for C<C_eta establish the lower bound outside finitely many primes; specialization of one minimizing generic triple supplies the upper bound at all good primes. For all-realizations complexity, the squeeze c_l≤c_all≤C_eta is sufficient. No claim of an effective numerical value or finite exceptional list is hidden here.
