\begin{abstract}
This working paper proves the precise bounded-specialization statement underlying sketch 2.5, extracts quantitative CM-transport bounds, and computes an exact local complexity for an explicitly normalized Weil class on the product of four Eisenstein elliptic curves. The complexity of that class is 16 at every geometric good reduction, both ordinary and supersingular. For any fixed algebraic class on a smooth projective arithmetic model, the least local complexity equals the characteristic-zero minimum outside finitely many primes. A separate candidate gives an explicit family bound through Schoen's Prym construction; it remains in the unchecked part until its independent review is complete. The unspecified class and polarization in T5 do not determine a numerical minimum. Cited 2026 results are treated as the task's inputs; no claim to rebuild their formal certificates is made.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope, conventions, and status}
This is an in-progress checkpoint edition. The complete proof memos and independent checks in the accompanying research directory are part of its audit trail. Each substantive claim has an identifier; an assertion marked as a source check is not a new proof of the cited theorem. ``Checked'' means that the entire argument was read and mathematically checked in this run, at the level recorded in \texttt{CHECK\_EVENTS.tsv}; it does not mean formal verification.

Let $X$ be a smooth projective variety, $H$ an ample integral polarization, and $t$ a rational codimension-$r$ class with compatible realizations. Effective cycles always have nonnegative \emph{integer} coefficients. Put
\[
c_H(t)=\min\{\max(N,\deg_H Z^+,\deg_H Z^-):N\geq1,\quad
\cl(Z^+-Z^-)=Nt\},\qquad \deg_H Z=H^{\dim X-r}\cdot Z,
\]
with value $\infty$ if the set is empty. Degrees count multiplicities and are not divided by factorials. In finite characteristic the cycle supports are over the algebraic closure of the residue field. We distinguish equality in one prime-to-characteristic $\ell$-adic realization from equality in all compatible realizations; the explicit cycles below give the latter.

The attached sketch T5 supplies neither a numerical rational vector $t$ nor a polarization normalization for an arbitrary member of its family. Accordingly, an exact computation is meaningful only after these data are fixed. Our exact test uses $K=\Q(\omega)$, $\omega^2+\omega+1=0$, $E:y^2+y=x^3$, $A=E^4$, product principal polarization $\theta$, and mixed $K$ action $\diag(k,k,\bar k,\bar k)$. With $B=E^2$ and $\Gamma_a\subset B\times B$ the graph of scalar multiplication by $a$, set
\[
t_*=[\Gamma_1]+[\Gamma_{-1}]-[\Gamma_\omega]-[\Gamma_{-\omega}].
\]
This is an integral Weil class, with $t_*^2=24$; it is not primitive in integral cohomology. The exact value 16 refers to this class, not to every vector of the Weil plane.
