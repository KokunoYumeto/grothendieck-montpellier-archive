# Arithmetic approaches after the first checkpoint

This records attempted routes and their current limits. It is not a list of additional proved theorems.

## Exact fixed-field descent

The positive-weight strict cubic group at coefficient prime3 is finite cyclic. Its exact exponent is now m_e = v3(#K2e(O_E)) - 2(1+v3(e)) + 1, with E=Q(omega,cuberoot269). AC-G26 proves the exact correction using ramified inertia to kill the inflation-restriction transgression. AC-K12 identifies the whole even K-group as a cyclic Z3[omega]-module. These results remove qualitative finiteness, residual-generator and integral-comparison ambiguity. They leave an exponent estimate.

## Testing existing general K-theory bounds

Soulé, A Bound for the Torsion in the K-Theory of Algebraic Integers, Documenta Math. Extra Volume Kato (2003), Theorem2 printed779 and Proposition4 printed779-784, gives a very large upper bound only after excluding small coefficient primes. The source was opened at https://www.maths.tcd.ie/EMIS/journals/DMJDMV/vol-kato/soule.dm.pdf . Its Theorem3 also retains an explicit lower cutoff on coefficient primes. Thus it does not estimate the required three-primary exponent. The displayed general size is also insufficient at the prescribed large weight scale.

The current preprint Alejandro de la Torre Durán, Perfect Forms and Torsion Bounds in Algebraic K-Theory over Totally Real and CM Fields, arXiv2611.06300v2 (19 November2026), Theorem4.5 printed17 and Lemma4.3 printed16, improves the bound for the part away from primes <=b, with b=dn+1 in general, n=2m+1. Its logarithmic size bound is exponential in a quadratic expression in n. Exact scope was checked in https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.06300 . It still omits coefficient prime3, and its field hypothesis does not include the non-CM splitting field E. No full independent proof review of this preprint is claimed, and no result of the current paper depends on it.

## CM coefficient transport

The Hom of the two actual CM elliptic twists realizes the coefficient integrally. Polarized scalar twisting preserves dimension, geometric CM type and stable height; its tame conductor increase is explicit. The literal source declarations still require actual theta lattices, local Frobenius classes and a cohomology tie on the growing host. A change of logarithmic coordinate height is not automatically a bound in the additive units of VVle. AC-G25 makes the distinction exact.

The next principal-polarization route under development is a regular cyclic permutation on A^3 with product theta line. It may avoid the nonprincipal degree3 lattice of the twofold scalar construction, but it changes the coefficient to the regular representation and requires an exact integral character extraction and source normalization. It is not used as a proved supply theorem in this checkpoint.

## Routes not promoted to claims

An Iwasawa specialization description might encode the sequence of exponents, but no explicit characteristic series, controlled specialization bound, or field-uniform estimate has been established in this run. Neither a finite number of residual generators nor finiteness in every weight bounds their depth. A possible high-level K-theory regulator bound would require a proved lower regulator estimate in the actual growing family. These remain research directions, not invoked inputs.
