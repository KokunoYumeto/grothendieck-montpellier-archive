This is a focused extension of AM-DO-030 and AM-R-012. It supplies
a nonzero selected first lift whose source input has the actual
initial generic-unipotent type at the auxiliary G places. It uses
a specific finite Steinberg vector, one actual permitted29
half-translate, and a compact matrix coefficient at each G place.
It computes the finite Schwartz norm and the source mass exactly.
The new finite vector is not identified with the prescribed raw
PhiG array. No later restricted-image assertion is included.

The global construction uses the already checked common host
E'=Q(zeta28), A=J², its trace-one line, and the selected even
edge U(a)→U(b), with a=k and b=k+t+1. A valid common splitting
character with the required infinity type is assumed fixed. It
is unramified off G and29, tame at G, and has at most the
residue-level compact conductor at29 used in R012. It may have
the literal tame G restrictions constructed in LIFT18, in which
case it is unramified at29. Replacing the splitting while keeping
its infinity exponents does not change the identity kernel value
or the Heisenberg reading; its finite compact action is explicitly
accounted for below.
The local argument works over every finite residue field; the
global extension to a different host additionally requires that
host's actual mixed-theta/DO030 comparison, not just an abstract
extension of its number field.

## 1. A primitive generic vector with a known metric

Fix one odd relative split auxiliary place with residue field
κ=F_q, q prime to11·29. Put

\[
 \bar G=\mathrm{GL}_a(\kappa),\quad B=TU,\quad
 n_U=a(a-1)/2,\quad d_\tau=q^{n_U}.
 \tag{DO33.1}
\]

Choose the precise nondegenerate character ψ_U of U required by
the initial column convention. For the source this is the
character λ_X,0; it is nontrivial on every simple-root subgroup.
Let \(\mathcal F=\mathbf C[\bar G/B]\), with its delta basis
orthonormal. The standard Steinberg submodule τ is irreducible
over C, has dimension d_tau, and has a one-dimensional B-fixed
space. These are the classical results of Steinberg(1957),
Theorems1–2, pp.348–350, and Geck(2015), Theorem2.1 and
Corollary2.3, pp.3–4. They are used as published results.

Let w0 be the longest permutation, and define

\[
 v=\sum_{u\in U}\psi_U(u)^{-1}[uw_0B],\qquad
                         u_0=q^{-n_U/2}v.
 \tag{DO33.2}
\]

Then

\[
 v\in\tau,\quad\tau(u)v=\psi_U(u)v,
 \quad\|v\|^2=q^{n_U},\quad\|u_0\|=1.
 \tag{DO33.3}
\]

Here is the explicit membership and normalization check. The
Steinberg vector in the flag model is
\(e=\sum_w(-1)^{\ell(w)}[wB]\). Apply
\(\sum_{u\in U}\psi_U(u)^{-1}u\) to it. For w≠w0 there is
a simple root subgroup in U∩wBw^−1 on which ψ_U is nontrivial:
otherwise w^−1 would send every simple root to a negative root,
forcing w=w0. Summing over that subgroup cancels its entire
contribution. For w0 its U-stabilizer is trivial, so the result
is \((-1)^{\ell(w_0)}v\). This proves membership. Left
translation reindexes the sum and gives the asserted character.
The q^{n_U} flags in the longest cell are distinct, so the
displayed norm is the ordinary flag norm. No orthogonality is
being asserted for the different classical basis \(\{ue\}\).

Set, with Hermitian inner product conjugate-linear in its first
argument,

\[
 m(g)=\langle u_0,\tau(g)u_0\rangle.
 \tag{DO33.4}
\]

Then m(1)=1, |m(g)|≤1, and every m(g) belongs to
q^−n_U times the ring generated by the values of ψ_U. This
follows because g permutes the orthonormal flag basis in
(DO33.2). In particular m(g) is integral at11, and m(1) is
a unit there. Also m(gu)=ψ_U(u)m(g), giving the exact
right-generic character needed below.

## 2. An actual residue-matrix Schwartz vector

Use the DO020/022 unitary split position model
\(M_{b,a}(F_v)\), with measure giving its integral lattice
volume1. Let η be the character on F_v^× such that the common
split character is \((\eta,\eta^{-1})\) in the selected E
half. It is tame at this place. The actual source action is

\[
 \omega(g)f(X)=\eta(\det g)^{-1}|\det g|^{b/2}f(Xg).
 \tag{DO33.5}
\]

Indeed DO20.11 is the inverse pullback by the Hom map
\(X\mapsto Xg^{-1}\). On the integral compact the absolute-value
factor is1 and η depends only on the residue determinant.
The inverse-argument operator gives the same spherical vacuum
coefficient but is not used as the source action here.

Put \(X_0=\begin{bmatrix}I_a\\0\end{bmatrix}\). The orbit
\(X_0\bar G\subset M_{b,a}(\kappa)\) is free. For v'∈τ
define a locally constant function, supported on its inverse
image in \(M_{b,a}(\mathcal O_v)\), by

\[
 \mathcal E(v')(X)=
 \begin{cases}
 \eta(\det t)\langle u_0,\tau(t)v'\rangle,
                    &X\bmod\mathfrak p=X_0t,\ t\in\bar G,\\
 0,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
 \tag{DO33.6}
\]

Freeness makes this unambiguous. The actual source action satisfies

\[
                  \omega(g)\mathcal E(v')
                        =\mathcal E(\tau(\bar g)v')
                      \qquad(g\in\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathcal O_v)).
 \tag{DO33.7}
\]

To check it, evaluate at X=X0t modulo the maximal ideal.
The scalar η(det g)^−1 in(DO33.5) cancels the new factor
η(det g) in η(det(tg)); the matrix coefficient becomes
\(\langle u_0,\tau(t)\tau(g)v'\rangle\). Off the orbit,
both sides vanish. Thus the determinant correction in(DO33.6)
is fixed by the prescribed splitting; it is not an arbitrary
type twist. In the literal source situation η=χ_0^−1.

Each residue cell has additive measure q^−ab. Finite Schur
orthogonality therefore gives the exact identity

\[
 \|\mathcal E(v')\|^2=\gamma_q\|v'\|^2,
 \qquad
 \gamma_q=q^{-ab}\frac{|\mathrm{GL}_a(\kappa)|}{d_\tau}.
 \tag{DO33.8}
\]

In particular the raw choice \(f_q=\mathcal E(u_0)\) has
\(f_q(X_0)=1\), norm squared γ_q, and a pure source τ-type.
Its values are algebraic and11-integral. Its unit-norm version
\(f_q^\circ=\gamma_q^{-1/2}f_q\) is an isometric τ vector.
This normalization is algebraic after adjoining the positive
square root; its possible11-adic denominator is retained in
section5.

## 3. The exact auxiliary Heisenberg expansion

The finite model is the normalized actual vacuum model of
DO029, not an unrelated finite permutation representation.
Here support is L, period is \(\mathfrak pL\), and the
finite Fourier quotient has size q^ab. Consequently

\[
 f_q=\sum_{y\in(\mathfrak pL)^\perp/L^\perp}b_{q,y}W_y\phi_{0,v},
 \qquad
 b_{q,y}=q^{-ab}\sum_{X\in M_{b,a}(\kappa)}
                              f_q(X)\psi_v(-B(X,y)).
 \tag{DO33.9}
\]

All actual Fourier and trace-one phases are included in this
formula, as in DO29.11–13. Its parameters have denominator
at most the rational auxiliary prime when the place is of
degree one. They are supported at this auxiliary place.
The nonzero coefficients number at most q^ab, and

\[
 \sum_y|b_{q,y}|^2=\gamma_q,
 \qquad \min_{b_{q,y}\ne0}v_{11}(b_{q,y})=0.
 \tag{DO33.10}
\]

The first equality is Parseval with the vacuum-normalized
measure. For the second, every coefficient is11-integral,
since q is a unit and all character values are roots of
unity. If every coefficient had positive valuation then
Fourier inversion at X0 could not give f_q(X0)=1. This
also proves that the auxiliary operator acts nontrivially
on the vacuum, the actual hypothesis needed in DO030.

Tensor this construction over all selected G places, and
keep the vacuum at the other auxiliary places. Write

\[
 \tau_G=\bigotimes_v\tau_v,\quad
 d_G=\prod_vq_v^{a(a-1)/2},\quad
 \Gamma_G=\prod_v\gamma_{q_v},\quad
 s_G=\#\{\text{nonzero tensor Fourier coefficients}\}.
 \tag{DO33.11}
\]

The raw finite auxiliary vector has norm squared Γ_G,
its nonzero coefficients have minimum11-valuation0, and
\(s_G\le\prod_vq_v^{ab}\). All these are finite, explicit
quantities independent of the selected polynomial degree D.
The corresponding unit auxiliary vector has norm1 exactly;
its coefficient minimum is \(-\tfrac12v_{11}(\Gamma_G)\).

For the actual degree12 host, six selected relative split
places occur at each of g0,g1. Thus

\[
 \begin{split}
 d_G&=(g_0g_1)^{3a(a-1)},\\
 \Gamma_G&=(g_0g_1)^{6(-ab+a(a+1)/2)}
       \prod_{\epsilon=0}^1\prod_{i=1}^a(1-g_\epsilon^{-i})^6,\\
 s_G&\le(g_0g_1)^{6ab}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{DO33.12}
\]

For a host with f selected places above each completely
split G prime, the same local product gives
\(d_G=(g_0g_1)^{f a(a-1)/2}\),
\(\Gamma_G=(g_0g_1)^{f(-ab+a(a+1)/2)}
\prod_{\epsilon,i}(1-g_\epsilon^{-i})^f\), and
\(s_G\le(g_0g_1)^{fab}\). These are local counting
identities, conditional globally on that host's actual
theta-family construction.

## 4. A nonzero selected first kernel and a genuine source input

Fix the actual primitive selected polynomial P of degree D
at infinity, as in R012, with factorial content bound B_P
from DO30.22. Apply DO030 to the raw auxiliary vector above.
Choose

\[
 e\ge1,\quad M=29^e\ge3s_G(D+1)-1.
 \tag{DO33.13}
\]

Then one individual permitted DP59 half-orbit translate λ
has

\[
 a_\lambda\ne0,\quad v_{11}(a_\lambda)\le B_P,\qquad
 \vartheta_{r_P\mathcal O_G\phi_\lambda}(1,1)
                                      =C^{2ab}a_\lambda.
 \tag{DO33.14}
\]

This conclusion uses simultaneous jets on the actual allowed
section lattice, with the fibre units and the common period
already established in DO030. It is not inferred merely from
nonvanishing of the finite Schwartz vector. The29 operation
commutes with every G-local compact action, so the resulting
kernel still has the pure finite source type τ_G and the
selected real source type \(\sigma=\rho_0^\vee\).

At29 take the same level K_a(M) as R012. At each G place
retain the full integral compact, and let the source function
vary there according to its finite matrix coefficient. At
every other place retain the original integral compact. Let
K* denote this product. Its volume is I_a(M)^−1, with

\[
 I_a(M)=M^{6a^2}\prod_{i=1}^a(1-29^{-i})^6.
 \tag{DO33.15}
\]

The rational stabilizer at the identity class is still
trivial: it is finite by definiteness and contained in a
torsion-free principal29 congruence. No extra principal
congruence at G is necessary to define a matrix-coefficient
function on this full compact class.

Let u_inf be the unit real source vector used in R012,
let u_G=⊗u0, and put

\[
 m_\infty(k)=\langle u_\infty,\sigma(k)u_\infty\rangle,
 \qquad m_G(k_G)=\langle u_G,\tau_G(k_G)u_G\rangle.
 \tag{DO33.16}
\]

On the identity finite K* class define
\(F(k_\infty,k_f)=\overline{m_\infty(k_\infty)m_G(k_G)}\),
and set F=0 on the other classes. The trivial stabilizer
makes this an unambiguous automorphic smooth function.
It is cuspidal because the rational source group is definite.
Its source real type is ρ0 and its finite G-type is τ_G^∨.

Two applications of compact Schur orthogonality, retaining
the full compact probability measures at G, give

\[
 \boxed{
 \Theta^{\rm bil}_{r_P\mathcal O_G\phi_\lambda}(F)(1)
       =\frac{C^{2ab}a_\lambda}{I_a(M)d_\rho d_G}\ne0,
 \qquad
 \|F\|_2^2=\frac1{I_a(M)d_\rho d_G},\quad\|F\|_\infty=1.}
 \tag{DO33.17}
\]

To verify the extraction rather than just its norm, write
the kernel on the supporting class as
\(L(\sigma(k_\infty)u_\infty\otimes\tau_G(k_G)u_G)\),
where L at the original tensor is the value in(DO33.14).
Averaging each factor against its conjugated matrix
coefficient returns that tensor divided by d_rho d_G.
The support volume is I_a(M)^−1. This proves the first
identity, and averaging the squares proves the second.

For every initial source unipotent u at a G place,
(DO33.4) implies F(gu)=ψ_U(u)^−1F(g). This is the
literal adelic initial column convention: `Columns/RecLevel.lean`,
lines50–52, defines `levChr=lam ∘ levRed` and
`levTyp=levChr⁻¹`. Thus the sign agrees with the source's
initial generic-unipotent type. The present conclusion does
not assert that F meets every other decoration or column
constraint of a full `ColV` datum.

The nonzero theta image in(DO33.17) has at least one
irreducible automorphic source constituent with this finite
Steinberg K-type. Such a constituent has a nonzero Iwahori-
fixed vector because τ^B is one-dimensional. The projection
to an irreducible automorphic constituent commutes with
the compact actions; a nonzero compact irreducible subspace
remains injective in any nonzero projected copy. Hence this
is a statement about an actual source constituent, including
its automorphic occurrence, not just a local abstract type.

## 5. Norm-one formulas and arithmetic costs

Let \(S_G=\sqrt{\Gamma_G}>0\), and use the same positive
square root at the chosen complex embedding. The raw finite
Schwartz vector has norm S_G, whereas the source input F
has the norm in(DO33.17). With unit-normalized finite input
and source input, the exact value is

\[
 \Theta^{\rm bil}_{r_P\,S_G^{-1}\mathcal O_G\phi_\lambda}
      (\sqrt{I_a(M)d_\rho d_G}\,F)(1)
       =\frac{C^{2ab}a_\lambda}
                    {S_G\sqrt{I_a(M)d_\rho d_G}}.
 \tag{DO33.18}
\]

The real Schwartz norm \(\|r_P\phi_{0,\infty}\|\),
including its Gaussian covolume and hook factor, remains
R011/R012's exact quantity. If that factor is also made
unit, (DO33.18) is divided by that norm.

At11, since every q_v is a unit,

\[
 v_{11}(d_G)=0,\qquad
 v_{11}(\Gamma_G)=
      \sum_v\sum_{i=1}^a v_{11}(q_v^i-1).
 \tag{DO33.19}
\]

Thus making the finite input unit norm has an exact local
scalar cost \(\tfrac12v_{11}(\Gamma_G)\). It is not
automatically a unit merely because the G primes differ
from11. For the primitive raw input, however, the selected
coefficient bound is

\[
 v_{11}\!\left(C^{-2ab}\Theta^{\rm bil}(F)(1)\right)
       \le B_P-v_{11}(I_a(M)d_\rho).
 \tag{DO33.20}
\]

The finite Steinberg dimension introduces no11 valuation.
Its archimedean source normalization cost is the additional
\(\tfrac12\log d_G\), and the finite input normalization
has the explicit S_G factor. For fixed ranks and fixed G
primes all these are fixed numbers. Equation(DO33.13)
gives \(\log M=O(\log(D+2))\), with the potentially large
explicit coefficient s_G retained. If the auxiliary primes
or ranks vary, their displayed factors must also vary in
the cost.

This proves an initial auxiliary-type construction with
the chosen ramified splitting characters when those are used.
It supplies neither a lower content bound for every row
nor the next prescribed raw-Xi map. It also leaves the
odd global etaT problem, later integral image saturation,
Kval and the MainX terminal mass outside its scope.
