             ON DOUBLING CONSTRUCTION
             FOR REAL UNITARY DUAL PAIRS

                           Takuya KONNO and Kazuko KONNO
                               (Received 10 November 2005)



Abstract. The Howe duality correspondence for a unitary dual pair over R is
usually formulated for the determinant cover of the dual pair. For applications to
automorphic theory, one has to use the Weil representation of the unitary dual pair
(not of the coverings) instead, which is constructed by the doubling argument of
Harris et al (Theta dichotomy for unitary groups. J. Amer. Math. Soc. 9(4) (1996),
941–1004). In this paper, we calculate explicit formulae for the Fock model of this
latter representation, and determine the Howe correspondence between K-types under
this. This yields a description of the Howe duality correspondence between unitary
groups in terms of certain ε-factors.
