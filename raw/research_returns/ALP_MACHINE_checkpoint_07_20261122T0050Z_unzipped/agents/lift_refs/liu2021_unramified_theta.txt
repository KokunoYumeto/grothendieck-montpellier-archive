  (2) By Theorem 1.2, when q is odd, almost unramified representations are exactly those
      representations whose theta lifting to the non-quasi-split unitary group of the same
      (even) rank has nonzero invariants under the stabilizer of an almost self-dual lattice,
      which justifies the terminology. In fact, these representations already appeared in
      the work [LTXZZ].

a CM extension of number fields; and we consider a cuspidal automorphic representation
π of Gr (AF ) such that its archimedean component is the holomorphic discrete series of the
“minimal” weight. Under the current technique, it is necessary to assume that E/F is
everywhere unramified, which forces [F : Q] even. In particular, the product of archimedean
root numbers ε(π∞ ) is always 1. Thus, if we want to put ourselves in the arithmetic situation,
that is, the global root number ε(π) is −1, then it is necessary to have at least one finite
place v of F inert in E such that ε(πv ) = −1. The almost unramified representations studied
in this note are in some sense least ramified among all representations with root number −1.
   In the course of writing, although results in the case where  = + are essentially known
from previous literatures, we will treat the two cases in a parallel way so that readers can
see the analogy very clearly.
