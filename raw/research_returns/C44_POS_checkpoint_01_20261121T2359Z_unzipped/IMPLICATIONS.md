# What this continuation changes

This is a working register. The general axis sign, the sign for every closed special cover, and the Fermat Abel–Jacobi injection remain open. Results using 032 or Milne’s 2026 report are conditional on those unrefereed preprints. A second, separate input is retained for the full geometric-abelian-type transfer.

## Cote 44: the axis and the fifth theorem

H9-T7 supplies the exact form (-1)^(m-1) integral_B ww′ on A_B=ker kappa_*. It makes these classes primitive for h_B itself, not only for h_B squared. C44-A01 identifies the entire primitive algebraic middle space as the orthogonal sum of the inherited ambient primitive space and A_B. Under 032, weak positivity on A_B is exactly the remaining weak Hodge-standard assertion for B. No numerical/homological equivalence on B is assumed.

C44-A05 proves that every exact smooth embedded axis lifts to characteristic zero, including its ample line bundle and defining sections. This closes the geometric lifting substep of task 1(a), not the lifting of its prescribed algebraic class. No class in A_B has been proved nonliftable in this run. Nonliftable ambient classes do not solve that problem because their variable projection is zero.

C44-A02 shows that every map from B to an abelian variety kills A_B under pushforward, by the Albanese factorization. Together with the already established ambient curve-test annihilation in H9-T13, this specifies why a genuinely new correspondence is needed for task 1(b). The required comparison is with the actual signed geometric pairing. An abstract motive injection does not constitute that comparison.

C44-A03 is an exact finite potential-Tate-root vanishing test for the even axis variation. H9-T5’s odd-pencil Newton witness does not test the same object. The existence of an algebraic axis class already forces the corresponding Frobenius eigenvalue to be q^(m-1) times a root of unity. No universal witness eliminating these classes was found.

C44-A04/A04a produce nonzero axis classes on special axes in abelian products, including supersingular products, and compute their full Gram matrix. Smoothness forces the numerical bound that makes that matrix semidefinite. This is a positive test family, not a counterexample and not a theorem about every class on those axes.

These findings concern Th. 5 on scan 004 and the primitive-decomposition margin on scan 008. They leave QH9-axis open. The C44-T05 synthesis retains the local Schur corrections, their radical obstruction, the injective-specialization case, the axis term, and the admissible Albanese-primitive domain after covers. Its predecessor formulas are cited rather than proved again.

## Special covers

C44-C01 separates the old positive surface-axis subspace from the genuinely new trace kernel in H9-T18. The statement that T19–T26 kill the entire incidence kernel is too broad: the old axis classes persist under pullback. The actual predecessor results concern new classes.

C44-C02 proves generic trace-kernel vanishing on every nonextremal triple-cover splitting stratum O(e)⊕O(f), e≤f<2e. It includes the first Maroni divisor in even genus at least six. The monodromy vanishing and norm-height identity are unconditional; semipositivity uses the conditional abelian sign input. C44-C03/C04 are written boundary-stratum extensions undergoing independent whole-proof review at this checkpoint.

Every generic-stratum conclusion is over an algebraic closure of its parameter function field. It does not prove the height sign for every closed cover over Fbar_p. QH9-cover-primitivep remains open at such points.

## Fermat arithmetic and rational equivalence

C44-F01–F07 give a prime-to-5 integral projector for N, a single model over Z[1/10], and exact motivic comparison sequences. The obstruction to scalar-extended AJ injectivity has two parts: the tensor-to-completion kernel and the intersection with the Tate module of projected unramified H3. On every rational plane spanned by eta and one other class, injectivity is equivalent to bounded 5-power denominators in the specified etale-motivic group. Equivalently, it is finite generation of that group on the fixed arithmetic model. Its completion is proved to have rank one; that does not prove finite generation of the group itself.

The isolated P18 component on D×J is a rational vector space in etale motivic cohomology and is invisible to all finite-coefficient cycle maps. Its vanishing, or componentwise Murre (D), is not proved. The exact Euler-system-to-Chow gap is the unproved AJ-injectivity arrow in Lilienfeldt–Shnidman §15, equation (99) and the implication display on author pp.72–73. Their theorem does not bound the whole Chow group without this extra hypothesis. The Gross–Schoen formula is not Zhang’s separate positivity conjecture or an equality criterion.

Thus QFermat-AJ, Q35-full and the algebraic-radical question are not closed. The accepted positive height of eta, analytic simple zero and Selmer line remain intact. These reductions use no 032 input.

## Theorem W

C44-W01 replaces the archimedean Wirtinger estimate by an unconditional algebraic degree/intersection bound on abelian varieties. It uses the difference map, the numerical parallelogram identity, nef intersection products and projection formula, including inseparable degrees.

C44-W02/W03 establish the exact two-generator positive Gram matrix and detector/division estimate in characteristic p, conditional on the unrefereed 032 Hodge-standard input. The lattice is the integral numerical algebraic lattice; integrality at one auxiliary ell is not substituted for a global denominator condition. W04 supplies detector existence under the corrected rational p-divisible signature and conditional Tate input. W05 gives an explicit bad-order example showing that a naive Lie determinant signature at a prime dividing the conductor can be vacuous.

This closes QW-charp at its explicitly corrected hypotheses. The corrected statement is fully proved, including the degree bound, instead of importing an archimedean norm into an ell-adic vector space.
