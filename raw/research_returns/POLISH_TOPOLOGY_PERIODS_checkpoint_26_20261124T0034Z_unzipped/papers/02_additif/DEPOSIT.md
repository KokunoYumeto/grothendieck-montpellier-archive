## Complete current abstract

Grothendieck's Additif proposes that collar and cone choices are contractible and uses this expectation to compare categories of spaces with boundary and spaces with marked points. We identify the obstruction to forgetting these choices as a concordance space. The six-dimensional torus disproves the unparametrized assertions; strict parametrized collars and fixed-cone rigidifications are contractible. Of the eleven attachment and forgetting functors, five are equivalences and six fail. We construct a rational semialgebraic torus loop with no finite semialgebraic filling carrying its specified face witnesses over any real closed field. A countable cycle presentation computes stable concordance components and unstable components from dimension six; stabilization on components is surjective in dimension five. We also compare ordinary TOP concordances with joint PL families in low dimensions. Galvin's relative construction and the surgery formula of Hambleton, Milgram, Taylor and Williams give a surjective Casson--Sullivan homomorphism for oriented three-manifolds with finite fundamental group. This obstruction vanishes on the standard three-torus. For real projective three-space, joint odd germs identify the full component group as an abelian kernel of forgetting antipodal symmetry. An intrinsic PL partition with saturated singular loci need not have conical charts along its strata. Coarsest embedded smooth partitions also exist for arbitrary semialgebraic sets, but depend on the embedding, as the compact cusp shows. The algebraic part answers Elliott's integer-valued polynomial conjectures. For every one-dimensional analytically irreducible Noetherian local domain, its ring of integer-valued polynomials is a free module of countably infinite rank. This contradicts the three published nonflatness conjectures for this class. Elliott had already announced freeness for the cusp example in 2013; we prove the full local-domain statement and give polynomial witnesses for both singular examples. Valuation orderings, finite-conductor approximation, Milnor patching and Kaplansky's theorem carry the proof. All multivariable tensor maps are isomorphisms and the compatible plethory is unique. The one-branch hypothesis distinguishes this result from the two-branch counterexamples of Ma and Jiang, Li, Sun, Wang, Xiao and Yu. Over a Noetherian base and any coefficient extension, injectivity of the binary polynomial tensor map forces isomorphisms in every arity; we give the exact criterion with zero divisors and coefficient torsion. Outside this setting, all tensor isomorphisms can hold without flatness. Finally, compact parameter resolutions and exact relative replacement prove that jointly PL and jointly definable concordance families have the same weak homotopy type over every o-minimal real closed field. The mathematics and text were produced and checked by GPT-6 Astra (ChatGPT Pro mode), with a separate editorial reading of the freeness conclusion by Claude Opus 5.5 at high effort; no human has checked them.
