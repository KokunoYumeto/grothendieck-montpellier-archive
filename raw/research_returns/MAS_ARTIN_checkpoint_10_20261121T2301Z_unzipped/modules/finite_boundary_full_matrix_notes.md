# MA-MO-027: finite boundary full-matrix extension

## Exact result

For the actual structural-S nontrivial finite-order character common space, and for the rational principal polar completion at S={infinity}, assume that every completed centered zero lies in |Re lambda|<=a, the edge divisor is finite, and all strictly interior zeros satisfy |Re lambda|<=a-delta for one delta>0. Then every compact smooth test has an actual trace-class full common-space matrix extension provided s>2m-1 at every edge zero of multiplicity m. Both the input norm and the rational polar source sections are unchanged.

The previously checked necessary boundary obstructions MA-MO-025/026 make this an exact necessary-and-sufficient condition within that finite-boundary, uniform-margin class. It does not establish the broad no-margin full-retention sufficiency question and makes no new arithmetic zero-location assertion.

## Proof mechanism

The genuine Laurent principal parts of the reciprocal on the positive edge have right boundary values in H^(-s/2) precisely at the stated threshold. After subtracting finitely many compactly localized principal parts, the regular reciprocal has the fixed-gap polynomial derivative bounds. The original wide inverse is identified on every line b>a first; its distributional limit is then identified with the original function at a, and A j_+=1 is proved before any commutator pairing.

A separate explicit Hermite expansion proves that a compact H^(-s/2) input distribution multiplied by a two-variable Schwartz kernel defines a trace-class map on H^(s/2). The commutator is rewritten using the removable divided difference of the forward multiplier. The crossed Fourier kernel retains the dual character inverse and the actual finite phases. Its denominator has real part -2a, so it is separated from the boundary pole.

For the rational matrix the two actual cutoff moments extend by the same Sobolev distribution pairing. The original inverse still has zero total Mellin moment at 1/2. The actual source functions, their moments, the centered theta residue, and the two Gaussian polar sections give exactly the signs in MA-MO.725. No polar mode is deleted.

Both actual smooth rows agree before completion. Their trace-class extensions have range in the closed diagonal of the two one-sided Hilbert spaces, which supplies the actual sum-norm endomorphism. Two fixed source columns complete the rational matrix.

## Scope of traces

The nontrivial ideal is invariant and the quotient is the actual negative convolution. All boundary jets are retained, so the quotient trace is the full completed-zero sum. The module states the actual triangular trace/determinant identity but does not borrow the old fixed-gap ideal trace evaluation at a boundary line. The independent MO028 ideal-return route may later supply that additional evaluation after its complete proof is checked.

The rational full matrix does not thereby preserve the positive source completion; MA-MO-023 remains applicable.
