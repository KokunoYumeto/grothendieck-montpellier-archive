# MA-LA-014: coupled local quotient limit

The proof is an actual operator comparison. With D_S the finite Euler all-pass unitary and Q_Y the ordinary real Fourier cutoff, the quotient operator is M_a C_g D_S Q_Y D_S*. The annulus is exactly the difference of two translated one-sided profiles once X>=2epsilon.

The essential order is E_S=(D_S* M_Phi D_S−M_Phi) C_g, with convolution on the right. Its trace is B_S by the adjoint version of LA013. The exact finite trace difference is Tr(E_S Q_(XY))−Tr(E_S Q_(epsilonY)). Strong cutoff convergence can be paired with this fixed S1 endpoint operator, so the sufficient rate gives the prime term and the large-inner-rate branch gives zero. Combining with LA007 proves A0+B_S for all compact h with h(1)=0.

An earlier working message placed C_g on the left in this last formula; that would require an unjustified commutation with Q_Y. It was corrected immediately before writing the theorem. The module uses only the correct order throughout.

The result uses no A, M or global zero location. It closes the coupled local rational trivial-sector cutoff limit, with finite-prime terms, on the explicitly repaired quotient Hilbert space. It does not identify that quotient cutoff with the global weighted orthogonal PW projection, and does not claim compactness on the additive cover. The algebraic unitary-phase version is valid with D_S Q_Y D_S*, without asserting a full character-sector section.
