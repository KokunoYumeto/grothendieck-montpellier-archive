## Integral untwisting and effective Chern cycles

**Findings:** S-17, DER4, DER7.

**Exact placement:** SGA6, ExposéV, Appendix7.1 pp360–361 and Proposition7.5 pp362–364 (PDF scans367–371); SGA6, Exposé0, §1, equations(1.1)–(1.3), p1 (PDF scan8); FGA, Bourbaki exposé221, Lemme2.4 p255 and Théorème3.2 p260.
