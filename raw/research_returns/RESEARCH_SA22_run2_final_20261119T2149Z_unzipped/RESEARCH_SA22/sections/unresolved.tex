\begin{question}[B.3, subject 21: the remaining original-source gates]

coefficient version is refuted even for an affine henselian pair by

assertion is now also refuted by the completely constructed
nonnoetherian nonseparated pair in
Theorem~\ref{C:subject21-torsion-counterexample}. Its degree-zero
comparison holds for every \'etale sheaf of sets, while comparison fails
