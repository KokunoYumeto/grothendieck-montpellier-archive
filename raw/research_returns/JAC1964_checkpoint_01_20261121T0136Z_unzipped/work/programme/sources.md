Publisher PDF: https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/8BE754AE13330E3050F3588FCD123D87/S0027763000024855a.pdf/abelian_varieties_attached_to_cycles_of_intermediate_dimension.pdf

Theorem (3.6)(i), pp.110–111: quotient of algebraically trivial cycles by incidence equivalence is Pic^p. Section4.1, p112: Poincare p-cycle inducing k_p id. ## Conrad: purely inseparable Hom rigidity

Brian Conrad, *Abelian varieties: geometry, parameter spaces, and arithmetic*, Example4.2.14, printedp38, https://math.stanford.edu/~conrad/papers/cmchina.pdf . For a purely inseparable field extension, the natural map on Hom groups of abelian varieties is an isomorphism. Used in JAC-P00 after a finite Frobenius twist, before explicit rational Galois descent.

## ACMV

Achter–Casalaina-Martin–Vial, *Decomposition of the diagonal, intermediate Jacobians, and universal codimension-2 cycles in positive characteristic*, arXiv:2007.07470v2, 20March2025, author PDF https://www.math.colostate.edu/~achter/math/diagonal.pdf . Definition12.1, Remark12.2, Example12.5, pp49–50 distinguish raw distinguished symmetric isogeny Lambda from Theta=(-1)^(n-1)Lambda and require removal of lower Lefschetz contributions for positivity. Theorem12.12 p53 proves distinguished auto-duality under Chow-decomposition hypotheses. Conjecture5.1 p26 and §13.2 pp56–57 explicitly leave positivity in positive characteristic open outside liftable/rational cases. This theorem is not an all-varieties/all-codimensions universal representative result.
