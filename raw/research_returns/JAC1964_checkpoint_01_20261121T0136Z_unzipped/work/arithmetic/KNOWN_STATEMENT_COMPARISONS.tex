\subsection{Explicit unresolved
assertions}\label{explicit-unresolved-assertions}

The proof notes establish a split positive Picard block, exact height
orthogonality, a nonzero complementary Fermat class, and its
local-height formula. They do not establish positivity on the
complementary Chow group; do not establish the Fermat height inequality;
do not supply c₂; do not assert a full Hecke-GRH theorem from the
Dirichlet interface; and do not turn a regulator determinant into a
positive quadratic form.
