# Bounded composite Lang search and direct criterion

The search has stopped at the executed finite ranges below; no additional example was found. This is not a nonexistence result.

For any genus-two characteristic-two curve and a finite rational subgroup H=J(F_Q)[3q], where q=5 modulo8 and 2 is primitive modulo q, every nonzero theta point in H has order3, q or3q. The signed doubling group is the full unit group at each such order. The geometric primitive-zero criterion A18 therefore applies. On rank-one bases A10 excludes order3, but that exclusion is not needed for ordinary bases. The Lang-cover lower bound A12 is applicable with a=[J(F_Q):H]; in the rank-one case, positivity is certified by Q+1-2(a+1)sqrt(Q)>a. Using J[3q] can decrease the index compared with J[q] by the factor |J(F_Q)[3]|, at most81. This is a direct finite test, not an incidence theorem.

Executed scope: four rank-one F2 models (the A09 and prime-five Weil polynomials and their quadratic twists) through extension degree60; the A16 F8 model through degree20; and all784 displayed F8 Artin-Schreier forms y^2+y=a3*x^3+a1*x+b/x+c with a3,b nonzero, a1 arbitrary and c in{0,1}. The latter counts gave26 distinct Weil polynomials; each was tested over extension degrees2 through12. Earlier narrower tests are contained in these stated ranges.

The torsion lower bound used was |J(F_Q)[3]| >= 3^deg(gcd(P(T) modulo3,T^m-1)). Candidate q were detected prime divisors with multiplicity one, q=5 modulo8 and multiplicative order of2 equal q-1. Factoring used a finite effort limit, so unidentified composite cofactors were not treated as fully factored. No case had a certified composite Lang bound when the corresponding prime-only bound failed. No exact nonexistence statement about theta torsion, no exhaustive classification, and no universal failure of the method is inferred.
