## Natural Dirichlet-only route checked

A plausible shortcut is to combine recursive small-degree cyclotomic construction with BGL's nonresidue Theorem3, hoping that a primitive r-th root alone suffices efficiently for the particular r. The original BGL paper explicitly blocks this reading: **§5, paragraph immediately after Theorem(5.1), printedp20**, says its Theorem3 requires roots for all prime divisors of the cyclotomic value Phi_k(p), and the largest such prime enters the running time even when only a nonresidue for the smallest is desired. Theorem(5.1) instead needs an irreducible degree-r polynomial when r does not divide k, which is precisely the missing construction in the minimal cyclotomic field k=ord_r(p)<r. Its proof constructs a degree-r field extension; it is not a prime-field-only shortcut. I read §5's full displayed algorithm through its conclusion at printedp24 to confirm that the large-prime dependencies are actually used at steps(5.9)–(5.10).

Thus this natural route does not close the general Dirichlet-only gap.
