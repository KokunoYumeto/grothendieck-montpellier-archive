### Transfer lemma (full proof)
For any finite abelian extension K/Q, standard abelian Artin factorization (including ramified Euler factors with primitive associated Dirichlet characters) gives ζ_K(s)=∏_{χ∈Gal(K/Q)^∨} L(s,χ). The nontrivial primitive characters have entire L-functions; the trivial factor is ζ_Q(s), with no pole inside 0<Re(s)<1. Under A_D every factor is nonzero in that strip away from the central line. Their finite product is therefore nonzero there. This proves RH for ζ_K for every such K. It also covers subfields of cyclotomic fields because those subfields are abelian over Q. It does not establish RH for arbitrary nonnormal K.

Under M set χ=1 for any number field K. The finite Hecke L-function is ζ_K(s). Archimedean gamma factors are holomorphic and nonzero in 0<Re(s)<1, so a zero there of ζ_K is a zero of the completed L-function. M forces it onto Re(s)=1/2. This proves Dedekind RH for all K conditional on M. Every finite-order ray-class character of K is a finite-order Hecke character, so M also supplies their GRH. No Artin holomorphy conjecture is required for either deduction.

Under M one may derive locations of zeros/poles of arbitrary finite-image Artin L-functions via Brauer induction and Hecke L-functions, but that would NOT prove Artin holomorphy at s=1/2 or at other potential cancellation points. Our complexity transfers need only Dedekind and finite-order Hecke cases; no Artin extension is used.

## CX-R02: concrete failure of a proposed rank-one dictionary
Let K=Q(cuberoot(2)). x^3−2 is irreducible by Eisenstein at2 and its discriminant −108 is not a rational square. The normal closure L/Q has Galois group S_3, acting transitively on the three roots. Its permutation character π has π(1)=3 and π((123))=0. Every one-dimensional representation of S_3 factors through S_3/A_3≅C_2, so it has equal values at1 and(123). The same equality holds for any integral virtual sum of one-dimensional characters. Hence [Q_l[Hom_Q(K,Qbar)]] is not in the subgroup of the representation Grothendieck group generated by one-dimensional representations. For the stronger assertion allowing arbitrary rank-one G_Q characters, choose u,v in G_Q mapping to (12),(23). Their commutator maps to a 3-cycle, while every rank-one character sends the commutator to 1. Hence every virtual sum of rank-one G_Q characters has trace on this commutator equal to its dimension; the permutation representation has trace 0 there and dimension 3. Thus the finite-etale pushforward for Spec K does not split virtually as cyclotomic rank-one local systems, even when their conductors differ. This refutes the proposed “all solution-field zeta functions are products of Dirichlet L-functions by representation decomposition” argument. It does NOT prove a logical independence statement A_D ⊬ RH(ζ_K), nor exclude a future analytic reduction.
