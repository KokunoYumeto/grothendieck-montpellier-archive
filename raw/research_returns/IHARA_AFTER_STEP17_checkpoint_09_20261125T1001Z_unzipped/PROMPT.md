# The Anderson–Ihara question on single even components, after Sharifi, and the once-punctured CM elliptic curve

## Why this matters

Anderson and Ihara asked whether the outer Galois action on the pro-p fundamental group of P¹ − {0, 1, ∞} detects the
whole maximal pro-p extension Ω_p of Q(μ_p) unramified outside p (Anderson–Ihara 1988, p. 272, question (a)). Sharifi
proved this at odd regular primes from Deligne's freeness conjecture, which Brown proved. Sharifi's 2019 survey (RIMS
Kôkyûroku 2120, p. 221) still recorded the irregular case as open.

Our note hexagon:2611.00939v2 (24 Nov 2026) proves the equality at every odd prime for which the plus part X_p^+ of the
cyclotomic Iwasawa module is finite. This holds in particular at every prime satisfying Vandiver's conjecture, and
Vandiver's conjecture is verified for every odd prime below 2^31.

Vandiver's conjecture at all primes is open. Its single eigenspaces, however, are now reached at every prime through
K-theory:
- by Kurihara's theorem, K_(4m)(Z) has no p-torsion exactly when the ω^(−2m)-part of the p-class group of Q(μ_p)
  vanishes;
- Mok and Zheng (Hexagon 2611.00998, 24 Nov 2026, unrefereed) state K_(4m)(Z) = 0 for 1 ≤ m ≤ 30.

The note's theorem cannot use this, because it needs the whole of X_p^+ (its Remark rem:ktheory). A form of the theorem
that works one even component at a time would turn each such vanishing into a statement about the Galois action at
every prime, above the range of any computation.

The second question is the same method on the once-punctured elliptic curve with complex multiplication. Ishii proved
the analogue of Sharifi's theorem there. His theorem is the regular case, and one of its hypotheses is his conjectural
analogue of Deligne's freeness. The note removed regularity for P¹ − 3 points; the analogue for E − O is open.
