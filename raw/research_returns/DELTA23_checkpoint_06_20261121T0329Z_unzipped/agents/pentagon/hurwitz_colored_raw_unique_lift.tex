\subsection{A two-primary kernel forces the unique colored raw lift}

\begin{statement}{D23.P33}{Proved}{The nonzero colored raw phases are impossible for an actual element}
\end{statement}
Among the three possibilities of P32, an actual normalized
full-GT element can have only assembly $0$. In particular
its degree-$32256$ colored raw action and all sixteen
last-meridian values in P32 are uniquely determined; the
canonical kernel-$46$ value is the identity.
This excludes assemblies $86$ and $172$, but leaves the
same original degree-$1344$ normal form $(1,0)$ open.

\begin{proof}
Write $A,B,C$ for the literal raw colored Hurwitz generators,
put $x=C^2$ and $y=(ABA)^2$, and set $M=\langle x,y\rangle$.
The mixed orbit numbered $32$ in the complete colored
inventory has degree $36$. Let $T$ be its full raw preimage,
of degree $216$. Both $x$ and $y$ preserve $T$.
Their permutation image $H$ on $T$ and their image
$\overline H$ on the underlying $36$ points have orders
\[
 |H|=15116544,\qquad |\overline H|=3779136.
\]
The six-point fiber partition supplies a literal surjection
$H\twoheadrightarrow\overline H$, whose kernel therefore
has order $4$.

For each of P32's three raw permutations $W_j$, with
$j\in\{0,86,172\}$, define
\[
                 t_j=(W_jxW_j^{-1})|_T.
\]
The certificate verifies that these restrictions are all
permutations of $T$. It constructs two explicit elements
$r\in H$ satisfying $t_0=rxr^{-1}$, and verifies them
in the freshly generated group $H$. In particular $t_0\in H$.
For $j=86,172$, the literal permutation
\[
                         d_j=t_jt_0^{-1}
\]
projects to the identity on the $36$ underlying points,
but is nonidentity and has order $3$.
If $t_j$ belonged to $H$, then $d_j$ would belong to the
kernel of order $4$, contradicting Lagrange's theorem.
Thus $t_{86},t_{172}\notin H$. The certificate also checks
these two non-memberships directly, but the kernel argument
already proves them from the displayed orders and the
literal order-three discrepancies.

For an actual full-GT element, the standard braid formula
and the two-cycle give the raw covariance
\[
 W C W^{-1}=RCR^{-1},\qquad R=f(C^2,(ABA)^2)\in M.
\]
The central correction from the usual first argument to
$(ABA)^2$ has zero exponent in the commutator word, as in
P18 and P25. Squaring gives
\[
                         WxW^{-1}=RxR^{-1}.
\]
Restriction to $T$ must therefore belong to $H$.
This implication does not assume that the kernel of the
finite mixed representation is GT invariant: it follows
from the explicit evaluation $R\in M$ and the actual
Hurwitz covariance. Hence assemblies $86$ and $172$
are impossible, and P32's exhaustive list leaves only $0$.
No claim that the surviving assembly comes from an actual
element is made.
\end{proof}
