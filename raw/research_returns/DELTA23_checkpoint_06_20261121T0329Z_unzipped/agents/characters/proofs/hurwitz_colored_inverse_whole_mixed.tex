\subsection{The whole colored mixed image does not retain the harmonic marking}

\begin{statement}{D23.C32}{Proved}{Exact marked $S_4$ nonfactorization for the degree-5376 sector}
\end{statement}
In the degree-5376 colored inverse-class sector of P29,
let $A,B,C$ be the literal first three braid generators
and put $x=C^2$, $y=(ABA)^2$.
The marked homomorphism
\[
                   F_2\longrightarrow S_4,\qquad
                   (X,Y)\longmapsto((123),(1234))
\]
does not factor through $\langle x,y\rangle$.
The common marked quotient is exactly $C_2$.
This is a statement about the entire mixed image;
failure of the individual transitive components alone
would not imply it.

\begin{proof}
The two literal generators have 68 orbits on the
5376-point sector.  The certificate first verifies
that the saved orbit sets partition the entire
sector and that each saved generator pair is its
exact restriction.

It retains the orbit components numbered
\[
                           52,58,32,34,33,50,
\]
whose degrees are $160,216,36,72,36,108$, with total
degree 628.  Every discarded coordinate is certified
to factor through the retained union.
Here is the exact criterion used: for two marked
finite representations $q,q'$, if
$|\operatorname{im}(q,q')|=|\operatorname{im}q|$,
the projection to the first factor is an isomorphism,
so $\ker q\subseteq\ker q'$.  At each discarded
coordinate, the certificate applies this equality
either to the union retained at that stage or to
one already retained component.  Later additions
only shrink the retained kernel, so all these
kernel inclusions remain valid.  This proves that
the final degree-628 action is faithful for the
whole mixed image.

The exact order of that image is
\[
            |M|=182510858770346449139559710400184320000.
\]
Join its two marked generators to
$p=(123)$ and $t=(1234)$ on four new points.
The exact joined order is
\[
 |J|=2190130305244157389674716524802211840000=12|M|.
\]
Thus the kernel of $J\to M$ has order 12.
It is precisely the image of $\ker(F_2\to M)$
in $S_4$, and is a normal subgroup of $S_4$.
The order-12 normal subgroup is $A_4$, proving
that the common marked quotient is
$S_4/A_4\simeq C_2$.
In particular the marked $S_4$ itself does not
factor through $M$.
\end{proof}
