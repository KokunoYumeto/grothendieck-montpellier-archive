\subsection{Common-word compatibility of the actual-source affine tables}

\begin{statement}{D23.F41}{Proved}{The surviving source tables have ordinary commutator-word witnesses}
\end{statement}
Let $T_{i,j}$ be the degree-$1948$ target of H9, consisting
of the first-pair maximal solvable quotient and the full
three-coordinate mixed Nielsen core. Use the literal marked
affine group $E_0=\operatorname{ASL}_2(\mathbf F_3)$ and the
canonical marked kernels of C34. The following finite
compatibility assertions hold.

First, all nine phase-zero survivors of C34 admit a common
ordinary commutator word which realizes $T_{i,j}$ and all
eight last-meridian values required by C34. Second, the
target $T_{1,0}$ admits such a word simultaneously with the
specified full thirty-eight-kernel gentle extension of the
colored raw assembly numbered zero in P32. The exact full
first-pair value $F_V$ can also be imposed. These assertions
do not construct an actual profinite gentle element or an
element satisfying the unrestricted pentagon.

\begin{proof}
Write $L=F(x,y)$ for the ordinary free group. Every finite
target below is a literal marked quotient of $L$, not an
abstract group recognition. Its two generators are stored
as permutations, and all arithmetic is integral. An element
is certified to belong to a finite permutation group by an
exact stabilizer chain and sifting, with every generator
relation interpreted in the given permutation action.

The affine inventory identifies a marked kernel by the
rooted, ordered Cayley graph of its two generators. Hence
the canonical value at a kernel determines the value at
every other pair with the same kernel by a unique marked
isomorphism. This uses the subgroup generated by the pair;
it does not replace marked-kernel equality by conjugacy
under ambient automorphisms of $E_0$.

For the eight kernels
\[
             37,42,49,58,61,66,73,78
\]
append the literal cubic detector pair of C34. Their joint
affine permutation representation has degree $81$, order
$7\,558\,272$, and derived order $419\,904$. Exhausting the
thirty-two value assignments in each of the $108$ original
raw fibers leaves exactly two assignments per fiber in its
derived image. This preliminary calculation is not used
to prove C34's actual-source exclusion: C34 independently
exhausts all $3456$ assignments before this word filter.

There is a second independent check within $E_0$. Close the
eight kernels under all six gentle orientations. The seven
orbits contain twenty-four kernels. Their possible gentle
extensions, after the eight C34 identity values are fixed,
number
\[
                    4\cdot1\cdot4\cdot3\cdot4\cdot4\cdot3
                         =2304.
\]
Append both orientations of the literal cubic detector,
with their prescribed mutually inverse values. The resulting
degree-$234$ group has order
$4\,627\,325\,525\,704\,704$ and derived order
$128\,536\,820\,158\,464$. Exactly twelve of the $2304$
extensions belong to its derived image. Thus the source
tables already have full finite gentle-orbit witnesses
before any braid coordinates are appended.

For the first assertion, append the eight affine coordinates
to the degree-$1948$ group $J_0$ of H9. Call the resulting
marked image $J_8$. Its degree and order are
\[
 \begin{split}
 \deg J_8&=2020,\qquad |\ker(J_8\to J_0)|=1944,\\
 |J_8|&=1471431161109188263286355468208876736741376.
 \end{split}
                                                        \tag{D23.F41.1}
\]
All $216$ preliminary assignments are tested in this full
joint image. Exactly $108$ pass, two for each of fifty-four
raw phase cases. In particular all nine phase-zero C34
cases pass, with the eight identity values. The certificate
retains the candidate, mixed-fiber, and deck-phase labels
separately; it does not combine unrelated phases.

Here membership is already derived membership. H9 proves
\[
                         J_0^{\rm ab}\cong C_{12}^{\,2}
\]
with the two source generators as the displayed basis.
Both original generators of $J_8$ still have order twelve.
Consequently $J_8^{\rm ab}$ is a quotient of $C_{12}^{\,2}$,
while its surjection to $J_0^{\rm ab}$ forces it to equal
$C_{12}^{\,2}$. Projection therefore induces an isomorphism
on abelianizations. Each tested target projects to H9's
derived target, so every passing target belongs to $J_8'$.
Since the image of $L'$ is precisely $J_8'$, an ordinary
commutator word realizes it. This argument, rather than a
new source-exponent calculation, is the abelianization
certificate recorded for this join.

For the colored assertion, retain the entire thirty-eight
kernel extension selected for assembly zero in P32. Append
its degree-$342$ permutation representation to $J_0$.
The resulting group $J_{38}$ has
\[
\begin{split}
 \deg J_{38}&=2290,\\
 |J_{38}|&=150139938756677399007199494985035583075310131740672,\\
 |\ker(J_{38}\to J_0)|&=198359290368.
\end{split}
                                                        \tag{D23.F41.2}
\]
The target consisting of $T_{1,0}$ and the specified
assembly-zero extension belongs to $J_{38}$. The same
order-twelve argument identifies its abelianization with
$J_0^{\rm ab}$ and proves that this target belongs to
$J_{38}'$. The selected extensions for assemblies $86$
and $172$ fail this particular membership test. That
failure alone says nothing about their other $20736$
possible orbit extensions; it is not used as an actual-source
exclusion. P33's separate raw-covariance test, when invoked,
has a different logical role.

For reproducibility, the large stabilizer chains are built
from lifted strong generators of $J_0$. Each lift is evaluated
using the exact straight-line word in the original two
generators, and its old projection is checked. The original
two generators occur among these lifts, while every lift
is a word in them. Thus the generated group is exactly the
joint marked image. This is an acceleration of an exact
calculation, without a probabilistic group-identification
step. The complete finite targets, group generators, and
stabilizer states are retained by the certificates.

Finally, let $P$ be the full first-pair image and let
$N=P^{(\infty)}$ be F35's perfect residual. The projection
$P\to Q_{\rm sol}$ has kernel exactly $N$. Every additional
target used here is solvable: F33 proves this for the full
mixed Nielsen targets, and it is immediate for a finite
product of copies of the solvable group $E_0$. For the joint
image in $P\times S$, where $S$ denotes all these solvable
targets, Goursat's common quotient is solvable. Hence it
kills $N$, and the joint image contains $N\times1$.
Because $N$ is perfect, its copy lies in the derived joint
image as well. A commutator word witnessing the target can
therefore be multiplied by a commutator word in $N\times1$
to correct its full first value to the exact $F_V$, while
preserving every recorded affine and mixed value. This
proves the final assertion and completes the proof.
\end{proof}
