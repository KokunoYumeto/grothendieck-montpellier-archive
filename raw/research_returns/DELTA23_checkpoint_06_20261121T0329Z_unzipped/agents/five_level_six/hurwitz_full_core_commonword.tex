literal two generators of $T$ on $1476$ points. Joining them on

its stored cyclic triple and test membership in this full joint

of that expression modulo twelve, recursively along the exact
straight-line-program graph, without expanding the word. Both

Here is why this is an exact derived-membership certificate.

$(12a,12b)$ and its two images have order twelve, the word
\[

\]

not an assumption that arbitrary coordinatewise commutators belong

\end{proof}
