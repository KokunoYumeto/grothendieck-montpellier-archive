## 1. Bounded identity of representations

The distinction between a determinant-character identity and an
identity in the representation ring is essential and is handled
correctly. Integral Brauer induction expresses an irreducible
character as an integral combination of monomial characters.

I independently checked the quantitative integer-column argument.
For a rank-r integer column matrix, an invertible r-by-r minor B
has determinant magnitude d<=r!M^r. The target lies in the subgroup
of Z^r/BZ^r generated by the remaining columns. A shortest path
using these columns and their negatives has at most d-1 edges,
since a shortest path cannot repeat a vertex. The residual vector
has sup-norm at most dM. Cofactor bounds and Cramer's rule give
each of the r remaining coefficients magnitude at most r!M^r.
Thus the total coefficient mass is at most(r+1)r!M^r.

Projection to the chosen rows is injective on the rational column
span, so the recovered equality is an equality in all irreducible
multiplicities. Taking r<=G and M=G proves the displayed
(G+1)G!G^G bound, and the slightly larger R is valid.

The revised coefficient-field choice is sufficient: a finite
Galois field containing all G-th roots of unity contains every
subgroup's linear character. A field splitting only the ambient
group need not have had this property. This was a precision issue
identified and corrected during review.

Semisimplicity gives an isomorphism of honest modules after moving
negative terms to the other side. Applying the contravariant
multiplicity functor yields dimensions6+k and k. Every repeated
induced summand contributes at most2g. Hence the actual number of
curve slots is l=6+2k<=2gR=L. Zero-dimensional multiplicities are
omitted only after taking that functor.

## 2. Explicit group-algebra maps and contraction diagrams

For a character idempotent e, Hom_A(Ae,U)=eU by evaluation at e.
An A-linear map Ae to Af is determined by an element t of eAf.
Under that identification its contravariant action is u to tu.
This proves that every representation-induced matrix entry is a
finite coefficient-field linear combination of actual deck
transformations. No arbitrary endomorphism of a multiplicity
Hodge structure is declared algebraic.

The revised primitive idempotent matches the final rational
root-line factor. Its conjugate pair is the rational projector p.
Any other preliminary primitive choice can be transported by an
explicit fixed group-algebra matrix entry.

The cup pairing identifies each e_(H,chi) piece with the dual
e_(H,chi^-1) piece, up to a Tate twist. Taking determinants of the
plus/minus multiplicity isomorphism and contracting the minus
determinant with its conjugate gives a nonzero map to det M_V.

Expanding this map chooses six retained plus slots, pairs the
remaining k plus slots with the k minus slots, and alternates.
Group invariance moves a pair of deck transformations onto one
edge. Therefore every term is represented by exactly k+6 deck
graphs on disjoint pairs among l+6 coordinates.

Each graph has degree12g for the sum polarization, and the
external product has degree(k+6)!(12g)^(k+6). Its codimension
is(l+6)/2, so its cohomological degree shift sends H^l(C^l)
to H^6(C^6). Character projectors and permutations only change
the edge labels or ordering, not this graph count.

The full H1 Kunneth projector is one tensor-product
correspondence operation. Its terms have degree at most
l!(12g)^l. It is not necessary to charge l separate intersection
operations.

## 3. Trivial characters

For a quotient B=C/H of genus b, the b-fold product of the
pullback diagonal has nonzero alternating H1 top component by
the perfect symplectic pairing. The fibre product C times_B C
has fundamental cycle equal to the sum of the deck graphs:
the equality holds over the free locus, and all components
have pure dimension one. Ramification affects intersections
of components, not the generic multiplicities.

Thus the degree is at most b!(12gG)^b. These are external Tate
cycle factors in the returning correspondence. They are not
incorrectly modeled as finite graphs over a point.

If l0 slots are trivial, the full returning correspondence has
dimension d+l0/2 and sends source degree2d-(l-l0) to degree l.
The source degree is even. The nontrivial cyclic supplier formula
also makes it nonnegative.

## 4. The cyclic incidence geometry and bound A

For every nontrivial character, its faithful cyclic quotient has
v=2b-2+r<=2g. When r>=3, the supplier is the stated projective
bundle over F12^(r-2), of dimension d=b+r-2<=v. The r=2 and
unramified projective-space cases have the stated dimensions.

With the line convention, xi+x is very ample. The section bundle
is O(1)^b plus O(13), whose summands separate base points and
base tangent vectors. At any point a nonzero fibre coordinate
selects such a summand; fibre directions are separated by the
full fibre linear system. This gives the claimed embedding.
Both xi and x are nef and globally generated.

The incidence equations have the exact universal-section line
classes. The curve factor has degree at most Gv<=2Gg. Since
H_C has degree6g, each incidence class is bounded above by
(G+1) times the total polarization with nef difference.

The incidence is finite flat over the supplier: a nonzero section
of the positive-degree line bundle on the curve gives a relative
effective Cartier divisor of constant finite length, and the
independent coordinate fibre products preserve finite flatness.
Consequently the v equations form the expected complete
intersection, with no excess-dimensional components.

The added component argument is correct. The linear systems are
basepoint-free; their general divisors are reduced and avoid the
finitely many branch points of the original C-to-B quotient.
The supplier dominates the linear system. Over the resulting
dense open, the incidence is finite etale. Distinct projected
zeros and a specified residual cyclic label define an open-and-
closed subset. Its component closures are exactly the needed
returning cycles. A resolution pushes to their fundamental
cycles, so no degree of an unspecified resolution is needed.

The projective-bundle relation gives

deg(Y) = 12 sum_(a=0)^(r-2) binom(d,a)12^(r-2-a)
       <= 12*13^d.

For a product of nontrivial suppliers and its returning incidence,
a sufficient bound is

(2L)! [936g(G+1)]^L.

If the trivial factors have total b=l0/2, their external product
has degree at most b!(12gG)^b. Forming the product with the

## 5. Effective Hodge generators and bound S

The projective-bundle formula is an isomorphism of rational Hodge
structures through algebraic maps. The proved Fermat bound therefore
supplies a finite set of effective integral generators on every base.
Flat pullback and general globally generated xi divisors give actual
effective integral cycles on the bundle, possibly zero.

For a generator xi^j pi^*W, one has j_i<=b_i. If n_i copies of xi_i
are chosen in the degree expansion, a surviving pushforward uses
a_i=j_i+n_i-b_i>=0 and contributes12^a_i. Since a_i<=n_i,
it is bounded by assigning weight12 to each selected xi_i and
weight1 to each x_i. The total coefficient sum is at most
(13k)^q, where q is the generator dimension. Here q<=d<=L
and k<=L.

Every remaining x-monomial has total degree dim W. Its intersection
with the effective W is nonnegative and no greater than the degree
for(sum x_i)^(dim W), by the positive multinomial expansion.
Projective-space factors and the point case satisfy the same bound.
Consequently

S=[26(L+1)]^L H12(L)

bounds every resulting generator in all codimensions. The monotonicity
of H12 used here follows immediately from its explicit positive
product formula.

## 6. Rational Hodge preimages and coefficient descent

This was the other important clarification requested during the
review. An isolated coefficient-field Hodge vector should not
simply be assumed to be spanned by rational Hodge cycles.

The revised proof instead expands every coefficient-field
correspondence into its integral algebraic terms and takes I to be
the sum of their rational cohomological images, with the necessary
Tate shifts. This is a rational Hodge substructure.

After extending scalars, I contains the supplied determinant line.
Its rationality forces containment of every coefficient-field
Galois conjugate. The natural representation has character field
Q(i); the conjugate pair spans the rational Weil plane. Thus I
contains the rational Weil plane itself, not merely a complex line.

A direct sum of the source cohomologies maps rationally onto I.
Semisimplicity of polarizable rational Hodge structures gives a
rational Hodge preimage of a nonzero rational Weil class. The finite
effective generators on the sources span this preimage. This
justifies the subsequent component selection without taking any
root of a cycle class or assuming an integral saturation statement.

A literal sum or trace of conjugate maps could cancel. The revised
image-span/direct-sum formulation avoids that problem.

## 7. Four moving operations and effective component selection

The four grouped operations are sufficient:

1. the returning cyclic/Tate correspondence;
2. the full tensor H1 Kunneth projector;
3. the complete deck-diagram determinant contraction;
4. a final coordinatewise primitive projector.

All character projectors, intertwiners and alternations are already
expanded into operation3. The fourth is harmless if redundant.

For W of degree E on X, its product with X' has degree at most AE:
the Segre binomial factor increases with dim X, and deg X>=1.
Truong's safe moving factor, followed by the correspondence
intersection and projection, gives each effective part degree at
most2(2L+1)A^(2L+3)E<=BE.

Projection does not increase degree, because the target hyperplane
pullback is nef and bounded by the total product hyperplane.
Components contracted in dimension have zero proper pushforward.

At each stage retain both effective parts as generators. Their
rational span contains the desired image. After all operations and
the fixed final map to the sixfold, some effective generator must
have nonzero Weil projection, since the span does. One of its
irreducible components still detects, with no greater degree.
Individual intermediate terms need not remain in every projector
image. This point is handled correctly.

The resulting C^6 degree is at most B^4 S and hence at most
B^(4L+21) S. The conservative exponent contains no hidden
unbounded sequence of tensor operations.
