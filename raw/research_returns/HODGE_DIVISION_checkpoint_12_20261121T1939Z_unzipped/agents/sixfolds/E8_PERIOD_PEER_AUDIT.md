## D-E8R-04: algebraic Hodge spaces and resonant frames

On the finite monodromy cover, a fundamental matrix and its inverse
are single-valued with moderate growth, hence meromorphic. The
equivariant-vector-form construction and its converse therefore give
the stated rational descriptions of the two Hodge multiplicity
spaces. There is no assumption that a numerical solution is itself
algebraic before the finite-monodromy proof.

The holomorphic Euler frames exist. The published Levelt form has a
commuting semisimple/unipotent monodromy decomposition. Finite
monodromy kills the unipotent part, and the nilpotent and positive
resonance coefficients occur in disjoint positions. Equivalently,
before pullback each residue spectrum lies in an interval of length
less than one, so a nonresonant holomorphic Euler frame exists and
pulls back. The repeated -1 eigenvalue causes no problem: finite
monodromy removes its nilpotent residue part.

The chosen normalization of every positive resonant coefficient to
zero is valid. Right multiplication by a constant unipotent matrix
changes the analytic frame by H(t)t^Lambda C t^(-Lambda). A coefficient
with Lambda_i-Lambda_j=m changes the resonant coefficient first at
degree m. Increasing m gives the asserted triangular normalization.
Thus exact zero resonant right sides follow geometrically. Their
interval containment of zero is correctly treated as a consistency
check.

The floor conditions are also exact: after t-p=y^e, a component has
order e*ord(f)-e*lambda+e-1. Nonnegativity gives ord(f)>=floor(lambda).
The dual condition substitutes -lambda. At the regular point infinity,
the dt pole gives order at least two.
