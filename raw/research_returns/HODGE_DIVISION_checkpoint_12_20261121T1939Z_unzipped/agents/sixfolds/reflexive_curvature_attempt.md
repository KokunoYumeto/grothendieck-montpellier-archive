# Attempted extension of SF-E811 to reflexive sheaves: exact unresolved step

Author: GPT-6 Astra Pro; inherited effort not exposed; HODGE_DIVISION /root/sixfolds. Date: 2026-11-21.

This is an attempted route, not a theorem extending SF-E811 or SF-E813.

The original published Bando--Siu paper, *Stable sheaves and Einstein-Hermitian metrics*, in *Geometry and Analysis on Complex Manifolds*, World Scientific1994, pp39--50, is available from the author's page https://people.math.harvard.edu/~siu/bando_joint_paper/index.html . All twelve source images were actually downloaded and OCRed under references_cache/bando1..12; these copyrighted source caches are excluded from the deliverable ZIP. The following exact passages were inspected: Theorem2 pp39--40 (admissible extension/removal), Theorem3 and Corollary3 p40 (admissible Hermitian--Einstein existence and Bogomolov equality), Lemma6 pp47--48, equation(4) p48, and the ensuing proof of Corollary3 pp48--49. Not the complete twelve-page proof was independently audited.

The limitation is substantive. Equation(4) gives an inequality between the topological discriminant paired with the ordinary power of the Kähler class and the curvature-energy integral. The printed proof does not by itself give the full rational Chern--Weil cohomology identity needed to pair the curvature expression with the particular mixed form theta_1 theta_2 in SF-E811. Therefore one cannot replace a stable bundle by a stable reflexive sheaf and repeat the mixed-energy proof without further work.

A second issue is geometric. The universal cover of the locally free locus is punctured by the lifted singular set, and is not automatically the full product V_1 times V_2 used in the labelled holonomy-algebra argument. Orthogonal parallel algebra projections, if first constructed globally on that locus, would extend as idempotent endomorphisms of reflexive sheaves by Hartogs, but their construction and their exact degree-four Chern classes still require a proof in this setting.

The useful source facts are retained as published inputs: equality in the ordinary Bogomolov inequality for a polystable reflexive sheaf forces a locally free projectively flat object, and an admissible Einstein-Hermitian metric extends smoothly wherever the reflexive sheaf is locally free. These facts do not by themselves establish the required mixed equality implication.

Parent explicitly requested that this route remain frozen as an exact limitation, while the concrete surviving Ext2 cone is developed. No reflexive, singular-factor, or all-semistable extension has been entered as a proved result.
