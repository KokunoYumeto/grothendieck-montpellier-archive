## Source errors identified, with explicit refinements

1. Theorem6.1 writes `d^1-2` where the product and its later table require `d^2-2`.
2. Theorem6.1 claims unrestricted g_k>g_(k-2) for k<=d^2/2. At k=2, g_2=0<1=g_0, for every d>=2. For d>=7 the theorem's own assumed range is included. This is refuted as F-007, and the needed support assertion is independently proved as F-006 by subset sums.
3. The general padding notation rho(N)=(N−|rho|,rho) is a partition only if N−|rho|>=rho_1. The source's prose `N>=rho_1` is insufficient. Every use in this worker imposes the actual condition (automatically satisfied in the positivity ranges). Do not reproduce the weak condition.
4. In the source's rectangular semigroup cone prose the nonnegativity x_(d^2)>=0 is tacit but necessary. A cone of nonnegative partitions cannot contain arbitrary all-negative weakly decreasing tuples.
5. Source proof of Proposition5.2 contains typographical `m`/`m^2` and d/m inconsistencies; use the exact theorem statement only unless independently verifying those finite base cases.

## Dictionary details to preserve

- Signed exterior powers of permutation modules are not the unsigned subset functor on finite sets.
- `g=dim kernel` is a positive dimension formula, but it does not give a positive effective Burnside class or a combinatorial basis of irreducibles.
- `g=p_r−p_(r−1)` as primitive Grassmannian cohomology is a dimension equality. No natural isomorphism with the multiplicity space has been constructed here.
- The two-row algorithm is polynomial in the integer N (unary size), not asserted polynomial in binary input length.
- All semisimplicity and Reynolds-projector arguments take place over Q or another characteristic-zero field. Integral representations and finite schemes spread the constructions over Z, but do not make integral semisimplicity true.
- For the Grassmannian, split Schubert cells show Tate cohomology and purity directly. Hard Lefschetz supplies the primitive quotient dimension. This does not use Alpöge, RH, GRH, or unproved cancellation claims.
- Exact SGA/EGA/FGA paragraph locators remain assigned to the archive/root worker; no locator should be fabricated from this note.
