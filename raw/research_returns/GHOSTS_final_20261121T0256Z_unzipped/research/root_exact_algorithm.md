# R40–R41: exact finite intersection search for local complexity

Root derivation, 21 November 2026. DRAFT pending independent full check.

Input model: a smooth projective abelian fourfold X over a finite field F_q, an explicit ample integral polarization H, a positive integer C known to bound c_H(t), and an actual rational cycle t=cl((Z+−Z−)/n) with explicit equations and positive integer n. The target may be obtained by specialization of a characteristic-zero cycle; its being merely named as an abstract flat class is not an executable input. Also require explicit presentations of the complete bounded-degree parameter scheme H_C from B01, including all multiplicity types, so no hidden computation of its degree bound is asserted.

R40. The exact minimum c_H(t) is computable by a finite procedure using these data and the cited Tate theorem for finite-field abelian varieties. The parameter-space construction is a finite preparatory input; the theorem does not estimate its computational size.

1. Compute the Frobenius polynomial of H^1 from point counts on X, and the eigenvalues of H^4(2) by the exterior-power rule. Determine which eigenvalues are roots of unity, with multiplicity. Let r be their total multiplicity. These algebraic-number operations are decidable, and r is the dimension of the geometric Tate subspace V. Frobenius on H^1 of a finite-field abelian variety is semisimple, hence so on H^4. Poincare pairing is nondegenerate on V: an eigenvalue pairs only with its inverse, and roots of unity and nonroots cannot pair.
2. Enumerate effective integral codimension-two cycles over finite extensions of F_q. Compute their rational intersection matrix and retain cycles beta_1,...,beta_r when the r by r Gram determinant is nonzero. This search terminates: Tate says their Q_l classes span V, and the restricted pairing is nondegenerate. The beta_i are a basis of V in the chosen l-adic realization. Enumeration can range over all homogeneous ideals over all finite fields; irreducible reduced supports and their degrees are computable by finite-field algebra.
3. Compute the finitely many geometric connected components of H_C. Choose a closed point on each; over the algebraic closure of F_q every closed point is defined over a finite extension. The relative cycle class on a connected component is constant because X is fixed. Record its degree and the vector (deg Z.beta_i)_i. This vector is a complete invariant of its class in V. Include the empty cycle of degree zero.
4. The target vector is rational and computable from the explicit rational cycle: v(t)_i=(deg Z+.beta_i−deg Z−.beta_i)/n. For each N=1,...,C and each pair of component labels with degrees at most C, test v(Z+)−v(Z−)=N v(t), using exact rational arithmetic. Minimize max(N,deg Z+,deg Z−). The upper-bound hypothesis guarantees at least one successful test. Exhaustiveness of H_C and nondegeneracy of the Gram matrix prove that the minimum is exactly c_H(t), not just an upper bound.

Intersection computability does not require proper intersections: on smooth X the coherent sheaves O_Z and O_beta are perfect, and Hirzebruch–Riemann–Roch gives
  deg([Z].[beta]) = chi(X,O_Z tensor^L O_beta)
when both have codimension two on a fourfold. Their Chern characters start in codimension two with the fundamental cycle, so higher terms and the positive-degree Todd terms cannot contribute in dimension four. Finite free resolutions and Hilbert polynomials compute this Euler characteristic. This is a computational form of the usual intersection pairing, not a numerical approximation to a period.

One chosen prime-to-characteristic realization is enough for this statement. Under the supplied identification of numerical with homological equivalence for every realization in use, the same test also certifies simultaneous equality. The target is an actual rational cycle, so this invokes no rationality oracle for arbitrary l-adic Tate vectors.

R41. For a fixed arithmetic fourfold, suppose an actual generic rational cycle representing t and its exact generic minimum C0 have been computed, together with B04's integer b_(C0−1). Outside the excluded rational characteristics and prime divisors of b_(C0−1), B03/B04 give c_v(t_v)=C0. Only finitely many places remain; specialize the known generic cycle to obtain the rational target input at each, apply R40 with C=C0, and obtain the entire function v->c_v. If C0=1, every minimum is1 and no b_0 is needed. This conditional procedure has a finite arithmetic output, but it does not silently assume the generic cycle or the Hilbert schemes are already explicit in T5.

Further possible refinement: explicit Betti rational vector + equations over a number field might allow computation of generic C0 and a global representative via algebraic triangulations of representatives on the finitely many bounded Hilbert components. This step needs a precise input model and an exact source for the topology algorithms before it can be claimed; do not include it as proved yet.
