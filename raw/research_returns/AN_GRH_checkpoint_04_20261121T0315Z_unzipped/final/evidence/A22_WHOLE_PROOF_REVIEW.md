The avoidance is geometric and covers all exponents and mixed orders supported on S. The proof begins inside any prescribed nonempty moduli open, so its Zariski-density conclusion follows. The empty S case is vacuous apart from choosing a finite-field closed point.

Pullback to the finite etale scheme J[N] minus its zero section, then finite projection, makes the bad N-torsion-incidence locus closed. A21 says its generic fibre is empty. Removing it is one finite avoidance condition; it removes mixed squarefree N-torsion, not only separate prime torsion. No infinite intersection of open sets is used.

## The Frobenius realization

In basis e1,e2,f1,f2 the diagonal matrix with entries u,u,u^-1,u^-1, where u=1+ell modulo ell^2, is symplectic. It has exact order ell, is the identity modulo ell, and its first nonzero difference quotient is diag(1,1,-1,-1), invertible even at ell=3. CRT gives a matrix gamma of order N.

The descent action v maps to F(v)gamma^-1 is semilinear and well defined. The deck automorphism is defined over the finite base field, so it commutes with arithmetic Frobenius. Its Nth iterate is the original descent action. This supplies a finite Galois descent datum over the extension of degree N. The torsor is affine, being finite over the affine base, so the descent is effective. The projection to the original base descends because the additional automorphism is vertical. A twist need not retain the original constant right-G torsor structure; the argument needs only the descended variety and its projection, which it has.

The twist remains geometrically integral. Lang–Weil gives points over every sufficiently large extension degree, so one may choose m=1 modulo N. On the corresponding geometric frame, the fixed-point equation is F^m(v)gamma^-m=v, equivalently F^m(v)=vgamma. This is the required Frobenius matrix over the stated field F_(q0^m). It is unnecessary for the image point to have minimal residue degree m.

## Exact rational exponent and geometric exclusion

The finite matrix congruence implies Phi-I=ell*A_ell with A_ell invertible over Zell. On the divisible torsion module (Qell/Zell)^4 its kernel is exactly the ell-torsion. Thus all J[ell] is rational, but no rational element has higher ell-power order. Combining distinct primary components gives the exact equality J(F_Q){S}=J[N], including mixed orders.

A19 applies with d=1 because Phi fixes every J[ell]. It places every geometric S-primary theta point inside the rational group just described. The finite avoidance open excludes every nonzero point of this group. This proves the full all-exponent, all-product statement. It does not prove or disprove an incidence assertion where the prime support may depend on the curve.
