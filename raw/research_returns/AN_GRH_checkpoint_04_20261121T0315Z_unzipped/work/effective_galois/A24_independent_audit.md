## Unconditional argument checked

The A19 genus-two translate-intersection bound gives the stated ordered-difference inequality in any finite additive subgroup. The characteristic-two Raynaud line satisfies B squared = canonical, so degree-one Riemann–Roch makes its actual theta support symmetric. This is the canonical theta support, not an arbitrary translated theta divisor.

The exact quadratic relation for four-power Frobenius follows from the checked A13 polynomial of two-power Frobenius by Cayley–Hamilton. It is not an inference from a squared characteristic polynomial alone. At an inert prime, the quadratic order is the unramified quadratic discrete valuation ring; the Tate module is torsion free and hence free of rank two over it. An exact-order prime-power point has a unit coordinate, making its generated local-ring line injective, of cardinality ell^(2r).

The actual signed-unit hypothesis at level ell^r gives order(4) = phi(ell^r)/2. The norm therefore forces the Frobenius order to grow by exactly ell^(r-1) from the residue level. At the residue level, the signed Frobenius subgroup has norm kernel of even order; kernel order two would put it entirely inside the scalar multiplicative group, contradicting irreducibility. Its order is consequently at least 2(ell-1). The unique involution in the odd local unit ring shows that adjoining -1 changes both levels by the same factor. The full signed orbit therefore has at least 2(ell-1)ell^(r-1) points, violating the difference bound for every inert eligible prime and every actual eligible exponent. The proof requires neither a uniform non-Wieferich statement nor finite small-prime checks.

The split residue classes 1,2,4,8 modulo 15, the ramified exceptions 3 and 5, and persistence of the 4159 witness are consistent with the conclusion. The final paragraph correctly declines to infer theta incidence from a positive-density arithmetic candidate set.
