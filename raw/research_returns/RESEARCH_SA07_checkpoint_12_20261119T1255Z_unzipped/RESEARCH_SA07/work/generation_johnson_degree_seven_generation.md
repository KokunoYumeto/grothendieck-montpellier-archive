# Exact generation statement for the R(J7) audit

19 November 2026. Independent primary-source recheck, requested by period_triangle. This note proves only the generation map used to test R; it does not replace the separate R-contraction calculation.

Let g>=3 and use a surface with one boundary circle fixed pointwise, equivalently one marked point with a nonzero tangent. Let t_(g,1) be the Malcev Lie algebra of the Torelli group and p_(g,1) the Malcev Lie algebra of the punctured surface group. Put w0=log of the actual boundary loop.

Hain1997, Theorem3.4 p605, gives the surjection of Torelli Malcev completion onto the unipotent radical of relative completion, with its central one-dimensional kernel. Proposition4.6 p610 makes H1 of that unipotent Lie algebra pure of weight-1. Lemma4.7 and Corollary4.8 pp610--611 identify the weight filtration with its lower central series. Theorem4.10 p611 makes the same identification on t_(g,1) for g>=3, and identifies the central line with Q(1). Therefore that central line lies in W_-2=L²; it is not an additional degree-two generator of the associated graded algebra.

The actual boundary action is explicitly constructed in Hain §14.4 p643:

    t_(g,1) -> Der(p_(g,1),w0).

The page explicitly states that the action and quotient maps in its final square are morphisms of mixed Hodge structures, and uses their strictness for the weight filtration. At each finite nilpotent level, strictness gives

    gr_W(image of the action) = image(gr_W of the action).

For the once-punctured surface, degree-one homology is pure of weight-1 and gr_W p_(g,1) is the free Lie algebra on V. Thus the target associated graded action is the special-derivation action used in F.3.24. The map from the relative completion gives the actual algebraic geometric action image; this is not a claim that an individual discrete filtration kernel is dense in an arbitrarily chosen algebraic kernel.

Since W=LCS on t_(g,1), its graded Lie algebra is generated in degree one. Its action image is therefore generated by the image of degree one. Johnson's map identifies that image, as a symplectic representation, with Lambda³V (with the usual Tate degree convention in Hodge notation). It follows that J7 is spanned by length-seven Lie words in these actual degree-one derivations. Jacobi expresses every such word as a linear combination of left-normed words, so a contraction calculation on those words suffices to test all of J7.

The original Enomoto--Satoh2014 paper gives this precise image-level statement directly in Theorem3.5, printed p640: the Lie subalgebra Im(tau_Q) is generated by its degree-one part Lambda³H_Q. Proposition3.6 on the same page identifies the rational Johnson-filtration image and Torelli lower-central-series image in each degree. I reopened and read both statements and the adjacent diagram. They are the exact source formulation required here; no quadratic presentation or stable-range assertion is needed for generation.

Stabilizing the surface by adding handles extends an actual boundary-fixed mapping class by the identity. Its action extends a derivation by zero on the added generators, and this respects Lie brackets. Therefore a universal contraction identity on these length-seven degree-one words transfers through the actual stabilization maps. This is the only stabilization used in the R audit.
