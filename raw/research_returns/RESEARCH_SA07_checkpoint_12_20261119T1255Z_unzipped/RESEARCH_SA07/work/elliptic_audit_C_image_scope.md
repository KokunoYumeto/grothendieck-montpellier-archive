## Verdict

Both statements pass in their actual stated categories. I found no gap
requiring withdrawal of either exclusion. In particular, C.1.4 excludes
its specified pair from the **abelian-motive** image; it does not claim
exclusion from every geometric motive. C.1.5 proves the stronger
exclusion for its different, noncomputable parameter, and states the
algebraic-correspondence hypothesis explicitly.

## C.1.4

For a>0 and v=(1,ia), the displayed C_a sends v to iv, which is exactly
i^(-3) v in the convention h(z)|H^(p,q)=z^(-p)bar(z)^(-q). Multiplication
of the displayed matrices gives EC_a=diag(a,a^-1)>0, f^tEf=-E and
fC_af=C_a^-1. The two Hodge lines are conjugate and complementary,
and f exchanges them. Thus the rational polarization and anti-Hodge
involution have the required maps to the weight-three Tate line.

For a=cuberoot2, a rational matrix preserves the first line exactly when
t+(u-r)tau-s tau²=0. Its imaginary part gives u=r; its real part then
uses the irrationality of tau²=-cuberoot4 to give s=t=0. This already
proves End_HS(V)=Q; the stronger degree-six assertion also follows
because Q(tau²)=Q(cuberoot2) has degree3 and tau is not in that real
field. A commutative Mumford–Tate group's Lie algebra would centralize
its Hodge homomorphism and therefore lie in End_HS(V). But the weight
scaling and nonconstant Hodge circle have independent real tangent
directions, so that Lie algebra has dimension at least2. This proves
the non-CM assertion directly.

I independently reopened Totaro's complete19-page author PDF,
https://www.math.ucla.edu/~totaro/papers/public_html/structure.pdf.
Printedp18, Theorem4.1 and Corollary4.2, with their proof, state exactly
the exclusion for non-CM polarizable Q-Hodge structures of weightw≥2
and Hodge numbers(n,0,...,0,n) from the tensor category generated by
curves. The actual forgetting-f composite from C.1.1 has image in that
category: Artin factors become trivial (0,0) objects, and abelian
cohomology is generated by H¹. This proves the stated pair exclusion
through a specified functor. No assertion of nongeometricity in the
larger category is needed or supplied by C.1.4.

## C.1.5

The machine enumeration is effective and universal; the diagonal
machine in the proof is another machine in that same fixed model.
The digit decoding works because the normalized n-th tail lies in
[0,1/3] or[1,4/3], with a gap2/3. An approximation of error<1/6
separates them. Thus a_H is not computable and lies in[1,4/3], giving
the positive parameter required by the preceding polarization check.
No finite calculation of the undecidable digits is claimed.

The critical geometric map is the following. A finite list of algebraic
varieties and correspondence/projector data overC descends to a finite
type model overQbar after recording its finitely many coefficients.
Shrinking the base provides smooth proper families and the relative
cycles. Their cohomological actions are horizontal. A projector
relation true in one fibre holds on the connected local system by
parallel transport; its image is therefore a rational variation of
Hodge structures, with constant Hodge numbers. This suffices also for
a homological projector, without claiming an effective algorithm for
finding its original presentation.

For the asserted rank-two image, F³=F²=F¹. Griffiths transversality
therefore makes F³ horizontal. The conjugate line is horizontal as
well, and transport along a path is a rational Hodge isomorphism.
Every analytic component of a smooth Qbar-variety contains Qbar-points:
an etale coordinate chart is locally biholomorphic, and a Qbar point
in its sufficiently small target neighborhood has only Qbar points in
its etale fibre, so its nearby inverse image is Qbar. This proves the
actual transport to an algebraic fibre, not a universal assertion that
all arbitrary complex coefficients are computable. The gap in the
Hodge numbers is essential to this step.

At that algebraic fibre, de Rham cohomology, its projector and its
filtration admit algebraic bases. Rational Betti bases have period
comparison matrices. I independently reopened the complete Yoshinaga
primary PDF, https://arxiv.org/pdf/0805.0349, whose displayed header
saysv1/3May2008 and internal date26November2024. Theorem18, printedp11,
makes real periods elementary and therefore computable. Real and
imaginary parts of the comparison entries have this property; twists
add integer powers of2pi i, and matrix inversion only uses the field
operations on computable complex numbers. A nonzero denominator can
be bounded away from0 by increasing the approximation precision, so
no undecidable equality test is being assumed. Nonzero minors and
ratios describing the rank-one filtration are computable by the same
argument.

An isomorphism produced by rational parallel transport changes the
line ratio by an invertible rational fractional-linear map. Its inverse
recovers the original ratio ia_H as a computable complex number,
contradicting the digit argument. Finally any pure motive overR in the
stated algebraic-correspondence category has a complex base change
with that same underlying Hodge structure. This exact basechange
functor transfers the contradiction toR. The proof expressly excludes
substitution of arbitrary Hodge projectors overC, and its conclusion
does not make that substitution.
