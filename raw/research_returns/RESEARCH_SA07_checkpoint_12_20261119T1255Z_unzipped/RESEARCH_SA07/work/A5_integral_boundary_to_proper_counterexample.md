# An actual integral boundary-moving automorphism on the A5 cover

Work proof, 19 November 2026. This is a counterexample to inferring a map on compactified homology from a generic profinite lift preserving one affine integral homology lattice. It is not asserted to be a Galois automorphism or a simultaneous transporter on all covers.

## 1. The actual cover and its two homology lattices

Let U=P1 minus {0,1,infinity}. Fix geometric generators X,Y around 0 and 1, and define the quotient

`F2 -> H=A5`,  `X -> x=(123)`,  `Y -> y=(12345)`.

Permutations are composed as functions, with `(ab)(i)=a(b(i))`. The group generated by x,y has order 60. Its third inertia generator is `z=(xy)^(-1)`, of order 5; x has order 3 and y has order 5. The corresponding connected finite etale cover Y of U over Qbar is an actual Galois cover, by the fundamental-group correspondence. Its smooth proper compactification C maps to P1 with these inertia orders. Fix an embedding into C to identify the topological and etale covers.

There are 20 points over 0, 12 over 1 and 12 over infinity. Thus the boundary D has 44 points. Riemann--Hurwitz gives

`2g(C)-2 = 60(-2+(1-1/3)+(1-1/5)+(1-1/5)) = 16`,

so g(C)=9. Put L=H1(Y(C),Z), let P be the subgroup generated by the oriented small loops around D, and put W=H1(C(C),Z). The exact sequence of the pair, with the local orientation maps, is

`0 -> Z -> Z[D] -> L -> W -> 0`,

where 1 maps to the sum of all 44 boundary points. Consequently L has rank 61, P has rank 43, W has rank 18, and P is saturated because W is free. All maps are H-equivariant.

This is the A5 monodromy in Bleher--Chinburg--Lubotzky v3, Example 5.3, printed p. 13. Their Theorem 5.1, pp. 12--13, detects a failure of descent using a shared irreducible constituent. The following construction gives a specific integral automorphism and its actual profinite lift.

## 2. A completely specified integral Schreier module

The cover of a two-loop spine of U is the Cayley graph with vertices h in H and directed edges `(h,X):h->hx` and `(h,Y):h->hy`. Its chain complex is

`0 -> L -> Z[H]^2 --d--> Z[H] -> Z -> 0`,

with `d(h,X)=hx-h`, `d(h,Y)=hy-h`. Deck transformation g acts by left multiplication on every vertex and edge. These are literal chain maps, so their restriction is the actual integral H-action on L.

The program enumerates H by breadth-first search from the identity, examining X before Y. The first edge reaching each new vertex forms a spanning tree. There are 59 tree edges and 61 non-tree edges. Let p_h be the tree path from 1 to h. For every non-tree edge e=(h,t), its cycle is

`c_e = p_h + e - p_(ht)`.

The 120 by 61 integer matrix C_cycle has these cycles as columns. Restriction to the non-tree edge coordinates is the identity matrix; it is therefore an actual integral inverse on the cycle module. Let rho(h) be the resulting 61 by 61 integer deck matrix. The certificate checks `C_cycle rho(h)=h C_cycle`.

For a left coset h<t> of each inertia group, the actual cusp cycle is the sum of the lifted word t around that orbit. The X and Y cycles use X^3 and Y^5. The z cycle uses `(Y^(-1) X^(-1))^5`, with reversed edges carrying negative coefficients. This produces a 61 by 44 matrix B, with 20,12,12 columns in the three blocks. Its columns sum to zero and have rank 43. Their image is precisely P.

All group elements, tree words, cycle columns, cusp representatives/orbits, the two generator action matrices, B, and the subsequent maps are stored in `build_A5_boundary_nilpotent.json`. The standalone script constructs them from the two permutations, without a stored homology matrix.

## 3. The shared standard representation, with literal maps

Let V be the sum-zero subspace of Q^5, with integral basis `f_i=e_i-e_5` for i=1,...,4. Write v(h) for its integral permutation-action matrices. This is the absolutely irreducible standard representation of A5: the action on five letters is doubly transitive, so the endomorphism algebra of its permutation representation has dimension two, and its complementary nontrivial constituent is absolutely irreducible.

The chain complex gives the character equality `L_Q = Q[H] + Q`, hence the multiplicity of V in L_Q is four. The actual boundary sequence gives

`P_Q = Q[H/<x>] + Q[H/<y>] + Q[H/<z>] - Q`.

The fixed-space dimensions of V under these three cyclic groups are 2,0,0. Thus its multiplicity in P_Q is two and in W_Q is two. The certificate also independently computes these multiplicities from the actual deck matrices and the boundary projector.

Take the nonzero x-fixed vector

`u=(1,1,1,-3,0) in V`, with coordinates `(1,1,1,-3)` in the chosen basis.

Define F:Q[D]->V on the actual boundary generators by

`F(h<x>)=v(h)u`,  `F(h<y>)=F(h<z>)=0`.

This is well-defined because x fixes u, and it is H-equivariant. The sum of its values is zero because averaging over H gives an invariant vector in the nontrivial irreducible V. Therefore it factors through the actual P_Q. It sends the cusp represented by `<x>` to u.

## 4. An explicit integral nilpotent

The certificate selects 43 independent columns B0 of B and a 43 by 43 unimodular row minor. Its inverse defines an integral left inverse J with `J B0=I`. Define the integral linear maps

`P0=B0 J : L->P`,  `T0=F0 J : L->V`,

where F0 consists of the corresponding columns of F. Thus P0 is a projection onto P and T0 agrees with F on P. These particular maps need not be equivariant. Average the first one:

`E = sum_(h in H) rho(h) P0 rho(h)^(-1)`,  `Q=60I-E`.

Then E is an integral H-equivariant map with

`E^2=60E`, `E B=60B`, `E Q=Q E=0`.

Indeed every summand has image in P and is the identity on P. Thus e=E/60 is an H-equivariant rational projection onto the actual boundary module, and Q/60 projects onto a complementary module mapping isomorphically to W_Q.

Let a:L coordinate be the second non-tree cycle (zero-based index 1). Let A:V->L send the first coordinate vector of V to a and the remaining three to zero. Define the integral equivariant average

`A_H = sum_(h in H) rho(h) A v(h)^(-1)`.

The exact matrices used by the construction are

`S=Q A_H : V->L`,  `T=T0 E : L->V`,  `N=(S T)/10 : L->L`.

Here S and T are H-equivariant. For T, this follows because E has image in P and T0 restricts to the H-map F there. Also `E S=0` and `T S=0`. The denominator 10 is exact: every entry of ST is divisible by 10, and 10 is their greatest common divisor. The resulting N is an **integral** 61 by 61 matrix. The certificate verifies

`N^2=0`, `rho(x)N=N rho(x)`, `rho(y)N=N rho(y)`, `rank(N)=4`.

Since x,y generate H, this verifies full H-equivariance. The largest absolute entry of N is 3156. This is a specified integral map, not merely an existence claim from semisimplicity: E,S,T and the full N are all retained in the matrix artifact.

Let b be the first column of B, the actual X^3 cusp at the identity. The first coordinate of Nb is 324. The first row q of Q is an integer row with

`q B=0`,  `q N b=19440`.

Explicitly, in the ordered 61-cycle basis,

```
q=(0,-15,0,-13,-27,0,6,-3,0,13,27,15,0,0,-24,17,3,8,13,-14,
   0,2,-1,0,3,11,25,12,1,3,-3,-8,-30,-5,1,0,-6,-1,-3,-11,
   -2,6,0,0,25,-1,15,5,8,-9,0,0,-9,-3,14,-25,-14,-9,0,3,0).
```

Thus q factors through the actual proper homology W and detects the image of the boundary class under N. In particular N(P) is not contained in P.

Set

`gamma=I+60N`,  `gamma^(-1)=I-60N`.

These are integral mutually inverse H-equivariant automorphisms of L. But

`q gamma(b)=60 q N b=1166400 !=0`.

Therefore gamma does not preserve P and cannot induce any endomorphism of the fixed quotient W=L/P: the commutative quotient square would send b, whose quotient is zero, to a nonzero quotient.

## 5. The actual extension class and an explicit correction cochain

Let K be the kernel of the discrete quotient F2->H. The graph identifies `K/[K,K]=L`. The BFS word w_h is a specified section of F2->H. Its factor set

`c(h,k)=[w_h w_k w_(hk)^(-1)] in L`

is the cycle of `p_h + h p_k - p_(hk)`. Since the unshifted tree paths have zero non-tree coordinates, its coordinates are obtained by restricting the shifted path `h p_k` to those 61 coordinates. This gives an explicit normalized cocycle for

`1 -> L -> F2/[K,K] -> H -> 1`.

The induced topology on the finite-index subgroup K is its full profinite topology: the core in F2 of any finite-index subgroup of K remains finite index. Therefore its completion is the kernel R of the completed quotient, and maximal abelian profinite quotients give

`R/[R,R] = Lhat`.

The same finite factor set represents the profinite extension class beta in `H^2(H,Lhat)` for `Fhat2/[R,R]`.

Define the literal cochain `b0(h)=sum_(k in H)c(h,k)`. Summing the cocycle identity over its last argument gives

`h b0(k)-b0(hk)+b0(h)=60 c(h,k)`.

This proves directly that 60 annihilates this cohomology class. Since N is H-equivariant, `eta(h)=N b0(h)` satisfies

`delta eta=60 N c=(gamma-I)c`.

The program checks both cochain identities on **all 3600 pairs** and stores b0 and eta. In the actual section coordinates on the extension, whose multiplication is

`(m,h)(n,k)=(m+h n+c(h,k),hk)`,

the explicit automorphism

`gamma_tilde(m,h)=(gamma m+eta(h),h)`

is therefore a group automorphism. Its inverse is

`gamma_tilde^(-1)(m,h)=((I-60N)m-eta(h),h)`,

because `N eta=N^2 b0=0`. The same formulas are continuous on Lhat times H. In particular gammahat fixes beta, with a specified correction cochain, and the extension automorphism induces the identity on H.

## 6. The actual profinite lift and its exact scope

Bleher--Chinburg--Lubotzky Theorem 2.1, printed p. 4, with its proof and Gaschütz Corollary 2.4 on printed p. 5, gives the surjection

`{alpha in Aut(Fhat2): pi alpha=pi} -> Aut_(H,beta)(Lhat)`.

Apply it to the concrete gammahat above. There exists an actual continuous automorphism alpha of Fhat2, fixing the specified quotient H, whose action on R/[R,R] is exactly gammahat. More explicitly, the extension automorphism gamma_tilde lifts by their Corollary 2.4; composing its quotient diagram with H shows `pi alpha=pi`. This is the precise lifting theorem used; no infinite profinite generator words are claimed to have been computed.

Its affine homology action is the completion of an integral lattice automorphism. It nevertheless does not preserve the actual peripheral subgroup and therefore does not descend to the proper homology quotient. This failure also persists in every positive power:

`gamma^r=I+60rN`,  `q gamma^r(b)=1166400r !=0`.

Any finite-index subgroup of the quotient-fixing profinite automorphism group contains a positive power of alpha. Hence no such finite-index subgroup has an action preserving this peripheral module. This is a concrete reconstruction of the failure illustrated in BCL Example 5.3.

There is also an exact strengthening on the base homology. Let A:L->Z^2 sum the coefficients of X-edges and Y-edges, respectively. This is H-invariant, whereas the image of N factors through the nontrivial standard representation V. Hence `A N=0`. Its cycle columns with zero-based indices 1 and 6 are `(2,-1)` and `(1,-1)`. Their based words are explicitly

`r=X Y Y X (Y Y Y)^(-1)`,  `s=Y X X Y X (X Y X Y Y)^(-1)`.

Their A5 monodromies are trivial by the specified tree endpoints. The words `r s^(-1)` and `r s^(-2)` have exponent vectors `(1,0)` and `(0,1)`, respectively. Thus A is integrally surjective. The actual inclusion `R -> Fhat2` and abelianization give the commutative square with Ahat, gammahat and alpha_ab. Since `A gamma=A` and Ahat is surjective, **alpha acts as the identity on the base abelianization Fhat2_ab**. The counterexample therefore exists inside the IA subgroup as well.

The lift cannot be the image of a Galois element in the Belyi embedding. Such an element, when it fixes this finite quotient, preserves its actual cover and permutes the cusp inertia subgroups, acting on their procyclic generators by units. Its induced homology action consequently preserves Phat. The nonzero value `q gamma(b)=1166400` in Zhat contradicts this required map. This proves the limitation directly from the constructed boundary map.

The exact obstruction concerns a generic profinite lift at this one actual cover. No claim is made that alpha lies in the Galois image, preserves all actual cusp conjugacy classes, or transports the simultaneous integral proper-homology lattices of every cover required in E.2.22. The demonstrated map is precisely the missing implication: preserving the present affine integral lattice and finite quotient does not supply the quotient map on the present proper integral lattice.
