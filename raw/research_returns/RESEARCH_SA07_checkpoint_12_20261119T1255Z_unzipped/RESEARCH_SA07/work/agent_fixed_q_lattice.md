# An actual fixed-Q lattice outside the admissible closure

## FQL-1 / proposed E.2.13. Explicit elliptic isogeny obstruction

Let

\[
 E:\ y^2=x(x-1)(x-3)=x^3-4x^2+3x,\qquad
 B:\ Y^2=X^3+8X^2+4X
\]

over Q. Their discriminants are 576 and 12288. The rational formula

\[
 q(x,y)=\left(x-4+\frac3x,\ y\left(1-\frac3{x^2}\right)\right)
\]

defines a degree-two isogeny E -> B with kernel {O,(0,0)}.
Indeed, substitution proves the equation of B; properness extends the
rational map between smooth projective curves. The involution
(x,y) -> (3/x,-3y/x^2), which is translation by (0,0), fixes both
displayed coordinates. The generic x-coordinate satisfies
x^2-(X+4)x+3=0, so the map has degree exactly two. It sends O to O
and therefore is a homomorphism of elliptic curves. Its two-point
kernel is as stated. Direct differentiation gives
q^*(dX/Y)=dx/y.

We use the actual integral homology H_1(-,Z), with its polarizable
Hodge structure of weight -1. Let C_adm(E) be its full rigid exact
tensor closure in integral polarizable Hodge structures under finite
sums, tensor products, duals, saturated Hodge subobjects and free
Hodge quotients. Morphisms are all integral Hodge morphisms between
the resulting objects. The fibre functor forgets the Hodge filtration.

Every element T of SL(H_1(E,Z)) is a tensor automorphism of this
forgetful functor. Here is the exact extension argument. The
j-invariant of E is 21952/9, which is not an algebraic integer, so E
has no complex multiplication. CM j-integrality is the primary input
already checked in Milne MF110, Theorem12.14 and Corollaries12.15-16,
pp119-120. The Mumford--Tate group of this non-CM elliptic Hodge
structure is GL_2, as proved/identified in A.4.9 using KSV
Proposition5.14. Thus every rational Hodge subspace in any tensor
construction from H_1(E,Q) is stable under GL_2, and every rational
Hodge morphism commutes with GL_2. This also follows by applying
the defining Hodge-tensor stabilizer to the graph or to a Hodge
projector supplied by a polarization.

An integral matrix T acts integrally and invertibly on every initial
tensor word. A saturated subobject N in a lattice P is exactly
N_Q intersect P, so T and T^-1 preserve it; T then acts on each
free quotient. Induction gives integral maps on all admissible
expressions. They commute with every morphism, because their
rational extensions are the same GL_2-action. In particular,
isomorphisms between different expressions identify the maps,
which proves presentation independence. Their tensor, unit and
dual compatibilities are those of the GL_2 representation. Since
det T=1, the action on the labelled Tate line and on the elliptic
polarization is the identity.

Choose a basis e_1,e_2 of Lambda_E=H_1(E(C),Z) in which (0,0)
is e_1/2 modulo Lambda_E. Such a basis exists because the nonzero
point in Lambda_E/2Lambda_E has a primitive lift, and a primitive
vector extends to an integral basis. Identify the tangent spaces
by dq. Then

\[
 \Lambda_B=\Lambda_E+\mathbf Z(e_1/2).
\]

This identity follows from the analytic quotient C/Lambda_E by
the subgroup generated by e_1/2. In the basis
mu_1=e_1/2, mu_2=e_2 of Lambda_B, q_* has matrix

\[
 Q=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Take the actual integral tensor automorphism of C_adm(E) whose
matrix on H_1(E,Z) is

\[
 J=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

An extension to H_1(B,Z) natural for the algebraic morphism q
would have to satisfy T_B Q=QJ and hence

\[
 T_B=QJQ^{-1}
     =\begin{pmatrix}0&-2\\1/2&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

This is not an integral matrix, so no such extension exists.
Consequently the actual H_1(B,Z) has no admissible presentation
from the actual H_1(E,Z) in the full integral-Hodge category above.
For if it had one, fullness would include q_*, and the previously
constructed tensor automorphism would extend and give the
contradiction just computed.

The same statement holds for a full integral diagram of abelian
motives whose realizations and admissible lattice operations are
the ones specified here: q is an algebraic morphism, and restriction
of its Betti realization to this generated diagram gives the exact
functor used above. A presentation in the motivic diagram would
therefore induce the forbidden Hodge-lattice presentation.
For cohomology, pass by T=(eta^-1)^dual; a cohomological
comparison exists exactly when the corresponding homology
comparison does.

This gives a concrete failure of the admissible-presentation step
needed to identify E.2.12 with the full geometric diagram, over the
single fixed field Q. Enlarging the generator to H_1(E) direct-sum
H_1(B) includes B and imposes the displayed additional congruence
condition. The example does not assert a failure of comparisons
between different places of Q, or settle the quartic full-category
transporter.

## FQL-2 / common scalar and the exact prescribed CM fibre

In E.2.9, apply the result to A times B and use the two inclusion
and projection morphisms. The maps T and rho on the product are
the direct sums of those on the factors. Since each factor has
positive rank, restriction of

    T_(A times B) tensor Zhat = rho_(A times B)(sigma) lambda_(A times B)

implies lambda_A=lambda_B=lambda_(A times B). There is therefore
one scalar lambda in Zhat^times for the whole geometric abelian
diagram, rather than a scalar chosen separately for each factor.
The same conclusion applies to abelian varieties defined over
finite extensions, using the Artin-selected block descent of
E.2.3/E.2.9.

Fix sigma in Gal(Qbar/Fcm) and prescribe on the CM subdiagram the
particular comparison alpha of E.2.6. On one CM elliptic curve C,
the main theorem of CM gives alpha_C tensor Zhat=rho_C(sigma).
This positive-rank factor forces lambda=1. Thus an integral
extension of this prescribed CM comparison must satisfy

    T_A tensor Zhat = rho_A(sigma)

for every abelian variety A and every selected embedding block.
Such an extension, if it exists, is unique on the full geometric
abelian diagram: the map from integral matrices to their profinite
completions is injective; H^* of abelian varieties is the exterior
algebra on H^1; the fixed Artin permutation, tensor products and
labelled projectors then determine every component. This is a
uniqueness assertion and necessary equality, not an existence
claim. It makes the exact additional obligation for the rational
point of E.2.11 explicit: an integral extension must equal every
actual etale local point on every abelian generator. The Hasse
argument only constructs a rational point in the prescribed CM
fibre and does not prove those equalities.

For the main place-pair problem, other extensions sigma of the
same field embeddings, and other CM comparisons compatible with
the pair, have not been excluded. Full pure motives add further
objects. Neither point is settled by this prescribed-fibre
uniqueness statement.
