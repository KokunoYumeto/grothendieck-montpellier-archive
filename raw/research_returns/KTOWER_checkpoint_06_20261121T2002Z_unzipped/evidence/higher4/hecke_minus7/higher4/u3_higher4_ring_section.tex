\label{prop:u3-minus7-higher4}

the following pair $(e,M)$:
\[
 \begin{array}{c|l}

 \end{array}
 \tag{U4.1}
\]
Thus the full two-class comparison at $2$ is a unit at every

\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The group, class set, stabilizers, and all thirteen actual frames
are those already proved in Theorem~\ref{thm:u3-minus7-unit}.
The full invariant source, target, and harmonic-kernel dimensions
in this case are
\[
 \begin{array}{c|r|r|r}

 \end{array}
 \tag{U4.2}
\]
These counts are obtained by independently enumerating all
simultaneous permutation orbits of pairs of three-part
compositions with even coordinate differences. The saved
harmonic columns satisfy the complete contraction equations
and have a $d$ by $d$ identity minor on distinct coordinates.
The split-contraction and residue-ring argument of
Lemma~\ref{lem:u3-residual-ring} therefore proves that they are
the entire invariant harmonic module at every relevant prime.

For the row of (U4.1), the exact evaluation determinant and
the determinant of the Schur endomorphism $D_e$ of (UR.7)
have zero $\omega$-coefficient and constant coefficients
\[
 \det A_0=65303065346,\qquad
 \det D_e=243170653113\pmod {697446897311}.
 \tag{U4.3}
\]
Both norms are coprime to $M$. The computation checks
$\gcd(M,336(2k+1)!)=\gcd(M,1+2^e+2^{2e})=1$
using exact integers. It uses the residue of the unreduced
comparison point $1+2^e+2^{2e}$, with no change of scaling.
The hypotheses (UR.1) and (UR.3) follow, proving the assertion.

\end{proof}
