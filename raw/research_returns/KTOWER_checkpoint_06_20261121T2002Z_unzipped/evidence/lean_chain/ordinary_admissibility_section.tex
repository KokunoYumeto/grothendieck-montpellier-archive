\section{Exact source dischargers for ordinary admissibility}
\label{sec:kt-ordinary-admissibility-source}

\begin{proposition}[KT-ORDINARY-SPHERICAL-ADM3]
Let $F$ be a CM number field, let $e\geq2$, and let
$\rho:G_F\longrightarrow\mathrm{GL}_3(\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell)$
be continuous and absolutely irreducible. Suppose that $\rho$ is
unramified away from $\ell$ and, at every place above $\ell$, has an
actual ordered upper triangular flag with inertia characters
$(\varepsilon_\ell^e,1,\varepsilon_\ell^{-e})$.
Suppose also that $r=\rho\varepsilon_\ell^{-1}$ is attached, in the
source's \texttt{AttC} normalization, to an everywhere finite spherical,
regular algebraic conjugate self-dual cusp representation $\pi_0$.
Then the literal pinned predicate
\[
                    \mathrm{Adm3U\_s}(\iota,e,2,\rho)
\]
holds. No upper bound on $e$ in terms of $\ell$ is required.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Choose all finite global characters in the level predicates to be one.
Unramifiedness gives \texttt{LevelAway}; the displayed flag gives
\texttt{FlagAt}. For $h=(e,0,-e)$, every difference $h_i-h_j$ belongs
to $\{0,\pm e,\pm2e\}$, hence is not one. If for a positive integer
$b$ two diagonal characters satisfied
$\kappa_i^b=(\kappa_j\varepsilon_\ell)^b$, their restrictions to
inertia would make
$\varepsilon_\ell^{b(h_i-h_j-1)}$ trivial. The cyclotomic inertia
image over a finite extension of $\mathbf Q_\ell$ is infinite, a
contradiction. This argument proves the nonadjacency alternative of
\texttt{LevelAt} and the extra inequality in \texttt{Th.Unl}.
It holds at every choice of a valuation above a place by conjugation.

Twisting by $\varepsilon_\ell^{-1}$ changes the ordered exponents to
$(e-1,-1,-e-1)$ and preserves their differences. The same argument
gives \texttt{PotUnl} for $r$ at every place above $\ell$, including
all positive powers. It therefore proves the first clause of
\texttt{WitA}, the implication from \texttt{Unl1} to
\texttt{PotUnl}. The source's closed theorem \texttt{potOfFlag}
is an exact discharger for this step.

For the other clause, the closed theorem \texttt{sphUnl} gives toral
and unlinked support for $\pi_0$ at every finite place. Apply the
closed theorem \texttt{winOf} once to $\pi_0$. Its existential real
parameter precedes its place quantifier, so one real $u$ satisfies
\texttt{WinAt}$(\pi_0,u,v)$ at every finite $v$.
Now let $\pi$ be any regular algebraic cusp representation attached
to $r$, as required by the universal quantifier in \texttt{WitA}.
The two attachments give identical characteristic polynomials at
cofinitely many places. Cancellation of the common nonzero
\texttt{algSat} factor and uniqueness of roots with multiplicities
give the same Satake multiset there. The closed theorem
\texttt{suppSmo}, proved using strong multiplicity one, transfers
the same window to $\pi$. It holds at all finite places and hence
at every place required by \texttt{WitA}. Thus \texttt{WitA} holds.

The given conjugate self-dual attachment supplies \texttt{AutCsd0}.
Its conjunction with the implication from continuity to the proved
\texttt{WitA} is exactly \texttt{IsAutCsdA}. With irreducibility
and the proved level conditions this gives \texttt{Adm3Alt}; the
proved \texttt{Th.Unl} completes \texttt{Adm3U\_s}.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scope of the application.}
KT-ORDINARY-SYM2-FAMILY supplies the hypotheses for the constructed
adjoint systems. Therefore its all-Galois residual congruences occur
inside the actual admissible comparison class, even on the
progressions whose indicated $K$-group components vanish.
This does not give a converse to the map from the chosen deformation
ring's points into that class. In particular, it does not force one
of these systems to belong to the existential finite set used in
\texttt{OneCharU\_s}.
