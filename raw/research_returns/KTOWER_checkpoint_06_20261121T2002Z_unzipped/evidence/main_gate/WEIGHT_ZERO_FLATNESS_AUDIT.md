## Why ambient flatness would still not suffice

For a=pi^n, take the O-subalgebra of O^3 with basis1=(1,1,1), u=(0,a,−a), v=(0,a,a). Its determinant is2a^2; its products are u^2=av, uv=au, v^2=av. It is finite free, reduced, local, complete and augmented, with generic fiberFrac(O)^3. The involution swaps the last two coordinates. Its fixed-locus quotient is O[v]/(av,v^2), which contains nonzero O-torsion. At the augmentation the odd cotangent is O/a. The standalone full proof is `fixed_locus_nonflat.tex`; unitary independently incorporated the same example into the audited appendix. This is an elementary scope example, not a claimed counterexample to all source M1–M4 conditions.
