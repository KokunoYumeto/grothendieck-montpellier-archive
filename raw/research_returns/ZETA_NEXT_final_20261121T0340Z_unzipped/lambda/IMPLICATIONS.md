## Grothendieck framework

The doubled-space example lies within ordinary Zariski gluing of schemes. Its zeta function is multiplicative under the point-count scissor relation, and the non-Tate bad factor realizes h1 of an elliptic curve. The positive proper theorem is about actual l-adic representations; its determinants then descend to K0 with exterior-power lambda operations. Semisimplicity is not recoverable from K0 alone, which is why NX-L02 is substantive.

Exact carried placement: SGA6 V §2 and §7 equation(7.1.1); cote60 scans3 and5 for exterior/symmetric powers, scan7 archivalp6 for the reciprocal characteristic polynomial, and scan9 archivalp8 for Weil/dual-Tate normalization. These archive locations are inherited from ZETA_TWO IMPLICATIONS §5 and have not been newly inspected. DP_MULT's cote153 PDFp3/archivep2 concerns binomial polynomials and supplies no bad-fibre splitting theorem.

The geometric and sheaf operations use SGA4 XII Theorem5.1/Corollary5.2, XVI Theorem2.1, and XVII §§5.1–5.3; local determinants use SGA5 XV §3 no2 equation(1). The perfectoid bridge is explicitly a later comparison theorem and is not ascribed to EGA/SGA/FGA. No algebraic correspondence, cycle projector, or motive-level decomposition is inferred from the l-adic derived splitting.

## What remains outside the resolution

A separated nonproper amendment of the factor conjecture is not settled here. The literal conjecture is already refuted, and the separately requested proper case is proved. Extending the positive theorem would require controlling the nonproper boundary or another argument; a Lambda-equivariant compactification is not silently supplied by ordinary compactification.
