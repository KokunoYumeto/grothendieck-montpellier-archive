\subsection{A limit on comparison through PD indecomposables}

\begin{proposition}[C26: \status{Proved}; free-resolution noninvariance]
\label{sa08:C26}
Fix a prime $p$ and let $R=\Z_{(p)}$. There is an augmented free
graded PD algebra $C$ in nonnegative homological degrees, with
triangular differential of degree $-1$ and all generators in positive
degrees, such that $C\to R$ is a quasi-isomorphism, universally under
base change, but
\[
 H_{2p^i+1}\bigl(Q_{\mathrm{PD}}C\bigr)=R/pR
 \qquad(i\geq1).
\]
Here $Q_{\mathrm{PD}}$ is the augmentation ideal modulo products
and higher divided powers of even elements. Thus PD indecomposables
do not preserve quasi-isomorphisms even between free triangular PD
resolutions. A specific comparison with a minimal unary closure
cannot be inferred from algebra quasi-isomorphism alone.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Use the algebra and differential formulas of
Statement~\ref{sa08:C18}, but give them the homological degrees
\[
 |y|=2,\qquad |x_i|=2p^i+1,\qquad
 |v_i|=2p^{i+1}+2.
\]
Thus
\[
 C=\Gamma_R(y)\otimes\Lambda_R(x_i:i\geq0)
           \otimes\Gamma_R(v_i:i\geq0),
\]
and, writing $y_i=\gamma_{p^i}(y)$ and
$y_i^p=p a_i y_{i+1}$ with $a_i\in R^\times$, set
\[
 dx_i=y_i,\qquad dy=0,\qquad
 dv_i=p a_i x_{i+1}-x_i y_i^{p-1}.
\]
These formulas lower homological degree by one and square to zero.
They are triangular, for instance in increasing homological degree.
All generators have positive degrees.

Modulo $p$, the same digit decomposition and exhaustive monomial
pairing as in C18 contract $C\otimes\mathbb F_p$ onto its
constants. Every homological degree is finite free over $R$.
Universal coefficients and Nakayama therefore give $H_0(C)=R$
and $H_j(C)=0$ for $j>0$. The cycle sequences split over the
PID $R$, weight by weight, proving arbitrary base-change acyclicity.

In PD indecomposables, $x_0\mapsto y$ is an identity pair.
For $i\geq1$, the differential
$dx_i=\gamma_{p^i}(y)$ becomes zero. The product term in
$dv_{i-1}$ also vanishes, leaving the independent pairs
\[
 Rv_{i-1}\xrightarrow{\ p a_{i-1}\ }Rx_i
\]
in homological degrees $2p^i+2,2p^i+1$. Their cokernels are
$R/pR$, as asserted. Since $Q_{\mathrm{PD}}R=0$, the
augmentation is the required counterexample.
\end{proof}

This proposition concerns arbitrary free triangular resolutions.
Minimal acyclic closures have additional conditions; invoking their
uniqueness would first require an actual minimal cancellation theorem
for the particular bar closure. No such cancellation is assumed here.


\paragraph{Comparison with derived PD literature.}
Here $Q_{\mathrm{PD}}$ kills higher divided powers as well as products.
It is different from Beck abelianization, which retains unary
PD operations; this distinction is explicit in
\cite[Sections~3 and~6]{A_kmail_kozak_miller2025}.
The derivations used in our Euler argument satisfy the special
rule $h(\gamma_n a)=\gamma_{n-1}(a)h(a)$; general Beck
derivations have the additional coefficient operations of
\cite[Lemma~5.2]{A_kmail_kozak_miller2025}.
Likewise, the cotangent complex in
\cite[Definition~2.6, Example~2.9 and Theorem~8.2]{A_dokas_frankland_ikonicoff2025}
is defined using genuine cofibrant simplicial resolutions.
That paper works over a fixed base field and does not identify
an arbitrary triangular DG PD resolution with such a resolution.
Thus neither its comparison theorem nor its definition contradicts
Statement~\ref{sa08:C26}, and neither supplies the missing
positive-cochain eventual comparison in C.3.
