\subsection{L7. What the documentary evidence does and does not date}

\paragraph{B-14. Not determined: the proposed 1982--83 date of scans
144--212.}
The inspected evidence does not establish either endpoint of this
date interval for those mathematical leaves. This is an unresolved
documentary claim, not a refutation of the proposed date.

The original Montpellier PDF was opened at scans 142--145. These
are PDF pages 142--145, whose printed archive folios are 141--144.
Scan 143, folio 142, has continuous-form paper with tractor holes
on the right and a mirrored JES2 printout, including the date
16 August 1982. There is no mathematical handwriting on the visible
side. Scan 144, folio 143, begins the handwritten heading on
coalgebras and algebras, and scan 145 continues that argument.
The inspected images do not establish that the dated printing and
the mathematical notes occupy the same physical sheet. In particular,
adjacency in this PDF is not a proved relation of recto and verso.

The archive catalogue independently describes the 1982 listings in
cote 136 as used ``en guise de sous-chemises'' (note 137). Its
introductory warning says that verso material often concerns other
subjects; note 138 points to the 1976 IH\'ES conference and records
two isolated loose sheets. Thus the printout supplies a date for
that piece of filing material and a lower bound for an assemblage
containing it. It supplies neither a lower bound for every enclosed
mathematical leaf nor an upper bound of 1983.

The thematic comparison with \emph{Pursuing Stacks} is real but does
not close this gap. Section 117 begins on 10 September 1983, and
section 139 on 29 October 1983. Section 124 discusses the earlier
divided-power de Rham approach; section 128, equation (8), uses
$V(M)(k')=\operatorname{Hom}_k(M,k')$; section 139 again asks about
Steenrod operations in a coalgebra setting. Conversely section 94,
original pages 339--342, recalls the earlier divided-power work,
including its origin around 1976. Recurrence of these topics in 1983
therefore cannot by itself date the loose automorphism calculations.

The exact source locators are: Montpellier catalogue HTML lines
124--125 and 1360--1365; original cote 136 PDF scans 143--145;
the attached transcription
\nolinkurl{MPL_136_142-188_transcription.tex}, lines 41--74;
\emph{Pursuing Stacks} arXiv v2 source
\texttt{ps5.tex}, lines 2364--2545,
\texttt{ps6.tex}, lines 2468--2473, 3932--3976 and 4847--4881,
and \texttt{ps7.tex}, lines 1560--1565 and 1720--1745.
The date 4 October belongs to the heading before section 123,
\texttt{ps6.tex}, line 3805; it should not be cited as a separate
dated heading before section 124.

The smallest remaining gate is a documentary link to these precise
mathematical leaves: for example, an independently dated reference
to their contents or a verified same-sheet dated substrate. A
substrate printed in August 1982 would establish only the lower
bound; the upper bound would still require independent evidence.
The selected original PDF pages and their renderings were inspected;
the retrieval receipt is preserved with this report. No broader dating claim is
inferred from the limited image inspection.
