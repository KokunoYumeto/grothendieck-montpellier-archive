\subsection{A.5. The complementary-weight-zero corner}

\paragraph{A-014. Proved: L3 with the graded PD convention.}
At complementary weight zero (total weight $q$ in exterior degree
$q$), the simplicial module is $\Lambda^q\Psi$. Its normalization
is $k[q]$, so maps $X\to\Lambda^q\Psi$ are ordinary normalized
$q$-cocycles. Exterior multiplication induces
$\binom{p+q}{p}$ times cup product, by A-003 with weights $p,q$.
For even $q>0$, its divided powers are exactly A-012 with $w=q$;
odd higher graded powers are zero. The degree-zero corner is $k$,
and arbitrary elements of this weight-zero coefficient ring carry
no assigned higher divided powers. These supply the
products and graded divided powers asked for in scan 77. The full
PD-DGA is A-010; its shadow properties are A-002--A-005 and A.2.
The shifted embedding by $e$ is only a module identification, as
proved in A-010, and must not be assigned its ambient zero product.
