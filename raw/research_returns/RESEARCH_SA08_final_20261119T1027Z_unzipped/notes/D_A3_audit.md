## Checks of the proof

- Canonical even exterior divided powers are integral universal polynomial expressions; every repeated exterior monomial vanishes, so dividing the m-fold product by m! counts an unordered family of distinct disjoint even monomials. The proof uses the universal torsionfree ring only to establish polynomial identities, then specializes; it does not cancel a nonunit in the target ring.
- The ideal generated by e=sum dX_i is stable under even divided powers: after an integral unimodular basis change, every positive exterior monomial contains e and any two such monomials have zero product. Hence its higher divided powers vanish. Quotient differential and PD structures are well defined.
- A_w^{q-1}→Z_w^q is a degreewise surjection and therefore a simplicial Kan fibration. Its source is contractible for w≥q>0. A nullhomotopy from zero lifts along X×{0}→X×Delta[1], so actual boundaries coincide with nullhomotopic maps. The nonlinear gamma_m is a pointed simplicial map on even cycles, hence preserves precisely the required homotopy relation. This is valid for arbitrary k and arbitrary X.
- The orientation form omega=X_0^[w-q] dX_1...dX_q is a normalized horizontal q-cycle: all faces have dimension q−1. It is vertically closed on Delta[q], where (q+1)-forms vanish. Thus sending k[q]'s generator to omega is an actual chain map, not a choice of cohomology representative without a lift.
- The cut-index cocycles alpha_c^*epsilon_q form a basis: evaluation on faces (0,j_1,...,j_q) gives a matrix with entries one exactly when c_r≤j_r<c_{r+1} (last c_q≤j_q). Each nonzero entry has c≤j and the diagonal is one, so inversion uses integer addition/subtraction and is valid in every ring.
- The same map induces the identity in its sole nonzero homotopy group, by integration. A weak equivalence of Kan simplicial modules induces a bijection on homotopy classes from every simplicial set, without a finiteness hypothesis.
- For the chosen orientation the Dirichlet integral of X_0^[a0]...X_N^[aN] dX_1...dX_N equals T^[N+sum a]. This is an equality in a universal integral free PD algebra, hence survives torsion base change. Its Stokes identity makes coefficient extraction a cocycle and independent of representatives.
- No cancellation is used except in A-013 under its explicit injectivity hypothesis. The odd F2 counterexample now specifies weight one, so the coefficient c(2,1)=1 makes the failed proposed formula concrete.
