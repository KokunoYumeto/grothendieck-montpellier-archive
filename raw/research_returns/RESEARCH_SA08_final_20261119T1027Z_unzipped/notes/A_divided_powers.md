# A.3 and A.5: descent, exact operations and the weight-zero corner

## Convention

We use graded divided powers: the algebra is strictly alternating (odd homogeneous squares vanish), and divided-power identities are imposed on even elements of the augmentation ideal. Higher divided powers on odd homogeneous elements may be denoted0, but the ordinary ungraded addition identity is NOT imposed on sums of odd elements. Otherwise even an exterior algebra on two odd generators would be a counterexample. The differential identity dγ_m(z)=γ_{m−1}(z)dz is imposed for even z. Scan220 explicitly states compatibility on Γ^+M, so it does not authorize imposing that identity on arbitrary odd forms.

## A-010: the canonical graded PD algebra and its quotient

For free modules V,W over any k, Γ_k(V)⊗Λ_k(W) has the canonical alternating divided-power structure. On a positive even exterior form a=Σ_I a_I e_I in an ordered exterior basis, γ_m(a) is the sum over unordered collections of m pairwise disjoint nonempty even subsets I, with coefficient ∏a_I and the sign of their wedge. For a fixed homogeneous positive even degree q all I have size q. Each decomposable positive exterior monomial has γ_m=0 for m>1. Degree0 divided powers are those of Γ(V); mixed even elements are handled by the PD addition identity.

Proof: over the universal torsionfree ring generated by the finitely many coefficients, the stated formula equals a^m/m!, and the repeated exterior monomials vanish. For polynomial PD monomials, integrality follows by counting partitions into m unlabeled equal multisets (or the explicit factorial formula for iterated divided powers). Hence all operations have integer polynomial formulas. The identities hold after rationalization in a torsionfree universal ring, hence integrally, and then after every base change, including k with 2- and 3-torsion. This also proves independence of ordered basis. Filtered colimits handle arbitrary free ranks. The pointwise modules here are finite free.

For Φ_n=k^{n+1}, put e=Σ_i dX_i. The differential algebra ΓΦ⊗ΛΦ has dX_i the exterior generators and d(X_i^[a])=X_i^[a−1]dX_i. Its quotient by the differential exterior ideal (e) is ΓΦ⊗ΛΨ with Ψ=Φ/kT. The ideal is stable under even divided powers: each even element in (e) is e∧b with b odd, and after choosing e as a basis vector all products of two of its exterior monomials contain e twice, so its higher divided powers vanish. Thus the quotient inherits the stated graded PD-DGA structure. It is functorial in simplicial operators and k.

This proves L3 for the geometric construction of scans74–78. The embedding into ΓΦ⊗Λ^{q+1}Φ is multiplication by e and anticommutes with d; it is not an algebra embedding, since two elements of its image multiply to0.

## A-011: divided powers descend on universal weighted cohomology

For every simplicial set X, every commutative ring k, and every w≥q≥0, the canonical even divided power on forms induces

γ_m:H^q(C_w(X;k))→H^{mq}(C_{mw}(X;k)).

It gives the entire cohomology augmentation ideal a graded PD structure. The same assertion holds for literal locally finite weights and for the total-weight completion; the weighted module formula is interpreted as in A-002–A-005.

Proof for q>0: let Z_w^q=ker d in the simplicial module A_w^q. The exact sequence A_w^{q−1}→Z_w^q is a Kan fibration with contractible source, because w−(q−1)>0. Consequently the quotient of actual simplicial maps X→Z_w^q by actual boundaries is exactly [X,Z_w^q]. A boundary is nullhomotopic because it factors through that contractible source; conversely a nullhomotopy lifts through the fibration starting at0. The divided-power operation is a map of simplicial SETS Z_w^q→Z_{mw}^{mq} (closed even forms stay closed by the differential identity). Every map of simplicial sets preserves simplicial homotopies. It therefore induces an operation on these homotopy classes and is independent of the cocycle representative. All PD identities then descend because they hold as identities of forms. No cancellation of m! is used. Degree0 has no boundaries, and is immediate. For the literal sum, the same source contractibility holds for ⊕_{w≥q}A_w^{q−1}; for the completion it holds for the product, using the same weight-preserving contractions. The operations are locally finite or convergent in the stated conventions.

## A-012: a finite integral formula determines every operation, including torsion

Here is an explicit recipe on ordinary normalized cocycles. It has no choices of primitives and does not require division by any integer. Fix an even q>0, w≥q and m≥1. Let z be an ordinary normalized q-cocycle with values in k. On an N=mq simplex σ, write z_σ for its restriction to Δ[N].

For each increasing q-tuple c=(c1,...,cq) with 1≤c1<...<cq≤N, let α_c:[N]↠[q] be the monotone surjection with consecutive blocks

[0,c1−1], [c1,c2−1], ..., [cq,N].

Let ε_c=α_c^*(ε_q), where ε_q is the normalized q-cocycle that evaluates1 on the unique q-simplex of Δ[q]. The ε_c form a Z-basis for Z^q(Δ[N];Z). To compute the coefficients u_c of z_σ=Σ_c u_c ε_c, order the tuples compatibly with componentwise order and solve the unitriangular system

z_σ(0,j1,...,jq)=Σ_c u_c ε_c(0,j1,...,jq).

The matrix entry is1 precisely when c_r≤j_r<c_{r+1} for r<q and c_q≤j_q; otherwise it is0. Its diagonal entries are1, and nonzero entries satisfy c≤j. Thus this recursion uses only addition and subtraction over k.

Define U_{c,r}=Σ_{α_c(v)=r}X_v and form

L_{w,q}(z_σ)=Σ_c u_c U_{c,0}^[w−q] dU_{c,1}∧...∧dU_{c,q}.

Expand γ_m(L_{w,q}(z_σ)) in the divided-power polynomial/exterior basis, replace dX_0 by −Σ_{v=1}^N dX_v, and collect the coefficient of dX_1∧...∧dX_N. Replace every monomial X_0^[a0]...X_N^[aN] in that coefficient by1 (all have Σa_v=mw−N). The resulting element of k is P_{m,w,q}(z)(σ).

The rule defines a normalized mq-cocycle, and under the fixed-weight comparison

γ_m([z]T^[w])=[P_{m,w,q}(z)]T^[mw].

For q=0 use the ordinary coefficient formula below; for q odd the higher graded divided powers are0. For a sum of even classes in different weights use the PD addition formula. These rules give every operation in every characteristic, so the formula is a determination, not a finite test or a division-by-factorial guess.

### Proof of the recipe

The form ω_{w,q}=X_0^[w−q]dX_1∧...∧dX_q on Δ[q] is a normalized simplicial q-cycle in Z_w^q: every face has dimension q−1 and a q-form restricts to0. Its integral is T^[w]. Sending the normalized generator of K(k,q) to this form is a chain map k[q]→NZ_w^q. Dold–Kan extends it to a simplicial k-linear map L_{w,q}:K(k,q)→Z_w^q. The displayed ε_c decomposition is exactly the Dold–Kan degeneracy decomposition, so the written formula is that map. Both modules have only π_q=k, and the map is the identity there by the integral of ω; hence it is a weak equivalence and induces the inverse of the weightwise cohomology comparison on maps out of every X.

Integration on an N-simplex sends the monomial X_0^[a0]...X_N^[aN]dX_1...dX_N to T^[N+Σa_v]. To verify the formula integrally, calculate over Q using iterated polynomial integration on the affine simplex of side T; the Dirichlet integral cancels all divided-power factorials. Both sides have integral coefficients in the torsionfree universal divided-power algebra, so the identity is integral and survives arbitrary k. Stokes likewise follows over Q and then in the universal integral algebra; no division is performed after base change. Thus the final coefficient-extraction step is precisely the canonical cocycle comparison on γ_m(Lz). The previous proposition proves its cohomological independence of z. Naturality also follows because the lift is simplicial and integration is natural on normalized cochains.

## A-013: useful closed cases and a warning about odd degrees

Put c(m,w)=(mw)!/(m!(w!)^m), an integer. In degree0,

γ_m(aT^[w])=c(m,w)a^mT^[mw].

For an even positive-degree class x the same formula holds whenever multiplication by m! is injective on H^{mq}(X;k); in particular it holds for CP² over Z in every nonzero target degree. Proof: m!γ_m(xT^[w])=(xT^[w])^m=((mw)!/(w!)^m)x^mT^[mw], and cancellation is legitimate under exactly the stated injectivity. This argument by itself proves no formula in m!-torsion. The preceding finite recipe, rather than an uncited cancellation argument, is the all-rings answer.

For odd x, the naive formula is false: with k=F2, X=RP², x the degree1 generator and w=1, γ_2(xT)=0 by the graded convention, while c(2,1)x²T^[2]=x²T^[2]≠0. The finite normalized bar/shuffle computations in code/A_exterior_pd_bar.py check the even minimal-weight formula on 15 monomials in H^*(B(C2)^2;F2), degrees2,4,6, with 13,291,408 partition evaluations. They are checks of those cases only, not a universal proof of the closed scalar formula.

## A-014: the weight-zero corner, exactly

The exterior model K(k,q)=Λ^qΨ identifies naturally with ordinary normalized cocycles Z^q(Δ[−];k), hence Hom(X,Λ^qΨ)=Z^q(X;k). Its product is the exterior product described above. Under ordinary cohomology it is binom(p+q,p) times the cup product, by A-003 with weights p and q. Its even divided powers are A-012 with w=q. Together with ΓΦ these are exactly the products and divided powers that fill the missing data on scan77; the full differential PD algebra is ΓΦ⊗ΛΨ, and its shadow properties follow from A-002–A-005. Thus L3 is proved with the graded-PD convention made explicit.
