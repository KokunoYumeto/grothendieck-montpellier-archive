## Scope correction

The claim that a torsion-free target forces preservation of all divided powers needs an ideal hypothesis. If S^+ is not generated by t, the underlying simplicial differential ring does not determine that larger ideal. Example: S=S'=Z[epsilon]/epsilon^2, t=t'=0, source PD ideal (epsilon), target PD ideal 0, and identity ring map. The underlying A's are identical, but the identity is not a morphism of the stated PD pairs.
