# Relative finite-arity bar extension under a dimension bound

2026-11-19, agent /root/topos. Proposed statement C29; full independent
audits requested. This assumes the entire prescribed bar boundary exists.
It does not supply that boundary from an arbitrary horizontal cycle.

## Setup

Work over Z. Let q=2r-1 be a positive odd form degree, and let U_r(M)
be D029's positive bar closure on the original disk M in degrees q,q+1.
In arity m its form degree lies between qm and (q+1)m. Write V_m(M)
for the arity-m part of the PD subalgebra generated by arities less than m,
and Q_m(M)=U_r(M)_m/V_m(M). For m>1 its free terms lie in degrees
qm,...,(q+1)m-1: the coefficient term at the top is already generated
by arity one. The freeness is the pointwise signed Lyndon result C24.

Let K=K(Z,n) and D=DK(D_{n+1}Z), with the standard degreewise split free
inclusion K into D. Suppose a full simplicial PD-DGA map U_r(K)->A is
already prescribed, and suppose a map from U_r(D) is prescribed through
arity m-1, agreeing with that full boundary map. Give each parameter
weight w. At weight mw require vertical comparison with the constant Z
through cochain degree (q+1)m and horizontal acyclicity of all rows from
0 through (q+1)m. These are finite requirements for any fixed m.

Claim: if n<=2q-1, this pair of maps extends through arity m.
Thus arbitrary compatible lower choices extend through any fixed finite
arity range for which the corresponding finite target conditions hold.
No uniform w for all m is asserted.

## Euler estimate for an arbitrary sphere dimension

The C27 proof works for every r. In a basis multidegree, the special
PD derivation homotopy for the multiplicity Euler derivation is obtained
by the same bar-length recursion, using the coefficient-insertion
contraction. Its degree is -1, its arity is zero, and it preserves every
multidegree. It descends to Q_m. Consequently each multiplicity annihilates
homology, an exponent-one component is contractible, and multiplication
by m is homotopic to zero on Q_m.

Because the first term Q_m^{qm} is free and no preceding term occurs,
H^{qm}(Q_m) is torsionfree. It is killed by m, so it is zero. Thus any
nonzero vertical homology F^j(M)=H^j(Q_m(M)) satisfies j>=qm+1.
Every nonzero basis multidegree uses at most floor(m/2) distinct variables.
For M=K(Z,n), a basis vector in simplicial dimension a is labelled by an
n-element cut set. If a>n*floor(m/2), all cut sets in any such component
miss a common position. That whole component is in one degeneracy image.
Hence N_a F^j(K)=0 for a>n*floor(m/2).

The identity-disk projective resolution of a bounded normalized complex,
and global dimension one of Z, give

    pd_sAb F^j(K) <= n*floor(m/2)+1.

If n<=2q-1 and m>=2, this upper bound is at most qm. Therefore

    Ext_sAb^{j+t}(F^j(K),constant Z)=0  for every t>=0.

The finite cohomology filtration of Q_m(K) yields

    H^{t>=0} RHom_sAb(Q_m(K),constant Z)=0.          (1)

For comparison, the weaker bound n<=2q gives vanishing only for t>0;
that is the general-degree version of the absolute C27 theorem.

## The actual relative arity quotient

Set

    R_m = Q_m(D)/Q_m(K).

The inclusion here is genuinely injective, and its quotient has free
terms at every simplex. Choose a basis of D_a extending a basis of K_a;
C24 makes the corresponding PD indecomposable generators a basis subset.
No coherent choices of these bases are needed for this module assertion.
Equivalently R_m is the quotient of U_r(D)_m by the sum of the entire
boundary U_r(K)_m and V_m(D). The intersection of those submodules is
V_m(K), by the same free-subalgebra basis. Thus the two prescribed maps
induce a well-defined map on that sum. This is the correct relative
extension problem; it retains the whole earlier boundary, including
its higher-arity values.

Every term of Q_m(D) is horizontally contractible. Indeed D admits an
additive simplicial contraction, and each term of Q_m is a reduced
functor on free modules; applying it to the homotopy operators gives
a simplicial contraction. The formula already proved in C22,

    RHom_sAb(Y,constant Z) = RHom_Z(NY,Z),

therefore makes RHom_sAb(Q_m(D),constant Z) zero, using the finite
cochain filtration. Applying derived Hom to the short exact sequence
0 -> Q_m(K) -> Q_m(D) -> R_m -> 0 gives

    RHom_sAb(R_m,constant Z)
       = RHom_sAb(Q_m(K),constant Z)[-1].           (2)

In particular the relative degree-one obstruction equals the degree-zero
absolute derived Hom group, which vanishes by (1).

## Strict extension, rather than only a derived map

The terms of R_m have projective normalized complexes over Z. Since the
target rows in the stated finite range are horizontally acyclic, every
termwise extension with those target rows splits in sAb. The obstruction
to extending the given boundary/lower-arity cochain map is consequently
an element of H^1 Tot Hom_sAb(R_m,A^{mw,*}). Applying the ordinary/derived
Hom comparison of C22 to R_m[-1], whose support lies in
qm+1,...,(q+1)m, identifies this group with

    H^1 RHom_sAb(R_m,constant Z)=0.

An actual simplicial cochain extension exists. All product and PD
identities whose output has arity m already lie in the prescribed
submodule generated by lower arities or by the whole boundary. Thus the
cochain extension is precisely a strict partial PD-DGA extension.

## The endpoint is a real obstruction group, not a counterexample

For q=1, n=2 and m=2, the absolute Q_2(K) is the complex
Sym^2(K) -> Gamma^2(K) in form degrees 2 and 3, with injective
multiplication map and cokernel K/2 in degree 3. Thus

    H^0 RHom_sAb(Q_2(K(Z,2)),constant Z)
        = Ext_sAb^3(K(F_2,2),constant Z)
        = Ext_Z^1(F_2,Z) = F_2.

By (2), the relative degree-one obstruction group is F_2. This proves
that the vanishing argument cannot include the endpoint n=2q. It does
not assert that a prescribed admissible target and lower-arity map
realize a nonzero obstruction class. Such actual realization requires
a separate construction.

## Relation to the unresolved global question

This is an extension theorem for targets A once a full U_r(K)->A map
is given. A bar-cell attachment to a current common source X still needs
U_r(K)->X extending the cycle that is to be killed. That source row may
be non-acyclic, and this theorem does not manufacture its boundary map.
The uniform-in-arity weight problem is also unchanged. The claim is a
controlled relative lifting result, not a completed C3 construction.

## Base change and the sharper local-prime range (proved; independently audited by B)

The preceding integral proof extends to every commutative coefficient
ring k, without flatness over Z, by deriving extension of scalars in the
abelian category of simplicial modules. Write Q_Z for the bounded cochain
complex Q_m(K(Z,n)). All its terms are degreewise free over Z. Hence
k tensor_Z Q_Z computes k tensor_Z^L Q_Z in D(sMod_k): a projective
simplicial-module resolution is levelwise a projective Z-resolution, and
the free terms kill every positive Tor. The same conclusion follows by
the finite vertical filtration for the bounded complex. Free-module
base change for Gamma, the bar construction, and the C24 basis subset
gives the strict identification with Q_m(K(k,n)). Derived adjunction gives

 RHom_sMod_k(k tensor_Z Q_Z,constant k)
      = RHom_sAb(Q_Z,constant k).

The projective-dimension bounds in the proof vanish against every target,
not only Z. They therefore prove the same absolute n<=2q and relative
n<=2q-1 ranges over arbitrary k. The analogous assertion holds for the
relative quotient R, whose terms are also degreewise free over Z.

More generally let R=Z_(p). In the Euler argument a multiplicity not
divisible by p is a unit. Thus a nonzero vertical homology component of
Q_m uses only variables whose exponents are positive multiples of p.
It has support at most floor(m/p), and normalization on K(R,n) is zero
above n*floor(m/p). The same PID projective-dimension bound is
n*floor(m/p)+1; H^j is still zero for j<=qm because R is torsionfree.
Therefore the absolute positive derived-Hom groups vanish if n<=pq,
and the relative degree-one group vanishes if n<pq. If p does not divide
m, total Euler already makes Q_m contractible over R. Derived base change
as above extends these bounds to every R-algebra k.

These are sufficient slope bounds, not a uniform result over arbitrary
horizontal cell dimensions. They still assume the full boundary map in
the relative problem and do not provide an all-arity weight choice.
