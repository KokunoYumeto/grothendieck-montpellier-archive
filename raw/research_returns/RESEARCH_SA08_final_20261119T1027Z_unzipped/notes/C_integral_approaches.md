## Current proved gate

The common-source assertion and terminal groupoid are proved over every Z_(p)-algebra (C09), hence every ring of positive characteristic (C10). For any ring, the special universally flasque category has contractible nerve (C06), and the standard elementary theory maps to each special object by lifting its marked section through a normalized boundary. Strict equalization is false over every nonzero ring (C07), including the special category. C17 now also proves both assertions for every ring with only finitely many noninvertible rational primes, with or without the termwise-flatness condition. The outstanding general coefficient-ring gate therefore has infinitely many residual prime characteristics, already k=Z in the entire category of eventual theories.

## The ordinary tensor construction really fails

Let S=Gamma_k(T), F=DR(Phi,T), Phi_n=k^(n+1). The degree-zero simplicial algebra F^0=Gamma(Phi) is a simplicial graded-S-module resolution of k=S/S^+. It is free over S in each simplicial degree: choose a vertex to split the marked line, giving Gamma(Phi_n)=S tensor Gamma(k^n). Normalization gives a projective S-resolution, since normalized modules are direct summands. Positive-weight homotopy vanishes by C05, and weight zero is constant k.

Eilenberg--Zilber over S therefore gives

    pi_i(F^0 tensor_S F^0) = Tor_i^S(k,k).

In particular pi_1 = S^+/(S^+)^2. If k=F_p, the weight-p^e class of T^[p^e] is nonzero for every e>=0: all its decomposable coefficients binom(p^e,a), 0<a<p^e, vanish modulo p. These classes occur in unbounded weights. Hence F tensor_S F fails scan119's eventual flasqueness already in exterior degree zero. This is a failure of the construction, not just an unavailable proof.

For k=Z, the same quotient in weight n is Z/d_n, where d_n=gcd{binom(n,a):0<a<n}. It is Z at n=1, Z/p at prime powers n=p^e, and zero otherwise. Proof: a prime p divides all internal binomial coefficients precisely when (1+x)^n=1+x^n in F_p[x], which by base-p expansion holds precisely when n is a p-power. At n=p^e, the coefficient binom(p^e,p^(e-1)) has p-adic valuation one, as follows by cancellation from p^e/p^(e-1) times binom(p^e-1,p^(e-1)-1), whose latter factor is a p-unit. Thus no p^2 divides the gcd. Integral failure is equally unbounded.

## Multiweight divided-power source

R=Gamma(U_n:n>=N), weight(U_n)=n, can map to S by U_n -> T^[n]. The source S tensor_R DR(direct_sum Phi_n) maps to any A once A^{n,0} is flasque for all n>=N. It resolves S in each simplex, by the relative PD Poincare contractions. However its horizontal degree-zero homology is Tor^R(S,k). The whole construction is now ruled out over Z by D022--D023 in sections/D_Cintegral_source_obstruction.tex: an arbitrary set of independently marked generators gives an admissible source only when there is exactly one generator of weight one with coefficient +1 or -1. The proof reduces modulo p, uses one p-power generator to make S finite free, and finds unbounded Tor_1 from every remaining generator. We independently audited the derived base change and infinite tensor-factor argument; no gap was found. This is an obstruction to the entire elementary-source strategy, not a counterexample to C.3(a).

Finite many weights cannot cure all residual primes merely by generating S: modulo a prime p larger than all chosen weights, T^[p] is indecomposable in the augmentation ideal of Gamma(T), so it cannot be generated by products and divided powers of those high weights (a divided power of an element of weight N>1 has coefficient divisible by p when it could land in weight p, and N does not divide p).

## Cofibrant resolution of the coefficient tail

A possible source starts with a simplicial PD S-algebra resolution of S/S^{>=N}, whose free generators have weights >=N. Maps into A^{*,0} can potentially be lifted because its high-weight normalized complexes are acyclic. Applying relative PD de Rham would preserve the simplexwise coefficient resolution if every simplex algebra were free. The unproved step is eventual flasqueness of the resulting cotangent/exterior pieces: the tail PD ideal has independent relations at unbounded prime-power weights, and its cotangent homology may persist in low exterior degree. No cotangent boundedness theorem with the required hypotheses has been established here.

## Arithmetic patching

Local p-power sources have first guaranteed flasque weight p^e. Taking products over infinitely many primes fails the required uniform eventual bound: at a fixed large weight n, factors with p^e>n still have a constant coefficient module in exterior degree zero.

A finite-prime/rational gluing approach cannot assume that non-special behavior is supported at finitely many primes. That assertion is false. For N>1 define the simplicial PD subalgebra A_N of F by A_N^{w,*}=S^w in weights w<N and A_N^{w,*}=F^{w,*} in weights w>=N. Products and divided powers preserve this subalgebra because weights are nonnegative and add (or multiply by the divided-power index). It resolves S in every weight and satisfies eventual flasqueness with the bound w>=max(N,q+1). But A_N^{1,0}=Z is constant, hence nonflasque over Q and at every prime. This explicit family rules out the hoped-for rigidity reduction to degree-one-flasque theories outside finitely many primes.

## Monoidal acyclic models

Opened Guillén--Navarro--Pascual--Roig, *Monoidal functors, acyclic models and chain operads*, published Sections8--9 and arXiv math/0506624v2 full source. Their comparison is in localized monoidal/associative DGA functor categories. The cosimplicial simple uses Alexander--Whitney, which does not preserve strict commutativity. It therefore does not automatically yield common sources or a terminal groupoid in the strict PD category here. Importing it without a PD-compatible totalization would change the category.

## Cell attachments / acyclic closures

Adjoining a degree-zero variable and its differential is a PD Poincare extension and preserves the vertical resolution. Killing horizontal classes in higher cochain degree would require acyclic PD extensions of odd-to-even disks. The naive free extension on x odd, dx=y even is not acyclic: d(x gamma_(m-1)(y))=m gamma_m(y). Tate-style higher generators can resolve this, but a complete construction with the manuscript's simultaneous weight/eventual bounds and maps to arbitrary A,B has not been supplied. A cell-attachment argument without that control would be a gap.

No item is declared proved by these attempts. The remaining task is to globalize C09 or construct an admissible integral counterexample to a) or to connected/simple connectedness.

## Weightwise non-elementary attachments (active, 06:55 UTC)

Positive weights make every new generator linear in its own weight; nonlinear PD effects occur only at larger weights. A simplicial cell that kills a horizontal class in exterior degree q can be adjoined together with its differential in degree q+1. In the current weight this is a vertical disk and does not alter vertical cohomology. In all weights the free PD extension is vertically harmless for q even. For q odd it introduces higher cochain-degree torsion; the first example is x in degree1, dx=y in degree2, with H4=Z/2 generated by gamma2(y). Thus bare transferred model structures on vertical quasi-isomorphisms cannot be used.

A Tate-type repair could kill the later vertical classes, leaving the finite initial weight exceptions allowed by the manuscript. For fixed exterior degree q, the targets have enough vertical exactness in all sufficiently high weights to choose individual primitives. The unresolved compatibility is simplicial: a projective simplicial module of generators lifts through the degreewise surjection A^{q-1}→Z^q(A), but the kernel in its resolution is generally only degreewise projective, not projective in simplicial modules. Its next lift can carry an Ext obstruction. One must not replace projectivity of terms by projectivity in sMod.

Over Z every abelian group has a free resolution of length one, so objectwise vertical killing in degrees q≥4 can be arranged by generators in degrees q−1,q−2; its nonlinear effects begin in degree2(q−2)≥q. This suggests a weight induction that preserves low-degree exactness, but the preceding simplicial compatibility still prevents a proof. No common source has been constructed by this argument.

## Arithmetic homotopy pullback: exact projection obstruction (C12)

The proposed arithmetic square with rational Thom--Whitney paths cannot glue the local G_{N_p} sources when N_p is unbounded, even if the arithmetic sequence itself is exact. Any integral admissible H makes T^[w] a normalized boundary in exterior degree zero for all w>=W, uniformly in p. Localization preserves that boundary. But the target G_{N_p} has constant nonzero Z_(p)T^[w] in q0 whenever w<N_p. Therefore no coefficient-preserving projections H_(p)→G_{N_p} exist for such w. Every strict or homotopy pullback retaining these projections fails before any rational overlap calculation. This does not rule out different local source models having uniform bounds, nor gluing that does not provide those projections; it rules out exactly the proposed G_{N_p} gluing strategy.

## Standard tails cannot map to every theory (C13)

The family A_N=S+F_{weights>=N} is not weakly initial, even over Q. None maps to the admissible G_2 with weight-two marked generator. In simplicial degree one a map would send every x^n, n>=N, to a homogeneous a_n in Q[T,y], wt(y)=2. Multiplicativity implies a_n=f^n for a common fraction f. Normality forces f into Q[T,y], and weight one forces f=cT. This has equal faces, whereas x^n has faces0,T^n. The full proof and hypotheses are in sections/C_tail_obstruction.tex. Thus the higher-weight relations in a standard tail do not themselves solve the comparison gate; the common sources guaranteed over Q must be more flexible than these particular tails.

## Finite-prime gluing completed (C16--C17)

The finite-prime arithmetic construction is now proved in sections/C_finite_prime.tex, with independent audits by root and automorphisms. It uses simplicial cotensors, so no weight-zero differential variable and no ambiguity about q<=w arises. For k over Z localized outside a finite set P, the pure arithmetic exact sequence holds for every module, including torsion. Finite local elementary sources and a common rational G_L glue to an admissible E. Canonical arithmetic path replacements A→J(A) are horizontal weak equivalences in every row. Their mapping-path trivial fibrations turn the glued zigzag into an actual common source. Marked squares of elementary maps fill by C02 Ext vanishing, proving equality of parallel maps in localization; a common source for a finite list then contracts every finite zigzag loop. Flatness is checked even in low weights by the explicit pi0 of rational G_N rows and the arithmetic kernel for P_w.

The strongest coefficient-ring gate is now genuinely smaller: only infinitely many residual prime characteristics remain. C12 still forbids an unchanged infinite product of G_{N_p}; a global extension must replace those sources or obtain uniform local bounds by another construction.

## Uniform global partial model and nonlinear local closure (C18,C21)

C21, now independently audited by root and automorphisms and written in sections/C_repairs.tex, constructs an actual common partial source P->A,B over every k. Its terms are free, its horizontal rows have uniform eventual acyclicity, and its simplexwise coefficient comparison is exact through degree d after inverting floor((d+1)/2)!. The construction keeps the boundary of every horizontal cell: DK(k[n]) is attached to the normalized identity disk, and the free PD differential algebra pushout adds independent vertical disks levelwise. Countable rounds kill all cycles; subsequent cochain-degree stages preserve lower rows. Over k_(p), the safe arbitrary-base range is d<2p-1, improved to d<2p for torsionfree coefficients.

C18 gives an explicit nonlinear acyclic closure of one odd-to-even disk over Z_(p), with new generators first in degree 2rp-2, universally under base change. Its mod-p monomial contraction and integral Nakayama argument are fully proved; coefficient-tail subalgebras permit arbitrary delays while retaining eventual vertical exactness. Both independent audits passed. This shows that the desired correction-degree behavior is algebraically possible for an individual disk.

The remaining joint gate is not silently removed: one must make these corrections for the boundary-relative simplicial P while extending its maps to both targets, then prove infinite arithmetic descent with uniform row bounds. Absolute contractions do not suffice because a horizontal cell's new quotient is a sphere. The original G_(N_p) projections remain forbidden by C12. The precise first quadratic lifting calculation is being investigated separately by automorphisms.

## Relative Weil construction scope

The tensor P=A tensor_k B, with T=T_A+T_B, has coefficient-preserving projections to A,B. Adding only a derivative of the extra coefficient variable would require a compatible connection. Such a connection is not automatic even for rational tails: R_N=Q[T]+Q[T,x]_(weights>=N), N>1, has no weight-minus-one derivation delta with delta(T)=1. Indeed f=delta(x^N) lies in Q[T] of weight N-1; extension to the fraction field gives delta(x)=f/(N x^(N-1)). Then delta(Tx^(N-1))=x^(N-1)+(N-1)Tf/(Nx). Polynomiality forces f=0, after which x^(N-1) is not in R_N, a contradiction.

A full super-PD de Rham totalization is a different construction and can introduce the original odd-disk problem. For an even generator X with dX=x, write delta X=u and delta x=v. The total differential has D(X)=x+u, D(u)=-v and D(x)=v. The change x'=x+u separates the harmless even-to-odd X->x' disk from the odd-to-even u->-v disk, whose divided powers have torsion. Thus this full totalization is not automatically the familiar coefficient-only marked direct-sum construction. A derived or coefficient-only universal variant would need a separate exactness and grading proof.

## Relative bar closure and the exact current lifting limit (C24,C26--C29)

C24 proves the actual vertical preservation property needed for bar cells:
for a degreewise split free inclusion M into N, U(M)->U(N) is free as a
graded PD extension and semifree/K-flat as a differential module; any
already specified U(M)->X gives a universal vertical quasi-isomorphism
X->X tensor_U(M) U(N). Signed Lyndon triangularity supplies the integral
basis and the new-bar-length filtration. This is a completed algebraic
step, not an assumption that the initial boundary map exists.

C26 shows why a shortcut through arbitrary acyclic PD resolutions is
invalid: an explicit positive homological free triangular resolution of
Z_(p) has PD indecomposables with F_p homology in infinitely many degrees.
The specific bar indecomposables need their own calculation. C27 instead
uses Euler homotopies: each basis multiplicity annihilates homology, so
a multiplicity-one component contracts. The resulting support estimate
settles all fixed absolute arities for K(Z,2), retaining earlier choices.
C29 treats a full prescribed sphere boundary K(Z,n) into its contractible
disk, for an original odd form degree q, when n<=2q-1. It uses the exact
relative quotient Q_m(D)/Q_m(K) and shifts the derived obstruction by one.
At q=1,n=2,m=2 the possible relative obstruction group is F_2; no actual
nonzero obstruction is claimed. Both results have explicit finite target
bounds and do not supply one weight for infinitely many arities.

The global source-boundary problem remains: to kill a horizontal cycle
z in X by a bar cell, one first needs U(K(n))->X extending z. The row of
X containing z is precisely a row which is not yet horizontally acyclic.
Target extension results into the eventual theories A and B therefore
do not produce this map into X. Even a perfect relative vertical
base-change theorem does not remove that missing input.

## Shadow-tail extension groups do not shrink with the cutoff

The general graded Ext theorem proved in D002 does not itself annihilate
the remaining horizontal coherent-primitives obstruction. Let I_N be the
weight tail of S, and let P be a simplicial k-module. Apply the exact
shadow left adjoint L and its right adjoint F(M)=M tensor_k S levelwise.
They remain exact adjoints on simplicial module categories, with
L(I_N tensor_k P)=P. Consequently

 RHom_sGrMod_S(I_N tensor_k P, constant S)
       = RHom_sMod_k(P, constant k).

This follows from the derived adjunction because L is exact and F
preserves injectives; no projectivity of I_N is asserted. Increasing N
induces the identity under this identification, by tail rigidity.
Thus delay alone does not remove a nonzero possible horizontal Ext
class. It can make the target's finite comparison range available, but
one still needs the actual class to vanish or a different relative
construction. Root independently raised and checked this limitation.

## Source search for a cotangent amplitude reduction (09:19 UTC)

A possible slope route would require a theorem that a chosen cellular PD
resolution of S/I_N has cotangent homology in horizontal degrees at most
two, so that the first odd-form cells have small dimension. No such
amplitude theorem has been proved. Ordinary complete-intersection
cotangent formulas cannot be imported into this special-PD setting.
Searches found Quillen/Beck cotangent theories and animated PD conormal
formulas, but no checked result with the required strict positive-cochain
and row-bound hypotheses. The existence of higher PD relations and the
C26 noninvariance warning make a blanket amplitude assertion unsafe.
