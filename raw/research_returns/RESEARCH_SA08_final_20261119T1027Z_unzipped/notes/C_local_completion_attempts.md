# Further local-completion attempts and their exact limits

2026-11-19 10:14 UTC, agent /root/topos. These are working-route records,
not additional claims that C3 or the local completion hypothesis is proved.

## 1. Ordinary cohomology does not refute an under-P repair

For the C21 partial model, H1 and H2 vanish at every prime, over every
coefficient ring: its safe range is d<2p-1, and p>=2. The pair target
A times_k B has only eventual H0, while H(f,g) has eventual H0,H1.
Thus every positive vertical cohomology class of P maps to zero in
these targets in the eventual range. This necessary condition gives no
contradiction to the proposed repair. C31 refutes a specified bar
extension, not arbitrary admissible factorizations under P.

A stronger possible invariant is the map on sections: if P factors
through an admissible E mapping to A and B, then the induced maps
H^i(P(X))->H^i(X;k) must agree in sufficiently high weights, since
both maps out of E are the canonical coefficient comparison. No pair
of C21 maps violating that condition has been constructed. Primary
classes gamma_p(dx) are controlled by factorization through divided
powers of an acyclic target row, as in C23; an actual obstruction would
have to survive the mixed-relation and coefficient-tail calculations.

## 2. A two-generator PD presentation, but no cotangent amplitude proof

Let R=Z_(p), p>N>=1, S=Gamma_R(T), and I_N be the full weight tail.
As a PD ideal, I_N is generated by T^[N] and T^[p]. For N<=m<p,
T^[N]T^[m-N] is a p-unit times T^[m]. The p-adic digit expansion
expresses every higher divided-power monomial using T^[p^i], and

 gamma_p(T^[p^i]) = u_i T^[p^(i+1)]

with u_i a p-unit, by Legendre's valuation formula. Thus the second
PD generator supplies every higher digit. The reverse containment is
immediate from weights.

There is even an explicit underlying-ring homological DG-Gamma
resolution of S/I_N with generators only in homological degrees1,2.
Write y_i=T^[p^i] and y_i^p=p a_i y_(i+1), with a_i units. Adjoin odd
e of weightN, odd f_i of weightp^i for i>=1, and even g_i of weightp^i
for i>=1, with homological degrees1,1,2 respectively. Set

 de=T^[N], df_i=y_i,
 dg_1=p u f_1-T^[p-N]e, where binom(p,N)=p u,
 dg_i=p a_(i-1)f_i-y_(i-1)^(p-1)f_(i-1), i>=2.

The differential squares to zero. Modulo p, the coefficient algebra
is the tensor of Fp[y_i]/y_i^p. The i=0 factor is the periodic
matrix-factorization complex with maps y_0^N and y_0^(p-N), up to
units; it resolves Fp[y_0]/y_0^N. Each i>=1 factor is the Cartan complex
with df_i=y_i and dg_(i+1)=-y_i^(p-1)f_i, resolving Fp. Thus the whole
mod-p complex has only H0=Fp[T]/T^N. Its integral H0 is S/I_N, because
the ordinary ideal generated by T^[N] and all y_i (i>=1) is the full
tail. Fixed-weight finite freeness, universal coefficients, and
Nakayama give the same integral resolution. The weightwise PID
splittings prove arbitrary base-change exactness.

This calculation does NOT prove that a cellular free simplicial PD
resolution has special cotangent amplitude at most two. The displayed
object is an underlying-ring homological DG-Gamma model; comparing its
PD indecomposables to those of a simplicial PD resolution is exactly a
step that needs proof. C26 disproves blanket indecomposable invariance.
No slope n<=2q for all necessary horizontal defect cells follows from
this finite-degree generator display alone.

## 3. A natural rational image and its target-map failure

For a single odd disk type D(M)=Gamma(My) tensor Lambda(Mx), let h be
the odd rational derivation h(y)=x, h(x)=0. The commutator dh+hd is
parameter arity. On the positive bar construction, a candidate natural
rational projection is

 phi(b0[a1|...|al]) = b0 h(a1)...h(al)
                   / product_i (m_i+...+m_l),

where m_i is the arity of a_i. The differential identity follows by
telescoping adjacent suffix sums. The shuffle identity follows by
splitting shuffles according to their first letter. For the bar
coefficient-insertion contraction kappa one has
phi kappa=(h/total arity)phi, because h squared is zero on every a_i.
These formulas suggest a naturally contracted image agreeing with the
original disk in arities less than p over Z_(p).

Regardless of the closure/exactness details, its target-map problem is
definitively fatal to a universal extension argument: it contains
x(u1)...x(up)/p!, which forces divisibility of a product of arbitrary
odd target elements. C35 gives a fully proved and independently checked
p=2,w=2 example in the standard admissible target, with acyclic input
and the stated acyclic output row. Therefore this image cannot replace
the full bar algebra when maps to the original targets must extend.
Nonlinear simplicial faces also need not preserve the Euler contraction
of a free linear-disk presentation. No repair theorem is inferred.

## 4. Brutal cochain splicing does not give a completion

A formal attempt to complete a finite truncation is

 E = T times_(T_<d) P_<d.

It is a strict PD-DGA, agrees with P below d, and has T's terms in
degrees at least d. But its boundary differential is
P^(d-1) -> T^d, induced by f. The degree-d cohomology can contain
B^d(T)/f(B^d(P)); degree d-1 can also gain classes whose differential
is killed by f. Knowing that P and T have the correct low cohomology
does not identify these boundary modules. Replacing the brutal
truncation by a good cochain truncation does not solve this formally:
good truncation is not automatically a strict multiplicative PD
construction in these positive cochain degrees.

## 5. Projective covers preserve rows but create lower relations

One can cover a connected simplicial cycle module by a direct sum of
contractible projective disks and adjoin primitives indexed by that
cover. Reduced free functors of the contractible parameter preserve
horizontal acyclicity. However the kernel of such a cover has H0 equal
to the original module's H1, and further syzygies need not have bounded
projective dimension in simplicial modules. The simple global-dimension-
one argument for ordinary Z_(p)-modules does not provide a short
simplicially projective resolution. Prescribing the images of those
relations in both targets reintroduces the coherent-primitive problem.
No nonnegative, uniformly high-degree repair has been built this way.

## Current precise scope

C30 and C32 prove arithmetic and loop descent conditional on actual
low-degree-preserving local repairs. C34 weakens those sufficient data
to compatible finite-form truncations, with no full under-P map.
No local completion theorem with depths tending to infinity has yet
been established. The strengthened sufficient condition is not known
to be necessary or equivalent to integral C3, so it is not by itself a
new coefficient case or an intrinsic smaller settled gate.
