## An independent elementary mismatch

For a p-torsionfree cochain complex \(C\), Definition 1.2 specifies

\[
D^n(C)=p^nZ^n(C)+p^{n+1}C^n.
\]

The usual décalage subcomplex has

\[
(\eta_p C)^n=p^n\{x\in C^n:dx\in pC^{n+1}\}.
\]

They differ even in a two-term free complex. Let \(C^1=C^2=\mathbb Z_p\), with differential multiplication by \(p\), and all other positive-degree terms zero. Then

\[
D(C)=\bigl[p^2\mathbb Z_p\xrightarrow{p}p^2\mathbb Z_p\bigr],
\qquad H^2D(C)=\mathbb F_p,
\]

whereas

\[
\eta_p C=\bigl[p\mathbb Z_p\xrightarrow{p}p^2\mathbb Z_p\bigr]
\]

is acyclic. The complexes are therefore not even quasi-isomorphic. An additional degree-zero copy of \(\mathbb Z_p\) can be included without changing this calculation.

In fact the additive cohomology of \(D(C)\) is always isomorphic to that of \(C\): its degree-\(n\) cocycles are \(p^nZ^n(C)\), and its degree-\(n\) boundaries are \(p^nB^n(C)\). Thus the ordinary-cohomology statement for the explicitly defined shifted complex does not justify identifying it with \(\eta_p\), which in this example removes the p-torsion.

This calculation refutes only the displayed décalage identification in this generality. It is not an audit of the entire construction of the forms \(\Omega\), nor a determination of the author's announced proof issue. In particular, the coefficient map \(\Gamma_{\mathbb Z}(T)\to\mathbb Z_p\), \(T\mapsto p\), need not allow replacing the derived-tail complexes of A-006 by the underived ideals generated by \(p^n/n!\). Doing that would be an unjustified base-change shortcut.
