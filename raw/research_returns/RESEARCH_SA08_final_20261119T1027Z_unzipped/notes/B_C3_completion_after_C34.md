# Remaining local completion gate after C34

2026-11-19, /root/automorphisms. This records the precise unproved
steps considered after the full C34 audits. It asserts no additional
theorem and no qualifying movement on global C3.

## What the weakened data actually prescribe

C34 retains only P/P^{>=d_p} and its map to T/T^{>=d_p} at prime p.
This is finite in form degree, but it is not finite in weights, simplicial
dimensions, number of generators, or number of operations in form degree
zero. One still needs an entire admissible E_p and an actual map E_p->T_p.
The requirement d_p->infinity is still quantified over all primes.

The three independent complete written audits (root, D, topos) agree
that finite-idempotent assembly and rational marked overlap are proved.
They do not prove that those local completions exist. No equivalence or
necessity of this sufficient hypothesis has been established.

## Direct replacement of the high cochain terms

At the level of graded modules it is tempting to use P^q for q<d and
T^q for q>=d, with the outgoing differential f d at d-1. Multiplication
can be defined using f whenever the output crosses the cutoff. But
the kernel in degree d-1 becomes ker(f d), not ker(d). Thus this splice
can introduce cohomology at the very boundary it was intended to
preserve. Without an injectivity or a suitable relative acyclicity
condition on f, it is not an admissible completion. C34 does not use it.

Rationalizing all higher terms has a related boundary defect: new
rational cycles need not lie in the image of the unchanged integral
boundary. Moreover a map from such new rational elements to the
specified p-local target need not exist. This does not provide the
required target factorization.

## Free closures and delayed weights

The p-local acyclic odd-disk closure of C18 introduces its first new
generators in degrees growing with p. This is useful for a single
algebraic disk. A simplicial map to T still requires compatible
primitives and all their relations; the relative source boundary
cannot be assumed (C31).

C28 gives coordinated absolute lifts through any fixed finite arity,
and the base-change refinement of C29 gives the valid p-local slope
bounds absolute n<=pq and relative n<pq. The latter still assumes a
full boundary map. They do not cover the unbounded simplicial cell
dimensions in the global horizontal acyclization.

There is also an independent weight issue. Closing arity m of a
parameter in weight w forces output weight mw and form range through
m(q+1). An eventual target has a threshold for that fixed form range,
but its axioms give no single w satisfying all such inequalities as
m grows. Failure at finitely many low weights is allowed, but its
coefficient multiples can lie in arbitrarily large weights. Killing
only the required coefficient tails is not the same as adjoining a
free disk on each weight independently: S-linearity imposes relations.
No coherent tail attachment preserving the low form truncation has
been supplied.

## Review judgment

C34 removes a genuine unnecessary requirement of the C32 sufficient
construction: the entire high-form map from the chosen P need not
survive. Nevertheless no new coefficient case of the main assertion,
actual local completion, intrinsic invariant, or counterexample is
obtained. Under the user's strict movement rule, this conditional
weakening alone should not be counted as a demonstrably smaller
main-question gate. The exact unresolved construction remains a
coherent admissible completion of the specified low form truncations
with depths increasing across all primes.
