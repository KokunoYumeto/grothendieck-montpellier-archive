# New finite obstruction for every rank-one numerical tail deformation

Let R=Int(Z) with degree filtration. Let M_i (i=1,2) be exhaustive filtered R-modules, bounded below in weight, with specified graded isomorphisms gr M_i=I_i over gr R=Gamma_Z(T). There is no filtered R-bilinear pairing M_1×M_1→M_2 whose associated graded is the PD coefficient multiplication.

Every finite filtration quotient has an integral basis, since each new graded group is Z, and M_i is torsion-free over Z. After rationalization its graded module is the shifted free Q[T]-module, so a lift m_i of the bottom generator makes M_i tensor Q=Q[T]m_i. This follows by eliminating the leading term of an arbitrary finite-degree element, inductively, and by the same leading-term argument for linear independence. Identify m_i with1. Choose integral lifts m_n (n>=i) of all specified graded basis elements. Then m_n is a polynomial of degree n-i with leading coefficient i!/n!.

The action of T has the exact form T m_n=(n+1)m_{n+1}+sum_{j<=n} a_j m_j, with integer coefficients a_j. Induction proves that every coefficient of m_n lies in (i!/n!)Z. This bounds lower coefficients as well as leading ones; arbitrary lower-weight deformation parameters cause no problem.

Fix an odd prime p, for example3. Work over Z_(p), and reduce integral polynomials modulo p. For M_1 put f=p m_p modulo p. It is a polynomial of degree p-1 with nonzero leading coefficient. The coefficient bound makes all m_n with n<p integral at p. The recurrence at n=p implies that pF_{p+1}M_1 modulo p is spanned by f and T f. But beta_p times the bottom generator belongs to F_{p+1}M_1, and p beta_p modulo p is a nonzero scalar multiple of P=T^p-T. Thus f divides P. It has degree p-1, so f=c P/(T-a) for some a in F_p and c nonzero. In particular f(a) is nonzero because P has distinct roots.

For M_2 write its chosen basis as n_j, with n_2=1. Put g=p n_p modulo p and h=p^2 n_{2p} modulo p. All basis elements of weight below2p have at most one p-denominator. Therefore p^2 F_{2p}M_2 modulo p is the single line spanned by h. The exact leading term in beta_p n_p is binom(2p,p)n_{2p}; all remaining terms have lower weight. Multiplication by p^2 and reduction modulo p gives (p beta_p mod p)g=binom(2p,p)h. The latter binomial coefficient is a p-unit. Hence h is a nonzero scalar multiple of P g.

A required pairing sends (m_1,m_1) to2 n_2 exactly, since F_1M_2=0. After rationalization it is therefore exactly2 times ordinary polynomial multiplication. Applying it to m_p,m_p, multiplying by p^2, and reducing modulo p gives 2 f^2 in the line spanned by h, hence in F_p·P g. Evaluation at a contradicts f(a) nonzero and p odd. This proves the module-pairing obstruction with only weights through2p; p=3 uses weights through6.
