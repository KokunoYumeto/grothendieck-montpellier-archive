\subsection{Integral uniqueness: the remaining exact assertion}
For a commutative ring $k$ with infinitely many noninvertible rational
primes, consider the category of generalized strict PD theories with
the uniform eventual conditions of scans~117 and~119, as in
Statement~\ref{sa08:C17}. The two assertions still to be decided are
whether every pair of objects has an admissible common source, and
whether the localization at all morphisms is terminal. The smallest
coefficient ring not settled here is $k=\Z$. We do not infer either
assertion from the local results: Statement~\ref{sa08:C12} excludes
the direct extension of the elementary-source gluing when its first
flasque weights are unbounded. Statements~\ref{sa08:C14} and
\ref{sa08:C15} exclude two free replacement constructions, without
refuting the assertions themselves. Statement~\ref{sa08:C07}
already refutes strict common-source equalization, which is a
different, stronger assertion. Statement~\ref{sa08:C30} proves an actual common-source construction
conditional on compatible local repairs of the partial model that retain
all form degrees below bounds tending to infinity with the prime.
Statement~\ref{sa08:C32} gives a conditional reduction of both assertions
to compatible local repairs for pair targets and the specified homotopy
equalizers. Statement~\ref{sa08:C34} weakens the sufficient local input to
agreement of finite form truncations, without retaining all higher
relations of the partial model. Those local completions have not been
constructed, and their necessity has not been proved.
Statement~\ref{sa08:C31} shows why an
automatic prescribed bar-boundary extension cannot be assumed, even
in an admissible local target. The remaining global statements are
unchecked; no proof of them is claimed in this paper.
