## What is established

The known Casalaina-Martin–Laza weight48 form, made by an E6 quasi-pullback of Borcherds Phi12 and restriction to the cubic-threefold 10-ball, maps to a nonzero scalar multiple of the degree80 discriminant under the actual Looijenga–Swierstra ring isomorphism. In the actual ordinary cubic plethysm algebra it belongs to stretch degree16. The polynomial subalgebra generated by this form is identified with the one generated by the discriminant, including multiplication. This is a concrete particular invariant, not an unidentified analogy or a positive formula for all multiplicities. No historical priority is claimed.
