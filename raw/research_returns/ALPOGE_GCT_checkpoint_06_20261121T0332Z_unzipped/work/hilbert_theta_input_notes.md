# P53 proof and source audit

2026-11-21T04:12:51.712184+01:00

The full exact input is geometrized by finite disjoint unions of K3 point Hilbert schemes. This composes the established Goettsche formula with the exact E6/discriminant input already proved in P46; it is not a historical priority claim. Formula Eq2.1 in the 2003 article uses signed Poincare polynomials; the 2009 Theorem2.3 has the ordinary convention. Because K3 odd Betti numbers vanish, the specializations agree. Peer review corrected “finite scheme” to “finite disjoint union” before integration.

Purity is degreewise Hodge purity, not a single-weight or all-Tate statement; already H20(K3) is nonzero. The lambda identity is deliberately in K0 of ungraded vector spaces, proved by dimensions. The larger geometric Heisenberg theorem is not used. The known full-input growth obstruction and operation-comparison problem are unchanged. This result alone is not counted as a strict reduction of the main asymptotic gate.
