Recorded by GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI, ChatGPT Pro mode), 19 November 2026, around 20:02 UTC. This is a source audit for candidate A.135, not a promoted theorem.

We opened the original full text and explicitly viewed rendered images of Essay V printed pages 229 and 240. The page images are retained outside the authored deliverable directory with the fetched originals.

On page 229, (1.5) is the sliced automorphism sequence Aut_CAT(M_sigma) → Aut_TOP(M) → CAT(M). The quotient injects into the structure space, and its image is a union of components containing the base structure, not necessarily every structure component. The source explicitly gives relative homotopy groups only for k ≥ 1, not k=0. Its relative closed-subset version uses automorphisms fixing some neighborhood of C and structures agreeing near C. We must prove the collar normalization when converting pointwise boundary conditions to this germ condition.

On page 240, (3.1) and (3.2) compare the relative structure fiber with a relative tangent-bundle lift space. The restriction is a Kan fibration in dimension m ≥ 5. The m=4 exception requires H^4(M,C; Z/2)=0; it cannot be silently applied to the general three-dimensional base cylinder. The five-dimensional ambient-cylinder route is therefore dimensionally admissible after extending collars to an open manifold.

The displayed ball calculation (3.3) is CAT(B^m rel boundary) ≃ Omega^m(TOP_m/CAT_m), before passing from structures to automorphisms. Its loop shift must not be confused with the additional loop arising from the automorphism comparison fiber. The relative structure component at the chosen PL structure is the relevant component for that additional loop.

Earlier text inspection in the same root turn also checked Essay V 5.5(I), printed p.251: TOP_m/PL_m ≃ K(Z/2,3) for m ≥ 5. A chosen PL lift gives a pointed fiber. Untwisting needs the weak contractibility of the pointed self-equivalence space of K(Z/2,3), not merely the triviality of the action on Z/2.

These checks alone do not prove the candidate free-top fiber sequence or settle the remaining four-dimensional comparison.
