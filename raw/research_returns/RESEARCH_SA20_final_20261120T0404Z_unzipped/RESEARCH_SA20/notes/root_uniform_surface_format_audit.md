# Root audit: a uniform finite format for individual surface approximants

We read the full candidate
`agents/maximal_locus/notes/uniform_surface_approximants_2104.tex`.
The mathematical proof passes, subject to the requested explicit empty
cases and inclusion of the ambient realization dimension in the certificate
index; the author subsequently confirmed both repairs. This audit is of
the format lemma only. It does not claim continuous dependence on the
parameters or resolution of the original A.5 gate.

The fixed structure is an o-minimal expansion of the real field. For a
definable family of continuous cell-like surjections between fixed compact
closed surfaces, one formula suffices to select a homeomorphism at every
positive error tolerance. The selector need not be continuous in either
the family parameter or the tolerance.

We checked the compactness argument as follows. Continuity of each map,
surjectivity, and compactness of its embedded mapping cylinder are
first-order assertions about the given fixed family and remain true in
elementary extensions. For each finite complex, finite ambient dimension
and finite graph formula, the displayed certificate quantifies only
finitely many realization vertices, graph parameters and scalar vertex
heights. Affine independence, exact face intersections, bijectivity,
epsilon–delta continuity in both directions, and the barycentric scalar
identity are all first-order predicates. The formula permits arbitrary
finite field parameters, so every actual definable scalar triangulation
is among the certificates.

Every model of the complete theory is o-minimal: each unary formula's
uniform finite component bound is a sentence of that theory. Shiota's
scalar theorem therefore supplies a certificate in every elementary
extension, independently of whether a cell-like condition is available
there. If no finite set of certificate formats covered the original
parameter set, the negations of every certificate would be finitely
satisfiable with the elementary diagram. Logical compactness would
produce an elementary extension and a parameter contradicting scalar
triangulation. This proves a finite subcover without assuming a countable
language, an effective theory, or an enumeration algorithm.

Definable choice in this finite list gives selected coefficients, with
no continuity conclusion. Passing to basis-vector realizations fixes the
abstract domain complex. A finite zero-vertex partition fixes its zero
subcomplex. Back over the real field, and only there, A5.016's cell-like
argument makes every occurring complex a PL three-manifold with that
zero subcomplex a union of boundary components. There are finitely many
pairs, so a fixed PL collar can be chosen for each pair. This use of
classical topology is not transferred to an arbitrary elementary extension.

One finite subdivision of each fixed collar makes all selected scalar
heights linear, because the original domain complex is fixed and only
its vertex values vary. The small-band bound is a positive definable
minimum on a fixed compact complement, together with finitely many
positive vertex heights. The mixed-simplex stellar point formula uses
the zero-face and positive-face barycenters and the ratio epsilon to the
positive barycenter height. It is interior, has exactly the desired height,
and depends rationally on the finite coefficients. Thus the matched-star
argument supplies one definable family of collars with fixed combinatorics.

The coordinate extraction of A5.016 then gives a single definable family
of homeomorphisms converging to each q_a as its internal collar parameter
tends to zero. The uniform error for fixed a is a definable maximum on
the compact source. Another definable choice selects a sufficiently small
internal scale for each requested tolerance. This proves the stated
finite-format selector.

We also checked the exact transition identity: for homeomorphisms u and v
of the same source onto the same target, both uniform distances of
u composed with v inverse and its inverse from identity equal the uniform
distance from u to v. No inverse of the limiting cell-like map occurs.
This supports a possible sewing argument but does not prove it. All
extra-parameter continuity and finite-pool contraction steps remain
separate until their own complete proof is audited.
