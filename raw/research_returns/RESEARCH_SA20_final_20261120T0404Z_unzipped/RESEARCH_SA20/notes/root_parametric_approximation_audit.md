## Gauld 1976: finite improvement versus the limit

We opened David B. Gauld, *Local contractibility of spaces of homeomorphisms*,
Compositio32(1), pp.3–11, original scan at
<https://www.numdam.org/item/CM_1976__32_1_3_0.pdf>.
Theorem1 and Propositions5–6 were checked, and the singularities agent
independently inspected the endpoint argument. Proposition5 uses finitely many
compositions of the input embedding, its inverse and radial PL maps.
Proposition6 repeats such improvements finitely many times for a fixed
improvement factor. Those constructions are plausible definable operations
when supplied with definable input. Section4, however, joins infinitely many
successive improvements to obtain the identity at its endpoint. The paper
does not establish joint definability of that infinite concatenation. Hence
it does not fill the semialgebraic concordance gate. Its finite stages remain
potential ingredients for a finite replacement argument.

## Yagasaki 1997: the exact meaning of PL

We downloaded the original *PL approximations of fiber preserving
homeomorphisms of vector bundles*, Tsukuba21(1), pp.181–198, from
<https://tsukuba.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/17163/files/14.pdf>.
The retrieval receipt is `logs/yagasaki_fetch.json`; the inspected original
is `source_external/yagasaki1997.pdf` in scratch. We read pp.181–183 and
visually checked printed p.182. Definition(i) requires the restriction to
each individual vector-space fibre to be PL. The main theorem, for vector
dimension at least five and countable-dimensional metrizable base, gives
approximation of stable fibre-preserving homeomorphisms in that sense.
It does not provide a finite joint PL or semialgebraic family across the
base. Thus its conclusion cannot be substituted for the projection-preserving,
jointly definable approximation required here.

Both are checked limitations of attempted routes, not refutations of the
main comparison theorem. Neither changes its remaining gate.
