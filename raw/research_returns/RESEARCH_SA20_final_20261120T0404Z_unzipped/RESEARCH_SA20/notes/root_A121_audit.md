# Independent root audit of A.121

Status: PASS in dimension at least nine, with no smoothability hypothesis on the original manifold.

We read the complete draft and the corrected locators. A collar push embeds a compact manifold with boundary into its interior without changing its homotopy type. Its three-skeleton has a finite derived regular neighbourhood N. The normalized barycentric-coordinate formulas retract N onto the skeleton and N minus the skeleton onto its boundary. Cellular approximation gives pi_i(M,N)=0 for i<=3. Relative general position in dimension at least9 gives pi0 and pi1 isomorphisms from the complement of the three-dimensional spine; for a disk argument one may first choose an embedded PL representative boundary loop. This verifies the boundary condition required by localization.

The finite simplex-thickening construction supplies handles of index at most3. The original Kirby–Siebenmann handle-smoothing argument makes N compatibly smoothable. Thus A.119 applies to N, even though M may be nonsmoothable. The BLR localization map is actual extension by identity. With r=3,k=0 its relative connectivity bound is min(3,1,5)=1, so it induces a bijection on components. It preserves composition exactly, making this a group isomorphism. Hence the complete presentation E(K^(3)) computes the whole component group, including its extension law. This does not rely on an unproved assertion that every nonsmoothable unstable group has stabilized.
