We independently opened Galvin–Nonino, *Pseudo-isotopy versus isotopy for
homeomorphisms of 4-manifolds*, at
https://dapgalvin.github.io/PI_vs_I.pdf (55-page original; root reference
`turn534500048view0`). Printed page 4, Remark 1.7, expressly distinguishes
their two obstruction maps from the high-dimensional exact sequence,
which fails in TOP dimension four even with infinite-cyclic fundamental
group. The preceding paragraph explains why the earlier Kwasik use of
the smooth invariant needs a separate TOP definition. Theorem 1.5 on
page 3 requires a nonambivalent fundamental group and lists lens spaces
only for p at least 3; it therefore supplies no RP3 instance. None of
these invariant-realization results proves the required PL-image
sufficiency for pseudo-isotopies of a three-manifold.

We independently opened Jahren–Kwasik, *Free involutions on S1 × Sn*,
Math. Ann. 351 (2011), 281–303, DOI 10.1007/s00208-010-0599-y, at
https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s00208-010-0599-y.pdf
(root references `turn534500048view1`, `turn485617086view0` and
`turn485617086view1`). Remark 1.6 on printed page 284 concerns concordance
classes of endpoint homeomorphisms, expressed through the block
homeomorphism space. Equation (3.4) on page 294 computes the relative
TOP structure set of the cylinder; for n=3 the group is F2.
Proposition 3.5 on the same page separates a tangential homotopy
equivalence with nontrivial normal invariant from the classes represented
by homeomorphisms. These objects and equivalence relations differ from
the component group of the actual pseudo-isotopy space. We therefore
do not identify its kernel or the PL image from these computations.
