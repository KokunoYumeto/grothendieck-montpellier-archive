\subsection{Transferred regularity criteria and graph representatives}

\begin{saresult}{A.149}{Proved under stated hypotheses}{The regularity criteria in theories with a real model}
Let \(M\) be a fixed o-minimal expansion of a real closed field whose
complete theory has a real model, and let \(B\) be a closed compact PL
manifold over \(M\). Write \(N=B\times I\) and \(P=I^k\), \(k\geq1\).
The following sufficient conditions place a based definable
concordance class in the image of
\(\pi_kP_{\rm PL}(B)\to\pi_kP_{\rm def}(B)\).
\begin{enumerate}
\item It has a representative \(V\) with a uniform forward parameter
Lipschitz bound:
\[
 \|V_p(x)-V_q(x)\|_\infty\leq L\|p-q\|_\infty
 \quad(x\in N,\ p,q\in P)
\]
for an element \(L\) of the field.
\item It has a representative \(s_pr_p^{-1}\), where \(r,s\) are joint
definable families of homeomorphisms of \(N\), preserve both ends
setwise, agree on the bottom and on the parameter boundary, and have
uniform forward parameter Lipschitz bounds.
\end{enumerate}
Conversely, every class in the PL image has representatives satisfying
both conditions. No bound on either inverse is required.
\end{saresult}

\begin{proof}
The original proof transferred the real criteria by finite coefficient
families. Its literal TeX was lost during the runtime replacement.
We give a complete replacement proof using the subsequently established
stronger result A.160; this changes the proof dependency, not the
displayed assertion.

A.160 states that every based definable concordance class over \(M\)
has a jointly PL representative \(W\), with a based definable
concordance homotopy from the original family. It applies to every
fixed o-minimal real closed field, so in particular to the model in
the statement. This proves sufficiency in the first case. In the
second case, the quotient \(s_pr_p^{-1}\) is a joint definable family
of homeomorphisms: the total inverse map is definable and continuous,
and composition preserves these properties. Equality of the factors
on the bottom makes the quotient fix the bottom pointwise, while
equality on the parameter boundary makes it based there. Preservation
of both ends gives the required top condition. It is therefore a
family to which A.160 applies.

For the converse, choose a jointly PL representative \(W\) of a class
in the image. Its total map on the compact finite polyhedron
\(N\times P\) has finitely many affine pieces. A finite PL map between
compact polyhedra is Lipschitz in fixed polyhedral embeddings; this
follows by the finite linear subdivision estimate, or by summing the
affine estimates along a segment after using finitely many fixed
polyhedral charts. Taking a maximum of the finitely many field-valued
bounds gives an element \(L\in M\). Holding the spatial variable fixed
gives the displayed forward parameter bound. Thus \(V=W\) satisfies
the first condition. For the second, take \(r_p=\operatorname{id}_N\)
and \(s_p=W_p\). Both preserve the ends, and they agree on the bottom
and on \(\partial P\), since \(W\) is a based concordance family.
They have the required forward bounds. This proves both directions.

We have not rounded \(L\) to an ordinary integer, nor asserted that a
path through PL coefficient data is a jointly PL homotopy. The latter
distinction is handled inside A.115 and A.160.
\end{proof}

\begin{saresult}{A.153}{Proved under stated hypotheses}{A graph-regular representative in a real-model theory}
Under the hypotheses on \(M,B,N,P\) in A.149, every based definable
concordance family \(U\) is definably homotopic, through based
concordance families, to a family \(V\) whose compact graphs are
uniformly Lipschitz in the Hausdorff metric. Thus there is an
\(L\in M\) such that
\[
 d_H(\Gamma V_p,\Gamma V_q)\leq L\|p-q\|_\infty
 \quad(p,q\in P).
\]
\end{saresult}

\begin{proof}
We give a replacement proof from the all-field regularization
A.157--A.158. The earlier real-model transfer proof only asserted
existence of a homotopic representative; it did not assert literal
precomposition over a nonreal field. The stronger input now available
does give such a precomposition, and we verify it here.

Choose a finite PL model and a fixed affine scaling so that its graph
ambient space lies in a unit cube. Let \(C_p=\Gamma U_p\).
Joint continuity of \(U\) on the definably compact domain implies
Hausdorff continuity of \(p\mapsto C_p\): if \(p\) approaches \(q\),
uniform continuity bounds \(U_p(x)-U_q(x)\), and matching equal input
points in the two graphs bounds their Hausdorff distance. Each graph
is nonempty and compact unless \(B\) is empty. The empty case has a
single concordance and is immediate. The family \(C_p\) is constant
on \(\partial P\), since \(U\) is based.

Apply the relative compact-family conclusion of A.158, proved using
the finite join resolution of A.157. It supplies a continuous
definable map \(g:P\to P\), equal to the identity on \(\partial P\),
for which \(C_{g(p)}\) is Hausdorff Lipschitz. Set \(V_p=U_{g(p)}\).
The total map and inverse of this family are continuous and definable
by composition. It fixes the spatial bottom, preserves the top, and is
the identity on the parameter boundary. The family
\[
 H_{p,t}=U_{(1-t)p+t g(p)},\qquad (p,t)\in P\times I,
\]
is defined because the cube is convex. At \(t=0\) it is \(U\), at
\(t=1\) it is \(V\), and on \(\partial P\) it is always the identity.
It is the required concordance homotopy. Undoing the fixed PL model
and affine scaling changes the finite Hausdorff constant by a finite
field-valued factor, because these coordinate changes are bi-Lipschitz.
This proves the stated conclusion.

The inputs used in this replacement proof are valid over every fixed
o-minimal real closed field. The real-model hypothesis retained in the
statement is therefore unnecessary after A.158; no claim that it was
already unnecessary in the earlier transfer proof is made.
\end{proof}
