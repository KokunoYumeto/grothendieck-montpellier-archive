\subsection*{Part A: exact current remaining gates}
The following are unresolved original task scopes. A proved restricted
case or a more explicit obstruction is not counted as a solution of its
larger scope.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{A.1(b), the main concordance computation.}
The PL and definable sides are now determined by an explicit whole-space
model. A.160 proves the weak equivalence of the actual jointly PL and
jointly definable concordance simplicial groups over every fixed
o-minimal real closed field, in every base dimension. This includes
non-Archimedean fields and theories without a real model. A.159 supplies
higher surjectivity with standard bounds; A.160 proves injectivity by
first using A.115 to move the PL boundary to real-algebraic coefficients,
then preserving that exact boundary in the relative filling.

A.161 gives the precise effective consequence. For a chosen finite PL
model $K$ of the compact definable base, there is an explicit zigzag of
weak equivalences from $G^{\rm PL}_{\mathbb R_{\rm alg}}(K)$ to the whole
definable concordance space. Finite codes have decidable validity and
equality and computable group and simplicial operations. Its full Moore
quotients give complete recursively enumerable presentations of
$\pi_0$ first and then every $\pi_r$. No oracle for the theory of the
o-minimal expansion is required. These are exhaustive countable
presentations, not finite invariant factors or a terminating isotopy
or word-problem decision. A.103 and A.119--A.130 retain their more
structural component calculations in their stated ranges.

The remaining original A.1(b) gate is the whole real TOP computation
and its actual comparison in base dimensions three and four. The same
effective model computes the real TOP space if every base component has
dimension outside $\{3,4\}$, by A.113 and A.134. The empty base and finite
disconnected products are included. A.132 already refutes a universal
PL--TOP weak equivalence: $\pi_2P_{\rm PL}(S^3)=0$ while
$\pi_2P_{\rm TOP}(S^3)=\mathbb Z/2$.

In dimension three, A.143 constructs the natural homomorphism
\[
 c_B:\pi_0P_{\rm TOP}(B)\longrightarrow H^2(B;\mathbb Z/2),
 \qquad \operatorname{im}(\pi_0P_{\rm PL}(B))\subseteq\ker c_B.
\]
The real definable image equals the PL image. A.142 gives an explicit
finite $\mathbb {RP}^3$ base and a nonzero ordinary concordance obstruction;
A.144 proves surjectivity of $c_B$ for oriented closed three-manifolds
with finite fundamental group. A.145 realizes the corresponding supported
connected-sum summands in arbitrary ambient bases. A.146 computes
$c_{T^3}=0$. None identifies all zero-class concordances with PL classes.
A concrete unresolved component test is whether
\[
 c_{\mathbb {RP}^3}([h])=0
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 [h]\in\operatorname{im}\bigl(\pi_0P_{\rm PL}(\mathbb {RP}^3)\bigr).
\]
Even a positive answer would leave the kernel of the PL-to-TOP component
map and the relevant extension data to be evaluated. Stable
pseudo-smoothability in Galvin's Proposition~2.27 is not the required
unstabilized ordinary isotopy statement \cite{Galvin2026}.

In dimension four, A.135--A.136 give the explicit based-fibre model
\[
 F_P\simeq\operatorname{hofib}\!\left(
 \beta_B:F_A\longrightarrow\operatorname{Map}(B,K(\mathbb Z/2,2))
 \right).
\]
The map $\beta_B$ is the actual loop of product-with-a-line stabilization
on sliced PL structures followed by the open five-dimensional
classification. Evaluating the base-automorphism comparison fibre $F_A$
and this map, including their components, is still required. A.137 gives
the exact component-image criterion: the TOP endpoint must first be
isotopic to a PL map; its relative cylinder class must then vanish modulo
$J_B=\operatorname{im}(\pi_0\beta_B)$. On the endpoint-admissible subgroup
$I_B$, this is a homomorphism to $H^2(B;\mathbb Z/2)/J_B$ whose kernel is
precisely the PL concordance image. The endpoint predicate, $J_B$, and
the obstruction image remain unevaluated in general.

A.140 gives assembly-controlled realizations and, for simply connected
$B$, ordinary TOP automorphism loops realizing all of $H^2(B;\mathbb Z/2)$.
A.141 realizes extension-by-zero classes from simply connected compact
codimension-zero supports, without imposing good-group or assembly
hypotheses on the ambient base. A.139 gives the entire ordinary TOP
sphere model as the fibre of the actual finite-rank stabilization
$Y_n\to Y_{n+1}$, with
$Y_n=\operatorname{hofib}(B\mathrm O(n)\to B\mathrm{TOP}(n))$.
Its four-sphere consequence gives vanishing through degree three;
these results do not evaluate the remaining general TOP model.

The previous higher PL--SA and PL--definable gates are closed by
A.160. Finite-format local contractions, coefficient-transfer methods,
scalar clocks, and Hausdorff regularization remain useful intermediate
results, but are no longer listed as open comparison gates. A.151's
homeomorphic-reparameterization obstruction is compatible with A.160,
whose regularizing parameter maps need not be injective.
\item \textbf{A.5 and the stated A.6 scope.}
The separate A.5 list retains maximal-triviality-locus definability for
fibres of dimension at least two over bases of dimension at least three.
A5.012 settles every proper definable finite-fibre map, and A5.013
settles every proper definable map to a target of dimension at most two,
over an arbitrary real closed field. A5.014 settles all compact graph
fibres in every target dimension using continuous definable anchors
and canonical length maps. A5.016 proves coherent definable approximation
for one real-definable cell-like map between closed surfaces, using
a finite height-preserving PL collar. It does not give continuous
dependence on extra parameters or extend that conclusion to every
real closed field; the maximal-locus gate is unchanged.
A5.017 supplies one finite definable format for arbitrarily close
homeomorphisms in a family of cell-like maps between fixed closed real
surfaces. A5.018 now proves finite-format continuous sewing, including
its explicit family-cylinder product; its surface application has real
ground field. A5.019 reduces exact local-bundle recognition to finitely
many radial normalizations and an angular homeomorphism certificate.
Each fixed certificate format defines a locus. The general all-model
definability assertion is equivalent to a finite pool of such formats;
that finite-pool assertion remains unproved. The arbitrary-field surface
application and general angular matching are not supplied by the
special real surface cylinder.
A5.011 refutes TOP axis-cancellation by an explicit
nonzero end obstruction and a homeomorphism fixing the axis. The strict
partition claim is refuted in PL, semialgebraic, complex algebraic and
complex analytic contexts. A.6 reconstruction is proved for the
explicit conical-control and compatible-corner data, with the exact
compact two-dimensional groupoid augmentation. No unrestricted
noncompact classification or identification with undefined manuscript
terms is inferred.
\end{enumerate}

A.1(d) is refuted in the categorical convention stipulated in the prompt

singular attachment is allowed. Its proof uses individual approximation
and uniform separation, without a parametric approximation assumption.
