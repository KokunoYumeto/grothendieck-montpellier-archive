# RP3 cylinder source-scope and access audit

Authored 2026-11-20 UTC during the A162 follow-up. This is a research note, not a promoted theorem. Compiled proof sources and aggregate records were left unchanged.

## Exact unresolved point

For a TOP concordance of RP3 with normalized PL top endpoint, vanishing of its formal relative Casson--Sullivan class is necessary for its component to contain a PL concordance. The missing assertion is an actual unstabilized TOP isotopy to such a PL map, retaining the bottom condition. An ordinary formal rank-four type calculation, an individual TOP product theorem, or a stable/sliced comparison does not establish that assertion.

## Freshly opened originals

1. S. Kwasik, *Low-dimensional concordances, Whitney towers and isotopies*, Math. Proc. Cambridge Philos. Soc. 102 (1987), 103--119, DOI 10.1017/S0305004100067098. Original PDF: https://www.maths.gla.ac.uk/~mpowell/Kwasik-low-dimensional-concordances-whitney-towers-and-isotopies.pdf . Web refs turn574664466view0--3. Proposition 3.2 (printed p108), Theorem 3.3 (p109), Remark 3.4 (p113), and Proposition 4.1 (p114) were read. These concern four-dimensional bases and selected obstruction realization; the simply connected isotopy theorem does not apply to RP3 times I. No full low-dimensional PL/TOP comparison exact sequence was obtained.

2. D. Galvin and I. Nonino, *Pseudo-isotopy versus isotopy for homeomorphisms of 4-manifolds*, author original https://dapgalvin.github.io/PI_vs_I.pdf . Fresh full original open: turn798827852view0, 55 pages, 3384 parsed lines. Theorems 1.1--1.2 (printed pp1--2) construct TOP obstruction maps and prove realization for good groups. Theorem 1.5 and its examples (p3) do not apply to RP3: lens-space applications require p at least 3. The paragraph beginning with the discussion of Kwasik on printed p4 explicitly explains why the smooth Hatcher--Wagoner invariant cannot simply be applied to a TOP pseudo-isotopy without a new definition and comparison proof. Remark 1.7 on that page explicitly states that the higher-dimensional exact-sequence assertion fails in TOP dimension four, even for infinite cyclic fundamental group. Thus this paper supplies no zero-CS sufficiency or RP3 PL-image equality. This observation is an exact limitation on the inference, not a claimed counterexample for the RP3 cylinder.

3. Kwasik--Schultz, *Pseudo-isotopies of 3-manifolds*, Topology 35 (1996), 363--376, DOI 10.1016/0040-9383(95)00017-8. Author-uploaded original text: https://www.academia.edu/84074109/Pseudo_isotopies_of_3_manifolds . Fresh refs turn441431292view0 and turn584197276search4. Intro and pp364--370 were read. The principal examples concern endpoint mapping classes of punctured properly metacyclic prism manifolds and connected sums. They do not identify components of the RP3 concordance space or prove the required PL-image criterion.

4. T. Ohta and T. Watanabe, *Unstable pseudo-isotopies of spherical 3-manifolds*, original https://www.math.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~tadayuki.watanabe/PI-cylinder.pdf . Refs turn516912887view0/2. The lens-space theta lower bound in Proposition 1.3 is zero at p=2. The displayed stable/block quotient is not the ordinary PL-to-TOP component kernel or image problem.

## Additional original subsequently audited

Jahren--Kwasik, *Free involutions on S1 times Sn*, Math. Ann. 351 (2011), 281--303, DOI 10.1007/s00208-010-0599-y; original arXiv:0802.2035. The published full original was subsequently opened at https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s00208-010-0599-y.pdf (turn249438676view0; 23 pages, 1059 parsed lines). Relevant exact passages were read: Remark 1.6, printed p284, computes concordance classes of endpoint homeomorphisms, namely pi0 of block Top(RPn). The structure-set definition is on p290, the product splitting is Lemma 3.2 on p291, and formula (3.4) on p294 gives the relative TOP structure set of I times RP3 as a two-element group. Proposition 3.5 and its proof on pp294--296 distinguish a tangential homotopy equivalence with nontrivial normal invariant from generators represented by homeomorphisms. These are structure-set and block/endpoint assertions, not a computation of ordinary isotopy components of the pseudo-isotopy space. No zero-CS PL-image sufficiency follows.
