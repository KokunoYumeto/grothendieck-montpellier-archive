\subsection*{A named open finiteness question contained in the TOP sphere model}

Write $T_4=\operatorname{Homeo}(\mathbb R^4;0)$ with its
compact-open topology and $Y_4=\operatorname{hofib}(BO(4)\to BT_4)$.
The natural joint-germ comparison and cone construction proved in
A.132 identify
\[
                    P_{\rm TOP}(S^3)\simeq_w\Omega Y_4.
\]
Consequently the following two conditions are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Every positive-degree homotopy group of $T_4$ is finitely generated.
\item Every positive-degree homotopy group of $P_{\rm TOP}(S^3)$ is
finitely generated.
\end{enumerate}

Indeed, $\pi_1Y_4=\pi_2Y_4=0$ and
$\pi_1T_4\cong\pi_1O(4)\cong\mathbb Z/2$ by the low-degree input
already checked in A.132. Each homotopy group of $O(4)$ is finitely
generated: its identity component has finite cover $S^3\times S^3$,
and positive homotopy groups of a simply connected finite CW complex
are finitely generated. The long exact sequence
\[
 \pi_jO(4)\longrightarrow\pi_jT_4\longrightarrow\pi_jY_4
     \longrightarrow\pi_{j-1}O(4)
\]
then shows for every $j\ge2$ that finite generation of $\pi_jT_4$
is equivalent to finite generation of $\pi_jY_4$. Here subgroups
and quotients of finitely generated abelian groups remain finitely
generated, and the group in the middle is an extension of the
corresponding kernel and image. Finally
$\pi_jY_4\cong\pi_{j-1}P_{\rm TOP}(S^3)$ for $j\ge2$.

The authorized author version of Baykur--Kirby--Ruberman,
\emph{K3: A New Problem List in Low-Dimensional Topology} (2026),
Problem 4.72, printed pp.~249--250, explicitly asks the first
finiteness question. Its adjacent Problem 4.73, printed pp.~250--251,
asks the distinct dimension-four DIFF Morlet correspondence. The
equivalence above uses no assertion of that open Morlet correspondence.
Original source: \texttt{https://bpb-us-e2.wpmucdn.com/websites.umass.edu/dist/b/22144/files/2026/04/K3-problem-list-watermarked.pdf}.
The complete authorized original was opened; exact retrieval locators
were \texttt{turn878909884view0} and
\texttt{turn346353683view0--1}.

This identifies a named open question within the \emph{TOP portion}
of a whole-space evaluation. It does not reopen the proved PL-to-definable
comparison, imply that a recursive presentation decides finite generation,
or supply a new computation of the RP3 image or kernel.
