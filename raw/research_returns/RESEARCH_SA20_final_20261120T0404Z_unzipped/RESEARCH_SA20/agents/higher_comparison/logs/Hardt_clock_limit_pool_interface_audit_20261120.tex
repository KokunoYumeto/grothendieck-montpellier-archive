\section*{Uniform Hardt frames and the remaining matching interface}

\textbf{The A.154 input is valid.}\quad
Let \(T\) be a definable parameter cell and let
\(e:T\times F\to[0,1]^N\) be a jointly continuous definable family
of embeddings of one compact definable polyhedron, each onto the
corresponding fibre.  A fixed PL model identifies \(F\) with the
cubical face-union \(Q\) used in A.154.  The fixed full-subcomplex
extension formula extends \(e\) to a bounded vector map on the
ambient cube times \(T\); its spatial retraction and barycentric
weight do not depend on the parameter.  Applying the clocks to
\((\operatorname{id},E)\) gives one definable family \(\chi_t\) of
face-preserving homeomorphisms and hence
\[
 r_t=e_t\chi_t|_Q:F\longrightarrow X_t.
\]
Every \(r_t\) is an onto embedding, and their spatial Lipschitz
constants have a common standard integer bound on the normalized
fixed model.  The proof only needs finite target dimension, not
equality of source and target dimensions.  Uniform monotonicity
counts exist over the entire definable parameter set.  Continuity
of the finite maximum for variation is local in \(t\) and uses
compactness of the spatial interval; compactness of \(T\) is not
required.  The explicit inverse-clock formulas give joint inverse
continuity.  Thus a Hardt cell supplies a single format of uniformly
Lipschitz frames, locally over any compact target block of a proper
map.

\textbf{What a genuine bundle chart forces on a frame limit.}\quad
Suppose \(a\) is an actual definable bundle centre, with a chart
\(h_t:X_a\to X_t\) converging uniformly to the ambient identity as
\(t\to a\).  Suppose a definable parameter path gives a uniform
limit \(r_t\to q\).  Then
\[
 h_t^{-1}r_t:F\longrightarrow X_a
\]
is one jointly definable family of homeomorphisms converging
uniformly to \(q\).  Indeed the displacement of \(h_t^{-1}\) equals
that of \(h_t\) on the corresponding compact fibres.  The chart
also shows that \(q\) is onto \(X_a\).
Thus every such frame limit at a genuine bundle centre satisfies
the coherent near-homeomorphism condition considered by the
maximal-locus agent.

\textbf{The exact estimate after a limit approximant is supplied.}\quad
For any homeomorphism \(a_s:F\to X_a\) approximating \(q\),
\[
 \sup_{y\in X_a}\|r_ta_s^{-1}(y)-y\|
 =\sup_{x\in F}\|r_t(x)-a_s(x)\|
 \le\|r_t-q\|_\infty+\|a_s-q\|_\infty.
\]
Hence a finite definable pool of such limit approximants gives
finite-format small matchers.  This paragraph is conditional on
the limit-pool and scalar-collar results supplied separately by the
maximal-locus agent; we have not relabelled its full proof as
independently read here.

\textbf{The two unsupplied conclusions.}\quad
These matchers need not admit a coherent choice across different
Hardt cells and directional frame limits.  On the angular model of
A5.019 the estimate gives proximity of the angular maps, whereas a
chart requires the exact equation
\(\Theta_t H_t=\operatorname{pr}_{\Sigma}\).
A.154 does not preserve that extra varying projection, and A.160
compares a supplied family rather than constructing the required
first compatible family.  A correction theorem supplying both
coherence and the exact angular equation has not been proved in
this audit.  No closure or strict reduction of the named angular
gate is counted from the estimate alone.
