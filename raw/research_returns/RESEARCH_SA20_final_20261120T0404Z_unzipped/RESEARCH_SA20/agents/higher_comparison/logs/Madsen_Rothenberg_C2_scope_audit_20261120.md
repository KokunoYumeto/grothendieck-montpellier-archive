## Decisive scope obstruction

The 1982 announcement expressly restricts its subsequent results to cyclic
odd-order groups before any numbered theorem. In particular Theorem F is
not a C2 theorem. The 1988 full-proof sequel retains odd-order groups in its
introduction and in the actual PL stabilization Theorem4.2 and TOP
stabilization Theorem4.6. Its printed p.67 goes further: it explains that the
odd-order restriction is necessary and that stable transversality can fail
for C2 even in the PL category.

Consequently none of these statements applies to the representation
4 sigma of C2 used by A.162. Its total dimension four cannot be inserted into
an announced connectivity expression while dropping the group hypothesis.
The OCR also damages fixed-space superscripts in several displayed formulas;
no numerical connectivity conclusion for 4 sigma is extracted from that OCR.

model and A.162's exact joint germs would not remove the odd-order restriction.
