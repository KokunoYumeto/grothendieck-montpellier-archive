## Ishikawa--Nguyen 2025

Original: *Atypical values at infinity of real polynomial maps with
2-dimensional fibers*, C. R. Math. 363 (2025), 917--932,
DOI 10.5802/crmath.761. The complete published article is exposed at
https://www.researchgate.net/publication/393929382_Atypical_values_at_infinity_of_real_polynomial_maps_with_2_-dimensional_fibers .
The complete author preprint https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.12709 is v2,
11 July 2024, 18 pages. Web-original refs: `turn180043151view0`,
`turn603214375view0`, `turn431295228view0`, `turn431295228view1`.

The introduction, printed p918, attributes semialgebraicity of the smallest
bifurcation set to references [13], [16], [18]. These are respectively
Kurdyka--Orro--Simon 2000, Thom 1969, and Verdier 1976. No category of
trivializing homeomorphisms is explicitly defined in that introductory sentence.

The substantive recognition theorem is published Theorem 17, pp930--931
(preprint Theorem 5.1, pp17--18). Its setting is a polynomial
F:R^n -> R^(n-2), and the target value is outside the closure of the critical
values. It chooses a sphere transverse to all sufficiently distant parts of
the distinguished fibre. The criterion uses nonvanishing of components at
infinity, absence of compact exterior components, and constancy of Euler
characteristic. The proof first trivializes the smooth compact core, then
uses the cited Serre-fibration criterion and Meigniez Corollary 32.

**Scope consequence.** This does not recognize topological product behaviour
at critical values of arbitrary continuous definable maps. For a proper smooth
map at a regular value, a sufficiently large compact core already contains all
nearby fibres. The theorem's end analysis adds no missing A5 certificate there.
The introductory assertion has not been turned into a proved exact-locus
theorem by this audit.

## Verdier 1976: fresh complete-original access

Original: *Stratifications de Whitney et theoreme de Bertini--Sard*,
Invent. Math. 36 (1976), 295--312, DOI 10.1007/BF01390015.
The original GDZ volume is PPN356556735_0036. Its public IIIF manifest is
https://manifests.sub.uni-goettingen.de/iiif/presentation/PPN356556735_0036/manifest?version=8b7f2484 .
Printed pp295--312 are canvases 00000301--00000318. All eighteen original
page images were retrieved, with OCR, under `/tmp/A5_originals_verdier1976`,
outside the authored project. Pages 311--312 were also visually inspected.
Read in this pass: pp295,303,306--308,310--312.

Theorem 4.14, p311, assumes a closed stratified subset, properness on that
subset, and transversality to its strata. It supplies a stratification-preserving
rough homeomorphism commuting with projection. The definition in Section 4,
pp307--308, requires smoothness on strata and local cross-stratum estimates;
it does not require a semialgebraic graph. Corollary 5.1, p312, supplies some
dense Zariski open with a topological locally trivial fibration for a morphism
of complex algebraic spaces. Neither statement identifies the maximal locus.
Remark 4.15's second-isotopy reference uses Remark 4.7, p310, whose lifting
statement expressly assumes absence of blowing-up.
