\subsection*{Finite-format sewing of homeomorphism approximants}

\paragraph{Candidate general sewing lemma.}
Let $R$ be a real closed field with an o-minimal expansion. Let $A,F,G$
be closed bounded definable sets, and let $q:A\times F\to G$ be jointly
continuous and definable. Suppose there is one definable family
\[
 \psi:A\times(0,1]\times F\to G
\]
whose slices $\psi_{a,s}:F\to G$ are homeomorphisms and satisfy
\[
 \|\psi_{a,s}-q_a\|_\infty<s.
 \tag{FS.1}
\]
No joint continuity of $\psi$ is assumed. Then there exist $s_0>0$ and
a jointly continuous definable map
\[
 h:A\times[0,s_0]\times F\to G
\]
such that $h_{a,0}=q_a$, every $h_{a,s}$ with $s>0$ is a homeomorphism,
and $h_{a,s}\to q_a$ uniformly in $(a,x)$ as $s\downarrow0$.

The empty parameter case and the case $F=G=\varnothing$ are immediate.
If $A$ is nonempty and exactly one of $F,G$ is empty, the assumed family
of homeomorphisms does not exist. Assume henceforth all three are nonempty.

\paragraph{1. A finite continuity partition and an exact height triangulation.}
There is a finite definable partition of $A\times(0,1]$ on whose cells
evaluation and inverse evaluation of $\psi$ are jointly continuous.
Here is the compact-graph justification. Apply Hardt triviality to the
projection of the graph family to its parameter. On a parameter cell $S$
the graph is homeomorphic over $S$ to $S\times Q$ for one compact graph
fibre $Q$. Composing this trivialization with projection to $S\times F$
is a continuous definable bijection. On each closed bounded parameter
neighborhood inside $S$ it is a homeomorphism by definable compactness.
Such neighborhoods cover the cell. Projection to $S\times G$ gives
the same conclusion for the inverse evaluations.

Refine into a finite partition with the frontier condition. Triangulate
the compact set $A\times[0,1]$, compatibly with the closures of its pieces
and the zero-height set, using exact scalar triangulation of its height.
We obtain a finite polyhedron $|K|$, a definable homeomorphism
$T:|K|\to A\times[0,1]$, and a nonnegative PL function
\[
 r=\operatorname{pr}_{[0,1]}\circ T,
 \qquad |L|=r^{-1}(0),
\]
where $L$ is a subcomplex. Each open simplex outside $L$ is contained in
a continuity piece for $\psi\circ T$. Define
\[
 Q(p,x)=q_{\operatorname{pr}_A T(p)}(x).
\]
This is jointly continuous on the closed bounded set $|K|\times F$.
Write $\psi_p$ for the original approximant at $T(p)$. Then
$\|\psi_p-Q_p\|_\infty<r(p)$.

Let $d=\dim K$. All format pools and contraction maps in the next step
are chosen for this integer $d$, before any small height cutoff is chosen.

\paragraph{2. Fixed finite closure pools, followed by error moduli.}
Let $\mathcal H_0$ be the family of maps $\psi_p$ for $p\in|K|\setminus|L|$,
quotiented by equality of whole maps. It is a fixed definable family of
homeomorphisms $F\to G$. Inductively, for $n=1,\ldots,d$, set
\[
 \mathcal D_{n-1}
   =\{uv^{-1}:u,v\in\mathcal H_{n-1}\}\subseteq\operatorname{Homeo}_{\rm def}(G),
 \qquad
 \mathcal H_n=\mathcal D_{n-1}\mathcal H_{n-1}.
 \tag{FS.2}
\]
Each is a definable family given by finitely many graph formulas, obtained
by finite composition and inversion. Since the identity belongs to every
$\mathcal D_{n-1}$, the pools $\mathcal H_n$ are nested. Definable parameter
restrictions can be incorporated in the formulas; this does not require
that the family be the unrestricted set of all maps in a syntactic format.

Apply A.138 to the self-homeomorphism family $\mathcal D_{n-1}$ at its
identity. With the metric
\[
 d_G(u,v)=\|u-v\|_\infty+\|u^{-1}-v^{-1}\|_\infty,
\]
choose an open neighborhood $U_n$, a radius $\rho_n>0$ with
$B_{d_G}(\operatorname{id},\rho_n)\subseteq U_n$, and one fixed contraction
\[
 C_n:U_n\times[0,1]\to U_n,\qquad
 C_n(z,0)=z,\quad C_n(z,1)=\operatorname{id},\quad
 C_n(\operatorname{id},t)=\operatorname{id}.
 \tag{FS.3}
\]
Evaluation and inverse evaluation are jointly continuous. The output
remains in $\mathcal D_{n-1}$ itself, so this contraction introduces no
unknown new format in (FS.2).

For $0\le u<\rho_n/2$ put
\[
 \omega_n(u)=\sup\{\|C_n(z,t)-\operatorname{id}\|_\infty:
 z\in\mathcal D_{n-1},\ d_G(z,\operatorname{id})\le u,\ 0\le t\le1\}.
 \tag{FS.4}
\]
This is a bounded definable nondecreasing function with $\omega_n(0)=0$.
It tends to zero as $u\downarrow0$: joint continuity at
$\{\operatorname{id}\}\times[0,1]$ and definable compactness of the time
interval give one input radius for each prescribed output error.
Explicitly, otherwise definable choice gives inputs $z(u)$ with
$d_G(z(u),\operatorname{id})\le u$ and times $t(u)\in[0,1]$ whose output
error stays bounded below. After shortening the interval, the bounded
definable curve $t(u)$ has a limit $t_*$; continuity at
$(\operatorname{id},t_*)$ is a contradiction. This uses no ordinary
finite subcover of an infinitesimal-mesh cover.
Extend $\omega_n$ to all nonnegative $u$ by a constant bound, for example
$\max(1,\operatorname{diam}G)$, once $u\ge\rho_n/2$. Only its germ at zero
will be used. This does not assert uniform convergence as $t\to1$ on an
arbitrary bounded set of inputs.

Define nondecreasing definable functions on $[0,\infty)$ by $E_0(t)=t$ and
\[
\begin{split}
 S_n(t)&=6t+2E_{n-1}(2t),\\
 E_n(t)&=\max\{E_{n-1}(t),t,
       \omega_n(S_n(t))+E_{n-1}(2t)+2t\}.
\end{split}
\tag{FS.5}
\]
Every $E_n(t)$ and $S_n(t)$ tends to zero as $t\downarrow0$. Choose a single
$s_0\in(0,1)$ so small that
\[
 S_n(t)<\rho_n/2\quad(0<t\le s_0,\ 1\le n\le d).
 \tag{FS.6}
\]
This choice occurs after all finite pools, contractions, and moduli have
been fixed, so no format/radius circularity occurs.

Cut the original finite polyhedron by $r\le s_0$ and triangulate the
resulting finite polyhedral cells compatibly with the old simplices. Keep
the notation $K,L,r,T$ for this refinement. Each new open simplex is
contained in an old continuity piece, $r$ remains PL, and dimension does
not increase. The already fixed pools contain all approximants on this
restricted domain and are not changed.

\paragraph{3. A continuous modulus for the target family.}
There exists a positive continuous definable function $\gamma(t)$ for
$t>0$ such that
\[
 \|p-p'\|<\gamma(t)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \|Q_p-Q_{p'}\|_\infty<t
 \qquad(p,p'\in|K|).
 \tag{FS.7}
\]
For clarity, this follows without any nondefinable modulus. The supremum
$D(p,p')=\max_{x\in F}\|Q(p,x)-Q(p',x)\|$ is continuous and definable
on the compact product. For $t>0$ let $\beta(t)$ be the minimum of $1$
and $\min\{\|p-p'\|:D(p,p')\ge t\}$, taking value $1$ if
there is no such pair. Compactness gives $\beta(t)>0$, and $\beta$ is
nondecreasing. Set $\beta(0)=0$ and, on a fixed bounded interval containing
all required heights, define
\[
 \gamma(t)=\tfrac12\inf_{0\le u\le1}\{\beta(u)+|t-u|\},
 \qquad 0\le t\le1.
\]
The infimum is definable and its envelope is Lipschitz. It is positive
for $t>0$: the ranges $u\le t/2$ and $u\ge t/2$ respectively give lower
bounds $t/2$ and $\beta(t/2)$. Also $\gamma(t)\le\beta(t)/2$, which implies
(FS.7). Its value at zero is zero.

\paragraph{4. Sewing one skeleton to the next.}
We construct a jointly continuous definable evaluation map $H_n$ on
$(|K^{(n)}|\setminus|L|)\times F$, with each slice a homeomorphism lying
in $\mathcal H_n$, such that
\[
 \|H_n(p)-Q_p\|_\infty\le E_n(r(p)).
 \tag{FS.8}
\]
For the zero-skeleton use $H_0(p)=\psi_p$. There are finitely many vertices,
so the required continuity is immediate.

Suppose $H_{n-1}$ is constructed. Consider an $n$-simplex $\sigma$ not
contained in $L$, with barycenter $c$. Every point of
$\sigma\setminus\{c\}$ has a unique representation
\[
 p=(1-u)b+uc,\qquad b\in\partial\sigma,\quad0\le u<1.
\]
The radial coordinates $(b,u)$ are definable and continuous away from $c$.
Put $M_\sigma=\max_\sigma r>0$ and $D_\sigma=\operatorname{diam}\sigma>0$.
For boundary points with $r(b)>0$ set
\[
 w(b)=\min\left\{\tfrac14,
       \frac{r(b)}{4M_\sigma},\frac{\gamma(r(b))}{2D_\sigma}\right\},
 \tag{FS.9}
\]
and set $w(b)=0$ on $\partial\sigma\cap|L|$. This is continuous on the
whole boundary, positive exactly on its positive-height part. The partial
collar $0\le u<w(b)$ is a neighborhood of
$\partial\sigma\setminus|L|$ in $\sigma\setminus|L|$ and avoids the
barycenter. On it,
\[
 \tfrac12r(p)<r(b)<2r(p),\qquad
 \|Q_p-Q_b\|_\infty<r(b)\le2r(p).
 \tag{FS.10}
\]
Indeed $|r(p)-r(b)|\le uM_\sigma<r(b)/4$ and
$\|p-b\|\le uD_\sigma<\gamma(r(b))$.

For an interior point in this partial collar let $v=H_{n-1}(b)$ and put
$z(p)=\psi_pv^{-1}\in\mathcal D_{n-1}$. The exact identity
\[
 d_G(\psi_pv^{-1},\operatorname{id})
       =2\|\psi_p-v\|_\infty
 \tag{FS.11}
\]
and (FS.1), (FS.8), (FS.10) give
\[
 d_G(z(p),\operatorname{id})
 \le 2\{r(p)+2r(p)+E_{n-1}(2r(p))\}
 =S_n(r(p))<\rho_n/2.
\]
Thus $C_n$ is defined on every required comparison map.

Choose the continuous piecewise-linear function
\[
 \lambda(v)=\max(0,\min(1,2-3v))\quad(0\le v\le1).
\]
It equals one on the inner third and zero on the outer third. Define
\[
 H_n(p)=
 \begin{cases}
 C_n(z(p),\lambda(u/w(b)))\circ H_{n-1}(b),
       &0<u<w(b),\ r(b)>0,\\
 \psi_p,&\text{outside that partial collar},
 \end{cases}
 \tag{FS.12}
\]
on the interior of $\sigma$, and retain $H_{n-1}$ on its boundary points
that belong to the domain. The values lie in $\mathcal H_n$ by (FS.2).

Here are the continuity details. In the open simplex $\psi$ and its inverse
are jointly continuous. The anchor $H_{n-1}(b)$ is jointly continuous, and
so is its inverse: this follows on closed bounded parameter neighborhoods
from compactness of $F,G$ and then locally everywhere. Hence $z(p)$ is
continuous in the map-and-inverse metric, and (FS.12) is jointly continuous
on the collar interior. On its outer third $\lambda=0$, so (FS.12) is
exactly $\psi_p$; there is therefore no gluing problem at the outer edge.
On its inner third $\lambda=1$, so it is exactly $H_{n-1}(b)$. Consequently
it extends continuously to every positive-height boundary point even if
$\psi_p$ has no limit there. This exact equality removes any need for
uniform endpoint convergence of contractions on bounded input sets.

If radial projections approach a deleted zero-height face while $p$
approaches an interior point of $\sigma$, then $w(b)\to0$ while $u$ stays
positive. The formula is eventually the unmodified $\psi_p$, so this does
not create a new discontinuity. The barycenter is likewise outside the
collar. There are only finitely many simplices; their common boundaries
retain the same $H_{n-1}$, so these constructions glue continuously on
the entire $n$-skeleton minus $L$.

For the error estimate, every collar point satisfies
\[
\begin{split}
 \|H_n(p)-Q_p\|_\infty
 &\le \|C_n(z(p),\lambda)-\operatorname{id}\|_\infty
       +\|H_{n-1}(b)-Q_b\|_\infty+\|Q_b-Q_p\|_\infty\\
 &\le\omega_n(S_n(r(p)))+E_{n-1}(2r(p))+2r(p)
 \le E_n(r(p)).
\end{split}
\]
Outside the collar the error is less than $r(p)$, and on the old skeleton
it is bounded by $E_{n-1}(r(p))$. This proves the induction.

\paragraph{5. Extension to height zero and the surface-family consequence.}
At dimension $d$ we have a jointly continuous definable family of actual
homeomorphisms on $|K|\setminus|L|$. Transport it through $T$ and call it
$h_{a,s}$ for $s>0$. Equation (FS.8) gives the uniform bound
\[
 \sup_{a\in A,x\in F}\|h_{a,s}(x)-q_a(x)\|\le E_d(s)\longrightarrow0.
\]
Define $h_{a,0}=q_a$. The bound and joint continuity of $q$ prove joint
continuity at every zero-height point. This proves the sewing lemma.

Over a fixed o-minimal expansion of $\mathbb R$, A5.017 supplies (FS.1) for
a jointly continuous definable family $q_a:F\to G$ of cell-like surjections
between fixed compact closed surfaces and compact definable parameter set
$A$. Hence such a family has a jointly continuous definable approximation
by homeomorphisms. Inverses $k_{a,s}=h_{a,s}^{-1}:G\to F$ are jointly
continuous for $s>0$, and
\[
 \sup_{a\in A,y\in G}\|q_a(k_{a,s}(y))-y\|
 =\sup_{a\in A,x\in F}\|q_a(x)-h_{a,s}(x)\|\longrightarrow0.
\]
In particular define the two-sided family cylinder over this compact band
by
\[
\begin{split}
 Z={}&\{(a,s,|s|x,q_a(x)):a\in A,\ 0<|s|\le s_0,\ x\in F\}\\
 &\quad\cup\{(a,0,0,y):a\in A,\ y\in G\},
 \qquad p(a,s,z,w)=(a,s).
\end{split}
\tag{FS.13}
\]
The explicit product homeomorphism is
\[
\begin{split}
 \Phi:A\times[-s_0,s_0]\times G&\longrightarrow Z,\\
 \Phi(a,s,y)&=
 \begin{cases}
 (a,s,|s|\,k_{a,|s|}(y),q_a(k_{a,|s|}(y))),&s\ne0,\\
 (a,0,0,y),&s=0.
 \end{cases}
\end{split}
\tag{FS.14}
\]
Both the embedded cylinder and its product map use $|s|$ in the middle
coordinate, as displayed in (FS.13)--(FS.14). This map is
definable, bijective, and satisfies $p\Phi(a,s,y)=(a,s)$. Boundedness of
$F$ and the preceding uniform error bound prove continuity at zero, and
continuity elsewhere follows from the jointly continuous inverse family.
Its domain is closed bounded, so the continuous definable bijection is a
homeomorphism. Thus $p$ is a definable product with fibre $G$ on the whole
displayed band. If the parameter lies in an open definable set, apply this
to a compact closed neighborhood of any specified parameter; the conclusion
is local there.

This would close the coherent-approximation gate for this special class
over the real field. The general radial angular criterion for an arbitrary
proper definable map has not been reduced to cell-like maps of closed
surfaces, so no conclusion for the whole remaining A.5 question is claimed.
The proof is a candidate pending independent audit; no included source or
ledger is modified during the checkpoint freeze.
