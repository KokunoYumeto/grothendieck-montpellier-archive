# Quantitative approximate choices do not supply the missing family

Opened 2026-11-19, 17:45 UTC.

Antonio Lerario, Luca Rizzi, Daniele Tiberio, *Quantitative approximate definable choices*, Mathematische Annalen 392 (2025), 1289–1319, DOI10.1007/s00208-025-03128-3.

Primary full published text: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00208-025-03128-3 ; web ref turn685240656view0. Inspected the abstract, Introduction Theorems A/B and their exact conditions (web lines84–145).

Theorem A constructs a closed low-dimensional semialgebraic subset in an epsilon neighborhood of a compact semialgebraic set, with projection Hausdorff-close to the original projection and controlled description complexity. Theorem B has the corresponding formulation for a locally Lipschitz semialgebraic map and estimates affine-section component counts.

Neither theorem produces an exact section of the original projection, a continuous parameterized choice, or a graph of homeomorphisms. Thus this result cannot by itself select jointly continuous PL approximations or fill the first real semialgebraic pi2 class. The higher PL-to-SA comparison gate is unchanged. In particular, Hausdorff approximation of sets does not repair the already exhibited failure of local face parameterization in A.118.
