## Target and persistent gap

The target is the kernel of the ordinary, jointly parameterized comparison
pi_0 P_PL(RP3) -> pi_0 P_TOP(RP3). The independent task on zero
Casson--Sullivan obstruction implying PL image belongs to the higher-comparison
agent. A PL total homeomorphism obtained by straightening a five-dimensional
TOP trace would in general be a PL pseudo-isotopy of the four-dimensional
cylinder. It is not automatically a PL isotopy preserving that cylinder's
isotopy parameter. This distinction blocks the tempting relative-five-dimensional
product-structure shortcut.
