Read Definition2.1 and Theorem2.2, pp304--305; Notes2.3(2),(5), p305. Note2.3(2) explicitly removes the dimension restriction for DIFF inside PL. The theorem and definition explicitly include prescribed boundary structures.

Conditional application, given the already checked low PL/O connectivity and relative stable smoothing classification: let G be a SINGLE PL homeomorphism of a smooth three-manifold cylinder N=B x I, with prescribed smooth maps on its whole boundary. First normalize it on collars so the pulled-back target smoothing equals the standard smoothing there. The two smooth reductions of the same PL N are relatively homotopic because PL/O is six-connected and N has dimension4. They are thus concordant as smooth structures relative to the boundary. Quinn's DIFF/PL isotopy statement gives a diffeomorphism tau from standard N to pulled-back N, fixed at the boundary and PL-isotopic to identity. Then G tau is a smooth concordance with the EXACT same boundary values as G.

Scope: this is single-map smooth replacement/surjectivity, not injectivity on smooth isotopy classes and not a parameter-projection-preserving smoothing of arbitrary families. In particular it does not repair the earlier unsupported base-four smooth/PL family comparison.
