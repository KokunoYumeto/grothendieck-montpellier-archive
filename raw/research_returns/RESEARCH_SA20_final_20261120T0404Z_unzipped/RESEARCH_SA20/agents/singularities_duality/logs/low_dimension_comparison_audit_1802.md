# Low-dimensional comparison audit, 2026-11-19 18:02 UTC

## Status and correction

No new low-dimensional PL concordance computation is promoted here. In particular, the tentative route from Ruberman's smooth examples to a counterexample to PL-to-TOP comparison in dimension four is **not established**. It requires a smooth-to-PL injectivity theorem in exactly the dimension not justified by the proof inspected below. A gap in that proof is not a proof that the comparison itself is false.

## Burghelea--Lashof originals actually inspected

- Part I, *Transactions AMS*196 (1974),1--36, author-uploaded full original transcription: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/259582790_The_homotopy_type_of_the_space_of_diffeomorphisms_I (refs turn272810480view0 and turn621170296view1). Theorem4.2(3t), printed28--29, excludes total dimensions4 and5. Theorem4.4(d), printed32, carries the corresponding exclusion. Consequently the TOP relative section-space comparison cannot be applied to a four-manifold times an interval, of total dimension5. The PL version is a different assertion and does not remove the TOP exclusion.
- Part II, *Transactions AMS*196 (1974),37--50, full original transcription: https://www.academia.edu/103489177/The_homotopy_type_of_the_space_of_diffeomorphisms_II (refs turn435916353view0 and turn429039168view0). Section6, printed41, defines the concordance groups using a differentiable triangulation. The displayed Theorem6.1 states the smooth-to-PL component comparison without an explicit dimension restriction. Its proof, printed42--43, invokes the vanishing of the relative unstable smoothing space from Theorem5.1. Theorem5.1 on printed37 has a high-dimensional bound; the low-dimensional observations preceding it do not cover PL4/O4. The displayed proof therefore does not by itself establish base dimension4. The Lie theory agent independently checked precisely this dependency. Existing uses in the checked dimension-at-least-five range remain separate.

The required map is a typed comparison using a compatible differentiable triangulation and the piecewise-differentiable intermediary. It is not the literal inclusion of arbitrary smooth coordinate maps into PL maps. A general assertion about stable PL/O cannot replace the missing relative unstable comparison: the top and bottom boundary are four-dimensional.

## Smooth candidate and actual pseudo-isotopy existence

- Ruberman1998, *An obstruction to smooth isotopy in dimension4*, original author version https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9807041 (refs turn600874159view0, turn994315344view0, turn749050012view0), constructs homotopic/topologically isotopic diffeomorphisms that are not smoothly isotopic. TheoremA/CorollaryB on printed2, Lemmas2.6--2.7 on printed8--9, and Theorem3.3 on printed14--15 were read. The additive one-parameter Donaldson invariant detects infinite-order endpoint classes in the stated construction. No explicit new fixed triangulation or PL representative is supplied by this inspection.
- Krannich--Kupers2024, *A note on homotopy and pseudoisotopy of diffeomorphisms of4-manifolds*, *C. R. Math.*362,1515--1520: full published original https://publikationen.bibliothek.kit.edu/1000177902/156447219 (turn564377349view0; exact proof turn707176036view1), also author arXiv2402.16167v3. TheoremB at1516 gives homotopy-implies-smooth-pseudoisotopy for closed orientable four-manifolds with free fundamental group. Its proof at1518--1519 includes a complete simply connected argument, so it gives an actual smooth pseudo-isotopy for Ruberman's endpoint. The final remark treats boundary connected sums of S1 times D3, including D4; it is not a theorem for arbitrary boundary.
- Gabai--Gay--Hartman--Krushkal--Powell2026, original ForumMathPi14e9, Theorem1.5, gives trivial TOP pseudo-isotopy components for simply connected compact four-manifolds. This does not establish either the smooth or PL conclusion.

Thus the smooth candidate is genuine, not merely an endpoint observation. The still missing input is its survival in the stipulated PL/definable concordance component group.

## Further low-three-dimensional sources

- Lin--Xie--Zhang2026, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.09454v1 (turn232442762view0), TheoremB printed2: for every compact oriented three-manifold with infinite fundamental group, the smooth and TOP concordance component groups contain infinite-rank abelian subgroups. This is not a complete group computation and the statement does not mention PL. Its introductory sentence about a general dimension-five Hatcher--Wagoner computation is not adopted; the manuscript retains the precise Igusa ranges already checked.
