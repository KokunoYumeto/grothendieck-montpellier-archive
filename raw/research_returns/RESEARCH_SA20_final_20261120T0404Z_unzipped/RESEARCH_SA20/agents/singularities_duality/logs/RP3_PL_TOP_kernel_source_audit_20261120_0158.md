# Ordinary PL-to-TOP concordance kernel: source and scope audit

Authored by `/root/singularities_duality`, 20 November 2026, during build32.
This is a research checkpoint, not a theorem or a claim of movement in an original task row. No compiled TeX or bibliography was changed. Original works were read through public web retrieval; no new local fulltext extract was created.

## Exact question

For the closed real PL manifold B = RP^3, let P_CAT(B) consist of homeomorphisms of B x I that fix B x {0} pointwise. The top boundary is free. For PL, paths and higher simplices are jointly finite PL and preserve their parameter projection; for TOP they are actual continuous families. Determine the kernel of pi_0 P_PL(B) -> pi_0 P_TOP(B).

The separate question whether every zero-Casson--Sullivan TOP component has a PL representative is assigned to the higher-comparison agent. The image obstruction A.143 and its surjectivity A.144 do not determine the kernel considered here.

## Original sources actually inspected

### Ohta--Watanabe, 2023

Yuji Ohta and Tadayuki Watanabe, *Unstable pseudo-isotopies of spherical 3-manifolds*, arXiv:2303.13877v2, 18 October 2023, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.13877 . Full original text opened; introduction pp.1--4, Theorem1.1, Proposition1.3, Corollary1.4, Theorem1.5 and Proposition1.7 inspected. Web retrieval: turn271860656view0.

The source defines the ordinary smooth concordance group. Its theta-graph invariant gives smooth lower bounds. The lens-space bound in Proposition1.3/Proposition5.2 is p_3(n-6), which is zero for n=2. A zero lower bound proves no vanishing. Theorem1.5 concerns stable pseudo-isotopy triviality of specified classes; Proposition1.7 concerns a block quotient. Neither supplies TOP-isotopy triviality or an injectivity theorem for the PL-to-TOP map on RP^3.

### Kwasik--Schultz, 1996

S. Kwasik and R. Schultz, *Pseudo-isotopies of 3-manifolds*, Topology35(2)(1996),363--376, DOI10.1016/0040-9383(95)00017-8. Author-linked original OCR opened at https://www.academia.edu/84074109/Pseudo_isotopies_of_3_manifolds . Introduction pp.363--364 and Theorem2.1/proof pp.373--375 inspected. Web retrievals: turn271860656view1, turn827811988search1, turn898256294search2.

The main examples are endpoint homeomorphisms of punctured nonabelian metacyclic prism manifolds that are pseudo-isotopic but not isotopic; the connected-sum corollary has the corresponding scope. RP^3 is not within that fundamental-group hypothesis. The function-space surgery calculation uses concordance/block homotopy groups. No statement here identifies ordinary concordance components with those groups or computes the required kernel.

### Kwasik, 1987

S. Kwasik, *Low-dimensional concordances, Whitney towers and isotopies*, Math.Proc.CambridgePhil.Soc.102(1)(1987),103--119, DOI10.1017/S0305004100067098. Full original PDF opened at https://www.maths.gla.ac.uk/~mpowell/Kwasik-low-dimensional-concordances-whitney-towers-and-isotopies.pdf ; pp.103--109, including Theorem3.3 p.109, inspected.

Theorem3.3 concerns a simply connected base of dimension four and the image of smooth concordances in TOP. It does not apply to the three-dimensional base RP^3, and it does not assert the needed PL-to-TOP injection. The side conventions and category maps must be retained; no reversal of its image statement is justified.

### Daher--Powell, 2026

M. Daher and M. Powell, *Smoothing 3-manifolds in 5-manifolds*, Invent.Math.245(2026),491--511, DOI10.1007/s00222-026-01413-9. Full published original opened at https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00222-026-01413-9 . TheoremA produces a small homotopy to a smooth embedding, and explicitly does not assert isotopy. Scholium5.1 adds two specified Kirby--Siebenmann conditions for isotopy of an individual 3-in-5 embedding. Theorem2.1 classifies structures on a5-manifold when its entire boundary already carries the fixed structure. ### Kwasik--Schultz, 1989: new original access in this interval

S. Kwasik and R. Schultz, *On s-cobordisms of metacyclic prism manifolds*, Invent.Math.97(1989),523--552, DOI10.1007/BF01388889. Followed the actual parent link from a public figure page to the author-uploaded original OCR at https://www.academia.edu/9803950/On_s_cobordisms_of_metacyclic_prism_manifolds . Opened introduction pp.523--525, the precise convention pp.526--530, and Theorem2.1 with its complete proof pp.530--532. Retrieval: turn599931568view0. The publisher PDF request itself only returned the subscription landing page; this audit does not claim that publisher PDF was read.

Theorem2.1 is a TOP product theorem for a4-dimensional h-cobordism of lens spaces preserving orientation and a fundamental-group generator. In the even-order case its proof finds two relative TOP structure classes on L x I; both use the same underlying product, and the nontrivial marking is a pinch-plus-suspended-Hopf self-homotopy equivalence. It is not a homeomorphism. This proves no PL-marked uniqueness, no parameterized uniqueness, and no ordinary isotopy theorem. The introductory discussion explicitly distinguishes the smooth/PL product problem. The source access is improved, but the component-kernel gate is unchanged.

### Bamler--Kleiner, 2019: the RP3 automorphism input

R. Bamler and B. Kleiner, *Ricci flow and contractibility of spaces of metrics*, arXiv:1909.08710v1, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.08710 . Full original opened and Theorem1.3 printedp.2 plus its scope discussion pp.2--3 read (retrieval turn475829424view1). It proves the natural inclusion of the isometry group of every round spherical3-form into its smooth diffeomorphism group is a homotopy equivalence. This includes RP3. The distinct earlier paper *Ricci flow and diffeomorphism groups of3-manifolds* excludes RP3; it is not the input used here.

Conditional only on the already separately checked natural ordinary Diff-to-TOP comparison in dimension3, covering-space lifting gives a two-sheeted diagram from O(4) -> Homeo_odd(S3) over PO(4) -> Homeo(RP3). The pullback over the isometries is exactly O(4), so weak equivalence of the lower map gives weak equivalence of the upper map. This supplies a middle-group input to the higher agent's proposed equivariant germ fibre sequence. It does not compute the odd germ group or the PL-to-TOP concordance kernel. The complete finite-family germ argument remains a separately audited candidate.

## Exact failed inference in the 5-dimensional trace route

Start with a PL concordance h on N=B x I that is joined to the identity by an ordinary TOP path. Its total path is a homeomorphism of the5-dimensional N x I preserving the last coordinate. Relative PL-structure classification could at most replace this by a PL homeomorphism with the desired boundary values, subject to its own obstruction and collar hypotheses. Such a replacement is a PL pseudo-isotopy of h; it is not automatically a PL isotopy. The missing conclusion is preservation, or subsequent straightening, of that last parameter. A structure theorem in total dimension five does not on its own supply that conclusion for the four-dimensional fibre N. This is why the ordinary dimension-three automorphism comparison from A.134 cannot simply be applied to the cylinder.

## Status

No injectivity, noninjectivity, finite bound, or nontrivial subgroup of this kernel has been established by this audit. In particular no statement about stable or block triviality is promoted to ordinary TOP triviality. The original TOP3/4 task gate remains unchanged.
