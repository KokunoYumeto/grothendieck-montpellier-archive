# Complete bar presentation for the stable component group — pending audit

This is a proposed proof, not yet a checked result or progress claim.

Let K be a finite connected pointed simplicial model of a space X. Form its reduced simplicial quotient by a maximal tree and its Kan loop group G. Let R=Sigma-infinity-plus G be the spherical group ring, with a fixed simplicial connective ring-spectrum model. For each n let H_n=GL_n(R), the simplicial monoid of stable homotopy self-equivalences of the free rank-n R-module, and let W_n=G^n semidirect Sigma_n. The permutation/unit action gives W_n -> H_n. Stabilization is block sum with the identity. Put

C = colim_n B H_n, D = colim_n B W_n.

Both are connected simplicial sets with one vertex. For either Y=C,D, let L_Y=H_1(Y;Z)=C_1(Y)/d C_2(Y). Its universal abelian cover Y^ab has q-simplices (a,sigma), a in L_Y, sigma in Y_q. For q>=1 the face d_0 changes a to a+[sigma restricted to edge(0,1)]; all other faces leave a unchanged. Degeneracies leave a unchanged. The simplicial identities follow from the triangle relations. The map D->C induces the based map D^ab->C^ab.

Define the abelian group E(K) by generators [z], one for every finite integral 2-cycle z in the normalized chain complex C_*(C^ab), with relations

[z+z']=[z]+[z'], [d c]=0 for c in C_3(C^ab), [f(w)]=0 for w in Z_2(D^ab).

Thus E(K)=coker(H_2(D^ab)->H_2(C^ab)); its entire group law is addition of 2-cycles. Unlike an exact sequence, this presentation includes every relation among lifts of Wh_2. It is independent of the models up to natural isomorphism by the proof below.

Proof route:
1. Jahren2010 p247 gives the group-completion models A(X)=Omega B coproduct_n BGL_n(QG) and Q(X_+)=Omega B coproduct_n B(G^n semidirect Sigma_n), with the latter-to-former map induced by permutation matrices and units. The stable group-completion component is C respectively D. Homotopy commutativity follows from block-permutation conjugation.
2. Gritschacher arXiv1709.02036v2, Proposition1.1 and proof pp1-7, proves that these group-completion maps are acyclic. The positive-rank sequence is cofinal and the components form N. Since the targets are infinite loop spaces, their fundamental groups are abelian, and the maps induce abelianization. Hence their lifts from C^ab and D^ab to the simply connected covers of the corresponding group-completion components are integral homology equivalences. This is explicitly the first reduction in that source's proof p3.
3. Hurewicz gives H_2(C^ab)=pi_2 A(X), H_2(D^ab)=pi_2 Q(X_+), compatibly with the unit map. Jahren2010 p247 and its basic diagram p248 give split A(X)=Q(X_+) times WhDiff(X), so the cokernel is pi_2 WhDiff(X). This is the stable smooth concordance component group, with its group structure, not only a set.
4. For a smoothable closed PL manifold M of dimension n>=9, choose a smoothing. Burghelea-Lashof1974II Theorem6.1 p41 identifies smooth and PL pi_0. Igusa stability as stated in WJR Theorem1.4.1 gives 1-connectivity of every stabilization when n>=max(9,7)=9, hence a pi_0 isomorphism to the stable group. A.113 transports this to definable pi_0 over every real closed field. Therefore E(K) computes pi_0P_def(M) for all such M, with no remaining extension-class ambiguity.

Finite-data obligation still being checked: specify one countable simplicial model of GL_n(R) from K whose simplices and all face/degeneracy/composition operations are generated by finite combinatorial data. Standard simplicial ring-spectrum cofibrant/fibrant replacement supplies this abstractly, but the manuscript must not describe an arbitrary singular simplicial model (which would use uncountably many maps) as a finite algorithm. One concrete route enumerates stable G-equivariant based maps on finite sphere-orbit generators, together with finite inverse/homotopy witnesses; all finite simplicial maps suffice up to mapping-space weak equivalence. A chosen effective Kan/spectral replacement should be stated, not merely assumed.

Safe scope: smoothable PL dimension>=9; all-field definable pi_0 via A.113. It does not prove unstable stabilization for nonsmoothable PL manifolds, does not compute dimensions<=8 in general, and does not affect the real SA pi_2-surjectivity gate. Whether an explicit countable additive presentation counts as the requested arbitrary-class computation is for root to assess; no progress-ledger movement has yet been claimed.
