# Why A.163 does not yet evaluate the whole free-top comparison

Authored scope check, 2026-11-20, following the observed 03:32:59 UTC clock reading. No theorem or movement claim.

A.163 computes the relative cylinder comparison and kills the contribution to beta_B from ordinary TOP loops, under its explicitly stated assembly and central-loop hypotheses. A point of the full automorphism comparison fibre F_A may instead consist of a PL automorphism a together with a TOP path from the identity to a. The endpoint a need not be in the PL identity component. Thus vanishing on ordinary TOP loops does not evaluate beta_B on all components of F_A.

A tempting attempted shortcut is to use Farrell--Jones assembly and five-dimensional relative Borel rigidity on B^4 times I to obtain a PL concordance from the identity to every PL automorphism homotopic to the identity. This is not justified. Ranicki's quoted structure-set identification and Bartels--Lueck's Borel conclusion are TOP statements. Replacing G/TOP by G/PL changes the normal-invariant problem: the TOP/PL fibre contributes precisely the relative degree-three class, which here is H^2(B; Z/2). The TOP structure-set vanishing does not remove that PL reduction datum. Calling the resulting TOP homeomorphism PL would assume the remaining beta_B obstruction away.

No geometric four-dimensional Borel result or PL rigidity statement is extracted.

Full original freshly opened at `https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.11905` (ref `turn606865880view0`). The known realizability statement is not a zero-obstruction sufficiency theorem.
