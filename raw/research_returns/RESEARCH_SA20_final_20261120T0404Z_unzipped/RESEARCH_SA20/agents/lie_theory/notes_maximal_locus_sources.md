# Maximal local-triviality locus: source audit

Updated 2026-11-19 17:48 UTC. No new general proof is claimed.

## Exact remaining assertion
For a continuous definably proper map of definable subsets over a fixed real closed field, with fibre dimension at least two and target dimension at least three, prove that the largest open definable-manifold-without-boundary locus of definable local triviality is definable. The nowhere-dense-complement part and triangulable maps are proved in A5.008. All finite-fibre cases (A5.012), all target-dimension-at-most-two cases (A5.013), and all fibre-dimension-at-most-one cases (A5.014) are independently checked. The dimension restrictions here describe the remaining gate, not extra conclusions inferred from the sources below.

## Sources actually inspected

Theorem4.14 assumes a proper map on a closed stratified subset and transversality to its strata. Corollary5.1 gives a dense Zariski open over which a complex algebraic morphism is topologically locally trivial. Neither identifies the maximal locus. Source: https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/download/pdf/PPN356556735_0036/LOG_0023.pdf . Local PDF and page images under source_external/lie_theory/verdier1976*.
- Anna Valette, *Stratified C^p-semialgebraic triviality*, Manuscripta Math.166 (2021), pp.315–322, complete primary HTML opened. Introduction distinguishes the minimal bifurcation set from Hardt exceptional sets; Theorem2 excludes the stratified generalized critical values. This is only a sufficient condition. Its phrase about a precise description being open does NOT establish that mere definability is open. Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00229-020-01227-w .
- Thom, *Ensembles et morphismes stratifies*, archive retypesetting retrieved from the actual 1968 bibliography link. Internal pp.37–38, Theorem3.C.1, produces a stratified morphism. Section1.G second isotopy theorem requires a morphism without blowing-up. Do not replace that stronger hypothesis by arbitrary stratifiability of the graph. Archive URL: https://www.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/thom/data/1968f7.pdf . Original AMS URL gave403. Internal and journal page numbers differ.
- Ishikawa–Nguyen, *Atypical values at infinity of real polynomial maps with 2-dimensional fibers*, CRMath363 (2025), pp.917–932. Original PDF downloaded via urllib; introduction p.918 asserts semialgebraicity of the minimal bifurcation set and cites Kurdyka–Orro–Simon, Thom, Verdier. The inspected Thom/Verdier statements do not by themselves justify the assertion in our broader arbitrary-continuous-definable scope. The paper itself concerns smooth polynomial maps and regular values, and its main theorem concerns 2-dimensional fibres. Source: https://comptes-rendus.academie-sciences.fr/mathematique/item/10.5802/crmath.761.pdf ; arXiv2404.12709 also opened.
- Kurdyka–Orro–Simon, JDG56 (2000), pp.67–92: currently only the indexed abstract/introduction and metadata read. Abstract asserts measure-zero generalized critical values and a fibration off that set, not equality with the minimal failure locus. Further source retrieval remains in progress.

## Failed approaches to preserve

1. A finite Hardt partition of f gives triviality over the pieces and some dense open union, but it does not show that the maximal open locus is a union of those pieces.
2. Hardt trivialization of a parameterized family of translated-ball preimages does not automatically commute with the original map to the full base. Adding that claim would be an unproved extra condition.
3. Finite pointed space-germ types prove definability of the manifold locus. Finite map-germ types do not follow; Thom families and the discussion after Coste Theorem5.24 supply the warning.
4. A scalar radial function ||f-y||^2 loses angular map information. Reconstructing the full map from it is not justified.
5. A union of all definable trivializing neighborhoods need not be definable merely because every member is definable; a uniform complexity or finite reduction is missing.

## Further routes tested during checkpoint06 freeze (15:26–15:31 UTC)

- The convex-fibre proof is fully written in notes_A5_convex_pending.tex but is not included in the checked chapter and is not counted as movement on the remaining general gate. Its useful mechanism is that properness plus pointwise openness gives Hausdorff-continuous fibres; convexity then supplies unique nearest-point projections, moving affine frames and continuous radial gauges. General fibres lack those canonical compatible identifications.
- Complete regularity of a compact-fibre map asks for arbitrarily small homeomorphisms between nearby fibres. Dyer–Hamstrom is a potential topological bridge to local triviality, but it does not itself make the condition first-order or produce definable parameter families. A primary-source survey by Repovs–Semenov, *Milyutin mappings and their applications*, Topology Proceedings22 (1997), Definition5.1 p.395 and Theorem5.3 p.396, was opened at https://topology.nipissingu.ca/tp/reprints/v22/tp22224.pdf. Theorem5.3 handles compact one-dimensional polyhedral fibres under complete regularity. This does not yet prove a definable trivialization, nor definability of complete regularity. The original Dyer–Hamstrom PDF https://matwbn.icm.edu.pl/ksiazki/fm/fm45/fm45110.pdf returned403 through the web reader; it is not counted as opened.
- Smoothness of all fibres is too weak to trivialize the map along a fibre. Koike–Shiota, *Non-smooth points set of fibres of a semialgebraic mapping*, JMSJ59 (2007), Example4.2 and Remark4.3, provides polynomial examples (x^2+y^2,(x^2+y^2)z^2) and (x^2,xy). Author primary OCR opened via https://www.academia.edu/21193908/Non_smooth_points_set_of_fibres_of_a_semialgebraic_mapping . Theorem4.4 is scalar-valued. No higher-dimensional-target conclusion follows.
- Escribano2002 was rechecked through complete primary article OCR. Its Theorem1.2 is explicitly scalar-valued g:U→R and supplies definable C^p trivialization on intervals avoiding critical and asymptotic critical values. The real-spectrum discussion does not state the needed arbitrary-map recognition theorem.
- A sufficient missing uniformity statement can be formulated exactly: there is a finite list of formulas for graphs of local trivializations, with parameters, such that every locally trivial value admits a chart from that list. All homeomorphism, continuity, inverse and projection conditions for any fixed formula are first-order; taking the finite union would define U. No proof of that uniformity has been found for general definable maps. It must not be inferred from ordinary Hardt triviality of f or from finite pointed space-germ types.

## Additional source conclusions, checkpoint09 (actual clock17:48:32 UTC)

1. The successful Dyer–Hamstrom direct PDF URL is precisely https://matwbn.icm.edu.pl/ksiazki/fm/fm45110.pdf . The older failed link above contains an extra `fm45/` path component. Only the successfully downloaded original supplies the p109 statement.

2. Valette's full primary HTML was reread. Theorem10 restates the Coste–Shiota first isotopy lemma under a supplied Nash Whitney stratification and proper submersion hypotheses. Lemma11 establishes a submersion condition for `(f,rho)` outside a large ball after excluding generalized asymptotic critical values. Neither supplies a converse detecting all locally trivial points or a uniform homeomorphism-chart formula. The general gate is unchanged.

3. Koike–Shiota, *Finiteness of semialgebraic types of Nash mappings defined on a Nash surface*, Math. Z.292 (2019),819–837, DOI10.1007/s00209-018-2104-3, was read through the complete primary-article OCR at https://www.academia.edu/115795868/Finiteness_of_semialgebraic_types_of_Nash_mappings_defined_on_a_Nash_surface . Publisher metadata opened at https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00209-018-2104-3 . Theorem1.1 assumes the Nash surface has isolated singularities. Theorem1.2 and Section5 build a compact algebraic surface consisting of two spheres meeting along a circle and a polynomial family of maps into R² with continuum many topological map types. The limiting relation on the common circle encodes a circle rotation. This is a genuine warning against assuming finiteness of map-germ types from finiteness of space-germ types. The source's stronger Assertion5.3 that all different displayed angle parameters give different types needs a reflection/sign qualification: simultaneous reflection conjugates a rotation to its inverse. The text's simplified proof comparing parameter0 to a nonzero parameter does not by itself justify the full all-parameter assertion. No unqualified use of that assertion has been promoted here.

4. Koike–Shiota Section5 explicitly reports a correction in2013 to the original Coste–Reguiat counterexample. A current search found no accessible full corrected version. The original chapter's actual public preview was opened at https://page-one.springer.com/pdf/preview/10.1007/BFb0084620 (two printed pages,193–194; web ref `turn299104811view0`). Its introduction specifies that Section4 gives finite trivializations for **scalar-valued** semialgebraic functions, using real-spectrum transfer; Section5 gives the contrasting plane-map counterexample. The preview does not provide the Section5 proof or its2013 correction. The publisher chapter page https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/BFb0084620 expressly identifies the full text as subscription content. The full PDF request failed. Author-page attempts at both `perso.univ-rennes1.fr/michel.coste/articles.html` and `perso.univ-rennes.fr/michel.coste/articles.html` returned503 or403; the HTTP version timed out. No unavailable correction has been treated as opened.

5. The direct blowdown example on Shiota2010 printedp33 continues to rule out the naive assertion that every compact definable map into R² is globally triangulable. Its injectivity away from a collapsed boundary edge gives the finite-simplex contradiction even when target triangulating homeomorphisms are allowed: subdivide to make the collapsed value a vertex; an adjacent source triangle has two vertices mapped to that value and hence cannot be injective off the edge. This does not contradict the punctured-edge local-model theorem used in A5.013.

6. A speculative extension of the p33 method to a higher-target codimension-two exceptional set is **not proved**. Uniform finite fibres for selected coordinate projections are insufficient to control frontier limits of all graph coordinates; blowdown already exhibits the obstruction. Likewise, the existence of arbitrarily small definable fibre homeomorphisms cannot be promoted to definable local triviality without a definable parameter-selection and gluing theorem. These are remaining proof requirements, not source conclusions.
