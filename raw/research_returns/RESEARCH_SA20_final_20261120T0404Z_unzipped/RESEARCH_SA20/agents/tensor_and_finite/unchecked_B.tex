\subsection*{Part B: no unresolved gate in the original requested scope}
B.082 gives a necessary-and-sufficient arithmetic syzygy criterion for
well-determined coaddition and comultiplication for every unital
commutative inclusion $R\subset S$. Its input is the explicit
Vandermonde-ideal coefficient ring, the module of power functions
$R\to C/R$, a finite matrix epimorphism test, Tor through degree two,
and two fully specified transgressions. Every constituent is defined
without a tensor power of $\Omega$; free relations and boundaries
needed to compute it have homological degree at most three.
This closes B.2(c), including arbitrary non-Noetherian, nonflat,
nonreduced and torsion coefficient extensions. It was independently
proved again by both root and the Lie-theory agent before promotion.
The previously listed connected monomial base is therefore subject
to the same complete criterion, although no separate simplification
of that criterion for this optional test family is asserted.

The criterion is not a uniform terminating algorithm for infinitely
presented rings. Binary existence and uniqueness do not silently
supply higher plethory coherence. Those stronger assertions are not
part of this theorem or required for the two tensors requested on
153/004. B.009 retains the earlier elementary tensor formulation;
B.082 replaces its unknown tensor kernel/image by explicit arithmetic
syzygy data. B.062 and B.063--B.081 give more concrete descriptions
for the stated large ring classes and preserve the failed-route
counterexamples.

B.066 settles faithful polynomial evaluation for every inclusion by
$\operatorname{Ann}_S D_d(R)=0$ for every $d$. The literal unqualified
reconstruction on 155/002 is refuted by B.002; the corrected theorem,
rigid case, inseparable diagonal and dual statements, and source
comparison are proved separately. No false literal statement is
recorded as proved. The plethory and divided-power questions retain
their explicit added-structure and nonunital-action qualifications.

Requested sources that could not be opened in full remain honestly
marked in the source inventory and bibliography. None supplies an
unproved mathematical dependency. No historical priority assertion
