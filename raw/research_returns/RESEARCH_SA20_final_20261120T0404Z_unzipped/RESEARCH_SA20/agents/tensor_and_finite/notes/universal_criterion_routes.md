# Universal arithmetic criterion: research status, 2026-11-19 17:54 UTC

This note is not a theorem inventory and does not close B.2(c).

## Proved necessary reduction, pending independent audit

With the effective coefficient ring C=union_d(R:_S D_d), injectivity of theta_2 implies that R→C is epic and C tensor_R Omega=C[X]. The latter is equivalent to C tensor_R N(R,C/R)=0, where N is defined directly by finite coefficient vectors (q_i) satisfying sum r^i q_i=0 for every r in R. Full proof is pending_B079.tex.

## Failed sufficient route, explicit certificate

Even C flat and epic plus that base-change isomorphism do not imply monic regularity or theta injectivity. R=F_q+t l[[t]], l infinite, C=l((t)); P=X^q-X; beta in l\F_q. The nonzero tensor z=beta P tensor P-P tensor beta P is killed by P in the first variable, because P^2/t belongs to Omega and t,t beta belong to R. Full proof is pending_B079.tex. Thus localization at monic coefficient witnesses loses an actual kernel.

## What has not been accepted as a structural advance

Merely presenting each bounded-degree coefficient module by evaluation kernels and enumerating its tensor relations would translate B.009, not solve the requested arbitrary-base classification. A usable universal criterion must extract ring ideals or finite syzygy invariants without presupposing those tensor-image decisions. Leading-coefficient ideals alone are unlikely to suffice: even positive nonnormal Noetherian examples can have nonprojective degree pieces, while their relations disappear after passing to higher degree. No necessity of flatness is asserted; B.067 already refutes it for general rings.

## Current potential route

For a flat epic coefficient extension C, let E be the R-module of finite power functions R→C/R, generated by r↦c r^i modulo R. The exact sequence 0→Omega→C[X]→E→0 suggests that the tensor kernel is Tor_2^R(E,E), because C tensor_R E=0 and C is flat. This would be a genuine homological arithmetic obstruction, but existence of the two co-operations still requires an explicit obstruction class. The general nonflat C case has additional Tor correction terms. This route is unproved in this note and is not promoted.
