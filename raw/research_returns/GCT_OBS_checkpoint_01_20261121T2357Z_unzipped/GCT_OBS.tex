\begin{abstract}
This continuing research package distinguishes proved comparisons from
uncomputed multiplicity gates. It reconstructs the twenty-five exceptional
seventh-minimum prolongation computations, gives an arithmetic proof of
CP border rank at least 43 for the integral determinant and permanent
tensors of size four in every characteristic, independently verifies the
quaternary permanent dimension certificates and their uniform insertion,
and supplies a full support-lemma proof and an explicit recomputation of
the degree-twelve LMR comparison $6>5$.
For the main padded problem, nested coefficient projections of determinant
pencils of sizes nine and twenty-three are completely classified. An
all-degree family at $(m,n)=(8,9)$ reduces to one degree-24 rank test with
padded multiplicity 804 and determinant multiplicity either 803 or 804.
No uncomputed rank is described as a multiplicity obstruction.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Conventions, provenance, and current scope}
The padding variable is independent of the $m^2$ permanent variables.
Consequently the elementary row cap under genuine padding is $m^2+1$;
S-004 checks the places where the inherited internal-padding proofs used

different field is explicitly specified. The border-rank statements use
the integral, divided-power coefficient tensors, as in BR\_PENCIL FL-01;

\section{The main padded witnesses are not yet established}
For $(8,9)$ the degree-six label $(15,13,13,13)$ has ambient
multiplicity one. A symbolic bracket construction is under examination;
a nonzero padded evaluation alone cannot establish the determinant
vanishing that is still required. Independently, S-005 reduces the
family $(9d-24,8,8,8)$ to its full 804-dimensional degree-24 determinant
evaluation map. Only the alternatives $K=803$ and $K=804$ are proved.
An evaluation on diagonal determinant pencils cannot alone settle this
gate, because that smaller Chow family need not retain all copies.

For $(17,23)$, OBS-001 gives an exact all-copy exclusion region.
The first unexcluded partial-Hessian ray stretch has billions of
ambient copies (OBS-002), so a direct dense basis computation in that
space is not a practical witness construction. Other directions and
smaller projections remain under examination. No astronomical
existence bound is substituted for an explicit equation.

\section{Border rank 44 and the remaining extension schemes}
The characteristic-independent seventh-minimum argument proves 43.
It does not establish $\mu_{10}\ge18$ or decide whether a ten-plane has
border rank17. The ongoing calculations in \texttt{work/border} impose
additional Koszul conditions on limiting extension tensors and coupled
multigraded incidence conditions. Any provisional enumeration is kept
separate from BR-001--BR-003 until its entire proof and coverage are
reviewed. The exact values of $\mu_{11},\mu_{12}$ conditional on a

\section{Covariants and ratios beyond the dimension criterion}
The full archived proofs of GCT\_KRON A-035--A-037 have been read.
The basic proper partial-Hessian copy has determinant multiplicity
equal to ambient multiplicity one. The stated binary Hessian coefficient
algebras restrict injectively in their specified ranges, and the
ternary example of A-037 supplies a fourth ambient copy outside its

its largest successful padding, and a finite all-copy comparison in
a nontrivial direction beyond $n/m=\sqrt2$ are still open here.
