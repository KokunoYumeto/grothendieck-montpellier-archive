\section{Scope and verification conventions}
The target questions ask for a decision of strict shortening over
$\mathbb C$ and $\mathbb F_2$, the exact class of prices on several finite
sets, and an optimal exponent for a specified family of lower-bound
arguments. The attached manuscripts are cited as unrefereed claims.
Neither their separation theorems nor their entire formal developments
have been verified in this run. The supplied Anthropic Lean file contains
definitions of theorem statements, not their proofs.

Every statement receives a stable identifier. Proof checks are recorded
in the separate file \texttt{CHECK\_EVENTS.tsv}, including the checker,
check type, and actual outcome. A computational check of finitely many
cases is never promoted to a universal proof. All logarithms are base two
unless otherwise indicated.
