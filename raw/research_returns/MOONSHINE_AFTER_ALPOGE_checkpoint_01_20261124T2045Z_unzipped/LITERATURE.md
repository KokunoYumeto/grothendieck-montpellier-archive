# Literature and source-use record

Searches and retrievals below are dated24November2026. These are bounded
searches, not an exhaustive-bibliography or priority claim. Retrieved
publications are excluded from every archive.

1. Alpöge's Dirichlet GRH: theorem declarations, source audit and primary
   community/kernel-verification reports opened; no local Lean rebuild.
2. Periodic Dirichlet coefficients and Davenport-Heilbronn zeros:
   Saias-Weingartner2009,pp335–336, primitive-character basis and zero theorem.
3. Epstein zeros and rank24 universality: Nakamura-Pańkowski2016,
   MainTheorems1–2 andTheoremE, preprintpp4–5; applies to all24 theta sums.
4. Harmonic-Maass Mellin transforms: Diamantis-Lee-Raji-Rolen,
   arXiv2107.12366v4,Definition4.2,Lemma4.4,Theorem4.5; regularization needed.
5. Ogg1974 original Numdam scan: fixed-point class-number formula already
   Ogg's; the explicit cutoffs and finite computation are the application.
6. Explicit L1 bounds: Lamzouri-Li-SoundararajanTheorem1.5 and
   Languasco-TrudgianTheorem1, the latter valid for allq≥3.
7. Cheng-Duncan-Mertens preprint and JNT277(2025),pp201–235:
   Theorem4.2.5,eq4.2.11,eq4.3.1–4. Direct normalization checks require a
   fixed cusp shift and a coefficient factor2.
8. Tunnell1983 original scan: definitionp323 andTheorem3,pp328–329.
9. Conway-NortonTable2,BorcherdsTheorem1.1,Koike1985TableI,
   Kondo1985TableI,pp355–357. All primary documents opened.
10. Huber-Liu-McLaughlin-Ye-Yuan-Zhang,Lemma2.1,eq2.2,2.4,2.10:
    primary-generator descriptions give the CM eta identities.
11. Bilu-Luca-MasserTable1,p1057: all13 rational singular moduli,
    including nonmaximal orders; only0 lies in the Niemeier target interval.
12. Current Hexagon listings, Hu's unitary extension, Liu2611.00741
    Theorem7.33 and Gao2611.00334Theorem1 read. Paper claims, source audits
    and independent local checks are distinguished in the companion note.
13. Recent OpenAI mathematics: opened the15November science blog and
    TheQuasi-RiemannHypothesis,30September2026,Theorem1.1p4,199pages.
    It concerns finite-order Hecke and Dirichlet characters, not the
    positive-infinity-type target. Its completed-index method is relevant.
14. Dunn-Radziwiłł,arXiv2109.07463v3: exact cubic Gauss normalization,
    Lemma5.4,eq5.61p28,and§13p69. Moments divisible by3 use HeckeGRH
    there and are not borrowed as unconditional estimates.
15. ECCC and web/journal/arXiv author-page searches for zero-free,
    metaplectic, rank24-Epstein and eta classification work found no directly
    usable ECCC result in the bounded pass.
16. Chandee2009,arXiv0906.4177,Corollaries1.1and1.3,p3:
    extra elliptic-twist GRH implies a Lindelof upper bound, not a
    distribution law for central values.
