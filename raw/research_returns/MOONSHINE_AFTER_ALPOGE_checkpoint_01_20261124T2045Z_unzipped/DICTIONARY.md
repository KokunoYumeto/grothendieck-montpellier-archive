# Geometric and arithmetic dictionary

## Unary shadows and Artin motives

Character orthogonality on finite unit groups diagonalizes each periodic
coefficient function after division by its gcd with the modulus. Each
one-dimensional constituent is a Dirichlet Artin character. Its theta
Mellin transform replaces the variable by2s−1 and supplies an explicit
scale. A sum of constituents gives a sum of L-functions; the L-function
of their direct sum would instead be a product. Mixed-shadow off-line
zeros therefore do not contradict an Artin RH assertion.

## CM cohomology, correspondences and symmetric powers

Over K=Q(ω), H1 of the j=0 curve splits into ψ and its conjugate.
Sym3 splits into the cube characters and H1 tensored with the norm.
This gives the full symmetric-cube identity, including bad-prime inertia.
The explicit degree-two cusp-curve isogeny is a correspondence identifying
rational H1 and hence every local L-factor.

## Lambda rings and finite colour

Symmetric powers of finite representations obey the usual lambda-ring
identities; these describe the lambency-six twining class functions.
The finite unit colour records an archimedean type only modulo6.
Tensoring by the conductor-one type6 character leaves the finite colour
unchanged and changes the infinity type. Finite representation theory
therefore does not determine the full CM motive. Atlas chainsC2,C3,C9
motivate this comparison; no further Grothendieck correspondence is
inferred without an archived statement showing it.

## Integral lattices, glue and modular forms

Isotropic discriminant subgroups select integral overlattices. Their
indices give the Fricke constants in the examples. A rational action need
not stabilize every neighbour: the full residue generator permutes the
six Leech lattices in two three-cycles. The Eisenstein/cusp decomposition
of a theta series is a linear decomposition, not an Euler-product
factorization of its scalar Epstein zeta function.

## Hermitian trace forms

For a free rankn Eisenstein Hermitian lattice, the trace determinant is
3^n(det H)^2, and the Z-dual is the inverse different times the Hermitian
dual. Hermitian self-duality differs from trace unimodularity. Alpöge's
growing-rank definite-unitary construction does not supply a rank24
Niemeier object without further integrality and determinant data.

## Class numbers and theta correspondences

The Hurwitz term counts quadratic orders with automorphism weights and
local isotropic-line conditions. Its Euler factors expose Dirichlet
L(1,χ). The cusp coefficient is linked by a half-integral-weight theta
correspondence to central values of quadratic twists of a weight-two
curve. Squaring loses its sign. Weights and RH alone determine neither
that sign nor central vanishing.
