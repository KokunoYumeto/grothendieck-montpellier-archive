# Locator conventions and source limitations

The Montpellier archive PDFs include a cover. In the documents inspected here, PDF page 45 of cote 16 has archive footer 44; PDF page 2 of cote 44 has footer 1. The attached sketch's phrase “scan 45” agrees with the PDF-page convention. Both numbers are therefore retained in new citations. No correction of the mathematical transcription follows from this one-page offset.

`TRANSCRIPTION_NOTES.tsv` has no data row unless a mathematical misreading of an original page is established. Empty data rows do not mean that every archival line was transcribed or checked. We have not attempted a general paleographic audit.

The task admits the attached 032 CM Hodge theorem and Alpöge Dirichlet GRH statements. We record their actual use and the available formal declarations. The Lean imports needed to compile Alpöge's proof are absent from the upload. Masullo's supplied file is a statement index; it does not contain the full §1.2 claim or the 940-page draft. All general Hecke-GRH dependence remains explicitly conditional.

References downloaded from the public literature are background sources. They are not counted as findings of this run. Source-specific check events distinguish inspection of a statement, reading its proof, using it as background, and an independent re-proof. Numerical tests are not described as formal proof certificates.
