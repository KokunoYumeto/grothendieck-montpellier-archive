# What this continuation changes

Status: work in progress, 22 November 2026. The integral connectedness and terminality questions remain active.

## Cote 136: admissible generalized de Rham theories

**Scans111–121, especially physical121/footer120.** ARCH-136-HOM-DEFINITION and ARCH-136-FRACTIONS give the quoted “equalizes” a precise meaning in the strict PD category: after an admissible precomposition, the two maps agree up to a finite chain of coefficient-preserving simplicial cotensor homotopies. Equality of original arrows in the all-arrow groupoid is equivalent to this condition. Hence accepted semilocal terminality really implies the proposed homotopy reading. Over Z, this is exactly the componentwise fundamental-group gate; it is not inferred from connectedness alone.

**Scans117 and119, conditions(3) and(4).** ARCH-136-ARITH-BOUND removes the sum of local prime powers in AQ-136-SEMILOCAL equation136.S2. A single rational model replaces their tensor. The remaining horizontal maximum still grows with the chosen local models. Fixed-degree vertical bounds no longer grow with the prime list. This isolates the horizontal issue more precisely without asserting a solution over all primes.

**The higher-skeleton gate.** ARCH-136-DISK-SUPPORT strengthens AQ-136-PARTIAL-SOURCE. Every finite family over Z has a strict common partial source with the required horizontal tails, exact vertical degrees0–3, and degree-d defects killed by the same L_d in every simplex and weight. Thus degree d involves only finitely many small primes. Completion must preserve this control; ARCH-136-LOW-REFINEMENT rules out a particular inverse tower that lifts low free generators. The original common-source conjecture is neither proved nor refuted.

**Concentration in one form degree.** ARCH-136-DEGREE-TWO-FREE gives a different common partial source, free in every underlying abelian group, satisfying every admissibility condition except eventual vertical exactness in degree2. Its H0 is S and H1 is zero in every weight. This reduction sacrifices the finite-prime bound of the preceding construction. ARCH-136-DEGREE-TWO-RESIDUE identifies the exact prescribed-repair obstruction as differences of canonical maps from H2[N] to coefficient vertex residues modulo N and a diagonal constant. Equal first-skeleton maps force equality of those residues, but ARCH-136-SKELETON-OBSTRUCTION proves that agreement on any fixed finite skeleton does not guarantee the next repair. No unrestricted relative-completion theorem is inferred.

**Which comparison routes are now closed.** ARCH-136-ASSOCIATIVE-TERMINAL proves terminality after changing the algebraic structure to associative differential algebras, and the degree-one Massey comparison uses one cutoff for every arity. The integral strict-PD end has an exact vertical contraction but retains Tor in its horizontal zero-form row: ARCH-136-TW-TOR gives Z/p at all weights p^a for the standard input B. Thus that proposed rectification cannot solve the integral question. The constant-input end remains admissible, and the rational tensor/end construction has a separate valid cutoff.

**A commutative operation test is also rigid.** ARCH-136-DETERMINANT-RIGIDITY treats every degree-one higher determinant product of Flynn-Connolly, Moreno-Fernández and Muro, arXiv:2611.25690v1, Definition5.1, with the stated compatible weights. Its full set is an image of a fiber under a local-coefficient connecting map. The integral gauge and group-ring projective resolution give one cutoff independent of arity. This is a separate strict commutative proof; associative comparison alone would not justify it. The result removes this family as a component detector without reducing the unresolved constructive gate.

**The direction of a repair matters.** ARCH-136-COSKELETON-FORWARD rules out forward completion of the actual pullback C×cosk(sk C)C over any finite skeleton, extending its two projections, even when C is the standard admissible model. A sphere class has different images under the projections. The diagonal C into that same pullback is an admissible inverse source. Thus the theorem excludes that prescribed extension route and leaves open inverse modification of a partial source to make its degree-two residues agree.

**Individual primitives versus their relations.** The one-disk p-local Moore tower is exact, but its native classes need not lift along a prescribed guarded target map. ARCH-136-GUARDED-PAIR supplies each sufficiently high coefficient multiple and its secondary primitive simultaneously. The zero-form secondary range is prime-uniform and coefficient-theoretic. Neither assertion supplies a family respecting all S-relations. Ordinary tail ideals have infinite projective dimension; the separately computed Beck conormal must not be confused with the special differential quotient. After finitely many primes are inverted, the large-prime underived special quotient has an explicit length-one resolution. Higher derived and multiplicative coherence remain unproved.

**Exact envelopes and their limits.** ARCH-136-SCALED-BAR gives an explicit functorial positive-form acyclic envelope with integral contraction. Its coefficient-scaled image omits indecomposable tail generators. The unscaled radial image agrees with the original algebra below form p over Z_(p), but ARCH-136-RADIAL-TARGET detects a mixed product that cannot be divided in the prescribed standard target. ARCH-136-RELATIVE-BAR-OBSTRUCTION gives persistent S/2 vertical cohomology even for a relative bar over the standard zero-form algebra. These statements have exact construction scopes; they are not counterexamples to conjecture(a).

**Two stronger coherence tests.** ARCH-136-FREE-ARITY-TAIL-OBSTRUCTION excludes a homogeneous repair with bounded flat coefficient blocks in each auxiliary arity: finite weight-supported cohomology forces multiplication by p to be invertible, contradicting a native torsion image that must be retained. ARCH-136-NATIVE-TAIL-NOSPLIT then constructs an actual integral admissible target where both individual Moore primitives exist for every sufficiently high coefficient multiple, but no S-linear family of first primitives exists on any tail. A single nonflat tail-module adjunction therefore also fails as a universal repair. More general nonflat presentations and changes to the source remain open.

**Integral periods constrain targets.** ARCH-136-VERTICAL-PERIODS obtains integral relative periods from the vertical condition alone. In particular every strict map B→E into a vertically admissible theory is injective, and its standard image is integrally saturated when E is torsion-free (ARCH-136-STANDARD-LATTICE-EMBEDDING). This rules out enlarging the rational standard lattice to repair it. Torsionful targets remain a distinct possible route. ARCH-136-INITIAL-TENSOR-PERIODS also shows that the initial tensor B⊗C has the required ordinary integral periods; this does not guarantee periods of subsequently adjoined primitives.

**Geometric interpretation.** These are results about strict PD algebra models over SpecZ and the coefficient PD envelope Gamma(T), with simplicial descent as in SGA4 Vbis§5. They do not by themselves assert a new crystalline comparison for schemes. For the latter, the exact PD-Poincare and affine comparison hypotheses are those in Stacks60.20.1–2 and60.21.3 (the Berthelot framework). The weight variable does not automatically define lambda operations or Adams operations in K-theory.

## Cote134–8: linear derived localization

**Physical49/footer48, scan049, F-SM44.** ARCH-134-02 refutes automatic surjectivity in degrees≥2 even with the scalar, tensor and interval hypotheses. The explicit failure is already a countable b_2. This closes the user's counterexample alternative for the higher-surjectivity gate.

**Localization conclusion.** ARCH-134-03 proves that this same example still localizes to the connective derived category. Therefore the auxiliary comparison is not necessary for the conclusion. The universal bare theorem requires a proof bypassing that comparison, or a different counterexample whose localization fails. No failure of the Thm(?) is claimed.

**Raw ordinary G.** ARCH-134-04 proves the SM25.2 test over commutative noetherian scalar rings of finite Krull dimension with the stated scalar-compatible interval. Published finite pure-injective resolutions replace a cardinality restriction. The general unrestricted test remains open. The geometric language here is linearization and derived scalar reflection, not a new six-functor theorem for arbitrary topoi.

## Cote15: actual correspondences, weights and motives

**Physical94–106, scans094–106; CC-XC03 andCC-XC11–15.** The paper specifies the exact mixed cycle map for an arbitrary cubic fourfold (codimension4) and finite-height K3 surface (codimension2). Under the fixed target's AB hypothesis, existence of actual forward Chow cycles is equivalent to the stated finite family of usual Tate-conjecture cases. The ordinary Tate theorem for X or for X×X alone is not silently substituted for a mixed X×Y theorem.

**New geometric sectors.** Split-variable cubic fourfolds in characteristic≠3 admit a degree3 domination by the indicated blowup of a product of cubic surfaces; the blowup formula supplies an explicit elliptic/Artin/Tate Chow target and exact Chow projector. Kummer K3 surfaces in characteristic≠2 have a degree2 Chow retraction from the blowup of an abelian surface. Products of ordinary elliptic curves and of one ordinary with one supersingular elliptic curve supply finite heights1 and2. Products C×C already lie in CC-XC08; their curve/Jacobian construction is not claimed new.

**Published replacements.** ARCH-15-ORDINARY-TARGET constructs a selected target for ordinary K3 surfaces and ordinary cubic fourfolds using Honda–Tate and Milne2001 AppendixA.7. Its full Tate realization uses published theorems, with no outside all-CM preprint. The mixed Chow cycle remains missing in general. In the explicit elliptic sectors, Spiess/Milne likewise suffices. For an arbitrary previously fixed spectral target, the all-abelian step still uses the named van Hoften–Sempliner preprint; Deligne's absolute Hodge theorem alone does not replace algebraicity. A, M, Hexagon2610.00226v1 and2611.00244v1 are unused in these new constructions.

**CC-AG14 and BR-15-CLASSBOUND.** The full local index calculation passes: over Q(sqrt−23), the Frobenius label has local invariants1/3 and2/3, index3, and dimension3. The argument checks all candidate labels for descent to F8, not merely the displayed one. This verifies the exact counterexample to Thakur2020 arXivv1 Proposition2.9. No statement about a later revision is made. These findings concern Frobenius spectra, effective motivic targets and L-function factors; they do not prove general Tate.

## Cote123: the letter to Kostant

**Physical22–23/footer21–22, scans022–023.** The companion assembles the complete inherited classification, opposition table, tangent rank, Borel-variety dimension and rational-singularity answers with exact citations. Richardson1987 Lemma5.1, crediting Slodowy, is explicitly credited for the subregular rank theorem. BR-12302 is displayed as the concrete sp4 instance.

**Normal form and outer symmetry.** ARCH-123-NORMAL verifies CC-AP04–05 for composite as well as prime n, closing the requested converse check in CC-APG04. ARCH-123-COUNT gives an explicit even-n fixed-orbit count and checks small cases computationally. The literature audit identifies known Coxeter-normalizer and diagram-exception results. It does not establish historical novelty of the exact opposition table or outer-invariant classification.

## Post-checkpoint03 refinement of the cote136 coherence route

**Scans117–121, relative coefficient tails.** ARCH-136-DERIVED-TAIL-COMPARISON replaces the impossible direct S-linear tail splitting by a finite system on a free resolution of the tail ideal. ARCH-136-RELATIVE-COHERENT-MOORE supplies all its linear homotopies, including the final form-zero relation. ARCH-136-COHERENT-PD-REALIZATION constructs the actual strict PD algebra and rational disk contraction; COHERENT-LOW-REPAIR then restores the inherited H0/H1 and horizontal conditions. This settles the existence of that finite coherent realization, without settling its higher integral cohomology.

**Nonflat PD envelopes.** ARCH-136-NONFLAT-QUOTIENT-DISK, NONFLAT-TAIL-DISK and NONFLAT-KERNEL-LATTICE give exact failures of an integral even-disk Poincare inference. Weightwise abelian freeness, coefficient torsion-freeness and one saturated-kernel lattice condition are insufficient. This concerns strict PD algebra models over SpecZ; it does not contradict the flat/local-coordinate crystalline Poincare hypotheses cited above. The actual higher syzygy, repeated new classes and prime-uniform form range remain to be controlled.

**Inverse modification.** ARCH-136-LOW-GENERATED removes degree-two cohomology by restricting the degree-two term to the subalgebra generated in degrees zero and one. Its new row-two and degree-three defects are explicit, including persistent torsion for a permitted but avoidable presentation. ARCH-136-TAIL-MOORE shows that even an actual nonzero coefficient-tail repair can introduce an annihilator class one degree lower. These exact route constraints do not establish nonexistence of common admissible sources.

## Coefficient duality and the fixed-first-skeleton refinement

**Cote136, scans117/119/121.** ARCH-136-GRADED-INJECTIVES and TAIL-EXT-VANISHING prove that positive coefficient tails have no higher graded Ext into S in every nonnegative shift, over Z and Z_(p). This concerns a particular target of Ext; the earlier infinite projective dimension of the tails is unchanged. The resulting derived Hom comparison is canonical and survives every finite-modulus cone.

**Coherent realization with actual edge control.** ARCH-136-SKELETON-COHERENT-MOORE extends every torsion pair in form degree≥4 after a sufficiently high coefficient tail, with all resolution relations and common graph values. The strict realization and RH1 keep the original first skeleton exactly. The actual linear relation classes from the first native degree-four repair have zero graph residue and explicit coherent primitives. The remaining nonlinear PD cohomology has not been proved to vanish.

**The bottom module is not a hypothetical risk.** ARCH-136-ACTUAL-TAIL-SYZYGY exhibits the zero-form defect in the actual bottom lattice for every allowed free resolution of each prime-power tail, and in the raw strict realization itself. The explicit minimal resolution, graded retract and order-p detector prove this without extrapolating from a generic nonflat example. The separately proved low-degree repair remains applicable.

## Extra constants and nonlinear even-disk cohomology

**Cote136, physical117/footer116/scan117 and physical121/footer120/scan121.** ARCH-136-EVEN-DISK-H1 and EVEN-DISK-H2 show that raw nonflat even disks can have positive-form cohomology, including homogeneous examples over the mod-p divided-power coefficient ring. Their explicit classes factor through extra closed zero-forms, so they do not survive the proposed quotient and do not refute the low-degree repair. The integral variants have free parameter modules over Z_(p), but a different square-zero coefficient ring.

**A completely repaired subclass.** ARCH-136-CYCLIC-DISK-QUOTIENT proves that, for an arbitrary direct sum of cyclic coefficient modules, quotienting the full PD ideal of extra closed zero-forms yields an actual contraction onto the coefficient ring. The proof uses actual tensor contractions and requires no flatness. It does not cover the noncyclic actual bottom lattice, nor its interaction with the higher coherent generators. The general admissible generalized de Rham theory and crystalline comparison questions retain their prior status.

**The standalone noncyclic quotient is now refuted.** ARCH-136-NONCYCLIC-CONSTANT-QUOTIENT gives an exact all-odd-prime one-relation detector, and ARCH-136-ACTUAL-TAIL-CONSTANT-QUOTIENT transfers it to every permitted free resolution of a prime-power tail. The resulting degree-one classes survive the full ideal of extra closed zeroforms in unbounded weights. This applies to the standalone even disk on the actual bottom module. It does not assert survival in the whole coherent realization after RH1, which is designed to kill degree-one cohomology.

**A genuine nonlinear component has a vanishing graph obstruction.** ARCH-136-NATIVE-MIXED-TOR computes the exact native auxiliary component of two independent degree-four coherent repairs, including the original differentials, as a truncated tensor resolution. It has unbounded degree-three Tor classes that survive the specified factorwise RH1 repair. ARCH-136-NATIVE-MIXED-RESIDUE nevertheless proves that every Moore pair in that component has zero individual graph residue: its cone cohomology is killed by the square of the coefficient tail, whereas the graph coefficient target has no such annihilator. This settles the displayed mixed quadratic component, not the diagonal divided-power components or a complete admissible source.
