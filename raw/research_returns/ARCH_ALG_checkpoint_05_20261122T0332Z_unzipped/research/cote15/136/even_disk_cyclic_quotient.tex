\subsection{Killing all extra zero-form constants repairs disks on sums of cyclic modules}
\label{arch136:cyclic-disk-quotient}

\paragraph{Archive and scope.}
This addresses the zero-form quotient proposed for the nonlinear
repair in cote136, physical p.~117/footer116/scan117 and
physical p.~121/footer120/scan121. All arguments are explicit,
tagged none. No flatness or finiteness assumption is used.
The statement concerns the special even disk itself; it is
not a theorem about arbitrary coherent realizations with
additional higher-form generators.

Let \(A\) be a commutative coefficient ring and let
\[
 M=\bigoplus_{i\in\mathcal I}(A/I_i)u_i,
 \qquad
 D=\Gamma_A(M[0])\otimes_A\Lambda_A(M[1]),
 \qquad du_i=e_i.                                      \tag{CQ.1}
\]
If the coefficient ring has a specified PD ideal, use the
compatible relative PD structure on the new even variables.
Let \(\epsilon:D\to A\) be the augmentation, and put
\[
 Z_{\mathrm{extra}}^0=\ker(d:D^0\to D^1)\cap\ker\epsilon.
                                                               \tag{CQ.2}
\]
Let \(J\) be the full differential PD ideal generated by
\(Z_{\mathrm{extra}}^0\). Thus coefficients are retained.

\paragraph{ARCH-136-CYCLIC-DISK-QUOTIENT.}
The quotient \(D/J\) admits an actual \(A\)-linear cochain
contraction onto \(A\). In particular
\[
                H^0(D/J)=A,\qquad H^{q>0}(D/J)=0.        \tag{CQ.3}
\]
If the ideals and the generators in (CQ.1) are homogeneous,
the contraction preserves weight. It also preserves each
parameter arity. Consequently the assertion has no prime,
weight, rank, or form-degree cutoff.

\begin{proof}
First take one cyclic module \(M=(A/I)u\). The free module
PD algebra on one generator has its arity-\(r\) part free
of rank one over \(A\). Imposing the full PD ideal of
\(Iu=0\) therefore gives
\[
                 \Gamma_A^r(A/I)=(A/J_r)\gamma_r(u)
                    \quad(r\geq1)                     \tag{CQ.4}
\]
for an ideal \(J_r\subset I\). The containment follows
also by base change to \(A/I\), where the free divided-power
basis survives. The exterior square of a cyclic module is
zero. Moreover
\[
 \Gamma_A^{r-1}(A/I)\otimes_A(A/I)\simeq A/I,
\]
including \(r=1\). Thus its entire positive-arity complex
is the two-term block
\[
       A/J_r\xrightarrow{\mathrm{quotient}}A/I
          \quad\text{in forms zero and one}.            \tag{CQ.5}
\]
There are no positive-form groups beyond it. The differential
has coefficient one because
\(d\gamma_r(u)=\gamma_{r-1}(u)e_u\).

The extra closed zero-forms in arity \(r\) are precisely
\((I/J_r)\gamma_r(u)\). Their sum over \(r>0\) is a
differential PD ideal in this cyclic disk. To check the PD
assertion directly, a divided power of
\(a\gamma_r(u)\), with \(a\in I\), has coefficient
in \(I\), since its scalar factor contains a positive
power of \(a\). Additivity handles sums, and ordinary
multiplication preserves the same coefficient ideal. Products
with a form-one factor are already zero, since \(Ie_u=0\).
Its quotient, denoted \(Q_I\), has constant summand \(A\)
and in each positive arity the block
\[
                          A/I\xrightarrow{1}A/I.        \tag{CQ.6}
\]
Sending \(\gamma_{r-1}(u)e_u\) to \(\gamma_r(u)\)
contracts this block. This is an \(A\)-linear homotopy,
without any division by \(r\) or by an integer.

Now use the direct sum in (CQ.1). Its disk is the tensor
product of the cyclic disks. Let \(J_{\mathrm{cyc}}\)
be the PD ideal generated by the extra closed zero-forms
coming from the individual factors. Quotienting gives
\[
                    D/J_{\mathrm{cyc}}
                           \simeq\bigotimes_i Q_{I_i}.  \tag{CQ.7}
\]
Each factor is \(A\) plus the direct sum of the contractible
blocks (CQ.6). Expand the tensor product according to the
finite support of its positive-arity factors. The empty
support gives \(A\). Every nonempty support has a
contractible block as one factor, so tensoring its actual
contraction with the other factors contracts that summand.
The Koszul signs cancel the cross terms in \(dh+hd\).
No tensor-exactness assertion or flatness is needed.

For an arbitrary index set, fix an ordering and use the first
positive-arity factor in each finite support. This defines an
actual contraction on the direct sum of all nonempty-support
summands. Every element has finite support, so the formula is
well-defined. All these maps preserve arities and weights.

It remains to verify that this is the full quotient asked for,
rather than a smaller one. By definition
\(J_{\mathrm{cyc}}\subseteq J\).
Every extra closed zero-form of \(D\) maps to a closed
zero-form of (CQ.7) having augmentation zero. But the latter
complex has \(H^0=A\) and has no negative forms. Its closed
zero-forms are therefore exactly its coefficient copy of
\(A\), so such an image is literally zero. It follows that
\(J\subseteq J_{\mathrm{cyc}}\), since (CQ.7) is a
differential PD quotient. Hence the two ideals agree, proving
all assertions.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Application and exact remaining class.}
This theorem repairs all of the finite line-ideal examples in
ARCH-136-EVEN-DISK-H1 and ARCH-136-EVEN-DISK-H2, including
their weightwise \(\mathbb Z_{(p)}\)-free variants over
square-zero coefficient rings. It explains the observed
factorization of their positive classes through closed
zero-forms and shows that no new cohomology appears after
the full quotient in these particular examples.

The homogeneous examples over \(\Gamma_{\mathbb F_p}(T)\)
also contain a noncyclic relation module; the theorem does
not assert exactness of their full quotient. Most importantly,
no decomposition as in (CQ.1) has been supplied for the
actual bounded lattice \(P+p^{-1}Q\) over
\(\Gamma_{\mathbb Z_{(p)}}(T)\). The general noncyclic
quotient and its role inside the higher-generator coherent
realization remain separate questions.
