\subsection*{Scope of the auxiliary real-multiplication object programme}
The SF-E8xx object constructions and exclusions below concern a polarized
abelian \emph{fourfold} with the stated real-multiplication tangent
splitting and balanced full Chern character. They are results about that
auxiliary object problem. They are not assertions about all perfect
complexes on the imaginary-quadratic Weil eightfold in target5.
In particular, a fourfold local Ext calculation cannot be substituted
for the target eightfold's deformation and full-character hypotheses.

The exact supplied interface is DEG\_L S-22 and its post-proof remark:
the balanced class is identified numerically with the real-multiplication
candidate in Markman's cited construction. That remark records numerical
eligibility; it does not assert a complete transfer theorem. DEG\_L S-14,
including all three hypotheses and the scope paragraph after its proof,
requires an actual object on the \emph{target} abelian scheme, the entire
character \(\lambda t+P(\theta)\) after its algebraic B-field, an actual
coherent finite translation/tensor action, weak semiregularity, and
ordinary vanishing of negative self-Ext groups. These target properties
have not been deduced for the auxiliary RM route in this run.

Accordingly QUESTIONS row Q5i records the auxiliary fourfold problem;
Q5au separately records its unproved bridge to the target eightfold.
The direct Gaussian CM-sixfold construction D-SR-01--04 is a separate
accepted result. All conditional Markman inputs keep their exact
conditional status. This scope correction is not credited as progress
on an existence or detector-degree gate.
