\section{The remaining large maximal alternating layer}
\label{sec:maximal-alternating-single-standard}

\paragraph{Derived status.}
HD-ML02 combines the accepted HD-ML01 and HD-FT26, and has no
separate movement or computational claim.

\begin{corollary}[HD-ML02]
In the actual primitive rank-nine source setting of HD-ML01, fix one
complete D-TD12 decomposition. Every extraction layer of maximal
alternating natural degree $n\ge N_*$ is a single ordinary standard
factor. Its entire external partner is retained. No bound on that
partner's order or on $n$ is asserted.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
HD-ML01 leaves a single ordinary standard factor or a genuine spin
layer. Its equation (ML.4) proves $N_*>65536$. For the same complete
decomposition and the same maximal layer, HD-FT26 excludes every
genuine spin layer at $n\ge65536$, with arbitrary tensor count and
the whole complement as partner. Thus only the first alternative
remains. This is exactly the asserted conclusion.
\end{proof}

The statement only narrows a possible finite-monodromy curve source.
It supplies no detecting cycle, descent, polarization or uniform
division bound. The already recorded condition-A, condition-I and
projector interfaces consequently retain their actual-cycle premise.
