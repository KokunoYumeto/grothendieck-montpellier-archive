\begin{theorem}[D-TD54]
Let an actual connected finite curve-cover family have a faithful
absolutely irreducible primitive coefficient representation V with a
model over its exact imaginary quadratic character field K. Suppose
its multiplicity variation has dimension six, signature \((3,3)\),
and actual period rank nine. Suppose its naturally polarized primitive
sixfold has nonsplit signed determinant type.

Assume the coefficient degree of this source is minimal among all
actual exact-K sources of signature \((3,3)\), actual period rank nine,
and this same natural signed determinant type. The comparison class
includes sources obtained from intermediate quotient covers; it is
not restricted to primitive representations or to irreducible
inductions of V.

Fix any one complete D-TD12 recursion of the primitive coefficient
image. Every alternating simple quotient in its quasisimple extraction
layers has natural degree strictly less than \(N_*\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}

 \quad x:J\twoheadrightarrow P\in\{A_n,S_n\},
 \quad b=\dim U,\quad e=(n-1)b,
 \quad r\ge12,\quad
 \sum_{i=1}^r\operatorname{rank}(1-T_i)=2e+6.
 \tag{MAC.2}
\]
Here the actual coefficient-image cover is over the sphere and its
original genuine inertia entries are retained. The natural quotient

Fixing ordinary standard models and absorbing their scalar phases
in the entire complement makes U a genuine representation of the

contrary to the stated nonsplit determinant type. Thus \(b\ge2\).
This is the precise use of nonsplitness; a scalar standard source
is not silently excluded merely by minimality.

D-TD48 shows that \(K_0=\ker U\) is nontrivial. Faithfulness of V

\[

 \tag{MAC.3}
\]

No bound \(n>168(b+1)\) is used in this post-vanishing argument.

Apply D-TD44 to the entire remaining original decomposition. Because

\end{proof}
