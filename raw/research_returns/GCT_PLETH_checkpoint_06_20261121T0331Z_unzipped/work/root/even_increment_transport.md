# Explicit multiplication transports for every outer Schur label

## G-024. Even increments preserve every multiplicity

Let \(m\ge1\), \(\lambda\vdash mn\) and \(\eta\vdash mq\)
have at most \(m\) parts, with \(n,q\ge0\). For every
\(\mu\vdash m\),
\[
\boxed{p_{\mu,(n+2q)}^{\lambda+2\eta}
\ \ge\ p_{\mu,(n)}^\lambda.}
\tag{EI.1}
\]
The comparison is multiplication by an explicitly specified invariant
in the balanced flag algebra. It is injective before taking any
irreducible multiplicity. The proof supplies that map over \(\mathbf Q\).

The known even-partition nonvanishing result is Bürgisser–Christandl–
Ikenmeyer, *Even partitions in plethysms*, **unnumbered Theorem in
§1.1, p.1**, with the balanced-weight statement **Lemma2.1, p.4**,
in the [16November2010 author text](https://www3.math.tu-berlin.de/algebra/work/weintraub.pdf),
published in *Journal of Algebra*328(2011),322–329,
doi:10.1016/j.jalgebra.2010.10.031. The short argument here gives the
specified all-label embedding, which that theorem does not state.

## 1. The polynomial and the exact injection

Use the actual algebra from G-009:
\[
R=\operatorname{Sym}(E\otimes F)^U
  =\bigoplus_\alpha S_\alpha E\otimes L_\alpha,
\qquad E=F=\mathbf Q^m.
\tag{EI.2}
\]
It is a subalgebra of the polynomial ring in the entries of a square
matrix \(X\). Its right highest-weight components multiply by addition
of their labels. Its balanced left-weight component is
\(B_{\alpha,t}=(S_\alpha E)_{(t^m)}\), with the literal row-permutation
action of \(S_m\). These identifications and the flag-minor generators
are proved in G-009, (G9.6)–(G9.8).

Choose the first semistandard tableau \(T\) of shape \(\eta\) and
content \((q^m)\) in lexicographic order on its row-reading word.
Such a tableau exists by the cited Lemma2.1 and its Kostka interpretation.
For the empty partition choose the empty tableau. For each column of
\(T\), take the flag minor with that column's strictly increasing row
indices and the first columns of \(X\), and let \(h_T\) be their
product. This is a nonzero polynomial of right weight \(\eta\) and
left weight \((q^m)\). In particular the tableau is a finite, deterministic
specification of a vector in \(B_{\eta,q}\).

Define
\[
f_\eta(X)=\sum_{g\in S_m}(g h_T(X))^2.
\tag{EI.3}
\]
Every summand is in the same right-weight component \(2\eta\) and
has left content \(((2q)^m)\). The group permutes the summands, so
\(f_\eta\) is invariant. It is nonzero over \(\mathbf Q\): at
the real matrix \(X_{ij}=i^{j-1}\), every ordered flag minor of
\(h_T\) is a nonzero Vandermonde determinant. Thus \(h_T(X)\ne0\),
and the sum of real squares in (EI.3) is strictly positive there.
For \(q=0\), it equals the nonzero constant \(m!\).

Multiplication in the single polynomial domain \(R\) now gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\iota_{\eta}:B_{\lambda,n}&\longrightarrow
 B_{\lambda+2\eta,n+2q},\\
v&\longmapsto f_\eta v.
\end{aligned}
\tag{EI.4}
\]
Its kernel is zero because \(f_\eta\ne0\) and \(R\) is a domain.
It is \(S_m\)-equivariant because \(f_\eta\) is invariant. Applying
\(\operatorname{Hom}_{S_m}([\mu],-)\) preserves the injection.
The exact normalizer comparison T-003 identifies its dimensions with
the two coefficients in (EI.1). This proves the claim, including all
zero-degree endpoints, by the displayed actual map.

## 2. Translated staircase cones

Let \(m\ge3\), let \(r\) meet G-022's bound (ST.2), and let
\(r(m-1)\) be even. For every partition \(\eta\vdash mq\) with
at most \(m\) rows and every \(d\ge0\), put
\[
\lambda=d\mathbf1+r(m-1,m-2,\ldots,0)+2\eta,
\qquad n=d+r(m-1)/2+2q.
\tag{EI.5}
\]
Every outer label has positive coefficient. More precisely, its
multiplicity is at least the staircase multiplicity in G-022, with
the transpose of the label at odd \(d\). Apply (EI.4) to the staircase
module, then multiply by the actual determinant section to shift all
row weights by \(d\); a permutation matrix acts on that section by
\(\operatorname{sgn}^d\). These are the two maps proving the stated
bound.

For \(m=5\), G-023 permits every \(r\ge2\) in (EI.5). In that
case its seven positive summands give a concrete lower bound, not
only a nonzero vector. The exact coefficient of the translated shape
is not asserted to equal this lower bound. The stronger family G-025
below uses finite seeds of all gap parities.

## 3. Geometric dictionary

Equation(EI.3) is a section of the indicated tensor square of the
Schur line on the flag scheme, obtained by the specified finite group
action. Equation(EI.4) is multiplication by that section in the
coordinate algebra generated by flag minors. Its injectivity is the
domain property of that integral affine flag cone; the torus-weight
and finite-normalizer maps are the exact maps of G-009 and T-003.
In the SGA6 representation \(K_0\), the resulting cokernel has a
class with nonnegative irreducible multiplicities and gives (EI.1).
The comodule/frame descriptions are SGA3 I4.5,4.7.2–4.7.3, the Schur
operations are SGA6 V4.2,4.4,4.8–4.9 and VI3.3, and the composed
operations are the ones transported from cote133/024–029 by A-010.
The polynomial maps are integral before the stated characteristic-zero
representation projectors are applied.
