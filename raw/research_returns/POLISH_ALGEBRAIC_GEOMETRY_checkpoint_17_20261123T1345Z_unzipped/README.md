# Grothendieck manuscript research papers — working package

This package contains five independently editable research-paper projects and
their current PDFs under `workbench/papers/`, plus an editable source comparison
for the sixth requested topic under `workbench/records/papers_5_6/`. It also contains a reconstructed
result register, current checking records, primary-source citations and
hashes, research gates, and new computational programs. The ledger states
the session schedule and what has actually been established.

The papers are working drafts until their mathematical, independent-check,
literature, and final reading gates are closed. A compiling PDF does not
certify a theorem. Historical attached checks are distinguished from checks
made in this session. Known results are credited. The sixth proposed paper is
not admitted as a research paper because its conclusions are covered by
Milne's work; the exact comparison and disposition are preserved in the records.

## Reading order

1. `LEDGER.md` for current progress and open gates.
2. `REGISTER.tsv` for the result inventory, including alias-reconciliation
   warnings while the reconstruction is in progress.
3. `workbench/papers/paper_01` through `paper_05` for standalone sources,
   PDFs, build commands, and deposit sheets; `workbench/records/papers_5_6/PAPER6_DISPOSITION.md`
   for the sixth topic and its source-comparison project.
4. `CHECK_EVENTS.tsv`, `CROSSWALK.tsv`, and the detailed records under
   `workbench/records/` for the exact extent of each check.
5. `LITERATURE.md`, `DICTIONARY.md`, and `CORRECTIONS_FOR_AUTHORS.md` for
   attribution, mathematical interpretation, and source-specific issues.

Only newly written work and records belong in this package. Original
attachments and published literature are not included. Source locators use
paths within the original attachment so that its owners can reproduce the
inventory without receiving a duplicate copy.

Permanent work numbers and remote deposit commits await the owner's filing
step. See `workbench/FORMAT1_FILING.md`; staging identifiers are not presented
as allocated permanent tags.
