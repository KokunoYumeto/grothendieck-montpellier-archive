# Implications and the remaining mathematical work

## GCT and P versus NP

The unrestricted asymptotic separation and polynomial ROABP hitting-set requests remain open in this run. The results establish exact bridges, restricted multiplicity identities, and smaller construction gates; no P versus NP separation or general PIT derandomization is claimed.

The moment-to-character bridge is exact after the correct Fourier projection. R01–R09 give the rectangular restriction identity, swap/tableau expressions, positive projector and Gram formulas, an exact ratios kernel for squared rectangular multiplicities, and the quotient numerator required for an orbit closure. Positive finite CUE dimensions alone do not force positive individual coefficients. R03 provides explicit counterexamples, and R04 shows why separate power-eigenvalue distributions do not determine the joint moments in plethysm.

The determinant boundary can be treated exactly in restricted sectors. All multiplicities with at most three rows equal their ambient plethysm multiplicities (G05). For determinant size four and at most four rows, G07 gives an exact subtraction by a determinant twist of degree D=320112. The certified permanent projection in R12 fills the quaternary coefficient space, so the same shifted plethysm term is exactly the permanent-minus-determinant multiplicity difference. In degree D+s the weight (D+4s,D,D,D) has difference one. This is a multiplicity obstruction in a fixed matrix-size case, not an occurrence obstruction or an asymptotic permanent lower bound.

R13 gives exact finite Jacobian certificates for quaternary permanent dominance in every size 1 through16 and a dimension exclusion for all larger sizes. Subsequent padding/dimension deductions are recorded under their live IDs in the paper and question register. G08–G09 give explicit universal low-variable determinantal constructions, hence exclude low-row multiplicity obstructions above their displayed padding bounds. The asymptotic uniform-representation exponent in G09 is credited to Boralevi et al. (2017); the finite construction and GCT transfer are stated separately.

Hoza's graph isolation gives polynomial hitting sets for independent path variables (I01). General ROABPs require preserving the monomial quotient and coefficient dependence. I03–I04 identify the sufficient coefficient-flag and matroid-cone conditions; I14 gives a weaker joint-span condition. Exact exponent-filter simulation, exact energy cubature, fixed affine decoders and naive specialization have proved, construction-specific limitations. None of these is a lower bound against all polynomial ROABP hitting sets. The precise matrix-invariant consequence is the conditional implication I08. The antecedent remains unresolved.

Purity supplies useful objects once their functions and geometric complexity are controlled: sparse multilinear hitting sets, sparse isolating weights, curve restrictions and trace-space Gram bounds. P09–P10 show that polynomial hitting sets can have exponentially poor coefficient-energy conditioning, so near-isometric energy recovery is a stronger and unsuitable general surrogate. P11 proves that an explicit common algebraic evaluation point and an explicit integer hitting set are polynomially interconvertible in the stated rational-coefficient model. GRH does not construct the missing encoding.
