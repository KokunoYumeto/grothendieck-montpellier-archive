## G-B04 — Integral representation rings

Serre, J.-P. (1968). Groupe de Grothendieck des schémas en groupes réductifs déployés. *Publications Mathématiques de l'IHÉS, 34*, 37–52. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02684589

Exact locators: Theorem 5, §3.7; §3.8 for GL_n. Author-hosted original scan inspected: https://www.college-de-france.fr/media/jean-pierre-serre/UPL8576975072934521334_Serre_Grpes_de_Gr.pdf . The theorem concerns Grothendieck groups for split reductive group schemes; it is not a claim that characteristic-zero simple decompositions remain semisimple in positive characteristic, or a solution of the distinct finite-S_n lattice question.

## G-B09 — Known optimal order for uniform determinantal representations

Boralevi, A., van Doornmalen, J., Draisma, J., Hochstenbach, M. E., & Plestenjak, B. (2017). Uniform determinantal representations. *SIAM Journal on Applied Algebra and Geometry, 1*(1), 415–441. https://doi.org/10.1137/16M1085656

Primary author copy: https://hochsten.win.tue.nl/pdf/siag-unidetrep.pdf . Exact locators: Theorem 1.3, printed p.417, and its construction in §4, printed pp.425–427; Proposition 4.5; Remark 4.8. The asymptotic order d^(v/2) for v fixed affine variables is a known theorem. G09 gives a displayed finite shared-chain bound and the low-row multiplicity consequence, without claiming the asymptotic exponent as new. Both independent reviewers checked the stated scope and the construction's coordinate-chart qualification.
