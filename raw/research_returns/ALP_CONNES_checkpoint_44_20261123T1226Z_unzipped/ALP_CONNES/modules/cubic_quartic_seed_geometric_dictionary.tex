\paragraph{Geometric dictionary for AC-G118.}
The construction takes place on the actual tame modular curve: cusp forms
are sections of $\omega^{47}(-C)$ on the compact full-level-$15$ curve,
and the order-$120$ projector selects the required character of its weight
line.  On the open fine curve, forgetting the added full level $5$ gives
a finite etale level cover of the full-level-$3$ curve; its finite covering
action has the usual fundamental-group interpretation from the already
audited SGA~1, Expose~V, sections~4 and~7.  This dictionary does not assert
that the Borel quotient is etale at the boundary.
After choosing the value field, the retained classical attachment theorem
gives a rank-two lisse $E'$-adic sheaf on
$\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z[1/(3\cdot5\cdot269)]$.
The operation $V=R_g\otimes\eta\psi c_{269}^{23}$ is its tensor product
with the stated finite Artin character and Tate character; modulo each
power of a stable-lattice uniformizer it is a finite continuous
representation in that same cover dictionary.
The final map $S.R\to A\to E'$ is the formal local deformation point proved
in AC-G118.  No additional motive or cohomological comparison is asserted.
