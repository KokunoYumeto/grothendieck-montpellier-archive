\section{The two quarter-residue strict rows for every pure cubic label}
\label{sec:pure-cubic-quartic-residue-strict}

\paragraph{Inputs and scope.}
This proof uses the two precisely stated class-sector conclusions
of AC-G156-class and its exact unit arrays.
It supplies the local unit and original-coefficient arguments
separately for the nontrivial and trivial cubic characters.
The quartic characters at $269$ occur only in residual Kummer
splitting fields.  No original finite host label is enlarged.

Put $p=269$, $K=\mathbb Q(\omega)$ and $t=\sqrt[3]p>0$.
Let $\chi:G_K\to\mu_3$ describe $K(t)/K$.
Write $c_p$ for the actual cyclotomic character and $\omega_p$
for its Teichmuller part; choose $i\in\mathbb Z_p$ with
$i^2=-1$ and $\bar i=82$.
For a discrete coefficient module $D$ use the full strict group
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 S_{\rm str}(K,D)=\ker\bigl(H^1_{\rm cts}(G_K,D)
                                   \longrightarrow\mathcal L(D)\bigr),\\
 \mathcal L(D)=\prod_{v\mid p}H^1(G_{K_v},D)
              \times\prod_{v\nmid p,\infty}H^1(I_v,D).
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{PCQS.1}
\]

\begin{theorem}[AC-G156: all pure cubic quarter-residue strict rows]
\label{AC-G156}
Let $E/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite and let $O$ be its original ring
of integers, containing the values of $\chi$.
Let $j\in\{0,1,2\}$, $m\in\mathbb Z$, $e\geq1$, and let
$\kappa:G_K\to1+\mathfrak m_O$ be continuous and unramified
at every finite place away from $p$.
If $e+m\equiv66$ or $200\pmod{268}$, then
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 D=(E/O)(\chi^{-j}\omega_p^{-m}\kappa^{-1}c_p^{-e}),\\
                          S_{\rm str}(K,D)=0.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{PCQS.2}
\]
The corresponding residual strict groups vanish over every finite
field containing their character values.
The original ring $O$ may be ramified over $\mathbb Z_p$.
For $m=0,\kappa=1$, these are two additional weight progressions
for the existing finite labels $1,\chi,\chi^2$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\proofstep{1. The two covers and the exact inverse characters.}
Let $\psi=\omega_p^{67}$, the primitive odd quartic rational
character of conductor $p$ with $\psi(2)=-i$.
The exact period/character dictionary of AC-G156-class supplies
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 F_-&=\mathbb Q(v),&v^4+pv^2+25p&=0,\\
 F_+&=\mathbb Q(w),&w^4-3pw^2+225p&=0,\\
 L&=F_-(t),&N&=KF_-=KF_+,\qquad M=KL.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{PCQS.3}
\]
Here $F_-$ is the field of $\psi$, $F_+$ is the field of
$\chi_{-3}\psi$, and $w=\sqrt{-3}\,v$ in $N$.
The second field is totally real and the first is imaginary.
The automorphism $b$ fixing $K$ has $\psi(b)=-i$ and acts by
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 b(v)&=-\frac{v^3+219v}{65},&
 b(w)&=\frac{w^3-657w}{195},\\
 b^2(v)&=-v,& b^2(w)&=-w.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{PCQS.4}
\]
In the plus-field dictionary it is represented by the CRT class
$271$, which fixes the conductor-three factor and is two modulo $p$.
This fixes the orientation of both projectors below.

The only quadratic subfield of the $S_3$ splitting field $K(t)$
is $K$, whereas the unique quadratic subfield of $F_-$ is
$\mathbb Q(\sqrt p)$.
Their intersection is therefore $\mathbb Q$.
Consequently
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \operatorname{Gal}(M/\mathbb Q)=S_3\times C_4,
 \qquad[M:\mathbb Q]=24,\quad[N:\mathbb Q]=8,\\
 \operatorname{Gal}(M/K)=C_3\times C_4,
 \qquad\operatorname{Gal}(N/K)=C_4.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{PCQS.5}
\]
Let $c(t)=\omega t$, $c(v)=v$; then $c$ commutes with $b$.

Let $k=O/\mathfrak m_O$, let $a$ be the residual coefficient
character in (PCQS.2), and put $r\equiv e+m\pmod{268}$.
The Kummer quotient $\eta=a/\bar c_p$ has the exact table
\[
 \begin{array}{c|c|c}
 r&\eta&\eta^{-1}(b)\\ \hline
 66&\bar\chi^{-j}\bar\psi^{-1}&187=-\bar i\\
 200&\bar\chi^{-j}\bar\psi&82=\bar i.
 \end{array}
 \tag{PCQS.6}
\]
For $j=1,2$ we use the cover $M/K$; for $j=0$ we use $N/K$.
Both have degree prime to $p$ and split the relevant $\eta$.
No $\psi$ or $\chi_{-3}\psi$ is inserted into the original
finite coefficient in (PCQS.2).

\proofstep{2. The precise class sectors and full-local Kummer descent.}
The class input is
\[
 \operatorname{Cl}(L)[p]=0,\qquad
 \operatorname{Cl}(F_-)[p]=\operatorname{Cl}(F_+)[p]=0,
 \tag{PCQS.7}
\]
proved in AC-G156-class, with its separate exact finite and
analytic review records.
For clarity, the sector deductions needed here are as follows.
A reflection of $S_3$ fixing $L$ acts by minus one on
$\operatorname{Cl}(M)[p]$, since norm and extension is $1+s$.
The same holds for its conjugate reflections, so their products
force $C_3$ to act trivially.  The nontrivial cubic class sector
of $M$ is therefore zero.

Write $c_K$ for conjugation on $K$ fixing $F_-$ in $N$.
The fixed fields of $c_K$ and $c_Kb^2$ are respectively
$F_-$ and $F_+$.
Both norm-extension operators $1+c_K$ and $1+c_Kb^2$
vanish on $\operatorname{Cl}(N)[p]$.
On the part with $b^2=-1$ their sum is two.
This part is zero because $p$ is odd; it includes both
$b$-eigenvalues $82$ and $187$.
No vanishing of any other class sector is inferred.

For $P=M$ or $N$, as prescribed above, prime-to-$p$ restriction
and inflation identify the residual strict group with
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 e_{\eta^{-1}}\ker\left(V_{P,S}\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k
                     \longrightarrow\mathcal P_P\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k\right),\\
 V_{P,S}=\{x\in P^\times:p\mid v(x)\text{ for }v\nmid p\}/P^{\times p},\\
 \mathcal P_P=\bigoplus_{v\mid p}P_v^\times/P_v^{\times p}.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{PCQS.8}
\]
Over $P$ the coefficient is $k(1)$; its additional quotient
twist is $\eta$, so invariants select $\eta^{-1}$.
The same prime-to-$p$ descent applies to each local decomposition
or inertia group and does not require adjoining $\mu_p$.
The full local power condition makes the valuations above $p$
divisible by $p$, as well as the away valuations already imposed.
For the resulting all-primes group $V_P$ the exact sequence is
\[
 0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_P^\times/\mathcal O_P^{\times p}
 \longrightarrow V_P\longrightarrow\operatorname{Cl}(P)[p]
 \longrightarrow0.
 \tag{PCQS.9}
\]
The selected class sectors just proved to vanish reduce the task
to injectivity of unit localization in those same sectors.

\proofstep{3. Exact local fields and their full automorphisms.}
Both quartic polynomials in (PCQS.3) are Eisenstein at $p$.
The quartic and cubic completions are totally ramified of
coprime degrees four and three; their compositum has degree twelve.
Since $K_p/\mathbb Q_p$ is unramified quadratic, the local degrees
are the entire global degrees of $M$ and $N$.
Thus each has one prime above $p$:
\[
                     (e_p(M),f_p(M))=(12,2),\qquad
                     (e_p(N),f_p(N))=(4,2).
 \tag{PCQS.10}
\]

In $M_p$ take $u=t/v$.
Elimination and its exact inverse formulas give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 15625u^{12}+52186u^6+269&=0,\\
 v&=\frac{52811u^3+15625u^9}{244},\qquad t=uv,\\
 c(u)&=\omega u,\\
 b(u)&=\frac{46711}{15860}u+\frac{3125}{3172}u^7
                      \equiv82u+218u^7\pmod{p,u^{12}}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{PCQS.11}
\]
Direct substitution checks the defining equations for $t,v$,
the action (PCQS.4), $b(t)=t$ and $b^2(u)=-u$.
The polynomial divided by $15625$ is Eisenstein.
Its twelve powers form the integral basis locally over
$\mathcal O_{K_p}$: the valuations of coefficient times $u^n$
are distinct modulo twelve, so cancellation cannot hide a
nonintegral coefficient.

For $N_p$ take $w$ itself as uniformizer.
Its four powers form the analogous full integral basis and
\[
          b(w)\equiv187w+229w^3\pmod{p,w^4},
          \qquad b^2(w)=-w.
 \tag{PCQS.12}
\]
In particular the full nonlinear terms in both local actions
are retained, rather than replacing $b$ by its leading scalar.

\proofstep{6. Full local power detection and residual injectivity.}
For either local field in (PCQS.10), the ramification index is
less than $p-1$, so logarithm and exponential identify principal
units with the maximal ideal.
The logarithm of a $p$th power principal unit has valuation at
least thirteen in $M_p$, and at least five in $N_p$.
The leading degrees one and seven in (PCQS.17), and three and
one in (PCQS.20), lie below those respective thresholds.
All four projected classes consequently have nonzero full
local Kummer image.
For clarity, a Teichmuller unit is a local $p$th power since its
order is prime to $p$.  If a principal unit has a $p$th root,
that root also has residue one, since Frobenius is bijective
on the finite residue field.
Thus neither test replaces the full local condition by a
weaker unit or valuation condition.

The archimedean places of $M$ above a fixed complex embedding of
$K$ form a regular $C_3\times C_4$-set; those of $N$ form a
regular $C_4$-set.
Their equivariant rational unit characters are the corresponding
regular characters minus trivial.
Neither field contains $\mu_p$, as $268$ divides neither
$24$ nor $8$.
Their prime-to-$p$ character multiplicities therefore persist
on the unit quotients modulo $p$.

For each $b$-eigenvalue $82,187$, the nontrivial cubic part of
the $M$ unit quotient is a simple two-dimensional
$\mathbb F_p[C_3]$-module: $X^2+X+1$ is irreducible over
$\mathbb F_{269}$.
The corresponding part of the $N$ unit quotient is a
one-dimensional line.
Localization is equivariant for the full relevant group.
The two nonzero values in (PCQS.17) prove injectivity on the
two simple cubic components, and (PCQS.20) proves injectivity
on both lines for $j=0$.
These injections remain injective after scalar extension to $k$.
The precise class-sector vanishings and (PCQS.8)--(PCQS.9)
now prove the residual strict assertion for all three $j$.

\proofstep{7. The original uniformizer and all continuous lifts.}
Let $\varpi$ be a uniformizer of the original $O$.
The module $D$ is divisible and $D[\varpi]\simeq k(a)$.
For $j=1,2$, residual cubic inertia at $p$ has order three
and cannot be canceled by a cyclotomic character of order
dividing $268$.
For $j=0$ its residual inertia character is
$\bar c_p^{-66}$ or $\bar c_p^{-200}$, of exact order
$134$ or $67$, respectively.
These orders are nontrivial because $K_p/\mathbb Q_p$ is
unramified and has the full residual cyclotomic inertia image.
The factor $\kappa$ reduces to one.
Thus in every case an inertia element acts by a scalar whose
difference from one is a unit, and $D^{G_{K_p}}=0$.

Away from $p$, the cyclotomic, Teichmuller and $\kappa$ factors
are unramified; the remaining finite inertia character has order
dividing three.  Its invariants are zero or all of the divisible
module $D$.  Hence $D^J/\varpi D^J=0$ for every local group
$J$ in (PCQS.1).
The multiplication-by-$\varpi$ long exact sequence makes
$H^1(J,D[\varpi])\to H^1(J,D)$ injective locally.
Every global strict class killed by $\varpi$ lifts to a residual
class, and local injectivity makes that lift strict.
It vanishes by Step6.
Finally a continuous cocycle from compact $G_K$ to discrete
$E/O$ has finite image and is killed by some power of $\varpi$.
A nonzero strict class would produce a nonzero one killed by
$\varpi$, a contradiction.  This proves (PCQS.2) on the original
coefficient ring without an unramified-coefficient assumption.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Actual labels and the exact new weight count.}
For $m=0,\kappa=1$ only the three labels $1,\chi,\chi^2$
occur.  They are characters of every actual host containing $K(t)$.
The new weights are $e=66+134n$, $n\geq0$, so for positive integer
$T$ the number of pairs with $0<e<T$ is
\[
                        3\left\lfloor\frac{T+67}{134}\right\rfloor.
 \tag{PCQS.21}
\]
Together with the previously proved pure cubic rows, the full
proved union is $e\equiv0\pmod{268}$ or $e\equiv-1\pmod{67}$,
with count
\[
              3\left(\left\lfloor\frac{T-1}{268}\right\rfloor
                         +\left\lfloor\frac T{67}\right\rfloor\right).
 \tag{PCQS.22}
\]
The two sets are disjoint, since a multiple of $268$ is zero
modulo $67$.
This is a strict-group statement and count, not a claim that
the corresponding positive derived indices individually vanish.
Their local comparison cost and common-window transport remain
separate arguments.
