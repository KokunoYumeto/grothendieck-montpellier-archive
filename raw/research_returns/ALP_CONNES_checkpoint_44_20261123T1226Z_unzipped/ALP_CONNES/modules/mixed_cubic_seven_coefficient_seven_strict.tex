\section{A residual rational cubic factor at coefficient seven}
\label{sec:mixed-cubic-seven-coefficient-seven}

Put $K=\mathbb Q(\omega)$, $t^3=269$, and
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 L=K(t),\qquad R=\mathbb Q(\zeta_7)^+=\mathbb Q(r),
       \qquad r=\zeta_7+\zeta_7^{-1},\\
 r^3+r^2-2r-1=0,\qquad B=\mathbb Q(t,r),\qquad F=LR.
 \end{gathered}\tag{M77.1}
\]
Let $\chi$ be either faithful Kummer character of $L/K$.
Let $\xi_7$ be a faithful rational conductor-seven character
of order six, valued in the original coefficient ring,
and put $\nu=\xi_7^2$. The latter is a faithful rational
cubic character of $R/\mathbb Q$. Restrict these characters
to $G_K$; the Kummer and rational cubic factors are independent.
Let $O$ be the original ring of integers of any finite extension
$E/\mathbb Q_7$ containing $\mu_3$, with original uniformizer
$\varpi$, residue field $k$, and embedding.
Let $\kappa:G_K\longrightarrow1+\mathfrak m_O$ be continuous
and unramified away seven. For $a\in\{0,1,\ldots,5\}$ and
any positive integer $e$, define on this literal original residue field
\[
                  \rho_{a,e}=\bar\xi_7^{\,a}\bar c_7^{-e}.
 \tag{M77.2a}
\]
It is a rational character of conductor dividing seven and order dividing six.
Admit exactly those $a,e$ for which $\rho_{a,e}$ has exact order three.
For these pairs it factors through $R/\mathbb Q$, and
we then use its restriction to $G_K$.
For every such pair and $j\in\{1,2\}$, put
\[
 D=(E/O)(\chi^j\xi_7^a\kappa^{-1}c_7^{-e}).
 \tag{M77.2}
\]
The full strict condition is zero restriction to
$H^1(K_v,D)$ at both $v\mid7$, and zero restriction to
$H^1(I_v,D)$ at every other finite place. Use the identical
conditions for $D[\varpi]$.

\begin{theorem}[AC-G213: a nontrivial residual rational cubic at coefficient seven]
\label{AC-G213}
For all the original choices above,
\[
             \mathcal S_7(K,D[\varpi])=0,
             \qquad \mathcal S_7(K,D)=0.
 \tag{M77.3}
\]
Consequently the actual finite labels
$\chi^j\xi_7^a$, $j=1,2$ and $a=0,\ldots,5$, have full
original-coefficient strict zero at coefficient seven and
every positive weight for which
$\bar\xi_7^{\,a}\bar c_7^{-e}$ has exact order three in the
prescribed original completion of (FG.3), (FC.1)--(FC.2).
At every weight exactly two values of $a$ are admitted.
This includes the pure Kummer labels $a=0$ when
$e\equiv2,4\pmod6$, and includes odd $a$ at odd weights.
Their two-power and auxiliary $q,d$ character factors are
trivial. No new finite label or proof-cover field is inserted
into the actual host.
For every integer $T\geq2$ and each prescribed original
completion, put
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal Q_{7,7}(T)=\{(7,\chi^j\xi_7^a,e):{}&j\in\{1,2\},\quad0\leq a\leq5,\\
                 &1\leq e<T,\quad\operatorname{ord}(\rho_{a,e})=3\}.
 \end{split}\tag{M77.4}
\]
Then, for every retained set $J$ of actual triples in (CP.3),
\[
 \begin{gathered}
                         |\mathcal Q_{7,7}(T)|=4(T-1),\\
                 \mathcal R_J=
                    \mathcal R_{J\setminus\mathcal Q_{7,7}(T)}.
 \end{gathered}\tag{M77.5}
\]
These are full mixed $K$-strict terms. No individual derived
index, pure Kummer row at coefficient seven with $6\mid e$,
or full seven-primary class group of $F$ is asserted zero.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\proofstep{1. Exact fields and the rational cubic automorphism.}
The polynomial $X^3-269$ is Eisenstein and has nonsquare
discriminant $-27\cdot269^2$. Thus $L/\mathbb Q$ is its
$S_3$ splitting field. The real cyclotomic cubic $R/\mathbb Q$
is cyclic of degree three. Since $S_3$ has no quotient of
order three, $L\cap R=\mathbb Q$. It follows that
\[
 [B:\mathbb Q]=9,\quad [F:\mathbb Q]=18,\quad
 \operatorname{Gal}(F/K)=\langle g\rangle\times\langle h\rangle
                                      \simeq C_3\times C_3,
 \tag{M77.6}
\]
where $g(t)=\omega t$, $g(r)=r$, and
$h(t)=t$, $h(r)=r^2-2$. The latter formula is the restriction
of $\zeta_7\mapsto\zeta_7^2$, of order three on $R$.
There is an involution $\tau$ fixing $t,r$ and sending
$\omega\mapsto\omega^{-1}$, with fixed field $B$ and
\[
           \tau h=h\tau,\qquad \tau g\tau^{-1}=g^{-1}.
 \tag{M77.7}
\]

The primitive element $u=t+r$ has polynomial
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 P(X)={}&X^9+3X^8-3X^7-821X^6-1614X^5-3207X^4\\
        &+212517X^3+209811X^2+1082181X-19172707.
 \end{aligned}\tag{M77.8}
\]
For completeness, its nine displayed conjugate sums are
all distinct. An equality $t_i+r_j=t_{i'}+r_{j'}$ gives
$t_i-t_{i'}\in L\cap R=\mathbb Q$.
If $i\ne i'$, an $S_3$ transposition exchanging these two
roots negates this rational difference, a contradiction.
Thus $i=i'$ and then $j=j'$. This proves primitivity;
taking the resultant gives (M77.8).

In $\mathbb Q[X]/(P)$ the exact inverse and automorphism are
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 r=\frac{3u^5+3u^4+3u^3-801u^2-804u-809}
          {6u^4+8u^3-3u^2+804u+274},\qquad t=u-r,\\
                         h(u)=u-r+r^2-2.
 \end{gathered}\tag{M77.9}
\]
The denominator is nonzero since it has degree four and
$P$ is irreducible of degree nine. Rational polynomial
reduction verifies $r^3+r^2-2r-1=0$, $t^3=269$,
$h^3(u)=u$, $h(t)=t$, and $h(r)=r^2-2$.
Thus the following ideal calculation uses this exact
rational cubic automorphism, not an unspecified conjugate.

\proofstep{2. The certified quotient and an explicit fixed generator.}
The completed degree-nine certificates retained from G209
prove that the actual class group of $B$ is a quotient of
\[
                             C_{14}\times C_2.
 \tag{M77.10}
\]
One root execution and a separately generated Trace
execution both completed the flag-one quotient certificate.
The latter produced the exact class structure used below.
No equality of the actual class number with $28$, and no
complete unit basis, is required.

Let $\mathfrak g_1,\mathfrak g_2$ be the displayed ideal
classes, the images of the two computed Smith generators.
This is the natural quotient through those ideal classes:
the primary PARI factorbase relation/Smith conversion
identifies the displayed \texttt{bnf.gen} ideals, and the
flag-one prime test proves that this same factorbase
generates the actual class group. It is not merely an
unspecified abstract quotient of the displayed group.
Set
\[
                            \mathfrak I=\mathfrak g_1^2.
 \tag{M77.11}
\]
Here and below the same notation denotes chosen ideal
representatives when an ideal is required.
The class of $\mathfrak I$ generates
$\operatorname{Cl}(B)\{7\}$, possibly the trivial group.
Indeed the $C_2$ generator has no seven-primary part,
and the square of the $C_{14}$ generator generates its
order-seven subgroup; this remains true after taking
the natural quotient.

The exact finite certificate proves
\[
                           h(\mathfrak I)=(\alpha)\mathfrak I.
 \tag{M77.12}
\]
In the ordered integral basis $z_1,\ldots,z_9$ printed

 \begin{aligned}

                    -148990569951z_3\\

 &+166972495953z_7-115548552354z_8+92662744332z_9.

Every basis polynomial, ideal-lattice entry and rational

The executable rational verification gives a particularly

                      h=1\quad\hbox{on }\operatorname{Cl}(B)\{7\}.
 \tag{M77.15}

\proofstep{3. Only the required two-cubic class sectors vanish.}
Put $C=\operatorname{Cl}(F)\{7\}$ and use additive notation.
Multiplication by two and three is invertible on this finite
group. Since $h$ has order three, the integral seven-primary
projector gives a direct sum
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 C=C_0\oplus C_*,\qquad
 C_0=\frac{1+h+h^2}{3}C,\qquad
 C_*=\left(1-\frac{1+h+h^2}{3}\right)C.
 \end{gathered}\tag{M77.16}
\]
Both projectors are integral endomorphisms of this seven-primary group.
The norm $N_{F/B}$ commutes with $h$. By (M77.15) its
restriction to $C_*$ is zero. Ideal extension followed
by norm on $B$ is multiplication by two, whereas norm
followed by extension on $F$ is $1+\tau$. In particular
\[
                              (1+\tau)C_*=0.
 \tag{M77.17}
\]
Both $g$ and $\tau$ preserve $C_*$: $g$ commutes with $h$
and $\tau$ commutes with $h$. Therefore $\tau=-1$ there.
Conjugating $g$ by this scalar involution in (M77.7) gives
$g=g^{-1}$ on $C_*$. Together with $g^3=1$, this yields
\[
                                    g=1\quad\hbox{on }C_*.
 \tag{M77.18}
\]
We have not asserted that $C_*$ or $C$ is zero.

Let $f:C\to k$ be an additive map transforming under
$g,h$ through any nontrivial cubic characters of each.
On $C_0$, $h=1$, so $f=0$ since its required $h$-eigenvalue
is different from one. On $C_*$, (M77.18) similarly forces
$f=0$ using its required $g$-eigenvalue. This proves precisely
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}(C,k)(\bar\chi^j\bar\nu^b)=0,
                                      \qquad j,b=1,2,
 \tag{M77.19}
\]
where the notation means the explicitly stated equivariance,
not an unstated inverse-character convention.

\proofstep{4. Residual full strict cohomology.}
Reduction preserves the order of the original sixth roots
of unity, since six is prime to seven. Both
$\bar\xi_7^a$ and $\bar c_7^{-e}$ factor through
$\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb Q(\zeta_7)/\mathbb Q)$.
Their admitted product has exact order three, hence is
trivial on that cyclic group's order-two subgroup and
factors through $R$. This proves the assertion after
(M77.2a) without fixing a Teichmuller normalization of $\xi_7$.
Since $\kappa$ is near one, the residual coefficient is
\[
                     A=D[\varpi]\simeq k(\bar\chi^j\rho_{a,e}).
 \tag{M77.20}
\]
It is trivial on $G_F$. The cover $F/K$ has degree nine,
which is prime to seven, even though the product character
itself has image of order three. The original finite factor
$\chi^j\xi_7^a$ can have order six; only the residual
coefficient is trivialized on this degree-nine cover.
Inflation--restriction
and averaging therefore inject $H^1(K,A)$ into
$H^1(F,k)=\operatorname{Hom}_{\rm cont}(G_F,k)$.

A residual strict class restricts to an additive character
which is unramified at every finite place of $F$.
Away seven this follows from the original inertia-zero
condition; over seven it follows from the stronger full
completion-zero condition. The Hilbert class-field
identification makes it a homomorphism
$\operatorname{Cl}(F)\to k$, hence a homomorphism $C\to k$.
Conjugation equivariance is
\[
 f(\sigma x\sigma^{-1})=
                  (\bar\chi^j\rho_{a,e})(\sigma)f(x).
 \tag{M77.21}
\]
The admitted $\rho_{a,e}$ is a nontrivial rational cubic
character, so this is exactly one of the equivariance
conditions in Step3. Hence $f=0$.
The injective restriction proves
$\mathcal S_7(K,A)=0$. No full class-primary vanishing
hypothesis has been substituted for the targeted argument.

\proofstep{5. The original uniformizer and all full local conditions.}
There are two places of $K$ above seven, each with completion
$\mathbb Q_7$. The extension $R/\mathbb Q$ is totally
ramified of degree three at seven. Its base change to each
$K_v$ consequently has inertia image $C_3$.
The Kummer extension $L/K$ is unramified at seven.
For each $v\mid7$, choose an inertia element on which
the admitted $\rho_{a,e}$ is a nontrivial cube root of unity.
Then $\bar\chi^j=1$ on that inertia element and
$\bar\kappa=1$; the product of the two remaining residual
factors is exactly $\rho_{a,e}$.
The full scalar action on $D$ therefore differs from one
by an $O$-unit. Thus
\[
                              D^{G_{K_v}}=0\qquad(v\mid7).
 \tag{M77.22}
\]
This argument uses inertia at both completions. It does
not rely on multiplying the two Frobenius values, or on
absence of a possible Frobenius cancellation.

At every $v\nmid7$, both $c_7$ and $\kappa$ are unramified.
The rational character $\xi_7^a$ is also unramified there;
the remaining Kummer inertia action has order dividing three.
If it is nontrivial, its difference from one is an
$O$-unit and $D^{I_v}=0$; otherwise $D^{I_v}=D$, which is
$\varpi$-divisible. This includes the places over $3,269$
and every other finite place. Consequently every local
invariant quotient modulo $\varpi$ is zero.

The local and global long exact sequences for
$0\to A\to D\xrightarrow{\varpi}D\to0$ contain
\[
 D^G/\varpi D^G\longrightarrow H^1(G,A)
                      \longrightarrow H^1(G,D)[\varpi]
                      \longrightarrow0.
 \tag{M77.23}
\]
A global strict class killed by $\varpi$ has a residual
lift. Each local restriction of that lift maps to zero;
the vanishing local invariant quotient makes the restriction
itself zero. The lift is full strict, hence zero by Step4.
Thus the original strict group has no $\varpi$-torsion.
Every continuous cocycle into the discrete $E/O$ has finite
image and is killed by a power of $\varpi$. A nonzero
strict class would therefore produce nonzero
$\varpi$-torsion. This proves (M77.3) on the original $O$.

\proofstep{6. The complete actual family and exact retained sum.}
The actual host (FG.3) contains $K,t$ and $\zeta_7$.
The Kummer and rational conductor-seven factors are independent
in (FC.1), so the twelve possible products $\chi^j\xi_7^a$
are distinct actual finite labels. Their values require only
$\mu_6$, already in each prescribed original completion
$O_7$ of $\mathbb Z[\zeta_{3\cdot2^h}]$.
Set $\kappa=1$ and identify the actual (FC.2) coefficient
with $(E_7/O_7)(\chi^j\xi_7^ac_7^{-e})$.
Inflation from its global ramification quotient to $G_K$
is injective and preserves every prescribed completion
and away-inertia kernel. Outside the source ramification
set its classes are inertia-zero. Thus the actual full
strict groups inject into (M77.3), with the same original
uniformizer, embedding, local conditions and rank $b_7$.

To count on each original embedding, note that
$\bar\xi_7^a$, $a=0,\ldots,5$, enumerate all six characters
of the rational cyclic group of order six. Restriction
to $G_K$ is injective on this group: the quadratic field
$K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})$ is not the quadratic subfield
$\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-7})$ of $\mathbb Q(\zeta_7)$.
Multiplication by the fixed character $\bar c_7^{-e}$
permutes these six characters. Exactly two have order three.
Thus each positive weight admits exactly two $a$ and
both Kummer powers, giving four triples per weight.
This proves $4(T-1)$ for integer $T\geq2$ on every literal
original embedding. No identification $\xi_7=\omega_7$
is assumed. The separate 72-row residue check tests both
primitive-sixth-root orientations and all weight residues;
the general permutation argument proves the entire count.
Every one of these mixed summands of (CP.3) is
$\log1/b_7=0$, so it can be deleted from any retained $J$.
The entire rational noncritical sum is unchanged and
no positive derived index is split. This proves the second
identity with no additional comparison cost or independently
chosen window. The set is disjoint from the G209 rows,
which exclude coefficient seven, and from the G211 rows,
whose rational conductor-seven factor is trivial and whose
nontrivial quadratic two-power factor distinguishes it even
from the newly admitted pure Kummer labels.
\end{proof}
