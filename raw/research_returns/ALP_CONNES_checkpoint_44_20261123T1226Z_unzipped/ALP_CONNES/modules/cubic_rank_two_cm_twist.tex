\subsection{An explicit rank-two normalizing character over the Eisenstein field}
\label{sec:cubic-rank-two-cm-twist}

This subsection supplies the character required by the literal rank-two
\texttt{Sd.IsTwist} interface.  The cyclotomic character in this subsection is
\(c_p\), the actual action on all \(p\)-power roots of unity; it is the source's
\texttt{cycBar}.  No inverse geometric convention is substituted for it.
We use geometric global Artin reciprocity: a finite uniformizer maps to the
inverse of arithmetic Frobenius.  This is exactly the convention in the
source declaration \texttt{IsGlobalArtin.frob}.

\paragraph{Finding AC-G106-twist: actual conductor-three supply.}
Let \(K=\mathbf Q(\omega)\), where \(\omega^2+\omega+1=0\), let \(p=269\),
and fix \(\iota:\overline{\mathbf Q}_p\simeq\mathbf C\) and a complex
conjugation \(c\in G_{\mathbf Q}\).  There is a continuous character
\[
 \kappa_2:G_K\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{K_p}^{\times}
       \subset\overline{\mathbf Q}_p^{\times}
 \tag{G106T.1}
\]
which satisfies every field of the literal
\(\texttt{Sd.IsTwist}\ \iota\ c\ \kappa_2\).
Here the chosen embedding of \(K_p\), the unramified quadratic extension
of \(\mathbf Q_p\), is allowed to depend on \(\iota\).  More explicitly,
\(\kappa_2\) has algebraic type \((-1,0)\), is unramified outside \(3p\),
and its inertia image at every place away from \(p\) has exponent dividing
\(6\).  For \(g\in G_K\) and \(t\in G_{\mathbf Q}\setminus G_K\),
\[
 \kappa_2(g)\kappa_2(cgc^{-1})=c_p(g)^{-1},\qquad
 \kappa_2(t^2)=-c_p(t)^{-1}.
 \tag{G106T.2}
\]
Thus the source's \texttt{ord} witness can be \(m=6\), which is prime to
\(269\).  In particular the rank-three normalization \(c_p^{-1}\), whose
conjugate product is \(c_p^{-2}\), is not being reused as a rank-two twist.

\paragraph{Proof: the finite and infinite parts.}
Write \(\mathcal O_K=\mathbf Z[\omega]\).  The imaginary quadratic
Minkowski bound is \(2\sqrt3/\pi<2\), so every ideal class has an ideal
of norm one; hence \(K\) has class number one.  The equation
\(N(a+b\omega)=a^2-ab+b^2=1\) gives exactly the six units
\(\mu_6\).  Moreover
\[
 \mathcal O_K/3\mathcal O_K\simeq
 \mathbf F_3[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2),\qquad
 \mu_6\xrightarrow{\sim}(\mathcal O_K/3\mathcal O_K)^{\times}.
 \tag{G106T.3}
\]
Indeed the units in the latter ring are the six elements
\(\pm(1+a\epsilon)\), \(a\in\mathbf F_3\), and the reductions of
\(\pm1,\pm\omega,\pm\omega^2\) are distinct.  Let \(s\) denote the
inverse of this reduction isomorphism.

Choose a complex embedding \(\tau:K\hookrightarrow\mathbf C\).
For a finite idele \(x=(x_v)\), choose \(a\in K^\times\) whose principal
fractional ideal is the ideal of \(x\).  Then \(u=a^{-1}x\) lies in
\(\widehat{\mathcal O}_K^{\times}\).  At the unique place over \(3\)
form its residue modulo \(3\mathcal O_K\), and put
\[
 j(x)=a\,s(\overline{u_3})\in K^\times.
 \tag{G106T.4}
\]
Replacing \(a\) by \(a\zeta\), \(\zeta\in\mu_6\), replaces
\(s(\overline{u_3})\) by \(\zeta^{-1}s(\overline{u_3})\), so this
definition is independent of the choice.  Multiplicativity follows by
using the product of the chosen generators.  Both the idele ideal and
the residue of \(u_3\) are locally constant, so \(j\), with its image
viewed discretely, is locally constant.  For a diagonal finite idele
\(b\in K^\times\), one has \(j(b)=b\).

Using \(\tau\) to identify the unique infinite completion with
\(\mathbf C\), define
\[
 Y(x_\infty,x_f)=\tau(j(x_f))x_\infty^{-1}.
 \tag{G106T.5}
\]
This is a continuous complex Hecke character, trivial on diagonal
\(K^\times\).  Its infinite component is \(z^{-1}\).  Thus with
\(n(\tau)=-1\), \(n(\overline\tau)=0\), its infinite component on
\(K^\times\) is \(\prod_\varphi\varphi(b)^{n(\varphi)}\).  This is
the literal \texttt{IsAlgPos} formula; there are no real positivity
conditions because \(K\) is imaginary quadratic.

For a place \(v\ne3\), a local unit has \(j=1\), so \(Y\) is
unramified there.  At \(3\), its character on local units is
\(u\mapsto\tau(s(\bar u))\), of exponent dividing six.  In particular
the finite conductor of \(Y\) divides \(3\mathcal O_K\).  If
\(\mathfrak a\) is a fractional ideal prime to \(3\), then its value
is \(\tau(\alpha)\), where \(\alpha\) is its unique generator
congruent to one modulo \(3\).  Denote this ideal character by
\(\psi(\mathfrak a)=\alpha\).  Uniqueness gives
\[
 \psi(\mathfrak a^c)=\overline{\psi(\mathfrak a)},\qquad
 \psi(\mathfrak a)\psi(\mathfrak a^c)=N\mathfrak a.
 \tag{G106T.6}
\]

\paragraph{Proof: the continuous avatar and the exact native formula.}
The embedding \(\tau_p=\iota^{-1}\tau\) of \(K\) extends to its
completion at the unique prime over \(p\).  There is only one such
prime because \(269\equiv2\pmod3\), and this completion is unramified
of degree two.  For a full idele, set
\[
 \beta(x)=\tau_p(j(x_f))\,\tau_p(x_p)^{-1}.
 \tag{G106T.7}
\]
This is continuous, multiplicative, trivial on diagonal \(K^\times\),
and independent of the infinite component.  Its values are units:
if \(x_f=a u\) as above, then
\(\beta(x)=\tau_p(s(\overline{u_3}))\tau_p(u_p)^{-1}\), and both
factors are units of \(\mathcal O_{K_p}\).

Global Artin reciprocity is an open quotient map to \(G_K^{\rm ab}\)
with kernel the closure of the subgroup generated by diagonal
\(K^\times\) and the connected infinite ideles.  The closed kernel
of \(\beta\) contains this subgroup and its closure.  Hence \(\beta\)
factors continuously through geometric reciprocity, defining
\(\kappa_2\) as in (G106T.1).  This uses the standard global class field
theorem already retained by the source; it does not require an extra
automorphic character existence hypothesis.

For clarity, the exact native \texttt{IsLAdicOf} formula is
\[
 r(\operatorname{art}(x))
 =\iota^{-1}\!\left(Y(x)\operatorname{infPow}(n,x)^{-1}\right)
     \operatorname{lPow}(\iota,n,x).
 \tag{G106T.8}
\]
In the present construction \(\operatorname{infPow}(n,x)=x_\infty^{-1}\),
and \(\operatorname{lPow}(\iota,n,x)=\tau_p(x_p)^{-1}\): the other
\(p\)-adic embedding has exponent zero, and away from \(p\) there
are no continuous embeddings into \(\overline{\mathbf Q}_p\).
Consequently (G106T.8) is exactly (G106T.7), proving the source's
\texttt{alg} field, with the specified \(\tau\) and \(n\).

The source's \texttt{IsLAdicOf.frob\_unr} formula, or directly its
geometric Artin normalization, now says that at an arithmetic
Frobenius \(\operatorname{Frob}_{\mathfrak l}\), \(\mathfrak l\nmid3p\),
\[
 \kappa_2(\operatorname{Frob}_{\mathfrak l})
       =\tau_p(\psi(\mathfrak l))^{-1}.
 \tag{G106T.9}
\]
Equations (G106T.6) and (G106T.9) give the first identity in (G106T.2)
on all such Frobenius elements.  Both sides are continuous characters;
Chebotarev density gives it on \(G_K\).  This argument also fixes the
orientation relative to the actual \(c_p\): on arithmetic Frobenius,
the product is \((N\mathfrak l)^{-1}\), not \(N\mathfrak l\).

The second identity needs no separate transfer convention.  A complex
conjugation has \(c^2=1\) and \(c_p(c)=-1\), and it is outside \(G_K\).
Write any \(t\notin G_K\) as \(t=cg\), \(g\in G_K\).  Then
\[
 \kappa_2(t^2)=\kappa_2(cgc^{-1})\kappa_2(g)
 =c_p(g)^{-1}=-c_p(t)^{-1}.
 \tag{G106T.10}
\]
This is the literal \texttt{ver} field, including its minus sign.

Finally local reciprocity identifies the image of inertia with the
image of local units.  Away from \(p\), (G106T.8) has no local
algebraic correction.  The local unit calculation after (G106T.5)
therefore gives trivial inertia outside \(3p\) and exponent dividing
six at \(3\).  For an arbitrary valuation subring of the algebraic
closure in the native statement, a nontrivial restricted valuation
lies above a finite place of \(K\); if it restricts trivially to
\(K\), algebraicity makes it trivial on the algebraic closure and
its inertia group is trivial.  Thus the same statements verify all
valuations quantified in \texttt{unr} and \texttt{ord}.  Since
\(\operatorname{disc}(K)=-3\), these are precisely the required
ramification conditions.  All fields of \texttt{Sd.IsTwist} are proved.
\hfill\(\square\)

\paragraph{Restriction to the actual hosts.}
For every CM host \(H\supset K\) in the construction, put
\(\kappa_{2,H}=\kappa_2|_{G_H}\).  Restriction preserves continuity,
the conjugate-product identity \(c_p^{-1}\), the bound six on inertia
away from \(p\), and unramifiedness away from \(3p\).  Its algebraic
type is the pullback of \((-1,0)\) along restriction of embeddings
\(H\hookrightarrow\mathbf C\) to \(K\).
The image remains in the same \(\mathcal O_{K_p}^{\times}\), so adjoining
these character values costs only an unramified quadratic coefficient
extension.  Moreover \(\mathcal O_{K_p}^{\times}\) has no nontrivial
\(p\)-primary torsion.  The character therefore kills the preimage of
the \(p\)-primary torsion in each local topological abelianization,
as required whenever the native \texttt{mu} condition is imposed on
this scalar character.  These statements do not assert the remaining
\texttt{GalLink}, \texttt{Frame.Ok}, \texttt{Big}, or \texttt{OffFin}
fields; those are checked separately in the endpoint construction.
