\section{The negative quadratic conductor-eight strict rows}
\label{sec:negative-quadratic8-strict}

\paragraph{Inputs and scope.}
This proof uses AC-G150-class for the two statements
$\operatorname{Cl}(\mathbb Q(t,\sqrt{-2}))[269]=0$ and
$\operatorname{Cl}(\mathbb Q(t,\sqrt{-538}))[269]=0$.
It uses actual global units, with exact integral relations, from
the same certificate.  The local computations below independently
retain every rational denominator.
The class proof, its analytic executions, this strict proof and its
finite local computations have separately recorded review scopes.
No automorphic endpoint or mixed realization is assumed here.

Put $p=269$, $K=\mathbb Q(\omega)$ and $t=\sqrt[3]p>0$.
Let $\chi:G_K\to\mu_3$ describe $K(t)/K$.
Let $\xi=\chi_{-8}|_{G_K}$ be the quadratic character of
$\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-2})$, and let $c_p$ and $\omega_p$
be the actual cyclotomic character and its Teichmuller part.
For a discrete coefficient module $D$ define
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 S_{\rm str}(K,D)=\ker\bigl(H^1_{\rm cts}(G_K,D)
                                    \longrightarrow\mathcal L(D)\bigr),\\
 \mathcal L(D)=\prod_{v\mid p}H^1(G_{K_v},D)
             \times\prod_{v\nmid p,\infty}H^1(I_v,D).
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{Q8N.1}
\]
The first local condition is zero in full local cohomology.

\begin{theorem}[AC-G150: the negative quadratic-eight rows on the original coefficients]
\label{AC-G150}
Let $E/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite and $O$ its original ring of integers,
containing the values of $\chi$.
Let $j=1,2$, $m\in\mathbb Z$, $e\geq1$, and let
$\kappa:G_K\to1+\mathfrak m_O$ be continuous and unramified
away from $p$.
Set
\[
          D=(E/O)(\chi^{-j}\xi\omega_p^{-m}\kappa^{-1}c_p^{-e}).
 \tag{Q8N.2}
\]
If $e+m\equiv267$ or $133\pmod{268}$, then
\[
                         S_{\rm str}(K,D)=0.
 \tag{Q8N.3}
\]
The corresponding residual strict group vanishes over every finite
field containing the character values.
There is no absolute ramification condition on $O$.
For $m=0,\kappa=1$, the finite labels are exactly
$\chi^{-j}\chi_{-8}$, with no additional conductor at $p$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\proofstep{1. The exact residual fields and their local places.}
Let $\tau_p=\omega_p^{134}$, the character of $\mathbb Q(\sqrt p)$.
Write $k=O/\mathfrak m_O$.
If $a$ is the residual character in (Q8N.2), the Kummer quotient is
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 a=\bar\chi^{-j}\bar\xi\,\bar c_p^{-(e+m)},\qquad
 \eta=a/\bar c_p=\bar\chi^{-j}\bar\xi\,\bar\tau_p^{\nu},\\
 \nu=0\quad(e+m\equiv267),\qquad
 \nu=1\quad(e+m\equiv133).
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{Q8N.4}
\]
The two congruences follow respectively from
$-(e+m+1)\equiv0,134\pmod{268}$.
Put
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 d_0=2,\quad d_1=538,\quad w_\nu^2=-d_\nu,\\
 F_\nu=\mathbb Q(w_\nu),\quad L_\nu=F_\nu(t),\quad M_\nu=KL_\nu.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{Q8N.5}
\]
The quadratic characters of $F_\nu$ are exactly
$\xi\tau_p^\nu$: their fundamental discriminants are $-8$ and
$-8p$, and multiplication of the quadratic characters gives
the indicated fields.
Neither $F_\nu$ equals the unique quadratic subfield $K$ of
the $S_3$ splitting field $K(t)$.
Consequently
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \operatorname{Gal}(M_\nu/\mathbb Q)=S_3\times C_2,\qquad
 \Delta_\nu=\operatorname{Gal}(M_\nu/K)=C_3\times C_2,\\
 [L_\nu:\mathbb Q]=6,\qquad[M_\nu:\mathbb Q]=12.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{Q8N.6}
\]
The character $\eta$ is faithful on $\Delta_\nu$.
Let $c(t)=\omega t$ generate $C_3$, and let $b(w_\nu)=-w_\nu$
generate $C_2$, both fixing the other displayed generators and $K$.

At $p$, $K_p/\mathbb Q_p$ is unramified quadratic.
For $\nu=0$, $X^2+2$ is irreducible modulo $p$, since
$(-2/p)=-1$ for $p\equiv5\pmod8$.
Thus $\mathbb Q_p(w_0)$ is the same unramified quadratic field
as $K_p$ inside a fixed algebraic closure.
Adjoining $t$ gives ramification degree three and residue degree two.
There are two primes in $M_0$ above $p$, each with $e=3,f=2$.
The automorphism $b$ exchanges them; it cannot act on one completion
while fixing $K_p$ and changing the element $w_0\in K_p$.
For $\nu=1$, the quadratic and cubic fields are totally ramified
of relatively prime degrees two and three.
Their compositum has degree and ramification degree six.
Adjoining the unramified $K_p$ gives the full degree twelve of $M_1$.
There is exactly one prime, with $e=6,f=2$.
We retain all these places in the local target.

\proofstep{2. Full Kummer descent and the class sector.}
The quotient order six is prime to $p$.
Inflation--restriction, globally and for every local subgroup,
identifies the residual strict group with
\[
 e_{\eta^{-1}}\ker\left(V_{M,S}\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k
            \longrightarrow\mathcal P_M\otimes_{\mathbb F_p}k\right),
 \tag{Q8N.7}
\]
where $M=M_\nu$ and
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 V_{M,S}=\{x\in M^\times:p\mid v(x)\text{ for }v\nmid p\}/M^{\times p},\\
 \mathcal P_M=\bigoplus_{v\mid p}M_v^\times/M_v^{\times p}.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{Q8N.8}
\]
Over $M$ the coefficient is $k(1)$; the extra quotient action on
Kummer cohomology is the twist $\eta$, so invariants select
$\eta^{-1}$, not $\eta$.
Away from $p$, zero inertia is the divisibility of each Kummer
valuation by $p$.  A full local $p$th power at a prime above $p$
also has valuation divisible by $p$.
Thus the kernel in (Q8N.7) lies in the all-primes valuation group
$V_M$, whose exact sequence is
\[
 0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_M^\times/\mathcal O_M^{\times p}
 \longrightarrow V_M\longrightarrow\operatorname{Cl}(M)[p]
 \longrightarrow0.
 \tag{Q8N.9}
\]

AC-G150-class proves $\operatorname{Cl}(L_\nu)[p]=0$.
Let $s$ be the reflection in the $S_3$ factor fixing $L_\nu$.
On $\operatorname{Cl}(M_\nu)[p]$, norm to $L_\nu$ followed by
extension is $1+s$ and is zero.
Hence $s=-1$; all conjugate reflections act in the same way.
Their products generate the cubic subgroup, which therefore acts
trivially on this class module.
Its nontrivial cubic part is zero.
This argument needs no assertion about the remaining class sector.

\proofstep{3. One nonzero global-unit value suffices.}
The six complex places of $M$ over a chosen complex embedding of
$K$ form a regular $\Delta_\nu$-set.
The equivariant Dirichlet unit character is
$\mathbb Q[\Delta_\nu]-\mathbb Q$.
There is no $p$-torsion in its roots of unity, since
$268\nmid[M:\mathbb Q]=12$.
Because $p\nmid6$, reduction modulo $p$ preserves the character
multiplicities.
The nontrivial cubic part $W_-$ of the $b=-1$ unit space has
dimension two over $\mathbb F_p$.
It is simple under $C_3$, since $X^2+X+1$ is irreducible over
$\mathbb F_{269}$.
Localization into the full direct sum in (Q8N.8) is equivariant.
A single nonzero local image from $W_-$ proves injectivity on
that space and, after scalar extension, on both cubic eigenlines
selected in (Q8N.7).

\proofstep{4. Exact integral units, including the denominator at $p$.}
Take unit number two in each of AC-G150-class's two exact unit
files.  The selected vectors and their monic degree-six relations
are reproduced in \path{selected_negative8_units.json}, with SHA256
\begin{quote}\ttfamily\small
223ba2ed1d0f0a86899b84445c18f6b7\\
5c77e2b03b602c1c88d50ac06d8ce245.
\end{quote}
The tensor basis is $t^a w_\nu^b$, with $0\leq a\leq2$,
$0\leq b\leq1$, ordered by $2a+b$.
The denominators of the selected vectors are one and $807=3p$.
All their integer numerators and complete integral annihilators
are part of this exact data, not rounded approximations.

For an explicit verification, let $T,W$ be the rational six-by-six
multiplication matrices in that tensor basis, determined by
$T^3=pI$, $W^2=-d_\nu I$, $TW=WT$.
The verifier forms the matrix of each supplied unit and computes
its characteristic polynomial over $\mathbb Q$.
It agrees exactly with its specified monic integral polynomial,
whose constant coefficient is one.
Cayley--Hamilton proves integrality of the unit; the same equation,
solved for its inverse, proves integrality of the inverse.
This checks all four supplied units, although only unit two in
each field is needed here.  It does not assume a fundamental basis.

For $\nu=0$, the tensor coefficients are $p$-integral and the
completion is $\mathbb Z_p[w_0][t]$.
For $\nu=1$ use the exact uniformizer
\[
 u=w_1/t,\qquad u^6=-8p,\qquad
 t=-u^2/2,\qquad w_1=-u^3/2.
 \tag{Q8N.10}
\]
The polynomial $X^6+8p$ is Eisenstein.
Its power basis is the full local integral basis: valuations of
the six coefficient terms are distinct modulo six, so a rational
power-basis expansion is integral exactly when every coefficient
is $p$-integral.
The verifier changes bases over $\mathbb Q$ and only then reduces
modulo $p$.  Equivalently it substitutes
$t^aw_1^b=(-1/2)^{a+b}u^{2a+3b}$ and reduces by $u^6=-8p$.
This step retains and cancels the factor $p$ in the denominator
$807$ exactly, rather than reducing that denominator modulo $p$.

\proofstep{5. The two-place calculation at residue $267$.}
At one chosen prime of $M_0$, write $w=w_0$.
Its residue field is $\mathbb F_p[w]/(w^2+2)$.
For the selected unit the exact reduction is
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 U=A+tB+t^2C\pmod{t^3},\\
 A=135+29w,\quad B=37+2w,\quad C=116+8w.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{Q8N.11}
\]
The element $A$ is nonzero in this field.
Normalizing by $A$ gives the truncated logarithm
\[
              \log(U/A)=4wt+137wt^2\pmod{t^3}.
 \tag{Q8N.12}
\]
Form the actual global unit
$V=U^3/(U\,c(U)\,c^2(U))$.
The constant factors cancel, and the operator
$3-(1+c+c^2)$ multiplies the coefficients of $t,t^2$ by three.
Apply $1-b$ globally, meaning the unit $V/b(V)$.
Since $b(w)=-w$ and $b(t)=t$, its logarithm at the chosen place is
\[
                    24wt+15wt^2\ne0\pmod{t^3}.
 \tag{Q8N.13}
\]
These equalities are exact finite-field arithmetic; the independent
matrix verifier records the normalized and projected vectors.
The operation is made on the global unit before evaluation.
We do not let the place-exchanging $b$ act as a $K_p$-linear
automorphism of one completion.  A nonzero component suffices
for the full two-place target in (Q8N.8).
As $v_t(p)=3<p-1$, the logarithm of a local $p$th power principal
unit has valuation at least four.
The nonzero linear term proves that (Q8N.13) is a nonzero Kummer class.

\proofstep{6. The single-place calculation at residue $133$.}
In the uniformizer (Q8N.10), the selected unit has exact reduction
\[
 U=(268,70,240,49,84,220)
                       \quad\text{in the basis }1,u,\ldots,u^5.
 \tag{Q8N.14}
\]
Its constant term is $268=-1$ in $\mathbb F_p$.
The truncated logarithm, after division by this constant, is
\[
                    199u+218u^5\pmod{u^6}.
 \tag{Q8N.15}
\]
Here $c(u)=\omega^{-1}u$ and $b(u)=-u$.
The same global cubic norm correction and quadratic projector
therefore give
\[
                   118u+232u^5\ne0\pmod{u^6}.
 \tag{Q8N.16}
\]
All denominators in the truncated logarithm, one through five,
are invertible modulo $p$.
The full local group acts on this unique prime, and the displayed
operators are its actual actions.
Since $v_u(p)=6<p-1$, the logarithm of a $p$th power principal
unit has valuation at least seven.
Thus (Q8N.16) is also a nonzero local Kummer class.

For completeness, in both local cases logarithm and exponential
are inverse isomorphisms on principal units, because their
nonlinear terms have strictly larger valuation when the absolute
ramification index is less than $p-1$.
The projected global units have residue one.
If one is a $p$th power of a unit, its root also has residue one,
since Frobenius is an automorphism of the finite residue field.
The preceding logarithmic tests therefore detect full local
$p$th powers, not merely powers within a smaller chosen subgroup.
By Step3 localization is injective on both required cubic lines.
Together with the vanished class sector and (Q8N.9), this proves
the residual strict-zero assertion for both residues.

\proofstep{7. Lift on the original coefficient ring.}
Let $\varpi$ be a uniformizer of the original $O$.
The module $D$ is divisible and $D[\varpi]\simeq k(a)$.
At $p$, the original character $\xi$ is unramified.
The residual cubic inertia has order three, which cannot be
cancelled by a residual cyclotomic character of order dividing
$268$; $\kappa$ reduces to one.
Some inertia element therefore acts by a scalar nontrivial
modulo $\varpi$, and $D^{G_{K_p}}=0$.
At every finite place away from $p$, the cyclotomic,
Teichmuller and $\kappa$ factors are unramified.
The remaining finite inertia character has order dividing six,
prime to $p$; its invariants are zero or all of $D$.
In every local group $J$ in (Q8N.1),
$D^J/\varpi D^J=0$.
Thus $H^1(J,D[\varpi])\to H^1(J,D)$ is injective.
Every global strict class killed by $\varpi$ lifts, by the global
long exact sequence, to a residual class; local injectivity
makes that class strict, so it is zero.
Finally every continuous cocycle from compact $G_K$ into discrete
$E/O$ has finite image and is killed by some power of $\varpi$.
A nonzero strict class would yield one killed by $\varpi$.
This contradiction proves (Q8N.3) without changing $O$.
\end{proof}

descent; it does not alter an original finite host label.
