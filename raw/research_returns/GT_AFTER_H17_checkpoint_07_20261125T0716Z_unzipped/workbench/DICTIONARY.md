# Mathematical dictionary

These translations explain the results in the language of arithmetic geometry. They do not assert additional comparisons with K-theory or motives.

## Arithmetic quotient and the normalized subgroup

The field K2 = Q(mu_infinity, 2^(1/infinity)) combines the cyclotomic tower with the Kummer tower of the rational unit 2. Its finite layers are finite etale away from their ramified primes. The pair (lambda,rho2) is the action on the corresponding roots of unity and radicals. Its image is the affine group with the quadratic parity condition imposed by Q(sqrt2). H17 Theorem2 supplies the GT factorization; the arithmetic quotient and its exact parity are proved in the arithmetic paper.

In the language of Grothendieck's programme, the normalized subgroup is the part left after those two arithmetic coordinates have been removed. The statement that it acts on the pure Teichmuller tower is background from Hatcher–Lochak–Schneps and H17, not an identification with a motivic Galois group.

## Relative abelian quotient

B = N/closure([GT,N]K) is the universal abelian obstruction to extending the known Galois action to all of GT. The proof is a compact-group gluing argument and Kronecker–Weber. A nonzero F_p-valued point of its continuous dual would distinguish GT from G_Q. Vanishing of every such point settles only the cyclotomic abelianization question; it does not identify the full groups.

There is no theorem here identifying B with an algebraic K-group, a lambda-ring operation, or an Ext group in a category of motives. Such a statement would require a specified comparison functor and proof.

## Cyclic covers and divided Kummer characters

The subgroup H_d is the etale fundamental group of the cover t -> t^d of the three-punctured projective line, with its roots-of-unity punctures. Preservation of the positive boundary class yields a symmetry in its two-step nilpotent homology. Passing from degree2d to degreed gives evenness of every integral Ihara cocycle at multiplier one. Division by two produces characters whose arithmetic restrictions are the quadratic extensions K2(q^(1/4))/K2. This is a statement about finite etale covers and their homology, not an invocation of an unproved arithmetic distribution relation.

Enriquez, Theorem 8.1 and Remark 8.6, proves the full integral distribution relation rho_ab=rho_a+rho_b on GT. Thus every divided multiplicative defect is zero. The earlier candidate status is superseded.

The field L_rad generated by all roots of unity and all positive rational radicals removes every rational Kummer coordinate simultaneously. Finite character separation and compactness give the full joint image, not just separate surjectivity. N_rad is their common kernel, and G=N_rad G_Q. The same relative quotient B is therefore computed on N_rad. The invariant character problem is a fiber product over the restriction to G_(L_rad).

## Weights and the pentagon

W is the graded Lie algebra of invariant special derivations for the punctured sphere. Its weights are word degrees. The pentagon cuts out grt inside W. Brown's motivic construction and the Deligne–Drinfeld theorem identify the rational pentagon algebra with a free Lie algebra on odd generators. Thus every rational even pentagon direction is decomposable. The compact-image and lifting arguments transfer complementary even directions to finite characters of the gentle group.

This comparison uses a proven rational Lie-algebra theorem. It does not identify the full profinite GT group with a motivic Galois group, and does not identify a mod-p pentagon kernel with its rational reduction without an additional integral argument.

## Degeneracy correspondences

The two maps from Gamma0(N) to PSL2(Z) are the group-theoretic realizations of the two degeneracy maps of the modular correspondence. Normalized relation (IV) proves their compatibility at N=2. Its defect lies in the congruence kernel; the parabolic centralizer meets that kernel trivially. Congruence matrices consequently cannot decide this full-group identity.

## The quartic layer
