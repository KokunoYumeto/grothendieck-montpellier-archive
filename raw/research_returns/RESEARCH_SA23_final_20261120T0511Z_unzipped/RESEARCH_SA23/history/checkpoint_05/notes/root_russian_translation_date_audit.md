# Russian 1958 Fredholm translation: bounded access record

Recorded 20 November 2026, before the fifth scheduled checkpoint. This is a
documentary route, not a change in the manuscript-date gate.

## Reason for the route

An original translator's or editor's note might identify correspondence or a
circulated proof before Lidskii's article. The existence of a translation
does not itself date the Montpellier leaves. We therefore seek the actual
1958 text and any editorial apparatus, not a second citation of the French
1956 article, which has already been read.

## Evidence actually obtained

The primary MathNet catalogue is indexed under Mi mat67 as A. Grothendieck,
“Теория Фредгольма”, *Математика* 2:5 (1958), pp. 51–104, translated from
*Bulletin de la Société Mathématique de France* 84 (1956), pp. 319–384.
The indexed primary text advertises a 3069 kB full-text PDF and gives the
translator as С. Н. Крачковского. We do not resolve a conflicting initial in
a secondary periodical list by silently choosing that list.

Primary catalogue URL:
https://www.mathnet.ru/php/archive.phtml?jrnid=mat&option_lang=rus&paperid=67&wshow=paper

## Failed step and next discriminating step

The failed step is access to the original translated pages. No content
comparison of the translation is claimed. An independently public copy or
publicly archived edition could permit inspection of the first and last
pages, translator's notes, and passages about Hilbert eigenvalues. A dated
editorial reference would then need an explicit connection to the trace or
genus-zero sheets; the translation's 1958 publication date alone would not
provide one. This route remains pending at this checkpoint.
