## 1956 metric Résumé

The entire 63-page modern transcription at <https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~leila.schneps/grothendieckcircle/AG/AG-22.pdf> was downloaded and searched/read structurally. The 80-page official USP facsimile reprint was then retrieved from <https://revistas.usp.br/resenhasimeusp/article/download/74836/78406>. Its article metadata dates the reprint to 1996, DOI 10.11606/resimeusp.v2i4.74836; it retains original printed numbering 1–79, with an offset in the later pages (PDF p. 55 = printed p. 54). Neither the metadata nor the reprint independently establishes the original publication date.

Original printed pp. 53, 54, 77 and 79 have now been visually inspected from local renders. Section 3, no. 6, p. 53 defines rho_i(u) as eigenvalues of |u|=(u*u)^(1/2), hence singular values. Theorem 5 on p. 54 asserts the eigenvalue summability criterion only for Hermitian u, and reduces general u to |u|. Its norm formula is the sum of absolute values, not the trace formula for nonnormal operators. The Hilbert–Schmidt formula Tr(u*u) and nuclear product inequality also concern singular values. Final note 8 bis, p. 77, gives the singular-value product inequality and invokes Weyl's convexity method. It explicitly mentions a 1954 São Paulo seminar which was not written up, and a December 1954 Bourbaki seminar concerning traces on involutive algebras. It does not print the manuscript three-sector genus-zero argument.

The full text architecture is: Introduction pp. 1–6; contents p. 7; tensor norms §1 pp. 8–18; C/L tensor norms §2 pp. 19–39; Hilbert norms §3 pp. 40–56; relations/applications §4 pp. 57–74; bibliography p. 75; final notes pp. 76–79. No determinant or genus-zero occurrence was found in the complete modern transcription. The introduction limits the supplied proofs; the closest operator section is the Hermitian/singular-value material above. A full page-by-page facsimile audit beyond the selected relevant pages is not claimed.
