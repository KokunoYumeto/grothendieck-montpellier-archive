\paragraph{Date of the manuscript sheets.}
The Montpellier inventory, reproduced in the archivists' 2019 presentation
(PDF p.~9, original inventory p.~34), describes cote~1 as undated mathematical
notes together with a letter dated 1953; its dates-extremes column reads
1953.  That description does not identify the date or recipient of the
particular genus-zero sheets.\cite{algMontpellierInventoryPresentation2019}
The archive's introductory notice explains that correspondents' letters
are not publicly disseminated without authorization; absence of an
identified letter from the public images therefore does not establish
absence from the physical dossier.  Its method account assigns the
lower-left HB pencil numbering to the archivists.  The visible sequence
in cote~1 begins with archival pencil~1 and~2 (physical PDF pages~2--3;
public PDF footers~1--2), followed by archival pencil~5 (physical page~4;
public PDF footer~3).  This discontinuity does not by itself
identify the missing numbers with the catalogue's letter.\cite{algMontpellierArchiveMethod}
The original letter to Dixmier of 24~January 1955, p.~1, directly witnesses
discussion of the product inequality
$\Delta_{|AB|}\leq\Delta_{|A|}\Delta_{|B|}$ and asks whether Kadison already
knew it.  It supplies a date for that related research topic, without
identifying these manuscript sheets or mentioning their genus-zero
argument.\cite{algDixmier19550124}
The seven available original letters in the public Dixmier set were
visually inspected in this run, and the eighth was read in modern
transcription; no such identification was found in this bounded set.
The ENS inventory identifies the group as JD~2/38--46,
20~November 1950--24~January 1955.\cite{algDixmierInventory2019}
