2. **SUB Göttingen, Cod. Ms. G. Köthe I6, *Vollstetige Operatoren*.** Kalliope record DE-611-HS-3176137, https://kalliope-verbund.info/de/ead?ead.id=DE-611-HS-3176137 . Catalogued1948–1952;27folios; author Köthe. Folios2–8 and9–20 are two versions on compactness and the Neumann series. Folios21–27 are titled *Zum Grothendieckschen Problem*. The record does not identify that problem. The title cannot responsibly be expanded into either the approximation problem or the Hilbert trace problem without reading the originals. This unit is the second Grothendieck name-index hit in the Köthe collection; it is not another correspondence collection.
