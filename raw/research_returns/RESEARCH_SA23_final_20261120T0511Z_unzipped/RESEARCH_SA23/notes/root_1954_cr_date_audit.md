## What the originals establish

Part I concerns preintegral and Hilbertian maps, their factorization and
tensor-norm consequences. Its p. 577 says that the main proof is to appear
in the São Paulo Boletim and that the thesis is not yet published. The
running header is 23 August 1954; the starred note separately identifies the
session of 28 June 1954. We retain both printed dates. Page 577's footer
explicitly gives volume 239. These are dates and references for this note,
not an identification of any Montpellier leaf.

Part II develops dual formulations, factorization through Hilbert spaces,
nuclear-space criteria and classes of tensor products. Its running header
is 30 August 1954; its starred note again gives 28 June 1954. Its cross-reference
to Part I prints volume 238, whereas the inspected Part I footer gives 239.
We do not silently transfer that conflicting volume number to the
bibliographic record. Neither note contains the spectral trace identity,
a genus-zero proof, a dated letter or an identification of the target
manuscript sheets.
