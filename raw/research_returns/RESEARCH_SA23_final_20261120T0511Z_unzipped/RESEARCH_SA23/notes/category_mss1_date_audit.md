linking these sheets to a dated event was found in the assigned range.
Several sheets are faint or heavily cancelled; the conclusion is that this
inspection found **no dating witness**, not that an unreadable mark has been

For these pages the printed public PDF footer is one less than the physical
PDF page number. The small archival pencilled sheet numbers are generally one more
than the physical PDF page number. Thus physical pages 79–80 carry public PDF
footers 78–79 and archival pencil numbers 80–81. Any final locator should specify
which numbering is meant rather than silently identifying all three.

Named antecedents such as Weyl or Schatten may identify mathematical
sources, but without a dated dependency or an external archival chain they
do not establish that the sheet was written before 1959. Nor does proximity
to an independently dated 1953 letter transfer that date to these sheets.
There is therefore no new upper dating bound from this page range.

The archive's official method identifies the lower-left pencil numbering as archival; the public PDF footer is a separate continuous sequence.
