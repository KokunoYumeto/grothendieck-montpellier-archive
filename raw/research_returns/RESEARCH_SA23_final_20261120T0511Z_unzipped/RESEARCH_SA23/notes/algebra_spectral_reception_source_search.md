## 4. Distinct original Dunford survey (1958)

An independently public academic mirror supplied **Nelson Dunford, “A Survey of the Theory of Spectral Operators,” Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society 64 (1958), 217–274**:

<https://pages.mtu.edu/~struther/Courses/OLD/5630/Refs/Dunford_BullAMS_1958_SpectralOperators.pdf>

The web reader timed out; one ordinary unauthenticated retrieval succeeded, giving the full **58-page** original article, **5,642,033 bytes**. Every page has extractable text (minimum **1,108 characters** per page). The entire native extraction was searched for `groth`, `grot`, `lidsk`, `schatten`, `trace`, `genus`, `communicat`, `unpublish`, `letter` and `acknowledg`. The saved output gives every hit and page, and records zero hits for the first six terms. This is a text-search result, not a claim that all 58 pages were visually read.

We directly displayed and read original printed **pp. 217, 221, 223, 225, 272–274**. These establish the following precise distinctions:

- **P. 217:** the lecture and editorial receipt are both dated **28 January 1958**; footnote 1 explicitly refers to the forthcoming Dunford–Schwartz Part II. Neither item identifies a Grothendieck manuscript.
- **P. 221, footnote 3:** a letter acknowledgment concerns **D. R. Smart**, about differential-operator examples. Native OCR misreads the footnote marker as 8; the original image confirms **3**.
- **P. 223, footnote 6:** the letter acknowledgment concerns **Fixman**, about examples involving spectral resolutions.
- **P. 225:** a communicated similar proof is credited to **Ciprian Foias**, concerning canonical reduction of spectral operators. This is not an acknowledgment of Grothendieck's trace/genus-zero proof, and no letter date is printed there.
- **Pp. 272–274:** the complete bibliography was visually checked; no Grothendieck entry or relevant enclosure is present.

These concrete communication acknowledgments were not repurposed as evidence for a different person's proof. The survey gives no date or identity for cote 1 in the inspected material.
