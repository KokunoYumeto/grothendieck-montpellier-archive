The initial fonds did not have a consistent thematic/chronological arrangement; dossier groupings were substantially preserved. Reused paper is frequent. The archivists explain that dated versos may give termini post quem, that original folders were separately conserved under A–C, and that blank versos/rare duplicates were not digitized. Separators represent formerly clipped/folded units. Thus a neighbouring PDF image is not automatically the reverse of a sheet and does not automatically date its mathematical writing.
- The public page provides no source-level identification of the 1953 letter within cote 1. No public scan of the separate original folders A–C was located in this search. No request to an archivist or other external recipient was sent.
- The home page's opening paragraph (local HTML line 123) expressly says that only about 18,000 of 28,000 fonds pages were publicly accessible, because correspondents' letters require authorization before dissemination. Thus failure to find the catalogue's letter in a public PDF would not establish its absence from the physical fonds. This general restriction does not itself identify which particular cote 1 sheets are absent. The lower-left HB pencil numbering is described as the archivists' numbering in lines 163–164, not as authorial dating evidence.
