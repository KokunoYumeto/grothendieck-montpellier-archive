## Result and limits

No authorial date, salutation, signature, address, or identifiable letter was found in these 24 images. Physical page 1 is a modern institutional cover, not an authorial dated sheet. The remaining images are a blank leaf, contents, and mathematical notes. Many have faint mathematical show-through and crossings-out; this negative report does not certify that every faint mark is decipherable. It does not identify the catalogue's 1953 letter, establish that no such letter exists in the dossier, or date any mathematical sheet by adjacency.

The pencil numbering on the scanned sheets jumps from 2 on physical page 3 to 5 on physical page 4. Physical pages 2–3 were displayed a second time in response to root's explicit numbering query; their lower-left numbers are clearly 1 and 2. The official home page explains that the archivists numbered sheets in HB pencil, generally at lower left (`sources/algebra_history_montpellier_home.html`, lines 163–164). Thus this pencil sequence must not be called an authorial pagination without separate evidence. The institutional PDF footer numbering remains consecutive.

This is an observed numbering discontinuity, **not evidence by itself** that pencil leaves 3–4 were letters, were dated, or were omitted improperly. The home page's opening paragraph explicitly states that only about 18,000 of 28,000 fonds pages were then publicly accessible because correspondents' letters cannot be disseminated without their authorization (local HTML line 123). Its digitization account separately says that blank versos and rare duplicates were not digitized (line 164). Neither statement specifically identifies the missing pencil numbers 3–4 in cote 1. No nonpublic image path was sought and no withheld letter was accessed.
