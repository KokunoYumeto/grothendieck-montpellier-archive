## Actually opened and read

The first web response exposed a five-page PDF arranged as two-page
spreads. Its returned text covered the article's header and printed
pp. 651–657, stopping during the discussion on the 656–657 spread. It
also included the preceding article's p. 650, which we did not confuse
with this source. We read the returned article text; **we did not read
the missing end on pp. 658–659, visually inspect the PDF, or read any
underlying letter**. The returned column order interleaves the facing
pages, so this is a bounded text reading rather than a visual collation.

The survey identifies **fonds 346, inventory 3**, containing 319 files
and 1,118 correspondence documents from 1943–1989, with chronological

describes collections and correspondents, without identifying a
Grothendieck trace/genus-zero proof or dating the target leaves. Its
correspondence statistics are a finding aid, not evidence about an
unopened letter's contents.
