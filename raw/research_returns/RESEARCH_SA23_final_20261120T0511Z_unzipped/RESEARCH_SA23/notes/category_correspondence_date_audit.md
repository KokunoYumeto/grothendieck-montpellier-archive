## Smallest remaining documentary requirement

A dated letter or archival entry must identify this proof, its distinctive
argument, an attached enclosure, or these particular leaves with sufficient
specificity to supply an upper bound before1959. Finding that the same result
was printed earlier settles the printed-result question; it does not date an
undated handwritten copy or draft. The cautious phrase *may predate* remains
possible, but is not promoted to an established priority claim.

An uncollated first printing is an edition qualification for the inspected
memoir, not an independently invented task. The exact prompt asks whether the
1955–56 works print the argument; it does not require collation of every
physical printing. The actual sheet-date question is a separate requested
historical issue and remains explicit.
