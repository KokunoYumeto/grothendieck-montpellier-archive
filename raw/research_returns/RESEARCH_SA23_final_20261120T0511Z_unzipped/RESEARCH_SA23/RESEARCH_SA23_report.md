## Main mathematical decisions

The main question A.1 has a complete negative answer. For every characteristic-zero extension k⊂K and n>0, the differential d(exp(t₁))−exp(t₁)dt₁ is nonzero and lies in every power t₁ⁿΩ. A projective module over the separated local power-series ring cannot contain such an element. This proves nonprojectivity of Ω and failure of discrete formal smoothness, including Q[[t]] over Q. No bound on Ω(K/k) is needed.

In characteristic p, the exact positive-dimensional criterion is separability of K/k together with finite absolute p-rank of K. The relative p-basis condition by itself fails over an arbitrary imperfect base. The proof separates infinite relative differentials from the harder case where those differentials are finite but absolute p-rank remains infinite.

For A.3 we retain the actual nonlocal noetherian adic scope. Cotangent complexes on the adic quotients are projective in degree zero; positive Ext groups with finite complete coefficients vanish. The square-zero obstruction theorem then supplies a flat lift, and the proof verifies its noetherianity, completeness, formal smoothness, uniqueness up to reduction-preserving isomorphism and derivation automorphisms. CHECK_EVENTS.tsv records actual event types and separates the constructing agent from root’s targeted audit. The current register corrects those event types, and an explicit erratum event preserves that fact; the earlier checkpoint remains unchanged.

Original attachments, previously saved checkpoint archives, retrievable full-text caches and the reproducible TeX runtime are not nested into it. checkpoint_manifest.json lists retained files, omitted cache hashes and earlier checkpoint identifiers.
