# The finite-field coefficient-duality dependency in B5

**Status:** proved equivalence for the specified rational Weil extension of
all Chow-heart coefficients. Beilinson and Parshin remain conjectural in
the stated universal scope. This records an additional finite-field
dependency of B5, not new movement of the universal gate.

## 1. Category, shifts and the meaning of the coefficient hypothesis

Fix a finite field k. Write D=DM_gm(k,Q), with its usual rigid tensor
structure and unit 1. For a smooth projective X/k of dimension d, write

    h_coh(X) = M(X)^vee = M(X)(-d)[-2d],
    P_(X,n) = h_coh(X)(n)[2n].

The rational Chow heart H is the additive idempotent-complete category
generated by these P_(X,n), for all X and integers n. In particular H is
closed under duality, and

    P_(X,n)^vee = P_(X,d-n).

For P in H put A^i(P)=Hom_D(1,P[i]). Our indexing gives exactly

    A^i(P_(X,n)) = H_M^(2n+i)(X,Q(n)) = CH^n(X,-i)_Q.

The right hand side is zero for i>0, because the higher-Chow complex has
no negative simplicial degrees. This is the instance of Chow negativity
needed below; it passes to finite sums and direct summands. The dual P^vee
also belongs to H, so A^i(P^vee)=0 for i>0 as well.

**Chow weight zero is not cohomological homogeneity.** The object
P_(X,n) can contain several cohomological weights. It is an object of the
Chow heart, but it need not be a homogeneous pure coefficient motive in
Grothendieck's graded sense. No Chow--Künneth decomposition is assumed.
Katz--Messing cohomological projectors by themselves do not supply such a
decomposition as actual mutually orthogonal rational Chow idempotents.

## 2. The specified Weil extension and its pairing

Use the rational Weil extension of ordinary geometric motives. With a
chosen generator e of degree one at Spec k, its cochain model is

    C_W(P) = E tensor_Q RHom_D(1,P),
    E = Q direct_sum Q e,  |e|=1,  e^2=0.

Thus

    W^i(P) = A^i(P) direct_sum e A^(i-1)(P).

This is the rational splitting of Kahn (2012), Remark 10.5.2(2) and
Corollary 11.2.2(c), with the projection formula of Theorem 11.2.1.
The monoid structure is part of that projection formula. Equivalently,
over Q the degree-one exterior generator gives a multiplicative formal
model for the rational complex of the point. We are using this model on
the images of ordinary geometric motives, not assuming that their image
is already a triangulated subcategory of the larger Weil category;
Kahn's Remark 11.3.2 explicitly warns that it is not.

Evaluation P tensor P^vee -> 1 and the normalized trace e -> 1 give

    b_P^i : W^i(P) x W^(1-i)(P^vee) -> Q.

Here e is placed on the left. If x=a+e b and y=c+e d have total degrees
i and 1-i, respectively, the exact Koszul convention is

    b_P^i(x,y) = (-1)^i ev(a,d) + ev(b,c).

Indeed moving a past e contributes (-1)^i. In these terms
a is in A^i(P), b is in A^(i-1)(P), c is in A^(1-i)(P^vee),
and d is in A^(-i)(P^vee). Both displayed evaluations have ordinary
cohomological degree zero. This convention fixes the point's duality
degree as 1, rather than 0.

## 3. Exact equivalence

The following statements are equivalent.

1. For every P in the Chow heart and every integer i, both groups in
   b_P^i are finite dimensional and the pairing is perfect.
2. For every smooth projective X/k, rational and numerical equivalence
   agree on every rational Chow group, and

       H_M^r(X,Q(n))=0 for every r != 2n and every integer n.

The second clause is Parshin's rational vanishing; vanishing above the
diagonal is already the higher-Chow negative-degree calculation.

### Proof that 1 implies 2

Chow negativity gives W^j(P^vee)=0 for j>1. If i<0, then 1-i>1,
so perfection forces W^i(P)=0. Since A^i(P) is a direct summand of
W^i(P), it vanishes for every i<0. Together with negativity this gives

    A^i(P)=0 for i != 0.

Apply this to P_(X,n). The indexing in Section 1 gives all of Parshin's
vanishing, not just the degree-one comparison.

Now W^0(P)=A^0(P) and W^1(P^vee)=e A^0(P^vee), and their pairing is
ordinary evaluation. For P=P_(X,n), it is precisely

    CH^n(X)_Q x CH^(d-n)(X)_Q -> Q,
    (alpha,beta) |-> deg(alpha beta).

Its kernel on either side is the numerically trivial subgroup.
Perfection therefore says that this subgroup is zero, which is
Beilinson's rational--numerical assertion.

### Proof that 2 implies 1

Parshin and negativity make A^i(P_(X,n)) vanish outside i=0, and hence
do the same for every P in the Chow heart. Beilinson makes the displayed
intersection pairing nondegenerate. Its numerical quotient is finite
dimensional: independent numerical classes admit a full-rank rational
intersection matrix, whose rank is bounded by the appropriate finite
Weil-cohomology dimension. Thus this nondegenerate pairing is perfect.

Finite sums preserve perfection. If p is a Chow idempotent, the dual
idempotent p^vee is adjoint under evaluation, so the images of p and
p^vee again pair perfectly. This proves the assertion for every P in H.
Only W^0=A^0 and W^1=e A^0 remain, and the two nonzero pairings are the
cross-evaluations already described. This proves 1.

This argument is the Chow-heart and Weil-degree version of Kahn (2012),
Proposition 10.5.1 and Remark 10.5.2(2), whose complete proofs have been
read. The source states the equivalent ordinary composition criterion
for every geometric motive and derives the bounded-derived numerical
description. No assertion that the conjectures hold is extracted from
that equivalence.

## 4. The same dependency already occurs on P1 with constant coefficients

Suppose the rational Weil theory has its ordinary projective-bundle
formula, cup product and Gysin trace. Let pi:P1_k -> Spec k and let P be
any Chow-heart object. Write h=c1(O(1)), of cohomological degree 2 and
Tate twist 1. Then

    W^i(P1,pi*P)
      = W^i(P) direct_sum h W^(i-2)(P(-1)),

and

    W^(3-i)(P1,pi*(P^vee(1)))
      = W^(3-i)(P^vee(1)) direct_sum h W^(1-i)(P^vee).

The trace has total degree 3, namely two geometric degrees plus the
degree-one Weil trace of the point. The identities pi_*(h)=1 and h^2=0
show that the pairing is block off-diagonal. With x,u and y,v denoting
the corresponding two components, it is

    (x,u),(y,v) |-> b_P^i(x,v) + b_(P(-1))^(i-2)(u,y).

There is no additional sign from moving h, which has even degree. The
unit--unit term is killed by the Gysin projection, and the h--h term is
zero because h^2=0. Perfection and finite dimensionality of the curve
pairing therefore imply the perfect point pairing b_P^i as a block.
Conversely point perfection for all P and shifts gives the curve
pairing. For this converse note that P(-1)=P(-1)[-2][2]; equivalently
P(-1)[-2] is again a Chow-heart object, and perfection is unchanged by
the indicated simultaneous degree shifts. More directly, set
Q=P(-1)[-2], so W^(i-2)(P(-1))=W^i(Q),
and Q^vee=P^vee(1)[2].

Thus full coefficient duality on the single curve P1 already has the
same Beilinson--Parshin dependency **when every Chow-heart coefficient
is included with its prescribed ordinary Weil extension**.

## 5. Exact limitation for the original homogeneous-pure formulation

If B5 quantifies only over homogeneous pure Chow summands, the previous
necessity argument applies to each such summand. To infer the universal
criterion one must justify extension to the whole Chow heart. An actual
finite Chow--Künneth decomposition supplies such a justification, as does
an explicit coefficient-category hypothesis including all those objects.
This is not obtained merely by having algebraic cohomological Künneth
projectors modulo homological or numerical equivalence.

Under the stronger coefficient hypothesis, the finite-field B5 request
therefore contains Beilinson and Parshin. Under B1's standing universal
Tate and semisimplicity hypotheses, the already verified Geisser/Kahn
implication makes this the same remaining universal Chow-phantom gate.
Without those standing hypotheses, it must be stated as Beilinson **and**
Parshin, not Beilinson alone. The arithmetic general-motive
f-admissibility gate remains a separate dependency. Neither is resolved
by finite-coefficient duality, by the proved Tate/Artin--Tate cases, or by
choosing arbitrary abstract dual completions unrelated to the prescribed
ordinary motivic groups.
