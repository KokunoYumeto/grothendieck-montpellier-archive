# Source attacks after formal review 02

## Codimension-two diagonal factorization

Fu–Vial (2023), *Cubic fourfolds, Kuznetsov components, and Chow motives*,
Lemma 5.1 and complete proof (p.841), was opened from the author's published
PDF. It proves rational equals numerical in codimension two under universal
curve support and vanishing of H3. Its actual argument factors a multiple
of the identity through divisors on an alteration. Over finite fields the
Picard-zero group is torsion, so H3 vanishing is unnecessary. Moreover,
surface support can be retained: its extra term factors through zero-cycles
on a surface, whose degree-zero subgroup is torsion over finite fields.
The complete independently written proof is
`sections/b1_surface_support.tex`; it does not claim full higher-motivic
comparison. Its generic-point hypothesis is essential and is not replaced
by the automatic finite-base CH0 statement.

The original Bloch–Srinivas (1983) Proposition 1 was sought via the IAS
repository, author publication list and publisher records. The repository
explicitly says full text is unavailable; no full original was obtained.
The actual decomposition used here is derived directly from the generic
point, spreading rational equivalences and Chow localization. Thus original
source access is not concealed as a proof input. Poonen (2004), Theorem 3.3,
Corollary 3.4 and their complete proofs, were opened to place any finite set
of closed points on a smooth geometrically integral curve over the same
finite field. This proves the needed rational CH0 vanishing using only the
finite Jacobian of that curve; no stronger uniform finiteness claim is made.

## Broe (February 2026), arXiv:2509.03602v3

The complete primary HTML was opened. Exact passages read were Proposition
2.2, Proposition 2.5, Corollary 2.6, Theorem 3.18 and its proof in Section
3.7, Proposition 4.10, the complete Theorem 6.4 proof, and Theorem 6.12.
Theorem 6.4 proves Beilinson–Bloch for generic cubic threefolds of pencils
on finite-field cubic fourfolds. It does not prove arbitrary finite-field
threefold cycle injectivity. Theorem 6.12 retains a full generalized
Tate–Beilinson hypothesis on the cubic fourfold for its complete motivic
description. Theorem 3.18's specialization statement has a lifting datum;
one cannot silently use it for every nonliftable positive-characteristic
variety with representable cycles. Known unirational-threefold motive
arguments are acknowledged separately from the broader surface-support
criterion above. This current source does not close the universal B1 gate.

## Scavia–Suzuki (September 2026), arXiv:2609.36188v1

Opened the primary 54-page PDF, read Theorems 1.1–1.2 (pp.1–3) and the
complete Corollary 7.7 proof (pp.47–48). The Fermat elliptic-cube result
proves degree-three unramified-cohomology vanishing and integral cycle-map
surjectivity with explicit prime restrictions. The general theorem retains
surface Tate and a smooth Witt lift and gives prime-uniform surjectivity
after finite extension. The ordinary elliptic cube's geometric Griffiths
group in Corollary 7.7 is primary torsion, explicitly a sum of two Prüfer
groups under its hypotheses. That torsion disappears after tensoring with
Q. None of these statements proves rational cycle injectivity on arbitrary
threefolds, and none is used as a solution of B1.

## Longo–Vigni (July 2026), DOI 10.1007/s40687-026-00646-7

Opened the full published HTML of *The Tamagawa number conjecture and
Kolyvagin's conjecture for motives of modular forms*, Research in the
Mathematical Sciences 13, article 64. Read Section 1.1.2, Conjecture 2.50,
Remark 2.51, Section 2.16, and Theorem 5.42 (Theorem B) with Assumption
5.38. The motivic-to-finite-Selmer regulator bijectivity needed by B5 is
precisely Conjecture 2.50 and remains among the theorem's assumptions.
The source does not prove that comparison as a consequence of its
Tamagawa-number theorem. It therefore does not remove B5's general
f-admissibility gate. This is a failed implication route, not an adverse
claim about the published theorem.
