## 2. Why field-extension nilpotence does not settle the K3 determinant

Here is a direct proof of the relevant rational-coefficient distinction.
Let \(X\) be smooth and projective over a field \(k\), let \(L/k\) be
any field extension, and let
\(\alpha\in\mathrm{CH}^r(X)_{\mathbf Q}\) restrict to zero over \(L\).
The finite data defining a rational equivalence descend to a finitely
generated intermediate extension \(L_0/k\). Choose a transcendence
basis \(t_1,\ldots,t_d\) and put
\(F=k(t_1,\ldots,t_d)\); then \(L_0/F\) is finite.
Finite flat pullback followed by proper pushforward gives

\[
[L_0:F]\,\alpha_F=0
\quad\text{in }\mathrm{CH}^r(X_F)_{\mathbf Q}.
\]

Thus \(\alpha_F=0\). For one transcendental variable, specialization
in the constant family over \(k[t]_{(t)}\) is a left inverse to flat
pullback from \(X\) to \(X_{k(t)}\). Applying this successively to all
the variables gives \(\alpha=0\). The specialization statement can be
checked directly on cycles: close a constant integral cycle in the
constant family and intersect with its reduced special fibre. The
same construction respects rational equivalence, as in the ordinary
Chow specialization map. No separability of \(L_0/F\) is required for
the finite push–pull formula.

Consequently rational Chow restriction is injective for every field
extension. This familiar fact is also stated and used in the complete
proof of Charles Vial's Lemma 4.5, author p. 13, in the version dated
11 May 2012 of *Chow–Künneth decomposition for 3- and 4-folds fibred by
varieties with trivial Chow group of zero-cycles*:

https://www.math.uni-bielefeld.de/~vial/CK-fibrations9.pdf

We opened the primary PDF and read that entire lemma and proof; we do
not claim to have audited the paper's main theorem. The retrieval
references are turn232244841view0 and turn218936363view0.

The author's publication page, independently opened as
turn460493693view0, identifies the later publication as *Journal of
Algebraic Geometry*, 24 (2015), 51–80, and links DOI
10.1090/S1056-3911-2014-00616-0. The linked publisher endpoint returned
HTTP 403 during this check. The mathematical locator above therefore
remains that of the actual 2012 author version, not an inferred
published-page locator. This metadata was obtained during the
checkpoint bibliography freeze and is retained here for the next
permitted bibliography update.

Apply the argument to the determinant correspondence on a power of a
K3 surface. The known vanishing is in a Weil realization. It is not a
proved Chow vanishing after any field extension. A theorem about the
kernel of Chow restriction therefore supplies no missing implication.
Over rational coefficients, a supplied field extension on which this
particular correspondence vanished would already prove its original
vanishing by the argument above. This also distinguishes specialization
of a nonconstant family, whose Chow map need not be injective, from
extension of the base field of one fixed variety.

This is a failed-method clarification. It neither disproves Kimura
finite-dimensionality nor gives a smaller verified vanishing condition
for the general K3 surface.

## 3. A motivic Clemens–Schmid complex is not yet invariant-cycle exactness

A further primary-focused search concerned local invariant cycles and
Clemens–Schmid exactness in mixed characteristic. It led back to the
already catalogued Binda–Gallauer–Vezzani source, c_bgv2024monodromy.
We read the entire local proofs of Proposition 4.64 and Corollary 4.70,
together with Remarks 4.65–4.71, author pp. 44–46, in the retained
arXiv v2 text at

Proposition 4.64 supplies the monodromy-fibre and supported-localization
triangles. Their octahedron gives the two chain complexes in
Corollary 4.70. The latter assertion is a chain-complex construction;
it does not assert exactness at every term. Remark 4.71 explicitly
obtains that extra conclusion under weight–monodromy. Consequently
this route does not prove the mixed-characteristic invariant-cycle
surjectivity needed for the unresolved endpoint. We have not audited
all earlier categorical foundations in this additional check; the
previous Part D audit records the relevant equivalence and realization
scope. No new universal result or movement is claimed.
