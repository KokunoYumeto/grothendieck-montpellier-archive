# Consani supplies monodromy cycles, not a prescribed-cycle realization

Checked 19 November 2026. This is a source-scope check, not a new converse or a negative theorem about possible geometric constructions.

Caterina Consani, with an appendix by Spencer Bloch, *The local monodromy as a generalized algebraic correspondence*, arXiv:math/9801080v1 (17 January 1998), starts with an existing normal-crossing degeneration. Its cycles describe that degeneration's monodromy and powers. The introduction, pages 1–3, explains the mixed-characteristic weight–monodromy assumption; Theorem 6.2, page 33, additionally uses Frobenius semisimplicity on inertia invariants. Theorems 4.2 and 5.3, pages 17 and 28–29, give the indicated explicit constructions. These statements do not construct a pure degeneration whose monodromy realizes an arbitrarily supplied residue correspondence.

The introduction also warns that the filtered construction does not have a canonical product on its first spectral-sequence page; the appendix supplies the product needed at the second page. This is consistent with retaining the whole-product operator workaround in our sufficient motivated-motive theorem.

The complete introduction and the named theorem statements were read. No complete proof audit of these theorems is claimed; in particular Theorem 6.2 refers its proof to an earlier source. The new capture script and hash log preserve the exact consulted arXiv version.
