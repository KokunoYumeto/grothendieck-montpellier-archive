# Products preserve the requested relations; parity need not be tautological

Independent argument sent to Part C, 2026-11-19. This concerns the exact
point-and-diagonal rings of Part C, not an enlarged ring with arbitrary
Künneth projectors.

## Product stability

Let (X,o_X) and (Y,o_Y) be smooth projective connected pointed varieties.
Suppose that for every k the cycle map is injective on each of
T_k^CH(X,o_X) and T_k^CH(Y,o_Y). Put Z=X×Y and o_Z=o_X⊠o_Y.

After reordering Z^k=X^k×Y^k, every point generator of T_k^CH(Z,o_Z)
is the exterior product of the corresponding point generators on X^k
and Y^k. Every partial diagonal is likewise the exterior product of
the corresponding two partial diagonals. Consequently

T_k^CH(Z,o_Z) ⊂ image[T_k^CH(X,o_X)⊗_Q T_k^CH(Y,o_Y)
                           → CH^*(X^k×Y^k)_Q].

The composite of the displayed exterior-product map with the cycle
map is the tensor product of the two injective cycle maps, followed
by the cohomological Künneth isomorphism. It is therefore injective.
If z in the smaller product tautological ring has zero cohomology,
choose a preimage u in the displayed tensor product. The injectivity
of this composite forces u=0, hence z=0. This proves product stability.
No assertion about injectivity of exterior products on the full Chow
rings is needed.

For an elliptic curve E with its origin and for P¹ with a point, the
hypotheses were already proved in Part C, Theorem c:chow-positive:
O'Sullivan's distinguished-cycle result applies to E and the usual
polynomial presentation applies to P¹. Hence the pointed pair

             (E×P¹, [0_E]⊠[point])

satisfies every requested relation on every power.

## A parity projector is nevertheless absent from the Frobenius diagram category

Use A=H^*(E×P¹,Q)=Λ(x,y)⊗Q[z]/(z²), with degrees (1,1,2),
unit 1, point xyz, and trace ε(xyz)=1. The odd derivation
D(x)=z, D(y)=D(z)=0 preserves multiplication, unit, point, trace
and the inverse trace pairing. The complete exact arithmetic
certificate is check_c1_odd_derivation.py and its JSON output.

Every even endomorphism obtainable from these tensors by rational
linear combinations, composition, tensor products, symmetric braidings,
duality and contraction commutes with D. One way to see this without
sign shorthand is to base change to Q[θ], with θ odd and θ²=0.
The even superlinear automorphism 1+θD preserves each named tensor,
and therefore every resulting diagram. For an even endomorphism f
defined over Q, invariance says fD=Df.

The parity map Π(a)=(-1)^deg(a)a does not satisfy this condition:

                 DΠ(x)=-z,      ΠD(x)=z.

Thus Π is not a morphism in that ungraded marked Frobenius tensor
diagram category. Its two parity projectors (1±Π)/2 cannot both be
present there; in fact neither is present because either would give Π.
Taking an idempotent completion does not alter the endomorphisms of
the already present object A, so it does not repair this obstruction.

The same reasoning allows an auxiliary even Tate line: let D act
trivially on it. All Tate-twisted structure tensors remain invariant.
This point is important because the diagrams retain Tate degree
while forgetting the full cohomological grading.

## Exact consequence and limitation

All requested Chow relations do not imply that the cohomological parity
projector belongs to their Frobenius diagram category. Therefore a
mixed-parity criterion that adds the existence of such a *tautological*
projector has an invalid converse, even for the positive example above.
The example does not say that parity is nonalgebraic: E×P¹ has algebraic
Künneth projectors. It says that this additional algebraicity is not
generated by the specified marked Frobenius tensors. It does not refute
the existence of a different finite criterion in mixed parity.
