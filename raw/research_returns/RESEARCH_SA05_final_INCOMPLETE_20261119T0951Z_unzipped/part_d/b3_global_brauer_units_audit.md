# Completion of the arbitrary-base Brauer gate by unit cups

**Date:** 19 November 2026, 06:21 UTC. **Authors of the combined argument:** Parts B and D and root. **Written full theorem:** `part_d/b3_all_bases_brauer_units.tex`, copied for integration to `sections/b3_all_bases_brauer_units.tex`. Root and Part B independently read the complete three-statement fragment and passed it. This is an equivalence with the universal surface-divisor Tate conjecture, not an assertion of that conjecture.

## The precise improvement

Fix p different from ell, put R=Z_(ell), and put D=Q_ell/Z_ell. For every finite-type F_p-scheme S, write A_S=H²_et(S,G_m) tensor R and let C_S be the image of the actual cyclic cup classes of global units with the arithmetic character, with coefficients D. Under divisor Tate for smooth projective surfaces over every finite constant extension, A_S/C_S is finitely generated over R. Conversely, this universal assertion, or just finite cokernel of unit cups in Br(S)[ell-infinity] for every S, implies surface Tate by taking S smooth and projective.

The conclusion includes nonproper, singular, nonreduced and nonseparated S. In particular, it controls the formerly unresolved saturation of the relative Picard obstruction inside non-torsion H²(G_m). It also gives a direct route that avoids that obstruction: for any morphism X→S identifying global units, C_X is exactly the image of C_S, so the relative ell-Brauer cokernel is a quotient of the finite group Br(X)[ell-infinity]/C_X. Smooth proper morphisms with geometrically connected fibres identify global units, including over nonreduced bases. Thus the universal curve-family assertion has exactly the surface-Tate gate, with no remaining singular-base qualification.

### 2. The smooth unit-cup cokernel

I read the complete written `sections/b3_unit_cup_generation.tex`. For an open in a smooth projective variety, codimension-one purity identifies the weight-zero quotient of H¹(-,Q_ell(1)) with the rational unit lattice: boundary divisor classes vanish rationally in cohomology exactly when their Picard class is torsion, since points of Pic⁰ over an algebraic closure of a finite field are torsion. For an arbitrary smooth quasi-projective scheme, projective alteration, trace and norm transfer this identification. The quotient has pure weight -1 and therefore has no arithmetic invariants or coinvariants. The remaining arithmetic H¹ is generated by unit cups. Surface Tate, through the already audited Kahn/Morrow reduction, supplies the required divisor classes in geometric H². Kummer finite cotype upgrades rational surjectivity to a finite cokernel.

### 3. The unit-cup kernel on singular intersections

The new unconditional lemma uses generic fields rather than purity on the possibly singular scheme. Its unit group modulo torsion is a finite free lattice. On normal projective models of the finitely many generic fields, divisors detect this lattice injectively, since a function with zero divisor is a constant in a finite field. Finitely many prime divisors detect all generators. Residues of unit cyclic cups give that divisor matrix multiplied rowwise by finite constant-field degrees, and by a fixed cup-order sign. Its column rank is unchanged, so Smith normal form gives finite kernel after tensoring with D. Nonreduced units are killed by a bounded power of p, and disappear here.

The source for the residue statement is the Colliot-Thélène–Skorobogatov author draft dated 31 October 2019, §1.4.1, pp. 33–37, especially the statement of Proposition 1.4.5 and its following cup-residue formula on p. 37. I opened that statement/formula, and root and Part B independently did so. ## The finite-error argument checked independently

Let K be the finitely generated kernel A_S→A_T, and let U_0,U_1,U_2 be the torsion-free unit lattices on S,T,T_1. Perfected multiplicative-sheaf descent gives rational exactness of U_0→U_1→U_2 at U_1. Smith normal form therefore shows that the middle homology after tensoring with D is finite. Choose an integer L annihilating it. Choose N annihilating the finite smooth unit-cup cokernel, and M annihilating the finite kernel of the unit-cup map on T_1.

For a in A_S, choose u in U_1 tensor D with Nq*a=c_T(u). Equality of the two restrictions of q*a implies c_(T_1)(partial u)=0. Hence M partial u=0, and LM u is the restriction of some v in U_0 tensor D. Thus h a-c_S(v) lies in K for the fixed integer h=LMN, independent of a.

The induced map A_S/C_S→A_T/q*C_S has kernel (K+C_S)/C_S, a finitely generated quotient of K. Its image is killed by h. The target is a quotient of the finite-cotype ell-primary group A_T, hence is also of finite cotype; its h-torsion subgroup is finite. Therefore A_S/C_S is finitely generated. Since C_S consists of actual torsion classes, its quotient's torsion is exactly Br(S)[ell-infinity]/C_S, which is finite. This last assertion does not require a false general principle that quotienting by a finitely generated subgroup preserves torsion saturation; the divisible unit-cup subgroup and the fixed finite error supply the needed structure.
