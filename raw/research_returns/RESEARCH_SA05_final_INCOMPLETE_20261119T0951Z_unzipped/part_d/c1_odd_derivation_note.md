# The mixed-parity scope of the ordinary invariant-theory argument

For any complex elliptic curve E, the cohomology algebra of E × P¹ is

\[
A=\Lambda_{\mathbf Q}(x,y)\otimes\mathbf Q[z]/(z^2),
\qquad |x|=|y|=1,\quad |z|=2.
\]

Take the unit 1, the point class o=xyz, and the trace ε satisfying
ε(xyz)=1 and ε=0 on the other seven monomials. The pairing ε(ab) is
nondegenerate. Let D be the odd derivation specified by

\[
D(x)=z,\qquad D(y)=D(z)=0.
\]

This is well defined: it preserves x²=y²=0, xy+yx=0, z²=0 and the
commutation relations with z. On the ordered basis
(1,x,y,xy,z,xz,yz,xyz) its only nonzero images are D(x)=z and D(xy)=yz.
Thus D²=0, D(1)=D(o)=0 and εD=0. The super-Leibniz rule gives

\[
\varepsilon(D(a)b)+(-1)^{|a|}\varepsilon(aD(b))=0.
\]

Consequently D is a nonzero odd element in the Lie superalgebra of
automorphisms of this marked Frobenius superalgebra. Equivalently, after
adjoining an odd square-zero parameter θ, the even transformation 1+θD
preserves the product, unit, point and trace. The diagonal/co-evaluation
tensor is preserved because it is the inverse of the trace pairing.

This example does **not** refute the existence of a finite tensor-identity
criterion for varieties of mixed cohomological parity. It does show that
one cannot silently replace the supergroup by its ordinary even subgroup
in the ordinary GL invariant-theory proof: the structure tensors need not
remove odd stabilizer directions. In particular, the exactness of
invariants for an ordinary reductive GL is not a theorem about GL(m|n).

The derivation has cohomological degree +1. It therefore ceases to be a
symmetry if the full cohomological grading is separately marked. Adding
that grading to a Chow tautological category requires additional algebraic
projectors or a proof that the corresponding grading maps are already
generated by the allowed tensors. The original collection of partial
diagonals, cup products, point, unit and trace does not itself mark those
projectors.

`check_c1_odd_derivation.py` checks the 64 super-Leibniz identities, all
64 pairing identities, all 512 associativity identities, unit and point
preservation, trace annihilation, parity, degree and D²=0 with integer
arithmetic. Its complete output is `check_c1_odd_derivation_output.json`.
The input is the explicitly displayed eight-dimensional algebra; the
certificate has precisely the scope of the preceding paragraphs.
