# Final independent audit of the B1 and B2 gates

Audit date: 19 November 2026. Auditor: Part D agent, independently of Part B and root. Both complete written fragments were read. Result: both pass within their explicitly stated scope.

## B1: diagonal Chow gate

The label `prop:B-diagonal-Chow-gate` correctly retains the universal Tate and eigenvalue-one semisimplicity hypotheses from the preceding phantom gate. Balkan–Schreieder, Corollary 1.3, Theorem 7.1(2), Remark 7.2 and the complete proof in section 7.4 were independently read in the archived original. The high-codimension reduction comes from affine cohomological dimension on cofinal affine opens of projective space; no Chow hard Lefschetz principle is used.

For the remaining range, put t=d−2i+1 and m=d−i+1. Then t≥0, d+t=2m−1, and i+t=m. Pullback followed by multiplication by the top projective-space class maps CH^i(X) to CH^m(X×P^t), preserves vanishing of the cycle class, and has left inverse given by projection pushforward. The diagonal conditions therefore detect every remaining ordinary Chow relation. This does not reduce all cases to codimension two on threefolds: for example the surface-square case (d,i)=(4,2) goes to (5,3).

The proposed extra surface equivalence also passes. Balkan–Schreieder Theorem 7.1(1), Remark 7.2 and Proposition 4.6 identify the surface Tate conjecture with the single vanishing H^3(F_0 P^3 over Fpbar,Qell(2))^GammaFp=0. Their section 2.1 defines a variety as an integral algebraic scheme, without geometric integrality; Notation 4.8 quantifies over every smooth projective variety over the fixed field. An integral smooth projective surface over a finite extension of Fp remains integral, smooth and projective over Fp. Its geometric cohomology is induced from the constant-field Galois group, so the fixed subspace is the fixed subspace over that constant field. Chow groups are intrinsic to the same scheme. Thus the one prime-field invariant group covers precisely all finite constant-field extensions. This records an equivalence, not an established vanishing.

## B2: conjectural comparison package and its specializations

The labels `def:B-corrected-comparison-package` and `prop:B-comparison-specializations` correctly separate a proposed enhanced global arithmetic category and functorial comparison from the proved checks of its required specializations. The package retains local Bloch–Kato f conditions at the auxiliary prime, the g condition for removed primes, the full local change complex, real modified cohomology, and dual completion. Merely specifying cohomology groups or chosen split complexes does not claim construction of a globally functorial arithmetic completion.

The finite-field specialization uses the Weil cone with its stated Geisser hypotheses and the multiplication-by-extension-degree normalization of the degree-one class. The number-ring Tate check is a check of the stated graded spaces and dual-completed complexes; it neither assumes a Leopoldt/Schneider vanishing nor proves the additional global category and gluing requirements. Localization in twists zero and one is compatible with these distinctions.

The label `prop:B-Scholbach-finite-support` gives an actual counterexample to identifying Scholbach's ordinary motivic Hom theory with the full requested Weil-absolute theory. For i:Spec(Fp)→Spec(Z), adjunction and the K-theory normalization give Hom(1,i_*1[1])=K_{−1}(Fp)_Q=0. For ell≠p, continuous H^1(Fp,Qell)=Hom_cont(Zhat,Qell)=Qell. Definitions 1.3 and 1.7, Axioms 1.1 and 1.8 and Remark 6.2 were now independently read in the archived original; this is a later source check than the preceding mathematical-only audit. The conditional generic f-cohomology comparison and its omitted crystalline cases remain separate.
