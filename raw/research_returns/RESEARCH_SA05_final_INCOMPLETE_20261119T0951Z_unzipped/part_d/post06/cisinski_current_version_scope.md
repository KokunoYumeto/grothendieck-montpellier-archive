# Cisinski preprint: current-version check and failed shortcut

Checked 19 November 2026. The primary identifier arXiv:2503.24033 now refers to Denis-Charles Cisinski, *Completion of motivic sheaves*, version 5 dated 23 April 2025. It is not an Ayoub preprint. The primary submission history records withdrawal of version 2 on 2 April 2025; the replacement explains that the earlier comparison strategy does not work.

The current introduction concerns compatibility of motivic completion with the six operations in equal characteristic and the failure of the analogous strong comparison in mixed characteristic. Those assertions are different from coefficient-prime independence of the unramifiedness flag for a prescribed pure number-field motive. We do not infer a counterexample to C6 from its Theorem 3.1 and do not use the obsolete independence claim as a theorem.
