# Independent written audit: target-only positivity refinement

19 November 2026, Part D. I read every line of `part_c/c4_target_positivity_note.md`, then checked the requested clarification of the pairing. The complete proof passes with that clarification. This is a sufficient standard-conjecture package, not a proof of the general threefold pairing condition or a universal-gate reduction.

The right-ideal argument is correct. For f:T→S and A=End(S), the set f Hom(S,T) is a right ideal of A. Semisimplicity gives eA for an idempotent e, and e=fg0 gives one image inclusion. Mixed trace nondegeneracy applied to (1−e)f gives the other. Source-side endomorphism semisimplicity is unnecessary.

The required primitive intersection is a kernel of (1−p)e inside the finite semisimple Karoubi category generated by S. The realization preserves its split exact sequences. This correctly produces an algebraic projector onto the intersection, instead of replacing the intersection by the primitive projection of the image.

On a surface, the positive-adjoint-trace argument must use the Lefschetz-modified polarization on full H2, not the raw intersection pairing. The latter is already indefinite on H2(P1×P1), where its adjoint trace on the full endomorphism algebra is not positive. Part C added the explicit modified form and the fact that it is minus raw intersection on primitive H2 under the chosen normalization. Their restrictions therefore have equivalent nondegeneracy. The radical is an algebraic subobject; a projector q onto a nonzero radical would satisfy q-adjoint times q=0 and contradict positive trace.

The mixed D(X×S) hypothesis has the right codimensions: a map H2 of a threefold to H2 of a surface is represented by codimension three, and its reverse by codimension two. Projecting a complementary cycle onto the relevant homogeneous block preserves the trace intersection. Thus D gives the precise mixed nondegeneracy needed. Target I(S×S), together with the known surface Lefschetz standard conjecture and its product consequences, supplies the finite-dimensional semisimple target algebra and its positive involution; those are not inferred from finite Q_l dimension alone.

If an algebraic inverse to L_X:H2(X)→H4(X)(1) is supplied, an ample surface j:T→X of class mL gives r=m^−1 L_X^−1 j_* with rj*=1. Hence rho r has exactly image rho, and only the mixed D(T×S) hypothesis remains on the source-target product. This does not remove target I(S×S). Individual-surface Hodge index or D for divisors does not establish either missing product statement.
