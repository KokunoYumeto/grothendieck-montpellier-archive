## New arguments and actual authorship

The weighted trivial-factor inequality was derived by this agent. For each actual central-product constituent pattern `J`, LST gives trace at most `gamma^(m-|J|)` times its dimension. The convex chord inequality bounds this by `gamma^m+(|J|/m)(1-gamma^m)`. The exact coefficient sum is the sum of actual fixed-space dimensions. This eliminates the wasteful factor `m` from a union bound while preserving all central compatibility and multiplicity information.

The central-quotient column estimate is proved explicitly: for central `Z` in finite `B`, every irreducible character satisfies `|eta(b)|^2<=|C_(B/Z)(bZ)|`. Central-character Fourier projection of column orthogonality gives a nonnegative sum. The relevant scalar-conjugacy set is a subgroup, and the centralizer index identity gives exactly the quotient centralizer. No faithful-character assumption is made. This covers genuine Spin characters in the large-block argument.
