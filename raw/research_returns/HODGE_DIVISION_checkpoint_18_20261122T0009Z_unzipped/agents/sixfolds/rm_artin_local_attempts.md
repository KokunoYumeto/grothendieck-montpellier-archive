# Artin-local construction attempts and exact limitations

Author: GPT-6 Astra Pro; inherited effort not exposed; /root/sixfolds. Date: 2026-11-21.

These are retained working deductions and failed constructions, not additions to the independently checked theorem sequence. In particular, no balanced semiregular seed is constructed here.

## A. One bivector really permits a reduced zero-net modification

Let R=C[[z1,z2,z3,z4]], and choose any nonzero constant bivector pi. On R[[h]] use the full formal Moyal product exp((h/2)pi). This product is well defined h-adically. Associativity follows by comparing three factors: the constant-coefficient operators on factor pairs commute, and either parenthesization applies the same exponential of the sum of the three pair operators. This is a direct argument for this one constant product, not an imported general quantization theorem.

Let W be this algebra and N the inverse image of the maximal ideal of R under reduction W→R. Equivalently N is the augmentation ideal generated by z1,...,z4,h. It is h-torsion-free as a submodule of W, hence flat over the DVR C[[h]]. The quotient M=N/hN has the exact sequence

0→k→M→m→0.

The kernel is hW/hN≅W/N=k; the cokernel of M→R is also k. In M, if ei is the class of zi and t is the class of h, then

zi ej−zj ei=pi_ij t, and zi t=0.

Thus its reduced extension tensor is precisely pi. The module N/h²N is a flat first-order deformation of M for the same bivector, including when pi is nondegenerate. The two independent bivectors in SF-E819R are genuinely needed: a single nonzero bivector cannot exclude every equal-net reduced modification. This is a local sharpness example, not a balanced global object.

## B. A further necessary equation in the common Artinian complete-intersection model

In the notation of the E820 Koszul calculation, now allow any four-generator Artinian complete intersection A=R/(f1,...,f4), with T=Q=A^m. Besides the reviewed equation C_pi B_ij=P_pi,ij I, lifting the annihilator relations suggests and directly gives the companion equation

B_ij C_pi=P_pi,ij I.

Here is the calculation. The lifted free-hull map exists by E819 because these are finite-length defects in dimension four. For a basis element t of T, write f_i t_lift=epsilon m_i. The hull map gives phi(m_i)=f_i c(t), where c(t) is a chosen lift of C_pi(t). Hence m_i differs from e_i(c(t)) by an element of T. Taking the commutator of f_i and f_j on t_lift and using I T=0 yields B_ij C_pi(t)=P_pi,ij t. This keeps the same left-module convention as E820.

The two companion equations would imply every coefficient minor

P+_ij P-_kl−P-_ij P+_kl=0 in A,

because C+ B_ij C- B_kl can be evaluated in the two associative orders. These are additional necessary conditions, not sufficient deformation equations. This calculation has not yet received a separate whole-proof audit and is not used as a canonical premise.

## C. A tempting matrix solution fails the companion equation

Work in A=C[x,y,z,w]/(x²,y²,z²,w²), with coordinate-plane bivectors and the square-generator ideal. Put

C+=[[x,0],[y,0]], C-=[[0,z],[0,w]],
B12=4[[y,x],[0,0]], B34=4[[0,0],[w,z]],

and all other B entries zero. The left equations give C+ B12=4xy I, C- B34=4zw I, and both cross products zero. This initially looked like a way to handle two bivectors using a nonreduced module.

It fails: B12 C+=8xy diag(1,0), which is not 4xy I. Thus this candidate does not even pass the full necessary relation test; it is not an actual quantized module or a seed. The failure is retained rather than silently discarding the extra relation.

## D. Pure exterior algebra does not settle the whole nonreduced gate

Over the same Artinian algebra, consider the matrix

J=[[x,0,z,0],[0,y,0,w],[y,0,w,0],[0,x,0,z]].

For the two coordinate-plane bivectors, their transformed two-forms are xy(e12+e34) and zw(e12+e34). All coefficient minors of these two forms vanish, but their mixed wedge is 2xyzw, which is nonzero, and det J=2xyzw. Therefore one cannot deduce a zero mixed wedge merely from the vanishing coefficient minors over a nonreduced ring.

This matrix is not the Jacobian of four functions: for example, the third row has incompatible mixed partial derivatives. It does not provide a counterexample in the actual complete-intersection deformation problem. It shows exactly why a proposed proof based only on exterior identities and coefficient minors would be incomplete; integrability and the actual module equations still matter.

## E. Current remaining local cases

E819 and E820 exclude nonzero net finite defects, reduced equal-net defects, and modules free over a common curvilinear quotient. The general noncurvilinear Artinian case is still open in the present arguments. The local sharpness example in A and the failed constructions in C/D do not change that status.

The surface-supported construction was investigated separately. Its actual gluable cones and local coisotropy obstruction are now written in E821/E821a, while coupled smooth-surface modifications are the subject of the separate E822 draft. None of these surface statements is inferred from the finite Artinian experiments above.
