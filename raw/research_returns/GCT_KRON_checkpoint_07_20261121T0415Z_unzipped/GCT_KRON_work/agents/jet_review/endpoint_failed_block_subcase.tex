\section{A failed block-gluing subcase for QA033}

Let the binary part of an \(m\)-by-\(m\) pencil be
block diagonal with block sizes \(b_1,\ldots,b_k\).
Suppose that every baseline transverse entry inside
each diagonal block is zero; transverse labels occur
only between distinct blocks. If every \(b_i\geq2\),
this subcase cannot have full transverse-order-two
differential in \(2m+1\) or more variables.

To prove this, let \(R\) be the transverse dimension.
For a variation supported inside block \(i\) and
linear in the transverse variables, its binary
cofactor is the block cofactor times the permanents
of the other binary blocks.
The binary block cofactor space has dimension at
most \(b_i\), since its forms have degree \(b_i-1\).
Among the \(b_i^2\) entry directions there is thus
a kernel of dimension at least \(b_i^2-b_i\).

Such a within-block variation has no quadratic
transverse term. The suffix-linear part of its
cofactor would require a single entry between
different blocks to complete a permutation of the
remaining balanced block row and column sets,
which is impossible. There are no baseline
within-block transverse entries by assumption.
Thus the binary-cofactor kernel, multiplied by
any transverse variable, is in the kernel of the
entire order-two differential. These independent
source subspaces give kernel dimension at least
\[
                    R\sum_i(b_i^2-b_i)\geq Rm,
\]
because \(b_i^2-b_i\geq b_i\) for \(b_i\geq2\).

At \(R=2m-1\), the source dimension is
\((R+2)m^2\), while the target dimension is
\[
                  (m+1)+mR+(m-1)\binom{R+1}{2}.
\]
Subtracting \(Rm\) from the source dimension gives
exactly one less than this target dimension.
The differential is therefore deficient.
If the projection were full at any larger \(R\),
restricting the extra variables to zero would
give a full differential at \(R=2m-1\), which
has just been ruled out. Equivalently the direct
dimension deficit increases for larger \(R\).

This rules out a particular attempted simplification
of block induction. It does not rule out binary
block bases with genuine internal transverse jets;
the successful finite block experiments use those
internal labels. It also does not imply any rank
defect at a generic unrestricted pencil.
All statements above concern actual coefficient
maps and their source kernels. Their coefficient
grading is the comodule construction of SGA~3,
I, Propositions~4.7.2--4.7.3, pp.~34--36.
