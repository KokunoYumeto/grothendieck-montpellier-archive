## Supported-module integrality

Koszul homology on generators of degrees not divisible by a root order
is annihilated by their ideal and is supported in the intersection of
the module support with the grading fixed locus. If this intersection
is supported at the origin, the homology has finite length. Its alternating
Hilbert identity excludes an actual pole at that order. This handles
nonreduced support and integral virtual classes, with a common grading
shift if necessary.

An integral series with root-of-unity poles has an integral numerator
after its reduced cyclotomic denominator is normalized to constant term
one. In characteristic three only the factors indexed by M times a
power of three can reduce to zero at the reduction of xi. Excluding the
higher factors makes every other denominator a local unit, including
(1-t^M)/Phi_M. Expansion in t-xi is integral. Changing to u=1-t^M
preserves integrality because the linear coefficient is a unit above
three. Thus every Laurent coefficient, not only the leading one, is
in the stated local ring.
