\begin{abstract}
For triple-rectangular Kronecker coefficients, numerical PH3 is false:
for every $n\ge2$ the Hilbert series has an actual pole at an order
$2^{n-1}3^r$ for some $r\ge0$. Every denominator product of factors
$1-t^a$ therefore contains an exponent $a\ge2^{n-1}$, against the
unified parameter $\operatorname{rank}(x)=n^2+5n$ (and also the introductory $h=n^4+O(n)$). The proof combines the cyclic tensor
trace with a weighted hook-module quotient and a modular pole-transfer
lemma. The exact dyadic order and the strict positivity and saturation
indices remain open in variable dimension. The complete dyadic
fixed-locus calculations in dimensions four and five give thirteen
and 424 curve components, respectively. Their exact sums are nonzero,
proving primitive-eighth- and primitive-sixteenth-root poles.
A general incidence-lattice bound makes all stabilizer-lift searches
finite. The proposed sharp character-order bound is refuted by a
polystable family of order $2^{n-1+\lfloor(n-2)/7\rfloor}$;
in a subfamily the full stabilizers and smooth fixed curves are proved.
The full inherited cubic-plethysm refutation is reproduced with its
source and proof audits; new estimates give the sufficient threshold
79,000 and exact small-rank witnesses. Strict saturation exceptions
are exponentially sparse in exact binary shells for each fixed proper
inner embedding. The full integral rank-four nonstandard Hecke order
is not locally free at $(2,v-1)$: its residue dimension is at least126,
whereas its generic rank is114, excluding every integral basis.
A split rational-$D$ model and all rank-four GT path lines are explicit.
The actual polynomial order has a certified114-element basis,
and an explicitly repaired bar-fixed minimization has a finite cutoff.
Its tested least-degree repairs fail, while a different maximal-scaling
rule gives a balanced triple and a complete114-element basis with all
12,996 products. An explicit coefficient refutes signed positivity
for this new basis.
For outer degree six, the scalar/two-row sector has a positive
formula using at most45 polytopes in11 variables; arbitrary flags
remain open.
The critical canonical-map annihilators for $r=3,4$ vanish by known
full rank laws. All remaining gates and the precise scopes of these
results are recorded separately. No consequence deciding $P$ versus
$NP$ is asserted.
\end{abstract}

\section{Conventions and exact scope}
GCT VI means Mulmuley's \emph{Geometric complexity theory VI: the flip
via positivity}, arXiv:0704.0229v4. The positivity and saturation indices
have the constituentwise meanings of Definitions 1.2.2 and 1.2.4,
printed pp.~13--14. Saturation tests every nonzero constituent at every
positive integer after shifting, not only on its own congruence class.

a saturation-index bound. SH, PH2 and PH3 mean Hypotheses 1.6.5,
1.6.6 and 1.6.7.

\section{R-NEW-001: a finite outer-six reference table}
For every $\mu\vdash6$ and every $\lambda\vdash6n$, $0\le n\le3$,
\texttt{certificates/outer\_six\_n\_le\_3.tsv} records two exact
computations of the multiplicity. All 5,214 entries agree, including
the eleven degree-zero entries. This is a finite computation receipt,
not the positive all-$n$ evaluator requested in Question 5.

The first calculation expands the power-sum plethysm
\[
 s_\mu[h_n]=\sum_{\rho\vdash6}\frac{\chi^\mu(\rho)}{z_\rho}
 \prod_{r\in\rho}\left(\sum_{\tau\vdash n}\frac{p_{r\tau}}{z_\tau}\right)
\]
and uses Murnaghan--Nakayama characters to extract Schur coefficients.
The second expands weights of $\operatorname{Sym}^n\mathbb C^6$,
applies the outer character, and extracts highest weights with the
six-variable Weyl alternant. Labels of length greater than six vanish
by successive one-row Pieri inclusions in six tensor factors. The outer
character table satisfies its exact orthogonality relations. Both
methods and every entry are reproducible with
\texttt{scripts/outer\_six\_finite\_check.py}. These are standard
character identities, not new positive formulas.

\section{Main asymptotic question}
Numerical PH3 is refuted by K-023, R-NEW-004, K-024 and PNEW-013:
the actual tensor Hilbert series has a pole at order $2^{n-1}3^r$
for some $r\ge0$ in every dimension $n\ge2$. The remaining exact-order
question asks whether one can take $r=0$. A full classification of
the tensor fixed locus is still one possible route, and K-009 makes
that search finite. The proposed sharp $2^{n-1}$ bound is false:
K-018 constructs polystable examples of determinant-character order
$2^{n-1+\lfloor(n-2)/7\rfloor}$ for every $n\ge2$.
Already at $n=9$, K-019 gives a smooth order512 curve and an
order256 fixed component of dimension four. K-021 gives growing
dimensions for further lower-order fixed loci. The dimension-five
repeated spectrum and every higher lift are resolved by K-010--K-014.
K-022 shows that exact cancellation is possible even for two smooth
fixed curves in a normal factorial Gorenstein ring with rational
singularities. The proved all-n pole theorem does not alone imply
exponential index bounds: ALPOGE\_GCT P-F33 gives a precise
counterexample to that inference for graded rings. K-026 classifies
the cyclically stable components meeting the effectively free locus:
only the chain occurs. K-027 reduces possible exact dyadic cancellation
to an integral coherent class on the nonfree locus and closures meeting
the higher colliding fixed locus. K-028 further removes finite
effective stabilizers of order prime to three. K-030 removes all finite
stabilizers, and K-031 removes every stabilizer exception: the chain is
the only cyclically stable irreducible fixed locus. Only the closures
meeting the nonzero higher colliding fixed locus remain. K-029 proves
that actual amplified components have this collision property.
K-032 identifies their normalization and an exact graded coefficient
formula when $n=2+56l$; its density, conductor and lower Laurent
terms are still unevaluated.
The exceptional class's simple Laurent coefficient must
cancel a unit modulo three. That exceptional contribution is not yet
determined. PNEW-014 shows that the positive cyclic-trace decomposition,
the dimensions, reciprocity and ambient bound alone do not transfer
large strict indices. A quantitative argument using further properties
of the actual tensor ring remains necessary for PH2 and SH(a).

\section{Other questions}
The varying-embedding narrow density clause, uniform positive bases
for varying tails, and arbitrary-output outer-six evaluator remain open.
PNEW-012 supplies the positive all-degree evaluator for the two-row
output and scalar blow-up sector, with at most45 polytopes in11 variables.
The fixed-embedding density theorem and the actual critical
annihilators are settled above with their exact scope and attribution.
R-NEW-003 gives a finite exact rational expression for
$H^{\mathrm{ten}}_4(t)$; its full expanded minimal numerator is
not computed. N-009 settles the full integral cone-basis
question negatively. The specified canonical $E(4)$ scalings and
label order remain separate from the rational split GT construction.
N-006 solves the literal fixed-$q$ stalk problem with the actual
coefficient objects and the actual $P_1,P_2$ actions. N-012 refutes
the printed external minimization prescription of GCT VIII.
N-017 and N-018 refute the two tested least-degree repairs.
N-019 supplies a different maximal-scaling construction, its balanced
triple, actual rational-order basis and full multiplication table;
the selected basis fails signed positivity. The requested canonical
positive basis and its geometric selection remain separate open questions.
N-020 computes the actual sign fibre, including its radical and split
quotient, and separates it from the smaller ambient image. N-021 proves
an explicit finite exponent reduction for rank-three integral Laurent
dimension tables. It does not compute a global minimum or choose a
canonical categorical realization.
