# Implications of the checked findings

## Geometric complexity theory

**Numerical PH3 fails for the triple-rectangular Kronecker family.** K-023, R-NEW-004, K-024 and PNEW-013 prove that, for every n≥2, the actual Hilbert series has a pole at an order2^(n−1)·3^r for some r≥0. Every representation with a denominator that is a product of factors1−t^a consequently needs an exponent a≥2^(n−1), which exceeds every polynomial in the unified parameter rank(x)=n²+5n (and also the introductory h=n⁴+O(n)). This refutes the existence clause, and hence also the canonical clause, of GCTVI Hypothesis1.6.7 as fixed in the prompt.

The proof uses the cyclic permutation of the three tensor factors. Its trace series is the invariant Hilbert series of the weighted representation W⊕S_(2,1)W, and is congruent to the tensor Hilbert series modulo3. The auxiliary quotient has one smooth maximal dyadic fixed curve, a normalized residue which is a unit at3, and no higher colliding poles of orders2^(n−1)·3^r. The exact cyclotomic transfer therefore forces an actual tensor pole of one of those orders. The trace identity is a specialization of the known higher Lie-character identity, credited to Scharf through the precise modern statement; the hook geometry and modular application are proved here.

The exact primitive2^(n−1) pole remains open in variable dimension, as do polynomial versus superpolynomial bounds on the strict positivity and saturation indices. The modular argument includes an explicit nonnegative rational counterexample to inferring the exact order from congruence alone. It gives no basis for transferring the plethysm index lower bounds to tensors without an additional quantitative argument.

K-026–K-031 give a more precise exact-order reduction. The chain is the only cyclically stable irreducible fixed locus, with no stabilizer restriction. Integral coherent localization groups the remaining nonexceptional strata in cyclic triples. Their full Laurent principal parts are integral at3 and occur with multiplicity3. Therefore any cancellation must come from an exceptional class supported only on component closures meeting the nonzero3M-fixed locus. K-029 proves that actual amplified components have this collision property. The exceptional class's simple coefficient must cancel the chain's unit modulo3. Its value remains undetermined.

PNEW-014 shows why the positive decomposition k=b+3c, c≥0, does not alone settle strict indices: formal rational characters can satisfy that decomposition, the actual dimensions and reciprocal exponents, initial vanishing range and tensor ambient bound, yet have exponential auxiliary strict indices and polynomial tensor-model indices. These are degreewise representations, not constructed invariant algebras, and the auxiliary model is not the actual B_n. A further property of the actual ring is required.

The checked dimension-four Kronecker result establishes a primitive-eighth-root pole after summing all thirteen fixed curves. The sum is (14−10√2)/256. Four spectral types contribute with multiplicities 3, 1, 3 and 6; one has the opposite phase. This closes the finite dimension-four cancellation gate and explains why the two inherited components could not settle the pole.

Thus order2^(n−1) is not a maximal two-primary order, and the requested classification cannot be an equality classification for that proposed bound.

## Normalization and the finite dimension-table search

K-032 models an actual collision family by a finite normalization, the invariant quotient of a framed tensor representation of three smaller block groups. Its coefficient formula is an exact double sum of ordinary Kronecker coefficients. The normalization preserves the highest Hilbert density, but its conductor and the moving coherent class can change lower Laurent terms. Thus this model does not complete the exact-order pole calculation; no three-adic integrality of its density is inferred from normality.

N-021 makes the unrestricted rank-three Laurent dimension-table minimum a finite decision problem with a fully explicit exponent bound. Its proof compresses monomial matchings of associativity identities by integer linear algebra. It preserves the cancelling-pair score, unit and stated symmetries. The general bound has14568 binary digits; it is a decidability and witness statement, not a practical optimizer. The separate symmetric-unimodal bound is9327. The minimum, its uniqueness, and a natural Hecke-category selection remain distinct unresolved questions. The theorem concerns integral trace tables, so it does not silently turn the rational N-019 table into an admissible dimension-ring problem.
