\section{K-019: a smooth order512 curve and an order256 component of dimension four}

\begin{proposition}[K-019]
The dimension-nine tensor constructed in K-018 has full
$GL_9^3$ stabilizer $S\times\mu_{512}$, with faithful determinant
character on the finite factor.  Its effective $SL_9^3$ stabilizer
is trivial.  In the smooth quotient near its image, the fixed
scheme of the grading subgroup $\mu_{512}$ has dimension one,
whereas the fixed scheme of $\mu_{256}$ has dimension four.
In particular the $\mu_{2^{n-1}}$ fixed locus is not always a
union of the one-dimensional components seen at $n=4,5$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Use the support and positive masses in
\texttt{n9\_period512\_counterexample.json}; they are obtained from
the explicit K-018 gadget at $r=1/2$.  The retained independent
integer/Fraction verification gives25 free incidence columns,
minor determinant512, and positive margin-one masses with
denominator512.  K-018 proves polystability and the full
diagonal stabilizer $S\times\mu_{512}$.

One diagonal generator has the following factor exponent lists,
modulo512:
\[
\begin{aligned}
A&=(36,79,228,86,132,100,74,98,486),\\
B&=(414,457,438,450,286,412,158,416,0),\\
C&=(488,19,26,476,510,500,380,126,0).
\end{aligned}
\]
Its determinant exponent is219, hence primitive.  All three
spectra are simple.  The first and third contain no antipodal
pair; the second contains precisely the pair of indices0 and6.
The complete zero-weight support consists of the25 selected edges.

To identify the identity component of the full stabilizer, use
the coefficient-one tensor on this support.  It is diagonally
equivalent to the critical tensor: the incidence columns are
independent, so the diagonal torus maps surjectively to the torus
of its nonzero coefficients.  Its infinitesimal action matrix
has729 rows and243 columns.  The retained integer row reduction
modulo10007 gives rank241, with all pivots recorded.  This is a
certificate for a nonzero241-by241 minor over $\mathbb C$.
The unavoidable scalar kernel has dimension two, so the rank
cannot exceed241.  Therefore the full stabilizer has identity
component exactly $S$.

The centralizer of the displayed generator in the full stabilizer
is diagonal, by its simple spectra, and hence equals
$S\times\mu_{512}$.  Apply K-012, with odd dimension9 and an
antipodal-free first factor.  This proves the asserted full
stabilizer.  Its intersection with $SL_9^3$ lies in $S$, since
the determinant character is faithful on the finite quotient.
That intersection is the usual ineffective scalar subgroup
$\mu_9^2$.  The effective quotient is consequently smooth at
the tensor image, with tangent representation
\[
                    V/(\mathfrak{sl}_9^3\cdot T).
\]

For the generator $g$ itself the fixed-space dimensions are
$\dim V^g=25$ and
$\dim(\mathfrak{sl}_9^3)^g=3(9-1)=24$, so the fixed tangent
dimension is one.  For its square $g^2$, reduce the displayed
weights modulo256.  There are30 zero-weight tensor coordinates.
The first and third spectra remain simple, while the second has
one eigenvalue of multiplicity two and seven simple eigenvalues.
Thus
\[
\dim(\mathfrak{sl}_9^3)^{g^2}
  =(9-1)+(2^2+7-1)+(9-1)=26.
\]
The fixed tangent dimension is $30-26=4$.  These two complete
weight counts are checked by the retained integer verifier.
A finite-order automorphism of a smooth complex variety has a
smooth fixed scheme, so the corresponding local fixed dimensions
are one and four.  As $\chi(g)=\zeta_{512}^{219}$ and219 is odd,
these are precisely the grading subgroups asserted in the statement.
\end{proof}

The order512 fixed curve is the closure of a single nonzero
grading orbit.  Indeed the fixed tensor space has exactly the25
independent support coordinates; semistability forces all of them
to be nonzero by uniqueness and strict positivity of the margin
solution.  The diagonal centralizer is transitive on those
nonzero coefficient choices.  The curve has graded density one:
its degree semigroup has group of differences $512\mathbb Z$,
because the stabilizer of its dense grading orbit is $\mu_{512}$,
and after division by512 that finitely generated semigroup is
cofinite.

For completeness its exact local moving-normal factor, at the
grading root $\xi=\zeta_{512}^{219}$, is
\[
C_{Y}(\xi)=
\frac{\displaystyle\prod_{J\in\{A,B,C\}}
             \prod_{\substack{a,b\in J\\a\ne b}}
                         (1-\zeta_{512}^{\,a-b})}
     {\displaystyle\prod_{\substack{a\in A,\ b\in B,\ c\in C\\
                                    a+b+c\not\equiv0\ (512)}}
                         (1-\zeta_{512}^{\,a+b+c})}.
                                                               \tag{K-019a}
\]
Here pullback is by the point action, exactly as in K-014 and
P-F23: if the differential on a tangent coordinate has eigenvalue
$\lambda$, its pullback on the corresponding linear coordinate
function also has eigenvalue $\lambda$.  Thus the tensor weights
are $\zeta_{512}^{a+b+c}$, the infinitesimal orbit weights are
$\zeta_{512}^{a-b}$, and the induced grading element is
$\xi=\chi(g)=\zeta_{512}^{219}$; no inverse grading convention is
being used.
Every factor in this finite expression is nonzero.  This is the
contribution of this curve in the local formula P-F23.  Other
components at order512, or higher-dimensional components there,
have not been classified here; no global pole or noncancellation
claim follows from (K-019a).
