\section{K-020: full stabilizers throughout an amplified family}

The variant below selects output edge755 of the same K-018
gadget.  This changes the selected-mass recurrence, while leaving
the dimension and determinant-period formulas unchanged.

\begin{theorem}[K-020]
For every $k\ge1$, in dimension $n=2+7k$ there is a critical
tensor with full $GL_n^3$ stabilizer
\[
                  S\times\mu_{\,2\cdot256^k},
\]
whose determinant character is faithful on the finite factor.
Its effective $SL_n^3$ stabilizer is trivial.  Its quotient image
lies on a smooth, density-one fixed curve of the grading subgroup
of order $2\cdot256^k$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Use the K-018 gadget and its boundary401, but after each gluing
select its output edge755.  Its mass is
\[
        F_+(r)=\frac{125+43r}{256},\qquad
        F_+(I)=(47/88,36/61)\subset I.
                                                               \tag{K-020a}
\]
The numerator remains odd at each stage.  All the incidence,
positivity, polystability and exact diagonal-period conclusions
of K-018 therefore apply verbatim.

We first establish the identity component of the full stabilizer.
The following general infinitesimal gluing observation is useful.
Suppose that an old coefficient-one tensor on a free support
$\Sigma$ has Lie stabilizer $S$, and a selected edge $e=(i,j,k)$
has pair-deletion coverage: every coordinate in every mode occurs
in an edge whose other two coordinates avoid the corresponding
coordinates of $e$.  Suppose that a coefficient-one gadget has
Lie stabilizer $S$, its distinguished boundary has pair-deletion
coverage, and no gadget edge contains two boundary coordinates.
Then the glued coefficient-one tensor also has Lie stabilizer $S$.

Here is a complete proof of that observation.  In each mode write
the vector space as $O\oplus B\oplus N$, where $O$ contains the
old coordinates other than the selected coordinate, $B$ is its
one-dimensional span, and $N$ consists of the newly added
coordinates.  An output coefficient with one coordinate in $N$
and the other two in $O$ can arise only by replacing one
coordinate of an old edge.  Freeness makes its source coordinate
unique; old pair-deletion coverage therefore forces every Lie
matrix entry from $O\oplus B$ to $N$ to vanish.  Interchanging
the roles of old tensor and gadget, output coefficients with
one coordinate in $O$ and the other two in $N$ force every entry
from $N\oplus B$ to $O$ to vanish.  Thus all three boundary lines
are preserved, and the matrices restrict to both the old and
the gadget coordinate spaces.

The two tensor variations lie respectively in
$(O_A\oplus B_A)\otimes(O_B\oplus B_B)\otimes(O_C\oplus B_C)$
and in the analogous space with $N$ in place of $O$.  These
spaces meet only in $B_A\otimes B_B\otimes B_C$.  Neither variation
has a coefficient there: the old selected edge was removed,
freeness excludes any other old edge with two boundary coordinates,
and this property was assumed for the gadget.  Each tensor
variation must consequently vanish separately.  The gadget Lie
stabilizer consists of scalars with sum zero, so the three
boundary scalars have sum zero.  Restoring the removed old edge
then shows that the old restriction stabilizes the original old
tensor.  Its Lie stabilizer is also $S$.  The two scalar
restrictions agree on each boundary line, yielding precisely
the global scalar two-dimensional Lie algebra.

For the present gadget, the complete finite checks are as follows.
Its coefficient-one infinitesimal action matrix has rank
$190=3\cdot8^2-2$ modulo10007.  Its boundary401 has pair-deletion
coverage and no edge contains two boundary coordinates.  Its
output755 also has pair-deletion coverage.  These checks are
retained with all modular pivots and are independent of the
search program.  The first glued tensor is the dimension-nine
tensor of K-019, whose Lie stabilizer is $S$.

Coverage for the selected output persists.  All three coordinates
of755 are new, relative to the gadget boundary401.  Every old
coordinate still occurs in an old edge after removal of the
selected edge, since its removed mass is less than one and its
margin was one.  Those remaining old edges avoid the new output
coordinates.  The new gadget coordinates satisfy coverage by the
finite gadget check.  The infinitesimal observation therefore
proves $L^0=S$ at every subsequent stage.  Since the independent
incidence columns make the nonzero coefficient torus a single
diagonal orbit, this applies to the critical coefficients as well.

We next verify the finite-stabilizer hypotheses uniformly.  A
generator of the gadget diagonal stabilizer modulo its scalar
torus, normalized to have boundary character exponent one
modulo256, has exponent lists
\[
\begin{aligned}
A_0&=(225,54,113,161,1,72,36,222),\\
B_0&=(0,220,202,192,131,128,125,237),\\
C_0&=(53,0,18,95,238,162,159,220).
\end{aligned}
                                                               \tag{K-020b}
\]
Its complete zero-weight support is exactly the22 gadget edges.
All factor spectra are simple.  The first and third have no
antipodal pair, and the second has exactly the pair of indices0
and5.  These are complete finite integer checks.

Consider one gluing from period $D$ to $256D$, and choose a
diagonal generator $g$ of order $256D$ with primitive determinant
character.  The boundary character has order256 in the new
incidence quotient.  Indeed its unique rational expression in
the new incidence columns has zero coefficients on the old
remaining columns and coefficients $u/256$ on the gadget columns;
some entry of $u$ is odd.  Since the new torsion group is cyclic
of order $256D$, this boundary character evaluates primitively
on $g$.  The gadget restriction is consequently a primitive
generator modulo its scalar torus and has the properties in
(K-020b).

Write the eigenvalue exponents of $g$ modulo $256D$.
Every gadget coordinate in a given factor is congruent modulo
$D$ to its boundary coordinate: their eigenvalue ratio has order
dividing256.  The sum of the three boundary exponents is also
divisible by $D$.  Thus reducing the old coordinates modulo $D$
gives a stabilizer of the old support, including the deleted edge.
Moreover
\[
 \sum_{\rm all}w\equiv\sum_{\rm old}w+
              7(w_{A,i}+w_{B,j}+w_{C,k})
          \equiv\sum_{\rm old}w\pmod D.
\]
The old determinant character is primitive, because the new total
is odd.  By the old cyclic diagonal-stabilizer statement, this
reduced element is a primitive old generator up to scalar factors
whose product is one.

Inductively the old spectra are simple and its complete
zero-weight support is the selected support.  Collapse all eight
gadget coordinates in each factor to that factor's boundary
coordinate.  A new zero-weight triple reduces to an old support
edge.  If the reduced edge is the deleted edge, the triple is
entirely in the gadget block and belongs to the gadget support
by (K-020b).  Every other old edge agrees with the deleted edge
in at most one coordinate.  Replacing that coordinate by a
different gadget coordinate changes its weight by a nonzero
gadget eigenvalue difference.  Hence there is no additional
zero-weight edge.  The complete new zero-weight support is
exactly the glued support.

The same reduction proves simplicity of the new spectra:
different old coordinates stay distinct modulo $D$, and the
eight coordinates in the only refined block are distinct by
(K-020b).  An antipodal difference modulo $256D$ is zero modulo
$D$, so every antipodal pair lies in the refined gadget block.
It follows that at every stage there is exactly one antipodal
pair in the second factor and none in the other two.  The
dimension-two parity seed has simple spectra and complete
zero-weight support, which starts this induction.

The centralizer of $g$ in the full stabilizer is diagonal and
equals $S\langle g\rangle$.  We have proved $L^0=S$, at most one
antipodal pair per factor, and antipodal-free first and third
factors.  K-016 applies in either parity of $n$ and gives
$L=S\langle g\rangle$.

As in K-019, the effective $SL_n^3$ stabilizer is trivial, so the
quotient is smooth.  The complete fixed tensor space has
$3n-2$ coordinates, whereas the fixed $SL_n^3$ Lie algebra has
dimension $3n-3$.  The fixed tangent is one-dimensional.
Positivity of the unique margin solution and transitivity on
the nonzero coefficient torus give a single dense grading
orbit; its stabilizer has order $2\cdot256^k$, giving density
one by the same degree-semigroup argument as in K-019.
\end{proof}

This theorem establishes an infinite family of actual smooth
fixed curves at the amplified orders.  It does not classify other
fixed components or assert that their global Hilbert residues do
not cancel.
