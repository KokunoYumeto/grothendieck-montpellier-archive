## Established results used or compared

Blasiak, J., Mulmuley, K. D., & Sohoni, M. (2013). *Geometric complexity theory IV: Nonstandard quantum group for the Kronecker problem*. arXiv:cs/0703110v4. Primary author PDF: https://websites.umich.edu/jblasiak/Apr13KroneckerGCT4.pdf

The complete Proposition 11.8 and Remark 11.9, printed p. 61, were reopened and read. Proposition 11.8 is generic semisimplicity. Remark 11.9 already distinguishes the abstract specialized algebra from its ambient image: at the paper's parameter q=1, its kernel is the radical and its semisimple quotient is Q[S_r]. The paper's q is the parameter called v in N020; that ordinary specialization is D=4, not N020's D=0. No novelty is claimed for failure of injectivity after specialization or for the existence of radicals in nonstandard specializations. The rank-three presentation is Theorem 11.16, equation (112), printed p. 65. The full rank-four generic dimension occurs in section 11.8, p. 67.

Blasiak, J. (2015). Nonstandard braid relations and Chebyshev polynomials. *Journal of Algebra, 423*, 375–404. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2014.09.035. Primary author preprint: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1010.0421

Theorem 3.7 and its complete proof, printed pp. 13–14 of arXiv:1010.0421v2, and Corollary 3.8, p. 14, give the rank-three generalized nonstandard braid presentation and freeness. For tensor exponent k=2 the ten normal words are precisely N020's displayed words. Remark 3.6, p. 13, explicitly corrects an earlier claim of semisimplicity under every specialization. N020's rank-three reductions reproduce this known presentation; their injection into the abstract rank-four sign fibre is separately verified from the actual rank-four table.

Blasiak, J. (2012). *Representation theory of the nonstandard Hecke algebra*. arXiv:1201.2209v2. https://arxiv.org/pdf/1201.2209

The introduction, printed pp. 1–2, identifies the studied algebra as the nonstandard Temperley–Lieb quotient. Corollary 7.1 and its proof, printed pp. 25–26, were reopened and read. Its generic rank-four dimension is 89. That quotient is not substituted for the full rank-four order of rank 114.
