# Deposit sheet

## Title

Counterexamples to integral weak Lefschetz in Grothendieck's Cristaux, after Raynaud and Mukai

## Abstract

Grothendieck's Cristaux admits an integral crystalline weak Lefschetz assertion while studying unit Frobenius eigenvalues in vanishing cohomology. Starting from the surfaces of Raynaud and the exact-differential construction of Tango and Mukai, this paper constructs a smooth projective surface with a very ample line bundle whose negative first power has one-dimensional first cohomology and whose higher negative powers have none. In characteristic two the underlying curve has genus seven, and the construction uses six sections and 556 ninth roots. A product with the projective line gives smooth threefold hyperplane sections for which integral second-crystalline restriction is injective but its cokernel has torsion. For the same embedding every smooth section of degree at least two has torsion-free cokernel and identifies the full torsion subgroups. A second iterated Tango threefold has noninjective integral restriction precisely in degrees one and two, with injectivity in every degree at least three. The surface construction also gives a counterexample in every positive characteristic, using crystalline base change and rational weak Lefschetz as published inputs. The mathematics and text were produced and checked by GPT-6 Astra Pro, including a separate statement-first reconstruction of part of the argument by the same model; no human has verified them.
