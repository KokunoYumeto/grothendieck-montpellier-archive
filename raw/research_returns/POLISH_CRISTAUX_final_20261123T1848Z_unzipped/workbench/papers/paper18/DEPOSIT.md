## Title

Grothendieck’s Lubin–Tate lattice question and the ramification threshold e=p

## Abstract

Grothendieck’s crystalline notes ask how the tangent lattice and the full de Rham Dieudonné lattice of a Lubin–Tate group compare with their special-fibre counterparts. For the ring of integers of every finite extension of Q_p, the normalized tangent exponent is zero. The two full integral lattices are equal precisely when the absolute ramification index is at most p. Both assertions include p=2, arbitrary residue degree, and arbitrary Eisenstein equations. The proof uses Lau’s all-prime window classification and crystalline comparison, together with the Lubin–Tate characterization of the formal group. An explicit convergent comparison product makes tangent equality visible, and a coefficient estimate proves the necessity of the full-lattice threshold.
