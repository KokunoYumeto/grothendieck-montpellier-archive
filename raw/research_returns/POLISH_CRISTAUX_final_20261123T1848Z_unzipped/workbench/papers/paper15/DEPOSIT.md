## Title

Primitive monodromy in Grothendieck’s crystalline programme, after Cartier and Sun

## Abstract

Grothendieck’s crystalline notes ask whether the monodromy of a varying hypersurface’s surviving Frobenius space is the whole linear group. We determine two primitive-system cases in characteristic two. Every fixed nonordinary abelian surface with an ample globally generated polarization of type (1,3) has relative geometric image GL2(F2); its nonzero Lang cover is connected of degree three. On the supersingular product E², where E:y²+y=x³, the lines L_m=O(mV+H+Gamma) have type (1,2m+1). For every m>=1, a general smooth member has genus 2m+2, two-rank 2m and exact relative image S_(2m+1) in its permutation quotient representation. Its nonzero Lang cover has exactly m connected components, of degrees binomial(2m+1,j) for 1<=j<=m. Thus unrestricted full linear image and nonzero-cover connectedness fail in every odd type at least five in this explicit family. These lines have degree two on an elliptic fibre and are not very ample, so the examples do not refute eventual assertions for powers of a fixed embedding line. The proofs use Cartier multiplication, two explicit Lang restrictions and a complete elliptic branch series obtained by relative Frobenius. The mathematics and text were produced by GPT-6 Astra Pro and checked within the scopes stated below; no human has checked the mathematics.

## Exact scope, credit and remaining questions

Theorem1.1 holds for every fixed nonordinary abelian surface with an ample globally generated type(1,3) line. The geometric nonvanishing of its Cartier tensor, the actual Euler-bundle Frobenius matrix and smooth-member existence are proved in full. Two actual restrictions of the same Lang system force full GL2(F2).

Theorem1.2 holds for every odd n=2m+1>=3 in the exact E² family O(mV+H+Gamma). It includes composite n. The complete branch-series proof gives rank n-1, exact S_n image and exact component degrees; it does not depend on a finite list of previously certified prime ranks. The original five-prime upper-image/lower-component claims are included, with their former scope kept explicit in the records.

Cartier multiplication is credited to the classical theory through Illusie; the full surface Frobenius filtration input to Sun; theta descent, theta representations, ample vanishing and Riemann–Roch to Edixhoven–van der Geer–Moonen; Picard identities to Milne. The natural branch module is a formal Artin–Schreier/finite-pushforward consequence, with a full sheaf proof. Relative-Frobenius factorization is a known curve mechanism, with its exact application and the completeness of the descended series proved here. The ordered-divisor symmetric-group argument is a classical Abel-fibre consequence proved in the text. No priority claim is made.

The full relative image and generic dual-Cartier rank for arbitrary primitive polarizations on other fixed surfaces remain outside these results. The examples have elliptic-fibre degree two and are not very ample. They therefore do not refute a sufficiently-large-degree assertion for powers of a fixed embedding line. Grothendieck’s exact original question is quoted from cote4, Section8.6, archive sheet11/PDF image12, with that original setting distinguished.
