## Title

Grothendieck’s Hasse and ordinary determinant duality, after Bijakowski and Serre–Tate

## Abstract

Grothendieck’s crystalline notes compare the Hodge determinant of an abelian scheme with that of its dual, and ask how this comparison acts on the Hasse invariant and on the ordinary étale determinant. We identify the trace normalization of the known Hasse-duality theorem of Bijakowski over every characteristic-p base. On the ordinary locus the determinant of a polarization divided by the positive square root of its degree is an integral isomorphism, independent of the polarization. With positive Poincaré biduality it becomes its inverse on the dual abelian scheme. Its reduction agrees with the inverse Hodge–trace comparison up to (-1)^{g(g+1)/2}. The product rule contains the sign (-1)^{gh}. The arguments include characteristic two, nonreduced bases, and polarizations whose degrees are divisible by p. The proof uses Serre–Tate canonical lifting, Riemann–Roch, and an explicit cup-product calculation.
