## Title

Grothendieck's generic Frobenius ranks for primitive odd polarizations, after Cartier, Sun, and Karpenko

## Abstract

Grothendieck's crystalline-cohomology notes ask how the generic stable Frobenius rank of hypersurface sections depends on their degree. We determine the relative rank for every fixed nonordinary abelian surface in characteristic two with a globally generated polarization of type (1, ell), for ell = 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17. A general smooth curve has genus ell+1 and 2-rank f_A+ell−1, where f_A is the 2-rank of the surface. For every odd prime the same conclusion follows from one explicit maximal-rank condition on the dual Cartier pencil. Cartier multiplication and Sun's Frobenius filtration give a nonzero theta-equivariant tensor. Karpenko's incompressibility argument forces its radical map to be dominant; an incidence calculation makes it birational. Exact polynomial certificates establish the required dual rank for the six listed primes. The type (1,3) example has a Fermat cubic as its nonbijective locus. Global generation remains an explicit hypothesis, and no monodromy conclusion is inferred. The mathematics and text were produced and checked by GPT-6 Astra Pro with the scopes stated at the end; no human has checked the mathematics.

## Exact mathematical scope

Theorem1.1 treats each fixed nonordinary abelian surface over an algebraically closed field of characteristic two and each ample globally generated type(1,ell) line for the six specified odd primes. It gives genus ell+1 and curve2-rank f_A+ell−1 on a nonempty smooth open, equivalently maximal relative stable rank ell−1. The perfect-field assertion is geometric after algebraic closure.

Theorem1.2 gives the same answer for any odd prime under the precise additional condition that the dual Cartier alternating pencil has generic rank ell−1. The polynomial certificates prove that condition for every nonzero coefficient vector only at the six listed primes, over every characteristic-two field. They cover all360 nonempty coefficient supports, including all255 supports at17; there is no extrapolation to other primes.

Companion results prove slope stability of F_*L for odd Euler characteristic, evaluation rank at least3 for odd ample globally generated systems, Cartier surjectivity on nonordinary surfaces, evaluation rank4 when3 does not divide the odd section number, and smooth-member existence. The conditional birational radical-map and constant-Pfaffian-gcd statements hold for every odd prime. Type(1,3) has the explicit smooth Fermat cubic, determinant multiplicity one, and generic first/stable rank one on that cubic.

The universal all-odd-prime dual-rank assertion remains open, as does its weaker version restricted to geometrically realized Cartier tensors. Global generation is assumed, not derived from type. No common wall, Lang-cover connectedness, local radical or full monodromy group is asserted. The parameter space is the complete linear system; an ambient hypersurface family requires its own section-surjectivity check.
