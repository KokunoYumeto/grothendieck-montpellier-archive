## Abstract

Grothendieck's crystalline notes ask whether sufficiently high-degree hypersurface sections, in every nonempty parameter open, realize every point-count residue modulo the characteristic after finite extension of the ground field. We prove this for every smooth projective connected ambient variety of dimension at least two over a finite field, with an explicit degree bound. Geometric connectedness and generic relative ordinarity are unnecessary. The relative Frobenius monodromy has every trace in the prime field. Independent permutations of the intersection points in a normal-crossing degeneration provide the varying traces; an order comparison isolates one alternating factor from all other cohomological contributions. A cancellation in the error dimension and a prime-adic order estimate improve the sufficient bound. In characteristic two, a further calculation gives every plane degree at least four. We use Katz's congruence formula, the finite-cover monodromy criterion of Entin, and finite étale lifting over henselian pairs.
