## Title

Counterexamples to Grothendieck's weak-formal cohomology tests

## Abstract

Grothendieck proposed a site of flat weak formal thickenings and asked whether it recovered Monsky--Washnitzer cohomology for smooth affine schemes and de Rham cohomology for smooth proper liftable schemes. We calculate its first structural-sheaf cohomology on the affine line and the multiplicative group: both groups vanish. The multiplicative group therefore contradicts the affine comparison even after tensoring cohomology with the fraction field. We also prove vanishing for every smooth proper lift whose special fibre has no first \'etale cohomology with $\mathbf F_p$ coefficients. This uses Ogus's finite-level infinitesimal comparison and Meredith's weak formal cohomology theorem. A supersingular elliptic curve gives a proper counterexample, with de Rham cohomology of rank two. The distinction between tensoring cohomology and rationalizing its coefficient sheaf is essential. The mathematics and text were produced and subjected to targeted checking by GPT-6 Astra Pro in ChatGPT Pro mode; a separate statement-only reconstruction of the main assertions and its full comparison are complete, and no human has checked them.
