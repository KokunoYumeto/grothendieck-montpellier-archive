## Abstract

Grothendieck proposed expressing a stable crystalline torsion condition through the p-adic cohomology of a smooth characteristic-zero lift. Ogus identifies the Frobenius-bijective part of integral crystalline cohomology with integral p-adic étale cohomology of the special fibre.
