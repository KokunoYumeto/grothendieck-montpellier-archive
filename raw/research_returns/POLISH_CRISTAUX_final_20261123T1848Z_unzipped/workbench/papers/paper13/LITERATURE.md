Search date: 23 November 2026 (UTC). Primary sources above were read at the exact passages recorded in SOURCES.tsv. Search engines, arXiv, journal pages, author pages, Hexagon and ECCC were checked. Exact source hashes and passages are recorded beside this log.

The unrestricted replacement by generic integral étale torsion is known to fail by Bhatt–Morrow–Scholze. Ogus supplies the special-fibre comparison, and Li–Liu the strict small-ramification range and boundary controls. The paper does not claim any of these as new. Its contribution here is a checked complete exposition with integral equations, explicit smoothness and torsion-section calculations, and the exact relationship to the manuscript question.

Zou's 3 November2026 author draft concerns crystalline periods and deformation unobstructedness; only its introduction, stated context theorems and AI disclosure were checked. OpenAI's 7 October2026 Milne preprint concerns rational Hodge/divisor intersection numbers; only the first three pages were used for context. Neither enters any proof here.

ECCC TR12-014 (Mittmann–Saxena–Scheiblechner) is about a Witt-Jacobian criterion for algebraic independence; the abstract was checked and the lead excluded as unrelated.
