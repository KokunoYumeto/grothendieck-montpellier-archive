## Title

Grothendieck’s comparison of infinitesimal crystals and ordinary stratifications

## Abstract

Grothendieck proposed extending the comparison between infinitesimal crystals and stratified modules from smooth morphisms to flat morphisms of finite presentation, with a proviso on Noetherian bases and coherent coefficients. We prove this qualified statement in every characteristic, using Jiang’s derived comparison and a separate passage to ordinary coherent modules. We also prove the comparison for arbitrary flat coefficients over a Noetherian base. Two explicit counterexamples explain why the qualifications matter: over a non-Noetherian base, even an automorphism of the structure crystal can be invisible on the underlying scheme; over a regular Noetherian base the same phenomenon occurs with a noncoherent target. In characteristic p, we describe arbitrary crystals by relative Frobenius-divided modules and obtain positive results for pure Frobenius and cuspidal normalizations.
