## Title

Grothendieck's generic Frobenius-rank question on mixed abelian surfaces

## Subject

Arithmetic algebraic geometry in characteristic two; polarized abelian surfaces; Frobenius and curve p-ranks.

## Keywords

## Abstract

Grothendieck's crystalline-cohomology notes ask how the generic stable Frobenius rank of hypersurface sections depends on their degree. For every abelian surface of $2$-rank one over an algebraically closed field of characteristic two and every ample bundle of positive ordinary polarization index, we prove that all sufficiently large complete systems $|M^{3e}|$ have generic curve defect two. No finite-field model or ordinary-index-zero ample class is required. We also determine exact ranks in every degree when the ordinary index is zero or is no larger than the local index, prove explicit bounds in the other cases, and reduce them by a maximal actual line-bundle root. For a pair $(A,M)$ over $\mathbf F_q$, with $q=2^f$ and ordinary index $a>0$, the sufficient range is $e>1+4q^{\lceil2a/f\rceil}/2^a$. The unrestricted eventual theorem follows by spreading and openness of stable Frobenius rank. Every mixed surface with a finite-field model admits an ordinary-index-zero ample class, whereas a geometric-generic quotient with $\operatorname{NS}(A)=\mathbf Z[L]$ and indices $(1,0)$ admits none. For the remaining smaller degrees, a trace-zero first-variation calculation identifies a sufficient determinant test with a restricted surface cup product and a residual-pencil boundary, including its full twisted contribution. Uniform determinant nonvanishing remains open. The inputs include integral Dieudonné duality, finite isotropic descent, principal cubic Cartier theory, nodal norms, Prym polarizations and Deuring–Shafarevich. The mathematics and text were produced and checked by GPT-6 Astra (ChatGPT Pro mode); the main rank and conditional determinant theorems received separate same-model reconstructions and full comparisons, and no human has checked the mathematics.
