## Exact sources and new deductions

Hoshi's original July2006 preprint [RIMS1558](https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/preprint/file/RIMS1558.pdf) was reopened. Corollary3.9, printed pp.31–32, requires a proper log-regular fs total space over a complete local fs log base. PropositionA.10, printed pp.57–58, gives equivalence with interior covers tame at all codimension-one boundary points. Its proof identifies the log structure with the divisorial one and invokes log purity for essential surjectivity. This supplies exactly the new theorem's modelwise comparisons in mixed characteristic; source references are `turn273047646view0`, `turn273047646view1`.

The new model-independence argument adds a valuative step. A codimension-one boundary local ring on a second integral normal model is a DVR. Properness extends its generic-point map to the first model. Its boundary-log sections pull back to nonzero regular elements, defining the log morphism from the trait. Kummer-étale base change then gives tameness at that valuation. Reversing the models proves equality of the two full subcategories of FEt(U), without a resolution or a log-regular common refinement. Root separately checked the exact source passages (`turn751185945view0/1`) and this deduction. This is not a novelty claim; log regularity must not be dropped in favor of an arbitrary normal compactification.

The mixed-characteristic framing uses only the maximal tame field `F^t=union F^nr(varpi^(1/m))`, with m prime to the residue characteristic. Fixing these roots gives a section of `G_F^t→G_k`; it does not split or identify the full absolute Galois group. The geometric kernel in the new exact sequence is explicitly the kernel of the tame augmentation, not an unproved identification with the full geometric group.

Mochizuki2007 Proposition1.2(ii), printed p.461, was reread in `sources/mochizuki_2007.txt`. It gives self-normalization of node inertia, so the character of its invariant conjugacy class is well defined. The extra branch sign is derived directly from the inverse Kummer identifications at the two branches, not from a misquotation of the twist-character theorem.

For the Frobenius counterexample the original official [Stacks Tag0BQN](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BQN), Proposition58.8.4, was opened with its full proof (`turn868660710view0`). The finite radicial surjective relative-Frobenius map meets its exact universal-homeomorphism hypothesis and is over k, so augmentation compatibility follows functorially. No claim that the absolute Frobenius fixes F16 is used. The new bibliographic record is `sources/part_c_a6_scope.bib`.
