## One source-typing clarification recommended

In `thm:B3-operators`, currently around lines 1442–1444, “finite R-modules” should explicitly mean **finite as sets**. Raynaud's original printed p.129 says exactly “ayant un nombre fini d'éléments”. The category `FinMod_R` and the finite-level abelian torsion application use that convention. Without the parenthetical, a reader could apply the usual commutative-algebra convention “finitely generated R-module”, which is not the source's hypothesis for arbitrary R. This is a clarification of the theorem already used, not a new restriction introduced to repair its proof. The recommendation was sent to the parent; this reviewer has not edited TeX.
