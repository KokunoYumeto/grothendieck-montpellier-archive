## Exact quantifiers

Test every nonfield DVR A with an R-algebra structure and an R-morphism SpecFrac(A) to U. A is not assumed excellent, henselian, finite type over R, or a local R-algebra. The generic map need not be dominant or algebraic over the function field of U. Traits where the uniformizer of R is a unit are included: horizontal boundary points must not disappear from the converse.

Give SpecA its divisorial log structure at the closed point. A finite etale cover of U passes the test only if its generic pullback is the generic fibre of an **actual finite Kummer-etale cover** of this log trait. The definition does not replace this condition by ramification index and residue-degree numerics. In particular, no finiteness of normalization for an arbitrary extension over a nonexcellent DVR is inferred.

## Galois proof

Hoshi RIMS1558 Appendix A.1–A.2 gives a Galois category of finite Kummer-etale covers for any connected locally noetherian fs log scheme and exact pullback along log morphisms. A divisorial DVR trait is fs and log regular. A connected finite ket cover dominates the trait by Proposition A.6; log regularity implies normality. A connected normal finite scheme over this affine trait is integral, so its generic fibre is connected. Therefore the generic restriction induces a surjection from the field Galois group to the log-trait fundamental group.

If J_A denotes its kernel, the essential image of generic restriction is precisely the finite field-Galois sets killed by J_A. It is fully faithful and closed under all finite Galois-category operations, including subquotients. For a fixed geometric point of U, each test induces a homomorphism alpha_A from that field Galois group to pi1(U), up to conjugacy. Quotient pi1(U) by the closed normal subgroup generated by all alpha_A(J_A). Finite sets for this quotient are exactly the covers passing every test. This proves that the intersection is a Galois subcategory, with its usual geometric fibre functor, and avoids any unsafe normalization or defect argument.

## Agreement with every existing model

If a proper integral noetherian fs log-regular model X over the divisorial log base exists with interior U, a model-tame cover extends Kummer-etale by Hoshi A.10. Every test generic map extends to X by the valuative criterion of properness. The divisorial description of the log structure extends this to a log map, since boundary-log functions pull back to nonzero elements of A. Kummer-etale base change proves that the cover passes all tests.

Conversely, each codimension-one boundary local ring of X is a tested DVR, including horizontal points. Passing that test gives tame ramification there. Hoshi A.10 on X yields a finite Kummer-etale extension, hence equality with the existing model-defined full subcategory. X is excellent as a finite-type scheme over the complete noetherian local R, so this converse has no hidden normalization-finiteness issue. The already proved Hoshi strict-special-log-fibre comparison then realizes the intrinsic category whenever that geometric model is available.

## Functoriality and arithmetic quotient

Composition of tests proves pullback functoriality along F-morphisms U' to U. Each trait has a log map to the divisorial log base, including when the base uniformizer pulls back to a unit, so finite tame base covers pass every test. Geometric connectedness of U preserves connected finite extensions of F, yielding the surjection from the intrinsic group to G_F^t. The already constructed uniformizer-framed section of G_F^t over G_k and its pullback extension are consequently available before a model is chosen. This does not identify the resulting kernel with the full geometric pi1.
