## Verdict

The claimed construction and model comparison are sound under the written hypotheses. The category is defined for every indicated nonempty variety before a proper log-regular model is known to exist. The later model realization is conditional on an existing proper model. This is a definition and categorical comparison, not a theorem on mixed-characteristic resolution, reconstruction from the quotient, or uniqueness of a geometric special fibre.

## Detailed checks

1. **All DVRs, including nonexcellent and horizontal traits.** A discrete valuation ring is noetherian; its closed-point divisorial log structure has the rank-one uniformizer chart and is fs/log regular. Neither the log-Galois premise nor the actual-extension definition needs excellence, henselianity or finite type. Since the test's generic point maps to an F-variety, every nonzero element of R maps nontrivially in its fraction field. A base uniformizer can be a unit in A, exactly as the statement permits. Horizontal codimension-one boundary traits must be included for the later converse; they are.

2. **The local essential image really is closed under subquotients.** A connected nonempty finite Kummer-étale cover of the log trait is surjective and log regular/normal. It is noetherian and finite over an affine trait; connectedness and normality force its finitely many irreducible components to reduce to one. Thus its generic fibre is nonempty and connected. The induced map from the generic absolute Galois group to the log fundamental group is surjective. Restriction is consequently fully faithful and its essential image consists exactly of finite sets annihilated by the kernel. This supplies subobject, quotient, finite-product and finite-sum closure. It avoids the tempting but unjustified claim that every finite extension of a nonexcellent DVR has finite normalization.

3. **The global quotient has the correct variance and path invariance.** Pullback along each generic test corresponds to restriction through its homomorphism into pi1(U). The condition on a finite pi1(U)-set is exactly triviality of each image of the local kernel. Taking their closed normal closure therefore gives the asserted quotient. Normal closure removes the path-conjugacy choices. Essential smallness of finite covers (or equivalently the set of closed normal subgroups of the fixed profinite group) removes any need to construct a set-sized moduli space of all traits.

4. **Functoriality and base augmentation.** Composing a generic trait test with an F-morphism proves contravariant functoriality of covers. Every test trait maps logarithmically to the base trait, even when the base uniformizer is a unit, because its pullback is nonzero and hence a section of the divisorial log structure. Thus every tame base cover passes every test. A connected finite separable constant extension remains connected on geometrically connected U, giving the stated surjection onto the tame absolute Galois quotient. No assertion identifies the resulting geometric kernel with the full geometric étale group.

5. **Existing model implies every trait test.** Properness extends every generic test to the model; no generic dominance is required. Every local boundary-log function is invertible at the image of the generic point, so it pulls back to a nonzero regular element on the trait. This yields the log morphism. Finite Kummer-étale base change supplies the extension, with no excellence assumption on the test trait.

6. **Every trait test implies model tameness.** The local ring of every codimension-one boundary point is a DVR, and its generic point lies in the interior. It is one of the permitted tests, including horizontal boundary points. Its Kummer-étale extension proves tameness there. Properness makes the model finite type over the complete noetherian DVR, hence excellent; finiteness of the normalization used for this global comparison is therefore justified. The opened log-purity comparison gives the reverse inclusion. A log-regular model with connected dense interior is automatically connected and normal, hence integral in this noetherian setting; no extra component is silently introduced.

7. **Specialization scope.** The strict special-log-fibre equivalence is exactly the already proved complete-local proper log-regular comparison. The intrinsic definition does not conjure a model or its special fibre when none is supplied. The wild-cover and relative-Frobenius counterexamples in the separate supplement remain applicable to the stronger assertions explicitly excluded by this proposition.
