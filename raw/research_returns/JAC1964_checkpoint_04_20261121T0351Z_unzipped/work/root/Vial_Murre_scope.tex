\subsection*{Source audit: Murre's theorem does not remove the Fermat gate}

Vial, \emph{Niveau and coniveau filtrations on cohomology groups
and Chow groups}, author manuscript of 19 March 2012,
Theorem 2(5)--(6), pp.~4--5, and Theorem 4.8,
Corollary 4.10, Remark 4.11, p.~24, were read directly at
\url{https://www.math.uni-bielefeld.de/~vial/coniveau11.pdf}.
Corollary 4.10 establishes Murre (A), (B), (C$'$), (D) for abelian
threefolds. Theorem 2(6) identifies the kernel of the
\emph{algebraically trivial} Abel--Jacobi map with the kernel
of the appropriate niveau projector. These statements do not
assert injectivity of the arithmetic map in AR-16a.

In our application the unresolved cycles belong to
$\operatorname{CH}^2_{(1)}(J)$, specifically the summand $N$.
Murre(D), or the grade-zero divisor/Fourier argument in AR-1a,
does not annihilate this degree-one summand. The stronger
additional condition on odd cohomology in Theorem 4.8 would require
$H^3$ to be generated by degree-one cohomology of curves with its
twist. It fails on $H(N)$, whose types are $(3,0),(0,3)$.
An algebraic curve correspondence instead has image of types
$(2,1),(1,2)$. Thus the source's extra hypothesis is unavailable.

The distinction is also visible in AR-15a: its possible
grade-zero obstruction lies on $B\times J$ for Jacobians $B$
of unbounded dimension, not on the threefold $J$ alone.
Neither Corollary 4.10 nor the threefold Fourier calculation
supplies grade-zero injectivity on all these products.
This is a scope check of cited background, not a new theorem
or a claim that the remaining injectivity statement is false.
