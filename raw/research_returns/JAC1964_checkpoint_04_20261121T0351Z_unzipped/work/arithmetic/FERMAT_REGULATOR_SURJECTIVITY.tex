\subsection*{AR-23. The positive Fermat classes span the actual Selmer image}

\begin{proposition}[AR-23]
Use the actual rational Fermat motive $N$ and class $\eta$ of JAC-P17,
and put $F=\mathbf Q(i)$,
\[
 V_k=\operatorname{CH}^2(N_k)_{\mathbf Q},\qquad
 W_5=H^3_{\mathrm{et}}(N_{\overline{\mathbf Q}},\mathbf Q_5)(2),
 \qquad S_k=H^1_f(k,W_5).
\]
Let
$\alpha_k:V_k\otimes_{\mathbf Q}\mathbf Q_5\to S_k$
be the scalar-extended etale Abel--Jacobi map used in AR-16a.
Then
\[
 \alpha_{\mathbf Q}(\eta)\ne0,\qquad
 S_{\mathbf Q}=\mathbf Q_5\alpha_{\mathbf Q}(\eta),
 \qquad
 S_F=\mathbf Q_5\alpha_F(\eta)
       \oplus\mathbf Q_5\alpha_F(T\eta).              \tag{AR.23a}
\]
In particular these regulators are surjective.  There are direct-sum
decompositions of $\mathbf Q_5$-vector spaces
\[
 \begin{split}
 V_{\mathbf Q}\otimes\mathbf Q_5
   &=\mathbf Q_5\eta\oplus\ker\alpha_{\mathbf Q},\\
 V_F\otimes\mathbf Q_5
   &=(\mathbf Q_5\eta\oplus\mathbf Q_5 T\eta)
                                      \oplus\ker\alpha_F.
 \end{split}                                                   \tag{AR.23b}
\]
No rational Chow splitting along these kernels, no vanishing of the
kernels, and no real-height orthogonality of the kernels is asserted.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
We first use more of Bloch's original argument than just the stated
infinite-order conclusion.  In \emph{Algebraic cycles and values of
$L$-functions}, J. reine angew. Math. \textbf{350} (1984), \S4,
p.~104, Bloch writes the projected integral Abel--Jacobi class
\[
 \beta_5\in H^1(F,M(\mathbf Z_5(2)))
\]
of the Fermat Ceresa cycle and states that it is nonzero.  Here $M$ is
the rank-two summand of types $(3,0),(0,3)$.  On the same page he proves
\[
 H^0\bigl(F,M(\mathbf Q_5/\mathbf Z_5(2))\bigr)=0
\]
using Frobenius at $11$.  The long exact cohomology sequence of the
lattice inclusion therefore makes
\[
 H^1(F,M(\mathbf Z_5(2)))\longrightarrow
 H^1(F,M(\mathbf Q_5(2)))
\]
injective.  Thus his nonzero integral class has nonzero rational
Abel--Jacobi image; this is not merely a torsion-valued nonvanishing.

The cohomological projector used by Bloch and the realization of our
$\pi_N=\pi_3p_G$ have the same image: the extreme Hodge types.  The
complement has only types $(2,1),(1,2)$, so there is no Hodge morphism
in either direction between these two parts.  Consequently the
projection onto the extreme part is unique, and the two projected
rational Abel--Jacobi classes agree under this identification.  The
algebraic projectors and comparison isomorphisms carry this equality
to the $5$-adic realization.

We may take the hyperflex Abel embedding used in AR-8.  Bloch's
Theorem (3.4), p.~102, treats the Ceresa cycle for a choice of
embedding.  Independence of the projected Abel--Jacobi image from
that choice can also be checked directly.  A change of embedding is
a translation, and its change of cycle is algebraically trivial.
Over a finite extension it is a sum of images of degree-zero
divisors on curves.  After projection to $N$, each corresponding
map $H^1(D)\to H^3(N)(1)$ is zero by Hodge type, hence also on
etale cohomology by comparison.  Functoriality of Abel--Jacobi kills
the resulting projected image.  Rational restriction to a finite
extension is injective by restriction--corestriction, so this
conclusion descends to $F$.

For that embedding, let $X_1$ be its Beauville grade-one curve class.
By AR-7 and AR-8,
\[
 \kappa=[C]-[-1]_*[C]=2X_1,
 \qquad \eta=\mathcal F(X_1),\qquad p_G\kappa=\kappa.
\]
The middle component of the Fourier correspondence is
$c_1(\mathcal P)^3/3!$ on $J\times\widehat J$; after the principal
polarization it induces an invertible map on $H^3(J)$ and on its
extreme summand.  Fourier commutes with $G$ because $G$ preserves
the polarization.  Functoriality now gives
\[
 \alpha_F(\eta)=\tfrac12\mathcal F_*\beta_5\ne0
\]
after rationalization and the indicated identifications.  Only
invertibility is needed here; no integral lattice index or unit
normalization of this Fourier map is asserted.

JAC-P17 places $N$ and $\eta$ over $\mathbf Q$ and identifies their
realization with the precise Hecke character used in AR-19 and
H9-T15.  Since restriction takes
$\alpha_{\mathbf Q}(\eta)$ to the just-proved nonzero class,
$\alpha_{\mathbf Q}(\eta)\ne0$.  The proven Selmer dimensions are
\[
 \dim_{\mathbf Q_5}S_{\mathbf Q}=1,\qquad
 \dim_{\mathbf Q_5}S_F=2.
\]
Complex conjugation $c$ fixes $\eta$, and $cT=-Tc$, $T^2=-1$.
The same identities hold on Selmer classes by functoriality.
Therefore $\alpha_F(\eta)$ and $\alpha_F(T\eta)$ are nonzero and
belong to the distinct $c=+1$ and $c=-1$ eigenspaces.  They are
$\mathbf Q_5$-linearly independent, proving (AR.23a).

Finally, for either field, the regulator restricts to an isomorphism
from the displayed $\mathbf Q_5$-span of the Fermat classes onto
$S_k$.  Subtracting the unique element of that span with the same
regulator value gives (AR.23b), including uniqueness.  This is
linear algebra over $\mathbf Q_5$ and does not use finite
dimensionality of the source Chow group.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Remaining arithmetic gap.}
The known positive classes now account for the entire Selmer target,
not just for some abstract subspace of the Chow group.  The remaining
issue is whether the scalar-extended Abel--Jacobi kernels in
(AR.23b) vanish, or whether a separately proved real-height statement
controls all additional classes.  Even a surjective map to a
one-dimensional $\mathbf Q_5$-space permits many independent rational
Chow classes, and the coefficients of their Selmer projection need
not be rational.  The real height pairing cannot be extended
canonically to $\mathbf Q_5$, so (AR.23b) is not an orthogonal
decomposition for that pairing.  In particular no real radical
factorization follows from this proposition.

There is no conflict with AR-20's vanishing of rational finite-field
specializations.  Bloch's detection keeps integral and torsion
cohomological information before rationalizing the global
Abel--Jacobi class.  The ordinary rational Chow class of each
specialized odd-degree motive is a different invariant and is zero
in AR-20.

\paragraph{Exact source scope.}
Bloch's original p.~104 was visually checked, including the integral
class, its nonvanishing, and the vanishing of the torsion-module
invariants; Theorem (3.4) and its embedding discussion are on p.~102.
The proof above transports that established class through the actual
Fermat projector and Fourier correspondence and combines it with the
new Selmer calculation.  The image in $H^1_f$ and functoriality are
the same etale Abel--Jacobi formalism already used in AR-16a; no
conjectural injectivity is added.  Primary scan:
\url{https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/fulltext/PPN243919689_0350/00000110?_format=xml}.
