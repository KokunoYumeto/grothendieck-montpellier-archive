\subsection*{AR-16a. The remaining injectivity question over \(\mathbf Q\)}

Use the actual \(\mathbf Q\)-motive \(N\), rational class \(\eta\),
and \(F=\mathbf Q(i)\)-defined operation \(T\) of JAC-P17.
Put
\[
 V_L=\operatorname{CH}^2(N_L)_{\mathbf Q},\qquad
 W=H^3_{\mathrm{et}}(N_{\overline{\mathbf Q}},\mathbf Q_5)(2),
 \qquad
 S_L=H^1_f(L,W),
\]
and write \(\alpha_L:V_L\otimes_{\mathbf Q}\mathbf Q_5\to S_L\)
for the scalar-extended arithmetic Abel--Jacobi map.

\begin{corollary}[AR-16a]\label{ar16a}
The established AR-19, H9-T15, and JAC-P17 give
\[
                   \dim_{\mathbf Q_5}S_{\mathbf Q}=1,
       \qquad      \dim_{\mathbf Q_5}S_F=2.                \tag{AR16a.1}
\]
Moreover the following statements are equivalent:
\[
             \ker\alpha_{\mathbf Q}=0
                    \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
             \ker\alpha_F=0.                             \tag{AR16a.2}
\]
If these equivalent injectivity statements hold, then
\[
             V_{\mathbf Q}=\mathbf Q\eta,
       \qquad V_F=\mathbf Q\eta\oplus\mathbf Q T\eta.
                                                               \tag{AR16a.3}
\]
In that case the real height is strictly positive on the full
complementary Chow group over both fields. Without the
injectivity hypothesis, (AR16a.1) is a Selmer-dimension theorem
and (AR16a.3) has not been proved.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
JAC-P17 identifies the \(\mathbf Q\)-realization with the
representation induced from the Gaussian character
\(\chi=\psi_+^3\). AR-19 proves the nonzero Katz premise and
H9-T15 gives a one-dimensional Bloch--Kato Selmer group for the
corresponding one-dimensional representation over \(F\).
Shapiro's lemma identifies this with \(S_{\mathbf Q}\), giving
its dimension one. Restricting the induced representation to
\(F\) splits it into the two conjugate character representations.
Conjugation identifies the dimensions of their Selmer groups,
so \(\dim S_F=2\).

For completeness the kernel comparison is an actual descent
argument, not a rank heuristic. Let \(c\) be the nontrivial
element of \(\operatorname{Gal}(F/\mathbf Q)\).
Restriction--corestriction in Galois cohomology identifies
\(S_{\mathbf Q}\) with \(S_F^{c=1}\).
The Bloch--Kato local conditions are preserved by restriction
and corestriction, so the same averaging argument applies to
these Selmer subspaces; division by two is allowed in
\(\mathbf Q_5\). Correspondence functoriality and
\(c(T)=-T\) give
\[
                S_F=S_{\mathbf Q}\oplus T S_{\mathbf Q}.
                                                               \tag{AR16a.4}
\]
Indeed \(T^2=-1\) exchanges the two eigenspaces of \(c\),
exactly as for the Chow group in JAC-P17.

The Abel--Jacobi map commutes with restriction, corestriction,
and \(T\). Under JAC-P17's rational Chow decomposition and
(AR16a.4), its kernel consequently decomposes as
\[
 \ker\alpha_F=
       \operatorname{res}(\ker\alpha_{\mathbf Q})
          \oplus T\operatorname{res}(\ker\alpha_{\mathbf Q}).
                                                               \tag{AR16a.5}
\]
This proves (AR16a.2).

If \(\alpha_{\mathbf Q}\) is injective, (AR16a.1) implies
\(\dim_{\mathbf Q}V_{\mathbf Q}\le1\) by extension of scalars.
AR-13 supplies the nonzero class \(\eta\), so it is a basis.
JAC-P17 then gives the second equality in (AR16a.3).
The known height matrices are
\[
 h_{\mathbf Q}(r\eta,r\eta)=r^2h_{\mathbf Q}(\eta,\eta),
 \qquad
 h_F(r\eta+sT\eta,r\eta+sT\eta)
       =(r^2+s^2)h_{\mathbf Q}(\eta,\eta),
\]
with \(h_{\mathbf Q}(\eta,\eta)>1/100\).
Both forms are strictly positive on nonzero vectors.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scope of the remaining question.}
The scalar extension in \(\alpha_L\) is indispensable, as
explained in AR-16. The corollary makes no uniform assertion
over further extensions of \(F\); new cycles and new Selmer
classes can occur there. It also does not identify a
\(p\)-adic Abel--Jacobi kernel with the radical of the real
height. Thus the numerical rank and field-of-definition
problems are resolved for these two fields, while the
Chow-theoretic injectivity requirement remains explicit.
