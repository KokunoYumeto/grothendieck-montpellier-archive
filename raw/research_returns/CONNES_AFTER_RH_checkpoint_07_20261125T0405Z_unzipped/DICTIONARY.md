# Dictionary to the arithmetic-geometric programme

These are comparisons of precisely specified invariants, not constructions of new cohomology groups.

## Harmonic zero measure and weights

For a centered Frobenius or zero parameter a+iγ, the finite model-space projection has boundary density |a|/[π((τ−γ)²+a²)], with δγ at a=0. The proof is the joint Blaschke model-space diagonal, not individual orthogonality. In a prospective cohomological interpretation, purity corresponds to a=0. The harmonic measure is a positive boundary functional for arbitrary a; its positivity alone does not prove purity. Identification with the geometric point-evaluation distribution is the additional statement.

## Multiplicity, K-theory, and a trace

A trace records m copies of a scalar eigenvalue and annihilates nilpotent commutators. A positive semisimple realization therefore permits m>1. A faithful *-representation of a self-adjoint nilpotent jet radical would force that radical to vanish. The two conditions must not be identified. This is the correction to the use of MT-M20/MT-M23 in the requested Dedekind-zeta comparison.

## Polar terms

The two principal coordinates transform by a^{-1/2} and a^{1/2}; before centering they are the characters 1 and |a|. This matches the formal H^0/H^2 character positions in Connes’s Lefschetz expression. The Hilbert completion does not thereby become the cohomology of a scheme over Z.
