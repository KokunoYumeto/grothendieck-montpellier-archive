\subsection{THETA3-B30: the genuine least-norm theta input has a common algebraic Serre-dual realization}\label{B30}

There is a further conclusion for the original fixed kernel which does
not require the period ratios in (B29k).  Its least-norm source input
is algebraic, up to one recorded scalar, in the \emph{Serre-dual
coherent cohomology} of the actual source.  This statement concerns a
different rational structure from that of a holomorphic source form.
It neither makes $Q_T^{-1}$ projectively algebraic nor identifies a
coherent cohomology class with an algebraic cycle.

\paragraph{The fixed spaces and the actual kernel.}
Keep all hypotheses and algebraic finite-data normalizations of
B25--B29.  In particular the source has a nonempty switch set,
$s=\dim Y>0$, and its complete scalar infinity type and central
character are fixed.  Use the full adelic realizations at the chosen
levels; $X$ and $Y$ may be disjoint unions of their smooth projective
components.  All finite-cover degrees, finite stabilizer masses and
compact-place volumes are retained.  The source line is exactly
\[
 \mathscr L=\bigotimes_{\tau\in I}\lambda_\tau^3\otimes\mathscr C
 \quad\hbox{from (B26b)},\qquad
 H=H^0(Y,\mathscr L),\qquad
 H^{\mathrm{SD}}=H^s(Y,K_Y\otimes\mathscr L^{-1}).
 \tag{B30a}
\]
In particular $\mathscr C$ and the determinant factors in $K_Y$
are still present.  These spaces and bundles are over
$\overline\Q$.  Fix B25's \emph{algebraic coherent} basis
$f_1,\ldots,f_r$ of $E_0\subset H^0(X,K_X)$.
It is not being replaced by a basis with algebraic Betti coordinates.
B26 gives the literal identity
\[
 K(g,h)=\sum_{a=1}^r f_a(g)e_a(h),\qquad
 e_a=\gamma g_a,\quad g_a\in H(\overline\Q),\qquad
 \gamma=\frac{C_x}{t_xt_y}\ne0.
 \tag{B30b}
\]
The scalar automorphic realizations of all sections are understood
in (B30b).  The $g_a$ are linearly independent: otherwise the
coefficient vectors of $K(\,\cdot\,,h)$ would lie in a proper
subspace of its range $E_0$.  Consequently
\[
 E_c=\operatorname{span}_{\overline\Q}\{g_1,\ldots,g_r\}\subset H,
 \qquad
 \mathcal K_0=\sum_a f_a\boxtimes g_a
       \in H^0(X\times Y,K_X\boxtimes\mathscr L)
                         (\overline\Q)
 \tag{B30c}
\]
are defined over $\overline\Q$, and the analytic section associated
to the original kernel is $\gamma\mathcal K_0$.
No second theta kernel or finite-frame replacement has been introduced.

\paragraph{The coefficient space splits even though it need not be a Hecke module.}
Apply B27 to the full finite-dimensional space $H$ with its prescribed
infinity type.  Its finite Hecke decomposition is multiplicity free:
\[
 H_{\overline\Q}=\bigoplus_\lambda V_\lambda,
       \qquad \langle\ ,\ \rangle_S|_{V_\lambda}
                                  =c_\lambda G_\lambda,
 \tag{B30d}
\]
where $G_\lambda$ is a positive Hermitian algebraic matrix and
$c_\lambda>0$.  If one initially used only the Hecke saturation of
$E_c$, its orthogonal complement in $H$ is a sum of the other
sectors, so the same argument applies after including them.
All these are coherent, rather than Betti, rational structures.

Let $p_\lambda$ be the source Hecke projector and let
$P_\lambda$ be the algebraic target projector of B29.
For the actual kernel the two operations agree:
\[
 (P_\lambda\otimes1)\mathcal K_0
                  =(1\otimes p_\lambda)\mathcal K_0.
 \tag{B30e}
\]
Indeed B27 decomposes $K$ by its source sectors, and B29 puts their
target images in the mutually orthogonal spaces $E_\lambda$.
Projection onto one target space therefore retains exactly the same
kernel summand.  Division by the single nonzero $\gamma$ proves
(B30e).

For completeness, (B30e) implies a decomposition of the actual
\emph{row space}, not merely of a larger Hecke module.  A linear
functional $\ell\in E_0^\vee$ extracts the row
$(\ell\otimes1)\mathcal K_0\in E_c$, and all rows of $E_c$
arise this way.  Applying $p_\lambda$ gives
$((\ell P_\lambda)\otimes1)\mathcal K_0$, again a row.
The projectors are algebraic, so
\[
                E_c=\bigoplus_\lambda E_{c,\lambda},
                   \qquad E_{c,\lambda}=E_c\cap V_\lambda
 \tag{B30f}
\]
is an algebraic orthogonal direct sum.  This does not assert that
$E_{c,\lambda}$ is stable under the full Hecke algebra of $V_\lambda$.

The actual Petersson-orthogonal projection
$\mathcal P_c:H\longrightarrow E_c$ is nevertheless algebraic.
Choose an algebraic column basis $U_\lambda$ of
$E_{c,\lambda}\subset V_\lambda$.  Its matrix on that sector is
\[
 \mathcal P_{c,\lambda}
     =U_\lambda(U_\lambda^*G_\lambda U_\lambda)^{-1}
                         U_\lambda^*G_\lambda.
 \tag{B30g}
\]
The matrix being inverted is positive definite.  Formula (B30g)
has algebraic entries, since complex conjugation preserves
$\overline\Q$.  It is exactly the projection for
$c_\lambda G_\lambda$: the two occurrences of $c_\lambda$
cancel.  Summing these matrices, with zero on the unused sectors,
proves the claim without comparing any two $c_\lambda$.
An algebraic matrix on coherent sections is all that is asserted;
no algebraic correspondence on $Y$ inducing this projection is needed.

\paragraph{Algebraic Serre contraction.}
Use the residue-normalized algebraic trace
$\operatorname{Tr}_Y:H^s(Y,K_Y)\to\overline\Q$, summed over
components.  Serre duality gives a perfect pairing
\[
 H\times H^{\mathrm{SD}}\longrightarrow\overline\Q,
       \qquad (\eta,\alpha)\longmapsto
                                  \operatorname{Tr}_Y(\eta\alpha).
 \tag{B30h}
\]
Contraction by the actual normalized tensor (B30c) is the algebraic map
\[
 \mathcal T_0:H^{\mathrm{SD}}\longrightarrow E_0,
      \qquad \mathcal T_0(\alpha)
                =\sum_a f_a\operatorname{Tr}_Y(g_a\alpha).
 \tag{B30i}
\]
Equivalently it is pullback to $X\times Y$, multiplication by
$\mathcal K_0$, and coherent trace along the projection to $X$.
Its surjectivity follows from the independence of the $g_a$ and
perfectness of (B30h).

There is a specified algebraic right inverse adapted to the original
source norm.  If $f=\sum_a q_af_a$, let $\ell_f\in E_c^\vee$
be defined by $\ell_f(g_a)=q_a$.  Define
\[
 \mathcal I_{\mathrm{SD}}(f)
       =\operatorname{SD}^{-1}(\ell_f\circ\mathcal P_c)
                    \in H^{\mathrm{SD}},
          \qquad \mathcal T_0\mathcal I_{\mathrm{SD}}=1_{E_0}.
 \tag{B30j}
\]
Here $\operatorname{SD}$ is the isomorphism from (B30h) to $H^\vee$.
Every map in this definition is algebraic over $\overline\Q$;
(B30g) makes the only metric-dependent step algebraic.
In particular (B30j) is one common linear map on the entire fixed
kernel image, not a choice made separately for each torus character.

\paragraph{Exact comparison with the original Haar integral.}
Let $d\nu$ be the actual positive smooth density on the source
components obtained by integrating the original quotient Haar measure
over the fixed compact and finite fibres.  Thus for two scalar
functions with opposite infinity characters the original integral
on $[S]$ is exactly their integral against $d\nu$ on $Y$.
No rescaling of $d\nu$ is made.  On a finite component it is
$\nu_j\prod_{\tau\in I}dx_\tau dy_\tau/y_\tau^2$, with the
specified positive component factor $\nu_j$.

Fix the actual invariant Hermitian metric on $\mathscr L$ used in
its scalar automorphic realization.  Locally let $u$ be a smooth
unitary frame, so a holomorphic section $\eta$ is represented by
its scalar automorphic coefficient $\eta_u$ in $\eta=\eta_u u$.
An input function $\psi$ has the inverse compact character; hence
$\psi u^{-1}$ is a well-defined smooth section of $\mathscr L^{-1}$.
Put $\epsilon_s=(-1)^{s(s-1)/2}$ and define $j_\nu(\psi)$ to be the
Dolbeault class represented, as an $\mathscr L^{-1}$-valued
$(s,s)$-form, by
\[
       \beta_\psi=\epsilon_s(2\pi i)^s
                            \psi u^{-1}d\nu.
 \tag{B30k}
\]
This is a global form independent of the unitary frame.  It is
$\bar\partial$-closed because its antiholomorphic degree is $s$.
It represents a class in $H^s(Y,K_Y\otimes\mathscr L^{-1})$.
We use exactly the Dolbeault comparison and residue trace of
Sastry--Tong, specified below; in those conventions
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}_Y[\beta]
       =\epsilon_s(2\pi i)^{-s}\int_Y\beta,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Tr}_Y\bigl(\eta\,j_\nu(\psi)\bigr)
                        =\int_{[S]}\eta(h)\psi(h)\,dh.
 \tag{B30l}
\]
The two $\epsilon_s$ factors cancel.  Every determinant-line period
and the original Haar measure are contained in the actual unitary
frame and $d\nu$ in (B30k).

An optional chart formula makes the orientation explicit.  If
$\ell$ is a holomorphic frame with squared norm $h_\ell$, then
$u=\ell/h_\ell^{1/2}$.  With all holomorphic differentials placed
before all antiholomorphic differentials, (B30k) is
\[
 \beta_\psi=(-\pi)^s\nu_j\,
       \psi h_\ell^{1/2}\prod_\tau y_\tau^{-2}\,
       \ell^{-1}dz_1\wedge\cdots\wedge dz_s
                       \wedge d\bar z_1\wedge\cdots\wedge d\bar z_s.
 \tag{B30m}
\]
Indeed that ordered differential product equals
$\epsilon_s(-2i)^s\prod_\tau dx_\tau dy_\tau$.
In an algebraic frame of the constant line, the norm in (B30m)
contains the retained factor $|\Omega_{\mathscr C}|$ from B26;
it has not been set to one.

Combining (B30b), (B30i) and (B30l) gives, for every smooth
source input of the prescribed inverse compact type,
\[
                       T\psi=\gamma\mathcal T_0(j_\nu(\psi)).
 \tag{B30n}
\]
Thus the coherent contraction uses the original theta integral,
with a single explicitly retained comparison scalar.

\paragraph{The actual least-norm input.}
For $f\in E_0$ put $\psi_f=T^*Q_T^{-1}f$, the unique least-norm
preimage from B17 and B28.  The adjoint kernel shows that
$\psi_f\in\overline{E_c}$ as a space of scalar automorphic
functions; multiplication by the nonzero $\bar\gamma$ does not
change that subspace.  In particular it is smooth.  Its functional
on $H$ under (B30l) vanishes on $E_c^\perp$, because
$\int\eta\psi_f=\langle\overline{\psi_f},\eta\rangle_S$.
The identity $T\psi_f=f$ also gives
\[
                  \int_{[S]} g_a(h)\psi_f(h)\,dh=q_a/\gamma.
 \tag{B30o}
\]
These two properties uniquely specify the functional on $H$ as
$\gamma^{-1}\ell_f\circ\mathcal P_c$.  Perfectness of Serre
duality proves the exact formula
\[
 \boxed{\displaystyle
       \gamma\,j_\nu\bigl(T^*Q_T^{-1}f\bigr)
                          =\mathcal I_{\mathrm{SD}}(f).}
 \tag{B30p}
\]
If $f$ has algebraic coherent coordinates, the right side is an
algebraic coherent cohomology class.  The formula is linear in $f$
and valid on the entire original image $E_0$.  It therefore applies
to the corrected vectors in B25 and to every finite source-character
family using these fixed data, without a new scalar per character.

There is no hidden existence issue in this comparison.  The map
$j_\nu$ restricted to $\overline H$ is an isomorphism onto
$H^{\mathrm{SD}}_\C$: its pairing with $H$ is the nondegenerate
Petersson pairing by (B30l), and Serre duality gives equal dimensions.
Its restriction to $\overline{E_c}$ gives precisely the functionals
vanishing on $E_c^\perp$.  Thus (B30p) specifies the same actual
finite-rank source input as the analytic least-norm construction.
It does not substitute a distribution or a limiting delta measure.
The resulting smooth input remains admissible in the analytic
last-step seesaw of B17, whose source hypothesis is compactness of
$[S]$, already satisfied here.

\paragraph{Source and arithmetic scope.}
For algebraic duality one may take the Stacks Project,
Lemma 48.15.7 (tag \texttt{0BRT}) and Remark 48.12.6
(tag \texttt{0G81}), with the invertible sheaf $\mathscr L$;
these identify the dualizing complex and the perfect trace pairing.
The exact complex normalization is Sastry--Tong,
\emph{The Grothendieck Trace and the de Rham Integral},
\emph{Canadian Mathematical Bulletin} 46 (2003), 429--440,
Theorem 4.1 and its complete proof, pp.435--436.
Their Sections 1--2 fix the Dolbeault comparison, and
Proposition 3.1 with its proof, pp.433--434, gives the
$\epsilon_s$ sign by comparison with a point residue.
We adopt their Lipman residue normalization.  A different
standard trace convention can change an algebraic sign; it
must not be silently substituted into (B30k)--(B30m).

The result is algebraicity in $H^{\mathrm{SD}}$, with the exact
common scalar $\gamma=C_x/(t_xt_y)$ and the comparison map
$j_\nu$ retained.  It is not algebraicity of the smooth function
in a holomorphic coherent basis, of a Betti class, or of the
operator $Q_T^{-1}$ on the target.  Recovering that last
projective algebraicity is still exactly (B29k).
Nor is a class in $H^{\mathrm{SD}}$ automatically an input to
the archive's totally definite \texttt{ClsK} arithmetic package.
What has been obtained is an algebraic Serre-dual realization of
the genuine prescribed-kernel input for the analytic last-step
identity, without an additional source-CM hypothesis.
