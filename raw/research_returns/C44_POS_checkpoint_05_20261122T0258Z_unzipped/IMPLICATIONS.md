## Fermat arithmetic and rational equivalence

C44-F01–F07 give a prime-to-5 integral projector for N, a single model over Z[1/10], and exact motivic comparison sequences. The obstruction to scalar-extended AJ injectivity has two parts: the tensor-to-completion kernel and the intersection with the Tate module of projected unramified H3. On every rational plane spanned by eta and one other class, injectivity is equivalent to bounded 5-power denominators in the specified etale-motivic group. Equivalently, it is finite generation of that group on the fixed arithmetic model. Its completion is proved to have rank one; that does not prove finite generation of the group itself.

The isolated P18 component on D×J is a rational vector space in etale motivic cohomology and is invisible to all finite-coefficient cycle maps. Its vanishing, or componentwise Murre (D), is not proved. The exact Euler-system-to-Chow gap is the unproved AJ-injectivity arrow in Lilienfeldt–Shnidman §15, equation (99) and the implication display on author pp.72–73. Their theorem does not bound the whole Chow group without this extra hypothesis. The Gross–Schoen formula is not Zhang’s separate positivity conjecture or an equality criterion.

Thus QFermat-AJ, Q35-full and the algebraic-radical question are not closed. The accepted positive height of eta, analytic simple zero and Selmer line remain intact. These reductions use no 032 input.
