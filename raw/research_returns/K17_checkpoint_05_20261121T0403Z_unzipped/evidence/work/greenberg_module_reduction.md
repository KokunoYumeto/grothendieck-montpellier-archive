## 1. Exact Greenberg hypothesis and primary source

Let p>=5, F=Q(mu_p), K=Q(mu_p^infinity), Lambda=Zp[[Gamma]], and R=Lambda[1/p]. Write X for the unramified Iwasawa module and M for the p-ramified module H_ab. The hypothesis considered here is the one-variable CM Greenberg assertion Xplus finite. It is not the multivariable pseudo-nullity conjecture discussed in Sharifi2002.

The primary source `work/wake_wang_erickson_pseudo.pdf` is arXiv1505.05128v3, 7 September2017, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1505.05128. Its §1.1 assumptions and CorollaryC p4 were read; more precisely, the full statements and proofs of Corollaries8.1.2–8.1.3 pp48–49 and Theorem8.2.3 pp50–51 were read. Corollary8.1.3(2) assumes that X_chi(1)[1/p] is cyclic over R/(xi) and that X_theta tensor R/(xi) is zero. It concludes integral isomorphisms between X_chi(1) and the Eisenstein quotient modulo its p-primary torsion, for both Upsilon and varpi. No simple-root condition is required. The source's §2.1, including Corollary2.1.3 and Conjecture2.1.5 p8, shows that the one-variable Greenberg hypothesis implies these conditions for all relevant characters. These parts were read in full.

Thus the primary modular-symbol input is already available under Greenberg. The extension problem is identifying its image with the actual quadratic companion data when the integral Iwasawa modules are not cyclic/free.

## 2. Rationalized Iwasawa modules under Greenberg

The ring R is a PID. Indeed, Lambda is a regular local UFD of dimension2 and its sole height2 prime containsp. Invertingp gives a noetherian integrally closed UFD of dimension1, hence a PID. It is essential that R means bounded-denominator power series, not Qp[[T]].

For an odd character a different from omega and omega^-1, apply the duality calculation in the full proof of WWE2017 Proposition2.2.6 to theta=omega*a^-1. It gives

    Ext^1_Lambda(M_a^#(1),Lambda)=X_theta.

After invertingp, the right side vanishes under Greenberg. A finitely generated R-module has zero Ext1 intoR only if its torsion summand is zero, by its PID decomposition. Hence M_a[1/p] is free. Its rank is1 by the local/global unit exact sequence: local units have rank1 in each such odd character, global minus units consist of roots of unity, and X_a is torsion.

The exceptional odd characters can be treated without Greenberg. The primary Coleman exact sequence was read in Sharifi's full notes Theorem5.5.31 p148 and Proposition6.1.1 pp151–152, `work/sharifi_full_iwasawa.pdf`, https://www.math.ucla.edu/~sharifi/iwasawa.pdf. At tame level1 it is

    0 -> Zp(1) -> U -> Zp[[Delta x Gamma]] -> Zp(1) ->0.

The unramified components X_omega and X_omega^-1 vanish by Stickelberger, as recorded explicitly in WWE2017 Lemma5.2.7. In the omega^-1 component (p>=5), the roots-of-unity terms and global minus units are absent, so M_omega^-1 is free of rank1. In the omega component, global minus units are exactly the kernel Zp(1), and M_omega identifies with the kernel of evaluation Lambda -> Zp(1). This is the principal ideal(gamma-u), also free of rank1. Therefore all odd components of Mminus_R are free rank1.

The plus module T=Mplus is finite free overZp: Kummer comparison makes it pseudo-isomorphic to (Xminus)^iota(1), Ferrero–Washington gives mu=0, and freeness of H excludes Zp-torsion. Under Greenberg, Xminus is pseudo-cyclic character by character (WWE §2.1), so T_e[1/p] is cyclic overR for every even e. Thus there is only one rational cyclic block per character, although its characteristic polynomial can have repeated roots. This removes the proposed multiple-block problem under this specific hypothesis.

## 3. Why integral nonfreeness can be handled degree by degree

The actual integral module Mminus need not be Lambda-free. Replacing it by a chosen free sublattice and only proving faithfulness on the subgroup generated by that lattice would be insufficient: finite index on abelianizations does not make that subgroup open in the free group. The following alternative keeps the whole H.

Choose pointed convergent Zp bases of the compact direct summands Mminus and Mplus. Such bases exist because a compact torsionfree Zp-module is a product of Zp's, and these modules are metrizable. Lift the convergent bases to convergent group elements. The quotient H->Hab is open, so the lifts can be chosen in arbitrarily small prescribed neighborhoods. For p odd, the exact Delta-eigenlift construction may be performed inside Delta-stable neighborhoods. The projective/Frattini argument already used in the main proof then identifies these lifts as a compact free pro-p basis of the entire H. Assign minus letters degree1 and plus letters degree2.

A rational R-basis of Mminus_R gives a bounded-denominator isomorphism from Mminus into R^r: finite generation supplies fixed powers ofp clearing the map and its inverse. On each completed tensor degree d, the two compositions are multiplication by a fixed power ofp. The tensor modules are Zp-torsionfree, so the map remains injective; after invertingp it is an isomorphism. Thus every fixed Magnus degree is represented by bounded-denominator measure transforms in the usual orbit variables. No denominator bound uniform over all degrees is needed.

For T_e, choose an integral multiple v_e of a rational cyclic generator and form its actual Gamma-conjugate lifts y_j=gamma^j y_0. Their abelian classes are a Qp-basis of T_e[1/p], although they may span only a finite-index integral lattice. The characteristic polynomial q_e has integral coefficients and gives an actual companion error in H'. In target generator length2, the conjugate coefficients are z^j C_e/q_e(z). An arbitrary actual integral plus-basis lift has a Qp-linear combination of these length2 coefficients plus a bounded quadratic transform of the odd letters: clear denominators, compare actual abelian classes, and take the commutator difference.

The finite rational change of plus variables and subtraction of those quadratic transforms is a homogeneous triangular automorphism of the assigned-degree completed tensor algebra. It preserves a nonzero first Magnus degree and its degreewise boundedness. The earlier partial-fraction and Laurent separation proof therefore extends under the rational module hypotheses, provided the actual C_e has nonzero restriction to every root divisor. This is a formal/module reduction, not verification of that last condition.

## 4. Exact cohomological target and twists

Let P_n=H semidirect Gamma^{p^n}. For finite coefficients A_r=Z/p^r(2), the Hochschild–Serre sequence and cd_p(H)=cd_p(Gamma_n)=1 give

    H2(P_n,A_r)^+ = H1(Gamma_n,Hom(T,A_r)).

Only T=Mplus contributes to plus parity, because the coefficient twist2 is even. Put V=Hom_Zp(T,Zp)(2). The right side is

    V/(p^r V + (gamma^{p^n}-1)V).

Under the usual generator description of H1 of a procyclic group, corestriction to the preceding cyclotomic layer is the natural quotient map on these representatives. The diagonal inverse limit n=r-1 is V: the two indicated operators tend to0 p-adically and the resulting ideals form a cofinal neighborhood system. This agrees with the known Kummer identification of H2_Iw(Zp(2))^+ with Xminus(1).

Consequently an eigenvalue alpha of T corresponds in the target to

    beta=u²/alpha,   Delta character theta=omega^{2-e}.

This is the required involution and Tate twist, not beta=alpha or u/alpha. All comparisons still need to be incorporated naturally with the cup map, not just their underlying module dimensions.

## 5. Repeated roots and an exact splitting criterion

The canonical assigned-degree-two source extension is

    0 -> W=completed wedge² Mminus -> E -> T ->0.

It comes from F2H/[H,F2H], where F2H is the inverse image of Mplus inHab. Since p is odd, the class-two logarithm is integral; the odd c-eigenspace provides a Gamma-equivariant section of the odd quotient. The even extension above contains exactly the companion error.

After a splitting scalar extension, suppose T_e is cyclic with polynomialq and generatorv. If the companion classC is divisible by gamma-alpha in W[1/p], choose A with(gamma-alpha)A=C. Then

    (q(gamma)/(gamma-alpha))*lift(v) - A

is a Gamma-eigenvector in E[1/p] lifting the nonzero alpha-eigenvector(q/(gamma-alpha))v ofT. This is true for repeated roots: that vector is the socle of the cyclic generalized-eigenspace. Conversely such a lift gives this divisibility. Since all coefficients have bounded denominators, a fixed power ofp makes the section integral on a lattice in that eigenline.

The desired final bridge is now precise. The odd cup product must be identified, via the Hochschild–Serre edge map, with pushout/transgression of this exact module extension. If that identification is proved, a split alpha-socle makes the inverse-limit odd cup image miss the corresponding root quotient of V after invertingp. Surjectivity of the FK map would then contradict the split. This is the repeated-root version of Pole-NV.

## 6. Verified coefficient-span step; remaining gate

Tripod is writing the cup/extension comparison. Its proposed Shapiro coefficient-span step has been independently checked as follows. FK's cyclotomic symbol element C is a measure in S[[Zp×]], whose finite-level coefficients are symbols{sigma_a c_r,c_r}, c_r=1-zeta_{p^r}. Let L be the closed Lambda-span of its S-valued clopen-measure coefficients. Conjugation supplies every unit-exponent pair; a p-divisible exponent is reduced to products of unit-exponent factors by the norm polynomial identity. FK finite-level symbol surjectivity makes L project onto every finite targetS_r. The diagonal inverse system defining the measure is cofinal in the two-index system, and compactness implies L=S.

What remains unproved in this checkpoint is the exact natural cup/transgression identification, with norm/corestriction compatibility, showing that those FK coefficients lie in the image controlled by the actual extension E. The calculation cannot be replaced by characteristic-ideal equality, finite-level mod-p surjectivity alone, or an unexplained generic-character specialization. No Greenberg-only kernel corollary is promoted until this step is written and independently checked.
