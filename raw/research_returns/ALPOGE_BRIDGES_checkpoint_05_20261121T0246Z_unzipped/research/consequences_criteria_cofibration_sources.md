# CC-CAT-LIT01: colimits from a cofibration structure without AB5

The precise source needed for the AB4 interval-localization argument is available without a sequential-colimit or accessibility assumption.

**Ramzi–Sosnilo–Winges (2025), Definition 2.5 and Theorem 2.6(1), (4), pp. 5–6.** For a small category of cofibrant objects, its Dwyer–Kan localization has finite colimits, and the localization preserves the initial object and pushouts along cofibrations. If the original category has \(\kappa\)-small coproducts and weak equivalences are closed under them, its localization has and preserves these coproducts. Definition 2.5 requires the initial object, all objects cofibrant, stability of cofibrations under pushout, stability of weak equivalences under pushout along cofibrations, factorization as a cofibration followed by a weak equivalence, and two-out-of-three. It does not assume sequential colimits or a presentability condition. An ordinary category is used through its nerve.

The complete proof of part (4), pp. 5–6, was read: localize the coproduct–diagonal adjunction and use commutation of localization with products of categories of cofibrant objects. The latter is the dual of Cisinski's Proposition 7.7.1. This is a source inspection, not an independent reconstruction of all the cited localization theory.

**Cisinski, precise direct source.** The author PDF presently served at the URL below is dated **30 July 2026**. In that version:

| Locator | Relevant statement |
|---|---|
| Definitions 7.4.6, pp. 360–361; 7.4.12, pp. 365–366; 7.5.7, p. 373 | Categories with fibrations/weak equivalences and their dual cofibration definitions; the all-fibrant/all-cofibrant specialization |
| Proposition 7.5.6, pp. 372–373 | Finite limits and preservation of pullbacks along fibrations after localization; dual gives finite colimits and preservation of pushouts along cofibrations |
| Proposition 7.7.1, p. 396 | Localization commutes with arbitrary set-indexed products of categories with weak equivalences and fibrations; dual version applies to cofibrations |
| Definition 7.7.2 and Proposition 7.7.4, p. 397 | The dual homotopy cocompleteness hypothesis requires the relevant coproducts and closure of cofibrations and acyclic cofibrations under those coproducts. The localization then has all colimits of the specified universe and preserves those coproducts and cofibration pushouts. |

The full short proofs of Propositions 7.5.6 and 7.7.1, and the proof of 7.7.4, were read. For an AB4 application with cofibrations the monomorphisms, exactness of coproducts supplies their closure. Closure of the chosen weak equivalences under coproducts must still be proved for that particular weak-equivalence class; the source does not provide it automatically. These are theorem applications to the axioms established in the separate interval module.

The stated smallness hypothesis can be met in a larger universe for a category that is small there. This does **not**, by itself, assert that the resulting mapping spaces are small in the original universe. No AB5, generator, accessibility, or local-smallness assertion in the original universe is imported by the citation.
