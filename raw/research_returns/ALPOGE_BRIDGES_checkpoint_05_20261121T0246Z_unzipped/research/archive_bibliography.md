# Primary bibliography and source audit for the AQ module

All mathematical attributions below distinguish the cited theorem from the deductions proved in `archive_questions.tex`. Page numbers for Milne, Achter–Casalaina-Martin–Vial, KSV and Caradot–Lin refer to the specified author/arXiv version, not a different published pagination. The original archive quotations come from the supplied transcriptions or the explicitly identified official scans recovered in this run; the intake reports are secondary records of earlier work and do not substitute for their absent proof files.
