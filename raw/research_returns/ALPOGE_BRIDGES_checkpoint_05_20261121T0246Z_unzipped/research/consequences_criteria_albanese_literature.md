# CC-ALB-LIT01: exact scope of the nearby Albanese literature

This note accompanies CC-ALB01–02 and preserves their checked proof unchanged. It compares overlapping results without a claim of priority or novelty.

| Inspected primary result | Exact hypotheses relevant here | Conclusion and limit of applicability |
|---|---|---|
| Binda–Krishna, arXiv:2202.06808v2, Theorem 1.4, p. 3; proof §3.6, p. 19 | Algebraically closed field; the reduced modulus pair of §1.1 has **smooth projective** \(X\), reduced effective Cartier divisor \(D\), and \(U=X\setminus D\) | Full torsion isomorphism from integral Suslin homology to the **generalized semiabelian** Albanese, including characteristic \(p\). Smooth projective compactification remains an assumption. |
| Binda–Krishna, Theorem 1.7, p. 5 = Theorem 5.1, p. 25 | Reduced projective pure-dimensional scheme over an algebraically closed field of **characteristic zero** | The lci Chow group → motivic cohomology map is surjective with uniquely divisible kernel. This is a different map from the ordinary Albanese map of arbitrary smooth quasiprojective \(T\). |
| Ghosh–Krishna, arXiv:2012.11249v3, Theorem 1.7, p. 4 | Smooth quasiprojective \(U\) over an algebraically closed field, **admitting an open immersion into a smooth projective variety** | Full torsion isomorphism to the generalized Albanese. The surrounding phrase “without resolution” does not remove the displayed compactification assumption. |
| Ghosh–Krishna, Theorem 1.6, p. 4 | Integral projective variety regular in codimension one | Comparison of Levine–Weibel zero-cycles with Suslin homology of its **regular locus**. An arbitrary smooth open subset is not thereby identified with the regular locus. |
| Krishna–Peláez, arXiv:1606.05810**v1**, Theorem 9.6, p. 37 | Smooth quasiprojective \(U\) of dimension \(d\), algebraically closed field, **normal projective compactification \(X\) with \(\dim(X\setminus U)\le d-2\)** | Earlier-version statement of a torsion isomorphism and abelianity of the Albanese. **The theorem and §9 are absent from v4 and the published article.** It is not cited as a theorem of the published paper. |

For the version comparison, the early Krishna–Peláez statement and its short proof were read, but the proof's Theorem 9.3 dependency was not independently reconstructed. No reason for the omission, and no conclusion that the earlier theorem is false, is inferred.

CC-ALB01 gives divisibility of the **ordinary abelian** Albanese kernel for every smooth connected quasiprojective variety over an algebraically closed field of positive characteristic, using the curve calculation, a connected pullback of a specified étale isogeny, and finite group schemes. It does not assert injectivity on \(p\)-primary torsion. CC-ALB02 combines that divisibility with the checked transfer in which one alteration degree annihilates the entire kernel image, proving the integral equality of the projective and quasiprojective parameter relations. The modern theorems inspected above have different hypotheses or concern different maps. This is a scoped comparison, not an exhaustive priority search.
