\paragraph{U-LIMIT (the stronger possible interpretation).}
The literal Fable proposal and the fixed finite polynomial identification are refuted. The remaining question is an explicit identification of the historical finite family with the constructed Xi operator, together with a direct transfer of its sign. No impossibility theorem about every such transformation is asserted. The direct signed-kernel and positive trace-coefficient candidates are refuted in \fid{DET-12}. The transformed kernel of \fid{DET-13} supplies positive trace-class frames, but no arithmetic measure with the Xi spectrum. The finite-target period frames of \fid{DET-14} are also genuinely positive; their fixed target bounds determinant degree and their numerical hypotheses bound the shift cutoff at fixed data. Those restrictions apply to that realization.

The stronger source construction in \fid{DET-15} closes the common-Hilbert-space and operator-existence gates: actual Bessel convolutions give infinitely many orthogonal rank-one projections on one fixed Bessel $L^2$ space. Every complex $\ell^1$ weighting gives a normal trace-class operator with an explicit measurable kernel, exact finite trace-norm approximants and the product determinant. The weights $\gamma_j^{-2}$ give $B\oplus0$ and the exact Xi determinant without RH. Under the stipulated RH theorem they are positive. The source actually instantiates this local projector construction within its retained global hypotheses.

\fid{DET-16} then closes the arithmetic scalar-endpoint reconstruction: the explicit prime-sum kernel $k$ determines its frequencies and integer multiplicities by Fourier averages, and those data prescribe a full-multiplicity positive operator and the exact weights on the Bessel projections. This construction has no input enumeration of zeros, but its positivity criterion is itself RH-equivalent. The remaining direct-sign gate is therefore a source-supported identification of the original finite period/determinant family with these operators or their positive kernel, with the sign transferred from that finite construction rather than inferred through RH. The historical $W$ and the source's weighted-character power-window specialization remain to be matched. Positivity and an arithmetic trace alone do not close this gap: \fid{DET-17} gives prime-weighted source convolutions with trace $-\zeta'/\zeta$, but proves their determinant at exponent $2$ differs from $G$ by a zero-count obstruction. Infinite unweighted or $0/1$ orthogonal families are not trace class. No universal impossibility statement is inferred from these particular failures.

A weaker scalar correspondence could already recover the sign consequence without identifying positive source Gram matrices. For example, a specified positive normalization of an actual source determinant equal to
\[
 W_{\rm std}(q_0+q_{-\log X})=2k(0)+2k(\log X)
\]
for all sufficiently large $X$ would, by its nonnegativity and \fid{STEP6-03}, give
$G_\zeta(X)\ge b(X)-2\lambda_1\sqrt X$, hence RH and then the stated positive $B$. This is an explicit sufficient reduction already proved by the Weil calculation; the missing input is the determinant, normalization and specialization realizing that scalar identity. The stronger claim that the original finite matrices are the constructed operator approximants needs its own correspondence. \fid{DET-19} rules out one proposed compatible flow: the arithmetic kernel and its pure-point translations cannot be the natural source Lorentz-boost trace coefficient and action. That obstruction leaves other specified flows and the scalar certificate route open.

The actual weighted-character scalar is also represented exactly in \fid{DET-18}: integer multiplication $M=2(3o+1)$ yields an ordinary completed-product determinant and a scaled resolvent trace with all Mellin corrections. Omitting this multiplication has the proved noncentral and central divisor-integrality criterion. Thus the remaining character/window issue is its correspondence with the original finite construction, not the existence of a weighted scalar determinant representation. The operator inequality encoding the retained Dirichlet GRH is $0\le C_{\mathcal L,M}\le4I$; positivity alone is not the complete condition for that transform.

This is supporting search evidence only, not a proof that an unnamed equivalent construction is impossible.
