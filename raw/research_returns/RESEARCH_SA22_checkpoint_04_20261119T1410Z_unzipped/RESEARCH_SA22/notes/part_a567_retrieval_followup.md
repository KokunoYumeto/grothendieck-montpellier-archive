* Section2, equations(2.1)–(2.2), pp.80–82: for genus p>=3, take curves X,Y of genera1,p−2, two marked points on each, disjoint local-unit-disc coordinates, remove the parameter-sized discs, and identify by z1w1=t and z2w2=t^(2p−3). This is a holomorphic family over the punctured disc.
* Section6, equations(6.1)–(6.2), p.88: the same fiber is obtained by cutting at radii |t|^(1/2) and |t|^((2p−3)/2) and gluing corresponding circles by those product equations. This directly supports the retained-coordinate-germ welding/plumbing comparison; it does not identify arbitrary canonical capped surfaces with a fixed plumbing atlas.
* Section7, Theorem3, pp.90–91: for homotopically independent simple curves on a closed surface of genus p>=2, let tau_j denote the **negative** Dehn twists with the article's monodromy convention. A holomorphic punctured-disc family with monodromy product tau_j^(n_j) exists exactly when every integer n_j is nonnegative. The necessity uses a holomorphic embedding Phi:T_p→C^(3p−3) with image in the product of upper half-planes and Phi(tau_j(t))=Phi(t)+e_j; the original expressly cites Kra1990 and also the unpublished joint Earle–Marden work. Lemma7.1 proves the sign restriction by the order of vanishing of the descended punctured-disc map. This is an original Earle–Sipe proof, not a newly proved assertion in the present report.
