     For each finite group impose membership in Av as follows. In a matrix model compute,

     For completeness, the truncated entries are computed without an infinite-series oracle.
Each nonzero basis entry has the form T e u for a known exponent e with |e| ≤ M and a
unit u given by the diagonal and weighted-shift formulas. Lift the required unit roots to
precision 3M + 3 by the simple-root Hensel iteration. After multiplication by T M , this
precision more than supplies all terms through T 2M . For the rational coefficients of (6.13),

of every basis vector. Taking their span gives B and retains a linear map with which to
lift its elements. The ansatz contains every section by Lemma 6.5; the imposed conditions
are necessary and sufficient at every exceptional place, and elsewhere the ansatz is already
integral. Thus the computed spaces are exactly those in (6.9).

6.5   Algorithm and polynomial bounds
The complete procedure can now be stated without any existential computational step.

1. Form the multiplication table of E in the basis Y a V b . Compute the square-free groups
   Hi and the local models, obtaining their residue roots by Lemma 2.2 and their needed
   precision by Hensel lifting.
2. Use (6.13) and the divisibility conditions above to compute S and its evaluation map to
   Mq (k).
3. In the image B, compute the trace-pairing kernel, its common column kernel, and a
   rank-one idempotent a0 = vw as in Lemma 6.4.
4. Lift a0 to b ∈ S by a linear solve and compute e = bq with the multiplication table.
