Proposition 7.1 (Class division). Let k, q, F , C, and σ be as in Proposition 5.1, and suppose
the auxiliary primary-group data required by Theorem 6.1 are available. Let P = (x, 0) be a
specified k-rational ramification point. Given a represented degree-zero divisor D satisfying
char k > (N + 2∥D∥ + 1)2 , under the promise that

                                  [D] ∈ (1 − σ) Pic0 (C)(k),

there is a deterministic procedure LambdaDivide(D, P ) returning a degree-zero divisor D′
with
                             ∥D′ ∥ ≤ 2g,  (1 − σ)[D′ ] = [D].
It uses polynomially many field operations in the representation size, q, N, ∥D∥, and log |k|,
with the uniform norm bound from Theorem 6.1. It is compatible with simultaneous execution
over the algebra of the unknown roots of F .

   The procedure consists of three steps.

1. Compute j = h+ /h− by Lemma 5.2. Put b = vX=x (j) and normalize G = cj so that
   G/F b has residue 1 at X = x.
2. Use Theorem 6.1 to find a with Norm(a) = G. Set E = D − div(a), and form the
   coefficientwise maximum V of 0, E, E + σE, . . . , E + · · · + σ q−2 E.
3. Return ReduceDivisor(V − (deg V )P ).

We prove first that the norm equation in the second step is solvable, and then that the
maximum gives the required class division.
