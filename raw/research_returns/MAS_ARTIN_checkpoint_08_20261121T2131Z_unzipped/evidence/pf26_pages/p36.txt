    The procedure LambdaDivide of Proposition 7.1 takes a reduced degree-zero divisor D
and a known ramification point Pi . Its promise is that [D] has a λ-division represented by a
divisor over k. It returns a reduced degree-zero divisor D′ over k satisfying

                                          λ[D′ ] = [D].

All these procedures use field arithmetic and scalar zero tests, so they admit the simultaneous
execution just described. At any test that distinguishes components, OddSplit stops and
returns the resulting factor.
