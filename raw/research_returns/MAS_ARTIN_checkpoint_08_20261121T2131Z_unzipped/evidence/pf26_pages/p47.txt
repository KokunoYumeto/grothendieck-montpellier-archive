     The argument also identifies a consequence of the algebraic reduction alone. GRH for
finite-order Hecke L-functions places their nontrivial zeros on ℜs = 1/2, and therefore implies
the strip used above. Thus the reduction yields deterministic polynomial-time factorization
under GRH independently of the companion’s proof of Theorem 11.1. Theorem 1.1 uses the
cited theorem to supply the same input without that assumption.
