This result uses the actual codimension-two closure and bounded moment from MO011; it does not assume an unproved Hilbert theta inverse.

Every nonzero compact smooth convolution has a surjective two-moment outgoing map on the genuine pole-free theta source. The five-bump witnesses are ordinary compact additive functions. Every nontrivial individual translation also fails to preserve the closed domain.

The stronger invariant-subspace statement uses beta_a nonzero almost everywhere and the L1 Fourier transform of (V_a y) beta_a. If the full orbit of y lies in K, that transform vanishes identically and y=0. Thus no nonzero translation-equivariant map can have range in K. This does not concern other target spaces or the already constructed quotient return maps.
