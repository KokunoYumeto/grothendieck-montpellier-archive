## Scope and conclusion

The primary sources below were inspected through their theorem statements and relevant surrounding definitions. Searches used both available search systems, including current queries with recency filters, combinations of determinant/permanent, border/symmetric border rank, det4/per4, 39/40/42, Farnsworth, border apolarity, and 2025/2026. Citation chains in the principal border-apolarity papers were followed. No polynomial-tensor CP or symmetric border-rank lower bound of at least 39 for det_4 or per_4 was located in the sources checked. That is an audit outcome, not a proof that none exists. The paper should say that its bound is proved here and improves the specific verified comparisons; it should not infer historical priority from this search.

Distinguish throughout:

| Object | Ambient tensor space over C | Rank conventions |
|---|---|---|
| Row determinant D_n | (C^n)^{tensor n} | CP rank and CP border rank |
| Row permanent P_n | (C^n)^{tensor n} | CP rank and CP border rank; P_n is also the polarized squarefree monomial |
| Polynomial determinant/permanent coefficient tensor S_det,n / S_per,n | (C^{n^2})^{tensor n}, invariant under slot permutations | CP rank/border rank allow independent factors; symmetric border rank permits powers |
| Ordinary polynomial det_n/per_n | Sym^n(C^{n^2}) | Waring rank and Waring border rank; identification with preceding row requires the characteristic-zero convention |

For a symmetric tensor, CP border rank <= symmetric border rank <= Waring rank. A Waring-rank lower bound alone is not a border-rank lower bound. A cactus-rank lower bound alone is not a border-rank lower bound either; do not silently replace cactus rank by its border version.

## How to compare the present method to border apolarity

Substitution of j dimensions is standard in LIT-LM. The new calculation to justify is the exact minimum over Gr(j,U), together with its lower and upper certificates. Degenerating a residual subspace by a torus to a coordinate subspace is a symmetry reduction of the kind used throughout tensor geometry; it must not be presented as invented here. Its elementary row-echelon proof and explicit coordinate support calculation can nevertheless establish a new evaluated minimum in the present setting.

LIT-CHL's fixed-ideal method produces a projective parameter space of candidate apolar ideals. LIT-BB26 Thm.4.7 provides the all-field simultaneous version directly. Neither source says that an arbitrary ideal with the expected Hilbert function suffices: one must preserve membership in Slip. The present proper Grassmann incidence and concrete flattenings are therefore a separate direct certificate, compatible with the apolar framework. No claim is made that they run the entire border-apolarity algorithm or classify its ideal space.

## FL-06. A false broad bound in a recent survey must not enter the comparison

[Dutta–Lysikov, *Recent Advances in Debordering Methods*, arXiv:2510.13049v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.13049v1), §4.3, printed p.29/PDF leaf29, immediately after Corollary56, states a lower bound binom(2n,n) for border Waring rank of both det_n and per_n. Taken literally this is false. At n=2 it would give6, but the two polynomials ad+bc and ad-bc have border Waring rank4 over C.

Proof of the counterexample. In either sign, the symmetric coefficient matrix is nonsingular, so the first catalecticant gives rank at least4, including on border decompositions. Conversely,

    ad ± bc = ((a+d)^2 - (a-d)^2 ± (b+c)^2 ∓ (b-c)^2)/4.

Scalars have square roots over C, so this is a sum of four squares after absorbing the coefficients. Hence the rank and border rank are4. This refutes the displayed universal claim. The standard middle-catalecticant comparison actually verified in LIT-LT is binom(n,floor(n/2))^2. We do not assert which correction the survey authors intended. QED.

The displayed bound was also checked visually in the primary PDF, printed p.29/PDF leaf29, on2026-11-21; the underline and binomial are present in the actual PDF, not merely an HTML/OCR artifact. Whole counterexample proof read by the author on2026-11-21; both the lower and upper parts checked. This is a targeted proof check.
