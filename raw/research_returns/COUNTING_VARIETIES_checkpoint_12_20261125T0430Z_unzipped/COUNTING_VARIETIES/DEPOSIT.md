## Title

Counting satisfying assignments on smooth projective varieties, after Katz

## Abstract

Katz expressed the Frobenius action on the top coherent cohomology of an anticanonical hypersurface as a coefficient of its equation. We combine this description with a Boolean coefficient encoding and an explicit smoothing construction. At every fixed prime, modular counting is complete even for smooth geometrically integral projective hypersurfaces whose top coherent cohomology is a line, when their equations are represented by arithmetic circuits. An affine-linear stabilization preserves the coefficient over an entire parameter space. Explicit sparse equations give several tractable cases: one bounded-degree residual cofactor suffices for deterministic scalar recovery from a saturated hypersurface presentation. In fixed codimension, bounded complete-intersection excess after certified coordinate, digit and prime-field relation reductions gives an always-correct randomized algorithm of expected polynomial time, with no bound on the other binary degrees; maximum-degree or linear-equation hypotheses permit deterministic recovery. A smooth cubic over the integers records the exact count in its Tate multiplicity, Chow groups, and algebraic K-theory. Further constructions treat structured witnesses, affine hyperplane complements, and fixed-field Frobenius multiplicities. Unrestricted saturation, effective projective compression, and modular encoding with small total Betti number remain open.
