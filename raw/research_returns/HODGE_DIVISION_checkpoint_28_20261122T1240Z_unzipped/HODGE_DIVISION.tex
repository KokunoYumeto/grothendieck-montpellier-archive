\begin{abstract}
We construct integral effective detectors and explicit division bounds for specified Weil families. An integral orthogonal generator of the Weil plane reduces the supplied detector exponent from four to two. The even principal Gaussian fourfold bound is48. Direct symmetric-product suppliers give principal sixfold bounds746496 for the Gaussian even lattice and30375 for the split Eisenstein lattice. Reconstructed reflection-group calculations have exact period ranks16 for $G_{34}$ and9 for $G_{24}$, with integral supplier bounds. An actual equivariantly semiregular CM sheaf, an explicit regularity calculation and a vector-bundle degeneracy cycle give
$\delta_W\le10^{258}d^{69}f^{33}\Delta^6$
for every split Weil sixfold over each of the nine class-number-one fields $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-d})$. Explicit CM-surface contractions give bounds for all fourfold determinant classes over those fields, and the $G_{34}$ construction covers every Eisenstein sixfold determinant. Standard imprimitive representations factor through actual cyclic quotient maps; for every $n\ge5$ their sharp maximal period ranks are $3n$ and $3n-1$. A further reconstructed E8 source gives period rank9 and an explicit integral bound on every specified Gaussian sixfold lattice of determinant class $[-3]$. The remaining nonsplit sixfold components are specified. Every bound states its polarization and integral normalization. Whole-proof readings, independent reconstructions and carried inputs have separate check events.
 Follow-up 1 proves $\delta_W\le36$ on the specified principal Eisenstein fourfold component and $c_t\le17\delta\lceil\sqrt T\rceil$, with actual multiplier $3\delta$. A matched-product sequence has $J_W=p^{4k}$ and detectors of degree8. Explicit conormal maps prove semiregularity for graphs and nearby smooth Schoen surfaces, and compute the proper whole-class deformation loci. The existing sixfold resolves the requested one-fibre S-14 existence question after the source hypotheses are stated exactly. New full-character and invariant trace calculations exclude the stated real-multiplication product and abelian-direct-summand suppliers. 
 The original canonical norm-image surface is now constructed and proved semiregular for every connected etale cyclic triple cover of a nonhyperelliptic genus-three curve: each component contracts one pencil, has degree36 and conormal rank12. Explicit integral cycles also represent $\theta^2/2$ and the index-three lattice $(1-\omega)W_{\mathbb Z}$. 
The terminal rank-four tensor alternative for missing sixfold components
is closed by actual block induction and an exact ramification identity;
the remaining natural-curve gate has every nonidentity moving rank at least five.
For a general cyclic triple cover of a genus-five curve, the canonical norm
fourfold has a normal Abel image with rational singularities. Its actual
derived pushforward is the simple sheaf $\mathcal O_Y$, with computed whole
character and Weil norm eighteen. Its full degree-two trace has an explicit
invariant kernel of dimension at least four on the retained geometric open.
Further full-character and filtered Hochschild arguments exclude the stated
coupled real-multiplication sources.
The remaining curve source excludes every sporadic projective socle,
nine specified Lie-type families, every Lie-type defining field of size
at least29, and the specified scalar-centre symplectic-type normal-subgroup
multiplicities through fourteen. An actual direct-sum tangent injection excludes
all nonzero virtual ranks with adjacent simple Hom-orthogonal RM endpoints.
The norm fourfold has the computed scalar-action algebra, injective
Picard pullback and finite faithful point actions by translations of order
one or three. Exact coherence rescaling and torsor descent identify their
trace kernels with the ordinary kernels on the actual quotient sheaves.
The actual normal-obstruction map proves failure of weak semiregularity
for these norm sheaves; the entire trace kernel is not computed.
Invariant endpoint maps further exclude all adjacent semistable
torsion-free RM pairs of virtual rank one in absolute value with
invariant forward cross-Hom dimension at most one and vanishing
negative invariant self-Ext, with arbitrary endpoint endomorphism
algebras. The exact point-action and coupling tangent injection
also holds for every adjacent balanced object. The norm fourfold
has irregularity eight and its actual Albanese map to the Prym
is an isomorphism. Its actual degree-two ambient restriction is an
isomorphism of dimension twenty-eight, and the excess boundary
map is injective. Every alternating projective socle is excluded.
The remaining classical Lie-type families have explicit finite natural-rank
bounds over the retained fields of size at most27.
At the original full-character weak semiregularity gate, adjacent
coherent RM endpoints of positive rank and invariant forward Hom
dimension at most three leave precisely the three rows of R35.
The norm obstruction extends through S40 to the stated complexes
locally annihilated by the support ideal in the derived category
off a codimension-two set, with nonzero signed generic rank.
An explicit gluable indecomposable dual cone verifies this scope.
Four nonempty rational nine-letter profiles have exact original
period ranks five, six, seven and eight; a prescribed-unit
polynomial matrix selects the rank throughout the stated range.
The unrestricted RM seed and the remaining norm-complex classes
retain their precisely stated open gates.
