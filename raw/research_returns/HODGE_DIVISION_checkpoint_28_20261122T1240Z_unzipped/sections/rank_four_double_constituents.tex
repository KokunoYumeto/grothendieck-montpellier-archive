\section{HD-I25: both doubled bireflection constituents are excluded}
\label{sec:rank-four-double-constituents}

In the complete source setting of HD-I23, the pairs
\((e,h)=(1,2)\) and \((2,1)\) supply no missing period-rank-nine
sixfold determinant. More precisely their actual original-field
coefficient sources either map by the polarized isogenies of
(I23.9)--(I23.12) to an already supplied rank-four source, or
have period rank at most eight. This closes both size-two
constituent alternatives, without assuming that a complex
constituent or its original block system is defined over \(K\).

\paragraph{1. The exact classification input.}
HD-I23, paragraph7, gives an actual irreducible constituent of
rank \(r\ge11\), whose faithful image \(B\) is generated by
actual bireflections and has no reflection. For \((1,2)\) it is
realized on the specified \(E\)-space \(U\), where
\([E:K]=2\). For \((2,1)\) its original \(K\)-realization is
the multiplicity-two representation on \(U\); we do not yet
choose a \(K\)-realization of the single constituent.

We apply the bireflection specialization of Guralnick--Saxl,
\emph{Generation of finite almost simple groups by conjugates},
J. Algebra \textbf{268} (2003), 519--571, Theorem8.4,
pp.557--558, and Theorem7.1, pp.548--549, with Lemma7.4,
p.551 and the scalar-retaining formulation Theorem7.A, p.521.
In characteristic zero and degree at least eleven the complete
possibilities are: a line system with permutation image \(A_r\)
or \(S_r\); two-dimensional blocks, \(r=2t\), with image
\(S_t\); or a primitive projectively deleted permutation
representation of \(A_{r+1}\) or \(S_{r+1}\).
The tensor alternative has degree four, and the remaining
primitive exception of degree greater than eight has degree
ten, so neither occurs. Only the actual codimension-two
specialization is used, independent of any general radical
threshold in the theorem's indexed text.

\paragraph{2. The monomial case, including the multiplicity-two descent.}
Let \(D\) be the diagonal kernel on the \(r\) original lines.
The field-free argument of HD-S40, paragraph2, proves that
\(D\) is not scalar for every \(r\ge7\). It applies to
this actual bireflection-generated group: its proof gives the
weighted three-cycle matrices and a literal perfect
point-stabilizer subgroup, then contradicts irreducibility
when the kernel is scalar. Every abelian normal subgroup of
\(B\) maps trivially to \(A_r\) or \(S_r\), so \(D\)
is its unique largest abelian normal subgroup and is
characteristic. Two-transitivity makes its line characters
\(\chi_j\) pairwise distinct; equality of all of them would
make \(D\) scalar.

For \((1,2)\), perform the following averaging on the actual
\(E\)-representation. For \((2,1)\), perform it on the
actual \(K\)-representation of dimension \(2r\). In either
case the literal projectors are
\[
                   f_j=\frac1{|D|}\sum_{d\in D}
                                  \chi_j(d)^{-1}\rho(d).
                                                               \tag{I25.1}
\]
On the single constituent they have rank one; on the
multiplicity-two realization they have rank two. Their images
are exactly the eigenspaces for the distinct \(D\)-characters,
so they are the complete primitive central idempotents of the
image algebra of \(D\). Adjoin the needed roots of unity to
the relevant base field, \(E\) or \(K\). Galois automorphisms
permute these projectors and commute with the original
\(A_r\) or \(S_r\) permutation action. Its permutation
centralizer is trivial: the point stabilizer fixes only its
own label. Thus each \(f_j\) is defined over that base field.
This proves descent directly on the original matrices also
when the constituent initially had no chosen \(K\)-form.

The full source stabilizer normalizes \(D\), including its
semilinear action in the \(E/K\) case, so it permutes this
same projector set. For \((1,2)\), transfer the rank-one
\(E\)-projectors through the inverse maps (I23.7); their
images are actual \(K\)-rank-two blocks. For \((2,1)\)
they already have \(K\)-rank two. Orthogonality follows
from the distinct finite-group characters and the original
positive coefficient form. The block-stabilizer coefficient
representation is absolutely irreducible and of exact field
\(K\), by the explicit induction argument in HD-I23.
Its actual quotient curves, rational corner, and polarization
isogenies are (I23.9)--(I23.12), now with coefficient rank two.

The parabolic dimension formula for this faithful rank-two
system is \(6=2(2g-2)+\sum c_j\), \(c_j\ge1\).
Its marked-base dimension is at most seven in genus zero,
at most six in genus one, and at most five in genus two;
higher genus is impossible. Thus both monomial cases have
period rank at most seven.

\paragraph{3. The two-dimensional-block case.}
Here \(r=2t\ge12\), so \(t\ge6\). Let \(T\) be the
kernel on the original complex two-dimensional blocks.
The abstract characteristic-kernel theorem HD-G28 applies to
the faithful single-constituent group \(B\). It gives actual
tree coordinates
\[
 B=T\rtimes S_t,\qquad T\subset L^t,\qquad (L')^t\subset T,
                                                               \tag{I25.2}
\]
where the coordinate group \(L\subset GL_2(\mathbf C)\)
is irreducible. In particular \(L'\ne1\), and localized
elements of \(L'\) distinguish the original block
representations of \(T\). They are irreducible and pairwise
inequivalent. These conclusions and the characteristicness
of \(T\) are statements about the faithful abstract image
\(B\); they do not require a prior \(K\)-form of its single
constituent.

Write \(\psi_j\) for these irreducible rank-two characters.
On the actual \(E\)-representation in case \((1,2)\), or
the actual multiplicity-two \(K\)-representation in case
\((2,1)\), use
\[
                   f_j=\frac2{|T|}\sum_{t\in T}
                                       \psi_j(t^{-1})\rho(t).
                                                               \tag{I25.3}
\]
Character averaging gives precisely the original constituent
block projectors, of rank two in the first realization and
rank four in the second. They are the full primitive central
idempotent set of the corresponding image algebra; multiplicity
two only tensors each matrix algebra with \(I_2\).
Galois automorphisms over the actual base field commute with
the full \(S_t\)-permutation action. Its centralizer on
the labels is trivial, so (I25.3) is over \(E\) or \(K\),
respectively. This is the literal calculation of HD-G29,
paragraphs2--3, with the multiplicity retained in every matrix.
Characteristicness of \(T\) makes the whole source stabilizer
permute these projectors, also under its possible field
involution. In both cases they give actual \(K\)-rank-four
orthogonal blocks.

Use (I23.9)--(I23.12) on one such block. A period-rank-nine
rank-four source must be over the sphere, have at least twelve
branch values, and total codimension fourteen. It therefore
contains a reflection. HD-S36 and HD-G27 give a supplied
determinant for exactly this actual quotient source. The
displayed polarized isogenies retain the determinant class
of the originally prescribed sixfold.

\paragraph{4. The primitive constituent is a literal alternating module.}
Put \(s=r+1\). The remaining classification alternative
provides a complex intertwiner to the augmentation module
\(W_0\subset\mathbf C^s\) such that every \(b\in B\)
acts as \(a_bP_{\pi_b}|_{W_0}\). The pair is unique:
a nonidentity permutation of \(s\ge12\) points cannot be
scalar on its augmentation module. Consequently both
\(b\mapsto a_b\) and \(b\mapsto\pi_b\) are actual
homomorphisms in these coordinates.

For a generating bireflection, \(a_b\ne1\) would require
fixed dimension at most \(\lfloor s/2\rfloor\). Indeed
each permutation cycle having eigenvalue \(a_b^{-1}\ne1\)
has length at least two and contributes only one eigenvector;
removing the constant summand does not increase that count.
But \(\lfloor(r+1)/2\rfloor<r-2\) for \(r\ge11\),
contrary to its actual fixed dimension \(r-2\). Every
generator therefore has \(a_b=1\). A permutation with
augmentation moving rank two is a three-cycle or two disjoint
transpositions, hence is even. The projective permutation
image from the classification is \(A_s\) or \(S_s\);
the generating observation makes it exactly \(A_s\).
Thus the supplied complex intertwiner identifies the actual
group with the literal rational augmentation \(A_s\),
without a remaining scalar extension. Its ordinary character
is rational.

For \((e,h)=(1,2)\), this contradicts the exact field
identity \(K\mathbf Q(\chi(B))=E\ne K\) from (I23.8).
For \((2,1)\), construct the actual original-field tensor
map as follows. Use the just specified homomorphism
\(B\simeq A_s\) to make \(W_K\) the rational augmentation
representation over \(K\), and set
\[
 M=\operatorname{Hom}_{K[B]}(W_K,U),\qquad
 \dim_KM=2,\qquad
 C:W_K\otimes_KM\longrightarrow U,\quad w\otimes f\mapsto f(w).
                                                               \tag{I25.4}
\]
The intertwiner equations have coefficients in \(K\), so
their rank is unchanged by extension to \(\mathbf C\).
There the known multiplicity is two; hence \(M\) has the
stated dimension and evaluation is an isomorphism. Explicitly,
choose a \(K\)-basis \(f_1,f_2\) of that finite linear
system and a basis \(w_j\) of \(W_K\); the columns
\(f_a(w_j)\) form the invertible matrix of \(C\), whose
inverse over \(K\) is the desired map. This proves the
tensor decomposition of the actual coefficient space.

The original source stabilizer \(H\) normalizes the literal
\(A_s\) on its first factor. Paragraphs 4 and 5 of HD-S41
prove its complex normalizer is \(\mathbf C^*S_s\), using
the actual invariant quadratic and cubic forms and their
rank-two Hessian locus. Combine this with the commutant of
the matrix algebra of \(A_s\) on (I25.4). Each transported
element of \(H\) has the unique form
\[
                               P_\pi\otimes B',\qquad B'\in GL_2(K).
                                                               \tag{I25.5}
\]
The field assertion follows by multiplying its original
\(K\)-matrix by \(P_\pi^{-1}\otimes I\): the result
is \(I\otimes B'\), so each entry of \(B'\) lies in
\(K\). Consequently the actual finite image satisfies
\[
                    A_s\times1\subset H\subset S_s\times GL_2(K).
                                                               \tag{I25.6}
\]
There may be a shared sign quotient; no splitting of it is
asserted. The source has no moving rank below four by
HD-I23, paragraph7. HD-I24 applies to precisely (I25.6),
with its normalized fibre-product curves and its actual
averaged corner projector. It proves period rank at most
eight for the originally specified polarized factor.
This closes the last alternative and completes both cases.
