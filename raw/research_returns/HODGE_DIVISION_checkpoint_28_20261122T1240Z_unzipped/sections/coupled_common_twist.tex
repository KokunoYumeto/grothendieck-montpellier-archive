\section{HD-G22: a common twist couples the ample line and preserves a trace kernel}
\label{sec:coupled-common-twist}

\paragraph{The actual modified construction.}
Keep the abelian eightfold, field action, polarization, signed graph
sums \(P_0,N_0\), and integral identity
\(\operatorname{ch}(P_0)-\operatorname{ch}(N_0)=16w_d\) of HD-G21.
Write \(\theta=\theta_1+\cdots+\theta_4\), where
\(\theta_j=d x_jy_j+u_jv_j\), and take the actual product line bundle
\(M\) with
\[
             D=c_1(M)=2\theta_1+2\theta_2.
\]
Put \(P=L\oplus(M\otimes P_0)\) and \(N=M\otimes N_0\).
For every actual degree-two map \(\delta=(\delta_L,\delta_0):P\to N[2]\),
form
\begin{equation}\label{eq:G22-object}
 N[1]\longrightarrow E\longrightarrow P
                         \xrightarrow{\delta}N[2].
\end{equation}
Both graph-to-graph maps and maps from \(L\) occur nontrivially.
All these objects have no negative self-Ext and whole character
\(e^\theta+16w_d\). Even when \(\delta_L\ne0\) genuinely
couples \(L\), their degree-two Atiyah trace has a kernel of dimension
at least twelve. The conclusion holds for the invariant trace under
every coherent finite point action after isogeny pullback, and after
actual Fourier--Mukai transport of the object and action.

\paragraph{Whole character and actual line-to-graph maps.}
In each elliptic pair one has the literal exterior identity
\[
 (d x_jy_j+u_jv_j)(u_j-sx_j)(v_j-sy_j)=0,
 \qquad s^2=-d.
\]
The only surviving two terms are \(d x_jy_ju_jv_j\) and its
negative. The same identity holds for \(-s\), so
\(\theta_j w_d=0\) for every \(j\). Consequently
\(e^Dw_d=w_d\), proving the stated whole character.

For a graph \(B_\epsilon\), let \(n_j=d+\epsilon_j^2>0\).
The actual line bundle \((M\otimes L^{-1})|_{B_\epsilon}\)
is the external product of elliptic line bundles with degrees
\[
                         (n_1,n_2,-n_3,-n_4).
\]
Positive elliptic degrees have only \(H^0\), and negative ones
only \(H^1\), with dimension the absolute degree. Thus Kunneth gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:G22-index2}
 \operatorname{Ext}^a(L,M\otimes\mathcal O_{B_\epsilon})=0
       \quad(a\ne2),\qquad
 \dim\operatorname{Ext}^2(L,M\otimes\mathcal O_{B_\epsilon})
                         =\prod_j n_j>0.
\end{equation}
Tensor a nonzero section in each positive factor with a nonzero
\(H^1\) class in each negative factor to obtain an actual nonzero
\(\delta_L\) on any selected negative graph summand. Serre duality
on the eightfold gives concentration in degree six in the reverse
direction. The common line-bundle tensor leaves every graph-to-graph
Ext group unchanged, so the six-dimensional edge spaces of HD-G21
are still available for \(\delta_0\).

Distinct positive and negative supports give no graph-to-graph Hom,
and \eqref{eq:G22-index2} gives no Hom from \(L\) to \(N\).
Thus \(\operatorname{Hom}(P,N)=0\). The exact negative-degree
isomorphism of HD-G21, applied to \eqref{eq:G22-object}, proves
\(\operatorname{Ext}^{<0}(E,E)=0\). All objects are perfect.

\paragraph{The graph part and the actual extension by \(L\).}
Define the actual graph cone
\[
 N[1]\longrightarrow G\longrightarrow M\otimes P_0
                             \xrightarrow{\delta_0}N[2].
\]
It is the tensor by \(M\) of the graph cone of HD-G21. In particular
the twelve scalar classes
\[
 f_{ij}=\eta_{ij}\operatorname{id}_G\in\operatorname{Ext}^2(G,G),
 \qquad i\ne j,
\]
are linearly independent and have zero full Atiyah trace, by the
exact restriction and Hom maps proved there. Their whole scalar
trace is also directly \(16\eta_{ij}w_d=0\).

The block decomposition of the actual cone
\eqref{eq:G22-object} gives a triangle
\begin{equation}\label{eq:G22-extension}
            G\xrightarrow{i}E\xrightarrow{q_L}L
                                      \xrightarrow{\kappa}G[1].
\end{equation}
One may construct it in the complex of sheaves by projecting the
\(P\)-block to \(L\); its fibre is the displayed graph cone.
The connecting class is the image of \(\delta_L\) under
\(\operatorname{Ext}^2(L,N)\to\operatorname{Ext}^1(L,G)\).
This map is an isomorphism: the two adjacent groups
\(\operatorname{Hom}(L,M\otimes P_0)\) and
\(\operatorname{Ext}^1(L,M\otimes P_0)\) vanish by
\eqref{eq:G22-index2}. Hence nonzero \(\delta_L\) yields a
nonsplit \eqref{eq:G22-extension}. The line summand has been
coupled by an actual nonzero extension.

Applying \(\operatorname{Hom}(L,-)\) to the graph triangle and
using \eqref{eq:G22-index2} gives concentration
\[
 \operatorname{Ext}^a(L,G)=0\quad(a\notin\{1,2\}),
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Ext}^1(L,G)\simeq\operatorname{Ext}^2(L,N),
 \quad
 \operatorname{Ext}^2(L,G)\simeq
                         \operatorname{Ext}^2(L,M\otimes P_0).
\]
In particular \(\operatorname{Ext}^3(L,G)=0\).
Serre duality gives \(\operatorname{Ext}^1(G,L)=0\), since
\(\operatorname{Ext}^7(L,G)=0\). These two exact vanishings,
rather than a splitting of \(E\), control the next calculation.

\paragraph{The exact endomorphism lift and its trace.}
Applying \(\operatorname{Hom}(-,G[2])\) to
\eqref{eq:G22-extension} gives a surjection
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}(E,G[2])\longrightarrow
       \operatorname{Ext}^2(G,G),\qquad \beta\longmapsto\beta i,
\]
because the next group is \(\operatorname{Ext}^3(L,G)=0\).
For each of the twelve specified \(f_{ij}\), choose a lift
\(\beta_{ij}:E\to G[2]\) and define the actual endomorphism
\[
                         z_{ij}=i[2]\beta_{ij}:E\longrightarrow E[2].
\]
These twelve classes are independent, for every choice of lifts.
Indeed, for a linear combination \(\beta\), an equation
\(i[2]\beta=0\) implies by exactness of
\(\operatorname{Hom}(E,-)\) that
\(\beta=\kappa[1]a\) for some \(a:E\to L[1]\).
Restriction to \(G\) gives
\(\beta i=\kappa[1]ai=0\), since
\(ai\in\operatorname{Ext}^1(G,L)=0\).
But \(\beta i\) is the same linear combination of the independent
\(f_{ij}\). All its coefficients therefore vanish.

Naturality of the Atiyah class and cyclicity of its trace give,
component by component,
\[
 \operatorname{tr}_E\!\left(i[2]\beta_{ij}
                 \frac{\operatorname{At}_E^a}{a!}\right)
  =\operatorname{tr}_G\!\left(\beta_{ij}i
                 \frac{\operatorname{At}_G^a}{a!}\right),
 \qquad \sigma_E^2(z_{ij})=\sigma_G^2(f_{ij})=0.
\]
The maps being interchanged have degrees zero and two, so no odd
cyclic sign appears. This proves the claimed trace-kernel bound with
the actual differential \(\delta\) retained. At the level of
extension classes the same calculation says that
\(\eta_{ij}\kappa\) has a nullhomotopy because its precise target
\(\operatorname{Ext}^3(L,G)\) vanishes; the lifts above supply
the resulting actual degree-two endomorphisms.

\paragraph{Arbitrary coherent finite point actions.}
The cohomology sheaves of \(E\) are \(N\) in degree \(-1\)
and \(L\oplus M\otimes P_0\) in degree zero. Exactly as in
HD-G21, the four positive graphs \(B_{e_j}\) force its translation
stabilizer to be the origin, and its rank-one quotient \(L\) forces
its flat-tensor stabilizer to be trivial.

After an isogeny \(p:A'\to A\), the same support equations show
that the complete point stabilizer of \(p^*E\) is \(\ker p\),
with trivial tensor coordinate. The graph-restricted line bundles
in \eqref{eq:G22-index2} retain cohomology concentrated in degree
two after this isogeny. To see the map explicitly, push each graph
component along its isogeny to \(B_\epsilon\); its structure
pushforward is a sum of torsion flat line bundles. Twisting the four
elliptic factors by such flat bundles preserves each positive or
negative degree and therefore preserves the index-two Kunneth
calculation. Thus the two vanishing groups used for the lifts and
their independence remain zero on \(A'\).

The map \(q_L\) in \eqref{eq:G22-extension} is intrinsic: it is
the canonical map to degree-zero cohomology followed by its
torsion-free quotient. Its graph fibre \(G\) is consequently
preserved functorially by every coherent point action on \(E\).
The same holds after pullback. Scalar classes
\(p^*\eta_{ij}\operatorname{id}_{p^*G}\) are invariant, since
translations act trivially on \(H^2(\mathcal O)\) and scalar
multiplication is natural. They remain independent by finite etale
pullback and the trace left inverse on self-Ext.

For any finite subgroup with any coherent action, average an arbitrary
lift \(\beta_{ij}\) over that group. Equivariance of \(i\) and
invariance of \(f_{ij}\) show that the averaged lift still restricts
to \(f_{ij}\). The corresponding \(z_{ij}\) is invariant.
The same exact independence and cyclic-trace proof applies, yielding
a twelve-dimensional kernel in invariant self-Ext. Averaging here
is an actual finite sum of the given action maps over \(\mathbb C\);
no unconstructed equivariant structure is assumed. An actual
Fourier--Mukai equivalence transports this object, these maps, the
coherent action and the trace diagram, as in HD-G19 and HD-G21.

\paragraph{Scope.}
The common twist supplies nonzero degree-two maps from the ample
line into the graph complex and makes the extension by that line
nonsplit. Its precise index-two cohomology nevertheless allows the
twelve trace-zero graph endomorphisms to lift. The proof makes no
assertion about twists with degenerate restriction, several different
twist packages, or longer complexes; those have different explicitly
computable connecting maps.
