\paragraph{Sharper division inequalities for HD-G08--HD-G09.}
Put $M_T=\lceil\sqrt T\rceil$. The Eisenstein eightfold row of
the checked small-projector theorem HD-D03 gives, on every fixed
split polarized maximal-order component,
\[
\boxed{c_t\le962620032542\,\delta M_T D_8(\Delta)^2.}
\]
For the order of conductor $f$, HD-G09 supplies detector degree
$f^{60}D_8(\Delta)$ and HD-D03 supplies the further multiplier
$f^{32}$. Therefore
\[
\boxed{c_t\le962620032542\,\delta M_T
f^{152}D_8(\Delta)^2.}
\]
These formulas use the same integral normalization of $t$, the same
polarizations and the same actual effective detectors as HD-G08--HD-G09.
They follow by the explicit substitutions
$f^{32}(f^{60})^2=f^{152}$ in HD-D03.
