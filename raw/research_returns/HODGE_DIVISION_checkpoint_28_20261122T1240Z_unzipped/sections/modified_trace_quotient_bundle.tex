\subsection{HD-I21: a vector bundle computing every forgetful tangent fibre}
\label{sec:HD-I21}

Keep all notation and the actual faithful imprimitive tower of HD-I20.
In particular, $B$ is the divisor of the $\nu$ nonreflection values,
$Q$ contains once every uncolored cycle above these values, and
$b$ counts all branch values. Define
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal V_Q=h_*\mathcal O_Y(Q),\qquad
 \mathcal W_Q=\mathcal V_Q/\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^1},\qquad
 L=T_{\mathbf P^1}(-B)=\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^1}(2-\nu).
                                                        \tag{I21.1}
\end{equation}
The inclusion in this definition is the literal pullback of constant
functions. We prove that its quotient is locally free, describe the
actual morphism computing the tangent fibre, and give the resulting
exact splitting-type formula on every Nielsen component.

\paragraph{1. The constant section is nowhere zero.}
The finite map $h$ is flat of degree $m$: locally over a target
discrete valuation ring, its direct image of a line bundle is a
finite torsion-free module, hence free. Thus $\mathcal V_Q$ is a
vector bundle of rank $m$.

Away from $B$, the section $1$ of this bundle is nonzero in every
fibre. At $b_i\in B$, use $w=z^\ell$ on a local branch. If the
point is not in $Q$, the constant is the nonzero class of $1$ in
$\mathbf C\{z\}/(z^\ell)$. If it lies in $Q$, the local generator
of $\mathcal O(Q)$ is $z^{-1}$, and the constant has coefficient
$z$ in that generator. Its class modulo the target parameter $w$
is zero exactly when $\ell=1$.

Consequently the constant section of $\mathcal V_Q$ could vanish
at $b_i$ only if every cycle over $b_i$ were uncolored and
unramified. Its natural monomial matrix would then be the identity,
contrary to its being a branch monodromy. There is therefore a
nonzero fibre value everywhere. Over each target local ring, some
coordinate of this section is a unit; elementary row operations
complete it to a basis. We obtain an actual exact sequence of
vector bundles
\begin{equation}
 0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^1}
      \longrightarrow\mathcal V_Q
      \longrightarrow\mathcal W_Q\longrightarrow0,
 \qquad \operatorname{rank}\mathcal W_Q=m-1.           \tag{I21.2}
\end{equation}
No global splitting of this sequence has been assumed. Its
failure to split is precisely one of the possibilities retained
by the general rank calculation.

Finite pushforward, projection formula and Riemann--Roch give
\begin{equation}
 \deg\mathcal W_Q=\deg Q+1-g-m,\qquad
 \chi(L\otimes\mathcal W_Q)=b-2n-g-2.                 \tag{I21.3}
\end{equation}
Indeed $\chi(\mathcal V_Q)=\chi(\mathcal O_Y(Q))=
\deg Q+1-g$, whereas a rank-$m$ bundle on $\mathbf P^1$ has
Euler characteristic $m+\deg\mathcal V_Q$. For the second
identity use $\deg Q=\nu m-(N+\nu)$ and
$N=2m+2n-b$, as proved in HD-I16 and HD-I20.

\paragraph{2. An actual sheaf morphism computes the tangent fibre.}
Inside $h_*E$, where $E=h^*T_{\mathbf P^1}$, let $\mathcal K$
be the subsheaf of local sections whose jets over each $b_i\in B$
satisfy the derivative and common-value equations of HD-I16.
It is the kernel of the local constraint quotient supported on
$B$. Its global sections are exactly the previously defined
$\ker J$.

The local common value can be lifted to a target vector field.
Subtracting its pullback leaves a section vanishing on
$D_{\mathrm{jet}}=h^*B-Q$. Conversely such a section and any
target vector field satisfy the required jet conditions. Hence,
as subsheaves of $h_*E$, there are exact equalities
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal K=h_*F+T_{\mathbf P^1},\qquad
 h_*F\cap T_{\mathbf P^1}=L,
 \qquad h_*F=L\otimes\mathcal V_Q.                   \tag{I21.4}
\end{equation}
For the intersection equality, a target vector field lies in
$h_*F$ exactly when its common value at every $b_i$ is zero.
Each nonreflection value has a nonempty retained jet, so a
nonzero constant there cannot vanish on $D_{\mathrm{jet}}$.
If the common value is zero, its pullback vanishes on the full
fibre and therefore on that divisor. This proves both directions.

Taking the quotient in (I21.4) gives the exact morphism
\begin{equation}
 0\longrightarrow T_{\mathbf P^1}\longrightarrow\mathcal K
       \longrightarrow L\otimes\mathcal W_Q
       \longrightarrow0.                             \tag{I21.5}
\end{equation}
It is the pushout of (I21.2), tensored by $L$, along
$L\hookrightarrow T_{\mathbf P^1}$. Since
$H^1(T_{\mathbf P^1})=0$, global sections identify
\begin{equation}
 \frac{\ker J}{h^*H^0(T_{\mathbf P^1})}
       \simeq H^0\bigl(\mathbf P^1,L\otimes\mathcal W_Q\bigr).
                                                        \tag{I21.6}
\end{equation}
The left side is the actual tangent fibre of the forgetful map
$\mathcal F$ by HD-I16. Thus (I21.6) is the requested precise
map, not just an equality of two dimensions. It applies for
every $\nu$, including zero, one, two and three.

It follows that the exact all-point rank is
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\operatorname{rank}d\mathcal F
          =b-3-h^0\bigl(\mathcal W_Q(2-\nu)\bigr),
 \qquad
 \dim\operatorname{coker}d\mathcal F
          =h^1\bigl(\mathcal W_Q(2-\nu)\bigr).}        \tag{I21.7}
\end{equation}
The second equality follows from (I21.3), the source dimension
$b-3$, and the cyclic-data tangent dimension $2n+g-1$.
It can also be read from the following exact cohomology sequence:
\begin{align*}
 0\to H^0(L)\to H^0(F)\to H^0(L\otimes\mathcal W_Q)
       &\to H^1(L)\xrightarrow{\kappa}H^1(F)\\
       &\to H^1(L\otimes\mathcal W_Q)\to0.
\end{align*}
Here $\kappa$ is literally the shared-value map of HD-I20,
by projection formula applied to (I21.2).

\paragraph{3. Exact integer formula through the splitting type.}
There are integers $a_1\geq\cdots\geq a_{m-1}$ with an
actual vector-bundle isomorphism
\begin{equation}
       \mathcal W_Q\simeq\bigoplus_{i=1}^{m-1}
                                     \mathcal O_{\mathbf P^1}(a_i).
                                                        \tag{I21.8}
\end{equation}
This uses the Birkhoff--Grothendieck theorem. The original
published locator is Grothendieck, \emph{Sur la classification des
fibr\'es holomorphes sur la sph\`ere de Riemann}, Amer. J. Math.
\textbf{79} (1957), 121--138, Th\'eor\`eme2.1 and its corollary,
printed p.126. For the algebraic statement over the function field
used below, an exact primary locator is Hazewinkel--Martin,
\emph{A short elementary proof of Grothendieck's theorem on algebraic
vectorbundles over the projective line}, J. Pure Appl. Algebra
\textbf{25} (1982), 207--211, Theorem4.1, p.211;
DOI\href{https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-4049(82)90037-8}
{10.1016/0022-4049(82)90037-8}. That theorem gives the splitting
and uniqueness over any field; its proof is Proposition3.1,
pp.209--210, in the
\href{https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/10153/10153D.pdf}{original published scan}.
We use the usual line-bundle cohomology
$h^0(\mathcal O(c))=\max(c+1,0)$ and
$h^1(\mathcal O(c))=\max(-c-1,0)$.

Substitution in (I21.7) proves the exact numerical formulas
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\begin{aligned}
 \operatorname{rank}d\mathcal F
       &=b-3-\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}\max(a_i+3-\nu,0),\\
 \dim\operatorname{coker}d\mathcal F
       &=\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}\max(\nu-a_i-3,0).
 \end{aligned}}                                      \tag{I21.9}
\end{equation}
In particular the forgetful differential is injective exactly
when every $a_i\leq\nu-3$, and surjective exactly when every
$a_i\geq\nu-3$. It is an isomorphism exactly when
$\mathcal W_Q\simeq\mathcal O(\nu-3)^{\oplus(m-1)}$.
These are consequences of the actual quotient and its cohomology;
they do not assume a balanced splitting type on an arbitrary
component.

The integers can also be recovered intrinsically from the bundle's
sections. If $H(t)=h^0(\mathcal W_Q(t))$, then
\begin{equation}
 \#\{i:a_i=-t\}=H(t)-2H(t-1)+H(t-2).                 \tag{I21.10}
\end{equation}
Thus the splitting gate refers to a concrete finite pushforward
and its quotient, rather than an unspecified choice of an
abstract vector bundle with the same rank and degree.

\paragraph{4. The precise remaining period gate.}
The quotient map in (I21.2) gives a surjection
$H^1(F)\to H^1(L\otimes\mathcal W_Q)$. Duality and the
identification (I20.4) therefore give a canonical injection
\begin{equation}
 \iota_Q:
 H^0\bigl(\mathbf P^1,\mathcal W_Q^*(\nu-4)\bigr)
       \hookrightarrow
 H^0\bigl(Y,\omega_Y^2(R-D_{\mathrm{ref}})\bigr)
       \hookrightarrow H^0\bigl(Y,\omega_Y^2(R)\bigr).
                                                        \tag{I21.11}
\end{equation}
The first arrow is the dual of that actual quotient map.
By the exact sequence in paragraph~2 and the Serre-dual trace
calculation of HD-I20, its image is precisely
$\ker T_0=\ker T_h$.

For the primitive multiplication $\mu$ of HD-I05, the exact
period rank on every individual component at every point is
consequently
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{rank}d\mathrm{Per}
   =\dim\operatorname{im}\mu
    -\dim\left(\operatorname{im}\mu\cap
       \iota_QH^0\bigl(\mathcal W_Q^*(\nu-4)\bigr)\right).
                                                        \tag{I21.12}
\end{equation}
For $g\leq n-2$, the multiplication is onto everywhere by
HD-I12, so (I21.9) itself computes the exact period rank.
For the three higher genera, (I21.11)--(I21.12) retain the
actual embedding of the trace kernel and the actual multiplication
image. A bundle splitting type alone is not used to replace
this additional intersection.

On an irreducible Nielsen component the bundle has a fixed
splitting type on a nonempty open subset. This can be seen
without an assumption about generic pencils. Pass to the usual
finite marking cover carrying the family and use three branch
values to trivialize its target $\mathbf P^1$-bundle. The above
construction gives an actual relative vector bundle. The
splitting theorem cited in paragraph~3 applies over the function field
of the component. The resulting isomorphism and its inverse
are given by finitely many rational functions and extend after
shrinking to a nonempty open subset. The ordered splitting
integers are unchanged by the finite marking cover, since the
dimensions in (I21.10) are unchanged by field extension.
Hence (I21.9) gives the component's generic forgetful rank,
and gives its generic period rank in the low genera, from
these particular generic integers.
