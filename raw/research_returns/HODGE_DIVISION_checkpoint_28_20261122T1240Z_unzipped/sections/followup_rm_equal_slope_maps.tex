\section[HD-F1-R21: actual equal-slope endpoint maps]{HD-F1-R21: maximal-rank forward maps and polystable reflexive endpoints are excluded at the adjacent rank-one gate}
\label{sec:followup-rm-equal-slope-maps}

Retain the genuine RM fourfold and the full balanced
character. Consider actual adjacent objects
\[
 A\longrightarrow E\longrightarrow T[-1]\longrightarrow A[1],
 \qquad \operatorname{ch}(E)=e^{-B}v_q,
 \qquad v_q=(1-qA_1)(1-qA_2),\quad q>0,
 \tag{F1R21.1}
\]
where $A,T$ are positive-rank torsion-free sheaves,
slope semistable with equal slope for one fixed ample
polarization. Thus $r_A=r_T+1$. No endpoint full
character is prescribed. We prove that every actual
map $A\to T$ has generic rank strictly less than $r_T$.
This is an obstruction from the whole character and
actual kernel/cokernel sequences; it needs neither
semiregularity nor equivariance.

Consequently every gluable weakly semiregular point-action
lift in this class must have two strictly semistable
endpoints, $r_T\ge2$, and invariant forward Hom dimension
at least two. At the first ranks $(r_A,r_T)=(3,2)$,
that entire invariant Hom space has one of the actual
rank-one factorizations proved below. Independently,
polystable reflexive endpoints of equal slope cannot
produce a gluable weakly semiregular adjacent object
of virtual rank $1$ or $-1$, for any endomorphism sizes
or coherent finite point action.

\paragraph{1. The actual determinant twist and the effective cycle.}
The determinant line of the actual perfect object in
(F1R21.1) has first Chern class $-B$, because its rank
is one and the normalized balanced character has no
codimension-one term. Let
$L=\det(E)^{-1}$ and apply the actual exact equivalence
$\Phi_L(-)=(-)\otimes L$ to the triangle. It gives
\[
 \operatorname{ch}(\Phi_L E)=v_q,\qquad
 c_1(\Phi_L A)=c_1(\Phi_L T)=:\ell.
 \tag{F1R21.2}
\]
Tensoring both endpoints preserves equality of slopes
and semistability. The ranks differ by one, so equality
of their slopes in (F1R21.2) forces
$\ell H^3=0$ and both slopes are zero. We henceforth
write the twisted actual sheaves as $A,T$.

When a point-action lift is present, it is transported
by the explicit conjugation
\[
 \Phi_L\,(t_x^*(-)\otimes\alpha)\,\Phi_L^{-1}
       =t_x^*(-)\otimes\alpha\otimes L\otimes t_x^*L^{-1}.
 \tag{F1R21.3}
\]
The added line lies in $\operatorname{Pic}^0(X)$.
Composition and coherence are transported by this very
equivalence; invariant Hom spaces and generic ranks
are identified by tensoring their maps. Thus the
normalization is an actual object/action operation.

Suppose $f:A\to T$ has generic rank $r_T$. Its actual
kernel and cokernel fit the exact sequence
\[
 0\longrightarrow K\longrightarrow A\xrightarrow{f}T
                          \longrightarrow Q\longrightarrow0,
 \qquad \operatorname{rk}K=1,\quad Q\text{ torsion}.
 \tag{F1R21.4}
\]
The rank-one kernel is torsion free. Additivity of
first Chern classes and (F1R21.2) give
$c_1(K)=c_1(Q)$. The latter is the effective divisor
cycle of the torsion sheaf. Semistability of $A$ of
slope zero gives $c_1(K)H^3\le0$. An effective nonzero
divisor has positive degree against $H^3$. Hence
\[
          c_1(K)=c_1(Q)=0,\qquad
                           \operatorname{codim}\operatorname{Supp}Q\ge2.
 \tag{F1R21.5}
\]
Here $c_1(Q)=0$ is the vanishing of its actual divisor
cycle, not merely of its degree.

The reflexive hull $K^{**}=M$ is a line bundle of
first Chern class zero. Its actual quotient $M/K$
has codimension at least two. Write $[M/K]_2$ and
$[Q]_2$ for their codimension-two support cycles,
with lengths at the generic points; both are effective.
Since $\operatorname{ch}_2(M)=0$, the exact sequence
(F1R21.4) and $0\to K\to M\to M/K\to0$ give
\[
 -q(A_1+A_2)=\operatorname{ch}_2(E)
       =\operatorname{ch}_2(K)-\operatorname{ch}_2(Q)
       =-[M/K]_2-[Q]_2.
 \tag{F1R21.6}
\]
Thus $q(A_1+A_2)$ would be the class of an actual
nonzero effective surface cycle. The complete Gauss-map
and rational-RM-eigenvector obstruction in DEG\_L S-22 excludes
that class. This proves the maximal-rank map obstruction.
Every operation in (F1R21.6) is the actual coherent
$K_0$ relation; no numerical class is represented by a
postulated sheaf or cycle.

\paragraph{2. Strict semistability of both surviving endpoints.}
For a nonzero map $f:A\to T$, let $I=\operatorname{im}f$.
The result just proved gives
$0<\operatorname{rk}I<r_T<r_A$. The kernel has positive
rank, and the exact sequence
$0\to\ker f\to A\to I\to0$ gives
$\mu_H(I)\ge0$ by semistability of $A$.
Semistability of $T$ gives $\mu_H(I)\le0$.
If $A$ were stable, its proper positive-rank kernel
would have strictly negative slope, and that exact
sequence would instead give $\mu_H(I)>0$.
If $T$ were stable, its proper positive-rank subsheaf
$I$ would have strictly negative slope. Both possibilities
are impossible. Therefore
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}_X(A,T)\ne0
       \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
       A,T\text{ are both strictly slope semistable},\qquad r_T\ge2.
 \tag{F1R21.7}
\]
The last assertion also follows directly because every
nonzero map to a rank-one torsion-free target has full
generic target rank, already excluded.

Now retain a gluable weakly semiregular lift of (F1R21.1).
R19 proves, without equal-slope or endpoint-character
assumptions, that its invariant forward Hom dimension
must be at least two. Thus its ordinary Hom is nonzero,
and (F1R21.7) applies. In particular there is no such
object with either endpoint stable or with $r_T=1$.
The reverse invariant Hom remains zero by the exact
gluability isomorphism (F1R19.14).

\paragraph{3. Averaged actual splittings exclude polystable reflexive endpoints.}
This argument applies in either nonzero rank order.
Let $A,T$ be polystable reflexive sheaves of the same
slope with the induced lifts of one coherent finite
point action. A nonzero morphism between stable
reflexive sheaves of that slope is an isomorphism,
and the endomorphisms of a stable reflexive sheaf are
scalars. The exact proof is the double-dual determinant
and big-open extension argument in R18. Consequently,
after grouping the actual stable direct summands by
isomorphism class, any morphism $A\to T$ is a direct
sum of scalar matrices on multiplicity spaces. Its
actual image is a direct sum of those same stable
reflexive sheaves, and its image projection and inclusion
have actual coherent-sheaf splittings.

Take a nonzero invariant morphism $f:A\to T$ and
factor it through its actual image:
\[
                       A\xrightarrow{p}I\xrightarrow{j}T.
 \tag{F1R21.8}
\]
The t-exact action and equivariance of $f$ induce the
actual coherent lift on $I$ and make $p,j$ equivariant.
Choose ordinary splittings $s:I\to A$ and $r:T\to I$
with $ps=rj=1_I$. The specified coherent structures
induce genuine group representations on these Hom
spaces: their coherence factors cancel in the
conjugation action on morphisms. Therefore the averages
\[
 s_G=\frac1{|G|}\sum_{g\in G}g\cdot s,\qquad
 r_G=\frac1{|G|}\sum_{g\in G}g\cdot r
 \tag{F1R21.9}
\]
are actual invariant maps with $p s_G=r_G j=1_I$.
Their composite is a nonzero reverse invariant map,
since
\[
                      p(s_G r_G)j=1_I.
 \tag{F1R21.10}
\]
This explicitly proves that a nonzero invariant
forward Hom forces a nonzero invariant reverse Hom
for these actual polystable reflexive endpoints.
Gluability excludes the reverse Hom, so the forward
Hom also vanishes. R18 then excludes every balanced
weakly semiregular adjacent object of virtual rank
$1$ or $-1$ in this class. No semisimplicity assertion
about an unspecified category is used in place of
the image, splittings and averaged maps.

\paragraph{4. The first surviving ranks force an actual rank-one factorization.}
Return to positive virtual rank one and the equal-slope
semistable torsion-free class of paragraphs 1--2.
Suppose $(r_A,r_T)=(3,2)$, and write
\[
                    W=\operatorname{Hom}_G(A,T).
 \tag{F1R21.11}
\]
For a putative gluable weakly semiregular object,
$\dim W\ge2$ by R19. At the generic point, let
$\mathbb K=\mathbf C(X)$ and let $W_{\mathbb K}$
be the span of the actual maps in
$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb K}(A_{\mathbb K},T_{\mathbb K})$.
Every element of $W_{\mathbb K}$ has rank at most one.
Indeed each two-by-two minor of a linear combination
of a fixed complex basis of $W$ is a polynomial in
its scalar coefficients, with coefficients in
$\mathbb K$. The maximal-rank obstruction makes it
zero for all complex values, so its polynomial
coefficients vanish. This proves the same identity
for arbitrary values in $\mathbb K$.

A nonzero linear space of rank-at-most-one matrices
has a common image line or a common kernel hyperplane.
Here is the complete elementary argument over
$\mathbb K$. Write two nonzero rank-one maps as
$u\otimes\alpha$ and $v\otimes\beta$. If both the
vectors $u,v$ and the functionals $\alpha,\beta$ are
independent, their sum has rank two. Thus either all
image vectors span one line, or choose two independent
image vectors. Their functionals are then proportional.
For any third rank-one map, its image vector is
independent of at least one of those two; pairing it
with that map forces its functional to be proportional
to the same one. In the latter case every map kills
the common hyperplane. A one-dimensional generic
space satisfies both alternatives. This proves all
cases, even when $\dim_{\mathbb K}W_{\mathbb K}<\dim_{\mathbb C}W$.

Both alternatives extend to exact global factorizations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item In the common-image case, let $I$ be the saturation
of the image of the actual evaluation map
$W\otimes A\to T$. It is a rank-one torsion-free
subsheaf with torsion-free quotient, and every
forward map factors as $A\to I\hookrightarrow T$.
\item In the common-kernel case, let
$K=\ker(A\to W^*\otimes T)$ for the actual simultaneous
evaluation map. It is saturated of rank two, because
$A/K$ embeds in the torsion-free target. Every forward
map factors as $A\twoheadrightarrow Q=A/K\to T$,
where $Q$ is rank-one torsion free.
\end{enumerate}
All these evaluation maps are equivariant because
$W$ consists of invariant maps. Images, kernels,
saturation and the indicated quotients therefore
carry their induced actual coherent lifts.

The rank-one factor has slope zero in both cases.
In the first case, semistability of $T$ gives
$\mu(I)\le0$. Any nonzero forward image $J\subset I$
is a quotient of $A$, hence has slope at least zero;
its full-rank inclusion into $I$ gives
$\mu(J)\le\mu(I)$. Thus both slopes are zero.
In the second case the quotient $Q$ has slope at
least zero, while any nonzero map from the rank-one
TF sheaf $Q$ to $T$ is injective and has a proper
rank-one image of slope at most zero. Again the
slope equals zero.

These are factorizations of the actual invariant
forward maps. They do not replace the adjacent
object by a direct sum or change its coupling class
$\delta\in\operatorname{Ext}^2_G(T,A)$. The exact
coupling derivative and local quotient algebra
restrictions in R20 continue to apply to that same
$\delta$.

\paragraph{Sources and exact remaining scope.}
The effective-cycle obstruction is the fully proved
DEG\_L S-22 statement on the actual RM eigenspaces. Rank-one
reflexive hulls and their coherent quotients provide
the cycles in (F1R21.6), via actual $K_0$ additivity
and the leading support term of the Chern character.
The stable-reflexive Hom identity is the proved R18
map, with its published big-open restriction/pushforward
foundation. The averages use the given coherent
finite action on Hom spaces, not a replacement by a
trivial group action. The bounded-rank linear algebra
and every global factorization are proved explicitly.

The maximal-rank and strict-semistability assertions
are for positive virtual rank one, equal slope and
semistable torsion-free endpoints. They do not assert
the same statement for a negative-rank object after
a derived dual, which could introduce additional
cohomology sheaves. Only the polystable-reflexive
exclusion has been proved in both rank orders.
Unequal endpoint slopes, strictly semistable
nonpolystable endpoints, and the actual rank-one
factorizations above remain part of the unresolved
adjacent-object problem. No object is asserted at
any remaining profile.
