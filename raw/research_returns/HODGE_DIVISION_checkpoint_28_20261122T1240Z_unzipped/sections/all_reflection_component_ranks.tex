\subsection{HD-I14: every reflection-only Nielsen component}
\label{sec:HD-I14}

For the standard representation of $G(d,e,m)$, with
$d\in\{3,4,6\}$, $e\mid d$, $n\geq5$, and
$m\geq\lceil(n^2-2n+3)/2\rceil$, every signature-$(n,n)$ Nielsen
component all of whose local monodromies are reflections has
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\quad
 \dim\operatorname{image}(\mathrm{Per})=2n+g-1,
 \qquad g=g(Y)=1-m+T/2.
 \quad}                                             \tag{I14.1}
\end{equation}
Here $Y=Z/H$ is the actual degree-$m$ quotient of HD-I02, and $T$ counts
the colored-transposition branch generators.  Thus the generic period
rank of each such component depends only on its intermediate genus.
HD-I12 proves (I14.1) for $g\leq n-2$, and HD-I13 proves it for $g=n-1$.
We prove the two remaining cases below.

\paragraph{1. The precise connectedness input and its relation to our factor.}
We use F.~Catanese, \emph{Irreducibility of the space of cyclic covers
of algebraic curves of fixed numerical type and the irreducible
components of $\operatorname{Sing}(\overline{\mathfrak M}_g)$},
\emph{Advances in geometric analysis}, Advanced Lectures in
Mathematics~21, International Press (2012), 281--306, Theorem~2.4.
The author version \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/1011.0316}{arXiv:1011.0316},
printed pp.~8--9, was read for this argument, including its proof.
It parametrizes all pairs $(X,C_d)$ with $X$ smooth connected of fixed
genus and the cyclic subgroup acting faithfully with fixed branching
datum by a connected complex manifold.  In its notation the branching
datum is the color sequence modulo $\operatorname{Aut}(C_d)$.
The proof also identifies its local deformation map with the full
equivariant Kuranishi space; the tangent space is
$H^1(T_Y(-R))$ for $Y=X/C_d$.  The quotient $X\to Y$ is therefore the
actual cyclic cover that enters HD-I02 and HD-I05.

For the three values of $d$ here,
\[
              (\mathbf Z/d)^\times=\{1,-1\}.
\]
Changing the cyclic generator thus either fixes or interchanges the
two primitive characters $\chi$ and $\bar\chi$.  On their period
codifferential the interchange is the exact tensor flip
\[
 H^0(\omega_Y L)\otimes H^0(\omega_Y L^{-1}(R))
 \longrightarrow
 H^0(\omega_Y L^{-1}(R))\otimes H^0(\omega_Y L),
 \qquad u\otimes v\longmapsto v\otimes u.
\]
Its product in $H^0(\omega_Y^2(R))$ is unchanged.  Equivalently the
rational primitive idempotent, the sum of these two character
idempotents, is unchanged.  This proves that the primitive period rank
is well defined on precisely Catanese's unmarked pairs; no change of
representation or polarization is being assumed.

The multiplication map varies holomorphically in this connected
manifold and its source and target dimensions are constant in each
of the cases below.  Once a full-rank point exists, the maximal minors
give a nonempty open full-rank locus whose complement has no interior.
This follows from the identity theorem in local vector-bundle frames
and connectedness.  Thus every nonempty open subset of the full
cyclic-cover deformation space meets this locus.

For each original Nielsen component the forgetful map retaining
$X\to Y$ and discarding $h:Y\to\mathbf P^1$ has surjective differential
at every point, by the explicit Cech calculation (I12.4).  All the
markings of the Galois closure, cyclic generator and branch labels are
finite choices; forgetting them does not change that local deformation
space.  Consequently the image of every Nielsen component contains
a nonempty open subset of the unmarked cyclic-cover space.  It meets
the full-rank locus just described.  The final polarized isogeny of
HD-I02 has invertible period differential.  Therefore a full cyclic
rank attained anywhere for a fixed branching datum is the generic
rank on every reflection-only Nielsen component with that datum.

\paragraph{2. No cyclic branch points: $g=n+1$.}
Here the cyclic cover is connected and unramified of degree $d$.
Its source genus is $d(g-1)+1=dn+1\geq2$, so the preceding theorem
applies.  The line bundle $L$ is a torsion bundle of exact order $d$;
both eigenspace dimensions are $g-1=n$, and the quadratic target has
dimension $3g-3=3n$.
The checked nodal construction HD-I06 and its one-node induction
HD-I10 give a smooth full-rank point in every such genus for each
$d=3,4,6$.  Section~1 therefore gives rank $3n$ on every component.
Full monodromy forces $e=d$ here, by the explicit colored-cycle
argument of HD-I04; conversely a reflection-only $G(d,d,m)$ component
has no diagonal branch reflections and hence lies in this case.

\paragraph{3. Two cyclic branch points: $g=n$.}
The two colors must be $s,d-s$.  For $d=3,4,6$, their unordered pair is
one of the following:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
 d&\{s,d-s\}&e=\gcd(d,s)\\ \hline
 3&\{1,2\}&1\\
 4&\{1,3\},\ \{2,2\}&1,\ 2\\
 6&\{1,5\},\ \{2,4\},\ \{3,3\}&1,\ 2,\ 3
\end{array}
\]
The graph and Bezout calculation in HD-I08 identifies the full tower
group with $G(d,e,m)$ for exactly the value of $e$ in this table.
Every listed pair occurs in the checked rank-$14$ construction
HD-I07, and HD-I10 supplies the smooth rank-$3n-1$ point for every
$n\geq5$.  Its source genus is $dg-e+1\geq2$, by Riemann--Hurwitz.
The two primitive eigenspaces have dimension $n$, and their quadratic
target has dimension $3g-3+2=3n-1$.
Catanese's fixed branching datum identifies the pairs $s,d-s$ and
$d-s,s$, precisely as handled by the tensor flip in section~1.
The full-rank locus is therefore dense on that space, and each
reflection-only Nielsen component has generic rank $3n-1$.
This finishes the proof of (I14.1).

\paragraph{4. Concrete component dimensions and remaining scope.}
At $n=5$ the ranks on reflection-only components are
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrr}
 g(Y)&0&1&2&3&4&5&6\\ \hline
 \dim\operatorname{image}(\mathrm{Per})&9&10&11&12&13&14&15.
\end{array}
\]
The genus-zero row requires $e=1$: on $\mathbf P^1$ connectedness
forces the branch colors to generate $\mathbf Z/d$.
For every proper divisor $e$ of $d$ all genera $1\leq g\leq n$ occur,
by using $n-g+1$ colors $e$ and $n-g+1$ colors $d-e$, a generating
handle character, and the pencil construction of HD-I12.
The $e=d$ case has $g=n+1$ only.
Each such Hurwitz component has dimension $2m+2n-3$ and generic
period-fibre dimension $2m-g-2$.

These statements refine the sharp maxima $3n$ and $3n-1$ to a
component-by-component answer on the full reflection-only locus.
They leave the branch data with at least one nonreflection generator
to the exact composite differential in HD-I02 and HD-I05.  Such a
generator can constrain the intermediate pencil through higher
ramification or colliding images; the map-lifting argument used here
does not impose those extra local conditions.  No equality of its
rank with (I14.1) is asserted without calculating that constrained
first differential.
