\section{A precise obstruction for ample secant seeds on fourfolds}

\paragraph{HD-N11 (the two-exponential character obstruction).}
Let $X$ be a complex abelian fourfold, let $H\in\operatorname{NS}(X)_{\mathbb Q}$
be ample, let $s^2=-d$ with $d>0$, and let $B\in\operatorname{NS}(X)_{\mathbb Q}$.
A perfect complex $F$ with
\[
 \operatorname{ch}(F)=e^B\bigl(\alpha e^{sH}+\bar\alpha e^{-sH}\bigr),
 \qquad \alpha\in\mathbb Q(s)^\times,                         \tag{N11.1}
\]
which is simple and has $\operatorname{Ext}^{<0}(F,F)=0$, is not
semiregular. Here semiregularity means injectivity of the full map
$\sigma_F^2:\operatorname{Ext}^2(F,F)\to HH_{-2}(X)$ used in
Perry Definition2.4. The proof gives the exact ranks
\[
 \operatorname{rank}c_F^1=8,\qquad
 \operatorname{rank}c_F^2=12,\qquad
 \chi(F,F)=32d^2\alpha\bar\alpha\int_X H^4/4!>0,             \tag{N11.2}
\]
where $c_F^j=\sigma_F^j\circ\operatorname{ev}_F^j$ is the
Hochschild action on the Chern character.

\paragraph{The actual trace and evaluation maps.}
Put $W=H^1(X,\mathcal O_X)$ and $V=H^0(X,T_X)$. The Hochschild
algebra of $X$ is $HH^*(X)=\bigwedge^*(W\oplus V)$ under HKR.
For $a\in H^{j-p}(X,\bigwedge^p T_X)$, evaluation is contraction
of $a$ with $\operatorname{At}(F)^p/p!$, and the trace components
are
\[
 \sigma_{F,q}^j(\xi)=\operatorname{tr}
                 \bigl(\xi\operatorname{At}(F)^q/q!\bigr)
       \in H^{j+q}(X,\Omega_X^q).
\]
The universal Atiyah/Chern-character identity gives
$c_F^j(a)=a\mathbin{\lrcorner}\operatorname{ch}(F)$.
These are the maps of Buchweitz--Flenner,
\emph{The global decomposition theorem for Hochschild (co-)homology
of singular spaces via the Atiyah--Chern character},
Advances in Mathematics217 (2008), 243--281,
Proposition6.2.1, Corollary6.3.2 and Theorem6.4.2;
Perry Remark2.5 identifies the categorical trace with this map.

Choose a nonzero holomorphic volume form on $X$. Serre duality
pairs $\operatorname{Ext}^2(F,F)$ perfectly with itself.
For $a\in H^{2-p}(\bigwedge^pT_X)$ and
$\xi\in\operatorname{Ext}^2(F,F)$, cyclicity of trace gives
\[
 \bigl\langle\operatorname{ev}_F^2(a),\xi\bigr\rangle_{
 \mathrm{Serre}}
 =\bigl\langle a,\sigma_{F,p}^2(\xi)\bigr\rangle.          \tag{N11.3}
\]
The pairing on the right is contraction followed by integration
against the volume form. A choice of HKR signs can multiply a
summand by a fixed sign; it does not change either kernel or rank.
Equation(N11.3) proves directly that $\sigma_F^2$ and
$\operatorname{ev}_F^2$ are transposes. The general categorical
adjunction is also given by C\u{a}ld\u{a}raru--Willerton,
\emph{The Mukai pairing. I. A categorical approach},
New York Journal of Mathematics16 (2010), 61--98, Section7.3,
printed pp.88--89, with functoriality and adjointness in Theorems6 and8.
The actual journal version inspected is
\url{https://nyjm.albany.edu/j/2010/16-6v.pdf}.

\paragraph{An explicit Clifford calculation of both ranks.}
Choose bases $x_1,\ldots,x_4$ of $H^{1,0}(X)$ and
$y_1,\ldots,y_4$ of $W$ such that
$H=\sum_i x_i\wedge y_i$. For $v\in V$ and $w\in W$ the
operator on the exterior algebra is
\[
 D_{v,w}=\iota_v+w\wedge,
 \qquad
 D_{v,w}(e^{\pm sH})=(w\pm s\iota_vH)\wedge e^{\pm sH}.
                                                               \tag{N11.4}
\]
All these operators anticommute, because a holomorphic vector
contracts an antiholomorphic one-form to zero. The invertible map
\[
 V\oplus W\longrightarrow W\oplus W,
 \qquad(v,w)\longmapsto(w+s\iota_vH,w-s\iota_vH)             \tag{N11.5}
\]
splits the operators into two four-dimensional graph spaces
$U_+$ and $U_-$. The first acts only on $e^{sH}$, the second
only on $e^{-sH}$. In degree two the mixed block
$U_+\otimes U_-$ annihilates both exponentials. The two other
blocks have images
\[
 \alpha\,\bigwedge^2W\,e^{sH},
 \qquad\bar\alpha\,\bigwedge^2W\,e^{-sH}.                 \tag{N11.6}
\]
Each has dimension six. Their intersection is zero: an equality
$\eta e^{sH}=\eta'e^{-sH}$ first gives $\eta=\eta'$ in its
lowest component, and then gives $2sH\wedge\eta=0$ in the
next component. The map
$H\wedge:\bigwedge^2W\to H^{1,0}\otimes\bigwedge^3W$
is injective. Indeed, its coefficients in the independent $x_i$
are $y_i\wedge\eta$; all four vanish only when $\eta=0$.
Thus $\operatorname{rank}c_F^2=12$.
Exactly the same calculation with $\eta\in W$ shows that the
two four-dimensional degree-one images have zero intersection,
so $\operatorname{rank}c_F^1=8$.
Multiplication of the character by $e^B$ conjugates the operators
by the invertible shear
$(v,w)\mapsto(v,w+\iota_vB)$ and hence leaves both ranks unchanged.
This proves the two rank assertions for the entire character(N11.1).

\paragraph{Euler pairing and the contradiction.}
The Todd class is one and derived duality sends $e^{sH}$ to
$e^{-sH}$. The top-degree term in
$\operatorname{ch}(F^\vee)\operatorname{ch}(F)$ is therefore
$2\alpha\bar\alpha(2sH)^4/4!$. This proves the last formula
in(N11.2). Simplicity and negative self-Ext vanishing, together
with Calabi--Yau fourfold duality, give
\[
 \chi(F,F)=2-2\operatorname{ext}^1(F,F)
                   +\operatorname{ext}^2(F,F).             \tag{N11.7}
\]
The rank-eight composite $c_F^1$ gives
$\operatorname{ext}^1(F,F)\ge8$.
Injectivity of $\sigma_F^2$ would make
$\operatorname{ev}_F^2$ surjective by(N11.3). Its composite
$c_F^2$ would then have rank $\operatorname{ext}^2(F,F)$,
so that this dimension would be12. Equation(N11.7) would give
$\chi(F,F)\le2-16+12=-2$, contradicting(N11.2).
This proves HD-N11. \hfill$\square$

\paragraph{HD-N12 (finite translation/tensor actions and their exact maps).}
Let $G\subset X\times\widehat X$ be a finite subgroup equipped
with an actual coherent action on $D_{\mathrm{perf}}(X)$ through
translations and tensor products with degree-zero line bundles.
For these actions the forgetful morphism
\[
 HH_*\bigl(D_{\mathrm{perf}}(X)^G\bigr)
       \longrightarrow HH_*(X)                             \tag{N12.1}
\]
is an isomorphism. Thus weak $G$-semiregularity in Perry
Definition2.6 equals injectivity of the ambient trace on
$\operatorname{Ext}^2(F,F)^G$ for these faithful point subgroups.
For every equivariant perfect complex one also has
\[
 \chi_G(F,F):=\sum_j(-1)^j\dim\operatorname{Ext}^j(F,F)^G
                    =\frac{\chi(F,F)}{|G|}.               \tag{N12.2}
\]
Consequently an object with character(N11.1), negative
self-Ext vanishing and
$\dim\operatorname{End}(F)^G=1$ cannot be weakly
$G$-semiregular. The coherent action itself, including its scalar
coherence, is retained throughout this statement.

\paragraph{The Hochschild comparison.}
Write $\mathcal C=D_{\mathrm{perf}}(X)$.
Perry, \emph{Hochschild cohomology and group actions},
Mathematische Zeitschrift297 (2021), 1273--1292, Theorem4.4,
gives the actual decomposition
\[
 HH^*(\mathcal C^G)=
 \left(\bigoplus_{g\in G}HH^*(\mathcal C,\phi_g)\right)^G.
                                                               \tag{N12.3}
\]
The inspected author version is arXiv1807.09268v2,
Theorem4.4 on printed p.10, with proof on pp.11--12.
For $g=(a,P)$ with $a\ne0$, the Fourier--Mukai kernel has
support the graph of a nonzero translation; this is disjoint
from the diagonal. Its derived Hom from the diagonal kernel
therefore vanishes in all degrees.
For $a=0$ and $P\ne\mathcal O_X$, the diagonal Koszul resolution
instead gives
\[
 HH^j(\mathcal C,(-)\otimes P)
   =\bigoplus_{p+q=j}H^q(X,P)\otimes\bigwedge^pV=0.
\]
Here is a direct proof of the vanishing used in the last equality.
Take a connected finite etale isogeny trivializing the torsion
line $P$. Its pushforward of the structure sheaf splits into
its character lines, including $P$. All deck translations act
trivially on the coherent cohomology of the covering abelian
variety, so only the trivial character contributes. Hence
$H^q(X,P)=0$ for every $q$.

The identity sector in(N12.3) is all of $HH^*(X)$:
translations act trivially, and tensoring with $P\in\operatorname{Pic}^0$
acts trivially because its Atiyah and rational Chern classes vanish.
The invariant category is Calabi--Yau of dimension four: its
Serre pairing is the restriction of the invariant volume-form
pairing, and taking invariants in characteristic zero preserves
its nondegeneracy. Thus(N12.3) also proves equality of the
Hochschild homology dimensions in every degree.
Let $\mathrm{Ind}$ and $\mathrm{Forg}$ be the actual induction
and forgetful functors. Their composition satisfies
$\mathrm{Forg}\,\mathrm{Ind}=\bigoplus_{g\in G}\phi_g$.
Functoriality and additivity on Hochschild homology give
\[
 \mathrm{Forg}_*\mathrm{Ind}_*=|G|\operatorname{id}.
                                                               \tag{N12.4}
\]
The surjection in(N12.4) is an isomorphism by the equal dimensions,
proving(N12.1). Functoriality of the categorical trace then
identifies the two semiregularity kernels exactly. This proves
the claimed weak/ambient comparison without replacing one
notion by another without a map.

\paragraph{A direct Euler-character proof of(N12.2).}
We show that every nonidentity $g$ has supertrace zero on
$\operatorname{Ext}^*(F,F)$; averaging then proves(N12.2).
First suppose its translation component $a$ has order $r>1$.
Let $E=R\mathcal Hom(F,F)$. The action on $E$ is honest, since
the tensor factors cancel in endomorphisms. Decompose $E$ into
the eigensummands of $g^r$, which acts over the identity of $X$.
On each summand multiply the action of $g$ by an appropriate
constant root so that it has order $r$. This gives a genuine
linearization for the free cyclic translation group
$\langle a\rangle$, and hence descent to $X/\langle a\rangle$.
The $r$ character Euler characteristics of this descended
perfect complex are equal: they differ by torsion line-bundle
twists, which have the same rational Chern character.
Their weighted sum at the nonidentity generator is zero.
Undoing the constant scalar and summing the eigensummands
proves the assertion for this $g$.

Now suppose $g=(0,P)$, where $P$ has exact order $r>1$.
The linearization of $F$ is a module structure over
$\bigoplus_{j=0}^{r-1}P^j$, after choosing the trivialization
of $P^r$ compatible with its coherence. On the associated
connected etale cyclic cover $p:X'\to X$, it is therefore
$F=p_*F'$ for an actual perfect complex $F'$ on $X'$.
The pullback is
\[
 p^*F=\bigoplus_{j=0}^{r-1}\tau_j^*F',
\]
where $\tau_j$ are deck translations; the operator $g$ has the
$r$ distinct character eigenvalues on these summands. Each
Euler pairing
$\chi(\tau_i^*F',\tau_j^*F')$ equals $\chi(F',F')$, by
Riemann--Roch and translation invariance of cohomology.
The supertrace on the pullback endomorphism complex is thus
\[
 \chi(F',F')\sum_{i,j=0}^{r-1}\zeta^{j-i}=0.
\]
Finite etale pullback multiplies each eigen-Euler characteristic
by $r$, so the supertrace on $X$ is also zero.
These two cases exhaust the nonidentity elements of the
faithful subgroup $G\subset X\times\widehat X$.

\paragraph{The invariant trace contradiction.}
The ambient evaluation maps take values in the invariant Ext
groups, because the Hochschild action is natural and $G$ acts
trivially on $HH^*(X)$. Equation(N11.3), restricted to invariants,
remains a perfect adjointness relation. Consequently ambient
trace injectivity forces
$\dim\operatorname{Ext}^2(F,F)^G=12$, while the rank-eight
composite in degree one gives
$\dim\operatorname{Ext}^1(F,F)^G\ge8$.
Equivariant simplicity, negative self-Ext vanishing and the
invariant Serre pairing therefore give
\[
 0<\frac{\chi(F,F)}{|G|}
    =2-2\dim\operatorname{Ext}^1(F,F)^G
           +\dim\operatorname{Ext}^2(F,F)^G
    \le-2,
\]
a contradiction. By(N12.1), this also excludes weak
$G$-semiregularity. \hfill$\square$

\paragraph{The inherited external-product and Fourier maps.}
For two such seeds with their respective groups, a nonzero
invariant trace-kernel class $\xi\in\operatorname{Ext}^2(F_1,F_1)^{G_1}$
maps to $\xi\boxtimes\operatorname{id}_{F_2}$ in the external
product. It remains nonzero by the derived Kunneth isomorphism;
its trace is $\sigma_{F_1}(\xi)\boxtimes\operatorname{ch}(F_2)=0$.
An actual Fourier--Mukai equivalence carries this class
isomorphically to a trace-kernel class, by the functoriality
diagram for the categorical trace. Thus the inherited product
action and its conjugate through the Orlov equivalence also
retain the obstruction. This conclusion uses these precise
functors and does not assert it for a different, unconstructed
action on the final eightfold.

\paragraph{Exact arithmetic certificate.}
Writing $\alpha=a+bs$, the standalone exterior-algebra certificate
\texttt{fourfold\_secant\_character.py} reconstructs all the
eight degree-one and twenty-eight degree-two operators.
It obtains the exact nonzero minors
\[
 -256d^4(a^2+db^2)^4,\qquad

of sizes eight and twelve respectively, verifies all sixteen

These are universal polynomial identities. The additional
twenty-seven numerical field/character evaluations in the

for their proof or for the categorical argument.

\paragraph{Scope and provenance.}
The conclusion concerns the exact ample two-exponential
character(N11.1), the indicated negative-Ext and equivariant
simplicity conditions, and the stated finite point subgroups.
It gives an obstruction for replacing the directly semiregular
threefold inputs of HD-N07--HD-N08 by fourfold inputs of this
same character form. It does not classify other characters,
objects with larger invariant endomorphism algebras, or general
perfect complexes on the final eightfold.
An unrefereed note by the account \texttt{lelong\_ridge},
\emph{A Chern-character test for semiregularity on abelian
fourfolds, and the failure of Markman's imaginary-quadratic
secant sheaves}, TheoremsB--C, October2026,
\url{https://rstation.ai/problems/hodge_conjecture/papers/37},
also describes the numerical obstruction. It was consulted as
research background; none of its theorem statements is used
as a premise here. The explicit operator, trace, induction,
Euler and finite-cover proofs above provide the asserted
results directly, and no priority claim is made.
