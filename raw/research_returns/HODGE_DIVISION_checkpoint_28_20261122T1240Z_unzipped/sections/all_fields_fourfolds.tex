\section{Every determinant in the nine class-number-one fourfold fields}
\label{sec:all-fields-fourfolds}

\paragraph{HD-N10 (an explicit integral product and contraction).}
Let
\[
 d\in\{1,2,3,7,11,19,43,67,163\},\qquad K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-d}),
\]
and let $(A,\theta_A)$ be a polarized Weil fourfold of signature
$(2,2)$ with integral action by the order
$\mathcal O_f=\mathbb Z+f\mathcal O_K$. Its ordinary Hermitian
determinant is arbitrary. Write
$\Delta=\frac1{4!}\int_A\theta_A^4$. Define the following integers:
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrrr}
d&1&2&3&7&11&19&43&67&163\\\hline
b_d&2&2&2&2&3&4&8&12&28.
\end{array}
\]
Then every such fourfold has an effective integral detecting surface,
and a reduced irreducible one, of degree at most
\[
 \boxed{\delta_W(A,\theta_A)
 \le b_d\,10^{258}d^{69}f^{46}\Delta^{12}.}                 \tag{N10.1}
\]
For a nonzero rational Weil class with $\delta t$ integral, put
$M_T=\lceil\sqrt{\int_A t^2}\rceil$. Let $A_{2,d}$ be the explicit
fourfold extraction coefficient in HD-D02, HD-D03, HD-D04 or HD-S16,
as specified for the field. The corresponding integral division bound is
\[
 \boxed{c_t\le A_{2,d}b_d^2\,10^{516}d^{138}
             f^{108}\Delta^{24}\,\delta M_T.}             \tag{N10.2}
\]
The smaller principal and field-specific bounds proved earlier remain
available on their stated components.

\paragraph{Proof: the actual split sixfold.}
First take $f=1$. Choose the integral generator
\[
 \alpha=\begin{cases}
 i,&d=1,\\
 \sqrt{-2},&d=2,\\
 (1+\sqrt{-d})/2,&d\equiv3\pmod4,
 \end{cases}
 \qquad \tau=\operatorname{Tr}(\alpha),\quad
 \nu=\operatorname{Nm}(\alpha).
\]
These generate the maximal orders for the displayed fields. Since
their class number is one, $H_1(A,\mathbb Z)$ is a free
$\mathcal O_K$-module. If $E_A$ is the Riemann form, the compatible
second-variable-linear Hermitian form is
\[
 h_A(x,y)=E_A(x,\alpha y)-\bar\alpha E_A(x,y).
\]
Its coefficients are integral. The identity Hermitian matrix induces
the unimodular alternating form in the integral basis
$(v_j,\alpha v_j)$. Replacing it by a matrix $H_A$ multiplies the
alternating determinant by $\operatorname{Nm}(\det H_A)$.
Thus $|\det H_A|=\Delta$, and signature $(2,2)$ fixes
$\det H_A=\Delta$.

Let $E_K=\mathbb C/\mathcal O_K$, with degree-one polarization
$\eta=[0]$. Form the actual surface
\[
 B=E_K^2,\qquad \alpha(x,y)=(\alpha x,\bar\alpha y),\qquad
 \theta_B=\eta_1+\Delta\eta_2.
\]
The second coordinate is the conjugate action; its Hermitian matrix
is $\operatorname{diag}(1,-\Delta)$. Therefore
\[
 X=A\times B,\qquad
 \Theta=p_A^*\theta_A+p_B^*\theta_B
\]
has signature $(3,3)$, determinant $-\Delta^2$, and polarization
product $\Delta^2$. The norm identity
$\operatorname{Nm}(\Delta)=\Delta^2$, followed by the explicit
Meyer and binary-plane splitting in HD-S03a, identifies its rational
Hermitian space with $U^3$.

HD-N09 applies to this polarized product, including this decomposable
point of its fixed moduli component. It supplies an effective integral
three-cycle $Y$ with nonzero Weil projection and
\[
 \int_Y\Theta^3\le 10^{258}d^{69}\Delta^{12}.               \tag{N10.3}
\]
The use of HD-N09 is the precise source of the numerical constant in
this statement.

\paragraph{Proof: four integral divisor contractions.}
Consider the four actual homomorphisms on $B$
\[
 f_1(x,y)=x,\quad f_2(x,y)=y,\quad f_3(x,y)=x+y,
 \quad f_4(x,y)=x+\alpha y,
\]
and put $L_j=f_j^*\eta$, $D_j=2L_j$. The elliptic line bundle
$\mathcal O(2[0])$ is globally generated, so the four $D_j$ are
classes of globally generated line bundles. Intersecting two distinct
pullback point classes gives the norm of the determinant of their two
row maps. Consequently their entire Gram matrix is
\[
 \left(\int_B L_jL_k\right)_{j,k}=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 0&1&1&\nu\\
 1&0&1&1\\
 1&1&0&\nu-\tau+1\\
 \nu&1&\nu-\tau+1&0
 \end{pmatrix},
 \qquad \det=\tau^2-4\nu=\operatorname{disc}(K)<0.          \tag{N10.4}
\]
In particular the four rational divisor classes are independent and
span the four-dimensional complex space $H^{1,1}(B)$. Their cup
functionals separate its rational subspace.

All four divisors obey the following nef inequality:
\[
 0\le D_j\le 2(1+\nu)\theta_B.                            \tag{N10.5}
\]
The coordinate maps and $x+y$ satisfy the smaller coefficient four.
For $x+\alpha y$, the difference of the polarization matrices is
\[
 2\left[(1+\nu)
    \begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&\Delta\end{pmatrix}
  -\begin{pmatrix}1&\alpha\\\bar\alpha&\nu\end{pmatrix}\right].
\]
Its diagonal entries are $2\nu$ and
$2((1+\nu)\Delta-\nu)$; its determinant is
$4\nu(1+\nu)(\Delta-1)$. They are nonnegative for
$\Delta\ge1$, proving (N10.5) by the actual Hermitian polarization
matrix. Thus no uncomputed comparison of polarizations occurs.

The exterior-product map places the complex Weil plane of $X$ in
the two endpoint lines
\[
 (\bigwedge^4 V_{A,\sigma}\otimes\bigwedge^2V_{B,\sigma})
 \oplus
 (\bigwedge^4 V_{A,\bar\sigma}\otimes
                     \bigwedge^2V_{B,\bar\sigma})
 \subseteq W_{A,\mathbb C}\otimes H^2(B,\mathbb C).
\]
It follows that $T_Y=(e_{W_A}\otimes1)[Y]_{4,2}$ is nonzero.
The Weil projector is a rational Hodge morphism; $[Y]$ has type
$(3,3)$ and $W_A$ has type $(2,2)$. Hence
\[
 T_Y\in W_A\otimes\bigl(H^2(B,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{1,1}(B)\bigr).
\]
By (N10.4), at least one of its four contractions against $D_j$
is nonzero. Choose a general divisor in that globally generated
system. Its pullback meets the finitely many components of $Y$
properly or avoids them. The cycle
\[
 Z_j=(p_A)_*\bigl(Y\cdot p_B^*D_j\bigr)
\]
is integral and effective; projection formula identifies its Weil
projection with the selected contraction. A contributing component
therefore has image of dimension two.

Set $a=p_A^*\theta_A$, $b=p_B^*\theta_B$. Every mixed nef
intersection on $Y$ is nonnegative. The same actual divisor satisfies
\[
 \deg_{\theta_A}Z_j
 \le 2(1+\nu)\int_Ya^2b
 \le \frac{2(1+\nu)}3\int_Y(a+b)^3
 \le b_d\,10^{258}d^{69}\Delta^{12},                       \tag{N10.6}
\]
where $b_d=\lceil2(1+\nu)/3\rceil$ gives exactly the displayed
integer table. A reduced irreducible detecting component has no
larger degree. This proves (N10.1) for the maximal order.

\paragraph{Proof: arbitrary fixed conductor and division.}
For an $\mathcal O_f$-lattice $\Lambda$ put
$\Lambda'=\Lambda+\alpha\Lambda$. The actual integral
$f\alpha$-action gives $f\Lambda'\subseteq\Lambda$.
The form $fE_A$ is integral on $\Lambda'$: both mixed terms are
integral, and
$E_A(\alpha x,\alpha y)=\nu E_A(x,y)$.
It is a positive Riemann form. The resulting quotient isogeny
$q:A\to A'$ of degree $e=[\Lambda':\Lambda]$ therefore satisfies
\[
 q^*\theta'=f\theta_A,\qquad
 \Delta'=\frac{f^4\Delta}{e}.
\]
Finite flat pullback preserves integral effectivity and induces an
isomorphism of rational Weil planes. Pulling back the maximal-order
detector multiplies its surface degree by $e/f^2$. Substituting into
(N10.6) gives the exact factor
\[
 \frac e{f^2}\left(\frac{f^4}{e}\right)^{12}
 =\frac{f^{46}}{e^{11}}\le f^{46},
\]
proving (N10.1). The specified fourfold extraction projectors have
coefficient $A_{2,d}$ and acquire the factor $f^{16}$ when every
endomorphism term $[u]^*$ is replaced by the actual $[fu]^*$.
Their integral Gram identity bounds the multiplier and each effective
signed part. Inserting (N10.1) yields
$f^{16}(f^{46})^2=f^{108}$ and proves (N10.2).
\hfill$\square$

\paragraph{Framework and source location.}
The construction uses the external product, the four displayed
elliptic homomorphisms, globally generated divisor intersections,
proper pushforward and finite flat pullback. These are the actual
Chow correspondences behind the exterior-power and Kunneth
realizations in the proof. Their finite-presentation spreads are
covered by EGA IV$_3$,8.8.2(i)--(ii). The product-and-contraction
operation is the one in Schoen1998, Proposition10, pp.332--333;
the full separating Gram and effective degree estimate above are
proved here. In the project notation, these bounds supply the
integral condition-A representatives of cote16, scan050, with
SIGN\_NEXT SN-C03/SN-C04 retained, and use the condition-I pairing
only at the subsequent extraction step. The result is obtained from
the explicit CM construction HD-N08--N09 and the Perry theorem named
in the prompt; no theorem of Markman's manuscript enters its proof.
