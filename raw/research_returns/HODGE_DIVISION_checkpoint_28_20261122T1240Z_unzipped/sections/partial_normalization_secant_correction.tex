\section{HD-R11: an actual partial-normalization secant complex and its twist}
\label{sec:partial-normalization-secant-correction}

\paragraph{Design provenance and the proved scope.}
The design is due to E.~Markman, \emph{Secant Sheaves and Weil
Classes on Abelian Varieties}, Proceedings of the ICM2026,
volume3, pp.586--602, Section12, printedp.600,
DOI\href{https://doi.org/10.1137/25M1803796}{10.1137/25M1803796}.
The author version arXiv:2509.23403v2, Section12, pp.21--22,
gives the same cycle of complete-intersection surfaces and the
same partial normalization. The published HTML and extracted
publisher PDF were read, and the author-version displayed
complex onp.22 was visually inspected. The displayed line twist
in both versions is $\mathcal O_X(\Theta)$.
No theorem of that source is adopted as a premise here.

We construct actual gluable perfect complexes on the CM fourfold
$E_i^4$ with the advertised imaginary-secant whole character.
The required line twist is the third power of the line bundle
defining the surface arrangement. The first-power twist appearing
in the source has a different second Chern-character component;
the exact relationship is tensoring by the inverse square.
The external products of the corrected complexes fall within
the already proved HD-R04 trace obstruction, with its precise
inherited-action scope. The weaker criterion posed as
Question11.4 in the survey is not asserted or refuted here.

\paragraph{1. Concrete CM data and an actual normal-crossing arrangement.}
Let $E_i=\mathbb C/(\mathbb Z+i\mathbb Z)$, let $X=E_i^4$,
and let
\[
 L=\mathop{\boxtimes}_{j=1}^4\mathcal O_{E_i}(3[0]),
 \qquad H=c_1(L),\qquad V=\int_XH^4/24=3^4=81.
                                                               \tag{R11.1}
\]
The plane cubic embedding on each elliptic factor, followed by
the Segre embedding, realizes $L$ as a very ample bundle.
Fix an odd integer $d\ge3$ and put $k=(d+9)/2\ge6$.
Choose $k$ general members $D_i$ of the complete linear system
$|L|$, cyclically indexed. Iterated Bertini applied to the
finitely many preceding smooth intersections gives smooth
transverse intersections in codimensions at most four and empty
intersections of at least five distinct members. The standard
published locator is Hartshorne, \emph{Algebraic Geometry},
II, Theorem8.18; only this ordinary hyperplane-section theorem
is used. At a point of a nonempty intersection the local defining
equations extend to a regular system of parameters, by the
independence of their differentials.

Set $Z_i=D_i\cap D_{i+1}$ and $Z=\bigcup_i Z_i$, with
its reduced scheme structure. Each $Z_i$ is a smooth surface.
Adjacent surfaces meet along the smooth triple intersection;
nonadjacent surfaces meet at the reduced four-divisor intersection
of $24V$ points. Among the latter pairs, distance two in the
cycle gives points on a third consecutive surface and on the
adjacent intersection curves. All remaining pairs give isolated
ordinary double points of $Z$. There are
\[
             M=12Vk(k-5)=V(d+9)(3d-3)                       \tag{R11.2}
\]
such isolated points. The count follows from
$\binom k2-k-k=k(k-5)/2$ unordered pairs of cyclic distance
at least three, each contributing $24V$. Distinct such pairs
have disjoint four-divisor intersections: distinct pairs using
different four-sets cannot share a point because five divisors
do not meet. Two distinct disjoint-edge pairs using the same
four vertices would form a four-cycle in the indexing cycle
$C_k$, which has no four-cycle for $k\ge6$.

Put
\[
                 q=V(d+9)(2d-1)=V(8k^2-38k).              \tag{R11.3}
\]
Both $q$ and $M-q=2Vk(2k-11)$ are positive integers.
Choose exactly $q$ of the isolated points and separate their
two branches, retaining all other identifications. This is an
actual finite partial normalization $\nu:\widetilde Z\to Z$.
One algebraic construction takes the full normalization algebra
and the inverse image of the length-$q$ direct summand of its
quotient by $\mathcal O_Z$ supported on these isolated points.
This inverse image is a finite coherent subalgebra: at a selected
point it is the normalization algebra, and at every other point
it is $\mathcal O_Z$. Its relative spectrum is the stated map.

At an isolated point the completed local ring is the union of
the transverse coordinate planes $(x_1,x_2)=0$ and
$(x_3,x_4)=0$. Its normalization has two regular local factors,
and the quotient is the difference of their residue values.
Faithful completion therefore gives the actual exact sequence
\[
 0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_Z\longrightarrow\nu_*\mathcal O_{\widetilde Z}
                  \longrightarrow\bigoplus_{p\in S}\mathcal O_p
                  \longrightarrow0,\qquad |S|=q.            \tag{R11.4}
\]
The sheaves are regarded as sheaves on $X$ through the closed
embedding throughout.

\paragraph{2. The exact closed-cover complex and its K-theory identity.}
The augmented alternating restriction complex for the closed
cover $Z=\bigcup_iZ_i$ is exact in this arrangement. This
claim includes its needed local justification. At each point,
complete the regular local ring in the normal-crossing
parameters. Every $Z_i$ through that point is defined by a
coordinate ideal. For a fixed power-series monomial, let $I$
be the set of these coordinate planes on which the monomial
survives. It survives on an intersection precisely for the
subsets of $I$. Its augmented restriction complex is therefore
the cochain complex of a full simplex, which is exact; the
empty set contributes zero. The usual contraction obtained
by inserting a fixed vertex preserves the monomial degree.
It acts continuously on the completed modules, proving exactness
there. Completion is faithfully flat, so the original coherent
complex is exact.

Write $u=1-e^{-H}$. The Koszul complex of an actual transverse
$j$-fold divisor intersection has character $u^j$.
The single surfaces number $k$. Adjacent pairs number $k$ and
have codimension three. The other pairs number $k(k-3)/2$
and have codimension four. Exactly $k$ triple intersections
of surfaces are nonempty: the three consecutive edges use
four divisors. All intersections of four surfaces use at least
five divisors and are empty. The exact restriction complex
therefore gives
\[
 \begin{split}
 \operatorname{ch}(\mathcal O_Z)
     &=ku^2-ku^3-\frac{k(k-5)}2u^4\\
     &=kH^2-2kH^3+\left(\frac{55k}{12}-\frac{k^2}2\right)H^4.
 \end{split}                                                \tag{R11.5}
\]
This uses the actual coherent K-theory identity; no general
inclusion--exclusion assertion for arbitrary closed covers is used.

The point class is $[p]=H^4/(24V)$. Equations(R11.3)--(R11.5)
determine the character of the actual two-term complex
\[
 F_\ell=\left[\mathcal O_X\longrightarrow
                       \nu_*\mathcal O_{\widetilde Z}\right]\otimes L^\ell,
           \quad\text{in cohomological degrees }0,1,
                                                               \tag{R11.6}
\]
as
\[
 \operatorname{ch}(F_\ell)=e^{\ell H}
      \left(1-kH^2+2kH^3+\left(\frac{k^2}{6}-3k\right)H^4\right).
                                                               \tag{R11.7}
\]
All terms in this equality are characters of the specified
perfect complexes: $X$ is smooth, so every coherent term is
perfect.

\paragraph{3. The corrected secant and literal line-twist map.}
For $\ell=3$, substituting $2k=d+9$ into(R11.7) gives
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
 \operatorname{ch}(F_3)
       &=1+3H-\frac d2H^2-\frac d2H^3+\frac{d^2}{24}H^4\\
       &=\alpha e^{sH}+\bar\alpha e^{-sH},
 \qquad s^2=-d,\qquad\alpha=\frac{1+3/s}{2}.
\end{aligned}                                                     \tag{R11.8}
\end{equation}
This last equality is checked term by term: the factorial
coefficients are exactly $(1,3,-d,-3d,d^2)$.
For $\ell=1$, instead,
\[
 \operatorname{ch}_0(F_1)=1,\qquad
 \operatorname{ch}_1(F_1)=H,\qquad
 \operatorname{ch}_2(F_1)=-\frac{d+8}{2}H^2.               \tag{R11.9}
\]
Every element of the span of $e^{sH},e^{-sH}$ with constant
term one has degree-two component $-dH^2/2$. Thus(R11.9)
proves the precise failure of that unshifted-span assertion
for the first-power twist. The exact repair is the actual
line-bundle equivalence
\[
                         F_1=F_3\otimes L^{-2}.              \tag{R11.10}
\]
In particular its character is the $e^{-2H}$ shear of the
secant(R11.8). Changing the cohomological degree convention
by a global shift multiplies both characters by the same sign
and does not remove the discrepancy in(R11.9).

The same calculation applies to the divisor arrangement specified
in the survey: each divisor has class $\Theta$, each reduced
fourfold intersection has24 points, and $V=1$. Its closed-cover
Koszul and partial-normalization sequences are precisely those
used above. Consequently the correction there is the literal
replacement of the displayed twist by $\mathcal O_X(3\Theta)$,
with the original object related by tensoring with
$\mathcal O_X(-2\Theta)$. This is an independently proved
character calculation on that stated arrangement, without a
semiregularity premise from the source.

\paragraph{4. Gluability and the already proved product obstruction.}
The actual cohomology sheaves of(R11.6) are
\[
 H^0(F_\ell)=\mathcal I_Z\otimes L^\ell,
 \qquad H^1(F_\ell)=\bigoplus_{p\in S}\mathcal O_p\otimes L^\ell.
                                                               \tag{R11.11}
\]
The two-step Postnikov filtration of the actual derived Hom
complex has as its only possible negative cohomology contribution
$\operatorname{Hom}(H^1(F_\ell),H^0(F_\ell))$ in degree$-1$.
All other graded contributions have nonnegative degree, and
degrees at most$-2$ have no contribution. The displayed Hom
is zero: a point-supported sheaf has no nonzero map to the
torsion-free ideal sheaf on the smooth integral $X$.
Therefore $\operatorname{Ext}^{<0}(F_\ell,F_\ell)=0$.
This uses the actual Postnikov maps and retains the nonzero
differential; it does not replace(R11.6) by its two cohomology
sheaves as a direct sum.

The corrected $F_3$ is thus an actual gluable ample fourfold
imaginary-secant seed on the CM fibre. It has precisely
the whole character and object hypotheses of HD-R04.
For every specified faithful coherent point action on such a
seed, degree-zero or degree-two trace has a nonzero kernel.
The actual external product of any two such seeds, with its
inherited product action, consequently fails weak semiregularity
by HD-R04's explicit Kunneth trace map. The literal common line
twist(R11.10) and an actual Fourier--Mukai equivalence transport
that kernel with the transported action. No different coherent
action or the survey's separate weaker criterion is inferred
from this statement.

The construction lives in coherent K-theory through the exact
Koszul, closed-cover and normalization sequences, and in the
derived category through the actual two-term complex. GRR and
the displayed exterior characters give its Hodge realization.
It supplies a concrete candidate and its failed trace route;
it supplies no bounded detecting family for a new component of
condition A and no use of positivity as a cycle-construction step.
