\section[HD-F1-S37: mixed ordinary-power modules]{HD-F1-S37: the normal obstruction for coherent modules with mixed ordinary-power generic fibres}
\label{sec:f1-norm-fourfold-mixed-power-modules}

Retain the actual normal integral norm image
$i:Y\hookrightarrow P$, its ideal $\mathcal I$,
$E=i_*\mathcal O_Y$, and the S29 geometric open.
Let $F$ be an actual coherent sheaf on $P$ with
set-theoretic support $Y$. At its generic point
$\xi$, require the actual module isomorphism
\[
 F_\xi\simeq\bigoplus_{a=1}^h
       (\mathcal O_{P,\xi}/\mathcal I_\xi^a)^{\oplus m_a},
 \qquad m_a\ge0,\qquad
 \ell=\sum_a m_a\binom{a+3}{4}>0.
 \tag{F1S37.1}
\]
The summands in (F1S37.1) need not extend to
global summands. All smaller-dimensional torsion,
additional thickening and coherent extensions
of the given sheaf are retained. We prove the
actual characteristic-image surjection
\[
\begin{split}
 \mathcal T_F:\operatorname{ev}_F
   \bigl(\mathfrak D_P\oplus H^2(P,\mathcal O_P)\bigr)
     &\longrightarrow W_E=\operatorname{ob}_E(\mathfrak D_P),\\
 \operatorname{ev}_F(\kappa+\eta)
     &\longmapsto-\ell\operatorname{ob}_E(\kappa).
\end{split}
 \tag{F1S37.2}
\]
For every such actual sheaf with the complete
S14 character, the invariant full degree-two
trace kernel has dimension at least
$16-r_C\ge4$, for every specified coherent
finite point lift. An explicit ordinarily
indecomposable sheaf in (F1S37.1), outside
every single-power generic-free class of S36,
is constructed in paragraph~6.

\paragraph{1. Actual diagonal ideal actions without lifted summands.}
Coherence and the support condition give an
integer $K$ with $\mathcal I^K F=0$.
Indeed on each affine chart the ideal is
contained in the radical of the annihilator;
finite generation gives a power, and a finite
cover gives one common power. Regard $F$
as the exact pushforward of this actual
$\mathcal O_{Y_K}$-module. The index $K$
may exceed every $a$ occurring generically.

Suppose $\operatorname{ev}_F(\kappa+\eta)=0$.
The precise Toda/BF comparison and S33
flat-sheaf extraction give an actual
epsilon-flat local sheaf $\widetilde F$
over $R_\epsilon=R[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)$,
with its original scalar-twisted overlap maps.
After clearing the finitely many denominators
of (F1S37.1) and its inverse, there is a
open $V\subset P$ with $U=V\cap Y$ dense
in $Y$ and contained in $Y_{\rm reg}$,
on which the actual central module decomposes as
\[
 F|_V=\bigoplus_a M_a,\qquad
 M_a=(R/I^a)^{m_a},\qquad
 \iota_a:M_a\longrightarrow F,\quad
 \pi_a:F\longrightarrow M_a.
 \tag{F1S37.3}
\]
Here and below summands of multiplicity zero
are omitted. These maps are $R$-linear maps
on the central fibre; no lift of their
idempotents to $\widetilde F$ is assumed.

For $f\in I^a$ and $v\in M_a$, choose a
lift $\widetilde v$ of $\iota_a(v)$.
Its product $f\widetilde v$ is in
$\epsilon\widetilde F\simeq F$. Define
\[
 b_f^a(v)=\pi_a\bigl(\epsilon^{-1}f\widetilde v\bigr),
 \qquad
 b^a:I^a\longrightarrow\operatorname{End}_{R/I^a}(M_a).
 \tag{F1S37.4}
\]
The inverse epsilon denotes that exact
submodule isomorphism. Replacing the lift
by $\widetilde v+\epsilon w$ changes the
answer by $\pi_a(fw)=f\pi_a(w)=0$.
Lifting $rv$ by $r\widetilde v$ proves
$R$-linearity in $v$; multiplication in
$R_\epsilon$ proves $R$-linearity in $f$.
Thus (F1S37.4) is an actual module map,
even though $f$ may act nontrivially on
the other central summands.

Apply the actual matrix trace and S35 map
to obtain
\[
 \delta_a=\frac1{m_a}\operatorname{Tr}_{R/I^a}(b^a),
 \qquad u_a=\rho_a(\delta_a),\qquad
 u=\frac1\ell\sum_a m_a r_a u_a,
 \quad r_a=\binom{a+3}{4}.
 \tag{F1S37.5}
\]
Each $u_a$ is an actual conormal functional
on $U$. The next paragraph proves that
their weighted sum is independent of the
chosen central decomposition, so specifies
a rational conormal functional on $Y$.

\paragraph{2. The moment trace of the actual coupled deformation.}
Work over a characteristic-zero field $L$
with $S=L[[x_1,\ldots,x_4]]$,
$\mathfrak n=(x_1,\ldots,x_4)$, and
$A_a=S/\mathfrak n^a$. Let $N_\epsilon$
be an actual epsilon-flat finite module
whose central module is
$\bigoplus_a A_a^{m_a}$.
It is finite free over $L[\epsilon]$:
lift a central basis, use $Q=\epsilon Q$
to prove generation, and use epsilon-flatness
to show the resulting kernel has zero
reduction and hence is zero.

Lift a central $L$-basis adapted to the
monomials of these summands. Multiplication
by $x_i$ is an actual matrix
\[
 X_i=X_i^0+\epsilon A_i,\qquad
 X_i^0=\bigoplus_a X_i^{(a)},
 \tag{F1S37.6}
\]
where $X_i^{(a)}$ is the usual truncated
monomial multiplication on $A_a^{m_a}$.
All off-diagonal blocks of $A_i$ are retained.
The diagonal block of the actual equation
$[X_i,X_j]=0$ is
\[
 [X_i^{(a)},A_j^{aa}]+[A_i^{aa},X_j^{(a)}]=0.
 \tag{F1S37.7}
\]
Consequently $X_i^{(a)}+\epsilon A_i^{aa}$
are commuting matrices on a free
$L[\epsilon]$-module. They define an actual
flat deformation $N_\epsilon^{(a)}$ of
$A_a^{m_a}$. Its action factors through
$S/\mathfrak n^{2a}$: an $a$-fold normal
product lands in epsilon, and two such
products vanish.

For every polynomial $f$, its diagonal
block on (F1S37.6) equals its value on
these diagonal-block matrices. A monomial
can leave a block and return only using
two off-diagonal factors, each divisible
by epsilon; their product is zero.
This proves the identity for every monomial
and then for $f$. All the formal series
act through a finite quotient, since
$\mathfrak n^{2K}N_\epsilon=0$ for a
common central annihilating power $K$.

The ideal-action map of
$N_\epsilon^{(a)}$ is precisely (F1S37.4):
both take the diagonal coefficient of
$\epsilon^{-1}f\widetilde v$ for
$f\in\mathfrak n^a$. Therefore the proved
single-power moment formula (F1S36.10)
and additivity of the matrix trace give
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \operatorname{Tr}_{L[\epsilon]}(\mu_f)&=0
                         &&(f\in\mathfrak n^2),\\
 \operatorname{Tr}_{L[\epsilon]}(\mu_f)
       &=\epsilon\sum_a m_a r_a u_a(f)
         =\epsilon\ell u(f)
                         &&(f\in\mathfrak n).
\end{aligned}
 \tag{F1S37.8}
\]
The left sides are traces of the given
whole module, hence do not depend on its
chosen central decomposition or on a lifted
basis. Two decompositions can be compared
after the same coefficient-field extension;
injectivity of that field extension then
identifies their rational conormal maps.
Formula (F1S37.5) already defines those
maps without choosing a coefficient field,
through the actual $R$-linear maps (F1S37.4).
Thus this comparison also verifies the
required coefficient-choice independence.

The modules $N_\epsilon^{(a)}$ in this
calculation are constructed diagonal
matrix representations. They are not
claimed to be submodules or quotients
of $N_\epsilon$. For example, for $c=4$
use the central basis $e_0,e_1,\ldots,e_4,f$
of $A_2\oplus A_1$ and set
\[
 X_i^0 e_0=e_i,\quad X_i^0 e_j=X_i^0 f=0,
 \qquad A_2 f=e_1,
 \quad\text{all other values of the }A_i\text{ zero}.
 \tag{F1S37.9}
\]
These actual epsilon matrices commute.
The central projection $P_0$ onto $A_2$
cannot lift to a commuting projection:
if $P_0+\epsilon P_1$ commuted with all
$X_i$, its block from $Lf$ to $A_2$
would require $z e_i=\delta_{i2}e_1$
for all $i$, where $z$ is the $e_0$
coefficient of $P_1f$. The equation for
$i=1$ gives $z=0$, contradicting $i=2$.
This verifies an actual coupled deformation
covered by (F1S37.8).

\paragraph{3. Regularity at all smooth support points and faithful descent.}
Fix any closed $y\in Y_{\rm reg}$, including
every defect point of $F$, and use the actual
compatible completed coordinates of S35:
\[
 \widehat R=B[[x_1,\ldots,x_4]],\quad
 B=\mathbf C[[t_1,\ldots,t_4]],\quad
 I\widehat R=\mathfrak n,\quad
 \widehat{\mathcal O}_{Y,y}=B.
 \tag{F1S37.10}
\]
Complete $\widetilde F$ by the flat map
$R_\epsilon\to\widehat R_\epsilon$.
Its central module is finite over $B$
because $I^KF=0$. Lifts of finite
$B$-generators generate the whole lifted
module over $B_\epsilon$, again by
$Q=\epsilon Q\Rightarrow Q=0$.
It remains epsilon-flat.

The exact finite-free resolution construction
in S36 paragraph~3 applies to this actual
module without any generic-freeness input.
Explicitly, the central module has a free
$B$-resolution of length at most four;
lift its generators and successive syzygies.
Each kernel is epsilon-flat, so reduction
is exact, and lifting the last free syzygy
terminates the resolution. Thus the actual
multiplication by $f$ has a categorical trace
\[
 \operatorname{tr}_{B_\epsilon}(\mu_f)
       =\sum_j(-1)^j\operatorname{Tr}(\mu_f^j)
                        \in B_\epsilon.
 \tag{F1S37.11}
\]
This is the same finite-resolution trace
whose homotopy independence, resolution
independence and base change were proved
in (F1S36.12)--(F1S36.13).

Put $L=\operatorname{Frac}(B)$.
Every element of $R$ outside the support
prime has nonzero constant image in $B$
and is invertible in the finite normal
quotient after this localization.
Hence the actual localization of
(F1S37.1) becomes the mixed module of
paragraph~2 over $L$. There is no exchange
of infinite completion and localization:
all actions factor through
$\mathfrak n^{2K}$.
The localized trace (F1S37.11) is the
ordinary trace (F1S37.8). Injectivity of
$B_\epsilon\to L_\epsilon$ consequently
gives, for each actual $f\in I_y$,
\[
       \operatorname{tr}_{B_\epsilon}(\mu_f)
                         =\epsilon\ell b_f,
              \qquad b_f\in B,
              \qquad b_f=u(f)\text{ in }L.
 \tag{F1S37.12}
\]

The value $u(f)$ belongs to
$\mathcal O_{Y,y}$ by faithful descent:
write it as $a/b$ in that domain.
Membership in its completion means
$a\in bB$; faithful flatness gives
$bB\cap\mathcal O_{Y,y}=b\mathcal O_{Y,y}$.
Thus every local ideal generator has a
regular value at every closed smooth
point. Finite generation and density
extend all linear relations and the
annihilation of $I^2$ throughout that
locus. Every height-one point of normal
$Y$ lies there. The normal-domain
intersection then extends the finitely
many generator values through its
remaining singular set, retaining all
relations by density. We obtain the
actual regular map
\[
                  u:\mathcal I/\mathcal I^2
                              \longrightarrow\mathcal O_Y.
 \tag{F1S37.13}
\]
No matrix trace with values in a singular
nonreduced structure sheaf is asserted
to extend.

\paragraph{4. Exact overlap gluing and the characteristic-image map.}
On each overlap, transport one central
decomposition (F1S37.3) through the
given central sheaf isomorphism. The
decomposition independence proved above
allows this choice in computing both
normal values. The actual maps
(F1S37.4) transform by conjugation,
plus multiplication by the ambient
derivative of $f$. Normalized matrix
trace therefore gives
\[
 \delta_{a,i}-\delta_{a,j}
                 =\partial_a(\kappa_{ij}),\qquad
 u_i-u_j=\frac1\ell\sum_a m_a r_a
                    \rho_a\partial_a(\kappa_{ij})
                 =\partial_1(\kappa_{ij}).
 \tag{F1S37.14}
\]
Here the last equality is the exact
S35 identity $\rho_a\partial_a=\partial_1$.
Scalar triple products from $\eta$
act trivially by conjugation. Density
extends (F1S37.14) to the regular maps
in (F1S37.13).

The actual local ideals
\[
 I_{\epsilon,i}=\{f+\epsilon h:f\in I,
                         \ h\bmod I=-u_i(f)\}
 \tag{F1S37.15}
\]
are ideals by conormal linearity.
Their quotients satisfy
$\ker\epsilon=\operatorname{im}\epsilon$,
by subtracting the displayed lift of
each constant term in $I$; hence they
are flat over the dual numbers.
Equation (F1S37.14) glues them in the
given ambient deformation. This constructs
an embedded deformation of the original
reduced $Y$ and proves, through the
actual S24 obstruction map,
\[
 \operatorname{ev}_F(\kappa+\eta)=0
          \ \Longrightarrow\ 
                       \operatorname{ob}_E(\kappa)=0.
 \tag{F1S37.16}
\]
Kernel inclusion makes (F1S37.2)
well defined and linear by subtracting
two representatives. It is surjective
because $\ell\ne0$ and $W_E$ is the
actual obstruction image. Its sign is
the fixed S31 jet/HKR sign.

\paragraph{5. The complete character and specified point lifts.}
The actual generic length is $\ell$,
so the localized leading-cycle map,
followed by the rational cycle-class map,
gives
\[
 \operatorname{ch}_j(F)=0\quad(j<4),\qquad
 \operatorname{ch}_4(F)
       =\ell[Y]=\ell\left(w+\frac{\beta^4}{72}\right).
 \tag{F1S37.17}
\]
Retain the complete specified equality
\[
 e^B\operatorname{ch}(F)=\lambda t+Q(\beta),
 \qquad B\in H^{1,1}(P,\mathbf Q),\quad
 0\ne\lambda t\in W_K(P),\quad Q\in\mathbf Q[x].
 \tag{F1S37.18}
\]
Comparison in degree four and cup product
with $\beta$ give $q_4=\ell/72$ and
$\lambda t=\ell w$, since $\beta w=0$
and $\beta^5\ne0$. Every higher defect
component remains in (F1S37.18).
The full BF exponential derivative gives
zero full trace on the actual sheared image
\[
 \operatorname{ev}_F
  \{\kappa+\kappa\mathbin{\lrcorner}B:
                                      \kappa\in\mathfrak D_P\}.
 \tag{F1S37.19}
\]
Its image under (F1S37.2) is $W_E$,
of dimension $16-r_C\ge4$.
Every specified coherent finite point
lift fixes this sheared image by the
actual Hochschild-action naturality:
translations and $\operatorname{Pic}^0$
tensoring act trivially on this source.
The precise R05 trace comparison then
gives the invariant full-trace kernel
bound. The negative self-Ext groups
vanish because $F$ is an actual sheaf.

\paragraph{6. An actual ordinarily indecomposable mixed-power sheaf.}
Choose two distinct closed points
$p,q\in Y_{\rm reg}$. Put
$N=\mathcal O_{Y_2}\oplus\mathcal O_Y$
and define the actual sheaf surjection
by the two fibre rows
\[
 N\longrightarrow\mathbf C_p\oplus\mathbf C_q,
 \qquad (1,1)\text{ at }p,\quad(1,2)\text{ at }q.
 \qquad M=\ker(N\longrightarrow\mathbf C_p\oplus\mathbf C_q).
 \tag{F1S37.20}
\]
Each row is nonzero, so this is a
surjection on the two stalks. Its
generic module is $A_2\oplus A_1$,
of length $5+1=6$.

Here is the exact extension of ordinary
endomorphisms used to prove indecomposability.
At each of $p,q$, the completed local
module $N$ is free of rank six over
$B=\mathbf C[[t_1,\ldots,t_4]]$.
Choose two actual support parameters
$s,t$; faithful flatness of completion
makes them a regular sequence on $N$
near that point. Such a module has the
punctured-neighborhood extension property.
Indeed $N_s\cap N_t=N$ in $N_{st}$:
from $t^b v=s^a w$, regularity of $t$
modulo $s^aN$ gives $v\in s^aN$.
That regularity follows by induction
from the regular sequence. A section
on the punctured neighborhood restricts
to this intersection and hence extends;
its difference from the extension is
zero on $D(s)$ and therefore zero,
since $s$ is a nonzerodivisor.
Shrinking and gluing proves
\[
             j_*\bigl(N|_{Y_2\setminus\{p,q\}}\bigr)=N.
 \tag{F1S37.21}
\]
For an ordinary endomorphism of $M$,
restriction away from $p,q$ and the
adjunction for this open immersion
therefore extend it uniquely to an
endomorphism of $N$. Its restriction
to $M$ agrees with the original map:
their difference is supported at
$p,q$, whereas $N$ has no such
submodule by the same nonzerodivisor.
Thus this extension preserves $M$,
and also preserves idempotence.

Reduce an endomorphism of $N$ modulo
$\mathcal I$. Its matrix on
$N/\mathcal I N=\mathcal O_Y^2$ is
\[
                         \begin{pmatrix}a&0\\c&d\end{pmatrix},
                                  \qquad a,c,d\in\mathbf C.
 \tag{F1S37.22}
\]
The entries are constant because
$H^0(Y,\mathcal O_Y)=\mathbf C$.
The upper-right entry is zero: over
$Y_{\rm reg}$ an actual map
$\mathcal O_Y\to\mathcal O_{Y_2}$
lands in $\operatorname{Ann}(\mathcal I)
=\mathcal I/\mathcal I^2$, and hence
reduces to zero; density proves zero
on integral $Y$.

Preservation of $M$ in (F1S37.20)
means each row $(1,\lambda)$, for
$\lambda=1,2$, is taken to a scalar
multiple of itself. The actual row
product gives $d=a+\lambda c$.
Hence $c=0$ and $d=a$.
For an idempotent, $a^2=a$, so
$a=0$ or $1$. In the first case
its image lies in $\mathcal I N$,
and its square lies in
$\mathcal I^2N=0$; it is zero.
Apply the same argument to its
complement when $a=1$.
Thus $M$ has no nontrivial ordinary
idempotent and is ordinarily indecomposable.

The action of $\mathcal I$ on its
generic $A_2$ summand is nonzero.
It therefore cannot be free over
$A_1$. Freeness over $A_2$ would
make its length divisible by five,
whereas its length is six. For
$a\ge3$, a nonzero free $A_a$-module
has nonzero $\mathfrak n^2$ action,
whereas $\mathcal I^2M=0$.
This proves through the actual ideal
actions and lengths that no single
ordinary power makes its generic
module free. Likewise it cannot be
$Ri_*G$ for an actual $G\in D^b_{\rm coh}(Y)$:
the cohomology of that exact closed
pushforward is annihilated by
$\mathcal I$, contradicting the
displayed action on $M$.

The actual exact sequence gives,
after the cycle-class map,
\[
 \operatorname{ch}(i_{2*}M)
  =\operatorname{ch}(i_{2*}\mathcal O_{Y_2})
      +\operatorname{ch}(i_*\mathcal O_Y)-[p]-[q],
 \qquad [p]=[q]=\frac{\beta^8}{\int_P\beta^8}.
 \tag{F1S37.23}
\]
Thus the full S14 form for the two
actual sheaves $i_{2*}M$ and $i_{2*}N$
is equivalent, with only the displayed
top polynomial term changed. The
kernel construction alone is not
asserted to prove that full character
form. In the Chow group the exact
point classes are retained before
applying cycle class.

\paragraph{7. Boundary and sources.}
The generic direct-sum isomorphism
(F1S37.1) is used, not an associated
graded equality. For example the
actual flat module
\[
 L[\epsilon][x_1,\ldots,x_4]/
                  (x_1^s-\epsilon,x_2,x_3,x_4),\qquad s\ge2,
 \tag{F1S37.24}
\]
has central composition factors all
$L$, but the trace of multiplication
by $x_1^s\in\mathfrak n^2$ is
$s\epsilon\ne0$. Its generic central
module is the given directional
thickening, not the direct sum of
those factors. This explicitly
prevents replacing (F1S37.1) by a
filtration condition. Arbitrary
generic module extensions and
multi-cohomology complexes remain
outside the proved scope.

The diagonal tangent equation
(F1S37.7) is the literal block
projection of the commuting-matrix
tangent map in Jelisiejew--\v Sivic,
\emph{Components and singularities
of Quot schemes and varieties of
commuting matrices}, J.~reine angew.
Math.~\textbf{788} (2022), 129--187,
DOI 10.1515/crelle-2022-0018,
Lemma~3.1; its complete supplied
proof was freshly read. Their
Proposition~4.27 and complete proof
were also read: its lifted direct-sum
decomposition uses the displayed
comaximal support ideals. Here all
generic blocks have the same support
prime, and the actual maps used are
(F1S37.4), the diagonal matrix
projection and the total trace;
(F1S37.9) checks that no lifted
direct-sum assertion is being used.
The finite coefficient resolution,
perfect trace, faithful descent,
normal extension, Toda/BF signs
and full-character comparison
retain their completely checked
S33--S36 primary scopes. Every
new mixed-block and indecomposability
map is proved above.
