\section{The precise scope of the primitive-reflection field search}

\paragraph{HD-I11 (trace fields and actual period factorizations).}
Among the natural primitive exceptional reflection representations
of rank at least three, the only nonreal character field beyond
$\mathbb Q(i)$ and $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})$ that embeds in an
imaginary quadratic field of class number one is
$\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-7})$, furnished by $G_{24}$.
The natural representation of $G_{12}$ has field
$\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-2})$ and rank two; every signature-$(3,3)$
Hurwitz source for that representation has period rank at most seven.
The statement concerns the natural representations and the specified
trace-field inclusion. The separate rational-block construction
described below also has an explicit period-factorization bound.

\paragraph{Published classification input and exact field test.}
The source is I.~Marin and J.~Michel,
\emph{Automorphisms of complex reflection groups},
Representation Theory \textbf{14} (2010), 747--788,
Theorem~1.2 and Table~1.
The directly inspected author version dated1 February2010 has the
trace-field statement and Theorem~1.2 on printed p.2, Table~1 on
printed pp.19--21, and the rank-two description of $G_4$--$G_{22}$
in Section9 on printed p.30. It is available at
\url{https://www.lamfa.u-picardie.fr/marin/arts/autgal.pdf}.
These author-version page numbers are distinguished from the
journal pagination. Both authors' publication lists independently
confirm the journal metadata. Jean Michel's primary four-page
table, at
\url{https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~jean.michel/papiers/table.pdf},
also lists the invariant degrees and fields; the number of
invariant degrees gives the rank, including the rank-four
group $G_{31}$ despite its five displayed generators.

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
 \text{group}&\text{rank}&\text{natural character field}\\ \hline
 G_{12}&2&\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-2})\\
 G_{23}&3&\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\\
 G_{24}&3&\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-7})\\
 G_{25},G_{26}&3&\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})\\
 G_{27}&3&\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3},\sqrt5)\\
 G_{28}&4&\mathbb Q\\
 G_{29},G_{31}&4&\mathbb Q(i)\\
 G_{30}&4&\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\\
 G_{32}&4&\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})\\
 G_{33}&5&\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})\\
 G_{34}&6&\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})\\
 G_{35},G_{36},G_{37}&6,7,8&\mathbb Q
\end{array}                                                   \tag{I11.1}
\]
The rational cases are the Weyl groups $F_4,E_6,E_7,E_8$,
whose root lattices themselves give the rational models.
Every other exceptional group $G_4$--$G_{22}$ has rank two.

Here is the exact obstruction used with this table. A matrix
realization $\rho:G\to\operatorname{GL}_m(K)$ induces the inclusion
\[
 \mathbb Q(\operatorname{tr}\rho(g):g\in G)\hookrightarrow K.
                                                               \tag{I11.2}
\]
Thus an imaginary quadratic character field embeds precisely when
it equals $K$. A real quadratic field cannot embed, since
$K\cap\mathbb R=\mathbb Q$. A quartic field cannot embed in a
degree-two field, by the tower law. Applying (I11.2) to (I11.1)
proves the stated field scope. Marin--Michel Theorem1.2 further
shows that a faithful irreducible representation preserving the
original reflections is a Galois conjugate of the natural one.
The same test therefore applies to those representations as well.

\paragraph{The rank-two dimension bound with arbitrary branch classes.}
Let $\rho$ be a faithful nonreal representation of rank $m$,
with conjugate holomorphic multiplicities both equal to $n$
for a connected $G$-cover of $\mathbb P^1$. Adding the two
Chevalley--Weil formulas gives
\[
 2n=-2m+\sum_{i=1}^b
                    \operatorname{codim}V^{g_i}.             \tag{I11.3}
\]
Indeed, for every nontrivial inertia eigenvalue with angle
$a\in(0,1)$, the two conjugate fractional contributions add to
$a+(1-a)=1$; an eigenvalue1 contributes zero. This verifies
the displayed sum independently of choices of inertia generators.
Every $g_i\ne1$ acts nontrivially by faithfulness, so each
codimension is at least one. It follows that
\[
 b\le2n+2m,\qquad
 \operatorname{rank}(d\,\mathrm{Per})\le b-3
                                  \le2n+2m-3.              \tag{I11.4}
\]
Here the first morphism is the finite Hurwitz map to the ordered
configuration space of $b$ branch values modulo
$\operatorname{PGL}_2$, followed by the actual polarized Jacobian
factor map. For $n=3,m=2$, the bound is seven, which is less than
the nine-dimensional signature-$(3,3)$ period domain. No
reflection-only restriction on the individual branch classes
was used.

\paragraph{Rational group-algebra blocks: the actual power isogeny.}
Suppose the chosen rational simple group-algebra block is
$M_m(\mathbb Q)$, and the sixfold and its desired $K$-action
are both cut out inside that same block. Fix the rational
idempotent selecting the sixfold, of matrix rank $k$, and fix
the embedding $K\hookrightarrow M_k(\mathbb Q)$.
Choose a self-adjoint rank-one idempotent $e_{11}$ in this
positive rational matrix algebra, extend it to matrix units
$e_{ij}$ in the corner, and let
$B=\operatorname{Im}(Ne_{11})^0$ for an integer $N$ clearing
their denominators. The algebraic group correspondences
$Ne_{i1}$ give the actual homomorphism
\[
 \alpha:B^k\longrightarrow A,\qquad
 (b_1,\ldots,b_k)\longmapsto\sum_{i=1}^k Ne_{i1}b_i.          \tag{I11.5}
\]
The matrix-unit identities give its inverse on rational
homology using $e_{1i}/N$. Consequently $\alpha$ is an isogeny.
It identifies the rational Hodge structure with
$\mathbb Q^k\otimes H^1(B,\mathbb Q)$, and the fixed $K$-action
is the specified matrix action on the first tensor factor.

This also identifies the polarizations rather than discarding
them. After using the self-adjoint projection for the polarized
factor, the pullback of the polarization has the form
$Q\otimes E_B$, with $Q$ a fixed positive rational symmetric
matrix. In fact each entry is obtained from
$(e_{i1})^{\dagger}e_{j1}$ in the one-dimensional corner
$e_{11}M_m(\mathbb Q)e_{11}$, hence is a rational multiple of
the restriction polarization on $B$. Denominators are already
realized by the isogeny (I11.5).
Thus, for the fixed matrix data, the period map factors through
the polarized moduli of $B$.

The inclusion $K\hookrightarrow M_k(\mathbb Q)$ makes
$\mathbb Q^k$ a $K$-vector space, so $k$ is even. As
$k\dim B=6$, the only possibilities are
$(k,\dim B)=(2,3)$ and $(6,1)$. The period image therefore has
dimension at most
\[
 \frac{\dim B(\dim B+1)}2\le6.                            \tag{I11.6}
\]
This is a consequence of the displayed algebraic isogeny and
polarized Hodge factorization.

\paragraph{The eightfold consequence of the same proved maps.}
For signature $(4,4)$, equation (I11.4) reads
\[
 \operatorname{rank}(d\,\mathrm{Per})\le 2m+5.
                                                               \tag{I11.7}
\]
A dominant source for the sixteen-dimensional Weil domain
therefore requires $m\ge6$. Among the nonreal natural primitive
exceptional characters in (I11.1) whose fields embed in an
imaginary quadratic field, the only such representation is
$G_{34}$ with field $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})$.
The Gaussian rank-four cases have rank at most thirteen,
$G_{24}$ has rank at most eleven, and $G_{33}$ has rank at most
fifteen. These inequalities again allow arbitrary nonidentity
branch classes, since their proof is (I11.3).

For the stated rational-block construction on an eightfold,
the same homomorphism (I11.5) and its polarization comparison
apply with $k\dim B=8$. The evenness of $k$ leaves exactly
\[
 (k,\dim B)=(2,4),(4,2),(8,1).
\]
Thus the period map factors through a polarized abelian moduli
space of dimension at most $4\cdot5/2=10<16$.
This proves the eightfold restriction for precisely the same
natural-character and fixed rational-block constructions.

\paragraph{Scope.}
Equations (I11.2), (I11.4), and (I11.5)--(I11.6) cover exactly
the stated matrix realizations and group-algebra constructions.
They do not classify arbitrary nonreflection characters,
additional endomorphisms outside the indicated block, or
families with varying rational correspondences.
The successful $G_{24}$ source and its effective suppliers are
proved separately in HD-G12 and HD-S13--HD-S16.
