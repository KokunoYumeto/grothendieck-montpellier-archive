\section{HD-G21: actual graph-grid cones with low-degree coupling}
\label{sec:clean-grid-cones}

\paragraph{The actual family and its conclusion.}
For every positive squarefree integer \(d\), we construct below a
finite collection of graph sheaves on a CM abelian eightfold of split
Weil type for \(K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-d})\). A multivariate interpolation
identity gives an integral difference of these graph sheaves with
character \(16w_d\), where \(0\ne w_d\in W_K\). Opposite signs
occur at adjacent grid points whose graph difference has rank one.
Their \(\operatorname{Ext}^2\) space has dimension six, so there are
actual nonzero low-degree coupling maps.

Let \(P=L\oplus P_0\) be the positive graph sum together with the
specified ample line bundle, and let \(N\) be the negative graph sum.
For every \(\delta\in\operatorname{Ext}^2(P,N)\), form the actual
triangle
\begin{equation}\label{eq:G21-triangle}
 N[1]\longrightarrow E_\delta\xrightarrow{q}P
                     \xrightarrow{\delta}N[2].
\end{equation}
Nonzero coupling maps give nonsplit triangles. Every \(E_\delta\)
is perfect, has no negative self-Ext, and has whole character
\[
                 \operatorname{ch}(E_\delta)=e^\theta+16w_d.
\]
Nevertheless its actual degree-two Atiyah trace has a kernel of
complex dimension at least twelve. The construction and proof below
give twelve explicit classes, independently of \(\delta\), including
all simultaneous edge couplings. Its finite point stabilizer is trivial.
After an isogeny pullback these classes remain invariant under every
coherent action by a subgroup of the point stabilizer, and retain a
nonzero trace kernel. Actual Fourier--Mukai transport of the object
and action preserves that conclusion.

This assertion treats the complete two-layer graph-grid construction
\eqref{eq:G21-triangle}. It makes no assertion about longer complexes,
additional graph-dependent line-bundle twists, or different objects.

\paragraph{The variety, compatible action, and graph classes.}
Put \(X=E_i^4\), \(A=X\times X\), where
\(E_i=\mathbb C/(\mathbb Z+i\mathbb Z)\). Let \(M=\mathcal O_{E_i}(0)\),
and choose the product line bundle
\[
 L=\boxtimes_{j=1}^4\bigl(p_{1j}^*M^d\otimes p_{2j}^*M\bigr),
 \qquad \theta=c_1(L)=dH_1+H_2.
\]
Use integral one-forms \((x_j,y_j,u_j,v_j)\) on each pair of elliptic
factors, with \((x_j,y_j)\) on the first copy. The action of
\(s=\sqrt{-d}\) is the actual endomorphism
\[
 J_d(z,z')=(z',-dz),\qquad
 J_d^*x_j=u_j,\quad J_d^*u_j=-dx_j,
\]
and likewise for \(y_j,v_j\). It squares to \(-d\), has
holomorphic multiplicities \((4,4)\), and is skew-adjoint for
\(\theta\): the matrix identity
\(J_d^t\operatorname{diag}(dI,I)
=-\operatorname{diag}(dI,I)J_d\) proves compatibility. The
explicit rational hyperbolic basis of HD-S21 identifies its Hermitian
space with \(U^4\); the same variety, action and polarization are
used here.

For \(\epsilon=(\epsilon_1,\ldots,\epsilon_4)\in\{-1,0,1\}^4\),
let \(B_\epsilon\) be the graph of
\(\operatorname{diag}(\epsilon_1,\ldots,\epsilon_4):X\to X\).
Set
\[
 f_j(t)=(u_j-tx_j)(v_j-ty_j),\qquad
 [B_\epsilon]=\prod_j f_j(\epsilon_j).
\]
The graph is the connected kernel of the surjection
\((z,z')\mapsto z'-\operatorname{diag}(\epsilon)z\); the displayed
formula follows by pullback of its point volume. Its tangent and
normal bundles are trivial. Thus Grothendieck--Riemann--Roch gives
the exact whole character \(\operatorname{ch}(\mathcal O_{B_\epsilon})
=[B_\epsilon]\). Define
\[
 F(s)=\prod_j f_j(s),\qquad
 w_d=\frac{F(s)-F(-s)}{2s}.
\]
Each factor \(u_j-sx_j,v_j-sy_j\) has field eigenvalue \(-s\),
so \(F(s),F(-s)\) are the two determinant volumes. The displayed
difference is a rational integral Weil class; expansion replaces
each odd power \(s^{2a+1}/s\) by \((-d)^a\). It is nonzero,
since a monomial using exactly one first-copy one-form has
coefficient \(-1\).

\paragraph{The complete integral grid identity.}
The following identity holds separately in each pair of factors:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:G21-one-pair-interpolation}
 f_j(s)=(d+1)f_j(0)+\frac{s-d}{2}f_j(1)
                              +\frac{-s-d}{2}f_j(-1).
\end{equation}
Indeed both sides have the same coefficients of
\(u_jv_j\), \(u_jy_j+x_jv_j\), and \(x_jy_j\), using \(s^2=-d\).
For a grid point with \(k\) nonzero coordinates, write
\[
 R_\epsilon+S_\epsilon s
       =\prod_{\epsilon_j\ne0}(-d+\epsilon_js)
                 \quad\text{in }\mathbb Z[d,s]/(s^2+d),
 \qquad
 c_\epsilon=2^{4-k}(d+1)^{4-k}S_\epsilon\in\mathbb Z.
\]
Taking the odd part of the product of
\eqref{eq:G21-one-pair-interpolation} gives the complete exact identity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:G21-grid-identity}
                     16w_d=\sum_\epsilon c_\epsilon[B_\epsilon].
\end{equation}
For each fixed integer \(d\), omit the zero coefficients and put
\[
 P_0=\bigoplus_{c_\epsilon>0}
          \mathcal O_{B_\epsilon}^{\,c_\epsilon},\qquad
 N=\bigoplus_{c_\epsilon<0}
          \mathcal O_{B_\epsilon}^{\,-c_\epsilon}.
\]
No graph occurs on both sides. In particular, for every standard
coordinate vector \(e_j\) and every pair \(i\ne j\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:G21-important-coefficients}
 c_{e_j}=8(d+1)^3>0,
 \qquad c_{e_i+e_j}=-8d(d+1)^2<0.
\end{equation}
These are actual adjacent grid points with rank-one slope difference.

\paragraph{Actual coupling maps and the absence of negative self-Ext.}
Distinct graph sheaves have no degree-zero Hom in either direction.
Any image would be supported on their intersection, a proper closed
subset of the smooth irreducible target graph, whereas its structure
sheaf has no such torsion subsheaf. Also
\(\operatorname{Hom}(L,\mathcal O_{B_\epsilon})=0\). More precisely,
\[
 L|_{B_\epsilon}=\boxtimes_j M^{d+\epsilon_j^2},\qquad
 \operatorname{Ext}^a(L,\mathcal O_{B_\epsilon})=0\quad(a\ne4).
\]
Each inverse elliptic factor has negative degree, hence has zero
\(H^0\); elliptic Riemann--Roch and Kunneth concentrate its product
cohomology in degree four. Thus \(\operatorname{Hom}(P,N)=0\).

For an explicit nonzero coupling, compare
\(B_{e_1}\) and \(B_{e_1+e_2}\). They agree in three elliptic
pairs; in the remaining pair the two graph curves meet transversely
in exactly one point. A single graph curve has self-Ext dimensions
\((1,2,1)\): the graph shear identifies its structure sheaf with
\(\mathcal O_{E_i}\boxtimes\mathcal O_0\), and Kunneth gives
that array. For the two transverse graph curves the local Koszul
resolution gives a single \(\operatorname{Ext}^1\simeq\mathbb C\).
Consequently
\begin{equation}\label{eq:G21-edge-Ext}
 \dim\operatorname{Ext}^a(\mathcal O_{B_{e_1}},
                   \mathcal O_{B_{e_1+e_2}})
       =\binom6{a-1}\quad(1\le a\le7).
\end{equation}
In particular its degree-two space has dimension six. A nonzero
degree-one transverse class tensored with a degree-one self-Ext class
on any shared factor gives a concrete nonzero degree-two class.
Insert it between one copy of each graph in
\eqref{eq:G21-important-coefficients}; this defines an actual nonzero
\(\delta\). All other degree-two edge classes may be included as
well, with arbitrary coefficients.

For completeness, the negative self-Ext calculation is exact for
any two sheaves in \eqref{eq:G21-triangle}:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:G21-negative}
 \operatorname{Ext}^k(E_\delta,E_\delta)
          \simeq\operatorname{Ext}^{k+1}(P,N)\qquad(k<0).
\end{equation}
Applying \(\operatorname{Hom}(P,-[k])\) to the triangle identifies
\(\operatorname{Hom}(P,E_\delta[k])\) with
\(\operatorname{Ext}^{k+1}(P,N)\), since both adjacent negative
self-Ext groups of \(P\) vanish. Precomposition by \(q\) is then
an isomorphism to \(\operatorname{Ext}^k(E_\delta,E_\delta)\):
the two adjacent groups are
\(\operatorname{Hom}(N[2],E_\delta[k])\) and
\(\operatorname{Hom}(N[1],E_\delta[k])\), both zero by the same
triangle and negative Ext vanishing for sheaves. The isomorphism
sends \(\alpha:P\to N[k+1]\) to the literal composition through
\(N[1]\to E_\delta\), shifted by \(k\), and \(q\).
Equation \eqref{eq:G21-negative} and \(\operatorname{Hom}(P,N)=0\)
prove the asserted absence of negative self-Ext. All graph sheaves
are perfect on the smooth variety, so the cones are perfect.
Their whole character follows from \eqref{eq:G21-grid-identity}.

\paragraph{A scalar trace kernel which every coupling preserves.}
Since \(\operatorname{Ext}^2(L,N)=0\), the cone has an actual
direct-sum decomposition
\[
 E_\delta\simeq L\oplus G_\delta,
 \qquad N[1]\longrightarrow G_\delta\xrightarrow{q_0}P_0
                     \xrightarrow{\delta_0}N[2],
 \qquad \operatorname{ch}(G_\delta)=16w_d.
\]
The vanishing of the two degree-zero cross Hom spaces between
\(L\) and \(G_\delta\) follows from this triangle:
\(\operatorname{Ext}^1(L,N)=\operatorname{Hom}(L,P_0)=0\)
and \(\operatorname{Hom}(P_0,L)
=\operatorname{Hom}(N[1],L)=0\). Thus its two projectors are
central in \(\operatorname{End}(E_\delta)\).

Let \(\chi_j=x_j-iy_j\) and \(\psi_j=u_j-iv_j\), and fix
the embedding \(s=i\sqrt d\). The antiholomorphic field eigenforms
are
\[
 \alpha_j^+=\psi_j+s\chi_j,\qquad
 \alpha_j^-=\psi_j-s\chi_j,
 \qquad J_d^*\alpha_j^\pm=\pm s\alpha_j^\pm.
\]
The sixteen mixed classes
\(\eta_{ij}=\alpha_i^+\wedge\alpha_j^-\in H^2(\mathcal O_A)\)
all satisfy \(\eta_{ij}w_d=0\). Each of the two determinant
volumes contains all the antiholomorphic factors of its field type;
one factor of \(\eta_{ij}\) therefore repeats a factor in each
volume. This also proves the vanishing directly in the exterior
algebra.

For any scalar class \(\eta\in H^2(\mathcal O_A)\), naturality
of scalar multiplication gives
\[
 q_0[2](\eta\operatorname{id}_{G_\delta})
                 =(\eta\operatorname{id}_{P_0})q_0.
\]
The map
\begin{equation}\label{eq:G21-precomposition-injection}
 \operatorname{Ext}^2(P_0,P_0)
       \xrightarrow{\ q_0^*\ }\operatorname{Hom}(G_\delta,P_0[2])
\end{equation}
is injective. In the exact Hom sequence its kernel comes from
\(\operatorname{Hom}(N[2],P_0[2])=\operatorname{Hom}(N,P_0)=0\).
It follows that independent scalar classes on \(P_0\) remain
independent as scalar self-Ext classes on \(G_\delta\), for every
choice of the actual differential \(\delta_0\).

Here are twelve independent such classes. Use the span of
\(\eta_{ij}\) with \(i\ne j\), and restrict to the four positive
graphs \(B_{e_a}\) of \eqref{eq:G21-important-coefficients}.
For a diagonal graph of slope \(\epsilon\), restriction is
\[
 \eta_{ij}|_{B_\epsilon}
       =(\epsilon_i+s)(\epsilon_j-s)\chi_i\wedge\chi_j.
\]
For each \(i<j\), the coefficients of \(\eta_{ij},\eta_{ji}\)
on the graphs \(B_{e_i},B_{e_j}\), in the ordered basis
\(\chi_i\wedge\chi_j\), are the rows of
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}
  d-s&-d-s\\ d+s&-d+s
 \end{pmatrix},\qquad \det=4ds\ne0.
\]
Different pairs use different exterior basis vectors. Hence the
combined restriction is injective on this twelve-dimensional span.
Restriction to a graph is exactly the scalar self-Ext map: the actual
graph shear identifies \(\mathcal O_{B_\epsilon}\) with
\(\mathcal O_X\boxtimes\mathcal O_0\), and its
\(H^2(\mathcal O_X)\otimes\operatorname{Hom}(\mathcal O_0,\mathcal O_0)\)
Kunneth summand is an injective part of self-Ext. Thus the restriction
calculation proves the claimed independence in
\(\operatorname{Ext}^2(P_0,P_0)\), and
\eqref{eq:G21-precomposition-injection} proves it on \(G_\delta\).

The full degree-two Atiyah trace of a scalar class is exactly
\[
 \sigma^2_{G_\delta}(\eta\operatorname{id}_{G_\delta})
     =\eta\,\operatorname{ch}(G_\delta)=16\eta w_d.
\]
This follows component by component from
\(\operatorname{tr}(\eta\operatorname{id}\,\operatorname{At}^a/a!)
=\eta\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{At}^a/a!)\); the degree is
even, so there is no additional sign. The twelve classes just
constructed have zero image. Their inclusion into the \(G_\delta\)
block of \(E_\delta=L\oplus G_\delta\) is injective and preserves
that trace. This proves \(\dim\ker\sigma^2_{E_\delta}\ge12\)
by actual maps, without any inference from a comparison of character
rank and self-Ext dimension.

\paragraph{Point stabilizers and coherent invariant trace.}
The cohomology sheaves of \(E_\delta\) are exactly
\(\mathcal H^{-1}=N\) and \(\mathcal H^0=L\oplus P_0\).
An isomorphism \(t_a^*E_\delta\otimes Q\simeq E_\delta\),
with \(Q\in\widehat A\), preserves the torsion part of degree-zero
cohomology. Translation cannot permute different graph slopes,
because their tangent subspaces are different. Thus
\(a\in\bigcap_{j=1}^4B_{e_j}\). This intersection is the origin:
writing \(a=(z,z')\), the equations give \(z'_j=0\) by taking
any \(e_i\) with \(i\ne j\), and then \(z_j=0\) by taking
\(e_j\). These are literal equations with coefficient one, so no
torsion points remain. The torsion-free quotient \(L\) now gives
\(L\otimes Q\simeq L\), hence \(Q=\mathcal O_A\).

Let \(p:A'\to A\) be any isogeny and put \(E'=p^*E_\delta\).
The same support argument forces \(p(a)=0\) for any point stabilizer
\((a,Q)\) of \(E'\). Its rank-one quotient is \(p^*L\);
translation by \(\ker p\) fixes this pullback, so again \(Q\) is
trivial. Conversely the pullback has its actual deck linearization.
Its complete point stabilizer is therefore \(\ker p\).

The two cross Hom spaces between \(p^*L\) and \(p^*G_\delta\)
remain zero. One can check the only needed vanishing by pushing each
graph component along its isogeny to the product graph: the pushforward
of its structure sheaf is a sum of torsion flat line bundles, and
each resulting negative-degree elliptic product has cohomology only
in degree four. Hence the projector to \(p^*G_\delta\) remains
central, independently of a choice of linearization.

Translations act trivially on \(H^2(\mathcal O_{A'})\), and the
scalar action is natural for every isomorphism. Thus all twelve
classes
\(p^*\eta\operatorname{id}_{p^*G_\delta}\) are invariant under
every coherent subgroup action in this point stabilizer, not only
under the standard deck linearization. They remain independent:
finite etale pullback on self-Ext has a left inverse up to \(\deg p\),
given by adjunction followed by the sheaf trace
\(p_*\mathcal O_{A'}\to\mathcal O_A\).
Their traces are still zero by the same scalar formula. Finally an
actual Fourier--Mukai equivalence carries these self-Ext classes,
their trace maps and any given coherent action through its exact
functorial diagrams; the graph isomorphism of HD-G19 also identifies
the point stabilizers. The invariant trace kernel therefore survives
these actual transports.
