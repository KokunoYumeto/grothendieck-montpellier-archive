\subsection{HD-N17: the full CM sixfold orbit has split target form}
\label{sec:HD-N17}

\paragraph{The actual objects and the precise assertion.}
Start with any actual perfect complex constructed in HD-N07 or
HD-N08, on the stated CM abelian sixfold, and take any finite
composition of actual Fourier--Mukai equivalences between abelian
varieties, isogeny pullbacks and pushforwards, tensor products with
arbitrary line bundles, translations, shifts and derived duals.
The arbitrary line bundles include mixed divisor classes on a product.
Every resulting whole character of the form
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{ch}(E)=e^B\bigl(P(\theta)+\lambda t\bigr),
 \quad B\in\operatorname{NS}(A)_{\mathbf Q},\quad
 P\in\mathbf Q[z],\quad \deg P\leq6,
 \quad0\ne\lambda t\in W_K(A),                       \tag{N17.1}
\end{equation}
on a polarized Weil sixfold of signature $(3,3)$ has split
rational $K$-Hermitian form. The target imaginary quadratic
field $K$ need not equal the field parameter used to construct
the source object. Thus these actual functorial orbits do not
supply a nonsplit sixfold whole character.

The assertion remains valid after multiplying a transported
character by any nonzero rational scalar. Consequently it includes
the actual equal-character retract of HD-N06, whose character is
$\frac1{16}S^*\operatorname{ch}(E)$ before the specified line twist,
and the normalization of HD-N09, which is an actual isogeny pullback
and line twist of the HD-N08 sheaf. No assertion about an arbitrary
idempotent summand with a different character is made. The obstruction
is at the level of the entire character and therefore applies to
every coherent action on each object in the stated scope; it does
not require an additional claim about preservation of semiregularity
under an unspecified action.

\paragraph{1. The exact source before its prescribed transform.}
Let $d_0>0$ be the source integer and put
$K_0=\mathbf Q(s)$, $s^2=-d_0$. The explicit quotient, pullback,
and line-twist calculation of HD-N07 gives on a CM abelian
threefold $X$ the actual sheaf character
\begin{equation}
 v=1+2H-2d_0H^2-\frac{4d_0}{3}H^3
    =\alpha e^{2sH}+\beta e^{-2sH},
 \quad\alpha=\frac{1+s^{-1}}2,\quad
 \beta=\frac{1-s^{-1}}2.                              \tag{N17.2}
\end{equation}
Here $H$ is the product principal polarization and
$\alpha\beta=(d_0+1)/(4d_0)\ne0$.
The HD-N07 complex is the specified Fourier--Mukai equivalence
applied to the external product of two such sheaves, with the
specified shear and line twists. The two different bielliptic
directions in the current HD-N08 construction give the same
character (N17.2), with $d_0=D=4d$. Its specified factor reflection
is an actual automorphism; its subsequent transform,
dual, and shift are the displayed actual functors of HD-N08.
Thus all source characters in the assertion are obtained from
\begin{equation}
                 v_1v_2\quad\text{on }X_1\times X_2,  \tag{N17.3}
\end{equation}
where each factor has (N17.2), by the actual operations listed
in the statement. We compute the full rational spin stabilizer
of (N17.3), and then give every functorial comparison used.

\paragraph{2. Clifford notation and the two actual pure-spinor lines.}
For a six-dimensional rational vector space $V=H^1(X,\mathbf Q)$,
put $\mathcal W=V\oplus V^*$ and
\[
 Q(v+\xi,w+\eta)=\xi(w)+\eta(v),\qquad
 c(v+\xi)=v\wedge+\iota_\xi
               \quad\text{on }S=\bigwedge^*V.
\]
The Clifford anticommutator is $Q$. The rational spin Lie algebra
is $\mathfrak{so}(\mathcal W,Q)$, acting in the decomposition
\begin{equation}
 \bigwedge^2V\ \oplus\ \mathfrak{gl}(V)\ \oplus\ \bigwedge^2V^*
 \quad\text{by}\quad
 \gamma\wedge,\quad D_T-\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}T,
                    \quad\iota_\zeta.                \tag{N17.4}
\end{equation}
These operators are the commutators of the displayed Clifford
operators. This fixes the scalar term and all rational structures.

Write $H^\sharp(\xi)=\iota_\xi H$, an invertible skew map
$V^*\to V$, and put $C=2H^\sharp$. Over $K_0$, the annihilators
of $\psi_\pm=e^{\pm2sH}$ are the literal graphs
\begin{equation}
 L_\pm=\{\mp sC\xi+\xi:\xi\in V^*_{K_0}\}
                          \subset\mathcal W_{K_0}.    \tag{N17.5}
\end{equation}
Indeed $\iota_\xi e^{\pm2sH}
=\pm2s(\iota_\xi H)\wedge e^{\pm2sH}$.
The two graphs are transverse maximal isotropic subspaces:
$C$ is invertible, and its skewness proves isotropy directly
in $Q$. Each annihilator has dimension six.

The relative Fock map is the actual Clifford-intertwining map
\begin{equation}
 \bigwedge^*L_-\longrightarrow S_{K_0},\qquad
 \ell_1\wedge\cdots\wedge\ell_j\longmapsto
                 c(\ell_1)\cdots c(\ell_j)\psi_+.    \tag{N17.6}
\end{equation}
It is an isomorphism. One explicit verification first conjugates
by $e^{-2sH}$: $\psi_+$ becomes $1$, and the exterior part of
the transformed Clifford operator for
$sC\xi+\xi\in L_-$ is $2sC\xi$, an invertible map to $V_{K_0}$.
The terms of maximal exterior degree in (N17.6), in increasing
degree, therefore form an invertible triangular matrix on the
standard exterior bases. Clifford anticommutation identifies
$L_+$ with contractions on this model. Its degree-zero line
is $K_0\psi_+$, and its top-degree line, annihilated by every
element of $L_-$, is $K_0\psi_-$.

\paragraph{3. The exact one-factor projective stabilizer.}
Under (N17.6), the spinor (N17.2) is
$a\cdot1+b\cdot\mathrm{vol}$ with $a,b\ne0$ in the exterior
algebra of a six-dimensional space. In the corresponding
version of (N17.4), suppose an element acts on this spinor
by a scalar $c$ times itself. Its wedge-bivector term has
degree-two output $a\gamma$, so $\gamma=0$. Its contraction
term has degree-four output $b\iota_\zeta\mathrm{vol}$;
contraction with the nonzero top volume is an isomorphism
in this degree, so $\zeta=0$. The remaining degree-zero
and degree-six equations are
\[
       -\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}T=c
                       =\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}T.
\]
Thus $c=0$ and $\operatorname{tr}T=0$. Conversely every
trace-zero $T$ kills both lines. This proves that the
projective and vector stabilizers coincide, and their
$K_0$-form in this model is exactly $\mathfrak{sl}_6$.
It also proves that the degree-one Clifford action
\begin{equation}
          \mathcal W_{K_0}\longrightarrow S^-_{K_0},
                     w\longmapsto c(w)v              \tag{N17.7}
\end{equation}
is injective: its two summands have respective Fock degrees
one and five, and the maps from $L_-$ and $L_+$ to those
degrees are isomorphisms onto their images. Here $S^-$ is
the odd exterior half of the spin module.

We identify the rational form by an explicit matrix. Define
\begin{equation}
 J=\begin{pmatrix}0&d_0C\\-C^{-1}&0\end{pmatrix}
       \quad\text{on }V\oplus V^*,\qquad
 h_0(x,y)=Q(x,y)+s^{-1}Q(x,Jy).                       \tag{N17.8}
\end{equation}
Then $J^2=-d_0$ and $J$ is skew-adjoint for $Q$. Its
$\pm s$ eigenspaces are exactly $L_\pm$.
Give $\mathcal W$ its $K_0$-structure by $sx=Jx$.
The second formula is a nondegenerate $K_0$-Hermitian form,
conjugate-linear in the first argument and linear in the second:
the identities follow at once from $J^2=-d_0$ and its
skew-adjointness. Its rational real-part form is $Q$.
The rational stabilizer computed above is consequently
\begin{equation}
 \mathfrak s_v=
 \{T\in\mathfrak{so}(\mathcal W,Q):TJ=JT,
                         \operatorname{tr}_{K_0}T=0\}
                       =\mathfrak{su}(h_0).           \tag{N17.9}
\end{equation}
This equality follows by scalar extension in (N17.5)--(N17.6):
commuting with $J$ preserves its two eigenspaces, preservation
of $Q$ makes their actions dual, and trace zero is exactly the
condition just proved. All these are rational linear equations.

The form in (N17.8) is explicitly split. Choose the actual
rational Darboux basis $a_i,b_i$ with
$H=\sum_{i=1}^3a_i\wedge b_i$, and its dual basis. Then
\[
 J a_i=\tfrac12 b_i^*,\quad
 J b_i=-\tfrac12 a_i^*,\quad
 J a_i^*=2d_0b_i,\quad J b_i^*=-2d_0a_i.
\]
Thus $(a_1,a_1^*,\ldots,a_3,a_3^*)$ is a $K_0$-basis
of $\mathcal W$: its $K_0$-span also contains all $b_i,b_i^*$.
Direct substitution gives
\[
 h_0(a_i,a_j)=h_0(a_i^*,a_j^*)=0,\qquad
 h_0(a_i,a_j^*)=\delta_{ij}.
\]
Sending the standard hyperbolic basis to these specified
vectors is an actual $K_0$-Hermitian isometry
$U^3\simeq(\mathcal W,h_0)$. Hence
$\mathfrak s_v\simeq\mathfrak{su}(U^3)$ over $\mathbf Q$.

\paragraph{4. The exact rational stabilizer of the product.}
For (N17.3), let $\mathcal W=\mathcal W_1\perp\mathcal W_2$.
Its spin module is the graded tensor product $S_1\otimes S_2$,
with $v_1v_2$ corresponding literally to $v_1\otimes v_2$.
The full Lie algebra decomposes rationally as
\[
 \mathfrak{so}(\mathcal W_1)\oplus
 \mathfrak{so}(\mathcal W_2)\oplus
                         (\mathcal W_1\otimes\mathcal W_2).
\]
The last summand acts, up to the fixed overall Clifford sign,
by $c(w_1)v_1\otimes c(w_2)v_2$. Its image lies in
$S_1^-\otimes S_2^-$, and (N17.7) makes this map injective.
The first two summands have image in $S_1^+\otimes S_2^+$,
so no cross term can occur in an annihilating Lie element.
For the remaining two terms,
\[
 (T_1v_1)\otimes v_2+v_1\otimes(T_2v_2)=0
\]
forces each $T_iv_i$ to be a scalar multiple of $v_i$, by
passing to $S_i/K_0v_i$. Paragraph3 forces both scalars to
be zero. Conversely the two factor stabilizers kill the
product. Descent from $K_0$ now proves the exact rational
equality
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\ \mathfrak s_{v_1v_2}
          =\mathfrak{su}(U^3)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(U^3).\ } \tag{N17.10}
\end{equation}
In particular both factors have rational dimension $35$.
The same equality holds for the projective stabilizer:
the proof in paragraph3 also excludes a nonzero scalar
on the product. Multiplying its character by a nonzero
rational scalar changes neither stabilizer.

\paragraph{5. Every transport used is a rational Lie intertwiner.}
We record the maps so that (N17.10) applies to the actual
functorial orbit, without assuming that arbitrary orthogonal
transformations are geometric. For an actual Fourier--Mukai
equivalence, let $L=\Phi^H$ be its cohomological transform.
Its actual graph isomorphism on $A\times\widehat A$ gives
the rational isometry $f$ of Clifford spaces satisfying
\begin{equation}
            Lc_A(w)L^{-1}=c_{A'}(fw).                \tag{N17.11}
\end{equation}
With the Poincar\'e swap
$\iota_A(v,\xi)=(\xi,v)$ identifying the Clifford space
with $H_1(A)\oplus H_1(\widehat A)$, this is
$f=\iota_{A'}^{-1}(f_\Phi)_*\iota_A$.
The exact source is Golyshev--Lunts--Orlov,
\emph{Mirror symmetry for abelian varieties},
J. Algebraic Geom.\ \textbf{10} (2001), 433--496,
Proposition4.3.7 and Lemma4.3.7.1 in its proof, combined
with the actual graph construction of Orlov,
\emph{Derived categories of coherent sheaves on abelian varieties
and equivalences between them}, Izv. Math.\ \textbf{66} (2002),
569--594, Theorem2.10. Their original proofs and the displayed
map have already been read and checked in HD-G19.
Commutators in (N17.11) identify the rational spin Lie
actions, and hence their stabilizers.

For an isogeny pullback, put $T=p^*$ on rational $H^1$.
The exterior map $\bigwedge T$ intertwines the Clifford
actions through
$(v,\xi)\mapsto(Tv,(T^{-1})^*\xi)$.
Pushforward is $\deg(p)$ times the inverse cohomological
map, so the nonzero scalar does not change a stabilizer.
Tensor by a line bundle of class $D$ is multiplication
by $e^D$; its exact Clifford shear is
\[
 e^D c(v+\xi)e^{-D}=c(v-\iota_\xi D+\xi).
\]
This applies to every rational divisor class used in
(N17.1), including every mixed class. Translations act
trivially on rational cohomology, and shifts contribute a sign.

Derived dual on even cohomology is the restriction of
$\tau|_{\wedge^jV}=(-1)^{j(j-1)/2}$.
Conjugation by $\tau$ multiplies wedge-bivectors and
contraction-bivectors by $-1$ and fixes the middle
operator in (N17.4). These explicit formulas define a
rational Lie automorphism, so again identify the stabilizers.
They also show directly why the dual operation used in
HD-N08 is included. The character identity for the
specified HD-N06 retract is a nonzero scalar of an isogeny
pullback, as proved by its actual character projectors;
it is therefore included without treating a general
retract as a Fourier equivalence.

\paragraph{6. The target unitary Lie algebra and its exact embedding.}
Let $h$ be the rational $K$-Hermitian form of the target
polarization in (N17.1). On its six-dimensional $K$-space
of homology, $\mathfrak g=\mathfrak{su}(h)$ has rational
dimension $35$. Its contragredient action on
$V_A=H^1(A,\mathbf Q)$ preserves $\theta$, since it
preserves the alternating form of the compatible polarization.
Its $K$-trace is zero, so it acts trivially on both
field determinant lines, and hence on $t$. Its rational
trace is zero as well. Thus, in the middle summand of
(N17.4), it annihilates $P(\theta)+\lambda t$ faithfully.
Conjugation by $e^B$ gives the explicit rational Lie
embedding
\[
 Z\longmapsto e^BD_Ze^{-B}=D_Z-(D_ZB)\wedge
\]
into the stabilizer of (N17.1).

Combining this injection with the actual intertwiners
in paragraph5 and (N17.10) gives a rational Lie injection
\begin{equation}
 \mathfrak{su}(h)\lhook\joinrel\longrightarrow
 \mathfrak{su}(U^3)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(U^3),           \tag{N17.12}
\end{equation}
where the right side is formed over the source field $K_0$.
The Lie algebra $\mathfrak{su}(h)$ is simple over
$\mathbf Q$: scalar extension to $K$ is
$\mathfrak{sl}_6(K)$. Explicitly, the scalar-extension
map sends $T\otimes a$ to $aT$. If $X$ has $K$-trace
zero, write $X^\dagger$ for its Hermitian adjoint and
choose $s_K\in K$ with $\overline{s_K}=-s_K\ne0$.
Then
\[
 X=\frac{X-X^\dagger}{2}
        +s_K\frac{X+X^\dagger}{2s_K}
\]
is a sum of $K$-multiples of two skew-Hermitian trace-zero
matrices. Thus that map is onto, and the equal dimensions
make it an isomorphism. A nonzero proper rational ideal
would consequently give such an ideal in
$\mathfrak{sl}_6(K)$ after scalar extension.
At least one projection of (N17.12) is nonzero; its
kernel is an ideal, so it is injective. Both dimensions
are $35$, hence this projection is an actual rational
Lie isomorphism onto one source factor.

\paragraph{7. The isomorphism produces an actual hyperbolic basis.}
In a source hyperbolic basis, take the three commuting
elements whose matrices are
\[
 A_i=\operatorname{diag}(0,\ldots,1,\ldots,0,
                        0,\ldots,-1,\ldots,0),
                         \qquad i=1,2,3,
\]
with $1$ in position $i$ and $-1$ in position $i+3$.
They preserve $U^3$, have trace zero, and are linearly
independent over $\mathbf Q$. Their adjoint actions are
diagonalizable with integral eigenvalues, as is seen on
the matrix units of $\mathfrak{sl}_6(K_0)$.
Take their preimages $X_1,X_2,X_3$ in
$\mathfrak{su}(h)$ under the isomorphism just obtained.
They commute and are independent, and their adjoint
actions have the same split integral spectra.

Consider each $X_i$ on the actual standard $K$-space
of target homology. It is semisimple: over an algebraic
closure, the nilpotent part in its matrix Jordan
decomposition would have zero adjoint action because
$\operatorname{ad}X_i$ is semisimple. That nilpotent
part is then scalar, hence zero. If its eigenvalues
are $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_6$, all differences
$\lambda_j-\lambda_k$ occur as adjoint eigenvalues on
matrix units and are rational. Its $K$-trace is zero,
so
\[
           \lambda_j=\frac16\sum_{k=1}^6
                             (\lambda_j-\lambda_k)\in\mathbf Q.
\]
It is therefore diagonalizable over $K$ itself. Since
the three $X_i$ commute, they are simultaneously
diagonalizable over $K$. Their joint weights belong
to $\mathbf Q^3$ and span its dual: a linear relation
vanishing on every weight would give the same zero
linear combination of the independent matrices $X_i$.

For vectors of joint weights $\lambda,\mu$, skew
Hermitianity gives
\[
       0=h(X_iv,w)+h(v,X_iw)
                     =(\lambda_i+\mu_i)h(v,w).
\]
The eigenvalues are rational, so conjugation of the
first scalar changes nothing. Thus $h$ pairs only
opposite weight spaces, and pairs them nondegenerately.
Weights spanning a three-dimensional space require
at least three nonzero opposite pairs. The target has
$K$-dimension six, so there are exactly three such
pairs, each space is one-dimensional, and the zero
weight space is absent.

Choose independent representatives
$\lambda^{(1)},\lambda^{(2)},\lambda^{(3)}$,
vectors $v_i$ in their weight spaces, and vectors
$w_i$ in the opposite weight spaces. Then
$h(v_i,w_j)=0$ for $i\ne j$,
$h(v_i,v_j)=h(w_i,w_j)=0$ for all $i,j$, and
$c_i=h(v_i,w_i)\ne0$. Replace $w_i$ by $w_i/c_i$.
The resulting six vectors have Gram matrix $U^3$.
Sending its standard hyperbolic basis to these vectors
is the promised explicit $K$-linear Hermitian isometry
\begin{equation}
                  U^3\ \simeq\ (H_1(A,\mathbf Q),h).
                                                        \tag{N17.13}
\end{equation}
The construction uses the actual preimage matrices,
their simultaneous kernels, and these normalizing
scalars. It does not assume that $K_0=K$, and does
not use a classification or a transitivity assertion
for rational unitary forms. This proves the assertion.

\paragraph{Check scope.}
The proof is an exact rational argument for the full
characters and actual functorial maps specified above.
Its source stabilizer calculation, product separation,
all Clifford intertwiners, and simultaneous-weight
construction are separate from the previously checked
geometric existence and semiregularity of HD-N07--HD-N09.
It gives a complete obstruction to this proposed
transport mechanism for nonsplit sixfold data. It
makes no claim about different CM complexes or
correspondences whose characters are outside this orbit.
The separate certificate
\texttt{sixfold\_\allowbreak cm\_\allowbreak spin\_\allowbreak certificate.py}
rebuilds all exterior and contraction operators. For twelve source
integers, at each of two fresh primes, it checks factor spin rank
$31$ with kernel $35$, product spin rank $206$ with kernel $70$,
and one-factor Clifford rank $12$. Adding the character column
increases each spin rank by one, checking the projective-scalar
calculation as well. The matrices $J^2=-d_0$, skew-adjointness,
and the displayed hyperbolic Gram matrix are checked exactly over
$\mathbf Q$ in each case. These finite computations supplement
the universal proof; they are not used to infer it by interpolation.
