\section{Exact product maps and the remaining nonsplit sixfold supplier}

This section proves the behavior of several concrete constructions.
It neither assumes a new eightfold supplier nor excludes constructions
using other objects or other correspondences.

\paragraph{HD-S17 (endomorphisms and determinant under the proposed maps).}
Every polarized Weil sixfold component has a dense complement of a
countable union of proper analytic subsets on which
\(\operatorname{End}^0(A)=K\). On that complement, \(A\) is simple
and \(\operatorname{NS}(A)_\mathbb Q=\mathbb Q\theta\).

Here is a direct verification of the endomorphism assertion. Over the
real numbers choose an orthogonal basis by successive replacements
\(v\mapsto v-\sum_j u_jh(u_j,v)/h(u_j,u_j)\), choosing at
each step a vector of nonzero norm. Rescale each resulting vector
by \(|h(u_j,u_j)|^{-1/2}\) and order the three positive vectors
first. The resulting change-of-basis matrix \(C\) satisfies
\(C^*HC=S=\operatorname{diag}(I_3,-I_3)\). This real
Hermitian isometry is used only to compute the centralizer.
The base complex structure is \(J_0=iS\), and the tangent complex
structures include
\[
 J_B'=i\begin{pmatrix}0&-2B\\2B^*&0\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad B\in M_3(\mathbb C).
\]
They are obtained by differentiating conjugation by the unitary Lie
algebra matrix \(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&B\\B^*&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)\).
A real-linear map commuting with the whole period domain decomposes
uniquely into a complex-linear and a complex-antilinear part relative
to the fixed scalar action of \(K\otimes\mathbb R=\mathbb C\).
Each part separately commutes with every complex structure.

The complex-linear part commuting with \(J_0\) is
\(\operatorname{diag}(P,Q)\). Its commutation with \(J_B'\), first
for \(B=I\) and then for every elementary real matrix, gives
\(P=Q\) and \(PB=BP\), hence \(P=Q=cI\).
Write the antilinear part as \(C\circ\mathrm{conj}\).
Commutation with \(J_0\) makes
\(C=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&U\\V&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)\).
Commutation with \(J_B'\) for real \(B\) gives
\(UB^t=BV\); taking \(B=I\) gives \(U=V\).
The equations \(UE_{ii}=E_{ii}U\) make \(U\) diagonal, while
\(UE_{ji}=E_{ij}U\), \(i\ne j\), make each diagonal entry zero.
Thus the real centralizer is exactly the scalar copy of \(\mathbb C\),
and its intersection with the rational endomorphisms is \(K\).

For each rational endomorphism outside \(K\), preservation of the
Hodge subspace defines a proper analytic subset: an analytic subset
containing an open would, by analytic continuation, contradict the
computed centralizer. There are countably many rational endomorphisms.
The Baire theorem on the connected period domain proves the assertion.
A rational Hodge endomorphism becomes an actual holomorphic torus
endomorphism after clearing its lattice denominators, and is algebraic
by GAGA, so this is the asserted algebraic endomorphism ring.

For completeness, a nonzero proper abelian subvariety
\(j:B\hookrightarrow A\) would give the rational idempotent
\[
 e=j\,(j^\vee\lambda_\theta j)^{-1}j^\vee\lambda_\theta
       \in\operatorname{End}^0(A).
\]
It satisfies \(e^2=e\), with image \(B\). A field has no such
idempotent, proving simplicity. The map
\(L\mapsto\lambda_\theta^{-1}\lambda_L\) sends divisor classes
to Rosati-symmetric endomorphisms, which here are exactly
\(K^{\mathrm{conj}}=\mathbb Q\). In particular every polarization
is a positive rational multiple of \(\theta\).

Consequently an isogeny from a split Weil sixfold, over any imaginary
quadratic field, to such an \(A\) preserves the determinant norm
class. Indeed conjugating its quadratic action through the isogeny
embeds its field into \(K\), so the fields identify, with either
the identity or conjugate embedding. Transport its polarization to
\(A\) as a rational polarization; it is \(c\theta\) with
\(c\in\mathbb Q_{>0}\). The change of \(K\)-basis multiplies
the Hermitian determinant by a norm, and the scalar multiplies it
by \(c^6=N_{K/\mathbb Q}(c^3)\). Conjugating the action changes
the matrix to \(-\overline H\), whose determinant in rank six
is the same rational number. Thus the split class \([-1]\)
cannot become a nonsplit determinant by this actual isogeny.

Every morphism from a product of abelian varieties to \(A\) is a
translation of the sum of its homomorphisms from the factors.
For a direct complex-analytic proof, lift the morphism to universal
covers. Its derivative is lattice-periodic and therefore constant
on the compact torus; the lift is affine-linear. A nonzero map from
a factor of dimension six to the simple sixfold \(A\) is an isogeny;
every map from a smaller-dimensional factor is zero. Therefore every
morphism from a product of the established split sixfold suppliers
and smaller-dimensional factors to a very general nonsplit \(A\)
is constant. This concerns maps of the ambient abelian varieties;
it makes no assertion about arbitrary new correspondences.

There is also an exact field obstruction to mapping the established
Eisenstein eightfold to such an \(A\) when \(K\ne\mathbb Q(\omega)\).
A surjection would, by the displayed polarization-complement
construction, give an isogeny decomposition \(X\sim A\times B\)
with \(\dim B=2\). Simplicity and dimensions make both cross Hom
groups zero. Thus
\(\operatorname{End}^0(X)\simeq K\times\operatorname{End}^0(B)\).
Projection of its unital Eisenstein field action to the first factor
would embed \(\mathbb Q(\omega)\) into \(K\), which is impossible
for distinct quadratic fields.

The standard source locators for the background maps are Milne,
\emph{Abelian Varieties}, Corollary2.2, p.3, Proposition12.1, p.19,
and Proposition17.2, p.35, in the author's 2022 version of the 1986
chapter, \url{https://www.jmilne.org/math/xnotes/AVs.pdf}.
The centralizer and determinant calculations above are proved here.

\paragraph{HD-S18 (the actual surface stabilization and all four contractions).}
Let the sixfold have maximal-order action and polarization product
\(\Delta\), so that the normalized Hermitian determinant is
\(-\Delta\). For an order of conductor \(f\), the already proved
actual saturation map first gives this situation with product
\(f^6\Delta/b\); it preserves the rational determinant norm class.
Put \(\mathcal O_K=\mathbb Z+\mathbb Z\alpha\),
\(T=\operatorname{Tr}(\alpha)\), \(N=N(\alpha)\), and take
\[
 B=E_K\times E_K,\qquad
 \theta_B=\eta_1+\Delta\eta_2,
 \qquad a\cdot(x,y)=(ax,\bar a y).
\]
This is an actual polarized CM surface with Hermitian matrix
\(\operatorname{diag}(1,-\Delta)\). The product
\[
 (X,\Theta)=(A\times B,\ p_A^*\theta+p_B^*\theta_B)
                                                               \tag{S18.1}
\]
has signature \((4,4)\), Hermitian determinant \(\Delta^2\)
and polarization product \(\Delta^2\). It is split. To give the
precise reduction, Meyer supplies an isotropic vector in the
underlying indefinite rational quadratic form of rank16. Choose
a Hermitian dual vector \(w\), and replace it by
\(w-h(w,w)v/2\); this splits off \(U\). Its rank-six complement
has signature \((3,3)\) and determinant class \([-1]\), hence
is the explicitly proved \(U^3\) of HD-S03a. Thus the product
is rationally isometric to \(U^4\).

Define four actual row homomorphisms
\[
 \ell_0=x,\quad\ell_1=y,\quad\ell_2=x+y,
 \quad\ell_3=x+\alpha y,
\]
and put \(D_j=\ell_j^*(2[0])\). They are globally generated:
a degree-two line bundle on an elliptic curve has no base point,
as the degree-one subtraction has exactly one section by
Riemann--Roch. The intersection matrix of the half-divisors is
\[
 G_\alpha=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 0&1&1&N\\
 1&0&1&1\\
 1&1&0&N-T+1\\
 N&1&N-T+1&0
 \end{pmatrix},\qquad
 \det G_\alpha=T^2-4N\ne0.                               \tag{S18.2}
\]
Each off-diagonal entry is the norm of the determinant of the
corresponding two row maps. Thus the four divisor classes are an
actual basis of \(H^{1,1}(B)\), which has dimension four.

As translation-invariant forms, all four satisfy
\[
 0\le D_j\le 2(1+N)\theta_B.                             \tag{S18.3}
\]
For the last row the difference is
\[
 2\begin{pmatrix}N&-\alpha\\-\bar\alpha&(1+N)\Delta-N\end{pmatrix},
\]
with leading entry \(2N\) and determinant
\(4N(1+N)(\Delta-1)\). For the row \((1,1)\) the determinant
is \(4(N+1)(N\Delta-1)\); the other two differences are
diagonal and nonnegative. These prove (S18.3) for \(\Delta\ge1\).

The actual Chow correspondences are
\[
 \mathcal T_j:\operatorname{CH}_4(X)\longrightarrow
 \operatorname{CH}_3(A),\qquad
 Z\longmapsto p_{A,*}\bigl(Z\cdot p_B^*D_j\bigr).        \tag{S18.4}
\]
General members give proper effective intersections on every
effective \(Z\). With \(a=p_A^*\theta\), \(b=p_B^*\theta_B\),
their degrees satisfy the unconditional bound
\[
 \deg_\theta\mathcal T_j(Z)
 \le2(1+N)\int_Za^3b
 \le\frac{1+N}{2}\deg_\Theta Z,                         \tag{S18.5}
\]
because \(4\int_Za^3b\le\int_Z(a+b)^4\) and all the other
binomial terms are nonnegative intersections of nef classes.

The nonvanishing mechanism is also an exact linear map. On the
\((6,2)\) Kunneth component it is
\[
 W_A\otimes H^{1,1}(B)\longrightarrow W_A^4,
 \qquad u\otimes v\longmapsto
 \bigl(u\!\int_BvD_0,\ldots,u\!\int_BvD_3\bigr).       \tag{S18.6}
\]
It is injective by (S18.2). For any algebraic four-cycle, its
\(W_A\)-projected \((6,2)\) component lies in this domain,
because \(W_A\) has Hodge type \((3,3)\). The total eightfold
Weil plane lies in \(W_A\otimes W_B\). Consequently the common
kernel of the four projected maps in (S18.4) is contained in
the kernel of the total Weil projector. This is the proved
contraction map; it asserts no existence of a new cycle in its
domain with nonzero eightfold Weil projection.

For the nine fields in increasing \(d\), the exact factors
\((1+N)/2\) in (S18.5) are
\[
 1,\quad\tfrac32,\quad1,\quad\tfrac32,\quad2,
 \quad3,\quad6,\quad9,\quad21.
\]
The established Eisenstein eightfold supplies the required cycle
for \(d=3\), giving the already proved all-determinant sixfold
result. For the other eight fields, the unfilled step in this
specific construction is a bounded detector on the split
eightfold family containing (S18.1).

\paragraph{HD-S19 (a genuine split twelvefold detector and its exact contraction).}
There is a concrete higher-dimensional construction valid for every
sixfold \(A\), including a nonsplit one. Give \(A\times A\) the
conjugate action on the second factor:
\[
 a\cdot(x,y)=(ax,\bar a y),\qquad
 \Xi=p_1^*\theta+p_2^*\theta.
\]
Its Hermitian form is \(H\oplus(-\overline H)\).
Successively choose a vector of nonzero norm and replace every
remaining vector \(v\) by \(v-u\,h(u,v)/h(u,u)\).
This gives an actual change of basis over \(K\) with diagonal
entries \(r_1,\ldots,r_6\in\mathbb Q^*\).
For each pair of corresponding basis vectors \(e_j,f_j\),
the vectors
\[
 v_j=e_j+f_j,\qquad
 w_j=(e_j-f_j)/(2r_j)
\]
have zero norms and pairing one. This is an explicit rational
isometry to \(U^6\); the twelvefold is split.

The diagonal \(\Gamma_A\subset A\times A\) is an effective
six-cycle detecting its Weil plane. To see this separately from
the splitting, choose generators \(\tau,\bar\tau\) of the two
sixfold Weil lines and let \(c=\int_A\tau\bar\tau\ne0\).
The twelvefold Weil lines for the conjugate action are
\(\tau\otimes\bar\tau\) and
\(\bar\tau\otimes\tau\). The diagonal's projection to their
sum is exactly
\[
 e_{W_{12}}[\Gamma_A]
  =c^{-1}(\tau\otimes\bar\tau+\bar\tau\otimes\tau)\ne0.
                                                               \tag{S19.1}
\]
This follows by evaluating the diagonal against the two dual
degree-six classes, or directly from its identity-correspondence
property. Its degree is explicitly
\[
 \deg_\Xi\Gamma_A=\int_A(2\theta)^6
                 =46080\Delta.                          \tag{S19.2}
\]

The exact contraction on the second factor is
\[
 p_{1,*}\bigl(e_{W_{12}}[\Gamma_A]\,p_2^*\gamma\bigr)
       =e_{W_A}\gamma\qquad(\gamma\in H^6(A)).             \tag{S19.3}
\]
In particular it is zero for \(\gamma=\theta^3\), and for
every algebraic class whose Weil projection is zero. Thus this
explicit split-twelvefold detector implements the projector
already sought on the other factor; it does not supply a
nonzero contraction from classes with zero Weil projection.
The detector is constructed on this product locus, with no
assertion that the locus contains an analytic open of the full
twelvefold Weil period domain.

\paragraph{HD-S20 (the full-character obstruction for the direct external product).}
Take the actual normalized sheaf \(F\) supplied by HD-N09 on its
split sixfold \(A_0\). The formulas already proved give
\[
 \operatorname{ch}_0(F)=r>0,\quad c_1(F)=0,\quad
 \operatorname{ch}_2(F)=a_2\theta_0^2,\quad
 \operatorname{ch}_3(F)=c\,t_0+a_3\theta_0^3,
 \qquad c\ne0.                                           \tag{S20.1}
\]
The complete character is a polynomial in \(\theta_0\) plus
\(ct_0\). Let \(B\) be a balanced CM elliptic surface, and let
\(G\) be any perfect complex on \(B\). This paragraph concerns
the actual external-product object \(F\boxtimes G\) and its
rational B-field twists; it does not concern new coupled objects.

Write \(\operatorname{ch}(G)=g_0+g_1+g_2\). First suppose
\(g_0\ne0\). For a twisted full character
\(w=e^{\mathcal B}\operatorname{ch}(F\boxtimes G)\) that
remains Hodge along a balanced eightfold deformation, its
degree-two part \(w_2\) also remains Hodge. Multiplying by
\(e^{-w_2/(rg_0)}\) preserves this property and gives the
exact, B-field-independent normalized expression
\[
 e^{-c_1(F\boxtimes G)/(rg_0)}
       \operatorname{ch}(F\boxtimes G)
 =\operatorname{ch}(F)\boxtimes
       \left(g_0+g_2-\frac{g_1^2}{2g_0}\right).          \tag{S20.2}
\]
Its pure \(\sigma^6\) component in degree six is a nonzero
multiple of the decomposable form
\[
 \tau_0\in\bigwedge^6H^1(A_0)_\sigma
       \subset\bigwedge^6H^1(A_0\times B)_\sigma.
\]
The total \(K\)-eigenspace projections are Hodge morphisms
throughout the deformation. Hence \(\tau_0\) remains of type
\((3,3)\), and is fixed by the Hodge circle at every point.
For any nonzero decomposable \(k\)-form \(\tau\) with
\(0<k<8\), its support is intrinsically
\[
 \{v:v\wedge\tau=0\}.
\]
Invariance of \(\tau\) therefore makes this support invariant.
Thus the original rational \(K\)-sixspace of \(A_0\) stays a
Hodge subspace, together with its polarization-orthogonal
\(K\)-two-dimensional complement. The sixspace retains its
original split Hermitian form. In fact the normalized degree-eight
Weil projection in (S20.2) is zero: the surface has no degree-two
term, and its degree-four term has both \(K\)-embedding weights.

Now suppose \(g_0=0\). If \(g_1\) has zero surface Weil
projection, every component of \(g_1\) has one factor from
each \(K\)-embedding, and \(g_2\) has two from each.
Every product with these classes, including multiplication by
\(e^{\mathcal B}\), retains both embedding weights. Thus the
eightfold Weil projection is zero. If \(g_1\) has nonzero
surface Weil projection, the leading class of the whole
twisted character is \(rg_1\). Its pure \(\sigma^2\)
component is a nonzero multiple of the decomposable top form
of \(H^1(B)_\sigma\). Its required Hodge type \((1,1)\)
forces this fixed two-space to remain Hodge by the same support
calculation. Its orthogonal sixspace is again the original
split space. The case \(\operatorname{ch}(G)=0\) has zero
whole character and supplies no nonzero rational Weil class.

These invariant rational subspaces give actual abelian factors.
The rational orthogonal projectors become integral after one
fixed denominator is cleared; being Hodge maps, they induce
actual abelian-variety endomorphisms. Their images, with the
addition map, give an isogeny from the product of the two
factors to the deformed eightfold. This is the precise
geometric consequence of the support calculation.

In particular these direct-product Hodge deformations do not
reach the stabilization (S18.1) of a very general nonsplit
sixfold. For such a target \(A\), simplicity gives
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}(A,B)=\operatorname{Hom}(B,A)=0.
\]
Its endomorphism algebra is the product
\(K\times\operatorname{End}^0(B)\). An idempotent with
six-dimensional image has first component one and second
component zero, since the second factor has dimension two.
Its image is therefore exactly the \(A\)-factor, whose
Hermitian form is nonsplit. This contradicts the split form
of the factor retained by the displayed external-product
construction. The contradiction concerns this proved family
of objects and maps, leaving the search for new eightfold
objects and other suppliers open.
