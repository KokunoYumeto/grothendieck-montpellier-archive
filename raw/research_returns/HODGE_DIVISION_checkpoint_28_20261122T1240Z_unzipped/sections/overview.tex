The degrees in this paper are unnormalized intersection degrees. For a
polarized abelian $2n$-fold $(A,\theta)$ and an effective $n$-cycle $Y$,
\[
 \deg_\theta Y=\int_Y\theta^n,\qquad
 \Delta(A,\theta)=\frac{1}{(2n)!}\int_A\theta^{2n}.
\]
Thus $\Delta$ is the product of the polarization elementary divisors.
For a nonzero rational Weil class $t$, the stated normalization is
$\delta t$ integral and $T=(-1)^n\int_A t^2>0$.
All numerical bounds below control effective integral cycles; the
division bounds control the positive integer multiplier and each
of its two effective parts separately.
