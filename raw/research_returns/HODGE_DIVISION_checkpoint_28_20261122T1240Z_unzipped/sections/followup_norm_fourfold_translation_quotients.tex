\section[HD-F1-S20: actual translations and quotient trace maps]{HD-F1-S20: actual point actions are translations of order one or three, with an exact quotient trace comparison}
\label{sec:f1-norm-fourfold-translation-quotients}

Retain the specified norm fourfold $T$, its proper birational
Abel morphism $b:T\to Y$, and $i:Y\hookrightarrow P$ from
S13--S19. For every specified coherent faithful finite point
action admitting a lift of $E=i_*\mathcal O_Y$, its subgroup
in $P\times\widehat P$ is in fact contained in $P\times\{0\}$,
and
\[
                              |G|\mid3.
 \tag{F1S20.1}
\]
Thus only the trivial group and a cyclic group of order three
remain as possible actions on this actual sheaf. No existence
of a nontrivial action is asserted. We also identify its full
equivariant trace with the ordinary trace of the actual quotient
structure sheaf, through the precise equivalences below.

\paragraph{1. The line coordinate of each actual point is zero.}
An actual lift at $(x,L)$ gives
\[
                     t_x^*\mathcal O_Y\otimes L
                                      \simeq\mathcal O_Y
 \tag{F1S20.2}
\]
as sheaves on $P$. Equality of supports gives $t_x^{-1}Y=Y$.
Both are reduced closed subschemes, so their ideal sheaves
coincide. Hence $t_x^*\mathcal O_Y=\mathcal O_Y$ canonically
as quotient $\mathcal O_P$-algebras. Equation (F1S20.2) now
forces $L|_Y\simeq\mathcal O_Y$. S19 proves that its only
solution in $\widehat P$ is $L=\mathcal O_P$. This proves
the claimed subgroup inclusion, retaining the given action.
Write $H\subset P$ for this actual translation subgroup.

\paragraph{2. Each translation lifts to the actual smooth canonical model.}
For $x\in H$, the birational map
\[
                       \phi_x=b^{-1}t_xb:T\dashrightarrow T
 \tag{F1S20.3}
\]
has a unique regular extension to an automorphism of $T$.
Here is the actual extension map. Resolve the graph of
$\phi_x$ by a common smooth projective resolution
$T\xleftarrow{u}W\xrightarrow{v}T$. The Jacobian determinant
gives $K_W=u^*K_T+D_u$, where $D_u$ is effective and
$u$-exceptional. For every $m\ge0$ one has
\[
                      u_*\mathcal O_W(mD_u)=\mathcal O_T.
 \tag{F1S20.4}
\]
Indeed a rational function in the left side has no pole
away from the codimension-at-least-two image of the
exceptional locus, so it extends by normality of $T$;
the reverse inclusion follows from effectivity. The same
argument applies to $v$. Projection formula therefore
identifies pullback with the two isomorphisms
\[
 H^0(T,mK_T)\xrightarrow{u^*}H^0(W,mK_W)
                  \xleftarrow{v^*}H^0(T,mK_T).
 \tag{F1S20.5}
\]
Their composition $(u^*)^{-1}v^*$ preserves multiplication
and is the pullback by (F1S20.3) on rational pluricanonical
forms. It is independent of the common resolution by that
identification.

S13 proves $K_T=\eta+\vartheta$ ample. Consequently the
natural pluricanonical map identifies
\[
                  T=\operatorname{Proj}
                       \bigoplus_{m\ge0}H^0(T,mK_T).
 \tag{F1S20.6}
\]
The graded ring automorphism (F1S20.5) induces the asserted
regular automorphism of this precise $T$. On the dense open
where $b$ is an isomorphism it equals (F1S20.3). Separatedness
therefore gives
\[
                           b\phi_x=t_xb
 \tag{F1S20.7}
\]
everywhere. Equality on that same dense open gives uniqueness,
$\phi_x\phi_y=\phi_{x+y}$, and $\phi_0=1$. Thus these are
an actual group action, rather than unrelated individual lifts.

This is the usual birational invariance of pluricanonical
forms and the recovery of a variety from an ample section
ring, with all maps displayed. Totaro, \emph{Euler and
algebraic geometry}, Theorems~2.1 and~2.3 and their complete
proofs give the same foundations. No general finite-generation
theorem is needed here, since $K_T$ is already ample.

\paragraph{3. Freeness and the additional integral divisibility.}
The action on $T$ is free. If $\phi_x(t)=t$, (F1S20.7)
would give $t_x(b(t))=b(t)$ in the abelian variety $P$,
which forces $x=0$. The actual quotient $T\to T/H$ is
therefore finite etale of degree $h=|H|$. The target is
smooth projective, its pulled-back tangent bundle is $T_T$,
and the top Chern class degree formula gives
\[
             7371=\int_T c_4(T_T)
                       =h\int_{T/H}c_4(T_{T/H}).
 \tag{F1S20.8}
\]
The number $7371$ is the component invariant computed in
S13, not the Euler number of the singular image. Both
integrals in (F1S20.8) are integral topological Euler
characteristics. Thus $h\mid7371$. S19 already gives
$h\mid6$, and $\gcd(7371,6)=3$. This proves (F1S20.1).

\paragraph{4. The exact descent and trace maps for every given lift.}
Let $q:P\to P_0=P/H$ be the actual etale quotient, and
write $Y_0=q(Y)$ with reduced structure. S19 proves the
scheme identity $q^{-1}Y_0=Y$. Therefore
$q^*\mathcal O_{Y_0}=\mathcal O_Y$, with its canonical
translation linearization $\epsilon_g$.

We retain the possibly noncanonical coherence of the
specified action. Relative to the canonical composition
maps of translation pullbacks, write its composition
maps as $c(g,h)\in\mathbb C^*$. These are scalars because
the natural automorphisms of the identity functor here
are $H^0(P,\mathcal O_P)^*=\mathbb C^*$. Write the given
lift as $u_g=\lambda_g\epsilon_g$, possible because
$\operatorname{End}_P(\mathcal O_Y)=\mathbb C$.
With the convention $u_g\,g^*u_h=c(g,h)u_{gh}$, its
actual coherence identity is
\[
                 \lambda_g\lambda_h=c(g,h)\lambda_{gh}.
 \tag{F1S20.9}
\]
Thus the given lift itself supplies a trivializing
cochain. Rescaling every equivariant structure by this
fixed cochain is the exact dg equivalence
\[
 (\mathcal F,v_g)\longmapsto
                  (\mathcal F,\lambda_g^{-1}v_g)
 \tag{F1S20.9a}
\]
from the specified coherent action to canonical
translation equivariance. Equation (F1S20.9) proves its
compatibility with compositions, and rescaling by
$\lambda_g$ is its inverse. In particular it sends the
given lift of $\mathcal O_Y$ to the canonical lift.
This is an equivalence of the actual actions, not an
assumption that their initial composition maps agree.

Faithfully flat descent for the finite etale torsor gives
the exact functors on the resulting canonical category
\[
 \begin{split}
 q^*&:\operatorname{Perf}(P_0)\longrightarrow
                              \operatorname{Perf}_{H,\mathrm{can}}(P),\\
 (q_*(-))^H&:\operatorname{Perf}_{H,\mathrm{can}}(P)\longrightarrow
                              \operatorname{Perf}(P_0).
 \end{split}
 \tag{F1S20.10}
\]
Their unit and counit are the torsor descent isomorphisms.
Together with (F1S20.9a), they send the given object
exactly to $\mathcal O_{Y_0}$. For completeness, another
fixed trivializing cochain $a_g$ would give the character
$\chi(g)=\lambda_g/a_g$. In that convention descent gives
$\mathcal O_{Y_0}\otimes M_\chi$, where $M_\chi$ is the
descent of the trivial ambient line with character
linearization $\chi$. This is a finite-order degree-zero
line bundle on $P_0$; tensoring by its inverse recovers
the same structure sheaf. Thus either specified choice
is related by an explicit ambient line-tensor equivalence.

These equivalences identify the self-Ext groups, their Yoneda
products, and the full categorical trace diagram
\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
 \operatorname{Ext}^2_{(H,c)}(\mathcal O_Y,\mathcal O_Y)
       &\xrightarrow{\ \sigma^2_{H,E}\ }&
                   HH_{-2}(\operatorname{Perf}_{(H,c)}(P))\\
 \big\downarrow\wr&&\big\downarrow\wr\\
 \operatorname{Ext}^2_{P_0}(\mathcal O_{Y_0},\mathcal O_{Y_0})
       &\xrightarrow{\ \sigma^2_{\mathcal O_{Y_0}}\ }&
                   HH_{-2}(P_0).
\end{array}
 \tag{F1S20.11}
\]
The vertical maps are induced by the explicit coherence
rescaling and torsor descent (and, in the alternative
convention, the indicated line tensor). Their commutation with the
full trace is its functoriality under these equivalences,
as in the identity-sector and full-trace comparisons
already retained in S-21 and R05. In particular the two
displayed trace maps have isomorphic kernels. This
comparison is exact for each given lift; it neither
postulates a new action nor computes either kernel.

The ordinary full degree-two trace on these actual
quotient structure sheaves remains to be evaluated.
S20 reduces the finite-action choices and identifies the
associated morphisms, without replacing that calculation
by the order bound or by an exterior-algebra quotient.
