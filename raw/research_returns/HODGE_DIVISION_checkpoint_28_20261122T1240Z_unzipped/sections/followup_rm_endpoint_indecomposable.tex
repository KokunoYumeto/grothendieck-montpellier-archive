\section[HD-F1-R24: indecomposable endpoints]{HD-F1-R24: indecomposable endpoints in the two rank-one factorizations}
\label{sec:followup-rm-common-image-indecomposable}

Retain the actual adjacent objects, determinant twist, coherent
finite point action and common-image factorization of R23.
Thus the torsion-free endpoints $A,T$ have ranks three and two,
are slope semistable of slope zero for the same ample class $H$,
and
\[
 \operatorname{ch}(E)=v_q=(1-qA_1)(1-qA_2),\qquad q>0.
 \tag{F1R24.1}
\]
Write $I\subset T$ for the actual saturated rank-one common
image, $L=I^{**}$, and
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}_G(A,I)\xrightarrow{\sim}
                  \operatorname{Hom}_G(A,T),\qquad
              u\longmapsto \iota_Iu.
 \tag{F1R24.2}
\]
Here $\mu_H(I)=0$ and (F1R24.2) is the already constructed
isomorphism of actual morphism spaces. The source $A$ is
indecomposable as an ordinary coherent sheaf. In particular,
the whole common-image branch contains no object with a split
rank-three source, including a source $F^{\oplus3}$ formed
from one rank-one torsion-free sheaf $F$. The asserted exclusion
does not require the splitting to preserve $G$. In the
common-quotient branch of R22, the actual rank-two target
$T$ is also ordinarily indecomposable, as proved by the
separate direct composition in paragraph~4 below.

\paragraph{1. A rank-one retract has at most one restriction row.}
Suppose that a rank-one torsion-free sheaf $F$ were an actual
ordinary retract of $A$, with maps
\[
        F\xrightarrow{j}A\xrightarrow{p}F,
                         \qquad pj=1_F.
 \tag{F1R24.3}
\]
Its complementary summand is torsion-free of rank two. Each
summand has slope at most zero by semistability of $A$, and
the weighted sum of their slopes is zero. Both slopes therefore
equal zero, in particular $\mu_H(F)=0$.

Every map $F\to I$ extends by double dual to a map between
the line bundles $F^{**}$ and $L$. This extension is injective
on Hom spaces: a map zero on the common big open set has
torsion image in the torsion-free target $I$, and so is zero.
A nonzero extended map has an effective zero divisor $D$ with
\[
           D H^3=(c_1(L)-c_1(F^{**}))H^3=0.
 \tag{F1R24.4}
\]
Ample degree is strictly positive on every nonzero effective
divisor. Hence $D=0$, the extended map is an isomorphism, and
all extended maps are its constant scalar multiples because
$H^0(X,\mathcal O_X)=\mathbf C$. This proves, including the
case of no nonzero map,
\[
                    \dim\operatorname{Hom}_X(F,I)\le1.
 \tag{F1R24.5}
\]
No equivariance was imposed on $F,j,p$ or on this Hom space.

\paragraph{2. The precise endpoint composition forces a forbidden character.}
Let $K=\ker c^2_{v_q}=K_1\oplus K_2$ be the balanced
degree-two Hochschild kernel in the normalized convention of
R19. Let $\delta:T\to A[2]$ be the actual connecting class
of the lifted canonical triangle. Weak semiregularity and the
identity-sector/forgetful comparison give
$\operatorname{ev}_E^2(K)=0$. The exact endpoint Hom sequence
of (F1R19.15) then says that, for each $k\in K$, an actual
$f_k\in\operatorname{Hom}_G(A,T)$ satisfies
\[
                \operatorname{ev}_A^2(k)=\delta f_k.
 \tag{F1R24.6}
\]
This is membership in the image of the same invariant Yoneda
map; it does not assert the vanishing of the endpoint action.
By (F1R24.2), write $f_k=\iota_Iu_k$. Naturality of the
ordinary Hochschild action at the actual retract maps gives
\[
 \begin{split}
 \operatorname{ev}_F^2(k)
   &=p[2]\operatorname{ev}_A^2(k)j\\
   &=\bigl(p[2]\delta\iota_I\bigr)(u_kj).
 \end{split}
 \tag{F1R24.7}
\]
The first factor is one fixed morphism $I\to F[2]$, while
the second lies in the ordinary space in (F1R24.5).
Consequently the entire image of $\operatorname{ev}_F^2|_K$
has dimension at most one, regardless of any choice of the
preimages $f_k$. Taking the ordinary full trace gives
\[
     \operatorname{rank}\bigl(K\xrightarrow{k\mapsto
              k\operatorname{ch}(F)}HH_{-2}(X)\bigr)\le1.
 \tag{F1R24.8}
\]

The full rational rank-one-action calculation (F1R19.12)
applies to this actual rational Hodge character and forces
\[
                       \operatorname{ch}(F)=v_q,
 \tag{F1R24.9}
\]
since $\operatorname{rk}(F)=1$. This use of R19 has no
assumption on the dimension of the complete forward Hom
space: only the restriction space in (F1R24.5) was bounded.

For completeness, the contradiction is the actual hull exact
sequence, not a virtual positivity assertion. Put $M=F^{**}$.
Then $M$ is a line bundle, $c_1(M)=0$ by (F1R24.9), and
$Z=M/F$ is supported in codimension at least two. Its leading
surface cycle satisfies
\[
          [Z]_2=\operatorname{ch}_2(Z)=q(A_1+A_2)=qC.
 \tag{F1R24.10}
\]
This is a nonzero effective cycle. The complete effective-cycle
proof of \emph{DEG\_L S-22}, in the supplied
\texttt{semireg\_t7\_proofs.tex}, excludes it. Its actual maps
are restriction of all mixed holomorphic two-forms to the
resolution of each component, followed by the induced
Albanese homomorphism. Positivity first forces each tangent
plane into a single RM eigenspace; the Albanese image then
would have nonzero rational homology in an irrational RM
eigenspace, which is impossible. Thus (F1R24.3) cannot occur.

Every nontrivial direct-sum decomposition of a rank-three
torsion-free sheaf has a rank-one torsion-free summand:
torsion summands vanish, and positive ranks add to three.
This proves the stated ordinary indecomposability.

\paragraph{3. Consequences for the actual remaining surface extensions.}
In R23's $h=2$ alternative, take its two actual invariant row
maps and form
\[
 \phi:A\longrightarrow I^{\oplus2},\qquad
 M_0=\ker\phi,\qquad J_0=\operatorname{im}\phi.
 \tag{F1R24.11}
\]
Its generic rank is two, so $M_0$ is torsion-free of rank one
and $J_0$ is torsion-free of rank two. The actual short exact
sequence
\[
                 0\longrightarrow M_0\longrightarrow A
                        \longrightarrow J_0\longrightarrow0
 \tag{F1R24.12}
\]
has a nonzero class in the ordinary group
$\operatorname{Ext}^1_X(J_0,M_0)$. A zero class would give the
forbidden retract (F1R24.3). Thus even after forgetting the
specified point action the actual row-kernel extension must
remain nonsplit. This assertion concerns (F1R24.12), and
does not replace it by its double-dual sequence.

In the $h=3$ alternative, R23 gives the actual hull
$A^{**}\simeq L^{\oplus3}$ and a necessary surface defect.
That defect cannot be imposed independently in three direct
summands: any actual decomposition $A=F\oplus B$, including
$A=F^{\oplus3}$, is excluded by the proof above. An
indecomposable surface modification remains a genuine
unresolved class. Neither this result nor R23 constructs an
object in it or decides its coupled degree-two trace.

\paragraph{4. The separate common-quotient target restriction.}
Now retain the actual common-quotient branch of R22, with
the same positive virtual rank one, endpoint ranks three and
two, slope-zero normalization and point action. Denote its
actual quotient by $q_0:A\twoheadrightarrow Q$, where $Q$
is rank-one torsion-free of slope zero. Its factorization
isomorphism writes each invariant forward map uniquely as
\[
              f=u q_0,\qquad u\in\operatorname{Hom}_G(Q,T).
 \tag{F1R24.13}
\]
Suppose that $F$ were an ordinary rank-one retract of $T$,
with maps $j:F\to T$, $p:T\to F$, $pj=1_F$. Its rank-one
complement and $F$ both have slope zero, by semistability
of $T$ and additivity of its degree. The identical actual
double-dual/divisor proof of paragraph~1, with source $Q$
and target $F$, gives
\[
                       \dim\operatorname{Hom}_X(Q,F)\le1.
 \tag{F1R24.14}
\]
The second endpoint Yoneda membership in (F1R19.15) writes
$\operatorname{ev}_T^2(k)=f_k[2]\delta$ with an actual
invariant $f_k=u_kq_0$. Consequently the precise ordinary
retract composition is
\[
 \operatorname{ev}_F^2(k)
       =p[2]f_k[2]\delta j
       =(p u_k)[2]\bigl(q_0[2]\delta j\bigr).
 \tag{F1R24.15}
\]
Here $q_0[2]\delta j:F\to Q[2]$ is fixed, and
$p u_k$ lies in the space of dimension at most one in
(F1R24.14). Thus the entire $K$-action on $F$ again has
rank at most one. The ordinary full trace, the rational
calculation (F1R19.12), and the actual hull-cycle
contradiction (F1R24.10) exclude this retract.

Every nontrivial decomposition of a rank-two torsion-free
sheaf has rank-one summands. Hence the common-quotient
target $T$ is ordinarily indecomposable. R22 already
identifies its actual hull with $L^{\oplus2}$ and forces
the specified surface-supported degree-two cancellation.
That cancellation therefore cannot arise from a direct
sum of two rank-one torsion-free target modifications.
The proof uses the actual maps (F1R24.13)--(F1R24.15);
no duality functor or inferred correspondence between
the two factorization branches is used.
