\section[HD-F1-S40: local derived annihilation]{HD-F1-S40: the normal obstruction for actual complexes locally annihilated by the reduced ideal in the derived category}
\label{sec:f1-norm-fourfold-local-derived-annihilator}

Retain the normal integral norm image $i:Y\hookrightarrow P$,
its ideal $\mathcal I$, $E=i_*\mathcal O_Y$, and the S29
geometric open. Let $F\in D^b_{\rm coh}(P)$ be an actual
complex set-theoretically supported on $Y$.
There is a closed $Z\subset Y$ of codimension at least two
such that, locally on $V=P\setminus Z$,
\[
  f\,1_{F|_W}=0\quad\hbox{in }\operatorname{End}_{D(W)}(F|_W)
       \qquad(f\in\mathcal I(W)).
 \tag{F1S40.1}
\]
Here $W$ ranges over an actual affine open cover of $V$.
It suffices to check the displayed equality on finitely
many ideal generators on each chart. The input is the
zero derived action on this given complex. No global
$Ri_*D^b_{\rm coh}(Y)$ presentation, simultaneous strict
nullhomotopy, or splitting of its differentials is assumed.

Put $U=Y\setminus Z$. Each cohomology sheaf on $V$ is
annihilated by $\mathcal I$, so is the exact pushforward
of a unique coherent sheaf $G^a$ on $U$. Define
\[
       r=\sum_a(-1)^a\operatorname{rk}_U G^a.
 \tag{F1S40.2}
\]
For $r\ne0$ we prove an actual surjection
\[
\begin{split}
 \mathcal T_F:\operatorname{ev}_F
       (\mathfrak D_P\oplus H^2(P,\mathcal O_P))
          &\longrightarrow W_E,\\
 \operatorname{ev}_F(\kappa+\eta)
          &\longmapsto-r\operatorname{ob}_E(\kappa),
 \qquad W_E=\operatorname{ob}_E(\mathfrak D_P).
\end{split}
 \tag{F1S40.3}
\]
The complete S14 character forces $r\ne0$ and gives
an invariant full-trace kernel of dimension at least
$16-r_C\ge4$ for every specified coherent finite point
lift. Paragraphs 6--8 construct an actual gluable,
ordinarily indecomposable example with the complete
character and local ideal action zero everywhere.
It has neither a global reduced-support derived
presentation nor any cohomology selection satisfying
the S39 hypothesis.

\paragraph{1. Intrinsic cohomology supertrace.}
On any open $W\subset V$, an actual degree-zero ambient
endomorphism $b$ of $F|_W$ induces $\mathcal O_U$-linear
endomorphisms of the coherent sheaves $G^a$ there.
This follows from exact fully faithful closed pushforward
on the abelian categories of sheaves. For a coherent
module over a normal domain, an endomorphism preserves
its torsion submodule. Its trace on the torsion-free
quotient is regular at every height-one point, where
that quotient is free over a discrete valuation ring.
The normal-domain intersection extends this generic
trace to a regular function. A torsion module has
generic trace zero. Thus the actual function
\[
       \operatorname{str}_U(b)=
         \sum_a(-1)^a\operatorname{tr}_{G^a}(H^a(b))
              \in\Gamma(W\cap U,\mathcal O_U)
 \tag{F1S40.4}
\]
is defined before any coefficient-field choice.
It commutes with restriction and with conjugation by
an actual derived isomorphism. On cohomology, every
ambient function $h$ acts as its restriction $\bar h$,
so
\[
 \operatorname{str}_U(hb)=\bar h\operatorname{str}_U(b),
       \qquad\operatorname{str}_U(1_F)=r.
 \tag{F1S40.5}
\]
All cyclic trace identities here concern actual
$\mathcal O_U$-linear cohomology endomorphisms,
or their finite-dimensional generic vector spaces.
No cyclicity for a merely complex-linear differential
operator is invoked.

\paragraph{2. The actual epsilon adjunction.}
Suppose $\operatorname{ev}_F(\kappa+\eta)=0$.
Smoothness of $P$ makes $F$ perfect. The fully checked
Toda/BF map gives its actual scalar-twisted perfect
lift on $P_\kappa$. On an affine chart satisfying
(F1S40.1), use a local trivialization
$R_\epsilon=R[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)$ and denote
the lifted object by $M$. For the central immersion
$s:\operatorname{Spec}R\hookrightarrow
\operatorname{Spec}R_\epsilon$, its epsilon triangle is
\[
 s_*F_R\xrightarrow{j}M\xrightarrow{p}s_*F_R
       \longrightarrow s_*F_R[1],
       \qquad pj=0,\qquad jp=\epsilon.
 \tag{F1S40.6}
\]
It is obtained from the literal exact sequence of a
bounded finite free representative, with first and
last complexes identified by multiplication by epsilon.
It makes no flatness assertion for the cohomology of $M$.

For $f\in I$, condition (F1S40.1) and naturality give
$p\mu_f=0$. Exact Hom for (F1S40.6) factors
$\mu_f=j a_f$. The actual derived adjunction is
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}_{D(R_\epsilon)}(M,s_*F_R)
   =\operatorname{Hom}_{D(R)}(Ls^*M,F_R)
   =\operatorname{End}_{D(R)}(F_R),
 \qquad b\longmapsto s_*b\,p.
 \tag{F1S40.7}
\]
On the finite free representative this is the equality
of Hom complexes after reduction modulo epsilon.
Consequently
\[
          \mu_f=\Psi_M(b_f),\qquad
          \Psi_M(b)=j\,s_*b\,p
 \tag{F1S40.8}
\]
for an actual derived endomorphism $b_f$.
No inference from $j a_fj=0$ or from a generic
cohomology splitting is used.

\paragraph{3. The supertrace kills the exact ambiguity.}
We prove
\[
          \Psi_M(b)=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
                         \operatorname{str}_U(b)=0.
 \tag{F1S40.9}
\]
The right side is already a regular function on the
integral support. It suffices to test its generic value.
At $\xi$, put $R_0=\mathcal O_{P,\xi}$ and
$K=\mathbf C(Y)$. Lift a transcendence basis of
$K/\mathbf C$ to $R_0$. Its rational-function field
$K_0$ embeds in $R_0$, since every nonzero denominator
has nonzero residue and is a unit there. Write the
finite separable extension as $K=K_0(\alpha)$, with
monic separable minimal polynomial $m_\alpha$.
Use the actual flat ring map
\[
 R_0\longrightarrow A=
       (R_0[T]/(m_\alpha(T)))_{\mathfrak q},
 \qquad T\longmapsto\alpha\text{ in }K.
 \tag{F1S40.10}
\]
Here $\mathfrak q$ is the kernel of the displayed
residue evaluation. The monic polynomial gives a
finite free algebra before localization. The map
$K_0[T]/(m_\alpha)=K\to A$ is an injective field
map, and the simple residue root gives
$A/\mathfrak m_{R_0}A=K$.
Thus the actual generic cohomology and its
endomorphisms are unchanged by this flat extension.

Forget the base-changed lift $M_A$ to
$K_\epsilon=K[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)$.
Its epsilon triangle has central term $F_A$, whose
cohomology is the finite-dimensional graded vector
space $G^a_\xi$. The long exact sequence gives
bounded finite $K_\epsilon$-cohomology for $M_A$.
A bounded finite free $A_\epsilon$ representative
has finite Tor amplitude over $K_\epsilon$,
because $A$ is a $K$-vector space. The exact
Noetherian pseudo-coherence and finite-Tor-amplitude
criteria therefore give
$M_A\in\operatorname{Perf}(K_\epsilon)$.

Choose a bounded finite free representative
$L^\bullet$ of this actual object and put
$Q^\bullet=L^\bullet/\epsilon L^\bullet$.
The transported $b$ is represented by a $K$-linear
chain map $B:Q^\bullet\to Q^\bullet$.
The functorial epsilon triangle identifies $jBp$
with the chain map having matrices $\epsilon B^a$.
Hence
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}_{L^\bullet}(jBp)
    =\epsilon\sum_a(-1)^a\operatorname{Tr}(B^a)
    =\epsilon\sum_a(-1)^a\operatorname{Tr}(H^a(B))
    =\epsilon\operatorname{str}_U(b)(\xi).
 \tag{F1S40.11}
\]
The second equality follows from the actual cycle
and boundary exact sequences of the finite
$K$-complex. If $\Psi_M(b)=0$, its base change
is nullhomotopic on $L^\bullet$. The homotopy is
$K_\epsilon$-linear, and the alternating matrix
trace of $dH+Hd$ is zero by ordinary cyclicity.
Equation (F1S40.11) proves (F1S40.9).
The field extension was only a faithful test of an
intrinsic regular function. No change-of-coefficient
homotopy or differential-operator trace is required.

\paragraph{4. Normal maps, Hartogs descent and the original ambient lift.}
For $f\in I$ set
\[
                 u(f)=r^{-1}\operatorname{str}_U(b_f).
 \tag{F1S40.12}
\]
Equation (F1S40.9) makes this independent of every
choice in (F1S40.8). Addition of factors gives
additivity, and (F1S40.5) gives
$u(hf)=\bar h\,u(f)$.
For $f,g\in I$, the actual product on the lift is
\[
          \mu_{fg}=j b_f p j b_g p=0,
 \tag{F1S40.13}
\]
so its value is zero by the same ambiguity result.
Thus $u$ is an actual conormal functional on $U$.
Also $\epsilon\mu_h=\Psi_M(h|_F)$, so changing
an ambient lift by $\epsilon h$ changes the
unnormalized supertrace by $r\bar h$.

Fix now an ambient affine chart $V_i$ meeting $Z$,
with one trivialization of $P_\kappa$ on that chart.
Refine $V_i\setminus Z$ by the affine charts above.
The values (F1S40.12) agree on their intersections:
their factorizations concern the same lifted
multiplication, and their differences have zero
$\Psi_M$. Conjugation invariance handles any change
of derived representative. They therefore give
one map $u_i^\circ$ on $(V_i\cap Y)\setminus Z$.

For every finite local ideal generator, its value
under this map is regular at all height-one points
of the normal affine domain $\Gamma(V_i\cap Y,\mathcal O_Y)$.
The actual intersection map in (F1S39.8) extends each
value uniquely to that domain. Every original

vanishes on the dense open and hence everywhere.
This gives the regular map
$u_i:(I/I^2)|_{V_i\cap Y}\to\mathcal O_Y$.

On an overlap, orient the ambient transition from
$i$ to $k$ as $1+\epsilon\kappa_{ik}$.
The original derived overlap isomorphism transports

$b_{k,f}$ by $\kappa_{ik}(f)|_F$, modulo
$\ker\Psi_M$. By (F1S40.5) and (F1S40.9),
\[
                         u_i-u_k=\partial_1\kappa_{ik}.
 \tag{F1S40.14}
\]
This first holds off $Z$ and then on the entire

The given triple scalar $1-\epsilon\eta_{ikl}$
acts trivially by conjugation, so supplies no

The actual ideals
\[
 I_{\epsilon,i}=\{f+\epsilon h:f\in I_i,
                             \ h\bmod I_i=-u_i(f)\}
 \tag{F1S40.15}
\]
are ideals by conormal linearity. Their quotients

Equation (F1S40.14) transports each ideal to the
next one in the original $P_\kappa$.
They give an actual embedded deformation of $Y$.
The exact S24 maps imply
$\operatorname{ob}_E(\kappa)=0$.
This proves the kernel inclusion defining the

\paragraph{5. Nonzero generic Euler rank and full invariant trace.}
The cohomology of $F$ is supported on $Y$, and

\[
 \operatorname{ch}_j(F)=0\ (j<4),\qquad
 \operatorname{ch}_4(F)=r[Y]
             =r\left(w+\frac{\beta^4}{72}\right).
 \tag{F1S40.16}

Retain the complete specified equality
\[
 e^B\operatorname{ch}(F)=\lambda t+Q(\beta),
 \quad B\in H^{1,1}(P,\mathbf Q),\quad
 0\ne\lambda t\in W_K(P),\quad Q\in\mathbf Q[x].
 \tag{F1S40.17}

Cup its degree-four part with $\beta$.

The actual BF full derivative annihilates

       \{\kappa+\kappa\mathbin{\lrcorner}B:

 \tag{F1S40.18}

\paragraph{6. An actual locally split choice of the S38 cone.}
Choose distinct smooth points $p,q,r$ and write
$E_s=i_*I_{s/Y}$ with its actual map $a_s:E_s\to E$.
Take nonzero $w_p,w_r\in W_E$ and target lifts
$d_s\in\operatorname{Ext}^2_P(E,E_s)$ as in S38.
The original S24 map puts $W_E$ in the image of
$H^1(Y,\mathscr N)$, so its restriction to every
affine ambient open is zero.

For an affine neighborhood $V_s$ of $s$, the exact
point-ideal sequence therefore gives
\[
 d_s|_{V_s}\in\operatorname{im}
       [\operatorname{Ext}^1_{V_s}(E,k_s)
                    \longrightarrow\operatorname{Ext}^2_{V_s}(E,E_s)].
 \tag{F1S40.20}
\]
The restriction
$\operatorname{Ext}^1_P(E,k_s)\to
\operatorname{Ext}^1_{V_s}(E,k_s)$ is an isomorphism.
Indeed a finite local free resolution of $E$ gives
sheaf Ext supported at the one point; all its
positive-degree sheaf cohomology vanishes, so the
local-to-global edge is an isomorphism on either
open. Subtract the boundary of the resulting
actual global class from $d_s$.
Its target pushforward remains $w_s$ and its
restriction to $V_s$ is now zero. Away from $s$
the target map $E_s\to E$ is an isomorphism,
so the adjusted $d_s$ is zero on affine opens
there as well. Thus it is locally zero everywhere.

Pull back these adjusted classes by the actual

\[
          \delta_s=a_q^*d_s
                    \in\operatorname{Ext}^2_P(E_q,E_s).
\]
They are locally zero and still have
$\Psi_{q,s}(\delta_s)=w_s\ne0$.
Let $F_0$ be the actual cone in
\[
 E_p\oplus E_r\longrightarrow F_0\longrightarrow E_q[-1]
          \xrightarrow{(\delta_p,\delta_r)[-1]}(E_p\oplus E_r)[1].
 \tag{F1S40.21}
\]
Each local triangle splits because its actual
connecting morphism is zero. Thus locally
$F_0\simeq E_p\oplus E_r\oplus E_q[-1]$,
and $\mathcal I$ acts by zero in the local
derived category. The splittings are not
asserted to glue. The complete S38 arguments
apply to these adjusted nonzero couplings:
$F_0$ is gluable and ordinarily indecomposable,
and the exact scalar-retraction comparison
shows it has no global $Ri_*D^b_{\rm coh}(Y)$

\paragraph{7. The actual dual, its four cohomology degrees, and the Hom gate.}
Fix a translation-invariant volume form on $P$
and use the actual duality
$\mathbb D=R\mathcal Hom_P(-,\mathcal O_P)$.
On finite locally free complexes it is the
degree-reversed dual complex with the usual
differential $-(-1)^a(d^{-a-1})^*$.
The evaluation biduality is an isomorphism,
and multiplication by every function dualizes
to multiplication by that same function.
Put $F_1=\mathbb D F_0$. Its ideal action is
therefore zero locally in $D(P)$.

The actual Cohen--Macaulay concentration proved
in S24 gives, with $\Omega=i_*\omega_Y$,
\[
        \mathbb D E=\Omega[-4],\qquad
        \mathbb D k_s=k_s[-8].
 \tag{F1S40.22}
\]
Dualize $E_s\to E\to k_s$ and take the exact
cohomology sequence. It gives
\[
 H^4(\mathbb D E_s)=\Omega,\qquad
 H^7(\mathbb D E_s)=k_s,
 \quad H^a(\mathbb D E_s)=0\ (a\ne4,7).
 \tag{F1S40.23}
\]
Dualizing (F1S40.21) gives the actual triangle
\[
 \mathbb D E_q[1]\longrightarrow F_1
       \longrightarrow\mathbb D(E_p\oplus E_r)
                         \longrightarrow\mathbb D E_q[2].
 \tag{F1S40.24}
\]
The degrees in (F1S40.23) have no possible
nonzero connecting map in this cohomology
sequence. Therefore its complete cohomology is
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
 a&3&4&6&7\\ \hline
 H^a(F_1)&\Omega&\Omega^{\oplus2}&k_q&k_p\oplus k_r,
\end{array}
 \qquad H^a(F_1)=0\text{ otherwise}.
 \tag{F1S40.25}
\]
The generic Euler rank is $-1+2=1$.
The only positive-generic-length cohomology
choices are degrees three and four. The actual
map $\Omega\to\Omega^{\oplus2}$ given by
inclusion of one summand is nonzero at the
generic point of $Y$. It is an outgoing
adjacent Hom map for the first choice and an
incoming one for the second. Neither image
can be contained in a codimension-two subset.
The point sheaves have generic length zero.
Thus there is no selection satisfying S39.

Actual perfect biduality and the dual shift
give, for every integer $a$,
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb D F_0,\mathbb D F_0[a])
    \simeq\operatorname{Hom}(F_0[-a],F_0)
    \simeq\operatorname{Hom}(F_0,F_0[a]).
 \tag{F1S40.26}
\]
This reverses composition on degree-zero
endomorphisms.
It preserves vanishing negative self-Ext and
preserves idempotents. Hence $F_1$ is also
gluable and ordinarily indecomposable.

\paragraph{8. Exact closed-immersion duality and the complete character.}
Here is the actual support comparison needed
for the dual. For a closed affine immersion
defined by $R\to S=R/I$, an injective
$R$-resolution $J^\bullet$ gives the literal
adjunction of Hom complexes
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}_R(H,J^\bullet)
    =\operatorname{Hom}_S
        (H,\operatorname{Hom}_R(S,J^\bullet)).
 \tag{F1S40.27}
\]
The latter inner complex is injective for the
derived Hom calculation over $S$: restriction
of scalars is exact, and its displayed right
adjoint carries injective resolutions to the
corresponding homotopically injective complex.
These canonical evaluation maps restrict
compatibly and give
\[
 \mathbb D(Ri_*H)
       \simeq Ri_*R\mathcal Hom_Y(H,\omega_Y[-4]).
 \tag{F1S40.28}
\]
The last dualizing object is precisely
(F1S40.22), with its actual $\mathcal O_Y$
action. For bounded coherent $H$, the right
side has bounded coherent cohomology: its
exact pushforward equals the bounded coherent
ambient dual on the smooth $P$, and exact
closed pushforward detects both boundedness
and coherence. Thus the right-hand argument
is an actual object of $D^b_{\rm coh}(Y)$.

A global presentation $F_1\simeq Ri_*H$
would therefore give, by biduality and
(F1S40.28), a global reduced-support
presentation of $F_0$. That contradicts its
explicit S38 $\Psi$ and scalar-retraction
comparison. This proves the claimed
nonmembership through the actual morphisms.

Finally, duality of an actual finite locally
free complex changes its degree-$2j$ Chern
component by $(-1)^j$. If
$\operatorname{ch}(E)=w+Q_E(\beta)$, the
actual cone character and duality give
\[
      \operatorname{ch}(F_1)
           =w+Q_E(-\beta)
                          -\frac{\beta^8}{\int_P\beta^8}.
 \tag{F1S40.29}
\]
Here the determinant component has degree
eight and therefore positive sign; the
point component has degree sixteen and
also positive sign under duality.
The point equality is after the rational
cycle-class map, with no Chow equivalence
of distinct points asserted. Equation
(F1S40.29) is the full S14 character with
$B=0$. The trivial point action supplies
one actual coherent lift. The general
theorem applies to every other specified
lift that exists.
