\subsection{HD-I15: explicit rigid nonreflection components}
\label{sec:HD-I15}

For every $n\geq5$, $d\in\{3,4,6\}$, and even integer
\[
 m\geq\max\left\{2n+2,
          \left\lceil\frac{n^2-2n+3}{2}\right\rceil\right\},
 \qquad e_0=\gcd(d,2),
\]
there is a connected $G(d,e_0,m)$-Galois cover of $\mathbf P^1$ with
exactly three branch values whose standard multiplicity factor has
signature $(n,n)$.  Its Hurwitz component modulo $\operatorname{PGL}_2$
has dimension zero, so its period-image dimension is exactly zero.
The following generating triple proves all these assertions explicitly.

Let $\zeta=\zeta_d$, $\tau=(1\ 2)$, and $\rho=(1\ 2\ \cdots\ m)$.
Use $P_\sigma e_j=e_{\sigma(j)}$.  Set
\[
\begin{split}
 &v_1=1,\qquad v_2=0,\\
 &v_j=1\quad(3\leq j\leq n+2),\qquad
 v_j=-1\quad(n+3\leq j\leq2n+1),\\
 &v_j=0\quad(2n+2\leq j\leq m),
\end{split}
\]
and define the actual monomial matrices
\begin{equation}
 A=\operatorname{diag}(\zeta^{v_1},\ldots,\zeta^{v_m})P_\tau,
 \qquad B=P_\rho,\qquad C=(AB)^{-1}.              \tag{I15.1}
\end{equation}
They satisfy $ABC=1$, and their exponent sums are $2,0,-2$.
They consequently belong to $G(d,e_0,m)$.

\paragraph{Exact generation.}
Let $\Gamma=\langle A,B\rangle$.  Its permutation image is $S_m$,
since $\tau,\rho$ generate $S_m$.  Write $p=2n+1$ and
$A'=B^{2n}AB^{-2n}$.  The permutation of $A'$ is
$(p\ p+1)$, disjoint from $\tau$.  In additive exponent notation,
monomial multiplication is
\[
       (v,\sigma)(w,\eta)=(v+\sigma w,\sigma\eta).
\]
Thus the commutator $AA'A^{-1}(A')^{-1}$ is diagonal with vector
\begin{equation}
 w=(1-(p\ p+1))v+(\tau-1)\rho^{2n}v.
                                                       \tag{I15.2}
\end{equation}
The first summand is $-E_p+E_{p+1}$, and the second is supported on
$\{1,2\}$.  Therefore $w_p=-1$, $w_3=0$, and $w$ is supported on
at most four coordinates.  Conjugation of a diagonal matrix by a
monomial matrix permutes its exponent vector, without any extra
diagonal term.  Since $\Gamma\to S_m$ is onto, conjugate by a lift
of $(3\ p)$ and divide the resulting two diagonal elements.
This gives $-E_p+E_3$ in the diagonal subgroup.  Conjugating again
gives every $E_i-E_j$, so
\[
 D_0=\{u\in(\mathbf Z/d)^m:\ \sum_i u_i=0\}
                         \subset\Gamma.               \tag{I15.3}
\]
This also describes explicit words if desired: the matrices
$B^{j-1}AB^{-(j-1)}$ lift the adjacent transpositions $(j\ j+1)$,
and the usual adjacent-transposition word lifts $(3\ p)$.

The quotient of the full monomial group by $D_0$ is
$(\mathbf Z/d)\times S_m$, via exponent sum and permutation.
The image of $\Gamma$ in this quotient contains $\{0\}\times A_m$:
the commutators project onto $[S_m,S_m]=A_m$, and their exponent
sums vanish.  Because $m$ is even, $\rho$ is odd; the image of $B$
is $(0,\rho)$.  Hence the image contains $\{0\}\times S_m$.
Dividing the image $(2,\tau)$ of $A$ by $(0,\tau)$ yields $(2,1)$.
It follows that this image is exactly
$e_0(\mathbf Z/d)\times S_m$.  Together with (I15.3), this proves
\begin{equation}
                         \Gamma=G(d,e_0,m).             \tag{I15.4}
\end{equation}
In particular no inference of full monodromy from its permutation
image alone is being made.

\paragraph{Signature and actual quotient curve.}
The colored cycles of $A$ consist of one two-cycle of color $1$,
$n$ fixed cycles of color $1$, $n-1$ fixed cycles of color $-1$,
and $m-2n-1$ fixed cycles of color zero.  The matrix $B$ has one
$m$-cycle of color zero.  The permutation $\tau\rho$ fixes $1$
and cycles $2,\ldots,m$.  The corresponding cycle sums of $AB$
are $v_1=1$ and $\sum_{j=2}^m v_j=1$; taking the inverse gives
two cycles of $C$, of lengths $1,m-1$, both of color $-1$.
Thus the entire triple has
\[
 \sum(\ell-1)=1+(m-1)+(m-2)=2m-2,
 \qquad r_1=r_{d-1}=n+1,
\]
with no other nonzero-color cycles.  The actual quotient
$Y=Z/H$ therefore has genus zero by HD-I03, and
\[
 S=\frac{(n+1)+(d-1)(n+1)}d=n+1,
 \qquad h^{1,0}_\chi=h^{1,0}_{\bar\chi}=n.
\]
Riemann existence applied to the product-one generating triple
(I15.1) produces the connected $G(d,e_0,m)$-cover $Z$; the quotient
maps of HD-I02 produce its cyclic tower and its actual polarized
isogeny to the standard multiplicity factor.  All three generators
are nonidentity.  Three ordered distinct branch values have one
$\operatorname{PGL}_2$-orbit, and the group is finite, so the resulting
Nielsen component has zero-dimensional base.  Its period rank is
therefore zero.

For example, $n=5,m=12$ gives such components for
$G(3,1,12)$, $G(4,2,12)$, and $G(6,2,12)$.  Each has intermediate
genus zero and signature $(5,5)$, while its period-image dimension is
zero.  This is an explicit constrained tower outside the
reflection-only locus, where HD-I14 gave rank $9$ at the same genus.
The differing ranks are proved through the actual maps and branch
constraints above.
