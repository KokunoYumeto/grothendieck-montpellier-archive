\section[HD-F1-S35: ordinary-power thickenings]{HD-F1-S35: normal obstructions for vector bundles on ordinary powers of the norm-image ideal}
\label{sec:f1-norm-fourfold-ordinary-powers}

Retain the normal integral norm image
$i:Y\hookrightarrow P$ on the S29 geometric open,
its ideal $\mathcal I$, and
$E=i_*\mathcal O_Y$.
For each integer $k\ge1$ take the actual closed
subscheme and closed immersion
\[
 Y_k=(Y,\mathcal O_P/\mathcal I^k),
 \qquad i_k:Y_k\hookrightarrow P.
 \tag{F1S35.1}
\]
Let $V$ be any actual vector bundle of positive
rank $m$ on $Y_k$ and set
\[
 F=i_{k*}V,\qquad
 r_k=\binom{k+3}{4},\qquad
 D=\mathfrak D_P,\qquad W_E=\operatorname{ob}_E(D).
 \tag{F1S35.2}
\]
For these actual sheaves there is a well-defined
surjection
\[
\begin{split}
 \mathcal T_{k,V}:
 \operatorname{ev}_F\bigl(D\oplus H^2(P,\mathcal O_P)\bigr)
     &\longrightarrow W_E,\\
 \operatorname{ev}_F(\kappa+\eta)
     &\longmapsto -m r_k\,\operatorname{ob}_E(\kappa).
\end{split}
 \tag{F1S35.3}
\]
Every such $F$ meeting the complete S14 character
\[
 e^B\operatorname{ch}(F)=\lambda t+Q(\beta),
 \qquad B\in H^{1,1}(P,\mathbf Q),\quad
 0\ne\lambda t\in W_K(P),\quad Q\in\mathbf Q[x]
 \tag{F1S35.4}
\]
has an invariant full degree-two trace kernel of
dimension at least $16-r_C\ge4$ for every specified
coherent finite point lift. This excludes the
whole stated ordinary-power vector-bundle class
from the weak semiregularity gate.

\paragraph{1. The exact lower-degree vanishing for a power ideal.}
First work over a domain $A$, with
$S=A[x_1,\ldots,x_c]$, $c\ge2$, and
$\mathfrak n=(x_1,\ldots,x_c)$.
Let
\[
             \delta:\mathfrak n^k\longrightarrow
                                      S/\mathfrak n^k
 \tag{F1S35.5}
\]
be an actual $S$-linear map.
For every multi-index $\alpha$ with $|\alpha|=k$,
write its value uniquely as a sum of homogeneous
polynomials $h_{\alpha,d}$ of $x$-degree
$0\le d\le k-1$.
For $\alpha_j>0$ and
$\beta=\alpha+e_i-e_j$, the literal monomial
identity gives
\[
 x_i\,\delta(x^\alpha)
      =x_j\,\delta(x^\beta)
                    \quad\hbox{in }S/\mathfrak n^k.
 \tag{F1S35.6}
\]
When $d\le k-2$, its degree-$d+1$ component is
the actual polynomial equality
$x_i h_{\alpha,d}=x_j h_{\beta,d}$ in $S$.
Consequently the fractions
$h_{\alpha,d}/x^\alpha$ in $\operatorname{Frac}(S)$
are equal for adjacent multi-indices.
The graph is connected: transfer one unit at a
time from a coordinate in which $\alpha$ exceeds
a target multi-index to a coordinate in which it
is smaller. Thus all these fractions equal
one $u_d$.

Compare $\alpha=k e_1$ and $\alpha=k e_2$.
The equality
\[
          x_2^k h_{k e_1,d}
                       =x_1^k h_{k e_2,d}
 \tag{F1S35.7}
\]
implies that $x_1^k$ divides $h_{k e_1,d}$.
Indeed $x_1$ is prime in the domain $S$ and does
not divide $x_2$; repeat this argument $k$ times.
It follows that $u_d\in S$.
Its homogeneous degree is $d-k<0$, so $u_d=0$.
All $h_{\alpha,d}$ with $d\le k-2$ vanish.
We have proved
\[
 \delta(\mathfrak n^k)\subset
                    \mathfrak n^{k-1}/\mathfrak n^k,
 \qquad
 \delta(\mathfrak n^{k+1})=0.
 \tag{F1S35.8}
\]
The second assertion uses $S$-linearity and the
annihilation of the displayed target by
$\mathfrak n$.
For $k=1$ the first assertion is the full target,
and the second follows in the same way.
Exactly the same calculation works for
$A[[x_1,\ldots,x_c]]$: the classes modulo
$\mathfrak n^k$ have unique polynomial
representatives of $x$-degree less than $k$.
Only the explicit linear relations (F1S35.6)
were used; no presentation theorem for all
syzygies is assumed.

\paragraph{2. The actual completion and descent maps.}
Let $U=Y_{\rm reg}$. Since $Y$ is a normal
complex variety, $Y\setminus U$ has codimension
at least two. At a closed point $y\in U$,
put $R=\mathcal O_{P,y}$ and
$A_0=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$.
These are regular local rings of dimensions
eight and four with residue field $\mathbf C$.
Choose $x_1,\ldots,x_4\in I_y$ whose classes
span the kernel of the cotangent surjection,
and choose lifts $t_1,\ldots,t_4$ of regular
parameters of $A_0$.
Their eight classes are a basis of
$\mathfrak m_R/\mathfrak m_R^2$.
The resulting actual continuous homomorphisms
on completions are
\[
\begin{split}
 \mathbf C[[t_1,\ldots,t_4,x_1,\ldots,x_4]]
                          &\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\widehat R,\\
 \mathbf C[[t_1,\ldots,t_4]]
                          &\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\widehat A_0.
\end{split}
 \tag{F1S35.9}
\]
Here each formal variable maps to the chosen
element. Surjectivity follows by successive
lifting in the maximal-ideal filtration;
injectivity is the regular complete-local
power-series argument of Stacks 0C0S(2).
Regularity and dimension are retained under
completion by 07NY and its dimension calculation.
The second map is the quotient of the first
by the chosen $x_i$, so
\[
                  I_y\widehat R=(x_1,\ldots,x_4).
 \tag{F1S35.10}
\]
Exactness of completion on finite modules
identifies the completed quotient with
$\widehat A_0$.

The map $R\to\widehat R$ is faithfully flat.
Thus an actual local morphism
$I_y^k\to R/I_y^k$ base changes to one in
the completed coordinates (F1S35.9).
Equation (F1S35.8), with
$A=\mathbf C[[t_1,\ldots,t_4]]$ and $c=4$,
holds after this actual base change.
Faithful flatness detects the vanishing of its
composites to $R/I_y^{k-1}$ and on $I_y^{k+1}$,
so the two assertions descend to $R$.
Closed points suffice, since a nonzero coherent
image on the finite-type smooth locus would
have a closed point in its support.
Consequently, for every local section
$\delta$ of $\mathcal Hom_P(\mathcal I^k,
\mathcal O_P/\mathcal I^k)$, its restriction
to $U$ canonically induces
\[
 \bar\delta:
 \operatorname{Sym}^k C\longrightarrow
                   \operatorname{Sym}^{k-1}C,
 \qquad C=(\mathcal I/\mathcal I^2)|_U.
 \tag{F1S35.11}
\]
The identifications
$\operatorname{Sym}^a C=\mathcal I^a/\mathcal I^{a+1}$
are the actual multiplication maps. They are
isomorphisms in the completed coordinates and
hence before completion by faithful flatness.
This proves both the required filtration
assertion and its coordinate independence.

\paragraph{3. A canonical normal retraction with its exact coefficient.}
For a section $v$ of the rank-four bundle $C$,
let $m_v$ be multiplication by $v$ on its
symmetric algebra. Define
\[
 \rho_k(\delta)(v)
       =\frac{1}{r_k}
          \operatorname{Tr}_{\operatorname{Sym}^{k-1}C}
                         (\bar\delta\circ m_v)
                    \quad\hbox{on }U.
 \tag{F1S35.12}
\]
This is a trace on an actual locally free
$\mathcal O_U$-module. It defines an
$\mathcal O_U$-linear map $C\to\mathcal O_U$,
independent of a frame because traces are
invariant under conjugation.

Here is its exact normalization, proved for
any rank $c\ge2$. For a covector $d\in C^\vee$,
write $\iota_d$ for the derivation of the
symmetric algebra that contracts one factor.
On the monomial basis $x^\alpha$ of
$\operatorname{Sym}^{k-1}C$, the endomorphism
$\iota_{e_j^\vee}m_{e_i}$ has trace zero for
$i\ne j$, since it changes the multi-index.
For $i=j$ its diagonal coefficient is
$\alpha_i+1$. Summing gives
\[
\begin{split}
 \operatorname{Tr}(\iota_d m_v)
  &=\left(1+\frac{k-1}{c}\right)
          \binom{c+k-2}{c-1}\,d(v)\\
  &=\binom{c+k-1}{c}\,d(v).
\end{split}
 \tag{F1S35.13}
\]
The equality
$\sum_{|\alpha|=k-1}\alpha_i
 =(k-1)\binom{c+k-2}{c-1}/c$
follows by symmetry in the $c$ coordinates
and summing $|\alpha|=k-1$.
For $c=4$ this coefficient is exactly $r_k$.
It is also the length of the actual truncated
polynomial ring in four normal variables,
by counting the monomials of degrees below $k$.

For a local ambient derivation $d$ define the
actual ideal-derivative maps
\[
 \partial_k(d):\mathcal I^k\longrightarrow
                   \mathcal O_P/\mathcal I^k,
 \quad f\longmapsto d(f)\bmod\mathcal I^k,
 \qquad
 \partial_1(d):\mathcal I/\mathcal I^2
                           \longrightarrow\mathcal O_Y.
 \tag{F1S35.14}
\]
The first is $\mathcal O_P$-linear: in $d(hf)$,
the term $d(h)f$ is zero modulo $\mathcal I^k$.
Leibniz's rule shows that its induced map
(F1S35.11) is $\iota_{\partial_1(d)}$.
Therefore (F1S35.13) proves the exact identity
\[
                       \rho_k\partial_k=\partial_1
                         \quad\hbox{on }U.
 \tag{F1S35.15}
\]

\paragraph{4. Extension over the actual singular support.}
The formula (F1S35.12) extends canonically to
all of $Y$. To check every relation, take an
affine open on $Y$ and finitely many ambient
ideal generators $f_1,\ldots,f_s$.
For a local $\delta$, the regular functions
$\rho_k(\delta)(f_j\bmod\mathcal I^2)$ on
$U$ extend uniquely to that affine normal
domain: they lie in each height-one local
ring, whose intersection is the normal ring.
Every relation among the classes of the $f_j$
holds on $U$, hence its evaluated regular
function is zero on the dense open and
therefore zero on the integral $Y$.
All $\mathcal I^2$ relations are retained in
this argument. Thus the extended values
define an actual map
$\mathcal I/\mathcal I^2\to\mathcal O_Y$.

Uniqueness of the extension proves additivity,
compatibility with restrictions, and
$\mathcal O_P$-linearity, with the target
action through $\mathcal O_Y$.
We have constructed the global sheaf morphism
\[
 \rho_k:
 \mathcal Hom_P(\mathcal I^k,\mathcal O_P/\mathcal I^k)
       \longrightarrow
 i_*\mathcal Hom_Y(\mathcal I/\mathcal I^2,\mathcal O_Y),
 \qquad \rho_k\partial_k=\partial_1.
 \tag{F1S35.16}
\]
The last equality extends from (F1S35.15) by
the same uniqueness.
Normality is used only on the reduced $Y$.
No normality of $Y_k$ is assumed.

\paragraph{5. The actual vector-bundle trace produces the thickened ideal deformation.}
Suppose
$\operatorname{ev}_F(\kappa+\eta)=0$.
Toda's actual lift, with the corrected S33
degree-one convention, and the exact
cohomology argument (F1S33.11)--(F1S33.13)
give an actual flat scalar-twisted sheaf
$M_\varepsilon$ lifting the given
$F=i_{k*}V$ on $P_\kappa$.
On a local split ambient algebra put
$J=I^k$ and $A_k=R/J$.
For $f\in J$, the actual module structure gives
$fM_\varepsilon\subset\varepsilon M_\varepsilon$.
Multiplication by epsilon identifies the
central $V$ with this latter sheaf.
For a section $v$ of $V$ and any lift
$\widetilde v$, set
\[
 a_f(v)=\varepsilon^{-1}(f\widetilde v)
                    \in V.
 \tag{F1S35.17}
\]
The symbol means the inverse of that specified
epsilon isomorphism. Changing the lift adds
$\varepsilon f w=0$, since $f$ annihilates
the central sheaf.
Commutativity gives
$a_f\in\mathcal End_{A_k}(V)$ and
$a_{hf}=\bar h a_f$.
For $f,g\in J$, their product acts through
epsilon twice, so $a_{fg}=0$.

Use the ordinary matrix trace of the actual
locally free $A_k$-module $V$:
\[
 \delta_i:J\longrightarrow A_k,\qquad
             \delta_i(f)=\frac1m\operatorname{tr}_V(a_f).
 \tag{F1S35.18}
\]
Local traces glue by their conjugation
invariance, even though $A_k$ is nonreduced.
This is an actual $R$-linear map killing
$J^2$. It is not the normal-domain trace
used for coherent sheaves on the reduced
support in S33.
The explicit ideals
\[
 J_{\varepsilon,i}
  =\{\,f+\varepsilon h:\ f\in J,\quad
                              h\bmod J=-\delta_i(f)\,\}
 \tag{F1S35.19}
\]
have flat quotients: their intersection with
$\varepsilon R$ is $\varepsilon J$, and
the kernel of epsilon on the quotient is
its image, by the representative calculation
of (F1S33.18).
This proves flatness of the constructed
ideal quotient directly; no ideal-filtration
factor of $M_\varepsilon$ is asserted flat.

The ambient transition is
$f+\varepsilon h\mapsto
 f+\varepsilon(h+\kappa_{ij}(f))$.
The actual central transition of $V$
conjugates $a_{i,f}$ to
$a_{j,f}+\kappa_{ij}(f)1_V$.
Taking its matrix trace gives
\[
              \delta_i-\delta_j
                             =\partial_k(\kappa_{ij}).
 \tag{F1S35.20}
\]
The scalar twisted triple products act
trivially under conjugation. Hence
(F1S35.19) glues to an honest embedded
deformation of $Y_k$ in $P_\kappa$.

\paragraph{6. Transfer to the reduced-support obstruction.}

$u_i=\rho_k(\delta_i)$.

                       u_i-u_j=\partial_1(\kappa_{ij}).
 \tag{F1S35.21}

The local ideals
$\{f+\varepsilon h:f\in I,\ \bar h=-u_i(f)\}$
therefore glue to an actual flat embedded

with flatness checked by the same explicit
epsilon-kernel criterion.
Equivalently, applying $H^1(\rho_k)$ to
the actual normal cocycle of $Y_k$ gives
the normal cocycle of $Y$.
The precise S24 ideal-obstruction maps yield
\[
 \operatorname{ev}_F(\kappa+\eta)=0
       \ \Longrightarrow\
 \eta_Y(\kappa)=0
       \ \Longrightarrow\
                         \operatorname{ob}_E(\kappa)=0.
 \tag{F1S35.22}

This proves the kernel inclusion required

\paragraph{7. Whole character and every actual point lift.}
At the generic point of $Y$, the ambient
local ring is regular of dimension four,
and the generic stalk of $V$ is free of
rank $m$ over its quotient by the $k$th
maximal-ideal power. Its length is $m r_k$,
as follows from the actual filtration
$\mathcal I^a/\mathcal I^{a+1}
 =\operatorname{Sym}^a C$ on $U$
and the monomial ranks counted in paragraph 3.
The actual localized-character pushforward
and cycle-class map audited in S33 give
\[
 \operatorname{ch}_j(F)=0\quad(j<4),\qquad
 \operatorname{ch}_4(F)
        =m r_k[Y]
        =m r_k\left(w+\frac{\beta^4}{72}\right).
 \tag{F1S35.23}
\]
This leading calculation uses the actual
generic length of the nonreduced module.
It does not replace that module by a
direct sum on its reduced support.
For a sheaf meeting (F1S35.4), cup product
of the leading equality with $\beta$ gives
$q_4=m r_k/72$, and then the unwedged
equality is $\lambda t=m r_k w$.

For the actual full-character shear
$a_B(\kappa)=\kappa+\kappa\mathbin{\lrcorner}B$,
the retained BF characteristic--trace identity
and its finite-exponential calculation give
\[
\begin{split}
 U_F&=\operatorname{ev}_F(a_B(D))
                                      \subseteq\ker\sigma_F^2,\\
 -\frac1{m r_k}\mathcal T_{k,V}|_{U_F}
                        &:U_F\twoheadrightarrow W_E.
\end{split}
 \tag{F1S35.24}
\]
Every specified coherent finite point lift
fixes $U_F$ by naturality, because point
functors act trivially on $HH^2(P)$.
The exact R05 equivariant comparison
therefore gives
\[
 \dim\bigl(\ker\sigma_F^2\cap
                   \operatorname{Ext}^2_P(F,F)^G\bigr)
                           \ge16-r_C\ge4.
 \tag{F1S35.25}
\]
All sheaves (F1S35.2) have no negative
self-Ext, and the obstruction above rules
them out at the actual trace gate.

\paragraph{8. Exact comparison with S34 and the retained boundary.}
For $k=1$, $r_1=1$ and
$\operatorname{Sym}^{k-1}C=\mathcal O_U$,
so (F1S35.12) is the identity normal map.
The matrix trace, ideal deformation and
characteristic-image quotient are then
the S33 maps for the actual vector bundle
$V$ on $Y$, and hence agree with S34.

For $k\ge2$, the actual additional object
$\mathcal O_{Y_k}$ has nonzero central
$\mathcal I$-action: at the generic point,
choose $x_1\in I_\xi$ with nonzero class
in $I_\xi/I_\xi^2$.
Multiplication by $x_1$ sends $1$ to
$x_1\ne0$ in $\mathcal O_{P,\xi}/I_\xi^k$,
since $I_\xi^k\subseteq I_\xi^2$.
Every actual $Ri_*\mathcal G$ from S34 has
cohomology sheaves annihilated by
$\mathcal I$. An isomorphism to
$\mathcal O_{Y_k}$ would give an isomorphism
on $H^0$ commuting with multiplication by
$x_1$, contradicting that displayed
nonzero action. Thus the new actual
thickenings are compared to the S34
scope through their explicit ideal action.

The S34 example
$R/(x_1^s,x_2,x_3,x_4)$ remains an exact
failure of its proposed epsilon-trace
map. Its defining ideal contains $x_2$,
whereas $(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)^s$ does not
when $s\ge2$. Its actual ideal-action map has
source and target $J\longrightarrow R/J$,
where $J=(x_1^s,x_2,x_3,x_4)$, whereas
the map used here has source and target
$I^s\longrightarrow R/I^s$ for
$I=(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)$. Thus the lower-degree
conclusion proved from (F1S35.6)--(F1S35.8)
for that ordinary-power source and target
does not apply to the displayed $J$-map.
Arbitrary coherent modules on higher
thickenings, and arbitrary coupled
derived objects on those thickenings,
remain outside the newly proved vector-bundle
scope unless an actual additional argument
establishes the needed maps.
