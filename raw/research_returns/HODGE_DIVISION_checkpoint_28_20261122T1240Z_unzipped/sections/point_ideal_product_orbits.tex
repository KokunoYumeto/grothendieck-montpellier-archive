\section{HD-R05: every product of point ideals of total dimension eight}
\label{sec:point-ideal-all-products}

\paragraph{The actual objects and functors.}
Let $X_1,\ldots,X_r$ be positive-dimensional complex abelian varieties,
let $k_i=\dim X_i$ with $\sum_i k_i=8$, and choose points
$p_i\in X_i$. On $X=\prod_iX_i$ take the actual perfect complex
\[
                         F=\boxtimes_{i=1}^r I_{p_i}.
                                                               \tag{R05.1}
\]
Apply any finite composition of actual Fourier--Mukai equivalences
between abelian eightfolds, finite isogeny pullbacks and pushforwards,
line-bundle tensors, translations, shifts and derived duals. No resulting
whole Chern character has the form
\[
             e^B\bigl(P(\theta)+\lambda t\bigr),\qquad\lambda\ne0,
                                                               \tag{R05.2}
\]
on an abelian eightfold of Weil type with balanced signature $(4,4)$,
where $\theta$ is a compatible polarization, $B$ a rational divisor
class and $t$ a nonzero rational Weil class. This includes every CM
product in (R05.1). The conclusion follows from the actual character
and its transported contraction maps; it does not require a self-Ext
or semiregularity assumption on the starting object.

\paragraph{The two contraction maps on an individual factor.}
Write
\[
 U_i=H^1(X_i,\mathcal O_{X_i})\oplus H^0(X_i,T_{X_i}),
 \qquad v_i=\operatorname{ch}(I_{p_i})=1-[p_i].
\]
The last identity is the same ideal-sheaf Chern-character identity used
in HD-G20. Choose holomorphic generators $x_1,\ldots,x_k$ and
antiholomorphic generators $y_1,\ldots,y_k$ on a factor of dimension k.
After multiplying the top monomial by its nonzero normalization,
$[p]$ is $x_1\cdots x_k y_1\cdots y_k$. The actual degree-one
operators are wedge by $y_j$ and contraction by $x_j^*$.

For $k\ge2$, their images on $1-[p]$ have respective Hodge bidegrees
$(0,1)$ and $(k-1,k)$, and give k independent monomials in each
summand. Thus $\operatorname{rank}c_{v_i}^1=2k$.
For $k=1$, both operations have image the same line spanned by $y_1$,
so the rank is one. This also follows directly from the actual equality
$I_p=\mathcal O_X(-p)$ on an elliptic curve.

In degree two, two wedges give the $\binom{k}{2}$ independent
monomials in $H^{0,2}$. Two contractions give the
$\binom{k}{2}$ independent monomials in $H^{k-2,k}$.
A wedge followed by a contraction gives zero: the top monomial already
contains every $y_j$, and contraction kills the scalar term.
For $k\ge3$ the two nonzero groups have different bidegrees.
For $k=2$ they have the same one-dimensional image. Consequently
\[
 r_1(k):=\operatorname{rank}c_{v_i}^1=
 \begin{cases}1&k=1,\\2k&k\ge2,\end{cases}
 \qquad
 r_2(k):=\operatorname{rank}c_{v_i}^2=
 \begin{cases}1&k=2,\\k(k-1)&k\ge3.\end{cases}             \tag{R05.3}
\]
The degree-two value for $k=1$ is not needed below: the degree-one
map already treats every product containing such a factor.

\paragraph{The exact product map and the complete partition list.}
The Hochschild--Kunneth isomorphism identifies $HH^1(X)$ with
$\bigoplus_iU_i$ and
\[
 HH^2(X)=\bigoplus_i\bigwedge^2U_i
                  \ \oplus\ \bigoplus_{i<j}(U_i\otimes U_j).
                                                               \tag{R05.4}
\]
The Chern character of (R05.1) is the tensor product of the nonzero
$v_i$. On the first degree-two summand the contraction is
$c_{v_i}^2\otimes\bigotimes_{h\ne i}v_h$; on the cross summand
it is $c_{v_i}^1\otimes c_{v_j}^1\otimes
\bigotimes_{h\ne i,j}v_h$, with the Koszul sign. This is an equality
of the actual wedge/contraction operators on the exterior algebra of
the product.

The target Hochschild multigrading places these maps respectively in
multidegrees with a single $-2$, or two $-1$ entries. The corresponding
subspaces are a direct sum. The same multigrading separates every
single $-1$ in degree one. Their ranks therefore add, giving
\[
 \operatorname{rank}c_F^1=\sum_i r_1(k_i),\qquad
 \operatorname{rank}c_F^2=
       \sum_i r_2(k_i)+\sum_{i<j}r_1(k_i)r_1(k_j)
       \quad(k_i\ge2\text{ for all }i).                       \tag{R05.5}
\]
A product with a dimension-one factor has
$\operatorname{rank}c_F^1=16-\#\{i:k_i=1\}<16$.
HD-N14 proves rank sixteen for every character (R05.2), which
excludes every such product by this exact map.

It remains to treat the partitions with all parts at least two.
Writing $a_2=\#\{i:k_i=2\}$ and using $\sum k_i=8$, (R05.5)
becomes
\[
                  \operatorname{rank}c_F^2
                          =120-\sum_i k_i^2-a_2.              \tag{R05.6}
\]
Here is the complete finite list:
\[
\begin{array}{c|r|l}
 (k_i)&\operatorname{rank}c_F^2&\text{exact remaining comparison}\\\hline
 (8)&56&\text{single point ideal, HD-G20}\\
 (2,6)&79&\text{degree-two rank excludes (R05.2)}\\
 (3,5)&86&\text{degree-two rank excludes (R05.2)}\\
 (4,4)&88&\text{point-ideal product, HD-G19}\\
 (2,2,4)&94&\text{degree-two rank excludes (R05.2)}\\
 (2,3,3)&97&\text{degree-two rank excludes (R05.2)}\\
 (2,2,2,2)&100&\text{degree-two rank excludes (R05.2)}
\end{array}                                                     \tag{R05.7}
\]
There are no other partitions: one part gives $(8)$; two give the
three unordered pairs displayed; three give $(2,2,4)$ or $(2,3,3)$;
four force every part to be two; five require total dimension at least
ten. HD-N14 gives the exact target rank set $\{56,72,88,104\}$.
This settles five rows directly. The two surviving rank values have
the full-orbit stabilizer obstructions proved in HD-G20 and HD-G19,
respectively; those proofs apply to arbitrary abelian factors of the
specified dimensions, including different fourfolds in $(4,4)$.

\paragraph{Transport through the specified actual functors.}
The Fourier--Mukai diagram in HD-G19, from its actual kernel and
Orlov's graph map, intertwines the Clifford action and maps
$U=H^1(\mathcal O)\oplus H^0(T)$ isomorphically to the corresponding
$U'$ on the target. Its exterior powers intertwine $c^1,c^2$ with
invertible maps of the Hochschild targets. The holomorphic isogeny
pullback has the same property; pushforward is its inverse multiplied
by the nonzero degree. Tensor by a line bundle is the stated
invertible $(1,1)$-shear on U. Translation acts identically on the
character, and a shift multiplies it by $-1$. For the explicit duality
operator $\tau|_{\bigwedge^a}=(-1)^{a(a-1)/2}$ put
$M(y,\xi)=(y,-\xi)$ on U. For the even character v the literal
identities are
\[
 \tau c(w)v=c(Mw)\tau v,\qquad
 \tau c(w_1)c(w_2)v=-c(Mw_1)c(Mw_2)\tau v.
                                                               \tag{R05.8}
\]
Thus the degree-one target map is $\tau$ and the degree-two target
map is $-\tau$; both are invertible. These are the precise duality
rank intertwiners. Composing these actual diagrams preserves both
ranks in (R05.5). Thus every rank exclusion in (R05.7) holds on the
whole specified orbit. The other two rows already have precisely that
orbit scope in HD-G19--G20. This proves the assertion.
