\section[HD-F1-S31: ambient bundle tensors retain the normal obstruction]{HD-F1-S31: every ambient vector-bundle tensor retains the actual normal obstruction, and none satisfies the weak semiregular S14 gate}
\label{sec:f1-norm-fourfold-bundle-tensor}

Fix the actual norm image $i:Y\hookrightarrow P$ and
$E=i_*\mathcal O_Y$ on the full geometric open of S29.
Throughout, $\operatorname{ob}$ is contraction of the
first-jet class with the diagonal convention of S24,
whereas $\operatorname{ev}$ is the Hochschild
characteristic map in the Buchweitz--Flenner
Atiyah--Chern convention. Their exact sign relation
is established in paragraph~2.
Write $\mathfrak D_P$ for its sixteen-dimensional
polarized Weil tangent, and put
\[
 W_E=\operatorname{im}\bigl(
       \operatorname{ob}_E:\mathfrak D_P
                         \longrightarrow\operatorname{Ext}^2_P(E,E)\bigr),
 \qquad \dim W_E=16-r_C\ge4.
 \tag{F1S31.1}
\]
Let $N$ be any actual vector bundle of positive rank $r$ on
$P$, and set $F=E\otimes N=i_*(N|_Y)$. The following
comparison holds without a character restriction on $N$:
\[
 p_{\rm ns}\operatorname{ptr}_N
       \bigl(\operatorname{ev}_{F}(\kappa+\eta)\bigr)
       =-r\,\operatorname{ob}_E(\kappa)
 \quad
 \left(\kappa\in\mathfrak D_P,\ 
                  \eta\in H^2(P,\mathcal O_P)\right).
 \tag{F1S31.2}
\]
Both maps on the left are constructed below. In particular
\[
 \ker(\operatorname{ob}_F|_{\mathfrak D_P})
            \subseteq\ker(\operatorname{ob}_E|_{\mathfrak D_P})
            =\operatorname{im}d\operatorname{Pr}.
 \tag{F1S31.3}
\]

There is no weakly point-equivariantly semiregular object
of this form $F$ satisfying the actual full-character
requirement of DEG\_L S-14:
\[
 e^B\operatorname{ch}(F)=\lambda t+Q(\beta),\qquad
 B\in H^{1,1}(P,\mathbf Q),\quad
 0\ne\lambda t\in W_K(P),\quad Q\in\mathbf Q[x].
 \tag{F1S31.4}
\]
More precisely, for every specified coherent lift of a
faithful finite point subgroup, its invariant full
degree-two trace kernel has dimension at least $16-r_C$.
The conclusion uses the entire displayed character and
does not impose simplicity or a scalar Atiyah class on $N$.

There are concrete bundles to which this statement
applies on every retained fibre: every isogeny pushforward
$h_*L$ of an actual line bundle, and every vector bundle
with a finite filtration by such bundles having one
identical rational divisor class $D$ as specified in
paragraph~5. All these tensors have
$e^{-D}\operatorname{ch}(F)=r\operatorname{ch}(E)$,
so their full S14 character is explicitly verified.

\paragraph{1. The actual scalar projection on the original quotient algebra.}
Let
\[
 I\longrightarrow\mathcal O_P\xrightarrow{u}E
                  \xrightarrow{\partial}I[1]
 \tag{F1S31.5}
\]
be the actual ideal triangle. Precomposition with $u$ and
multiplication in the actual algebra $\mathcal O_Y$ give
\[
 r_2:\operatorname{Ext}^2_P(E,E)\longrightarrow
                           H^2(Y,\mathcal O_Y),\qquad
 s_2:H^2(Y,\mathcal O_Y)\longrightarrow
                           \operatorname{Ext}^2_P(E,E).
 \tag{F1S31.6}
\]
Their equality $r_2s_2=1$ is the unit identity.
Equivalently, S18 constructs it from
$\mathcal O_Y\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal O_P}\mathcal O_Y$,
whose multiplication has the section induced by $u$
in its second factor. Define the actual projector
\[
                  p_{\rm ns}=1-s_2r_2.
 \tag{F1S31.7}
\]
For an ambient scalar $\eta\in H^2(P,\mathcal O_P)$,
evaluation is $s_2(i^*\eta)$; hence this projector kills it.

Atiyah naturality at $u$, together with
$\operatorname{At}(\mathcal O_P)=0$, gives
\[
               \operatorname{ob}_E(\kappa)\,u=0.
 \tag{F1S31.8}
\]
Thus $r_2\operatorname{ob}_E(\kappa)=0$ and
$p_{\rm ns}\operatorname{ob}_E(\kappa)
=\operatorname{ob}_E(\kappa)$.
This is also the literal factorization
$\operatorname{ob}_E(\kappa)
=d^{\rm ideal}_2j\eta_Y(\kappa)$ through the ideal
boundary in S24. In particular (F1S31.7) preserves
the computed space $W_E$ and removes the scalar
terms through an actual global map.

\paragraph{2. Partial trace through the given ambient bundle.}
Use the evaluation and coevaluation of the actual
locally free bundle $N$. For
$\alpha:F\to F[m]$, define
\[
\begin{split}
 \operatorname{ptr}_N(\alpha):\quad
 E&\xrightarrow{1_E\otimes\operatorname{coev}_N}
                  E\otimes N\otimes N^\vee\\
  &\xrightarrow{\alpha\otimes1_{N^\vee}}
                  E[m]\otimes N\otimes N^\vee
   \xrightarrow{1_{E[m]}\otimes\operatorname{ev}_N}E[m].
\end{split}
 \tag{F1S31.9}
\]
Associativity identifies these tensors with the displayed
sources and targets. These are global derived morphisms;
no choice of a local splitting of $N$ enters their
definition. Since the ordinary trace of $1_N$ is $r$,
\[
 \operatorname{ptr}_N(\xi\otimes1_N)=r\xi,\qquad
 \operatorname{ptr}_N(1_E\otimes v)
                          =\operatorname{ev}_E(\operatorname{tr}_N v)
 \tag{F1S31.10}
\]
for $\xi\in\operatorname{Ext}^m_P(E,E)$ and
$v\in\operatorname{Ext}^m_P(N,N)$.
The second identity is precisely contraction of the
$N,N^\vee$ pair in (F1S31.9). Scalar multiplication
on $F$ similarly has partial trace $r$ times scalar
multiplication on $E$.

For completeness, the tensor Atiyah formula with the
fixed diagonal convention of S24 is
\[
 \operatorname{At}(E\otimes N)
   =\operatorname{At}(E)\otimes1_N
                +1_E\otimes\operatorname{At}(N),
 \tag{F1S31.11}
\]
with the usual symmetry moving the differential-form
factor to the right. This follows directly from the
first-jet Leibniz rule. One may check it on a bounded
locally free resolution of $E$: the tensor connection
is $\nabla_E\otimes1+1\otimes\nabla_N$; commutators with
the differential and the differences of the local
connections both satisfy the displayed sum. These
represent the global Atiyah classes on the same
resolution, so the formula remains valid for the
singular-support sheaf $E$.

Here is the sign calibration for the actual jet extension.
Write $\operatorname{At}_{\rm jet}$ for the class
denoted $\operatorname{At}$ in (F1S31.11) and in S24.
Use the first diagonal factor as input and the second
as output, with
$1\otimes f-f\otimes1\mapsto df$.
For a line with frames $e_j=g_{ij}e_i$, take the
output-linear local splitting $s_i(e_i)=e_i\otimes1$.
Then
\[
 s_j(e_i)=g_{ij}e_i\otimes g_{ij}^{-1},\qquad
 (s_j-s_i)(e_i)=-e_i\otimes d\log g_{ij}.
\]
We use the Cech differential $\delta s=s_j-s_i$ and
the first-Chern cocycle $d\log g_{ij}$. For a bundle,
the same matrix calculation, followed by trace,
gives $-\operatorname{tr}(G_{ij}^{-1}dG_{ij})$.
Consequently the precise identity is
\[
                     \operatorname{tr}\operatorname{At}_{\rm jet}(N)
                                      =-c_1(N).
 \tag{F1S31.12}
\]
This is also the sign in Buchweitz--Flenner:
their Proposition~4.1.7 identifies the actual
first-jet extension with their Atiyah class using
exactly $df=1\otimes f-f\otimes1$, and their
Atiyah--Chern character is
$\exp(-\operatorname{At}_{\rm jet})$.
By their Corollary~5.2.3, its degree-one component
therefore gives, for every perfect $M$,
\[
 \operatorname{ev}_M(\kappa)
       =-\kappa\mathbin{\lrcorner}\operatorname{At}_{\rm jet}(M)
       =-\operatorname{ob}_M(\kappa),
 \qquad
 \operatorname{ev}_M(\eta)=\eta\,1_M.
 \tag{F1S31.12a}
\]
All shifts in the first equality are the composition
$M\to M\otimes\Omega_P^1[1]\to M[2]$.
In particular the minus sign relates specified
morphisms, while the jet obstruction in S24 is
retained without alteration.
Thus (F1S31.9)--(F1S31.12a)
give the exact equality
\[
 \operatorname{ptr}_N
    \bigl(\operatorname{ev}_F(\kappa+\eta)\bigr)
 =-r\,\operatorname{ob}_E(\kappa)
   +\operatorname{ev}_E\bigl(
                    \kappa\mathbin{\lrcorner}c_1(N)+r\eta\bigr).
 \tag{F1S31.13}
\]
Applying the projector (F1S31.7) proves (F1S31.2).
Its specialization $\eta=0$ proves (F1S31.3).
No vanishing of the second term before projection
has been assumed.

\paragraph{3. The full S14 character gives the actual trace-zero image.}
For the given class $B$ in (F1S31.4), define the
explicit linear shear
\[
 a_B:\mathfrak D_P\longrightarrow HH^2(P),\qquad
 a_B(\kappa)=\kappa+
              \kappa\mathbin{\lrcorner}B
 \in H^1(P,T_P)\oplus H^2(P,\mathcal O_P).
 \tag{F1S31.14}
\]
The second summand acts by cup product, and the first
by the Kodaira--Spencer derivation $D_\kappa$.
S23 proves directly in the actual $K$-eigenbasis that
$D_\kappa$ annihilates $\beta$ and both determinant
classes. Hence it annihilates
$w_F=\lambda t+Q(\beta)$. The exact exponential identity is
\[
 \begin{split}
 a_B(\kappa)\mathbin{\lrcorner}\operatorname{ch}(F)
  &=\left(D_\kappa+
          (D_\kappa B)\wedge\right)(e^{-B}w_F)\\
  &=e^{-B}D_\kappa w_F=0.
 \end{split}
 \tag{F1S31.15}
\]
The equality differentiates the finite cohomological
exponential. It does not require an actual line bundle
with first Chern class $B$, or a tensor operation by
such a line.

Let
\[
            U_F=\operatorname{ev}_F\bigl(a_B(\mathfrak D_P)\bigr)
                                \subset\operatorname{Ext}^2_P(F,F).
 \tag{F1S31.16}
\]
The full Hochschild characteristic-trace identity gives
\[
              \sigma_F^2\operatorname{ev}_F(a_B(\kappa))
                    =a_B(\kappa)\mathbin{\lrcorner}\operatorname{ch}(F)=0.
 \tag{F1S31.17}
\]
This is the full trace to $HH_{-2}(P)$, with Todd
correction equal to one on the abelian variety.
The actual restriction of (F1S31.2) is the surjection
\[
 \left.-\frac1r\,p_{\rm ns}\operatorname{ptr}_N\right|_{U_F}:
                         U_F\twoheadrightarrow W_E,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{ev}_F(a_B(\kappa))\longmapsto
                                      \operatorname{ob}_E(\kappa).
 \tag{F1S31.18}
\]
It is well-defined because it is the restriction of
a specified linear map on the whole self-Ext group.
In particular $\dim U_F\ge16-r_C$.

This is a surjection onto the previously computed
normal-obstruction image. The tensor obstruction
need not vanish on every vector in
$\ker(\operatorname{ob}_E|_{\mathfrak D_P})$.
Consequently no descent of its source to
$(\ker\mu_C)^*$, or equality of its image dimension
with $16-r_C$, is asserted.

\paragraph{4. Every specified point lift retains the entire image.}
A point functor $t_x^*(-)\otimes M$, with
$M\in\operatorname{Pic}^0(P)$, acts identically on
$HH^2(P)$. In the invariant HKR model translation
fixes forms and vector fields, and the line-tensor
shear is zero because $\operatorname{At}(M)=0$.
Naturality of the characteristic action under any
given coherent lift of such a functor on $F$ therefore
fixes $\operatorname{ev}_F(a)$ for every $a\in HH^2(P)$.
Thus the whole $U_F$ lies in
\[
                \ker\sigma_F^2\cap
                         \operatorname{Ext}^2_P(F,F)^G
 \tag{F1S31.19}
\]
for every specified faithful finite point-action lift.
This step does not require the lift to preserve
$E,N$, their tensor presentation, or the projector.

R05 identifies this ordinary invariant full-trace
kernel with the weak equivariant kernel through
the actual forgetful map on Hochschild homology.
Its proof uses vanishing of all nonidentity point
sectors and the identity
$\operatorname{Forg}_*\operatorname{Ind}_*
=|G|\operatorname{id}$, so the forgetful map is an
isomorphism. Applying that exact comparison to
(F1S31.19) proves the stated failure. The sheaf $F$
is coherent and perfect on smooth $P$, with negative
self-Ext zero; this conclusion therefore concerns
the actual semiregularity requirement itself.

\paragraph{5. Concrete ambient bundles with the required whole character.}
Let $h:P'\to P$ be any actual finite isogeny and
$L$ any actual line bundle on $P'$. Put $N=h_*L$,
a vector bundle of rank $r=\deg h$.
There is a unique rational class $D$ with
$h^*D=c_1(L)$, since $h^*$ is a rational cohomology
isomorphism. It is an algebraic divisor class:
explicitly
\[
                      D=\frac1{\deg h}\,h_*c_1(L).
 \tag{F1S31.20}
\]
The proper pushforward here is the actual divisor
cycle pushforward. Translation acts trivially on
its cohomology class. Finite flat base change gives
\[
 h^*h_*L=\bigoplus_{k\in\ker h}t_k^*L,
 \qquad
 h^*\operatorname{ch}(h_*L)
       =\sum_{k\in\ker h}e^{c_1(t_k^*L)}
       =r e^{c_1(L)}.
 \tag{F1S31.21}
\]
Injectivity of $h^*$ proves the entire identity
\[
                          \operatorname{ch}(N)=r e^D.
 \tag{F1S31.22}
\]
These are actual semihomogeneous bundles: for $x\in P$
choose a lift $x'\in P'$. The line
$t_{x'}^*L\otimes L^{-1}$ lies in $\operatorname{Pic}^0(P')$.
The dual isogeny $h^*:\operatorname{Pic}^0(P)\to
\operatorname{Pic}^0(P')$ is surjective, so choose its
preimage $M$. Base change and projection formula then
give the actual isomorphism
$t_x^*h_*L\simeq h_*L\otimes M$.

More generally, let $N$ have an actual finite filtration
by subbundles with locally free quotients $N_j=h_{j*}L_j$
whose classes $D_j$ from (F1S31.20) are all the same
rational divisor class $D$. Coherent $K_0$ additivity
on those exact sequences gives
\[
 \operatorname{ch}(N)=\sum_j(\deg h_j)e^D=r e^D.
 \tag{F1S31.23}
\]
The condition is equality of rational divisor classes,
not equality of their numerical slopes for one ample
polarization. In particular this includes actual
filtrations by degree-zero lines, with $D=0$.

For each of these bundles the actual tensor product has
\[
                    e^{-D}\operatorname{ch}(E\otimes N)
                                   =r\,\operatorname{ch}(E).
 \tag{F1S31.24}
\]
The full right side is precisely the nonzero
determinant-plus-polarization character computed in S14.
Thus $B=-D$ verifies (F1S31.4) on every retained fibre.
Here an even stronger unprojected formula follows from
$c_1(N)=rD$:
\[
 \operatorname{ptr}_N
     \operatorname{ev}_{E\otimes N}
          \bigl(\kappa-\kappa\mathbin{\lrcorner}D\bigr)
                     =-r\,\operatorname{ob}_E(\kappa).
 \tag{F1S31.25}
\]
It proves the same lower bound without discarding
any term of the character or asserting
$\operatorname{At}(N)=D\,1_N$.
