\section[HD-F1-R23: common-image maps force a surface defect]{HD-F1-R23: the actual common-image evaluation has two or three rows, and a source or evaluation surface defect is necessary}
\label{sec:followup-rm-common-image-surface-defect}

Retain the actual positive-virtual-rank-one adjacent class
of R21, at endpoint ranks $(r_A,r_T)=(3,2)$, with
equal-slope semistable TF endpoints for an ample class
$H$. Apply its actual determinant twist, including the
specified conjugation of the coherent finite point
action. Thus
\[
 \operatorname{ch}(E)=v_q=(1-qA_1)(1-qA_2),\qquad
 C=A_1+A_2,\qquad q>0,
 \quad \mu_H(A)=\mu_H(T)=0.
 \tag{F1R23.1}
\]
The weakly semiregular gluable lift gives
$h=\dim\operatorname{Hom}_G(A,T)\ge2$ by R19.
Consider the actual common-image alternative of R21:
every such map factors through the specified saturated
rank-one subsheaf $I\subset T$. Put
\[
 W=\operatorname{Hom}_G(A,I)=\operatorname{Hom}_G(A,T),
 \qquad L=I^{**},\qquad Q=T/I.
 \tag{F1R23.2}
\]
The equality means the actual isomorphism induced by
the monomorphism $I\hookrightarrow T$ and its unique
factorizations. Both $I,Q$ are TF of rank one and
slope zero. We prove $h\in\{2,3\}$ and the following
strict defect restriction on the same objects.

When $h=2$, the actual evaluation on the source hull is
\[
 \Phi:A^{**}\longrightarrow W^*\otimes L=L^{\oplus2}.
 \tag{F1R23.3}
\]
Its kernel is a line bundle $M$, and its image $J$ has
quotient $Z_J=L^{\oplus2}/J$ of codimension at least
two. At least one of $A^{**}/A$ and $Z_J$ has a
nonzero codimension-two part. In particular, this
alternative is excluded when $A$ is reflexive and
the cokernel of (F1R23.3) has support of codimension
at least three. When $h=3$, the corresponding
$\Phi:A^{**}\to L^{\oplus3}$ is an actual equivariant
isomorphism, and $A^{**}/A$ itself must have a
nonzero codimension-two part. Thus a reflexive source
is excluded in that case. These assertions retain
the indicated surface cancellation in the remaining
TF cases.

\paragraph{1. The actual evaluation has generic rank equal to $h$.}
Each invariant row $A\to I\to L$ extends uniquely
to $A^{**}\to L$ by double dual. The passage is
injective on Hom, since a map to the TF target which
is zero generically is zero. The hull $A^{**}$ is
semistable of slope zero: for every subsheaf
$B\subset A^{**}$ its intersection $B\cap A$ has
the same rank and first Chern class, because the
hull quotient has codimension at least two. The
semistability inequality for $B\cap A$ is therefore
the required inequality for $B$.

Let $r$ be the dimension over $\mathbf C(X)$ of the
span of these rows. Choose $r$ independent rows and
form their actual morphism
\[
 \Phi_r:A^{**}\longrightarrow L^{\oplus r},\qquad
 J_r=\operatorname{im}\Phi_r,
 \qquad K_r=\ker\Phi_r.
 \tag{F1R23.4}
\]
Here $1\le r\le3$. The TF quotient $J_r$ of
$A^{**}$ has nonnegative degree against $H^3$:
for $r<3$ this follows by subtracting the degree of
the proper subsheaf $K_r$, and for $r=3$ the kernel
is zero and the degree is zero. On the other hand,
$J_r\subset L^{\oplus r}$ has the same rank as its
target. Its determinant quotient has an effective
divisor cycle $D_r$, and
\[
 \deg_H(J_r)=-D_rH^3\le0,
              \qquad \deg_H(L)=0.
 \tag{F1R23.5}
\]
Thus $D_r=0$. The actual inclusion is an isomorphism
outside codimension at least two, and its double
dual identifies $J_r^{**}=L^{\oplus r}$.

Every remaining row vanishes generically on $K_r$;
its target is TF, so it vanishes there everywhere.
It therefore factors through the actual quotient
$A^{**}\twoheadrightarrow J_r$. The resulting map
$J_r\to L$ extends uniquely to
$J_r^{**}=L^{\oplus r}\to L$. Such a map is a
constant row, since $H^0(X,\mathcal O_X)=\mathbf C$.
Consequently all rows are complex linear combinations
of the $r$ selected rows. This proves
\[
                            h=r\le3.
 \tag{F1R23.6}
\]
Since $h\ge2$, only the two stated values remain.
Taking an actual basis of $W$ gives the canonical
evaluation map in (F1R23.3), or its three-row version.
All rows are invariant. Kernels, images, quotients,
and double duals therefore carry their induced
coherent lifts, and the displayed maps are maps for
that very action. No replacement of the original
action by a linear automorphism action is made.

\paragraph{2. A product of two primitive divisor forms cannot dominate $qC$.}
We first prove the precise positivity fact needed
for the surface-cycle identity. Represent $H$ by its
translation-invariant positive Hermitian form, and
represent real divisor classes $m,\ell$ by their
unique invariant real $(1,1)$ forms. Suppose
\[
                         mH^3=\ell H^3=0.
 \tag{F1R23.7}
\]
The invariant $(2,2)$ form
\[
                                 m\ell-qC
 \tag{F1R23.8}
\]
is not weakly positive.

To prove this, choose an $H$-unitary basis
$e_1,\ldots,e_4$ diagonalizing $m$. Write its real
eigenvalues as $\lambda_i$, and write $\mu_i$ for
the diagonal entries of the Hermitian matrix of
$\ell$ in that same basis. The primitive equations
are exactly
\[
                       \sum_i\lambda_i=\sum_i\mu_i=0.
 \tag{F1R23.9}
\]
Normalize evaluation on a complex coordinate
two-plane by its positive volume form. The value
of $m\ell$ on the $(e_i,e_j)$ plane is
$\lambda_i\mu_j+\lambda_j\mu_i$. Let $c_{ij}$
be the value of $C$ in this normalization. Since
each $\theta_i$ is semipositive on its own
two-dimensional RM block,
\[
                 c_{ij}\ge0,\qquad
                 \sum_{j\ne i}c_{ij}>0\quad\text{for every }i.
 \tag{F1R23.10}
\]
For the strict assertion, fix any nonzero vector
$e_i$. Its projection to at least one RM block is
nonzero. Choose a vector in that block independent
of the projection. The corresponding Hermitian
Gram determinant of $\theta_a$ is positive, so
$C(e_i,v)>0$ for that vector $v$. The form
$v\mapsto C(e_i,v)$ is positive semidefinite;
its trace in the chosen unitary basis is therefore
strictly positive. This trace is the sum in
(F1R23.10), and the $j=i$ term is zero.

Weak positivity of (F1R23.8) would imply, for every
$i\ne j$,
\[
                 \lambda_i\mu_j+\lambda_j\mu_i\ge q c_{ij}.
 \tag{F1R23.11}
\]
Summing over $j\ne i$ and using (F1R23.9) gives
\[
             -2\lambda_i\mu_i
                    \ge q\sum_{j\ne i}c_{ij}>0.
 \tag{F1R23.12}
\]
Thus every $\lambda_i$ is nonzero and $\mu_i$
has its opposite sign. Among four nonzero real
$\lambda_i$ there are two of the same sign. For
that pair both terms on the left of (F1R23.11)
are negative, whereas its right side is nonnegative.
This is the contradiction.

Here is the actual cycle-to-form map used with this
fact. For an effective surface cycle $D$ on $X$,
take its closed positive integration current and
average its translated pullbacks against Haar
probability measure on the compact torus:
\[
                 [D]_{\rm inv}
                       =\int_{x\in X}t_x^*[D]_{\rm current}\,d\mu(x).
 \tag{F1R23.13}
\]
The result is a translation-invariant positive
current, hence a constant-coefficient smooth
weakly positive $(2,2)$ form. Translations act
as the identity on de Rham cohomology, so it
represents the actual cycle class of $D$.
Invariant forms represent the exterior cohomology
of a torus injectively; therefore (F1R23.13)
is the unique invariant representative of that
class. In particular no effective surface cycle
can have class (F1R23.8). This conclusion uses
the displayed averaging morphism and the proved
Hermitian inequalities, rather than an unspecified
positivity comparison.

\paragraph{3. Two rows: the exact four quotient cycles.}
Suppose $h=2$, and write $J=\operatorname{im}\Phi$.
Its kernel $M$ is reflexive: in
$0\to M\to A^{**}\to J\to0$, the middle sheaf
is reflexive and the last is TF. Locally the depth
lemma gives the $(S_2)$ condition for $M$, and
normality identifies TF plus $(S_2)$ with
reflexivity. A rank-one reflexive sheaf on the
smooth factorial variety is a line bundle.
Degrees in this sequence give $\mu_H(M)=0$.
Define the four actual defect sheaves
\[
 Z_A=A^{**}/A,\quad Z_J=L^{\oplus2}/J,
       \quad Z_I=L/I,\quad Z_Q=Q^{**}/Q.
 \tag{F1R23.14}
\]
Each has support of codimension at least two.
Put $m=c_1(M)$, $\ell=c_1(L)$, and let $D_*$
denote the effective codimension-two support cycle
of $Z_*$, with its actual generic lengths.
The determinant equality from (F1R23.1) gives
\[
 c_1(A)=m+2\ell=c_1(T)=\ell+c_1(Q),
                         \qquad c_1(Q)=m+\ell.
 \tag{F1R23.15}
\]
The actual short exact sequences just displayed
give the complete $K_0$ character identities
\[
 \begin{split}
 \operatorname{ch}(A)&=e^m+2e^\ell
                    -\operatorname{ch}(Z_J)-\operatorname{ch}(Z_A),\\
 \operatorname{ch}(T)&=e^\ell+e^{m+\ell}
                    -\operatorname{ch}(Z_I)-\operatorname{ch}(Z_Q).
 \end{split}
 \tag{F1R23.16}
\]
In degree two, their difference and (F1R23.1) give
\[
                  qC=m\ell+D_A+D_J-D_I-D_Q.
 \tag{F1R23.17}
\]
Both $m$ and $\ell$ satisfy (F1R23.7).
If $D_A=D_J=0$, (F1R23.17) says that
$m\ell-qC=D_I+D_Q$ is the class of an actual
effective surface cycle, including the possible
zero cycle. Its invariant representative would
be weakly positive by (F1R23.13), contrary to
paragraph2. Hence
\[
                              D_A+D_J\ne0.
 \tag{F1R23.18}
\]
This is the asserted surface defect of the actual
source or its actual two-row evaluation. It does
not assert that the four-cycle cancellation in
(F1R23.17) is impossible when that defect occurs.

\paragraph{4. Three rows: the source hull and a positive defect degree.}
Suppose $h=3$. The map $\Phi$ has zero kernel and
is an isomorphism outside codimension at least
two by paragraph1. Both $A^{**}$ and $L^{\oplus3}$
are reflexive. Restriction to this big open set
and the actual inverse pushforward extend the
isomorphism and its inverse globally. Thus
\[
                    \Phi:A^{**}\xrightarrow{\sim}L^{\oplus3}
 \tag{F1R23.19}
\]
is the asserted equivariant isomorphism. Now
$c_1(Q)=2\ell$, and the actual hull and quotient
sequences give
\[
           \operatorname{ch}(A)=3e^\ell-\operatorname{ch}(Z_A),
 \qquad
           \operatorname{ch}(T)=e^\ell+e^{2\ell}
                            -\operatorname{ch}(Z_I)-\operatorname{ch}(Z_Q).
 \tag{F1R23.20}
\]
In particular
\[
                  D_A=D_I+D_Q-\ell^2+qC.
 \tag{F1R23.21}
\]
The invariant Hermitian trace formula and
$\ell H^3=0$ give $\ell^2H^2\le0$:
in an $H$-unitary diagonalization it is a positive
normalization times
$(\sum_i\mu_i)^2-\sum_i\mu_i^2=-\sum_i\mu_i^2$.
Effective cycles have nonnegative degree against
$H^2$, while $CH^2>0$ by (F1R23.10). Therefore
\[
                       D_AH^2\ge qCH^2>0.
 \tag{F1R23.22}
\]
The source hull quotient has a nonzero surface
part, as asserted. In fact the original rows
factor through $I$, so (F1R23.19) also gives the
actual inclusion $A\hookrightarrow I^{\oplus3}$;
its quotient $R$ has codimension at least two and
$D_A=3D_I+[R]_2$. This additional identity retains
the same source and the same factor maps.

\paragraph{Framework and remaining scope.}
The only use of weak semiregularity here is the
proved lower bound $h\ge2$ at this adjacent gate.
The image and quotient maps then act on the actual
coherent sheaves. Their characters are computed
by the actual exact-sequence map to $K_0(X)$;
the leading codimension-two Chern character is
the support cycle with its generic lengths, as
in DEG\_L S-22. The point-functor action commutes
with kernels, saturation and double dual through
its exact pullback and line-tensor maps. Thus no
unrelated equivariant structure or derived dual
with extra cohomology is substituted.

The reflexive kernel and extension maps are the
ones in the Stacks Project, Lemmas31.13.7,
31.13.12--31.13.13, with their local algebra
proofs. The positivity step is supplied by the
explicit integration-current average and the
complete four-dimensional Hermitian calculation
above. The precise published
cycle-to-invariant-form positivity statement is
Debarre--Ein--Lazarsfeld--Voisin,
\emph{Pseudoeffective and nef classes on abelian
varieties}, Compositio Math.~147 (2011),
Definition~1.1, Remark~1.3 and Lemma~1.5;
the complete proof of that lemma was checked.
The result excludes the indicated absence
of a source surface defect. It preserves both
the actual coupling class and every surviving
surface term; it neither constructs a weak seed
nor excludes the remaining surface-cancellation
profiles.
