## 1. The finite algorithm

All calculations use rational arithmetic in the free associative algebra truncated by arithmetic weight, with one letter in each odd weight at least 3. Thus the displayed exponential, logarithm and conjugation sums are finite and exact. The standard Lyndon factorization gives the Hall brackets. Their associative leading words are distinct and have coefficient one, so triangular subtraction recovers their Lie coordinates; the code asserts that the remainder is zero on each use.

The map D is weight scaling by 5 followed by Ad(exp(-a/2)), with a the sum of the odd letters. Its inverse is weight scaling by 1/5 after Ad(exp(a/2)); the added implementation has this order exactly. Every elimination power q has nonnegative 2-adic valuation. Every normalization power has valuation zero. Consequently these are legitimate Z_2 powers in any closed unipotent pro-2 subgroup; they do not adjoin square roots merely because the ambient algebra is rational.

The mutation phase constructs every new basis element from elements already in the closed Gamma-orbit group, using D, commutators and Z_2 powers. A replacement retains the former generator by reducing it against the new one and any later pivots. No termination theorem for arbitrary inputs is needed: this run stops, and the final basis is independently checked for membership of h, D of each basis element, inverse D of each basis element, and every potentially nonzero pairwise commutator.

## 2. Why the finite membership checks certify a compact subgroup

Write g_i=exp(b_i) for the ordered final basis and let

    C_i = g_i^(Z_2) g_(i+1)^(Z_2) ... g_r^(Z_2).

These sets are compact by continuity. A commutator of two basis elements has strictly greater arithmetic weight than either of them, so its zero reduction expresses it entirely in later basis elements. Descending induction proves that C_i is a subgroup. Suppose C_(i+1) is a subgroup. The checked commutators imply that conjugation by g_i sends every later generator into C_(i+1), hence sends the whole compact subgroup into itself. Conjugation by g_i^(-1) also preserves it: g_i^(2^n-1) tends to g_i^(-1), and every nonnegative power already preserves the closed tail. The same continuity argument works for all exponents in Z_2. Therefore g_i normalizes C_(i+1), and C_i is a compact subgroup.

The complete product C=C_1 contains h and is stable under D and inverse D by the final membership checks. It therefore contains the entire continuous Gamma orbit and its closed generated subgroup H_W. Conversely, each basis element was constructed in H_W, so C is contained in H_W. Thus C=H_W, rather than merely a lattice containing some tested words.

Triangular leading coordinates make the ordered product parametrization injective. In particular, an element has arithmetic weight at least n exactly when all its coordinates of weight below n vanish. At weight n its associated graded image is the Z_2-span of the weight-n parts of the b_i whose pivots have weight n. This proves that the computed actual rows give the filtered compact group's complete graded lattice.

The odd Hall rows lie in this lattice because a filtered group's graded object is a Lie algebra and the selected odd elements belong to it. Equal determinant valuations therefore imply equality. The independent two-way integral matrix check also verifies this equality without relying only on the determinant calculation.

## 3. Rational normal form and the arithmetic compact image

## 4. Closing the exact pro-2 versus rational-filtration comparison

Hain--Matsumoto Proposition 8.2 alone gives finite kernel and cokernel on the graded comparison. That statement was reread in full and is not silently strengthened. For the free geometric fundamental group, the following additional elementary argument upgrades it to equality of filtrations.

Let Pi be a finitely generated free pro-p group of rank at least two, Gamma_n its closed lower central series, and L_n=Gamma_n/Gamma_(n+1). The primary input is Koch--Kukkuk--Labute, *Nilpotent local class field theory*, Theorem 2.1: gr(Pi) is the free Lie algebra over Z_p on Pi_ab, for every prime p. The complete statement and proof were freshly read in the author PDF, pages 4--5; the journal locator is Acta Arithmetica 83 (1998), 45--64, pages 48--49. Author URL: https://www.math.mcgill.ca/labute/papers/kkl.pdf .

Put A_n=ker[Aut(Pi)->Aut(Pi/Gamma_(n+1))], n>=1. The degree-n Johnson map sends alpha to the homomorphism

    Pi_ab -> L_(n+1),    x -> alpha(x)x^(-1) modulo Gamma_(n+2).

The commutator identities show that this is Z_p-linear, additive in alpha, and has kernel A_(n+1). Thus A_n/A_(n+1) injects into Hom_Zp(L_1,L_(n+1)).

Let O_n=ker[Out(Pi)->Out(Pi/Gamma_(n+1))]. Every class in O_n has a representative in A_n: lift the element of Pi/Gamma_(n+1) giving its inner action and compose by the inverse of that integral inner automorphism. Moreover an inner conjugation by g lies in A_n exactly when g lies in Gamma_n. For the nontrivial direction, a first nonzero degree k<n of g would centralize every degree-one generator in the free Lie algebra, which is impossible.

For completeness, the free Lie algebra over any field has zero center in positive degree. Embed it in the free associative algebra. A polynomial commuting with one free letter X contains only powers of X: compare coefficients and move all leading X's to the right to see that every word containing a different letter has zero coefficient. Commuting with a second independent letter then forces the polynomial to be scalar, and a positive-degree scalar is zero. The same conclusion over Z_p follows by tensoring with Q_p.

It follows that O_n/O_(n+1) injects into

    Hom_Zp(L_1,L_(n+1)) / ad(L_n).

Indeed the ambiguity in an A_n representative is exactly an inner element of Gamma_n, and if its Johnson class is ad(z), choose a lift of z in Gamma_n and adjust it to kill that class. The submodule ad(L_n) is primitive: reduction modulo p has zero kernel because the free Lie algebra over F_p has zero center. More explicitly, if ad(z) is divisible by p, then z is divisible by p; dividing and iterating proves that the displayed quotient has no p-torsion. Hence every positive outer graded quotient is torsion-free, and so are the graded quotients of any arithmetic subgroup with the induced filtration.

Let I^n be the pro-p outer-action filtration and J^n its rational pullback. They agree at n=1, since identity on integral abelianization is equivalent to identity after tensoring with Q_p. Suppose I^n=J^n. By Hain--Matsumoto Proposition 8.2 the map gr_I^n to gr_J^n has finite kernel; its source is torsion-free by the preceding argument, so that kernel is zero. Every element of J^(n+1) belongs to I^n and maps into this kernel, hence belongs to I^(n+1). The reverse inclusion is known. Induction proves I^n=J^n for all n, including p=2.

This supplies the exact lattice identification required for the finite arithmetic conclusion. It does not assume a general torsion-freeness statement for fundamental groups of arbitrary varieties.
