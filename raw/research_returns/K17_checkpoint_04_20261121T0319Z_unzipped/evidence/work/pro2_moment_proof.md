# A checked deduction for the pro-2 tripod kernel

**Status, 21 November 2026.** This is a proof developed and checked in this run from the cited published results. It is not presented as a theorem located in the existing literature or as a consequence of Alpöge's step 17. The initiating researcher and two other GPT-6 Astra Pro agents each read the complete argument; the parent researcher also read it. Their checks found no remaining mathematical gap. ## Statement and conventions

Let `X=P^1_Q - {0,1,infinity}`, let `Pi=pi_1^et(X_Qbar)^(2)`, and let

`rho_2:G_Q -> Out(Pi)`.

Let `Omega_2` be the maximal pro-2 extension of Q unramified at every finite prime other than 2, allowing the real place to complexify. Let `Omega_2^*` be the fixed field of `ker rho_2`. Then

`Omega_2^* = Omega_2`.

Equivalently, the induced representation of `P=Gal(Omega_2/Q)` is faithful. The convention at infinity matters: replacing Omega_2 by its maximal totally real subextension would give a different assertion.

The argument proves faithfulness using a rational realization, but retains the full pro-2 topology on the source. It does not assert that the integral weight-graded Galois Lie algebra is freely generated by odd Soulé elements.

## 1. Published inputs

**Arithmetic group.** Gras–Jaulent, *Note on 2-rational fields*, J. Number Theory129 (2009),495–498, arXiv:0805.1168, Theorem/Definition0(iv) pp1–2 and Theorem2 p3: for Q the unrestricted-at-infinity maximal 2-ramified group is `C_2 * Z_2`. To check the Q specialization directly, an element of Q× with even valuations away from 2 is `epsilon 2^e y^2`, with `epsilon=+/-1` and `e=0 or1`. If it is a square in Q_2, valuation forces `e=0` and nonsquareness of -1 forces `epsilon=1`. The global and local 2-primary root-of-unity groups both have rank1. Their criterion therefore applies. Kronecker–Weber identifies its abelianization with the cyclotomic character `chi:P->Z_2^×`.

**Rational Galois image.** Hain–Matsumoto, *Weighted completion of Galois groups and Galois actions on the fundamental group of P^1-{0,1,infinity}*, arXiv:math/0006158v2, §8.3/Proposition8.3 pp25–26, Theorem8.4 p26, Lemma8.5 and Remark8.6 p27, identifies the prounipotent radical `U` of the actual rational outer Galois-image closure, the density of the cyclotomic kernel in U, and its weight-graded Lie algebra. Their AppendixA.6,A.9,A.10 gives the functorial rational outer action induced by the pro-2 action. Brown's rational Deligne–Ihara theorem says that this graded Lie algebra is free on one generator in each odd weight `m>=3`. A direct primary account of this precise outer-action formulation for every prime is Brown, *From the Deligne–Ihara conjecture to multiple modular values*, arXiv:1904.00179, §2.1 Conjecture1 p2 and §2.3 Theorem2.1 and its following paragraph p4. The proof is Brown's *Mixed Tate motives over Z*, Annals175(2012),949–976, Theorem1.1 and its consequence p950.

**Magnus embedding.** For a finite-rank free pro-p group, `g_i |-> 1+X_i` is an injective continuous Magnus map into noncommutative power series over F_p. Mináč–Pasini–Quadrelli–Nguyễn Duy Tân, *Koszul algebras and quadratic duals in Galois cohomology*, arXiv:1808.01695v3, §5.1 p21 equation(5.1), states this for every p. The integral Z_p-valued Magnus map is consequently injective, since its mod-p reduction is the cited injection.

The URLs and precise retrieval references are collected at the end. These are cited inputs, not claims that this run rederived Brown's or the arithmetic structure theorem's proofs.

## 2. The cyclotomic kernel as a limit of finite-rank free groups

Write `P=<c>*<gamma>`, where `c^2=1` and `<gamma>~=Z_2`. The cyclotomic character maps c to -1. Replace gamma by `c gamma` if necessary so that

`u=chi(gamma) in 1+4Z_2`.

This replacement is an automorphism of the free product. Since chi is the abelianization, u topologically generates `1+4Z_2`.

Let `P_i=ker(chi mod4)`. It is free pro-2 on `gamma` and `c gamma c`. One can verify this directly: the semidirect product of the free pro-2 group on two symbols a,b with the involution exchanging them has the universal property of `C_2*Z_2`, with `a=gamma,b=c gamma c`. Its index-2 kernel is the asserted free group. Put

`h=(c gamma c) gamma^(-1)` and `H=ker chi`.

The pair `(gamma,h)` is another free pro-2 basis of P_i. Thus H is the kernel of `P_i -> Z_2` sending gamma to 1 and h to 0. Write

`h_x=gamma^x h gamma^(-x)` for `x in Z_2`.

For `n>=1`, let N_n be the closed normal subgroup of P_i generated by `gamma^(2^n)`. Then

`P_i/N_n ~= C_(2^n)*Z_2`.

The kernel H_n of its map to `C_(2^n)` is free pro-2 on the `2^n` generators `h_i`, `i mod2^n`. Again this follows directly from a universal property: the cyclic permutation action on the free pro-2 group on those symbols produces a semidirect product with the universal property of `C_(2^n)*Z_2`.

The intersection of the N_n is trivial. Indeed, in any finite 2-group quotient of P_i, gamma has finite 2-power order, so N_n lies in the kernel for all sufficiently large n. Compactness then gives

`P_i ~= lim_n P_i/N_n`.

Surjectivity here follows because a compatible family of quotient cosets is a nested family of nonempty closed subsets of the compact group P_i. Taking the kernel of the map to `lim C_(2^n)=Z_2` yields

`H ~= lim_n H_n`.                                                    (1)

The transition maps fold the finite bases according to reduction modulo `2^n`. The h_x topologically generate H. Indeed, the kernels of `H->H_n` decrease to1; compactness shows that every open normal subgroup of H contains one of these kernels. Every finite quotient of H therefore factors through an H_n, where the images of the h_x generate.

The finite-rank Magnus injection also has a short direct verification. For a finite 2-group Q, the augmentation ideal of F_2[Q] is nilpotent: choose a central element z of order2, use `(z-1)^2=0`, and induct on Q/<z>. A map from a finite-rank free pro-2 group to Q therefore permits substitution `X_i -> image(g_i)-1` in its mod-2 Magnus series. That substitution recovers the given quotient map. An element with trivial Magnus expansion is thus trivial in every finite 2-group quotient, hence trivial. Reduction modulo2 then proves injectivity of the integral map as well.

Apply the integral Magnus map to each H_n. Their degree-r coefficients are compatible under the folding maps and thus lie in

`M_r=lim_n Z_2[(Z/2^n)^r]=Z_2[[Z_2^r]]`.

This is the module of bounded Z_2-valued measures on `Z_2^r`. The resulting map

`theta:H -> 1+product_(r>=1) M_r`,

with `theta(h_x)=1+delta_x`, is injective by the finite-rank Magnus theorem and (1). In particular, for every nontrivial g in H, theta(g)-1 has a **first finite nonzero homogeneous degree**. Completion at `(2,I)` does not invalidate this statement: the completed algebra is still the product of its homogeneous coefficient modules, and the zero element is the element with all coefficients zero.

## 3. The actual rational image and its completed free Lie algebra

Let B be the proalgebraic closure over Q_2 of the rational outer image induced from the tripod action. It is a negatively weighted extension

`1 -> U -> B -> G_m ->1`,

and the map on the reductive quotient is chi. The induced continuous map

`rho_U:H -> U(Q_2)`                                                (2)

has Zariski-dense image by Hain–Matsumoto Theorem8.4. The original pro-2 representation factors through P by the following direct argument, which does not invoke an odd-prime formulation of Ihara's theorem.

The geometric group Pi is free pro-2 of rank2, and its Frattini quotient is F_2(1)^2. The Galois action on that quotient is trivial. For a finite 2-group Q, the kernel of Aut(Q)->Aut(Q/Phi(Q)) is a 2-group: fix a basis of its Frattini quotient of size d; its |Phi(Q)|^d lift tuples all generate Q, and the automorphism kernel acts freely on this set. Its order divides a power of2. The characteristic finite quotients of Pi given by its descending Frattini series are cofinal. Passing to their automorphism kernels shows that the corresponding kernel for Aut(Pi) is pro-2. Inner automorphisms belong to this kernel, so the kernel for Out(Pi) is also pro-2. Thus the Galois outer image is pro-2.

At an odd prime, the marked curve (P^1,{0,1,infinity}) has good reduction with disjoint sections. Prime-to-residue-characteristic specialization therefore makes inertia act trivially on its outer pro-2 fundamental group. The all-primes factorization is also recalled in Hain–Matsumoto §8.4 p27. Hence the image is unramified outside2 and factors through P. In particular (2) is an actual representation of H, rather than an assumed faithful motivic realization.

Use positive arithmetic weights, so Brown's theorem gives

`gr Lie(U) ~= FreeLie_Q2(s_3,s_5,s_7,...)`, `weight(s_m)=m`.

Here is the precise completion step. If a complete separated positively filtered Lie algebra has a graded Lie algebra free on a graded vector space with finitely many generators below any given weight, choose lifts of the graded free generators. The universal property gives a continuous map from the weight-completed free Lie algebra to the original filtered algebra. It induces an isomorphism on associated graded objects. Induction on finite weight truncations, using the short exact sequences for successive filtration steps, makes it an isomorphism on every truncation. Taking inverse limits proves it is an isomorphism on the completed algebras.

For compatibility with the G_m action, use a Levi splitting of B (Hain–Matsumoto Lemma3.7 p9, or its inverse-limit version) and choose weight-homogeneous lifts. Its completed universal enveloping algebra is then the weight-completed free associative algebra on the symbols s_m. Every finite weight quotient is finite-dimensional. We shall use **generator length** in this algebra; it is distinct from weight and from the geometric depth filtration on the tripod.

Let `ell_m:U(Q_2)->Q_2(m)` be the m-th coordinate of its abelianization. Conjugation by gamma acts on this coordinate by `u^m`; a unipotent part of its lift acts trivially on abelianization. Set

`a_m=ell_m(rho_U(h))`.

For every x in Z_2,

`ell_m(rho_U(h_x))=a_m u^(mx)`.                                   (3)

Every a_m is nonzero: otherwise ell_m would vanish on all h_x and hence, by continuity and topological generation, on H. This contradicts density in U and the nonzero m-th abelian quotient of U. This step uses only nonzero rational coordinates. It makes no integral primitivity assertion about Soulé characters.

## 4. Lowest Magnus degree produces vanishing moments

Suppose `g in H`, `rho_U(g)=1`, but `g!=1`. Let r be the first nonzero Magnus degree of theta(g)-1, and write

`mu=mu_r(g) in Z_2[[Z_2^r]]`, `mu!=0`.

In the completed universal enveloping algebra, put `Y_x=rho_U(h_x)-1`. It has zero constant term. Its generator-length-one part, by (3), is

`sum_(m odd>=3) a_m u^(mx) s_m`.

There is a well-defined substitution `delta_x |-> Y_x` on the completed tensor algebra of measure coefficients. To see this rigorously, truncate the target at weight N. Only terms of Magnus degree at most `floor(N/3)` can contribute. Every remaining coefficient is an integral of a bounded continuous Q_2-valued function on a finite product of the compact space Z_2. Such integrals against bounded Z_2-valued measures are defined by uniform approximation by locally constant functions. They are continuous in the measures. Fubini's formula and the multiplicativity of this substitution follow first for locally constant functions and then by the same uniform approximation. No integral lattice uniform in all weights is required.

On each h_x, the substitution sends theta(h_x) to rho_U(h_x). Both sides are continuous homomorphisms in each finite weight quotient, so topological generation of H makes them agree on all of H. This avoids any use of a free-pro-2 universal property with the noncompact target U(Q_2).

Since all Magnus degrees below r vanish, the coefficient of the ordered length-r word `s_(m_1)...s_(m_r)` in rho_U(g)-1 is exactly

`(product_j a_(m_j)) * integral_(Z_2^r) product_j u^(m_j x_j) dmu(x)`.

Terms of higher Magnus degree have generator length greater than r; terms of lower Magnus degree are zero. Thus, because rho_U(g)=1 and the a_m are nonzero,

`integral product_j u^(m_j x_j) dmu(x)=0`                          (4)

for all independent choices of odd integers `m_j>=3`. The ordered variables have been retained; no symmetrization is involved.

## 5. An analytic identity theorem forces the measure to vanish

Consider the Amice transform

`A_mu(T_1,...,T_r)=integral product_j (1+T_j)^(x_j) dmu(x)`.

Its binomial-coefficient expansion lies in `Z_2[[T_1,...,T_r]]`, and it converges when every `|T_j|_2<1`. The transform is injective: the products of binomial polynomials are the multivariate Mahler basis of continuous functions on `Z_2^r`; their integrals, the coefficients of A_mu, determine the measure. Equivalently this is the canonical Iwasawa-algebra isomorphism `Z_2[[Z_2^r]]~=Z_2[[T_1,...,T_r]]`.

Put `t_m=u^m-1` for odd m at least3. These points lie in `4Z_2`. The distinct points `t_(1+2^j)` converge to `u-1`, an interior point of the open unit disc. They are distinct because u has infinite order. By (4), A_mu vanishes on every Cartesian grid point `(t_(m_1),...,t_(m_r))`.

The one-variable nonarchimedean identity theorem applies: a power series with bounded coefficients converges on the radius-1 disc, and its restriction to the closed radius-1/2 disc is a restricted power series, which cannot have an interior accumulation of zeros unless it is zero. Fixing grid values in all but one variable, apply that theorem; then apply it successively in each remaining variable. It follows that A_mu is identically zero. Hence mu=0, contradicting its choice.

Therefore rho_U is injective on H. The kernel of the pro-2 tripod action lies in H, since its action on abelianization records chi. Every element trivial under the pro-2 outer action is trivial under the induced rational outer action, and hence under rho_U. It is therefore 1. This proves `Omega_2^*=Omega_2`.

## 6. Scope of the conclusion

This argument uses the special explicit arithmetic source `C_2*Z_2`, together with all odd rational generators, to recover each integral Magnus coefficient as a bounded measure. The regular-prime proof of Sharifi does not itself include2; this is a separate deduction that deals with the involution by passing to Q(i) and then returning to the cyclotomic kernel. No conclusion about integral odd free generation follows from this proof. It also does not transfer to irregular primes merely from the abstract freeness of a cyclotomic-kernel group: the explicit Gamma-equivariant basis and its measure coordinates are essential, and the relevant Iwasawa module can have additional torsion components.
