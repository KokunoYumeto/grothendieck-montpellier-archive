## Mathematical checks

### 1. Base Vandiver really gives Xplus=0

The proof does not assume a possibly misstated control isomorphism. In a cyclic p-extension L/K with one ramified finite prime and Cl(K){p}=0, the Hilbert p-class field E/L is characteristic and hence Galois over K. Its Galois group G is a finite p-group. Inertia I at the sole ramified prime has order p because L/K is totally ramified there and E/L is unramified. Quotient by the normal closure of I is everywhere unramified over K. A nontrivial finite p-group has a nontrivial abelian p quotient, contradicting the zero p-class group of K; hence G is normally generated by I. The Frattini quotient is then generated by one element. A finite p-group with this property is cyclic, and the normal closure of I in a cyclic group is I itself, giving G=I and E=L. Odd p introduces no ramification at real places. Apply this to every successive real cyclotomic layer. Norm and extension across the quadratic CM extension are inverse up to2 on the plus p-part, so all complex plus p-class groups, and their inverse limit, are zero.

### 2. The cyclicity hypothesis is supplied with the correct reflected character

For each relevant even theta, chi=omega^-1 theta is odd. Set theta'=omega²theta^-1=omega chi^-1; its associated chi' is chi^-1. The excluded pair {1,omega²} is stable under this reflection. Vandiver gives both X_theta=0 and X_theta'=0. WWE Proposition2.2.6 applied to theta' makes frakX_chi^#(1) rank-one free, not X_chi itself. Undoing inversion and twist transports the Iwasawa algebra by automorphisms and preserves rank-one freeness. The quotient map frakX_chi -> X_chi then supplies cyclicity of X_chi. This checks both hypotheses of Corollary5.1.2; no unsupported inference from a characteristic polynomial to cyclicity occurs.

### 3. The theorem is integral even with repeated roots

Under C(h), Q_Q is rank-one over A=Lambda/(xi)[1/p]. Consequently the endomorphism1-Upsilon varpi is a scalar a in A. The derivative relation xi'a=0 and FK Lemma10.2.2 make a nilpotent. Torsionfreeness of Q lifts that nilpotence to the integral module; the finite geometric sum in1-f is an integral inverse to f. After adjusting Upsilon by f^-1, varpi splits. The characteristic/Fitting-ideal argument in FK §7.1.3 leaves only a finite complementary submodule in the p-torsionfree unramified Iwasawa module, hence zero. This is stronger than a finite-index rational isomorphism. Simple roots are required for the stronger normalization that the two original maps are mutual inverses, not for the isomorphism/surjectivity used here.

### 4. Finite-level coefficients and all character components

FK §7.1.10 explicitly passes integral varpi surjectivity to finite-level cyclotomic-symbol generation. At level1, the target is (Cl(F1)/p) tensor mu_p. Its theta character selects the class-group character chi=omega^-1 theta. The excluded theta1 andomega² select chi=omega^-1 andomega, which vanish by the cited Stickelberger fact. All odd theta target components are zero under Vandiver, because twisting by mu_p changes the zero even class-group part to odd parity. Therefore the collected even-character results generate the entire target, rather than only a rational subspace or one eigenspace.

### 5. Exact odd-H1 cup hypothesis

The separately audited parity lemma applies: modulo p, minus p-units form the root-of-unity line; self-cups on that line vanish by alternatingness for odd p, and mixed parity has zero target. Thus full p-unit cup surjectivity reduces to plus Kummer classes. Untwisting by Fp(1) gives the odd-character summand of H1(P,Fp). This is the exact hypothesis in the already audited general theorem. The comparison from full etale/Galois cohomology to the p-ramified pro-p presentation is the same checked one used in that lemma. At p=3 the target is zero, so the cup hypothesis is vacuous.
