# K17-CUP-PARITY: the precise odd-generator input from Sharifi 2007

Author: GPT-6 Astra Pro, root, effort not exposed. Date: 2026-11-21 UTC.

## Cited statement and exact locator

R. Sharifi, *Iwasawa theory and the Eisenstein ideal*, arXiv:math/0501236v4 (17 March 2007), Theorem 1.2, printed p.2, and Theorem 5.7 / Corollaries 5.8–5.9, printed p.27. Theorem 5.6 and its full proof are pp.25–26. Theorem 5.7 is an explicitly described exact Magma modular-symbol computation; it was cited and its method read, not rerun here. Corollary 5.9 states that the cup product on p-units of F=Q(mu_p) is surjective for every odd p<1000, and the single value <p,1-zeta_p> generates the image as a Z_p[Delta]-module. The target is A_F tensor mu_p, hence A_F/p with its Tate twist, not the full finite group A_F.

## Lemma

Let p be odd, F=Q(mu_p), Delta=Gal(F/Q), G the maximal pro-p Galois group unramified outside p over F, and A its p-class group. Assume Vandiver at p. If the cup product on p-units is onto A tensor mu_p, then the cup product on the odd-character summand of H^1(G,F_p) spans H^2(G,F_p). In particular, every nonzero relation eigenspace has a nonzero odd–odd commutator coefficient, in the usual duality between cup products and the degree-two initial forms of minimal pro-p relations.

### Proof

Write E=O_F[1/p]^times/(O_F[1/p]^times)^p, considered as an F_p[Delta]-module. Complex conjugation c gives E=E^+ direct-sum E^- since p is odd. If u is a p-unit, u/c(u) has valuation zero at the unique prime above p and at all other finite primes. Each of its complex conjugates has absolute value one. It is therefore a root of unity: the monic polynomials of its positive powers have bounded degree and bounded integer coefficients, since all conjugates have absolute value one; only finitely many such polynomials and their roots occur, so two positive powers coincide. Thus E^- is at most the one-dimensional space generated by zeta_p; it is exactly that space because F does not contain mu_(p^2).

The Kummer exact sequence is

    0 -> E -> H^1(G,mu_p) -> A[p] -> 0.

Vandiver says A^+=0. Taking plus eigenspaces is exact, so E^+=H^1(G,mu_p)^+. Tensoring by mu_p^(-1) identifies this space with H^1(G,F_p)^-, the sum of its odd Delta characters. Likewise the Brauer term vanishes for this totally imaginary number field with exactly one omitted finite prime, and H^2(G,mu_p^2)=A tensor mu_p. The last space is a plus space, since both A and mu_p are minus spaces under c.

Mixed plus–minus pairs in E have cup value in the minus summand of that plus target and therefore vanish. The alternating cup product on the one-dimensional space E^- also vanishes: p is odd, so x cup x=0. It follows that the full image of E cup E equals the image of E^+ cup E^+. Surjectivity of the former therefore proves surjectivity of the latter. Untwisting both inputs and the target gives the asserted odd-character cup surjectivity.

Finally the standard minimal-presentation cup/relator duality identifies the coefficient of [x_a,x_b] in a relation initial form with the cup value of the dual H^1 basis vectors, up to the fixed sign convention. A nonzero coefficient over F_p is a p-adic unit in any integral lift. This final use is precisely the form in McCallum–Sharifi, *A cup product in the Galois cohomology of number fields*, section 8 (the presentation and relation formula), and is not an assertion about higher Massey products. QED.

## Character bookkeeping

If x_a has Delta character omega^a, its dual H^1(G,F_p) character is omega^(-a), and its Kummer-unit character is omega^(1-a). A relation of character omega^e is paired by H^2(G,F_p) character omega^(-e), equivalently by A tensor mu_p character omega^(2-e). Therefore a nonzero odd–odd coefficient in that relation requires a+b=e modulo p-1. This convention matches the weight-32 relation at p=37. The parity proof does not assume that the pairing with p alone is always surjective: that stronger fact is used only in the cited computed range p<1000.
