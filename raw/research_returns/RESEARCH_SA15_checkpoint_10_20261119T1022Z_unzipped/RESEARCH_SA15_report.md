## Checkpoint 05: new results and exact scope

### A.1: a full coherent restriction counterexample in every prime

The unrestricted coherent assertion is now refuted. The apolar functional based on (u0*z1-u1*z0)^(p-2) proves nonvanishing for every prime, rather than inferring a general statement from samples. The branch is a fully specified generic form in 896 ambient coordinates over a specified perfect field. The Jacobian and Bertini arguments prove both smoothness assertions. ### A.3–A.4: exact product invariants and effective full images

For X equal to a product of n ordinary elliptic curves with product polarization of degrees a_i>=3, the generic relative rank is d^n*product(a_i)-1 for every d>=1. If p divides every a_i*d, the entire geometric invariant covector space has dimension n for odd p and 2^n-1 for p=2. These are equalities, not lower bounds. The proof extends a covector across parameter-space divisors, identifies its lift with an endomorphism of the section space modulo scalars, and solves the Cartier identities using the character decomposition of Frobenius. A proposed intermediate shortcut asserting that Cartier roots preserve character spaces was rejected. The repaired proof raises the identity to the pth power, uses an eigenvector and the linear parameter coefficient, and proves character-space preservation instead. The correction is recorded as part of the audit.

Over F3 the explicit surface built from y²=x³+x²+1 has relative rank9d²-1 and exactly two arithmetic and geometric invariant covectors in every degree. A separate theorem gives a terminating exact algorithm for the complete geometric and arithmetic matrix groups from equations over a finite field, for every d>=max(1,reg(omega_X)-1). ### B.3: complete full-nine-torsion range

For p=3, an additive elliptic curve with full rational nine-torsion exists over a complete discretely valued field of absolute ramification e and perfect residue field if and only if 6 divides e and e>=18. All examples are over finite extensions of Q3. At the endpoint, E0:y²=x³-x over Q3(i) has division field L=Q3(i,E0[9]) of ramification72; its Z3[i]-module structure and exact primitive formal valuation1/72 prove this degree without an assumed local reciprocity dictionary. Taking the fixed field of the scalar i gives e18. A primitive formal parameter u transforms by i, and y²=x³-u4*x descends with all nine-torsion rational and exact defect4.

For every e=6d>18 the field is exactly K=Q3(i,eta,pi), where eta is a primitive ninth root and pi^d=eta-1. Its residue field is F9 and pi is a uniformizer. The displayed quadratic Hensel lift and a separately rescaled cubic Hensel lift supply three cube roots of (t-theta)/9 for theta=1,zeta,zeta². Fisher's original full-level equations and moduli interpretation then give E_t[9]=mu9 direct sum Z/9. The curve is y²+3txy+(t³-1)y=x³, with v(t)=9-e, v(j)=27 and v(Delta)=81-6e congruent9 modulo12. Thus it is potentially good but additive. The four former gaps e24,30,42,48 all have defect12. The proof explicitly avoids the invalid unrescaled Hensel inequality v(error)>2v(3); its integral rescaling has a simple linear reduction. The entire construction has independent parent and obstructions/crystals audits, with an independently executed exact symbolic certificate.

The fixed-e higher wild problem therefore starts at full27 and e216. Its previously proved one-level numerical bound and the sharp global ramification minimum remain valid. Higher-dimensional potentially good sharp thresholds remain open; the nine-torsion theorem does not assert them.

### E.2: counterexamples and relative positive theorem

Let I=Fp[t,t^-1]/Fp[t] and R=Fp[t] semidirect I with I²=0. Set s_n=(p^n-1)/(p-1), b_n=[t^-s_n] in I and X=Spec R[x]/(x^p-tx). The base is fully specified and non-Noetherian. The morphism is finite free of rankp, flat, finitely presented, and all its fibres are complete intersections. On any nilpotent test U inside T, choose n killing the p^n powers of its ideal and a local lift z of x. Multiplication by b_n*z^(p^n) defines a morphism delta of the structure crystal. Its definition is independent of every choice because t^(p^n)b_(n+1)=b_n. It is zero on X but nonzero on the finite free square-zero thickening T=Spec R[x]/(x^p-tx)^2, where it equals b1*x^p. As delta²=0, 1+delta is a nonidentity automorphism inducing the identity after restriction to stratifications. This is a direct proof on every infinitesimal test, independent of the Frobenius-divided comparison.

The same construction with the regular Noetherian base R0=Fp[t] and coefficient crystal O_T tensor_R0 I gives a nonzero morphism O_inf to that quasi-coherent crystal with zero restriction. This stronger variant uses noncoherent coefficients. It does not settle the manuscript's separate inserted restriction to a locally Noetherian base and coherent coefficients. That insertion is quoted from the supplied transcription, and the absent scans are not claimed to have been inspected.

The relative positive theorem proves that, over any Fp-algebra R, quasi-coherent infinitesimal crystals on a locally finitely presented X/R are equivalent to relative Frobenius-divided systems. When every relative Frobenius twist is pure, they are equivalent to ordinary stratified quasi-coherent modules. The proof uses finite free Frobenius on affine space, relative nilpotent envelopes, and effective pure module descent; it does not assume that R is perfect, reduced or Noetherian. As a concrete singular family, R[x,y]/(xy) satisfies the criterion for every Fp-algebra R via an explicit relative-Frobenius splitting.

### A.4: actual full images in two degrees on a fixed abelian surface

For E:y²+xy=x³+1 over F2 and X=E×E with L=O_E(3O) external tensor O_E(3O), the geometric and arithmetic groups both equal GL8(F2) in degree one. In degree two both equal the entire pointwise stabilizer of the three previously constructed covectors, namely the matrices [A B;0 I3] with A in GL32(F2) and B an arbitrary32-by3 binary matrix. In particular the translation subgroup has order2^96. These conclusions persist over every perfect extension of F2. The fixed geometric base point and Fp-structure remain those of the relative Lang local system; dual and inverse-Frobenius conventions are specified in the proof.

The argument certifies all divisorial inertia rather than extrapolating from point samples. On the whole projective linear system, including singular sections, the relative coherent bundle is the cotangent bundle and its Frobenius is represented by an explicitly constructed linear Cartier matrix. A line with ordinary leading coefficient meets every geometric determinant component. Exact finite-field ranks of M and M^[2]M at every root on the line prove stable rank loss exactly one at every generic Hasse divisor. A henselian finite Lang subgroup then fixes a hyperplane, so characteristic-two inertia consists of transvections. Purity and the absence of connected finite etale covers of projective space prove generation by these transvections.

Irreducible nonreciprocal Frobenius polynomials of degrees8 and32 supply primes17 and65537, respectively.
