\paragraph{Square-zero $K_2$ and the first normal-motion symbol.}
Let $k$ be a perfect field of characteristic $p>2$, let $V/k$ be smooth,
and let $L$ be a line bundle. For the split thickening
$V_2=\operatorname{Spec}_V(\mathcal O_V\oplus L)$, with $L^2=0$,
there is a natural isomorphism of relative Zariski $K$-sheaves
\[
 \mathcal K_2(V_2,V)\simeq L\otimes\Omega^1_{V/k}.
\]
We choose the sign normalization
\[
              \{1+tc,u\}\longmapsto tc\,d\log u.
\]
This is the negative of the map in
\citet[Chapter III, Exercise 5.14, pp.~46--47]{frWeibelKbookIII}
with his modern Dennis--Stein convention. Indeed his Definition 5.11,
p.~43, gives $\langle r,s\rangle=\{r,1-rs\}$ when $r$ is a unit,
and his exercise sends $\langle m,r\rangle$ to $m\,dr$.
Our normalization therefore sends $\langle m,r\rangle$ to $-m\,dr$.

The isomorphism for a ring $R$ with $1/2\in R$ is the unnumbered
theorem and corollary of \citet[p.~A1204, proof pp.~A1205--A1207]
{frVanDerKallen1971}; it has no characteristic-zero hypothesis.
The construction is natural in $R$ and homogeneous in the square-zero
coordinate, so it glues for $L$. Since $k$ is perfect of positive
characteristic, $\Omega^1_{k/\mathbb Z}=0$, and absolute differentials
on $V$ agree with $\Omega^1_{V/k}$.

Locally let $x,y$ be regular parameters on $V$, and let $t$ generate
$L$. The difference of the two Koszul symbols for a first normal motion
is
\[
 \{x-at,y-bt\}-\{x,y\}
       \longmapsto t\frac{b\,dx-a\,dy}{xy}.
\]
The term with two infinitesimal units has zero image. If
$\eta=t^{-1}(\eta_x\,dx+\eta_y\,dy)$ is a local section of
$\Omega^1_V\otimes L^{-1}$, wedging the displayed form with $\eta$
in that order and taking the local residue gives
\[
                              a\eta_x+b\eta_y.
\]
The simultaneous sign of the Koszul orientation and the chosen
$K_2$ map is thus fixed. This is a local symbol computation; the
global Chow-to-$K$-cohomology comparison needed in C.3an is a separate
argument in that statement.
