# I35 known-background proof audit

The optimal Hankel/completely-entangled-subspace statement is known, not a new finding. Primary source reopened and local proofs read: Parthasarathy (2004), arXiv:quant-ph/0405077v1, Propositions1.3–1.4 and Theorem1.5, printed pp4–6, web reference turn918939176view0. The source uses complex Hilbert spaces; its Vandermonde/product-vector proof gives the displayed construction.

The following algebraic-closure dimension proof was checked for the field-general statement and kept outside the main new-deduction text:

Over an algebraically closed field, $2r-1$ is the minimum possible
dimension for such a subspace. The projective rank-one locus is
the Segre variety $\mathbb P^{r-1}\times\mathbb P^{r-1}$, of dimension
$2r-2$, inside $\mathbb P^{r^2-1}$. If $\dim W\le2r-2$, its projective
annihilator has dimension at least $r^2-2r+1$. The projective
dimension theorem forces it to meet that Segre variety. This gives
a nonzero rank-one annihilator. The lower bound is asserted over
an algebraic closure; it need not hold over a field with fewer
rational boundary pairs.
