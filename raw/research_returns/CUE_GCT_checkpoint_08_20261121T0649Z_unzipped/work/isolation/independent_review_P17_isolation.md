## Whole-proof checks

1. **Published exterior decoder.** I checked the ordered Vandermonde scaling and the common kernel of the three contraction operators. Every nonzero vector in that kernel has support at least four because any three distinct-node columns form an invertible Vandermonde matrix. The stated degree interval is exactly `3 <= d <= n-3`.

2. **Three consecutive degree blocks.** If the total rank of the three nonzero blocks is at most three, each has rank one and the displayed form `A+LB+QN` follows. Pole extraction gives each partial derivative in the prime one-pair ideal. A direction annihilating L therefore annihilates Q, because that ideal contains no nonzero product of linear forms. I independently checked the characteristic-two-safe matrix argument: a symmetric zero-diagonal matrix with kernel containing `ker(l^T)` is `c*l*l^T`, and its diagonal forces c=0. No differentiation multiplicity or division by two is concealed here.

3. **Decomposable cubics.** The first prefix with column rank two has a suffix of rank at least two; otherwise a row combination would have support one. If the suffix rank is three, the three consecutive blocks are all nonzero. In the two/two case the two supported kernel vectors each have at least four nonzero coordinates. Weighted evaluation of quadratics on those nodes is injective. At least two distinct nonzero wedge pairs occur, since a single pair would force one kernel vector to have support two. Thus each of the two middle blocks has rank at least two. This covers every ordering.

4. **At most seven variables and reversal.** I checked the dimension-three/four decomposability assertions, the degree interval, reversal of numerator/denominator rational functions, and the exact complement permutation of each coefficient matrix. 5. **Eight-variable cubic and the general three-coordinate anchor.** Projection of K to the complement of any three coordinates is an isomorphism, so its third exterior power cannot kill a nonzero W. 6. **Nine- and ten-variable cubics.** For nine variables the two end kernels are complementary three-planes, and no third exterior summand can contain at least two factors from each. Consistent minimal tensor-train cores have a side of size at most two and give the singular affine representation required by I-37. Neither complete reducibility nor Lie algebra weights are used.
