# Next-row attempts and exact scope

## Rank-deficient residual moment

At r=2m+1, one can tilt the padding line so C=diag(delta)-a ell ell* has rank2m, with one zero eigenvalue and rational positive remaining eigenvalues. This alone does not continue R30. In the nearrectangular direction delta=b0 I+a e0 e0*, b0=m/(2m+1), rank deficiency forces the nonconstant two-plane eigenvalues to be2b0 and0, with2m-1 remaining eigenvalues b0. The padding line and auxiliary line have components only in this two-plane. Every orthogonal m+m product decomposition therefore leaves one constant-spectrum block untouched by padding and flag, with a residual compact torus. This is a failure of this particular orthogonal-block construction, not a general instability theorem.

## Structural scalar period

R31 gives a stronger exact audit of the whole padded two-product family in odd-m nearrectangular directions. Independent factors and an independent padding line put its generic form into z^a(prod x+prod y). A compensated factor transposition fixes both that form and a generic auxiliary covector, has determinant minus one, and after SL normalization scales the enhanced tensor by minus one. Odd-degree invariants vanish identically on this common cone. No stable no-scaling point exists anywhere on it.

## Routes not yet proved

A common pencil with additional matchings, or separate stable/no-scaling points in the two full cones, could avoid this period. Generic finite projective stabilizers alone do not prove stability. A possible local route starts from the polystable residual-torus point and computes the actual available normal weights in the determinant and permanent pencil images. Balanced positive/negative normal weights would still require a valid local moment or slice argument; no such uniform theorem is claimed here. The generic semistability of arbitrary linear restrictions is not being assumed.
