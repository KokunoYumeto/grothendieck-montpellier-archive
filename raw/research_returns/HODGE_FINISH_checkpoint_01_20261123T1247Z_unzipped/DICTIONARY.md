## Bounded cycles and schemes over the integers

The degree condition produces finitely many projective relative Hilbert schemes. Over a Dedekind base their vertical irreducible components lie above finitely many primes. At every other prime a bounded-degree cycle has a characteristic-zero generization, and the trace construction transports its cohomology class. This is the precise connection to FGA Exposé221, SGA6 XIII6.11, and SGA4 XVIII2.9. It is geometric properness plus a compatible cycle class, not a Tate-conjecture assertion.

## Analytic perfectness and coherent sheaves

A perfect object is locally represented by a bounded complex of vector bundles; a single global such complex is a stronger assertion outside projective geometry. The point sheaf separates the two notions even with injective semiregularity and no negative self-Ext. On very general unpolarized Weil tori the only rational Hodge classes are the Weil plane, rank and top class; a suitable Kähler form annihilates the Weil plane. Voisin's reflexive-sheaf argument then forces every coherent sheaf to have only rank and top Chern character. Buchweitz–Flenner's convergent relative sheaf deformation proves the pure-character coherent-sheaf exclusion. An extension to arbitrary perfect complexes needs the corresponding relative analytic-object theorem; it is not inferred from a K-theory equality or a global resolution assertion.
