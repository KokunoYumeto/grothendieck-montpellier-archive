\subsection{Exact residual support without integer factorization}
\label{sec:residual-support-gcd}

\begin{lemma}[K17-SUPPORT-GCD]\label{lem:comparison-support-gcd}
Let \(a_1,\ldots,a_r,b_1,\ldots,b_s\) be nonzero integers, and put
\[
 C=\gcd\left(\prod_i a_i,\prod_j b_j\right)>0.
\]
Every prime divisor of \(C\) divides at least one
\(\gcd(a_i,b_j)\). Consequently exact gcd splittings and
replacement of a verified perfect power by its base can
produce a list of positive integers with exactly the same
prime support as \(C\), without asserting primality of those
integers.

More generally, fix \(B>2\). Suppose an exact certificate gives
\[
 C=c\,b_1'\cdots b_t'R,\qquad
 1<b_i'<B,\qquad R\ge1,
 \tag{RS.1}
\]
where every prime divisor of \(c\) is proved to be below \(B\).
Suppose positive integers \(M_1,\ldots,M_h\) are supplied, and
the recursion
\[
 R_0=R,\qquad
 R_{j+1}=\frac{R_j}{\gcd(R_j,M_1\cdots M_h)}
 \tag{RS.2}
\]
reaches \(1\). Then every prime \(p\ge B\) dividing \(C\)
divides at least one \(M_i\). The integers \(b_i'\) and \(M_i\)
need not be prime or squarefree.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
A prime dividing both products divides one factor of each,
and hence their gcd. Conversely every gcd of two such factors
divides \(C\). The replacements
\[
 (a,b)\ \longmapsto\
 \bigl(\gcd(a,b),a/\gcd(a,b),b/\gcd(a,b)\bigr)
\]
and \(d^v\mapsto d\), when the displayed power equality is
verified exactly, preserve the union of prime supports.
Deleting entries equal to one and repeated entries also
preserves it. No irreducibility of any originating polynomial
factor is required.

For the final assertion, a prime \(p\ge B\) dividing \(C\)
must divide \(R\), by (RS.1). If it divided none of the \(M_i\),
it would divide none of the denominators removed in (RS.2).
It would therefore persist as a divisor of every \(R_j\),
contradicting the final value one. This proves the assertion.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Application to full Hecke comparisons.}
At each auxiliary prime the factors \(a_i\) or \(b_j\) may be
the evaluations of a verified product decomposition of the
entire unitary characteristic polynomial, together with the
entire classical comparison value. Verifying multiplication
of the polynomial factors and evaluating them exactly makes
their products the full two-branch comparison integers.
The lemma therefore gives a complete list of residual
integer moduli sufficient for the alternate-field unit tests.
The final recursion is a separate, directly checkable
certificate of coverage even if the discovery used additional
gcd refinements.
