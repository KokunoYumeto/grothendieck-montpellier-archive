# Independent review of the fixed-weight reformulation

Statement: KT-JACOBI-FIXED-WEIGHT-REFORMULATION in evidence/main_gate/jacobi_fixed_weight_reformulation.tex.

Verdict: PASS. I read the complete new proof and the complete four-page text of Guerzhoy, Glasgow Mathematical Journal 41 (1999), 141–144. The imported scope is Proposition 1 and formula (2) on page 143, and Proposition 2 with formula (4) on pages 143–144. The measure's construction is cited by that paper and is not independently reconstructed here; the final type-a/type-b argument is not used.

For p congruent to 3 modulo 4 and odd fundamental D prime to p, the product D(-p)=p|D| is a positive fundamental discriminant. The primitive character product is correct. The exponent b-1=(p-1)/2+1 gives the required equality of the two integrands modulo p by Euler's criterion.

The CRT choice c congruent to 1 modulo p with chi_D(c)=-1 is valid because the prime-to-p quadratic character is nontrivial. It makes both regularizers congruent to 2. The first Euler factor is a unit congruent to 1; the second is exactly 1 because the second character has conductor divisible by p. Guerzhoy's Proposition 2 expressly permits moduli Delta*p^m and therefore permits this second character. Proposition 1 and division by those units establish integrality of both special values before the congruence is divided.

The minus sign in L(1-r,chi)=-B_(r,chi)/r occurs on both sides and cancels. The previously proved all-weight transport gives the e+1 condition at b and the 2e+1 condition at 3. The B1 condition is the class-number condition with only unit roots-of-unity factors. The size implication uses 3p+6<4p and the already-reviewed class-number estimate.

The result expresses the two remaining values at the same discriminant. It asserts neither simultaneous nonvanishing nor any new original rational-component vanishing.
