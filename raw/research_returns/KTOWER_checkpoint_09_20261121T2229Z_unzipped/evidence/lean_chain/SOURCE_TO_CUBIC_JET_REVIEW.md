## Flat-domain counterexample

For R = Z_p[[x,y,z]]/(pz-x^2 y), the weighted filtration with weights 1 on p,x,y and 2 on z is separated and cofinal with the maximal-ideal filtration. The ambient associated graded ring is a polynomial ring. For a principal ideal, multiplicativity of initial forms in that domain gives the displayed associated graded quotient. The initial polynomial is primitive and linear in Z over F_p[P,X,Y,Z]; its coefficients P and X^2Y are coprime. Gauss's lemma and unique factorization give a domain. The surviving initial form of p implies torsion-freeness and hence Z_p-flatness.

The characteristic-zero specialization (p,p,p^2) satisfies the relation. Its two opposite entries are nonzero. The quadratic jet exists; in any cubic extension with the given first-order values the relation forces the nonzero monomial s^2t to vanish. Higher corrections to x or y cannot affect degree three.

The final text correctly describes a continuous formal action of the algebraic group G_m, algebraic on every invariant quotient R/m^n. An unqualified algebraic action on Spec of a completed power-series ring would not express the intended completed grading. The involution and both weights preserve the relation. This is an algebra counterexample, without an asserted Galois realization.

## Coefficient truncation and global extraction

The strict inequality v_pi(p)>3d ensures that O/pi^(3d+1) has characteristic p. Its finite residue field has the unique coefficient-field lift obtained from the roots of X^|k|-X. Successive expansion in the uniformizer identifies the quotient with k[pi]/pi^(3d+1). The subalgebra generated by pi^d has exactly four successive powers, and is k[lambda]/lambda^4.

The finite Teichmüller residual representation is constant in that quotient. Since N^4=0 and p>3, the polynomial logarithm and exponential through degree three are inverse. No analytic convergence assertion is required.

Under the explicitly assumed sparse support, the homomorphism law gives the two first-order cocycle laws and the prescribed second-order Z equation. The U and V projections of the cubic coefficient are invariant residual character lines. Projecting the degree-three BCH identity gives precisely the two equations of KT-ORDINARY-CUBIC-MASSEY-CONDITION. Other cubic Lie components occur linearly and do not enter these equations. Substituting independent monomials s,t,st,s^2t,st^2 therefore gives an actual two-parameter homomorphism with the required grading, involution, and determinant.

## Local conditions

Away from p the representation and its residual lift are both the identity on inertia over E. Every extracted coefficient therefore vanishes there. For the negative character, the remaining rational-inertia quotient has order at most two and the character is unramified; its additive p-cocycle is zero.

At p, an inertia element with the three distinct residual eigencharacters exists because the residual cyclotomic exponent has order greater than two. The exact ordinary inertia characters become constant after reduction to the characteristic-p quotient. Polynomial eigenprojectors have unit denominators and produce the unique ordered eigenflag near the standard flag. Since the matrix entries lie in the lambda subalgebra, its normalized lower flag matrix also lies there. The stipulated semidirect Borel condition makes that flag stable for the full rational decomposition group.

The first-order lower term is only Y, so the first flag coefficient is a single constant a and v=beta a-a. Conjugation by exp(lambda aY) gives the displayed diagonal second-order correction. No lower term survives through degree two, so uniqueness of the eigenflag makes its remaining lower correction start at degree three. The exact inertial eigenvalues force the diagonal Z correction to vanish on inertia.

The remaining V projection is a local coboundary killed by the degree-three lower flag coefficient. It is exactly the st^2V coefficient after separating parameters; discarded cubic components cannot interact before degree four. The U term is upper triangular already. Thus the extracted two-parameter family satisfies the exact ordinary condition, not merely a condition on its semisimplification.

## Boundary

The argument is conditional on both the strict valuation inequality and the global sparse logarithmic expansion in the stated basis. Ramified coefficient extension scales the numerator and entry valuations together and cannot create the inequality. The separate archive-source audit records which point and tangent statements are actually available; this whole-proof PASS does not promote those extra hypotheses to source consequences.
