The full draft and independent review were read. Its Chow-theoretic reduction, full-fibre SNC construction, ampleness composition, Hartogs sign and positive support argument pass. However, the root review required a sharper justification for simultaneous projectivity and preservation of the regular puncture in the final modification. Kollár–Witaszek's definition of a resolution does not itself include preservation of the regular locus. Their Corollary3 and Cossart–Piltant's regular-locus clause cannot simply be combined as two unrelated existence statements. Freshly opened Bhatt et al., arXiv2012.15801v3, Proposition2.14 and Remark2.15, author pp15–16, explicitly warn that their projectivization need not preserve the SNC locus.

The owner identified a repair using Cossart–Piltant, arXiv1412.0868v2, Proposition4.4, author p141. We freshly opened and read its complete statement and proof (turn865327784view1): a nonzero ideal on a regular excellent threefold can be principalized by regular-centre blowups supported where the ideal is not locally principal. Applied to the ideal obtained by dividing the torsion-free pullback of the rank-one sheaf by its reflexive hull, this produces a projective regular common model without modifying the puncture. The dimension-two version still needs its exact statement and citation in the revised draft. The revised complete argument is being audited.
