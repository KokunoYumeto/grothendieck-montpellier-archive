\paragraph{C.3al (Proved/refuted in the stated ranges): principal Gysin images and ordinary restriction kernels.}
Let $R,X,Y$ have the local hypotheses of C.3ak, and
write $i:Y\hookrightarrow X$ for the principal Cartier
divisor. For every $r\ge1$ we have, integrally,
\[
 \operatorname{im}\!\left(
 i_*:\mathrm{CH}^{r-1}(Y)_{\mathrm{alg}}
              \longrightarrow\mathrm{CH}^r(X)_{\mathrm{alg}}
              \right)
 \subset
 \ker\!\left(
 i^*:\mathrm{CH}^r(X)_{\mathrm{alg}}
              \longrightarrow\mathrm{CH}^r(Y)_{\mathrm{alg}}
              \right).
\]
Here $i^*$ is the refined restriction, preserving
codimension, and $i_*$ raises codimension by one.

Consequently, for every $r\ge2$ and
\[
                         r+1\le N\le2r-1,
\]
there is a completely specified complex local pair with
regular punctures for which ordinary restriction
$J^r(X)\to J^r(Y)$ is not injective. The kernel contains
a specified class of infinite order before tensoring
with $\mathbb Q$. For $N\le r$ the source Chow group is
zero for every pair, so injectivity always holds.
This argument alone leaves $N\ge2r$; C.3am below
handles $N=2r$, and C.3ao handles every $N\ge2r+1$.

\begin{proof}
The ideal of $Y$ is generated globally by the
nonzerodivisor $f$. Hence
\[
 \mathcal O_X(Y)\simeq\mathcal O_X,
 \qquad
 N_{Y/X}=\mathcal O_X(Y)|_Y\simeq\mathcal O_Y.
\]
The normal-bundle identification is
\citet[Lemma 31.15.2, Tag 0B3P]{cyStacksPrincipalGysin}.
For a cycle $\beta$ on $Y$, the Cartier-divisor
definition of the refined restriction gives
\[
                    i^*i_*\beta
                  =c_1(N_{Y/X})\cap\beta=0.
\]
This identity can be checked on each integral component
$W\subset Y$: its restriction as a cycle on $X$ is
$c_1(\mathcal O_X(Y)|_W)\cap[W]$, which is zero.
These are precisely Definition 42.29.1, Tag 02T8, and
Remark 42.29.6, Tag 0B6Z, of the opened source.
The identity therefore holds integrally and commutes
with passage to the strict-local limit. Proper
pushforward and refined restriction preserve ordinary
algebraic equivalence, since the same operations on
the fixed product with a parameter curve commute with
its refined fibres. This proves the displayed inclusion.

For $r\ge2$ in the asserted interval, take exactly the
ring, principal equation, and actual family of C.3t.
In its notation
\[
 m=N-1-r,\quad 0\le m\le r-2,\quad
 T:\sum_{j=0}^{r+1}z_j^{r+2}=0,\quad
 V=\mathbb P^m\times T,\quad
 L=\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^m}(1)\boxtimes\mathcal O_T(1).
\]
The source is the strict-local punctured cone on
$(V,L)$. The principal divisor is the cone section
defined by C.3t's explicit smooth incidence equation
\[
 \sum_{j=r-m}^{r}x_{j-r+m}
                 (z_j-u_jz_{r+1})=0.
\]
All coefficients and the generic point $P$ and
algebraic point $a$ are the ones fixed in C.3t.
That proof constructs an actual ordinary class
$\beta\in\mathrm{CH}^{r-1}(Y)_{\mathrm{alg}}$ with
\[
       \gamma=i_*\beta
          =q^*\operatorname{pr}_T^*([P]-[a])
               \in\mathrm{CH}^{r}(X)_{\mathrm{alg}}
\]
of infinite order. Its generic-point non-support and
strict-cone injection establish non-torsion on the
strict henselization itself. Applying the principal
self-intersection identity gives $i^*\gamma=0$,
as required. This proves failure for the closed
restriction map, in addition to the open restriction
already recorded in C.3t.

For $N\le r$, the puncture $X$ has no codimension-$r$
points, since its dimension is at most $N-1<r$.
Thus $\mathrm{CH}^r(X)=0$, proving the positive claim.
\end{proof}

For completeness, degree one has already been settled
separately: C.3o gives failure at $N=2$, injectivity
at every $N\ge3$ in the stated complex coefficient-field
scope, and zero source at $N=1$. No conclusion for
$r\ge2$, $N\ge2r$ is inferred from the principal
self-intersection identity alone.
