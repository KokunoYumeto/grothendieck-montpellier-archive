# C.3af: ordinary local restriction in every codimension

Finalized, independently passed by root and frobenius, and integrated after C.3ae. For every r≥2 and N≥4^(r−1)+2r−1, the specified product-K3 construction gives an integral ordinary restriction cokernel surjecting onto Pic⁰(C)(C), where C is the Fermat plane quartic. Hence the ordinary rational J^r map is not surjective in this entire range. The first thresholds are N=7 for r=2, N=21 for r=3, and N=71 for r=4.

The proof retains a simple full-basis construction using 3·4^(r−1) sections as an independently checked first stage. The sharper construction uses 4^(r−1) sections whose coefficients are exact products of the four integer matrices in C.3ae. There is no generic section choice or unspecified degree.

The reduction in section count has two separate proofs. For base point freeness, every pair of columns of the original4×3 matrix B(w) is independent at every point of the Fermat K3 surface. Twelve exact finite-field chart ideals and properness of the projective determinantal locus prove this in characteristic zero. Three pure tensors with pairwise independent first and remaining factors are linearly independent. The values of the new sections are the linear combination of these three tensors with coefficients z0,z1,z2, so they cannot vanish simultaneously.

For the deformation obstruction, the actual correlated-coefficient map is the tensor power of the original injective57→64 map, precomposed with the linear diagonal on the three-dimensional curve factor, e_k↦e_k^tensor-s. This is injective for every s=r−1. It does not replace the fixed curve by a product of curves. All extra Kunneth components are excluded by their separate cohomology and differential degrees. Each exceptional boundary correction inserts a K3 hyperplane class killed by its test class, or a curve canonical class with the wrong cohomology degree. Thus the same actual proper-family detector is zero on the ordinary source image.

The target product of the displayed lines with a degree-zero divisor on C maps by multiplication by (−9)^(r−1). This is surjective on Pic⁰(C)(C); a geometric generic point difference gives a specified nontorsion witness. The proof applies integrally to ordinary algebraic-equivalence families and also after tensoring with Q. It does not assert that all cycles on the source are killed, does not settle intermediate dimensions, and gives no characteristic-p extension.

The all-codimension proof is a tensor argument, not an extrapolation from a finite list. The finite coefficient and determinantal checks have reproducible scripts, outputs and runtimes.
