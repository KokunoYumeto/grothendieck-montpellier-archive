## New argument proved in the text

None of the opened sources is cited as stating the required first-conormal trace formula verbatim. The proof derives it as follows. Contract the absolute degree-two cycle class by **minus** a coefficient-field derivation, then project to relative one-forms. The minus sign is forced by contraction of `dt wedge omega_coeff`. Wedge the result with the globally defined meromorphic relative two-form `eta/t`, where eta is a section of K_V tensor L^{-1}; its frame transformations cancel those of the normal coordinate. Simple-pole residue gives the Serre pairing of the first-conormal coefficient. Supported duality and normalized residue trace then commute with proper pushforward, retaining every branch multiplicity. This proves the desired formula after testing against every coefficient-field derivation. A Tor-independent Cartier square separately proves preservation of the exceptional refined restriction.

The proof explicitly moves the two-divisor product so that its curve supports contain no exceptional component and meet the exceptional surface in a finite set. All constructions use actual proper pushforwards and one fixed proper parameter curve. We do not discard ramification, assume the source pullback line bundle is ample, or infer algebraization of arbitrary formal cycles.
