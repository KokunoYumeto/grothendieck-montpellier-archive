\paragraph{C.3ap (Proved): the complete ordinary local restriction table over $\mathbb C$.}
We use exactly the local complex rings and regular
punctures of C.3ak, with the same coefficient field,
principal divisors and ordinary algebraic equivalence.
For each fixed $r\ge1$ and $N\ge1$, the following
table gives the universal assertions for
\[
                   J^r(X)\longrightarrow J^r(Y).
\]
The table is unchanged for the integral ordinary
groups $\mathrm{CH}^r(-)_{\mathrm{alg}}$.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\toprule
Codimension & Universally injective & Universally surjective
                              & Universally an isomorphism \\
\midrule
$r=1$ & $N=1$ or $N\ge3$ & $N\le2$ & $N=1$ \\
$r\ge2$ & $N\le r$ & $N\le r+1$ & $N\le r$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Here ``universally'' means for every pair of the given
dimension, and each complementary dimension has a
specified counterexample. The injectivity failures
contain infinite-order kernel classes.

\begin{proof}
For $N\le r$, the puncture has dimension at most
$N-1<r$, so the entire source Chow group is zero.
The target is zero as well. Thus both assertions and
the isomorphism hold integrally in this range.
For $r=1$, ordinary integral injectivity for every
$N\ge3$ is C.3n in characteristic zero. The explicit
non-torsion failure at $N=2$ is C.3o; $N=1$ has
zero source and empty divisor puncture.

For $r\ge2$, the counterexamples exhaust all
remaining dimensions:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
Local dimension & Infinite-order ordinary restriction kernel \\
\midrule
$r+1\le N\le2r-1$ & C.3al: principal Gysin image \\
$N=2r$ & C.3am: cubic and rational cubic-surface section \\
$N\ge2r+1$ & C.3ao: absolute Picard product on a strict cone \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Every class belongs to an actual ordinary family and
vanishes integrally after restriction. Its non-torsion
source proves failure both integrally and rationally.
This establishes the injectivity column. The
surjectivity column is exactly C.3ak. Combining the
two columns proves the isomorphism column, without
requiring the injectivity and surjectivity
counterexamples to be the same pair.
\end{proof}

These examples have normal local rings and regular
punctures; no extra depth assumption is imposed.
This settles the universal
ordinary local restriction problem over $\mathbb C$.
It does not settle the smooth-projective Lefschetz
injectivity questions, the top local quotient by
ordinary algebraic equivalence, or the higher-degree
positive-characteristic restriction questions.
