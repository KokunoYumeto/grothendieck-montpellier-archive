## A precise sheaf quotient, with its limit

Ayoub–Barbieri-Viale (2009), author arXiv math/0607738v2, gives the left adjoint Alb from etale sheaves with transfers to the cocomplete Serre category of one-motivic sheaves (Proposition1.3.11, Lemma1.3.12). The generator map Z_tr(T) to the Serre-Albanese scheme is an epimorphism (Lemma1.3.3); the target category is closed under subobjects, quotients and sums (Corollary1.3.5).

The separate E3s draft uses these facts to identify the point kernel of the Chow-sheaf unit with N integrally. Its construction starts with actual cycles, not arbitrary globally unrepresented sheaf sections. An epimorphism from their sum of representable transfer sheaves descends, after killing the individual Albanese kernels, to an epimorphism from the sum of Albanese schemes. The quotient is therefore one-motivic and has the required universal property. Evaluation at the algebraically closed point is exact; smooth projective compactification and Chow localization turn every nonproper parameter kernel into an ordinary projective Albanese-kernel generator. The component quotient is ordinary cycles modulo algebraic equivalence by Theorem3.1.4. This draft awaits independent auditing and is not yet registered as a proved row.

The source's Theorem1.3.10 explicitly permits a discrete torsion-free kernel and a filtered colimit of abelian varieties. Thus a one-motivic quotient need not be an abelian variety. The quotient of an elliptic curve by the constant lattice generated by a nontorsion point is a concrete categorical countermodel: the canonical quotient family cannot be a morphism to an abelian variety, since its kernel would have to contain the whole Zariski closure. This is not asserted to occur as the Chow quotient of a specified X.

The compactness shortcut also fails at its premise. Proposition2.4.7 preserves compact motives rationally, but pp46-47 explicitly show that the effective Chow internal Hom for a generic quintic threefold is nonconstructible, using its infinitely generated codimension-two Néron-Severi group. This does not prove nonconstructibility of the connected quotient by itself. Remark3.3.6 p52 anticipates, without proving, the comparison of the higher Albanese sheaf with Walker's morphic Abel-Jacobi map. No weight argument removing its discrete kernel or bounding its connected part has been established in this investigation.

Barbieri-Viale (2007), Section1.3.1, had already distinguished a universal Ind-object from the finite-type algebraic-representative problem. ACMV (2026), Regular homomorphisms with a twist, starts with a given regular map and constructs torsors for unpointed parameters. Its TheoremA/Theorem2.1 does not close a generated-kernel comparison over C, and Section3.1 explicitly retains the hypothesis that an algebraic representative exists.

## Retained failed geometric replacement

A finite-type witness to an isolated rational-equivalence relation may kill one point of an abelian parameter without killing the whole subgroup variety it generates. Replacing that relation by its Zariski closure would silently replace a lattice quotient by an algebraic quotient. Normalization and connected exceptional-fibre arguments can handle connected geometric fibres, but no proof was found that arbitrary pinched or reducible parameter relations reduce to such fibres. Dense torsion and divisibility alone do not supply that missing replacement.

The established equivalences E3m/E3n remain the precise test, and E3o/p give the exact relation-product and surface-generator form. The broad codimension-two threefold case without CH0 support on a surface remains an unresolved test in this report; existence of Murre's algebraic representative does not identify its kernel with N.
