# C.4x — full real multiplication in positive characteristic

The original Yu 2003 paper gives a direct argument in positive characteristic. No canonical lift, supersingular realization, or ordinary hypothesis is needed.

## Exact proved scope

For a dimension-g abelian variety over an algebraically closed field of characteristic p, with a full degree-g totally real integer-ring action and a compatible polarization, the polarization Euler-characteristic gcd is a p-power. Under the intrinsic equality of Lie-module and regular-module classes in the Grothendieck group, that gcd is one. Total ramification of p forces the equality because the coefficient algebra is local Artinian with a unique simple module and both modules have length g.

Combining with the previously proved actual-factorization bounds gives s_J dividing 2's p-part for surfaces and 6's p-part for threefolds. The Lie condition removes the remaining characteristic-prime factor. No failure statement is inferred if the condition fails.

## Lead status and limits

The initially found Yu 2015 Proposition 5.3(2), on supersingular modules with totally ramified real multiplication, is superseded for this deduction by the more direct original 2003 result. The 2015 source was opened, but neither it nor an asserted supersingular crystalline full-faithfulness theorem is needed in C.4x.

This positive subfamily is not counted as a resolution of the main residual cases. In particular, it supplies no real multiplication action on an arbitrary supersingular threefold, and it says nothing new about the generic endomorphism-ring-Z type-(1,2) surface. Those cases remain subject to the actual numerical Poincare-curve criterion already recorded.

The recent Engel–Schreieder author PDF dated 6 August 2026, On the degree of subvarieties on abelian varieties, Theorem 1.1 p. 1 and Theorems 1.8/2.6 pp. 3/5, was also opened. Its very-general principally polarized or explicitly matroidal-family hypotheses do not automatically apply to the special product A times its dual in the remaining surface case. The EDF A6 paper, dated 19 December 2025, Theorem 1.2 p. 2, likewise does not make that specialization. These are source-scope checks only.
