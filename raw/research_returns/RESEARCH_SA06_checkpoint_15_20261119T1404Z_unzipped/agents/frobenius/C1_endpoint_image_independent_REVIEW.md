# Independent audit of C.1n

The current endpoint-image argument in research/agents/cycles/endpoint_image.tex passes. The proposed stronger fixed-target formulation also passes: for any fixed smooth projective connected complex X and codimension r in its range, the image criterion for every smooth projective source, source codimension, and correspondence of the indicated shift is equivalent to universal numerical equals Betti homological equivalence D_C together with the vanishing of the rational Griffiths kernel in codimension r on X.

For necessity of D_C, choose a numerically trivial but Betti-nonzero a of codimension s on a d-fold Y, let j=d-s, and take a times h_X^r. The correspondence has codimension d+r-j. Its cycle action is zero by numerical triviality, while Poincare duality supplies a rational cohomological preimage of the nonzero h_X^r class. The preimage need not be algebraic; exactly that mismatch contradicts the proposed image criterion. This works also in target codimension zero, for a zero-dimensional connected target, and for projective-space targets in every codimension. Taking the zero correspondence proves necessity of the target Griffiths-kernel vanishing.

For sufficiency, Jannsen's semisimplicity theorem applies to all rational numerical motives without assuming algebraic Kunneth projectors. Under D_C on every product, the ordinary total graded Betti realization is well defined and faithful: cohomological Kunneth and Poincare duality identify the total class of a correspondence with its total graded action. Any additive functor out of a semisimple abelian category preserves the split exact sequences used here. Choose a generalized inverse to the correspondence morphism to form the image projector p. The realized class of z is fixed by p, and faithfulness gives (1-p)z=0 as a morphism from the unit motive. The resulting numerical-cycle preimage has the required source degree because morphisms from the unit to h(Y)(j) are precisely numerical codimension-j cycles. Under D_C, its discrepancy from z is homologically trivial; target Griffiths-kernel vanishing makes that discrepancy algebraically trivial.

The use of only degree zero of the twisted realization is legitimate: the unit motive has no other realized degree, and correspondences preserve the grading after the stated Tate shifts. No motivic Kunneth decomposition has been introduced. Numerical equals algebraic equivalence rationally for target codimensions 0, 1, and n, so the asserted endpoint equivalences follow; the same holds in every codimension for projective space. The known intermediate-dimensional counterexamples and the conditional endpoint criterion are logically compatible.
