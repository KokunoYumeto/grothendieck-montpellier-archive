# A restricted Orlov-transform construction does not remove the even-surface denominator

## Status and scope

This note records a proved limitation of one attempted construction. It is not an obstruction to all algebraic cycles on A×Adual, and it does not resolve the remaining type(1,2) surface case. In particular we do not infer that the integral numerical class P²/2 is nonalgebraic.

The construction studied takes exterior products of algebraic K-classes on a fixed polarized abelian surface, applies the standard Orlov equivalence, and then forms integral sums, differences and tensor products before taking second Chern classes. The calculation below shows that when the polarization degree e is even, all resulting second Chern classes have an even coefficient in the P²/2 direction of the rational divisor-generated degree-four subspace.

## Primary source and exact normalization

We opened Markman's manuscript *Secant sheaves on abelian n-folds with real multiplication and Weil classes on abelian 2n-folds with complex multiplication*, arXiv:2509.23079v1, at https://arxiv.org/html/2509.23079v1 . Section10.1, equation(10.1.3) and the following sentence define the equivalence Φ as the inverse of μ_* composed with the Fourier–Mukai transform on the second factor, where μ(x,z)=(x+z,z). This is the normalization used here.

The same manuscript uses normalized characteristic classes κ(E)=ch(E)exp(−c1(E)/rank(E)) for deformation arguments. These are rational classes. The present fixed-surface integral calculation does not test all those deformed constructions, nor does a rational algebraicity conclusion by itself establish the needed integral class.

## Exact calculation

Let A have Néron–Severi group generated by a polarization θ of type(1,e). Its integral algebraic even Mukai lattice has basis 1,θ,pt, with θ²=2e·pt. Write

\[
 v=r+d\theta+s\,\mathrm{pt},\qquad
 w=r'+d'\theta+s'\,\mathrm{pt},
\]

with integral coefficients. All such basis classes are represented by virtual algebraic K-classes: θ=ch(L)−1−e·pt. Todd classes are trivial on abelian varieties.

With x the first A-coordinate, z the integrated A-coordinate and y the dual coordinate, Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch gives

\[
 \operatorname{ch}\Phi(v\boxtimes w)
   =\int_{z\in A}v(x+z)\,w(z)\,\exp(-P(z,y)).
\]

In dimension two the inverse Fourier transform has this sign; replacing y by −y only reverses the sign of P and leaves the parity conclusion unchanged.

Put D=pr_A^*θ and Dhat=pr_Adual^*θdual. We resolve degree-four classes in the linearly independent rational basis

\[
 D^2/2,\quad DP,\quad DDhat,\quad P^2/2,\quad PDhat,\quad Dhat^2/2.
\]

The symbolic exterior-algebra calculation gives

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \operatorname{rank}\Phi(v\boxtimes w)
   &=rs'+sr'+2edd',\\
 b:=\operatorname{coeff}_{P}(c_1\Phi(v\boxtimes w))
   &=sr'+edd',\\
 q:=\operatorname{coeff}_{P^2/2}
        (\operatorname{ch}_2\Phi(v\boxtimes w))
   &=sr'.
 \end{aligned}
\]

These identities follow by bilinearity from the nine basis calculations recorded in the output file. Since

\[
 c_2(E)=c_1(E)^2/2-\operatorname{ch}_2(E),
\]

the coefficient of P²/2 in c2 is b²−q. For even e we have q≡b modulo2, so b²−q is even.

This congruence persists under integral sums and differences of the K-classes, since both b and q are additive. It also persists under tensor products: if E and F have ranks r_E,r_F, then

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 b(EF)&=r_Eb(F)+r_Fb(E),\\
 q(EF)&=r_Eq(F)+r_Fq(E)+2b(E)b(F).
 \end{aligned}
\]

The other products of degree-two basis vectors do not contribute to the P²/2 coordinate. Thus q≡b modulo2 remains true throughout this ring of constructed classes. Products of two integral divisors likewise have even P²/2 coefficient.

Consequently the integral span of these particular second Chern classes and divisor products cannot contain a class with odd P²/2 coefficient, and in particular cannot produce P²/2 itself.

## What has not been shown

There is no claim that the full algebraic K-group of A×Adual is generated integrally by the transformed exterior products. A derived-category generation statement with idempotent completion would not establish the required generation of K-classes without further argument. Nor is there a claim about arbitrary surfaces, arbitrary sheaves on the product, cycles obtained after varying the surface, or all characteristic classes arising from Markman's deformation methods.

The exact residual question therefore remains the integral numerical algebraicity of P²/2 from C.4r. This calculation explains why this fixed-A method does not settle it.
