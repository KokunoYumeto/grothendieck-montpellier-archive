# Exact obstruction for Lagrangian-surface divisor pushforwards

Status: **proved method exclusion; no main-gate movement and no priority claim**.

We prove that every divisor on each of two proposed full-span Lagrangian surfaces has an even mixed Matsusaka coefficient after mapping to `A × A^dual`, when `A` is a generic type-(1,2) surface with `End(A)=Z`. This checks all divisor classes, not merely the fundamental surface class or the restrictions of ambient divisors.

The decisive property is that the surface's Albanese variety admits an odd-degree isogeny from a power of `A`. On `A^n`, every integral curve homomorphism is `lambda_d S`, with `S` a symmetric integral matrix. A homomorphism to `A × A^dual` has rows `(r, lambda s)`, and its mixed curve coefficient is `2 r S s^t`. Finite-flat pullback along an odd-degree isogeny preserves the parity obstruction. Albanese factorization handles every divisor curve on the surface.

The full proof is `C4_lagrangian_divisor_obstruction.tex`. It is intentionally unnumbered and separate from the integrated main results. The parent may assign a label if it is useful in the final methods discussion; it should not be counted as a resolution of the remaining surface case.

Their associated type-(1,2) surface is `A_0/G`, with `|G|=3`, and the quotient surface has Albanese variety `(A_0 × A_0)/diag K`, with `K=(Z/3)^2`. The two are related by odd-degree isogenies. ## Independent check

The cycles agent independently checked the full algebraic argument and its treatment of arbitrary surface divisors. In particular, that audit confirmed the direction of the isogeny, the factor of two in the mixed block, the odd-degree pullback argument, and the absence of any ambient-NS-generation hypothesis.

## Remaining gate

The argument controls only curves obtained from varieties whose Albanese is odd-isogenous to a power of the original surface. It does not control all curves spanning `A × A^dual`. The exact integral numerical Poincare-cycle criterion remains unchanged. Likewise no consequence for arbitrary supersingular threefolds in characteristics two or three is asserted.
