# Independent audit of C.3q

The current local-Picard abelian-subgroup lifting theorem in research/agents/real_weil/C3_abelian_lifting.tex passes the independent proof and exact-source audit, in its stated normal essentially-finite-type positive-characteristic scope.

Kollar (2016), Definition3.4 and its following paragraph, printed p468, explicitly states finite generation of the local Neron–Severi group in positive characteristic when the source is normal. The introductory statement about only the prime-to-p part in greater generality is not a contradiction: normality is the additional hypothesis used here. The proof needs only bounded prime-to-p torsion and chooses one prime ell not occurring in that finite torsion subgroup. Theorem1.7(ii), printed p463, gives the finite-type unipotent restriction kernel for a normal source of dimension at least4; Definition3.3 explains the kernel even when the divisor itself is not S2. Replacing the divisor by its finite normalization preserves its regular puncture and therefore that kernel.

C.3p lifts every ell-power torsion point of B uniquely to ell-power torsion in G. Its component-group image vanishes, so it lies in the reduced identity component G0. The image of a homomorphism of algebraic groups is closed, and ell-power torsion is Zariski dense in B. Thus B is contained in r(G0). Taking the reduced identity component of its inverse image still maps onto B: finitely many translates of a proper connected closed image could not cover B. This justifies the passage to a smooth connected commutative finite-type extension E of B.

The unipotent kernel U has bounded p-power exponent as a group scheme, not just on k-points. The upper-unitriangular representation argument proves this even for nonreduced unipotent group schemes: for p^e at least the matrix size, (1+M)^(p^e)=1. Multiplication by p^e on the commutative E is U-invariant and therefore descends along the faithfully flat quotient E→B to a morphism B→E. The resulting composite to B is multiplication by p^e. Its scheme-theoretic image A is proper, connected, and reduced, hence an abelian variety over the perfect field. The first isogeny's kernel is contained in B[p^e]; the composition identity then shows the second isogeny also has finite p-primary kernel. All equalities are scheme-theoretic.

The theorem proves a lift into the specified henselized-product local Picard functor. It correctly does not claim that A has an actual universal family on the fixed product X times A or that all of its points are ordinarily algebraically equivalent to zero there. That further realization assertion remains separate. Consequently this result supplies a precise group-functor reduction and does not by itself close ordinary degree-one surjectivity in positive characteristic.
