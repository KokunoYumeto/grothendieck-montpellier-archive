# C.4v: full real multiplication

We prove a new affirmative family rather than merely a reformulation of the residual gate. For a characteristic-zero abelian variety of dimension g with the full ring of integers of a totally real degree-g field acting and fixed by the Rosati involution of a polarization, the gcd of Euler characteristics of line bundles is one. Therefore all such surfaces and threefolds are actual direct summands of products of Jacobians by C.4j.

The proof is an integral trace-lattice argument. The homology is projective of rank two over the full integer ring. Its balanced integral alternating forms form an invertible codifferent-valued determinant module. A local generator supplies a perfect pairing at any chosen rational prime; its residue lifts globally, and a large positive multiple of that prime times an existing polarization supplies positivity without changing perfection. All balanced forms have Hodge type (1,1), as shown explicitly on the real two-plane decomposition. The prime may be two; no inversion of two is hidden.

The same result includes simple varieties whose full endomorphism ring is the maximal order of a CM field, using the unique positive involution.
