# Independent audit of C.3r

The complete construction in research/sections/C3_fermat_boundary.tex passes the independent mathematical audit for every integer r at least1. It proves failure of ordinary rational J^r restriction surjectivity in local dimension exactly N=2r+1, over C, with regular source and divisor punctures. It makes no unsupported assertion at all larger N with fixed r.

## The integral left inverse

The smooth Fermat cubic V has dimension2r-1. Each join R_lambda has dimension r, and its incidence projective bundle Z_lambda over the plane cubic E has fibre P^(r-1); thus [Z_lambda] has exactly codimension r in E times V. The homogeneous fibre notation is consistent with that bundle and does not require coordinates trivializing O_E(1).

For lambda=1 and a primitive cube root zeta, the two equations in each coordinate pair have invertible determinant zeta-1. All first2r-2 coordinates vanish in the intersection, leaving precisely E. This intersection misses both linear vertices. Along E, the two joins have complementary paired-coordinate normal directions in V; their common tangent directions are exactly T_E. The correspondence intersection in E times V times E is therefore proper and transverse, and its scheme-theoretic support is the diagonal copy of E. The multiplicity is1. Hence the composite is integrally Delta_E, not merely a nonzero multiple or an equality in cohomology. For r=1 the two chosen correspondences are already Delta_E.

## Survival modulo the hyperplane ideal

The cohomology degrees used in weak Lefschetz are strictly below the middle degree2r-1: H^(2r-3)(V,Q)=0 and H^(2r-2)(V,Q) is generated by H^(r-1). Thus every rational codimension-(r-1) cycle b splits as cH^(r-1)+b0 with b0 homologically trivial. Its Abel–Jacobi target is zero. Compatibility of Abel–Jacobi with multiplication by the divisor and with the transpose correspondence sends H b0 to a homologically trivial divisor on E with zero Abel–Jacobi class. For divisors on E that class map is the identity on Pic0, so the divisor itself vanishes in Pic(E) tensor Q. This is exactly the stronger conclusion needed, rather than merely cycle-class vanishing.

The degree of the transpose image of H^r is the degree of the join R_zeta, namely3: it is the scheme-theoretic intersection of the cubic V with r-1 independent hyperplanes. Applying the integral left inverse to a putative equality Gamma1 alpha=Hb and taking degrees forces3c=0, hence c=0 and alpha=0. The r=1 argument by degree3 gives the same conclusion. The point with a transcendental first coordinate determines an infinite-order Pic0(E) class since the elliptic curve, its chosen origin, and all its torsion points are defined over Qbar.

## Passage to the puncture and vanishing on the source

The homogeneous cubic cone is projectively normal and its only affine singularity is the vertex. Its strict henselization is the displayed quotient of the regular strict-local ring. The previously independently checked C.3e injection therefore preserves the nonzero rational quotient class. The projective incidence is an actual family over E, and its flat pullback along the punctured cone and then the strict-local limit is still an actual family on the fixed puncture. Thus the target class is ordinarily algebraically trivial in precisely the convention stated in C.3r.

The independent primary vanishing input is Skalit, Corollary6.4(i), author p27. It applies to any geometrically regular map of noetherian local rings with henselian target. The map C→R in this construction is geometrically regular and the base has dimension0, so CH^r(R)=0 for every r>0. Chow localization makes CH^r(R)→CH^r(X) surjective; the source puncture's positive-codimension Chow groups vanish as claimed. This argument alone establishes the required source vanishing and avoids any need to extrapolate a Gersten theorem to mixed characteristic or an unproved class of local rings.
