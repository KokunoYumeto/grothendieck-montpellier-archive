## Proof checks

1. **Smooth top weights.** For a smooth m-dimensional stratum over k=Fpbar, Levine's rational comparison identifies weight w of K_n with CH^w(-,n). The degree-n chain group vanishes if w>m+n. In weight m+n the chains are interior closed points of the n-cube over closed points of the stratum. Every such point is k-rational; proper pushforward from the point gives the asserted surjection from copies of CH^n(k,n). There are no chains in degree n-1, so this argument really applies to all cycles and then to their boundary quotient. Totaro's integral comparison identifies CH^n(k,n) with Milnor K_n(k). For n>0 its rationalization is zero because k* is torsion. The projective n=0 degree statement follows by a smooth projective curve through each pair of points and torsion of its finite-field-defined Jacobian points.

2. **Closed-union descent.** A union of SNC components has a reduced scheme-theoretic intersection in the decomposition used in the induction; the affine square is a Milnor square of surjective ring maps. Geisser--Hesselholt's Theorem C applies without a regularity assumption and in every integer degree. Thus the square of nonconnective K-theory spectra is cartesian after inverting p. TT open-cover descent globalizes the assertion.

3. **Adams operations: exact scope, with no regular-only shortcut.** Levine first describes Soule's sheaf operations for a regular scheme on p257. The next paragraph on p258 explicitly changes to Grayson's construction for an arbitrary scheme. It describes functoriality in the exact category of vector bundles, extension to any iterated homotopy fibre, agreement with the classical operations on K0, and delooping of the Adams operation. Apply this construction to the closed-union square itself; the connecting map is the homotopy-fibre boundary and therefore commutes with the same operation. There is no use of a support-to-subscheme devissage identification and hence no Thom/codimension weight shift. Lemma2.1 itself has regular-ambient/normal-crossing hypotheses. We do NOT apply that statement to the singular union. Instead we use the elementary Q[L]-module filtration argument proved at the beginning of its proof on p259: finite generalized-eigenspace decompositions are stable under the subquotients and extensions in the descent sequence. Induction from smooth strata supplies exactly the needed hypotheses. I requested that the owner make this source-scope distinction explicit; it is already the mathematical argument of the typed proof.

4. **SNC induction.** For C of dimension m-1, the predecessor K_(n+1)(C) in weight m+n is its top weight of a positive K-group and is zero. Above that weight it vanishes by the upper bound. At n=0 the end groups are K1(C) in weight m (top positive weight) and K0(C) in weight m (above dimension). Both vanish. This proves K0(E) in weight d is the direct sum of CH0 of the smooth components, hence Q^c. Empty/disconnected intersections do not affect the induction.

5. **Finite detection.** KST applies to the stated noetherian abstract blow-up square. The local K0 groups of R and its artinian quotients are Z. Quotienting their diagonal structure-sheaf graph leaves a constant pro-group K0(Z) mapping monomorphically to the thickening system. The kernels have inclusion transition maps, so pro-zero implies an actual finite-stage zero kernel. GH nilpotent invariance after inverting p then injects rational K0(Z) into K0(E). The finite smooth-model Chern-character comparison is the same previously checked input as C3az and is compatible with component restriction. Thus the component-degree map on CH^d(Z)_Q is injective. No identification of K with KH is used.

6. **Exceptional intersection matrix.** The matrix is symmetric because the same ample H restricts to both sides of each intersection. Off-diagonal entries are nonnegative. For the positive exceptional coefficient vector a, Ma has strictly negative coordinates because -D is ample on each component. The displayed weighted energy identity proves negative definiteness without requiring a connected dual complex. The exceptional complete-intersection curves therefore span all component degree vectors. Combined with injectivity this gives the exact claimed isomorphism and Chow localization kills the entire top Chow group of the puncture.
