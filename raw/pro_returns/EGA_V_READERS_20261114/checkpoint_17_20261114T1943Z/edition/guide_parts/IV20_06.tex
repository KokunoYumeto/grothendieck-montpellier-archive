\subsection*{IV 20.6: Connexion d'une section hyperplane quelconque (cote 25, sites 25--26)}
The short connectedness section combines the geometric connectedness of the generic hyperplane section from 3.3 with the Zariski connectedness theorem in the form cited as15.6.3. Its argument uses the proper incidence family, dominance of the dual projective space by its irreducible components, and the geometric unibranchness of the regular parameter space, then applies the cited result to all fibres. The author adds properness of $X$ in the margin of site25 and strengthens the dimension comparison by a handwritten lower bar. The final paragraph on site26 asks for a generalization in the sense of4.4, expressly includes sections of excess dimension, and proposes collecting all the connectedness material, including3.3 and4.4, in one number. The next proposed application heading is cancelled on site26 and reappears as7 on site27. Numbering is part of the unfinished revision: the handwritten proposition and the two references on26 read6.1, while the overwritten section numeral on25 remains uncertain between6 and5 and its initial capital has overlapping C/E traces. These are retained rather than reconciled by renumbering. The first connecting pen stroke in the properness paragraph has no visible arrowhead, while the two later occurrences do; the checked source preserves that graphical evidence. Sources: \href{https://grothendieck.umontpellier.fr/web/viewer.html?file=../25.pdf\#page=26}{site25} and \href{https://grothendieck.umontpellier.fr/web/viewer.html?file=../25.pdf\#page=27}{site26}; the explicit published EGA IV reference is15.6.3.
