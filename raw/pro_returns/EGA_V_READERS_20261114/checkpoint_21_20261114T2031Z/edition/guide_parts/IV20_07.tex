\subsection*{IV 20.7: Application to hyperplane sections and multisections of specified type (cote 25, sites 27--30)}
This section turns the preceding open subsets of the dual projective space into families of hyperplane sections and then into multisections. The discussion moves from rational points over an infinite field to sections near a chosen base point, and then to finite separable residue-field extensions and an étale change of base. The author redirects the flatness argument from the old references4.7--4.8 to the revised number5 on site28. A cancelled preliminary passage on that page gives way to the remords on site29: the singular locus for a property P is allowed to have a specified dimension, and the resulting formulations7.2--7.3 track its dimension after taking a hyperplane section. The margin adds another case to4.3 through published EGA IV12.2.1, with the final parenthesized reference still doubtful. The two expanded subscripts on site29 visibly read $\xi,\xi(s)$, while the intervening G has only $\xi(s)$; the neighbourhood is printed as a neighbourhood of s ``dans E''. Those forms remain in the text. Proposition7.4 and remarks7.5 develop the construction of étale multisections, including the successive reduction of fibre dimension; the final marginal note proposes avoiding an ensemble whose faint adjective remains doubtful. The cancelled discussion across sites29--30 invokes EGA III7 and Stein factorization for geometric connected components. Its replacement margin cites EGA IV12.2.1, with the first Roman numeral unresolved and the second legibly(xi). The earlier cancelled Remords on site27 separates openness by the valuative criterion from the flatness conclusion using EGA IV15.2.2; it records a proposed reorganization of4.6--4.8, rather than a completed renumbering.
