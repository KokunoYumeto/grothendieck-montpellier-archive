These remain reported manuscript citations, without an invented replacement or an assertion that the published numbered statement was verified. A search result for the preliminary chapter 0,15.2.2 was excluded from this concordance: its displayed subject is regular sequences, and the reference explicitly includes chapter 0.

Large primary PDFs sometimes exceeded the retrieval tool's size limit. Only portions actually returned and read were used. Search results also surfaced an EGA V translation; it was not opened or used to decide, corroborate or correct a reading. Subsequent searches were restricted to the primary publication hosts.
