\uline{Corollary} 16.10. Let k be a field, E a vector space of finite dimension
over k, $Q \hand{\in} \mathrm{Sym}^2(E)$. Equivalent conditions:

(i) Q is smooth.

(ii) $\overset{\hand{\sim}}{d}(Q) \hand{\neq} 0$.

(iii) There is a \var{\xout{finite}}{}{typewriter strike-out}{ega25_25_178-v0001} extension k' of k such that $Q\hand{\otimes} k'$ is
isomorphic to the standard form.

(iii bis) As in (iii), with k' a finite extension of k.

This is a trivial consequence of 16.9. Note that when k is
algebraically closed, (iii) and (iii bis) are replaced by the
more striking condition: Q is isomorphic to the standard form.

Proof of 16.9. We \var{\xout{obviously have}}{}{typewriter strike-out}{ega25_25_178-v0002} can obviously
suppose E of constant rank n. We shall obviously have
\[ \text{(iii bis)} \hand{\Longrightarrow} \text{(iii)} \hand{\Longrightarrow} \text{(ii)} \]
given that for the standard form the modified discriminant is 1.
We shall now prove (ii)\hand{$\Longrightarrow$}(iii bis), then (i)\hand{$\Longleftrightarrow$}(ii).

Let $\underline{E}_o = \underline{O}_S^{\ n}$ , $Q_o$ the standard form on $\underline{E}_o$ . Consider the
functor
\[ \underline{\mathrm{Isom}}( (\underline{E}_o,Q_o),(\underline{E},Q)) :\ (\mathrm{Sch})_{/S}^{\ o} \hand{\longrightarrow} (\mathrm{Ens}) , \]
whose value at every S' over S consists of the set of isomorphisms
$\underline{E}_{o\,S'} \hand{\to} \underline{E}_{S'}$ compatible with the forms $Q_{o\,S'}$ and $Q_{S'}$ . It is immediate
(without any condition on \var{\inserted{$\underline{E}$ or}}{$\underline{E}$ or}{insertion}{ega25_25_178-v0003} Q) that this functor is representable by a prescheme
P affine and of finite presentation over S, which is a \var{\inserted[typed]{sub}}{sub}{insertion}{ega25_25_178-v0004}\unsure{pre}scheme of W =
$W(\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}_{\underline{O}_S}(\underline{E}_o,\underline{E}))$ , and a closed subprescheme of \var{\deleted{$\underline{\mathrm{Isom}}(\underline{E}$}}{}{deletion}{ega25_25_178-v0005} the open
$\underline{\mathrm{Isom}}\ (\underline{E}_o,\underline{E}_1)$ of W \hand{/}. The implication (ii)\hand{$\Longrightarrow$}(iii bis) will be proved if
we show that when $\overset{\hand{\sim}}{d}(Q)$ is invertible, P is faithfully flat
over S: we shall indeed take S'=P.
