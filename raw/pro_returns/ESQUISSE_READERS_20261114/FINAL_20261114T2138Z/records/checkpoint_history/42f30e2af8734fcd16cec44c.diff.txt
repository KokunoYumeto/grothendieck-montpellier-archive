Checkpoints01–04 predate embedded inventories, so their numbered inventories were derived from the preserved ZIP bytes. Later inventories preserve the embedded CHECKPOINT_CONTENTS.tsv verbatim. [records/CHECKPOINT_MANIFEST_PROVENANCE.tsv](records/CHECKPOINT_MANIFEST_PROVENANCE.tsv) records that distinction together with ZIP and inventory hashes and counts. Runtime downloads, font caches, temporary TeX outputs and duplicate intermediate diagnostic renderings are reproducible intermediates; they are excluded from publication while sanitized logs, source hashes, measured results and actual review observations are retained. The diagnostic build baseline is recorded in [ORIGINAL_BUILD_BASELINE.json](ORIGINAL_BUILD_BASELINE.json); its unchecked PDFs are not readers.
