## 4. The function-field model (proved, classical)

For q = 1 mod 3 and f in F_q[x] squarefree of degree d, |sum_x chi_3(f(x))| <= (d - 1) sqrt(q) (Weil); the curve
y^3 = f(x) has genus d - 1 when 3 does not divide d. Stepanov's method (W. M. Schmidt, Equations over finite fields)
proves it with multiplicity supplied by derivatives in characteristic p ((x^q)' = 0). Over Z there are no such
derivatives; Alpoge's replacement is the chain of theta lifts on the metaplectic double cover, about sqrt(X) of them.
The cubic analogue needs a multiplicity source compatible with the three-way phase.

## 5c. Pushing on: the exceptional definite hosts, and the mirror deciding which fields they take (25 Nov ~19:0x UTC)
