## The two trace statements

In their Proposition 6.2 and Corollary 6.3, positivity of the trace pairing on the tests for \(C_k\) is equivalent to RH for the Grössencharacters **of that \(k\)**. The exact source file in the checkpoint is `input_material/main/LIT_CCM_Weil_proof_adele_class_space/Yuri70v2.tex`, lines 2070 and 2104. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0703392)

The unconditional T50 gives the nonnegative lower limit of the defect on a convolution square. Assuming the trace-limit identity, this positivity passes to the Weil form and gives \(A\) back. ## The actual cubic obstruction

Put \(K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})\), \(F=\mathbb Q(\sqrt[3]{269})\), \(E=KF\), and let \(L=L_K(s,\eta)\) be the Hecke function of either nontrivial character of \(E/K\). It has rank one over \(K\) and degree two over \(\mathbb Q\). The archive records \(\zeta_F=\zeta L\) and \(\zeta_E=\zeta_K L^2\), including bad factors. Its logarithmic-derivative ramp needs the one-sided bound H04.4 on arbitrarily large multiplicative-power windows. A single bounded positive Weil window is insufficient for that step. Exact places are `modules/kummer_lambda.tex`, lines 11–20, 542–561; the 12th-cyclotomic base change in `modules/bcm_neutrality.tex`, lines 383–456; the author’s principal scope in `ALP_CONNES.tex`, lines 59–109. The quadratic original Type II endpoint exclusion at coefficient prime 269 is a scoped arithmetic exclusion. It leaves the all-character arithmetic bound open, as specified in `modules/cubic_quadratic_typeii_exclusion.tex`, lines 148–167 and `ALP_CONNES.tex`, line 593.

The workshop note `cubic_prime_test.tex` gives a second, independently derived diagnostic. Under the two inputs RH for \(\zeta\) and \(L(s,\chi_{-3})\), cubic RH is equivalent to square-root error, up to two logarithms, for the log-weighted primes \(p\equiv1\bmod3\) at which \(269\) is a cube. This follows from ordinary Dedekind factorization, the prime-ideal explicit formula and a reverse Mellin argument. It specializes Lagarias–Odlyzko's Theorem 9.1. [Paper and theorem](https://aareyanmanzoor.github.io/assets/articles/lagarias-odlyzko.pdf)

The note also proves a precise limitation on a proposed local projection. For each fixed \(m\), subtracting any function of \(p\bmod m\) from the trace \(a(p)=\#\{x^3=269\bmod p\}-1\) leaves prime mean-square error at least one. The best predictor is zero. The optimum exact-value prediction from any fixed congruence modulus makes mistakes on \(1/6\) of primes. Both statements are elementary deductions from equidistribution in the compositum of the \(S_3\) splitting field and the cyclotomic field. The same mean-square obstruction survives each finite sum of fixed unitary norm twists. The proof identifies which prime powers retain the cubic residual and extends the mean-square calculation to irreducible nonlinear finite-image characters of arbitrary Galois groups. This concerns the specified projection. It gives no independence theorem about RH statements.
