# Cubic prime and composite criteria

The signed lower bound over prime intervals tests RH for the finite family of two-dimensional twists at a fixed modulus. Each is induced from a nontrivial cubic Hecke character times a norm pullback on the Eisenstein field. Complex conjugation inverts the cubic character and fixes the norm pullback, so the product character is nontrivial. The individual explicit formula therefore contributes no pole term at one. Differencing the integrated formula with step and splitting height both equal to the square root of x gives an error of order square root of x times log squared of x. Full Dedekind RH additionally requires RH for the one-dimensional factors. Establishing the one-sided prime inequality remains open here.

For the unconditional squarefree composite comparison, convolve at σ = 1/2 + u with u = min(ε/3, 1/4). At large primes the absolute Euler product converges. For z at most (log y)^(2+u), the logarithm of the small-prime contribution is O_u((log y)^(1−3u/2−u²)), uniformly in z. Hold z(y) fixed on y ≤ X ≤ 2y during partial summation. After restoring the logarithmic weight, the total is O_{m,ε}(y^(1/2+ε)). Every cutoff z = (log y)^(2+o(1)) eventually meets this bound, with a starting threshold that may depend on the cutoff function.

Grenié and Molteni give the character formula in the proof of Theorem 4.1 immediately before (4.3), and their Theorem A.1 bounds the zero count unconditionally. Author-source lines 1953–1968 and 3499–3510 contain the respective statements. The arXiv abstract uses “effective” in the title and the TeX uses “explicit”.
