After Alpöge's proof

On 20 November L. Alpöge proved the Riemann hypothesis for every Dirichlet L-function, ζ included, and it is formalized in Lean. Everything below builds on that: what the three Claude sessions (main, S6, pequalnp) and their agents found and did in the days since.

2The strongest new results of the last runs

From the intake wave of 23 Nov: nine runs, each read by the field expert who knows it, most useful first.

checkedFinite fields of prime degree with no Riemann hypothesis

GRH_ALGORITHMS_FINISH (P145/P144) builds F_{p^r} deterministically in time polynomial in r, log p and a cyclotomic cofactor of Φ_d(p). It replaces the analytic auxiliary prime by algebra: adjoin Φ_ℓ, take a root, its norm, then Bézout, since r ∤ ℓ−1. Lenstra's construction problem gets a strictly smaller gate, unconditionally. The 159-page paper has every fix of its finishing prompt and is close to postable.

checkedThe Hodge conjecture for abelian varieties as a uniform bound over finite fields

HODGE_FINISH delivered two papers.

  • Paper A (66 pp.) proves the criterion from published inputs, with an explicit Weil engine. It includes the new theorem that every integral Gaussian Hermitian lattice of class [−3] embeds in U ⊥ U ⊥ ⟨1, −3⟩.
  • Paper B (88 pp.) gives construction obstructions and criteria for Mumford's fourfold: if one quadratic Lie block is algebraic, the Hodge conjecture holds for every power of it.

checkedGrothendieck's question on cote 8, sheet 49, answered

POLISH_CRISTAUX's Paper 19: the two Lubin–Tate lattices agree exactly when e ≤ p, and their tangent images always agree. Paper 8 adds that the least rank of a local system with a non-split co-Lie complex is 4, 3, 2 for p = 2, 3, ≥ 5. Papers 20–21 give full mod-2 monodromy, GL₈ and GL₈₀, for sections of the supersingular cubic square.

checkedSimple zeros as the growth of a finite positivity test

ZETA_HEAT, prompt 62, at its first checkpoint: under Alpöge's theorem, the simplicity of the zeta zeros is equivalent to an exact growth rate of the backward-heat Toeplitz minors, with the explicit constants π and 2π/√3. There is also a trace certificate of simplicity below any given height.

checkedThe cubic gate, from the arithmetic side

ALP_CONNES G219: at coefficient 7 the strict Selmer groups of the matched cubic Kummer family are nonzero. So the cubic-RH gate cannot close by "all strict groups vanish"; it has to absorb a measured, mostly bounded term. ALP_MACHINE's LIFT110 shows that abelian coefficient fields cannot carry normalized ideal generators, a second structural constraint on step 8 of Alpöge's formal development.

checkedAnd from the polish runs

Grothendieck's II c) holds exactly when the parametrizations fix the base pointwise; otherwise the space is equivalent to Homeo(Y). A collar removed from a hyperbolic surface can make the conformal factor change sign. Necessary conditions for Picard kernels on surfaces in characteristic p bear on Grothendieck's Picard question.

checkedLate on 23 Nov: the second intake wave

  • A finite test for simple zeta zeros, exact at every height (ZETA_HEAT, checkpoint 3). Under Alpöge's theorem, the zeros up to height T are all simple exactly when some finite trace defect of Suzuki's compressions, computed from primes and integrals with no zero locations, falls below 2/T^(2k). The supremum of the test is the first multiple zero, if there is one, and the heat-flow limit equals half its square. Every zero up to height 97.632 is certified simple from prime powers below e¹². The proof was followed step by step; four of the six certificate rows were recomputed, without rigour, to 9–10 digits.
  • Grothendieck's question on cote 133 answered no (NIGHT_GROTHENDIECK_QUESTIONS, Part A). He asked whether a local ring, or more generally K₀(k) = Z, is enough. Over the dual numbers Z_(p)[ε]/ε² the trivial classes do not generate, at every prime, already in degree p. Characteristic 0 is necessary. Over Z_(p) a square-zero extension works exactly when its ideal is a Q-vector space. Our reading of the page stands, with two corrections.
  • Picard kernels in characteristic p (POLISH_ALGEBRAIC_GEOMETRY). On a normal surface, an algebraically trivial p-torsion line bundle that is trivial on a curve of positive self-intersection never reaches conductor equality: a short proof, checked in full. At equality on a very ample curve the run now pins the shape: a ≥ 5, a ≠ 6, K ∼ (pa − 1)Y, a strange embedding, a general-type quotient.
  • Flatness is not needed for Elliott's plethory (POLISH_TOPOLOGY_PERIODS). A non-Noetherian inclusion has every polynomial tensor map an isomorphism, yet neither S nor its differentials are flat. The same run finds π₂ of the TOP concordance space of S³ to be Z/2, against 0 in PL.
  • Alpöge's formal interface needs metabelian coefficient fields (ALP_MACHINE, LIFT112–113). Normalized generators for an odd class subgroup of order g force degree divisible by g over the abelian part, and that degree is attained. Over Q(ω), which has class number one, the obstruction is empty, so it does not touch the moonshine prompt's route.

3One map across the fields

You asked how the Riemann proofs map through Hodge, NCG, GCT, moonshine and towards global Langlands. Eight structural notes now lay our results on one ladder: Dirichlet (GL₁/Q), Hecke characters, Artin, GL₂, GL_n. Two map agents also wrote 60 maps onto the P vs NP fleet's proof graph. Nothing is graded. The findings below are the notes' own, with their check state.

The convergence. Four of our lines wait on one next rung: RH for the cubic Hecke character of Q(ω). These are GRH_ALGORITHMS Q02 (the field-construction gate at r = 3), ALP_CONNES and ALP_MACHINE's H04.4, ALP_MACHINE Q3, and Koiran's HN for cubic solution fields. For F = Q(∛269) this is ζ_F/ζ: the L-function of a weight-one dihedral newform of level 3·269² = 217083, with the coefficients matched prime by prime. P017 and H04.4 turn out to be the same one-sided estimate for one prime sum.

  • on paperOne mechanism in four places. Positivity of a Toeplitz form detects zeros: Weil windows, backward-heat minors, Li sections, GCT structure constants. In each, the defect is positive unconditionally, and the hypothesis is that its limit vanishes. Heat flow damps with a Gaussian; the window truncates.
  • on paperPositivity breaks at the next rung by induction. Connes-type positivity fails for the Dedekind zeta of any non-abelian field, which has multiple zeros. It has to be posed per irreducible representation, where MAS_ARTIN's S₅ chain makes it automatic through Cohen's theorem.
  • on paperThe arithmetic quotient of GT̂. Λ₀ is normal in GT̂, and GT̂/Λ₀ ≅ Gal(Q(μ_∞, 2^(1/∞))/Q). Alpöge's theorem governs its cyclotomic and mod-2 layer; the mod-4 layer runs into weight-one theta series over Q(i).
  • checkedPermanent and determinant as one zeta function. perm X is the x₁⋯x_n coefficient of det(I − diag(x)X)⁻¹, and det X is the same coefficient of det(I + diag(x)X): the symmetric-power denominator against the exterior-power numerator. Checked exactly for n ≤ 4.
  • on paperThe fleet's hard link L-gct-086 in cumulant form. Take the logarithm of the rook polynomial. Its leading terms (k−1)!/k·p_k give the coefficients 1/2, 2/3, 3/2 found earlier, and 3p₂² in L-gct-134 is exactly the disconnected part.
  • on paperLevel spaces are deformations of special points. A level space with one symmetry has dimension at most n²/q < n, so the extremal ones are not special points. The PSL₂(7) slice is Klein's quartic, with 24 level lines (numerical so far).
  • checked#SAT mod 2 is a Hasse–Witt scalar, a Frobenius on a Hodge piece (NEG_NIGHT). The hardness lives in the multiplicity and the rank, not in p.
  • on paperWhere the K-theory size argument stops. The class-number size method clears cyclotomic class components exactly when φ(d) ≤ 2, the orders KTOWER cleared. Order 5 needs an arithmetic annihilator.
  • on paperOur lattice J carries a new Hecke eigenform: a level-3, weight-12 newform. The Leech lattice's theta series has only Δ.

7What the second passes taught

48,992 decided places from the returned passes, analysed by page type and by kind of error. This is round 4 of the analysis, and it continues.

  • Content drives difficulty more than handwriting. Formula-dense pages in the late hand carry about 13 reading corrections per 100 words, 16 a page. Prose and typed pages carry 2–4 per 100 words. Even on the dense pages, most corrections are in the words between formulas and in the struck and inserted text.
  • One correction in five has no flag. The first reading had no doubt mark, and Hua's text agreed or was absent. Only a full pass finds these.
  • Hua's text flags but never decides. Where it differs from ours it was right 5–6 % of the time, in prose and formulas alike. The recollection that it is better on prose is not borne out by these returns.
  • A blind third reading (ChatGPT Work) flags 62–91 % of the corrections no cheap check finds, but is itself right there only 0–24 % of the time. It locates; the Pro pass decides on a crop.
  • Crops: 36 % of the decisions in SP72–SP117 had no usable crop. A deciding crop is now at most 40 % of the scan's width, from the full-resolution scan.

All of this went into the error sheet that every pass carries. All 94 waiting prompts carry it, and the loop runs again after every wave.
