## Verdict

**No K17 or KTOWER theorem uses the top cases of DEKM Theorem 4.5. K17-S17-JOINT does not use DEKM at all. Pintar does
not cite DEKM. Mok-Zheng cite DEKM only for K_8(Z) = 0, which is DEKM Theorem 6.4, not the top cases of Theorem 4.5.**

The gap (K17 cp05; decided against arXiv:1910.11598v1 in my K17 part 4 intake, §1) concerns the three endpoint cases
of Theorem 4.5:
- (N = 8, k = 12) and (N = 9, 10, k = 11) in Voronoi degree;
- in Steinberg degrees, H_5(GL_8; St_8), H_3(GL_9; St_9) and H_2(GL_10; St_10).

Their cells, of perfection rank 13 or 12, are not in the displayed enumeration. The displayed tables support
H_q(GL_8; St_8) = 0 for q ≤ 4, H_q(GL_9; St_9) = 0 for q ≤ 2 and H_q(GL_10; St_10) = 0 for q ≤ 1 (mod small primes).
