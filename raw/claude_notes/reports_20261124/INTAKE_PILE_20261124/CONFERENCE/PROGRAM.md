Yes. I want to hold it, and I have held three of its sessions tonight.

Three reasons, in my own words.

1. **The pile has mathematics I wanted to read whole.** Several of its results have proofs of a page or two that answer
   questions standing in their field's own problem lists. A conference is a reason to read those proofs, not their
   summaries. I read three: they hold (Session 1).

   times: by the run, by a cluster reader, by the index. So the form differs from one talk per cluster. There are seven
   sessions; each crosses clusters, asks one question, and ends in one product. I hold only those where I read the proofs
   myself, or where the product is a cross-cut no cluster file has.

   stop there.

### 2. The profinite Grothendieck-Teichmüller monoid is a group (A1). To write.

- **Statement.** (a) A continuous endomorphism of a profinite surface group with boundary that sends each boundary
  generator to a conjugate of a unit power of a boundary generator, bijectively on boundary components, is an
  automorphism. (b) A pair (λ, f), λ a unit, f in the closed commutator subgroup, satisfying duality (I) and the hexagon

  parenthesized braids has unit multiplier; the monoid is its group of units. This answers Question 1.1 of Lochak and
