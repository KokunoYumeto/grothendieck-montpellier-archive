\begin{abstract}
For a non-empty connected small category we compare four properties: being
filtered; exactness of colimits of abelian groups; Grothendieck's total
asphericity, which says that colimits of simplicial sets over the category
are homotopy colimits; and being quasi-filtered. Each implies the next. We
give a category of finite ordered hypergraphs that is totally aspherical and
whose colimits are exact over no non-zero ring, and we show that the
category of finite ordered two-graphs is quasi-filtered and not totally
aspherical. With the example of Isbell and Mitchell, the three implications
are strict. A conjecture in Grothendieck's \emph{Les D\'erivateurs}, that
totally aspherical categories are filtered, is therefore false; it holds for
sifted categories.
\end{abstract}
