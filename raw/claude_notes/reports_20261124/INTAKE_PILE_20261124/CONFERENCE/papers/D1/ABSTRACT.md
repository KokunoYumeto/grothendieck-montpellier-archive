# Exact, total and filtered small categories

**Abstract.** For a non-empty connected small category we compare four properties: being filtered; exactness of
colimits of abelian groups; Grothendieck's total asphericity, which says that colimits of simplicial sets over the
category are homotopy colimits; and being quasi-filtered, which means that two arrows with a common source can be
completed to a commutative square and that two parallel arrows are equalized by an integral combination of arrows
whose coefficients sum to 1. Each implies the next. We give a category of finite ordered hypergraphs that is totally
aspherical and whose colimits are exact over no non-zero ring, and we show that the category of finite ordered
two-graphs (sets of triples that meet every four-element set in an even number of triples) is quasi-filtered and not
totally aspherical. With the example of Isbell and Mitchell, the three implications are strict. A conjecture in
Grothendieck's *Les Dérivateurs*, that totally aspherical categories are filtered, is therefore false; it holds for
sifted categories, those over which colimits of sets commute with finite products.
