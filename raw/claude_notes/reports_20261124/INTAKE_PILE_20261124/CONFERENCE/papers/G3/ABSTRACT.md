# Multiplication by 2 and by 6 through products of Jacobians, and the Jacobian index of a very general abelian variety of dimension 4, 5 and 6

For an abelian variety A over an algebraically closed field, let s_J(A) be the least positive integer n such that multiplication by n factors through a product of Jacobians of curves; s_J(A) = 1 exactly when A is a direct summand of such a product. We prove, in every characteristic, that multiplication by 2 factors through a product of Jacobians on every principally polarized abelian variety of dimension at most 5, and multiplication by 6 on every one of dimension 6; that s_J(A) divides gcd(2, delta(A)) for every abelian surface and gcd(6, delta(A)) for every abelian threefold, where delta(A) is the greatest common divisor of the Euler characteristics of line bundles on A. The known bound is (2g-1)!. For a complex principally polarized A with minimal class gamma = theta^{g-1}/(g-1)!, let m(A) be the least positive multiple of gamma that is the class of an algebraic cycle; Matsusaka's construction gives s_J(A) | m(A), and we prove m(A) | s_J(A)^2. With the theorems of Engel, de Gaay Fortman and Schreieder this gives the exact values s_J(A) = 2 for a very general principally polarized abelian variety of dimension 4 and 5, and s_J(A) = 6 in dimension 6; and every prime p <= g/2 divides s_J(A) in dimension g. Finally, s_J(A) is exactly the least integer n for which n times the inverse of the isomorphism H^{2g-1}(A)(g-1) -> H^1(A^) is induced by an integral algebraic cycle; this answers, for abelian varieties, the second question that Grothendieck asks on sheet 70 of cote 14 of his Montpellier archive.
