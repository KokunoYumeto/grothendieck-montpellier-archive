# Exactness of colimits and amenability are independent for categories of finite substructures

**Abstract.** Let M be a countable homogeneous structure, G its automorphism group and A the category of its finite
substructures and embeddings. The colimit functor on diagrams of R-modules indexed by A is exact if and only if the
functor of coinvariants N ↦ N_G is exact on the R[G]-modules in which every element has an open stabilizer; this is a
property of the topos of continuous G-sets. We compare it with amenability of G, the existence of invariant
probability measures on compact spaces on which G acts, and show that neither implies the other. For the generic
partially ordered set with an independent linear order, colimits are exact over every ring, while the group is not
amenable by a result of Kechris and Sokić. For the vector space of countable dimension over the field with two
elements, with a generic linear order on its non-zero vectors, the group is amenable and colimits are exact over the
rational numbers, but they are exact over no ring in which 2 is not invertible. The first statement rests on a
transfer theorem: if a subgroup H has exact coinvariants and finitely many orbits on each G/G_B, then exactness for G
over a field k is equivalent to the existence of a G-invariant, finitely additive, k-valued function of total mass 1
on the expansions that H defines; positivity is not required. For posets this function has integer values, given by
Euler characteristics of cells of order cones, which are values of the measure of Harman and Snowden for the
automorphism group of the ordered real line. A consequence is that the separation property proved by Evans for Ramsey
structures holds for a structure that is not a Ramsey structure but is obtained from one by forgetting a relation.
