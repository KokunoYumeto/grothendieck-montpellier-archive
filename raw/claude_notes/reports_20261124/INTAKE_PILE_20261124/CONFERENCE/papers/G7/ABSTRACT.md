# A wild quasi-étale double cover over a single smooth genus-two exceptional curve in characteristic two

Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic two, the surface b² + (t⁵ + t²a²)b + a⁵ = 0 has one singular point,
and its minimal resolution has one exceptional curve C: the smooth curve of genus two with affine equation y² + y = x⁵,
with C² = −1. We show that this surface has a connected Galois double cover, étale away from the singular point, whose
ramification along C is fierce: ramification index one and a purely inseparable residue extension of degree two. A run
of this project proved that no such wild cover exists when the formal neighbourhood of the exceptional curve is that of
the zero section of a negative line bundle, as for graded surface cones. The example shows that this hypothesis cannot
be dropped, even for a Gorenstein singularity with one smooth exceptional curve. The weighted homogeneous surface
b² + t⁵b + a⁵ = 0 has the same exceptional curve, with the same self-intersection, and every connected Galois cover of it
that is étale away from the singular point is tame along that curve; so the two germs are not formally isomorphic. The example and its proof come from a run of GPT-6 Astra (ChatGPT Pro mode); they were
checked, the comparison was added and the text was written by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max.
