# The first L²-Betti number of aspherical manifolds and Poincaré duality groups of dimension at least three

**Abstract.** The L²-Betti numbers b_p⁽²⁾ of a finite complex measure the square-summable harmonic p-chains on its
universal cover. Singer's conjecture says that for a closed aspherical manifold of dimension n, one whose universal
cover is contractible, they vanish outside the middle degree n/2. A manuscript of OpenAI dated 25 September 2026,
released on 6 October 2026, proves the conjecture in dimension four; it is not refereed. Its proof assumes b₁⁽²⁾ > 0,
builds from functions of finite energy on the fundamental group Γ a compact boundary on which Γ acts, and reaches a
contradiction; dimension four enters only in a preliminary section. We show that the same argument gives
b₁⁽²⁾ = b_{n−1}⁽²⁾ = 0 for every finite aspherical Poincaré complex of formal dimension n ≥ 3, hence for every closed
aspherical topological manifold of dimension at least three (Theorem A), and for every finitely presented Poincaré
duality group of dimension n ≥ 3 (Theorem B). To do so we state what Sections 3-10 of the manuscript prove as a
criterion for groups (Theorem C): a finitely presented torsion-free group with one end, with H²(Γ; F₂Γ) = 0 and with
no splitting over a finite or two-ended subgroup, has b₁⁽²⁾ = 0; and we prove, in every dimension n ≥ 3, the facts
about Γ that the criterion needs. Theorems A and B depend on the manuscript's argument, which we cite and do not
reproduce. One AI referee has followed that argument line by line and found no fault; no human has checked it, or this
paper.
