## Findings

1. **Thirteen assertions or questions have a verdict with a proof checked outside its run.** Twelve of the thirteen are
   refutations or corrections, five of them with a positive statement in place of the one that fell (rows 7, 8, 9, 10,
   11); one confirms (row 3). That proportion says something about the source, not about its author: these are working
   notes, letters and margins, written fast and never sent to print, and the runs were pointed at the places marked
   doubtful.
2. **Independent agreement is on record in four places.** Row 9: three runs found counterexamples to Th. 5 separately.
   Row 3: three runs proved A1. Row 22: two runs found the same list. The topos-pushout example of SA10 B2.2 and LM5 C.3
   is the same example twice. This is a check type the board does not name; it should be written into the check events
   of those rows as "found independently by", with both locators.
3. **The runs sort by paper; the reader sorts by cote.** Cote 4 alone is behind six polished papers of POLISH_CRISTAUX;
   cote 149 behind H07, the Fermat line, the reconstruction rows, H16 and the tripod; cote 44 behind three runs. Nothing
   public says, for a given page, "this was decided, here".
4. **Three rows are closer to a text than the board shows.** Row 8 needs one reader for a page of the geometry map. Row
   13 is at stage W with only a literature search left. Row 5 needs one source opened.

## Decisions

1. **The register becomes a companion page of the archive edition**, built from the TSV, one line per assertion, each
   line linking to the posted text that proves the verdict. It is not a Hexagon paper: a paper must carry its proofs,
   and this page points to them. A row appears on the public page only once its verdict has a posted text; until then
   the TSV is our working list.
2. **Its tone.** The public page lists what was asked and what is now known. It keeps no tally of errors. This follows
   the user's standing decision that the data bank of Grothendieck's foibles is published only on the user's word
   (`PENDING.md`, section 6).
3. **Locators are checked on the scans before a row goes public.** INDEX already corrects one (row 1: 31/085 is a PDF
   page, the sheet is 84). The archive readers do this, row by row, when the row's text is posted.
4. **Row 8 gets its second reader now** (a0012f1f, Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max, who checked SA06 C.4m and C.4o): the
   map's NK2, about a page, and the abstracts-only citations of Beckmann-de Gaay Fortman and
   Engel-de Gaay Fortman-Schreieder opened at the statements.
5. **Rows 31 and 12 are the two open assertions worth a small prompt** (proposals 15 and 16); row 32 waits.
