- **Kummer characters of cyclotomic fields.** By row 2's first paper (its Theorem 1.1, not yet checked outside the
  run), GT̂ modulo Λ₀ is Gal(K₂/Q) with K₂ = Q(μ_∞, 2^{1/∞}), and Lochak-Schneps's question on the abelianization of GT̂
  is, modulo 2, the kernel of restriction to G_{K₂}. The layers Q(μ_n, 2^{1/n}) are the fields of Hooley's theorem on
  Artin's conjecture; their zeta functions are products of Dirichlet L-functions, which A settles, and of Kummer
  characters, which row 4's method reaches over Q(√−3). The quadratic characters of row 2 restrict to fourth-root Kummer
  characters.
- **Dirichlet L-values and K-groups of Z.** Row 5 and the lifting obstruction of SECTION_GHOSTS B.25 (N3) are governed
  by the eigenspaces of the class groups of Q(μ_ℓ). On Mok and Zheng's claim (hexagon:2611.00998, unrefereed) the
  obstruction vanishes for even r ≤ 60 at every prime.
- **Jacobi sums.** The L-functions of the Fermat Jacobians of row 1 are Hecke L-functions of Jacobi-sum characters of
  Q(μ_n); the same sums are the values of Ihara's power series, which gives the Galois action behind rows 2 and 5.
