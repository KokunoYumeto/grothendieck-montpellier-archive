## The question

LM7_PUSH E.65 (GPT-6 Astra, ChatGPT Pro mode; refereed by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max) attaches to an element of
`GT_0-hat` two frames, the unique pure conjugators of the torsion elements `s` (order 2) and `r` (order 3) of
`C_2 * C_3`, and from their classes modulo 12 the character `Delta`, which vanishes on Galois. These two frames come
from the 2-cycle and the 3-cycle relation. The pentagon is a relation of order five in the group of the sphere with
five points. **Main question: does the pentagon give a third frame, at the point of order five, and do the frames in
the five-point group give further characters of `GT-hat` that vanish on Galois; in particular, do the relations of
the five-point group force `Delta = 0` on `GT-hat`?**

Notation for the items: `Gamma` is the full mapping class group of the sphere with five unordered points,
`Gamma-hat` its profinite completion, `P Gamma-hat` the pure subgroup; `a`, `b`, `d` are standard elements of orders
5, 4, 3 (rotations with five points on a circle; four on a circle and one at a pole; three on a circle and two at the
poles); an element `F = (lambda, f)` of `GT-hat` acts on `Gamma-hat`.

## Items, each to be proved or refuted

- **O.1 Frames exist and are unique.** Every element of finite order of `Gamma-hat` is conjugate to an element of
  `Gamma`. For `t` in `{a, b, d}` the centralizer of `t` in `Gamma-hat` meets `P Gamma-hat` trivially; determine it
  if not. For `F` in `GT-hat`: `F(t) = alpha_t(F) t^{lambda} alpha_t(F)^{-1}` with a unique `alpha_t(F)` in
  `P Gamma-hat`, continuous in `F`.
- **O.2 Frames as primitives.** The pentagon relation says that `f` is a coboundary for the automorphism of order
  five of `P Gamma-hat` (Lochak-Schneps 1997, from memory), and `alpha_a(F)` is its canonical primitive. Write the
  cocycles of which `alpha_b(F)` and `alpha_d(F)` are the primitives, as words in `f`.
- **O.3 Coordinates.** `alpha_t(FG) = F(alpha_t(G)) alpha_t(F)`. Determine the finite quotients of `Gamma-hat` in
  which, after the correction that plays the role of the shear of LM7_PUSH E.61, the class of `alpha_t(F)` lies in
  the centralizer of the image of `t`; prove that the resulting coordinates are homomorphisms on the multiplier-one
  subgroup of `GT-hat`. For `t = a` the centralizer is a group of norm-one units of a quotient of `Z[zeta_5]`.
- **O.4 Projection to four points.** When a point is forgotten, the frames of `b` and `d` go to the frames of `s`
  and `r` of E.65. So the two parities of `Delta` are read off `alpha_b(F)` and `alpha_d(F)`.
- **O.5 The relations of the five-point group.** `Gamma` is generated by `a` and `b`; give its presentation in these
  generators. Derive the relations between `alpha_a(F)`, `alpha_b(F)`, `alpha_d(F)` that the relations of `Gamma`
  force. Reduce them at the levels where `Delta` is read and decide whether they force the two parities to agree:
  this proves `Delta = 0` on `GT-hat`, or shows that the five-point group imposes nothing on them. (This is route (b)
  of S.1 in `PUSH_ITEMS_LM7_CONTINUED.md`.)
- **O.6 Galois values.** For a Galois element the frame at the point fixed by `a` is the path cocycle to the special
  fibre there (five points at the fifth roots of unity; the curve `y^2 = x^5 - 1`, with complex multiplication by
  `Z[zeta_5]`, from memory). Compute the coordinate of O.3 on Galois as the action on the torsion of that fibre.
  Determine, over `Q^ab`, the intersections of the fields generated by the torsion of the three special fibres
  (`Z[i]`, `Z[zeta_3]`, `Z[zeta_5]`), two at a time and all three.
- **O.7 The characters.** Each intersection found in O.6 gives a relation that the frame coordinates satisfy on
  Galois, and so a continuous character of `GT-hat`, of finite order, that vanishes on Galois. List them all. For
  each: prove that it is zero on `GT-hat`, or exhibit an element of `GT-hat` on which it is not zero; the second
  outcome gives an element of `GT-hat` outside the Galois image and has to be written out step by step.
- **O.8 Six and seven points.** The same for the spheres with six and seven points: the torsion classes, their
  frames, the special fibres and their fields, and the characters. State which characters are new at each step and
  which are pulled back from fewer points.

## Hunches (the expert's; checked by no one)

- Frames exist and are unique in genus zero: torsion of the profinite groups is conjugate to geometric torsion and
  the action of `GT-hat` on it is conjugation and the power `lambda` (Collas 2012, from memory).
- The coordinate at the point of order five is a homomorphism into a projective norm-one group of `Z[zeta_5]`.
- Coincidences between the torsion fields of different special fibres are few and of small order, as for the two
  elliptic points, where there is one, of order two (the source of `Delta`).
- The relations of the five-point group tie the frames of orders 4 and 3. If they tie their parities, `Delta = 0`
  on `GT-hat` follows from genus zero alone; if not, the question stays with the harmonic equation
  (`EXPERT_IMPLICATIONS_E65_LM_part2.md`, section 3).
