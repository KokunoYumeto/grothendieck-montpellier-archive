## 1. The strongest candidate bridges

1. **AG-01, finite geometry over F_q onto the meta NOT ladder** (L-meta-660, 661, 714, 715, the heavy-input step,
   885/890/894/899/900, 766). The min-max of 715 needs, for each weight W, the largest number ext_q(W) of lines of
   PG(2,q) missed by W points: an extremal problem on arcs, conics, unitals, maximal arcs and blocking sets.
   meta.md 5.2 already reduces 660 and 661 to explicit constructions (a Denniston arc in PG(2,8), a conic in PG(2,7)).
2. **AG-02, linear spaces on the level hypersurface and on the permanent hypersurface (Fano schemes, Gauss map)
   onto L-gct-086, 036, 037.** The projective closure of {perm(J+t) = n!} meets infinity in {perm_n = 0}, whose
   singular locus is the object of 036/037. A Gauss-map inequality would tie large level spaces to large linear spaces
   inside the singular locus of the permanent hypersurface.
3. **AG-03, linear spaces on Fermat complete intersections {p_2 = ... = p_k = 0} onto L-gct-061, 067, 122 and the
   086 core.** The core kills p_2 and p_3; equality classes (Codex: direct sums of triple lines for p_2, p_4) plus the
   order-4 identity of L-gct-134 are a concrete route from n^2/3 towards n^2/4 for the core.
4. **AG-04, ideal certificates (Nullstellensatz/IPS) from NEG_NIGHT NN-C029 and NN-C021 onto pcx and alg.** E builds
   subexponential ideal certificates sum a_i f_i = 1 for unsatisfiable fixed-density systems, where monomial polynomial
   calculus needs 2^{Omega(n)}: an algorithm family whose certificates lie outside every proof system with a checked
   lower bound on the fleet's board (lib_alg.md 5.4).
5. **AG-05, quadrics over F_2 and theta characteristics onto the lifted line of L-pcx-071** (route M, node M3 of
   pcx.md 5.3). The IP gadget y0y1 + y2y3 has 10 zeros and 6 ones: the even and odd theta characteristics of a genus-2
   curve. A sum of k gadgets is the Arf map on theta characteristics of a genus-2k abelian variety, and pcx's covering
   statement becomes a statement about affine flats of odd theta characteristics.
6. **AG-06, reduction mod 2 and specialization of smoothness onto L-gct-025** (the open link of Line B before the
   cubic target 026): over F_2, perm = det, and smoothness of the mod-2 reduction forces smoothness in characteristic
   0. Our P90a (hexagon 2611.00936) marks where characteristic-2 vanishing fails.
