## 1. The strongest candidate bridges

1. **HT-01, the level-space core as a point of a period domain, and the barrier families as Weil-type (CM) points,
   onto L-gct-086.** p_2 is positive definite on the real doubly centred matrices, so a core K (isotropic for p_2) has
   K ∩ K̄ = 0 automatically: it is the H^{2,0}-part of a weight-2 Hodge structure on DC with definite polarization, a
   point of the isotropic Grassmannian OG(k, (n-1)^2) = SO((n-1)^2)/(U(k) x SO((n-1)^2 - 2k)). The higher invariants
   S_3, S_4, ... are sections of Sym^j of the dual tautological bundle. The F_q barrier families of gct.md 5.1(c) are
   eigenspaces of a Z_q symmetry, the mechanism of Weil-type Hodge classes (S_k(omega t) = omega^k S_k(t)), which is the
   mechanism our H12/H19/H25 Hodge work studies for abelian varieties.
2. **HT-02, Hasse-Witt (Frobenius on the Hodge piece H^{n}(X, O_X) in characteristic p) onto parity: L-alg-001,
   L-clb-008/032, L-meta-126.** NEG_NIGHT NN-C023/C024: a 3-CNF Phi gives in polynomial time a form F of degree r in r
   variables over F_2 with H^{r-2}(X, O) = F_2 and Frobenius = #SAT(Phi) mod 2; smooth versions exist (randomized
   reduction): a polynomial-time evaluator on smooth inputs would give ⊕P ⊆ BPP and NP = RP.
3. **HT-03, Hodge-Riemann relations and Lorentzian polynomials onto the Hessian-signature nodes of GCT Line A** (036,
   037 real case, 028, 158, the O-011 eigenvalues of the level-space quadratic layer). Real isotropic dimension is a
   signature count; Lorentzian polynomials (one positive Hessian eigenvalue on the positive orthant) are the Hodge-index
   class.
4. **HT-04, hard Lefschetz versus GCT positivity: our H23, H02, H02b and GCT_NSRH onto L-gct-011 (the flip).** Hard
   Lefschetz forces unimodality; H23 refutes the unimodality clause of GCT VIII's Positivity conjecture at r = 3, so no
   polarized Lefschetz module has those graded dimensions.
5. **HT-05, Hodge loci of hypersurfaces containing linear subspaces (Fermat) onto L-gct-061/067/122 and the 086 core**
   (arXiv:2112.14818).

## 3. Notes on the top maps

**HT-01.** The translation is exact at the level of objects: the core of a complex level space is a point of a compact
Hermitian symmetric space carrying the Hodge bundle, and the level conditions are sections of its symmetric powers.
The barrier theorem's families are the fixed points of finite-order symmetries, which is where Weil-type
constructions live; route R3 (bounded defect from a symmetric space) is the same question as "how far is a Hodge
structure from a CM point" in this dictionary. The first computable item is the equivariant Chern-class count on the
fixed loci for n = 4, 5 against the checked values kappa(4) = 3 (216) and the open 199.

**HT-02.** This is the one map in the three files whose source is a complexity theorem already: NEG_NIGHT proves the
hardness of the Frobenius scalar on a Hodge piece. On the fleet's board it sits next to the alg program's "value-only
evaluators" (lib_alg.md 5.4): a Hasse-Witt evaluator is an algorithm family the program has no tool to kill.

**HT-04.** H23 and H02 are refutations; their translation onto 011 is an obstruction statement (no Lefschetz module, no
positive formula of the hypothesized shape), and Q4-PRIMITIVE is the positive side (purity where it holds).
