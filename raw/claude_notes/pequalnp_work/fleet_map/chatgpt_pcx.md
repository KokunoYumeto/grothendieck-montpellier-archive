## The fifth and sixth SAT returns against the exact hypotheses

**CP05.** In Paper I, “Exact moment rank” at line 4312 and the projector-jet collision at line 4470 concern sparse root projectors. A cheap membership coefficient can coexist with a Hankel moment matrix of rank equal to the selected-root count. The local simultaneous-collision argument supplies fixed finite projector and gcd observations with opposite SAT outcomes. These test the information content of those observations. A conversion to lower bounds on parity-resolution lines would require a simulation of every permitted refutation through those same observations. The return gives no such simulation. Primary source is the checkpoint 05 ZIP, member “workbench/papers/paper_I/main.tex”.

**CP06.** Paper I’s growing comparison family, proposition “factor-growing” beginning at line 1580, is a closer match. Its negative instance has $2r^2$ right vertices each requiring a selected edge, while left degree requirements permit exactly $2r^2-1$ selected edges. Its named affine sector procedure visits all $2^{2r^2-1}$ sectors. The actual presentation imposes pins, port identifications, exact left degrees and allowed right degrees $\{1,2\}$. These additional constraints and algorithm-specific costs must be reconciled before invoking a graph-pigeonhole lower bound. Its 2-adic projector proposition at line 4935 locates exact counting in a cyclic constant coefficient; it gives no bound on parity derivations.

**An explicit failure already at two pigeons.** L046 and L049 both require that each set $T$ of at most $s$ pigeons have at least $2|T|$ unique neighboring holes. In CP06 each toroidal-grid cell is replaced by a four-cycle of ports in cyclic order $N,E,S,W$, with one external neighbor per port. Take two opposite same-color ports as pigeons. The two intermediate ports within the square are neighbors of both. The two external hole neighbors are distinct for even $r\ge4$ and each has multiplicity one. Thus $|\partial T|=2 < 4$. Choosing a square away from the removed $u_0$ preserves the witness in the negative graph $G_r-u_0$. The assumption fails for every $s\ge2$. More generally, in any bipartite graph of pigeon degree at most three, three pigeons joined successively along a simple alternating path use two distinct holes at least twice apiece. Their nine or fewer incidences leave at most five unique holes. Consequently, even eliminating the four-cycles cannot give factor-two expansion up to size three in a cubic graph containing such a path. The L046/049 route needs a different graph or a lower-bound proof under weaker hypotheses.
