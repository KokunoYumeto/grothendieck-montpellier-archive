## Jaswal, *An effective André-Oort theorem for Shimura varieties*, hexagon:2611.00339v3 (dated 18 Nov 2026)
Read at the source (PDF from hexagonmath.org): abstract, contents, §1 opening, §3 (Lemma 3.1, Lemma 3.2, Proposition 3.3
with its proof opening), Table 1.
- Main theorem: effective bounds for maximal special subvarieties of Shimura varieties (degree unconditionally;
  discriminant of L_V and the Klingler-Yafaev invariant under a zero-free half-plane QRH(δ; L_V)); with
  Cureton-Thorner (hexagon:2610.00200, δ = 1/32 for every number field) unconditional; listing algorithm for abelian type.
- Proposition 3.3: under QRH(δ; L), |Im ρ_L| >= c(δ) d_L^{δ'} with δ' = 1/(16 t s n_T (n_T - 1)) independent of δ:
  Ullmo-Yafaev's Galois-orbit bound (their Thm 6.1 assumed GRH) with an explicit constant, the split primes coming from
  Lemma 3.2. Here d_L is the discriminant of the splitting field L of the torus and n_T bounds [L_V : Q].
- Against A.ORBIT on A_g: his exponent is measured in the Galois-closure discriminant d_L and is 1/(16 t s n_T(n_T-1)),
  with n_T up to the degree of the Galois closure of a CM field of degree 2g (up to 2^g g!); ours is 1/(4g) in the
  discriminant of the CM field itself. For A_g ours is far stronger in exponent; his covers every Shimura variety and
  yields effective André-Oort, which is his (with Cureton-Thorner), cited and not claimed. Inputs differ: his zero-free
  half-plane for Dedekind zeta functions of the Galois closures; ours Alpöge's theorem (Dirichlet) through the height
  bound plus the isogeny theorem of Gaudron-Rémond.
