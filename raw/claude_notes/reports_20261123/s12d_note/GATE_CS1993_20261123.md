## What it proves
- Thm 1 (§1): (A, m) complete normal 2-dimensional local domain, algebraically closed residue field, pi_1^{(p)}(Spec A - m)
  finite (prime-to-p completion) => (a) Cl(A) is an extension of a finite group by a group with a composition series with
  factors k^+; (b) for an SNC resolution, the exceptional curves are rational and the intersection graph is a tree.
- Lemma 2: a purely inseparable smooth cover gives pi_1(Spec A - m) = 0.
- Thm 3 (with Lemma 4): for SNC resolutions with simply connected graph of nonsingular rational curves, a presentation of
  pi_1^{(p)}(Spec A - m) = pi_1^{(p)}(X - ΣE_i) = (F_n/N)^{(p)}; Cor 5: an arithmetic proof of Mumford-Flenner (char 0).
- §2 (Lemma 5, Thm 6): finite quotients of order prime to p of the groups F(e_1..e_t)/(e_1...e_t = 1, e_i^{x_i} = 1).
- §3: Brieskorn singularities k[[x,y,z]]/(x^a+y^b+z^c) in characteristic p > 3 (Thm A in the introduction for p != 2, 3):
  Props 7-11 (reduction to p-free exponents, star-shaped resolution graph, the genus g_R, presentations); Thm 12:
  pi_1 = 0 <=> a purely inseparable cover by a power series ring <=> some exponent is a power of p.

## Relation to S12d
S12d (RESEARCH_SA12 rows 433-435, D_wild_genus2_curve.tex): in characteristic 2, a normal surface singularity
b^2 + (t^5 + t^2 a^2) b + a^5 = 0 whose resolution has one smooth genus-two exceptional curve C, C^2 = -1, with a
connected Z/2 Galois cover etale off the point and wild at C. CS1993 contains no such example: it has no characteristic-2
statement (its §3 assumes p > 3), and its p-part results concern purely inseparable covers of Brieskorn singularities.

quotient is outside its scope. **The gate is closed: S12d is not in CS1993.** Cite CS1993 as context (Thm 1, Thm 12),
