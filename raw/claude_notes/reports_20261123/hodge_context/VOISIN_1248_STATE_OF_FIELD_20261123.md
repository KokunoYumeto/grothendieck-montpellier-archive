## What the exposé states (paraphrased; locators exact)

- **Théorème 1.3 (Markman 2025), p. 1248-03:** the Hodge conjecture holds for abelian varieties of dimension at most 5.
- **Théorème 1.4 (Markman 2025), p. 1248-03:** the Hodge conjecture holds for the Weil classes on Weil abelian varieties
  of dimension 6 and discriminant 1. Théorème 1.3 is reduced to 1.4 through the study of Mumford-Tate constraints by
  Deligne (1982), Tankeev (1982) and Moonen-Zarhin (1995, 1999) (p. 1248-03; the reduction itself in Section 2.3).
- **Antecedents, p. 1248-03:** Markman (2023) proved the analogue of 1.4 for Weil abelian varieties of dimension 4 and
  discriminant 1; Floccari and Fu (2025) gave another proof, also through hyperkähler geometry; Schoen (1988, 2007) had
  proved the same statement for specific quadratic fields K.
- **Absolute Hodge classes, p. 1248-09:** Deligne (1982) and André (1996) study Hodge classes that specialize to
  algebraic classes on some fibre (Deligne's absolute Hodge classes, André's motivated classes); Deligne (1982) shows that
  Hodge classes on abelian varieties are generated by such classes. The specialization to Weil abelian varieties used by
  Markman in dimension 6 is the one Deligne used (pp. 1248-16 to 1248-17).
- **Integral version, p. 1248-09:** Kollár's counterexamples (1990) show the integral variational form is false.

## A context paragraph for the paper (our words; to be adapted by the finishing run)

"For abelian varieties the Hodge conjecture is known in dimension at most five: this is Markman's theorem (2025), as
presented by Voisin [Bourbaki exposé 1248, Théorème 1.3], which reduces, through the Mumford-Tate analysis of Deligne,
Tankeev and Moonen-Zarhin, to the algebraicity of the Weil classes on Weil abelian varieties of dimension six and
discriminant one [ibid., Théorème 1.4]; the case of dimension four and discriminant one is Markman's earlier theorem
(2023), reproved by Floccari and Fu. Hodge classes on abelian varieties are absolute Hodge classes (Deligne, 1982). In
October 2026 two preprints announced the rational Hodge conjecture for abelian varieties with complex multiplication
(OpenAI; van Hoften and Sempliner); they are cited here with their status, and the theorems of this paper do not use
them."
