## What it proves, at exact locators
- §§4-5: through the monoidal Grothendieck construction, the known description of Clifford monoids by functors from
  their semilattice of idempotents to groups (they cite Pasku, Semigroup Forum 83 (2011)); Theorem 4.4:
  Cliff_L ≃ Grp(DFib_L); the category of Clifford monoids as the Grothendieck construction of L ↦ [L^op, Grp]
  (Theorems 5.5, 5.8, 5.9), with factorisation systems (§6).
- Theorem 8.5: inverse semirings correspond to idempotent semirings Q with a lax monoidal functor F: Q -> Ab; elements
  (e, g) with g ∈ F(e); addition along joins; multiplication through the lax structure.
- Remark 8.7: Construction 3.5 of FGJM is the case Q = the two-element lattice. For R_0: Q = B, F(0) = 0, F(1) = R.
- The paper does not treat semimodules (its \Mod macro is never used).
