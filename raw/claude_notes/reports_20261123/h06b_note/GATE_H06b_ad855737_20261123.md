# H06b gate: is "Vandiver at p ⇒ positive answer to Anderson-Ihara's kernel question at p" already known?

By ad855737 (Claude Opus 5.5 at effort high), 23 Nov 2026 (file renamed from GATE_H06b_20261123.md, written by the same agent before the change of task), for the main session 329fb1e6 (Claude Opus 5.5 at effort
max).

**The question.** Anderson and Ihara asked whether the fixed field Ω_p* of the kernel of the pro-p outer Galois
representation on π_1(P¹ − {0,1,∞})^{(p)} equals Ω_p, the maximal pro-p extension of Q(μ_p) unramified outside p
(Anderson-Ihara, Ann. Math. 128 (1988), p. 272, question (a)).

## Verdict

**Not in the literature. The gate passes.**

What I found at the source:
- the implication is proved for odd **regular** primes, conditional on Deligne-Ihara, which is now Brown's theorem;
- Sharifi states the irregular case as open in 2019;
- Ishii's papers of January and September 2026 still record only the regular case;
- no Hexagon post and no 2025-2026 arXiv paper found treats irregular primes.

The run's theorem therefore answers the question at every irregular prime below 2^31 (all of them satisfy Vandiver,
by Hart-Harvey-Ong) and at every Vandiver prime. This is a meaningful contribution to a question that was asked in
print and recorded as open.

## What the run adds, and whether it contributes

- **The Vandiver form.** For every odd p with p ∤ h(Q(μ_p)^+), Ω_p* = Ω_p. With Hart-Harvey-Ong (Vandiver holds for
  p < 2^31) this answers the question at every prime below 2^31, irregular ones included: 37, 59, 67, 101, 103,
  131, 149, 157, …. Before it, only regular primes were known.
  - **Inputs.** The run's own argument:
    - a cup-product-to-pole comparison;
    - separation of Magnus coefficients by poles at the roots of the even Iwasawa characteristic polynomials.

    Published theorems:
    - Fukaya-Kato with Wake-Wang-Erickson: Vandiver implies that p-unit cups span H²;
    - Hain-Matsumoto and Brown: the rational image is free on odd generators;
    - McCallum-Sharifi: the presentation.
  - **Contribution: yes, substantial.** It settles a question recorded as "a very interesting open question" (Sharifi
    2019) for an explicit infinite-looking family: every Vandiver prime, and every prime below 2^31.
- **p = 2.** Not found in the sources opened. Anderson-Ihara 1988 was not opened, so it is not excluded that p = 2
  is treated there. The run's paper proves p = 2 separately; the Fable editor's note leaves p = 2 out, so this gate does not bear on it.
- **Greenberg form** (X_p^+ finite suffices; the run's paper). It is a weaker hypothesis, so a stronger statement, but
  it rests on §§3-5 of the run's paper, which I have not checked. It is not in the note's theorem; it is mentioned in
  the scope section as the producing model's claim.

**What the literature already has, exactly.**
- Vandiver ⇒ Sharifi's conjecture (WWE 2017, abstract and Cor. 5.1.2).
- Sharifi's conjecture (surjectivity of ϖ) ⇒ the McCallum-Sharifi generation of H² by cups of cyclotomic p-units
  at every level (FK §7.1.10).
- Greenberg ⇒ the same modulo torsion (WWE 2018, Cor. 8.1.3(2)).

So the arithmetic input of the run's theorem is known. What is new is the passage from finite-level cup generation
to the kernel field: the comparison with the class-two extension, and the pole separation of Magnus coefficients.
None of the sources makes or announces it.
