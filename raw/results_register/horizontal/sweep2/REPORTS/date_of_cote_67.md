## The two layers

1. **1976–78.**
   - The surgery sheets 003–007 under the title sheet dated "(1976 ou 77 ?)" (SA22-3, first part).
   - The genus-one calculations on envelopes 009–014, one of them metered at Nagoya University on 20.12.77 (SA22-13).
2. **December 1983 to April 1984.**
   - "Courbes elliptiques réelles" (091–101, Déc 83; SA22-9).
   - "Chirurgie conforme" (053–083, dated 11 or 16.1.84, 12.1, 13.1; SA22-6, SA22-7).
   - "Moduli M0,4 et l'octaèdre" (084–089; SA22-8).
   - The anabelian sheets (048–051, dated 15.1; SA22-5).
   - The letter to Bers (016–021, 15.4.1984; in SA22-3).
   - The metric dossier "Métriques canoniques sur surfaces conformes" (024–046; SA22-4) is undated. It is on computer
     paper with show-through, like the 1983–84 sheets (my inference).

The catalogue's "s.d., 1983-1984" fits only the second layer.

The title sheet is in his hand (compare "Chirurgie conforme" on 054). The "(1976 ou 77 ?)" is therefore a later dating
of his own, with a question mark (my reading).
