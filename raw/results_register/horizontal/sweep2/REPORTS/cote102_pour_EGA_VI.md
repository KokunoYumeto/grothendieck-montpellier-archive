# Cote 102, "Pour EGA VI": gluing objects of a site along a class of morphisms

Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max, agent a6d42bf6fe2b0d8a8, horizontal sweep 2 part 2, 18 Nov 2026.

**Verdict:**
- **The heading is typed and unambiguous.** It reads "Pour EGA VI" on scan 3, above "Recollements d'objets d'un site."
- **What the folder holds:** a draft for the unpublished EGA VI. It sets out the general device for gluing objects of a site along a class M of morphisms, together with a plan for properties of morphisms of sheaves and a sheet of "Questions pour Artin". This is the formalism that defines schemes (M = open immersions) and algebraic spaces (M = étale; the folder itself attributes this example to Artin) as sheaves glued from objects of the site.
- **Where it belongs:** the folder is filed in the archive's section 11, combinatorial geometry, by catalogue. Its mathematics belongs with descent and the EGA themes (Part III of the outline; the archive's section 14, Descentes).

## What the pages contain

| Scans | Content |
|---|---|
| 2 | Cover: "Recollements" |
| 3-4 | **The typescript, with his corrections.** A site S with a class M of arrows that are (a) quarrable and stable under base change, (b) contain the identities and are stable under composition, (c) are local on the target, and (d) satisfy a saturation condition; in the margin, "essayer de ne pas le mettre ici, en saturant plus tard". **Proposition:** there is a unique class M̃ of arrows of the topos S̃ satisfying the analogues of (a)-(d) together with (e) M̃ ∩ S = M and (f) every M̃-arrow F → X with X in S is covered by M̃-arrows Xᵢ → F with Xᵢ in S. Uniqueness is proved first when the diagonal of F is representable, then in general by comparing Xᵢ ×_F Xⱼ with Xᵢ ×_X Xⱼ. The verification of the six conditions is "laissé au lecteur". |
| 4, 6 | The category S̃_M of sheaves covered by objects of S through M̃-arrows: the objects obtained by gluing. A crossed-out proposition. |
| 5 | Stability: representables; passage along M̃-arrows; sums; quotients by equivalence relations whose first projection is in M̃; change of presenting site. His remarks: "On laisse aussi (v) au rédacteur"; "il est douteux que ça marche pareil pour un noyau". |
| 7 | Examples: open immersions of affine schemes give schemes; étale morphisms give the example attributed to Artin. A proposition on the slice site S(X, M). |
| 9 | **"Questions pour Artin":** (1) for S local, henselian and noetherian and S′ → S faithfully flat, if a descent datum on X′/S′ is effective over the closed fibre, is it effective? Case S′ = Ŝ? (2) descent of varieties; the margin singles out commutation with limits. (3) representability: "X_s repr. mais X non repr. ??". (4) fpqc descent of "small" sheaves. |
| 10 | A different reused typescript, folio 65, n° 387: valuations and linear compactness |
| 11 | **A plan for properties of morphisms of sheaves:** representability, finiteness, formal and infinitesimal conditions, immersions, separation, properness, surjectivity and radicial morphisms, connectedness. The definitions must agree with the scheme notions when a morphism is representable, tested through X ×_G F → X. |
| 12 | Reduced fibres and a Hilbert functor |
| 13 | "Caractérisation des morphismes étales de foncteurs …": a criterion for an open immersion of functors over a locally noetherian base (commutation with limits, an infinitesimally étale monomorphism) |
| 14 | A boxed plan grouping the conditions |
