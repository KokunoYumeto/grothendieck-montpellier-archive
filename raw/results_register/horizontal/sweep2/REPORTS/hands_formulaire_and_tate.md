## 1. The Formulaire, cote 63 (Codex folders H63-03, H63-04, H63-09)

**What the sheets are.** A typescript "COURBES ELLIPTIQUES : FORMULAIRE", "d'après J. Tate", "mis au goût du jour par
P. Deligne" (scan 16 on; typed pages "- 10 -" to "- 17 -" on scans 25-32), and a second version "(Traduction libre d'un
manuscrit inédit de TATE)" (scan 87 on). Typed text: §5 the reduced forms in characteristics 3 and 2, §6 applications,
Proposition 6.9 on automorphisms, §7 "Anneau des formes modulaires entières", Proposition 7.1 (the ring of integral
modular forms is generated over Z by c4, c6 and Delta with the single relation (7.2) c4^3 - c6^2 = 1728 Delta).

"Reste à prouver que si un polynôme ..." on scan 32 is typed: it belongs to the Formulaire's own proof of Prop. 7.1, not
to the margin. The part-1 flag that read it as a margin was wrong (correction recorded in SA16 CORRECTIONS).

**Whose hand.** The pencil is a fast French cursive with flattened middle letters, a long descending f and s, a word-final
s reduced to a stroke, and "st" written as in his "structures" on 13:14 ("Hodge structures"); the "structure ?" of 63:30
and the "Sur le site modulaire" of 63:32 have the same letterforms as his letter and as his cote 62 notes. Decision:
**probably Grothendieck's hand**, annotating Deligne's typescript. Not certain: no signature, and a single pencil hand
could in principle be another reader's.

**The mathematics of the margins.**
- "faux si S pas connexe" is right: over a non-connected base, E has the automorphism that is the identity on one
  component and the symmetry on another, so Aut(E) = {1, -1} holds only for S connected (or as a statement about the
  sheaf Aut, which is Z/2). A correction to the Formulaire's statement, recorded in SA16 CORRECTIONS.
- "structure ?" asks for the group of order 24 in characteristic 2 with j = 0; it is SL_2(F_3) (Q_8 semidirect Z/3), the
  standard answer.
- The NB on 63:32 is a correct sharpening of Prop. 7.1 (the divisors of c4, c6 and Delta on the moduli stack), and the
  two lines on 63:33 say the same for a1 and b2 in characteristic 2.

Decision: only the faint sheet 39 is in another hand, Tate's (the cover names him, the text is English and states the
lemma at the start of Tate's Hodge-Tate theory). The "Questions diverses en théorie de Tate-Hodge" (40-46) are
Grothendieck's own questions and conjectures. (mine) His conjecture on 6:45 is the Hodge-Tate decomposition of
H^i(X, C_p), proved by Tate for p-divisible groups (1967) and by Faltings in general (1988); question a) of 6:41 is a
comparison isomorphism of the kind Fontaine's programme later made precise.

The Pro note MPL_6_038-067+7_001-060#1 left the hands open; this settles them (SA15-18). The part-1 flag on it is closed.
