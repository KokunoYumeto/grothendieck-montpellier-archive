## What depends on what (sweep2/SA15/DEPENDS.tsv)

Divided powers (SA15-14) -> the crystalline site (SA15-1) -> F-crystals (SA15-2) -> Dieudonné-Manin (SA15-3) and the
tannakian categories (SA15-6). The p-adic cohomology draft (SA15-15) is answered by the site (7:54-60 follows it).
Integral de Rham (SA15-9) uses the site and F, V (4:2-4). Crystalline Dieudonné theory (SA15-16) uses the site, F and V,
and divided powers; it is used by the duality over A_n (SA15-10), the Hodge summand (SA15-11), Hodge-Tate (SA15-18), the
elliptic reduction (SA15-17) and good reduction (SA15-19, through "bonne réduction" of G and its p-divisible group,
100:51).

## Agreements and conflicts

- The notes are faithful to the pages wherever I could compare them with the scans read in part 2 (6:38-45).
- Several notes are the note-writer's own recomputations ("Confidence: high. Reason: ...") and research questions; they
  are notes about the page, not his statements, and stay with the item of the page they discuss.
- His own withdrawals are on the pages and the notes keep them: 6:22 "je m'étais canulé", 6:27 section 2.7, 7:17
  "C'est faux malheureusement", 100:17 "Douteux, sans doute faux".
