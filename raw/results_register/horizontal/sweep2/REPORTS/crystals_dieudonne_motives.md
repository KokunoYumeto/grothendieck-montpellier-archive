## The dependency picture (my reading)
- **Level 1.** p-adic cohomology before crystals (H0LM) and divided powers (H0LL) lead to the crystalline site (H0A9). Integral de Rham and Hodge cohomology (H0AK) leads there too.
- **Level 2.** The crystalline site and F-crystals (H0AE) give:
  - Dieudonné-Manin (H0AF), the classification of isocrystals by slopes;
  - crystalline Dieudonné theory of Barsotti-Tate groups (H0LN, cote 6), which is the same programme as the Montreal notes (H024, cote 93): to be decided on reading, see below.
- **The de Rham side** feeds the Barsotti-Tate crystals. It is:
  - connections, Gauss-Manin, the Cartier operation and p-curvature (H026, H020, H02J);
  - the universal vector extension (H0A1);
  - Oda's comparison (H0C8): the Dieudonné module of the p-torsion against H¹_dR.

  The classical theory of formal groups and Witt rings (H0CF) is the earlier, non-crystalline form.
- **Barsotti-Tate crystals** lead to:
  - filtered modules and T_cr (H00D);
  - good reduction through Barsotti-Tate groups (H037, cote 100, March 1967);
  - the reduction of elliptic curves (H0LO), which links to SA16.
- **The bridge to p-adic Hodge theory.** Tannakian categories of crystals (H0AH) need the crystalline site and Dieudonné-Manin, and point to p-adic representations (H02Y) and Hodge-Tate theory (H0LP).
- **The motives side.** The tannakian formalism (H0MG) underlies:
  - the motivic category (H04F) and the motivic Galois group (H03P);
  - effectivity (H03Z);
  - the standard conjectures (H003).

  Motives over finite fields (H046) and Deuring's gerbes (H045) are the finite-field part, Tate classes (H041) the conjectural link to cycles, and periods (H04E) together with the Hodge group and the Mumford letter (H0MH) the complex side.
- **Two links tie the motives theme to the crystals strand:**
  - Tannakian categories of crystals (H0AH) are the crystalline realization;
  - the Weil-number protorus (H046) matches the slopes of Dieudonné-Manin (H0AF). The slopes of the Frobenius isocrystal are the p-adic valuations of the Weil numbers. This is my inference, not on the pages.
