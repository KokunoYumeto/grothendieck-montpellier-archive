## The flags that matter

1. **Deligne's example, 144/010.** In a 1981 letter, H = ⟨x, y | x³ = y³ = 1, [x, y] central⟩ is said to have an
   "(infinite cyclic)" commutator subgroup.
   - With c central, [x³, y] = c³, so c³ = 1. H is the Heisenberg group of order 27 (note N010.2; I checked it).
   - The intended relations must make x³ and y³ central, not trivial.
