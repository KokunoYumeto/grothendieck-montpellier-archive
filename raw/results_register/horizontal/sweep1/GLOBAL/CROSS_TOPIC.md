## The files in GLOBAL
- **GLOBAL_OBJECTS.tsv**: one row per global object, with a tag H??? (H and three base-36 characters). A row holds the items of every topic that are the same object.
  - Items are merged only on four grounds:
    - a topic agent states the equality ("= SA18-12", "= H03F", "same package");
    - two topics placed the same note entries (overlap of at least 0.5);
    - the two items are the same research-run family;
    - the names are near-identical (Jaccard at least 0.6 over at least three words).
  - Two items of the same topic are never merged, because the topic agent kept them apart. The merges this rule refused are listed in merge_log.tsv.
  - The merges rest on the topic agents' OBJECTS rows (names, cross_topic, entry lists). They say that two agents' items are the same object. They check nothing in the notes or on the pages.
- **THEMES.tsv**: TH-nn, related objects across topics. Themes are built from:
  - the cross_topic links;
  - the crossings a28ea31fa17dd65e1 reported;
  - partial entry overlaps;
  - weaker name matches where one item names the other's topic.

  TH numbers change at every build, so cite H tags.
- **LINKS.tsv**: every pointer in a cross_topic field (item id, topic id, cote, tag), resolved to its topic.
- **CONCEPTS.tsv**: notions with two meanings, which need two tags. It now holds one: "totally aspheric", HTA1 (Pursuing Stacks §65 A; cote 114 Prop. 4) and HTA2 (Les Dérivateurs XII), from a34c04448e8299803's SA27 flag 1.
- **COVERAGE.tsv**: for each topic, what has no PLACEMENT row.
- **TAG_ALIASES.tsv**: when two tagged objects merge, the earlier tag stays and the other becomes its alias, so a cited tag still resolves.
- **merge_log.tsv** and **shared_cotes_packages.json**.
