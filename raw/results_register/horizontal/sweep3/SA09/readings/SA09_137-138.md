**Conventions on these pages** (re-derived; they agree with the Pro notes):
- "½ simplicial" is the old word "semi-simplicial", i.e. simplicial in today's sense, since Dold-Puppe applies
  throughout.
- Φ_* is the simplicial module n ↦ k^{[n]} of functions on the vertices of Δ^n (cosk_0 of k, which is contractible).
  T ∈ Φ is the constant function 1 (a map k → Φ), and Ψ_* = Φ_*/kT. So rank Φ_n = n+1 and rank Ψ_n = n.
- Λ^jΨ_* is exactly the Dold-Puppe object of k[j] (rank C(n,j) in degree n).
- Λ^iΦ_* (i ≥ 1) is the Dold-Puppe object of the disk k →id k in degrees i, i-1 (rank C(n+1,i)).

### SA09-2 · ∂-perfect half-simplicial complexes (137 scans 2-20)

- **Scan 2, Lemma 1 and its NB.** Lemma 1: a simplicial k-module with projective components and all π_i = 0 is
  projective. The NB weakens the hypothesis to π_i = 0 for i > 0 and π_0 projective.
  - Checked. By Dold-Puppe, an acyclic bounded-below complex of projectives splits into disks P →id P, and these are
    projective in Ch_{≥0}. P[0] is projective too, since Hom(P[0], C) = Hom(P, C_0).
  - So the NB is the exact characterisation of projective objects.
- **Scan 2, Cor.** Γ^iΦ ⊗ Λ^jΨ and Λ^iΦ ⊗ Λ^jΨ (i > 0) are projective. Checked:
  - a degreewise functor F with F(0) = 0 preserves the simplicial contraction of Φ, so Γ^iΦ and Λ^iΦ are contractible;
  - any tensor product with a contractible object is contractible (Eilenberg-Zilber).
- **Scan 4, Lemma 2: Hom(L ⊗ Λ^qΨ, Λ^{q'}Ψ ⊗ M) = 0 for q ≥ q' ≥ 0.**

  - For q = q' the Hom is Hom(π_q(L ⊗ Λ^qΨ), M) = Hom(π_0(L), M). So the lemma needs π_0(L) = 0, or at least
    Hom(π_0 L, M) = 0.

  - Projectivity alone does not suffice. Take L = k constant, M = k, q = q': the Hom is k (as R004 of the Pro notes of
    MPL_137_002-044 says).
  - **Decided on the image:** the proof line reads "π_•(L_•) = 0". The subscript of π is the same small dot as in
    "L.", i.e. flasqueness; it is not "π_q" as transcribed.

- **Scan 4, Théorème.**
  - The claim: a map of complexes u exists from 0 → Γ^nΦ → Γ^{n-1}Φ⊗Ψ → … → Λ^nΨ → 0 to 0 → Φ → Λ²Φ → … → Λ^{n+2}Φ

    Ker(Λ^{n+1}Φ → Λ^{n+2}Φ) = T∧Λ^nΦ.

    - Augmented by k, the bottom row is the Koszul complex of the unimodular section T, hence exact.
    - The top row is the divided-power Koszul complex of Φ → Ψ. Degreewise it is exact except H^0 = Γ^n(kT) = k.
    - u exists by lifting from right to left. The terms Γ^{n-i}Φ⊗Λ^iΨ with n-i > 0 are projective by the Cor., and
      the bottom row is exact.
    - The induced map on the augmentations k → k is a unit. Both rows are n-extensions of Λ^nΨ by k with contractible
      middle terms, so every connecting map is an isomorphism.
