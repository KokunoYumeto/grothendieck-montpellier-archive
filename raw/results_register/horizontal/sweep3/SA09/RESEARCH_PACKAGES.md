## SA09-RP1 · Integral reconstruction from the universal cosimplicial object, and the polynomial categories

**Question.**
1. Is a nilpotent space of finite type recovered, up to the relevant completion, from C^*(X, Z)? The structure on
   C^*(X, Z) is the Γ-operations and the cup product defined through the universal property of cote 137 (Props 1
   and 5, Ex. 1-2).
2. Are his polynomial categories (scans 75-86) the categories ΓP of strict polynomial functors?
   - Settle his closing question: is f ↦ Γ^p(f) a polynomial law on the Hom modules?
   - Settle his forgotten axiom (scan 83): 1 ≅ Γ^q(1) does not follow from the others.

**Why it matters.**
- (1) is his own question (scan 39), and it decides what the de Rham programme of 1975-76 reconstructs integrally.
- (2) dates a structure that is now standard.

**What it rests on.**
- Cote 137, scans 2-86: the scan-4 theorem, Props 1 and 5, the Σ-exact theorem with its corrected essential image, and
  the polynomial categories.
- The SA08 PD de Rham material of cote 136.

**Strongest known reduction.**
- The weightwise comparison: weight n of PD de Rham ≃ τ_{≤n}C^•, proved on both sides (SA09-5 and SA08-4).
- Horel's binomial-ring theorem, Toën's affine stacks, and Mandell's E_∞ theorem (as the Pro notes R080-R081 cite
  them; to verify).

   Z/4 torus map).
2. Finish the computation of scans 106-112:
   - the polynomial P(c) for z³(z−1)²(z−c);
   - the field it defines;
   - the Galois orbit of the three trees of passport [3,2,1; 2,2,1,1; 6].
3. Correct the catalogue of scans 113-120 and the operations of scans 121-132:
   - the orange's automorphisms;
   - the incomplete exception list;

Belyi's 1979 theorem on the dated sheets.
