**H2. The polynomial categories of scans 75-86 are the categories ΓP of Friedlander-Suslin, twenty years earlier.**
- **Grounds:**
  - P_n(X,Y) = Hom(Γ^n X, Y) (scan 83);

  - the closing question whether f ↦ Γ^p(f) is a polynomial law.
- **The withdrawn uniqueness of the grading (scans 76-77):** it fails because λ^n ≡ λ^{n'} mod p whenever
  (p−1) | (n−n'). That is the known reason strict polynomial functors carry the degree as data.
