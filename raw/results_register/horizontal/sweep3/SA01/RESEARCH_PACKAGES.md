### From the reading cote72

- **P72-1: the arithmetic of pinned regular realizations (SA01-1, -3, -4; = SA04's P3-2).**
  - **Question:** for a Schläfli symbol [p_1, ..., p_n] with α_i = 2cos(2π/p_i), find the invariant form, its discriminants, and its Stiefel-Whitney and Brauer classes over the ring of the α_i. Prove that the degenerate characteristics are exactly those dividing the β_i, ρ' and σ'. Answer 72/113 for n = 2.
  - **Why it matters:** it unifies 70/68-72 and 72/45-113 and turns his programme of an arithmetic theory of regular polytopes (70/122-123) into theorems.
  - **What it rests on:** cote 72 as read and checked here (SA04-R72-01..08); 70/68-70.
  - **Strongest reduction on the pages:** δ_V = β_0^n···β_{n-1}·L_{n+1}(β) and δ_E = β_0^n···β_{n-1}·L_n(β_1..β_{n-1}) (72/53); w_* by orthogonalization (72/108-110).
  - **Material:** `sweep3/SA04/readings/cotes69-74.md` (cote 72), the reader's `chk70.py` and `chk72.py`, K72-01..07, MPL_72_035-081 (with 72/35 corrected).

### From the reading cote81

- **P-R4-4. Cote 81 against cote 154: what the non-crossing structures are for.**
  - Question: do the ordered models and non-crossing partitions of 81/26-53, and the edge gluing of 81/54, supply the local data of the relative-position surface of 154/46-76? Write the dictionary: "rives" and birive/monorive edges, folded maps ("Dessin plié", "folding", "double folding"), polygon structures and polygonal transport. For each statement of 154 that uses such a structure, give its counterpart in 81 or record that there is none.
  - Why it matters: SA01-9 to -12 (the relative-position surface of an added pseudoline, Esquisse §8) are open constructions on his pages. If cote 81 is their combinatorial basis, its proved statements (with SA01-K81-01 and -02 corrected, and the reconstruction of trees cited from the literature) become the first fixed pieces.
  - Strongest reduction on the page: the corrected 81/40 and the four characterizations of non-panaché pairs (81/43-44), both checked.
  - Material: J81-04 to J81-07; `c81checks.py` in reader R4's scratchpad; the SA11 readings of 154/46-76; the Pro notes MPL_81_038-055+154_046-076 (cote 154 only).

### From the reading cotes71_75

- **P75-1: the general regular icosahedron over a ring finite over ℤ (75/33; SA01-41 with SA01-1, SA04-21, SA01-16).**
  - **Question:** construct the universal pinned realization of {3, 5} and {5, 3} over ℤ[φ] or the ring it requires, with its invariant quadric. Describe its fibres in characteristics 2, 3 and 5. Relate the characteristic-2 fibre to A_5 ≅ SL(2, F_4), and the characteristic-5 fibre to P²(F_5) and the bi-icosahedra.
  - **Why it matters:** it is the announced aim of the 1977-78 DEA course, and it ties cotes 70, 72 and 75.
  - **What it rests on:** 70/68-72, 72/14-17 (C_0 ≅ Γ(S, O_S)^{2n+1}), 72/45-55 (continuants) and 75/31-33.
  - **Strongest reduction:** the discriminants at the solids (H75-2) and the polygon case (H75-1), both computed.
  - **Material:** `cotes71_75.md`; `sweep3/SA04/readings/cotes69-74.md` (cotes 70 and 72); the reader's `chk70.py`, `chk72.py` and `chk75b.py`; K70, K72 and J75.
- Cote 71: none. Its results are known (Meisters 1975; Toussaint 1991).
