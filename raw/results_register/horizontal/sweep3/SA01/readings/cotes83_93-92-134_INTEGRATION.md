## HUNCHES

- **H83-1 (the discriminant of a pair of quadratic algebras).** His Δ is the obstruction to a rank-4 Azumaya algebra being generated by two given quadratic subalgebras. Its quadratic polynomial in t has as roots the "commutative" traces. This should be the same object as the products of quadratic algebras of cote 84 (SA02), and as the composition of binary quadratic forms in Bhargava's sense. A full reading of 83/102-177 with cote 84 would decide it.
- **H93-3 (characteristic p uniformization).** His computations over B = A[T]/(T^{p²} − α) (93/123-124) are the place where his characteristic-p doubt about the uniqueness of G_0 (93/074) is tested. They likely show uniqueness for low-order perturbations s = t + at^d with d ≤ p² − 1, and leave open the role of D^{(p)}. This sharpens SA04's H93-2.
