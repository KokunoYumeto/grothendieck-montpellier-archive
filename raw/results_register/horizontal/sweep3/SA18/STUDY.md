## What is new, wrong or settled

- **45:8-9, Lemma 4, rests on a false step.** The step is N_G(𝔥)·K = G for 𝔥 a Cartan subalgebra of an infinitesimal
  kernel K. SL_2 with its Frobenius kernel refutes it. So his surjectivity theorem for H¹ and his theorem on nonabelian
  H² over fields of dimension ≤ 1 stay unproved for non-solvable infinitesimal kernels. This is package R3.
- **47:11:** the Fourier box drops the convolution.
- **45:59:** his claim fails for inner forms; SL_1(D) is a counterexample.
- **55:17:** his "non-algebraic torus" is a product of elliptic curves.
- **Decided on the scan:** "monomorphisme" stands on 46:47. Sweep 2 decided 47:9, the χ factor (his slip, the weaker
  claim true).
