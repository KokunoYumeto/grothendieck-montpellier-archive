### From the reading cote84

- **P-R4-5. Quadratic covers over an arbitrary base: cotes 77 and 84 as one theory.** This takes over P-R4-1 of the SA04 readings, the cote 77 question that moves into SA02's prompt.
  - Questions:
    - (a) Prove in full the classification of quadratic covers: by (L, D, b_0) = (L, δ, T_0) with T_0² ≡ δ (4) where 2 is regular (77/53-64, 74-86; 84/44-56); by (L, P_1, T, α) in characteristic 2 (84/57-58); by (L, P_1, Q) in general (84/59-62). Compare with Voight 2016, Wood 2011, Loos 1997 and Dallaporta 2025.
    - (b) The contracted product: the integral formula, the kernel Ann(2, b_1, b_2)·U of A → A_1 ⊗ A_2, the fixed-ring criterion 2O + b_1O + b_2O = O, and the Theorem of 84/90.
    - (c) χ-trivialized extensions: one coherent sign convention (the plus convention; the sign of the dual, SA02-R84-16), and the coherence constraints.
    - (d) The generalized Arf-Stiefel-Whitney invariants announced on 84/43. What is "Arf(E)" for a quadratic form of rank n over a base with 2 not invertible, how is it finer than c_1(E), and how does it relate to Loos's graded discriminant algebra (H84-2)?
  - Why it matters: a complete characteristic-free theory of quadratic covers and their products, with the Arf invariant over Z. This is his stated aim (84/43), and it joins cotes 77, 83 and 84.
  - Strongest reduction on the page:
    - the explicit formulas, all checked (SA02-R84-07, -08, -10, -11, -17);
    - the Theorem of 84/90, proved;
    - the Proposition of 84/86, checked over Z;
    - the classification statement of 84/49 when 2 is regular.
  - Material: K84-01 to K84-07 (2-111); MPL_84_097-145 and MPL_84_146-193; `c84a.py` in reader R4's scratchpad; cote 77 (K77-02 to K77-06); the SA01 readings of cote 83; the stage-A dossier's comparisons (Voight, Loos, Wood, Dallaporta).

### From the reading cotes90_91-2-18

- **P91-1: purity and representability without projective hypotheses (91/5-14).**
  - **Question:** prove that, for a flat group scheme of finite type over a locally noetherian base, condition R is equivalent to S-purity. Then prove the representability criteria for ∏_{X/S} Y/X and for the flattening functor in the corrected form (L) (special fibre), together with Corollary 3 and its consequences (Cent, Transp, Norm, pseudo-torsors with G flat, G/N).
  - **Why it matters:** these are the "méthodes non-projectives" announced on 91/3, and the base of the Hom theorem continued in SA03.
  - **What it rests on:** 91/5-14 as read and checked here (SA02-R91-01..04; corrections SA02-K91-01..03).
  - **Strongest reduction on the pages:**
    - the adic condition ⇔ (L) with the special fibre, by the local flatness criterion;
    - for groups, R reduced to discrete valuation rings;
    - Corollary 3 as the engine of the quotient statements.
  - **Material:** `cotes90_91-2-18.md`; K91-01; MPL_90+91_002-018 (second block); the stage-A dossier §4.1; SGA 3 VIII §6; Raynaud and Gruson 1971.
- **P90-1: the scope of the KG(X) notes (90/2-24).**
  - **Question:** decide for which flat affine G over a Dedekind ring A[G] satisfies Lemma 1 a) (H90-2). Then state and prove the resulting resolution theorem for equivariant coherent sheaves in the generality of the notes, and compare it with Thomason 1987.
  - **Why it matters:** it fixes the reach of the KG(X) method and its place relative to Serre 1968.
  - **What it rests on:** 90/2-24 (SA02-R90-01, R90-02); the correction SA02-K90-01.
  - **Strongest reduction on the pages:** Lemma 2 (stable saturated finite subcoalgebras under a)) and the U_i-module argument.
  - **Material:** `cotes90_91-2-18.md`; K90-01, K90-02; MPL_90+91_002-018 (first block); the stage-A dossier §3.
