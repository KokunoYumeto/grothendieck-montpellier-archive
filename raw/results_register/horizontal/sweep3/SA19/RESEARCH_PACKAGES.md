## From readings/c43_c44.md (7. Research packages (concepts))

- **RP1. A presentation of the endomorphism algebra of the residual complex (43:50-69).**
  - *Question.* Let A be a regular local ring essentially of finite type over a field (or excellent regular), X =
    Spec A, K^• = ⊕_x K(x) its residual (Cousin) complex with K(x) the injective hull of k(x) placed in degree codim x.
    Let 𝒜^* be the completed graded A-algebra generated by 𝒜^0 = ∏_x Ô_x (with its idempotents π_x) and one element θ of
    degree 1, subject to θ^2 = 0 and π_{x'} θ π_x = 0 unless x' is an immediate specialization of x, completed for the
    topologies of the Beilinson completions as on 43:65-66. Is the map 𝒜^* -> End^*_A(K^•), θ -> the differential, an
    isomorphism of graded topological algebras? Settled by a proof or by a counterexample (for instance in degree 2 for
    A = k[[s,t]]).
  - *Why it matters.* It is his "𝒜 libre pour ces propriétés", a presentation by generators and relations of the
    endomorphism algebra of Grothendieck's dualizing complex, written about thirty years before Yekutieli's explicit
    construction of that complex from the same completions; a yes gives a combinatorial description of Hom between the
    pieces of the dualizing complex; a no locates the missing relations.
  - *Rests on.* 43:50-69 (SA19-5), with 45-49 for the completions; §3.8-3.9 here.
  - *Strongest reduction known.* Degree 0 holds (Matlis: End K(x) = Ô_x); dimension 1 holds in all degrees (checked
    here: for a DVR, 𝒜^1 = Â ⊗̂ K = K̂ = Hom(K, K/A), 𝒜^{>=2} = 0); in dimension 2 the question is the degree-2 piece
    Hom(K(η), K(m)) against ⊕_p A_{[m,p,η]} modulo the image of the θ^2-relation.
  - *Material.* Transcriptions K43-04_2 and K43-05 (pages 49-78) and this file's §3.7-3.10; Hartshorne, Residues and
    Duality (LNM 20), ch. IV-VI; Beilinson, "Residues and adeles" (1980); Huber, "On the Parshin-Beilinson adeles"
    (Abh. Hamburg 1991); Yekutieli, "An explicit construction of the Grothendieck residue complex" (Astérisque 208, 1992)
    (locators unverified).
- **RP2. Geometric class field theory in all degrees (43:32-34, 43:80).**
  - *Question.* Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic p > 0, C a smooth curve over k with function field K,
    and J_{K/k} = lim J_m the pro-system of generalized Jacobians (Rosenlicht-Serre). In Serre's category of commutative
    proalgebraic groups (or on the perfect étale site of k), is Ext^i(J_{K/k}, G) = 0 for every i >= 2 and every
    commutative algebraic group G over k, so that H^*(K, G) = Ext^*(J_{K/k}, G) in all degrees (his box on 80)? Settled by
    a proof or by a G and an i >= 2 with Ext^i(J_{K/k}, G) != 0.
  - *Why it matters.* It completes his derived form of geometric class field theory; the degrees 0 and 1 are the
    classical theory, and H^i(K,G) = 0 for i >= 2 (Tsen) makes the question exactly the vanishing on the Ext side; it
    is also the input of the long sequence with the class ζ ∈ Ext^2(P, G_m) on 32-33.
  - *Rests on.* 43:24-35, 43:80 (SA19-1); 43:181-202 for the unipotent part (SA19-6).
  - *Strongest reduction known.* Degrees 0, 1 (Serre, Groupes algébriques et corps de classes, VI); the reduction to
    G_a and to finite G by dévissage of J_{K/k} (Albanese x torus x pro-unipotent) and of G; Serre's category has
    cohomological dimension 2 in characteristic p, so Ext^2 into G_a is the case to decide.
  - *Material.* K43-02_2, K43-06 transcriptions and §3.4, §3.13 here; Serre, "Groupes proalgébriques" (Publ. IHES 7,
    1960) and Groupes algébriques et corps de classes; Breen, "Extensions du groupe additif sur le site parfait" (1981);
    Milne, ADT III (locators unverified).
- **RP3. The middle self-duality of the algebraic representative in characteristic p (44:2 Th. 3-4, 44:8-9).**
  - *Question.* Let X be a smooth projective threefold over an algebraically closed field of characteristic p and Ab^2(X)
    the algebraic representative of algebraically trivial codimension-2 cycles (Murre). Is there a canonical isomorphism
    Ab^2(X) -> Ab^2(X)^dual induced by the intersection of cycles (Th. 3 with n = 3, i = 2), and is it, up to the sign of
    Th. 5, a polarization (Th. 4 with m = 2; the letter's positivity of D = r_*(p^*z.q^*z))? Settled by a proof or a
    counterexample.
  - *Why it matters.* The letter reduces Hodge's positivity theorem to this statement; in characteristic 0 it follows
    from the Hodge-Riemann relations; in characteristic p it is a case of the standard conjecture of Hodge type for the
    level-one part of H^3.
  - *Rests on.* 44:2, 44:4, 44:8-9 (SA19-14 = SA18-14); with SA18-12 (Lefschetz algebras, standard conjectures).
  - *Strongest reduction known.* m = 1 (curves: the Θ-polarization); the existence of Ab^2(X) and the injectivity of its
    Tate module into H^3(X, Z_ℓ(2)) (Murre 1985, via Bloch's map); the run must first check the literature on canonical
    polarizations of Ab^2 for threefolds in characteristic p (Achter, Casalaina-Martin and Vial's work on algebraic
    representatives; locator unverified).
  - *Material.* The cote-44 transcriptions (Pro and K44-01) and §2 here; Murre, "Applications of algebraic K-theory to
    the theory of algebraic cycles" (LNM 1124, 1985); the ACMV papers; Kleiman, "Algebraic cycles and the Weil
    conjectures" (locators unverified).
