## What the pages contain

**Duality for commutative group schemes over a local field and over a curve (43:94-142, SA19-2).**
- the "quasi" site, the Néron functor N, and the relative functors H_{S/k}, Ext_{S/k};
- the plan, items 1-14 with its list of applications;
- the functor T from complexes of K-groups to k-quasi-groups;
- the square of three dualities over a finite field;
- abelian varieties over K: H¹(K, A), Néron models as k-groups, and the pairing of component groups (43:116-122);
- Ext with supports and formal completions;
- the curve programme: C-groups, f_*, the trace, the duality theorem, and the table for μ_n, μ_p, α_p and G_m;
- local duality on the henselian trait.

**Class field theory and its geometric form (43:1-93, SA19-1 and SA19-5).**
- class field theory of number fields;
- local symbols and the Lang pairing as a biextension;
- divisors with values in G and the bicomplex DD(G);
- "Théorie de Rosenlicht": relative Picard schemes of curves, generalized Jacobians in families, and H*(X, G);
- "Adèles des proschémas" and the big Witt ring scheme;
- higher adèles over chains of points, with iterated completions and double duality;
- local cohomology of commutative algebraic groups on regular local schemes;
- the local relative Jacobian J_{A/k} and flat local cohomology;
- the curve as a complex of Jacobians, H*(X, G) = Ext*(𝒥_*, G);
- the Weil topology on Spec V.

**Further material on cote 43 (43:143-208).**
- Tate's duality theorems, a reused manuscript with his remark;
- "Groupes de Galois comme groupes analytiques sur Q_ℓ, d'après Serre";
- the Hom/Ext tables between the elementary commutative groups;
- Cartier duality, Dieudonné rings and hyperalgebras (SA19-6).

**Cote 44: the "Programme de travail" in seven theorems, and the letter to Serre of 12.8.1964 (SA19-14 = SA18-14).**
- Th. 1-4: algebraic equivalence modulo Albanese equivalence as an abelian variety;
- Th. 5 "Néron";
- Th. 6 "Lefschetz";
- Th. 7 "Matsusaka": numerical equivalence = τ-equivalence.
