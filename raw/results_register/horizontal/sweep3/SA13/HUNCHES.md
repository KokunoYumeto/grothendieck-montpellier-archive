## Sharpest (taken into the research prompt)

1. **The global 2n bound for injective dimension** [c33_c36_c39 H3; 33/170-178].
   - His Theorem (ii) on 33/172 reads: Ext^i(F, C) = 0 for i > 2n, with C having injective stalks. It reduces to his own question on 33/174, a Lazard theorem for étale sheaves of Z/N-modules. Print (SGA 5 I 1.5) has ≤ 4n.
   - It holds for constructible C, by the codimension ≥ j/2 support bound for ℰxt^j.
2. **The strong form of Théorème 7.1 of the Griffiths draft** [c33_c36_c39 H5; 36/10-27].
   - Print took the weak form, SGA 7 XXI 1.4 (non-nilpotence of Frobenius).
   - The strong form, Frobenius elements of G₀ with every prescribed trace on V₀, gives Cor. 7.2 and "G₀ n'est pas un p-groupe".
   - Linked to the monodromy of unit-root parts and the ordinarity of generic hypersurfaces (Katz, Deligne, Illusie; locators unverified).
3. **Ker(Γ → NS(X₀)) = Z/d in every relative dimension?** [c33_c36_c39 H7; 36/71, "demander Raynaud si Ker est donc Z/dZ"].
   - It is true in relative dimension 1.
   - In general, the class O(Σ(d_i/d)D_i)|X₀ is d-torsion and numerically trivial; the question is whether it lies in Pic⁰(X₀).
4. **The "théorème de 1/2 continuité" in full** [c27_c28_c41_c42 H2; 27/28-37].
   - Semicontinuity of ρ_ab, ρ_aff, ρ_r, ρ_u, ρ_n and ρ_ss for smooth group schemes over any base, four of the inequalities without flatness.
   - The "lemme à rapporter" (27:34) and ③④ (27:37) are open on the page.
5. **The étale scheme of irreducible fibre components** [c27_c28_c41_c42 H3; 41/4-14].
   - Is his valuative condition (i) necessary and sufficient for a non-separated étale M?
   - Is the functor representable by an étale scheme or algebraic space?
6. **Networks of local rings** [c27_c28_c41_c42 H4; 42/3]. Is ρ fully faithful on all S-préschémas locally of finite type? Which finiteness is needed on Y?
7. **Tori of the Cremona group** [c27_c28_c41_c42 H1; 28/5].
   - The corrected equivalence: (G_m^(n−d) ⊂ Cr_n always lies in a G_m^n) ⇔ (stably rational ⇒ rational in transcendence degree d, with d added variables).
   - Combined with Beauville-Colliot-Thélène-Sansuc-Swinnerton-Dyer, it gives non-extendable tori.
   - Compare Popov, "Tori in the Cremona groups" (2013) (locator unverified).
8. **33/110: "peut-être vrai dès que Y est noethérien"** [c33_c36_c39 H2].
   - The candidate counterexample: Y the spectrum of a strictly henselian DVR of mixed characteristic (0, p) with residue field of infinite p-degree, j the generic point, F = Z/p, cd_p(K) = ∞ (Kato; Gabber-Orgogozo; locators unverified).
9. **32/37 margin, "g loc. acyclique": does local acyclicity suffice for g'^*Rf^! ≅ Rf'^!g^*?** [c32_c35_c37_c40 H2]. Print has it for smooth g. Modern statements by Gabber and Fujiwara (locators unverified).
10. **Cote 39 axioms are Weierstrass systems** [c33_c36_c39 H8].
    - Does f′ (henselian) imply f (inverse functions) under a)-e)? His answer: "il ne semble pas".
    - Is S_n faithfully flat over A_n when k is faithfully flat over k₀ (39/13)?
11. **The [M → G] duality of 36/29-33 is Deligne's 1-motives** [c33_c36_c39 H1; c32_c35_c37_c40 H1; 37/79 "Faux schémas abéliens (ou Motifs mixtes effectifs de poids 1)"].
    - The pairing on _n[M → G] proved by filtration should be the Weil pairing of the dual 1-motive (Deligne, Hodge III §10; locator unverified).
