## Cote 40, "Calculs [affines sur des] fibrés principaux affines", scans 2-28 (SA13-66; his hand per the inventory)

### 40/2-16: affine group schemes and torsors in Hopf-algebra language
- **What he does.**
  - 40/2-3: an affine group over A is a commutative Hopf algebra B (diagonal p_B, augmentation ε, antipode x ↦ x̌, an
    involution); when B is finite free, the dual U is a cocommutative coalgebra with an associative unital multiplication
    and an involutive anti-automorphism.
  - 40/4-5: an action is a comodule algebra C (q_C: C → B ⊗ C); dually, a U-module algebra with u·1 = ε(u)1 and
    u(xy) = Σ(v_ix)(w_iy).
  - 40/6: C is principal homogeneous if (q_C, p_2): C ⊗ C → B ⊗ C is an isomorphism (equivalently g_C: B → C ⊗ C with (*),
    (**)).
  - 40/7: the categorical quotient is the algebra C' of invariants ("relativement primitifs").
  - 40/8: "ce résultat ne doit pas faire illusion": for E connected linear non-solvable and G a Borel subgroup over k
    algebraically closed, E/G exists as a scheme (projective), while O(E)^G = k.
  - 40/8-9: for principal C with A·1_C a direct summand, C' = A; the N.B.: C = B/JB (J ≠ 0) satisfies the axioms but has
    invariants A/J.
  - 40/10-15: for free C, φ: C ⊗ C → Hom_A(U, C) is an isomorphism iff ψ: C ⊗ U → Hom(C, C), ψ(x ⊗ u)(y) = x·u(y), is
    one. Proved through g_C, with ψ'(w) = Δ_C(w ⊗ id)g_C inverse to ψ, and the converse for C with a finite basis.
- **Checked (re-derived).**
  - The antipode is an involution because B is commutative.
  - The module-algebra axioms are dual to the comodule-algebra ones.
  - The Borel example: E/G is projective, so its global functions, the G-invariants of O(E), are k.
  - The direct-summand argument: write x = a + m with m in a complement M; then x ⊗ 1 = 1 ⊗ x forces m = 0.
  - The B/JB example: (q, p_2) is the reduction mod J of an isomorphism, and the invariants are A/J.
  - ψψ'(w) = w and ψ'ψ(v) = v in Sweedler notation from (i) and (ii) of 40/13. No finiteness is used for this direction.
  - Context: this is the Galois condition of Hopf-Galois theory (Chase-Sweedler 1969; Kreimer-Takeuchi 1981), where the
    two conditions agree for C finitely generated projective (locators unverified).
- **Open on the page.** 40/16: the converse construction g_C = γ ∘ ψ'_1 stops at "Pour ceci, – – – –" (margin "???").

### 40/17-28: torsors under α_p
- **What he does.**
  - 40/17: U = A[X]/X^p with X primitive, ε(X) = 0, X̌ = −X; it is self-dual. An action on C is an A-derivation D with
    D^p = 0.
  - 40/18-19: C is principal homogeneous iff C is free of rank p and the D^i (0 ≤ i < p) form a C-basis of End_A(C).
    Proposition: if C has a one-element p-basis u (C = A[u]/(u^p − a)), D u = 1 makes C principal homogeneous. Proof:
    the matrix (D^i(u^j)) is upper triangular with diagonal i!, so its determinant 0!1!...(p−1)! is a unit.
