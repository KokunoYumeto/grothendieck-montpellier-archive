## 1. The motivic Galois group of F_p is commutative, and its gerbe (15:2-14) — SA05-18

- **15:2** cover: "Groupe de Galois motivique commutatif. Cas d'un corps de base fini".
- **15:3** M the category of motives over F_p, with fibre functors T_l over Q_l for l != p and, for l = p, crystalline
  cohomology T_p (F-crystals over Q_p, "Attention, ici on ne peut remplacer Z par Ẑ !"). Niv^0(M)^0, the motives of
  level 0 and weight 0 (the Artin motives), has a natural intrinsic fibre functor F^0 over Q (F^0(H*(X)) = f_*(Q_X) for X
  finite). The gerbe of fibre functors of M contains the subgerbe of those extending F^0; its band is
  H = Ker(G^0 -> G_m, epsilon). "En vertu des conj. de Tate et de Tate-Honda", G is the group of multiplicative type over
  Q whose character group is the group Gamma of Weil numbers (15:5: algebraic, l-adic units for l != p, all conjugates of
  absolute value p^(nu/2)).
- **15:5** G^0 has characters Gamma / (roots of unity); Ker(epsilon | G^0) has characters Gamma' = Gamma / <roots of unity,
  p>, whose torsion is Z/2; H^0 is a compact torus; i: G_m -> G restricted to mu_2 splits H = H^0 x mu_2.
- **15:7** M with its grading and augmentation, the T_l and F^0, is determined by the class of the H-gerbe,
  xi_0 in H^2(Q, H) = H^2(Q, H^0) x H^2(Q, mu_2); the grading is i*(lambda) = nu iff |lambda| = p^(nu/2). For l != p,
  F_l extends F^0, so the gerbe splits at every finite l != p.
- **15:7-9** at p, T_p and F_p differ by a torsor under (G/H)_{Q_p}, an extension of G/G^0 = Ẑ by G^0/H = G_m that splits,
  so its class eta_p lies in H^1(Q_p, Ẑ) = Hom(pi_1(Q_p), Ẑ) and is "l'isom Gal(Q̃_p/Q_p) = Ẑ donné par Frobenius";
  xi_p = d(eta_p). The H^0- and mu_2-components of xi are determined by their local components; for mu_2, by
  sum of inv = 0, the finite places suffice.
- **15:11** the compact-torus component at infinity vanishes; "on sait que M n'a pas de foncteur fibre sur R": M is
  polarizable with H disconnected, or a rank-1 degree-1 object would appear, or "l'argument de Serre sur la courbe
  elliptique d'invariant de Hasse nul". So xi_infinity != 0, its mu_2-component is the nonzero element, and so is the
  mu_2-component of xi_p.
- **15:13-14** the group of Weil units Gamma^0, epsilon i(lambda) = lambda^2, the canonical Frobenius element of G(Q);
  for a simple motive M, End(M) has centre generated by Frobenius and End(M) (x) Q_p = End(T_p(M)) (Tate-Honda for
  abelian varieties); H^0 is dual to the group of Weil numbers.
- **Checked** (check events SA05-S3-1 to -4): the torsion of Gamma' is Z/2, generated by the class of sqrt(p) (a Weil
  number with sqrt(p)^2 = p and no smaller power in <roots of unity, p>; any torsion class is a root of unity times
  p^(m/n) and the weight condition forces m/n in Z/2); epsilon i(lambda) = lambda^2 matches the weights (Q(-1) has
  weight 2); "Serre's argument": a fibre functor over R would embed End^0 of a supersingular elliptic curve, the quaternion
  algebra ramified at p and infinity, into M_2(R), impossible for a division algebra; so the mu_2-components at p and at
  infinity are the nonzero ones, consistent with sum of inv = 0.
- **What it is (mine).** This is the pseudo-motivic Galois gerbe of F_p: the Weil-number protorus and the class of its
  gerbe, trivial at l != p, given at p by the Frobenius character and at infinity by mu_2. It is the object of
  Langlands-Rapoport (Shimuravarietäten und Gerben, Crelle 378, 1987) and of Milne ("Motives over finite fields", in
  Motives, Proc. Symp. Pure Math. 55, part 1, 1994). Status: his construction, conditional on the Tate conjecture as he
  says; the gerbe is known conditionally (Milne 1994) and its existence unconditionally as the pseudomotivic gerbe.

## 2. Deuring categories (15:16-22) — SA05-19 (= SA06-8)

- **15:16** definition (D): an A-preadditive category C (A Dedekind with fraction field K) is "de Deuring" if some object
  X has O = End(X) a maximal order of an Azumaya algebra over K and P -> P (x)_O X is an equivalence from right projective
  rank-1 O-modules to C; then every object works (15:16-17).
- **15:17-18** two algebras give equivalent categories iff there is an invertible bimodule; for maximal orders this
  happens iff O (x) K = O' (x) K; so classes of strictly Deuring categories correspond to Br(K).
- **15:18-19** the A-linear autoequivalences form a Gr-category with pi_1 = Brd(O), the Brandt group, pi_0 the central
  units (A* in the strict case), trivial action, and a canonical xi in H^3(Brd(O), A*); Deuring categories form a
  2-groupoid.
- **15:20-21** the banalisations of C form a gerbe on Spec A with band G_m; a Deuring-Azumaya category is essentially a
  G_m-gerbe with a rank function n(x); det X is an object of Rep of the gerbe, with det(P (x)_O X) = det(X) Nrd(P).
- **15:22** a canonical system of isomorphisms between the det(X) exists if Pic(A) = 0, Phi n A* = {1} and Phi A* = K*,
  Phi = Nrd(E*); "Ces conditions sont vérifiées si A = Z, E_R pas [déployée]".
- **Checked** (SA05-S3-5): for A = Z and E a definite quaternion algebra over Q, the norm theorem (Hasse-Schilling-Maass)
  gives Nrd(E*) = Q_{>0}, so Phi n {+-1} = {1} and Phi {+-1} = Q*; with Pic(Z) = 0 the three conditions hold.
- **What it is (mine).** The supersingular elliptic curves over F̄_p with their Hom groups form a Deuring category
  for the maximal order of the quaternion algebra ramified at p and infinity: the Deuring correspondence as an equivalence
  of categories (Deuring 1941; in categorical form e.g. Voight, Quaternion Algebras, 2021, ch. 42). The gerbe and the
  canonical determinant are his own packaging. The class in H^3 of 15:19 vanishes: on 15:74-78 he makes Eq(C) a strict
  Picard category under his condition (C), which holds for maximal orders (sweep3/SA06/STUDY.md §3).

## 3. The letter to Mumford (cote 13) — SA05-21

Decided in part 2 (REPORTS/mumford_letter_cote13.md): he withdraws the conjecture that the modular families of Hodge
structures on gamma/Gamma are algebraic (it contradicts Tate's conjecture when G(R) is compact) and keeps a necessary
genericity condition. The French draft of 13:4-10 sets up the formalism: Hodge structures on Rep(G) as homomorphisms
S = R_{C/R} G_m -> G_R, the weight i: G_m -> G, the Tate object through epsilon: G -> G_m with epsilon j = Norm, and a
polarization theorem.
