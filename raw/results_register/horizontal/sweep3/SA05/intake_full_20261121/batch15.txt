prop:B-phantom-gate || independent re-proof || the reduction step holds: under strong Tate every numerical motive is of abelian type; lifting inverse numerical isomorphisms f, g and using Kimura nilpotence of u = 1 - fg on the abelian-type N, f' = (1 + u + ... + u^{r-1}) f gives f'g = 1 - u^r = 1, so gf' is an idempotent and M = N + P with P numerically zero; conversely rational = numerical makes the quotient faithful; the equivalences with integral comparison and finite generation are cited (Kahn 2003 Theorems 5.1-5.2), and the vanishing of numerical phantoms stays open
prop:B-diagonal-Chow-gate || targeted proof check || the dimension-raising reduction holds: for dim X = d and 2 <= i <= ceil(d/2), Y = X x P^t with t = d - 2i + 1 has dimension 2m - 1 with m = d - i + 1, and z -> pr^*z . h^t is split by pr_X* (deg h^t = 1), so injectivity for Y gives it for X; the step from the Tate hypotheses to (B.9b) and the form (B.9c) are cited from Balkan-Schreieder 2026 (Corollary 1.3, Theorem 7.1, Remark 7.2), a 2026 source I have not opened; the injectivity itself stays open
prop:B-surface-supported-CH2 || independent re-proof || holds: the generic support gives a Bloch-Srinivas decomposition N.Delta = Gamma_1 + Gamma_2; for numerically trivial z in CH^2(X)_Q, a^*z is a degree-zero zero-cycle on the surface S' (degree by the projection formula), hence torsion over a finite field (a curve through its support, Poonen's Bertini, finite Jacobian), and the D'-part is a numerically trivial divisor, hence torsion; so N z = 0; the Q_l-injectivity follows from an invertible rational intersection matrix
cor:B-uniruled-threefold || independent re-proof || holds: a dominant T x P^1 -> X with T a surface moves the generic point along a rational curve to the image of T x {a}, so the class of the generic point is supported on a surface, and prop:B-surface-supported-CH2 gives rational = numerical in codimension 2; the other degrees (0, divisors, zero-cycles) hold over a finite field anyway
