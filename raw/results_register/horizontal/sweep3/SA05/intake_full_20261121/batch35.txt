thm:B-finite-duality || targeted proof check || holds as a citation chain: Geisser-Schmidt Theorem 4.6 over Q gives the pairing of H^i_c with Ext^{2-i}(F, Z^c(0)); for regular pure-dimensional X, Z^c(0)/m = Z/m(d)[2d] and their Lemma 3.7 shifts degree 2 - i to 2d + 1 - i, which is (B.26); the shift arithmetic checked, the source not opened by me
cor:B-Artin-Tate-duality || targeted proof check || holds: for W trivialized by a finite etale Galois cover Y -> X with group G, R Gamma_abs(X, W(n)) = (R Gamma_abs(Y, Q(n)) (x) W)^G; taking invariants is exact (projector |G|^{-1} sum g), commutes with every extension to Q_l, and restriction-corestriction preserve the local f and g conditions; so the Tate-case duality descends
prop:B-general-rational-gate || targeted proof check || the construction is right: setting A^2(M) = A^1(M*)^dual and A^3(M) = A^0(M*)^dual gives a perfect pairing RA(M) (x) RA(M*) -> Q[-3] by evaluation, and the Poitou-Tate calculation supplies the degree-2 and degree-3 comparisons; conversely such a completion forces the f-admissibility conditions; conditional on those, as stated
prop:B-finite-field-coefficient-gate || targeted proof check || the formulas hold: A^i(P_{X,n}) = H_M^{2n+i}(X, Q(n)) = CH^n(X, -i)_Q vanishes for i > 0, and the rational Weil splitting gives W^i(P) = A^i(P) + e A^{i-1}(P) with e^2 = 0 (Kahn's Remark 10.5.2(2)); perfect duality for all Chow-heart coefficients is then equivalent to Beilinson plus Parshin, both open
