# full depth: B lemmas and gates (2)
lem:B-unit-cup-prime-uniform || targeted proof check || holds in outline: on U open in a smooth projective Y the sequence 0 -> kbar^x -> E -> B -> Pic(Ybar) -> Pic(Ubar) -> 0 makes E/kbar^x a finite free lattice with finite Galois action, and kbar^x has no higher Galois cohomology (Hilbert 90, Lang); the prime-uniform generation then follows with one integer; conditional on divisor Tate at one prime
lem:B-divisor-splitting || targeted proof check || holds: on a smooth projective compactification the intersection form with an ample class is nondegenerate on NS_Q (Hodge index), so its orthogonal complement splits the divisor subspace of H^2 (Yuan's argument, no Tate needed); the restriction kernel is spanned by boundary divisor classes inside that summand, so the splitting descends to the open Y
prop:B-relative-curve || targeted proof check || holds: a degree-d multisection gives a line bundle of fibre degree d, so cup with c_1(L) is multiplication by d from R^0 to R^2(1), Deligne's 1968 Proposition 2.4 splits the Leray filtration over Q_l with pieces H^2(S, Q_l(1)), H^1(S, V_l J), Q_l, and the middle piece is V_l of Br(Y)/im Br(S) up to the finite cokernel of the degree
prop:B-finite-chain-proper || targeted proof check || holds: [a] on B = A^t acts by a^j on R^j, splitting the Leray filtration; the a-eigenspace of H^2 is H^1(Sbar, V_l A), and on Pic, [a]^* acts by a on Pic^0 = A, by a^2 on relative NS and by 1 on the base, so the a-eigenspace of the divisor image is the Kummer subspace of sections; the four conditions of his chain 12:74-80 are then equivalent
thm:B-relative-surface-gate || targeted proof check || holds in outline as a citation chain: Kahn 2023 (Theorems 6, 8) and Morrow 2019 (Proposition 4.1, Lemma 4.2, Theorem 4.3) carry divisor Tate from surfaces to all smooth separated varieties, which gives the relative-curve finiteness; the converse through Lefschetz-pencil families was read; surface Tate stays open
prop:B-singular-proper-Brauer-gate || targeted proof check || holds in outline: Chow's lemma and de Jong alterations give a smooth projective proper cover, the Kahn-Morrow reduction gives divisor Tate there, properness kills the weight -1 arithmetic H^1 term, and on a regular scheme H^2(G_m) is torsion (Grothendieck Brauer II 1.4, 1.8); descent to the singular proper base then adds no further prime-to-p gate
