## Cote 123

**H20-1. Cote 123 holds his own notes on the simultaneous resolution, over a general base, with Picard groups.**
- *Grounds:* 123/004–014. The theorem on 011 states the resolution ℬ → G′ = G ×_{T/W} T: p is projective, f̃ is smooth,
  p is an isomorphism exactly over G′^reg, and the codimensions are 2 and 3. The Pic statements of 011–014 are with
  their proofs, and 014 explains why the Weyl base change is needed.
- *Assessment:* the published accounts (Brieskorn 1970; Slodowy 1980, chapter 3 and §4) work over a field, as far as I
  recall. The base-S form, the Pic sequence 0 → Pic(D) → Pic(ℬ) → H¹(Γ, M), and the remark on 014 may never have been
  published as such.
- *Link:* SGA 3, which this dossier uses ("SGA 3 XIII" on 005).

**H20-2. The letter of 22.10.1969 is the dated primary source of Grothendieck's dimension conjecture for Springer fibres.**
- *Grounds:* 123/023, read on the image. The literature calls dim Z_G(u) = r + 2 dim B_u a formula "conjectured by
  Grothendieck" (Douglass–Röhrle 2008, abstract), proved by Steinberg (1976) and Spaltenstein.
- *What is not known:* whether the letter is the source the literature has in mind (Brieskorn's ICM talk? Steinberg's
  paper?).
- *Value:* the letter gives the conjecture in his words, with its motivation: rational singularities of the fibres of
  the adjoint quotient. The Hesselink and Kempf-type results on rational singularities came later (from memory).

**H20-3. "Quelle extension de (Z/hZ)* par Z/hZ obtient-on ?" (123/002), and the "sous-groupes remarquables" of a Weyl
group (029).**
- *The question:* whether N_W(⟨c⟩) is split over ⟨c⟩.
- *What I know:* in type A it is the affine group Z/h ⋊ (Z/h)*, which is split.
- *To do:* it is computable type by type, E₈ included (|N| = 240). Whether the extension N ∩ N′ of (Z/hZ)* by μ_h × Z of
  029 splits is the same kind of question.

**H20-4. "A-t-on un isom can 𝔤_ψ ≅ Δ ?" (123/034).**
- *What it asks:* a canonical isomorphism between the highest-root line and Kostant's line Δ (cyclic lines mod μ_h*),
  equivariant for outer automorphisms.
- *A test case:* the outer automorphism of A_n (n ≥ 2) acts on 𝔤_ψ by −1, by the bracket expansion of e_ψ.
- *To do:* computing the sign on Δ type by type settles his question and the margin on 042 ("peut-on choisir un gén.
  can. de e_{−ψ} ?").

**H20-5. His programme on 019: "déterminer exactement les classes de sous-groupes paraboliques de Dynkin–Kostant …"**
- *Grounds:* 123/019. Rational nilpotent orbits and forms of groups; the "Deligne" filtration attached to a unipotent
  (019) is the later Deligne–Jacobson–Morozov filtration.
- *A concrete subquestion:* the classes c with P = Borel. These are the even orbits without zero nodes; in type A, the
  partitions [n], and [n−1, 1] for n−1 even. They are where his NB on outer forms bites.

**H20-6. The margin on 047 ("nilpotente régulière ssi tous les autres ne s'annulent pas ?").**
- *My answer:* yes in type A and no in general. A vanishing coefficient at a node of label a_i > 1 gives a distinguished
  non-regular nilpotent: Kac coordinates and the Kostant–Kac classification of graded nilpotents (from memory).
- *To check* in R20-2.
