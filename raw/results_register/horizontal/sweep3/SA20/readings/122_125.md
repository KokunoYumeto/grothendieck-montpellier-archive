### Re-derivations (each a check event)

1. **(A_m) ⟺ (C_m).** The long exact sequence H^p_Y(X,F) → H^p(X,F) → H^p(X−Y,F) → H^{p+1}_Y(X,F) → H^{p+1}(X,F)
   gives: H^p(X−Y) = 0 for all p ≥ m iff H^p_Y → H^p is onto for p ≥ m and H^{p+1}_Y → H^{p+1} is one-to-one for
   p ≥ m. That is (C_m). Agrees.
2. **(A_m) ⟺ (B_m)** ("kif kif").

   - Choose 0 → K → ⊕O(−n_j) → F → 0 with all n_j ≥ n₀, and argue by descending induction on p from p = dim + 1.
   - Only finitely many p matter, so one n₀ serves.

3. **The NB "p = m peut être omis".**

     r).

     and it supports reading m in the header.
4. **(C_m) ⟺ (E_m) (the arrow "dualité").**

   - Formal duality: H^p_Y(X, F)^∨ = Ext^{r−p}(X̂; F̂, Ω̂^r). That is his 005, via H^p_Y = lim→ Ext^p(O_{Y_n}, F) and
     the algebraic dual of a colimit being the limit of the duals (SGA 2 XII §1, Hartshorne, Ample subvarieties III.3;
     from memory).
   - The dual of a surjection of vector spaces is an injection and the dual of a bijection a bijection. With q = r−p,
     p > m becomes q < r−m and p = m becomes q = r−m. Agrees.
   - This is the same check as the batch-K return's M1 (a computation by GPT-6.2 Astra at reasoning effort max, ChatGPT
     Work), now with the duality itself re-derived rather than carried.
5. **(E_m) ⟺ (G_m).**

     free sheaves.
   - (G_m) for O(n) ⊗ Ω^r is (D_m), hence (A_m), hence (C_m) for every coherent F, hence (E_m).
   - So the chain closes. Agrees.
6. **(F_m) = (F′_m).** Serre vanishing H^q(X, O(n)) = 0 for q > 0, n ≫ 0. Bijectivity for 0 < q < r−m becomes

7. **The oblique NB** (X = P^r, or any X with H^p(X, O(k)) = 0 for 0 < p < r and depth_Y O_X ≥ r−n).

   - So H^p(X−Y, O(k)) ≅ H^p(X, O(k)) = 0 for 0 < p and p+1 < r−n, i.e. p < r−n−1.
   - H^0 is an isomorphism when H^0_Y = H^1_Y = 0, i.e. r−n ≥ 2 (n < r−1, Hartogs). Agrees.
   - For a general projective X^r the NB needs those two hypotheses, which the page does not state.
8. **005, "Son dual est".**

### How it connects

- 003 and 008 are the duality criterion for the cohomological dimension of a complement: cd(X−Y) < m iff cohomology
  agrees with that of the formal completion below r−m.
- The criterion is in SGA 2 XII §1 ("théorème de dualité projective") and XIII. It was later systematised by Hartshorne
  (Ann. of Math. 88 (1968); Ample subvarieties, LNM 156 (1970), III.3.4), Ogus (Ann. of Math. 98 (1973)) and Speiser in
  characteristic p (all from memory).
- Hartshorne, Compositio Math. 23 (1971), Prop. 2.9, for X = P^n, is cited by Michel Hua's project, not checked here.
- The "Cas important" m = r−n, the least possible cd, is the set-theoretic complete intersection problem.
- 008's item 2 and 006 point to the Lefschetz theorems of SGA 2 X–XII.
- Cote 122/028's "Lefschetz local" is the local counterpart (SA20-18). The two folders are the projective and the local
  sides of the same SGA 2 programme.
- Paper and title date the folder to the SGA 2 period, about 1961–62: the EGA-type typescript of 004 and 007, and
  "dualité locale", the subject of SGA 2 V. This is my inference; nothing on the sheets is dated.
