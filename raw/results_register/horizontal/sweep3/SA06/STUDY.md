## 1. Weil numbers and CM fields (15:96-108, 15:128-133) — SA06-9

- **15:128-130** the maximal totally real field 𝕂 of Q̄ (its group pi' is the closed normal subgroup generated by complex
  conjugation tau); H = Gal of the maximal abelian extension of 𝕂 is killed by 2; there is a unique quadratic extension 𝕃
  of 𝕂 Galois over Q (e.g. 𝕂(sqrt(-1))); corollary: a subfield of Q̄ lies in 𝕃 iff it is totally real or a totally
  imaginary quadratic extension of a totally real field; 𝕃 contains Q^ab.
- **15:131-133** for Q in Q* and lambda algebraic, the equivalent conditions: (a) every conjugate has |lambda_alpha|^2 = Q;
  (b) lambda generates over a totally real K a CM extension (or lies in K) with lambda lambda-bar = Q; (b'') lambda is a root of
  T^2 - aT + Q with a totally real and 4Q - a^2 totally >= 0; (c) lambda in 𝕃 with lambda lambda-bar = Q. Remark: for every
  totally real K and integer Q > 1 there is a Weil Q-integer of weight 1 generating a CM field containing K.
- **15:96-98** the units of a CM field with u u-bar = 1 at primes over p: Prop. (x = y^2/N(y) has divisor 2h(p - p') and
  x sigma(x) = 1); N.B.: if no prime of K over p splits in L, every lambda with lambda lambda-bar = p^i is p^(i/2) times a root of
  unity.
- **15:100-106** necessary conditions for lambda to be a Frobenius eigenvalue over F_q: integral, in Q'(sqrt(-1)) (= 𝕃),
  |lambda_alpha| = q^(i/2); the group M(q) they define (weight = i). "Conjecture forte": the conditions suffice, which
  determines the motivic Galois group over F_q as the dual of M(q); G/G^0 is dual to the roots of unity, i.e. to Ẑ;
  M(p^infinity) = lim M(q) is torsion-free, its dual the motivic Galois group of F̄_p, G = D(M_0 (x) Q) x G_m with
  epsilon(x, lambda) = lambda^2. "Conjecture faible": a power lambda^m is a Frobenius eigenvalue over F_(q^m); margin:
  "Résulte probablement de la théorie des multiplications complexes: cf. Taniyama-Shimura".
- **Checked** (SA06-S3-6 to -8): the Proposition of 15:96 and its N.B.; the equivalences of 15:131-132 and the corollary of
  15:130; G/G^0 and the torsion-freeness of M(p^infinity) on 15:102.
- **Status.** Known: weight 1 of both conjectures is the Honda-Tate theorem (Honda, J. Math. Soc. Japan 20 (1968);
  Tate, Sém. Bourbaki 352 (1968)), proved through CM lifting as his margin guesses; the field 𝕃 = Q^CM and the Weil-number
  conditions are standard. Open (his): the strong conjecture in weight i > 1 as stated (every Weil q-integer of weight i a
  Frobenius eigenvalue on H^i of some smooth projective variety over F_q): I know no reference settling it.

## 2. Commutative motivic Galois groups and their compact part (15:84-86, 15:135-137) — SA06-9, SA06-10

- **15:84** M a motivic category over k with commutative band G (multiplicative type over Q); for a faithful E and a
  polarization, phi(u) phi(u)' = epsilon(u) on A = End(E); so U = Ker(epsilon) maps into the unitary group of a positive
  involution, which is compact: U^0 is a compact torus.
- **15:86** B = the algebra generated by G(Q) in A is a product of fields L_i, "chaque L_i ext. quadratique d'un corps de nb
  K_i tt. réel", with complex conjugation as the involution; B^u = H^u(Q), H^u a compact torus. **Refined** (check event
  SA06-S3-5): a commutative semisimple Q-algebra with positive involution is a product of CM fields and of totally real
  fields with trivial involution; the totally real case occurs (Frobenius sqrt(p) on a weight-1 motive over F_p, the real
  Weil number whose class generates Gamma'_tors on 15:5). The conclusion stands: the unitary group of a totally real factor
  is {+-1}. His own corollary on 15:130 includes the totally real case.
- **15:135-137** "Groupes de Galois motiviques commutatifs": M_0 generated by Q(1) (band G_m, trivialized because
  H^1(Q, G_m) = 0), the gerbe G_phi with band U = Ker(epsilon); M' the Artin motives (G -> G' with kernel G^0), a fibre
  functor psi from a choice of k̄, a gerbe with band G^0 ("un choix très particulier de trivialisation", since
  H^1(Q, Gamma) != 1 in general); M^(0) generated by M_0 and M' with group G' x G_m. This is the general frame of 15:3-7
  (SA05-18).

## 3. Deuring categories, the Brandt Picard category and the Deuring element (15:34-78) — SA06-8 (= SA05-19)

- **15:34** rigidified Deuring categories (C, tau), tau in V_A(C); couples (C, tau) as mu_n-gerbes; the autoequivalences
  are bimodules P with N_{O/A}(P) = A, a strictly commutative Picard category with Aut(1) = mu_n(A), containing Tors(S, mu_n).
- **15:36** pi_1 sits in a sequence with Ram(C/A) = Brd(C)/Pic(A) = sum_p Z/e_p (e_p the ramification); base change to
  A' gives torsor-categories under Eq(C_A', T_A') and Eq(C', T'), with a canonical equivalence between the two, margin
  "À vérifier!!". (Mine: 15:74-78 supply this verification in the unrigidified case, under condition (C).)
- **15:40** the case of the quaternion algebra of index 2 "ramifié exactement en 2 et ∞" (the ink reads 2; the page needs p:
  correction below): Brd(C) is the split Picard category with invariants Z/2, Z/2, the generator rho of pi_0 is
  E -> E^(p) = E/Ker F_E, the unique prime ideal m of O over p, rho-bar = p. For p invertible in A' the image of (rho, rho-bar)
  in H^1(S', mu_2) is the class of p mod squares; over Z_p the group is Z_p*/Z_p*^2 x Z/2 (generated by (m, p)).
- **15:42-50** gluing: on A' = prod_{l != p} Z_l the gerbe of square roots of A'(1); at p, F-crystals over F̄_p of rank 2
  and slope 1/2 with the Tate functor; at infinity the standard mu_2-gerbe over R. This gives a torsor T'(C) under
  Gamma' = A'*/A'*^2 x (Z_p*/Z_p*^2 x Z/2) x R*/R*^2 on which Frobenius f acts by (p, (0,1), p); the quotient T(F̄_p) is a
  canonical torsor under H^1(𝔸, mu_2) = prod_l Z_l*/Z_l*^2 (p and infinity included), and Ẑ acts trivially on it. A
  canonical mu_2-gerbe G(xi) on the adèles, trivial, whose sections up to isomorphism form T. Deuring's theory of the
  supersingular curves gives (a) an element xi_p of T_p, "l'élément de Deuring", and (b) a section of G(xi_p).
- **15:52** at infinity: C(xi_p)_infinity is the category of right quaternionic lines over H with the reduced norm; for the
  Deuring element one gets "un foncteur ess. canonique des courbes elliptiques de Hasse nul vers les vectoriels quatern.
  unitaires de dim 1"; the norm corresponds to the polarization. (This is the real counterpart of "Serre's argument" on
  15:11: no real fibre functor, but a quaternionic one.)
- **15:54-56** "Détermination de xi_p?": an element of T_p is a rule (M, O) -> u_xi(M, O) in Isom(wedge^2 M, T_p)/Z_p*^2
  for rank-2 F-crystals M over F_(p^2) with O-action, stable under isomorphism and compatible with P (x)_O M through
  N_{O/Z}(P) = Z; equivalently on triples (M_0, O, i), i: R -> O, R the integers of Q(sqrt(-p)), i.e. a descent of M to F_p
  with F^2 + p = 0. The page stops there: xi_p is not determined.
- **15:60-61, 66-78** the general theory: "produits tensoriels raisonnables", "jolie" categories, special functors; the
  Brandt category B, the Deuring category C, the category 𝒪 of related orders; local case Aut ext(O) = Brd(C) = Z/e (15:70:
  e classes of automorphisms, so a local obstruction to automorphisms being inner); the 2-categories B_2 -> B_1 -> B_0
  (Brandt 2-groupoid of bi-ideals in E, bimodules, Deuring categories) "n'ont pas de quasi-inverses naturels"; under (C)
  (bi-ideals commute, LM = ML) a strict commutativity constraint on Eq(C), independent of the object X (Proposition, proved
  on 15:76, "OK."). **Conclusion (15:78):** the Eq(C) are canonically strict Picard categories, all canonically equivalent
  to one Brandt Picard category B; C' -> Eq(C, C') is an equivalence from B_0 to the torsor-categories under B; base change
  gives B -> B'.
- **Checked** (SA06-S3-1 to -4): the class of p in H^1(S', mu_2) and the group over Z_p on 15:40; the splitting
  Gamma' = Gamma'_0 x f(+-1) on 15:42; the Proposition and Conclusion of 15:74-78, with the consequence that the
  Gr-category class of 15:19 vanishes under (C) (a strictly commutative Picard category over a point is split:
  SGA 4 XVIII 1.4, Ext^2_Z = 0); (C) holds for maximal orders, whose two-sided ideals form a free abelian group.
- **Status.** Known in substance: the Deuring correspondence (Deuring 1941). His own: the Brandt Picard category and the
