## Cote 1, scans 3-36 (Pro MPL_1_002-050): rearrangements, Weyl functions, traces, the determinant Δ

**Printed counterpart (mine, locator unverified):** these sheets are the manuscript of Grothendieck, "Réarrangements de
fonctions et inégalités de convexité dans les algèbres de von Neumann munies d'une trace", Séminaire Bourbaki, exposé 113
(1955). Today's names: φ_A is the generalized singular-value function μ_t(A) of Fack-Kosaki (Pacific J. Math. 123,
1986), and Δ is the Fuglede-Kadison determinant (Ann. of Math. 55, 1952). Sweep 2 named only Fuglede-Kadison.

- **3 (plan, page).** Seven numbers: rearrangements; Weyl functions; fundamental inequalities (A ∈ ā); spectral measures
  and decreasing rearrangement of an operator; complements on the determinant; the fundamental inequality for AB; other
  inequalities.
- **4-6 (multipliers, page).** For a C*-algebra C, the continuous maps commuting with left translations are x ↦ ax with
  a in Γ_g = {a ∈ C'' : aC ⊂ C}; Γ = {a ∈ C'' : aC ⊂ C, Ca ⊂ C}. If C sits weakly dense in a von Neumann algebra R, the
  normal map ψ: C'' → R is an isomorphism of Γ onto Γ_1 = {x ∈ R : xC ⊂ C, Cx ⊂ C}, with inverse φ. **Checked (mine):**
  the injectivity step on 6 is right: ψ(xy) = ψ(x)y = 0 and xy ∈ C give xC = 0, so x = 0 (an approximate unit of C
  converges weakly to 1 in C''). Today: the multiplier algebra M(C) as the idealizer of C in C'' or in any such R
  (Busby 1968; Akemann-Pedersen-Tomiyama 1973; locators unverified). **Read on the scan (4, 200 dpi):** the ink defines Γ_1 by "xC ⊂ C, Cx ⊂ R"
  (Pro reads "Ca(?) ⊂ R", HC1-01 "Cx ⊂ R(?)"). That is his slip: Γ_1 must be *-stable ("c'est une sous C*-algèbre",
  and φ(Γ_1) ⊂ Γ through hermitian parts), which needs Cx ⊂ C.

  1 + f; Prop. 2, the Hardy-Littlewood inequality ∫ fg ≤ ∫ φ_f φ_g (Hardy-Littlewood-Pólya, *Inequalities*, ch. X,
  locator unverified).

    Counterexample: M = [0,2], f = 1 on [0,1], g = 1 on ]1,2]; at t = 3/2, φ_{f+g}(t) = 1 while φ_f(t) + φ_g(t) = 0.

- **12-18 (Polya and Weyl functions, page).** Polya function: symmetric (P1), convex (P2), lower semicontinuous under
  convergence in measure (P3), equivalent to weak* lsc on L^∞(K) (P'3) by Banach-Dieudonné. Prop. 3 (15-16): P(f) =

    continuity in the trace norm); then his reduction of Prop. 8 to a finite trace stands.
