## A2_161-4_161-6_162

## Hunches (with grounds)

- **A2-H1 (dating, 161-4).** Scan 23 plans two English summer courses, "Introduction to A.G." and "Introduction to
  algebraic groups", and scans 23-32 develop affine algebraic geometry through representable functors on k-algebras.
  This matches his 1973 summer school at SUNY Buffalo ("Introduction to functorial algebraic geometry, part 1: affine
  algebraic geometry", notes by F. Gaeta; locator unverified). Comparing Gaeta's table of contents with (1)-(11) of scans
  23-32 would settle it. The French descent notes (scans 3-17) look like the plan of another course ("Traversée du
  désert").
- **A2-H2 (dating, 161-6).** Rotation systems for graphs on oriented surfaces, the mapping class group called "groupe de
  Teichmuller", and the icosahedron with its group A_5 as finite étale objects over Z[1/5] are the themes and vocabulary of
  "Esquisse d'un programme" (1984): the sheets are probably from about 1977-84.
- **A2-H3 (161-6 scan 22).** His three statements on icosahedra over a base S (étale-local existence for each root c of
  c^2 + c - 1 = 0, local conjugacy under SO(Q), automorphism group finite étale with fibres A_5) should hold over
  Z[1/2, 1/5] with Q a non-degenerate quadratic form of rank 3; a short proof (via the étale-locally constant
  configuration of 12 points and B'(x,y)^2 = Q(x)Q(y)/5) would make a clean note.
- **A2-H4 (162-5).** His question "y aura-t-il une symétrie s ?" has a positive answer for adic formal categories with
  Sym Ω ≃ Gr(A): the shear map (g, h) |-> (g, gh) is an isomorphism on associated graded, hence an isomorphism; in
  characteristic zero it also follows from "Quillen's theorem" as he states it. His "Θ-Algèbre de Lie" of 6.9.68 is the
  definition of a Lie algebroid (Pradines 1967; Lie-Rinehart pairs, Rinehart 1963), and his 5. is the correspondence
  with differential graded structures on Λ*Ω (Vaintrob 1997); locators unverified.
- **A2-H5 (162-5).** "Peut-il s'y ramener, en raisonnant dans un topos convenable ?!": the topos of C-stratified
  sheaves (the stratifying topos of the formal category) should reduce Quillen's theorem with coefficients to the
  crystalline starting theorem, as the infinitesimal topos does for X/S.
- **A2-H6 (162-1).** The loan list spans about 1966-70, so "mes notes sur tapis Quillen I, II" lent to Mme Hakim (after
  6.9.68) and the Deligne, Katz and Berthelot papers give a dated network for other undated folders (crystals, 161-5).


## A_161-1_161-3

## Hunches (with grounds)

- **H1 (161-2 scans 12, 64; 7-9).** His question "des conditions de type (i') peuvent-elles être exprimées comme
  conditions de type (ii') ?" has a positive answer in Cat: fullness (surjectivity on Hom) of restriction along φ is
  essential surjectivity of restriction along the inclusion of R-bar ⊔_{R ⊔ R} (R × [1]) ⊔ ... into R-bar × [1]
  (cylinders), and faithfulness likewise with a double cylinder; this is how the 0-, 1-, 2-faithful conditions of the Δ_f
  theorem (Schanuel's "conditions d'exactitudes généralisées", scan 64) reduce to one kind. Not proved here; the
  2-categorical orthogonality literature (Kelly 1989) would confirm.
- **H2 (dating, F13).** The accessible-category sheets (161-2 scans 55-62, 78) were written with the printed numbering
  of SGA 4 I §8-9 at hand (every locator I checked matches); "(modifié 9.4?)" and "9.12. (Condition c) renforcée)" read
  like proposals for the text of Exposé I, so a date during the preparation of the 1972 edition (about 1968-72) is the
  most economical reading. Checking whether the 1964 multigraphed Exposé I has §9 with these numbers would decide
  between "before printing" and "after".
- **H3 (priority, F13).** If H2 holds, his definition "π-accessible = L_π + a small strictly generating subcategory of
  π-accessible objects; in general Ind_π(C)" and the theorem "E π-accessible iff E ≃ Ind_π(C) iff E_π small and strictly
  generating" (scans 55, 60) predate Lair (1981) and Makkai-Paré (1989) by about a decade, and the cocomplete case meets
  Gabriel-Ulmer (1971). Worth a comparison with the printed SGA 4 I 9.4-9.11 to see how much of it reached print.
- **H4 (dating, SA23-54).** "Topos modulaire" on scan 112 says the 2-fibred product of topoi is "pas encore prouvé",
  while scan 22 gives a construction it calls "± connue": scan 112 likely precedes scan 22, and both precede or are
  contemporary with Giraud's "Classifying topos" (1972) and Hakim (1972).
- **H5 (161-2 scan 79).** His question on finite-product theories (which Σ give the same S) is the Morita problem for
  Lawvere theories; the answer should be: full subcategories of the perfectly presentable objects of S, closed under
  finite coproducts, whose idempotent completion contains them all (Adámek-Rosický-Vitale 2011, ch. 15; locator
  unverified).
- **H6 (161-2 scan 44-50, the ring catalogue).** The "topologie hensélienne" of item 3) is the Nisnevich topology (1989)
  and the fppf item 5) the topology of "ultralocal" rings; the catalogue may be the earliest systematic list of
  classifying topoi of ring-theoretic properties; to compare with Hakim (1972) and Wraith/Johnstone (1970s).
- **H7 (161-2 scan 93).** "La réciproque est vraie, et connue": the known result is Olivier's absolutely flat
  universal ring (Séminaire Samuel 1967-68), which would date the sheet after 1967 (locator unverified).
- **H8 (161-3 scan 15).** His "contre-exemple pour des produits infinis" of discrete spaces may need I uncountable: I
  recall (unverified) Isbell's results that countable localic products of complete metric spaces are spatial, while
  uncountable products of infinite discrete spaces are not.
- **H9 (161-3 scan 11).** The "Raffinement" (no non-empty locally closed part without relatively quasi-compact
  neighbourhoods suffices for Top(X × Y) ≃ Top(X) × Top(Y)) is sharper than the local-compactness criterion of the
  literature; I have not seen it stated (search needed).
- **H10 (dating).** The English sheets of 161-3 (scans 35-41, 49, 53-55) and 161-2 (scans 34-35) are notes for, or of,
  an English course or talk on topoi and triples, about 1966-73; Grothendieck's 1973 Buffalo lectures on topoi are a
  candidate for 161-3 (not established).
- **H11 (161-1 scan 20).** His Lemma (extension from a strictly generating C ⊂ E closed under finite inductive limits)
  is true when the objects of C are finitely presentable in E and is open in general; 161-2 scan 57 g) states a version
  with finite projective limits instead.


## B_1_2_21

## Hunches

1. **Lidskii's theorem, in his hand (strong).** Cote 1 states the trace formula (K1-02, 71; struck on K1-03, 79) and
   proves it. The struck page 80 gives the chain in his words: summable eigenvalues, coefficient domination
   |α_n(u)| ≤ α_n(|u|), a genus lemma "supposé connu", and the easy case of det(1+zh) with h ≥ 0. K1-05 (111-112)
   proves the lemma with an elementary three-sector argument. Lidskii published in 1959. Whether "La théorie de
   Fredholm" (Bull. SMF 84, 1956) or "Produits tensoriels topologiques" (Mem. AMS 16, 1955) prints this argument for
   Hilbert space is the first thing to check. If the print lacks it, these sheets hold an earlier proof, a fact to
   state with the dating of the sheets: they are undated, and the cote carries a 1953 letter.
2. **Seiler-Simon and McCarthy-Rotfel'd before 1967-1975.** Two inequalities appear on these sheets:
   - det(1 + |A+B|) ≤ det(1 + |A|) det(1 + |B|) (K1-02, 72; K1-06, 125);
   - S_p(A+B) ≤ S_p(A) + S_p(B) for 0 < p < 1, even in the truncated Ky Fan form, with his Stieltjes-type device f_φ
     (K1-02, 75-76; K1-06, 130).

   The same check of the 1955-56 prints applies.
3. **The Séminaire Bourbaki exposé 113 (1955)** is, in my identification, the printed form of the Pro sheets 3-36.
   Comparing the two would date the sheets and show whether the infinite-trace slip of 29-30 reached print.
4. **Several of his questions have sharp answers today** (each answer is in its section):
   - 1/76: the class of functions f is the concave ones;
   - 1/146 and 1/143: true by Ando 1988 and Birman-Koplienko-Solomyak 1975;
   - 1/144: no individual bound |λ_i| ≤ C ρ_{⌈ci⌉}, only the geometric-mean bound;
   - 1/143: the Kadec-Klee form for 1 ≤ p < ∞.

   A short note could state them in his own formulations.
5. **The l¹ function S (1/120-122)** is a natural example of an entire function of unbounded type on a Banach space.
   The Laplace form S(x) = ∫_0^∞ e^{-t} Π(1 + t x_i) dt proves this in two lines. It may be an early example of the
   phenomenon (unverified).
6. **Cote 2, the Chern ring CK against gr_γ K.** The kernel of φ_K is killed by (i-1)! in degree i, and the P^{n-1}
   example reaches this in degree n. His margin points at a natural question: does adding the tangent class to the
   geometric list always make φ_K injective for K(X) of a smooth projective X?
7. **Cote 2, 32-38: covariant Chern characters and "bivification".** The programme matches the Riemann-Roch theorem of
   Baum-Fulton-MacPherson (1975) and the bivariant theories of Fulton-MacPherson (1981):
   - Chern characters ch^{X/S} compatible with proper direct images "sans plus";
   - K and Chow groups with supports;
   - Riemann-Roch as the computation of ch^{X/S} on the image of K•(X).

   The leaves 34, 36 and 39 are reused SGA 4 typescripts citing EGA IV (1964). So cote 2 mixes layers: the curve and
   surface sheets (3-8, 41-45) are on the "LINEN TYPEWRITER / ARGENTINA" paper, while the sheets on supports sit
   among the 1960s typescripts. The "[1957]" of the catalogue (Pro note S06) should not be applied to all of them.
8. **Cote 2, 10-14: the elliptic × P³ class with trivial Chern classes.** It is an explicit early instance of torsion
   defeating Chern classes, with his general mechanism ((r-1)! c(x) = 0 ⇒ C̃(i_!x) = 0, and (i_*)_r is not injective
   under torsion). The general statement belongs with Hua's register entry 2-trivial-total-chern-class and with
   Riemann-Roch without denominators.
9. **Cote 21, "Notes pour Dieud." § 9.** The sheets contain:
   - the Eilenberg-Watts theorem for A-C'-modules;
   - I-torsion left-exact functors as I-torsion modules;
   - the Ext test for I-depth (2.12);
   - the injectivity criterion 2.15 and Γ_I of an injective (2.16);
   - P_1, P_2, P_3 for sheaves and fibred categories.

   This is the material of SGA 2, Exp. II-III and X, and of EGA IV 5.9-5.10 (locators unverified). The printed form
   should be located, to see whether the stack hypothesis that P_3 needs was added there.
