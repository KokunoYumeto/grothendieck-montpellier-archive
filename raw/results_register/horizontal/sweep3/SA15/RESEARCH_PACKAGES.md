## RP-SA15-1. Monodromy of the Hasse-Witt local system of hypersurface sections, and point counts mod p (4:12, H1, H2)

- **Question.** For X smooth projective over a finite field and d large, determine the image of pi_1(U_d) on
  V = E(Y, O_Y)_ss (the semisimple part of the coherent vanishing cohomology of the hypersurface sections Y, an
  F_p-local system on the open set U_d of 8.5.9): is it all of GL(V)? Then prove his consequence: every nonempty open set of
  hypersurfaces of degree d contains, over a finite extension, a Y with card Y(k') congruent mod p to any given residue.
  Settle his "n° 7" (dim E(Y, O_Y)_SS > 0 for d large) on the way, and state 8.5 unconditionally now that crystalline
  cohomology exists (8.5.7 is proved: sweep3/SA15/STUDY.md §1).
- **Why it matters.** It is a precise question of his, with a complete reduction on the page (Woods Hole formula plus
  Chebotarev), that Katz and he could not settle; even plane curves (X = P^2) are open on the page.
- **Rests on.** 4:2-12 (SA15-9); the unit-root count of 8.5.4; SA06-6 (Lefschetz theorems).
- **Strongest known reduction.** His: big monodromy implies the point-count statement. Known since: crystalline
  universal coefficients and duality (Berthelot, LNM 407, 1974); generic ordinarity of complete intersections (Illusie,
  Grothendieck Festschrift II, 1990); big-monodromy theorems for the étale p-torsion in other families (Ekedahl;
  Faltings-Chai (locator unverified)).
- **Material.** Transcription and scans of cote 4 (and the Codex witness K4-01); the missing n° 7 if it is found in the
  archive; Berthelot 1974; Illusie 1990; Katz, "Slope filtration of F-crystals" (Astérisque 63, 1979); SGA 4 1/2 ("Fonctions
  L modulo l^n et modulo p") for the trace formula mod p.
