**What he is interested in.** First the spatial ateliers: the set-theoretic ones, those defined by ordered sets, and the maquettes. Among these, the local ones. Then the tame ateliers, "qui incluent les maquettes".

**His presumption (scans 2-3).** "Je présume qu'il y a lieu pour les ateliers modérés d'être spatiaux (voire pas néc. locaux), et peut être aussi que l'ensemble ordonné Σ des supports soit 'modulaire' i.e. A ∧ Sup_i B_i = Sup_i A ∧ B_i, donc une 'algèbre de Boole'. Il est possible que cela implique que l'atelier est aussi fidèle."
- The 156-8 reading proves the converse direction: faithful ⇒ Σ is the complete Boolean algebra of regular open sets (SA21-R8-25).
- The direction he presumes (Boolean ⇒ faithful) is false as it stands: M = {x, y}, two lieux with |∘| = ∅, has Σ = {∅, M} Boolean and is not faithful.
- It holds once his Question below is added (SA21-R9-10): under divisibility and Mag L2, the magasin is faithful iff Σ is Boolean and the supp°x are distinct atoms.

**The Question (scan 3).** For a lieu x, is supp°x minimal in Σ* = Σ ∖ {0}, so that supp°X ⊂ supp°x forces X = x? In the local case he reduces it to: for lieux x ≠ y, find Z with Z |∘| x and not Z |∘| y. "Je n'y ai pensé que dans le cas x non-|∘| y."
- **Strongest reduction, mine (SA21-R9-09).**
  - In a faithful divisible magasin the answer is yes: Σ ≅ P(𝓛) (156-8/78) and supp°x = {x}.
  - In a local magasin his reduction is the condition {x}^⊥⊥ = {x} in (𝓛, ∥).
  - It holds in the "ficelle à deux brins" ℚ × ℚ of 156-7/84: given w ≠ z comparable, take u comparable with z and not with w.
  - The general case stays open.
- "Requestion: Opération de saturation d'un atelier, en sens contraire, faisable dans le cadre des ateliers spatiaux sans plus ? J'en doute !" (margin 15.7). This is the plan of 156-6/89.

## 2. 5 July: the restart from an ordered set (scans 5-9)

"L'affaire a tendance à s'enliser, et j'ai envie de tout reprendre, d'une façon un peu différente."

**The setting (scan 5).** L is an ordered set, connected, with card L ≥ 2.
- The maquette 𝓜 = 𝓜₀ ⨿ 𝓜₁ has 𝓜₀ = L (virtual vertices, "lieux") and 𝓜₁ = Drap₂(L), the intervals I_{a,b}.
- ∂, δ and the "naive" support |I_{a,b}| = [a,b], |X|° = ]a,b[ or {a}.
- ◁ is incidence. X ≪° Y ⇔ |X| ⊂ |Y| and not X ◁ Y, with the three cases. X ≪ Y ⇔ |X| ⊂ |Y|.
- This is the magasin of an ordered set of 156-8/90-92, renamed.

**The positional order (scan 6).** X < Y iff X ≠ Y and every point of δX is ≤ every point of δY: a < I_{a,b} < b, and I_{a,b} < I_{b,c}.
- Proposition: X′ ≪ X, Y′ ≪ Y and X < Y give X′ < Y′, or X′ = ex X = Y′ = or Y. Hence the Cor: with ≪° in place of ≪, X′ < Y′.
- Déf: X |∘| Y ⇔ X < Y or Y < X, i.e. {X, Y} ∈ Drap₂(𝓜).
- "Le corollaire précédent montre Maq 3, et Maq 4 immédiat, car a < I_{a,b} < b".
- Targeted proof check (SA21-R9-01): holds. In this model the two-strata form of Mag 4 also holds, since the two ends of an interval are comparable. So the comparable form he states (cf. SA21-K8-03) does no harm here.

**Proposition (scan 7): X ≍ Y ⇔ X = Y or X |∘| Y.** Re-derived (SA21-R9-02). X̃ ∩ Ỹ = δX ∩ δY has at most one point; in the case of one common point, the only compatible pairs that are not incidences are I_{a,b}, I_{b,c} with a < b < c.

**Corollary: a finite Φ ⊂ 𝓜 is a préfigure iff it is totally ordered by ≤.**
- So Préfig(𝓜) = Drap*(𝓜, ≤), and Fig(𝓜) = the finite chains of (𝓜, ≤) that are closed for ⊴ (scan 8).
- **The figures are the flags of the positional order.** This confirms H10 in the form found in 156-8/106-107: |∘| is a comparability relation.
- The general case asked for in H10, "the poset of strata of a regular cell complex with its flags", is not what the page does. The flags here are chains of the positional order <, not of the incidence order ⊴. H10 is refined accordingly (H9-1 below).

**Completion by infinite intervals (scans 8-9).** 𝓜̂ adds I_{<a} and I_{>a}, so that the complement Φ′ of a polyfigure has δΦ′ ⊂ δΦ: "alors que dans le contexte des polyfigures 'ordinaires' ... les extrémités de L viennent nous emmerder".

## 4. 9 July: the terminology swap, supports, spatial ateliers (scans 38-60)

## 5. 9-10 July: disjoncteurs and spatios (scans 61-111)

- **Disjoncteurs** (E, |∘|): a set with a symmetric relation. A correspondence α between E and E′ induces α_* on supports (scans 61-75).
  - Disjunctive correspondences, transposition (β = α*), and the "disjoncteurs triviaux" (|∘| = ≠, the set-theoretic case).
  - A counterexample with the tautological disjunction (scan 65).
  - Statement lines only.
- **10 July, Définition 1 (scan 78, read in full): a spatios is (Σ, ≤, C) with**
  - Spat 1: a complete lattice;
  - Spat 2: C an order-reversing involution;
  - Spat 3: x ∧ Cx = 0.
- **So a spatios is exactly a complete ortholattice.** Its canonical disjunction is x |∘| y ⇔ x ≤ Cy. Every Σ_E of a disjoncteur is a spatios (scan 79).
- **Proposition 1 (scan 80): Σ → Σ_Σ, x ↦ supp°{x}, is an isomorphism, with inverse S ↦ Sup S.** Re-derived (SA21-R9-03): cosupp(A) = ↓C(Sup A), so supp(A) = ↓Sup A. It is the standard representation of a complete ortholattice by its own orthogonality relation (see G. Kalmbach, *Orthomodular lattices*, Academic Press 1983, ch. 1-2; locator to be checked). This is reader B's H7-2 ("supports are an orthogonality lattice") stated by him, on 10 July, as the definition.
- **Scans 81-108** (statement lines): Prop 2, the Lemma of scan 83, Cor 1-5 on dense parts, correspondences of spatios (Prop 3, 4; "prétransposés"), and the category of disjoncteurs.
  - His questions: whether Corr(Σ, Σ′) carries a spatios structure; whether α_*, β_* commuting with C makes them inverse isomorphisms ("(oui.)").
  - A margin refers to "CF VIII exemple non général (cont. top.) 72", that is 156-8/81, his drawing showing that supports do not commute with ∩.
- **The orthomodular lemmas (scans 109-111, read in full).**
  - Lemma 1: for b ≤ c, there is b* with b* |∘| b and b ∨ b* = c iff b ∨ (Cb ∧ c) = c, and b₀* = Cb ∧ c is the largest such b*. Re-derived (SA21-R9-04).
  - Lemma 2: b ≤ c generate a Boolean subalgebra (of order 2, 4 or 8) iff both (b, c) and (Cc, Cb) satisfy Lemma 1.
  - Cor 1, equivalent conditions on Σ: (i) Lemma 1 for all b ≤ c; (ii) Lemma 2 for all b ≤ c; (iii) orthogonal pairs generate Boolean subalgebras; (iiii) the same for finite orthogonal families.
  - **Condition (i) is the orthomodular law.** (ii) and (iii) are its known characterizations by Boolean subalgebras generated by comparable or orthogonal pairs (Kalmbach 1983, ch. 1 §3; locator to be checked) (SA21-R9-05).
- **His Questions (scan 111).** Answered (SA21-R9-06, -07):
  - **"Les conditions envisagées au Cor. 1 sur Σ sont elles tjrs vérifiées ? J'en doute fort ! Le sont elles si Σ est fini ? J'en doute également."** His margin: "Non, contrexemple avec Card Σ = 10". Right. The smallest counterexample is the hexagon O₆, with 6 elements.
  - **"Il faudrait absolument avoir le cœur net de ce qui en est, quand Σ est défini via un ens. ordonné L, muni de la relation x |∘| y ssi x < y ou y < x."** Settled: for the 4-element fence a′ < a > b < b′, the comparability graph is the path a′–a–b–b′, and Σ is O₆. With {b} ≤ {a′, b}: {b} ∨ ({a′, b} ∧ C{b}) = {b} ∨ ∅ ≠ {a′, b}. So orthomodularity fails already for an ordered set of 4 elements (the same lattice as reader B's SA21-R7-27).
  - **"Si on suppose ces conditions satisfaites, Σ est-il nécessairement une algèbre de Boole ?"** No. MO₂ (0, a, Ca, b, Cb, 1) is orthomodular and not distributive, and it is Σ of the comparability graph of two incomparable 2-chains (reader B's SA21-R7-21). It satisfies the infinite-family variant he asks next, being finite.

## 6. The axiom of supports in 156-9 (scans 115-129)

**Scan 114** is a Récoltes et Semailles typescript leaf, typed page 1074, with footnotes of 8 May 1986 on Kashiwara. No mathematics.

**Scans 115-116.** Lemma 1 on a "qtopos" (the transcription's word; his "spotos" or "spatios"). A family of disjoint nonzero e_i with Sup 1 lies in a Boolean subalgebra iff J ↦ e_J commutes with C. Cor 1 gives "admissible" families. Not re-derived.

**Axiome supp 1 (scan 117): for every figure F, the family (X°)_{X∈F̃} is admissible in Σ_𝒜.** This is the 156-9 form of 156-8/88.
- "Cet axiome n'est pas redondant": for the atelier of finite spatial figures of a qtopos Σ, it is equivalent to "si e |∘| e′, alors e′ = (Ce) ∧ (e ∨ e′)". That is the orthomodular law in Kalmbach's form (SA21-R9-08), "ou encore, la validité des conditions du lemme 2 (p. 105) sur Σ".
- **So the axiom of supports is the orthomodular law, imposed on the families of open strata of figures.** It fails for Σ = O₆. That confirms his margin "Oui !" of 156-8/89: in disjunctive magasins the axiom can fail.
- By SA21-R8-25 it holds in every faithful magasin.

**Scan 118:** he strengthens the axiom to finite disjoint families; "Axiom Atm. g) γ³" follows on scan 122. Statement lines only.

**His critique (scans 124-128):** "Critique philosophique de cette approche vers les supports, via les 'spatios' ou 'espacons'":
- co-supp° and supp° compose strata that are neither compatible nor commensurable;
- "je ne suis intéressé aux supports ... que dans le cas des supports constructibles";
- "Σ, le mal-aimé";
- and the diagnosis: **"Ce sont mes 'cordes', pseudo-simplexes topologiques et tutti quanti, associés à l'intuition des 'lieux' remplaçant les 'points', qui causent tous les ennuis pour l'instant, et les seuls ennuis."**

This is exactly what the 156-8 reading proves for the faithful case. Where distinct lieux are disjoint (faithful magasins), Σ is a complete Boolean algebra. All the pathologies (non-distributivity, non-orthomodularity) come from cords, where distinct lieux are not disjoint.

## 7. 12 July, CF IX bis: morphisms of ateliers (scans 130-140; statement lines only)

- **Three situations he wants morphisms for** (scan 130): sub-ateliers; maps f: E′ → E of sets or tame spaces; and their composite.
- **Sub-ateliers ("cf CF IX p. 27", scans 31-33):**
  - Ex 1: Omb°(φ) for a préfigure φ is a sub-atelier.
  - Ex 1 bis: a figure is a sub-atelier, and a maquette for the induced structure. Checked (SA21-R9-12): X ≪° Y′ ⊴ Y inside the figure gives X = Y′, by Cor 2 of 156-8/10.
  - Ex 2: {X ∥ S} is a sub-atelier.
  - Ex 2 bis: comp°(S) = {X |∘| S} is not in general, but {X | X |∘| φ and X ≍ φ} is.
- **Scan 132:** f* for f: E′ → E non-surjective lands in A′ ∪ {∅}. It commutes with ⊴, ≪° and |∘|, and on supports with Sup, nonempty Inf and ∩, "mais pas à C si g non bijective".
- **Définition (provisoire) (scan 134):** a homomorphism is f: A → A′ ⨿ {∅} preserving ⊴, ≪° and |∘|, with the conventions X′ ⊴ ∅, ∅ ≪° X′, X′ |∘| ∅, and Ker f.
- **Subdivisions (scans 138-139):** G subdivides F iff no Z ≪ F has Z |∘| G; in the divisible case, no lieu x ≪ F with x |∘| G. This is 156-4/42's criterion, "no lieu of the shadow of F disjoint from F′", in |∘|-form. Statement line; not re-derived.
