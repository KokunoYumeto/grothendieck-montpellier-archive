## 1. The infinite intervals and the complement duality (scans 9-16, 5 July)

**The completion 𝓜̂ (scans 9-11).** The magasin 𝓜 of an ordered set L (points a, intervals I_{a,b}) is completed by symbols I_{<a} (a not minimal) and I_{>a} (a not maximal), with |I_{<a}| = L_{≤a}.
- ≪° is extended by I_{<a} ≪° I_{<b} ⇔ a ≤ b, and by X ≪° I_{<a} for X ∈ 𝓜.
- **On that rule, decided with the image and the computation.** The transcription has "X ≪° I_{<a} ⟺ X ≤ a". At 180 dpi the sign has a struck lower part. The rule that works is X < a in the positional order (X ≠ a and every point of δX ≤ a): the point a itself is incident to I_{<a}, not inside it. With "<" his duality theorem holds on all 21 492 polygons tested (SA21-R9b-29). With "≤" it would put a ≪° I_{<a} (SA21-K9b-02).
- **The two sentences about I_{<a} and I_{ω₀,a} contradict each other as transcribed** ("on aura I_{<a} ≪ I_{ω₀,a}, mais la relation inverse ... est vraie"). In the ink both places have struck words (scan 10, 180 dpi).
  - His own rule a), "On n'a jamais une relation I_{<a} ≪° X pour X ∈ 𝓜", fixes the mathematics: I_{<a} ≪ I_{ω₀,a} never holds, while I_{ω₀,a} ≪° I_{<a} does, "malgré que |I_{ω₀,a}| = |I_{<a}|". The finite interval is "une subdivision effective de I_{<a}".
  - The words are read provisionally as "dans ce sens" and "on n'aura pas" (SA21-K9b-01).
- The positional order on 𝓜̂ (nothing lies before I_{<a}, nothing after I_{>a}) and |∘| = comparability. "Mog 1 OK, Mog 2 OK, Mog 3 OK ... Mog 4 clair".

**Lemma (scan 12): 𝓜 is "très dense" in 𝓜̂.** X |∘| Y as soon as all their refinements in 𝓜 are |∘|. Cor: Σ_𝓜̂ ≅ Σ_𝓜. Re-derived (SA21-R9b-01): for X = I_{<a}, a refinement I_{a′,a} forces Y ≥ a, because Y ≤ a′ would give Y ≪° I_{<a}.

**Polygons (scans 13-15).** These are the finite chains of (𝓜̂, ≤): a flag T of L and some of its generalized interstitial strata [I_{<t₁}], t₁, I_{t₁,t₂}, …, t_n, [I_{>t_n}], each t ∈ T lying in Φ or on the boundary of a member.
- The complementary polygon is Φ* = Φ̂ ∖ Φ.
- His formula δΦ* = δΦ ∖ Φ_red (scan 14) fails at a vertex that is extreme in L and lies in Φ with its only adjacent interval. For L = {0 < 1} and Φ = {0, I_{0,1}}, δΦ* = {1}. He repairs this himself on 6 July (scan 18): "il faut rajouter les s ∈ ∂I tels que ord(s, Φ) = 1" (SA21-K9b-03).
- Φ** is obtained by effacing the redundant vertices, that is, by composing the intervals adjacent to them.

**Théorème (scan 16): cosupp°_𝓜(Φ) = cosupp°(Omb°_𝓜(Φ)) = Omb°_𝓜(Φ*).**
- **Independent re-proof (SA21-R9b-02).**
  - ⊇: every stratum of Φ* is positionally comparable with every member of Φ, and |∘| descends along ≪°.
  - ⊆: a Z ∈ 𝓜 comparable with every member of the chain Φ lies in a gap between consecutive members X_i < X_{i+1}. A short case analysis on the gap shows Z ≪° the stratum of Φ* filling it, or Z = that stratum. A gap between two strata that are adjacent in Φ̂ admits no Z.
- **Computation:** it holds for all 21 492 polygons of the 203 orders.
- **Cor 1** (under Hyp, "pour simplifier"): supp°(Φ) = Omb°(Φ**). It holds in every finite case tested, with Hyp (3 520 cases) and without it (15 532) (SA21-R9b-03).
- **Cor 2** is left unfinished: "Soit T ∈ Drap(L). Considérons l'ens des figures telles que" ends the page.

## 2. 6 July: the oriented segment, vertex types, constructible supports (scans 17-28)

- **The "segment orienté combinatoire" (I, ω₀, ω₁)** (scan 17), an ordered set with a least and a greatest element. When card I = 2 this is the indivisible segment, "une maquette simpliciale ... Δ¹". No infinite intervals are used there.
- **Vertex types under complementation (scans 18-20).** Isolated and lacunary vertices are exchanged (Φ*_is = Φ_lac, Φ*_lac = Φ_is). ∂*Φ is invariant. The redundant ("évanescents") vertices vanish, and the "semi-évanescents" on ∂I reappear by biduality.
- **Constructible parts (scans 20-21).** Omb°(Φ), with Φ ↦ Omb°(Φ) bijective; Φ is recovered from the ≪°-maximal elements. The cosupport of a constructible part is constructible: cosupport ↔ complementary polygon, support ↔ irredundant envelope.
  - Computation (SA21-R9b-05): the map is injective on all finite polygons tested.
  - It is not injective once the infinite intervals are admitted over a non-divisible L. For L = {0 < 1}, Omb°_𝓜(I_{<1}) = Omb°_𝓜{0, I_{0,1}} (5 163 collisions). So the bijection holds in his segment setting, where no I_{<a} is used, and for M̂ it needs divisibility (SA21-K9b-07).
- **At supp 1 for the oriented segment (scans 21-22).** For a flag T ⊃ ∂I and Φ ⊂ Φ_T, he shows (CΦ)** = Φ*, hence Cosupp°Φ = supp°(CΦ). So Φ ↦ S_Φ = supp°Φ, P(Φ_T) → Σ_cons, preserves sums and complements, hence intersections: "C'est l'axiome des supports At supp 1."
  - **Re-derived (SA21-R9b-06):** a vertex of T not in δΦ is redundant in CΦ, and effacing it merges exactly the intervals of δΦ.
  - **Computation (SA21-R9b-28):** Axiome supp 1 (156-8/88 form) holds for all 4 724 figures of the magasins of the 203 connected orders with at most 5 elements.
  - **This answers P8-1's first item for the magasin of an ordered set: the axiom of supports holds there, by his own proof.** The failures are in the lieux-only disjoncteurs, such as the fence's O₆, not in the magasin with its segments.
- **Commensurable polygons** (δΦ ∪ δΨ a flag) reduce every operation to ∪, ∩, C in a P(Φ_T); there are 2^{2n+1} supports subordinate to a flag of length n (scan 23). Injectivity is consistent with the computation.
- **Residual magasins (scans 24-28).** M(J, ω₁), M(J, ω₀), M(J), with J_{<a}, J_{>a} and the object [J] standing for I_{ω₀,ω₁}. "l'intervalle ouvert indivisible est représenté par l'ens ordonné vide !"
  - The general form is (I, Ω₀, Ω₁).
  - The sub-magasin on a connected constructible part Φ is M(J, Ω₀, Ω₁), with J the lexicographic sum of the |X|°.
  - Divisibility of M; the compact strata are "très denses".

## 4. 9 July: spatios and spatial ateliers as figures in a spatios (scans 50-60)

- **The first definition of spatios** (scans 50-52): Spat 1-3. The supports of a symmetric relation satisfy Spat 1-2, and Spat 3 iff self-disjoint elements are disjoint from all.
- **Atspat(Σ) (scans 53-56).** The elementary spatial figures (Φ, ρ) of a spatios: ρ an order with a greatest element, distinct members disjoint. ⊴ is induced, ≪° is defined through the increasing ψ preserving the tops, and |∘| through the tops.
  - "At 1 Clair ... At 4 Clair." Checked (SA21-R9b-11): At 4 is the disjointness of distinct members; Mag 2′ a) holds because a nonzero element lies under at most one of a family of disjoint elements.
  - Σ_{Atspat(Σ)} ≅ Σ, and A → Atspat(Σ_A), X ↦ Dép X, is a faithful homomorphism. A is spatial iff it is injective.
- **Scholie (9 July, scans 57-60): a spatial atelier is a spatios Σ with A ⊂ Figspatél(Σ) satisfying Atspat 1′ (closed for ⊴) and Atspat 2′ (the tops X° are join-dense in Σ).** "On a gagné!"
  - I re-derived the core (SA21-R9b-12). For a join-dense D ⊂ Σ with x |∘| y ⇔ x ≤ Cy, the support lattice of (D, |∘|) is Σ, via supp(A) = D_{≤Sup A}, which is the Dedekind-MacNeille-type representation (Michel Hua's project names it).

  - "Atens 1 (cf CF VIII p. 309)" is read as CF VIII p. 9 = 156-8/18, by content (SA21-K9b-08).

## 5. 9-11 July: disjoncteurs, correspondences, transposition (scans 61-77, 81-108)

**Disjoncteurs (scans 61-62, 76-77).** A set with a symmetric relation |∘|, with E₀ = the self-disjoint elements. Antireflexive disjoncteurs are the same as strict graphs, the edges being the non-disjoint pairs. A correspondence α: E → P(E′) is "compatible" if it carries disjoint pairs to disjoint sets. Then α_*(S) = supp°(ᾱ(S)).

**Commutation with C fails in general (scans 63-66).** His counterexample with the tautological disjunction (α_* commutes with C only if α is bijective) is checked. The "draconiennes" conditions follow (scans 66-68).

**The transpose (scans 69-75).**
- He seeks β with x |∘| β(x′) ⇔ α(x) |∘| x′; it exists iff γ(x′) = {x | α(x) |∘| x′} is closed (scan 70). In the trivial case it is the transposed relation.
- **Lemme 1 (scan 72) is false as stated** (SA21-R9b-14, SA21-K9b-04). It claims a bijection between all maps E → Σ_E′ and sup-preserving maps Σ_E → Σ_E′.
  - Countermodel: the twin disjoncteur x–w, y–w, where σ(x) = σ(y) = {x, y}. No sup-preserving map gives α(x) ≠ α(y).
  - His margin doubts it at once ("il n'est pas clair qu'on ait la formule ..., mais seulement ⊃"). He repairs it himself in Prop 4 (scan 97), adding condition b), "que j'ai dû rajouter après-coup".
- **Lemme 2 and the Définition (scans 73-74).** The correspondances disjonctives and their transposes, with ᵗ(ᵗα_*) = α_*.
  - His margin lists two "Autres conditions ? équivalentes": (iv) preimages of closed sets are closed, and (v) the continuity formula α_*(supp°A) = supp°(ᾱA).
  - Scan 75: "J'ignore si la validité de cette formule ... indique que c'est une corr. disjonctive".
  - **Settled: yes.** (v) ⇔ (iv) ⇔ (iii) ⇔ (i), re-derived (SA21-R9b-15, -16). For instance (iii) ⇒ (v): if z ∈ supp°A and x′ ⊥ supp°(ᾱA), then A ⊂ γ(x′), which is closed, so z ∈ γ(x′).

**Prop 1-2 and the corollaries (scans 80-88).**
- Prop 2, the universal property of σ_E: E → Σ_E (a) disjunction reflected, b) density), is re-derived (SA21-R9b-12).
- **Cor 1 (scan 84).** For E′ ⊂ E: (i) density ⇔ (ii) S ↦ S ∩ E′ injective ⇔ (iii) ⇔ (iv) ⇔ (v).
  - His proof of (ii) ⇒ (i) goes through Cor 2, which needs S ∩ E′ ∈ Σ_E′; that is not shown.
  - A direct proof (SA21-R9b-13; SA21-K9b-05): S₀ = supp°(S ∩ E′) ⊂ S has S₀ ∩ E′ = S ∩ E′, so injectivity gives S₀ = S.
  - Computation: no counterexample among all graphs on at most 5 vertices and all their subsets (32 767 cases).
- Cor 5 (dense subsets of a preordered disjoncteur) is the general form of "très dense" (scan 12) and of 156-8/77-78 (Σ_M ≅ Σ_𝓛 under Mag L2).

**Prop 3 (scans 88-93).** "Prétransposés" α, β, with x |∘| β(x′) ⇔ α(x) |∘| x′, are automatically continuous (Cor 1, scan 92), re-derived (SA21-R9b-17).

**Prop 4 (scans 95-97).** A correspondence admits a pretranspose iff α_* is sup-preserving and α_*(σx) = supp°α(x), iff the continuity formula holds, iff preimages of closed sets are closed, iff the γ(x′) are closed.
- Computation (SA21-R9b-15): 3 000 random pairs, with no mismatch among (iii), (iv) and continuity.
- In 989 of them α_* preserves joins while b) fails. So his "rajout après-coup" is necessary.

**Scans 98-100.** Bijections Corr(Σ, Σ′) ≅ Corr(E, E′) ≅ Corr^×(E, E′): relations whose rows and columns are closed sets. Checked (SA21-R9b-18).
- These are the bonds of formal concept analysis between the orthogonality contexts. Michel Hua's project names them (it cites arXiv:2012.08268; locator to be checked).
- On 11 July (margin) he forms the category of disjoncteurs with composition supp°∘(β ∘ α), "équivalente à la catégorie des ... spatios", by "[Analogie avec les espaces de Hilbert ... et les opérateurs adjoints]".

## 8. The open questions of the packages, settled here

**The Question of 156-9/3 (supp°x minimal; P9-1).**
- **Proved for the magasins of connected ordered sets** (SA21-R9b-25). For lieux x ≠ y: if they are comparable, Z = y separates; if not, Z is the segment from x to an element comparable with x. For a segment X, one of S_{c,d}, S_{c,x}, S_{x,d} or S_{x,m} separates. So supp°x is an atom, and supp° is injective there.
  - Computation: 988 lieux of the 203 orders, no failure.
- **Refuted for local ateliers in general** (SA21-R9b-26):
  - the four lieux a′–a–b–b′ with ∥ the path (the comparability of the fence) give supp°b ⊊ supp°a′;
  - twin lieux give supp°x = supp°y.
- His reduction (scan 3), separation of lieux, is exactly the criterion in the local case. It fails in precisely the case "x non-|∘| y" that he says he considered.

**Hunch H9-1 (orthomodular ⇔ cograph): the "⇐" is proved, the "⇒" is refuted** (SA21-R9b-27).
- **Proof of "⇐".** For a disjoint union of graphs, the polarity lattice is the horizontal sum of the two lattices; for a join, it is their product. Both operations preserve orthomodularity, and K₁ gives 2. So the lattice of a cograph, and of a series-parallel ordered set, is orthomodular.
- **Computation:** over all labelled graphs on at most 6 vertices, none is P₄-free without being orthomodular.
- **The converse fails:**
  - 60 graphs on 5 vertices, and 2 640 on 6, have an induced P₄ and an orthomodular lattice.
  - All 60 on 5 vertices are comparability graphs. An example is the order 1 < 2 < 0, 4 < 0, 1 < 3, which contains the fence 4 < 0 > 1 < 3: its Σ is the Boolean algebra 2³, with atoms {0}, {1}, {2}.
  - So for the question of 156-9/111, "quand Σ est défini via un ens. ordonné", orthomodularity is not decided by N-freeness. The criterion is the "Dacey graph" condition, characterized in the literature (D. P. Sumner, "Dacey graphs", J. Austral. Math. Soc. 18 (1974) 492-502; locator to be checked).

**Axiome supp 1 in the magasin of an ordered set (P8-1).** It holds: by his proof for figures subordinate to a flag (scans 21-22), and by computation for every figure of every connected order with at most 5 elements (SA21-R9b-06, -28).
