## 2. Oriented magasins and oriented maquettes (scans 4 and 6)

**The data (scan 4).** M, ◁ (immediate incidence), ≪°, |∘|, an involution X ↦ X⁻ without fixed point ("multistrate orienté opposé"), and a part M₀⁺ ⊂ M. The axioms:
- Magor 1: ◁ antireflexive, generating a strict order < with finite chains ("Ainsi ◁ revient à donner un ordre strict vérifiant la cond. des chaînes descendantes"); ≪° an order; |∘| antireflexive; the involution.
- Magor 1′: ◁, ≪° and |∘| are compatible with the involution. "NB On peut avoir à la fois X ◁ Y et X ◁ Y⁻."
- Magor 2-4: as Mag 2-4, with Magor 4 in the form "X < Y ⇒ X |∘| Y".
- Magor 5: the elements of M₀⁺ have dimension 0, and M₀ = M₀⁺ ⨿ a(M₀⁺).
- Magor 6 a), read on the image at 300 dpi: "Soient Z < X, codim(Z, X) = 2. Alors il n'existe qu'un seul Y♯ avec Z ◁ Y♯ ◁ X, et de plus il existe Y♭ avec Z⁻ ◁ Y♭ ◁ X". The superscript on the last X is struck. Codex's "codim(Y, X)" is a misreading: the first argument is the barred Z of "Soient Z < X" (SA21-K8-09).
- Magor 6 b): every X of dimension 1 has exactly one x ∈ M₀⁺ with x ◁ X.

Magor 6 a) is the oriented "diamond" condition, the combinatorial form of ∂∂ = 0. Michel Hua's project makes the same link in its note to this page and leaves the signs unreconstructed.

**Oriented maquettes (scan 6):** ≪° = equality and |∘| = ≠.
- **Dimension ≤ 1: "Les maquettes orienteuses de dim ≤ 1 sont les 'graphes'".** Put S = M₀⁺ (vertices), A = M₁ (oriented edges), the involution on A, and σ: A → S from Magor 6 b). Magor 6 b) applied to X⁻, with Magor 1′, gives the second end, σ(X⁻). These are exactly Serre's graphs: vertices, oriented edges with a fixed-point-free reversal, and an origin map (J.-P. Serre, *Arbres, amalgames, SL₂*, Astérisque 46, 1977, ch. I §2; locator to be checked). Michel Hua's project gives this identification first, with that locator. I re-derived it from Magor 1′, 5 and 6 b) (SA21-R8-32). Loops and multiple edges are allowed.
- His NB, read on the image: "Cette structure ne peut être décrite par une maquette, sauf quand il n'y a pas d'auto-boucles". Then a struck phrase, probably "boucles jumelles", not decided. This is right: the unoriented face poset of a loop is v < e, which cannot tell a loop from an edge with one end. Without loops a graph is a regular CW complex and its face poset determines it.
- **Dimension 2, his own "Sauf erreur", read on the image: "Cas de dim 2. Sauf erreur, on trouve la description des complexes cellulaires combinatoires de dim ≤ 2."**

**What dimension 2 actually gives** (SA21-R8-33, refined statement):
- Apply Magor 6 a) to Z and to Z⁻. For each 2-cell X and each vertex v below X, exactly one edge of ∂X ends at v and exactly one starts at v.
- So an oriented maquette of dimension ≤ 2 is a Serre graph together with 2-cells X (with X⁻ as the reversed cell, by Magor 1′), where each ∂X is a set of oriented edges forming a vertex-disjoint union of directed cycles. Loops (monogons) and the back-and-forth cycle e e⁻¹ are allowed.

Against "the combinatorial cell complexes of dimension ≤ 2" (2-complexes whose 2-cells are attached along closed edge paths), the axioms differ in two ways:
- **Too strict.** The one-vertex torus (word a b a⁻¹ b⁻¹) violates Magor 6 a): four edges lie between the vertex and the cell. So does every cell whose boundary path passes a vertex twice.
- **Too loose.** Nothing forces ∂X to be connected. A "cell" bounded by two disjoint cycles satisfies Magor 1-6.

With an added axiom "∂X connected", the oriented maquettes of dimension ≤ 2 are the 2-complexes whose 2-cells are attached along closed edge paths through no vertex twice; the boundary word is recovered by following the unique out-edge at each vertex. The regular CW complexes of dimension 2 are those with, in addition, no loops and no cell using an edge in both directions. There Magor 6 a) is the diamond property of Björner's characterization (A. Björner, "Posets, regular CW complexes and Bruhat order", European J. Combin. 5 (1984) 7-16; locator to be checked). This settles H9's prediction for dimension 2: the "Sauf erreur" claim holds only for a corrected class (SA21-K8-12).

## 3. The récapitulation in magasins (scans 8-14, 26-27 June)

**Mag 1-4 (scan 8).** A set M with ≤, ≪ and |∘|, "cf CF VII, p. 110" (156-7/111):
- Mag 1: ≤ and ≪ are orders, and ≤ ⇒ ≪.
- Mag 2: for X ≪ Y, Ỹ^X = {Z ∈ Ỹ | X ≪ Z} has a least element; X ≪° Y when it is Y.
- Mag 3: |∘| is symmetric and antireflexive, and stable under ≪°.
- Mag 4: Y, Z ≤ X with Y ≠ Z ⇒ Y |∘| Z. His NB: "Cela établit élégamment l'axiome AtL3".

X ≍ Y iff x′ |∘| y′ for all strata outside X̃ ∩ Ỹ, and X ∥ Y iff all strata are |∘|. So X ∥ Y ⇔ X ≍ Y and X̃ ∩ Ỹ = ∅ (checked: a common stratum z would give z |∘| z).

**Figures (scan 9).** 𝓕_M is the set of ≤-closed sets of pairwise compatible elements, the "Nablien universel" (reading Nablien/Nablium open). The polyhedral restriction: two strata that meet have an Inf. F ≪ G strata-wise. F ≍ G ⟺ {F, G} bounded ⟺ F ∪ G ∈ 𝓕.

**What is proved from Mag 1-4 alone (scans 10-14).** Each step was re-derived and holds:
- Prop 1: ≍ is reflexive, symmetric and closed downward. Prop 2: on compatible elements, |∘| is ≠ (SA21-R8-01, -02).
- Cor 1-2: for X ≍ Y and Z ≪° X, Z ≪ Y ⇔ X ≤ Y, and two compatible elements with a common internal refinement are equal (R8-03). Cor 3: compatible + ≪ ⇒ ≤, which is 156-6's At 5.
- **Prop 3 (scan 11): X ≪° Y ≪° Z ⇒ X ≪° Z.** If X ≪° Z′ < Z, Mag 4 gives Z′ |∘| Z, and Mag 3 b) applied twice gives X |∘| X (R8-04). **So 156-6's axiom At 7 is now a theorem.** His NB: X ≪ Y ≪ Z with X ≪° Z gives Y ≪° Z, but not X ≪° Y.
- Prop 2.F: in a figure, ≪ and ≤ coincide (R8-05).
- **Prop 3 (scan 12): G ≪ F, F′ ≤ F ⇒ G_{F′} := G̃ ∩ Omb(F′) is Inf^≪(F′, G),** and F′ ↦ G_{F′} commutes with all Sup and Inf (R8-06). The proof I give uses Mag 2, Prop 3 and Cor 2: the least stratum W₀ of F through which an X ≪ G refines is unique. This is 156-4's axiom Cond 8, and 156-6's theorem.
- **Prop 5 (scan 13): for F ≍ G, L = F ∩ G, F′ ≪ F, G′ ≪ G: F′ ≍ G′ ⇔ F′_L ≍ G′_L, and F′ ∥ G′ ⇔ F′_L ∥ G′_L** (R8-07).
  - Re-derived: for x ∈ F̃′ and y ∈ G̃′ with least strata A ∈ F̃ and B ∈ G̃, A ≠ B gives A |∘| B, hence x |∘| y. A = B forces A ∈ L̃.
  - **So 156-7's "axiomes obligatoires" At CD (compatibility and disjunction of refinements tested on the intersection) are theorems of Mag 1-4.** This is what the "innovation d'avant-hier" of scan 31 did to "tout le fouillis des axiomes de compatibilité-disjonction". Its corollary is At D₀.
- Prop 4 (scans 13-14): gluing of refinements, Raff(F) → ∏ Raff(F_i) ⇉ ∏ Raff(F_i ∩ F_j) exact, and Raff F = lim_{X∈F̃} Raff(X) (R8-08). The gluing uses Prop 5 a): compatible restrictions on F_i ∩ F_j give pairwise compatible pieces. **156-4's C7 and 156-6's At 11 are theorems here too**, for finite index families.

His list of what remains (scan 14): lieux and ombres; the set-theoretic cases; above all the supports; and expressing ≪ through ≤ and |∘|, which needs "X° ⊂ Y°", so supports first.

## 4. Lieux, strictly divisible and set-theoretic magasins (scans 15-18)

- Lieux are the ≪-minimal elements. On lieux ∥ = |∘|, x̃ = {x}, and x ∈ omb(X)° ⇔ x ≪° X (checked).
- Mog 𝓛1 (divisibility): omb(X)° ≠ ∅. Mog 𝓛2: if all lieux of omb(X)° and omb(Y)° are disjoint, then X |∘| Y. Mog 𝓛2 ⇒ Mog 𝓛1 by antireflexivity (R8-34). He spells "Mog" throughout; it is his own name, "magma", used beside "magasin" on these pages.

**The Proposition-Scholie of scan 16, and its quasi-set-theoretic form on scan 17, is false as stated** (SA21-R8-09, computation; SA21-K8-01).
- **The claim.** A strictly divisible magasin is the same as (≤, ≪) with Mag 1, Mag 2 and Mog 𝓛1, plus a relation ∥ on the lieux satisfying only div 3 (symmetric, antireflexive) and div 4 (lieux of two distinct strata of one X are ∥). The definition X |∘| Y :⇔ omb(X)° ∥ omb(Y)° is then said to satisfy Mag 3 and Mag 4 "immédiatement".
- **The countermodel.** M = {x, y, Z, X′, X} with Z ≤ X, and ≪ generated by x ≪ Z, x ≪ X′, y ≪ X′, X′ ≪ X. Its lieux are x, y, with x ∥ y. Mag 2, Mog 𝓛1, div 3 and div 4 hold, yet x ≪° X′ ≪° X while x ≪° Z < X.
- **What fails.** The defined |∘| violates Mag 3 b): Z |∘| X but not Z |∘| X′ (`156-8_checks.py`, item 1).
- **The repair:** add "x ≪° X′ ≪° X ⇒ x ≪° X" for lieux x. That is 156-6's At 7, which 156-7's quasi-set-theoretic pre-ateliers carried through the induced-refinement axiom. With it the correspondence holds, and I re-derived both directions.
- In the ≪°-primitive form of scans 30-33 the problem disappears, because ≪° is an order by axiom.

**The rest of the section:**
- Scan 17, "pour mémoire" a): under Mog 𝓛1, Ssfig(F) ≅ Ssfig(mulomb F) (R8-10, through Cor 1 of scan 10).
- b) and c), which reflect ≪ and ≍ through the enveloppe, may fail even in the quasi-set-theoretic case. He calls a magasin "ensembliste" when they hold. "Voir exemple dans CF VIII p. 57" is scan 66.
- **Scan 18, Proposition-Scholie.** A set-theoretic magasin is a set L with M ⊂ Figél(L), stable under sub-multistrates (Magens 1) and containing the {{x}} (Magens 2): "à peine plus compliqué que la définition d'un espace topologique !", "Ça c'est donc pas mal dégonflé !!!".
- His judgment: "le point de vue 'atlas' des figures nous avait plutôt compliqué la vie. Ici il vaut décidément mieux partir du magasin".

## 7. Trivial magasins = maquettes; indexed figures; morphisms (scans 34-41)

**Magasins triviaux (scan 34):** ≪° = equality and |∘| = ≠, so the datum is an ordered set (M, ≤). Then ≪ = ≤, ≍ always holds, X ∥ Y ⇔ X̃ ∩ Ỹ = ∅, lieux = minimal elements, and omb(X)° is {X} or ∅.
- His margin's equivalences (i)-(v) were checked (SA21-R8-16): trivial; ≍ always; figures = initial sections; M a figure; X ≠ Y ⇒ X |∘| Y.
- So were (vi) ⇔ (vii), which are strictly weaker.
- Mag L1 forces discreteness.
- **"On peut appeler simplement un tel magasin une maquette (!)".** So a maquette is an ordered set, that is, a T₀ Alexandrov ("ultra") topological space, as the margin of scan 3 says. It need not be finite (H9 sharpened: the maquettes are the T₀ Alexandrov spaces, finite or not).

**Indexed figures (scans 34-36).** φ: I → M defines a figure indexed by I iff (a) φ is increasing and injective; (b) each I_{≤i} → X̃_i is an isomorphism; (c) i ≠ j ⇒ X_i |∘| X_j. Re-derived (SA21-R8-17).
- A by-product: **𝓕_M is exactly the set of ≤-closed sets whose distinct elements are pairwise |∘|.** By Prop 2, ≍ on a figure is "= or |∘|".
- The polyhedral variant is free: it reads on the maquette F̃.

**Morphisms of magasins (scans 36-38):** a) ≤, b) ≪° and c) |∘| preserved, plus d) X̃ → φ(X)~ bijective.
- d) makes each X̃ → φ(X)~ an isomorphism of ordered sets; I checked the inverse is increasing. Cor 1-5 hold (R8-17).
- His margin of 12 July: "Cette condition d) devrait rester facultative. Il y a des 'sous-mag.' qui ne sont pas fermés par ≤ !"
- His doubt on c): "J'ai pas l'impression d'avoir bien saisi le rôle exact de l'hypothèse c), qui semble très forte".
- Examples: M → Figél(M), X ↦ Mulomb(X); M → Figél(𝓛) under Mag L1; f*: Figél(E) → Figél(E′) for f surjective. "la notion de comorphisme proposée à la page 27 [scan 36], n'est pas trop idiote".

**Faithful magasins (scans 39-40).** X |∘| Y ⇒ Omb°X ∩ Omb°Y = ∅ always, and the converse is the faithfulness condition.
- **Proposition (scan 40): the magasins (M, ≤, ≪°) with |∘| defined by "no common ≪°-lower bound" are exactly those satisfying Mag 1′ and Mag 2′.** "C'est pourtant aussi simple que le cas ensembliste (p. 9) !"
- Re-derived (SA21-R8-18): Mag 3′ by transitivity, and Mag 4 (two strata) from Mag 2′ a).
- The hierarchy: general ⇐ faithful ⇐ quasi-set-theoretic ⇐ set-theoretic.
- I checked that quasi-set-theoretic magasins are faithful (divisibility puts a lieu under any common ≪°-lower bound), and so are the maquettes.

## 8. Support functions into a set P (scans 42-54)

**Conditions (scan 42):** (i) X ≪ Y ⇒ |X| ⊂ |Y|; (ii) |X|° ≠ ∅; (iii) X |∘| Y ⇔ |X|° ∩ |Y|° = ∅; (iv) normalization. "Comparer CF VII, p. 73" (156-7/74, reader B's realization by supports).

- As transcribed, his last step "que si le deuxième ⋃ ≠ ∅" needs "= ∅".

**The indexed Proposition (scans 46-52)** is 156-5's Proposition 1 in indexed form: "la prop. 1 p. 1 de CF V" (156-5/2). This is the "variante indexée formellement plus forte, voir GF VIII, pg 37" of 156-5's margin; pg 37 is scan 46, not 38 as the DEPENDS candidate said (SA21-K8-10).
- For X_i increasing in i, with X_i° = X_i ∖ ⋃_{j<i} X_j, the following are equivalent:

**My check (SA21-R8-25): his claim holds, but not for his reason.** He says it holds "automatiquement ... pour les magmiens fidèles (car Ouv y est une fonction support ext. pleinement fidèle)".

## 11. 1-4 July: the magasin of an ordered set (scans 90-127)

**The construction (scans 90-92, 1 July).** M = {P_x} ⨿ {S_{a,b} : a < b}, with P_x = {{x}} and S_{a,b} = {[a,b], {a}, {b}}.
