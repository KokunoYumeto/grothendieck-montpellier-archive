## RP-SA07-1. The Hodge splitting of elliptic curves over Q̄ (from H1)

- **Question.** For E over Q̄ without CM, is the Hodge-splitting parameter h (18:13 c) transcendental? With CM, is it
  algebraic, and what is it?
- **Why it matters.** It is his own explicit question, and the first case of the part of 18:11 that goes beyond the
  period conjecture (the splitting beta). It is a single-variable problem in a well-tooled area (Eisenstein series).
- **Rests on.** 18:11-13 (SA07-2); 18:36-38 (conjugate periods); SA16-12, SA16-22.
- **Strongest known reduction.** CM: the splitting is algebraic (eigenspaces). Non-CM: h is a normalized value of
  E2*(tau) at a tau with j(tau) algebraic; Nesterenko (1996) bounds trdeg Q(q, E2, E4, E6) from below by 3.
- **Material.** The transcription and scans of 18:11-13; Nesterenko, "Modular functions and transcendence questions"
  (Mat. Sb. 1996); Masser, Elliptic functions and transcendence (LNM 437, 1975); Chudnovsky (1976 and the 1984 book);
  Bertrand's surveys on periods and Ramanujan functions (locator unverified).

## RP-SA07-2. The period conjecture with the Hodge splitting (18:11; H2)

- **Question.** State 18:11 precisely (alpha, beta, gamma generic over k in P, Q, P''), prove the reduction of the
  beta-statement to the joint transcendence of the periods and their complex conjugates, and settle the cases where the
  period conjecture is known: CM elliptic curves (Chudnovsky; for CM abelian varieties in general it is open, and
  Wüstholz's analytic subgroup theorem, Ann. of Math. 129 (1989), gives only the linear relations), 1-motives (linear
  relations: Huber-Wüstholz).
- **Why it matters.** It is the strongest form of his "conjecture de transcendance N° 1" on these pages, and it
  connects the motivic Galois group (SA05-8) with Hodge theory on both sides of the comparison.
- **Rests on.** 18:11 (the torsors P, P', P'', the varieties Fil and Big and his trdeg list), 18:36-38, 18:13.
- **Strongest known reduction.** For alpha alone: André's formulation (Une introduction aux motifs, 2004, ch. 7 and 23);
  Huber and Müller-Stach, Periods and Nori motives (2017); for 1-motives Huber-Wüstholz (Transcendence and linear
  relations of 1-periods, 2022).
- **Material.** The cote 18 transcription with scans 18:4-13 and 18:31-41; the works above.

## RP-SA07-3. Betti lattices under different embeddings (18:42-49; H5, H6)

- **Question.** For a motive over a number field and complex embeddings v_1, ..., v_n, describe the comparison of the
  Betti lattices M_v^Z inside prod_l M(l): prove the bound trdeg <= (n - 1) dim G, the parabolic bound of 18:45, and,
  using Serre's open image theorem and the lemma of 18:49, genericity for non-CM elliptic curves and their products.
- **Why it matters.** It is the programme of 18:43 (problems 1 b and 2) and a provable case of his transcendence
  philosophy with the tools of 1972 (Serre).
- **Rests on.** 18:42-49 (SA07-2), 18:27-29 (lattices and orientations).
- **Strongest known reduction.** The lemma of 18:49 (checked); Serre's open image (Invent. Math. 15, 1972); Deligne's
  absolute Hodge cycles (LNM 900) for the comparison of embeddings.
- **Material.** Cote 18 transcription and scans 18:27-29, 18:41-49; Serre 1972; Deligne LNM 900.
