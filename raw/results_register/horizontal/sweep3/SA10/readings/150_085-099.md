## §16. Σ_J and Σ_{J,L} (085-088)

**Setting (from §14, 073-076).** Closed tubes T_i around the open strata, open tubes T̊_i, boundaries Ṫ_i; the deployed
strata Σ_i = X_i ∖ X_i ∩ T̊_{<i} (42) and the pieces Σ_ij = Ṫ_i ∩ Σ_j (49), one Σ_d for each flag d.

**085, the band Σ_J.**
- The head line "Σ_J ∼ Σ_{J,L}." is read as a formula in the text; Michel Hua's project reads it as the heading "16. Σ_J et
  Σ_{J,L}", which fits the "17." of 089. Open.
- (70): for a band J, Σ_J = X_J^* ∖ X_J^* ∩ ⋃_{i∈J̄∖J} T̊_i ⊂ X_J^*. Only the tubes of the strata below J and outside it
  are cut out. (71) follows: Σ_∅ = ∅, Σ_{{j}} = Σ_j, Σ_I = X, and Σ_J = X_J for an ideal (R150g-01).
- The NB line beneath (a struck vertical "=" and struck ⋃Σ_j, then "NB") writes X_J ∖ X_J ∩ ⋃T_i with the stars struck.
  Dropping the stars is right, because J̄∖J is closed and its strata lie in the removed tubes (R150g-02). So Σ_J is closed
  in X, given "T̊ open" (H150a-3).
- (72), "sauf erreur": Σ_J is the colimit of the Σ_j along the spans Σ_i ← Σ_ij → Σ_j (i, j ∈ J), and that of the Σ_d
  over Drap(J). This is the reconstruction (19) of 033-035 for the stratified space X_J^* of type J, with the strata of
  J̄∖J left cut out. It holds under the conical structure (R150g-03).
- (73): Σ_J ↪ X_J^* is a homotopy equivalence, by the collars of the removed tubes (R150g-04).
- **(74) is right in its first line and wrongly indexed in the next two** (R150g-05, SA10-K150g-01). Σ̇_J = X_J ∩ (bord de
  T_{J̄∖J}) is the boundary of Σ_J; it lies over the strata of J̄∖J. The page then writes ⋃_{i<j, i,j∈J} Im(Σ_ij → Σ_J)
  and the colimit over the flags "ayant au moins 2 termes". For i < j in J those pieces are the seams of (72), inside Σ_J.
  Example: origin < x-axis < plane, J = {1,2}: Σ_J = {|z| ≥ 1}, its boundary is the unit circle, while Σ_12 runs along the
  axis tube inside Σ_J. The right index is i ∈ J̄∖J, j ∈ J: the flags from J̄∖J to J. The 086 margin indexes Ṫ_J the same
  way ("él. initial de d ∈ J").
- The diagonal margin proposes another viewpoint, "plus commode": the **"éclatés orientés"** (oriented blow-ups), with
  Σ_i ⊃ X_i^* instead of Σ_i ⊂ X_i^*, and X_J^* = Σ_J ∖ Σ̇_J, suited to X = lim Σ_d over Drap(I). This is his own name for
  the deployment as a real oriented blow-up, the reading H150b-1 gave it (DEPENDS). The exponent there is L (Σ_J^L).

**086, two disjoint bands J ≺ L in a band I'.**
- J is an ideal and L a coideal of I', with I' ⊂ J̃ ∩ L̄, "en fait on aura alors I' = J̃ ∩ L̄"; then J' = I'∖L is an ideal
  and J ⊂ J' ⊂ I' (R150g-06). This is the band Lemma of 082 (R150f-26).
- The two "cart" squares, on the indices (J̄ ⊃ J_0 = J̄∖J, and so on) and on the X's, are cartesian (R150g-07).
- The inclusions Σ_J ⊂ Σ_{J'} ⊂ Σ_{I'} hold, each Σ being the X minus the open tubes of I'_0 = Ī'∖I' (R150g-08). A small
  mark beside the third "=", which Hua reads as his "?", is kept as \unsure{?}.
- **The diagonal margin defines T_J ≝ Σ_J ∩ ⋃_{j∈J} T_j, T̊_J and Ṫ_J, then Σ_{J,L} = Ṫ_J ∩ Σ_L, and gives two flag
  colimits with question marks.** Read literally, Σ_{J,L} would be empty, since Σ_J and Σ_L are disjoint. The ambient
  Σ_{I'} is meant: then T_J is (75) and Σ_{J,L} is (77) (R150g-09, SA10-K150g-02). The two "≟" (Ṫ_J as the colimit over
  the flags starting in J; Σ_{J,L} over the flags from J to L) are his self-flags; (76)-(78) take them up.

**087, the tube of Σ_J in Σ_{I'} and its boundary.**
- (75): T_{Σ_J,Σ_{I'}} = Σ_{I'} ∩ ⋃_{j∈J} T_j, with plain T (the 17 Nov correction of the first pass's T̄). The
  parenthetical "(= Σ_{I'} ∩ ⋃_{j∈J̄} T_j)" carries his margin "mais en général c'est différent…", and **he is right**: Σ_{I'}
  removes only the open tubes of J_0 ⊂ I'_0, so the closed T_i (i ∈ J_0) still meet Σ_{I'} along Ṫ_i. With origin < x-axis
  < plane, J = {1}, the right side adds the unit circle (R150g-10).
- (76): the boundary Ṫ_{Σ_J,Σ_{I'}} is the colimit of the Σ_d over the flags of I' from J to I'∖J.
- (77): Σ^♭_{J,L} = Ṫ ∖ Ṫ ∩ T̊_{J'} = Ṫ ∩ Σ_L. The open index J̄'|J' is immaterial: inside Σ_{I'} the tubes of J'_0 are
  already gone (R150g-11).
- (78), "sauf erreur": Σ^♭_{J,L} ≃ the colimit over the flags of J ∪ L from J to L. Checked in the cases {j},{l} and
  {j},{l<l'} under control, and in general by the corner structure of the deployment. It needs L = I'∖J, as his
  "rappelons-le" says; with elements of I' in neither J nor L, the flags through them must be added (R150g-12,
  SA10-K150g-05).
- (79): the span Σ_J ← Σ^♭_{J,L} ↪ Σ_L, the band form of Σ_i ← Σ_ij → Σ_j.

**088.**
- (80)-(81): a flag of J ∪ L is (j_1…j_p l_1…l_q) uniquely; deleting the L-part is a fibration and deleting the J-part a
  boundary embedding, as in 039 (1)-(2) (R150g-13).
- **Remarque:**
  - Σ_{J,L} and Σ_{J,L} ↪ Σ_L have an intrinsic meaning "dans une catégorie isotopique convenable", independent of the
    local conical retractions, at least in tame topology.
  - The map Σ_{J,L} → Σ_J does use the local \unsure{fibrations|rétractions}.
  - Conical structures are an "anodine" extra datum "en topologie modérée, et dans \unsure{le cas équisingulier|la géom.
    algéb.}".
  - The page stops on "il est plus commode,".
  - This is a programme statement, not proved on the page. It is the uniqueness up to isotopy of tubes and control data,
    known for Whitney stratifications (Thom-Mather theory) and in o-minimal geometry (definable local conic structure);
    exact locators to be checked. See the K notes of the first pass, kept on 17 Nov.

## §17. Récapitulation (089-099)

**089-090, the topology of an ordered set, and bands.**
- The closed parts (down-sets) form a topology, the "lower" topology (an Alexandrov topology). Ā = ↓A (82); the open
  parts are the up-sets, "cofermés"; B̃ = ↑B is the closure for the upper topology, and the smallest open containing B (83)
  (R150g-14).
- J is locally closed ⟺ J = J̄ ∩ J̃ ⟺ J is order-convex: a **bande**. Bands are closed under intersection, and B(J) =
  J̄ ∩ J̃ is the band generated by J (84) (R150g-15, -16).
- (85)-(86): domination J ≺ K ⟺ J ⊂ K̄ and K ⊂ J̃. It is a preorder on P(I), J ∼ K ⟺ B(J) = B(K), and the quotient is the
  set of bands ordered by ≺ (R150g-16).
- **For I a chain, the bands with ≺ are the arrow poset Ar(I)**: [a,b] ≺ [c,d] ⟺ a ≤ c and b ≤ d (R150g-19, own).
- (87) and the converse are the band Lemma of 082 in the language of closure and density (R150g-17).
  - In "i.e. I' ⊂ K̄ et I' ⊂ J̃" the first sign is doubtful in the ink (Hua: "="). The mathematics decides ⊂: I' = K̄ fails
    for J = {1}, K = {2} in {0<1<2} (R150g-18, SA10-K150g-06).

**091-092, 094, stratifications and the axioms.**
- A stratification \unsure{ordinaire|indicielle} of type I is a map i ↦ X_i with:
  - (Strat 1): the X_i are closed and form a locally finite cover;
  - (Strat 2): it is increasing;
  - (Strat 3): X_i ∩ X_j = ⋃_{k∈ī∩j̄} X_k.
  - The page announces "les deux conditions" and gives three (SA10-K150g-13).
  - These are 004's 1°-2° and 151/045-052's a)-d) (DEPENDS).
- **(88)-(89) hold** (R150g-20). X_d = X_{i_0} for d = (i_0<…<i_n) is a semi-simplicial resolution of X.
  - The flags d with x ∈ X_d are those of ↑i(x), a cone on its least element, so the augmented sheaf complex is exact on
    stalks.
  - The spectral sequence H^p(I, i ↦ H^q(X_i,F)) ⇒ H^{p+q}(X,F) follows, with E_2 the higher inverse limits over the poset.
    This answers the first pass's A08 ("en un sens convenable").
- (90)-(91): the open strata X_i^* partition X, and X_i = ⋃_{j≤i} X_j^* (R150g-21).
- (Strat 4) fidelity, X_i^* ≠ ∅ (not stable on open sets); (Strat 4 bis), the X_i^* connected and non-empty.
- **(Strat 5), density: X_i = closure(X_i^*), "i.e. j<i ⇒ X_j \uline{rare} dans X_i".**
  - "Rare" is the classical word for nowhere dense. It is the word of 004's 4°, and it settles the "non dense" question
    (A10, edition matter g656).
  - The equivalence holds for a locally finite family of closed sets (R150g-22, -23).
  - (Strat 5) is stable on open sets.
  - The margin: under (Strat 5), fidelity ⟺ X_i ≠ ∅ (R150g-24).
- 094: X_i ⊂ X_j is read off the non-empty strata; for a faithful stratification X_i ⊂ X_j ⟺ X_i^* ⊂ X_j ⟺ i ≤ j (92), so
  I is a family of closed sets ordered by inclusion (R150g-25, -26).
  - The sentence "Si on veut Strat 5, cela s'exprime par l'axiome plus fort que dans tt strate fermée" stops there.
  - The phrase that ends 092, "donc on aura, si i,j∈J", has J in the ink, a slip for I (SA10-K150g-12).
- **Own statement (R150g-27).** The dense faithful stratifications are exactly the locally finite partitions into non-empty
  locally closed strata with the frontier condition, I being the closure order. Density is what makes his I the
  adjacency order. Without it I can be strictly finer (two points, X_i = {a} ⊂ X_j = X).

**093-095, X_J^* and X_J.**
- (93): X_J^* = ⋃_{j∈J} X_j^* and X_J = ⋃_{j∈J} X_j. J ↦ X_J^* preserves unions and intersections (94).
- X_J = X^*_{J̄} (95); X_J^* = X^*_{J̄} ∖ X^*_{J̄∖J} (96). For a band, J̄∖J is closed, so X_J^* is locally closed (R150g-28).
- Under density, closure(X_J^*) = X_J (97); under density and fidelity, X_J^* is closed ⟺ J is closed; (98) needs no
  hypothesis (R150g-29).
- The "dualement" margin: X^*_{J̃} is the smallest saturated open set containing X_J^* (R150g-30).
- 095: under density and fidelity, X_L^* is open ⟺ L is open, and X_J^* is locally closed ⟺ J is a band (R150g-31).

**095-096, the pro-neighbourhoods V_i.**
- (94) V_i = V(X_i^*, X). The margin: X_i^* ↪ V_i is a homotopy equivalence, "X métrisable". The word is sure; Michel Hua's
  project reads "unibranche", which the ink does not support. The equivalence itself needs the conical structure
  (R150f-13).
- (95): V_i ∩ X_j^* ≠ ∅ ⟺ V_i ∩ closure(X_j^*) ≠ ∅ ⟺ X_i^* ∩ closure(X_j^*) ≠ ∅ ⇒ X_i^* ∩ X_j ≠ ∅ ⇒ i ≤ j, the last two being
  equivalences when the stratification is dense, resp. faithful. All four steps hold (R150g-32). The second arrow's ink
  (⟸|⟺) does not matter: both directions hold.
- (96) ⟹: V_i ∩ V_j ≠ ∅ ⇒ i, j comparable, under "si X métrisable, donc tt **partie ouverte** de X est **normale**".
  - That reading is decided: the ink, his own margin on 083 ("les ouverts de X soient normaux"), and the mathematics agree
    (SA10-K150g-14).
  - The statement is complete normality, which separates the separated sets X_i^*, X_j^* (R150g-33).
  - **(96) ⟸ under "strat fidèle" alone is false** (two points, X_i = {a} ⊂ X_j = X); it needs density too, as in the
    sentence before (R150g-34, SA10-K150g-07).
- 096: V_ij = ∅ for incomparable i, j and V_ii = V_i, so the building blocks are the V_d of strict flags (98), "les
  pierres de construction" (R150g-35). Their footnote now reads "(et \unsure{des|les} protopos homotopes
  V^Z_{i_0,…,i_n} plus bas)".

**097, V^Z, and the self-flagged (100).**
- (99): V^Z_d = V_d ∩ Z.
- For Z locally closed containing X^*_{i_n}, (100) says V^Z_d ↪ V_d is a homotopy equivalence; he writes "? à vérifier!"
  in the margin.
- **His doubt is justified** (R150g-36, SA10-K150g-08). X = ℝ, X_0 = {0} < X_1 = ℝ, Z = {0} ∪ {1/n}: V_0^Z is not
  pro-equivalent to a point (R150f-18's example for 078). It holds for Z a locally closed union of strata, under control
  (R150f-19).
- The same refines the parenthesis "(\unsure{de sorte que|on sait que} X_{i_n} ↪ V^Z_{i_n} ↪ V_{i_n})", whose first term
  must be X^*_{i_n} (R150g-37, SA10-K150g-09).
- The struck hypothesis "X_{i_n} − Ẋ_{i_0}" shows him moving from the closed X_{i_n} of 078 to the open stratum.
- (101): V_J = V(X_J^*, X) = ⋃V_j = lim→_{Drap(J)} V_d, for any part J, as his NB says (R150g-38).
- **(103) X_J^* ↪ V_J holds for bands, not for every J**. For J = {0,2} in point < line < plane, X_J^* has three
  components while its neighbourhoods are contractible (R150g-39, SA10-K150g-10).
- **(104) "si Z ⊃ X_J" fails for arbitrary Z** (the same Z as above). It holds for Z a locally closed union of strata
  containing X_J^*, which is the form of 098 l.5 (R150g-40, SA10-K150g-11). That settles E02: the star does not matter
  once Z is a union of strata.

**098-099, V_{J,L}, V_J^L and V_{J_0…J_n}.**
- (105)-(106): V_J^L = V_J ∩ X_L^* = V_{J,L} ∩ X_L^* = V^{X_L^*}_{J,L}. The exponent X_L^* is written over a Z, and "avec Z =
  X_L^*" stands beneath. The sign before V_{J,L} ∩ X_L^* is an equality either way (R150g-41).
- The "Suggestion" margin: V^♭_{J,L} for V_J^L, and V^♭_{i_0…i_n} = V^{X^*_{i_n}}_{i_0…i_n}.
  - The ♭ is the "b" of his 077 margin (V_{ij}^b = V_i ∩ X_j^*) and the exponent of Σ^♭_{J,L} on 087: the trace on the top
    open stratum.
  - I propose to read ♭ in all three places (SA10-K150g-15), to be checked side by side on the native scans.
- (107)-(108) and the NB: V_{J,L} is the union over J ∩ L, over j < l and over l < j, and the colimit over the flags meeting
  J and L. Under J ≺ L the third union is absorbed and the J-terms of a flag come first (R150g-42).
- (109), for any J, L, and **(110)**: V^Z_{J,L} ↪ V_{J,L} for bands J ≺ L and Z containing X_L^*.
  - He first wrote "clairement" and struck it for "donc si" with the added hypotheses.
  - (110) follows from (100), through the homotopy colimit of an open cover, so it needs the refined hypothesis.
  - **(111), V_J^L ↪ V_{J,L}, takes Z = X_L^*, which satisfies it**: (111) holds under control. It is the margin (69) of 083
    (R150g-43, R150f-29).
- (112)-(113): V_{J_0,…,J_n} for J_0 ≺ ⋯ ≺ J_n, three expressions, which hold for any parts (R150g-44). The scribbled block
