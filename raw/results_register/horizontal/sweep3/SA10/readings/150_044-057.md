## 0. What the block is, and what this reading finds

**What the block is.** These are his sheets 18-24 of "Espaces stratifiés (étude par voisinages coniques)". The sheet
numbers are top left on 045, 047, …, 057. The bottom-left numbers 43-56 are the archivists' pagination, as Michel Hua's
project reads them. The block falls into two movements.
1. **044 to 048 l.2: the end of §10.** §10 ("Reprenons la description ab ovo", 043) is in the case 𝓜 = Top. The deployment
   of 002 is restated as one ordered space Σ_* over the index poset I:
   - conditions ⓐ-ⓓ: closed embeddings, the intersection formula (28), disjointness, transversality;
   - the equisingular, nonsingular and mixed conditions e)-g);
   - the boundary strata of every Σ_d, indexed by flags.
2. **048 to 057: §11, "Digression : où on laisse tomber I".** The same structure, without I: a topological ordered set
   Σ with its target map b and its spaces of strict flags Σ^<(n). He writes it twice:
   - **048-055:** ⓐ; the cartesian squares (31)-(32); the transversality (33)-(34); the conditions of circumstance
     ⓒ-ⓔ; b_1)-b_3); the functor Γ; and the relative order (35)-(36);
   - **056-057, the restart:** after "Je m'aperçois finalement que j'ai … mélangé des propriétés purement ensemblistes
     …, avec leur interprétation semi-simpliciale, et les aspects topologiques. Je reprends", he gives three conditions,
     one of each kind: ⓐ topological, ⓑ set-theoretic, ⓒ geometric. The restart runs on into 058.

**What this reading finds:**
1. **(32) is formal.** If (31) is a pushout of ordered sets, the square (32) of strict-flag spaces is cartesian for any
   ordered topological space, by the universal property (R150d-17). So (32) is not an axiom. The squares that carry
   geometry are the ones with a common final element. Those are not pushouts, and condition b) is their transversality
   (R150d-18). The example he gives of a pushout is degenerate as written. The intended example is the concatenation
   square (K150d-03).
2. **The restart's ⓑ repairs §10.** On 056, ⓑ asks for Σ_{≤x} to be finite and totally ordered. For a deployment over I,
   this is equivalent to (29) together with the disjointness of the faces of noncomparable strata (R150d-07). That
   disjointness is the hypothesis that the 045 margins lack (N01, the diamond, @836; R150d-06, K150d-07), and that 065
   later states. The repair is therefore already on 056, in index-free form.
3. **In the restart, the continuity of the relative order is automatic.** It follows from ⓐ, the separation of Σ and ⓑ
   (R150d-33). So 055's clopen Γ_1 needs no separate hypothesis.
4. **The redundancy conjectured on 051 is refuted in Top.** The margin "c'est peut être une conséquence de c) et de d)…"
   (F03, claims #837) refers to the mixed condition ⓔ. It fails in Top: take a proper, locally trivial projection with
   manifold total space and base, whose fibre is the suspension of a non-simply-connected homology 3-sphere (double
   suspension theorem). It holds smoothly and PL, given the boundary formula of the 051 margin (R150d-25, K150d-09). So
   the conditions stay separate hypotheses in Top, as the edition matter #837 says.
5. **Readings decided on the native scan:**
   - 044 margin: z, j;
   - 047 l.6: the ink reads Σ_{d'}, which is his slip for Σ_d;
   - 048 l.2: "stricte";
   - 050 l.20: "voit", with "Car" following, so N02 stands;
   - 052 l.13: 1<d.

   On 057 l.3, N(x) = card(Σ_{<x}) is fixed by the mathematics, while the ink stays doubtful. On 055, (35) is off by one
   against Γ(d) = Σ^<(d+1) (K150d-05). This settles the first pass's R02.

### 044 (bottom number 43): §10, the ordered space over I

  flags of I. His margin: "OK", and "permet ainsi l'énoncé de la condition (b)".

**Checked:**

### 045 (sheet 18): conditions ⓑ, ⓒ, ⓓ

**ⓑ, (28).** For i<j<k, Σ_ijk = Σ_ik ∩ Σ_jk inside Σ_k, "a priori ⊂". He reformulates it with the hooked arrows settled on
17 Nov: for z over k, Σ_* → I induces (29) (Σ_*)_{≤z} ↪ I_{≤k}, and "l'ordre de (Σ_*)_z est induit par celui de
I_{≤k}".
- **Re-proved (R150d-04).**
  - The inclusion ⊂ is transitivity.
  - Equality says that y_i ≤ z and y_j ≤ z with i < j force y_i ≤ y_j, which is (29) with the induced order.
  - This is the 042 margin ("il suffit de le vérifier pour n=2"), now made a condition.
- **The upper-left margin** says: for flags d, d' with the same final element i whose union is a flag,
  Σ_{d''} = Σ_d ∩ Σ_{d'} in Σ_i. Re-proved from ⓐ and ⓑ (R150d-05). By ⓐ the last-vertex map Σ_d → Σ_i is injective, and
  by (29) the elements below z over d ∪ d' form a chain.

**ⓒ.** "si i,j ≤ k n'ont pas de minorant dans I, alors Σ_ik ∩ Σ_jk = ∅". The parenthesis "(si i,j ont un minorant l,
alors Σ_ik∩Σ_jk ⊂ )" is unfinished; nothing follows ⊂ on the page (R01).
- **The two margins drawn from ⓒ are refuted as written (R150d-06, K150d-07).** They are "Implique que si d,d' sont deux
  drapeaux ayant final k, et si d∪d' n'est pas un drapeau, alors Σ_d∩Σ_{d'}=∅" and "ⓑ et ⓒ impliquent … ⋂Σ_{d_α} est vide
  si ⋃d_α n'est pas un drapeau, et =Σ_{∪d_α} sinon".
  - The counterexample is the first pass's N01, the diamond P = {0<a, b<t} with Σ = P discrete, which I checked again.
  - ⓐ and ⓑ hold, and ⓒ is vacuous, because 0 is a common minorant.
  - Yet Σ_{(a,t)} ∩ Σ_{(b,t)} = {t} although {a,b,t} is not a flag.
  - The "=Σ_{∪d_α} sinon" half is right; it is R150d-05 iterated.
- **What the parenthesis was reaching for.** In a deployment the faces of incomparable strata are disjoint even when a
  lower stratum lies below both. Take two lines crossing at a point of ℝ². The deployed plane is the plane minus the tubes
  of the point and of the two lines, the point's tube removed first. Outside the point's tube, thin tubes of the lines
  are disjoint. So the right ⓒ is "i, j incomparable ⇒ Σ_ik ∩ Σ_jk = ∅", which is what 065(b) states. On 056 ⓑ it comes
  in index-free form; see below.

**ⓓ.** For fixed k, the Σ_ik (i ∈ I_{<k}) are "sous-espaces bord-ts de Σ_k" and are transversal. "(grâce à (30) et 28,)",
for a flag d = (i_0,--i_n,k), the Σ_{i_0k}, …, Σ_{i_n,k} cut each other transversally along Σ_d = Σ_{i_0-i_n,k}. The
margin is read on 17 Nov as "(i.e. but pour d'⊋d, et d cofinal dans d') …". It says that inside Σ_d the
Σ_{d∪{i}} = Σ_d ∩ Σ_ik are bordant and transversal. Checked in the local corner model (R150d-08): a transversal family of
coordinate half-spaces restricts to one on each face.

### 046 (bottom number 45): e), f), g)

**e) equisingularity.** The Σ_ij → Σ_i are fibrations, that is, the source map Σ_** → Σ_* is one. The margin: "Σ_d → Σ_{d'}
est une fibration, si d'⊂d est un segment initial de d".
- **Re-proved (R150d-09).** By the concatenation Σ_{(i_0…i_n)} = Σ_{(i_0…i_{n-1})} ×_{Σ_{i_{n-1}}} Σ_{i_{n-1}i_n}, which
  is a chain by transitivity, an initial-segment projection is a composite of base changes of source maps.
- For Hurewicz or Serre fibrations this is immediate. For locally trivial bundles one also uses properness ("fibrants
  propres" later) or the homotopy invariance of bundles over U × F with U contractible.

**f) the nonsingular case.**
- The Σ_i are manifolds with boundary, the Σ_ji (j<i) are submanifolds with boundary of the boundary Σ̇_i, and they form
  a cylindrical stratification of it.
- The sentence as corrected on 17 Nov ends: the Σ_d = Σ_{i_1,--i_n} "sont alors des variétés à bord p. et les Σ_{d'}
  (d'⊋d, d cofinal dans d') forment une stratification cylindrique du bord Σ̇_d de Σ_d".
- The two margins identify the index set of the boundary strata of Σ_d, first as a product of Drap̂'s minus one point,
  then as "Drap(I_{<i_1} ∐ I_{i_1<·<i_2} ∐ … ∐ I_{i_{n-1}<·<i_n})".
- **Re-proved (R150d-10).**
  - d' = d ∪ e with max e < i_n; e ∪ d is a chain iff e meets each gap I_{<i_1}, I_{(i_k,i_{k+1})} in a chain.
  - The ∐ must be the ordinal sum. That is the order induced from I on the union of the gaps, since x < i_k < y across
    gaps. With the plain disjoint union, a flag could not meet two gaps.
  - The interval between d' ⊃ d'' is the Boolean lattice of subsets of d' ∖ d''. So the boundary stratification of Σ_d is
    precylindrical in the sense of 028 c), a manifold with corners whose face poset is the poset of these flags.
- This is 002's general a), stated with the order on Σ_* (R150d-39).

**g) the mixed condition (046-047).** The fibres of Σ_ij → Σ_i are manifolds with boundary. The bundle of their
boundaries is stratified by the Σ_d over the flags d ⊋ (i,j) with first element i ("pour être fibré sur Σ_i") and last
element j ("pour être contenu ds Σ_ij"), which correspond to Drap(I_{i<·<j}). It is empty iff j is a successor of i,
"qui est donc la condition pour que Σ_ij−Σ_i soit lisse".
- **Re-proved (R150d-11).** The faces of Σ_ij are the d with max d = j and (i,j) cofinal. Those with min d = i are the
  vertical ones, the boundaries of the fibres. Those with new elements below i lie over ∂Σ_i.
- **The struck draft at the top of 047** wanted "Drap̂(I_{i<·<j}) × Drap̂(I_{>j})" and the condition "et que j soit maximal
  dans I". Flags above j do not index faces of Σ_ij, so he was right to strike it.

### 048 (bottom number 47): §11 begins

**The setting.** "Soit donné un ens. ordonné topologique": Σ(0) is a space, and Σ(1) ⊂ Σ(0)×Σ(0) is closed and is the graph
of an order.

**ⓐ.** "Σ(1) →^{but} Σ(0) est une immersion localement bordante, et propre (donc finie)".
- **"Donc finie": re-proved (R150d-14).** A local immersion has discrete fibres. Properness makes them compact, so they
  are finite.

**"Cela implique que Σ(0) →^{diag} Σ(1) est un isomorphisme sur une partie ouverte et fermée."**
- **Re-proved, with a precision (R150d-15, K150d-04).**
  - Openness holds. Near (x,x), b is injective on a neighbourhood U. For w near x, (w,w) ∈ U is the only point of U over
    w, so U ∩ b^{-1}(W) = diag(W).
  - Closedness needs Σ(0) separated. On 056 he says exactly that: "l'application diagonale est ouverte, donc si Σ séparé,
    son image est une partie à la fois ouverte et fermée".

**The strict flags.** Σ^<(1) is the complement of the diagonal. Σ^<(n) is the space of strict flags of length n. "Les
Σ^<(n) forment un espace pré-simplicial (NB pas d'applications de dégénérescence …)"; on 17 Nov "semi" was found struck
and "pré" written above it.
- **Checked (R150d-16).** Face maps keep strictness. Degeneracies would repeat a vertex. Σ^<(n) is open and closed in
  Σ(n), because each consecutive inequality is the pullback of the clopen diagonal.
- Today this is a semi-simplicial space, a functor on Δ_inj. See C. P. Rourke and B. J. Sanderson, "Δ-sets I: homotopy
  theory", Quart. J. Math. Oxford 22 (1971), 321-338; locator to be checked.

### 049 (sheet 20): (31)-(33)

**(31)-(32).** "Si on a un diagramme d'inclusions (croissantes) d'ens tot. ordonnés (31) [d ← δ = d∩d' → d', with
d''=d∪d'] tel que ce soit un diagramme cocart. dans la cat. des ens. ordonnés (p.ex …) alors le diagramme correspondant
(32) est cartésien."
- **Re-proved, and it is formal (R150d-17).**
  - Σ^<(e) is the space of strictly increasing maps e → Σ.
  - If d'' = d ∪ d' is the pushout of ordered sets, its order is the transitive closure of those of d and d'. A pair of
    strict maps that agree on δ then extends to a strict map on d'', because the order of Σ is transitive.
  - The topologies agree: Σ^{d''} embeds in Σ^d × Σ^{d'} as the pairs that agree on δ.
- So (32) holds for every ordered topological space. The corrected text agrees: the circled b) before "Cela implique" on
  048 is struck, and the live ⓑ stands at (33). Michel Hua's project heads the page "(b) cartesian squares (31)-(32)". By
  this reading, (32) is a consequence, not a condition.

**The example "(p.ex δ segment initial de d, et d' contenant l'élément final de d)".**
- **Refined (R150d-18, R150d-19, K150d-03).**
  - The native image at 1.15× confirms the wording.
  - As written it is degenerate. The final element of d lies in d' ∩ d = δ, and an initial segment of d containing that
    element is d itself, so d ⊂ d'. (Michel Hua's project reads "et contenant l'élément final de d", which is degenerate
    too.)
  - The intended example has δ an initial segment of **d'** and d' containing the final element of d. Then δ is a final
    segment of d and an initial segment of d', and the square is the concatenation, as for Σ_ijk = Σ_ij ×_{Σ_j} Σ_jk of 002.
- **Which squares carry the geometry.** The squares with a common final element are not pushouts: d = {i,k}, d' = {j,k},
  δ = {k}, whose pushout is the poset with i, j incomparable. For these, (32) says nothing. They carry the old (28) and
  transversality. That is why "Les conditions b c d du n° précédent deviennent ici une seule (du type d)".

**ⓑ, (33).**
- For d = (i_0,-i_n) of "dim" ≥ 1, the maps Σ^<(d) → Σ^<(i_α,i_n) →^{φ_α} Σ^<(i_n) are immersions. On 17 Nov the bordant
  ones were read "φ_0, φ_1, φ_{n-1}", as in the diagram.
- 050: "on suppose qu'au voisinage de chaque pt de Σ^<(d) … Σ^<(d) soit l'intersection dans Σ^<({i_n}) ≃ Σ(0) des
  Σ^<({i_α,i_n})_α (≃ Σ^<(1)), et que cette famille soit transversale".
- All the φ_α are the same map b. The statement is about **germs** at the points of a chain: the germ of b at
  (x_α, x_n) is a bordant germ B_α at x_n. The long margin of 049 ("Plus précisément nous voulons que pour x∈Σ^<(0), les
  z_α … sont en corr. biunivoque avec les germes B_α …") and b_1)-b_3) of 052 make this precise.

### 051 (sheet 21): the conditions of circumstance

**ⓒ.** "L'application Σ^<(1) →^{source} Σ^<(0) est propre et fibrante (``condition d'isotrivialité'')", which implies that
Σ^<(d') − Σ^<(d) is fibrant when d is an initial segment of d'.
- **Re-proved (R150d-22).** By the formal concatenation squares (R150d-17), the projection to an initial segment is a
  composite of base changes of the source map. The proviso for locally trivial bundles is as for 046.
- Michel Hua's project reads "condition d'équisingularité". That place is still open (17 Nov, place 8).

**ⓓ.** "Σ^<(0) est une variété à bord, et Σ^<(1) ↪^{but} Σ^<(0) a comme image son bord. (``condition non singulière'')
donc aussi les Σ^<(d) (car un sous-espace bordant d'une variété à bord est une variété à bord [de dim < bord|déterminer le
bord], de = dim que celui-ci ?? ou on exige que les Σ^<(d) soient des variétés à bord…)".
- **Checked (R150d-23, K150d-08).** For the faces of a manifold with corners, "de = dim que celui-ci" (the dimension of
  the boundary) is right.
- The claim that a bordant subspace is a manifold with boundary holds in the smooth and PL settings. In PL, a link whose
  cone is a ball is a sphere or a ball.
- In the topological setting a collar B × [0,1) can be a manifold although B is not, for factors of the kind in R150d-25.
  So his "??" is justified, and his alternative ("on exige …") is the safe axiom.

**The long margin (F02).** "il faudrait vérifier que le bord de Σ^<(d) est l'image des Σ^<(d') pour d'⊃d cofinaux à d. Si
c'est vrai, ça doit être délicat ! (d'après le cas où Σ est au dessus de I = {0,1,2})". The index set reads {0,1,2} first
(17 Nov).
- **A local proof, conditional (R150d-24).**
  - Suppose b_1)-b_3) of 052, or ⓑ-ⓒ of 056-057, and ⓓ.
  - Near a chain with top x, Σ^<(d) is the face ⋂_{β} B_β of the corner at x.
  - By ⓓ, the boundary of Σ(0) near x is the union of all the B_γ at x. In the corner model, the boundary of the face is
    the union of its intersections with the B_γ not already used.
  - Each such intersection is the germ of Σ^<(d ∪ {γ}). Because the germs at x are totally ordered (b_2, 056 ⓑ),
    d ∪ {γ} is a chain with the same top.
- So F02 reduces to the local corner model of "transversal", that is, to the definition. The "délicat" is the global
  statement, that Σ^<(d) is a manifold with boundary, for which ⓓ's own alternative is the axiom.

**ⓔ, the mixed condition.** The source map Σ^<(1) → Σ^<(0), "propre et fibrant par c)", "est à fibres des variétés à bord,

  between y and z, which is the image of the face that deletes the middle vertex (R150d-24).

**The margin "c'est peut être une conséquence de c) et de d)…" (F03).** It stands beside ⓔ, and 063's margin asks the
same about the mixed condition 3° (F07). So "it" is ⓔ.
- **Refuted in Top, refined (R150d-25, K150d-09).**
  - The counterexample: let H be a homology 3-sphere with π_1 ≠ 1 and ΣH its suspension. ΣH is compact and not a manifold.
    By the double suspension theorem ΣH × ℝ ≅ Σ²H minus the two suspension points ≅ S⁵ minus two points ≅ S⁴ × ℝ. So
    pr: ΣH × ℝ → ℝ is proper and locally trivial, with total space and base manifolds, and its fibre is not a manifold.
    See J. W. Cannon, "Shrinking cell-like decompositions of manifolds. Codimension three", Ann. of Math. 110 (1979),
    83-112, and R. D. Edwards' double suspension theorem; locators to be checked.

  - In the smooth setting a proper submersion has manifold fibres (Ehresmann). In PL, F × ℝ a PL manifold forces F to be
    one, since the links are suspensions and a suspension that is a sphere has a sphere as link at its cone points.
  - Given the boundary formula (F02), the second clause of ⓔ follows: a local trivialisation over U is a homeomorphism,
    and it carries ∂U × F ∪ U × ∂F onto the boundary.
- So the conjecture holds in the smooth, PL and, presumably, o-minimal settings. In Top the conditions stay separate, as
  #837 says.
