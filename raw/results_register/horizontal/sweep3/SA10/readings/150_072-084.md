## 0. What the block is

**The pages** are his sheets 31 (verso) to 38 of "Espaces stratifiés et voisinages coniques" (déc. 81 - janv. 82): the
top-left numbers 32-38 on 073, 075, 077, 079, 081, 082, 084 are his; the bottom-left 71-83 are the archive's pencil
(scan − 1). Three movements:
1. **072**, the end of §13: a Remarque comparing two "déploiements" through an ordered space Σ with source s and target b.
2. **073-076**, §14 "Description heuristique des Σ_i, Σ_{ij}": the deployed strata and their tubes of raccord are cut out
   of X by tubular neighbourhoods, coherently, through a dimension function δ and a filtration X^{(d)}.
3. **077-084**, §15 "Introduction des proespaces": the pro-neighbourhood V_i of each open stratum, the intersections
   V_{i_0…i_n} over flags, X recovered by gluing, the relative versions on a locally closed union of strata, ideals and
   bands, V_J and the raccords V_J^L between two bands, the band Lemma, the reduction to X' = X^*_{I'} where J is closed and
   L open, the flag cover of V_J^L and the spectral sequence (73). Scan 080 is a cancelled draft of the same.

**The thread.** §14 builds the standard presentation of 039-043 concretely (Σ_d for each flag, inclusions for cofinal
deletions, retractions for the others). §15 replaces the cut-out manifolds Σ_d by pro-spaces V_d, which need no choices,
and computes with them. The two models agree flag by flag (R150f-36).

## 1. Page by page

### 072 (sheet 31 verso): the Remarque

- **The setting:** an ordered topological space Σ with arrow space Σ_(1), source s and target b, and
  a) s a proper fibration; b) b a proper immersion; c) x ≤ y in one connected component ⇒ x = y.
- **The first context** ("une notion assez anodine de déploiement", the open reading of the second line is §4):
  Σ = ∐ X_i, Σ_1 = ∐_{X_i ⊂ X_j} X_i. **Checked (R150f-01):** s is étale with fibre I_{≥i} over X_i, so a finite covering,
  proper exactly when each X_i lies under finitely many X_j ("(localt fini donc)"); b is a closed embedding on each copy;
  c) holds since a component lies in one X_i. This is the half-simplicial X_{Δ0}, X_{Δ1} of 151/51-52.
- **The second context** ("la théorie développée ici des déploiements de stratifications équisingulières"): b bordant,
  nothing special on s.
- **The margin:** c) is a consequence of a), b) "si ... Σ(1)^* → Σ est rare, et que les comp. connexes de Σ de dim
  finie…". **Checked with a precision (R150f-02, K150f-13):** the dimension argument needs s locally trivial and
  components in which a closed set of full dimension has interior (topological manifolds); finite dimension alone does not
  give it. The same step is on 069.
- **The sorite of Ĩ = π_0(Σ)** applies in both contexts: in the first, the stratification by the X_i is replaced by the
  components of the X_i; in the second, "itou" for the X_i^* = X_i ∖ ⋃_{j<i} X_j. **Checked (R150f-03):** the second
  refinement is by components of the open strata, because Σ_i is a deformation retract of X_i^*; ℝ ⊃ {0} shows the two
  refinements differ. The open reading X_{ij}|X_j is immaterial (same set).

### 073 (sheet 32): §14, Σ_i and Σ_{ij} by tubes

- **(42):** Σ_i = X_i ∖ X_i ∩ T̊_{<i}, T_{<i} a closed standard tube of Ẋ_i = ⋃_{k<i} X_k, T̊ = T ∖ Ṫ. The ink of the
  summand is open; the mathematics needs X_k (K150f-01; Michel Hua's project reads X_k). **Checked (R150f-04):**
  Σ_i ⊂ X_i^*, Σ_i ≃ X_i^*; Σ_i is closed only if T̊ is open in X, the hypothesis that the reading of 001-016 found missing
  (H150a-3).
- **Σ_{ij} (43):** a tube T'_{ij} of Σ_i in X'_i = X_j ∖ X_j ∩ T̊_{<i}; the trace on its boundary of the strata strictly
  between i and j is the singular part SṪ'_{ij}, empty when j is a successor of i; Σ_{ij} = Ṫ'_{ij} ∖ T̊(SṪ'_{ij}).
  **Checked (R150f-06):** the two open readings of the trace (i<k<j|i≤k<j, X_{jk}|X_k) give the same set.
- **074, "de sorte qu'on aura Σ_{ij} ⊂ Σ_j, à condition d'avoir ajusté de façon évidente les choix".** **Refuted as an
  independent choice and refined (R150f-05, K150f-06):** in ℝ² ⊃ {0}, a tube T'_{01} smaller than T_0, as l. 25 suggests,
  gives Σ_{01} ∩ Σ_1 = ∅. The evident adjustment is to take T'_{ij} as the trace of T_i, which is what 076 does:
  Σ_{ij} = Ṫ_i ∩ Σ_j.

### 074 (sheet 32 verso): the dimension function and the filtration

- To place the Σ_{ik} and Σ_{jk} (i<j<k) "en position relative correcte" so that their intersection deserves the name
  Σ_{ijk}, he takes δ: I → ℕ strictly increasing, "on ne lui en demande pas plus".
- (44) X_(d) = ⋃_{δ(i)≤d} X_i; (45) the chain up to X; (46) a decreasing filtration by closed X^{(d)}; (47)
  X^{(i)} ⊂ X ∖ X_{i−1} (K150f-02: X_{(i−1)}, his own "i.e." line). **Checked (R150f-07):** X_(d) is the closed union of an
  ideal; strata of equal δ are incomparable; X^{(N+1)} = ∅. A cancelled first attempt with T_0, T_1 precedes.

### 075 (sheet 33): the tubes T_d and the formula for Σ_i

- T_(0), X^{(1)} = X ∖ T̊_(0), then T_1, X^{(2)} = X ∖ (T̊_0 ∪ T̊_1), then (48) X^{(d)} = X − ⋃_{α<d} T̊_α. Margin: "OPS
  T_d = ⋃_{δ(i)≤d} T_i". **Checked (R150f-08):** the identities hold, with T_1 transversal to Ṫ_0; closedness again needs
  T̊ open.
- **(49):** Σ_i = X_i ∩ X_{(d)} = X_i ∖ ⋃_{α<d} X_i ∩ T̊_α = X_i ∖ ⋃_{j<i} X_i ∩ T̊_j ⊂ X_i^*.
  - **Slip (K150f-03):** the ink has the subscript X_{(d)}, which would give X_i; X^{(d)} is meant (076 l. 23 has it).
  - **Reading (K150f-04):** T̊_α, by (48).
  - **Checked with a precision (R150f-09, K150f-05):** the second equality needs the tubes standard (admissible): T_j meets
    X_i only if j ≤ i. For arbitrary tubes it fails (a point off a line with a wide disc).

### 076 (sheet 33 verso): flags, transitions, the complete structure

- (49) Σ_{ij} = Ṫ_i ∩ Σ_j ⊂ Σ_j; (50) Σ_{i_0…i_n} = Ṫ_{i_0} ∩ … ∩ Ṫ_{i_{n−1}} ∩ Σ_{i_n} = ⋂_k Σ_{i_k i_n}. **Checked
  (R150f-10):** for n = 2 this is 002's corner square.
- "On a de façon évidente les morphismes de transition … pour les inclusions cofinales de drapeaux, mais non pour des
  inclusions quelconques, dont la définition dépend du choix de rétractions des T_i^{(d)} sur X_i^{(d)}", giving
  Σ_{ij} ⊂ Ṫ_i^{(d)} ⊂ T_i^{(d)} → X_i^{(d)} = Σ_i and "la structure complète ((Σ_i), (Σ_{ij}), Σ_{ij} ⇉ Σ_j, Σ_i)".
  **Checked (R150f-11):** these are conditions (2) and (1) of 039's standard presentation.
- "Mais il faut des hyp. d'équisingularité … et une condition de locale trivialité sur les rétractions … pour que les
  Σ_{ij} → Σ_i soient des fibrations". **Re-proved (R150f-12):** if the retraction r_j preserves the tube coordinate of
  each lower X_i (ρ_i ∘ r_j = ρ_i, Mather's second control condition), then Σ_{ijk} ≅ Σ_{ij} ×_{Σ_j} Σ_{jk}: the cartesian
  concatenation (3) of 040. So his condition on the retractions must contain this control.

### 077 (sheet 34): §15, pro-neighbourhoods and gluing

- **V_i,** "le provoisinage tubulaire (au sens des proespaces) de X_i^* dans X"; **(51)** X_i^* → V_i "équivalence
  d'homotopie de proespaces". **Checked (R150f-13)** for a conical structure, with tubes of variable radius.
- **(52)** V_{ij} = V_i ∩ V_j. **NB:** V_i ∩ V_j = ∅ for incomparable i, j, "du moins si les ouverts de X sont normaux,
  p.ex. X métrisable"; V_{ii} = V_i. **Re-proved (R150f-14):** X_i^* and X_j^* are then separated sets, and his hypothesis
  is exactly hereditary normality, the condition for separated sets to have disjoint neighbourhoods.
- **The margin question:** "Pourquoi V_{ij}^b = V_i ∩ X_j^* ↪ V_{ij} est une équivalence d'homotopie ?" (the target V_{ij}
  is a low-confidence reading of 17 Nov). **Answered under control (R150f-16):** the radial retraction of the tube of
  X_j^*, preserving the tubes of X_i, deforms V_i ∩ V_j onto V_i ∩ X_j^*. Control data exist for Whitney stratifications,
  so the answer holds there. 083's (69) asserts the same in general (R150f-29).
- **The diagram** V_i ↩ V_{ij} → V_j with s_{ij} a hooked inclusion (17 Nov), and **(53)** X = lim→(V_i, V_{ij}, b_{ij},
  s_{ij}): "X est obtenu par recollement des V_i suivant les V_{ij}". **Re-proved (R150f-15):** it holds level by level,
  since any neighbourhoods U_i ⊃ X_i^* cover X.
- **(54)** V_{i_0…i_n} = ⋂ V_{i_k} for a flag; NB: defined for any set of indices, empty unless it is a flag (by R150f-14).

### 078 (sheet 34 verso): the flag system and relative pro-neighbourhoods

- "C'est un système inductif de proespaces sur l'ens ordonné opposé de celui des drapeaux de I …, dont le lim est
  également ≈ X." **Re-proved (R150f-17):** with incomparable pairs disjoint, the Čech diagram of the cover is the
  system over Drap(I)^op; its colimit and its homotopy colimit are X.
- **(55)-(56):** for Z locally closed and X_i^* ⊂ Z (reading, K150f-08), V_i^Z = V_i ∩ Z, and X_i^* ↪ V_i^Z ↪ V_i are
  homotopy equivalences. **Refuted as stated and refined (R150f-18, K150f-07):** Z must be a union of strata;
  Z = {0} ∪ {1/n} in ℝ ⊃ {0} is a counterexample. All his uses have Z = X_J^*.
- **(57)** V^Z_{i_0…i_n} ↪ V_{i_0…i_n}, "(si X_{i_n} ⊂ Z)". **Re-proved with the refined hypothesis (R150f-19,
  K150f-09):** a locally closed union of strata containing X^*_{i_n} suffices; the written X_{i_n} ⊂ Z does not cover the
  band case (60).
- **(58)** X_J^* = ⋃_{i∈J} X_i^*, X_J = ⋃_{i∈J} X_i.

### 079 (sheet 35): ideals and bands

- For an ideal, X_J^* = X_J is closed. For a band (convex J), equivalently J' = J̃ ∖ J an ideal, **(59)**
  X_J^* = X^*_{J̃} ∖ X^*_{J'}, locally closed. **Re-proved (R150f-20):** both directions of the equivalence. In the terms of
  003-005, ideals are the closed and bands the locally closed subsets of Esp(I), and X_J^* = φ^{-1}(J).
- The X_i ∩ X_J^* (i ∈ J) form a stratification. **Re-proved (R150f-21):** convexity is exactly what keeps the intersection
  axiom inside J.
- **(60)-(61):** the V^J over flags of J, and X_J^* ≃ lim(V_i^J, V_{ij}^J) ≃ lim over Drap(J). **Checked (R150f-22).**

### 082 (sheet 37): the band Lemma

- a) J, L bands with J ≺ L ⟺ b) there is a band I' in which J is an ideal, L a coideal, every element above an element of
  J and below one of L. Then I' is unique, is the smallest band containing J ∪ L, and **(67)**
  I' = {i : ∃ j ∈ J, l ∈ L, j ≤ i ≤ l}. **Re-proved (R150f-26);** his last line needs "élément de J" (K150f-11).
- **The struck 3°** ("si j ∈ J, l ∈ L sont tels que l < j, alors j, l ∈ J ∩ L !") **follows from 1°-2° (R150f-27).** That is
  why it is struck, and 083 uses it.

### 083 (sheet 37 verso): the reduction to X' and the flag pieces

- **(68):** V_J^L is the V'^L_J of X' = X^*_{I'}, stratified by the X'_i, where X'_J is closed and X'_L open. **Re-proved
  (R150f-28),** with the union V_J^L = ⋃_{j≤l} V_{j,l}^{X'} and its case list: incomparable pairs empty (under the starred
  reservation "sous réserve que les ouverts de X soient normaux, p.ex. X métrisable"), l < j only inside J ∩ L.
- **The margin (69):** for J ≺ L, V_J^L ↪ V_J ∩ V_L (=: V_{J,L}) is a homotopy equivalence. **Re-proved under control
  (R150f-29):** by (57) in X', and because X'_L is open in X'. **The hypothesis is needed (R150f-30):** for L below J,
  V_J^L = ∅ while V_J ∩ V_L ≠ ∅. With J = {i}, L = {j} it is the answer to 077's question. The union display above it is
  unnumbered (17 Nov): (69) is this equivalence.
- **The four-term statement:** V_{j,l} ∩ V_{j',l'} is empty unless {j, j', l, l'} is a flag, of at most four terms, at
  least one in J and one in L. **Checked (R150f-31);** the struck "deux dans J, deux dans L" was rightly struck.
