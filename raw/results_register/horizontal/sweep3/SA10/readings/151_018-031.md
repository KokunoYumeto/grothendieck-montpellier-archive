### 028: the provisional germ, and what it does

- **"techniquement [simple|souple]"** (open; Michel Hua's project reads "souple", the word shape of 030 l.3). For X
  paracompact he takes "(à titre provisoire !)" the germ of X around Y, "un topos, sav. la Lim← toposique des
  voisinages ouverts de Y dans X". "Intuitivement": 𝒱 ↪ X behaves like an open immersion, Y ↪ 𝒱 like a closed one.
- **The topos limit collapses for his X (R151b-25, K151b-05).**
  - The open neighbourhoods U of Y are open subtopoi of Sh(X), and the limit of the cofiltered family is their meet.
  - For X regular, as a paracompact Hausdorff space is, every closed sublocale is the meet of the open sublocales
    containing it. So 𝒱_{Y,X} = Sh(Y): the germ is Y itself.
  - Then 𝒱^*_{Y,X} = 𝒱 ∩ X^* is the empty topos, since a closed subtopos and its open complement meet in the
    degenerate topos.
  - The span (7) of 029 becomes X^* ← ∅ → Y, whose colimit X^* ⊔ Y is not X unless Y is open.
  - So the provisional definition makes (4) true trivially and empties (5)-(10).
  - The construction keeps its content in two other settings: as a pro-object (the pro-space or pro-topos of the U and
    of the U ∖ Y), which is what cote 150 §15 adopts with its pro-neighbourhoods; or on sites that are not T₁. In the
    Zariski or étale topology the limit of the neighbourhoods of a closed point is the local or henselian scheme, and
    𝒱^* is not empty. That is the étale setting he names on 032.
  - Michel Hua's project's note accepts the topos limit as written ("exact au sens où la limite projective d'un système
    de topos ouverts en est un").
  - Literature, locators to be checked: J. Picado and A. Pultr, *Frames and Locales*, Birkhäuser 2012, ch. V (regular
    locales are fit); P. T. Johnstone, *Sketches of an Elephant*, A4.5 (open and closed complementary subtopoi).
- **The struck paragraph (l.5)** (local normal triviality implies, "moyennant des conditions de 'modération'
  convenables", the inclusion (4)) is cancelled in the ink by diagonal strokes, a stroke through its first line and a
  frame. **The margin replacing it** (l.7): the main interest of the factorization is that "l'inclusion" Y → 𝒱_{Y,X} is
  an "équivalence d'homotopie" "(indépendamment de toute hyp. de locale trivialité)".
- **Checked (R151b-26, K151b-08):**
  - in the pro sense the system of the U is the shape of Y (Y closed in a metrizable ANR);
  - for Y an ANR, the tame case, Y → 𝒱 is a homotopy equivalence;
  - for a wild Y (the Warsaw circle) it is only a shape equivalence;
  - so the margin is right about local triviality, and the moderation of the struck paragraph is still needed.
  - Literature, locator to be checked: S. Mardešić and J. Segal, *Shape Theory*, North-Holland 1982.
- **"(moyennant des hyp. de modération, l'hyp. sur Z devrait être une conséquence de la première hypothèse)"** (l.14):
  that Z^* = Z ∖ {z} is a manifold when Y and X^* = X − Y (with "des" struck before "variétés") are.
  - **False in Top (R151b-27, K151b-06).** Y' × Z^* ≅ X' ∩ X^* is a manifold, but a factor of a manifold need not be one.
    R. H. Bing's dogbone space B satisfies B × ℝ ≅ ℝ⁴ ("The cartesian product of a certain nonmanifold and a line is
    E⁴", Ann. of Math. 70 (1959), 399-412; locator to be checked). Take Y' = ℝ and Z the cone on B at z.
  - **True in PL and smoothly:** the link of a point of Y' × Z^* is an iterated suspension of a link in Z^*, and a
    suspension that is a PL sphere has a PL-sphere link at its suspension points.
  - So his "moyennant des hyp. de modération" is exactly what is needed, as for the cote-150 margins 051, 063 and 067.
- He ends with the wish to rebuild X from the manifolds Y and X^* ("reconstituer", decided on 17 Nov).

### 030-031 (p. 3): van Kampen

- **(8) holds for an open U ⊃ Y with no connectivity hypothesis (R151b-29).** This is the groupoid form of van Kampen

  checked). Michel Hua's project makes the same remark.
- **(9) needs 𝒱^* connected (R151b-30, K151b-07).** He states (9), π₁(X) ≃ π₁(X^*) ∧_{π₁𝒱^*} π₁(𝒱) with

  point presupposes 𝒱^* connected, and Y, X, X^* connected do not give it.

- **"isom. extérieurem^t" and the margin, checked (R151b-31).** The margin reads "cette flèche définie moy^t [d'un|des]
  choix suppl. (pt base de Y et classe de chemins de x vers y)".
  - π₁(𝒱, x) ≅ π₁(Y, y) through 𝒱 ≃ Y needs a path class from x to y, and changing it composes with an inner

- **(10)** reduces (8) to the two maps π₁(𝒱^*) → π₁(Y) and π₁(𝒱^*) → π₁(X^*). "C'est ici que l'hypothèse" (031 l.22)

  at z). So 𝒱^* → 𝒱 ≃ Y is locally a product, hence a fibration with fibre the local link 𝒱^*_{z,Z}. That is (11) of
