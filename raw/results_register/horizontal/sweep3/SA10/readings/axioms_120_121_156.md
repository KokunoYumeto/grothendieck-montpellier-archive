## 1. Three kinds of system

The drafts axiomatize "tame" (modéré) geometry in three different ways.

1. **Coordinate families.** A family M_n of subsets of Rⁿ (or of subobjects of Iⁿ in a category), closed under
   operations along the maps of finite sets. Cote 120 (M0-M11); 121/054-058 (M0-M5 of the moderate category, M′0-M′4);
   121/059-063 (Ⓐ-Ⓓ, 5°-6°, F0-F4); the list of 121/065; the C_* draft of 121/070-073 (a)-d), ⑤-⑧).
2. **One space with a family of subsets.** A Boolean algebra F of subsets of one space X with closure, density, chain and
   component axioms: 121/004-010 (a)-f), α-δ)), with cellular decompositions and the existence theorem (121/011-017); the
   admissible partitions of 121/049-052 belong here, without the Boolean family.
3. **Intrinsic systems without points.** The figures, contrées, ateliers and magasins of cote 156 (1986): strata,
   refinement, subdivision, compatibility, lieux, supports. 156-4/2-3 takes a stratified space as its model; 156-3 takes a
   line (tronçon).

The cote 151 axioms a)-d) on closed strata (1981, 151/45-52) are the bridge between kinds 2 and 3.

**What corresponds, checked on both pages.**
1. **120's M1 + M4 is 121/070's a)-d).** 070 closes C_* under permutations, projections, repeated coordinates and
   products, and its NB restates this as closure under all α^* for maps of finite sets: that is 120's M1 with comment ①
   (M_I for finite I) and ② (permutations, faces, degeneracies). 120 adds M2 (inverse images along the diagonal
   embeddings R^n ↪ R^{n+1}) and derives intersections from M1, M2, M4: A ∩ B = δ⁻¹(A × B). **This is exactly the axiom
   whose absence makes 121/070's intersection Proposition unprovable** (my SA20 reading, countermodels). So 120/003 is the
   repaired form of 121/070.
2. **M6 and M7 are 121/061's 6° and 5°.** M7 (extension of moderate functions from Z ⊂ X) is 5° (I₀ injective). M6 (Z the
   common zero set of finitely many moderate functions) is 6°, plus a clause that the functions separate the points of

   (under M5, M6, M7) is the result of 121/061-062 (under 5°, 6°, with unions). 121/067-068 supplies the missing

3. **The pushout question of 121/073 is answered by 120.** 073 asks for pushouts under the extension condition alone and
   writes "? à tirer au clair". 120 adds M6 to M7 and proves it (013-014). This is what my SA20 reading proposed: extension

4. **M11 is the correct form of 121/056.** 121/056 claims a field of moderate points and a vector space of moderate maps

5. **The reconstruction.** 120/008 states that M_* is equivalent to (𝓔, R, |·|) with finite limits, a left exact |·| and

**What each coordinate system allows or excludes.**
- **None of 120, 121/054-063 and 121/070-073 excludes wild sets.** All compact subsets with all continuous maps satisfy
  them (the Pro notes' TC06; Tietze and distance functions give 5°/M7 and 6°/M6), and a Cantor set is a member. Only the

## 3. One space with a family: 121/004-017, 121/049-052, 151/45, and the figures of 156-4

**121/004-010** (a9e0641820ca2be57's reading; the lines checked): a Boolean algebra F of subsets of one space X (a)
complements, b), b′) unions and intersections), c) closures, d) the interior of A relative to Ā is dense in Ā, e) no
infinite chain of closed members each nowhere dense in the previous, f) finitely many connected components for locally
closed members, each a member. **This is the only system in cotes 120-121 with complements and finiteness of components**,
the two ingredients of o-minimality, but on one space only: there are no products and no projections.

**The existence theorem (121/015-017).** For F with a)-e) and finitely many members Φ, there is a coarsest F-admissible
cellular decomposition in which the members of Φ are unions of leaves; the construction starts from U = X ∖ ∪_{Y∈Φ} Ẏ, the
complement of the union of the boundaries (015, checked). **That is the first open stage I proposed as the correction of
121/049** (where he wrote X^[1] = ∪ T̊_α). So 049's claim is proved on 015, for finitely many members of a Boolean family;
049 extends it to locally finite families in the named contexts and stops. Cor 2 of 016: the admissible decompositions have
suprema of finite sets for refinement, that is, coarsest common refinements (checked). The Remarque of 017 does the same with
connected leaves. My SA20 hunch H20-121_049-073_153_155-3 (the coarsest partition of 049 exists in the tame contexts) is
therefore proved in the Boolean setting of 121/004-017; what remains is the manifold (regular) version, where 121/017-018
needs its regularity axiom and has a gap (a9e0641820ca2be57).

**The finite quotient (121/013-014).** "Les décompositions cellulaires (finies) de X correspondent aux espaces quotients finis
de X qui sont présobres, et tels que X → X/R soit ouverte" (014, checked): a finite stratification is an open map onto a
finite T₀ space, with incidence as specialization.

## 4. The intrinsic systems of cote 156 against 120-121

**4.1 "Contrée modérée" (156-4/70) and the hunch H2.** The page: "deux subdivisions F′, F″ de F sont comparables … si
elles admettent une subdivision commune"; "la contrée est modérée si deux subdivisions d'une même F sont toujours
comparables"; margin: "On sera amené à renforcer encore cette condition". H2 reads this as the Hauptvermutung (Shiota's
theorem in definable settings; Milnor's counterexample in general).
- **In the topological model it fails, more simply than by Milnor.** Take F the closed disc, F′ a subdivision by a
  diameter, F″ one by a topological arc A between the same end points that crosses the diameter infinitely often near
  its middle. Both are figures. A common subdivision would have each point of A ∩ diameter in a stratum inside
  A ∩ diameter, a totally disconnected set, so as a point stratum; these accumulate, against b) (local finiteness).
- **In o-minimal models it holds without any triangulation theorem:** finitely many definable sets have a common definable
  stratification with the frontier condition and connected manifold strata of increasing dimension (van den Dries, Tame
  topology and o-minimal structures, 1998, ch. 4; locator to be checked), and that is a common subdivision.
- **In the Boolean setting of 121/004-017 it is a theorem:** Cor 2 of 121/016 gives coarsest common refinements of
  admissible decompositions (with connected leaves, 017).
- **So H2 is sharpened, and its identification refuted:** "modérée" on 156-4/70 is the common-refinement property of tame
  geometry, not the Hauptvermutung. The Hauptvermutung (uniqueness of the PL structure) enters only where subdivisions must
  be triangulations and compared as such, as in the extension axioms At 14 of 156-6 (H6-2), or in the strengthening the
  margin announces. This also corrects H5-4's second clause ("comparability of subdivisions is the Hauptvermutung"): both
  of its clauses are common-refinement statements, existence (156-4/70) and a coarsest one (156-5/29).
- **On cotes 120-121** there is no comparability or Hauptvermutung axiom: 121/065 asks that every member be triangulable and
  that triangulations of a closed part extend; 120/025 leaves generic triviality "à prouver".

**4.2 The Inf for ≪ (156-5/29).** "Cette propriété d'existence d'un Inf pour la relation ≪ s'apparente à une propriété de
'modération'" (checked). In the Boolean setting of 121 this Inf is Cor 2 of 016 (for admissible decompositions; with
connected leaves by 017). 156-4/70 expects an inf of two subdivisions only "parfois". The two statements are compatible: the
coarsest decomposition of 121 need not have manifold leaves (it can keep a cone point inside a two-dimensional leaf: the
boundary of a solid double cone), so it need not be a figure in the sense of 156-4 d). Whether a coarsest figure exists is
the regular question of 121/017-018, which has its regularity axiom and a gap (a9e0641820ca2be57's reading).

**4.3 Intersections and relative position (156-3/20).** "Soit X, Y deux parties fermées modérées, en pos. rel. modérée.
Alors X ∩ Y est une partie fermée modérée …" (checked). In 1986 tameness on a tronçon is a property of pairs as well as of
parts: two tame parts need not be in tame relative position, and a part need not have a tame closure (his margin: A =
{0, 1/2, 1/3, …} in [0, 1]). Cote 120 derives intersections for all pairs (M1, M2, M4); 121/070 asserts them without
proof. **So the coordinate systems exclude by fiat what 156-3 allows**, non-mutually-tame pairs, which occur on non-total
tronçons; on a totally ordered tronçon the tame parts are the finite unions of points and open intervals (the 156-3 reading),
the o-minimal sets, and all pairs are in tame relative position.

**4.4 Finite quotients and maquettes.** 121/014's correspondence (finite cellular decompositions = finite T₀ quotients with
open quotient map) is the spatial origin of the 1986 maquettes and of the Alexandrov picture of ateliers (SA21 H9, H5-1,
H6-1; not re-checked on the 156 pages): 156 takes as primitive the finite T₀ space that 121 obtains as a quotient.

**4.5 Faithful ateliers (156-9/4) and the hunch H9-3.** The margin: "À vérifier que la bonne notion d'atelier modéré doit
bien impliquer qu'il est, non seulement spatial mais encore, fidèle" (checked). Faithfulness (156-8/39-40, per the 156-8
reading): X |∘| Y ⇔ the open shadows of X and Y are disjoint. **In every model built from a space of the 120-121 kind it
holds:** multistrates are closed strata of decompositions of X, lieux are points, the open shadow of X is its open stratum
as a set of points, and X |∘| Y means that the open strata are disjoint. In the Boolean setting of 121/004 the supports are
members of F, a Boolean algebra. **So H9-3 holds for the spatial tame models of 120-121**; the non-faithful examples (cords,
ficelles) are non-spatial. Whether the abstract axioms of a tame atelier imply faithfulness is a question about 156 alone;
cotes 120-121 do not address it.

## 5. What each system allows or excludes, in one paragraph each

- **Cote 120 (M0-M11):** coordinated families on all Rⁿ with images, inverse images, unions, zero loci, extension, a field
  R₀; compact members; locally moderate spaces. It allows wild compact sets (no finiteness), and it has no dimension theory.
- **121/054-063:** the same in categorical form, with the precise reconstruction (Ⓐ, Ⓑ) and gluing (5°, 6°); no finiteness.
- **121/065:** adds triangulability with relative extension; this excludes Cantor sets and forces finiteness in dimension one
  for compact members (TC08). It is the only coordinate system that does.
- **121/070-073 (C_*):** images and products only; it allows families not closed under intersection (the countermodels of my
  SA20 reading), which 120 excludes by M2.
- **121/004-017 (one space):** Boolean, with closure, density, finite chains and finitely many components; it proves the
  canonical decompositions and their common refinements; it allows no change of space (no products, no maps).
- **156 (1986):** no points and no coordinates; tame lines are o-minimal (156-3); "modérée" is the existence of common
  subdivisions (156-4/70); ateliers allow non-spatial models (cords, pseudo-discs, maquettes) that no 120-121 system has.
