## 0. What the block is

**The pages.** These are his manuscript pages 10 to 17 of "Espaces stratifiés (étude par voisinages coniques)" (déc.
81-janv. 82), with their unnumbered versos 034, 036, 038, 040 and 042. The order of writing is 029 → 031 → 030 → 032. The
§7 sheet 030 was scanned before 031, but 031 (sheet 10) continues 029 and 032 (sheet 11) continues 030. Michel Hua's
project notes the same interchange.

**What he does, in order:**
1. **031:** he finishes the cylindrical notions and adds the intersection axiom (0), which he says he had forgotten.
2. **§7 (030, 032-035):** he cuts a stratified space open along its minimal strata (the data (18)). He glues it back by
   (19) and proves the **Théorème** that the two kinds of system are equivalent.
3. **§8 (035-036):** he defines a **conical structure** by recursion on card I.
4. **037-038:** three remarks follow (existence, independence of the filtration, cylindrical stratifications), and §9
   is announced.
5. **039-042:** the **présentation standart** of an equisingular stratification. This is a functor Σ on the flags of I
   with three conditions; it is repackaged as an internal category, which is in fact an ordered space.
6. **043:** the pairs of flags, and the simultaneous mapping cones. These he postpones ("On reviendra sur cette
   construction plus tard"), and §10 restarts with M = (Top).

### 036 (verso of 13)

- **The definition, by recursion on card I:**
  - I = ∅: the empty structure.
  - I ≠ ∅: I_0 is the set of minimal elements, and a conical datum is ⓐ a strict conical neighbourhood T̄_{I_0} of
    X_{I_0}, transversal to the (B_α, ℰ_α) and conically transversal to the X_i. This gives the structures (19), in
    particular:
    - (22): X^*, its stratification (X_i^*)_{i∈I^*}, and the transverse family ((B_α, ℰ_α), (Ẋ_β, ℰ_β));
    - (23): the minimal strata with their induced collars.
  - ⓑ a conical structure on (22).
- **Checked (R150c-16).** The recursion is well founded, (22) is a system of the same type (with B_α^* for B_α;
  K150c-08), and (23) needs nothing because the X_β are minimal.
- **What the recursion is.** Iterated, it cuts out every stratum in turn, and each cut adds its boundaries Ẋ_β to the
  collared family. The end state is X cut open into its strata, each with transversal collared boundary pieces.
  - This is the folder's "déploiement", the hunch H150b-1 of the previous reading, now on the page.
  - Michel Hua's project calls it, "à la topologie près, la récurrence des données de contrôle de Mather".

### 037 (manuscript page 14): Remarques

1. **Existence:** "Avec des hypothèses de 'non sauvagerie' … il doit être vrai qu'il existe une structure cônique."
   - In the equisingular case there should be an equisingular one, and "il suffit de savoir construire un tube
     équisingulier autour des X_{I_0}".
   - This is a conjecture with its reduction: by the recursion of §8, the tube around the minimal strata is the only
     step.
   - For Whitney stratifications it is the theory of control data (J. Mather, "Notes on topological stability", 1970,
     Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 49 (2012), 475-506, §§7-8; locator to be checked). His commuting collar and cone
     homotheties of 025 c) are Mather's commutation relations (H150c-1). The germ version needs compactness
     (SA10-K150b-11).
2. **Independence of the filtration:** any increasing chain I_0 ⊂ I_1 ⊂ ⋯ ⊂ I_{n-1} ⊂ I_{n|N} = I of parts, whose
   differences consist of minimal elements, defines a recursion. "on peut montrer que cette notion ne dépend pas en fait,
   essentiellement, de la filtration choisie."
   - The page has I_{n-1} where the first pass read "I_n ⋯".
   - **Re-proved, up to germs (R150c-17).** Two filtrations differ by exchanges of incomparable elements. When both
     are minimal their current strata are disjoint, so their cones can be chosen disjoint, and the order of cutting
     does not matter.
3. **The B-family as a stratification:** "Au lieu d'une famille des (B_α, ℰ_α) … une stratification cylindrique (le cas
   envisagé est celui où l'ens ordonné qui {décrit|indexe} la stratification cylindrique est 𝒫(Λ)°." Checked
   (R150c-18): the B_J = ∩_{α∈J} B_α.

### 039 (manuscript page 15): "présentation standart"

- **The setting:** a "catégorie topologique modérée" M, "en un sens non précisé", Top included. The presentation is a
  contravariant functor Σ: Drap(I) → M, (i_0, …, i_n) ↦ Σ_{i_0…i_n}, with:
  - (1) for d an initial segment of d', Σ_{d'} → Σ_d is **fibrant**\*. "il suffit de le supposer pour" deleting the last
    vertex; checked (R150c-21), for Hurewicz or Serre fibrations, with properness for bundles.
  - (2) deleting a vertex other than the last, Σ_{i_0…i_n} → Σ_{i_0…î_p…i_n} (p ≠ n), is a **plongement-bord**, "i.e.
    plongement [bord] cylindrique"; for p = n it is fibrant.
- **His objects.** By (2) the Σ_j are spaces with boundary, and Σ_{ij} → Σ_j is a face. So Σ_j is the stratum X_j° cut
  open along the lower strata, and Σ_{ij} → Σ_i is the bundle of links of X_i in X_j. This is the convention that
  matters for EXIT_TOWER (§3).
- **The margin \*:** "Cela est trop faible, \unsure{si on veut} l'équisingularité — il faut supposer que pour d fixé, et
  pour d' {⊃|=} d ∐ δ variable (… δ un drapeau quelconque de l'ens I' des i ∈ I fort^t supérieurs à max i_p) la
  stratification qu'ils forment au dessus de Σ_{d'} est loc. triviale."
  - The native scan has Σ_{d'}; the fixed base Σ_d is meant (R150c-22, K150c-10).
  - This is the whole-family equisingularity of 018-019. EXIT_TOWER A.12 proves it for M̄_{g,n} over pinned strata
    (R150c-46).
- **The Cor.:** "Soit d ↪ d' tel que d et d' aient = objt maximal, alors Σ_{d'} → Σ_d est un plongement." It is checked,
  as a composite of embeddings given by (2) (R150c-20).
- **The left margin** announces cylindrical structures on the faces and conditions stronger than (2).
