## 0. What the block is

The block is his manuscript pages 3 (end) to 9, then the unnumbered §7 page (scan 030), of "Espaces stratifiés (étude
par voisinages coniques)", 1981-82. Its frame is fixed on 012-016:
- a **conical neighbourhood** ("voisinage conique absolu") of Y is a space T̄ ⊃ Y with an action of the monoid I = [0,1]
  such that T̄ is the cone C(f) of f = 0·(−): Ṫ → Y (012 (1)-(4)); today this is the mapping cylinder of f;
- a **partie bord** is a closed part with a cylindrical neighbourhood T̄ ≃ Y × I, a collar (014 (9));
- X^* = X ∖ T̊, and T̄ is **strict** when Ṫ is a partie bord of X^* (015);
- a **conical subspace** T̄' ⊂ T̄ is I-stable and conical, so T̄' = I·Ṫ' ∪ Y' with Y' ⊃ 0·Ṫ', and it is **pure** when
  T̄' ∩ Y = 0·Ṫ' (016 (12) and margin).

On these pages he builds four things:
1. compatibility, transversality and equisingularity of a neighbourhood with a family of closed parts (017-020);
2. the germ of a conical neighbourhood and the theorem that it is the same as a "découpage cônique", the space cut open
   along Y with its collared boundary and the map onto Y (022-024);
3. the compatibility of a collar with a cone, and families of transversal collars with E_J ≃ B_J × I^J (024-028);
4. precylindrical stratifications, whose local structure is a corner of a cube, and the intersection axiom (028-030).

Read together, this is the **deployment** of the folder's second title ("ou : déploiement des espaces stratifiés"):
cutting a space open along a stratum replaces the stratum by the boundary of a collar (023), and after all strata are
deployed one has a space whose closed strata are faces with transversal collars meeting in cube corners (028-029),
that is a manifold with corners in the sense of Douady and Jänich (A. Douady, "Variétés à bord anguleux et voisinages
tubulaires", Séminaire Henri Cartan 14 (1961/62), exp. 1; K. Jänich, "On the classification of O(n)-manifolds", Math.
Ann. 176 (1968), 53-76; locators to be checked). This reading is mine; he does not say it in these words on these
pages, but the theorem of 023 and the definitions of 027-029 say it in substance.

## 1. Page by page

### 017 (end of manuscript page 3, and the cylindrical notions)

- **(13), strict compatibility:** an exterior collar E ≃ Ṫ × [0,1] of Ṫ in X^* in which X'^* ∩ E = X'_0 × [0,1]. This
  ends the definition of "strictement compatible" begun on 016.
- **Cylindrical compatibility:** for a strict partie bord B with collar E ≃ B × [0,1], a closed X' is compatible with E
  if X' ∩ E = B' × [0,1] (then B' = X' ∩ B). The margin draws the consequence that B ∩ X' is a partie bord of X'
  (checked, R150b-02). A second margin proposes "transversale" for this notion.
- **The comparison:** the cylindrical notion "n'est pas un cas particulier" of the conical one, "mais est {plus|bien}
  exigeante : correspond au cas où on {lui demandait|… devait} T'∩Y = 0.Ṫ'". **Checked (R150b-01):** a collar is a
  conical neighbourhood whose base map is a homeomorphism, and cylindrical compatibility is conical compatibility plus
  purity. The struck formula below records the weaker conical possibility X'∩E ≃ B'×[0,1] ∪ B×{0}.
- **Families:** "On définit de = la compatibilité pour une famille ~~finie~~ … parties (X_i)": one collar compatible with
  each X_i.

### 018 (manuscript page 4)

- **Families and strict compatibility.** A partie bord is compatible with a family (X_i) if one cylindrical
  neighbourhood is compatible with each X_i. A strict conical T̄ is strictly compatible with (X_i) if it is compatible
  with each X_i, "(X_i∩T = I.X_i∩Ṫ −)", and Ṫ is a partie bord of X^* compatible with the X_i^*. So X_i ∩ Ṫ is a partie
  bord of X_i^* (by 017).
- **The margin defines transversality as purity:** "0.Ẋ_i = X_i ∩ Y, i.e. X_i = \overline{X_i∖X_i∩Y}". The equivalence
  holds when the closure of ]0,1]·Ẋ_i is [0,1]·Ẋ_i, for instance Ṫ compact (R150b-03).
- **④ Equisingularity:** T̄ is equisingular if Ṫ → Y is locally trivial. For a family T̄_i ⊂ T̄ he first asks that the
  pieces be conical and each Ṫ_i → Y locally trivial, "— et même mieux", then (019) that the family of the Ṫ_i ⊂ Ṫ be
  locally trivial over Y.
  - The strengthening is needed: two sections of R × S¹ crossing over 0 are separately trivial, but not as a family
    (R150b-04).
  - The letter in "les {Y_i} sont coniques" is a Y in the ink (decided today, R150b-29). The sense requires the T̄_i,
    so this is his slip (SA10-K150b-03).
- **The arrow:** "Ṫ − Y" in l.33 has no head (kept as \unsure{-|→}).

### 019

- **The definition ends:** "la famille des Ṫ_i⊂Ṫ au dessus de Y est ~~globalement~~ loc. triviale". "globalement" is
  struck; the line cuts the letters (decided today, R150b-30).
- **The definition is extended** to a family (X_i) of X: T̄ is equisingular for (X_i) if it is so for (X_i ∩ T ⊂ T).
- **(14), the alternative:** if every X_i ∩ Y is open and closed in Y and Y is connected, then X_i ∩ T̄ = ∅ or X_i ⊃ Y.
  **Re-proved (R150b-05):** local triviality makes 0·Ṫ_i clopen. The clopen hypothesis on X_i ∩ Y is used only for the
  components without link (extra base points), so for pure X_i it is automatic.
  - The "f ?" that the first pass read before "que" is the "(X_i)" of the margin note (second pass, 17 Nov), so the
    implication carries no query.
- **The margin:** for T̄ equisingular, λT̄ (λ ∈ ]0,1[) is strictly equisingular. **Checked (R150b-06):** the shell
  between λṪ and Ṫ is Ṫ × [λ,1], an exterior collar compatible with the X_i.
- **Global equisingularity:** X, (X_i) is "(globalement) équisingulier le long de Y" if some conical neighbourhood is.
  The letter of that neighbourhood carries a dot in the ink ({Ṫ|T̄|T}); a neighbourhood is meant.

### 020 (manuscript page 5): the first reconstruction

- **The Proposition:** systems (X, Y, (X_i), T) with T conical "compatible avec (X_i)", isomorphisms only, are
  equivalent to systems (X^*, Ẋ, (X_i^*), Y, Ẋ → Y).
- **As stated it is false (R150b-07, SA10-K150b-01).** In X = [0,1] with Y = {0}, the parts X_1 = ∅ and X_1 = {0} are
  both compatible and give the same tuple. This was first seen by the first pass's note R21a (edition matter @820,
  confirmed).
- **It holds for X_i transversal in the sense of the 018 margin (pure),** or with Y_i = X_i ∩ Y added to the tuple. The
  later reconstructions keep Y' separately (023-024).
- **His slip:** the second category calls Ẋ, X_i^* "parties fermées de X"; X^* is meant (SA10-K150b-02).
- **The two characterizations that follow** (strict transversality: Ẋ is a partie bord of X^* compatible with the
  X_i^*; equisingularity: Ẋ → Y with the (Ẋ_i) is a simultaneous locally trivial fibration) restate the definitions.
  The diagram writes (Ẋ_i) ∪-included in Ẋ (corrected on 17 Nov).

### 021 (cancelled sheet)

- **The aim:** "Pour faire des récurrences dans nos constructions, on suppose donné de plus un sous-espace bord Ẋ de
  X, strictement compatible avec la famille des X_i". The first pass read "faire intervenir"; "des récurrences" was
  confirmed today on the native image (R150b-33) and is Hua's reading too.
- **The notion:** he wants the "cônification (comp.|resp. avec Ẋ) (de la stratification)", defined by induction:
  - for I = ∅ it is empty;
  - for I = {i} it is a strict conical neighbourhood of Y compatible with Ẋ, with T̈ = Ẋ ∩ Ṫ a partie bord of Ṫ,
    0·T̈ = Ẋ ∩ Y = Ẏ ("a priori ⊂"), and Ṫ → Y fibred "dans un sens à préciser".
- **What it shows:** this is the first statement in the folder that the boundary-relative notion exists for the
  induction carried out in §7 (030, "le pas de récurrence").

### 022 (manuscript page 6): germs

- **⑤ Germs:** two conical neighbourhoods T, T' of Y are equivalent if λT = λ'T' with their conical structures.
  - **Re-proved (R150b-08):** this is an equivalence relation. The margin "donc pour ε∈]0,1[, ελT = ελ'T'" is the step
    that transitivity needs.
  - **Checked (R150b-09):** compatibility with a germ does not depend on the representative.
  - The T, T' are plain on this page (corrected on 17 Nov).
- **Cylindrical germs:** equivalence preserves cylindricity, and a cylindrical germ exists only if Y is a partie bord
  ("Y borde X").
- **The construction:** given (X̃^*, Ṫ) with a germ of collar and a map Ṫ → Y, the pushout X = X̃^* ⨿_Ṫ Y carries a germ
  of conical neighbourhood, "isotrivial ssi Ṫ→Y est fibrant." (asserted, no query). **Checked (R150b-10):** the image of
  the collar is the cone C(f) of 012.
- **Reading:** the sign after "s'il" (l.18) is an insertion arrow, not "est" (decided today, R150b-32, SA10-K150b-12).

### 023: the Theorem of the découpage cônique

- **The Theorem:** systems a) (X̃^*, Ẋ, ℰ, Y, f) and b) (X, Y, 𝒯) are equivalent by the natural functor, X = X̃^*⨿_ẊY,
  with isomorphisms only ("pour simplifier !").
- **Re-proved (R150b-11).** The inverse functor is the **deployment of X along Y**: X̃^* = (X ∖ Y) glued to Ṫ × [0,1]
  along Ṫ × ]0,1] ≅ T̄ ∖ Y, with boundary Ẋ = Ṫ × {0} and f = 0·(−). It is well defined because 012 c) gives T̄ the
  quotient topology of C(f).
- **The margin:** "(X̃^*,Ẋ) joue le rôle d'un « découpage cônique » de X suivant (Y,𝒯)". In the conical setting this is the
  real blow-up of X along Y: Y is replaced by its link bundle Ṫ, which becomes a collared boundary.
- **The NB on subsets** is checked (R150b-12). It feeds the Corollary on the next page.
- **Literature.** The germ classification is the strict, cone-like case of the neighbourhood-germ theory of F. Quinn
  ("Homotopically stratified sets", J. Amer. Math. Soc. 1 (1988), 441-499) and of B. Hughes, C. Taylor, S. Weinberger
  and B. Williams ("Neighborhoods in stratified spaces with two strata", Topology 39 (2000), 873-919). There the
  mapping-cylinder neighbourhood is replaced by the holink fibration. Locators to be checked.

### 024 (manuscript page 7)

- **The Corollary:** closed parts X' compatible with 𝒯 correspond to couples (X̃' ⊂ X̃, Y' ⊂ Y) with X̃' cylindrical for
  ℰ and f(Ẋ') ⊂ Y'. Then X' = Im(X̃' par X̃ → X) ∪ Y' ("par", not a hook arrow), and X' is "cônique strict" iff
  Y' = Im(Ẋ' → Y), which is purity.
- **Re-proved (R150b-13).** The correspondence keeps the extra base points in Y'. That is why the later reconstructions
  carry Y' (compare SA10-K150b-01).
- **Compatibility of a collar with a cone:** let X_0 be a bordant part with a germ of collar ℰ, and 𝒯 a conical germ
  around Y. They are compatible if there are representatives E ≃ X_0 × I and T such that a) T̄∩E, Ṫ∩E and Y∩E are
  cylindrical parts of E. The margin proposes "transversal" for this.

### 025

- **b) and c):** b) E∩T, Ė∩T and X_0∩T are conical subspaces of T. c) The homotheties λ_E and λ_T commute in T∩E.
  Hence λ_T acts on T∩E ≃ T_0 × I as λ_{T_0} × id_I.
- **The Condition de compatibilité:** "Donc les conditions a) b) c) équivalent à celle-ci": an identification
  T∩E ≃ T_0 × I, compatible with E ≃ X_0 × I, under which the conical structure of T is the product by I of a conical
  structure of T_0 over Y_0.
- **Re-proved (R150b-14), with a precision:**
  - a map commuting with every collar contraction has the form (x, s) ↦ (g(x), s·h(x));
  - b), the stability of Ė∩T = T_0 × {1} under the cone, is what forces h = 1;
  - without b), the diagonal action λ_{T_0} × λ would also commute.
- **Two slips as written:** "Y∩E ≃ Y_0∩E" at the top of the page (Y_0 × I meant), and the uncertain verb before "sur
  T_E sur Y_E" ({s'identifie|induite|standarde}). Neither changes the Condition.

### 026 (manuscript page 8)

- **The NB:** rescaled λ_0E and λ'_0T stay compatible, and for two cylindrical structures the condition is symmetric
  (checked, R150b-15).
- **The Prop.:** if (X, Y, 𝒯) is described by (X̃, Ẋ, ℰ_Ẋ, Y, p), the collars (X_0, ℰ) compatible with 𝒯 correspond to
  systems (X̃_0, ℰ̃_{X̃_0}, Y_0, ℰ_{Y_0}) with:
  a) ℰ̃_{X̃_0} compatible with ℰ_Ẋ;
  b) p compatible: Ẋ_0 = p^{-1}(Y_0), and p is p_0 × id_I on representative collars.
- **Checked one way (R150b-16):** the direction (⇒) follows from the product Condition. The converse is not checked.
- **Notation:** the label of the arrow is p (settled on 17 Nov). The inverse image is written with a β-like letter

### 027 (end of the Prop., and transversal families of collars)

- **The end of the Prop.:** "p|Ẋ_0×I … s'identifiant à p_0×id_I, où p_0: X_0 → Y_0". Ẋ_0 is meant (SA10-K150b-05).
- **Transversality of a family (B_i, E_{B_i}) of collars:** for every J ⊂ I, "I fini", E_J = ⋂_{i∈J} E_{B_i} is
  B_J × I^J, and λ_{E_j} acts on the j-th coordinate.
  - The margin says the identification is canonical; it is (R150b-17): the projection is the zero element of I^J.
  - These are the compatible collars of a manifold with corners. G. Laures, "On cobordism of manifolds with
    corners", Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 352 (2000), 5667-5688, proves their existence for ⟨n⟩-manifolds (locator to be
    checked).
  - "Cette relation de transversalité passe aux germes."
- **Transversality of the family with a cone T:** E_J ∩ T ≃ T̄_J × I^J, with a cup sign under this row, then
  Ė_J ∩ Ṫ ≃ Ṫ_J × I^J and E_J ∩ Y ≃ Y_J × I^J; the conical structure is the product by I^J of one on (T̄_J, Y_J) (028).
  - The dot on E in the second row is in the ink (decided today, R150b-31).
  - With it the row does not fit the product structure: Ė_J lies over the outer corner of I^J. The row meant is
    E_J ∩ Ṫ ≃ Ṫ_J × I^J (R150b-18, SA10-K150b-04).
- **The heading:** "~~finie~~" is struck there (Hua reads it live). Either way the condition "pour tout J⊂I, I fini"
  makes the family finite.

### 028 (manuscript page 9 = EXIT_TOWER's folio 9)

- **⑥ Precylindrical stratifications** of type I (I a finite ordered set; X_i an increasing family of closed parts):
  a) for j' ↔ j, j a successor of j', X_{j'} ↪ X_j is bordant;
  b) for fixed j, the predecessors' X_{j'} are transversal bordant parts of X_j (the margin: "munis des voisinages
     cylindriques transversaux");
  c) for i ≤ j, the interval I_{i,j} is the poset of subsets of a set S, the predecessors of j in I_{i,j}.
- **The relation signs** in a) and c) are his double arrows j' ↔ j, i ↔ j (settled on 17 Nov).
- **The definition of s_k** reads "j ≥ s_k", with s_k on both sides. j ≥ k is meant (R150b-20, SA10-K150b-06).
- **The gloss** "i.e. des éléments {maximaux|minimaux} de I_{i,j}∖{j}" is open on the ink. Hua reads "minimaux", which
  would contradict "prédécesseurs".
- **The NB** "il suffit d'exiger dans b) pour j maximal" holds given c) and the intersection axiom (R150b-19).
- **The margin:** "On veut de plus que les Inf dans I_{i,j} correspondent à l'intersection {des X_k|dans X_j}… cf. (0)
  ci-dessous" announces the axiom of 030-031.
