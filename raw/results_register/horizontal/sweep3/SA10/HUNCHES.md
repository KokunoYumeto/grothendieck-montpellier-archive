## H5. Cote 151 is the topological side of La Longue Marche's Teichmüller tower

**The hunch:**
- 151/44 asks for the homotopy type of the Teichmüller moduli spaces and their compactifications from the fundamental groupoids of the K(π,1) strata and their tubular neighbourhoods.
- That is the gluing of the Teichmüller groupoids along the boundary of M̄_{g,n}, which La Longue Marche (1981) treats algebraically.
