## STUDY

Cote 93 (scans 47-91, 221-236) and cote 161-5, read in full by reader R1 (Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max), 19 Nov 2026: the Pro text (GPT-6 Astra, ChatGPT Pro mode) page by page, the Codex-web folders compared word by word, scans of 93 viewed where a reading mattered. Full reading: `cote93_crystals.md`.

**The Barsotti-Tate programme (93/47-54; SA04-1, SA04-3).** 47-48 are a typed draft of ch. V of the Montréal notes (the crystalline comparison for abelian schemes, deformations, Serre-Tate after Messing). The typed "Rabiots" (49, 51) and "Sur un théorème de Tate" (53-54) pose Problems 4-6, the "foncteur mystérieux" from filtered F-crystals to Galois representations. They guess that it is fully faithful with a quasi-inverse on a subcategory of Hodge-Tate modules, and that Tate's theorem holds without characteristic 0. All of this was later proved: Fontaine (1982), Colmez-Fontaine (2000), Kisin (2006), de Jong (1998). The Proposition of 49 (isogroups are insensitive to nilpotent thickenings) agrees with Drinfeld's rigidity. Two reused typed leaves (50, 52) carry exercises on pseudo-resolvents and Cohen's factorization (SA04-2, decided in sweep 2).

**Euler characteristics (93/55-66; SA04-4).** His axioms (a)-(h) for χ and χ_! were re-derived. The theorem of 66 lives on E_∞, the locally compact spaces whose one-point compactification is locally conical. He leaves two questions there: whether (g) holds without the restriction to relative polyhedra, and whether (a)-(e) characterize χ and χ_! on all of E_∞. **Both have affirmative answers:**
- compact members of C_∞ are ANRs (Hanner), hence of finite CW homotopy type (West);
- cutting at infinity reduces the non-compact case to the compact one;
- the Leray and Serre spectral sequences then give the product formulas;
- homotopy models give uniqueness.

The Pro notes left the first open (EC37) and the second conditional (EC39); the condition falls away, since the doubtful letter in the definition of C_n does not change the class. His doubt about compact triangulability is right from dimension 4 on (Freedman-Casson; Manolescu). Seven readings were decided on the scans, among them the swapped arrows of 56 (d) and the cancelled E_3 bracket of 62.

**The Schwarzian and the écarts (93/72-84; SA04-7, -8).** G = −2(S(g) − S(f)) dt² and G = 12 G_0 hold. The ink of 73 has the wrong test value dt²/(2t); it is not a misreading, and the right value is dt²/(4t²). The n-differential formulas of 80/82 check, up to two slips. An open point: his "faux?" on the uniqueness of G_0 in characteristic p for operators of order above 3.

**Projective bundles and opers (93/86-91; SA04-9).** The relation c₁₂ = −2c₂₃ and the canonical triple (g, L, c_Ω) with L ≅ P¹(ω) ⊗ ω⁻¹ (the Atiyah extension) check. This is the Borel reduction of the canonical PGL₂-oper. It needs 2 invertible, as he says, and continues on 93/135 (SA01).

**Periods and the torsor-extension construction (93/221-236; SA04-11 to -13).** These pages hold:
- Picard-Fuchs expressions and periods ∫_a^b ω;
- the Gauss-Manin connection on f_*(ZΩ¹)/df_*O, with a correct Stein restriction;
- differential operators with constant coefficients on stratified modules (characteristic 0);
- a general device on 234-236: a torsor under an abelian sheaf, with affine maps into Hom•, gives a morphism in the derived category through the extension of Z by the torsor.

Read additively, the device is sound: it compares de Rham complexes of local liftings without a global lifting, the step 48 postulates.

**Cote 161-5 (SA04-14 to -16).** Scans 3-22 are printed text by Serre: the Driebergen paper of 1967 and the course résumé of 1968-69. His marginal notes cite Sen (1973). His notes (23-29) put the mysterious functor in Tannakian form, with torsors for the algebraic envelope G. They ask two questions:
- whether the crystalline data extend as ⊗-functors to the category generated by Barsotti-Tate representations: yes, by Fontaine's D_cris;
- whether all extensions of a section over π are conjugate: no, already in rank one. The kernel of H¹(Γ, G_m) → H¹(π, G_m) maps onto Z by the valuation (the slopes), as Michel Hua's project found; my re-proof confirms it.

## HUNCHES

- **H93-1 (Euler characteristics: settled here; a link).** The theorem of 93/066 holds on E_∞ without either of his two restrictions. Grounds: proofs SA04-R93-11, -12. Further, his χ_! on E_∞ should agree with the o-minimal Euler characteristic on locally closed definable sets, whose one-point compactifications are locally conical by definable triangulation (L. van den Dries, *Tame topology and o-minimal structures*, LMS Lecture Note Series 248, CUP 1998, ch. 4 and 8, locator to be checked). That would make 93/55-66 the first written form of the tame programme of cote 156 (SA21). It sharpens the SA21 hunches on χ in tame topology; I did not read H1-H12 of SA21.
- **H93-2 (characteristic p Schwarzian).** His margin on 93/074, "faux? pour un op. diff. d'ordre ≤ 3", is likely right in the other direction. In characteristic p, cocycles invariant under PGL₂ and built from Hasse derivatives of unbounded order may not all be multiples of G_0, because D^{(p^k)} gives new invariants of the étale coordinate. Grounds: in characteristic p the étale maps f and f + h^p (h a function) share all Hasse derivatives below order p. Untested.
- **H93-3 (the cancelled first value on 93/073).** The heavily cancelled value before "1/(2t)" may be the correct 1/(4t²). That would make the printed value a late slip. This is a reading question for the edition, at 400-600 dpi.
- **H93-4 (the torsor-extension device).** The construction of 93/234-236 is the device that Deligne-Illusie (1987) use for liftings mod p². His version is general: any torsor under an abelian sheaf with an affine map into Z⁰Hom•. The other parts of cote 93 (135-220, SA01) may use it for the canonical projective connection over a base, which would join SA04-9 and SA04-13.
- **H1615-1 (the slope as the missing condition).** The non-uniqueness on 161-5/029 is exactly the slope (Newton) data, and the Néron-model computation at the foot of the page shows he was computing it. The natural completion of his torsor description replaces "all extensions conjugate" by "conjugate once the slopes (equivalently weak admissibility) are fixed". This would make 161-5/23-29 a Tannakian precursor of Colmez-Fontaine. It needs a careful reading of 25-29 on the scans before it can be stated.

## PACKAGES

- **P93-1: χ and χ_! on locally conical spaces.**
  - *Question:* write the complete theorem of 93/066 on E_∞, with both of his "j'ignore" questions resolved, and determine whether his χ_! is the o-minimal Euler characteristic on definable sets.
  - *Why:* it completes his own axiomatics and connects cote 93 to the tame programme of cote 156.
  - *Rests on:* Hanner (1951), West (1977), the Leray and Serre spectral sequences, van den Dries (1998).
  - *Strongest reduction:* proofs SA04-R93-11, -12 in this reading, and Pro EC21, EC27-EC39.
  - *Material:* 93/055-066 (Pro text with the corrections SA04-K93-01 to -08), Pro notes EC01-EC46.
- **P93-2: the projective invariant in characteristic p.**
  - *Question:* over F_p, classify the differential operators G(f, g) in Hasse derivatives of any order, with values in ω^{⊗2}, satisfying the cocycle condition b), PGL₂-invariance c) and non-vanishing. Is every such G equal to c·G_0 with c ∈ F_p^*, as his Remark 3 on 93/074 claims, or does the uniqueness hold only up to order 3, as his margin "faux?" suggests?
  - *Why:* it decides his own doubt, and it bears on projective structures and opers in characteristic p (SA04-9, SA01).
  - *Rests on:* 93/072-074, the uniqueness of the Schwarzian in characteristic 0 (Ovsienko-Tabachnikov 2005, ch. 1, locator to be checked), and p-curvature of opers (Y. Ihara; S. Mochizuki, *Foundations of p-adic Teichmüller theory*, AMS/IP 1999, locator to be checked).
  - *Strongest reduction:* his statement for order ≤ 3 and SA04-R93-15.
  - *Material:* 93/072-084.
- **P1615-1: the Tannakian mysterious functor of 161-5/23-29.**
  - *Question:* state his torsor data (ρ, j, Q_L, R_L, the descent and Frobenius data, the extension E of Γ) precisely. Prove that the extensions of α₀ are classified by the slopes, and that fixing them, or imposing weak admissibility, makes the solution unique up to G'_L(L).
  - *Why:* it is the most developed form of the "foncteur mystérieux" in the archive, and it predates Fontaine's period rings.
  - *Rests on:* Fontaine (1982); Colmez-Fontaine (2000); Kisin (2006); the rank-one computation SA04-R1615-03.
  - *Strongest reduction:* rank one, settled.
  - *Material:* 161-5/023-029 after a reading on the scans; 93/049-054.
