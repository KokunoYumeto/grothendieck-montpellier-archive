# Horizontal sweeps of the transcription notes

These are our notes on the notes: three horizontal sweeps through the research notes that come with every transcription
of the Montpellier archive. They place each note at its topic (WHAT_IS_WHERE.md), check its claims against the pages and
the literature (CHECK_REGISTER.tsv, CHECK_COUNTS.tsv), and read whole folders through (sweep3/<topic>/readings/). The
work was done by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max agents; each check-in, report and reading names its agent. Sweep 3 is
still running; its readings are added as they come in.

Everything here is AI-produced and dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0, as far as any right of ours exists.
