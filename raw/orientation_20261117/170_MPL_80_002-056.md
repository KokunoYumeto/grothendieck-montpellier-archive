## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** scans 2-56 of cote 80, "Systèmes de pseudo-droites : notes manuscrites", from 1981 on.
- **Where they sit:** rank 18 of the plan, Book V (Jordan, immersions, pseudolines; tags "forms, strata"): arrangements of pseudolines as maps (cartes) on the real projective plane. Cote 80 goes on in 171_MPL_80_057-073+81_002-037.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** none directly; the ten matched rows (from RESIDUE, PATHS, EXIT_ONE_TYPE, EXIT_TOWER, LM4) came through the tags and concern derivators, paths and the tower. What the pages may open up instead: a classification of pseudoline arrangements by small configurations, and their reconstruction from the 1-skeleton.
- **Our results to use and check:** none on these subjects.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** none listed.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 80: curves that behave like lines in ℝP², their drawing read as a map in Grothendieck's sense. Hua, findings register: **80-crucial-arrangements-theorem** (our scans 023-025): the Théorème of his p. 24 (no II₅, not covered by two pencils, hence III₆ or IV₇), refuted by his project with a triangle plus a further line; **80-lattice-criterion** (our scans 025, 027): the "cas latticiel" criterion, refuted by his project; **80-bigon-free-five-line-dichotomy** (our scans 021, 025, 029), which his Lean card marks false as on the page; **80-skeleton-full-faithfulness** (our scans 040-055; his search found no reference): a bigon-free arrangement is determined, functorially, by its 1-skeleton with the edges grouped into pseudolines. Our intake: all new to us.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. The Théorème of scan 025 with every definition it uses (pencil, bigonal, general position, II₅, III₆, IV₇); then test Hua's counterexample against those definitions, not modern ones.
  2. The same for the lattice criterion and the five-line dichotomy: does a marginal correction or a later page repair them? Give the corrected statements.
  3. On scans 040-055: is full faithfulness stated, proved or conjectured, and for which functor exactly?
  4. Any tie to maps and dessins (the carte structure) or to the immersions of Book V.
