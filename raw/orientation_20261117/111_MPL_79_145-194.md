## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** scans 145-194 of cote 79, "2-polyèdres réguliers triangulés, et modules libres de rang 2 sur les anneaux Λ = ℤ/nℤ et Λ = ℤ" (from 1980).
- **Where they sit:** regular triangulated maps from rank-2 modules: from (ℤ/nℤ)² regular triangulated surfaces (possibly the modular curves X(n)), from ℤ² the Farey triangulation. Cote 79 begins in 51_MPL_79_001-045, 109_MPL_79_046-094, 110_MPL_79_095-144 and ends in 112_MPL_79_195-244, 113_MPL_79_245-297.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38** and **E.10**: the congruence kernels; its level-7 and level-11 compactifications have genera 3 and 26, those of X(7) and X(11).
  - **LM4 F.7** and **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.39**: centralizers of A5 = PSL2(F5) (level 5, the icosahedron).
  - **PUSH_ORDER5_FRAME Itemsfromt 2** (O.20, rigid frames in Γ̂_{0,[5]}): possibly, through level 5.
  - **EXIT_TOWER E.24**: the arithmetic image of the geometric modular groups; possibly, if the leaves treat a Galois action on these maps.
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say:** MPL_72_035-081 (its own follow-up tasks, with confidence and reasons; its primary sources).
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 79 starts from the plane of integer points and makes it draw a surface. Hua, findings register, 79-homothety-distance-three (scans 188-189): by its id a distance-three statement involving homotheties; our digest cuts it, so read it in the results block. His 79-semilocal-diameter-three (scans 020-021, in 51_MPL_79_001-045) bounds by 3 the distance between any two vertices of SP_M over a semi-local ring. Our intake: new to us.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  - Scans 188-189: the homothety statement as written, its proof, and its relation to the diameter bound of scans 020-021.
  - Any computation at levels 5, 7 or 11 (vertex and face counts, genera, automorphism groups), set against LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38.
  - Whether a Galois or Teichmüller action on these maps is discussed (the plan's tags galois and teich).
  - Which follow-up tasks of MPL_72_035-081 these scans address.
