## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** the first 45 scans of cote 43, "Dualité des groupes. Jacobiennes généralisées etc.": notes and copies of notes, from 1961 to about 1968. The plan calls this package the probe of 43.
- **Where they sit:** duality of commutative group schemes and generalized Jacobians (rank-19 subjects: duality of groups, Picard, abelian varieties). Cote 43 continues in 189_MPL_43_046-093, 191_MPL_43_143-192 and 192_MPL_43_193-208+45_002-032.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** the matched rows are tower rows that came in through the tags abelian and galois. Possibly **LM7 E.23** and **LM6 E.21** (section classes on the cubic): the abelian level of a punctured curve's π₁ is read off its generalized Jacobian, if the leaves go that way.
- **Our results to use and check:** none in our records.
- **What other packages' notes say:** MPL_57_271-315 R06, a reading question on the finite-subgroup wording in the Igusa construction.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 43 describes the folder as a working binder of about fifteen bundles, each under a jacket titled in Grothendieck's hand (our digest stops there). No Hua findings fall in these scans; his 43-ext-ga-zp-perfection (Ext¹(𝔾_a, ℤ/pℤ) as the perfection of 𝒪_S) sits at scans 166-170, in 191_MPL_43_143-192.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  - The binder's structure: each bundle and its jacket title in these scans, with dates, as a map for the later packages of 43.
  - Which duality each bundle develops (Cartier duality, duality of abelian schemes, generalized Jacobians with modulus, Ext and Hom of commutative group schemes), and what is proved versus announced.
  - Generalized Jacobians and the abelian coverings they classify (geometric class field theory): any statement on the abelianized π₁ of a punctured curve (possibly LM7 E.23).
  - The Igusa wording of R06, if it appears here.
