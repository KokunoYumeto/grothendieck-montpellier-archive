## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** scans 51-98 of cote 12, "Généralités (sous-groupes de Galois motiviques) (1964 ?)": manuscript notes and a typescript, 1964 to about 1971.
- **Where they sit:** motives and motivic Galois groups (plan rank 6), with cotes 11, 14, 15, 16 and 18 of the motives line. Cote 12 begins in 85_MPL_12_002-050 and continues in 87_MPL_12_099-149.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **LM6 A.22**: the normalizer of a procyclic subgroup in the full CM group over Q(i); possibly, if the leaves treat CM motives or the motivic Galois group of CM abelian varieties.
  - **FLAGGED_149 B9** and **LM7 E.23** are matched too; possibly only through weights over finite fields and abelian quotients.
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say:** MPL_15_087-138 T26 (the limit of the period-conjecture evidence), L01, §2 (whether motives are built from cote 16's Lefschetz correspondence rings) and §125 (searches for CM, Taniyama, Shimura found none in cote 15); MPL_11_043-058+56 N031 (a projector splitting in the Chow construction), N104 (the maximal-torus converse deferred to exposé XIV); MPL_14_087-132 M01.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 12: motives as the one object behind all cohomologies, with realizations as functors; the category of motives with a realization is the representation category of the motivic Galois group, whose quotient by the neutral component is the Galois group; Tate and Hodge become one statement on the size of that group; over finite fields the weights, and a notion he calls the level (niveau), order everything. No Hua findings fall in these scans.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  - How the motivic Galois group is defined here (before Saavedra's Tannakian categories, 1972), and what is said of its neutral component and its subgroups.
  - Weights and level: exact definitions, and each claim ordering cohomology by level; possibly compare with "Hodge's general conjecture is false for trivial reasons" (1969).
  - Which questions of MPL_15_087-138 (T26, §2) and MPL_11_043-058+56 (N031) these scans answer.
  - Any CM motive, Frobenius computation or Taniyama-type group (LM6 A.22).
