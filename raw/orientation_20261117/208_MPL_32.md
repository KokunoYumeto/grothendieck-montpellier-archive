## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 32 (scans 2-48), "Corrections (références, rajouts) [dont SGA 4]" (c. 1963-1977): correction slips, a typescript and notes for the Springer edition of SGA 4.
- **Where they sit:** SGA 4 and étale duality (plan rank 21: SGA 4, 5 and 7 drafts). Neighbouring SGA 4/5/7 cotes have returned packages: MPL_31_002-051, MPL_33_002-049, MPL_33_147-181+34_002-017, MPL_36_002-052.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** the three matches (LM7 E.23, LM4 D.12, LM4 F.7) come from a subject match and do not bear on étale duality. What the pages may open up: the compatibilities of trace, Gysin map and fundamental classes that the adjunction Rf_! ⊣ Rf^! leaves to be checked, and Hua's index statement below.
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** only register sections of neighbouring packages (MPL_33_002-049 §2 and §96, MPL_31_002-051 §117, MPL_36_002-052 §102) list them; no entry treats these pages.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 32: the workshop of SGA 4 before its Springer edition, almost all on étale duality: an author's typescript telling the redactor what the paragraphs on Gysin and the local fundamental class should hold, correcting itself as it goes; a redaction of the construction of Rf^! in another hand, annotated in pencil (references, notation, a new plan); a letter of Deligne, redactor of exposé XVIII, listing what is missing; sheets on derived functors of non-additive functors (symmetric powers). Hua, findings register, 32-pullback-kernel-index (our scans 011-012; candidate): for f: X → Y proper over a field, Y irreducible (smooth, as assumed there), N the gcd of the degrees of 0-cycles on the generic fibre, a map H*(X, G_X) → H*(Y, G), functorial in G, composes with f* to N·id, so Ker f* is killed by N.
- **What the notes should look for:** (1) Hua's index statement against scans 011-012: hypotheses, construction of the map, proof; (2) Deligne's letter: date, and every item it lists as missing; (3) each compatibility (trace, Gysin, fundamental class, base change): stated, checked or left open; (4) what the symmetric-power calculations serve (possibly the trace of a finite flat morphism; our guess); (5) which corrections reached the printed SGA 4.
