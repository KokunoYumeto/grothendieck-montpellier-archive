## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 37, scans 23-74, from "Rééditions SGA, EGA [SGA 4 à SGA 7, EGA I et EGA II]": typescripts, annotated copies, letters and handwritten notes, 1967-1971.
- **Where they sit:** plan rank 24 (SGA 3 and the reeditions): the Springer reeditions of SGA and EGA, with topos theory in the margins.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** **LM1 E.11**: the source theorem for arbitrary separated locally finitely presented group schemes, and the Q-algebra statement without linearity, remain unproved; possibly, if the reedition papers correct statements on unipotent group schemes. The pages may open instead: corrections to SGA 4 that never reached print.
- **Our results to use and check:** **LM1 E.2** (that run's claim: over a Q-algebra, every fibrewise-unipotent section of a closed linear group scheme is uniquely exp(t log g)).
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** MPL_33_147-181+34_002-017 cites cote 37 only in its tables.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 37: how the IHES seminars became Springer books; his 1970 departure from IHES over military funding, in an earlier state of a preface; his later refusal to spend time on the SGA 4 reedition; and topos-theoretic mathematics, starting with a handwritten dictionary of finite spaces, finite posets, finite lattices and finite topoi. Hua, findings register: **37-ind-topos-exercise** (scan 023; marked refuted by his project): a criterion for Ind(C) to be a topos (Karoubi envelope, finite colimits, condition ST) of SGA 4 I 8.8 f)). **37-smooth-base-change-stacks** (scans 030-031; matched by his project only through a secondary citation, to Giraud): smooth base change in degrees ≤ 1 with non-commutative coefficients, proposed as "2.1.1" of a correspondent's chapter VII. Both new to us.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. Scan 023: the exercise as written, what exactly is refuted, and the correct criterion; settle it.
  2. Scans 030-031: the correspondent and the chapter (possibly Giraud's Cohomologie non abélienne, 1971); check the statement.
  3. The finite-topos dictionary, if in these scans: state and check it.
  4. Letters: correspondents, dates, and each decision on what was corrected or left incomplete.
