## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 79, scans 46-94, from "2-polyèdres réguliers triangulés, et modules libres de rangs 2 sur les anneaux Λ = Z/nZ et Λ = Z": handwritten notes, from 1980. The rest of the cote is in packages 51_MPL_79_001-045, 110_MPL_79_095-144, 111_MPL_79_145-194, 112_MPL_79_195-244 and 113_MPL_79_245-297.
- **Where they sit:** plan rank 10 with 79 (maps, Galois, Teichmüller): regular triangulated surfaces through rank-2 modules over Z/nZ, that is, through SL_2(Z/nZ) and the modular group (our gloss).
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38**: K_5 = 1 open; levels 7 and 11 (genera 3 and 26) undecided; possibly the congruence triangulations of these pages.
  - **LM4 F.7**: centralizers of a geometric A_5; the case n = 5.
  - **LM4 D.12**: torsion in GT̂_0 conjugate to (-1,1); possibly, through orientation-reversing symmetries of regular maps.
  - **LM7_PUSH E.3** and **LM6 A.22**: the CM coset and the full CM group of the Fermat tower; possibly, as Fermat curves carry regular triangulations.
- **Our results to use and check:** none named in our digest.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** MPL_72_035-081 cites cote 79 in its own follow-up tasks and its primary sources.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register, **79-commutant-octahedral-exception** (from their p. 92, our scan 093, on into package 110; the rest is cut in our digest): read it in your results block. His modernised reading of cote 79 did not reach our digest.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. The dictionary: how a regular triangulated 2-polyhedron is attached to a free rank-2 module over Z/nZ (flags, the action of SL_2 or GL_2(Z/nZ)), with valency and genus.
  2. Which regular triangulations arise, and whether all are claimed to (non-congruence regular maps exist; our pointer).
  3. Orientation-reversing symmetries (GL_2 against SL_2); compare LM4 D.12.
  4. Scans 093-094: the start of Hua's commutant computation.
  5. Genus computations for n = 5, 7, 11 (0, 3, 26); compare B.38.
