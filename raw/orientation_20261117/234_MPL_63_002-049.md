## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** scans 2-49 of cote 63, "Formulaire courbes elliptiques": notes and copies of notes, a typescript, copies of articles (from 1964 to about 1970).
- **Where they sit:** elliptic curves over a general base, filed with Picard schemes (plan rank 23). Cote 63 continues in 235_MPL_63_050-101; cote 62 ("Courbes elliptiques, vieille rédaction") is in 233_MPL_62_051-097.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** the matched rows came in through the tags abelian and galois. Possibly **LM7 E.23** and **LM6 E.21** (section classes on the cubic U_3/Q): the degree-3 Fermat curve is an elliptic curve with j = 0. Possibly **LM6 A.22** (the CM normalizer over Q(i)), through the curve with j = 1728.
- **Our results to use and check:** none in our records.
- **What other packages' notes say:** our digest lists none.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 63: a working binder on elliptic curves, most of it not by Grothendieck: Tate's tables (writing the equation, changes of coordinates, recognizing bad reduction mod p), copied or typed, and their reworking by Deligne over a general base (our digest stops there). No Hua findings fall in these scans; his 63-level3-tangents-at-2 is at scans 098-100, in 235_MPL_63_050-101.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  - Separate the hands: Tate's tables, Deligne's reworking, Grothendieck's own notes and annotations; date each where possible.
  - The formulas over a general base (a_i, b_i, c_4, c_6, Δ, j, characteristics 2 and 3) against Deligne's published "Formulaire" after Tate (1975), with every discrepancy.
  - Grothendieck's own additions: questions, level structures, moduli, the curves with j = 0 and j = 1728 (possibly the cubic rows above).
  - The typescript and the article copies: authors, titles, dates.
