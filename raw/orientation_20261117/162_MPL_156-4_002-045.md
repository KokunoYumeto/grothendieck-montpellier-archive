- **What these pages are:** cote 156-4, scans 2-45: the first half of chapter IV, "Analysis situs (première mouture)", handwritten from 10 June 1986; by Hua's reading, a first axiomatics of figures cut into strata, without points.
- **Where they sit:** the 1986 Géométrie des formes (156-1 to 156-7), rank 17 with the second and third moutures (156-6, 156-7); 156-3 and 156-5 frame it; scans 46-89 are folder 163. Possibly the tame topology that the tower's Lego (strata, frames) presupposes: Hua's reading of 156-6 ties the series to the Esquisse.

- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** **MPL_156-1+156-2 §122** tabulates the author's own questions, amendments and retractions in 156-1 and 156-2; continue it here.
- **Michel Hua's project:** Hua, modernised reading of cote 156-4 (whole folder, 10-14 June) reads it as the search for a "contrée", a form described by how its pieces fit: strata, figures, multistrates; sub-figure and subdivision, combined into refinement; lieux as the finest objects; the shadow (ombre) a figure casts on the lieux. It counts five restarts in five days, two forced by a pseudo-disc example, and ends with two orders on figures (part-of, re-cutting) and their axioms. His project's findings on this cote (Hua, findings register, 156-4-figures-as-configurations and 156-4-supports-boolean-presumption) lie on scans 054-069, in folder 163.
