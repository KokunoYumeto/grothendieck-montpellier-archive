## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** scans 170-171 and 179-194 of cote 15, "Théorie arithmétique. Théorie de Galois des motifs (1965)": manuscript notes and a typescript, 1965 to about 1977. An earlier package of cote 15 never returned these scans; this package fills that gap.
- **Where they sit:** motives and their Galois groups (plan rank 6), after cote 12. The returned MPL_15_087-138 and MPL_15_195-228+18_002-017 lie on either side; 89_MPL_15_034-086 is waiting.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **LM6 A.22**: the full CM group over Q(i); possibly, as these folders build CM motives and the field where complex conjugation is central.
  - **ANABELIAN_SUCCESSOR A.57** (programme map): the universal supersingular-correspondence assertion; possibly, through the quaternion orders and Brandt groupoid of supersingular curves.
  - **LM7 E.23** and **FLAGGED_149 B9** match too, possibly only through Weil numbers.
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say:** MPL_18_018-062 P10 (the changes of place depend on M; one word reverses the expectation); MPL_33_002-049_chatB T09 (a cancelled filtration leaf keeps its source header and revised title); MPL_15_087-138 §125 (its search found no CM, Taniyama or Shimura vocabulary) and §123.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 15: over a finite field the motivic Galois group is commutative, its characters the Weil numbers; what it misses (gluing across primes, models over ℝ) is stored in a gerbe whose class is computed place by place; at the real place the obstruction is Deuring's quaternion algebra of supersingular curves, treated through a Brandt groupoid and a Picard category; then the largest extension of ℚ where complex conjugation is central, and motives with commutative motivic Galois group. No Hua findings are listed for these scans.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  - Which stations of Hua's reading fall in scans 170-171 and 179-194, each checked against the ink.
  - The gerbe's local classes and the real place; whether P10's dependence on M shows here.
  - CM motives and their Galois group: possibly an early form of the Taniyama group, against the negative search of §125.
  - Supersingular curves, quaternion orders and Brandt groupoids (ANABELIAN_SUCCESSOR A.57).
