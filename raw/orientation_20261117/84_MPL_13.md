Findings (Hua, findings register; our intake, Claude Fable 5.2 at effort medium: new to us): **13-double-lattice-invariants** (scans 034-035; conjecture, no reference found): for fibre functors T over k and T′ over K, isomorphic over L, the vectors in the K-structure T′(V) are the invariants of a k-subgroup H of Aut⊗(T) generated by the image of a closed subgroup over K; **13-double-niveau-de-rham-betti** (scans 048-049; matched in the literature): is the de Rham–Betti realisation over an algebraic extension of Q fully faithful? It is reduced to Q̄ and recast as V^G = V ∩ g(V̄); the Tate case works.
