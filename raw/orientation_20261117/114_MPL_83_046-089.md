## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** scans 46-89 of cote 83, "[Icosaèdres, bi-icosaèdres et algèbres d'Azumaya de rang 4]", manuscript notes of 1982-83 and 1986.
- **Where they sit:** the polyhedra and maps line beside the tower: the icosahedral group A5, rank-4 Azumaya (quaternion) algebras, whose automorphism groups are the forms of PGL₂. Cote 83 is also in 52_MPL_83_001-045, 115_MPL_83_090-133, 116_MPL_83_134-177 and 117_MPL_83_178-222.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **LM4 F.7** and **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.39**: the centralizer of a geometric A5 in the profinite genus-zero mapping class group; the nonsolvable comparison is open.
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.41**: Δ on a hypothetical nontrivial kernel and its extension along icosahedral marked-orbit loci.
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38**: the congruence kernels, K_5 = 1 first; possibly, since level 5 is icosahedral (PSL2(F5) = A5).
  - **PUSH_ORDER5_FRAME Itemsfromt 2** (O.20, rigid frames in Γ̂_{0,[5]}): possibly, through order-five symmetry.
- **Our results to use and check:** **EXIT_TOWER E.49** claims Δ = 0 on GT̂ (unrefereed; GT_TOWER_CHECK checks it); possibly tested by any Galois computation on icosahedral data here.
- **What other packages' notes say:** our digest lists none.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register, 83-two-generator-azumaya-criterion (scans 084-089, continuing at 105-110 in 115_MPL_83_090-133): over any commutative ring, the rank-4 algebra with basis 1, u, v, uv, given by Tr u = b, det u = c, Tr v = b′, det v = c′, Tr uv = t, is Azumaya iff Q = t² − bb′t + b²c′ + b′²c − 4cc′ is invertible; his literature check matches it through Voight (2011). Our intake: new to us. Our digest keeps none of his modernised reading.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  - Scans 084-089: the criterion as written, the base rings allowed, the proof, and what it adds to Voight.
  - How icosahedra enter the algebras: A5 inside the automorphism group of a rank-4 Azumaya algebra, and what a "bi-icosaèdre" is; possibly the same objects as the PGL₂-forms with A5 of cote 85 (149_MPL_74_121-136+85_002-034).
  - Fields or rings of definition of the icosahedral data (√5, F4, F5) and any Galois action on them (LM4_SUCCESSOR B.41).
  - Any statement on A5 acting on the sphere, its orbits, centralizer or normalizer (LM4 F.7).
