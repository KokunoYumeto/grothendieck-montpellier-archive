- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 78: what a regular polygon is once lengths, angles and real numbers are removed, in an affine plane over any field, finite fields included; regularity is redefined by symmetry (every symmetry of the combinatorial polygon is realised by a transformation of the plane), so every triangle and every parallelogram is regular; a regular polygon becomes a representation of the dihedral group (σ₀, σ₁ with product the rotation by one step) with a starting vertex, and one number then describes everything. Our digest lists no finding of his for these scans.
- **What the notes should look for:** (1) the definition by symmetries as written, and the single number that classifies regular n-gons (possibly the trace of the rotation, a root of a real cyclotomic polynomial; our guess); (2) every count of regular n-gons over F_q and its dependence on q mod n, checked by computation; (3) the cases where the characteristic divides n or 2n; (4) how star polygons are told apart; (5) any tie to the regular polyhedra of cote 72.
