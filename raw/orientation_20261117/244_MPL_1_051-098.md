## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** scans 51-98 of cote 1, "Analyse fonctionnelle": manuscript notes and a letter, 1953.
- **Where they sit:** Grothendieck's functional analysis (plan rank 25): singular values of compact operators, Schatten classes, Fredholm determinants; the years of Produits tensoriels topologiques (1955) and La théorie de Fredholm (1956). Cote 1 continues in 245_MPL_1_099-150.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** none of our open rows is matched. The pages may open instead the operator-ideal line: inequalities for singular values of sums and products, and the determinant route to them.
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say:** five entries cite cote 1 (MPL_147_138-190 190-04, MPL_33_147-181+34_002-017, MPL_36_002-052, MPL_36_053-085+39, MPL_7_061-072+8_001-078 scan 078); by their titles none is about functional analysis.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 1: an operator measured by its sequence of singular values; Weyl's 1949 majorizations for sums and products, the Hardy-Littlewood-Pólya lemma, hence Schatten's theory (norms, Hölder, duality), redone on their pp. 81-104 (our scans 082-105); an older version on their pp. 123-149 goes through det(1 + zu) instead of Weyl's lemma. Hua, findings register, 1-sigma-nm-system (scans 072-074): for compact u, v and 0 ≤ m ≤ n, the elementary symmetric functions of the first n singular values satisfy σ_n^m(u+v) ≤ Σ_p σ_n^p(u) σ_n^{m−p}(v), a system stronger than the product inequality ∏(1 + r s_i(u+v)) ≤ ∏(1 + r s_i(u)) ∏(1 + r s_i(v)); unsearched, Lean card partly proved. Our intake: new to us.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  - Scans 072-074: the system as written and its proof; possibly compare with the determinant inequalities of the trace-ideal literature (for example Simon, Trace Ideals).
  - Scans 082-098: each step of the Schatten theory (Weyl, Hardy-Littlewood-Pólya, Hölder, duality) against the ink, with what Grothendieck proves himself.
  - Whether these leaves presuppose the older determinant version (their pp. 123-149, in 245_MPL_1_099-150), and how the two versions are ordered in time.
  - The 1953 letter, if it falls here: correspondent and content.
