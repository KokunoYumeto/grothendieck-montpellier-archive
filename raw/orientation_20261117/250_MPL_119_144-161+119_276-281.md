## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 119, scans 144-161 and 276-281: handwritten leaves in the dossier of the "Esquisse d'un programme" assembled for the 1984 CNRS application (copies of typescripts, some annotated; 1968-1991).
- **Where they sit:** the plan group "API-route cotes: their handwritten leaves (129, 119)"; subjects Teichmüller tower, maps, homotopy, strata: the Esquisse and the tower.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **EXIT_TOWER E.T3**: cote 143 defines a modular topos over Q and asks for the automorphisms of its geometric base change; Hua's finding here maps topoi to a "multiplicité".
  - **IMPL P3**: is the index category of the Teichmüller tower quasi-filtered, total or exact, and is the two-level principle a finality statement? The Esquisse states that principle.
  - **EXIT_TOWER E.24**: the arithmetic image on the geometric modular groups; the Esquisse's view of G_Q acting on the tower.
  - **LM4 D.12** and **LM4 F.7**: torsion and finite subgroups in GT̂_0 and the mapping class groups, part of the Esquisse's Lego.
- **Our results to use and check:** **EXIT_TOWER E.49** claims Δ = 0 on GT̂ (unrefereed; GT_TOWER_CHECK is checking it); test it against anything these leaves say about the tower's automorphisms.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** none in our digest.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register, **119-foliation-quotient-topos-equivalence** (our scans 145-146; no reference found by his search; new to us): the folder states without proof an equivalence between pairs (X, R), R a local equivalence relation on a space X, locally open with locally connected fibres, and triples (X, Y, f), f: Top(X) → Y a locally open geometric morphism with locally connected fibres to a "multiplicité" (cut in our digest).
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. Scans 145-146: the exact categories and hypotheses, any argument, and whether it is an equivalence; compare the modular multiplicities of the Esquisse, and possibly Moerdijk's classifying topoi of groupoids (a reference Hua's search may have missed).
  2. Any statement of the two-level principle or of the Lego; compare IMPL P3.
  3. What each leaf annotates, with its date; what scans 276-281 are.
  4. Any claim on the Galois action on the tower; compare EXIT_TOWER E.24 and E.49.
