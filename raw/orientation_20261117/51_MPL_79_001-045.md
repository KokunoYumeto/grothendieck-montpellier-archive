## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** the first 45 scans of cote 79, "2-polyèdres réguliers triangulés, et modules libres de rang 2 sur les anneaux Λ = ℤ/nℤ et Λ = ℤ" (from 1980); the plan's probe of 79.
- **Where they sit:** regular maps and the modular tower: from (ℤ/nℤ)² one gets regular triangulated surfaces (possibly the modular curves X(n): tetrahedron, octahedron, icosahedron for n = 3, 4, 5); from ℤ² the Farey triangulation. Cote 79 continues in 109_MPL_79_046-094, 110_MPL_79_095-144, 111_MPL_79_145-194, 112_MPL_79_195-244, 113_MPL_79_245-297.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38**: the congruence kernels K_5, K_7, K_11; its level-7 and level-11 compactifications have genera 3 and 26, the genera of X(7) and X(11).
  - **LM2 B.f7**: the PSL2(F7) arithmetic image and finite pentagon data (level 7).
  - **LM4 F.7** and **FERMAT_SUCCESSOR C.20**: A5 = PSL2(F5) centralizers and icosahedral membership tests (level 5).
  - **PUSH_20261114 Part2Succe 26**: the icosahedral characters on the cover branched at the twelve cusps.
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say:** MPL_72_035-081 (its own follow-up tasks, with confidence and reasons; its primary sources).
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 79 starts from the plane of integer points and makes it draw a surface (our digest stops there). Hua, findings register, 79-semilocal-diameter-three (scans 020-021): for M free of rank 2 over a semi-local Λ and any gauge e, any two vertices of SP_M (vertices M*/±1, edges the pairs with x ∧ y = ±e) are at distance at most 3; no reference found. Our intake: new to us.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  - The definition of SP_M and its identification, for Λ = ℤ/nℤ, with a regular triangulated surface and its automorphism group (PSL2(ℤ/n) or SL2(ℤ/n)/±1).
  - Scans 020-021: the diameter claim as written, with a computational check for small n.
  - Any computation at levels 5, 7 or 11 (orbits, genera, automorphisms), set against LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38 and LM2 B.f7.
  - The folder's structure, as a map for the later packages of 79.
