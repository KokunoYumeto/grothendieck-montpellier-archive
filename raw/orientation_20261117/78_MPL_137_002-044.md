## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 137, scans 2-44, "Dévissage des complexes ½ simpliciaux ∂-parfaits et structures multiplicatives… (1975 ou 1976)": handwritten notes, about 1975-1976. Scans 45-86 are package 79_MPL_137_045-086.
- **Where they sit:** plan rank 9 continued (cotes 137 and 133, II.9): homological algebra of semi-simplicial complexes with multiplicative structure, between the 1960s and Pursuing Stacks (1983).
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **GALOIS G.6**: no functorial derivator D' with W_D' = W_lc for a derivator without monoidal enhancement; possibly, as multiplicative structures are such an enhancement.
  - **RESIDUE I.7**: accessible localizers between integral homology equivalences and W_0; the linear side of these pages.
  - **TOTASPH D.7** and **RESIDUE F.5**: exactness of colim_A on Ab^A for totally aspherical A; possibly, if the dévissage uses exactness of colimits.
- **Our results to use and check:** none named in our digest.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** MPL_133 (the sister cote): B01 (the adjective "binomial" belongs to the rank target), B05 (the strongest bridge is a correction and a new question in 1983), B06 (strict cosimplicial commutativity is not strict DGA commutativity).
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register, **137-perfect-complexes-universal-sigma-exact** (their pp. 39-44, 48-56; here our scans 040-044; no reference found by his search; new to us): a universal property of the k-linear category 𝒫 of bounded complexes of finitely generated free k-modules with Σ (cut in our digest; read it in your results block).
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. Scans 040-044: the universal property in full; check it.
  2. The definitions: ½-simplicial complexes, ∂-perfection, and what the dévissage reduces to what.
  3. The multiplicative structures: commutative up to which homotopies; compare MPL_133 B06 and B01, and possibly the modern binomial-ring models of homotopy types (Toën's schematization; Horel, 2022) as references.
