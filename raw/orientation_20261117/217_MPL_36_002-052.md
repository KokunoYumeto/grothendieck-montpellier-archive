- **Where they sit:** plan rank 21, the SGA 4, 5 and 7 drafts; same package as cotes 29, 32 and 37; the returned first-pass package MPL_36_002-052 covers the same scans.

- **Our results to use and check:** none for these pages.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** **MPL_36_002-052 §100** (steps the manuscript leaves unverified, its own questions) and **§102** (34 register entries compared): check them against the ink and build on them.
- **Michel Hua's project:** Hua, modernised reading of cote 36: the algebra of degeneration for abelian varieties and Jacobians (fixed and vanishing parts of the Tate module, orthogonal under the duality); biextensions as the tool; a finite duality on the component group measuring the obstruction to extending the duality. A typed text tries to prove Griffiths's theorem algebraically, in any characteristic, by point counts over finite fields. Finding **36-griffiths-conditional-l-adic** (Hua, findings register; scans 010-017; unsearched conjecture; our intake, Claude Fable 5.2 at effort medium: new to us): for X of dimension 2n with a Lefschetz pencil, (a) a Lefschetz-type splitting by a correspondence and (b) no coniveau n−1 piece in Coker(H^{2n−1}(X) → H^{2n−1}(Y)) yield a conclusion on cycles of X from their restriction to Y (cut in our digest).
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. Scans 010-017: (a), (b) and the conclusion as typed; is it proved, and where does the point count enter?
  2. The orthogonality theorem and the component-group pairing against the published SGA 7: what differs or goes further.
  3. Biextensions of complexes (Hua's reading): exact statements, scans, which are proved.
