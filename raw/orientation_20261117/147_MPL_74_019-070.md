## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 74, scans 19-70, from "Complexes cubiques" (from 1976): the graph of the 27 lines on a cubic surface and its smaller relatives.
- **Where they sit:** plan rank 14 (regular polyhedra, graphs; calculation folders). Cote 74 opens in 146_MPL_72_082-113+74_002-018 and goes on in 148_MPL_74_071-120 and 149_MPL_74_121-136+85_002-034; the 27 lines also fill cote 69 (142_MPL_69_002-049 and after).
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** the 34 matches come from the subject "maps" and concern the tower; none bears on these graphs. What the pages may open up (Hua's reading): the finite graphs in which every edge lies in exactly one triangle and every point off a triangle is joined to exactly one of its vertices; possibly, in today's terms, the generalized quadrangles of order (2, t), whose finite ones have t = 1, 2, 4 (our identification from that description).
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** none yet.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 74: such graphs are few: nine points in a three-by-three square ("bitriangle", then "pavé"), fifteen points, and twenty-seven, the lines of a cubic surface joined when they meet, which the pages call "graphe cubique" without speaking of the surface; the opening leaves are algebra (a Latin square, when it is a group table, an identity of Moufang type forcing associativity); then counts on the 27 lines (45 triangles, 72 bases of six disjoint lines, 36 double-sixes, 120 pavés, 720 triples of disjoint lines) and the symmetry group.
- **What the notes should look for:** (1) every count against the ink and against Hua's figures; (2) whether he proves that the 9-, 15- and 27-point graphs are the only finite non-trivial ones, or states it; (3) the symmetry groups and their orders (the 27-line graph has W(E₆), order 51,840); (4) where the Moufang-type identity sits, if in this range; (5) ties to cote 69 and Hua, findings register, 69-triangle-q8-baer-torsor (a marked triangle as a torsor under a 2-group).
