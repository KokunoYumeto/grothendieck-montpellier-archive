- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 10: whether a group is known from its representations; the decompositions of tensor products carry the missing information; a ⊗-category with a fibre functor (equivalent to an algebra, ⊗ an extra operation on it) and constraints (associativity, commutativity, unit, duals). His reading places a nine-chapter plan of an unwritten treatise on their p. 114, our scan 115, in this package. Our digest lists no findings of his project for these scans.
