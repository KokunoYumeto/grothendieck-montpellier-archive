## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** two whole dossiers: cote 94, "Dualité des modules / Construction de faisceaux de modules" (scans 2-24, notes from 1965), and cote 97, "Classes de Chern des représentations" (scans 2-19, a letter of 1966 and notes).
- **Where they sit:** plan rank 20 (K-theory, Néron, residues): representability of functors on modules, beside cote 91 (package 200), and Chern classes of representations, possibly the material of "Classes de Chern et représentations linéaires des groupes discrets" (1968), our guess.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** none of ours concerns cotes 94 or 97; the five rows matched by subject (LM7 E.23, FLAGGED_149 B9, LM1 D.11, LM4 D.12, LM4 F.7) are about sections, points and GT̂.
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** none in our digest.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 94: when is a functor on modules of the form Hom(P, −) with P finite? Left exactness, filtered colimits, a completion condition, finite values, generic regularity tested against one module, the "faisceau fondamental" (the canonical module); the criterion (his theorem 12) is applied to families of projective varieties. Hua, findings register (new to us): **94-representability-criterion** (scans 016-017; no reference found by his search), the criterion over a quotient of a regular ring; **94-rank-constructibility** (scan 008; marked refuted by his project); **94-generic-tor-regular-sequences** (scans 009-010, 020; no reference found), Tor-vanishing against regular sequences on a dense open, replacing generic flatness over a non-reduced base; **97-borel-cartan-simplicial-forms** (scan 016; his project matched it to Bott 1973 and Shulman 1972), H*(BG, R) from forms on the simplicial manifold.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. Scans 016-017: every condition of the criterion, and why A must be a quotient of a regular ring.
  2. Scan 008: the claim, and the counterexample behind Hua's refutation.
  3. Scans 009-010, 020: prove or refute the generic Tor-vanishing.
  4. Cote 97: the 1966 letter (correspondent), which Chern classes (discrete or algebraic groups, which cohomology); check the simplicial-forms computation.
  5. Where the criterion is applied; compare cote 91.
