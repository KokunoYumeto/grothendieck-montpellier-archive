## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** scans 1-45 of cote 154, "[Système de pseudo-droites]", manuscript notes of 1983-1984 on arrangements of pseudolines in the projective plane.
- **Where they sit:** combinatorial topology of the Esquisse period; the plan calls this package a probe of 154. Cote 154 continues in 172_MPL_81_038-055+154_046-076.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** the ten matched rows came in through the tags forms and strata; none is about pseudolines. Possibly **EXIT_ONE_TYPE P3c-general** (a stratal zigzag through good pairs), since the folder builds a surface by gluing cells. Otherwise the pages open a line of their own: the cell surface of relative positions and its monodromy.
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say:** the results block lists notes of returned packages that meet this folder, among them MPL_156-1+156-2 §122, a table of the standing questions and expectations the author marks; see which of them these leaves take up.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 154 (written, it says, between Christmas 1983 and February 1984): the relative positions of a moving pseudoline among n given ones are finitely many, adjacent across one crossing point; the folder wants their set as a cell surface, glued from discs, carrying the 2n-gon of crossings, and the monodromy (rotation or flip) of that polygon. Hua, findings register, 154-cyclic-order-determines-position (scan 019): asserted without proof, a position other than the D_i is determined by the antipody-compatible cyclic order in which its double cover meets the oriented pseudolines of Σ; his search found no reference. Our intake: new to us.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  - Scan 019: is that claim stated, proved later or used, and with which hypotheses?
  - How far the surface of positions gets: cells, gluing, global topology (possibly the dual projective plane, by line-point duality), and the monodromy of the 2n-gon.
  - Possibly the oriented-matroid literature on pseudoline arrangements (Folkman-Lawrence, Goodman-Pollack), since Hua's search found no reference.
  - Dates on the leaves, and any reference to the Esquisse d'un Programme (January 1984) or to the tower.
