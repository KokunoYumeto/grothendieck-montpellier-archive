## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** the last scans (178-222) of cote 83, "[Icosaèdres, bi-icosaèdres et algèbres d'Azumaya de rang 4]", manuscript notes of 1982-83 and 1986.
- **Where they sit:** the polyhedra and maps line beside La Longue Marche and the tower: the icosahedral group A5 (= PSL2(F4) = PSL2(F5)), quaternion (rank-4 Azumaya) algebras, finite subgroups of mapping class groups. The rest of cote 83 is in 52_MPL_83_001-045, 114_MPL_83_046-089, 115_MPL_83_090-133 and 116_MPL_83_134-177.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **LM4 F.7**: the centralizer of a geometric A5 in the profinite genus-zero mapping class group; the nonsolvable comparison is open.
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.39**: the nonsolvable direction of that comparison; A5 is its first case.
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.41**: Δ on a hypothetical nontrivial kernel and its extension along icosahedral marked-orbit loci.
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38**: the congruence kernels, above all K_5 = 1; possibly, since level 5 is icosahedral (PSL2(F5) = A5).
  - **PUSH_ORDER5_FRAME Itemsfromt 2** (O.20, the rigid frames Θ3, Θ4, Θ5 in Γ̂_{0,[5]}): possibly, through order-five symmetry.
- **Our results to use and check:** **EXIT_TOWER E.49** claims Δ = 0 on GT̂ (unrefereed; GT_TOWER_CHECK checks it); possibly, any icosahedral computation of Δ on these leaves tests it.
- **What other packages' notes say:** our digest lists none.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 83 opens by looking for the icosahedron (12 vertices, 20 triangles, five at each vertex) among 2×2 matrices, starting over the field with four elements; our digest stops there. It lists no Hua findings for these scans.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  - What a "bi-icosaèdre" is here, and how the icosahedron sits in PSL2(F4), PSL2(F5) or a rank-4 Azumaya algebra, with scans.
  - Any statement on A5 acting on the sphere (its 12-, 20- and 30-point orbits, centralizer, normalizer): test it against LM4 F.7 and LM4_SUCCESSOR B.39.
  - Any Galois action on icosahedral data (fields of definition, characters): test it against LM4_SUCCESSOR B.41 and EXIT_TOWER E.49.
  - Which leaves are of 1986 and what they change from 1982-83.
