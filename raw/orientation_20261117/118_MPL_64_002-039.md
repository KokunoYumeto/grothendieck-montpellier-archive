## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** scans 2-39 of cote 64, "Modules courbes elliptiques en caractéristiques ≠ 2", manuscript notes from 1980-1982.
- **Where they sit:** the genus-one stage of the tower, written beside La Longue Marche (1981): moduli of elliptic curves over ℤ[1/2], level structures, the λ-line. Cote 64 continues in 119_MPL_64_040-077, 120_MPL_64_078-115 and 121_MPL_64_116-157.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38** and **E.10**: the congruence kernels (K_5 first; levels 7 and 11, compactifications of genera 3 and 26); level-N moduli of elliptic curves are their natural ground.
  - **EXIT_TOWER E.24**: the arithmetic image of the geometric modular groups of cote 143; possibly its genus-one case.
  - **LM6 A.22** and **LM7_PUSH E.3**: CM images; possibly, through the curves with j = 1728 (over Q(i)) and j = 0.
  - **SECTIONS_OVER_Q A.24** (programme map, the Legendre route to A1): possibly, since away from 2 the moduli is read on the Legendre λ-line over the tripod.
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say:** MPL_93_002-046 E14 (formal deformation versus the coarse invariant), E16 (families realizing the two smallest displayed orders), C29 (a first-order bridge back to the elliptic deformation sheet), P09 (what a comparison with cotes 159 and 64 must establish), C35.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** our digest cuts his findings for these scans and keeps none of his modernised reading; read both in the results block and check them against the leaves.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  - Which moduli the leaves build: the λ-line, the j-line, the stack over ℤ[1/2], the S3 action, and how the tripod P¹ − {0, 1, ∞} enters.
  - Level structures and quotients (levels 3, 4, 5, 7): any computation bearing on LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38.
  - The curves with extra automorphisms (j = 0, 1728) and their fields.
  - The comparison P09 asks for, and the questions E14 and C29 of MPL_93_002-046, where these scans settle them.
