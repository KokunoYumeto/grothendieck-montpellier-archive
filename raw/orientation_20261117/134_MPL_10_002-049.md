## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 10, scans 2-49, the opening of "Catégories tensorielles": handwritten notes and typescripts, from 1958 on (197 leaves in all, per Hua's reading). Scans 50-198 are packages 135_MPL_10_050-098, 136_MPL_10_099-150 and 137_MPL_10_151-198.
- **Where they sit:** plan rank 13 (tensor categories, cycles, L-functions): the Tannakian programme behind motives and motivic Galois groups.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** none of ours concerns cote 10; LM7 E.23, matched by subject, is about sections. The pages may open instead: a group rebuilt from its ⊗-category of representations, and polarisations over R, which our motives notes use.
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** MPL_33_147-181+34_002-017 cites cote 10 only in its tables. By subject, MPL_15_087-138 §111 (sl₂ and the Tate group in the Tate tensor subcategory) and MPL_15_195-228+18_002-017 S15 (a comparison with rational Nori motives) use this formalism.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 10: whether a group is known from its representations; for non-commutative groups the decompositions of tensor products supply what the list lacks; a ⊗-category with a fibre functor (equivalent to an algebra, ⊗ an extra operation on it) and constraints (associativity, commutativity, unit, duals); a nine-chapter plan of an unwritten treatise on their p. 114 (our scan 115, package 136). Hua, findings register, **10-hodgian-polarisations-torsor** (scans 012-017; conjecture, unsearched; new to us): for a real algebraic group G with G_m → G → G_m (i, then ε), η = i(−1), G′ = Ker ε, two elements C, C′ of G′(R) with C² = C′² = η defining polarisations of Rep(G) are, after conjugation by G′(R)°, related by C′ = zC with z central of order dividing 2 (cut in our digest).
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. Scans 012-017: the polarisation statement in full, what z ≠ 1 gives, and the proof; compare possibly Saavedra Rivano (1972) and Deligne-Milne (1982).
  2. The axioms fixed here for a ⊗-category and a fibre functor, and how the fibre functor becomes an algebra.
  3. Any reconstruction theorem stated, with its hypotheses (base field, rigidity, neutral or not).
  4. Dates: separate the 1958-era notes from later typescripts.
