## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 79, scans 95-144, from "2-polyèdres réguliers triangulés, et modules libres de rangs 2 sur les anneaux Λ = Z/nZ et Λ = Z": handwritten notes, from 1980. The cote runs to scan 297: packages 51_MPL_79_001-045, 109_MPL_79_046-094, 111_MPL_79_145-194, 112_MPL_79_195-244, 113_MPL_79_245-297.
- **Where they sit:** plan rank 10 with 79 (maps, Galois, Teichmüller): regular triangulated surfaces through rank-2 modules over Z/nZ, that is, through SL_2(Z/nZ) and the modular group (our gloss).
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38**: K_5 = 1 open, levels 7 and 11 (genera 3 and 26) undecided; triangulations from (Z/nZ)² are possibly the quotients at these levels.
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR E.10**: the six congruence-kernel computations (K_c^τ and H^1(C_2, K_c)).
  - **LM4 F.7** and **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.41**: the icosahedral case n = 5 (A_5 = PSL_2(F_5)).
  - **EXIT_TOWER E.24**: Galois acting on these maps, at the (0,4) level.
- **Our results to use and check:** none named in our digest.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** MPL_72_035-081 cites cote 79 in its own follow-up tasks (with confidence and reasons) and its primary sources.
