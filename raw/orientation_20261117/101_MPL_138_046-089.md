## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 138, scans 46-89, the middle of "Cartes. Etude arithmétique (1976, 77 ?)": an order form and undated handwritten notes, about 1976-1978. Scans 1-45 and 90-133 are packages 54_MPL_138_001-045 and 102_MPL_138_090-133.
- **Where they sit:** plan rank 10 (the rest of 138, III.7): maps (dessins), Galois action, Teichmüller; the arithmetic of maps that leads to La Longue Marche (1981) and the tower (cotes 141-152).
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38**: the congruence kernels K_N; K_5 = 1 is open, levels 7 and 11 (quotient genera 3 and 26) undecided. Maps on congruence quotients are a natural subject here.
  - **EXIT_TOWER E.24**: the arithmetic image on the geometric modular groups of cote 143; the Galois action on maps is the action on π̂_1 of the line minus three points, the (0,4) level.
  - **LM4 D.12**: whether all torsion in GT̂_0 is conjugate to (-1,1); possibly, as complex conjugation acting on maps (mirror maps) is its first case.
  - **LM7_PUSH E.3** and **LM6 A.22**: the CM coset and the full CM group of the Fermat tower; possibly, since Fermat curves are maps (covers of the line branched over 0, 1, ∞).
- **Our results to use and check:** none named in our digest for these scans.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** our digest lists none for scans 46-89.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 138: a graph drawn on the sphere, cutting it into regions, carries a rigid structure: essentially one way to see the sphere as the completed complex line (our digests have only this opening). No findings of his project fall on these scans.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. The definition of a map and of its group (the cartographic group); whether maps are identified with finite-index subgroups or with covers of the line minus three points; whether definability over Q̄ is claimed (before Belyi, 1979).
  2. Every Galois invariant of maps proposed, with the examples worked.
  3. Congruence and non-congruence maps, and regular maps on X(7) or X(11); compare LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38.
  4. Complex conjugation on maps; compare LM4 D.12.
  5. Links to cote 70 (realisations of regular polyhedra, from 1976) and cote 86 (regular polyhedra, from 1977).
