- **What these pages are:** all of cote 123 (scans 2-48), "[Autour de Kostant]": typescripts, handwritten notes and a letter (1969).
- **Where they sit:** filed at rank 22 of the plan (descent, heights, étale cohomology); by subject, reductive group schemes over a general base, the SGA 3 line.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** none directly; the five rows matched by the tags "etale, abelian" (**LM7 E.23**, **FLAGGED_149 B9**, **LM1 D.11**, **LM4 D.12**, **LM4 F.7**) concern sections, points and GT̂ torsion. What the pages may open up instead: simultaneous resolution over a base, and Picard groups of the Grothendieck-Springer space.
- **Our results to use and check:** none on these subjects.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** only register-disposition tables matched by subject (MPL_22+23_002-016 P03, MPL_36_002-052 §102, MPL_131 10).
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 123: reading notes on two papers of Kostant, rewritten for families over any base; the Kostant map 𝔤 → 𝔱/W and the Steinberg map G → T/W; sheets 4-13 resolve the family after the base change 𝔱 → 𝔱/W (adjoin a Borel containing the element) and show by Picard groups that none exists without it; a second thread on the principal SL(2) and the Coxeter element. Hua, findings register: **123-steinberg-no-resolution-picard** (our scans 011-014) and **123-grothendieck-springer-pic-twisted-torus** (our scans 011-013), conjectures his project did not search; **123-kostant-rigidifications-count** (our scans 022, 029, 031, 035; his search found no reference): for simple adjoint G with (h, p) = 1, G acts freely on quadruples (T, B, T′, B′) with T, T′ in apposition. Our intake: new to us.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. On scans 011-014: the exact hypotheses of the no-resolution claim (iso over G^reg, exceptional locus of fibrewise codimension ≥ 2) and the Picard argument.
  2. The twisted-torus statement: injectivity of Pic(Bor) → Pic(G̃) and the cokernel inside H¹(Γ, X*(T)); proved or asserted?
  3. The free action on quadruples: where (h, p) = 1 is used, and what fails without it.
  4. The 1969 letter: recipient and date. Possibly compare the resolution with the published accounts (Brieskorn 1970, Slodowy 1980), which credit it to Grothendieck.
