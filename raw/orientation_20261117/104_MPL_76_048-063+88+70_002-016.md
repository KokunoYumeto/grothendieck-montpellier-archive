## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** the end of cote 76, "n-cartes cellulaires" (scans 48-63; about 1977); cote 88, "Cartes standards" (scans 2-16; undated); and the start of cote 70, "Réalisations géométriques de structures combinatoires (n-polyèdres, n-hyperpolyèdres [2-polyèdres réguliers]…) (Vieilles rédactions)" (scans 2-16; from 1976).
- **Where they sit:** rank 10 of the plan (maps on surfaces and the icosahedron; tags "maps, galois, teich"). Cote 76 starts in 103_MPL_76_002-047; cote 70 goes on in 105_MPL_70_017-064, 106_MPL_70_065-109 and 107_MPL_70_110-126+75.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.41** and **LM4 F.7**: the icosahedral marked-orbit locus; centralizers of a geometric A₅. The icosahedron is one of the regular polytopes realized here.
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38**: the congruence kernels at levels 5, 7, 11; possibly, if regular maps of those levels are realized.
  - **PUSH_ORDER5_FRAME Itemsfromt 2** (O.20, rigid frames in Γ̂_{0,[5]}): possibly, if the "standard maps" include the five-point case.
- **Our results to use and check:** none listed.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** none listed.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register, **70-universal-formulae-double-cosines** (their pp. 9-25, 64-69; our scans 010-016 here, the rest in the next parts of cote 70; his search found no reference): "universal formulae" for a regular affine realization by reflections of the polytope {p₁, …, p_n} in its vertex frame, σ_i = id + ℓ_i ⊗ (s_{i+1} − s_i), with parameters that must be roots of the minimal polynomial of 2cos(2π/p_i). Our intake: new to us. Read his modernised readings of cotes 70, 76 and 88 in the background.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. On scans 010-016: the formula, the frame, the parameters, and their constraints; check them on {3,5}, where 2cos(2π/5) is a root of x² + x − 1.
  2. The definition of an n-cellular map in cote 76, and how it extends the 2-dimensional maps (dessins).
  3. What a "carte standard" is in cote 88, and whether it is a universal object for maps of a given type.
  4. Any Galois action or Teichmüller data on these maps (the tags "galois, teich").
