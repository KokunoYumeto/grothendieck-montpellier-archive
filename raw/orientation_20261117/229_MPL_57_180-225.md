- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register, **57-conic-nonseparated-pic** (their pp. 213-218; our scans 214-219): for the conics xy = tz² over k[t], two lines at t = 0, the Picard functor is represented by a scheme locally of finite type but not separated; his literature note cites Kleiman's Example 4.14 (x² + y² = t over ℝ). **57-cone-completion-class-group** (their pp. 188-192; our scans 189-193): at the vertex of the affine cone over a regular X ⊂ ℙʳ, Pic(S − a) → Pic(Ŝ − â) is bijective if H¹(X, 𝒪_X(n)) = 0 for all n ≥ 1, and only if, when dim X = 1; his note relates it to Danilov's theorem as restated by Manning. Our intake: both new to us. Read his modernised reading of cote 57 in the background.
