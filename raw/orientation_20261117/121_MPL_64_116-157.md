## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 64, scans 116-157: the last part of "Modules courbes elliptiques en caractéristiques ≠ 2" (from 1980-1982), notes on the moduli of elliptic curves away from characteristic 2.
- **Where they sit:** the base of the tower, the modular level (plan rank 10; subjects maps, teich, galois, crystals). Cote 64 starts in 118_MPL_64_002-039, 119_MPL_64_040-077, 120_MPL_64_078-115. MPL_93_002-046 P09 pairs it with cotes 93 and 159.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38** (congruence kernels K_N; K_5 = 1 the main open assertion; levels 7 and 11, compactifications of genus three and twenty-six): level structures on elliptic curves are this folder's subject. **EXIT_TOWER E.24** (the arithmetic image in the geometric modular groups, exact indices). Possibly **LM6 A.22** and **LM7_PUSH E.3** (the CM image; open image in G_Q(i)), through the curve with complex multiplication by Z[i]. Possibly **LM4 D.12** (torsion in GT̂_0 and the low-type groups), through the finite automorphism groups of elliptic curves.
- **Our results to use and check:** none named in our digest.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** MPL_93_002-046 P09: what a future comparison with cotes 159 and 64 must establish. E14: formal deformation versus the coarse invariant (a conjecture). E16: families realizing the two smallest displayed orders. C29: a first-order bridge back to the elliptic deformation sheet.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** his project has a modernised reading of cote 64 (our digest cuts it before its content; read it in the results block). No finding of his is listed for these scans.
- **What the notes should look for:** (1) which moduli problem is set up (level structure, normal form, stack or coarse space) and over which base ring; (2) the automorphism groups at j = 0 and j = 1728, and how characteristic 3, where they meet, is handled; (3) the comparison with cote 93 that MPL_93_002-046 P09 asks for (Picard-Fuchs, Hasse-Witt, p-curvature); (4) any statement on the fundamental group of the moduli stack or the Galois action on it, tested against EXIT_TOWER E.24 and LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38.
