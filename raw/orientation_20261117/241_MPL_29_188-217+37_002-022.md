What the pages may open up instead: an axiomatic theory of ramification and of the degree of a morphism.

Of cote 37: how the IHÉS seminars became Springer books in 1967-1971, with editors to chase. Hua, findings register: **29-ramification-domain-axioms** (our scans 191-194): axioms R1-R4 for a "domaine de ramification", R2 claimed to follow from the others; **29-domram-morphisms-fully-faithful** (our scan 198); **29-kummer-gerbe-root-stack** (our scans 200-201; his register identifies it with the root stack); **29-degree-n-as-primitive** (our scans 213, 215): six axioms making "degree n" primitive. Our intake: new to us.
