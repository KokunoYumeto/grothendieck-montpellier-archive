- **What these pages are:** scans 50-98 of cote 10, "Catégories tensorielles : notes manuscrites (s.d.), tapuscrits (s.d.)", from 1958 on.
- **Where they sit:** rank 13 of the plan (tensor categories, cycles, L-functions; tags "tensor, motives"): the Tannakian foundations of motives. Cote 10 runs over 134_MPL_10_002-049, this package, 136_MPL_10_099-150 and 137_MPL_10_151-198.

- **Our results to use and check:** none on these subjects.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** the motives notes ask whether motives are built from cote 16's Lefschetz correspondence rings (MPL_14_002-044 §2, MPL_15_195-228+18_002-017 §2); MPL_15_087-138 §111 computes the Tate tensor subcategory over ℂ; MPL_15_195-228+18_002-017 S15 compares claim by claim with rational Nori motives.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 10: is a group known from its representations? With the tensor law, yes; a fibre functor amounts to an algebra and ⊗ to one more operation; then the constraints (associativity, commutativity, unit, duals); page 114 holds the nine-chapter plan of an unwritten treatise. Hua, findings register, **10-numerical-character-rank-one-torus** (our scans 063-066; his Lean card: statement, no verdict): a Tannakian category whose band is a one-dimensional torus is fixed by its "numerical characters" (classes of simple objects, structure constants of K, centres with Brauer classes, rank). Our intake: new to us.
