## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** scans 66-113 of cote 34, "SGA 7": manuscript notes, about 1967-1973.
- **Where they sit:** SGA 7, Groupes de monodromie en géométrie algébrique (Bures, 1967-69), in the SGA 4, 5 and 7 drafts (plan rank 21). Cote 34 is also in MPL_33_147-181+34_002-017 (returned), 213_MPL_34_018-065, 215_MPL_34_114-161 and 216_MPL_34_162-210; the plan of SGA 7 (cote 35) is in 63_MPL_35+48+102+127.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** the three matched rows (LM7 E.23, LM4 D.12, LM4 F.7) are tower rows. Possibly **EXIT_TOWER E.24** (programme map): its arithmetic image preserves the boundary-inertia classes, and the inertia at a degeneration acts by the Picard-Lefschetz transvections these leaves study.
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say:** MPL_36_002-052 N92 (shared obstruction language does not settle the arithmetic register); MPL_33_147-181+34_002-017 8.3 (the fifteen retained leaves of cote 34 it read) and D21 (two paired research programmes, 33/166); 76 entries in all meet this folder.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 34: mostly Grothendieck's own draft of the first SGA 7 exposés; one question in six forms, monodromy around a degeneration; the monodromy theorem (quasi-unipotence), with inertia in place of a loop; a short arithmetic proof (Frobenius makes the monodromy conjugate to its q-th power, so its eigenvalues are roots of unity) and a geometric one through a simplified degeneration. No Hua findings are listed for these scans.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  - Which exposé drafts these scans hold, in what order, against the published SGA 7 and the plan of cote 35.
  - The arithmetic proof of quasi-unipotence: exact hypotheses (residue field, ℓ ≠ p, finiteness), as on the page.
  - The geometric route: semi-stable reduction, vanishing cycles, the Picard-Lefschetz formula, with what is proved and what is announced.
  - Which questions of MPL_33_147-181+34_002-017 (8.3, D21) and MPL_36_002-052 N92 these scans answer.
