Our intake: both new to us. Read his modernised reading of cote 57 in the background.

  1. On scans 214-219: the exact example, how non-separation arises (the two components of the special fibre), and whether representability is proved there.
  2. On scans 189-193: the hypotheses, the direction proved, and the curve case of the converse; date the page against Danilov's theorem.
  3. Possibly one theme with Hua's 57-analytic-pic-completion-criterion (scans 227-270, next package) and the cone text of cote 101 in Hua's reading (205_MPL_101): a punctured neighbourhood against its completion. Say whether the pages link them.
  4. What the annotations change, and any letter in these scans (writer, recipient, date, subject).
