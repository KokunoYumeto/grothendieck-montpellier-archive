## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** scans 59-98 of cote 91, "Autour de Néron / Greenberg-Néron. Foncteurs Hom (méthodes non-projectives)", notes (1964 to about 1970) and a letter (1967).
- **Where they sit:** rank 20 of the plan (K-theory, Néron, residues, intersections), SGA/EGA line. Previous part: 200_MPL_91_019-058; heights folder: 224_MPL_130.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** none directly; the five rows matched by the tags "etale, abelian" (**LM7 E.23**, **FLAGGED_149 B9**, **LM1 D.11**, **LM4 D.12**, **LM4 F.7**) are about sections, points and GT̂ torsion. Instead the pages may open up Hom and Aut schemes without projectivity.
- **Our results to use and check:** none on these subjects.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** MPL_31_002-051 N070 flags the author's missing noetherian assumptions; MPL_22+23_002-016 P05 and MPL_31_002-051's numeral index also cite cote 91.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 91: spaces of maps without a Hilbert scheme, by rigidification (near a given map, X → Y is fixed by its restriction to a thickened finite Z if that restriction is unramified); a letter of September 1967 systematizes the key step of Matsumura's proof on Aut of a proper variety; half the folder is on Weil restriction and its generalization. Hua, findings register, **91-local-functor-right-adjoint** (our scans 061-080; published case by case, per his register): Weil restriction and the Greenberg functor as cases of the right adjoint W^a of a functor "of local nature"; **91-weil-restriction-finite-etale-criterion** (our scan 059; his search found no reference): equivalent conditions on a finite locally free T → S, starting with ν(s) locally constant. Our intake: new to us.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. On scans 061-080: is "of local nature" defined, and is each property of W^a proved, under which hypotheses? Is the unified statement in print?
  2. Give the criterion's full list of equivalences: it starts in 200_MPL_91_019-058 and ends on scan 059.
  3. Mark each place where a noetherian or finiteness hypothesis is used but not stated (MPL_31_002-051 N070).
  4. If the 1967 letter is here: recipient, date, and how it differs from Matsumura's argument (possibly Matsumura-Oort 1967, the published theorem on Aut of proper schemes).
