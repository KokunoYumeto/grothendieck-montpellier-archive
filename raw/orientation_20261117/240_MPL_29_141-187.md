## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** scans 141-187 of cote 29, "Groupe fondamental [Autour de SGA 4 et SGA 7]": letters (1959, 1967, 1969), annotated typescripts, notes.
- **Where they sit:** rank 24 of the plan (SGA 3 and the re-editions; tag "groupschemes"). Cote 29 runs from 237_MPL_29_002-048 to 241_MPL_29_188-217+37_002-022.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** the one matched row, **LM1 E.11** (the source theorem for arbitrary separated locally finitely presented group schemes), came through the tag; it bears only on general group schemes. They may open up instead: ramified coverings, the fundamental group scheme, torsors under discrete groups.
- **Our results to use and check:** **LM1 E.2** (claimed: fibrewise-unipotent sections of closed linear group schemes over Q-algebras are uniquely exp(t log g)), if such sections appear.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** none; MPL_11_043-058+56 N063 and N100 (cote 56) match by subject only.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 29: coverings with ramification; a tame covering carries a structure, not only a property; the folder opens with Murre's doubts (writing SGA 1's tame exposé) and Grothendieck's reply listing five false statements; the 1967 "donnée de ramification" is an arbitrary catalogue giving the same category. Hua, findings register, **29-fundamental-group-scheme-via-torsors** (our scans 141-143; scans 125-130, 137-140 in 239_MPL_29_095-140): the fundamental group scheme by pro-representability of pointed torsors under finite group schemes, without Tannakian input, lim ₙX on an abelian variety; already credited in the literature, per his register. **29-h1-five-classes-infinite-group** (our scans 171-172; unsearched): G-torsors for a discrete G over five classes of covering families. Our intake: new to us.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. On scans 141-143: the definitions, the infinitesimal part, the abelian-variety computation, and the date (possibly it anticipates Nori's fundamental group scheme).
  2. On scans 171-172: the five classes, each comparison claimed, its hypotheses (the Dedekind case) and its proof.
  3. Each letter: correspondent, date, and the statements it corrects.
  4. Where the ramification data are defined or changed, and the ties to SGA 1, SGA 4 and SGA 7.
