## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** scans 45-86 of cote 158, "[Autour des Dérivateurs]", manuscript notes of about 1990-1991.
- **Where they sit:** Les Dérivateurs (cotes 157-160): totality, exactness and asphericity of small categories. Cote 158 begins in 67_MPL_158_002-044.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **TOTASPH D.7** and **RESIDUE F.5**: does total W∞-asphericity of a connected category force exactness of colim into Ab? Possibly the finite case follows if Hua's 158-finite-category-cofiltered holds.
  - **TOTASPH F.27** and **RESIDUE F.6**: composition of W∞-dwyerian sieves.
  - **RESIDUE F.7**: universal Cat-colimit shapes without a weakly terminal object.
  - **GALOIS B.13**: accessibility of the localizer from the derivator axioms alone.
- **Our results to use and check:** the chain filtered ⊊ exact ⊊ total ⊊ quasi-filtered (programme map; our runs' claims): does it survive for finite categories?
- **What other packages' notes say:** our digest lists none.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register, 158-finite-monoid-right-zero (scans 045-047): a finite monoid in which any u, v have uu' = vv', with trivial group completion, has p with up = p for all u, hence is cofiltered; his Lean card marks it false as on the page (our digest cuts the rest). 158-finite-category-cofiltered (scans 051-054): a finite category in which every connected presheaf has a simply connected category of elements is cofiltered, so for finite categories that condition, tied on their p. 70 (our scan 069) to total asphericity of X°, is cofilteredness; no reference found. Our intake: new to us.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  - Scans 045-047: the monoid statement as written, the hypothesis that fails, a corrected form.
  - Scans 051-054 and 069: the cofilteredness claim and its link to total asphericity, with the variance (X or X°) exact.
  - Whether, for finite categories, filtered, exact and total coincide on these leaves, and what that gives for TOTASPH D.7.
  - The definitions of total, exact and aspherical used here, against those of our runs.
