## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** scans 1-45 of cote 83, "[Icosaèdres, bi-icosaèdres et algèbres d'Azumaya de rang 4] : notes manuscrites" (1982-1983, 1986).
- **Where they sit:** a probe package of the plan (tag "maps"), after La Longue Marche (1981), beside the congruence maps of cote 79 (51_MPL_79_001-045 to 113_MPL_79_245-297). Cote 83 goes on in 114_MPL_83_046-089, 115_MPL_83_090-133, 116_MPL_83_134-177 and 117_MPL_83_178-222.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38**: 𝒦₅ = 1 is its main unresolved assertion; possibly these pages model level 5, because the icosahedron is the level-5 congruence map (A₅ ≅ PSL₂(F₅)).
  - **PUSH_20261114 Part2Succe 26**: characters of F ∈ GT̂₀ with λ(F) ≡ ±1 mod 5 on the cyclic degree-six cover branched at the twelve cusps, through the centralizer of A₅.
  - **FERMAT_SUCCESSOR C.20** and **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.41**: the remaining icosahedral membership tests; the icosahedral marked-orbit locus.
  - **LM4 F.7**: centralizers of a geometric A₅ in Γ^+_{0,ν}.
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** none.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 83: the icosahedron found among 2×2 matrices (over F₄, twelve matrices of trace 1 and suitable determinant, joined when the trace of their product takes a given value; the same from nilpotent matrices mod 5, and mod n the congruence maps of cote 79); then the rank-4 Azumaya algebra over any base, 2 not inverted: trace and determinant of two elements and the trace of their product rebuild the algebra they generate, with a cross-ratio of the two pairs of fixed points deciding when it is the whole algebra; for three elements tr(xyz) is no longer determined. No finding of his register is listed for these scans.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. The exact vertex set and edge rule over F₄ and mod 5; check twelve vertices, thirty edges, five per vertex, and the A₅ action.
  2. The five trace invariants and the cross-ratio criterion over ℤ: statement, proof, and what changes at 2.
  3. Any A₅-centralizer, twelve-point-orbit or degree-six-cover computation usable for PUSH_20261114 Part2Succe 26 and LM4 F.7.
  4. What the "bi-icosaèdres" of the title are, and whether they are tied to congruence maps or to a Galois action.
