Scans 2-56 of cote 80 are package 170_MPL_80_002-056; scans 38-55 of cote 81 are in 172_MPL_81_038-055+154_046-076.

The pages may open instead: arrangements rebuilt from their skeleton; combinatorial criteria for immersions.
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** none in our digest.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 80: pseudolines in the real projective plane form a map; does the "dimension 1" arrangement (each line with the order of its crossings) determine the "dimension 2" drawing? His answer, stated without full proof: yes, bigons (a line with two crossings) excepted, and then the symmetries of the skeleton are symmetries of the drawing; the route: three pseudolines (half an octahedron), a bookkeeping of orientations, the circular order at a crossing read from a distant line; written three times. Hua, modernised reading of cote 81: a disc cut along chords, networks on the sphere, points on a circle in packets: what in each arrangement depends only on the combinatorics; the inventory's title is not read on the pages (cut in our digest). No findings of his project here in our digest.
