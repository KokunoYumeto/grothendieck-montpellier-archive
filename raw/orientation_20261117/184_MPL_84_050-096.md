- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register: 84-two-regular-triplet-classification (our scans 050-054; matched on isomorphism classes, Voight): with 2 a non-zero-divisor, quadratic covers are triples (L, δ, T₀) with T₀² ≡ δ mod 4. 84-product-quadratic-algebras-formula (our scans 063-080, 089-091; matched in the literature): over any base, without dividing by 2, quadratic algebras have a commutative product with L(X₁ * X₂) = L₁ ⊗ L₂ and, in split coordinates, b = b₁b₂, c = c₁b₂² + c₂b₁² − 4c₁c₂, hence δ = δ₁δ₂. 84-product-coinvariants-one-etale (our scans 084-087; candidate): A₁ * A₂ → A₁ ⊗ A₂ identifies the product with the invariants of σ₁ ⊗ σ₂, universally, iff at each point of residue characteristic 2 one of A₁, A₂ is étale.
