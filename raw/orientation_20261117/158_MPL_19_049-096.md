## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** scans 49-96 of cote 19, "Topos : notes manuscrites", from 1958 to 1973.
- **Where they sit:** rank 16 of the plan (categories, universal algebra, topos; tags "topos, derivators"): the topos foundations of SGA 4, on which Les Dérivateurs later builds.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** none directly; the 45 matched rows (from TOTASPH, CATHOT, GALOIS, RESIDUE, POLES, BISITE) came through those tags and concern localizers, asphericity and derivators. What the pages may open up instead: forgetful functors of structures, and chains of adjoints between presheaf topoi, such as the u_! ⊣ u* ⊣ u_* on which the derivator axioms rest.
- **Our results to use and check:** none on these subjects.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** MPL_7_061-072+8_001-078 cites cote 19 in its note on scan 078.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register, **19-adjoint-chains-length-n** (their pp. 79-80; our scans 080-081; unsearched): for every n some presheaf topoi carry a chain of n successive adjoints, none fully faithful; whether a chain can go on indefinitely both ways is left open on the page. **19-faithful-transportable-is-forgetful** (their pp. 71-75; our scans 072-076): a functor is the forgetful functor of a structure exactly when it is faithful and transportable (our digest cuts the statement; his register calls it the standard characterization). Our intake: new to us. Read his modernised reading of cote 19 in the background.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. On scans 080-081: the examples for each n, why no functor is fully faithful, and the question exactly as written; settle it if the notes can.
  2. On scans 072-076: the definitions of structure and of transportable, and the hypotheses; set the page against Bourbaki's species of structures and SGA 4, by date.
  3. Every statement on the functors u_!, u*, u_* induced by a functor between small categories, and on their further adjoints.
  4. A date for each part within 1958-1973.
