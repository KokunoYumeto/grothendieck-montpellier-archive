## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 138, scans 90-133, the end of "Cartes. Etude arithmétique (1976, 77 ?)": undated handwritten notes, about 1976-1978. Scans 1-45 and 46-89 are packages 54_MPL_138_001-045 and 101_MPL_138_046-089.
- **Where they sit:** plan rank 10 (the rest of 138, III.7): maps, Galois action, Teichmüller; on the way to the Esquisse, La Longue Marche (1981) and the tower (cotes 141-152).
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **EXIT_TOWER E.24**: the arithmetic image on the geometric modular groups; Galois acting on maps is its (0,4) level.
  - **LM4 D.12**: torsion in GT̂_0 conjugate to (-1,1); possibly, as complex conjugation acting on maps is its first case.
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38**: the congruence kernels K_N (K_5 = 1 open); maps on modular curves are natural examples.
  - **LM7_PUSH E.3**: whether GT̂ reaches the extra CM coset; possibly, through Fermat maps.
- **Our results to use and check:** none named in our digest.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** none in our digest.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 138: a map on the sphere gives essentially one way to see the sphere as the completed complex line. Hua, findings register, **138-operations-belyi-extending** (their pp. 122-124, 127; our scans 123-125, 128; no reference found by his search; new to us): an "operation" on oriented maps, given by a subset A of the reference sphere pulled back along each map's cover, is, apart from one exceptional case, composition with a cover φ of the sphere ramified only over 0, 1, ∞, with φ⁻¹{0,1,∞} ⊃ {0,1,∞} and ramification 2 over 1, so that A = φ⁻¹([0,1]) (cut in our digest).
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. Scans 123-125, 128: the definition of an operation, the exceptional case, and the proof; compare possibly M. Wood's "Belyi-extending maps" (2006), a reference Hua's search may have missed.
  2. Whether operations commute with the Galois action, and whether they separate Galois orbits of maps.
  3. The conclusions of the study: tables of maps with their Galois orbits, fields of moduli, any plan.
  4. Any trace of the tower or of a Teichmüller-type group in these last pages.
