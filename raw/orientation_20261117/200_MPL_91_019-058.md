## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 91, scans 19-58, from "Autour de Néron / Greenberg-Néron. Foncteurs Hom (méthodes non-projectives)": handwritten notes and a letter (1967), 1964 to about 1970.
- **Where they sit:** plan rank 20 (K-theory, Néron, residues, intersections), the FGA line.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** none of ours concerns cote 91; the five matched by subject (LM7 E.23, FLAGGED_149 B9, LM1 D.11, LM4 D.12, LM4 F.7) are about sections, points and GT̂. Possibly **LM1 D.11** (integral μ_2-extensions at 2), if the Néron or Greenberg part treats integral models of finite group schemes. The pages may open instead: Hom and Aut schemes of proper non-projective schemes.
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** MPL_31_002-051 N070 (self-flagged): the author's missing noetherian assumptions; repeat that check here.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 91: Hom(X,Y) built without an embedding by rigidification (near a given map, a map is fixed by its unramified restriction to a finite thickened piece Z; maps from a finite Z form a Weil restriction); a letter of September 1967 here systematizes the central step of Matsumura's proof that the automorphism group of a proper variety is algebraic. Hua, findings register (candidates without a reference found; new to us): **91-hom-unramified-rigidification** (scans 019-021, 031-032), Hom_S(X,Y;φ) → Hom_S(Z,Y) representable by unramified morphisms; **91-res-representable-by-scheme** (scans 025-029, 032), Res_{X/S}(P), hence Hom_S(X,Y), a scheme under three conditions on closed subschemes Z_i; **91-weil-restriction-finite-etale-criterion** (scans 054, 056-058), equivalent conditions on a finite locally free T → S, starting with local constancy of the number of geometric points of its fibres.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. Each finding against its scans: exact hypotheses, proved or asserted, and whether a scheme (not an algebraic space) is reached; compare Matsumura-Oort (1967).
  2. The 1967 letter: correspondent, date, and what it adds to Matsumura's argument.
  3. The full list of equivalent conditions in the finite-étale criterion, with proof.
  4. Where Néron models or the Greenberg functor enter these scans.
