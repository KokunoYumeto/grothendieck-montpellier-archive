## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 43, scans 46-93, from "Dualité des groupes. Jacobiennes généralisées etc": notes and copies of handwritten notes, from 1961 to about 1968. Scans 94-142 are returned (MPL_43_094-142).
- **Where they sit:** plan rank 19 (duality of groups, Picard, abelian varieties): duality of commutative algebraic groups and generalized Jacobians (the Rosenlicht-Serre line; our gloss).
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** none of ours concerns cote 43; the 37 rows matched by subject are about GT̂, sections and CM. Possibly:
  - **LM7 E.23**: metabelian section classes of U_3/Q; the abelian layer of π_1 of an affine curve is the Tate module of its generalized Jacobian.
  - **LM6 E.21**: the ramified class-two inputs on the cubic; same reason.
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** MPL_57_271-315 R06: a reading question on the finite-subgroup wording in the Igusa construction, citing cote 43.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register, **43-local-cohomology-concentration** (their pp. 70-74, our scans 071-075; conjecture, unsearched; new to us): for a regular local ring A of dimension d containing an algebraically closed field k, and a commutative algebraic group G over k, a statement on the Zariski local cohomology H^i_a (cut in our digest; its id says concentration). Hua's modernised reading of cote 43 did not reach our digest.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. Scans 071-075: the full statement (which degrees, which coefficients), and its proof for G_a, G_m and abelian varieties.
  2. Which duality is meant (Cartier duality, duality of generalized Jacobians, Picard against Albanese), and what is proved.
  3. The construction of generalized Jacobians (modulus, local symbols); does π_1^ab of an affine curve appear as a Tate module? Compare LM7 E.23.
  4. Whose notes the copies are, and their dates.
  5. The Igusa-construction passage of R06, if it is here.
