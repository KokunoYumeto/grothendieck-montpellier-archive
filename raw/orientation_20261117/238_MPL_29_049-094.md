## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 29, scans 49-94, from the box "Groupe fondamental [Autour de SGA 4 et SGA 7]" (1959-1969): letters, annotated typescripts and copies, notes on tamely ramified coverings.
- **Where they sit:** the fundamental group from SGA 1 to SGA 7; plan rank 24 (SGA 3 and the reeditions). The rest of cote 29: 237_MPL_29_002-048, 239_MPL_29_095-140, 240_MPL_29_141-187, 241_MPL_29_188-217+37_002-022.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** the one match, **LM1 E.11** (the source theorem for arbitrary separated locally finitely presented group schemes), comes from the subject "group schemes" and bears only if group schemes appear. What they may open up: tame π₁ defined by ramification data, and the extension class of a formal neighbourhood (Hua's finding below), possibly the material of the Grothendieck-Murre notes on the tame π₁ of a formal neighbourhood of a normal crossings divisor (LNM 208; our guess from that finding).
- **Our results to use and check:** none bearing directly (LM1 E.2, unipotent sections over Q-algebras, only if group schemes appear).
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** none on these scans (MPL_11_043-058+56 N063 and N100 concern group schemes in cote 56).
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 29: what a covering is when ramification is allowed; a tame covering carries a structure, not a property; the opening leaves record the exchange with J.-P. Murre where Grothendieck lists five false statements of his notes; the box then studies the category of coverings, the 1967 "donnée de ramification" being an arbitrary scaffolding. Hua, findings register, 29-formal-tame-pi1-self-intersection (our scans 088-094): for a regular formal scheme whose special fibre X_o is a component of a normal crossings divisor D, π₁ᵗ(𝔛/D) is a central extension of π₁ᵗ(X_o/D′_o) by a quotient of μ^∞, with class the Chern class of the self-intersection of X_o.
- **What the notes should look for:** (1) Hua's central extension against scans 088-094: hypotheses, Chern class, the case X_o = P¹; (2) how a ramification datum is defined here; are two data giving one category compared; (3) dates and correspondents of letters in this range; (4) which typed sheets belong to the Murre exposé or to SGA 4/7 drafts, and what his hand changes.
