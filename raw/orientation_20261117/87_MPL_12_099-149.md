## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** scans 99-149 of cote 12, "Généralités (sous-groupes de Galois motiviques) (1964 ?)": manuscript notes and a typescript, 1964 to about 1971.
- **Where they sit:** motives and motivic Galois groups (plan rank 6). Cote 12 precedes in 85_MPL_12_002-050 and 86_MPL_12_051-098; the motives line runs through cotes 11, 14, 15, 16 and 18.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **LM6 A.22**: the full CM group over Q(i); possibly, if the leaves treat CM motives.
  - **FLAGGED_149 B9** (the prime-to-p point question) and **LM7 E.23** are matched too; possibly only through weights and abelian quotients.
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say:** MPL_15_087-138 T26 (the period-conjecture evidence limit), L01 and §2 (motives from cote 16's Lefschetz correspondence rings); MPL_11_043-058+56 N031 and N104; MPL_14_087-132 M01 (a notation table at 14/117; rings and categories in cote 16).
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 12: motives and their realizations, the motivic Galois group, Tate and Hodge as one statement on its size, and weights and level (niveau) as the organizing notions. Hua, findings register, 12-immersion-level-bound (scans 116, 118, 120): for an immersion f: X → Y with X regular and n = dim of the closure of f(X), the constituents of R^i f_* ℚ_ℓ have weight ≤ 2 inf(i, n) and level ≤ inf(i, n − 1), one less than the level allowed for H^i of a smooth n-dimensional variety; no reference found. Our intake: new to us.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  - Scans 116, 118, 120: the bound as written, its hypotheses, and whether it is argued; possibly compare with the weight bounds of Deligne's Weil II (1980) and the niveau of Grothendieck's 1969 note on Hodge's general conjecture.
  - The exact definition of level here, and every statement that uses it.
  - Which motivic Galois subgroups the title refers to (Mumford-Tate, Lefschetz or others), and what is claimed about them.
  - Which of our notes' questions (MPL_15_087-138 T26, §2; MPL_11_043-058+56 N104) these scans answer.
