## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** scans 2-44 of cote 158, "[Autour des Dérivateurs] : notes manuscrites", about 1990-1991.
- **Where they sit:** Les Dérivateurs and the homotopy of Pursuing Stacks (cotes 157-160); rank 9 of the plan (tags "derivators, strata, topos"). Cote 158 goes on in 68_MPL_158_045-086.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **TOTASPH D.7** and **RESIDUE F.5**: does total W∞-asphericity of a connected small category force exact colimits? The monoid criterion below is the one-object case of this kind of question.
  - **RESIDUE F.7**: when universal Cat-colimit comparison forces filteredness, without a weakly terminal object.
  - **RESIDUE A.8** and **BISITE B.21**: bifibrations over small monoids (CATHOT G.10's seven-element monoid; the five-element monoid R); possibly informed by monoid examples here.
  - **TOTASPH F.27** and **RESIDUE F.6**: dwyerienne sieves and total presheaves, if sieves or presheaves on monoids appear.
- **Our results to use and check:** none listed here; the programme map's chain filtered ⊊ exact ⊊ total ⊊ quasi-filtered is the frame.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** none listed.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register, **158-minimal-monoid-cofiltered** (their pp. 21-41; our scans 022-042), a conjecture his project did not search, whose Lean card marks it false as on the page (the verdict is cut off in our digest): a monoid M with an element ψ in uM for every u is cofiltered once every connected right M-set has a simply connected category of elements; otherwise the folder builds such an M-set, M/R_φ, with π₁ of its category of elements mapping onto ℤ. Our intake: new to us. Read his modernised reading of cote 158 in the background.
