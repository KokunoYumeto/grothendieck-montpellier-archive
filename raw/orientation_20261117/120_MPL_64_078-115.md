- **What these pages are:** scans 78-115 of cote 64, "Modules courbes elliptiques en caractéristiques ≠2 : notes manuscrites", from 1980-1982.
- **Where they sit:** rank 10 of the plan, with 64 (tags "maps, teich, galois, crystals"): moduli of elliptic curves, at the base of the tower of La Longue Marche. Cote 64 runs over 118_MPL_64_002-039, 119_MPL_64_040-077, this package and 121_MPL_64_116-157.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **LM4 D.12**: torsion and involutions in the full low-type groups, type (0,4); possibly, as away from 2 the Legendre form ties level-2 elliptic curves to the four-pointed line.
  - **EXIT_TOWER E.24**: the arithmetic image of the geometric modular groups in genus zero.
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38**: congruence kernels at levels 5, 7, 11; possibly, if level structures appear.
  - **LM7_PUSH E.3** and **LM6 A.22**: the CM coset over ℚ(i); possibly, if j = 1728 is treated.
