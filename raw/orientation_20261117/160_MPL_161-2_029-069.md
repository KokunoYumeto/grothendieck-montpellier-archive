- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register, 161-2-vn-regular-cartesian-presheaf-classifier (our scans 042-043; his project calls it settled as far as the C∞ case in print goes): commutative von Neumann regular rings ("pseudo-corps") form a cartesian theory, since every f has a unique idempotent e with ef invertible in eA and (1 − e)f = 0. 161-2-field-classifier-absolutely-flat-site (our scans 042-043, with later pages in 161_MPL_161-2_070-112; candidate): the classifying topos of fields is the topos of sheaves on the opposite of finitely presented absolutely flat rings, for the topology generated by Spec A_f ⊔ Spec A/f → Spec A, with the generic field as structure sheaf. A third entry of his is cut in our digest.
