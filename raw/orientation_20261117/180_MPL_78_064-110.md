## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 78, scans 64-110, from "[Polygones réguliers et polynômes cyclotomiques]": undated handwritten notes, from 1977. The rest of cote 78 is in packages 178_MPL_77_125-158+78_002-016, 179_MPL_78_017-063 and 181_MPL_78_111-130+82_002-028.
- **Where they sit:** plan rank 18, Book IV (polygons, quadrics, graphs): regular polygons defined by their symmetries over any field or ring, characteristic p included.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38**: congruence kernels at levels 5, 7, 11; possibly, as u_α (Hua) has linear part of trace α and determinant 1 (our reading): regular n-gons over F_p are orbits of elements of SL_2(F_p).
  - **PUSH_20261114 Part2Succe 26** (S4): icosahedral characters valued in units of Ẑ[ω]; possibly, through these cyclotomic polynomials.
  - **CYCLOTOMIC_KERNEL D13**: matched by name only (deck groups, commensurators).
- **Our results to use and check:** none named in our digest.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** none in our digest.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register (new to us): **78-closure-criterion-over-rings** (scan 110; no reference found by his search): over any commutative ring, u_α(x, y) = (1 − y + αx, x) satisfies u_α^n = id iff F_n(α) = 0, with F_n(T + T⁻¹) = (T^n − 1)/(T − 1)·T^{−(n−1)/2} for n odd and (T^n − 1)/(T² − 1)·T^{−(n−2)/2} for n even. **78-parabola-p-gon** (scans 088-089, 096, 101; matched by his project through a survey): in characteristic p ≥ 3, α = 2 gives a regular p-gon with no centre, inscribed in a parabola where the rotation is a translation, and Ψ_p ≡ (U − 2)^{(p−1)/2} mod p. **78-strip-2p-gon** (scans 085-086, 095-096, 099-100; no reference found): in odd characteristic p, regular 2p-gons with vertices alternately on two parallel lines, one with α = −2.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. Scan 110: the criterion over rings, zero divisors included; check F_n for small n.
  2. The definition of regularity by symmetries, and every regular n-gon in characteristic p; test both characteristic-p findings.
  3. The polynomials F_n and Ψ_n, their link to cyclotomic polynomials and to 2cos(2π/n) (compare cote 70), and their factorization mod p.
  4. Whether the polygons are read as orbits in SL_2 or in congruence quotients; compare B.38.
