- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **TOTASPH D.7** and **RESIDUE F.5**: does total W_∞-asphericity of a small connected A force exactness of colim_A on Ab^A? Cotes 113-114.

  - **RESIDUE I.7**: accessible localizers between integral homology equivalences and W_0. Cotes 112-113.
  - **GALOIS G.6**: a functorial higher-coefficient derivator D' with W_D' = W_lc. Cote 112.
  - **BISITE F.18**: derivators indexed by strict higher categories. Cote 115.
