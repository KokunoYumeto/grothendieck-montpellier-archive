## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 64, scans 40-77, from "Modules courbes elliptiques en caractéristiques ≠2": undated handwritten notes, from 1980-1982. The rest of the cote is in packages 118_MPL_64_002-039, 120_MPL_64_078-115 and 121_MPL_64_116-157.
- **Where they sit:** plan rank 10 with 64 (maps, Teichmüller, Galois, crystals): the moduli of elliptic curves, the (1,1) level of the tower, in the years of La Longue Marche (1981).
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38**: the congruence kernels K_N (K_5 = 1 open; levels 7 and 11, genera 3 and 26); the modular curves of elliptic curves with level structure are these quotients.
  - **LM4 D.12**: torsion in GT̂_0 and the full low-type groups; possibly, as automorphisms of orders 4 and 6 of elliptic curves give torsion at the (1,1) level.
  - **EXIT_TOWER E.24**: the arithmetic image on the geometric modular groups; Galois acting on the (1,1) level.
  - **ANABELIAN_SUCCESSOR A.57** (programme map): the universal supersingular-correspondence assertion is not established; possibly, if the pages treat supersingular curves in characteristic p.
- **Our results to use and check:** none named in our digest.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** MPL_93_002-046: E14 (formal deformation against the coarse invariant, a conjecture), E16 (families realizing the two smallest displayed orders), C29 (a first-order bridge back to the elliptic deformation sheet), P09 (what a comparison with cotes 159 and 64 must establish).
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** his findings and his modernised reading of cote 64 did not reach our digest; read them in your results block and credit them there.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. How the moduli are built in characteristic ≠ 2: Legendre form, level-2 structure, the λ-line and its S_3 action, the points j = 0 and 1728; is the moduli stack ("multiplicité") treated?
  2. The deformation sheet and the coarse invariant: settle E14, test E16 and C29 on these pages, and record what P09 asks of cote 64.
  3. Fundamental groups: SL_2(Z), its profinite and congruence completions, any congruence-subgroup statement; compare B.38.
  4. Characteristic p: Hasse invariant, supersingular points, Frobenius, any crystalline argument.
