- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 34: mostly Grothendieck's own draft of the first exposés of SGA 7 (Bures, 1967-1969); monodromy around a degeneration is quasi-unipotent, with an arithmetic proof (the monodromy is conjugate to its q-th power, so its eigenvalues are roots of unity) and a geometric one. Hua, findings register, **34-lci-local-1-asphericity** (their pp. 153-155; our scans 154-156; his search found no reference): over a strictly local trait, f flat with lci closed fibre whose non-smooth locus has codimension ≥ 2 is locally 1-aspherical for primes ≠ p; for f proper, specialization H¹(X₀, G) → H¹(X_η̄, G) is bijective for finite G of order prime to p. Our intake: new to us.
