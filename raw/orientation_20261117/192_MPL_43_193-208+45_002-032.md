## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** the end of cote 43, "Dualité des groupes. Jacobiennes généralisées etc" (scans 193-208; notes and copies, 1961 to about 1968), and cote 45, "Groupes semi-simples" (scans 2-32; notes and an annotated typescript of 1961).
- **Where they sit:** rank 19 of the plan (duality of groups, Picard, abelian varieties), in the SGA line. Earlier parts of cote 43: 56_MPL_43_001-045, 189_MPL_43_046-093, 191_MPL_43_143-192.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** the 36 rows shown were matched by the tags "abelian, galois" and are about GT̂, sections and mapping class groups. Possibly **LM7 E.23** and **LM6 E.21** (section classes of the metabelian and class-two quotients of the Fermat cubic), because their abelian layer is the Tate module of a generalized Jacobian: record any statement on generalized Jacobians of affine curves.
- **Our results to use and check:** none on these subjects.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** MPL_57_271-315 R06, a reading question on the finite-subgroup wording in the Igusa construction; see whether these pages explain it.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register, **45-h1-surjectivity-dim-one** (their pp. 3-10; our scans 004-011), a conjecture his project did not search: over a field k with Br(k′) = 0 for every finite separable k′/k, H¹(k, G) → H¹(k, G′) is surjective, in flat cohomology (our digest cuts the statement there; read it whole in the background). Our intake: new to us. Read his modernised reading of cotes 43 and 45 in the background and credit it when used.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. On scans 004-011: what G → G′ is, why flat cohomology (inseparable isogenies?), and the proof. Possibly compare with Serre's Conjecture I and Steinberg's theorem (1965), since Br(k′) = 0 for all finite separable k′ is Serre's "dimension ≤ 1"; a 1961 date would put the page before Steinberg.
  2. On scans 193-208: which duality of groups is stated (Cartier duality, duality of generalized Jacobians), with its hypotheses.
  3. The annotations of the 1961 typescript: corrections, added hypotheses, deletions.
  4. Links to the Picard typescript of cote 57 (225_MPL_57_002-045 to 230_MPL_57_226-270).
