- **What these pages are:** cote 109 (scans 2-38), "Tables [Corrections et préparation à l'édition]" (1983-1984): notes and annotated copies preparing the edition of Pursuing Stacks.
- **Where they sit:** Pursuing Stacks (plan rank 9: the handwritten leaves of Pursuing Stacks, 158 and the dossier). Neighbours: 71_MPL_118 (cohomology properties of maps in Cat) and cote 158 (67_MPL_158_002-044, 68_MPL_158_045-086).
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** the 49 matches come from the subjects "derivators, strata, topos"; an editorial folder settles none of them. Its tables may still locate, by page of Pursuing Stacks, notions our runs use: possibly asphericity and total asphericity (**TOTASPH D.7**, **TOTASPH F.27**) and finite tests for homotopy types (**CATHOT D.6**, the two-sphere test).
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** none yet.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 109: in 1983, after twelve years of mathematical silence, he writes Pursuing Stacks in English, born of a letter to Daniel Quillen, and plans it as the first volume of a series, "Réflexions Mathématiques"; this folder is the workshop of that edition and proves nothing: a plan, a table of notations, an index of terms, instructions for the printer, and readers for the English (our digest cuts the rest). No finding of his is listed.
- **What the notes should look for:** (1) the planned edition: series title, volumes, chapter plan; (2) the table of notations and the index, every entry with its page reference, so that other packages can locate definitions; (3) each correction he lists, the mathematical ones above all: which statements change; (4) names of readers or helpers, and dates; (5) whether a correction touches asphericity, test categories or modelizers (possibly TOTASPH D.7, CATHOT D.6).
