## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** all of cote 98 (scans 2-44), "Schémas de Hilbert. Normes : notes manuscrites", from 1966-1967.
- **Where they sit:** rank 20 of the plan (K-theory, Néron, residues, intersections), in the SGA/EGA line: tools for moduli spaces.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** none directly. The five matched rows (**LM7 E.23**, **FLAGGED_149 B9**, **LM1 D.11**, **LM4 D.12**, **LM4 F.7**) came through the tags "etale, abelian" and concern sections, points and GT̂ torsion. What the pages may open up instead: norms and traces for finite locally free morphisms in general, and bounded families as the finiteness input for moduli.
- **Our results to use and check:** none on these subjects.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** the entries listed (MPL_22+23_002-016 P03, MPL_36_002-052 §102, MPL_131 10, MPL_31_002-051 §117) are register-disposition tables that meet this folder by subject; none reads cote 98.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 98: every norm and trace comes from one canonical map, S → Sym^r(T) for a finite cover T → S of degree r; its properties become axioms for G-sets, schemes and topological coverings; G-sets lead to group cohomology read as cohomology of a space, with a spectral sequence containing Leray, Cartan-Leray and Hochschild-Serre; then bounded families, and when sheaves over different base fields are "the same". Hua, findings register, **98-axiomatic-norms-can-section** (our scans 011-014; his Lean card: partly proved): axioms for a class of morphisms with a degree and canonical sections Can_{X/Y}: Y → Sym^r_Y(X). Our intake: new to us.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. Test Hua's 98-axiomatic-norms-can-section on scans 011-014: the exact axioms (stability, the quotients (X^n_Y)/H, the degree formula), and which of them the pages prove.
  2. Whether Can_{X/Y} is built for every finite locally free X → Y; possibly compare with Deligne's trace for finite flat morphisms (SGA 4 XVII 6.3), the published construction of the same trace.
  3. Write out the G-set spectral sequence as on the page and check each special case claimed.
  4. The definition of a bounded family and the criterion for "the same sheaf" over different fields.
