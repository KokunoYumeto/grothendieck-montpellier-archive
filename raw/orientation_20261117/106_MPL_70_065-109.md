## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 70, scans 65-109, the second part of "Réalisations géométriques de structures combinatoires (n-polyèdres, n-hyperpolyèdres [2-polyèdres réguliers]…) (Vieilles rédactions)": handwritten old drafts, from 1976. Scans 17-64 are package 105_MPL_70_017-064.
- **Where they sit:** plan rank 10 (maps on surfaces and the icosahedron), beside cote 138 (maps, 1976-78) and cotes 85-86 (regular polyhedra).
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38**: K_5 = 1 is open; the icosahedron reduced mod 5 is possibly the finite picture of level five.
  - **LM4 F.7** and **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.41**: centralizers of a geometric A_5; Δ along the icosahedral marked-orbit loci.
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.12**: quadratic and quintic characters on GT̂; possibly, through the field Q(√5) of the icosahedral realisation.
- **Our results to use and check:** none named in our digest.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** none in our digest.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register, **70-icosahedron-integral-faithful** (their pp. 74-75, 82-94, 101-102, 106; our scans 075-076, 083-095, 102-103, 107; no reference found by his search; new to us): the folder writes an affine realisation of the regular icosahedron over Z[α]/(α²+α−1) by three reflections in a vertex frame, and, with his edition's completion, it stays a faithful icosahedron after reduction at every prime, 2 and 5 included (twelve distinct vertices, …; cut in our digest). His **70-universal-formulae-double-cosines** continues on their pp. 64-69, our scans 065-070 (see package 105).
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. The realisation over Z[α]: the three reflections, and faithfulness of every reduction; separate what is on the page from Hua's completion. At 2, α²+α−1 stays irreducible (F_4); at 5 it is a square: check whether A_5 ≅ SL_2(F_4) ≅ PSL_2(F_5) appears (our pointer).
  2. Scans 065-070: the end of the universal formulae with 2cos(2π/p_i).
  3. Whether the reductions are read as finite regular maps or as congruence quotients; compare LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38.
  4. Any n-hyperpolyhedron (regular 4-polytopes) realised, and over which ring.
