## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** all of cote 21 (scans 2-29), "[Chapitre] 0 III": an annotated typescript and copies, with manuscript notes, about 1960-1967.
- **Where they sit:** the EGA plans (plan rank 3, with cotes 127 and 128): preliminaries on depth for sheaves of sets and for stacks, the material of SGA 2 XIV §1 by Hua's literature check.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** none of our open rows is matched. The pages may open instead extension and purity questions: when an object known off a closed subset extends, pointwise and for stacks.
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say:** none cite cote 21 directly; MPL_22+23_002-016 P02 and P03 (the EGA chapter plan), MPL_131 10 (descent and representable functors) and MPL_57_271-315 8 (steps the manuscript flags as unverified) meet this folder.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 21: if an object is known off a small closed part, is it known everywhere (Hartogs)? Depth along the closed part, under a jacket titled "profondeur ≥ 2 pour faisceaux d'ensembles": (P1) sections determined off the closed set, (P2) sections extend; the aim is pointwise, computable tests; eleven typed leaves, then manuscript. Findings register: 21-depth-sheaves-of-sets-pointwise (scans 016-021, 028-029; matched in substance to SGA 2 XIV §1) and 21-stack-p3-pointwise (scans 022-027: for any fibered category on a noetherian spectral space, restriction off Y is an equivalence iff it is so on each localization at x ∈ Y; no reference found). Our intake: new to us.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  - Scans 016-021 and 028-029 against SGA 2 XIV §1: what the leaves add or state differently.
  - Scans 022-027: the stack criterion (P3) as written, with its proof and hypotheses.
  - Typed versus handwritten leaves: dates, and which chapter 0 (of EGA III or IV) they were for.
  - Possibly the use of (P3) for the stack of étale covers, which gives purity and Lefschetz statements for π₁, the subject of cote 58 (197_MPL_59+58_002-019).
