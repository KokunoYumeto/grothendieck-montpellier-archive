## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** all of cote 101 (scans 2-43), "Cohomologie locale : notes manuscrites", from 1967-1968, with an older text marked "Notes antiques 1958" (per Hua's reading).
- **Where they sit:** rank 20 of the plan (K-theory, Néron, residues, intersections); the local-cohomology line of SGA 2.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** none directly; the five rows matched by the tags "etale, abelian" (**LM7 E.23**, **FLAGGED_149 B9**, **LM1 D.11**, **LM4 D.12**, **LM4 F.7**) concern sections, points and GT̂ torsion. What the pages may open up instead: local duality for sheaves that do not come from modules, and the early search for the canonical module.
- **Our results to use and check:** none on these subjects.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** only register-disposition tables matched by subject (MPL_22+23_002-016 P03, MPL_36_002-052 §102, MPL_131 10, MPL_31_002-051 §117).
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 101: three texts; a short comparison of the cohomology of a projective variety with the local cohomology of its cone; an attempt to extend local duality to all sheaves of modules, working in dimension 1 and failing in dimension 2, where topological duals would be needed; and the "Notes antiques 1958": the fundamental (canonical) module, local duality, then a graph of implications among conditions for Cohen-Macaulay and Gorenstein rings, ending in "Problème A, B, C". Hua, findings register: **101-sheaf-duality-dim-one** (our scans 011-012; his Lean card: holds) and **101-sheaf-duality-dim-two-fails** (our scans 011, 015-016; his search found no reference): the duality map over a complete DVR, and its failure for j_!𝒪_U on a complete regular local ring of dimension 2. Our intake: new to us.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. Problems A, B and C verbatim, with the implication graph; for each, whether it is settled today, and by whom.
  2. Scans 011-016: the exact duality maps, the dimension-1 proof, the dimension-2 failure, and the remark on topological duals.
  3. Date evidence for "1958" against the folder's 1967-1968.
  4. The cone comparison of the first text; possibly one theme with 57-cone-completion-class-group (Hua, findings register; in 229_MPL_57_180-225), Pic at the vertex of a cone against its completion.
