- **Where they sit:** plan rank 6 (motives): the standard conjectures and motives, beside cote 16 (Lefschetz rings) and cote 15.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** none of ours concerns cote 14; the three rows matched by subject (LM7 E.23, FLAGGED_149 B9, LM6 A.22) are about sections and CM. Possibly **LM6 A.22** (the full CM group over Q(i)), if the pages test Hodge or Tate on CM abelian varieties. The pages may open instead: the first exact form of the standard conjectures.
- **Our results to use and check:** none.

- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 14: invariants deciding when cycles are equivalent; an "Albanese equivalence" between rational and algebraic equivalence; the hyperplane section (Lefschetz) as guiding image. A letter to Serre of 27 August 1965, in the middle of the dossier, states conjectures A and B, the origin of the standard conjectures, says they would give the Weil conjectures and motives, and that the decisive step is missing. What is proved is in the lowest degree, read on two abelian varieties attached to the variety.
