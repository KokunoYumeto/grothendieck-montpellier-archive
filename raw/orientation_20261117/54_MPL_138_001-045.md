- **What these pages are:** cote 138, scans 1-45, the opening of "Cartes. Etude arithmétique (1976, 77 ?)": an order form and undated handwritten notes, about 1976-1978. Scans 46-89 and 90-133 are packages 101_MPL_138_046-089 and 102_MPL_138_090-133.
- **Where they sit:** the plan's probe for cote 138 (maps, Galois): the arithmetic of maps that leads to the Esquisse, La Longue Marche (1981) and the tower of cotes 141-152.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **EXIT_TOWER E.24**: the arithmetic image on the geometric modular groups of cote 143; Galois acting on maps is the action on π̂_1 of the line minus three points, the (0,4) level.
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38**: the congruence kernels K_N (K_5 = 1 open; levels 7 and 11, genera 3 and 26, undecided); maps on modular curves are natural examples here.
  - **LM4 D.12**: torsion in GT̂_0 conjugate to (-1,1); possibly, through complex conjugation acting on maps.
  - **LM6 A.22** and **LM7_PUSH E.3**: the CM group and the CM coset of the Fermat tower; possibly, as Fermat curves are covers of the line branched over three points.
