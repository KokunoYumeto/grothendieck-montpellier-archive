## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** scans 95-140 of cote 29, "Groupe fondamental [Autour de SGA 4 et SGA 7]": letters (1959, 1967, 1969), annotated typescripts, manuscript notes.
- **Where they sit:** the fundamental group of SGA 1, 4 and 7: tame ramification along normal-crossings divisors; possibly the material of Grothendieck-Murre, LNM 208 (1971), whose subject the findings below share. Cote 29 is also in 237_MPL_29_002-048, 238_MPL_29_049-094, 240_MPL_29_141-187, 241_MPL_29_188-217+37_002-022.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** **LM1 E.11**: the source theorem for arbitrary separated locally finitely presented group schemes; possibly, where the leaves treat finite group schemes and their torsors.
- **Our results to use and check:** **LM1 E.2** (the return's claim): over a Q-algebra every fibrewise-unipotent section of a closed linear group scheme is uniquely exp(t log g).
- **What other packages' notes say:** MPL_11_043-058+56 N063 (a cancelled theorem's rank and descent claims, 56/004) and N100 (a test of the Weyl-subquotient hypothesis, 56/016) meet this folder.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 29: the folder asks what a ramified covering is; tame ramification is a structure, not a property, and one studies the category of coverings. Hua, findings register: 29-formal-tame-pi1-self-intersection (scans 095-098: the tame π₁ of a regular formal scheme along a normal-crossings component is a central extension by a quotient of μ^∞, classified by the Chern class of the self-intersection); 29-inertia-matrix-determinant (scan 121: tame inertia given by the multiplicity matrix N, an isomorphism iff equal index sets and det N = ± a power of p; no reference found); 29-fundamental-group-scheme-via-torsors (scans 125-130, 137-140: the fundamental group scheme from pointed torsors under finite group schemes, no Tannakian input; on an abelian variety the limit of its n-torsion; his check finds it in the literature). Our intake: new to us.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  - Each finding against the ink: statement, hypotheses, proof or sketch.
  - The date of the leaves on the fundamental group scheme, set against Nori's (1976) and his abelian-variety computation (1983).
  - Which letters fall in these scans: correspondents, dates.
