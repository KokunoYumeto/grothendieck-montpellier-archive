## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** all of cote 130 (scans 2-50), "Hauteurs et symboles locaux : notes manuscrites", about 1971.
- **Where they sit:** rank 22 of the plan (descent, heights, étale cohomology): abelian varieties and Néron models. The other Néron folder is cote 91 (201_MPL_91_059-098, this batch).
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** none directly; the five rows matched by the tags "etale, abelian" (**LM7 E.23**, **FLAGGED_149 B9**, **LM1 D.11**, **LM4 D.12**, **LM4 F.7**) are about sections, points and GT̂ torsion. What the pages may open up instead: Néron's local symbols as intersection numbers on models, plus a correction seen only by the components of the special fibre.
- **Our results to use and check:** none on these subjects.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** the entries listed (MPL_22+23_002-016 P03, MPL_36_002-052 §102, MPL_131 10, MPL_31_002-051 §117) are register-disposition tables matched by subject, not readings of it.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 130: on an abelian variety the Néron symbol splits into an integral intersection number on the Néron model and a correction with denominator the number of components, giving a ℤ/mℤ-valued pairing; general varieties reduce to the Albanese; a sequence on surfaces (a pencil, the Jacobian of the generic curve) ends on a question left open: is the intersection on the surface, up to sign, the height on the Jacobian?; a last sequence rebuilds Weil functions. Hua, findings register, **130-neron-symbol-component-correction** (our scans 004-006) and **130-quasi-function-sheaf** (our scans 040-049), both conjectures his project did not search. Our intake: new to us.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. On scans 004-006: the four axioms for the correction v(X, a), what m is (number of components, or order of the component group), and whether m·v(X, a) ∈ ℤ is proved.
  2. Possibly the ℤ/mℤ pairing is Grothendieck's pairing on component groups (SGA 7 IX), since both see only the components; compare.
  3. What the page says on the surface question, and whether a sign or a correction term is stated.
  4. On scans 040-049: the sequence 0 → Φ_X → QF_X → Div_X → 0, the kernel of M_K-units, and the vanishing in H^1(X, Φ_X) for quasi-projective X: proved or claimed?
