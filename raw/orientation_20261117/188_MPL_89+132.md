## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** two whole cotes: 89, "[Hexagone] combinatoire. [Ensembles 3-3]" (scans 2-21, undated notes), and 132, "Plongements dans surfaces (Ladegaillerie)" (scans 2-32, notes and offprints of 1974).
- **Where they sit:** plan rank 18, Book IV (polygons, quadrics, graphs): graphs on surfaces, the topology under the maps of cote 138.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **LM4 D.12**: torsion in GT̂_0; its step D.29 uses an involution preserving a complete peripheral system, the structure Hua's 132 finding formalizes.
  - **LM4 C.12**: its level-0 kernel π_1(C_{A^0}(u(Γ)),1) is a π_1 of an identity component; possibly within the extension theory of cote 132.
- **Our results to use and check:** **LM4 D.29** (an involution with a complete preserved peripheral system refutes the finite geometric-recognition assertion for (0,4)).
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** none in our digest.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register: **132-peripheral-gr-category** (scans 006-009; conjecture, unsearched; new to us): for a compact orientable surface with n ≥ 1 boundary components, the 2-group of self-equivalences of (π_1, boundary loops, tethers) has π_0 the mapping class group with free boundary and π_1 zero except for the disc (Z, canonically) and the cylinder (Z, not canonically). **132-nonconnected-extension-classification** (scans 017-021; no reference found by his search): given an extension prolonging the universal cover of H^0, extensions of H by any discrete N are pairs (an extension of π_0 H by N, an equivariant morphism φ from π_1(H^0)). **132-taylor-obstruction** (scan 020): his project marks it as Taylor's obstruction restated (classes under H^2(π_0 H, π), obstruction in H^3).
