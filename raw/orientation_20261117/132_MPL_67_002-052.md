## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** scans 2-52 of cote 67, "Chirurgie des surfaces conformes": notes (s.d., 1983-1984) and letters (1984), from 1977 on.
- **Where they sit:** rank 12 of the plan (tags "maps, teich, galois"): cutting and gluing Riemann surfaces, the geometry under the tower of La Longue Marche. Cote 67 goes on in 133_MPL_67_053-101.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **EXIT_TOWER E.45** and **EXIT_TOWER E.24**: restriction and arithmetic images of the geometric modular groups; possibly, as surgery gives the gluing maps these restrictions follow.
  - **PUSH_ORDER5_FRAME Itemsfromt 2** (O.20, the pentagon as the Jacobi identity; rigid frames Θ₃, Θ₄, Θ₅ in Γ̂_{0,[5]}): possibly, if relations come from decompositions of the five-holed sphere.
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR E.10**: congruence-kernel computations for involutions, with the five oval types; possibly, if anticonformal involutions appear.
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.39**: the nonsolvable direction of centralizer comparison in genus-zero mapping class groups, if finite symmetries are treated.
- **Our results to use and check:** none listed.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** MPL_152+120 Q1-07 gives a checked pointed-disc shadow map in 67, its comparison unresolved; MPL_152+120 also keeps a source and search ledger.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** the digest has only the header of his modernised reading of cote 67 (his pages 1-100, from 1977 to 1984); read it in the background and cite it as Hua, modernised reading of cote 67. No finding of his register is listed.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. Where the pointed-disc shadow map is defined; check it against MPL_152+120 Q1-07 and against the "ombre" of cote 136 (asked in MPL_121_001-048 Q1), possibly the same notion.
  2. Each surgery operation (cutting along curves, gluing discs, annuli, pairs of pants) and the maps it induces on moduli spaces or mapping class groups.
  3. The letters of 1984: correspondents, dates, subjects; possibly tied to the Esquisse d'un programme (January 1984).
  4. Any relation (pentagon, hexagon, commutation) obtained by surgery, for PUSH_ORDER5_FRAME Itemsfromt 2.
