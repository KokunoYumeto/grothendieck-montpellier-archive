- **What these pages are:** cote 12, scans 2-50: the opening of "Généralités (sous-groupes de Galois motiviques)" (1964?; notes and a typescript, to c. 1971).
- **Where they sit:** motives and their Galois groups (plan rank 6). Cote 12 continues in 86_MPL_12_051-098 and 87_MPL_12_099-149; cote 15 is in 89_MPL_15_034-086 and its returned packages; cote 16, whose correspondence rings MPL_15_087-138 §2 asks about, is the cote our notes cite most.

LM7 E.23 and FLAGGED_149 B9 do not bear directly.

MPL_14_087-132 M01: a notation table at cote 14, scan 117.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 12: what a variety has in common with all its cohomologies, its motive; motives form a category with sums, direct factors and tensor products, each cohomology a realisation; with a realisation it is the category of representations of a group, the motivic Galois group, whose quotient by the neutral component should be the ordinary Galois group; the first notebook states Tate and Hodge as one sentence on the size of this group; over finite fields, weights and a notion of level ("niveau") order everything.
- **What the notes should look for:** (1) which correspondences and which equivalence define the category here, and how idempotents are split (N031); (2) the exact statement joining Tate and Hodge in one sentence on the motivic Galois group, with its hypotheses; (3) the definitions of weight and "niveau"; (4) whether the extension of the Galois group by the neutral component is stated or proved; (5) the date (1964?) and which leaves are typescript.
