## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 156-5, chapter V, "Algèbre des figures", from 14 June 1986 (scans 2-49); by Hua's reading, a set-theoretic model of the axioms for figures.
- **Where they sit:** the 1986 Géométrie des formes, rank 11 with 156-3; after the first Analysis situs (156-4, folders 162-163), before the second (156-6).
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** none directly; the matched rows came by subject word. Possibly **EXIT_ONE_TYPE P3c-general**: if figures become ordered sets of pieces (below), refinement and subdivision become maps of such sets, the combinatorial side of stratal zigzags.
- **Our results to use and check:** none yet on this series.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** **MPL_156-1+156-2 §122** (the author's marked questions and retractions); this chapter adds several.
- **Michel Hua's project:** Hua, modernised reading of cote 156-5: a figure becomes a family of subsets in which each point has a smallest piece containing it and each piece a nonempty interior. On 18 June this is seen as a topology in which every union of closed sets is closed, the pieces being point closures; the three comparisons become closed subspace, continuous inclusion and finer topology, and extending a subdivision of one piece becomes gluing two topologies along a closed set. The reading records his self-corrections: a characterisation on its p. 41 marked false in margins on pp. 42, 47 and 48 and undone by a three-point counterexample, with the right condition after it; a margin on p. 42 says it was first forgotten on p. 31. It also questions a connectedness claim from its p. 12 on (cut in our digest).
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. The characterisation, the counterexample and the corrected condition (Hua's pp. 41-48, presumably our scans 042-049): transcribe them exactly and check the corrected condition.
  2. The connectedness claim from Hua's p. 12: test it against the ink and decide it.
  3. Where the identification with such (Alexandrov) topologies is made, and whether he states the correspondence with ordered sets.
  4. Whether this model satisfies the axioms of 156-4, and whether it excludes the hexagon of Hua, findings register, 156-4-supports-boolean-presumption.
