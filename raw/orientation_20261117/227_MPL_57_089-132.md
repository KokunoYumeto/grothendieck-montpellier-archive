## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 57, scans 89-132, the end of "Picard : tapuscrit annoté (s.d.), lettres (s.d., 1962)": an annotated typescript and letters, 1962 to about 1968. Scans 46-88 are package 226_MPL_57_046-088.
- **Where they sit:** plan rank 23 (Picard schemes), the FGA line. Possibly a typescript of the 1962 Bourbaki talks on the Picard scheme (FGA V-VI): our guess from the title and the date.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** none of ours concerns cote 57; the 38 rows matched by subject are about GT̂, sections and CM. Two may meet Picard theory, possibly:
  - **LM7 E.23** (section classes of the metabelian quotients of U_3/Q): the abelian layer of π_1 is the Tate module of the generalized Jacobian.
  - **LM6 A.22** (a closed procyclic subgroup for the full CM group over Q(i)): that CM comes from Fermat Jacobians, which are Picard varieties.
  The pages may open instead: Lefschetz-type comparison of H^1 and Pic between a variety and its generic hyperplane section.
- **Our results to use and check:** none bears directly.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** none cite cote 57.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register, **57-generic-hyperplane-h1-injective** (their pp. 98-99, our scans 099-100; a conjecture, unsearched, in his project; new to us): for X ⊂ P^r normal, integral, of dimension ≥ 2 over an algebraically closed field, and Y the generic hyperplane section over the algebraic closure of k(t_1,...,t_r), H^1(X,G) → H^1(Y,G) is injective for G = G_a and for finite commutative G, μ_p and α_p included. His modernised reading of cote 57 is in your results block; our digest did not carry it.
