## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 10, scans 151-198: the last part of "Catégories tensorielles" (from 1958; notes and typescripts).
- **Where they sit:** tensor categories, the frame of motivic Galois groups (plan rank 13: tensor categories, cycles, L-functions). Cote 10 starts in 134_MPL_10_002-049, 135_MPL_10_050-098 and 136_MPL_10_099-150; motivic Galois groups are in cote 12 (85_MPL_12_002-050 and after).
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** the one match (LM7 E.23) comes from a subject match and does not bear. The pages may open up which ⊗-categories are categories of representations.
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** MPL_15_087-138 §111: an unconditional calculation over C with projective-line correspondences, sl₂ and the Tate tensor subcategory. MPL_15_195-228+18_002-017 S15: a claim-by-claim comparison with rational Nori motives and compatible fibre functors. MPL_14_002-044 §2 and MPL_15_195-228+18_002-017 §2: whether motives are built from cote 16's Lefschetz correspondence rings (not shown by those excerpts).
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 10: is a group known from its representations; for non-commutative groups the missing information is how tensor products decompose; a ⊗-category with a fibre functor, the fibre functor amounting to an algebra and ⊗ to an extra operation on it; then the constraints (associativity, commutativity, unit, duals); a nine-chapter plan of an unwritten treatise sits at their p. 114. Hua, findings register, 10-split-ring-ga-rtimes-gm (our scans 188-189, 192-198): over a field of characteristic 0, for G_a ⋊ G_m with G_m acting by λ ↦ λ^p, the indecomposable rational representations are the σ_n ⊗ e_{0,k} (n ∈ Z, k ≥ 1), e_{0,k} = k[Z]/Z^k with weights 0, p, …, p(k−1), pairwise inequivalent.
- **What the notes should look for:** (1) Hua's classification against scans 188-198: hypotheses, and whether inequivalence and duals are proved; (2) whether these last leaves reach the reconstruction of a group from a rigid ⊗-category with a fibre functor, and in what generality (possibly material later published by Saavedra Rivano, 1972; our guess); (3) the commutativity constraint and its signs; (4) which leaves are typescript, of what text.
