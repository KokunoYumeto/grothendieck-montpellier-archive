## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 34, scans 162-210: the last leaves of his handwritten notes for SGA 7 (c. 1967-1973).
- **Where they sit:** SGA 7, monodromy (plan rank 21: SGA 4, 5 and 7 drafts). The rest of cote 34: the returned package MPL_33_147-181+34_002-017, then 213_MPL_34_018-065, 214_MPL_34_066-113, 215_MPL_34_114-161.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** the three matches come from a subject match. Possibly **LM7 E.23** (section classes for the metabelian quotients of U_3/Q): local conditions on sections at bad primes depend on how inertia acts on the prime-to-p fundamental group, the subject of Hua's findings here. LM4 D.12 and LM4 F.7 do not bear.
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** MPL_36_002-052 N92: shared obstruction language does not settle the arithmetic register (an unpursued implication citing cote 34). MPL_33_147-181+34_002-017 §123 accounts for the earlier leads on this folder.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 34: mostly his draft of the first exposés of SGA 7 (Bures, 1967-1969), one question in six forms, what happens going round a point where a family degenerates; monodromy is quasi-unipotent, by a short arithmetic proof (the monodromy is similar to its q-th power) and a geometric one. Hua, findings register, 34-wild-inertia-outer-pi1 (our scans 164-168, 170-181; his project calls the tameness clause known, Kisin): an open subgroup of inertia acts tamely by outer automorphisms on π₁^(p′)(X_K̄), so wild inertia has finite image in Out. 34-multiparameter-tame-monodromy (our scans 168, 176-181; candidate): over a strictly local base of any dimension, with normal crossings outside codimension 2, the image of π₁(U) in Out(π₁^(p′)) of a geometric fibre is abelian of order prime to p, of rank at most ν.
- **What the notes should look for:** (1) both of Hua's statements against the ink: hypotheses (finite type or proper, codimension 2), what is proved and what is asked; (2) whether the outer action on π₁ itself, not only on cohomology, is his subject here; (3) which proof of the monodromy theorem these last leaves hold; (4) what in them did not reach the published SGA 7.
