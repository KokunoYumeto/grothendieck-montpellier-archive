## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 70, scans 17-64, from "Réalisations géométriques de structures combinatoires (n-polyèdres, n-hyperpolyèdres [2-polyèdres réguliers]…) (Vieilles rédactions)": handwritten old drafts, from 1976. Scans 65-109 are package 106_MPL_70_065-109.
- **Where they sit:** plan rank 10 (maps on surfaces and the icosahedron): the geometric side of the theory of maps, beside cote 138 (Cartes, 1976-78) and the regular polyhedra of cotes 85-86.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **LM4 F.7**: centralizers of a geometric A_5 in Γ^+_{0,ν}; the icosahedral group on the sphere is realised here.
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.41**: Δ on a hypothetical kernel, extended along the icosahedral marked-orbit loci.
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38**: K_5 = 1 open, levels 7 and 11 undecided; possibly, as regular polyhedra and regular maps arise as quotients at these levels.
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.12**: the quadratic-character domain and quintic homomorphisms on GT̂; possibly, as the icosahedral realisation needs Q(√5), from 2cos(2π/5).
- **Our results to use and check:** none named in our digest.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** none in our digest.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register, **70-universal-formulae-double-cosines** (their pp. 9-25 and 64-69; here our scans 017-026; no reference found by his search; new to us): "universal formulae" for a regular affine realisation by reflections of the polytope {p_1, …, p_n} in its vertex frame, σ_i = id + ℓ_i ⊗ (s_{i+1} − s_i), with n parameters that must be roots of the minimal polynomial of 2cos(2π/p_i), so that the tetrahedron, cube and octahedron… (cut in our digest). Its pp. 64-69 (our scans 065-070) are in package 106.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. Scans 017-026: the formula, the parameters, and the proof that they are roots of the minimal polynomial of 2cos(2π/p_i); which polytopes are realised, over which fields.
  2. Whether Galois conjugates of a realisation are treated (for p = 5 they give star polyhedra; our pointer).
  3. The definitions of n-polyhedron and n-hyperpolyhedron, and any classification of regular 2-polyhedra.
  4. How these old drafts relate to cote 138 and to the later drafts of cote 70; date them.
