- **Michel Hua's project:** Hua, modernised reading of cote 156-6: rules for speaking of figures without points, an "atelier" or algebra of figures, answering the Esquisse's call for a tame topology. The primitives are the figures and two comparisons, sub-figure and refinement; multistrates are the indecomposable pieces, lieux the minimal figures. The organising idea is the shadow and the multishadow (the shadows of the pieces); an atelier is "ensembliste" when a figure is recovered from its multishadow. Then come axioms: enough lieux, disjointness seen on lieux, gluing of refinements given piece by piece; subdivision is defined last.
