## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 161-2, scans 70-112, the end of "Algèbre universelle [ou catégories]": undated handwritten notes, about 1963-1973. Scans 2-28 and 29-69 are packages 159_MPL_161-1+161-2_002-028 and 160_MPL_161-2_029-069.
- **Where they sit:** plan rank 16 (categories, universal algebra, topos): algebraic theories, their categories of models and their classifying topoi, in the SGA 4 years.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** none of ours concerns universal algebra; the 45 rows matched by subject concern the homotopy theory of small categories. Possibly **RESIDUE F.7** (must universal Cat-colimit shapes without a weakly terminal object be filtered?), if the pages ask which colimits commute with finite limits in categories of algebras.
- **Our results to use and check:** none.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** none in our digest.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register, **161-2-field-classifier-absolutely-flat-site** (their pp. 41-42 and 92-95; here our scans 093-096; no reference found by his search; new to us): the classifying topos of fields as sheaves on the opposite of finitely presented commutative absolutely flat (von Neumann regular) rings, for the topology generated by the covers Spec A_f ⊔ Spec A/f → Spec A, with the generic field as structure sheaf. Its pp. 41-42 (our scans 042-043) are in package 160. His modernised reading of cote 161-2 did not reach our digest.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. Scans 093-096: the site, the covers, the generic field; prove that this topos classifies fields, and say for which field axiom.
  2. Compare cote 161-3's field axiom (package 122, Hua's 161-3-fields-partition).
  3. Which universal-algebra theorems are stated: free algebras, categories of models, commutation of filtered (or sifted) colimits with finite limits (or products); compare RESIDUE F.7.
  4. Any early form of the classifying topos, and its date against SGA 4 (1963-64).
