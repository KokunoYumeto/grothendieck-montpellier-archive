## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** scans 195-244 of cote 79, "2-polyèdres réguliers triangulés, et modules libres de rang 2 sur les anneaux Λ = ℤ/nℤ et Λ = ℤ", notes from 1980 on.
- **Where they sit:** rank 10 of the plan (the rest of 79; tags "maps, galois, teich"): regular triangulated surfaces from rank-2 modules, the congruence maps, in the Longue Marche period. Neighbours: 111_MPL_79_145-194 before, 113_MPL_79_245-297 after.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38**: 𝒦₅ = 1 is its main unresolved assertion; the level-7 and level-11 quotients have genus three and twenty-six, possibly the level-7 and level-11 maps of this cote.
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR E.10**: six congruence-kernel computations (K_c^τ, H¹(C₂, K_c)); possibly fed by explicit level-n maps.
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.41** and **LM4 F.7**: the icosahedral marked-orbit locus; A₅ centralizers (level 5 is the icosahedron).
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.12**: its quadratic character and quintic homomorphisms; possibly, if the homothety groups (ℤ/nℤ)^× at n = 5 appear.
- **Our results to use and check:** none listed.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** MPL_72_035-081 cites cote 79 in its own follow-up tasks and in its primary sources (section H).
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua's modernised reading of cote 83 describes cote 79 as building the congruence maps mod n from vectors of the plane. Hua, findings register, **79-homothety-groups-as-subgroups**: our digest cuts off its pages and statement; read it in the background, credit it to his project and test it on the ink. Read his modernised reading of cote 79 there too.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. How the homotheties of (ℤ/nℤ)² sit among the automorphisms of the level-n map, and the exact statement Hua's finding names.
  2. Tables for small n (genus, vertex, edge and face counts, groups), above all n = 5, 7, 11, to set against LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38.
  3. Whether a Galois or GT-type action on these maps is written, and through which character of (ℤ/nℤ)^×.
  4. Links to the icosahedra of cote 83 (52_MPL_83_001-045, 116_MPL_83_134-177) and to MPL_72_035-081's follow-up tasks.
