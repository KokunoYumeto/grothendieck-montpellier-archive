- **Where they sit:** rank 14 of the plan (regular polyhedra, graphs; calculation folders; tag "maps"). Cote 85 starts in 149_MPL_74_121-136+85_002-034 and ends in 151_MPL_85_083-098+86.

- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 85: a rotation is known by its trace 2cos θ, equal traces mean conjugate, and order 5 means 2cos θ is a root of x² + x − 1; the pages push this through the transformations of the projective line. Hua, findings register, **85-a5-pgl2-moduli-spec-z-phi** (their pp. 10, 40; our scan 041, and scan 011 in the previous package; his search found no reference): pairs (G, φ), G a form of PGL₂ over S and φ: 𝔄₅ → G a monomorphism, form a rigid groupoid represented by Spec ℤ[T]/(T² + T − 1), so icosahedral subgroups exist and are unique up to unique isomorphism. Our intake: new to us.
