## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 58, scans 20-67: notes on the theory of "Lefschetz-Grauert", comparing a scheme with its formal completion along a closed subscheme, and its applications to π₁ and Pic (from 1964).
- **Where they sit:** the territory of SGA 2, in plan rank 19 (duality of groups, Picard, abelian varieties). Scans 2-19 of cote 58 are in package 197_MPL_59+58_002-019.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** the 57 matches come from the subjects "abelian, galois" and nearly all concern the tower; none is about formal completions. Possibly **LM6 F.6** (the unrestricted higher-dimensional sufficiency assertion and the reduction from number fields remain unproved): Lefschetz theorems for π₁ are the usual way to pass from higher-dimensional varieties to curves, so a statement here may be a tool there.
- **Our results to use and check:** none for this cote.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** none yet.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register, 58-lef-permanence-projective-flat (our scans 041-044; candidate, their search found no reference): if X is quasi-projective over a noetherian ring and (X, Y) satisfies (LG)_n (H^i(X,F) → H^i(X_/Y, F_/Y) bijective for i ≤ n and injective for i = n+1, F locally free), then (W, f⁻¹(Y)) satisfies it for every W projective and flat over X; likewise for LG and for Lef of SGA 2 X. Hua, findings register, 58-local-hom-comparison-depth-off-y (our scans 046-052): a comparison of Hom over a local noetherian ring with depth conditions off Y (our digest cuts its statement).
- **What the notes should look for:** (1) the exact hypotheses and proof status of Hua's permanence claim at scans 041-044; (2) the full local statement at scans 046-052 and its depth hypotheses; (3) how his (LG)_n and LG compare with Lef and Leff of SGA 2 X; (4) each application to π₁ and Pic, with hypotheses, and whether it reduces a question to hyperplane sections or curves (possible use for LM6 F.6); (5) what goes beyond SGA 2.
