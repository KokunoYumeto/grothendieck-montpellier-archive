## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 83, scans 90-133, from "[Icosaèdres, bi-icosaèdres et algèbres d'Azumaya de rang 4]" (1982-1983, 1986): rank-4 Azumaya algebras given by two generators, and icosahedra.
- **Where they sit:** the finite symmetries of the tower (plan rank 10 with cote 83; subjects maps, galois, teich). Cote 83 also fills 52_MPL_83_001-045, 114_MPL_83_046-089, 116_MPL_83_134-177 and 117_MPL_83_178-222.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** **LM4 F.7** (the centralizer of a geometric A_5 in a genus-zero mapping class group, and in its completion): the icosahedral group is A_5. **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.39** (the nonsolvable direction of that centralizer comparison). **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.41** (extension along the icosahedral marked-orbit loci). **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38** (K_5 = 1, the level-five congruence kernel). **PUSH_20261114 Part2Succe 26** (icosahedral characters on the cover of the sphere branched at twelve cusps, through the centralizer of A_5).
- **Our results to use and check:** none named in our digest.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** none on these scans.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register, 83-two-generator-azumaya-criterion (our scans 090-100, 105-110; matched in the literature through Voight 2011): over any commutative ring, the rank-4 algebra with basis 1, u, v, uv, where Tr u = b, det u = c, Tr v = b′, det v = c′ and Tr uv = t, is Azumaya iff Q = t² − bb′t + b²c′ + b′²c − 4cc′ is invertible. A second entry of his for this cote is cut in our digest.
- **What the notes should look for:** (1) Hua's criterion against the ink: is Q his, how he derives it, does it cover characteristic 2; (2) what a "bi-icosaèdre" is on these pages (possibly the binary icosahedral group in the units of a quaternion algebra; our guess from the title); (3) any computed centralizer, normalizer or orbit of the icosahedral group on the sphere or on twelve points, as data for LM4 F.7 and LM4_SUCCESSOR B.39, B.41; (4) the dated leaves (1982-1983, 1986) and what each adds.
