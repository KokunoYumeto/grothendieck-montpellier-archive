- **What these pages are:** scans 143-192 of cote 43, "Dualité des groupes. Jacobiennes généralisées etc.": notes and copies of notes, from 1961 to about 1968.
- **Where they sit:** duality of commutative group schemes, Ext computations and generalized Jacobians (plan rank 19), in the years before SGA 7. Cote 43 is also in 56_MPL_43_001-045, 189_MPL_43_046-093 and 192_MPL_43_193-208+45_002-032.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** the matched rows are tower rows that came in through the tags abelian and galois. Possibly **LM7 E.23** (section classes of the metabelian quotients of U_3/Q) and **LM6 E.21** (the cubic): the abelian level of a punctured curve's π₁ is read off its generalized Jacobian, if the leaves go that way.
- **Our results to use and check:** none in our records.
- **What other packages' notes say:** MPL_57_271-315 R06, a reading question on the finite-subgroup wording in the Igusa construction.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, findings register, 43-ext-ga-zp-perfection (scans 166-170): over any base S of characteristic p, Ext¹(𝔾_a, ℤ/pℤ) in S-groups is the global sections of the perfection of 𝒪_S; unsearched, its Lean card partly proved. Our intake: new to us. Our digest keeps none of his modernised reading of cote 43.
