## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** scans 134-177 of cote 83, "[Icosaèdres, bi-icosaèdres et algèbres d'Azumaya de rang 4] : notes manuscrites" (1982-1983, 1986).
- **Where they sit:** rank 10 of the plan (the rest of 83; tags "maps, galois, teich"). Cote 83 runs over 52_MPL_83_001-045, 114_MPL_83_046-089, 115_MPL_83_090-133, this package and 117_MPL_83_178-222.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:**
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.38**: 𝒦₅ = 1, its main unresolved assertion; possibly, as the icosahedron is the level-5 congruence map (A₅ ≅ PSL₂(F₅)).
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR B.41** and **LM4 F.7**: the icosahedral marked-orbit locus; centralizers of a geometric A₅.
  - **LM4_SUCCESSOR E.10**: congruence-kernel computations; possibly, if level-5 data appear.
- **Our results to use and check:** none listed.
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** none listed.
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua's modernised reading of cote 83 (in 52_MPL_83_001-045): the icosahedron among 2×2 matrices over F₄ and mod 5, then rank-4 Azumaya algebras over any base, with trace invariants and a cross-ratio criterion for generating the whole algebra. Hua, findings register, **83-twist-line-degenerate-locus-is-product** (their pp. 126-143; our scans 134-144 here, from 127 in 115_MPL_83_090-133; his search found no reference): for quadratic algebras H, H′ over any base, the Hamilton-Cayley algebra structures on P = H ⊗ H′ restricting to the given ones (our digest cuts the statement; the id says the degenerate locus of the twist line is a product). Our intake: new to us.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. On scans 134-144: the definitions (Hamilton-Cayley structure, twist line, degenerate locus) and the claim, tested against Hua's finding.
  2. What the "bi-icosaèdres" are, if defined here, and their relation to H ⊗ H′.
  3. Where the trace invariants and the cross-ratio criterion of the first part are used, and what happens over ℤ and at 2.
  4. Links to cote 82, SO(3) ≃ GP(1) (182_MPL_82_029-079), and to 85-a5-pgl2-moduli-spec-z-phi (Hua, findings register: A₅ in forms of PGL₂, over ℤ[T]/(T² + T − 1); 150_MPL_85_035-082).
