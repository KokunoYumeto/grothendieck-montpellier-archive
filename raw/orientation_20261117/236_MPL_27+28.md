## Orientation for this package (curated 17 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max from our records; leads, unrefereed)

- **What these pages are:** cote 27, "SGA D [SGA 3]. Compléments XII à XVII" (scans 2-37; annotated typescript and notes, about 1962-1970), and cote 28, "[Groupes algébriques (SGA 3)]" (scans 2-11; notes, about 1970).
- **Where they sit:** rank 24 of the plan (SGA 3 and the re-editions): material around the SGA 3 exposés on tori, Cartan subgroups and regular elements.
- **Our open questions they may bear on:** **LM1 E.11** (the Q-algebra statement for group schemes that are not linear); possibly, because dropping linearity needs the affine and unipotent parts in families, which the semicontinuity pages treat.
- **Our results to use and check:** **LM1 E.2** (claimed: fibrewise-unipotent sections of closed linear group schemes over Q-algebras are uniquely exp(t log g)).
- **What other packages' notes say about these cotes:** MPL_11_043-058+56 N063: a cancelled page of cote 56 (56/004) states semicontinuity and rank claims, withdrawn in the same text; N100 tests a Weyl-subquotient hypothesis (56/016).
- **Michel Hua's project (credit it to his project whenever you use it):** Hua, modernised reading of cote 27: workshop scraps of SGA 3 ("Divers pour XII à XIV"); the last third proves that under specialization the abelian part can only shrink, abelian plus multiplicative too, and the unipotent part only grow, by counting torsion points. Hua, findings register, **27-semicontinuity-proof** (our scans 028-037; no reference found by his search): these semicontinuities for smooth separated finitely presented group schemes over any base, with a proof, where SGA 3 X 8.7 states them. **28-enriques-systems-classification** (our scans 005-011) and **28-torus-extension-stable-rationality** (our scan 005): Cremona subgroups containing a split n-torus, by "systèmes d'Enriques"; both in Demazure 1970, per his register. Our intake: new to us.
- **What the notes should look for:**
  1. On scans 028-037: each rank, its direction of semicontinuity, the hypotheses and the torsion-point proof; is it complete over an arbitrary base?
  2. Whether these pages correct the cancelled claims of cote 56 (N063).
  3. Cote 28: the five axioms (the binomial condition in k), the torus statement, and what the pages add to Demazure 1970.
  4. The pen corrections on the typescript of cote 27, set against SGA 3 XII-XVII as published.
