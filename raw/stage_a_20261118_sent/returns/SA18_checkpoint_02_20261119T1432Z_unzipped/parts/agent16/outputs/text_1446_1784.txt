1451: \uline{Lemma 3.3}: Let $A$ be a noetherian ring, let $\mathfrak p$ be a prime ideal of $A$, let $I$ be an injective hull of $k(\mathfrak p)$, and let $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$. Then the sheaf $\widetilde I$ is injective in the category $C_m$ of all $\mathcal O_X$-modules.
1452: 
1453: \uline{Proof}: Let $x\in X$ be the point corresponding to the prime ideal $\mathfrak p$. Then $\widetilde I$ is the direct limit of the sheaves $\widetilde E_i$, where the $E_i$ are as in Proposition 3.2. Since $E_i$ is an Artin module over the local ring $A_{\mathfrak p}$, $\widetilde E_i$ is the constant sheaf $E_i$ on $\overline{\{\unsure{X|x}\}}$, and $0$ elsewhere. Thus $\widetilde I$ is the constant sheaf $I$ on $\overline{\{\unsure{X|x}\}}$, and $0$ elsewhere.
1454: 
1455: We now show directly that $\widetilde I$ is an injective $\mathcal O_X$-module. Indeed, if $F$ is any $\mathcal O_X$-module, to give a sheaf homomorphism from $F$ into $\widetilde I$ is the same thing as to give a homomorphism from the stalk $F_x$ into $I$ ! Thus if $F\subseteq G$, and we are given a homomorphism $F\longrightarrow\widetilde I$, it extends to $G$, since we need only extend the map to the stalk $G_x\supseteq F_x$, and $I$ is an injective $\mathcal O_{x,X}$-module.
