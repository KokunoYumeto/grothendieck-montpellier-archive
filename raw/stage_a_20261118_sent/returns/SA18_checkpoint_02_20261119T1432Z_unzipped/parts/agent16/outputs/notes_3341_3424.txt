3347: ### F15 — Cote 47, scan 9, question about torsion
3348: 
3349: **Kind:** unpursued implication. **Manuscript:** “ces formules sont-elles bien vraies mod torsion?”
3350: 
3351: A useful later comparison distinguishes a particular bundle’s integral Chern classes from an integral Fourier transform on the entire Chow group. Beckmann and de Gaay Fortman (2023, Theorem 3.8) characterize integral lifts of the Poincaré character using a specific one-cycle; under a symmetric ample principal polarization, they also relate this to preservation of Chow groups modulo torsion and to weak integral Fourier transforms. These additional hypotheses and the distinction between rational identities and integral lifts prevent the manuscript’s local heuristic from being treated as a general integral-transform theorem.

3353: ### F16 — Cote 47, scan 10, opening Riemann–Roch formula and \(T^2\) cancellation
3354: 
3355: **Kind:** own idea. **Manuscript:** “par R.R.”; “\(T=\operatorname{Todd}(t_A)\)”.
3356: 
3357: **Editorial calculation:** in a rational intersection theory where GRR applies to the complexes used, write \(T_A=\operatorname{Td}(\operatorname{Lie}A)\) and \(\Psi_A(a)=p_{B*}(e^{c_1(P)}p_A^*a)\); then GRR and the projection formula give
3358: \[
3359: \operatorname{ch}(x*_Ay)=(\operatorname{ch}x*_A\operatorname{ch}y)T_A,\qquad
3360: \operatorname{ch}\Phi_P(x)=\Psi_A(\operatorname{ch}x)T_A.
3361: \]
3362: These identities explain the two factors of \(T_A\) in the surviving calculation, and cancellation is legitimate when positive-degree classes are nilpotent, since a Todd class has constant term one (The Stacks Project Authors, 2026, Tags 02UO and 02UN). The cited GRR statement explicitly has smooth proper and local-freeness hypotheses; no theorem for arbitrary \(S\) or an undefined \(\operatorname{Gr}\) is inferred here. **Confidence: high**, conditional on the specified RR framework.

3364: ### F17 — Cote 47, scan 11, first three displays, relative Todd accounting
3365: 
3366: **Kind:** own idea. **Manuscript:** “\((-1)^nT\operatorname{ch}\omega_A\)”.
3367: 
3368: **Editorial calculation:** retaining \(T_B=\operatorname{Td}(\operatorname{Lie}B)\), the tensor-to-convolution identity and item F16 give
3369: \[
3370: \Psi_A(ab)=(-1)^g\bigl(\Psi_A(a)*_B\Psi_A(b)\bigr)
3371: T_AT_B\,\operatorname{ch}(\omega_A).
3372: \]
3373: Output convolution contributes \(T_B\), the inputs contribute \(T_A^2\), and cancellation of the left-side \(T_A\) leaves the displayed factor, which the Hodge exact sequence \(0\to(\operatorname{Lie}A)^\vee\to H^1_{\mathrm{dR}}(A/S)\to\operatorname{Lie}B\to0\) identifies with \(\operatorname{Td}(H^1_{\mathrm{dR}})\).
3374: For connected \(S\) smooth of finite type over a Dedekind ring, Moonen (2026, §2.1, Corollary 2.7(1)) proves that this Todd class is \(1\) in \(\mathrm{CH}(S)_{\mathbf Q}\), without assuming a separable polarization, so the full product cancels in that setting.
3375: **Confidence: high** under those hypotheses; the literal factor \(T_A\operatorname{ch}\omega_A\) still requires a convention check, since dropping only \(T_B\) differs from cancelling the full product, and the absent convolution star remains a separate reading issue.

3377: ### F18 — Cote 47, scan 11, product formula over a field
3378: 
3379: **Kind:** unproved claim. **Manuscript:** “le facteur \((-1)^n\)”.
3380: 
3381: For the normalized Poincaré correspondence on rational Chow groups of a complex abelian variety, Beauville (1983, Proposition 3, p. 243) states
3382: \[
3383: \widehat{\mathcal F}\mathcal F=(-1)^g(-1)^*,\quad
3384: \mathcal F(a*b)=\mathcal F(a)\mathcal F(b),\quad
3385: \mathcal F(ab)=(-1)^g\mathcal F(a)*\mathcal F(b).
3386: \]
3387: This establishes the parity in the converse product identity in that setting. It supports the mathematical sign issue motivating the manuscript, while the absent stars in the two scan-11 cycle formulas remain textual omissions to be recorded, not supplied in the transcription.
