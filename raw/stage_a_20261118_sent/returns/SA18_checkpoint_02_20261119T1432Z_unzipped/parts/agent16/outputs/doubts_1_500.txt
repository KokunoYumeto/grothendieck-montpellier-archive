1: # Shared notation table
2: 
3: This table governs transcription throughout the package. It records transcription conventions; entries marked “verify locally” do not assert that a particular glyph has already been identified. Distinct forms on the scans are retained, and uncertain identities are marked in place. No mathematical expectation licenses normalisation.
4: 
5: | Visible form | LaTeX rendering | Reading discipline |
6: |---|---|---|
7: | Exclamation-mark superscript | `^!` | Inspect both upright stroke and dot; never replace by prime. |
8: | Prime / double prime | `'` / `''` | Inspect at each occurrence; distinguish from exclamation marks. |
9: | Straight or curved double quotation marks | two backticks to open, two apostrophes to close | Use ` ``...'' `; use `« ... »` only for visible guillemets. |
10: | Script capitals | `\mathcal{A}`, etc. | Verify alphabet locally; use `\unsure` for an unresolved script/plain distinction. |
11: | Gothic capitals or lowercase | `\mathfrak{A}`, `\mathfrak{g}`, etc. | Verify locally; do not infer from conventional category names. |
12: | Bold capitals | `\mathbf{A}`, etc. | Preserve visible weight when meaningful. |
13: | Blackboard bold | `\mathbb{Q}`, etc. | Use only when ink/type identifies it. |
14: | Plain mathematical letters | ordinary math italic, e.g. `H`, `X`, `f` | Do not substitute a fancier alphabet. |
15: | Roman mathematical words | `\operatorname{Hom}`, `\operatorname{End}`, `\operatorname{Tr}`, etc. | Only spell the letters actually present. |
16: | Subscripts and superscripts | `_{...}`, `^{...}` | Preserve nesting and placement; retain all `0`, `1`, `l`, degrees, signs. |
17: | Bar, hat, tilde, overarrow | `\bar{...}`, `\hat{...}`, `\tilde{...}`, `\overrightarrow{...}` | Choose observed decoration, not expected object. |
18: | Ordinary arrow | `\to`, `\leftarrow`, `\longrightarrow` | Inspect arrowhead and direction at every use. |
19: | Inclusion / injection | `\subset`, `\subseteq`, `\hookrightarrow` | Distinguish relation from arrow by ink. |
20: | Equality / equivalence / isomorphism | `=`, `\equiv`, `\simeq`, `\cong`, `\approx` | Preserve drawn sign; mark ambiguity. |
21: | Inequalities | `<`, `>`, `\leq`, `\geq`, `\neq` | Read direction from ink. |
22: | Visible abbreviations | exact visible text | No expansions in transcription; expansions are confined to the abbreviation list below. |
23: | Underlining / double underlining | `\uline{...}` / `\uuline{...}` | Keep distinction from deletion and from a fraction bar. |
24: | Deletion | `\deleted{...}` | Include recoverable original, including struck formulae. |
25: | Insertion / separate margin note | `\inserted{...}` / `\margin{...}` | Follow caret or leader; do not relocate by semantic convenience. |
26: | Uncertain reading | `\unsure{reading}` or `\unsure{reading\|alternative}` | First reading follows ink. Use at symbol level in formulae/diagrams. |
27: | Physically unreadable words | `\illegible{n}` | Only after attempted reading; never as a formula-symbol placeholder. |
28: | Diagram / table | `\diagram{\begin{tikzcd}...\end{tikzcd}}` or array | All objects, arrowheads, labels, cancellations retained. |
29: | Non-diagram drawing | `\figure{\begin{tikzpicture}...\end{tikzpicture}}` | Reproduce geometry and labels; no embedded scan or verbal substitute. |
