116: - **C1. Dating lead, scans 44 and 48.** The second letter is dated "6.1.1966" (48:3; the year confirmed on an enlargement, and by Michel Hua on the facsimile), the first "4.1.67" (44:2). The second is the sequel of the first: it opens "Here a few more comments to my talk on the conjectures" (48:7), and its proposition, which "shows that the conjecture C(X) is independant of the choosen polarization" (48:7), answers the doubt of the first, "it is not clear to me if in car p>0 the conditions A(X,ξ) and C(X,ξ) are independant of the polarization" (44:9). So 1966 is a slip for 1967 (6 January 1967, two days after the first letter). Hua's project reached the same conclusion (batch-03, note at his p. 47). The cote's archival dating "(1966-1967)" may rest on this slip. Confidence high.
117: - **C2. Hodge 1938 and all the π_i, scan 47.** With the corrected "the π_i for a surface are algebraic" (47:3), the letter credits Hodge (through Tate) with the algebraicity of every Künneth projector of a surface, that is C(X) for surfaces, consistent with 46:22 ("C(X) is known if dim X ≤ 2", and π_0, π_2n, π_1, π_2n−1 known in general; π_2 of a surface is then the diagonal minus the other four). The typed reference "Algebraic correspondances between surfaces, Proc. London Math. Soc. Seeies 2, Vol XLIV, 1938, p.226" should be checked against Hodge's paper for what it proves. Kind: unproved claim, reference to check.
118: - **C3. The inserted operator on scan 40, own idea.** Degree count for 40:36, φ_* p_Y^{i,j} [·] φ^* = p_X^{i,j+1}: φ^* has degree 0, Λ_Y degree −2, p_Y^{i,j} degree 0, φ_* degree +2, so with Λ_Y the composite has degree 0, as a projector must; with L_Y it would have degree +4. This agrees with the Λ shape of the ink; the margin "L?" remains his query. A full check of the identity needs the identification (α) of scan 36. Confidence high for the degree count.
