79: ## Conventions
80: 
81: 1. **The numbers at the foot of each page are the archive's pencilled foliation, not his pagination.** The first pass
82:    writes `\mspage{N}` at the end of every scan for the pencilled number bottom-left (scan 3 bears 2, scan 4 bears 3,
83:    …, scan 18 bears 17: always scan − 1; the 5 bis leaf bears 7), and `\mspage{n}` at the top for his own number
84:    (top right: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 5bis, 6, …, 14). The prompt reserves `\mspage` for Grothendieck's own page numbers. Hua
85:    batch-01 identifies the bottom-left numbers as the archivists' pencil. I kept the first pass's treatment, which is
86:    uniform over the cote (changing one block would make the cote inconsistent); an edition must not cite the foot
87:    `\mspage` as his pagination. This holds for every scan of cote 16, including the other checkers' blocks.
88: 2. **« = » for « même ».** First occurrence: scan 7 l.13 « De $=$ pour (3.2)_i » (kept as written by the first pass);
89:    the first pass expanded the same sign on scan 17. I followed the first occurrence (change 42).
90: 3. **« élt » / « élément ».** The first pass writes « élt » on scan 10 l.33 (« l'élt $L$ ») and scan 18, « élément » on
91:    scan 9 l.13 and scan 10 l.33 (« L'élément Λ »). The ink at the last two may be the abbreviation; Hua also writes
92:    « élément » there, so I did not change them.
