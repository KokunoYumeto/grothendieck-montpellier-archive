130: 
131: **Kind:** own verification answering a manuscript question. **File/scan:** 46:009, final paragraph; compare 008's isomorphism $E^{(p)}\to E$. **State:** no directly matching settled entry; WG2 §1.1 #3; methodological link to context 16:052.
132: 
133: For a finitely presented module M over any characteristic-p ring R, an isomorphism $F_R^*M\simeq M$ implies finite local freeness: base change of Fitting ideals gives $I_i=I_i^{[p]}$, where $I_i=\operatorname{Fitt}_i(M)$ and $I^{[p]}$ is generated by p-th powers. 135: ### N17 — Finite generation alone admits a counterexample
136: 
137: **Kind:** own verification. **File/scan:** 46:009, same question. **State:** no directly matching settled entry; same methodological context as N16.
