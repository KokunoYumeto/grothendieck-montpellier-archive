classified as a later publication authored by Grothendieck.

Own summaries and locators:

- §1 no.1, pp442–443: absolute Frobenius and naturality.
- §1 no.2, definition3, p444: relative Frobenius; proposition1, p445:
  transitivity and base change.
- Proposition2, pp445–447: integral/radicial/surjective properties; open and
  closed immersion cases; for a locally finitely presented morphism,
  unramifiedness and étaleness characterized by relative Frobenius.
  The original writes the same J^p notation on p445. Consequently the
  manuscript's I^p is an interpretive alarm (Frobenius versus ordinary power),
  not a confirmed erroneous transcription. The several-variable calculation
  still proves the difference between the two possible meanings.
- §1 no.3, pp448–450: module pullback, right exactness, preserved properties,
  symmetric constructions, line-bundle pth tensor power, projective O(1), and
  the coefficient pth-power formula.
- §1 no.4, pp451–452: iterated Frobenius and twists over perfect/finite fields.
- §2 no.1, pp452–453, and proposition1 p454: canonical coefficient comparison
  on the étale site, functoriality/base change, and the Cartesian criterion.
