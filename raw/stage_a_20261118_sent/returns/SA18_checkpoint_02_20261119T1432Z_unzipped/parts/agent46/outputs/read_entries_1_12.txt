33: ### N03 — Later mathematical statements are different evidence from an archival witness
34: 
35: **Kind:** reading question / literature comparison. **File/scan:** context 47:005–008; current-package contact 46:003. **State:** WG2 §2.4 #4.
36: 
37: Mukai (1981, Theorem 2.2, p. 156) proves Fourier inversion over an algebraically closed field, and Laumon (1996, Lemma 1.2.5, p. 5) states the relative Poincaré direct-image isomorphism over a locally Noetherian base with its determinant-line factor. Laumon's proof only recalls the construction of the canonical morphism, so it is not the missing written compatibility computation in another Grothendieck manuscript. These sources refine the leads in context F3–F8 while leaving the documentary question open.

64: 
65: **Kind:** reading question; external later reassessment. **File/scan:** compare context 44:004,006,008–009; 46:003. **State:** WG H9; WG2 §2.3 #12; WG2 §2.4 #4.
66: 
67: The August 9, 1968 letter to Mumford links the intended Hodge–height comparison to numerical versus τ-equivalence, whereas the September 4 letter reports that Griffiths's result changed his assessment of the proposed connection and distinguishes τ from homological equivalence (Grothendieck, 1968a, p. 143; 1968b, p. 145). This supplies authorial evidence requiring the unchecked hunch WG H9 and context D12–D14 to be tested rather than promoted to a result. It is an external reassessment, not a withdrawal within the supplied manuscript, and it does not determine every corrected formulation of Theorem 5.
