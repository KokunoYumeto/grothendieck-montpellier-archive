  622: 
  623: **Witness:** 56/018. **Kind:** own idea. **Confidence:** high, by the coordinate formulas and fibre isomorphisms.

The scheme is an open subscheme of the affine line over R and is smooth, yielding the rank change described by the source in a completely specified example.
