# SA04 — ranked directions for subsequent work

This list consolidates the distinct leads in the three note-audit streams and the source-derived problem register. It proposes future tasks; it does not develop them. An unmatched bibliography entry or an unverified extension of a standard theorem is not thereby an open research problem. Every task below states whether its first output is a formulation, a mathematical result, an exposition, or a source identification.

**Ranking.** Interest rank is the order in the table. The separate feasibility score measures the chance of producing the stated bounded output in one research session of a few hours: **5** very high, **4** high, **3** medium, **2** low, **1** very low. It does not estimate the probability of proving a general conjecture. “With scans” means the score depends on access to manuscript images, which this audit did not have.

**Provenance.** “Manuscript” denotes an explicit question, conjecture, unfinished argument, or proposed generalization in the supplied pages. “Earlier run” denotes an idea or connection proposed by the indexed notes rather than by the manuscript. “SA04 proposal” denotes the present audit's way of making a follow-up concrete. No direction is attributed to Grothendieck merely because a reused typescript occurs in his folder. The final merge aliases are used: C-SA04-217 is absorbed into C-SA04-122, MP-SA04-201 into MP-SA04-164, and MP-SA04-204 into MP-SA04-168.

## 1. Integral universal Schwarzian with a generated target

**Starting records:** SA04-152, SA04-153, SA04-202, SA04-204; C-SA04-122, C-SA04-123, C-SA04-201, C-SA04-203; MP-SA04-24, MP-SA04-51, MP-SA04-52. **Provenance:** manuscript universality claim; the target-generation repair is an earlier-run objection and an SA04 formulation proposal.

**Establish:** a definite universal property for base-natural finite-order differential cocycles, with the recipient generated by their values and the allowable base changes stated. **Known limits:** the integral Hasse composition identity is checked, including small characteristics; adjoining an unused coherent summand defeats the unrestricted recipient-isomorphism claim. Deligne's complex normalization and the smooth relative cocycle theorem do not supply the all-base classification. **First step:** define the finite-jet group-scheme category over Z and write the order-three cocycle equations, separating torsion at two and three. A finite calculation must remain a finite-order result. Interest 1; feasibility 3/5.
