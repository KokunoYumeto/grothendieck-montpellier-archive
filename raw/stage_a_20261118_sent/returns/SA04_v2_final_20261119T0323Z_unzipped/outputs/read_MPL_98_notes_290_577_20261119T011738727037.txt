00335 #### MPL98.G04 — Čech spectral sequence and coinduced contraction
00336 
00337 
00338 **File / scan:** 98.pdf, 018.  
00339 **Kind:** unproved claim / targeted proof check.  
00340 **Brief source quotation:** “utiliser essentiellement les faits suivants”; “Hom_Z(Z(G),M)=M^G”.  
00341 **Context:** Hua98, “La suite spectrale A,” p. 17 (scan 018).  
00342 **Register:** RS–Ø; ordinary independent proof, no formal certificate.
00343 
00344 
00345 For nonempty \(T\), the augmented complex \(\mathbf Z[T^{p+1}]\to\mathbf Z\) is exact after choosing a point of \(T\), and the bicomplex \(\operatorname{Hom}_G(\mathbf Z[T^{p+1}],I^q)\) gives the displayed Čech-to-group-cohomology spectral sequence. The notation \(M^G\) here denotes the coinduced function module explicitly identified by the preceding Hom expression, not invariants. An injective G-module is a retract of a coinduced module, and insertion of a chosen point contracts the resulting ordinary function-valued Čech complex; an arbitrary coinduced module need not itself be injective. This supplies the details behind the source’s sketch and makes Hua’s shorthand explicit; see COH02–COH03 and Hochschild and Serre (1953, I §3).

00362 
00363 
00364 **File / scan:** 98.pdf, 020.  
00365 **Kind:** unproved claim / targeted proof check.  
00366 **Brief source quotation:** “La suite spectrale B n’est donc pas essentiellement plus générale que A”.  
00367 **Context:** Hua98, “La suite spectrale B,” p. 19 (scan 020).  
00368 **Register:** RS–Ø.
00369 
00370 
00371 For \(T\to S\) surjective, the relative nerve gives an abutment \(\operatorname{Ext}^{p+q}_{\mathbf Z[G]}(\mathbf Z[S],A)\), the cohomology of the slice topos. For subgroups \(F'\subseteq F\subseteq G\), when \(S=G/F\) and \(T=G/F'\), the slice is the category of F-sets and the fibre is \(F/F'\), which proves the asserted reduction. This checks Hua’s comparison with Hochschild–Serre under its actual group-quotient hypothesis (Hochschild & Serre, 1953, II §4).
