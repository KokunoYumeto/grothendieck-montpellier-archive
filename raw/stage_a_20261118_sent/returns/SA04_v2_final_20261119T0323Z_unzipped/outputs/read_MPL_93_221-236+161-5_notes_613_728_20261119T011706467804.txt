00643 ### N02 — The neutral restriction fibre is exactly the relative cocycle quotient
00644 
00645 **Location:** 161-5/029, trivial-on-\(\pi\) condition and “noyau nul”. **Kind:** own idea. **Confidence:** high; direct normalization of cocycles, with no normality assumption.
00646 
00647 For the action supplied by one extension, the neutral fibre of \(H^1(\Gamma,A)\to H^1(\pi,A)\) is \(\{z\in Z^1(\Gamma,A):z|_\pi=1\}/A^\pi\), because a neutral restriction can be normalized and any conjugator between two normalized cocycles must lie in \(A^\pi\). It follows that a trivial neutral fibre is equivalent to a single conjugacy orbit of extensions of \(\alpha_0\), once one extension exists. Context comparison: this checks the equivalence already added by Hua, modernised reading of cote 161-5, IV, scans 028–029, and strengthens the manuscript's “il suffirait” only under its explicit existence condition.

00650 ### N03 — Trivial kernel and full injectivity are distinct assertions
00651 
00652 **Location:** 161-5/029, parenthesis after “injectif”. **Kind:** reading question. **Words:** “ou ... injectif, i.e. noyau nul”, abbreviation doubtful.
00653 
00654 For nonabelian \(H^1\), triviality of the neutral fibre does not imply that all fibres are singletons, and the sheet explicitly offers the weaker condition as sufficient for the chosen extension. 00678 ### N07 — Valuation detects an obstruction even for semilinear Frobenius
00679 
00680 **Location:** 161-5/027, Frobenius datum; 029, trivial-kernel question. **Kind:** own idea. **Confidence:** high under the explicitly stated valued-field model.
00681 
00682 Let an automorphism \(\sigma\) of a discretely valued field F preserve its valuation, and let \(\mathbf Z\) act through \(\sigma\) on \(F^\times\); then every coboundary \(\sigma(a)/a\) has valuation zero, so a uniformizer gives a nontrivial class in \(H^1(\mathbf Z,F^\times)\). Thus adding semilinearity by itself does not remove the rank-one freedom, although admissibility or slope restrictions may exclude this particular class. Context comparison: this extends the valuation aspect of Hua, modernised reading of cote 161-5, IV, scans 027–029, without claiming that the manuscript's undefined \(\Gamma\), or every admissible comparison problem, has this exact cohomology.
