02745 ### HC06 — Cartier representatives versus cohomology classes in the modernized context
02746 
02747 **File/location:** cote93, scans072–074, compared with Hua §XIII “L'opération de Cartier”, pages168–172. **Kind:** own idea. **Confidence:** high for the elementary countercheck; no claim about the unseen manuscript's wording. **Context queries:** Cartier; psi; formes exactes; Hasse.

02749 Hua's §XIII writes a map to closed forms with `psi(dx)=x^(p-1)dx` for every x, but that assignment is not additive before passage to cohomology: over `F2[x,y]` the defect on `d(x+y)` is the nonzero exact form `x dy+y dx=d(xy)`. This supports the distinction in **MPL_93_002-046 E26/P06** and is a check of Hua's interpretation, not a correction of any original page beyond091.
