00958 
00959 
00960 **File / scan:** 98.pdf, 006.  
00961 **Kind:** reading question / targeted proof check.

00964 **Register:** RS–Ø.
00965 
00966 
00967 The quoted phrase is self-referential, whereas the quotient-subspace criterion requires that every relatively saturated subset \(B\subseteq A\) open in \(A\) have \(f(B)\) open in \(f(A)\), equivalently the trace on \(f(A)\) of an open subset of the quotient. 01104 
01105
