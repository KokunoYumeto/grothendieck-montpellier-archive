00215 ### D03 — The formal Poincaré hypothesis cannot be discarded in characteristic p
00216 
00217 **Location:** 93/231, “Supposons” paragraph and characteristic-zero example; compare 221. **Kind:** own idea. **Confidence:** high for the elementary obstruction.
00218 
00219 Over a field of characteristic p, the closed formal one-form \(x^{p-1}dx\) is not the derivative of a formal power series, because differentiation gives zero as the coefficient of \(x^{p-1}dx\). Thus the displayed formal Poincaré hypothesis has real content, and a positive-characteristic replacement must account for Cartier classes instead of copying the characteristic-zero exact sequence. Context comparison: this supports the circled question on 221 and Hua, modernised reading of cote 93, XIII and XX, scans 168–187 and 231–233; it connects to MPL_93_002-046 E26 in notes/RESULTS_STATE.md without re-proving that earlier note's Hasse–Witt interpretation.

00222 ### D04 — A conditional answer to “injectif ?”
00223 
00224 **Location:** 93/232, circled question after the corollary. **Kind:** own idea. **Confidence:** high, conditional on the factorisation and quotient asserted on 231–232.
00225 
00226 If \(D=\Delta d_E^0\) and \(\Delta\) is changed by \(B d_E^1\), then its restriction to \(\ker d_E^1\) is unchanged, so the proposed map is well defined under the asserted quotient description.
