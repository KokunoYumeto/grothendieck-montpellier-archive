00422 ### S16 — Two weights yield a short Lie grading, with a Jordan-pair route
00423 
00424 **Location:** 161-5/021, “n'ayant que deux poids”. **Kind:** own idea. **Confidence:** high for the grading calculation; medium for the precise classification intended by the annotation.
00425 
00426 If the representation has weights 0 and 1, conjugation on its endomorphism algebra has weights −1, 0, and 1, so a stable Lie subalgebra inherits a three-term grading whose outer pieces are abelian. The iterated-bracket construction on the outer pieces is the standard route to a Jordan pair in characteristic 0 [B08], but obtaining a unital Jordan algebra requires additional structure and the calculation alone does not identify a minuscule representation or complete Serre's classification. Context comparison: this makes one part of the link proposed by Hua, modernised reading of cote 161-5, I, scan 021, explicit while preserving its remaining hypotheses.
