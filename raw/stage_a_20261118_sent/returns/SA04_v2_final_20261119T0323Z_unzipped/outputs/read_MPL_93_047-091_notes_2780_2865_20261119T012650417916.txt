02781 ### HC12 — Actual period integrals would supply the missing Picard–Fuchs data
02782 
02783 **File/location:** cote93, scans072–074 and080–082; Hua §XIX “Intégrales de formes fermées, et équations de Picard–Fuchs”, pages221–229. **Kind:** unpursued implication. **Context queries:** périodes; intégrales; Picard; Fuchs.
02784 
02785 Hua's §XIX describes integrals along relative paths and operators annihilating periods, including an explicitly admitted differentiation formula. Those are exactly the sorts of family, cycle, and period data absent from the current Schwarzian formulas, so the later section gives a concrete next comparison rather than confirming it by terminology alone. It sharpens **MPL_93_002-046 P04/P07/P08**, with the admitted later step remaining unverified in this package.

02787 ### HC13 — Differential-operator factorization is not automatic from the first jet
02788 
02789 **File/location:** cote93, scan091; Hua §XX “Opérateurs différentiels à coefficients constants”, pages230–232. **Kind:** unpursued implication. **Context queries:** opérateurs; formellement exacte; Poincaré; principal.
02790 
02791 The universal first-order operator on091 is a concrete instance of principal parts, whereas Hua's §XX describes broader exactness and factorization assertions for differential operators and a formal Poincaré hypothesis. The first-jet proof OP44 does not establish those higher-order exactness assertions or the Picard–Fuchs application. No exact settled result-state entry covers the latter; this is a credited later method related to **MPL_93_002-046 P04/P08**.

02793 ### HC14 — Canonical comparisons of lifts have a sheaf-level descent step
02794 
02795 **File/location:** cote93, scans047–048; Hua §XXI “Construction de la connexion canonique en cohomologie de De Rham”, pages233–235. **Kind:** unpursued implication. **Context queries:** cristal; comparaison; homotopie; torseur; Gauss.
02796 
02797 Hua's §XXI reports construction of canonical maps using torsors of identifications and homotopies, while expressly limiting its claimed formal check to torsors under groups with a point rather than general sheaf torsors. The transitivity and choice-independence needed by the present universal-extension continuation must therefore still be checked in the relevant sheaf setting. This bears directly on **MPL_93_002-046 C30/C33** and adds a specific later comparison to **P08**, without upgrading an unavailable Lean development into a proof of the original construction.
