00590 #### MPL98.B09 — Universal algebraically closed field and automorphism orbits
00591 
00592 
00593 **File / scan:** 98.pdf, 031.  
00594 **Kind:** unproved claim / independent re-proof.  
00595 **Brief source quotation:** “de degré de transcendance infini”; “Aut(𝑘̃/k)”.  
00596 **Context:** Hua98, “Un corps universel,” p. 30 (scan 031), modernized corollary numbered 5.  
00597 **Register:** RS–Ø.
00598 
00599 
00600 For coherent sheaves on finite type \(X/k\), an algebraically closed \(\Omega/k\) of infinite transcendence degree realizes every equivalence class, and equivalence of representatives on \(X_\Omega\) is exactly the \(\operatorname{Aut}(\Omega/k)\)-orbit relation. The proof first descends both objects and a witnessing isomorphism to finitely generated coefficient fields over the ground field, then extends their embeddings to automorphisms of \(\Omega\) using transcendence bases and algebraic closures. This verifies Hua’s interpretation and the source’s substantive assertion; BR04 records the finite-data argument, while R04 records the different corollary number.

00604 
00605

00609 **Context:** Hua98, pp. 29–30 (scans 030–031), conventions on universes and a universal field.  
00610 **Register:** RS–Ø.

00612 
00613 The field \(\Omega\) is universal for these finite-presentation objects, not a set-sized field containing every field extension of \(k\). Finite transcendence degree is insufficient uniformly: a cyclic sheaf on the affine line defined by a monic degree-\(n\) polynomial with \(n\) algebraically independent coefficients retains those coefficients in its annihilator and cannot be represented over a field of smaller transcendence degree. This adds the size qualification underlying Hua’s explanation and separates the source’s set-of-classes assertion from an unrestricted universal-field claim.

00635 #### MPL98.B12 — Reduced support and inseparability
00636 
00637 
00638 **File / scan:** 98.pdf, 034.  
00639 **Kind:** own idea / boundary counterexample.  
00640 **Brief source quotation:** “O_(Supp F)_réd”; “K alg. clos”.  
00641 **Context:** Hua98, p. 33 (scan 034), reduced support among the operation examples.  
00642 **Register:** RS–Ø.
00643 
00644 
00645 Over an imperfect field \(k\) of characteristic \(p\), the reduced algebra \(k[x]/(x^p-a)\), for \(a\notin k^p\), becomes nonreduced after adjoining \(a^{1/p}\). Consequently reduction of support cannot be assumed to commute with arbitrary field extension without a geometric-reducedness condition. The source’s algebraically closed-field restriction addresses this obstacle for finite type schemes, so the example explains rather than removes that restriction and qualifies a broader version of Hua’s operation list.

00648 **Confidence and reason:** High: the polynomial becomes a (p)-th power after the stated extension.

00657 **Context:** Hua98, p. 33 (scan 034), primary-filtration and component operations.  
00658 **Register:** RS–Ø.
00659 
00660 
00661 Canonical support filtrations and primary components attached to isolated support components should be distinguished from arbitrary choices of embedded primary components. For example, \((x^2,xy)=(x)\cap(x^2,y-a x)\) for every \(a\in k\), so an embedded primary component is not unique. The source’s set-valued formulation and its reference to support components leave room for that distinction; this adds a caution to Hua’s list rather than claiming that the manuscript asserts uniqueness of every primary decomposition.

00680 #### MPL98.B15 — Hom base change and generic flattening
00681 
00682 
00683 **File / scan:** 98.pdf, 035–036.  
00684 **Kind:** self-flagged: lemma requested / unproved claim.  
00685 **Brief source quotation:** “Rédiger sous forme de lemme”; “localement sur X”.  
00686 **Context:** Hua98, pp. 34–35 (scans 035–036), Hom base change and the request for a local lemma.  
00687 **Register:** RS–Ø.
00688 
00689 
00690 After restricting the inputs to a stratum, a finite local presentation of the first sheaf expresses Hom as a kernel between finite sums of the second, and generic flatness of the coefficient module and cokernel makes that kernel commute with further base change. A finite affine cover and common refinement of the parameter strata supply the global boundedness conclusion. This agrees with Hua’s reconstruction and explains why the source asks for a local lemma; it does not silently replace the literal second arguments \(F\) in the displayed Hom kernel by the mathematically intended \(G\). This computes Hom afresh on each stratum; it does not assert that the original global Hom module already commutes with the restriction to that stratum.

00694 
00695 
00696 **File / scan:** 98.pdf, 036–037.  
00697 **Kind:** unproved claim / independent re-proof.

00699 **Context:** Hua98, finite-range complex lemma, pp. 35–36 (scans 036–037).  
00700 **Register:** RS–Ø.
00701 
00702 
00703 For a fixed finite cohomological range, only finitely many terms, cycles, boundaries, and cokernels are needed, and generic flatness on reduced integral strata followed by noetherian induction gives a finite stratification with the desired fibrewise base-change property. 00723 
00724 
00725 **File / scan:** 98.pdf, 039–040.  
00726 **Kind:** own idea / refutation of a proof step.  
00727 **Brief source quotation:** “résolution libre … comme B_i-module”; “⊗_B L^(i)”.  
00728 **Context:** Hua98, Tor proof p. 39 (scan 040), which repeats the same resolution-ring choice; coefficient-ring doubts on scan 039 remain separate.  
00729 **Register:** RS–Ø; proof step refuted, intended base-change theorem not thereby refuted.
00730 
00731 
00732 Take \(A=k\), \(B=k[x]\), \(B_1=B_2=k=B/(x)\), and \(M_1=M_2=k\). 00739 
00740 
00741 **File / scan:** 98.pdf, 039–040.  
00742 **Kind:** own idea / necessity check.  
00743 **Brief source quotation:** “que les F_α soient plats sur S”; “comme M_i est plat sur A”.  
00744 **Context:** Hua98, Tor-lemma editorial flatness footnote, pp. 38–39 (scans 039–040); see reading disagreement R09.  
00745 **Register:** RS–Ø; only the weakened statement omitting input flatness is refuted.
00746 
00747 
00748 Set \(A=k[t]\), \(B=A[x]\), \(M_1=B/(tx)\), and \(M_2=B/(x)=A\). The two-term resolution gives \(\operatorname{Tor}_0^B(M_1,M_2)=\operatorname{Tor}_1^B(M_1,M_2)=A\) and higher Tor zero, but after \(t=0\) the modules become \(k[x]\) and \(k\), whose first Tor is zero. The frozen lemma explicitly contains input flatness, so this validates the need for its assumption and differs from Hua’s claim that the hypothesis had to be supplied.

00754 #### MPL98.B20 — Corrected Tor base-change mechanism
00755 
00756 
00757 **File / scan:** 98.pdf, 040–041.  
00758 **Kind:** own idea / refined proof.  
00759 **Brief source quotation:** “la cohomologie de K′ … Tor”; “suite spectrale homologique”.  
00760 **Context:** Hua98, pp. 39–40 (scans 040–041), tensor-resolution proof.  
00761 **Register:** RS–Ø; refinement of the proof step refuted in B18.
00762 
00763 
00764 Let \(A\) be noetherian, \(B\) finite type over \(A\), and \(B_i\) finitely many finite type \(B\)-algebras with finite \(B_i\)-modules \(M_i\); choose polynomial \(B\)-algebras \(C_i\twoheadrightarrow B_i\) and finite-rank \(C_i\)-free resolutions of the \(M_i\), which are \(B\)-flat resolutions. Their total tensor complex has finite terms over \(C=\bigotimes_B C_i\), and the kernel ideals act null-homotopically, so its finite homology is a module over \(\bigotimes_B B_i\), giving coherence on the intended fibre product. With \(B\) and the \(M_i\) flat over \(A\), the resolutions survive arbitrary \(A\)-base change, and the first-quadrant hyper-Tor spectral sequence gives the requested range when the output Tor groups in that range are \(A\)-flat. This adds a concrete correction to Hua’s repeated proof step without changing the manuscript; BR08R gives the complete argument and Stacks Tag 0620 supplies the two-module comparator.
00765 
00766 
00767 **Confidence and reason:** High under the stated noetherian, finite type, and flatness hypotheses; this is a proof repair, not a claim of novelty.

00831 #### MPL98.B25 — Canonical comparison maps must glue
00832 
00833 
00834 **File / scan:** 98.pdf, 042.  
00835 **Kind:** self-flagged: verification left to the reader.  
00836 **Brief source quotation:** “on laisse au lecteur le soin de vérifier … ‘se recollent’”.  
00837 **Context:** Hua98, p. 41 (scan 042), passage from affine calculation to sheaves.  
00838 **Register:** RS–Ø.
00839 
00840 
00841 The affine comparison maps must be independent of a chosen resolution and compatible with restriction, not merely abstract isomorphisms of the correct modules. Comparison maps between full projective resolutions are unique up to homotopy, and the canonical tensor–Hom map commutes with restriction; restricting the calculation to \(p\le N\) therefore supplies the required gluing in the finite free local setting. This fills the specific verification left to the reader and agrees with the intended global assertion in Hua’s reconstruction.
