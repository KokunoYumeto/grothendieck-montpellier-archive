00083 #### MPL98.N01 — Integral alternating tensor line
00084 
00085 
00086 **File / scan:** 98.pdf, 003.  
00087 **Kind:** own idea / targeted proof check.  
00088 **Brief source quotation:** “Ils opèrent en particulier dans la partie … Λⁿ_A M”.  
00089 **Context:** Hua98, “Le déterminant comme loi multiplicative,” p. 2 (scan 003), especially its factorial/anti-invariant footnote.  
00090 **Register:** H98; its determinant mechanism is checked here, not its reported Lean certificate.
00091 
00092 
00093 For a free rank-\(n\) module, the image of \(\operatorname{Alt}=\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\sigma\) is the free line generated by the alternating basis tensor, which has a coefficient \(1\), so \(\Lambda^nM\) embeds in \(M^{\otimes n}\) without inverting \(n!\). Every tensor endomorphism commuting with the permutations preserves that image, and localization gives the finite-projective version; the full proof is NORM01 in the norm memorandum. This differs from identifying the exterior power with the whole sign-eigenspace, which has dimension \(3\), rather than \(1\), for \(M=\mathbf F_2^2\). Thus Hua’s warning about the full eigenspace is correct, but its claimed need to abandon the integral image construction is too strong; Deligne (1973, XVII §6.3.1) is the published comparator.
00094 
00095 
00096 **Confidence and reason:** High: an integral basis proof and a separate characteristic-two counterexample distinguish the two assertions.

00145 
00146 
00147 **File / scan:** 98.pdf, 005,007.  
00148 **Kind:** unproved claim.  
00149 **Brief source quotation:** “Fonctorialité en X,Y,S”; “symm”.

00157 #### MPL98.N06 — Disjoint sums and multiplicativity
00158 
00159 
00160 **File / scan:** 98.pdf, 007,012.  
00161 **Kind:** unproved claim.  
00162 **Brief source quotation:** “Cas où r=0, r=1”; “Additivité en M”.  
00163 **Context:** Hua98, normalization/additivity, pp. 6 and 11 (scans 007 and 012).  
00164 **Register:** H98; sum compatibility independently checked.
00165 
00166 
00167 For \(B=B_1\times B_2\), block-diagonal multiplication gives \(N_{B/A}(b_1,b_2)=N_{B_1/A}(b_1)N_{B_2/A}(b_2)\), and the section lies in the \((r_1,r_2)\) component of the symmetric power of the disjoint union. The rank-one identity is immediate, while degree zero gives the empty symmetric product and the chosen nullary value, which is the identity in the additive or multiplicative unital cases. This verifies, rather than merely repeats, the additivity diagram used by Hua; the separate problem with a single numerical degree on the empty target is N10 (Deligne, 1973, XVII §6.3.5).

00170 #### MPL98.N07 — Norm transitivity left unproved
00171 
00172 
00173 **File / scan:** 98.pdf, 005,007,009,012.  
00174 **Kind:** unproved claim.  
00175 **Brief source quotation:** “Transitivité”; “Je dis qu’on a”.  
00176 **Context:** Hua98, “La transitivité,” pp. 4–8 and axiom (ii ter), p. 11 (scans 005–009,012).  
00177 **Register:** H98; transitivity independently re-proved.
00178 
00179 
00180 For finite locally free \(A\to B\to C\), the required identity is \(N_{C/A}=N_{B/A}\circ N_{C/B}\), with the corresponding flattening of symmetric powers. The norm memorandum proves it integrally by a block-determinant identity, using a monic-polynomial localization and then descent back to the original ring. Because the proof works after every scalar extension, it establishes equality of polynomial laws and of the sections, a stronger check than agreement on geometric fibres alone. This supplies the proof behind the manuscript’s final assertion and Hua’s reconstruction; the exact published comparator is Deligne (1973, XVII Lemma 6.3.3 and §6.3.15(v)).

00196 #### MPL98.N09 — Orbit-sum degree computation
00197 
00198 
00199 **File / scan:** 98.pdf, 011–013.  
00200 **Kind:** unproved claim / targeted proof check.  
00201 **Brief source quotation:** “card(Eⁿ/G)”; “très facilement”.  
00202 **Context:** Hua98, “Trois exemples,” pp. 10–12 (scans 011–013), including its added permutation-basis proof.  
00203 **Register:** H98; Hua’s added proof credited and independently checked.
00204 
00205 
00206 If \(B\) is free with basis indexed by \(E\), then \(B^{\otimes n}\) has a permutation basis indexed by \(E^n\), and the \(H\)-invariants have the orbit sums as a basis. Consequently their rank is \(\#(E^n/H)\), and this particular invariant module commutes with every base change, without inverting \(|H|\). This rechecks Hua’s explicitly added argument and supplies the missing detail behind the manuscript’s ease assertion. It must not be extended to arbitrary group actions: the involution \(\epsilon\mapsto-\epsilon\) on \(\mathbf Z[\epsilon]/\epsilon^2\) gains extra invariants modulo \(2\).

00209 #### MPL98.N10 — Empty-target degree convention
00210 
00211 
00212 **File / scan:** 98.pdf, 011–013.  
00213 **Kind:** own idea / axiomatic boundary.  
00214 **Brief source quotation:** “Soit deg une fonction”; “deg(id_X)=1”.  
00215 **Context:** Hua98, degree axioms and Lean footnote, pp. 10–12 (scans 011–013).  
00216 **Register:** H98; literal single-function formulation refuted, degree-predicate refinement checked; no Lean execution.
00217 
00218 
00219 With arbitrary pullback in schemes or G-sets, pulling a degree-\(r\) arrow back to the empty target gives the empty identity, so a single base-change-invariant numerical degree with identity degree one would force \(r=1\). Additivity applied to two identities then forces \(2=1\), showing that the literal single-function formulation needs refinement. The source’s own later wording “dit de degré r” and Hua’s reported Lean predicate hasDeg suggest a suitable repair: treat degree as indexed data or a fibrewise predicate, which is vacuously true for every \(r\) over the empty target. This differs from refuting the canonical-section construction or the uninspected Lean development.
00220 
00221 
00222 **Confidence and reason:** High: the contradiction is formal, while the predicate formulation directly removes it.

00226 
00227
