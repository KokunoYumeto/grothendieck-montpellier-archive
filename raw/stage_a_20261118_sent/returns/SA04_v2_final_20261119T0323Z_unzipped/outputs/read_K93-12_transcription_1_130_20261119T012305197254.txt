00006 Let $y$ be in $Y$. Then $\theta(y)$ is in the norm closed convex hull of $\{t(y):t\in T\}$ by [3, Th. 13, p. 422]. Therefore, given any $\varepsilon>0$ there exist $t_1,\ldots,t_n$ in $T$ and $\alpha_i\geqq 0$ $(i=1,\ldots,n)$ such that $\sum\alpha_i=1$ and
00007 \[
00008 \lVert\theta(y)=\sum\alpha_i t_i(y)\rVert<\varepsilon/(M+1),
00009 \]
00010 where $M=\sup\{\lVert t\rVert:t\in T\}$ (which is finite by the uniform boundedness principle.) Then if $t\geqq t_1\unsure{\mathbin{\mathrm{o}}|\circ}\ldots\mathbin{\mathrm{o}}t_n$,
00011 \[
00012 \begin{aligned}
00013 \lVert\theta(y)-t(y)\rVert&=\lVert t[\theta(y)-\sum\alpha_i t_i(y)]\rVert\\
00014 &\leqq M\varepsilon/(M+1)<\varepsilon,
00015 \end{aligned}
00016 \]
00017 so $\displaystyle\lim_{t\in T}\lVert\theta(y)-t(y)\rVert=0$. \hfill Q.E.D.
