## The manuscript’s sequence

The pages first broaden the projective differential invariant to translations, scalings, affine transformations, and scalings together with inversion. The successive quotient objects matter: ordinary one-forms, frames of one-forms, connections on a line bundle, and projective-connection torsors are different sheaves. The source revises domains and ranges as it tests local solvability. This is an active construction, with visible corrections, rather than a single finished list of equivalent invariants.

The n-differential construction then takes a local root and a primitive. It produces the coefficient

\[
c_n(f)=-\frac2n\frac{f''}{f}+\frac{2n+1}{n^2}\left(\frac{f'}{f}\right)^2,
\]

with nonzero n in the complex analytic setting. The maps beta, gamma and delta on scan 82 express the same construction through line connections and projective connections. All displayed dependent calculations were redone using exact symbolic arithmetic. The coefficient 2n+1, the tensor-power identity, the horizontal-frame minus sign, the quadratic variation, and the exponential correction are preserved with their hypotheses. An expression valid only for n=1 is not promoted to arbitrary n.

Scans 86–88 move to projective line bundles, Azumaya algebras, Lie algebras, marked sections, extension data, and then two disjoint sections. The point of the local-versus-global distinction is already visible: a Brauer–Severi curve need not be P(E) for a global rank-two vector bundle. A marked section supplies a rigidified degree-one bundle and an actual extension, while an unmarked projective object can have a Brauer obstruction. Trace kernels and scalar quotients use divided and symmetric squares respectively; their agreement after inverting 2 must not erase their difference at 2.

The final sequence turns the marked extension into a filtered adjoint bundle with connection. The explicit matrix yields the factor `c12=-2c23`. The author corrects the earlier inner-derivation assertion by requiring 2 invertible. The universal first-order operator then identifies restricted transverse data with a principal-parts bundle. It leaves full connections in a quadratic-differential torsor. Restricted-data uniqueness does not select a unique full connection, and local nonemptiness does not by itself give a global section.

## What the earlier notes got right, and what changes

The notes correctly located the principal mathematical boundaries: local sheaf surjectivity versus global sections, frames versus all forms, invertibility of 2, the difference between an integral jet invariant and p-curvature, and the distinction between a marked projective connection and a specified Gauss–Manin family. They also correctly supplied several useful counterexamples. Those counterexamples and their previously stated local rigidity theorems have been independently re-proved here, with their earlier-run authorship retained.

Several qualifications are now necessary because the second transcription changes the evidential situation. Pro scan75 reads an epimorphism from local coordinates to all one-forms; Codex reads all holomorphic functions. The former fails at the target -df, while the latter is locally true. Thus the counterexample is certain but the manuscript error is not. Pro’s isolated “faux” on scan87 is Codex’s “plus bas”; a retraction cannot be assigned on the word alone. The first-jet quotient has I²/I alternatives and the two witnesses differ about the direction of the extension-recovery question.

The zero-index note is especially important methodologically. The earlier run explicitly offered a **possible** group-level interpretation using the correct identity mu_0=G_m. That mathematical identity is not refuted. Codex’s n≠0 reading lowers confidence that it explains this particular arrow. The revised verdict therefore retains the idea as conditional and asks for a scan check; it does not pretend that the ink has been resolved.

Some mathematical errors do not depend on deep theory. The expansion `(dg/g)/(df/f)` requires f/g, not g/f. The mixed c_n formula must use the derivative of its own input. The delta coefficient must include the linear f term and 5/4. The quadratic `f+1/f=h` need not have an etale solution just because h is nonzero. Each is supported by a named exact computation or a short proof. None is silently substituted into the diplomatic text.

## Established theory and the remaining exact questions

The strongest primary comparison on projective geometry is Deligne’s *Équations différentielles à points singuliers réguliers*, I§5: osculating projective lines, the quadratic-differential torsor, the cocycle, and the second-order differential-equation comparison. The volume’s introduction distinguishes the Harvard autumn1969 seminar from publication in1970. Formula5.9.2 uses theta=S/6, so the present normalization is G=-12theta. This is a useful exact match, not evidence that an undated manuscript leaf precedes Deligne. The later Beilinson–Drinfeld text, written1993 and posted2005, supplies the precise smooth complex-curve oper formulation in§2.7 and§3.1–3.3. These exact locators are in the reference records and checked links.

Grothendieck’s own Brauer-group exposition gives an equally direct published comparison for the unmarked projective/Azumaya discussion. The opened source is dated May1965 for the Bourbaki talk and1966 for the seminar volume. Its§5 and§8 distinguish etale-local matrix/projective forms from a global splitting and identify the Brauer obstruction. Class A means an exact own-published formulation was found; it does not manufacture chronology for these undated leaves. Class E marks elementary classical facts used in the pages, without claiming a first-discovery date.

The characteristic-two failures are concrete. The ordinary Lie algebra pgl2 has an abelian derived plane W and relation `[h,w]=w`; its automorphism group is GL(W) semidirect W, of dimension six. It therefore has more automorphisms than the three-dimensional projective group. It also has an outer derivation sending e to f, and its filtered duality can fail for a nonsplit extension on an elliptic curve. These are exact independent checks. The fppf quotient SL2/mu2=PGL2 remains valid; failure of a Lie identification is not failure of that group quotient.

The universal coherent-target claim and the fixed-characteristic uniqueness proposal remain different questions from the existence of the classical Schwarzian. Adding an unused coherent direct summand defeats the unrestricted target-isomorphism assertion. A meaningful repaired theorem must specify naturality, differential order, target generation, and the base category. Hoshi’s paper, first circulated April2020 and published2024, gives a characteristic-two comparison through locally exact differentials; its exact target differs from the manuscript integral quadratic differential. Wakabayashi’s2021 framework explicitly assumes an odd prime. Neither source was used as a substitute for the manuscript’s unrestricted uniqueness claim.

The draft scan81 diagram is class F where its spaces and arrows cannot be identified. A formal resemblance to path-space or associated-bundle constructions is not an exact literature match. Its matrix and genus calculation survive only as a conditional reconstruction, with compactness, connectedness and genus at least2 stated. The margin’s cancellation scope still needs the image.
