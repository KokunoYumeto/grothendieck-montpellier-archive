## Mathematical review of root's written arguments

### Baire avoidance

The argument is sound with two points explicit. In a positive-dimensional complete noetherian local domain use a finite injective Cohen normalization A=V[[T1,…,Tr]], where V is a complete DVR; in equal characteristic choose one parameter for V. For each proper closed Z_i, a nonzero element of its ideal can be chosen in A because the finite integral map is dominant and the domain's generic point is not in Z_i. Evaluate the variables in powers of the maximal ideal of V. Each nonzero series has a closed zero set with empty interior, by the one-variable nonarchimedean identity argument applied successively. Baire supplies one specialization avoiding all chosen zero loci. A prime of the finite algebra over the kernel gives a one-dimensional closed integral subscheme not contained in any Z_i. The conclusion concerns noncontainment of a curve, not disjointness from every Z_i. Reduce an arbitrary complete local ring to its finitely many irreducible components; the zero-dimensional case is immediate. Root's completed proof reflects these qualifications.

### Smooth nilpotent algebraization

The smooth nilpotent-thickening argument is sound. A proper algebraization of a smooth formal scheme is smooth: the proper image of its closed nonsmooth locus would meet the closed point of the local base if nonempty. For a square-zero base thickening, the proper smooth deformation obstruction, choices, and automorphisms are controlled by H², H¹, and H⁰ of the tangent sheaf tensored with the ideal. Proper coherent formal comparison identifies all three with their formal counterparts. An existing formal lift thus supplies the vanishing obstruction and the appropriate isomorphism class; iterate a finite nilpotence filtration. This proves the smooth source lemma, not an arbitrary nonflat or nonproper algebraization theorem.

### Monodromy and stratified lines

The source explicitly uses an enlarged group that need not be profinite on nonnormal schemes. A finite-dimensional discrete representation has finite image only after the relevant profiniteness hypothesis is justified. Ordinary arithmetic π1 over a non-algebraically-closed field is also not automatically the Tannakian relative group. The opened dos Santos Proposition13 and its following warning make this distinction explicit. The relative Π question has its own margin question mark and is not established by the elementary construction from torsors. Supplement698/699 preserve these distinctions.

### GIT example and geometric reductivity

No overclaim was identified in root's specific SL2 example proof. The normalizer quotient, invariant (αβ)², stabilizer computation, openness through a finite flat square map, and nonproper one-parameter limit all support the stated conclusions over C. The action is free in the modern stabilizer sense and not proper; the source's old terminology must remain distinct. The p-power invariant-degree refinement is independently proved in `git_primary_followup.md`, so Haboush's arbitrary-positive-degree statement exactly supplies the manuscript's p^ν complement. Kempf's perfect-field hypothesis is present in source scan36 and must stay. The unrestricted finite-isometry fixed-point statement is refuted by the Q8 action, as root now records under681; it is not identical to the modern Centre Conjecture.
