# Primary-source memo for the root c100 audit

Primary source: Grothendieck, *Un théorème sur les homomorphismes de schémas abéliens*, Inventiones2(1966),59–78. GDZ volume identifier `PPN356556735_0002`; article images67–86. The saved program `programs/a_groth1966_ocr.py` downloaded those page images and OCRed them outside the deliverable; metadata/stdout are in `outputs/a_groth1966_ocr.txt`. The exact displayed formulae were interpreted with their surrounding sentences, not from keyword hits alone.

- **§1, TheoremA and Corollaries1–2, pp62–63.** Serre–Tate for a local Artinian base with residue characteristicp. Corollary2 extends the morphism-lifting assertion to a complete noetherian local base, citing EGAIII5.4.1. A andB already exist as abelian schemes; an existing p-system homomorphism algebraic on the closed fibre lifts algebraically. This does not construct an abelian scheme from arbitrary formal data.
- **§1, TheoremB, p63.** Tate’s generic full faithfulness is stated on a locally noetherian normal irreducible base with characteristic-zero function field. The following sentence says the characteristic restriction is plausibly superfluous, with equal characteristicp the remaining case. This is an exact published parallel to c93 scans53–54.
- **Theorem1.1, pp63–64.** S is locally noetherian, A andB abelian, and a morphism (u_p:T_p(A)\to T_p(B)) is given globally. For every associated point s of S there must be a specialization s′ of characteristicp and an algebraic fibre map realizing (u_p) there. Then a unique global algebraic map realizes (u_p).
- **Proposition1.2, p64.** The same locally noetherian base and global (u_p); every associated point has a specialization s′ with a faithfully flat cover of (operatorname{Spec}O_{S,s'}) on which the map is algebraic. Then it is uniquely algebraic globally. This is a proposition, not Theorem1.2. Its proof uses the separated unramified Hom scheme and its valuative properness, termed “essentially proper.”
- **Lemma1.2.1, p65.** For noetherian S, an unramified essentially proper H/S and a section over a schematically dense open U, extension is equivalent to compatibility on the double overlap for every finite S′/S carrying a candidate extension, with U′ dense. This is not unrestricted effective descent of arbitrary abelian objects along every proper epimorphism.
- **Theorem1.3, p66.** S is locally noetherian, normal, irreducible; (U=S\setminus V(p)). A map between Tate systems on U is assumed algebraic at one point of U that specializes into (V(p)). It then comes from a global algebraic map.
- **§2.1, p67.** For S reduced and locally of finite type over a characteristic-zero field, the algebraicity locus of a fixed Tate-system homomorphism is both open and closed; when it is allS, a global algebraic map exists. The paragraph separates specialization stability from the characteristic-zero generalization argument. It does not state representability of an arbitrary-base closed algebraicity locus.
- **§4 heading, p72.** The complement is explicitly dated5August1966.
- **Corollary4.2, pp72–75.** S locally noetherian normal of characteristiczero, U dense open, A abelian overU. Extension overS is equivalent to the prime-to-residue Tate system being unramified overS. In this characteristic-zero formulation any prime is allowed. The footnote says the result and proof principle come from a letter to Serre dated24November1964.
- **Corollary4.5, p76.** S regular of characteristiczero and complement ofU of codimension at least2: restriction of abelian schemes is an equivalence. Essential surjectivity uses purity; full faithfulness holds more generally for normalS and denseU.
- **Remark4.6, pp76–77.** In characteristicp, a varying height-one subgroup of a supersingular abelian surface gives an abelian scheme on the punctured plane that does not extend. This is the explicitly provided failure of the preceding characteristic-zero extension theorem. The source points toSGA3VI/VII4 for the technical subgroup construction.
- **§4.8, p77.** Asks an up-to-isogeny extension variant with a prime different from all residue characteristics; gives some simply connected finite-type positive cases but expresses doubt about the general statement.
- **§4.9, pp77–78.** Retains the locally noetherian normal/dense-open context of4.2, drops characteristiczero, assumes the boundary (S\setminus U) has characteristicp and the p-divisible group ofA extends toS; asks whether A extends. This is the exact question to compare with the root’s Krishnamoorthy–Pál2021 result. The article does not answer it here.

The memo records what the source states. The root audit owns the comparison with c100, EGA formal algebraization and subsequent extension theorems.
