## 1. The kernel of the realized diagonal algebra

The pages give distinguished generators and multiplication rules, but the word “base” does not survive realization on every allowed variety. The current P¹ example establishes a relation; it does not determine the relation ideal for an arbitrary Poincaré algebra. Hua’s November2026 preprint proves an ungraded diagram multiplication rule under explicit axioms, with no claim to compute every realization kernel.

The next task should fix a coefficient field, a particular graded Poincaré algebra, a normalized top class and a precise diagram algebra. Its deliverable would be the kernel of the realization map in a fixed number of factors, with a proof of the proposed generating relations. The first concrete step is to choose one model whose cohomology has an odd component, so that the task tests information absent from the current even P¹ check. This is a current direction, not a conjecture attributed to Grothendieck.

## 2. A single graded correspondence convention

The source alternates between Cy and yC and uses target-first indices. Its missing outer pushforward is a local type error, while the global order choice affects all Koszul signs. The next task would write one explicit graded convention and prove action compatibility, associativity and units from a minimal, stated axiom set, then compare each displayed manuscript formula with that convention. It should also say exactly which stable-system operations survive the cancelled extraordinary-pullback paragraph.

The first step is a variance-and-degree table for each map on11/044–048, followed by one odd-degree example. The ungraded theorem already checked does not decide the graded reconstruction. This can be finished as a focused companion section without launching a general classification of bivariant systems.

## 3. Integral quadric normalizations and specialization

The manuscript’s separate tables become consistent only after distinguishing the ruling basis, the index2 sum/difference lattice, integral versus finite coefficients, and the relative-dimension convention. Its monodromy assertion also needs regular total space or an equivalent ordinary-smoothing assumption. All these distinctions affect the proposed primitive generator.

The next task would produce a single dimension-indexed table of hyperplane restriction, Gysin pushforward, primitive kernels, local cohomology and specialization. The first step is to fix the integral ruling classes and Tate twists, and ask the scan reader to decide the listed degree and sign alarms. The task should retain the extra adjacent group for finite2-primary coefficients. It is a correction and exposition project, not a claim that classical quadric cohomology remains open.

## 4. The nested-torus statement in SGA3

The broad statement is false for explicit nested tori inSL4, while the usual maximal-torus version has a short proof by conjugacy in the centralizer. The checked2008 retypesetting retains the broad wording; the original1970 typography and any unpublished errata have not been collated.

A focused task could turn the current calculation into a concise correction note with exact lattice data, a geometric centralizer argument and the refined statement. The first step is to inspect the original1970 page and compare editions. Any contact with editors or authors is outside this task and would require a separate instruction. The mathematical content should remain independent of whether the defect originated in the original text or a later transcription.

## 5. Delzant’s questions and comparison maps

Both offprint questions are solved, but the explanation is incomplete if one merely writes “Merkurjev”: Delzant works with the rank-zero ideal inGW, while the standard comparison uses the fundamental ideal inW. The current audit supplies that ideal identification and the degree-two symbol computation. The Clifford-descent normalization still lacks a direct check of Springer’s original1959 paper.

The next task would produce a historical companion of a few pages: precise source notation, the two questions, the comparison square, and the chronology of Milnor1970 and Merkurjev1981. The first step is to obtain and read the exact Springer passage to settle the remaining normalization issue. This is a documentary completion, not a new open norm-residue problem.

## 7. Reflection typescript provenance and proof repair

The no.383 sheets are plausibly within the Bourbaki reflection-group development, but that exact document identity is unverified. Their chamber definition needs a complement, and their dual-angle equality fails in essential typeA3. The nonnegative-angle corollary is nevertheless true by inverse-Gram positivity. The affine local-finiteness proof ends mid-sentence and has a short completion under the stated properness assumption.

The first step is a scan and edition collation of III-2 andIII-5, then a search for the precise numbered typescript. The next deliverable should distinguish an incorrect equality from a correct corollary with a defective proof. It should not assign the reused theorem to Grothendieck merely because the sheets were preserved among his papers.

## 8. The two Deligne reports

The perfect-complex λ report already announces a solution, limited to the scope then written. The Leray report is conditional on Lefschetz and needs an actual relative operator. Modern theorem statements are available, but they do not identify the manuscript that Grothendieck saw or its first circulation date.

The first step is a dated concordance between B12/121 and123, SGA6 editorial material, Deligne’s1968 paper and contemporary correspondence if supplied in a later task. The outcome would clarify what was known and written at that moment. It should merge with the main cote12 companion and preserve the distinction between the secretary’s27March1968 letter and the undated mathematical reports.
