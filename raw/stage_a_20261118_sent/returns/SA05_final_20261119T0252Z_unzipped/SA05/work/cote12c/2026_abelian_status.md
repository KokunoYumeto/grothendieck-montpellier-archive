**Ready-to-use wording.** October2026 preprints claim the rational and generalized Hodge conjectures for complex CM abelian varieties, with consequences for rational Tate over finite fields and Hodge-standard positivity for abelian varieties in arbitrary characteristic. Milne’s9November report treats these consequences as theorems. This dossier checked their precise statements and implication scope, rather than independently verifying the new proof. They do not establish Hodge for arbitrary complex abelian varieties, integral cycle surjectivity, or the manuscript’s full geometric-level programme for arbitrary varieties.
