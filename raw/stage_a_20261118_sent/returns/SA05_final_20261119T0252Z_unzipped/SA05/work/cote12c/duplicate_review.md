### Details needed to avoid losing a distinct result

D01 is an item merger, not a claim identification. Keep C62, the motivic K0 measure, and C3001, its complex weight-graded numerical shadow, as distinct propositions with their own coefficient and target hypotheses.

D04 must retain separate theorem paragraphs. Jannsen Theorem1's numerical-motive comparison belongs with root56; the abstract End-ring criterion in Jannsen Lemma2 remains late3050/C3056. Arapura's rational characteristic-zero/smooth-base hypotheses differ from the field-embedded-in-C and PID hypotheses of Choudhury–Gallauer. The bounded October/November2026 abelian update also remains explicit.

For D05, keep the connectedness condition of the checked Huber formulation. A genericity statement is additional to existence of a torsor, and neither package has the missing18/059 source.

For D06, keep the distinction between Corollary5.11 for abelian varieties and Corollary5.12 for proper smooth varieties with X(k) nonempty. The latter is an injection. None of this removes the prime-to-characteristic condition in étale Kummer theory.

For D08, the modern sequence has neutral compatible fibres and effective finite Galois descent as hypotheses. Exactness, geometric-kernel connectedness, identity-component identification and splitting remain different statements. The checked arXivv4 Kahn locators and the journal's later revision must not be mixed silently.

For D09, cite Deligne–Milne Proposition2.23 in its connected-affine-group, characteristic-zero form. The centre-free hyperbolic fundamental-group theorem is a different comparison, not a consequence for a motivic group.

## Claim redirect

**SA05-C3016 → SA05-C75** is the single actual claim duplicate identified.

Both target the upper bound

\[
w\le j+\min(2i,2n)
\]

for ordinary cohomology on a smooth base, with the root witness retaining its own n versus2n reading uncertainty. The late margin asks the two component inequalities separately. Its coefficient assumption is stronger: a direct summand of smooth-projective R^j. The Legendre sheaf satisfies that stronger assumption, and H¹ has weight4 while i=n=j=1 would permit at most3.

The merged record must preserve documentary status by location: the bound is asserted at scan51 and questioned at scan108. It must not overwrite the witness uncertainty or present the later question as an unqualified theorem.

Do not merge any of the following into C75:

- C76, the arithmetic H¹ coefficient-window assertion, even though the same family after a twist refutes it.
- C3017, the compact-support lower-bound question.
- C3030, the local inertia weight bound at the missing point.
- C3018 or C3026, the independent missing-coefficient and level-formula alarms.

For indexed-entry routing, late #19 (R04, the global example) can point to root62; late #17 (W13, the local Tate degeneration) should remain at late3016.
