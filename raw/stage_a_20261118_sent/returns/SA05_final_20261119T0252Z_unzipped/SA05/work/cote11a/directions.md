## 1. Graded conventions and exchange

The outcome would be a single, checkable statement of the kernel category with the exact maps, degrees, symmetries and exchange squares required for associativity, units, graphs and tensor products. The even formalism is established in Stacks0FFZ; the manuscript itself rejects a naive all-degree tensor formula. The scalar-gauge Cartesian-square counterexample shows why cartesianness is insufficient in the abstract gauged category. The first step is to make a table of every square used in the fourfold-product and graph proofs, then select one explicit tensor-duality convention before comparing each manuscript exponent. This does not require proving a new general base-change theorem.

## 2. Diagonal data and special power systems

The outcome would distinguish three statements: ordinary finite-projective Frobenius comultiplication, special commutative ungraded systems with multiplicative gauges, and the wider unfinished graded/degenerate theory. Kock2003 Proposition 2.4.10 settles the first; Hua2026 Theorem 1.2 gives the second with its stated restrictions. The partition-system counterexample shows that unrestricted evaluation at one point is not an equivalence. The first step is to compare the exact manuscript hypotheses on scans38–42 with the theorem’s gauge, specialness and empty-set conventions, resolving the v1/v2 variance difference without treating reported Lean verification as an executed certificate. Only then formulate a residual theorem about degenerate or graded systems.

## 3. Total-space integrality

The outcome would identify whether a published theorem answers the draft’s intended normal-base question, with its exact flatness, excellence, residue-field and closed-fiber hypotheses. The literal unreduced wording is false, while Murayama2022 Corollary 4.1 proves a precise statement about all fibers. Its Remark 4.2 excludes a generic domain-ascent shortcut. The first step is to obtain and read the final EGAIV2 §7.9 statement and the cited earlier localization sources, then explicitly prove or disprove the bridge from fiberwise punctual integrality to the intended total-space assertion. The current dossier does not declare that intended question open in the present literature.

## 4. Generic freeness and separating families

The outcome would identify the cancelled lemma’s actual module and every missing condition, distinguishing it from EGAIV3 11.9.7–11.9.10 and the separate locally-projective question11.9.8. The two supplied textual witnesses repeat the same malformed B_h/A_h expression under different scan labels. The first step is to recover the preceding proposition and inspect the relevant scan; then compare the support-induction proof line by line with final EGA. No new general generic-freeness theorem is required.

## 5. Nilpotent density

The outcome is an exact editorial correction of the ordinary lifting lemma and a separate assessment of its universal-relative variant. The explicit square-zero counterexample settles the literal ordinary assertion negatively, and SGA3II IX4.2 supplies a sufficient locally-free-layer hypothesis. The first step is to check whether this condition was inherited from preceding manuscript context. The relative assertion should then be examined on its own, since the counterexample does not satisfy universal density over the closed base.

## 6–7. GIT exercises and the configuration continuation

For rational attainment, a short proof should reduce the finite rational-weight maximum to rational polyhedral faces and check nonempty direction space and rational norm. Kempf’s theorem is a comparison with a stronger instability problem, not a substitute for spelling out the exercise. For the determinant criterion, both attached continuation pieces have already been read; the remaining local gap is the final equivalent criterion on typed page3.16. The first step is to collate the main GIT proposition and its independent-set Appendix proof, preserving the nonzero separating-functional condition and the stable/semistable distinction. These are established theorems needing exact source reconstruction, not new conjectures.

## 8–11. Exact statements before broad comparisons

The selected-map and nonadditive variants need their missing axioms recovered before a meaningful universal theorem can be tested. External cote 16/47/54 comparisons need the absent source witnesses, so their first step is attachment-level recovery, not further mathematical extrapolation. The modern perfect-kernel model and Oda’s exact degree-one corollaries are already available as bounded comparison points. For the English cycle-class sheet, authorship and preceding trace/twist conventions come first; the familiar cycle-class theorem does not identify the author or justify Poincaré duality on every singular cycle.
