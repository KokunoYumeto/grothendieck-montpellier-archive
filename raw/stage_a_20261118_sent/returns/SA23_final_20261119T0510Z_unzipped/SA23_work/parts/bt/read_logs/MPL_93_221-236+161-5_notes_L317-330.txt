324: ### S02 — Semisimplicity does not eliminate the component group
325: 
326: **Location:** 161-5/005–007, semisimplicity, reductivity, and algebraic envelopes. **Kind:** unpursued implication. **Words:** “semi-simple”; “composantes connexes”.
