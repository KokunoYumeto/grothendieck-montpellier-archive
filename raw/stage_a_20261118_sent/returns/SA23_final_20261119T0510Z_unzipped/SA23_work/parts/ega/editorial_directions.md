# Editorial directions — ega

These reader/editorial directions are preserved outside Stage6. Their earlier ratings are historical judgments, not an authorial-problem ranking.

## 1. The non-Cohen–Macaulay construction

ID: D-SA23-ega-01. Interest: 5/5. Feasible progress in one run: 3/5.

Items: SA23-1301. Claims: C-SA23-ega-077.

Authorial problems: none. Editorial tasks: ED-SA23-ega-41.

Known: The fibre-product exact sequence and its extra Tor term are checked in this run. The gap is earlier: normality and failure of Cohen–Macaulayness do not alone isolate the bad locus sufficiently for the intended hypersurface choice. Stacks00RD gives the standard finite-type openness context, but does not supply the missing projective input.

Residual: A precise example with the intended unusual change in the non-Cohen–Macaulay locus, including every geometric existence choice and local depth calculation.

First step: Specify the desired dimension and isolated bad locus on the projective scheme, then find one explicit normal projective example whose local cohomology is computable. A useful first run can finish that example or demonstrate that the proposed dimension pattern is incompatible with the chosen geometry.

## 4. The withdrawn compactness proposition

ID: D-SA23-ega-04. Interest: 5/5. Feasible progress in one run: 3/5.

Items: SA23-1303. Claims: C-SA23-ega-053.

Authorial problems: none. Editorial tasks: ED-SA23-ega-26.

Known: The original linear-variety proof is invalid and the1967 errata expressly withdraw the proposition. The finite-solvability polynomial example checks that proof mechanism only. Successive countable adic lifting has a different argument.

Residual: Either a counterexample satisfying the exact hypotheses of withdrawn EGA0.19.3.12, or a corrected theorem with additional countability or topology assumptions.

First step: Extract the exact original theorem and define its square-open ideals and topology independently of the defective proof. Compare countable versus uncountable inverse systems before attempting a general algebra-lift example. The task should begin from the withdrawal, not by treating the old assertion as established.

## 8. Lang’s alleged gap

ID: D-SA23-ega-08. Interest: 3/5. Feasible progress in one run: 3/5.

Items: SA23-111. Claims: C-SA23-ega-006.

Authorial problems: none. Editorial tasks: ED-SA23-ega-03.

Known: Jouanolou points specifically to separability of a completion fraction field. Nagata’s1957 note removes a normic-form assumption in related C_i results, which is not enough to identify this analytic gap. The original Lang proof has not been read in this run.

First step: Obtain the original1952 paper, locate the convergent-series/complete-valued-field argument, and trace every use of separability. Report a proof comparison with exact pages, even if the outcome is that Jouanolou’s allegation cannot be confirmed.

## 12. Differential-field and discriminant collation
