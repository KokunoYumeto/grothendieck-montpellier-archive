771: \uline{The notion of a topos}. \inserted{(gener\unsure{.?|al.?|.}) ``topology'' $=$ study of topoi,}
772: Why it is a natural and useful generalisation of the notion of topological space?
773: 
774: a) It is the natural setting for most ``algebraic'' (and non algebraic) structures.
775: 
776: b) As such, it allows to construct classifying topoi for such structures (possibly all): algebra = topology !

781: $*$ clutching together of pieces à la Artin

788: f) They give the ultimate outcome for \inserted{the intuitive notion of} localization.
