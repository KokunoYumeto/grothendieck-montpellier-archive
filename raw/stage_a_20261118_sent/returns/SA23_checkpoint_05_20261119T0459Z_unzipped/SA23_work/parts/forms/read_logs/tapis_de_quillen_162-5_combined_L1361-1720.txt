1377: There is an extensive literature (e.g. [1], Ch.VI) concerned with
1378: Hilbert's problem of representing a positive-valued form with real
1379: coefficients as a sum of squares. The corresponding problem for her-%
1380: mitian forms does not seem to have been touched. In this paper we
1381: present a theorem about hermitian forms which arose in connection
1382: with a priori estimates for partial differential equations. This theorem is
1383: proved by the methods of a priori estimates and one of its corollaries
1384: is the Hilbert Nullstellensatz over $\mathbf{C}$, so we obtain a direct analytic proof
1385: of the latter theorem.
