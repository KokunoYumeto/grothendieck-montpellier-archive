250: 
251: **Location:** `68.pdf`, scan 056, a)–c), especially the unrestricted commutative base ring k. **Kind:** own idea / proposed obstruction. **Context:** H68, “Les déterminants gradués”, his p. 55 / scan 056, its paragraph mentioning SK₁(k). **R:** none. **Confidence:** high for the conditional construction; no ring with a required nontrivial character is asserted here.
