## LM6: procyclic normalizers

The original `LM6_POINTS_AS_SECTIONS_paper.tex` was read at physical lines 1255–1290 (A.22) and 2854–2908 (A.50, statement and opening proof). A.22 retains an unresolved common-kernel procyclic-normalizer residual, including the subgroup denoted R_nil(F′) inside F″. A.50 concerns a nontrivial closed procyclic geometric subgroup A of an affine hyperbolic curve over a number field, normalized by a subgroup with open arithmetic image. For a support prime ℓ, a specified power of the conjugation character descends. The asserted dichotomy is cuspidal inertia or, after finite extension, a weight-one Jacobian line. It is not a theorem that every such normalizer character is cyclotomic. The original statement, not just the truncated status-register excerpt, was checked. The complete external proof was not independently re-proved here.

## LM7: open Fermat curves

`LM7_return/sections/C.tex`, lines 12–91, states and proves the returned C.1 description: the Picard 1-motive is [Div_D^0→J], with generalized-Jacobian dual, torus (Res_D G_m)/G_m, character lattice Div_D^0, and cocharacter lattice Z[D]/Z·ΣD. The reported weight ranks are 3n−1 and (n−1)(n−2), totaling n²+1. C.8, lines 401–436, refutes the earlier reduction of the whole Fermat weight extension to one rank-two toric tower; it distinguishes that tower and an elliptic generalized Jacobian from the full extension, whose reported order is n. `sections/E.tex`, lines 1063–1111, retains E.23 as an open unrestricted section-image problem for the marked metabelian/depth-two quotients. This is a comparison of formulations and scope, not endorsement of every subsequent external ledger refinement.
