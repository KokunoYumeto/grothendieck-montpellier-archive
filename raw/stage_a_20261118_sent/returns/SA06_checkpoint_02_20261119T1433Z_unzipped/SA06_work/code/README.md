# Reproducibility and audit policy

Source-reading utilities enumerate lines and indexed note entries only. They do not
decide verdicts, merge items, infer mathematics, or classify literary fates.
Those decisions are authored after reading by the research agents. Saved source
reading outputs are documentary audit material, not proof certificates.

Line locators refer to 1-based physical lines in the supplied transcription files.
Scan locators are exactly the arguments of `\\scanpage{}`. Alternate Codex readings
are evidence of a transcription disagreement; without images neither is adjudicated.

The source archive was extracted exactly once at initialization. No second-pass
directory occurs in it. Only cotes 14 and 15 belong to this part; the other cotes in
TOPIC.md are recorded as other-part scope, never represented as reviewed here.
