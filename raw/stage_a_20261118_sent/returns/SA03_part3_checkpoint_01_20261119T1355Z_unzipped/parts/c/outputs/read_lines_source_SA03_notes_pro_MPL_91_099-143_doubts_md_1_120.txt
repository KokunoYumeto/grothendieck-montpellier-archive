   49	## Doubt register
   50	
   51	Every explicit `\unsure` and `\illegible` occurrence in the page fragments is indexed below. Manuscript line bands are approximate visual locations, counted in reading orientation; diagrams and marginalia use their position instead. The separate TeX-line column gives an exact reproducible location in the individual page fragment. Handwritten line breaks inside paragraphs have not been forced into the transcription.
   52	
   53	Confidence concerns the first reading only. Medium means that the visible form supports that reading but leaves an unresolved stroke, ending, alphabet, or punctuation; low marks an obscured cancellation, longer conjectural reading, or a passage with no recoverable word. Alternatives are visual possibilities, not mathematical corrections. A blank alternative means that no second reading is offered.
