ENTRY 33 ### B02 — Kummer classes and constant cyclic groups are not interchangeable

**Source:** 62.pdf, cote 62, scan(s) 019; Kummer group labels.  
**Kind:** own idea.  
**Manuscript:** “Ext. Kummérienne de gr Z/3”.

Over a base where three is invertible, adjoining a cube root of a unit naturally defines a \(\mu_3\)-torsor, and identifying this with a torsor under the constant group \(\mathbf Z/3\) requires a primitive cube root of unity. Over general schemes even \(H^1(S,\mu_n)\) need not be only units modulo nth powers, because the Kummer sequence also sees n-torsion line bundles; for an affine local base the line-bundle term vanishes (Stacks Project, n.d., Tag 03PK). These are necessary qualifications for interpreting the scratch correspondences and the later scan 048 assertion.

ENTRY 35 ### B04 — The queried cubic discriminant has a sign and coefficient problem

**Source:** 62.pdf, cote 62, scan(s) 021; product-of-derivatives calculation and boxed question.  
**Kind:** self-flagged.

ENTRY 40 ### B09 — The normalizer problem has a short tame-geometric solution

**Source:** 62.pdf, cote 62, scan(s) 030; opening ‘Prouver’.  
**Kind:** self-flagged.  
**Manuscript:** “Prouver : le normalisateur … est S₃×(Z/2)”.
