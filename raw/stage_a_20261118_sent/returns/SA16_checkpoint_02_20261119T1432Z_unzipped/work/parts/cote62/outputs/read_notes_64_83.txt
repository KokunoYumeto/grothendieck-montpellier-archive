ENTRY 73 ### C24 — Lifting a triple to an elliptic curve has a square-root obstruction

ENTRY 81 ### D08 — The completion lemma needs strict topological homomorphisms

**Source:** 62.pdf, cote 62, scan(s) 043; Lemma 1 and marginal ‘topologt’.  
**Kind:** reading question.  
**Manuscript:** “homomorphismes topologiques”; marginal “topologt|topol.?”

In the older terminology, a topological homomorphism may mean that the quotient topology agrees with the topology induced on the image, a condition now often called strictness. Mere continuity does not suffice: the identity \(\mathbf Q_{\mathrm{discrete}}\to\mathbf Q_{\mathrm{usual}}\), followed by the zero map to zero, is exact, whereas completion gives \(\mathbf Q\to\mathbf R\to0\), which is not exact in the middle. The lemma is therefore to be compared with strict exact sequences, not arbitrary algebraically exact sequences of continuous maps.
