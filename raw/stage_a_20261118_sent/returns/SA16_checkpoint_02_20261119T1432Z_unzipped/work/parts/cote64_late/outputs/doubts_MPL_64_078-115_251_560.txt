0514 ## Legibility and reliability
0515 
0516 All 38 full scans and their 76 supplied halves were read directly by the main transcriber without OCR, followed by a second visual reading of every page after all drafts existed. The main text is usually recoverable, but the hand is very compressed, and sparse strokes often do not determine word endings or tiny indices. The least secure areas are cancelled writing, the clipped lower note on scan 84, the crowded upper-right note on scan 99, the overwritten lower lines on scan 109, both diagonal notes on scan 110, and the insertion on scan 111. Enlarged and rotated crops were used for those areas.
0517 
0518 Confidence labels concern readings, not mathematical validity. Medium means a plausible ink reading with a live doubt; low means strongly overwritten, clipped, or otherwise weakly resolved. No uncertain reading is labelled high merely because it would give a familiar theorem. The transcription preserves apparent mistakes, incomplete formulas, duplicate words and unfinished arguments. The working apparatus and audit log preserve superseded first-pass readings, so they should not be mistaken for the current text.

0520 All manuscript page numbers are separate from archive foliation. Scan 101 is a title sheet; no author page number has been supplied on scan 114 where none is visible.
