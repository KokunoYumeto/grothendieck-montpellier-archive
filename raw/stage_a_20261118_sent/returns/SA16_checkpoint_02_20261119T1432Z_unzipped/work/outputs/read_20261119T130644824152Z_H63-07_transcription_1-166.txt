   11: 1. \handwritten{\uline}{Introduction}. \quad In \handwritten{\uwave}{(2)} the au\deleted{l}thor has given an account of some results on congruences for modular forms based on real time order of discovery,ind\deleted{\unsure{i}}\unsure{e|i}spensably aided by a computer.    13: We give no proofs in this paper. Our purpose here is to indicate that some \reversemarginpar\margin{\handwritten{German\\ `pee'}}\normalmarginpar``$\handwritten{\underline}{\handwritten{\mathfrak{p}}}$-adic'' theory of Hecke operators exists analogous to the exact classical theory.
