# Method and reproducibility

Run: SA25 part 1, 19 November 2026. Model: GPT-6 Astra Pro. The runtime did not expose the inherited reasoning-effort value; none is guessed. Three section readers and a coordinating reader worked from the supplied entry index, notes, doubts and diplomatic transcription. The source archive was extracted once. No scan images or second-pass files were supplied.

The source IDs, quotations, verdicts, merge decisions, claim formulations, classifications, problem assessments and link judgments were authored after reading. The Python programs serialize these decisions, normalize explicitly approved aliases, calculate finite examples and validate coverage; they do not infer verdicts from headings or keywords. `work/merge_decisions.json` is the explicit cross-reader reconciliation. Individual source comparisons survive in each merged item's components.

Physical line numbers refer to the supplied LaTeX file. The independent source files are omitted from this deliverable as requested; `source_manifest.json` gives their identities and hashes. Every output under `outputs` has a corresponding program or recorded tool request. External sources are represented by queries, opened URLs, exact locators and concise findings, not full copied articles. Bibliographic `verified` means the URL or DOI was opened; the scope of mathematical checking is separately recorded in `CHECK_EVENTS.tsv`.

The web-search requests and their source identifiers are in the root outputs and section search logs. Section coverage tables map claims and problems to those actual requests. A general or negative search never establishes universal nonexistence, current openness, or novelty. Failed retrievals remain visible. Private project targets absent from the attachment and not retrieved remain explicitly unchecked leads, separate from checked public manuscript counterparts.

Formal arguments are in the proof notes. Finite certificates only establish the bounded algebraic, set-theoretic or graph calculations stated in their outputs. They do not replace unbounded arguments, topos reasoning, or CW-topology proofs. Refined statements record the exact extra hypotheses used after a failed proposal.

The clock service supplies UTC run and checkpoint times; filesystem modification times are not used to infer chronology. Checkpoints are immutable ZIPs at 45-minute work intervals, contain all authored work available then, omit the supplied attachments and older checkpoint archives, and are saved independently. The final ZIP is the sole requested final download.
