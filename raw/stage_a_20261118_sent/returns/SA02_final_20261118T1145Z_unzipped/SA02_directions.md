# SA02 research directions (provisional)

These are research leads extracted from the indexed notes, not results. They are ranked by the combination of mathematical interest and tractability in a short follow-up. No direction is developed here.

1. **Quadratic-algebra product and discriminant identity (cote 84, scans 172/192).** Sources: entries whose headings concern the local quadratic-algebra product, Hua's formula and the discriminant relation. Establish whether the manuscript's product formula is exactly equivalent to the published local construction after generator change, with all base-ring hypotheses explicit. First step: formalize the two presentations over a universal coefficient ring and prove the change-of-generator identity; then compare exact statements in the literature.

2. **Tensor-realization kernel in characteristic two (cote 84, scans 172/192).** Sources: notes on the abstract product versus its tensor realization and the annihilator ideal. Establish the precise kernel under the monic local hypotheses and delimit when the tensor map is injective. First step: write the map on a free basis and compute its kernel over a general commutative ring, separating sufficient from necessary conditions.

3. **Five-axiom norm package versus multiplicativity (cote 84, scans 108–110).** Sources: notes on the omitted multiplicativity condition and the finite Z/4 example. Establish whether the author's recap is a definition, a theorem under hidden hypotheses, or an incomplete list. First step: reproduce the finite counterexample independently and formulate the minimal added axiom.

4. **Cote 90 saturation criteria and countability boundary.** Sources: N90-01 onward. Establish the exact equivalence and where countable generation enters. First step: transcribe the four conditions with all module hypotheses and compare them to standard purity/saturation criteria.

5. **Cote 91 non-projective Hom / Greenberg–Néron construction.** Sources: the N91 entries. Establish the exact functorial statement and hypotheses, separating the manuscript's construction from later unramified-functor results. First step: formalize the represented functor and compare with the exact Murre exposé passages cited by the notes.

6. **χ-split extension product and sign conventions (cote 84, scans 124–144 and 170).** Sources: extension-product notes. Establish the precise endpoint-fixed isomorphism question and isolate which sign convention causes the obstruction. First step: write both extension cocycles with fixed source/target identifications and check the induced map.

Priority is provisional: the first three directions have substantial algebraic detail already present in the supplied notes; the latter three need a more exact source-level formulation before literature conclusions are possible.
