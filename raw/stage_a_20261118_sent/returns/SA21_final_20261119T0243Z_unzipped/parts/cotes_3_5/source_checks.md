# Exact reference checks and search limits

All access dates are 19 November 2026. `verified` means the cited link or DOI was opened and the stated content actually inspected; it does not mean every theorem in the work was proved here. The source keys are in `references.bib`.

## Pre-1986 sources

**BK-1 — Birkhoff1948, IV.5–6, pp.54–57.** Polarities/Galois connections give complete closed-set lattices; symmetric irreflexive polarity gives orthocomplementation. Booleanity is not automatic.

**BK-2 — Birkhoff1948, IX.4–5, pp.139–140.** The finite distributive representation and the join-prime role of irreducibles supply the comparison; ordinary irreducibility in an arbitrary lattice is insufficient.

**BK-3 — Birkhoff1948, X.3, p.154, Theorem3.** Boolean algebra/ring equivalence, with join x+y−xy.

**BK-4 — Birkhoff1948, XI.3, p.174, Theorem3; XI.5, pp.176–177, Theorem5.** Boolean spectrum and regular-open completion. The point/ultrafilter identification for P(L) is also checked directly.

**ST-1 — Stong1966, pp.325–327, Propositions1–6.** Finite minimal neighborhoods, T0/order correspondence and comparison-chain connectivity. These pages were rendered and visually read. **AX-1 — Alexandroff1937:** metadata verified; unreliable redirect/403 prevented full-text checking. The historical attribution is supported by Stong’s reference; detailed arbitrary-cardinality facts are re-proved from definitions here rather than assigned an unseen theorem number.

**KS-1 — KochenSpecker1967, pp.65–66.** The authors define partial Boolean algebra and mutually compatible Boolean contexts. The source does not supply Grothendieck’s added moderated closure axioms. The issue year and publisher DOI year differ; both are retained.

**QUI — Quillen1973, §1 Theorem A, printed p.93 in the opened copy.** Contractible comma categories imply a homotopy equivalence of nerves, also in the dual orientation. For posets the relevant comma category is a principal-ideal inverse image. The manuscript’s stronger universal assertion is not identified with Theorem A without extra definitions.

**BORG — Borges1967, §§1–2, Lemma2.1, pp.474–476.** Simple topology extensions and their restrictions were inspected. Its T1 assumptions and single-added-set construction differ from V’s arbitrary prescribed finer topology on a closed subspace. This was a backward historical lead, not an exact match.

## Later results

**COUR — Courcelle2020, §2, Theorems3.5–3.6, pp.5–6.** Finite/countable convention and strong B-minimal reconstruction criterion were checked. First arXiv circulation21April2020 differs from journal2020. Bankston2013 §3.1 distinguishes road betweenness from Birkhoff betweenness; its open road-axiomatization question does not refute the manuscript reconstruction.

**WWY-1 — WaasWoolfYokura2026, Proposition2.6; Proposition3.3, Corollary3.4, Proposition3.9.** Preorders/Alexandrov equivalence and quotient stratification under local finiteness were read with their hypotheses. First arXiv25July2024; v2 8July2025; journal2026. This is a precise comparison for the quotient aspect, not a proof of the entire figure calculus.

**HUA — Hua2026, §§78–79, pp.366–370.** Exact matches: order reconstruction from cuts, and a negative answer to Boolean virtual supports. The statement hypotheses and finite countermodel were independently checked. The paper uses project modernized readings, so it is not an independent archival witness. Public Lean sources were fully read; they were not compiled here. Its figure-axiom omission is addressed by this run’s Python certificate. Publication status: November2026 preprint, version2; exact first-circulation day unverified.

## Published-work links and negative searches

Esquisse1984, published1997, end§5 and§6 pp.32–35: stratified spaces, tame dévissage and tubular-neighborhood aims provide a conceptual predecessor, not an exact theorem about these two orders or moderated algebras. Malgoire2002 is an institutional presentation notice. CruzMorales2021 is a historical overview; only abstract/metadata was used. None establishes an A-class later publication of the specific SA21 claims.

Named-source searches included zbMATH Open, arXiv, MathOverflow, Numdam, EGA/SGA, the Montpellier archive and the CSG bibliography. Direct zbMATH access failed; indexed searches were run and the failure is disclosed. EGAI original full PDF exceeded the web size limit. SGA modern translation hits are not used as primary mathematical evidence; the parent run checks original editions. MathOverflow contains recent project-dependent answers and is used only as a bibliographic lead. A D entry means no exact match found in these bounded searches. It is never a claim that no result exists, and it does not mean that the statement is mathematically open after a direct proof or counterexample here.
