## Searches that did not establish an exact fate

Queries and full returned search records are in `search_log.tsv` and `outputs/search_*.json`, with opened-page records in `outputs/open_*.json`. They cover the manuscript's French terms; complete ortholattices, Galois maps and relations; tensor products; factorization and tabulation; named searches of zbMATH, arXiv, MathOverflow, Numdam, EGA and SGA; and backwards references from later papers. A Numdam paper supplied the Shmuely bibliography and zbMATH link. The latter access was blocked. No exact external resolution was identified for the scan104 span with the particular morphism/comorphism definitions. The independent refutation P10 is not presented as a published result. No exact published Grothendieck passage was found for the objects in these five folders; a generic topical resemblance is not entered as a checked publication link.

The Ischi–Seal Chu-construction paper and a Numdam Baer-semigroup seminar were checked as leads. Their more restrictive hypotheses do not answer the general span question. A separate RIMS finite-context paper was not mistaken for the arbitrary-closure-space theorem of Domenach–Leclerc. No mathematical fate is inferred from MathOverflow discussion alone.
