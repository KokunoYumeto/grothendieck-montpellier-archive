# Other-project endpoints in the SA21 notes

## Scope and result

All **125 Context paragraphs** in `MPL_156-1+156-2_notes.md` were read in full,
with their stable note IDs and source lines. The separate nine-row open-register
comparison in note **#118** was also read, together with the source’s context
qualifications in **#115** and bibliography in **#121**. This audit identifies
**54 targets**, counting secondary GALOIS, PATHS and TOTASPH IDs explicitly
named inside those paragraphs and the two original context documents.

The recovered material supplies **20 located textual endpoints**: eleven full
return rows, six selected register excerpts, one run-summary entry, one complete
earlier research note, and one reproduced manuscript passage in an earlier
companion. Two further targets are present only as indirect ID mentions.
Thirty exact targets remain absent from the recovered selections, and the two
original package-48 context files have not been recovered. These are bounded
documentary findings. They establish neither truth of an earlier run’s theorem
nor absence of its proof elsewhere.

The complete per-target record is `project_links.tsv`, with stable source IDs,
exact source lines, checked destination lines, the statement actually present,
the return’s own status, and the limitations. `links.tsv` has the requested
Stage5 schema. `located_links.tsv` selects the twenty located endpoints;
`unresolved_leads.tsv` keeps the other thirty-four leads visible. The two
indirect references are deliberately not counted as exact statement matches.

## The recovered packages are not the original SA21 context

The SA21 notes identify their context as package48, cotes156-1/156-2, with a
register built on14November2026 at18:02UTC. Their README’s finite-cutting,
pro-object and workshop interpretation was explicitly a planning guess made
before the pages were read (source lines939–941,1192–1196).

The first recovered archive instead concerns **package69**, cotes108,112–115.
Its README is about the dossier around *Pursuing Stacks*, and its register was
selected on17November at03:18UTC. Its planning selection contains **zero of
twenty-three** possible war-game entries (RESULTS_STATE:513–515). The second
archive concerns **package102**, cote138 scans90–133, with a register selected
on17November at03:20UTC. Its README concerns the arithmetic of maps and its
war-game selection contains different Galois and cartographic questions
(RESULTS_STATE:440–449). Neither has WG2 B15 (§1.2), WG2 §3.1 #2, or the
original README’s SA21 guess. No historical instruction in either archive was
treated as an instruction for this task.

Register section numbers must also be distinguished from the stable IDs in
NOTES_INDEX. The register counts both level-two and level-three headings.
Thus **MPL_147_138-190 §69** is the source note **151-33**, the65th `###`
heading after four `##` headings. Likewise the recovered register’s SA21 §122
corresponds to stable note#116 after six level-two headings. This is a numbering
convention; that mismatch alone does not show that the note content changed.
`ordinal_checks.json` records the exact counts.

## What the located comparisons actually support

The finite-poset comparison is the best recovered geometric link. STRATA A.4
is selected at package69 RESULTS_STATE:454, and STRATA0.1 at476. The full
BISITE G.10 row is at DOSSIER_STATEMENTS_WITH_STATUS:223. It concerns a given
finite poset P, its minimum-vertex stratification on |NP|, opposite-poset exit
variance, and the specified coefficient categories of constructible sheaves.
That matches the SA21 notes’ use of it as a comparison model. It supplies no
identification of a réseau with an arbitrary poset and no reconstruction from
unlabelled incidence data. The purported all-small-poset STRATA A.12 row is
missing, so its hypotheses cannot be imported from the finite theorem.

PATHS E.10 and D.13 have full rows at DOSSIER lines104 and79. The former rejects
the sufficiency of components of a marked subcategory for general exit-path
localization mapping spaces. The latter records a failure of rectification for
relative-poset localizations, with specified circle and signed-face examples.
The SA21 notes use them as limitations on broad comparisons, not as theorems
about ficelle cutting categories. Their status remains “Refuted” as reported
by the earlier return, and the actual assertions being refuted are preserved.

INTAKE20261114 §13.2 is present at RESULTS_STATE:491. It locates uncertain
“tubulure/ombre” at149/133 and distinguishes the surrounding conical notions.
The passage ends in an ellipsis. It verifies a terminology lead and does not
identify those notions with SA21 regular immersions. The separate §13.6
reconstruction discussion is absent.

EXIT_ONE_TYPE P1a1 is only mentioned inside an intake and an expert excerpt
(RESULTS_STATE:490,494). These point to normal-link homotopy fibres and stress
the distinction between individual fibrancy and local triviality of the whole
stratified family. They do not supply P1a1’s complete statement. P4e is absent.
The run-level review summary at531 cannot supply the missing quantifiers or
serve as independent verification of either result. STRATA C.8 is likewise
only an ID in another package’s cross-reference at510; its actual gluing
theorem is not present. Among its secondary GALOIS comparisons, B.3 has a
selected excerpt at453, but it ends before the conclusion; A.8 and B.5 are
absent.

The second archive supplies the complete earlier note
MPL_147_138-190 §69, note151-33, lines286–288. It proposes organizing relative
construction pieces by exit data retaining directed specialization and
homotopies. It leaves the germ-flag comparison and conical/tame hypotheses to
be proved. This exactly supports the SA21 notes’ classification of that link
as an earlier run’s own idea, with medium confidence. It is not a manuscript
reconstruction theorem. The original147 scans are not supplied by this archive.

The bridge to *Les Dérivateurs* XVI ms87–96 can be read in the recovered
companion, DER_XVI.tex:2641–2653 and2706–2770. Its reproduced manuscript text
discusses a hypothetical finer fundamental localizer, simple homotopy,
maquettes/finite posets, elementary moves, and the difference between
categorical and topological homotopy under subdivision. Manuscript page94 is
explicitly missing. The adjacent commentary is separately the earlier run’s
analysis. This checks the thematic endpoint proposed in SA21 note#107;
neither the reproduced text nor that commentary identifies SA21’s own network
or cutting operations with those elementary moves. No fresh theorem about
minimal localizers or simple homotopy is claimed in this endpoint audit.

## Evidence and reproducibility

All exact destination passages were read, not inferred from their headings.
For a missing target the search boundary includes **106 textual files** in
the two recovered archives. The broad short-ID search catches collisions such
as R29 in unrelated La Longue Marche packages; those are rejected by manually
checking run/file identity. A bibliography row or source filename is not
counted as the mathematical target it happens to name.

`collect_candidates.py` records query expressions and every matching file/line
with a line digest, without turning a hit into a verdict.
`curate_links.py` serializes the manually authored comparisons.
`finalize_project_links.py` validates note ordinals and coverage only.
`check_events.tsv` records fifty-four textual endpoint checks, expressly
excluding an independent proof of the returns’ mathematics. Hashes of the
checked sources and the complete bounded search manifest are retained.
The recovered inputs and duplicate full reading outputs listed in
`DELIVERY_EXCLUSIONS.txt` are excluded from the final ZIP; the curated
citations, programs and metadata suffice to reproduce the checks with those
inputs.
