# Ranked next tasks arising from VI–VII

These are task definitions, not developments undertaken here. Parent may merge their identifiers into the full Stage6 index.

1. **Prove the final Mag1–4 reconstruction with one fixed vocabulary.** Sources: C-SA21-7-048–052, MP-SA21-7-15; internal VII111→VIII8 link. Establish the exact correspondence between primitive interior-disjointness data, compatible lower figures and the two orders, including the finite-principal-ideal criterion. Standard downset representation is known; the complete two-order equivalence was not found published in the bounded search. First step: write a dependency graph for least receivers, compatibility heredity and common-refiner lifting, then prove each lemma. High interest; one focused run could verify a substantial finite-figure core.

2. **Identify the missing axiom behind local Boolean support complements.** Sources: C-SA21-7-041–046, MP-SA21-7-13–14. The P4 clique atelier refutes the L1 rescue under all enumerated hypotheses; optional AtSupp1 was not included and fails in that model. Orthomodularity repairs the isolated relative-complement identity, but whether a natural geometric axiom enforces the required support lattice behavior is separate. First step: state the exact local formula and compare each proposed absorption condition with the orthomodular identity, without claiming equivalence in advance. High interest; one run can settle implications/countermodels for a finite list.

3. **Separate local lattice maps from faithful global atelier morphisms.** Sources: C-SA21-6-013–018,043; C-SA21-7-002,018–019; MP-SA21-6-03 and MP-SA21-7-07. The complete-downset-homomorphism theorem is proved here, while the global converses fail in the manuscript examples. First step: fix the strict refinement convention and define each morphism direction, then repair the naive inverse-image construction by an explicit admissibility criterion. High interest; feasible as a rigorous definition-and-examples task.

4. **Audit finite type, finite deployment and admitted infinite families.** Sources: C-SA21-6-003,040; C-SA21-7-012,051; MP-SA21-6-10 and MP-SA21-7-05. The stationary-family assertion is valid under complete join-primality and the local supremum hypothesis. What remains is the systematic separation of hereditary finite generation from finite principal ideals and arbitrary admitted unions. First step: formulate the minimal family-closure theorem and generate counterexamples to each omitted condition. Medium-high interest; likely substantial progress in one run.

5. **Specify a geometric category for relative subdivision extension.** Sources: C-SA21-6-036–039, MP-SA21-6-09. Ordinary simultaneous/normal triangulation is established, while strict simplicial elementary fillers fail and canonical uniqueness is not supplied by those theorems. First step: choose PL or semianalytic objects and state exactly which exterior incidence is to remain fixed; compare that formulation with the opened theorem hypotheses. Medium interest; a few hours can establish a precise existence comparison, not a universal canonical extension theory.

6. **Complete the interval-poset example only after fixing its domain.** Sources: C-SA21-7-024–025, MP-SA21-7-09; III9→VII71 link. The cited ordered-tronçon definition has directedness/interpolation hypotheses absent from the broad arbitrary-poset slogan. First step: test the exact face and refinement axioms on small non-total posets, then isolate the valid total-order construction. Medium interest; bounded and suitable for finite certificates.

The pseudodisk/linking-data program (MP-SA21-6-04) ranks below these because no precise invariant or equivalence is specified on the pages. Its first task is definition work, not a theorem claim.
