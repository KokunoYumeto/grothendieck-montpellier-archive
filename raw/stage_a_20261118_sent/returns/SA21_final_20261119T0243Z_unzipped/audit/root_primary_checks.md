## Exact chronology that changes the assessment

The1984 Esquisse predates these June–July1986 notes even though its publication came in1997. It is therefore not counted as a classA later resolution of a1986 claim. The2026 exact matches are separated from classical polarity and sheaf ingredients known before1986. Hua's version is a recent preprint with public formalization claims, not a journal article or an independently checked archival transcription. The later orthoset papers are exact for specified polarity/adjointability statements but do not automatically settle every atelier axiom.

## Search documentation

Root queries and opens are in sources_web/root_*.json. The five root search families cover network/branch/order, ficelle/cutting, regular neighborhoods, rives/relative spectrum and realization. They include indexed zbMATH Open, arXiv, MathOverflow and Numdam searches, the author's published/unpublished inventories, EGA/SGA inspection, and backward reference following. A failed direct database endpoint is not reported as a successful full-database search. Each root claim points to its actual family and files; the master search map combines the other readers' explicit mappings.

The packaged retrieval JSON preserves requests, source URLs, tool reference IDs, hashes and access outcomes rather than redistributing entire publisher texts. Curated statements and locators remain in this ledger, the bibliography and the per-claim records. All mathematical proof assertions supplied by this run are separately labelled in audit/root_proofs.md and the readers' proof files.
