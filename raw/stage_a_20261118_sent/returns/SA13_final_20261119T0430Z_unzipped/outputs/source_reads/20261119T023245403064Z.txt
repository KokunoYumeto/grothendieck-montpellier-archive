36: ## Editorial conventions for the doubts register
37: 
38: The register below includes every `\unsure` and `\illegible` occurrence in the final individual page files. “Line” means the approximate line in that page's UTF-8 LaTeX source, counted from its first line, not a reconstructed physical manuscript line; several short lines of handwriting can occur within one source paragraph. Alternatives are reported without replacing the first reading in the transcription. Confidence measures the first reading, not the truth of its mathematics: **high** means a residual small uncertainty, **medium** a plausible but genuinely uncertain reading, and **low** damaged, crossed, clipped or severely compressed strokes.
