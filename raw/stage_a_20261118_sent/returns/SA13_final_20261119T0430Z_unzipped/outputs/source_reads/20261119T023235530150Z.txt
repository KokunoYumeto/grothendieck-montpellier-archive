### N40. A controlled finite-level compatibility test

**File:** cote 33. **Scan(s):** 128, 132, 146. **Approx. source line(s):** 128:2–4; 132:5–10; 146:13–18. **Kind:** own idea.

A useful intermediate test for the proposed “passage à la limite” is a compatible system of bounded finite-projective complexes over $A_\nu=\mathbb Z/\ell^\nu\mathbb Z$, with endomorphisms and compatible derived coefficient reduction. Degreewise matrix trace commutes with reduction, so their supertraces form an element of $\varprojlim A_\nu=\mathbb Z_\ell$; the additional task is to prove that the geometric cohomology complexes admit the required compatible perfect models. This gives a concrete sufficient mechanism for the manuscript's expectation while leaving that geometric inverse-limit problem open in these pages.

**Confidence:** medium — compatibility of matrix traces is immediate; compatible geometric perfect models have not been proved here.
