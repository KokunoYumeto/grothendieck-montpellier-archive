ENTRY MPL_33_098-146#12 (notes source line 115)
### N10. A point already exhibits infinite Tor

**File:** cote 33. **Scan(s):** 108, 112. **Approx. source line(s):** 108:10; 112:15–17. **Kind:** own idea.

A concrete check of “même si $X$ … un pt géométrique” is $A=\mathbb Z/\ell^2\mathbb Z$ and $M=A/\ell A$: the resolution with every differential multiplication by $\ell$ is exact, while tensoring it with $M$ makes every differential zero. Hence $\operatorname{Tor}_i^A(M,M)\cong M$ for every $i\geq0$, so locally constant finite modules do not automatically give bounded derived tensor products. This is our elementary example, adding an explicit witness to the manuscript's warning, not a claim that the example is written there.

**Confidence:** high — exactness and the tensor calculation are checked directly.

ENTRY MPL_33_098-146#18 (notes source line 167)
### N16. An explicit characteristic-p failure in degree one

**File:** cote 33. **Scan(s):** 118. **Approx. source line(s):** 6–9. **Kind:** own idea.

For algebraically closed $k$ of characteristic $p$, Artin–Schreier gives $H^1_{\mathrm{ét}}(\mathbb A_k^2,\mathbb F_p)=k[x,y]/\{h^p-h\}$ (The Stacks Project Authors, n.d., [Tag 0A3J](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0A3J)). The class of $xy$ does not come from the sum of the two one-variable cohomology groups: an equality $xy=a(x)+b(y)+h^p-h$ would leave, after discarding pure-variable monomials, a mixed polynomial $q$ with $q^p-q=xy$, which is impossible by its highest total degree. This checks the manuscript's failure claim and is consistent with the doubtful addition “même pour $H^1$,” but it gives no permission to remove that paleographic doubt from the frozen text.

**Confidence:** high — the Artin–Schreier quotient and the mixed-monomial obstruction are explicit.
