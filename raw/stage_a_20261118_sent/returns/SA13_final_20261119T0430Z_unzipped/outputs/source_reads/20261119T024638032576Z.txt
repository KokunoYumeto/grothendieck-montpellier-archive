675: Lemma 2.1: Let $S$ be a local $R$-algebra and $T$ a local $S$-algebra.
676: If $S$ is an unramified $R$-algebra, then the homomorphism $T_R^f\longrightarrow T_S^f$ induced
677: from the natural map $T\otimes_R T\longrightarrow T\otimes_S T$ is an isomorphism. In particular
678: if $T$ is unramified over $R$, then $T_R^f\approx T_T^f=T$.
679: 
680: \uline{Proof}: Since $S$ is an unramified $R$-algebra, the exact sequence
681: $0\longrightarrow\unsure{\mathfrak g|\mathfrak J}\longrightarrow S\otimes_R S\longrightarrow S\longrightarrow0$ splits as $S^e$-modules ([5]). Therefore
682: $0\longrightarrow T_R^e\otimes_{S^e}\unsure{\mathfrak g|\mathfrak J}\longrightarrow T_R^e\longrightarrow T_R^e\otimes_{S^e}S\longrightarrow0$ is exact and splits as $T_R^e$-
683: modules. By one of the standard associativity laws we have that $T_R^e\otimes_{S^e}S=
684: (T\otimes_R T)\otimes_{S\otimes S}S=T\otimes_S T=T_S^e$. Thus we find that the natural epimorphism
685: $T_R^e\longrightarrow T_S^e$ splits as $T_R^e$-modules. Let $\underline N$ be the maximal ideal of $T$ and $\unsure{\eta|\mathfrak N}$ the
686: preimage of $N$ under the map $T\otimes_R T\longrightarrow T$. Localizing by $\unsure{\eta|\mathfrak N}$, we see that
687: $T_R^f\longrightarrow T_S^f$ splits as $T_R^f$-module since $(T_R^e)_{\unsure{\eta|\mathfrak N}}=T_R^f$ and $(T_S^e)_{\unsure{\eta|\mathfrak N}}=T_S^f$ also.
688: Consequently $T_R^f=T_S^f$ since $T_R^f$ is a local ring.
