The hypotheses also give a short independent proof: regular associated graded rings and common minimal generators imply (R/m^q\simeq\bar R/\bar m^q\) for every q; hence completions agree. Since the completion of \(\bar R\) is faithfully flat over \(\bar R\) and the common completion over R is faithfully flat, the contraction identities follow. Minimal-generator counts, or the source's order argument, give principality descent. If (z/t\in\operatorname{Frac}(R)\cap\bar R\), then (z\in t\bar R\cap R=tR\), so (z/t\in R\). This proves the actual general lemma under the stated hypotheses.
