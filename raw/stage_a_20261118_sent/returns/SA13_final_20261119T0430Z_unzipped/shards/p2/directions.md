These are follow-on tasks. This consolidation does not develop them. Interest and achievable progress in one research run are ranked separately; scan-dependent alternatives must first be checked against the ink.

## 3. Constructible approximation preserving injective stalks

**Interest high; one-run progress medium.** Sources: SA13-131–132; C-SA13-113–114; MP-SA13-38. First step: fix a noetherian scheme and a finite self-injective coefficient ring, then test finite diagram/stratification categories and transition maps. No exact theorem in the searched sources matched the full question; that does not establish modern openness.
