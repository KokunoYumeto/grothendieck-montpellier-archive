# P3 primary-source research evidence

Date of review: 2026-11-19 UTC. This file records the evidence actually inspected,
including unsuccessful access and distinctions between bibliography and theorem
verification. It is the completed source-evidence record accompanying the claims map.

## Opened and inspected

* Deligne, *Théorie de Hodge III*, Publ. Math. IHÉS **44** (1974), 5–77,
  DOI 10.1007/BF02685881, <https://www.numdam.org/item/PMIHES_1974__44__5_0.pdf>.
  The relevant exact locations are 10.1.3–10.1.10 and 10.2.1–10.2.6. Section
  10.2.1 states the derived tensor interpretation for complexes in degrees
  -1 and 0 on a topos. The torsion formula of 10.2.5 is explicitly over an
  algebraically closed field of characteristic zero. It must not be cited as an
  unqualified all-characteristic theorem. Section 10.2.4 controls the sign on
  interchanging the factors.
* The IHÉS prepublication M/72/04 cover is dated January 1972:
  <https://repo-archives.ihes.fr/FONDS_IHES/I_Prepublications/DELIGNE/1972-1983/M_72_04/M_72_04_web.pdf>.
  Its 10.2.1 occurs on typed page 98. This is documented circulation, distinct
  from the 1974 publication; no earlier first-circulation date is asserted.
* Raynaud, *Spécialisation du foncteur de Picard*, Publ. Math. IHÉS **38**
  (1970), 27–76, DOI 10.1007/BF02684651,
  <https://www.numdam.org/item/PMIHES_1970__38__27_0.pdf>.
  Proposition 6.2.1 on printed page 50 gives the finite locally free Cartier-dual
  comparison under proper flat finite presentation and universal cohomological
  flatness in degree zero (or the alternative multiplicative-type hypothesis).
  This is a relative sheaf statement, not an arbitrary equality of absolute H1.
* Bertolin, *Homological interpretation of extensions and biextensions of
  1-motives*, <https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.3267>, first submitted 24 August 2008,
  revised 29 May 2012; J. Number Theory **132** (2012), 2103–2131,
  DOI 10.1016/j.jnt.2012.04.005. The abstract identifies the extension of the
  formalism to three 1-motives over a separably closed field. Until the PDF is
  read, only that scope and the bibliographic identity are verified.
* Milne, *Abelian Varieties*, author-posted version dated 2 January 2022 of the
  1986 chapter in *Arithmetic Geometry*, pp. 103–150:
  <https://www.jmilne.org/math/xnotes/AVs.pdf>. Remark 15.3 and section 16 were
  read. They state the pointed finite étale cover theorem, the geometric Tate
  description of pi1, finite Weil pairings and their compatibility. Lemma 16.1
  explicitly includes the power in the transition identity.
* Oda, *The first de Rham cohomology group and Dieudonné modules*, Ann. Sci.
  ÉNS (4) **2** (1969), 63–135:
  <https://www.numdam.org/item/ASENS_1969_4_2_1_63_0.pdf>. Introduction read;
  thesis submitted June 1967. The paper distinguishes the Picard scheme from
  its reduced identity component and identifies a subspace, not automatically
  the whole de Rham H1, for a general proper smooth scheme.
* Stacks Project tags 03AV (21.10), 03PK (59.28), 0A3J (59.63), 0BQP (20.43),
  and 03R1 (59.61) were opened. Relevant restrictions: Kummer is étale for
  invertible n and fppf for arbitrary n; Artin–Schreier retains the H0 term;
  the displayed local-to-global Ext reference is for ringed spaces with the
  stated boundedness conditions. It is not itself a reference for every
  unbounded complex on every topos.
* Mehidi–Poiret, *Moduli of finite flat torsors over nodal curves*, JLMS
  **112** (2025), e70220, first published 25 June 2025:
  <https://londmathsoc.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1112/jlms.70220>.
  Its finite-flat theorem is a comparator for the singular-curve question;
  it does not settle the handwritten unrestricted abelian-torsor/Ext formula.
* Robert, *Theory of abelian varieties and their moduli spaces*, author draft
  March 2021, updated 7 March 2023:
  <https://www.normalesup.org/~robert/pro/publications/books/avtheory.pdf>.
  Proposition 3.1.5 states the Néron-model base-change theorem, including the
  completion case, with ramification index one and separable residue extension.
  It points to BLR for the theorem. The surrounding 3.1.10 and 3.1.17 distinguish
  good and semistable reduction and the relevant inertia criteria.
* BLR, *Néron Models* (1990), DOI 10.1007/978-3-642-51438-8:
  <https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-51438-8>.
  Publisher bibliography opened; full chapter proof not accessible there.
* SGA7 I publisher records for VII, VIII, IX were opened at DOI suffixes
  BFb0068692, BFb0068693, BFb0068694. These verify titles, authors, 1972 print
  publication and page ranges. They do not by themselves verify a numbered
  proposition in the chapters. The later eBook date is not the publication date.

## Newly read A36-01 and A36-02: direct published continuation

All 186 lines of A36-01 and all 123 lines of A36-02, plus both Codex note files,
were read on 2026-11-19. They are not repetitions of the Pro leaves. The first
contains the primitive Leray class, its functoriality under correspondences,
the Griffiths argument, and the strong Hasse–Witt trace claim. The second
continues the trace argument, weakens its conclusion through stable subspaces,
and gives the finite-field interpolation and specialization lemmas.

* Katz, SGA7 II, Exposé XX, *Le théorème de Griffiths*, pp. 341–362:
  <https://web.math.princeton.edu/~nmk/old/thmgrif.pdf>. Original printed chapter
  opened in the author's archive. Its introduction explicitly attributes the
  algebraic proof to Grothendieck. Section 1 (especially 1.1, 1.3, 1.4, 1.5.3)
  is a precise continuation of scans 010–012. The individual matches include §§3.3–3.4,4.1–4.4 and5.1–5.2; the title match alone is not used as evidence.
* Katz, SGA7 II, Exposé XVIII, *Étude cohomologique des pinceaux de Lefschetz*,
  pp. 254–327, <https://web.math.princeton.edu/~nmk/old/etudecoho.pdf>.
* Katz, SGA7 II, Exposé XXI, *Le niveau de la cohomologie des intersections
  complètes*, pp. 363–400,
  <https://web.math.princeton.edu/~nmk/old/niveaucoho.pdf>.
* Katz, SGA7 II, Exposé XXII, *Une formule de congruence pour la fonction zêta*,
  pp. 401–438, <https://web.math.princeton.edu/~nmk/old/congzeta.pdf>.
  These three complete original chapters have been opened; individual
  proposition inspection is tracked in the claim map and search log.
* The SGA7 II publisher table of contents was opened at
  <https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/BFb0060505>; it confirms 1973 print
  publication, LNM 340, and the above pagination. A modern independently
  transcribed/translated SGA website was used only as a discovery lead, not as
  authority for correcting these transcriptions or for theorems.
* Milne, *Grothendieck's standard conjecture of Lefschetz type over finite
  fields*, version 1.1 dated 14 July 2022,
  <https://jmilne.org/math/articles/LFF.pdf>, opened. It carefully distinguishes
  the strong algebraicity assertion from hard Lefschetz and explains remaining
  conditional consequences. The A36-01 claim that a lift to characteristic zero
  suffices for algebraicity of Lambda cannot be accepted as a known theorem
  merely because hard Lefschetz is known there.

## Search and access limits actually encountered

* SGA7 I original at the SLMath repository exceeded the web fetch size limit
  (37,362,787 bytes). The iut-theory mirrors timed out. The geotoppos SGA7I PDF
  contains only the twelve-page introduction; it was not mistaken for the book.
* Original SGA4 XVIII at Orgogozo's CNRS site repeatedly returned HTTP 502.
  Deligne's IAS collected-works record was opened, but Number4.pdf exceeded the
  fetch size limit (10,920,779 bytes). This is an access limitation, not absence
  of the result. The Raynaud torsor result referred to in XVIII 1.2.9 still needs
  a primary full-text match before its exact hypotheses are used.
* A wstein page image could not be visually inspected: the tool returned only
  an image placeholder, and the local urllib attempt returned HTTP 403.
* zbMATH-targeted searches for biextensions and monodromy produced no uniquely
  matched direct entry. This is recorded as a search failure, never evidence
  that a mathematical claim was undeveloped.
* MathOverflow's abelian-torsor torsion discussion was opened as a reference
  trail to Raynaud, not used as primary theorem authority. It warns that
  normality in dimension two is insufficient. The shard therefore does not
  extrapolate a regular/smooth-base theorem to arbitrary normal bases.


## Completed original-print and later-literature checks

The SGA7 I size limit was subsequently overcome by p2's successful transfer of the original volume. This reviewer inspected the original VIII1.1.3–1.1.4 and1.3–1.4 at printed220–225, IX3.5 at350, IX5.12.1 at387 and IX5.13 at388. The last concerns a Barsotti–Tate good-reduction implication; it is not an unrestricted equivalence between p-adic inertia semisimplicity and potential good reduction. The complete categorical gluing proposition of36:048–050 remains unverified at its full stated generality, despite the exact Tor and finite-pairing comparators. Other agents independently own the wider VII/VIII/IX original-print audit.

Koblitz, *p-adic variation of the zeta-function over families of varieties defined over finite fields*, Compositio Mathematica31(2)(1975),119–218, original Numdam PDF, I§5 Theorem2 printed141, bounds the nilpotent rank by O(d^(n−1)). The total-rank leading term yields growing stable rank. This is a later strengthening of Katz's nonnilpotence theorem, not the whole-space every-trace assertion. The exact original page is retained as work/p3_primary/koblitz141.png. The finite-field nonconiveau deduction in review.md is explicitly this reviewer's inference, with fixed ambient zeta factors included.

Benoist–Ottem2021, Duke Mathematical Journal170(12),2719–2753, original introduction and Theorem1.1, distinguishes rational proper smooth equality from integral and open failures. Ancona and collaborators2025, JLMS111(4),e70133, gives special settings for the Lefschetz standard conjecture; it does not prove that an arbitrary characteristic-zero lift makes Lambda algebraic. The exact bibliographic records and primary scopes are in references.bib. Griffiths's IAS bibliography was opened to disambiguate the similar period-paper titles; an unseen original proof is not claimed.

The root Tate audit follows original Zarhin2011 introductory references to Moret-Bailly1985, XII0.1 and2.4–2.5, printed239 and244–245. The last gives the integral Hom isomorphism and rational semisimplicity over finitely generated positive-characteristic fields with ell different from p, and calls Mori's characteristic2 contribution unpublished there. Gajda–Petersen Theorem2.3 and Lemma3.4, and Achter–Casalaina-Martin–Vial2023 Proposition4.5(ii), were opened for the general finitely-generated-field theorem and normal-base extension of homomorphisms. See root_lie_audit.md for the exact arithmetic-model rather than ordinary-geometric-pi1 conclusion. MathNet's original records for Zarhin1975 and1976 were opened, but their full proofs were not used independently.

For A36 Lemma7.8, the exact source text permits an arbitrary t in normal S, while its final proof reduces to t generic. The explicit nonsplit nodal counterexample and every hypothesis are in review.md, C-SA13-355, and executable specialization_counterexample.py. The repaired generic-point theorem C-SA13-363 uses opened Stacks tags0BTN,04GK,02LP. The counterexample and repaired proof are reviewer contributions and are not assigned a historical publication date.
