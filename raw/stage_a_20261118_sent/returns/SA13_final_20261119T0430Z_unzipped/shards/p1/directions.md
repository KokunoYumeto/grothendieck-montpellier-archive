# Directions from the derived-functor and trace leaves

These are ranked task specifications. The source checks and counterexamples in
the dossier establish their starting point; none of the proposed tasks is
developed here. Global assembly combines overlapping directions with p2.

## P1-D1. Type and compare the local correspondence pairing

**Origin:** SA13-32,SA13-33,SA13-34,SA13-35,SA13-37;
C-SA13-036,C-SA13-037,C-SA13-038; MP-SA13-09.
**Interest:** high. **One-run reach:** high, for one diagram.
**Would establish:** a completely typed local construction and its precise
relation to integration of the global trace, with both Hom factors and shifts
fixed. **Known:** perfect evaluation and published Lefschetz–Verdier formalism;
the finite-Tor condition alone does not supply all finiteness.
**First concrete step:** write the four objects and adjunction morphisms in the
144→146→148 sequence, then compare the exact SGA5 local-pairing diagram.

**Origin:** SA13-9,SA13-10,SA13-11; C-SA13-012,C-SA13-013; MP-SA13-03.

**Origin:** SA13-25,SA13-26,SA13-36; C-SA13-024,C-SA13-025,C-SA13-026,C-SA13-039;
MP-SA13-07.

## P1-D4. Make the noetherian-base hope precise

**Origin:** SA13-8; C-SA13-009; MP-SA13-02.

## P1-D5. Reconstruct the finite-level to adic transition

**Origin:** SA13-21,SA13-22,SA13-25,SA13-28,SA13-30;
C-SA13-024,C-SA13-030,C-SA13-034,C-SA13-035; MP-SA13-06,MP-SA13-08.
**Interest:** medium-high. **One-run reach:** medium.
**Would establish:** the exact uniform amplitude, finite generation and limit
hypotheses used by the manuscript's adic trace, rather than only its elementary
matrix congruence. **Known:** the finite-projective calculation and SGA5 V–VI;
the latter explicitly does not construct a full adic derived category.
**First concrete step:** state the compatible finite-level system used on128,
then trace its required properties through one proper-pushforward example.
