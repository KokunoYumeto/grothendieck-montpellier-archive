# Directions from the depth, model-group and weight appendices

These are task specifications, ranked for interest and achievable progress in a
few hours. They do not claim that bounded negative searches certify new open
problems. None of these directions is developed here.

## RC-D1. Identify d2 and its duality signs in one normal-crossing complex

**Origin:** SA13-68; C-SA13-659,C-SA13-660,C-SA13-661,C-SA13-662; MP-SA13-203.
**Interest:** high. **One-run reach:** high.
**Would establish:** a single coherent restriction/Gysin diagram matching the
manuscript's Leray indexing, including the E3 terminal page.
**Known:** weight separation, the Kummer rank distinction and a nonzero d2
example; the page's motivic compatibility remains a separate obligation.
**First concrete step:** choose a two-component proper normal-crossing example
and write the support and open-complement triangles with the same purity sign.

## RC-D2. Recover the precise geometric-depth lemma

**Origin:** SA13-62; C-SA13-603,C-SA13-604; MP-SA13-190,MP-SA13-191.
**Interest:** high. **One-run reach:** medium-high.
**Would establish:** the sharp localization and flat-local-map statement for
the minimum-generator version of geometric depth, with the residue-extension
assumption either proved necessary or removed under explicit hypotheses.
**Known:** SGA2 XIV5 and Illusie's finite-type relative version; the exact
all-noetherian wording was not matched in the searches.
**First concrete step:** express the invariant through the first two terms of
the cotangent complex for a regular presentation and identify every use of
separability in the flat-map comparison.

## RC-D3. Turn the four-space sketch into one typed cone diagram

**Origin:** SA13-63; C-SA13-609,C-SA13-610,C-SA13-611,C-SA13-612;
MP-SA13-194.
**Interest:** high. **One-run reach:** high.
**Would establish:** the delayed part(b) with fixed boundary and Tate-twist

## RC-D4. State the model-group Tate theorem as a complete proposition

**Origin:** SA13-66,SA13-67; C-SA13-650,C-SA13-652,C-SA13-653; MP-SA13-200,MP-SA13-201.
**Interest:** medium-high. **One-run reach:** high.
**Would establish:** a publication-ready exact-scope completion of this appendix,
including normal-base extension and the stage at which component groups vanish.
**Known:** the arithmetic Tate theorem and Gajda–Petersen's normal-base lemma;
the manuscript's question is mathematically settled in the stated model setup.
**First concrete step:** formulate the filtered Hom category for a normal qcqs
base and check the passage from an individual commuting matrix to a finite model.

**Origin:** SA13-65,SA13-66; C-SA13-631,C-SA13-633,C-SA13-635,C-SA13-637,
C-SA13-647,C-SA13-648; MP-SA13-198,MP-SA13-199.
**Interest:** medium-high. **One-run reach:** high.
**Would establish:** an exact comparison statement replacing the conflicting
recovery sentence and infinite-dimensional parenthesis.
**Known:** the fibre-functor reconstruction, the rational-component obstruction,
and the finite-field analytic-dimension counterexample are already audited.
**First concrete step:** transcribe the source's category and natural arrows on

from equality of all rational points.

## RC-D6. Test the fixed-square Bertini suggestion

**Origin:** SA13-64; C-SA13-622; MP-SA13-196.
**Interest:** medium. **One-run reach:** medium.
**Would establish:** an exact small-degree example or obstruction for the
prescribed-section hyperplane assertion, without confusing a fixed exponent
with sufficiently high powers. **Known:** published hyperplane reduction and
Orgogozo's prime-to-p Lefschetz theorem; neither proves this exponent-two claim.
**First concrete step:** recover the exact line bundle and section constraints
on177, then test the lowest-dimensional embedded examples.

## RC-D7. Map the nonabelian depth proposal to established local Lefschetz results

**Origin:** SA13-62; C-SA13-607,C-SA13-608; MP-SA13-192,MP-SA13-193.
**Interest:** medium-high. **One-run reach:** medium.
**Would establish:** a theorem-by-theorem boundary between the trait result and
the arbitrary excellent local-base proposal.
**Known:** Raynaud1974 and Orgogozo2003 with their precise restrictions; the
broader wording cannot be declared solved merely by a subject-level reference.
**First concrete step:** make one table of base, codimension, coefficient group,
and degree for the manuscript and each exact comparison theorem.

## RC-D8. Reduce one weight-piece independence question to algebraic ranks

**Origin:** SA13-68; C-SA13-655,C-SA13-662; MP-SA13-202.
**Interest:** very high. **One-run reach:** low for the general conjecture,
medium for a finite known-case reduction.
**Would establish:** the exact conditional implication for a chosen
normal-crossing complex, with its differential represented algebraically.
**Known:** van Dobben de Bruyn's equivalence theorem; general independence
remains conjectural. **First concrete step:** identify the correspondence
controlling one d1 or d2 and list what its rank independence actually requires.
