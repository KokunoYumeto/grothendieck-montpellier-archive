## Depth and the shifted vanishing-cycle convention

The convention is a mathematical prerequisite: the manuscript's Phi equals
usual RPhi[-1], as explicitly witnessed on K34-05scan94 andK34-08scan145.
Consequently isolated lci concentration at manuscript degree n+1 corresponds
to usual degree n. C-SA13-600–608 keep this convention, the all-generizations
vanishing hypotheses, and the difference between geometric and ordinary depth.
The cancelled dimension term at151 conflicts with the surviving proof at152;
no scan was supplied to decide the intended reading.

SGA2 XIV5.2–5.6 defines geometric depth using the minimum number of generators
in a regular presentation. Raynaud1974 III1.2–1.3 has precise nonabelian and
base restrictions. Orgogozo2003 removes old resolution-dependent inputs in
specified prime-to-p results. Neither is used as a blanket proof of the
arbitrary excellent-base proposal on156. Illusie2003's original introduction
states its exact depth/perversity and isolated-lci theorems; the full later
proof was not accessible through that reproduction, and the audit says so.

The global amplitude on154 needs a pointwise support bound, not only a coarse
cohomological dimension estimate. The independent proof note explains this:
combining sheaf amplitude with2d alone gives too weak an upper bound. Perversity
supplies the stronger support control in the modern stated lci setting.

## Analytic and algebraic envelopes; arithmetic model systems

C-SA13-627–653 distinguish a profinite group, its representation envelopes,
the corresponding Lie pro-objects, and the inverse limit of a system of
arithmetic models. The independent second review is
`shards/p3/root_lie_audit.md`. Tannakian reconstruction requires the fibre
functor for its canonical form; with it, the component quotient recovers pi.
The warning on185 therefore cannot simply be repeated beside the contrary
formula on186 without qualification.

Two literal assertions fail for explicit reasons. The normalizer of the
norm-one torus for Q3(sqrt3)/Q3 has constant component quotient C2 but no rational
points in its other component: norm−1 is excluded by reduction modulo3.
For X=Spec(Fbar_p), finite-field models give a one-dimensional analytic
pro-Lie algebra, whereas independent exponential characters give algebraic
quotients of arbitrarily large dimension. Both counterexamples check all
ambient hypotheses; neither decides what the ink may have intended.

The arithmetic-model Tate endomorphism question is settled after the specified
normal-base and finite-presentation descent. Gajda–Petersen Theorem2.3 and
Lemma3.4 provide exact field/base statements; Moret-Bailly XII2.5 includes
characteristic2 and records Mori's earlier contribution as unpublished.
Replacing the model pro-group with ordinary geometric pi1 would destroy the
formulation. Lie centralizers require passing to a model where the finite
component obstruction of the particular representation has disappeared.

## Weights and interleaved printed GIT

C-SA13-654–663 distinguish proper Cech E2 degeneration from Leray E3
degeneration. The independent audit in `shards/p2/weights_audit.md` checks both
the interior and q=0 boundary rows. The divisor complement has r local Kummer
generators, while geometric nearby cycles impose the diagonal relation and
have r−1. In the trait calculation geometric special-fibre cohomology abuts
to cohomology over Knr. The inertia exact sequence on209 needs the coinvariant
Tate twist. The duality exponent on205 is consistent with dimX'=n−1 and is
not emended to a different dimension convention.

A split Tate elliptic curve refutes the literal claim that invariants and
coinvariants both have weight i: for H¹ their weights are0 and2. This is fully
compatible with monodromy-weight. Van Dobben de Bruyn2020 gives the exact
equivalence of the rank and Betti-independence conjectures behind the draft's
conditional program. Its first arXiv version is2018-08-01; publication is
2020-11-30. The broad independence question remains open in the current
Wan–Zhang primary text. Ito–Zou's October2026 hyper-Kähler preprint is kept
within that geometric scope and marked as a preprint.
