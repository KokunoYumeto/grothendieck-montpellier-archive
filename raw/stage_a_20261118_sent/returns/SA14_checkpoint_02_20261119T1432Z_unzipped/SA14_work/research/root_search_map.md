## Known access limitations retained

The publisher metadata for Boutot was available, while the whole monograph was not reread; its geometric theorem is checked through Kollár's explicit statement. One AMS book-metadata route and the direct Fringuelli–Pirisi DOI route failed, so the actual preprint/author PDF and author-institution records are the witnesses. The1952 Chow–Kodaira source is marked unverified as a direct theorem read; the1960 primary paper explicitly supplies the historical comparison. The RaynaudIII4.3 chapter remains an exact-source retrieval task. None of these failures has been concealed by changing `note` to mean a theorem was proved.
