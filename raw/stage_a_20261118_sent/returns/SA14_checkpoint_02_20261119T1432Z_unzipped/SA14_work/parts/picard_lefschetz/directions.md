# Research directions from the late Picard and Lefschetz packets

These are separate future tasks. They are ranked by mathematical or archival interest; the final column ranks what one focused run of a few hours could actually accomplish. A manuscript question being unanswered here does not establish that it is open in current mathematics. Each task begins with the exact checked result and the remaining difference in hypotheses. No direction is developed beyond the consolidation checks in this package.
