### F03 — Finiteness in projective descent is expressly questioned

**File / scan / locus:** P57, 57:117, Theorem 3 and first remark.  
**Kind:** self-flagged.  
**Bears on:** no direct RS entry; WG2 §1.1 #3 is a descent-method link.  
**Search:** J'ignore | fini | projectif | descente | Weil

After the finite-cover theorem for quasi-projective/projective descent, the author says “J'ignore” whether finiteness is necessary. The same paragraph claims a formally weaker sufficient condition through local finite faithfully flat covers mapping to \(S'\), which needs an explicit descent-transitivity argument. H's scope note omits this reused typescript, while the current relative-Picard construction at 126 expressly assumes effective descent rather than proving this weakening. CT 54:4, 6's flattening material and SC P09 at 57:283 are related uses of flatness, not an answer to this projectivity question.

### F04 — Completion and quasi-finite covers are singled out as test cases

**File / scan / locus:** P57, 57:117, second half of the remark.  
**Kind:** conjecture.  
**Bears on:** no directly matching RS item; completion comparisons are methodological only.  
**Search:** complété | quasi-fini | projectif | question | Spec

For \(S'=\operatorname{Spec}\bar A\) over a noetherian local ring and for a quasi-finite nonfinite cover, the typescript asks whether projectivity of \(X\times_SS'\) implies projectivity of \(X\). This is separate from existence of an already given descended scheme and from descent of affine morphisms. The two cases should be retained as the author's explicit tests, with no later resolution inferred from the neighboring Picard proof.

### F06 — Left exactness of inverse limits is not a Picard-completion theorem

**File / scan / locus:** P57, 57:130, internal p.0-ii, inverse-limit diagrams.  
**Kind:** unpursued implication.  
**Bears on:** no direct settled entry; the Hua analytic-completion lead cited by SC C09 only as a methodological boundary, not an item verified in this RS.  
**Search:** varprojlim | exact à gauche | Ext | complétion | filtrant

The reused page proves that the inverse-limit functor is “exact à gauche” and commutes with ordinary direct image of sheaves. Neither assertion gives exactness on the right, commutation with higher cohomology, or effectiveness of formal line bundles without further hypotheses. SC C09 at 57:296 discusses a different derived-limit typescript with filteredness and finiteness cautions, while CT 44:3/CN D07 supplies another completion-related warning. The current leaf adds elementary sheaf-level identities, not the analytic/formal Picard criterion mentioned in those earlier notes.
