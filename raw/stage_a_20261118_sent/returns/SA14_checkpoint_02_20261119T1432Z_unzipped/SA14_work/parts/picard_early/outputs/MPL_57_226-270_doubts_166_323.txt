0317 # Legibility and limits
0318 
0319 The typescripts are generally legible, including the reused sheets; their handwritten corrections, colored type, unusual spellings and indices still require caution. The fast handwritten mathematical drafts are substantially less secure: the most doubtful areas are the upper scratch and canceled N.B. on 239, the cramped annotations on 241 and 243, both oblique marginal proofs and the canceled third diagram row on 256, the bracket and heavily canceled band on 264, and the diagonal request on 268. The full images and their supplied halves were personally inspected; enlarged and rotated crops were used for small or inverted writing, without OCR.

0321 Four regions retain `\illegible{n}`: two canceled passages on 237 (estimated three and two words), three canceled words on 239, and two words beneath opaque hatching on 258. These are estimates of physically obscured words, not missing mathematical symbols; doubtful formulas and diagrams instead retain explicit tentative readings. Initial readings revised during audit remain traceable in the correction record, while this table and the page files give the final pre-context readings.
