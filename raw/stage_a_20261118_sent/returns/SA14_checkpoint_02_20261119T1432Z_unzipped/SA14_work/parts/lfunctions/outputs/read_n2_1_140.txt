0112 ### LF11 — The modified Euler characteristic has a precise conductor formula
0113 
0114 **Witness:** 60/047:L14;055:L26;059:L2. **Kind:** own idea. **Quote:** “Donner une formule genre … Ogg”.
0115 **Context search:** `Artin|Ogg|conducteur|Swan` in CT, CN and RESULTS_STATE; CT 16/028 lists Ogg's 1962 paper and CT 44/009 mentions a letter to Ogg; neither passage states this conductor formula, while CT 16/037,039 uses Artin modules in reused algebra text.
0116 
0117 **Confidence: high under the stated curve hypotheses**, because for a smooth proper geometrically connected curve \(X/\mathbf F_p\) of genus \(g\), a lisse sheaf \(E\) of rank \(r\) on an open \(U\subset X\), and \(\ell\ne p\), applying the Grothendieck–Ogg–Shafarevich formula to \(i_!E\) and adding 047's point terms gives
0118 \[
0119 \chi(\overline X,E^\natural)=r(2-2g)-
0120 \sum_{x\in X\setminus U}\deg(x)\bigl[\operatorname{Sw}_x(E)+r-\dim E_x^\natural\bigr],
0121 \]
0122 where the sum is over closed points and \(\dim E_x^\natural\) means the rank of the virtual averaged fibre.
0123 Consequently the modified local conductor is the usual Artin conductor minus \(\operatorname{rank}\alpha_x\), and purely unipotent tame monodromy contributes zero to it.
0124 This **adds** a precise interpretation of 055(b)/059 to Hua **60-inertia-average-duality**; CT 16/028's Ogg bibliography and CT 44/009's reference to an Ogg letter are leads, while Raynaud (1966, Theorem 1, p. 133) and Laumon (1987, §§3.1.5.1–3, p. 185) give the relevant published conductor frameworks.
