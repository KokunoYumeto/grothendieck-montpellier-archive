## 1. Determinants, weights and functional equations

The first handwritten sequence starts with elementary determinant identities, then uses duality, rationality, weights and coefficient selfduality to obtain functional equations. These are several different inputs. Deligne1973Constants §§10.3–10.5 is the exact determinant and duality comparison for C-SA14-401–405,409–410,417–426. The paper's first seminar version is recorded as 1969–1970, while the expanded LNM publication is 1973; neither date is assigned to the manuscript. The reciprocal identities are independently derived in `proof_notes.md` §§1–2.

Grothendieck1964Lefschetz, Bourbaki exposé279, Theorem5.1 and Corollary5.2, supplies the trace/rationality mechanism. The talk was in December1964 and the volume appeared in1966. Its internal statement about an April1963 result is reported as an assertion in that published source, not a date established for cote60. Illusie2026SGA5 gives the separate chronology of the1965–1966 lectures and1977 book; the author-hosted paper was opened, while the journal landing page returned403. This source is used for chronology, not as a substitute for the original functional-equation proof.

Deligne1974WeilI Theorem1.6, §2.6 and Theorems2.8/2.12, together with Deligne1980WeilII §§1.8.4,1.8.8,3.3–3.4, give precise geometric weights, pairings, local monodromy and middle-extension results. C-SA14-406–416 therefore distinguish four propositions: purity controls absolute values; rationality constrains the determinant; a perfect coefficient pairing supplies selfduality; an alternating polarization fixes the normalized sign. The rank-one examples in CALC-03 only test the absence of the extra assumptions. They do not refute the intended rational polarized setting.

Jannsen1992Numerical Theorem1 proves numerical semisimplicity. Corollary1 has a separate statement about the homological-to-numerical passage under its Künneth hypothesis. Neither theorem identifies numerical and homological equivalence in full generality. The note's absent cote16 comparison is quarantined even though Jannsen's published endpoint was checked. The rational-Witt comparison is anchored by BGT2013Endomorphisms, equation5.8 and §6: arXiv2013, electronic publication2015, issue2016. It is a later conceptual identification, with no claim of transmission to these undated pages.

## 5. Exact Samuel matches recovered by backward tracing

The first searches found Samuel's1961 factoriality papers and ZinnJustin1967Derivations, which established the broad derivation method but did not yet verify the exact N(i,j) family. Following their primary references led to Samuel1962Derivations and the official Samuel1964TIFR volume. The earlier matching limits were then replaced, with the search history retained.

Thus C-SA14-496 is established; for infinite k and i=j=2 the Dedekind localization has infinite class group.

These are explicitly external antecedents. C-SA14-492 remains classF as an incomplete archival claim, even though the likely published example and its computation are now identified.

Samuel1964TIFR chapterI Theorem8.1 exactly contains the two weighted-cyclic factoriality alternatives. The printed field specialization with pairwise coprime exponents follows under its field hypothesis. Theorem8.2 exactly matches the nondegenerate quadric threshold n≥5, characteristic not2. Samuel1964Mechants §III Proposition2 gives another exact statement of the cyclic alternatives; Proposition1 explicitly requires positive i,j,k for the formal-series construction. This corroborates the independently proved correction to the archival nonnegative range without deciding whether the discrepancy is in the ink or the transcription. The separate within-block x^(-1) hint is also refuted under positive coprime exponents; the displayed coefficient normalization requires division by y (C-SA14-567, CALC-25).

Chronology needs care. The Dubreil exposé carries the talk date19November1962 and a1962–1963 volume designation, but its surviving bibliography cites1965 publications. ## 6. Meaning of the classes and the remaining limits

The class codes are subject-specific concordance labels. A and B identify exact established frameworks in the author's or others' later publications. E identifies classical algebra or analysis, especially the separately attributed printed exercise corpus. For an undated reused draft, E is not a proof that a theorem chronologically predates the handwriting. No classD claim is inferred from a failed search.

The three classC records are the admitted universal coefficient selfduality, the insufficiently specified motivic decomposition, and the rejected global recipe whose proposed replacement has a more precise local form. The eleven classF records preserve genuinely missing predicates or antecedents. Their queries, negative results and partial exact matches are all recorded. External project/cote comparisons stay in `project_leads.tsv`: checking an Oda or Jannsen publication does not check an absent archival endpoint.
