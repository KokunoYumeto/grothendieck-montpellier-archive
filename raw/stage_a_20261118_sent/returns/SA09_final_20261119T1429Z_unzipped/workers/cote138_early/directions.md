# Ranked follow-on directions for early cote 138

These are task boundaries, not further developments carried out here. Each direction separates settled input from the new editorial or mathematical work it would establish. Interest and feasibility refer to one research run of a few hours.

## 1. Recover and reconstruct the eighteen-transport diagram

**Interest: high. Feasibility: medium, dependent on scan review.** Sources: SA09-136, SA09-157–161 and SA09-194; claims CL-SA09-138E-039–045; problems MP-SA09-44 and MP-SA09-48.

The task would specify the finite action groupoid of branch, centre and midpoint fibres, identify the two proposed representative transports, and prove exactly the supported S3 and complex-conjugation compatibility. The deck groups, root torsors and finite symmetries are known; full G_Q-equivariance is not supported by the surviving text. First recover the η/ζ and θ/θ⁻¹ readings on scans 38–42, list the eighteen source-target arrows explicitly, and compute the stabilizers of two candidate representatives. Do not assume that equivariance alone proves all composition relations.

## 2. Write the exact radical-of-two companion

**Interest: high. Feasibility: high.** Sources: SA09-131–136 and SA09-138–139; claims CL-SA09-138E-018, 020–024, 036–037 and 042; problems MP-SA09-41–43 and MP-SA09-49.

The task would translate the manuscript's affine torsor notation into the exact image (-1)^b=(2/a), explaining the index-two obstruction, the nonsplit arithmetic extension and the distinction from an origin-split ambient affine group. The decisive theorem is already established by LMS2014 Theorem 2.9; the rank-r rational analogue is MoreeStevenhagen2014 Theorem 2.5. First make a coordinate dictionary with the manuscript's K(1/2), then show precisely which choices change b by sign or a coboundary. This is a companion exposition and historical source comparison, not a new open-image theorem.

## 3. Repair the descent dictionary with the fixed-section distinction

**Interest: high. Feasibility: high.** Sources: SA09-114–124 and SA09-172; claims CL-SA09-138E-001–013; problem MP-SA09-40.

## 4. Establish the precise cover-to-Jacobian comparison

**Interest: medium to high. Feasibility: medium.** Sources: SA09-140–144, SA09-154, SA09-156 and SA09-171; claims CL-SA09-138E-025–029 and 041; problem MP-SA09-46.

The task would distinguish the maximal abelian cover of the three-punctured line, the punctured Fermat cubic subgroup of rank ten, and the proper cubic's rank-two Tate module, with exact maps for killing peripheral inertia. Any link to de Rham or external CM-parity work requires an additional specified comparison map.

## 6. Separate rational, number-field and finite-field Kummer scopes

**Interest: medium. Feasibility: medium.** Sources: SA09-132, SA09-151–153; claims CL-SA09-138E-020, 024, 035–038; problems MP-SA09-41–43.

The general number-field finite-index assertion is settled by the opened statement of PeruccaSgobba2019 Theorem1.1 and the bounded-index argument; the rational saturated exact index is 2^r. First recover the unclear dimension glyph, define the torus represented by D, and choose an explicit independent coordinate lattice. A concrete exact-index calculation, rather than another general existence proof, would be the useful next result. In finite characteristic, impose p∤n, separate characteristics two and three, and use only the appropriate tame or prime-to-p quotient.
