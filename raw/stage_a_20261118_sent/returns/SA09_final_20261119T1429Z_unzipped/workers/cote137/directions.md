## 1. Complete the polynomial-category equivalence

**What it would establish.** A theorem with explicit data and morphisms on both sides of the announced strict-polynomial/tensor-PD equivalence, or a counterexample showing that its stated axioms do not suffice. The immediate target is the ordinary categorical theorem, before any derived enhancement.

**What is already known.** The homogeneous α composition core passes a direct proof check. Roby/SGA4 establish the module model, and Friedlander–Suslin provide a precise finite-dimensional field theory. The contemporary Hua2026 treatment explicitly excludes the full tensor/multigrading equivalence. These sources do not discharge the remaining mixed-degree requirements.

**First concrete step.** Transcribe the live axioms c)–g), all strict representation clauses and degree0/1 conditions into one typed specification. Expand composition of a degree(1+2) map with a degree2 map and require its mixed summands to have a unique definition. Produce a dependency table separating assumed exponential comparisons from conclusions. This tests the narrowest remaining gap rather than beginning a homotopy theory prematurely.

## 2. Prove existence in the multiplicative extension square

**What it would establish.** A sufficient and necessary, where feasible, kernel condition that extends multiplicative data from the disk category K to the selected larger category, followed by a precise coherence statement.

**What is already known.** The manuscript square proves uniqueness from monicity. The audit gives a conditional factorization if X*Y is the appropriate simultaneous admissible kernel and F preserves it. K has no unit, and the source supplies a separate unit datum after enlargement.

**First concrete step.** Fix two finite cone presentations of X and Y and compute the kernel of I₀*J₀→(I₁*J₀)⊕(I₀*J₁) using the actual transported simplicial tensor. Test D₁(2), S₀ and a positive sphere before formulating general coherence. Do not replace * by ordinary total tensor without a comparison theorem.

## 3. Reconstruct the weight-n PD comparison with the correct truncation

**What it would establish.** A natural comparison of the exact weight-n complex to good τ≤nC, with its top cycles and homotopy class specified. Product or PD compatibility would be separate later outputs.

**What is already known.** The source gives an early damaged diagram and a later explicit correction to a degree bound. Dold–Puppe supplies the comparison tools. Brutal truncation fails the promised H^n test. The projectivity-only equality case of the opening vanishing lemma is false as transcribed.

**First concrete step.** After resolving the essential scan4 generator glyphs, write matrices for weights1,2,3 and calculate their terminal kernels. State the corrected vanishing lemma under explicit acyclicity. A successful finite computation would guide a general proof; it would not itself establish all weights or homotopy reconstruction.

## 4. Determine the precise relative exact structure on P and Q

**What it would establish.** A valid exact-category or relative-homological framework for the proposed injective resolutions and Ext assertions, with its source and target admissibility conditions fixed.

**What is already known.** Bounded nonnegative finite-free P with degreewise split sequences supports the ordinary additive evaluation argument. The split-homology Q is not extension-closed in P, and the source’s P/Q image descriptions conflict. Standard exact-category resolution comparison applies only after its axioms are verified.

**First concrete step.** Enumerate the live source conditions at scans49–63 and test pushouts/pullbacks and composition using rank-one disks, especially the differential2 extension. Determine whether a smaller exact structure, an exact hull, or the full P is required. Then isolate the extra assumption needed for part(b), exact functors versus evaluated resolutions.

## 5. Identify the manuscript’s complete structured cochain object

**What it would establish.** A functor from explicitly specified manuscript operations to one modern structured-cochain category, with a checklist of preserved units, constants, substitutions, weak equivalences and mapping objects.

**What is already known.** Additive evaluation and several low-order operations are understood. Rational models, integral E-infinity cochains, binomial cochains and affinization solve different reconstruction problems under different hypotheses. No identification of the precise137 structure with any one of them was found.

**First concrete step.** Choose one target only, write the data required for its objects and morphisms, and map each required operation to a precise137 formula or mark it missing. Test K(Z,1), K(Z,2) and one p-torsion Eilenberg–Mac Lane space with the retained operation algebra stated explicitly. The expected single-run output is a comparison specification and obstruction, not a sweeping equivalence.

## 6. Compare binomial and PD operations while retaining constants

**What it would establish.** The exact information lost by passing from integer-valued polynomial operations to associated-graded divided powers, especially under substitution.

**What is already known.** The rank-one associated-graded multiplication matches Γ. Positive-degree leading composition has the PD factorial coefficient. Equal leading symbols can have different values on constants. A free PD algebra need not be binomial, and ordinary finite-group Burnside rings need not be binomial. The completed infinite-cyclic Burnside/Witt theorem is a separate object.

**First concrete step.** Construct the filtered substitution law for one generator through weight3, including degree0, coaddition and comultiplication. Compare it with the α maps and PD evaluation maps separately. A minimal additional lower-filtration datum would be more informative than merely re-proving the associated-graded algebra identity.

## 7. Extend the PID brick category to Dedekind bases

**What it would establish.** A coefficient-model category that retains projective ideal-class information and finite torsion data instead of attempting classification by ranks.

**What is already known.** PID Smith forms give scalar disks. Over a Dedekind base, torsion-free finite modules are projective, but nonfree projectives obstruct rank-only classification. The source explicitly asks to generalize.

**First concrete step.** Use R=Z[√−5] and its nonprincipal invertible ideal to write the simplest disk/sphere/torsion objects and their Hom modules. Formulate the extension-class data needed for composition. This is a manageable refinement of an existing theorem, not a claim that the general classification is new.

## 8. Make density and dual density statements exact

**What it would establish.** A corrected theorem for the two restricted nerves and their finite additive envelopes, with canonical presentation and enrichment hypotheses.

**What is already known.** Mere colimit generation fails in the set-valued case. The finite additive-envelope matrix argument works. Isbell's adequate-subcategory framework supplies the relevant distinction; higher space-valued project claims concern a different enrichment.

**First concrete step.** Separate the source’s four assertions: full faithfulness, essential image, restriction of functors, and tensor/Hom reconstruction. State each with its own hypotheses and test it on the one-object Q-vector-space example. Resolve the opposite-category diagram only after the logical structure is fixed.

## 9. Run the decisive manuscript scan collation

**What it would establish.** An image-based reading for every mathematically consequential alarm, preserving authorial errors where the ink confirms them.

**What is already known.** The50-row register supplies counterexamples or type checks and candidate alternatives. No source reading has been altered. Highest-priority sites are scan4's hypothesis/inequality,17's μ deletion,43's pairing shift,60–63's category labels,73's negation/truncation, and81's associativity superscript.

**First concrete step.** Review those six sites with original-resolution images and nearby handwriting, recording accepted/rejected alternatives and image evidence. Do not normalize the text merely because an alternative proves a desired theorem.

## 10. Recover the programme’s exact historical trajectory

**What is already known.** The supplied137 leaves, later public PS passages and the exact modern comparison theorems can be located. The two Sullivan inserts have identifiable provenance but unresolved version dating. Internal project statuses are often reported from absent context, and a newly found2026 report cannot establish older priority.

**First concrete step.** Assemble a table with date, object, functor, coefficient ring, finiteness/connectivity, conclusion and cited dependence. Begin with137/39, PS§119–121, and the opened reconstruction sources. Keep explicit author references apart from this run's inferred mathematical links.
