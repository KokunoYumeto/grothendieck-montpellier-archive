## Interpretation of the correspondences

### Derivators and higher coefficients

The older notes' GALOIS and RESIDUE summaries match their actual project texts. Their unresolved quantifier is construction on **all** bare derivators with specified functoriality, rather than the existence of some enhanced coefficient model realizing a weak-equivalence class. Cote137 discusses additive coefficient functors and later tensor/polynomial structures; it does not supply a derivator, a chosen monoidal enhancement of every derivator, enriched diagram shapes or the required iteration construction. This is a precise research connection and a precise limit, not a proof dependency (GPT-6 Astra,2026, GALOIS E.11/G.6; RESIDUE I.7).

### The changing total-asphericity project status

TOTASPH D.7 and RESIDUE F.5 expressly distinguish ordinary parallel arrows from arbitrary affine parallel pairs. They call the latter equalization problem unresolved in their own work, and TOTASPH explicitly avoids claiming it was open in the literature. TOTEX2 checkpoint03 later claims a counterexample: its ordered predimension category is said to be total and nonexact. The report separately uses ordinal injections for exact-but-nonfiltered and a repaired AKR category for quasi-filtered-but-nontotal. Those are three distinct roles.

The chronological disagreement therefore has a documentary explanation: the older returns leave a gap, while the later return claims a new separator. Reading D.1/D.2 and their stated dependencies is enough to establish that chronology. It is not an independent verification of the entire predimension construction or of every set-valued action. The notes' separate favorable intake/review status was not located and remains reported. None of these project statements converts ordinary colimit generation into the set-valued density assertion on137/29–34 (GPT-6 Astra,2026, TOTASPH D.7; RESIDUE F.5; TOTEX2 D.1–D.2).

### Enrichment and degeneracies

CATHOT F.8's target consists of **spaces** of tests. Its proof passage uses a right Kan extension from finite CW tests and an existing density theorem. The compactness obstruction concerns the specified inclusion and its Ind extension. Cote137's ordinary Hom-valued argument does not contain that enhancement or those comparison maps. The coincidence of the word density cannot remove those differences (GPT-6 Astra,2026, CATHOT F.8).

TOTASPH C.10 uses strictly increasing injections of nonempty finite ordinals. These lack the degeneracies present in the older Dold–Puppe terminology used by137. The example is therefore a useful terminological check, not a replacement for the manuscript Dold–Kan equivalence. PRETOT A.12 was not obtained; its separate attribution is retained as a lead instead of being absorbed into the verified TOTASPH endpoint.

### Reviews

The accessible LM4 and LM7 excerpts establish that bounded favorable reviews of named assertions exist. Their stated scopes are narrower than a blanket approval of their projects; they say nothing about137. No review proof or program was rerun for this dossier, and no such external review is counted as an independent proof check of a137 claim.
