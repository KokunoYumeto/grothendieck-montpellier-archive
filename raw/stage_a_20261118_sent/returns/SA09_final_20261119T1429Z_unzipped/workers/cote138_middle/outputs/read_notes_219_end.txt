243: ### N36 — Preserving the relation still gives only an outer involution
244: 
245: **Source:** 138/085 L9–26. **Kind:** own idea; clarification of a self-flagged step. **Confidence:** high, from a general word calculation and independent finite-rank checks. **Context/relation:** Hua §11 L1354–1367 gives another lift of the same outer reversal — adds the square of the particular lift. **Register:** **LM4 D.12 [Open, return's claim]** is not resolved by this representative calculation.
246: 
247: For \(\nu\geq2\) and the lower-index 1 alternative, put \(a=\lambda_0\), \(P_j=\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_j\), so \(P_\nu=a^{-1}\); the reversal satisfies \(t(P_k)=a^{-1}P_{\nu-k}^{-1}\), hence \(t^2=\operatorname{Inn}(a^{-1})\). Already in rank 2, \(t(a)=a\), \(t(b)=a^{-1}b^{-1}\), and \(t^2(b)=a^{-1}ba\), so this representative is not an order-two automorphism, although its outer class is an involution. Hua's replacement is \(\operatorname{Inn}(a)\circ t\), in the same outer class, and the strict \(\mathbb Z/2\)-action apparently advertised in \(\widetilde A_\nu\) therefore still needs a representative-level justification; the full conditional proof, rank-one exception, and rank 2–8 word checks are in `research_records/algebraic_context_checks.md` and `reversal_word_check.json`.

286: 
287: **Source:** 138/084 L4–6; /087 L13–22. **Kind:** unpursued implication; external comparison. **Context/relation:** *Esquisse* §4, scan 119/20 L864–871 defines regularity; `README.md` L22 names the later theorem; [E2] and [E3] — adds separately verified modern results. **Register:** **EXIT_TOWER E.24 [Open, return's claim]** is not settled by faithfulness.

289: González-Diez–Jaikin-Zapirain [E2, author version Theorem 18] proves Galois faithfulness even for regular dessins of any fixed hyperbolic type; choosing type \((3,2,7)\) gives the oriented regular-map case with index 2 above 1.
