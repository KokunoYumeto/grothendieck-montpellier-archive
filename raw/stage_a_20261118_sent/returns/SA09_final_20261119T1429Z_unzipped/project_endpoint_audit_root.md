# Additional project endpoint audit

The original part-2 attachment does not contain the named LM project returns.
Targeted retrieval during this run found `LM4_SUCCESSOR_background.zip` and
`LM4_SUCCESSOR_checkpoint_40.zip`. The former contains the earlier LM4 return,
selected LM7_PUSH sources, referee reports, an LM6 return and cote145. The latter
contains the actual successor register and section sources in a nested data TAR.
Only selected textual members were extracted from that checkpoint. Its recovery
instructions and included programs were treated as source data and were not run.

The background and checkpoint are additional research inputs. Their full bytes
are deliberately not repackaged in SA09; exact identities, retrieved member
paths and member hashes are retained. This audit concerns correspondence between
the manuscript and project statements. It is not a referee report on those
projects, and none of their model-generated proofs determines a literature class
in the SA09 claim map.

## The five checked comparisons

1. **Cote138/70,75 and LM4 C.12.** The manuscript title concerns surfaces with
   operators, while its marked isotopy discussion remains ordinary surface
   topology. The actual C.12 statement, lines5610–5628 of the supplied project
   paper, isolates the stronger kernel π1(C_A0(uΓ),1) for its hyperbolic setting.
   It still records the general zero-level decision as unresolved. This confirms
   the older note's warning: contractibility of Homeo0 alone cannot replace
   contractibility of an operator centralizer. The many special-case project
   results listed after that question were not used as inputs here.

2. **Cote138/62,85 and LM4 D.12.** The manuscript offers a graph involution and
   a flagged reversal formula. The actual D.12 question, beginning at line28889,
   asks about full profinite torsion and, specifically, involution conjugacy in
   GT0. It retains that conjugacy problem after recording narrower torsion
   restrictions. The present word calculation concerns one representative of
   one outer class; it neither settles nor refutes that universal question.

3. **Cote138/66–68 and LM4 F.7.** The manuscript's finite PGL2 classification
   concerns tame geometric sphere quotients. F.7, lines46183–46206, asks for
   equality of a completed A5 centralizer and the closure of the discrete
   centralizer. The project explicitly does not call the published assertion
   false. This dossier verifies that scope and wording; it does not adopt the
   project's criticism of a published proof without its own separate review.

4. **The same manuscript range and successor B.38.** The actual
   `sections/b_remaining.tex` and register agree that K5 and the level-seven and
   level-eleven questions remain open in that return. It expressly requires
   equivariant covering comparisons before transferring membership between
   levels. A numerical spherical signature is not such a comparison.

5. **The same range and successor B.39.** The actual statement distinguishes a
   claimed solvable repair from the unresolved nonsolvable question and says
   that its audit does not refute the cited published conclusions. This is a
   checked project-status report, not a mathematical finding of SA09 about those
   publications. No proof status has been imported into the manuscript ledger.
