Run started 2026-11-19 13:01:58 UTC. Sources are the attached part-1 archive; its TOPIC.md includes other-part folders for orientation only. No second-pass files and no scan images are supplied.

One source package is assigned to each reviewer. Each reviewer reads notes and transcription passages, records raw physical `.tex` line locators and scan markers, and writes manually reasoned verdicts. Programs organize evidence and check consistency; they do not decide verdicts. Codex transcripts are independent witnesses, not authoritative corrections.

All four stable source IDs receive exactly one aggregate verdict; all 56 internal notes are evaluated separately and linked to their parent source ID. No new synthetic NOTES_INDEX rows are inserted.

Disjoint item/claim/problem ID ranges prevent collisions between reviewers. Final consolidation keeps the established identifiers and records manually adjudicated aliases in SA12_id_map.tsv; it does not renumber surviving records. The original review records remain available. Partial duplicates retain their additional clauses with specific occurrences transferred to the canonical object.

The source, literature, and inference layers stay explicit. A reference can be verified as a real opened document while its relevance to an exact manuscript claim remains qualified. A physical cancellation is not automatically a mathematical refutation. A historical date is not inferred from a later publication date. A failed retrieval is logged and does not establish absence from the literature.

Mathematical check events distinguish a targeted argument check, an independent proof, a computation, a rechecked use and background use. Finite computations are not certificates of universal assertions. Literature searches are recorded with exact queries, claim IDs, opened URLs, and applicability limits.

The root reviewer saves complete checkpoint ZIPs at 45-minute intervals only, beginning at 13:46:58 UTC. The final ZIP is separate from checkpoints and includes source-read records, scripts and outputs, but excludes the supplied sources and earlier checkpoints.

All claim statuses and source-entry verdicts are reviewer decisions recorded after reading. Assembly checks schemas, ID references, the342-entry bijection,354 supplied scan loci, search-map coverage, and direction provenance. It cannot prove mathematical completeness from those structural checks; the saved source-range logs and independent checks supply the substantive audit. Refined statements remain at stable REF identifiers even when the refuted source variant receives a canonical claim alias.
