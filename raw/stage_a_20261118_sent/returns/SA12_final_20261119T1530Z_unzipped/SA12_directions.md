## 1. Formal Picard roots with the global-unit obstruction retained

**Ranks:** interest 1/22; few-hour progress 5/22.

**Sources:** items SA12-67, SA12-69, SA12-68, SA12-71; claims CL-SA12-091, CL-SA12-092, CL-SA12-093; problems MP-SA12-30.

**What the task would establish.** Find a usable sufficient hypothesis for the root-pair comparison to pass from finite thickenings to a formal limit, while retaining the quotient by global units.

**What is already known.** The unrestricted Picard-kernel uniqueness statement is false; for a fixed L and invertible n, the finite-level groupoid of pairs (M,M^n≅L) is the correct established object. Properness, unit lifting and inverse-limit effectiveness are separate inputs.

**First concrete step.** Write the exact unit/Picard sequences for one proper formal pair and one nonproper pair; list exactly which transition-surjectivity or Mittag–Leffler condition is available in each.

**Bounded endpoint.** A few hours can produce a sharply stated lemma with explicit sufficient limit hypotheses and decide whether an opened theorem already proves it. The task does not promise a new general formal-algebraization theorem.

## 15. Divisor root stacks, root gerbes and Kummer realization
