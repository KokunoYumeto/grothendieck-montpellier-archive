# SA07: ranked directions for subsequent research runs

Status date: **19 November 2026**. These are proposed tasks, not results established by this dossier. The order balances mathematical interest, importance for an accurate companion, and the prospect of a concrete result in a run of a few hours. Scores run from 1 to 5; a high feasibility score concerns the stated first task, not resolution of the surrounding major conjecture. Reading an absent scan or draft is a prerequisite where indicated. Every direction gives an indexed item, a claim, and a problem; supplementary Codex material is explicitly identified when it supplies the main mathematics.

## 1. Replace the false smooth tower in the comparison argument

**Origins:** SA07-31, SA07-33, SA07-34; CL-SA07-030, CL-SA07-032, CL-SA07-033, CL-SA07-134; MP-SA07-19, MP-SA07-21, MP-SA07-22. **Interest 5; feasibility 4.**

**Would establish:** a precisely delimited comparison of the relevant Betti fibre functors that does not use an impossible exhaustion by smooth maps with geometrically connected fibres. The separate countable inverse-limit lemma remains valid when its surjectivity hypotheses hold.

**Known boundary:** the algebraic closure of a transcendental function field supplies the obstruction to the proposed geometric tower. EGA IV.3 gives descent for finite presentation and finite diagrams. Bakker–Tsimerman, Lemmas 4.12–4.13, address a related comparison over finitely generated regular extensions. Wibmer's algebraically closed coefficient-field theorem, where applicable, concerns a different category from integral Z-valued fibre functors. See the exact scopes in the cited claim records.

**First concrete step:** write a list of the finite objects, comparison maps, and transition surjectivities actually required by the manuscript proof, then compare that list with the hypotheses of the available finite-stage results. A useful single-run output would be one valid restricted comparison proposition or an explicit remaining compatibility obstruction. Do not promise the full integral assertion.

## 5. Make the relative Fourier inversion formula canonical

## 6. Classify the real tensor obstruction in explicit small categories

**Origins:** SA07-10, SA07-411, SA07-412, SA07-413, SA07-416; CL-SA07-009, CL-SA07-412, CL-SA07-413, CL-SA07-418, CL-SA07-420; MP-SA07-02, MP-SA07-206, MP-SA07-207, MP-SA07-210. **Interest 4; feasibility 5.**

**Would establish:** a classification of when the ordinary and twisted real fibre functors are tensor-isomorphic in a carefully specified Artin–Tate or elliptic tensor category.

**Known boundary:** W=V⁺⊕iV⁻ is compatible with tensor structures, but the evident additive map has a minus sign on the negative-negative product. The sign Artin motive obstructs a real tensor isomorphism. A sign Artin motive tensored with Q(−1) also disproves the unrestricted even-Tate classification. **First concrete step:** list the generators, relations, and character group of one small category and solve the corresponding tensor-isomorphism torsor. This can produce a complete example illustrating all three distinctions: real form, real point of a torsor, and a chosen comparison point.
