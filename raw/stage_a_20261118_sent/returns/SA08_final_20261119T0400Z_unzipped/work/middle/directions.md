## 1. Corrected interval-extension theorem

**Origin:** items SA08-239–251; claims SA08-C143–152; MP-SA08-43 and MP-SA08-44. Principal passages: Q scans190–202, especially Q:195–222,301–345,373–467, then scans204–212, Q:504–757. Here Q denotes `MPL_136_189-234_transcription.tex`.

**Target:** a precise necessary-and-sufficient statement for a natural map of the specified simplex PD differential algebras, with a separate statement for parity-preserving maps, maps forgetting parity, fixed coefficients, and semilinear coefficient changes. The manuscript retracts the unrestricted interval-extension assertion and derives the condition 2B_i=0. Cumulative coordinates establish determination by the interval component; they do not themselves establish every required relation.

**Known:** the mixed-coordinate commutator and the lowest-degree cancellation are checked here; the displayed scalar-composition law is substitution. [Stacks09PF] and [Roby1963] supply the ordinary graded PD background, but do not automatically prove the inhomogeneous morphism theorem. [LiMondal2022, Th1.1 p2] treats another de Rham input category, so its semidirect monoid is not a substitute.

**First concrete step:** put the maps to Δ1, face and degeneracy identities, parity convention, and PD-product identities into one checklist, then test whether the author's corrected 2B_i condition implies every mixed relation in the universal two-coordinate algebra. Keep sufficiency distinct from the already checked necessity. A few hours should yield either a complete restricted statement or the first additional obstruction.

## 2. Precise uniqueness category and comparison axioms

**Origin:** items SA08-206–212 and SA08-234; claims SA08-C102–115 and SA08-C188; MP-SA08-30–35. Principal passages: P scans96–108, P:36–396, and scans111–121, P:416–676; P is `MPL_136_095-141_transcription.tex`.

**Target:** determine whether the author's intended localization is equivalent to a terminal groupoid and whether a workable left or right fraction calculus proves that assertion. A further contractibility claim about its entire nerve would require a separately stated target.

**Known:** Cartan's comparison theorem has checked axioms and a specific weighted realization [Cartan1976]. The monoidal acyclic-model theorem gives unique localized maps under modelwise acyclicity and presentability, with a PID/free-module application [GuillenNavarroPascualRoig2008, Th8.1 p372, Th9.2 p373]. Mandell's uniqueness theorem uses E∞ cochain theories with different axioms [Mandell2002, p549]. None has yet been identified with the exact τ∞-localized PD category on these leaves. The failed flat-to-contractible argument must not be reused.

**First concrete step:** write a two-column definition sheet for the manuscript category and each candidate comparison category: objects, arrows, weights, coefficient object, weak equivalences, and required splittings. Then inspect whether an acyclic-model resolution preserves its strict PD structure and admissibility. This is a bounded category-identification task, not a promise to settle the conjecture in one run.

## 3. Exterior model versus the uncertain divided/symmetric-power NB

**Origin:** items SA08-262–269; claims SA08-C165–171; MP-SA08-48–51. Pivotal passages: Q:1062–1167 (225–226, live and cancelled variants), Q:1271–1318 (229), and the continuation `MPL_136_235-281_transcription.tex`:154–170 (239).

**Target:** identify the exact functor and variance intended in each NB and record which statements are correct. The exterior identity NΛ^iΨ=k[i] is secure. Γ²Ψ and Sym²Ψ have different integral normalized complexes; the later unsure Π_*|K_* notation must be read literally before identifying an Eilenberg–Mac Lane object.

**Known:** the saved quadratic calculation has differentials ±2 and ±1. [BreenMikhailovTouze2016, Prop3.5 p270] gives derived-power décalage with finite-projective hypotheses. The cancelled Λ^(i+1) draft cannot repair the live formula by editorial inference.

**First concrete step:** collate scans225,226,229 and239 side by side and decide the star position, Γ/Λ/Sym letter and tensor insertion. Only after that should the verified derived-power theorem be matched. A scan run can settle the textual issue quickly even if the intended generalization remains incomplete.

## 4. Representing coalgebra and dual augmentation

**Origin:** items SA08-213–216,235–236,304 and314; claims SA08-C116–123 and SA08-C189–192; MP-SA08-36–37 and MP-SA08-137. Principal passages: P:680–748,775–912,929–1066 (123–135).

**Target:** a complete, convention-fixed equivalence between the displayed coalgebra/coPD coefficient datum and its represented completed simplex algebra, including morphisms, base change and the augmentation. This is narrower than an equivalence with a modern category classifying homotopy types.

**Known:** Hom turns the ordinary simplex-category colimit into a limit; finite homogeneous pieces supply the polynomial/PD duality [Roby1963; BreenMikhailovTouze2016, pp270,288]. The restriction Hom(S,k′)→Hom(Sbar,k′) is not automatically onto. Its failure for 2Z⊂Z identifies a real missing condition. The represented point also retains its coefficient object and must be compared before importing an affinization claim.

**First concrete step:** formulate the construction for degreewise finite projective S with an explicitly split Sbar⊂S, verify each dual structure under that hypothesis, and list exactly which uses of splitting survive under pure inclusions or restricted coefficient targets. The first deliverable should be a correct finite-projective theorem and an explicit boundary of generality.

## 5. Integration conventions and the final recurrence

**Origin:** items SA08-217–220 and232; claims SA08-C124–128; MP-SA08-38–39. Passages: P:1122–1163 (137),1205–1262 (139),1307–1378 (141).

**Target:** an annotated derivation of the integral normalization and Stokes equation, in exactly the manuscript's weight and exterior-degree conventions. The result should distinguish the chain-map construction from the later comparison theorem and its product factor.

**Known:** the beta integral gives γ=1; the final recurrence with the inserted binomial factor agrees with that value, while omitting it or restoring the deleted outer sign fails immediately. All Hasse operators are needed for the invariant description in positive characteristic. The top exterior degrees are determined by rank, although the ink is undecided. [DLMF514, 5.14.1–2] supplies the standard integral; [Cartan1976] supplies the published comparison context.

**First concrete step:** inspect the crowded insertions on141, fix the orientation of every face and the precise divided-power convention, then rewrite each intervening equality with its sign and weight shown. Existing computations should be reused; optional wider testing would not replace this textual work.

## 6. Completed weights and enhanced homotopy models

**Origin:** items SA08-229–238,270–273,298–314; claims SA08-C109–123,172–174,190–192; MP-SA08-52 and MP-SA08-137. Start with Q:1381–1475 (231–233) and the actual continuation scans235–239.

**Target:** identify which additive, multiplicative and higher structures the completed weighted object retains and compare only those structures with one chosen modern model. The first task is not to prove a universal equivalence among PD, numerical, binomial, affine-stack and E∞ models.

**Known:** later comparisons have different domains and structures: numerical models [Ekedahl2002], binomial cochains [Horel2024], affinization [Toen2020], E∞ cochains [Mandell2001; Mandell2006], diagram algebras [RichterSagave2020] and E∞ coalgebras [BachmannBurklund2026]. The point calculation, weightwise range and binomial product factors already prohibit several naive identifications. No unital integral specialization T↦1 exists in the free PD ring.

**First concrete step:** choose one model and compute its value, augmentation, endomorphisms and coefficient specialization on the point and on one Eilenberg–Mac Lane test object, preserving the manuscript weights. State the proposed comparison functor before asking whether it is fully faithful. A few hours can decide a concrete mismatch or identify a plausible restricted functor.

## 7. Inhomogeneous PD identities

**Origin:** items SA08-252 and the scan217 supplement; claims SA08-C153–154; MP-SA08-45–46. Passages: Q:781–797 (215) and Q:827–876 (217).

**Target:** specify precisely when γ_i(x+u)=γ_i(x)+γ_(i−1)(x)u defines the proposed operations on even-plus-odd elements. The restricted sum/scalar conditions and the unverified iteration identity deserve separate statements.

**Known:** graded alternating PD algebra conventions are standard [Stacks09PF; Roby1963]. Graded commutativity alone does not say that products of distinct odd elements vanish. The handwritten discussion changes its allowed hypotheses; a universal assertion must preserve them.

**First concrete step:** extract the live axiom list and build the universal algebra with two independent odd generators, first over Z and then with explicit two-torsion. Determine whether the intended operation is defined on a subideal or requires a restricted notion of addition. This would close a local proof gap and clarify Direction1.
