# Middle packages: findings for the SA08 dossier

The two packets contain a substantial continuous argument about weighted divided-power forms, interrupted by reused typescripts on abelian varieties and by two other donor texts. Their mathematical centre is the construction and comparison of simplicial de Rham theories with two gradings, a distinguished coefficient T and divided powers. The pages do not assert that an ordinary unweighted strictly commutative integral cochain algebra recovers every homotopy type. Several earlier-run proposals make such a comparison, but those proposals belong to the commentary layer and must retain that attribution.

This contribution covers all nine indexed composite entries of `MPL_136_095-141_notes.md` and all95 indexed entries of `MPL_136_189-234_notes.md`. The composite entry contains69 N notes,35 R notes,12 O notes and15 L notes, all separately read and cross-mapped in `embedded_coverage.tsv`. Both transcriptions were read continuously: P has1378 lines and Q has1528. P122 and Q214 are absent from the supplied sequence; Q224 is blank. No manuscript scans and no second-pass files were supplied. Every proposed reading correction therefore remains an alarm for a future scan examination.

For compact locators below, **P** means `MPL_136_095-141_transcription.tex`; **Q** means `MPL_136_189-234_transcription.tex`. Full file/scan/line locators and literal TeX quotations are in the records. The present-day source audit is dated19November2026. ## 2. The special de Rham construction and its incomplete localization proof

The first construction starts with an extension 0→kT→Φ→Ψ→0, with Ψ flat, and forms Γ(Φ)⊗Λ(Ψ). The quotient Φ→Ψ defines the differential. The free-algebra property supplies a canonical map to a special de Rham object with the same degree-one data. The text then attempts a stronger assertion about the category obtained by inverting its morphisms. These are distinct statements: existence of a free object, construction of comparison arrows, connectedness of a localization and triviality of all automorphisms require different arguments. [P:36–64,96–105,132–218; C101–106.]

Scan100 explicitly retracts the inference from flat and flasque to null homotopic. The supplied correction leans toward projectivity; it is not permission to reinstate the unrestricted argument. The dossier gives a nonsplit acyclic complex of flat Z-modules involving Z[1/p] as a counterexample to the naive chain-complex inference. That counterexample does not by itself identify every historical use of “universally flasque.” For the standard Φ used later, scan227 supplies an actual simplicial contraction, which remains a contraction after applying any ordinary functor. This is a valid special argument and does not say that arbitrary nonadditive functors preserve every quasi-isomorphism. [P:132–137; Q:1173–1228; C103–104,166; mathematical_checks.md.]

The primed categories introduce completed maps which can forget grading. The uncertain word at104 cannot override the explicit announcement at106 of two groups still to be determined. Connected groupoids can have nontrivial automorphism groups. Likewise, a terminal groupoid completion does not establish contractibility of the full nerve. The earlier notes are strongest when they preserve these distinctions and weakest when they import a general higher uniqueness slogan. [P:246–324; C107; items SA08-208,234; MP-SA08-31.]

## 3. Generalized theories, uniform weights and uniqueness

The axiomatic passage asks for contravariant functors with differential PD algebra values, two gradings, a coefficient object and a stable-weight extraction τ∞. The comparison diagram is RΓ(X,S)→RΓ(X,C)←Γ(X,C), with the stated extraction applied. The wording is repeatedly weakened in the draft: a desired property is not automatically a proved axiom, and a suggested converse is not a theorem. The question about λ_n(p) is whether the eventual-weight threshold for a fixed exterior degree p can be chosen independently of simplex dimension n. It is not a single uniform threshold across all cohomological degrees. [P:416–630; C109–113.]

The connected and simply connected localization, common-refinement and equalization proposals occur together at119–121. The evidence supports a genuine open step on those pages. It does not support calling the exact formulation either newly discovered or still open in2026. Cartan establishes comparisons in a carefully specified setting; later E∞ and monoidal acyclic-model theories establish other strong uniqueness results. The exact input category, coefficient conditions, weak equivalences and morphisms must be compared before transferring them. [P:618–676; C114–115,188; MP-SA08-34–35.]

A particularly close later source is Guillén Santos–Navarro–Pascual–Roig. Its Theorem8.1 gives unique localized monoidal lifts under acyclicity and presentability. Theorem9.2 applies to simplicial dg theories over a PID with free modules in each bidegree, simplexwise cochain homotopy equivalence and contractible simplicial modules in every nonnegative degree. This is meaningful partial development, but its hypotheses are not verified for every weighted PD theory proposed in the manuscript. The proof also makes the role of splitting visible. Mandell's2002 uniqueness theorem works with E∞ cochain theories and different axioms. [GuillenNavarroPascualRoig2008, pp372–376; Mandell2002, pp548–549.]

## 4. Coefficient coalgebras and the dual model

The next section changes viewpoint. The datum S, Sbar, S+, co-divided-power maps and a functional t represents the coefficient algebra by Hom_k(S,k′). Eliminating one barycentric coordinate and one differential yields the displayed representing module S⊗k[Y0,…,Y_(n−1)]⊗Λ(y0,…,y_(n−1)). The distinction between a direct sum and a product matters: duality of finite homogeneous pieces leads to completed PD series, while an unrestricted dual of an infinite direct sum is a product. The homogeneous weight of an i-th divided power is i times the weight of its generator. [P:680–748,775–871,929–1016; C116–122,189–192.]

There is a real qualification at the dual augmentation. An inclusion Sbar⊂S does not make Hom(S,k′)→Hom(Sbar,k′) surjective for every coefficient module. The restriction from Hom(Z,Z) to Hom(2Z,Z), for example, misses a homomorphism taking2 to1. The manuscript arrow therefore needs a split/pure inclusion or a more restricted assertion; the diagram is not silently repaired. The extension from simplices is an ordinary colimit, and Hom converts it to a limit. Homotopy invariance and exactness under additional coefficient changes are further questions. [P:801–803,894–912; C118–120; MP-SA08-36–37.]

The primitive polynomial coalgebra k[u] is paired with the completed PD coefficient ring, with t extracting the coefficient of u. This represents a substantial concrete construction. It is not by itself an identification with a later numerical-space, binomial, affine-stack or E∞-coalgebra category. Such comparisons remain labelled as earlier-run ideas or as explicitly proposed future tasks. Even the value on a point retains its coefficient object, which must be checked before claiming an equivalence with another model. [P:929–1066; C122–123,190–192; items SA08-236,304,314.]

## 5. Integration and the arithmetic of weights

The dual integration maps κ_n are required to satisfy a Stokes relation. This gives a chain map once the choices and signs agree; existence of a chain map alone does not prove a quasi-isomorphism. The later flasque-resolution argument supplies a distinct comparison mechanism. At139, the polynomial subring defined by Z_i=U+Y_i is characterized by invariance under simultaneous Y_i↦Y_i+h and U↦U−h. Every coefficient of h must vanish. In characteristic p, the ordinary first derivative cannot detect a p-th power, so replacing the family of Hasse operators by one derivative changes the claim. The saved proof works over any commutative base ring by an invertible change of variables. [P:1122–1163,1205–1235; C124–125; HoffmannKowalski2014, Def2.1, Prop3.4/Ex3.5.]

The exterior component is the exterior algebra of a zero-sum kernel of rank n inside a free module of rank n+1. Its determinant degrees are forced by these ranks. The transcribed n−1/n alternatives cannot both be correct, but only the scans can determine what was written. The simplex coefficients γ equal1 with the stated positive orientation and normalization. The beta integral and the final recurrence were recomputed independently:39 exact iterated integrals and84 recurrence cases agree with the general proofs. Omitting the inserted binomial coefficient gives0 in the first relevant example; restoring the deleted outer sign gives−1. The crowded intermediate insertion still needs scan review. [P:1240–1262,1307–1378; C126–128; DLMF514, 5.14.1–2.]

The product structure must retain its weights. In the reported weighted theory, multiplication involves a binomial coefficient relating the chosen weights. The earlier notes' conditional CP² obstruction is useful precisely because it targets a stronger, unweighted PD ring equivalence: a positive even class whose square is not divisible by2 cannot satisfy that proposed integral identification. It does not refute the weighted construction. Likewise, T²=2T^(2) blocks a unital specialization T↦1 over Z, but it does not block linear extraction of a chosen weight. [Items SA08-230–232; mathematical_checks.md; Cartan1976, §7.]

## 6. Endomorphisms, the prime two and semilinear coefficients

Q begins by analyzing maps of the interval algebra. A fixed-base map has A1=X0+f with f in the two endpoint PD ideals, and its images involve coefficients B_i multiplying dX0. The formula for reversal of the vertices is actually computed on192, so it should not remain an unresolved question in the problem list. The saved binomial-convolution calculation verifies that reversal twice is the identity. The literal image “1+df” for dX0 is a type error under the surrounding formula; dX0+df is a mathematical alternative to check on the scan. [Q:21–144; C143–144; item SA08-251.]

The unrestricted extension statement on194 is retracted by the author. Images such as X+dX in two independent coordinates have commutator2dX∧dY, and the later calculation derives 2B_i=0. This is a necessary compatibility condition; the consolidation does not announce that all sufficiency checks have thereby been completed. The coefficient a11=1+positive weight is cancellable by a lowest-degree argument in the specified graded algebra. Over the completion it is a unit. A bare constant-term slogan for an arbitrary coefficient algebra would be insufficient. [Q:195–222,373–467; C145–147; MP-SA08-43.]

The coefficient triples have morphisms (u,λ) satisfying u(t)=λt′. The scale is genuine data unless t′ is regular. Composition is substitution, not ordinary multiplication of λ's. The manuscript changes “group” to “monoid,” and a question-marked semidirect diagram remains a question. The intersection conditions λ0=1 and (i+1)λ_i=0 allow extra torsion phenomena. A Q-algebra assumption cannot be weakened to characteristic zero: Z has characteristic zero but does not invert2. Finally, the endpoint formula A1=X0+f assumes the coefficient map fixes T; a general semilinear map has endpoint φ0(T). [Q:504–757; C148–152; MP-SA08-44.]

## 8. What the later homotopy literature does and does not identify

The source checks establish several substantial later theorems, each with its actual algebraic structure. Mandell's2001 theorem uses E∞ cochains over an algebraic closure of F_p for connected p-complete nilpotent spaces of finite p-type; its appendix distinguishes other coefficient fields. The2006 integral theorem classifies finite-type nilpotent spaces but gives a faithful, non-full functor. Horel's binomial model recovers connected nilpotent finite-type spaces and gives Z-completion in the stated broader range. Ekedahl's numerical models are unique up to numerical homotopy, not strict isomorphism. Toën's affinization has a different outcome and is not full. [Mandell2001, Main Theorem and AppendixA; Mandell2006, Main Theorem/Th0.1; Horel2024, Th5.4/6.1; Ekedahl2002, Lem2.8/Th2.9; Toen2020, Th0.1/4.1.]

Richter–Sagave put strict commutativity in a category of diagrams with Day convolution, whose homotopy colimit carries the E∞ comparison. Bachmann–Burklund's2026 theorem uses E∞ coalgebras and p-complete nilpotent spaces without the older finite-type or connectedness restrictions; its essential-image statement has additional conditions. These are valuable comparison directions, but the manuscript's coPD operations and its ordinary coefficient coalgebras have not been identified with those enhancements. The independent search also found Li–Mondal's endomorphism theorem for algebraic de Rham cohomology of smooth algebras; its different input category prevents its semidirect monoid from answering the interval-map question here. [RichterSagave2020, Th1.1–1.2; BachmannBurklund2026, Th1.2/8.1 and1.3/8.2; LiMondal2022, Th1.1.]

## 9. Separate donor mathematics and its fate

The SGA donor record has its own precise claims map. Formal pairing properties, compatible finite-level pairings, inertia comparisons, period lattices and component-group statements match the original Exposé IX. The deeper integral positivity theorem requires a henselian trait, semistable reduction and an ample polarization for positivity. Rational nondegeneracy of the component pairing, including the p-primary part, is distinguished in the published remark from the deeper integral, ℓ-independent assertion. Nothing in the fact that these leaves were reused turns them into later discoveries after1975. [C130–142,175–186,197–199,501–502; SGA7IX1972, §§9.4–10.4,11.3,11.5,12.4,13.1–13.3,14.1–14.2.]

A genuinely later source removes a particular obstacle: de Jong's Corollary1.2 gives generic-fibre full faithfulness for p-divisible groups over a DVR in characteristic p. The finite p-basis condition belongs to his F-crystal theorem and is not copied into the corollary. The good/semistable reduction statement is Criterion2.5, not Theorem2.6. His1999 erratum adds a kernel/cokernel condition to the semistable definition after identifying a false lemma; it does not undo the full-faithfulness corollary. This distinction is central to the fate of the donor's Tate proviso. [C137,179; deJong1998; deJong1999Erratum.]

Some questions remain only partially traced: the integrality question in the potentially semistable normalization and the purely algebraic description/sign of the uniformization lattice also appear in the original publication. The search found relevant later pairing literature but did not verify a theorem answering those exact formulations. Coleman remained inaccessible in full; the bibliography says so. The correct conclusion is a bounded search boundary, not a declaration of novelty or current openness. The Stoia passage is different again: its scheme-theoretic openness statement matches the checked flat, locally finitely presented theorem in Stacks, while the later Bănică–Stoia analytic analogue and its bibliography identify a1975 note without replacing the missing original. [C139,183–187; MP-SA08-41,57–59; Stacks0C09; BanicaStoia1979; Stoia1975.]

The N27 English donor receives similarly bounded treatment. Sullivan's construction and nilpotent finite-type comparison are exact matches for parts of the discussion. His printed nonnilpotent real statement explicitly leaves part of its proof incomplete. The donor's broader wording is not converted into a theorem for arbitrary fundamental groups, and a mathematical match is not an attribution of authorship. [C129,193–196; MP-SA08-136; Sullivan1977, §5, Th8.1/8.1′/10.1.]

## 10. Consequences for the earlier notes and the next commentary

The earlier notes correctly identify most constructions, conjectural transitions and visible retractions. Their strongest defects are loss of hypotheses, merging of a question with a later answer, and movement between categories without a comparison functor. The revised records preserve the flatness retraction; distinguish a groupoid from its higher nerve; restrict the ordinary integral PD obstruction; keep the interval-extension condition separate from sufficiency; distinguish total weight from degree; separate the exterior model from Γ/Sym; and preserve the inclusive truncation boundary. The SGA donor, Stoia attribution and uncertain English donor remain separate mathematical layers.

Every literal quotation in the middle records has undergone a final source-line audit. The earlier automated broad excerpts sometimes began on an intervening SGA leaf; those excerpts have been replaced by manually selected single-scan passages. Every problem occurrence was rebuilt from the corrected claim quotations. Literature-comparison and earlier-run-idea items do not invent an author quotation: their `his_words` field is empty where there is no author statement asserting that idea, and their manuscript anchors are labelled accordingly.

The mathematical evidence is reproducible in `middle_math_checks.py`, its actual results and `mathematical_checks.md`. A finite computation is not presented as an unlimited proof; the general arguments and unverified compatibility steps are separated. Every claim has a searched fate in `search_coverage.tsv`, linked to the saved retrieval payloads and an exact-source audit. Readings remain undecided without scans, and external-project links are isolated in their own table. The ranked follow-ups in `directions.md` are deliberately bounded tasks: they state the intended result, existing evidence and first concrete step without developing a new research programme inside this consolidation.
