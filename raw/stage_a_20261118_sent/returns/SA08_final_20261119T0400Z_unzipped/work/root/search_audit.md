## Search coverage at the claim level

`SA08_claim_searches.tsv` gives one row per original claim occurrence, resolved to its canonical claim ID, with the relevant batch/family and bibliography keys. The early, middle and late source audits preserve their own exact routes: the later numerical/binomial/affine homotopy papers, the original SGA donor locators, the completed-coefficient comparisons, the equal-characteristic Tate input, monoidal acyclic models and the2026 coalgebra update. These routes are used only for the claims whose hypotheses they address.

For root claims SA08-C901–SA08-C958, the `fate` field identifies the original result or the exact bounded negative search. A classical formula can have classE and still be false as transcribed; a modern neighboring theory can be established without identifying the manuscript's category. Three explicitly labelled refined reconstruction records in the late contribution are retained as targets of refutation/check events, not passed off as authorial statements.

## Source-reading limits

- Dold1961: original printedpp258–263 read; later OCR files were generated but are not represented as read. The decisive theorem and separate-resolution construction are within the read range.
- Breen1978: the named category, theorem, Frobenius formula and the relevant later sections were read; the full proof is not labelled independently re-proved. Formula pages were visually checked.
- Mathew: the exact perfect-site AppendixA theorem and its distinction from the nonperfect problem were read; metadata establishes the2022 arXiv and2023 journal dates.
- Milnor K-theory book, Breen1981 and some historical dependencies: metadata access and later primary exposition are separated from original-proof access in the bibliography annotations.
- Manuscript scans: none supplied. Published PDF images are available for source collation, but do not settle the ink of the manuscript/transcription.
- External research-project context: named note attributions are preserved, but the independent target project files were not supplied. They are unverified provenance leads and do not serve as mathematical premises.

The result of a negative title search was never treated as a final fate. The bibliography and backward-reference routes were followed where the first search was inconclusive. Remaining nonmatches are stated as nonmatches within these recorded routes.
