## 1. Multiplication in the infinite weighted recovery

**Target.** Identify a particular localizing category and prove that recovery
preserves the product and the necessary higher coherence, rather than only
objects or cohomology groups. A useful outcome would be a correctly typed
comparison diagram whose arrows are genuinely monoidal in the chosen sense.

**Known.** The positive tail unit and finite derived recovery are proved over
arbitrary coefficient rings. A specified localizing Serre closure also gives
derived recovery of the coefficient embedding. Cartan1976 §7 already recovers
the ordinary integral cup product from the family of weighted binomial products.
Richter2009, Richter–Sagave2020, Mandell2006 and Horel2024 give different precise
structured models; none identifies the missing category here.

**First step.** Write the proposed tensor bifunctor on the chosen quotient,
test whether its negligible class is a tensor ideal, and determine whether the
comparison is lax, strong, or only derived. Do this before importing an
E-infinity or binomial equivalence. A several-hour run could isolate the first
obstruction and its exact hypotheses; it should not promise a full homotopy
classification.

## 2. Completed comparison

**Target.** Determine whether the surviving inclusive degree bound on237 extends
the published weighted model under a precise complete filtered coefficient
hypothesis, and identify any necessary derived-completion correction.

**Known.** Cartan's weighted comparison is explicit. Garvín et al.1998
Theorems4.1–4.2 have strict \(p<q\), and Remark3.3 gives a sharp failure of the next
extendability condition. The \(p\)-adic test on239–241 is now settled: cofinality
does not imply the required free associated graded. Thus neither the general
completion statement nor its boundary follows from those citations alone.

**First step.** Fix the completed tensor/PD polynomial presentation and prove
that its differential is continuous. Then compute the associated graded and
the relevant derived inverse-limit terms for one nontrivial coefficient ring
satisfying the graded-free hypothesis. The first run should decide the
convergence conditions and the endpoint, not propose an unrestricted theorem.

## 3. Universal power data, including zero indices

**Target.** Replace the partial list on320–325 by a complete typed equivalence
among natural operations, divided coproducts, and convolution PD structures.

**Known.** Yoneda gives the representing maps. Flat coalgebras admit the
canonical construction proved here, and nonflat coalgebras can fail to admit
any convolution PD structure. The alternative family over \(\mathbb F_2\)
refutes uniqueness only with positive-index composition and fixed-object
relations; it fails the \(q=0\) augmentation condition and full additive
naturality.

**First step.** State explicitly the ranges \(p,q\geq0\), the counit equations,
the direct-sum additivity square and the class of coefficient maps. Check each
direction of the claimed equivalence on the universal pair
\(A\to A\oplus A\). This is a tractable formalization task; it must not begin
by treating the restricted counterexample as a refutation of the stronger
axioms.

## 4. Continuous duality and completed exponential

**Target.** Identify a complete coalgebra/algebra category in which the
cancelled exponential comparison has a meaningful universal property.

**Known.** The ordinary cofree cotensor identity is valid without flatness.
The sequence with unbounded denominators blocks ordinary tensor from commuting
with an infinite product. These checks distinguish a correct elementary
identity from the unresolved meaning of the annotation NON and the topology
of the completed object.

**First step.** Specify whether duals are continuous, degreewise finite, or
pro-objects, and define the completed tensor accordingly. Test the claimed
base-change map on finite truncations, then state the exact inverse-limit
condition. This should produce a well-posed statement before seeking a
historical theorem with the same name.

## 5. The recursive coalgebra

**Target.** Determine whether the particular recursive construction on283–285
stabilizes or has a universal property in a specified category.

**Known.** The flat primitive coalgebra counterexample disproves an arbitrary
tensor/intersection interchange. It does not, by itself, show that the
manuscript's recursively defined sequence has the bad behavior.

**First step.** Transcribe the recursion with all ambient subobjects and maps
typed, then calculate its first stages in finite projective examples and in a
flat infinite-rank example. Either prove a special purity/Mittag–Leffler
condition or find a counterexample that follows the actual recursion. A broad
flatness counterexample alone is already available and need not be repeated.

## 6. Curve bundle: an editorial decision before a new theorem

**Target.** Decide whether the final bundle is holomorphically split, smoothly
split, or an associated graded, and identify the line bundle denoted Omega.

**Known.** The literal compact nonzero-degree split holomorphic interpretation
has no projective connection. The nonsplit jet bundle \(J^1(L^{-1})\), with
\(L^2=K_X\), is an exact modern oper comparison. It is not the same written
bundle. Pointed centered-disc normalization and the factor-two Euler
distinction are separate issues.

**First step.** Inspect327 and the supplied328 continuation on the scans,
especially the cancelled analytic/topological adjective, direct-sum sign,
Omega convention and group component. Only then formulate the connection
problem. A short run could settle these editorial branches; constructing a
new canonical connection before doing so would answer a different question.

## 7. Operation categories and the arbitrary-topos extension

**Target.** State the exact functor category classified by the proposed
operations object, including admissible limit shapes and idempotent splitting.

**Known.** Kelly's free-cocompletion theorem and its selected-shape version
cover the ordinary categorical part. The page's arbitrary-topos question does
not specify whether the functors must preserve colimits, sheafification or
finite limits. The marginal variance correction is substantive.

**First step.** Choose one precise category of coefficients and one small
class of shapes. Derive the ordinary universal property with all opposites
visible, then determine what sheafification changes. This can produce a
bounded theorem without pretending that the unspecified original L has
already been recovered.

## 8. Historical algebraization follow-up

**Target.** Identify an exact later theorem, or a counterexample, for the
general proper-analytic-space questions in SGA7IX13.5.3 and the stronger
direct ample-line-bundle descent question in7.1.3.

**Known.** The original SGA7 statements, Artin1969's effective formal-moduli
theorem and Raynaud's exposition are read. Their hypotheses do not
automatically settle the two strongest formulations. The source construction
uses a workaround and does not depend on promoting the doubtful direct
descent step.

**First step.** Follow the exact Artin–Deligne and Raynaud references from the
published passages, keeping analytic versus formal, schemes versus algebraic
spaces, and a chosen ample bundle versus some polarization separate. This is
a source-identification task; no current openness or novelty is inferred.

## Resolved diagnostics that should not become new open directions

The exact left coefficient adjoint, finite induced-target Ext vanishing,
positive binomial rigidity, negative \(\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z}(\mathbb Q,M)\)
formula, \(p\)-adic ideal minimum, elementary cotensor identity and flat
convolution construction have checked proofs in this dossier. Their value for
the next run is as constraints and reusable lemmas. Likewise Cartan's
binomial-gcd cup-product recovery is already printed in1976; it must not be
advertised as an unpursued deduction found here.
