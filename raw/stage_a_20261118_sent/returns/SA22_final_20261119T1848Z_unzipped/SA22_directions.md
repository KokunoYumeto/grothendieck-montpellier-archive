# SA22 — ranked research and companion tasks

**Assessment date: 19 November 2026.** These are specifications for future work. None of the directions below has been developed as a new research project in this consolidation. The proofs and counterexamples already recorded in the claims map check existing assertions; they are the starting evidence, not promised results of these tasks.

The ranking balances the importance of the question, its closeness to the manuscript, and a realistic deliverable from a run of a few hours. Mathematical research, historical source recovery and companion preparation are explicitly distinguished. “Unmatched” means that this run did not identify the exact result in its recorded search; it does not certify novelty or an open problem recognized by the literature. A scan-dependent first step requires the images in the next run. It is not permission to change the current diplomatic transcription.

The 28 direction records from the four parts are consolidated into 27 tasks. The early and late radius-map proposals are one direction, D-SA22-01. Other nearby tasks are retained where they have different objects or intended conclusions. All item, claim and problem identifiers below use the agreed canonical merges. In particular, MP-SA22-105 includes the former207 capping problem, and MP-SA22-106 includes the former205 shadow-injectivity problem. Cote66 has no indexed Pro-note entries: its tasks cite the actual passage and the claim/problem records without inventing an item. A direction arising from a theorem or editorial feature rather than an authorial question says explicitly why it has no MP identifier.
