555: # Legibility and limits
556: 
557: All 46 retained scans were read directly in one sequence, using the whole images, both supplied halves and additional enlarged or rotated crops; no OCR was used. A second visual reading of every page was completed before any context archive was opened. The reused typescripts are often readable, but overtyping, handwritten renumbering, clipped edges and unfilled mathematical slots require care. The handwriting ranges from clear propositions to very compressed connected prose; mathematical familiarity has not been used to complete a missing term or repair a surprising statement.
558: 
559: The least secure material is the faint cover wording/counts (002, 010, 019, 033), the heavily overwritten additions on 012–018, the rapid prose and cancelled diagrams on 031, the cancelled formula at the foot of 036, the margins and overlapping corollary on 042, both margins and the bottom-edge formula on 043, parts of the cancelled idelic draft on 045, and overwritten coefficients/superscripts on 051. The long cancellation strokes on reused sheets 012, 014, 016 and 018 cross the main typed block; their encoding as whole-block deletion records the apparent scope, which remains less secure than the readable words. Local deletion and insertion states inside those blocks are retained separately.
