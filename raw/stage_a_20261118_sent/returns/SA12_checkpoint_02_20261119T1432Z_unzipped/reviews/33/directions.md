## 2. Identify and repair the partial-range duality inference

**Origin:** SA12-626; CL-SA12-651,678; MP-SA12-161,165. This is a direction of this review, prompted by a false authorial inference; MP-SA12-161/165 supply the surrounding finiteness and derived-compatibility programme rather than an invented explicit authorial question.

**What it would establish:** A precise additional condition under which a partial range of locally constant compact-support cohomology controls the complementary ordinary-image range. One should distinguish local constancy of cohomology, truncation of the derived object, and the influence of higher internal Ext of the omitted tail.

**Known:** Over Λ=Z/n, self-injectivity makes the all-degree locally constant statement valid; freeness of stalks is unnecessary. The smooth morphism G_m×A¹→A¹ refutes the written partial-range extrapolation even for constant field coefficients. The index reversal j=2d−i is correct, so arithmetic alone cannot fix it. SGA4 XVIII3.2.5–3.2.6 supplies the surrounding derived duality but was not found to state the unrestricted partial sentence.

**First concrete step:** Write the dual of the truncation triangle and identify exactly which cohomology of the dualized tail can enter the claimed range. Compare that obstruction with the skyscraper in the saved example. Stop after a precise candidate hypothesis and a test against the example; proving a maximal criterion is a separate task.

## 3. Recover the exact relative-analytic comparison category

**Origin:** SA12-604–606; CL-SA12-609–612; MP-SA12-152–155.

**What it would establish:** Whether the relative scheme over a complex analytic base in scan8 has a recognizable published definition and whether both displayed comparison maps are covered by a theorem with the same hypotheses. It would also settle the interpretation of analytic constructibility in scan6.

**Known:** SGA4 XVI4.1 covers finite-type morphisms of complex algebraic schemes, with properness or constructibility. Algebraic strata are essential to the natural categorical comparison. The scan8 category and its local-ring continuity statement were not exactly identified by the completed searches; this is not evidence of a surviving open theorem.

**First concrete step:** Recover the preceding SGA11/16 analytic-topos conventions and the sources referred to in the original draft context, then define the relative étale category and analytification functor explicitly. Make a hypothesis-by-hypothesis comparison table before attributing any later theorem to the leaf.

## 4. Match the local-class and global-trace normalizations

**What it would establish:** An exact commentary diagram connecting the divisor Kummer boundary, products of regular equations, proper-support point classes and the normalized smooth trace. The result should explain the revised scan36 diagram with the correct shifts and Tate twists.

**Known:** SGA4 XVIII and Riou XVI2.3/2.5.13 provide canonical Gysin and trace constructions. The source’s divisor-unit invariance and support edge map have been checked here. **First concrete step:** Choose the source’s Kummer boundary sign and the direction of its Tate trivialization; compute the P¹ point-class normalization with those exact conventions; then write one derived square and map each visible source arrow to it. Compare the resulting Chern/extension construction with cote29 only after both loci have been read.

## 8. Build a source-aware chronology of the finiteness and purity programme

**What it would establish:** Which ingredients were already available in the seminar, which relied on resolution, and which later Gabber theorems removed the draft’s restrictions, without assigning the catalogue’s1965 label to every reused leaf.

**First concrete step:** Align the draft’s qualified statements with the original SGA seminar statements and the exact later theorems, recording a date only when a primary bibliographic or textual witness supplies it. Keep the reused typescripts in a separate chronological strand.
