222: $g:v\longrightarrow(v,f(v))$ of $V$ into $V\times W$ and if $f$ is rational over $k$, so is $g$ and so is
223: its image $\Gamma$. Conversely, assume $\Gamma$ rational over $k$; there exists therefore a
