975:

980: The source's local Kummer equations and Chern-class target suggest separating two objects that Hua's later root-stack interpretation puts close together: the gerbe of roots of a line bundle and the root stack of its defining section. For X=Spec k[x], D=(x), and n>1 invertible in k, the divisor root stack [Spec k[z]/μₙ], x=zⁿ, has trivial stabilizer off D and μₙ at D, whereas the root gerbe of the trivial line bundle O_X(D) is Bμₙ everywhere. This explicit distinction refines the modern comparison and suggests tracking the section as well as the line bundle; it does not establish an equivalence with the archival covering category or a novelty claim.

1044: #### N056 — Reducedness remains essential for the stated pointed rigidity
1045: 
1046: **Witness:** cote 29, scan(s) 126, 138, provisional condition (vi); Proposition 3 and pointed automorphisms. **Kind:** own idea.
1047: 
1048: **Source words:** essentiellement provisoires; S réduit connexe; Aut(P,ξ).
1049: 
1050: **Confidence:** high — The two sections and pointed translation are checked directly in k[ε]/(ε²), with ε^p=0 and ε nonzero.
1051: 
1052: The kernel-uniqueness check should not be used to remove reducedness from the separate rigidity claim on scans 126 and 138. Over S=Spec k[ε]/(ε²) in characteristic p>0, the trivial α_p-torsor has distinct sections 0 and ε with the same value at its geometric point, and translation by ε is a nonidentity equivariant automorphism fixing that marking. This refutes the version with reducedness omitted and sharpens the distinction in Hua's ‘Le dispositif’ and ‘Les groupes admissibles’, while leaving the source's qualified assertion intact.
