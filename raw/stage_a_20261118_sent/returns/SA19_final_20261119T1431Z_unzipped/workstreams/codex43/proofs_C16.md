# C16. Three questioned geometric Ext cells and a finite-kernel reduction

Throughout this check k is an algebraically closed field of characteristic p>0. Ext means Yoneda extensions in the abelian category of commutative algebraic k-group schemes. It does not mean the internal Ext sheaf on every k-algebra. This is the interpretation actually indicated by the heading on scan164.

Hazewinkel and Oort's report7214 (23June1972), Lemma2.1 with n=1, states that Ext¹(Ga,Ga) is the free left k[F]-module generated by the length-two Witt extension e. Here left coefficients act by pushout, F is output Frobenius, and Fa=a^pF. This theorem is read in its full primary report, rather than inferred from a publisher's description of Oort's book. The report points back to Sharma1967, Theorem7.3; that earlier primary theorem has not been independently opened, so 1972 is a verified latest settlement date, not a claimed first discovery date.

The Witt Frobenius endomorphism of W₂ makes a diagram of e with Frobenius on both its kernel and quotient. Consequently F*e=F_*e. It follows that both precomposition by F and postcomposition by F are injective on Ext¹(Ga,Ga): in the normal form sum a_iF^i e they shift degree up by one, with the coefficient Frobenius for postcomposition. On Hom(Ga,Ga)=k[F] the same two maps are injective and, because k is perfect, have cokernel the constant term k.

Apply Hom(Ga,−) to 0→αp→Ga→Ga→0. The relevant exact portion is

    Hom(Ga,Ga) --F_*--> Hom(Ga,Ga) → Ext¹(Ga,αp)
        → Ext¹(Ga,Ga) --F_*--> Ext¹(Ga,Ga).

The last map is injective, so Ext¹(Ga,αp)=coker(F_* on k[F])≅k. The contravariant sequence is different:

    Ext¹(Ga,Ga) --F*--> Ext¹(Ga,Ga) → Ext¹(αp,Ga)
        → Ext²(Ga,Ga) --F*--> Ext²(Ga,Ga).

Thus the preceding degree-one calculation gives only an injection k→Ext¹(αp,Ga); it does not by itself prove surjectivity. Fanelli and Schröer (2020, p. 8, paragraph after Theorem 1.8) explicitly state that Ext¹(αp,Ga) is one-dimensional and identify Oort (1966), table II.14-2, as its source. That additional published input completes the equality. This is a targeted proof check using an opened theorem, not an independent proof of the entire contravariant calculation. The peer review of this run identified and removed the initial gap. The construction specifies a vector-space identification with its chosen scalar action; it does not identify an arbitrary-base representing sheaf with Ga by evaluating only k-points.

For an abelian variety A, Hom_k(Ga,A)=0: a morphism A¹→A extends to P¹ by properness and is constant; a group homomorphism preserves zero. In the category above, apply Hom(Ga,−) to 0→A[p]→A --p--> A→0. Multiplication by p acts as zero on Ext¹(Ga,A), since pGa=0 and the two scalar actions on Ext agree. Exactness therefore yields

    Ext¹(Ga,A[p]) ≅ Ext¹(Ga,A).

This verifies a precise finite-kernel reduction for the Ga/A cell. It does not verify the separate αp/A or μp/A comparison cells: replacing the source by αp changes the relevant Frobenius exact sequence. Those cells remain explicitly distinguished in the audit.
