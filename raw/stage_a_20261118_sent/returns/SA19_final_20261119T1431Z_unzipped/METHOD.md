Source order: original supplied transcriptions, with any explicitly applicable second-pass readings taking precedence. No scans were supplied and no ink decision is made. Transcription line numbers refer to the untouched input .tex file, counted from 1; the source file is named whenever ambiguous. Scan numbers are archival scan labels, not PDF pagination reconstructed by the run.

The index labels all mixed-package entries cote 44. Actual passages include cotes 16, 47, 54 and 44. Every index entry is audited, with its actual cote recorded. Contextual cotes remain visibly separated from the core cotes 43–44.

A literature classification concerns the precisely stated proposition or its explicitly labelled refinement; it is not a claim that a later author read or was influenced by this manuscript. Where manuscript dating is unavailable, chronological priority cannot be proved. Publication and first circulation dates are separated when supported.

Check events record the strength of work actually performed. Reading a passage establishes what the transcription says; it is not an independent proof of its mathematics. Script-generated source indices do not generate verdicts.

All six stages are integrated across workstreams. Interim files in workstreams document progressive work; the consolidated top-level files are authoritative in the final deliverable.
