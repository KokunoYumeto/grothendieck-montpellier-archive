1129: **Kind: own idea.** *Location/topic: rationalization test.* **Manuscript anchor:** “objets de”. The abstract W-module M=⊕_(n≥1) W/p^nW is torsion, but its identity is killed by no single power of p, so that identity survives in End_W(M)⊗Q. A quotient that kills every torsion object would kill M; this test concerns the unrestricted module category, not a demonstrated counterexample among complete or finite-type crystals. **Confidence: high; reason:** localization of a morphism to zero requires a uniform annihilator, whereas elementwise torsion need not provide one.
