1737: **Kind: own idea.** Test “un sous-groupe ouvert” with $E:y^2=x^3-x$ over $K=\mathbf Q_3(i)$: $\Delta=64$ is a unit, and the zero $x^2$ coefficient in $x^3-x$ gives supersingular reduction by Silverman (2009, V, Theorem 4.1(a), p. 148). This does not decide the unread simplicity qualifier.
