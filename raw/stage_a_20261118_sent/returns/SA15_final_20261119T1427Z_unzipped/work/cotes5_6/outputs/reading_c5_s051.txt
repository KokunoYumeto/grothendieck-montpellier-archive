0881: **Kind: own idea.** **Confidence: high. Reason:** The letter's “coéfficients constants $\underline Z_p$” can already be distinguished from adic coefficients on a smooth connected algebraic elliptic curve E over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field: $H^1(E_{\mathrm{ét}},\mathbf Z_p^{\mathrm{disc}})=0$, since a continuous homomorphism from its profinite fundamental group to the discrete torsion-free group $\mathbf Z_p$ has finite, hence zero, image. The torsor description and the profiniteness for geometrically unibranch schemes are compatible with Bhatt and Scholze (2015, Lemmas 7.4.6 and 7.4.10 in the author version); this calculation isolates the coefficient issue before any rigid-analytic comparison is attempted.
