1679: ## Cote 6, scan 032, item 2
1680: 
1681: **Kind: conjecture.** In the special residual-group case referred back to 3.1, with perfect residue field, liftings over the complete discrete valuation ring are proposed to be “entièrement équivalente” to liftings of the specified filtration. The written module rank is $g+g'$, whereas the filtration type is $(g,g^*)$; both are retained, and no ramification bound or divided-power hypothesis is stated here. Messing’s p-divisible-group theorem (1972, V, §§1.1–1.6, pp. 150–151) instead assumes locally nilpotent divided powers and locally nilpotent p, and uses the locally liftable special-fibre subcategory. It therefore does not directly settle the asserted classification over an arbitrary ramified complete discrete valuation ring.
