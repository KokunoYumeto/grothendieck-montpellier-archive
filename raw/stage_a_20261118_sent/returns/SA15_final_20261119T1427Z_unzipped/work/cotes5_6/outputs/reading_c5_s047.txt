In the DVR case, counting multiples of each p-power in n! gives v_p(n!)=(n−s_p(n))/(p−1), where s_p(n) is the sum of the base-p digits; hence the supremum is approached along n=p^r. At e=p−1, v_π(π^(p^r)/(p^r)!)=1 for every r, so these divided powers neither vanish modulo π^m for m>1 nor converge to zero π-adically. **Confidence: high; reason:** both statements follow directly from the displayed valuation formula and distinguish integrality from the stronger boundary conditions.
