0541: **Kind: own idea.** **Confidence: high. Reason:** the listed finite monomial quotients, including « \(k[x,y]/\mathfrak m^2\) », have homogeneous defining ideals and therefore an Euler derivation. On the ordinary algebraic de Rham complex, \(d\iota_E+\iota_Ed\) acts as the positive weight \(w\), so \(w^{-1}\iota_E\) contracts each positive-weight part in characteristic zero. This verifies vanishing in positive degrees and constants in degree zero for those finite graded examples, conditional on that being the version of the Poincaré lemma intended.
