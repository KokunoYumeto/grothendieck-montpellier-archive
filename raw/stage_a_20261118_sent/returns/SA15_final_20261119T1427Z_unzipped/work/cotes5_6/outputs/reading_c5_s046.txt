0765: **Kind: unproved claim.** *Location/topic: upper second paragraph.* **Manuscript anchor:** “représente le foncteur covariant”. The text says that the finite module of analytic differentials ‘représente le foncteur covariant’ of derivations on finite modules, and that ‘Il est clair’ that it equals ordinary differentials when the latter are finite. A proof needs the precise topology on the target modules and the universal factorization that precedes this paragraph; the claim should not be read as an unrestricted representability assertion on all modules.
