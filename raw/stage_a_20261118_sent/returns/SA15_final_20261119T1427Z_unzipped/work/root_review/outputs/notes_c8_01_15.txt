103: - **E.07 — Reading question; 8:002, 004–005 — Cartier duality requires the correct module dual.** The coexistence of “$G^*$”, checked cotangent complexes, and $D^*(G)$ makes variance a necessary preliminary check. Oda proves $M(G^D)\simeq D(M(G))$, where for a finite-length module $D(M)=\operatorname{Hom}_{W(k)}(M,K/W(k))$, with $F,V$ exchanged by semilinear transpose; it is not the ordinary $W(k)$-dual $\operatorname{Hom}_{W(k)}(M,W(k))$, which vanishes on torsion modules (Oda, 1969, Definition 3.5, p. 82; Theorem 3.19, p. 89). This is the relevant comparison for **8:002/004–005 ↔ 54:005/007**, where Cartier duality and transposed exact sequences are explicitly at issue.

107: - **E.34 — Unpursued implication; 8:002, 013, 022 — Oda's general smooth-proper result is narrower than the abelian case.** For a smooth proper $X/k$ over a perfect characteristic-$p$ field $k$, with a $k$-point, Oda's Corollary 5.12 gives an injection from the dual Dieudonné module of the $p$-torsion of the Picard scheme into $H^1_{\mathrm{dR}}(X)$, with image the subspace on which all the relevant iterates of Cartier remain closed; it is an isomorphism in the abelian case (1969, p. 131).
