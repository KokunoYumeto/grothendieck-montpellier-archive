479: - **T.02 — Reading question; Cote 8, scan 061 — “un réseau invariant par $F$”.** Inclusion $F(\Lambda)\subseteq\Lambda$ and equality $F(\Lambda)=\Lambda$ are different conditions: already over $\mathbf Q_p$, $F(e)=pe$ satisfies the former and cannot satisfy the latter for any lattice. 509: - **T.09 — Own idea; Cote 8, scan 063 — explicit counterexample to the formal implication.** Let $G=\mathrm{SL}_2$ over a characteristic-zero field and let $B\subsetneq G$ be its upper triangular Borel: the $B$-invariants in $\mathrm{Sym}^n(k^2)$ vanish for $n>0$, because the upper unipotent subgroup fixes only the highest-weight line, on which the diagonal torus has weight $n$. Decomposing $V^\vee\otimes W$ into these irreducibles gives $\operatorname{Hom}_B(V,W)=\operatorname{Hom}_G(V,W)$, so restriction is fully faithful, although the standard representation has a $B$-stable line which is not $G$-stable. **Confidence: high**, because this is a direct representation-theoretic proof over a split field, extending to any characteristic-zero base by scalar extension. It refutes the abstract implication in item T.08; it does not assert that this particular $B$ is the image of the manuscript’s unidentified arithmetic functor.
