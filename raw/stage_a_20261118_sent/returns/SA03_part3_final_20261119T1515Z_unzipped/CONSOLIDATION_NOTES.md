# Consolidation decisions

The root tables apply the explicit authored decisions in `ITEM_MERGES.json`,
`CLAIM_MERGES.json`, `PROBLEM_MERGES.json` and `LINK_REVIEW.json`.
These are semantic reviews after reading the part records and the source
endpoints; no heading-matching algorithm makes the decisions.

## Cross-package identities

The repeated finite-étale envelope, homogeneous criterion, finite-support
argument and open-factorization construction in cote 91 are merged at item
level. Distinct test categories, the proper-source variant, the n(s)-bounded
affine-neighbourhood condition and the general-W gluing theorem remain distinct
claim scopes. Two counterexamples to open-union preservation are retained in
the same item, with their earlier-run attribution.

The normalized principal-parts extension on cote 93 scan 135 is identified
directly with the construction on scan 91. The latter gives L⊗ω=P¹(ω) and the
universal first jet, while the former repeats that extension and its quadratic-
differential ambiguity. This resolves the algebraic object even though scans
92–134 are absent. It does not supply the separate “canonical transcendental”
construction introduced later on135.

## Two-endpoint links

Project leads with unavailable destination files are excluded from the
confirmed-link table. Two historical book-theorem locators in part c were
available only as metadata and are retained as leads. A modern Stacks theorem
read in full supports the pro-Hom comparison independently of an unread EGA
locator. The Brion theorem supports the automorphism comparison separately from
the metadata-only original Matsumura–Oort endpoint.

## Chronology and present mathematical status

No record receives class A solely because an undated sheet matches an author's
published theorem. Reused established material is class E with chronology
explicit; scope gaps are class C only with the precise unresolved formulation
or comparison stated. The notebook date, public correspondence date, talk date,
preprint date, publication date and checked revision date are distinct evidence.

Reading a paper's theorem, independently verifying a short argument, checking
a selected proof passage and re-proving the whole theorem have different check
event types. The bibliography's access flag does not erase these differences.
The direct Messing access obtained during consolidation improves the earlier
indirect reference and closes the p-local comparison/cocycle question in its
published scope. It does not automatically prove every all-base or all-nil
version in the historical programme.

## Formula diagnostics

Witness conflicts and published inherited errors remain explicit. No source
file is edited, including when a formula has a decisive counterexample.
Exact computations test the formula stated, while their proof notes say what
is and is not established for all fields, all primes or all degrees. The
Verschiebung-section and Cartier-Jordan counterexamples have independent
arguments checking their full hypotheses, not just finite numerical samples.
