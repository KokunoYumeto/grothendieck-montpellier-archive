## The paper's abstract

We investigate whether an ordinary completed unit-equation pair for \(\mathbb P^1_{\mathbb Q}\setminus\{0,1,\infty\}\) that admits a continuous section of the full geometrically pronilpotent quotient must have finite prime support. We have not proved this assertion or constructed a counterexample. We establish the precise fixed-pair compactness reduction, reconstruct the factorial-prime ordinary ghosts, and retain the strict continuous-cohomology obstruction, including classes invisible at all finite coefficient levels. Two exceptional-prime tests select a unique class-two candidate for the original ghosts, reducing their class-three problem to explicit scalar residue sequences. We distinguish this fixed-pair compactness from the false compactness assertion for a varying ghost family.
