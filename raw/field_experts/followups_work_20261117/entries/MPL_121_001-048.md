#### Q3.2 — Verification of the operations and morphisms in the compact comparison

**Kind:** own explanation — explicit operation check. **Anchor:** 120/003, M3; 006, graph; 017, M9.

**Context:** `MPL_147_138-190_transcription.tex`, scans 181–184; current 121/004–010. **Relationship:** specifies the compact comparison on objects and maps. **Register:** INTAKE 20261114 §13.2 [intake lead]. **Confidence:** high; each closure operation and the graph projection are explicit.

The inclusion \(K_n(\mathcal S)\hookrightarrow\mathcal S_n\) preserves finite unions, intersections, products, coordinate images, and the restricted inverse-image operation \(A\cap(u^*)^{-1}(B)\) for compact definable \(A,B\), since the last set is closed in \(A\). A compact definable graph \(\Gamma_f\subseteq A\times B\) projects continuously and bijectively onto compact \(A\), so the projection is a homeomorphism and \(f\) is continuous; conversely a continuous definable map on compact \(A\) has compact graph. The definable function \(f(0)=1\), \(f(x)=0\) for \(0<x\leq1\) has closure point \((0,0)\) missing from its graph, making the continuity restriction concrete. Likewise \([0,1]\in K_1\) and \(\mathbb R\setminus[0,1]\notin K_1\), so the inclusion does not preserve 121's complement operation within the compact family.

#### Q3.4 — Two explicit tests of the one-space axioms

**Kind:** own explanation — explicit examples. **Anchor:** 121/004–010 and 011–014.

On \(X=\mathbb R\), \(F=\{\varnothing,\mathbb R\}\) satisfies the bare a–f: closures and relative interiors stay in the family, the only proper rare closed member is empty, and \(\mathbb R\) is connected. Its inclusion into the standard unary definable family omits \(\{0\}\), and its open members do not give the optional basis; this family lies outside the image of \(\mathcal S\mapsto F_{\mathbb R}(\mathcal S)\) with real parameters, since every family in that image contains \(\{0\}\). #### Q3.6 — Analytic-geometric categories: an established inverse dictionary

**Kind:** unpursued implication / literature comparison. **Anchor:** 121/019–024, “Localisation”.

**Context:** `MPL_147_138-190_transcription.tex`, scans 182–184; `MPL_149_091-136_transcription.tex`, scan 133. **Relationship:** adds published maps relating global definability and local geometry. **Register:** INTAKE 20261114 §13.2 [intake lead]; WG2 B15 (§1.2) [unchecked plan].

Van den Dries–Miller give inverse constructions between analytic-geometric categories \(\mathcal C\) and o-minimal expansions of \(\mathbb R_{\rm an}\):
\[
\mathcal S(\mathcal C)_n
=\{A\subseteq\mathbb R^n:A\in\mathcal C(\mathbb P^n(\mathbb R))\},
\qquad x\longmapsto[1:x],
\]
and the reverse construction uses local analytic charts [vdDM, §3 and Appendix D.10]. The correspondence retains ambient manifolds, locality, products, and proper analytic images; its category morphisms are continuous maps whose graphs belong to the specified family in the product manifold [vdDM, §1]. These are concrete comparison data for the localization problem on 121/020–024, with the expansion-of-\(\mathbb R_{\rm an}\) requirement included.

#### Q3.8 — The local/global map on an explicit subset of the line

**Kind:** own explanation — explicit example. **Anchor:** 121/020–024, \(F\to\overline F\).

**Context:** `MPL_147_138-190_transcription.tex`, scans 182–184; see N23–N35 for the current manuscript's questions. **Relationship:** tests the localization against the compactification in Q3.7. **Register:** WG2 B15 (§1.2) [unchecked plan]; BISITE D.12 [Open, return claim]. **Confidence:** high; the local representatives and their accumulation are explicit.

For the semialgebraic family \(F\) on \(\mathbb R\), every point has a neighbourhood meeting \(\mathbb Z\) in at most one point, so \(\mathbb Z\in\overline F\). Its infinitely many isolated components exclude it from \(F\), explicitly showing the strict inclusion \(F\hookrightarrow\overline F\) on this set. Under \(\tau_1\), those points accumulate at \(\pm1\), and every neighbourhood of either endpoint meets infinitely many separated image points, so local semialgebraicity fails there. This explains the compactification test through the actual maps, without assigning an all-arity first-order structure to an arbitrary sheaf on one space.
