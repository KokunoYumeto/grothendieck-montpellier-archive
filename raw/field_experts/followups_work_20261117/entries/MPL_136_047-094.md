### R061 — A counterexample to the arbitrary-presheaf interpretation of the flagged step

**File/scan:** 136, 064; compare 058–062. **Kind:** own idea. **Ink anchor:** “sans hypothèse de loc. constance” and “à vérifier ce point.” **Confidence:** high for the explicit presheaf calculation, independently checked; applicability is to the literal arbitrary-presheaf interpretation, not an unstated restricted coefficient category.
