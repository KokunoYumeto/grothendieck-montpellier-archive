### C24 — Filling every hole restores the global surface relation

**Location:** Scans 121–122, “dans chacun” of the presentation cases and the instruction to kill peripheral loops.  
**Kind:** unpursued implication

For an orientable compact connected surface of genus \(g\) with \(b>0\) punctures, filling all holes turns the free group of rank \(2g+b-1\) into the closed-surface presentation with its product-of-commutators relation.
