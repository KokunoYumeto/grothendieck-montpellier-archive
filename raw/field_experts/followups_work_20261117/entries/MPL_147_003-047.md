### N026 — A graph automorphism need not lift to an automorphism of a particular curve

**Scan/location:** 021, the action of Γ = Aut G on (23).

**Kind:** own idea.

**Source:** “opère sur ... le système ...”.

**Note:** For a geometric curve $C$ of graph type $G$, there is an exact sequence $1\to\prod_\alpha\operatorname{Aut}(Y_\alpha,\widehat I_\alpha)\to\operatorname{Aut}(C,I)\to\Gamma_C\to1$, where $\Gamma_C$ is the subgroup of graph automorphisms preserving the isomorphism class of the decorated collection of components.
