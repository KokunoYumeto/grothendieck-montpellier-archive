## Theorem 1: (IV) holds on all of GT̂ `[F18]` `[ae4-23]`

**Statement.** For every F = (λ, f) ∈ GT̂,
f(u, v⁴) = v^{8ρ₂} f(u², v²) u^{4ρ₂} (uv)^{−6ρ₂} in B̂₃, with ρ₂ = ρ₂(F).

**Proof in outline (§4):**
1. **The coloured four-point group.** R₀ = ⟨d, z | (zd)²⟩ ≅ C₂ ∗ Z. Map it to B by d ↦ u, z ↦ v²; the image has index 3.
   F acts by Φ_R(d) = d^λ and Φ_R(z) = h z^λ h⁻¹, with h = f(z, d²).
2. **Proposition 8, the swap.** There is g₀ with SΦ_R S = Ad(g₀)Φ_R, where S exchanges d and z.
   - The setting: the ten-point Klein configuration in Γ_{0,[10]}, with H = C₂² acting by t ↦ −t and t ↦ 1/t.
   - Lemma 9 (Boggi–Zalesskii, Theorem A) gives e₁ ∈ P̂ ∪ P̂θ with Ad(e₁⁻¹)A_F fixing H pointwise.
   - The centralizer satisfies N/H = π₁(P¹_w ∖ {∞, 2, −2}), with w = t² + t⁻².
   - Two forgetting maps, to the quartets {0,∞},{t,1/t} and {1,−1},{t,−t}, satisfy q_a = T_* q_b, where
     T(w) = 2(w+6)/(w−2) swaps the two cusps.
   - Out(R₀) = {1, ι, t, tι}, derived from Fouxe-Rabinovitch's generators. So T_* = Ad(g₁)S.
3. **Proposition 10.** Two centralizers are cyclic (Herfort–Ribes): g₀h ∈ ⟨z⟩ and k⁻¹g₀ ∈ ⟨d⟩, with k = f(d, z²).
   - K₀ = ker(d ↦ 1) is free on z and d².
   - Abelianizing z^s = k d^t h gives s = 4c and t = −4c, where c = c(f) is f's class (0, c, −c) in E^ab.
   - Lifting through J = ⟨u, v²⟩, whose kernel is the central (uv)³, and applying the character u ↦ 1, v² ↦ 0 gives
     (IV) with c in place of ρ₂.
4. **Proposition 11: c = ρ₂.** Put j = Ad(u⁻¹s)∘ι₀. Then jφj⁻¹ = Ad(u^{λ−1})Φ_F, using f = ϑ(β)β⁻¹ and (uv²)² = 1 in B.
   Evaluating SφS = Ad(g)φ on a = zd and applying the character L (z ↦ 1, d ↦ −1) gives 4(c − ρ₂) = λ − 1 − 2m = 0.

**Good for.** The subgroup IΓ of Nakamura–Schneps is cut out of GT̂ by (III′) alone. Equivalently, NS Prop. 5.3,
ρ₂ = −A_F(−1,1), holds on all of GT̂. Lochak–Nakamura–Schneps (Feb 2026) called this unclear.

## Theorem 2: GT̂ = Λ₀·G_Q with Λ₀ ⊂ Λ `[ae4-23]`

**Statement.** Every F ∈ GT̂ is F_*σ with σ ∈ G_Q and F_* normalized (λ = 1, ρ₂ = 0). Every normalized f lies in the
group Λ of Hatcher–Lochak–Schneps.

**Proof in outline (§5):**
1. **The decomposition.**
   - Choose σ₀ with χ(σ₀) = λ_F. Then ρ₂(Fσ₀⁻¹) ∈ 2Ẑ.
   - On G_{Q^ab} the Kummer cocycle κ₂ is a homomorphism with image exactly 2Ẑ: 2^{1/n} ∈ Q^ab exactly for n ≤ 2, since
     √2 ∈ Q(μ₈) and Q(2^{1/n}) is not normal for n > 2.
   - Take σ₁ ∈ G_{Q^ab} with κ₂(σ₁) = ρ₂(Fσ₀⁻¹).
2. **Λ.**
   - (IV) at λ = 1, ρ₂ = 0 gives f(u, v⁴) = f(u², v²) = f(u⁴, v).
   - Schneps (Math. Nachr. 2006, p. 670) makes Π, the left side of (R), central modulo the centre of B̂₄, so Π = Ω^n with
     Ω = (abc)⁴.
   - Π lies in the closed commutator subgroup and Ω has degree 12, so n = 0. This is (R).

**Good for.** Up to Galois elements, every element of GT̂ acts on the whole pure tower by the HLS formulas. Before this,
the only explicit elements of Λ were Galois (HLS p. 3).

## The application: Theorem 3 and Corollary 4 `[F23]` `[ae4-22]` `[ae4-23]`

**Theorem 3.** δ = a₂ + a₃ = 0 on normalized elements. Here a₃ and a₂ are the sign classes of the level-12 frames
q₁₂(A_F) and q₁₂(β_F) on H₀ = {λ = 1, t_F = 0}. The shear t_F satisfies t ≡ 0 mod 4 and t ≡ −ρ₂ mod 3 (Lemma 13).

**Proof in outline (§§7-9):**
1. **§7.** For normalized F, f ∈ Λ acts on the genus-one mixed groups by HLS. Lemmas 16 and 17 compare that action with
   Φ_F and A_F on Γ̂_{1,1} and Γ̂_{1,2}.
2. **§8, Proposition 18, the hexagonal self-similarity.** Ψφ = Ad(h)φΦ_F with h ∈ G₃ = q⁻¹⟨uv⟩.
   - Step 1: the nine points T[3]; BZ at p = 3; ε = det ≡ 1 mod 3.
   - Step 2: the centralizer of the involution ι, with full topology through twelve points and Asada's faithfulness.
   - Step 3: HMM Corollary C, Out(Γ̂_{0,8}) = GT̂ × S₈, gives parameter F′ = F.
   - Step 4: δ₅ = Ad(c₃)A_F on D₅ = Γ_{1,2}.
   - Step 5: the double cover branched at the 3-division x-roots is Y² = Z³ + D/108. So B̂ ≅ G₃ ⊂ Γ̂_{1,2}.
   - Step 6: the centralizer of uv in Γ̂_{1,1} is ⟨uv⟩.
3. **§9, the parity algebra.**
   - On K^ab: TC = R₊^k C M (mod 12).
   - M = +1 on ker(R + I) mod 3, and M ≡ (−1)^{a₂} R^j mod 4 (Lemma 15: ν ≡ 1 mod 3, ν ≡ (−1)^{a₂} mod 4).
   - Hence a₃ ≡ k ≡ a₂.

**Corollary 4.** Every homomorphism Δ: GT̂ → F₂ that is trivial on G_Q and equal to δ on Λ₀ vanishes. This follows from
