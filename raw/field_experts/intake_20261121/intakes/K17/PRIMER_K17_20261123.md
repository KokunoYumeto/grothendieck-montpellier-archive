## Main results

**K17-S17 and K17-S17-UNIT (unit criterion; checked).**
- *Statement.* Step 17 of Alpöge's remark is the Lean Selmer bound. Its intermediate form `OneCharU_s` gives
  length S_ℓ(Q, 2k) ≤ 2 Σ_{ρ∈X} v_ℓ(tr ρ(s) − t), over the admissible three-dimensional Galois representations ρ
  (one per isomorphism class), under the datum GD.
- *Consequence.* If every tr ρ(s) − t is an ℓ-unit, then S_ℓ(Q, 2k) = 0. The strict Selmer group is Pontryagin dual to
  K_{4k}(Z){ℓ} (Rost–Voevodsky; Kurihara 1992, Cor. 1.5; Weibel VI.8.2–8.3, 10.9).
- *Locators.* `DenseThresholdCases/NumberU3US.lean`; proof in `FOneCharU.lean:75–95` from nine hypotheses, discharged
  in `CorGKOf.lean:10–25`.

**K17-K12-VANISH: K_12(Z) = 0 (proof read; classical half recomputed by me).**
- *Setup.* Take ℓ > 2^31, E = Q(√−3), e = 6, s = Frobenius at 7 = (3 + ω)(3 + ω̄), and t = 7^{−6} + 1 + 7^6.
- *Coverage.* Every admissible ρ comes from

  - a form in S_13(Γ_0(3), χ_{−3}), with trace 1 + a_7/7^6.
- *Certificate.* The product of the differences is N_7 = 2^15·3^19·7·13^5·37·43^3·47^3·61·271^3. All its primes are

  Rognes–Weibel.

**K17-P2-MOMENT: Ihara's kernel question at p = 2 (checked in full).**
- *Statement.* The pro-2 outer action of G_Q on π_1(P¹ − {0,1,∞}) has kernel field Ω_2, the maximal pro-2 extension of
  Q unramified at the finite primes other than 2, with the real place allowed to complexify.
- *Mechanism.*
  - Gras–Jaulent give P = C_2 ∗ Z_2.
  - H = ker χ is free pro-2 on the conjugates h_x.
  - Magnus coefficients are measures on Z_2^r.
  - The Hain–Matsumoto density and Brown's free odd generators turn a kernel element into vanishing moments
    ∫ ∏ u^{m_j x_j} dμ = 0 on the grid of odd m.
  - By the Amice transform and the identity theorem, μ = 0.
- *Locator.* 8622–8826.

**K17-POLE-GENERAL and K17-VANDIVER-KERNEL (checked, with Fukaya–Kato and Wake–Wang-Erickson carried).**
- *Statement.* If p satisfies Vandiver, then Ω_p* = Ω_p. So the equality holds for every p < 2^31.
- *Mechanism.* Vandiver gives:
  - X^+ = 0, by persistence up the tower;
  - Sharifi's map ϖ is an isomorphism (Wake–Wang-Erickson Prop. 2.2.6, Cor. 5.1.1–5.1.2);
  - cup products of p-units generate H² (Fukaya–Kato §7.1.10);
  - by parity, odd cups span H²(P, F_p).

  The pole theorem then separates Magnus coefficients through the companion poles at roots of the even characteristic
  polynomials q_e.
- *Locators.* 9041–9215 and 9305–9444.
- *Greenberg version.* K17-GREENBERG-KERNEL (9678–9971; statement level) assumes only that X^+ is finite. It became the
