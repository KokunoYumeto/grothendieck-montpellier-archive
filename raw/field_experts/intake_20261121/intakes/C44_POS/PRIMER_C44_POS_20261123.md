## 1. The main theorem: fourfold axes whose every power satisfies the standard conjectures (no unrefereed input)

### C44-A13, closed rank-one axes `[TPC]`, `[COMP]` (lines 2627–2946)

**Statement.**
- Let E: y² = x³ − x, A = E⁶, H = Σh_i and Y = E² × {o}⁴.
- For t large, B_u = D₀ ∩ D_u in |tH| are smooth axes of genuine Lefschetz pencils containing Y.
- For infinitely many p ≡ 3 (mod 4), a Zariski-dense set of closed points over F̄_p has A_B = Qw, with:
  - w = [Y]_B − t^{−2}κ*α₀;
  - α₀ as in (A13.3), with H²α₀ = [Y];
  - ∫w² = 6t² − 1/(4t²).
- The variable rank is 3600t⁶ − 165.

**Proof.**
1. A blow-up of Y identifies the complement W of the ambient classes and w with the vanishing cohomology of an auxiliary
   pencil (A13.8–A13.10).
2. h^{4,0}(W) = 62t⁶ − 5 > 0, so the monodromy is infinite (SGA 7 XIX 3.4) and full orthogonal (Weil II 4.4.1).
3. The monodromy specializes at good primes (SGA 1 XIII 2.9–2.12).
4. Closed-point selection, C44-A12 `[TPC]`: Chebotarev in a finite quotient (Entin, IMRN 2021, Th. 3) gives Frobenius
   without root-of-unity eigenvalues on W. Rational coefficients come from the Gram matrix.

### C44-A28 and C44-A30, every power `[TPC]` (lines 27076–27466, 27722–28166)

**Statement.**
- On a Zariski-dense set of these closed axes, for every N, every ℓ ≠ p and every product polarization Σρ_i pr_i*h, the
  Hodge standard conjecture holds on B^N.
- So do Lefschetz type, D (numerical = homological), and Tate in every codimension over every finite extension, with rank
  equal to the pole order.
- Frobenius is semisimple, and the degree-zero correspondence algebra has a positive adjoint trace (cote 16, scan 049).

**Proof.**
1. Chin, JAMS 17 (2004), Th. 5.7. Its hypotheses are checked: pure, Q-rational, plain away from p, bounded slopes with
   denominators ≤ b!. It gives closed points whose Frobenius generates a maximal torus of SO(W).
2. Invariant tensors in W^{⊗2s} are spanned by pair tensors of Frobenius graphs.
3. Their Gram matrix is that of Tr_{E₀/Q}(a ā) on the product of CM fields Q[Φ]. **It is positive by the Weil conjectures
   alone.** Rational coefficients are recovered from intersection numbers, so neither Tate nor D is presupposed.
4. On the ambient part every class is a Lefschetz class of a supersingular power. Milne 2002 (Ann. of Math. 155,
   Rem. 3.7–3.8, the unconditional part) gives the sign, transferred along the lift by a positive-spectrum comparison.
5. The blocks are orthogonal.

## 3. Lifting the primitive class: excluded in a bounded ramification range

- **C44-A14 `[TPC]`, `[COMP]`.** For p ≡ 3 (mod 4) there is a smooth determinantal surface S ⊂ E⁴ with no lift to
  characteristic 0.

  - h¹(O_S) = 4.
  - Its top cup form on H¹ has zero infinitesimal stabilizer; we recomputed the rank as 64 of 64 mod 5.
  - A Hodge-circle argument then excludes any lift.

  model with ramification e ≤ 5 (p = 3) or e ≤ 3p (p ≥ 7). The chain A22–A31r behind this was not read by us.
