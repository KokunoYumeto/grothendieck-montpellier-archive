## Part A: Galois orbits of CM points (`<R>/workbench/schemes-and-moduli-workbench/papers/cm-orbits/main.tex`)

### A.ORBIT (Theorem 1.1)

**Statement.** Every CM point x ∈ A_g(Q̄) satisfies

  |Gal(Q̄/Q)·x| ≥ D_x^{1/(2g)}/(s_g C_g W_g log D_x) ≥ c_g D_x^{1/(4g)},

where D_x = |disc Z(End A_x)| is the discriminant of the centre of the endomorphism ring, under its own trace form. The
constants are:
- a_r = 2^{r−1}(75/2 + 16 r!), b_r = 4r(2^r − 1);
- A_g, B_g their maxima over r ≤ g;
- C_g = 241(eg)^{2g}g^5, s_g = 2·3^{4g²};
- W_g = g(A_g + ½) + (gB_g + 3g/2 + 1)/log 3;
- c_g = e/(4g s_g C_g W_g).

More precisely, if all geometric endomorphisms of A are defined over K, then
[K:Q] ≥ D^{1/(2g)}/(C_g W_g log D).

**Lemmas used:**
1. **A.ZEROS (Lemma 3.1).** For K ≠ Q, with β the possible exceptional zero in Stark's square of side 1/(4 log D_K):
   Σ_{ρ≠β} Re 1/(1−ρ) < (75/2) log D_K. *Mechanism:* at σ = 1 + 1/log D, every zero outside the square has
   Re(1/(1−ρ)) < 25·Re(1/(σ−ρ)), and Σ Re 1/(σ−ρ) < (3/2) log D because the archimedean terms are negative.
2. **A.HEIGHT (Prop. 3.2).** CM by O_E, [E:Q] = 2r, any type: h_F(A) ≤ a_r log D_E + b_r. *Mechanism:*
   - write −L'(0)/L(0) for L = ζ_E/ζ_F as a zero sum, by the functional equation;
   - Lemma 3.1 bounds all zeros but β;
   - Stark's Lemma 9 sends a β ≥ 1 − 1/(16 r! log D_E) to an imaginary quadratic subfield, where **Alpöge's theorem
     (1.1)** excludes it, so 1/(1−β) < 16 r! log D_E;
   - the averaged Colmez formula (Yuan-Zhang; AGHMP) then bounds the average height over the 2^r types;
   - isolate A's type, using h_W ≥ −(r/2) log 2π (GR14 Cor. 8.4).
3. **A.SATURATION (Lemma 4.1).** There is a K-isogeny A → B of degree ≤ I^{2g}, I = [O : R], with End B maximal (the
   lattice OΛ). Then h_F(A) ≤ g(A_g + ½) log D + g B_g, by Rémond's splitting, additivity, Raynaud's isogeny estimate
   and log I ≤ ½ log D.
4. **A.GALOIS (Lemma 4.2).** The Z_ℓ-algebra S_ℓ generated by the ℓ-adic Galois image lies in R⊗Z_ℓ (Galois commutes
   with End_K, integral Tate) and spans E⊗Q_ℓ (semisimplicity, double commutant). Hence D | ∏_ℓ disc S_ℓ.

**Proof.**
- Gaudron-Rémond 2023 give ∏ disc S_ℓ | d (Thm 1.5(2)), d ≤ Υ^{2n} (after Notation 1.2), and
  Υ ≤ 241(en)^{2n}n^5 k max{1, log k, h_F(A) + 3g/2} (Thm 1.8). So D ≤ (C_g k M)^{2g}.
- Lemma 4.1 gives M ≤ W_g log D. Solving gives the bound for k.
- Level-3 descent (Tsimerman 2018, Lemma 4.1 form) passes to the field of moduli at a cost of s_g.

### C.IHARA (`ihara/main.tex`; deposited as hexagon:2611.00939, odd primes)

**Statement.** For odd p with X_p^+ finite, in particular every Vandiver prime and every odd p < 2^31, the fixed field
of the kernel of G_Q → Out(π_1(P¹ − {0,1,∞})^{(p)}) is the maximal pro-p extension Ω_p of Q(μ_p) unramified outside p.
The paper also proves p = 2, with the real place allowed to complexify; the deposit leaves p = 2 out.

**Proof in outline.**
1. *Rational image.* Hain-Matsumoto: the image of H = Gal(Ω_p/Q(μ_{p^∞})) is dense in the prounipotent radical U_p.
   Brown: gr Lie U_p is free on s_m (m odd ≥ 3). A Levi normalization gives γ s_m = u^m s_m.
2. *Arithmetic input.*
   - Greenberg gives the following, via Wake-Wang-Erickson 2017 Prop. 2.2.6 and 2018 Cor. 2.1.3, 8.1.3(2):
     U = M^- is rationally free of rank one per odd character; the even components T_e[1/p] are cyclic; and ϖ is
     surjective.
   - Fukaya-Kato §7.1.10 then show that the even H² at every level is generated by cups of cyclotomic p-units, and
     the parity argument reduces these to plus classes.
3. *Lemma 4.1 (cup comparison).*
   - The plus part of the class-two quotient of H is an extension 0 → ∧²U → E → T → 0.
   - If the companion numerator C_e vanished on D_α = (1+T_1)(1+T_2) − α, then E would split over the α-eigenline.
   - A cochain identity (dk = p^b f ∪ g on H) would then kill a fixed multiple of every cup on the dual line, which
     contradicts cup generation.
   - So C_e is nonzero on every D_α = 0.
4. *Lemma 5.1 (pole separation).*
   - Write the Magnus expansion of H on a compact free basis (odd letters of degree 1, even companion letters of
     degree 2).
   - The length-two kernels of the even letters are C_e/((1+T_1)(1+T_2) − α)^k, after partial fractions.
   - For a kernel element g, take its first nonzero degree. Extracting the coefficient of the maximal pole pattern in
     Laurent variables S_j = D_{j,α} leaves a product of nonzero factors, a contradiction. So ρ is injective on H.
5. *Vandiver ⇒ X^+ = 0* (Lemma 7.1: trivial p-class groups persist up a totally ramified Z/p tower).
6. Hart-Harvey-Ong verify Vandiver below 2^31.
