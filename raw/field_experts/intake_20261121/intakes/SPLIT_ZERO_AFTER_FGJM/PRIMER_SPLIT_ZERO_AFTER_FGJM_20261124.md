## 1. Setting

For a commutative ring R, R_0 = R ⊔ {⊥}: the new element ⊥ is the additive identity and absorbs products; the old
zero e = 0_R stays, with e + e = e and e² = e (Faul-Goswami-Joubert-Manuell, Definition 3.6). Two homomorphisms:
ρ: R_0 → R (⊥ ↦ 0) and the support map σ: R_0 → B (⊥ ↦ 0, R ↦ 1). An inverse semiring S is, by Theorem 8.5 of
Caviglia-Faul-Manuell-Mesiti, a pair (Q, F): Q the idempotent semiring of S, F: Q → Ab lax monoidal, elements (q, a)
with a ∈ F(q). R_0 is Q = B, F(1) = R. A **supported zero** is an element (q, 0) with q ≠ 0.

  M_{l∨l′} = M_l + M_{l′}, M_l ∩ M_{l′} = M_{l∧l′}; so the fibre at an ideal I is ⊕_{p∈I} V_p with

## 4. Circuits (paper4/paper4.tex)

Circuits: fan-in two, every gate counted, variables as the only inputs. f_G = OR over edges of x_u ∧ x_v; τ the
cover number; P_C the formal polynomial (OR → +, AND → ×); Sup(G) the positive polynomials whose monomials all have
support containing an edge and such that every edge is the support of a monomial.

- **Prop 2.1, Cor 2.2** [checked]: R_0[M] = pairs (finite S ⊂ M, a: S → R), the inverse semiring of
  (B[M], S ↦ R^S); a circuit with +, ×, − in Z_0[x] returns its polynomial and the set of monomials generated.
- **Thm 1.2** [checked]: T_V = pairs (f, p), f monotone, p ∈ Z[x] with monomials supported on true inputs of f; a
  circuit evaluates to (f_C, P_C); Mon(f_G) = min_{P ∈ Sup(G)} Arith(P); syntactic degree = degree of P_C. The
  support criterion is credited to Jukna.
- **Lemma 4.1** [checked]: if sets S_i meet pairwise in at most one element, a monotone circuit with the outputs
  OR over S_i has at least Σ(|S_i| − 1) OR gates, AND gates and constants allowed. Mechanism: at the input that is 0
  on S and 1 off S, build a graph on S; an OR gate false there that joins two components is charged; a charged gate
  is true on two singletons of S, so it cannot be charged for another set.
- **Thm 1.1** [checked]: G = (X ⊔ Y, E) bipartite without a 4-cycle. Among circuits for f_G whose formal monomials
  contain at most one distinct variable of X: #OR ≥ |E| − 1, #AND ≥ τ, and the minimum is |E| + τ − 1. Mechanism:
  (1) core = gates whose polynomial involves two or more X-variables; a core AND is (core wire) × (Y-only wire), a
  core OR has two X-dependent operands, the base wires entering the core are x ∧ h(Y) (Lemma 5.1); (2) set the
  X-inputs to 1 in the base and run the core backwards: all sums OR over N(x) are computed with at most
  O_0 + M − |X| + 1 OR gates (Lemma 5.2), and Lemma 4.1 gives O_0 + M ≥ |E| − 1; (3) for an edge xy take a monomial
  with support {x, y} and the lowest multiplication joining x and y in a parse tree: either it involves only x, or
  it is a core AND whose rectangle A_g × U_g ⊂ E is a star; the centres cover the edges (Lemma 5.3). This contains
  the fleet's L-meta-647 in its fixed-side form and our meta.md 5.1.
- **Prop 6.1** [checked]: for any graph without a 4-cycle and P ∈ Sup(G) containing every x_u x_v: at least |E| − 1
  additions and τ multiplications (lower envelope, stars, Lemma 4.1).
- **Petersen** [checked by recomputation]: the fleet's 20-gate circuit has 13 OR gates; four edges have no
  quadratic witness; so #OR ≥ |E| − 1 fails outside the class of Thm 1.1 while the total 20 = |E| + τ − 1 stands.
- [run] Decoding over F_{2,0}: Bool/4 ≤ Inv ≤ 3·Bool and Mon ≤ Clean ≤ 3·Mon (prop:clean); with support decoding
  the size is exactly the monotone size. ψ and program transfer (prop:sign-quotient, prop:program-transfer); the

## 6. K-theory of the semilocal one-zero C*-algebras (paper3) [run]

boundary and the ideal; the full groups for two primes (torsion Z/2 exactly when both sign translations are
nontrivial); stable place balancing; coherent transition maps; the sequence
0 → coker ∂_P → K_1(N_P) → ⊕_p C_p → 0 is nonsplit exactly when |P| ≥ 2 (thm:universal-nonsplitting); for every

two-primary (thm:all-torsion; the example for order 4 is r = 3, q = 37, 61, 8941).
