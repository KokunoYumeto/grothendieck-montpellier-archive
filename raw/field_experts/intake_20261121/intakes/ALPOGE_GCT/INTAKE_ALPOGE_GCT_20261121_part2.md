## 2. What is new

- **P-F31 is the main result of the run so far.** I know of no refutation of the existence clause of Mulmuley's PH3 in
  the literature. The obstruction comes from a single family: ordinary plethysm with inner Sym³ and a growing number
  of variables. Its mechanism is an equivariant localization formula for Hilbert series at roots of unity (P-F23),
  applied to the unique stable point with an extremal stabilizer (P-F30).
- **P-F23 is a clean general tool.** It gives the leading coefficient at a pole as a fixed-point contribution
  e_Y / det(1−g | conormal), a Lefschetz-Riemann-Roch formula for graded rings. It is related to known formulas
  (Atiyah-Bott, Thomason's Lefschetz-Riemann-Roch, Stanley's pole-order results), but this exact form is useful and,
  as far as I know, not in the GCT literature.
- **P-F30** is new combinatorics; it is elementary but delicate.
- P-F35 rests on classical inputs (Salmon, Huybrechts).
- P-F45 restates Allcock-Carlson-Toledo and Laza/Looijenga (balls for cubic surfaces and threefolds, a type IV domain
  for fourfolds), so it is classical. Its use as the concrete "theta-lift" object of Alpöge's steps 7-8 is new as a
  bridge.
- **Against tonight's other runs:** no overlap with GCT_PLETH, which does not treat PH3.

## 4. How the findings bear on the open problems, and the attack they open

1. **What positivity GCT can still ask for, after checkpoint 05.**
   - PH1 (homogeneous) is gone: P-K1.
   - PH3 is gone in both clauses for ordinary plethysm: P-F19, P-F31.
   - The general-form SH(a), PH2 and PH3 are gone as stated: P-F14, P-F15.
   - What remains in GCT VI's positivity hypotheses is PH2 and SH for the irreducible-inner families (Q-SH-REVISED),
     plus shifted counting.
   - P-F12 (PH3 ⇒ PH2) remains true, but its premise is now false. PH2 must be proved directly.
2. **The exact obstacle for PH2 on the cubic family.** P-F34 reduces PH2 for this family to an estimate on the
   "pole-at-1" polynomial: limsup n⁻⁴ log₂ U_n < 1/24. P-F42 proves only log₂3/6 ≈ 0.264, against 1/24 ≈ 0.042. So the
   gap is a constant factor of about 6.3 in the exponent.
   **Next attack:** bound U_n through the Weyl-Brion fixed-point expansion of P-F44 (B_n = K_n + R_n). Make the
   remainder R_n explicit through the strata of the stable locus with large stabilizer: Fermat-type cubics and
   Klein-type cyclic covers. P-F35's n = 4 value (indices 23) is the first calibration point; n = 5 through the
   cubic-threefold ring (Allcock-Carlson-Toledo) is the next. A computation of the n = 5 indices from its Hilbert
   series would show whether the indices grow polynomially. That would be evidence for PH2, or the first sign against
   it.
3. **Alpöge's steps 7-8, made concrete.** P-F37, P-F41 and P-F46 identify, for n = 4 and 5, the actual plethysm
   multiplicity ring (cubic invariants) with rings of automorphic forms on unitary ball quotients, the discriminant
   being a Borcherds product. This is the first exact object on which a theta-lift statement can bear: unitary groups
   of signature (1, n−1) over Q(√−3), not Alpöge's definite groups.
   **Next attack:** compare Alpöge's iterated definite theta lifts with the Kudla-Millson / Borcherds lift on the
   signature (1,3) and (1,4) Eisenstein lattices. Ask whether "one order of multiplicity per lift" corresponds to the
   order of vanishing of the Borcherds form along the discriminant. P-F45 bounds this route to n = 4, 5 for this
   construction.
