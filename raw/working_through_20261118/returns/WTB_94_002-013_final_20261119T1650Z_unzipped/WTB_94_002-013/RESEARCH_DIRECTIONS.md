## 94.RD02 — Exact obstructions to zeroth-cohomology base change

**Origin.** The Tor criterion on scan 10 uses Z¹ where the nearest sufficient correction uses B¹. Merely replacing that letter leaves open what the exact obstruction is.

**His terms and our formulation.** Write K = [U → V], H = ker(d), I = im(d), C = coker(d). For any A-module M, with no flatness or finiteness assumptions, our exact sequence is

\[
0\longrightarrow
\operatorname{coker}\bigl[\operatorname{Tor}_1(M,U)\to\operatorname{Tor}_1(M,I)\bigr]
\longrightarrow H\otimes_A M
\longrightarrow H^0(K\otimes_A M)
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{coker}\bigl[\operatorname{Tor}_1(M,V)\to\operatorname{Tor}_1(M,C)\bigr]
\longrightarrow0.
\]

**Work and state.** Proved in `PROOFS.tex`, RD02, by the tensor long exact sequences for 0 → H → U → I → 0 and 0 → I → V → C → 0. Thus the comparison is an isomorphism exactly when both displayed Tor maps are onto. If U and V are flat, the two obstructions are Tor₂(M,C) and Tor₁(M,C); the certified counterexample in C11 fails through the first one. The second check, 16:11 UTC, independently recomputes the connecting maps and dimension shifting.

**Further prompt.** None is required for this exact two-term criterion. No unworked higher-complex generalization is added as a deliverable item.
