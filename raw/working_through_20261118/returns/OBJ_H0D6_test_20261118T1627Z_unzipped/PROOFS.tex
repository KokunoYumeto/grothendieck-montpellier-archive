\section{H0D6.1: field-base criterion (literature result)}
\textbf{Statement.} Let $k$ be a field and $A$ a Noetherian local
$k$-algebra. EGA $0_{\mathrm{IV}}$, (22.5.8), proves equivalence of:
(a) $A$ is formally smooth over $k$ for its preadic topology;
(b) $A$ is geometrically regular over $k$;
(b') $A\otimes_k k'$ is regular for every finite extension $k'/k$
with $k'^p\subset k$, where $p$ is the characteristic exponent.
This is cited as a literature proof, not reconstructed as an independent
proof in this run. The hypotheses are matched to the field-base version
of the question, not to every relative formulation in scan 20.

\section{H0D6.3: localization stability (literature result)}
Let $A$ be as in EGA $0_{\mathrm{IV}}$, (22.5.8), and let
$\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}A$. For each finite $k'/k$ with
$k'^p\subset k$, the ring $A\otimes_k k'$ is regular by (22.5.8).
There is a natural identification
\[
 A_{\mathfrak p}\otimes_k k'\simeq
 (A\otimes_k k')_{S},\qquad
 S=A\setminus\mathfrak p
\]
(with the image of $S$ inverted). A localization of a regular ring is
regular. Thus the criterion (22.5.8) applied to $A_{\mathfrak p}$ gives
formal smoothness for the $\mathfrak p$-preadic topology. This is the
argument explicitly given in EGA $0_{\mathrm{IV}}$, (22.5.9).

\section{H0D6.2: discrete topology}
No complete proof or counterexample is supplied in this return.
EGA $0_{\mathrm{IV}}$, (22.6.7)(i), proves that for $C$ of finite type
over a separable extension $k/k_0$, the relevant local formally smooth
locus for the discrete topology, the preadic topology, and geometric
regularity coincide and form an open subset. This is a restricted
literature result, not a proof of the unrestricted question.
