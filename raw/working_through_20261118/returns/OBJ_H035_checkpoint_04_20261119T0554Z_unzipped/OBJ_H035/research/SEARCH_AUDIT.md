## Source-matching cautions

EGA III_2 7.7.8 has an additional locally free presentation hypothesis;
7.7.9 promises to remove it. Hall Theorem E has a derived tensor product,
whereas the manuscript's higher coefficient functors use underived tensor.
Jaffe's definition in the 1994 version concerns functors on commutative
algebras. These are substantive distinctions, not bibliographic details.

Theorem 12 is proved directly here; absence of a matching title search is
not a claim of historical novelty. The finite-Hom-presentation category is
closed under composition, but substituting it for the larger manuscript
class would leave H035.39 unanswered. No assertion is made that the residual
completion question is a recognized open problem in published mathematics;
it is unresolved in this run.
