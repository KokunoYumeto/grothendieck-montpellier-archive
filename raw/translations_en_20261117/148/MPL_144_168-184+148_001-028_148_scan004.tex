That said, if we are given a
multimarking of $\partial X$, namely for
each $i\in I$ an $n_i\in\mathbb N^*$ and a
sub-\inserted{\unsure{$\mathbb Z_{n_i}$|$\mathbb U_{n_i}$|elementary $n_i$}-}torsor
$T_i(n_i)\subset T_i$,
\deleted{\unsure{the groups|of group}} that is
an $s_i\in T_i^{\wedge n_i}$, whence
$\deleted{\unsure{\uline{s}\in}}$
\[
\uline{s}\in\prod_{i\in I}T_i^{\wedge n_i},
\]
then the \unsure{multimarked} Teichmüller group
$S_{\unsure{*|1}}T(\unsure{X},\uline{s})$,
generalizing the preceding one, is
embedded in $\operatorname{SRT}(X)$ in a natural way,
by taking an $s_i^0$ in each $T_i(n_i)$, whence
$\uline{s}^0\in\prod T_i$ and
$ST(X,\uline{s}^0)\subset\operatorname{SRT}(X)$,
and we take the subgroup
\[
ST(X,\uline{s})=ST(X,\uline{s}^0).
\prod_{i\in I}\frac1{n_i}\mathbb Z_i
\]
where $\mathbb Z_i$ is the $i$th factor of
$\mathbb Z^I\subset\operatorname{SRT}(X)$.
