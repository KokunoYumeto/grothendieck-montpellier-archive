3) \deleted{Erasure} \inserted{Filling holes, or ``erasure''} of gluing circles.

This operation is relative to the datum of a closed 1-submanifold of $\partial X$, that is, of a subset $A'_l$ of $\pi_0(\partial X)\simeq A_l$ (set of free edges of the graph).
It consists in filling the corresponding holes, by means of the cones on the connected components of the boundary of $\Sigma'_l$.
At the level of the graph, this consists in erasing the corresponding free edges, with of course also the marking markers that lie above --- \deleted{but} the weights of the vertices remain unchanged.
