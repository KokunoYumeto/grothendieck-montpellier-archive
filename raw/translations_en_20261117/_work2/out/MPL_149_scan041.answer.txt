2. The question of full faithfulness.

Let $K,K'$ be two finitely generated extensions of $\mathbb Q$ --- is it true that every $\Pi_{\mathbb Q}$-homomorphism $\Pi_{K'}-\Pi_K$ comes from a field homomorphism $K'\longrightarrow K$? One is immediately reduced to the case where --- an algebraic closure $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ of $\mathbb Q$ having been chosen, whence a $\Gamma_{\overline{\mathbb Q}/\mathbb Q}$ --- $K$ and $K'$ have isomorphic subfields $k,k'$ (algebraic closure of $\mathbb Q$ in $K$ respectively $K'$), with \deleted{isomorphisms} \inserted{embeddings} $k,\deleted{k'}\longrightarrow\mathbb Q$ of $\unsure{\overline m}$ image, \unsure{whence} $E_K$ and $E_{K'}$ can be considered as extensions of the same group $\Gamma=\Gamma_{\overline{\mathbb Q}/k}$ by $\pi_K$ respectively $\pi_{K'}$. The question is then whether \uline{every} homomorphism from $\pi_{K'}$ to $\pi_K$ which commutes with the action of $\Gamma$, is induced by a homomorphism $K\hookrightarrow K'$.
