\uline{Conjecture B'} \unsure{Let|Let} $K$ be a field finitely generated over $\mathbb{Q}$ (or over a cyclotomic field, that will perhaps suffice), $\bar K$ an algebraic closure of $K$, $\mathcal U$ a Poincaré curve over $\bar K$, with completion $\widehat{\mathcal U}=\mathcal X$. Let us consider an action of $\Gamma=\Gamma_{\bar K|K}$ on $\mathcal U$, compatible with its action on $\bar K$, whence an action of $\Gamma$ on $\mathcal X$. This being so :

there exists one and only one fixed point of $\Gamma^\circ$ acting on $\mathcal X$

($\Gamma^\circ$, kernel of the cyclotomic character $\Gamma\xrightarrow{\chi}\widehat{\mathbb Z}^{\!*}$).

The difference from conjecture B, which this one strengthens, is that here one does \uline{not} assume that the action of $\Gamma$ on $\mathcal U$ normalizes the action of a profinite group $\pi$, acting freely on $\mathcal U$ in such a way \unsure{that|that} $\mathcal U/\pi$ is an anabelian algebraic curve over $\bar K$.
