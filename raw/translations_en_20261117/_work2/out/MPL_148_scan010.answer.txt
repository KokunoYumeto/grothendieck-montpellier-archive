\uline{SURNAME and first name of the candidate} : MOUFADDAL Mohamed

\uline{Title of the thesis} : ``CONTRIBUTION TO THE STUDY OF THE POSSIBILITIES FOR EXPANSION OF THE ATLAS CEDAR OUTSIDE ITS CURRENT RANGE''

\uline{Planned date, time and place of the defence} : 28 October 1983 at 14:00 at the Institute of Botany

\uline{Name of the thesis supervisor} : Mister GODRON Michel

\uline{Designation of the laboratory where the thesis is being prepared} : Louis Emberger Centre for Phytosociological and Ecological Studies (National Centre for Scientific Research)

\uline{Aanlysis of the work} : The object of this study being to define the possibilities for expansion of the Cedar (\uline{Cedrus atlantica} Manetti) outside its current range, it was necessary, after an analysis of the history of the Cedar's distribution, to specify the climatic factors responsible for the current distribution.

The regions where the introduction of the Cedar is possible, are determined from the climatic study of stations located inside and outside the Cedar's range, and by a comparative ecological and dendrometric study of a few plantations.

\uline{Approximate duration of the research leading to the thesis} : 5 years
