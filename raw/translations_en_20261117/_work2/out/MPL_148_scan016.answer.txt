\uline{Title of the Thesis} : ``SEXUAL REPRODUCTION OF HEVEA BRASILIENSIS (A HISTOLOGICAL AND EXPERIMENTAL APPROACH)''

\uline{Scheduled date, time and place of the defence} : 27 October 1983 at 9:30 - Lecture room number 9 - (Administrative building ) University of Sciences and Techniques of Languedoc.

\uline{Name of the thesis supervisor} : Misters Charles HEBANT - BONNET

\uline{Designation of the laboratory where the thesis is prepared} : Laboratory of Plant Biology - University of Sciences and Techniques of Languedoc.

\uline{Analysis of the work} : The genetic improvement of Hevea is hampered by a very low success rate for controlled pollinations.

The histological study shows that the reproductive process (gametogenesis, fertilization and embryonic development) proceeds normally. An exception is noted : the case of clone GT 1 whose male sterility is described. Fertilization takes place less than 24 hours after pollination.

The experimental study, carried out in Côte d'Ivoire, concerns the patterns of flowering and fruiting under natural and artificial conditions. The majority of the flowers undergo natural elimination within 5 to 15 days after pollination. Hormonal treatments (naphthaleneacetic acid, cobalt chloride) can significantly increase the rate of fruit set.

A physiological explanation of the low fertility of Hevea is proposed.
