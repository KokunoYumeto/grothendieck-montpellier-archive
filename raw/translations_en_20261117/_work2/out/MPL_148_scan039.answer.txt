Changing the basis $e_1$ to $\zeta e_1$ $(\text{where }\zeta^4=1)$
\deleted{amounts to changing \unsure{by}} \unsure{changes}
$\{\xi,\eta\}$ to $\{\zeta^2\xi,\zeta^2\eta\}$, hence $\mu$ to
$\zeta^2\mu$, which is therefore equal to $\mu$ if $\zeta\in\{\pm1\}$,
equal to $-\mu$ if $\zeta\in\{\pm i\}$.

The moduli manifold for the structures considered,
\uline{rigidified} by specifying $e_1$, is therefore
$\mathbb P^1\setminus\{0,\pm2,\infty\}$.
It maps into the manifold $M_{0,5}$ --- one should
look at the effect on the fundamental groupoids.
