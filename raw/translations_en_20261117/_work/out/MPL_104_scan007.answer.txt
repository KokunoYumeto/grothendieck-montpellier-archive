Let \(M\) be a category (``\unsure{models|models|models}''). We assume

(i) Every arrow of \(M\) is \unsure{monomorphic}

(ii) Every \deleted{\unsure{autom|autom.}}\inserted{end} of an element of \(M\) is the identity.

Ex : \(M\) a category defined \unsure{by} an ordered set

For every \(D\in\mathrm{Ob}\,M\), let \(\widetilde D\) be the set (ordered by ``inclusion'') of subobjects of \(D\). We identify \(D\) with the greatest element of \(\widetilde D\)

Let \(D',D\in\mathrm{Ob}\,M\), then the map
\begingroup\makeatletter\tagsleft@true\makeatother
\[
\begin{aligned}
\underset{M}{\mathrm{Hom}}(D',D)&\longrightarrow\widetilde D\\
u&\longmapsto u(D')
\end{aligned}
\tag*{(\(\ast\))}
\]
\endgroup
is injective by \unsure{(i)|[i]}(ii), its image consists of the set \deleted{\unsure{of}}
\(\underset{\inserted{M}}{\unsure{\mathscr L|\Phi|L}}_{D'}(D)\subset\widetilde D\)
of the subobjects \(\Delta\) of \(D\) which are isomorphic to \(D'\).
