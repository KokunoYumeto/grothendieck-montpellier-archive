1. Structure of (Gr)-categories.

Our terminology is that of Saavedra \handwritten{[\, ]}. We are inte\unsure{rested|rested|rested} in categories $C$ equipped with a binary operation $(\deleted{\unsure{x|Y}}X,Y)\handwritten{\mapsto}X\otimes Y$ (func\deleted{\rlap{xxx}\unsure{teu|te}}tor from $C\mathbin{\mathrm{x}}C$ to $C$) associative and unital up to a given isomorphism (satisfying compatibility conditions made explicit in loc. cit.), also called $\otimes$-categories AU (associative-unital). We say that such a category is a (Gr)-category if \inserted{\handwritten{it is a groupoid, and if}} every object $X$ of $C$ is ``invertible'', i.e. admits an ``inverse'' object $Y=X^{-1}$ (satisfying $X\otimes Y\handwritten{\simeq}Y\otimes X\handwritten{\simeq}1_C$, where $1_C$ denotes the unit object of $C$).
