\unsure{Lodève} \unsure{on}\unsure{.|,} 7.8.74\\
Dear Deligne,\\

Being perhaps prevented by my leg from \unsure{giving|arriving} a $1^{\mathrm{er}}$ \unsure{cycle} course in the $1^{\mathrm{er}}$ \unsure{term}, I will perhaps \unsure{in} its place give a small algebra seminar, and am considering doing it on the gadgets of \unsure{Mrs} Sinh, \unsure{possibly} \unsure{transposed} into the context of ``stacks''. On this subject, I \unsure{come} across the following thing,
% margin note written sideways
\margin{\unsure{I have not} \unsure{found} the \unsure{letter} \unsure{on the} \unsure{Gr-Stacks|Gr-stacks} that you cite}
which for the moment \unsure{remains} heuristic. If $M,N$ are two abelian sheaves on a topos $X$, and $\tau_{\leq 2}\mathbb{R}\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(M,N)=E(M,N)$ is the complex having the invariants
\[
\left\{\begin{aligned}
\underline{H}^{\unsure{i|\bullet}}&=\underline{\deleted{\unsure{\mathrm{Hom}|\mathrm{Ext}}}\mathrm{Ext}}^{i}(M,N) &&\text{for }0\unsure{=|\leq}i\leq 2\\
\underline{H}^{\unsure{j|i}}&=0 &&\text{if }i\notin[0,2]
\end{aligned}\right.
\]
there must be a canonical \unsure{distinguished} triangle
\diagram{\begin{tikzcd}[ampersand replacement=\&,column sep=small]
{} \& {} \& \underline{\unsure{\mathrm{Hom}|\mathrm{Hm}}}(M,{}_{2}N)[-2]\arrow[dl] \& {}\\
(T) \& \unsure{E|\widehat{E}}(M,N)\arrow[rr] \& {} \& E'(M,N)\arrow[ul]
\end{tikzcd}}
