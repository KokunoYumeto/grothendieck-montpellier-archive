\uline{Corollary} $p_B^*: \widehat A\mathbin{-}\widehat{(A\times B)}$ commutes with $\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}$
(\unsure{$d^{\circ}$} symmetric for $p_A^*$)

It suffices to take $\deleted{\unsure{M'=e_{\widehat B},M=}}\quad M'=M=e_{\widehat B}$. One finds
\[
\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(L'\boxtimes M',L\boxtimes e_{\widehat B})\simeq\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(L',L)\boxtimes e_{\widehat B}
\]
in particular, setting $L'=e_{\widehat A}$ and changing $M'$ to $M$
\[
\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(e_{\widehat A}\boxtimes M,L\boxtimes e_{\widehat B})\xleftarrow{\sim}L\boxtimes e_{\widehat B}
\]
--- one recovers here the \deleted{\unsure{can. arrow|arrow}} \unsure{the|this} canonical arrow
\diagram{\begin{tikzcd}\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(P,Q)&Q\arrow[l]\end{tikzcd}}
valid in tt \unsure{categories|category}, \deleted{\unsure{equipped with products}}, \deleted{\unsure{finite}}.

In the notation $X^M$, if one identifies $M$ with $e_{\widehat A}\boxtimes M=p_A^*(M)$, and \unsure{likewise} $L$ with $L\boxtimes e_{\widehat B}\simeq p_B^*(L)$, one thus finds
\[
L^M=L\qquad\text{for }\unsure{L\in\operatorname{Ob}\widehat A|L\operatorname{Ob}\widehat A},\ M\in\operatorname{Ob}\widehat B
