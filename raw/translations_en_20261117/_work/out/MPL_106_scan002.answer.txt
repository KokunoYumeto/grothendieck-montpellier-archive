Let $(C,W_e,\mathrm{cof})$ be a category satisfying \unsure{C1 . C3|C1 : C3} of \uline{Brown}, (\unsure{for mem. C0}).
\inserted{We assume that there is initial object $\varnothing$}
(E.g. we could have $\mathrm{cof}=\unsure{\mathrm{cof}_{W_e}}$). Then it is known that
\[
W_{\mathrm{Cof}}^{-1}\mathrm{Cof}\longrightarrow W^{-1}C
\]
is an equivalence of categories.

To see this, we are going to define a functor in the reverse direction (which will be quasi-inverse)
\[
\Phi:W^{-1}C\longrightarrow W_{\mathrm{Cof}}^{-1}\mathrm{Cof},\quad\text{i.e.}\quad C\longrightarrow W_{\mathrm{Cof}}^{-1}\mathrm{Cof}
\]
which makes the $u\in W$ invert.

For all $x\in C$, using C3 for $\varnothing\longrightarrow x$, we find
\[
\tilde x\xrightarrow[\sim]{\alpha_x}x\qquad\text{with}\quad \tilde x\in\mathrm{Cof},\ \alpha_x\in W.
\]
Let then $x\xrightarrow{f}y$ in $C$,
\diagram{\begin{tikzcd}
\tilde x \arrow[d,"\alpha_x"',"\sim"] \arrow[dr,"f\alpha_x"] & \tilde y \arrow[d,"\alpha_y"]\\
x \arrow[r,"f"'] & y
\end{tikzcd}}
consider
\diagram{\begin{tikzcd}
\unsure{\varnothing} \arrow[r] \arrow[d] & \tilde y \arrow[d,"\alpha_y","\sim"']\\
\tilde x \arrow[r,"f\alpha_x"'] & y
\end{tikzcd}}
apply the ``weak lifting lemma'', we find
\diagram{\begin{tikzcd}
& \tilde y \arrow[r,hook,"i","\sim"'] \arrow[d,"\unsure{\alpha_y}"',"\sim"] & \tilde y' \arrow[dl,"\unsure{\alpha'_y}","\sim"']\\
\tilde x \arrow[r,"f\alpha_x","g"'] \arrow[urr,bend left=12,"\tilde f"] & y &
\end{tikzcd}}
a lift $\tilde f$ \deleted{\unsure{as one of the}} \inserted{of $f\alpha_x$} \deleted{$f\alpha_x$} not to $\tilde y$, but to $\tilde y'$.

Consider \unsure{then|then}
\[
[i]^{-1}\tilde f:\tilde x\longrightarrow\tilde y\qquad\text{in }\mathrm{Hom}_{W_{\mathrm{Cof}}^{-1}}(\tilde x,\tilde y).
\]
I say that it does not depend on the choice of $(i,\alpha'_{\unsure{y}},\varphi)$. For two such choices, we
\diagram{\begin{tikzcd}
& \tilde y'_1\\
\tilde y \arrow[ur,hook,"i_1"] \arrow[dr,hook,"i_2"'] &\\
&\tilde y'_2
\end{tikzcd}}
\unsure{if necessary introducing} $\tilde y'_3=\tilde y'_1\vee_{\tilde y}\tilde y'_2$,
SPS that $\tilde y'_1=\tilde y'_2$, $i_1=i_2$, $\alpha'_1=\alpha'_2$, whence \unsure{we have two lifts} $\varphi_1,\varphi_2$ of $f\alpha_x$ to \unsure{$\underset{g=f\alpha_x}{y}=\tilde y'$}, \deleted{SPS $=$ \unsure{that $\tilde y$}} and we are reduced (writing $y'$ instead of $\tilde y'$, $x'$ instead of $\tilde x$, but $x$ cof)
\diagram{\begin{tikzcd}
x' \arrow[r,shift left=.65ex,"\varphi"] \arrow[r,shift right=.65ex,"\psi"'] & y' \arrow[r,"\alpha","\sim"'] & y
\end{tikzcd}}
\diagram{\deleted{\begin{tikzcd}
& \unsure{y'}\ \mathrm{cof} \arrow[d,"\alpha","\sim"']\\
x'\ \mathrm{cof} \arrow[ur,shift left=.65ex,"\varphi"] \arrow[ur,shift right=.65ex,"\psi"'] \arrow[r,"g"'] & y
\end{tikzcd}}}
prove that if $x',y'\in\mathrm{Cof}$, and $\alpha\varphi=\alpha\psi\deleted{\unsure{g}}$, then
\[
[\varphi]=[\psi]\quad\text{in } W_{\mathrm{Cof}}^{-1}\mathrm{Cof}.
\]
\margin{\diagram{\begin{tikzcd}
x' \arrow[r] & y\\
x'\amalg x' \arrow[u,"\nabla_{x'}"] \arrow[r,"(\varphi,\psi)"'] & y' \arrow[u,"\in W"']
\end{tikzcd}}
\diagram{\begin{tikzcd}

\varnothing \arrow[r] & \unsure{x} \arrow[d]\\
& y
\end{tikzcd}}}
If this lemma is valid, we find a functor
\[
\deleted{\unsure{W}}C\longrightarrow W_{\mathrm{Cof}}^{-1}\mathrm{Cof},
\]
for the compatibility of $u\mapsto\Phi(u)$ with identities and compositions is immediate.
