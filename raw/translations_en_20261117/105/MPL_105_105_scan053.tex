\deleted{Strong version}\inserted{Corollary of W7 bis} (\deleted{\unsure{W8}} apparently stronger form)
\uline{W7 \unsure{ter|bis}} Let $\mathcal X$ be smooth over $\Phi=\diagram{\begin{tikzcd}&a\arrow[dl]\\0\arrow[dr]&\\&b\end{tikzcd}}$, whence diagram (comm. in $\mathrm{Hot}_W$)
\[\diagram{\begin{tikzcd}&X&\\X_a\arrow[ur,"i'_b"]&&X_b\arrow[ul,"i'_a"']\\&X_0\arrow[ul,"i_a"]\arrow[ur,"i_b"']&\end{tikzcd}}\]
Then
\[i_a\in W\Longleftrightarrow\unsure{i'_a|i'_0}\in W\]
\[(\text{thus }i_b\in W\Longleftrightarrow i'_b\in W)\]
(NB This implies the dual statement W7' ter, with $X$ proper over $\Psi=\diagram{\begin{tikzcd}&a\arrow[dl]\\0&\\&b\arrow[ul]\end{tikzcd}}$)

Corollary of W8 bis (apparently strengthened form).
\uline{W8 \unsure{ter|bis}} Let $\mathcal X,\mathcal X'$ be smooth over $\underline\Phi=\diagram{\begin{tikzcd}&a\arrow[dl]\\0&\\&b\arrow[ul]\end{tikzcd}}$, and $f:\mathcal X-\mathcal X'$ a $\underline\Phi$-morphism. Let $f_a,f_b,f_0$ be induced on the fibres, and the conditions
\[\begin{array}{ll}a)&f\in W\\b)&f_0\in W\\c)&f_a\text{ and }f_b\in W\end{array}\]
Then two of these conditions imply the third.
