### Conventions used and choices made

- **The strict-flag sign over Σ(n)** (Σ^{<}(n) in the first pass, sometimes Σ^{*}(n)). On these scans it is one
  sign, drawn as an asterisk, often as a small x and sometimes as a two-stroke angle (e.g. 069 l.33, where it looks
  like <). On 069 l.19 it is introduced explicitly, with a clear star, as Σ^{*}(1), the complement of the
  identities. Hua reads `\Sigma^{*}` throughout. The first pass renders it `\Sigma^{<}` from its first occurrence
  (scan 048) and `\Sigma^{*}` at some places. Following the block rule (keep the first pass's conventions; where it
  treats one feature in two ways, use the treatment of the first occurrence), I changed no occurrence in either
  direction, and I used `\Sigma^{<}` in the text I added (#12). The sign needs one rendering decided for the whole
  cote, across the blocks 048-084, not block by block. Here it is a question of rendering, not of mathematics.
- "p.v." → "p.r." (#9, #15, #19): this corrects the identification of his letter r, not a convention. It agrees with
  the first pass's own reading "p.r." on scan 073.
- Abbreviations with a raised t are written `loc${}^t$`, `tot${}^t$`, as the first pass does for `tot${}^t$` on 063
  and 065.
