## My own notes (Claude, second pass), same kinds

- **M1 (074 l. 6 and 076 l. 15; reading, unpursued implication).** The same phrase "de façon évidente" occurs twice:
  the choices giving Σ_i, Σ_ij are to be adjusted "de façon évidente" (074 margin), and the transition maps between the
  Σ_{i_0...i_n} for cofinal inclusions of flags exist "de façon évidente" (076 insertion). He treats both as routine; the
  non-routine step he names is the arbitrary inclusions of flags, which need chosen retractions plus equisingularity and
  local triviality (076 l. 17-42). Edition notes and prompts that quote "de façon cohérente" (first-pass F12, N15, T04,
  T06, T09) should quote "de façon évidente". Confidence: high for the reading.
- **M2 (077 l. 19-23 and 083 l. 37-41; withdrawn-in-text check, unproved claim).** If the target on 077 is V_ij (C20,
  low confidence), his question "Pourquoi V_i ∩ X_j^* ↪ V_ij est une équivalence d'homotopie ?" is answered, as an
  assertion, by the marginal formula (69) on 083 taken with J = {i}, L = {j} (then J ≺ L iff i<j): V_J^L = V_J ∩ X_L^*
  ↪ V_J ∩ V_L = V_{J,L}. Neither page proves it. Confidence: medium for the link, conditional on the 077 target.
- **M3 (083 l. 33-41; numbering).** (69) numbers the homotopy equivalence V_J^L ↪ V_J ∩ V_L (for J ≺ L), not the union
  formula V_J^L = ⋃_{j≤l} V_{j,l}^{X'}, which is unnumbered. Cross-references to "(69)" in editions, companions and
  prompts should follow this. Confidence: high.
- **M4 (077 l. 31-34; reading).** s_ij is drawn as an inclusion (hook), like the hooked arrows of (56)-(57) on 078; the
  datum glued in (53) is the span of inclusions V_i ↩ V_ij → V_j, with b_ij the second inclusion. If Hua's ⇉ in (53) is
  right (U35), (53) names the pair of maps explicitly. Confidence: medium.
- **M5 (075 l. 28-29; reading).** The margin on the tubes is "OPS T_d = ⋃_{δ(i)≤d} T_i, où T_i est un vois. tub. de
  X_i dans X": a simplifying assumption he allows himself ("OPS", as on 080), not an observation. Confidence: high for
  the reading; the expansion of "OPS" is not written on the page.
- **M6 (073 l. 27-28; reading).** "(\unsure{donc c'est} vide si j est un successeur de i)": the trace of the
  intermediate strata in the boundary of the tube T'_ij is empty exactly when nothing lies strictly between i and j,
  which favours the index reading i<k<j over i≤k<j (U16) on mathematical grounds; the ink does not settle it. Own
  idea, confidence medium.
- **M7 (072 l. 31-33; reading question).** "on peut appliquer \unsure{le\|la} \unsure{sorite\|sortie}
  \unsure{du\|des} Ĩ = π_0(Σ)": the first pass's "sur" is excluded by the ink (the word begins with d). With "sorite",
  he refers back to the component construction Ĩ = π_0(Σ) of 067-071; with Hua's "sortie" the sentence would not
  parse as well. Confidence: medium for excluding "sur", low for the rest.
