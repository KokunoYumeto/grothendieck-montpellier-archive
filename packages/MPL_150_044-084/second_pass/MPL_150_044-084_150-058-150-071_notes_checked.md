## 2. Own notes (Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max; mine, not Grothendieck's)

- **O1 (scan 065, l.36; own idea, confidence medium).** With "=", condition c) of the Récapitulation is the Cartesian
  square Σ_ijk = Σ_jk ×_{Σ_j} Σ_ij, and the hexagon (41) labels both squares "cart". Read literally, the written
  b_ij^{-1}(Σ_ij) is Σ_ij itself (b_ij is the closed embedding Σ_ij ⊂ Σ_j of 065 l.21-22), which does not lie in
  Σ_jk, so the formula as written is not even type-correct. The intended s_jk^{-1}(Σ_ij) agrees with (41) and with
  059 (38). Quote the condition with s_jk and a note saying so, not silently. Reason: the identification on 065
  l.22, and the arrows of (41).
- **O2 (scan 067, l.13-21; own idea, confidence medium).** The two boundary statements on 067 fit together after
  correction. In the nonsingular case the boundary of Σ_ij is ∪_{k<i} Σ_kij ∪ ∪_{i<k<j} Σ_ikj (margin, #21). In the
  equisingular-nonsingular case the fibre-boundaries of the source map form ∪_j Σ_ijk, which matches 063 3° (the
  image of s′, (x≺y≺z) ↦ (x≺z)) only if the map is s_ik : Σ_ik → Σ_i. The source reads Σ_ik (#22). The label s_ij
  and the target Σ_j are overwritten and not decided. An edition should state the case for s_ik : Σ_ik → Σ_i and
  record the overwritten indices.
- **O3 (scan 069, l.28-36; own note, confidence high for the logic).** With "imm. rare" the argument for
  Σ_i ≅ Σ_ii runs: (a) Σ*_ii is fibred over Σ_i by s, so if nonempty, dim Σ*_ii ≥ dim Σ_i; (b) b : Σ*_ii → Σ_i is an
  immersion with rare image (condition b)), so dim Σ*_ii < dim Σ_i; finite dimensionality (condition c)) excludes
  ∞ = ∞. Step (a) needs the fibres or the local trivialisations to be nonempty over an open set. Step (b) needs a
  dimension theory in which nowhere dense subsets drop dimension. Both are standard for definable sets in an
  o-minimal structure, which bears on the cote's tame-topology question (Q9 of the first pass).
- **O4 (scans 061, 065; own note, confidence high).** The margin name "stratification cylindrique localement
  totalement ordonnée" (061, #11) is the same hypothesis as the NB on 065 (the Σ_<x totally ordered). The
  equivalence asserted in the 065 NB holds in the presence of the other conditions, not from b) alone.
  (i) If Σ_<x is totally ordered, then b) holds: two lower elements of x over noncomparable i, j would be
  comparable, and since φ is strictly increasing so would i and j. This is the argument written on 071 l.9-14.
  (ii) Conversely, b) only excludes lower elements over noncomparable indices. For y, z < x over i < j, the
  Cartesian condition c) (Σ_ijk = s_jk^{-1}(Σ_ij), see O1) puts z over Σ_ij, so some y′ ∈ Σ_i satisfies y′ < z < x.
  The margin's injectivity of φ on Σ_<x (065 l.13-17) then gives y′ = y, hence y < z. So the NB's "équivaut" uses c)
  and the injectivity requirement. Either way, N01's diamond example is excluded on these pages by stated hypotheses.
- **O5 (scan 070, l.24-27; own note, confidence medium on the reading).** "(correspondant moralement à un
  déploiement d'une stratification locale équisingulier)": with "moralement" the identification of these
  topological ordered spaces with deployments of equisingular stratifications is announced informally, not claimed.
  A companion should not cite 070 as a statement of that correspondence. It bears on Q7 and on the realization
  question (Q2) of the first pass.
- **O6 (scans 068, 070; own note).** Twice he replaces another word by "morphisme" for the map Σ̲ → I: 070 l.28
  (\deleted{factorisation}\inserted{morphisme}) and probably 068 l.21 ([morphisme) above "foncteur"). The canonical
  map to the component poset is thought of as a morphism of topological categories with a universal property
  (069 l.3-4), not as a factorization. This is a terminological lead only.
- **O7 (cote-wide; reading question).** The sign written `\Sigma^{<}(n)` by the first pass is a star on the page
  (see the convention note in the changes file). It matters for the edition: on 069 l.19 the author introduces
  Σ^{*}(1) as the complement of the identity arrows, and that is the same object as the strict-flag space Σ^{<}(1)
  of 048-067. A reader of the transcription would otherwise take them for two notations. One rendering should be
  chosen for blocks 048-084 together.
