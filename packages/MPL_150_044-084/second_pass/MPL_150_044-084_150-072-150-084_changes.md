## Conventions used (lesson 2)

- **Même.** The cote's first occurrence (072, c)) writes his m with a bar as `$\unsure{\overline{\mathrm m}}$`; the
  same sign on 073 (two parallel strokes after "lui") had been written `lui-m\textsuperscript{e}`; I used the 072 form.
- **Visible dash between indices.** The first pass keeps a visible dash on 044 (`\Sigma_{i_0-i_n}`) and 084
  (`(i_0-i_n)`), but wrote `\ldots` for single long dashes on 078 and 079. I followed the first occurrence (044): a single
  long dash is written `-`; dots and series of short dashes stay `\ldots`.
- **Wavy mark under an index.** The first pass wrote the wave under α in (49) on 075 as `\uline{\alpha}` and the wave
  under j in the next line as `\underset{\sim}{j}`. Here I did not follow the first occurrence: `\uline` is the prompt's
  macro for straight underlining, and the ink under α is wavy like the one under j, so both are now `\underset{\sim}{}`.
