**C1. Scan 023, l.4 and l.6. Reading question.** In "(dans la représentation régulière de 𝔖_3)" and in the display
X'_3 ≃ {e_3} ⊔ (𝔖_3 ∧_{{1,σ_1}} X_2^*), the group letter is crossed by an X-shaped stroke; in the display its index is
a 3 written over a 2. If the stroke is a correction, the parenthesis may name another group: for a 3-element group the
word "régulière" would be literally right for the 3-element orbits, which it is not for the 6-element group (B08). The
original or a better scan should decide before an edition prints either reading. Confidence: medium that the stroke is
deliberate, low on what it means.

**C2. Scan 023, l.18 with 024. Own idea.** The page claims no surjectivity of p^*, and the relations of 022-025 do not
force it. With m = |X_2| ≥ 3, the pairs (ξ, η) of X_2^* × X_2^* with η ≠ σξ must come from X_3^* (B11), and their first
coordinates cover X_2^*, so p^*(X_3^*) ⊃ X_2^*; nothing on these pages forces e_2 ∈ p^*(X_3^*). The single-headed arrow
is therefore the claim to edit, and any later argument that needs p^* onto must supply e_2. Confidence: medium, because
it uses only the relations written on these pages.

conclusion "pξ = e_2 ⟹ i = 1, Qed" (l.62) holds only with 2 there. If the ink is 1, the page has a slip; the edition
should print the ink and add this note. Confidence: high on the mathematics, the reading itself unresolved.
