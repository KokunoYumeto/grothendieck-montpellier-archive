## 2. Own notes (Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max), same kinds

**O1 — 092 l.14, (Strat 5), own idea (proof), confidence high.** With the page's "rare", the equivalence "X_i^*
dense dans X_i ⟺ (j<i ⇒ X_j rare dans X_i)" holds under (Strat 1)-(Strat 2). By (90), X_i^* = X_i ∖ F with
F = ⋃_{j<i} X_j; F ⊂ X_i by (Strat 2), and F is closed as a locally finite union of closed sets (Strat 1). X_i^* is dense
in X_i iff F has empty interior in X_i. If F has empty interior, so has each X_j ⊂ F. Conversely, if each closed X_j
(j<i) has empty interior in X_i and U ⊂ F is open in X_i and non-empty, take x ∈ U and a neighbourhood W of x meeting only
X_{j_1},…,X_{j_m}; then U∩W is a non-empty open set inside a finite union of closed sets with empty interior, which is
impossible (remove them one at a time: U∩W∖X_{j_1} is open and non-empty, and so on). This settles A10 and edition matter
g656 in his favour.

**O2 — 092 l.24, reading question.** "donc on aura, si i,j∈J" leads into (92) on scan 094, a statement about i,j∈I (J
first appears in (93), on the same scan 094). The J is presumably a slip of the pen; the transcription keeps J, the
editions should not silently print I.

**O3 — 091 l.4-6, reading question (terminology).** He first wrote a heading "Une \unsure{filtration} … de type I",
struck it, and defines "une stratification \unsure{ordinaire|indicielle}, de type I, de X". If "ordinaire" holds (Hua
batch-05; no "-ielle" ascenders in the ink), prompts and companion notes that call (Strat 1)-(Strat 3) a "stratification
indicielle" use a word that is not his.

**O4 — 087 l.5, (75), unpursued implication, confidence medium.** Why "en général c'est différent": J is an ideal of
I', so J̄∩I' = J and J̄∖J ⊂ Ī'∖I' = I'_0. Σ_{I'} = X_{I'} ∖ X_{I'} ∩ T̊_{I'_0} (086 l.53) removes only the open tubes of
the strata of I'_0, so for i ∈ J̄∖J the closed tube T_i still meets Σ_{I'} along its boundary Ṫ_i. Hence
Σ_{I'}∩⋃_{j∈J̄}T_j = Σ_{I'}∩⋃_{j∈J}T_j ∪ ⋃_{i∈J̄∖J}(Σ_{I'}∩Ṫ_i), and the last part is not contained in ⋃_{j∈J}T_j in
general.

**O5 — 097 l.6 and (100), self-flagged step, confidence low-medium.** The parenthesis gives the one-stratum fact
V^Z_{i_n} ↪ V_{i_n} (h.e.) for Z locally closed containing X^*_{i_n} (both sides are homotopy equivalent to X^*_{i_n},
cf. (94) and the margin of 095). (100) asks the same for V_{i_0…i_n} = V_{i_0}∩…∩V_{i_n}; this does not follow from the
one-index case by intersecting, since V_{i_0…i_{n-1}} need not be compatible with the retraction of V_{i_n} onto
X^*_{i_n}. A compatible system of retractions (control data, or a tame triangulation adapted to the strata) is what a
proof needs; this is exactly the input that A17 shows (110)-(111) inherit.

**O6 — 095 l.13 and l.24, (95)-(96), own idea (proof), confidence high for the mathematics.** (a) Whatever the second
arrow of (95) is, the converse holds: if X_i^*∩X̄_j^* = ∅, then X∖X̄_j^* is an open neighbourhood of X_i^* missing
X̄_j^*, so V_i∩X̄_j^* = ∅ for the pro-neighbourhood V_i. (b) For i, j incomparable, X_i^*∩X_j ⊂ X_i∩X_j = ⋃_{k∈ī∩j̄}X_k
(Strat 3) ⊂ ⋃_{k<i}X_k, which misses X_i^* by (90); symmetrically X_i∩X_j^* = ∅. So X_i^* and X_j^* are separated
sets, and they have disjoint open neighbourhoods as soon as X is completely (hereditarily) normal; a space is completely
normal iff every open subspace is normal. That is precisely "donc tt partie ouverte de X est normale", and metrizable
spaces are completely normal. This supports the reading "partie ouverte … normale" (row 37) and gives (96) ⟹ and the
"V_ij = ∅" of 096 l.3 for i, j incomparable.

**O7 — 098 l.12 and 099 l.11, reading confirmation.** The definition V_J^L = V_{J,L}^{X^*_L} is on 098 (exponent X^*_L
