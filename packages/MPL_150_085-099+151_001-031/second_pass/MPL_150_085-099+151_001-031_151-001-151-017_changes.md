## Conventions followed (the cote's first pass)

- **Overwritten characters** are given in their final, top form. This is what the first pass does almost everywhere, for
  example the δ bracket and the boxes of scan 003. Its one `\deleted{}\inserted{}` rendering of an overwritten index,
  p_{21→23} on scan 003 l.22, is kept. The doubt it put on δ_{3|2} (l.13) was a doubt, not a convention, so the ink
  settles it.
- **His "à" written as a colon-like mark** is transcribed "à", as in the first pass (scans 007, 011, 013, 014, 015).
  Scan 011 ("viennent : l'esprit") confirms this reading of the mark.
- **A raised t after an abbreviation** is written `\textsuperscript{t}`, as in the first pass's
  "extérieurem\textsuperscript{t}" and "moy\textsuperscript{t}" (scan 031). This gives "ex.\textsuperscript{t}" on scan 015.
- **The S/G group letter** keeps the cote's notation-table form `\unsure{\mathfrak S_n|\mathcal G_n}` in all 29 places
  of this block. The glyph is the usual handwritten 𝔖, and Hua batch-01 reads \mathfrak S throughout. The choice should
  be made once for the whole cote, not block by block.
- **E and 𝔼**, and the struck-cross deletion of Hyp 1′ on scan 011, are kept as the first pass gives them.
