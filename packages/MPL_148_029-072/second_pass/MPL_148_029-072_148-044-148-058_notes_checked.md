## Own notes (second pass, Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max)

- **N1, 044, notation decision not applied (reading question / unpursued implication).** The margin at 044:17-21 decides
  that the bioriented groupoid T^± "mérite" the simplest notation T and that the oriented one is T^+. The main text of
  044-058 keeps the earlier convention: T or T^+ for the oriented groupoid and T^± for the bioriented one (044:9, 044:37
  "T^+_top = T_top", 046:37-38 "S_*TD_top ⊂ S_*TD^±_top → [±1]", 052-053 "S_*TD^±_top"). Michel Hua's project notes the
  same (b3 p.43 note). So the decision is later than these pages or was not carried out; a reader of 044-065 must take T
  as the oriented groupoid. Confidence high (the main-text readings are clear).
- **N2, 049, A = A_0 in the bioriented case (reading question).** 049:38-42: "Le cas de T^±_top correspond à
  \unsure{choisir|faire} ρ = 0, donc la situation est donnée par A = A_0 ↓ S →γ N". The ink and Hua both read A_0. With
  the later notation of 054:11-12 (A_0 = the ordinary edges, π0 of the cutting circles) and of 055:6 (A_ℓ = π0(∂X), the
  free edges), the case Σ = ∅ has only free edges, so one would expect A = A_ℓ. Either A_0 here has another meaning, or
  it is a slip of his. Confidence medium.
- **N3, 054, π0(Σ') = A_0 (reading question).** 054:11 reads "une partie A'_0 de π0(Σ') = A_0" with a clear prime on Σ
  (crop s054_pi0). The construction needs A'_0 = π0(Σ') as a part of A_0 = π0(Σ \ ∂X) (the ordinary edges); as written
  the equality puts π0(Σ') equal to all of A_0. A slip for "π0(Σ') = A'_0 ⊂ A_0" is likely; the transcription keeps the
  ink. Confidence high for the reading, medium for the slip.
- **N4, 053 and 057, two strengthenings (unpursued implication / reading question).** With the polarity of 053 settled,
  053:40 says that erasing all the marks of a component C of Σ^+ is a strengthening of structure ("renforcement"), and
  057:11 says that the partial uniform erasure is a strengthening, "pas un affaiblissement, et inversement pour le
  surmarquage uniforme". So on these pages both total erasure on a component and partial uniform erasure are called
  strengthenings, while 053 calls the operation in general an apparent weakening. 053:41 proposes to separate the
  "essentielles" erasures, those that change the automorphism group, from the others. A check worth making against the
  recap of 061 (outside this block; the first pass reads "fortement irréversible" there): which erasures change the
  automorphism group, and in which direction. Confidence medium.
- **N5, 056, the first name of surmarking (unpursued implication).** The heading at 056:5 was first "Opération
  d'affaiblissement de [marquage]" and then "Opération de surmarquage". This agrees with 057:11, where the uniform
  surmarking is the inverse case of the partial erasure, that is a weakening of structure. Confidence medium (reading of
  the struck word).
- **N6, 047, free edges as "sommets" (unpursued implication).** The inserted gloss at 047:10 calls the free edges
  ("sommets", "trous|arcs"), the struck block at 047:60-68 builds S̃ = S ⨿ A_ℓ, the set of all vertices, "marqués" (in S)
  and "libres" (one for each α ∈ A_ℓ), and 047:100 strikes "ou ``sommets-trous''". So for a while he treated each boundary
  circle as an extra vertex (a "hole vertex") rather than as a free edge, before settling on free edges with an
  application A_ℓ → S (047:101). This is the graph-theoretic choice the later maquettes have to respect. Confidence
  medium.
- **N7, 050, the circular order as the source of p (reading question).** The struck clause at 050:52 reads "et p déduit
  de l'ordre circulaire dans|sur les R_a" (Hua: "dans"): before replacing the map p: R → A⃗ by the torsor family, he
  derived it from the circular order of the marks on each circle. The torsor under Z/r(a)Z ∧^{ω(a)}_{\{±1\}} is the
  same datum made orientation-free. Confidence medium.
