batch-03 (his pp. 58-60) and scans 062-072 in batch-04 (his pp. 61-71). His page number is our scan number minus one,
and equals the running number in the lower-left corner of each sheet. His text was opened only after the ink had

evidence column says so ("Hua b03/b04 p. N").
