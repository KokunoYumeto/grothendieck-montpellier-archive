## 1. Scan 063 and "the strata of dimension at most two determine the tower" (STRATA F.17)

The notes of this package cite 063 in R33, U26-U28, G01, Q2 (Γ11-Γ16) and Q3a-Q3d. To each of these they attach
**STRATA F.17**, "Proved, return claim", from the results register. The corrected 063 says the following:
- **063:4-9.** "La question essentielle est donc comment reconstituer, pour un graphe pondéré à repères (disons)
  Γ̲, le groupoïde 𝒯^±_top(Γ̲), avec sa dépendance fonctorielle en Γ̲, et l'explicitation des sept opérations, à
  l'exception de 4°) et 5°)." The word "groupoïde" was missed by the first pass and is restored (ch. 22).
- **063:10, margin.** The decorations S and D are dropped here, being contained in Γ̲.
- **063:12-14.** Operation 4°) "sera obtenue automatiquement" from the explicit description of the 𝒯^±_top(Γ̲). That
  description is to be done at the same time and in the same way as for the [S?]𝒯^±_{g,ν} and S𝒯^±_{g,ν}
  "ci-dessus|connus" (ch. 23).
- **063:15-18.** Filling holes "pourrait s'expliciter" by special cases of these operations for S𝒯_{g,ν} and
  𝒯_{g,ν}, "qu'il faudrait jeter dans le sac des structures ``élémentaires'' [à déterminer | : diminution]".
- **063:20-26.** The functor (**), S𝒯^±_{g,ν,ν′} → S𝒯_{g,ν?ν′}:
  - both S are struck, with the margin note "avec ou sans S?";
  - the sign between ν and ν′ is unread (ch. 27, U-26);
  - the source is defined by a chosen subset I′ ⊂ I(X) with card I′ = ν′.

**What the page supports.** A programme, put as a question, to rebuild the topological groupoids over weighted
graphs with frames from the elementary groupoids S𝒯_{g,ν} and 𝒯_{g,ν}, of every type (g,ν), together with the
operations. Operations 4° and 5° are set aside. On the next page, 064:3, he turns to 5° (erasing cutting circles) to
reach lower genus.

**What it does not contain.** The page has no dimension bound, no stratum, no word "tour", and no statement that
anything determines anything. It is a question ("comment reconstituer"), not a claim. Scan 063 is therefore a
witness for the reconstruction programme. **It is not a witness for "the strata of dimension at most two determine
the tower".** A citation of 063 for that statement should be removed, or replaced by a page that says it. The nearest
thing in this package is the margin of 033, "dimension relative ≤ 1", recorded in Q1a. That page is outside this
block and was not checked here.

The same holds for the other scans of the block. No page of 059-072 states a dimension bound or a determination of
the tower.
