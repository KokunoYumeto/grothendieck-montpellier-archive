## My own notes (Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max)

- **Scan 17 l.19, own idea (consequential).** The Corollary's existence criterion, read « (w_iL^{n−i} − 1)π_{2n−i} » by
  both witnesses, is unsatisfiable at i = 0 once π_{2n} ≠ 0, as shown under 16:17:2. The true criterion is (6.3)_i for
  0 ≤ i ≤ n (scan 9 l.16-21); with it the Corollary follows from the Proposition of scans 15-16. Confidence high (a
  two-line computation from (1.4) and (3.0)).
- **Scan 14 l.44, reading question (consequential, routed).** « défini par (6.5) » or « (6.15) » decides whether the
  page identifies the contraction with the (6.15) pseudo-inverse (then false as soon as n ≥ 2: in degree 0 the
  contraction gives ΛL(1) = n·1, the (6.15) operator gives 1; R2) or appeals to the unwritten (6.5). Worth a look by the user on the facsimile or a second Pro reading.
- **Scan 15 l.58, unpursued implication.** With « comme au n° 1 » and « E ss-anneau gradué de End(M*) », the
  Proposition is a criterion for an arbitrary graded subring of End(M*) to be a Lefschetz ring with Λ in it: (i)
  Lefschetz + Λ ∈ E ⟺ (ii) a graded map Φ_0 → E sending L_0 to L and the model projectors to the π_i. The earlier
  reading « n° 6 » would have assumed the Lefschetz situation in the hypothesis.
- **Scan 4 l.17, reading question (routed).** If the margin reads « gr. ab. », the generalisation of the grading group
  is to abelian groups, which is what the formulas (sums of degrees) need.
- **Scan 13 l.5, (6.12).** The formula Λπ_{iα} = L^{α−1}w_{i−2α}L^{n−i+α}π_{iα} (α ≥ 1) is the unit-step lowering:
  L^{n−i+α} carries L^αP^{i−2α} to the top L^{n−i+2α}P^{i−2α} of its string, w_{i−2α} brings it back to P^{i−2α}, and
  L^{α−1} lands in L^{α−1}P^{i−2α}. It agrees with ⑥ of (6.14) (l.29) and with (6.15). Confidence high.
- **Scan 9 l.29, reading question.** « (1.1) et (1.7) »: no (1.7) exists in the text; the defining conditions are (1.1)
  and (1.3), as scan 17 l.14 says (« par les conditions (1.1) et (1.3) »). Keep « \unsure{1.7\|1.3} » and note it.
