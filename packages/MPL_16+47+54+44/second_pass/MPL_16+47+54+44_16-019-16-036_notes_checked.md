**C5, scans 25 and 27, own idea, verified.** The margin of scan 25 and the factorisation of scan 27 say the same
thing. Put F(x,y,z,t) = (xy)(zt) for x,y,z,t ∈ V.
- F is alternating in (x,y) and in (z,t), and symmetric under (x,y) ↔ (z,t), since H² is commutative.
- By anticommutativity the boxed identity (xy)(tz) = (tx)(yz) is F(x,y,z,t) = F(x,t,y,z): invariance under the
  3-cycle of (y,z,t).
