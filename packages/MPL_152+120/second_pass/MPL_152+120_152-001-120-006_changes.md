## Conventions followed

- Colons and "à": where the ink has a colon after a verb of identification or equivalence (152/003 l.77, 152/008 l.10,
  152/013 l.5, 120/006 l.46) I wrote the colon, as the first pass already did elsewhere in the package.
- His "même" is written `$\overline{\mathrm m}$`, as at its first occurrence on cote 120 (120/003 l.37); used in the
  struck line of 120/003 l.35.
- Overwritten letters (120/003 l.13 B over A; 120/004 l.38 M1 over a blot): the first pass of cote 152 records an
  overwrite inside a formula with `\deleted{old}new` (152/008 l.31), cote 120 only with `\unsure`. Since the earlier
  letters are not legible, I give the final letter and record the overwrite here.
- The vertical inclusion of 120/005 is set as an array with `\cap` under R_0^I; the first pass's vertical
  membership signs on 120/006 (rotated ∈ in tikz-cd) are left as they are.
- A superscript argument written by him (`\sigma_C^a`, 152/011) is kept as written, not as σ_C(a).
