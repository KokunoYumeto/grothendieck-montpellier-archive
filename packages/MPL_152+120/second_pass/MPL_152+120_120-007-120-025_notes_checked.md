**O1 (009, own reading-based note; confidence: reading medium, mathematics high).** The revised Proposition on 009 is
about finite sums: the struck line reads "E il y a des sommes finies", the live remainder "(moyennant (M5)) et X ↦ |X|
y commute", with the hypothesis changed from "R_0 ≠ ∅" to "Card R_0 ≥ 2" (changes 16-18). The circled M5 is the
finite-union axiom of 010 (the label M5 had first been given to "R_0 ≠ ∅" in the struck block of 007). This is the right
pair of hypotheses: X ⊔ Y is realized as X × {a} ∪ Y × {b} with a ≠ b admissible points, and that union needs M5. It
belongs beside A120-02 (scans 4-5), which records the same revision for the empty-set argument.

**O2 (015 against 009, own note; possible inconsistency; confidence medium).** 015 l.20 still says "Si R_0 ≠ ∅ (idem
dans Esp_M* il y a sommes disjointes finies) il y a des sommes disjointes \unsure{(infinies !)|(en principe !)} dans
Esp→_M*". After 009, finite sums need Card R_0 ≥ 2; with R_0 = {e} the construction X × {a} ∪ Y × {b} is not available,
and these pages do not show that finite sums exist then. Either 015 was written before the revision on 009, or "R_0 ≠ ∅"
there stands for the revised hypothesis. With "idem" (change 36) the parenthesis is a parallel statement for Esp_M*,
not an equivalence "R_0 ≠ ∅ i.e. finite sums".
