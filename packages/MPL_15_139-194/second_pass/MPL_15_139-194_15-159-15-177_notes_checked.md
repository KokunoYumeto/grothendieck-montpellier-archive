## Notes of the second pass (Grothendieck's claims, checked)

- **Scan 159 (second pass).** The abelianized Galois group of K is described as an extension of Pic(A) by A*(Ẑ)/E⁰.
  The sweep-3 reader corrects this mathematically to an extension of the narrow class group Pic⁺(A) by
  Ô*/closure(E⁰). His second formula, (A*(Ẑ×R*))/A*(Z) divided by the connected component, is the version with
  Pic(A). The ink is as transcribed.
- **Scan 165 (second pass).** With the ½-linéaire reading, his condition is the standard one for a real place. The
  Hodge structure on V ⊗ C is to be stable under the antilinear involution frob_ξ ⊗ conj, which fixes H_ξ(V)
  pointwise.
- **Scan 168 (second pass).** His inequality "0 ≥ r₁ + 2r'₂" holds for every degree p ≥ 2. From r₁ + 2r₂ =
  p(r'₁ + 2r'₂) and r₁ + r₂ = r'₁ + r'₂ one gets 0 = r₁ + (p−2)r'₁ + 2(p−1)r'₂. Only the left-over
  "[K:K'] = 2" is inconsistent with his revision.
- **Scan 173 (second pass).** The margin "H^i(Spec Z, Q(n)) ⥲ H^i(U, Q(n)) si ... n ≠ 0" is right only for n ≠ 1.
  The localization terms H^{i−2}(x, Q(n−1)) vanish exactly when n ≠ 1. At n = 1 the units of U enter, as his own
  scan 174 computes under "Si n = 1". So scan 174's "Si n ≠ 1" is the corrected form of scan 173's margin.
- **Scan 174 (second pass).** "H^i(X, Q(1)) = 0 sauf pour i = 3, où on trouve Q !" is his fundamental-class
  computation for Spec Z; the sweep-3 reader identifies the theory with Weil-étale cohomology.
- **Scan 176 (second pass).** Proposition 5.4 follows from H¹(X, Div_X) = 0 through the sequence
  0 → G_m → R*_X → Div_X → 0. Its Corollary 5.5 (Br(X) → Br(K) injective, for X regular enough) is the known
  injectivity of the Brauer group into that of the function field. This is in the published "Le groupe de
  Brauer II", §1; I have not opened it to check the exact number.
