## Notes of the second pass (Grothendieck's claims, checked)

- **Scan 179 (second pass).** The argument is the one that 15:176 (block 1) uses. The Leray spectral sequence for
  y → X shows that H^p(X, R*_X) is torsion for p > 0, and (5.1.) 0 → G_m → R*_X → Div_X → 0 then gives
  H^i(X, Div_X) → H^{i+1}(X, G_m) bijective modulo torsion for i ≥ 1. The margin "donner le corollaire 5.4." ties the
  page to Proposition 5.4 and Corollary 5.5 of 15:176. This confirms the block-1 reading "H¹(X, Div_X) = 0" against the
  first pass's "D⁰|Z⁰".
- **Scan 183 (second pass).** "d'où un gpe \inserted{physique} G_R": he calls the group attached to a chosen ℝ-point
  motivique the physical group. The classes ζ(s') ∈ H¹(R, G^or) and the homomorphisms u(s') are the archimedean data
  of a Chebotarev-type distribution of Frobenius elements in motivic Galois groups (the cover on 182).
- **Scan 185 (second pass).** His claim that "l'ens des polarisations d'un gpe réel H, défini en termes d'hom
  μ_{2,C} → H_C, est fini" is right in the form he restricts it to, the commutative case. For a group of
  multiplicative type, the homomorphisms μ₂ → H_C up to the relevant equivalence form a finite set, because
  Hom(μ₂, T) = X_*(T)/2 is finite for a torus T. His margin "? à vérifier" stands for the general case. I have not
  re-derived the noncommutative statement.
- **Scan 187 (second pass).** "les frobenius f(s') ... ont la vertu que leurs valeurs propres ... sont des entiers
  algébriques": the Frobenius eigenvalues on effective motives are algebraic integers (Deligne, Weil I). The cone of
  effectivity "engendré par les rayons définis par les v(s')" is his formulation of that integrality in analytic form.
- **Scan 191 (second pass).** The program is the fundamental pro-torsor (T_α) of motivic fibre functors, with three
  realizations: ℓ-adic points over the henselization, crystalline points over W_p(k) ("DeRham-Hodge"), and the
  reduction over k. His expectation, "T_∞(k) = lim T_α(k) n'a qu'un élément à iso près, celui donné par la théorie de
  DeRham-Hodge", sits with the motivic gerbe of F_p (15:2-14) and its crystalline point (15:3). The margin "F et V
  ½-linéaires" uses the same ½ sign as 15:165 (block 1).
