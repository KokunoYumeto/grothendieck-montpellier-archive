**SP-8 (scans 101-103; own idea, conjecture refinement).** In residue characteristic 0, condition e) of the
"Conjecture-à-tout-hasard" holds as soon as U_η has potentially good reduction as a pointed curve, so e) cannot be equivalent to
a) (good reduction); the natural candidate is "potentially good reduction". Proof of this direction: if U_η acquires good
reduction over K' = K(t^{1/n}), the Kummer splitting of (30) fixing a compatible system of roots of t maps ℰ_k into ℰ_{K'},
where the outer action is that of the special fibre of the good model; any other splitting differs by a cocycle with values in
T, which acts through μ_n by automorphisms of that special fibre, so the pulled-back outer action is that of a twisted form over
k: geometric (RN103-03-04 are the case n = 2). The converse is open here: when the stable reduction has a cycle in its dual
graph, the weight argument of RN103-05 (a weight-0 constituent in H_1) applies; when it is of compact type, the residual action
on every graded piece of the lower central series of the pro-ℓ π is pure of the geometric weight (Gr^1 = H_1 is the H_1 of the
good reduction of the Jacobian, and the graded Lie algebra is generated in degree 1), so weights alone cannot decide, and an
invariant of the extension classes would be needed. Confidence: high for the "potentially good ⇒ e)" direction and for the
purity statement; no claim for the converse.
