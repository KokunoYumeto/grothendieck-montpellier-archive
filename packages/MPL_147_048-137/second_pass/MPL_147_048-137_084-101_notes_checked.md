**O4. Scans 091–092; kind: unproved claim, checked.** "Le m.graphe complet de X' se déduit de celui de X, en prenant
la = application genre, et I'→S induit par I→S" (the last word of 091 is "m.graphe", not "marquage"). Keeping the
genus function is right when g(s) is the geometric genus of the normalized component, the stable-graph convention:
normalizing a node, whether it joins two components or is a self-node of one, does not change the normalization of
any component, while the arithmetic genus of the curve drops by one for each non-separating node. With arithmetic
genera of components the claim would fail at loops. Confidence: high; reason: the normalization of each component
is untouched by partial normalization at nodes.
