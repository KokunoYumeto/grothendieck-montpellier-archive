## 2. Claude's own notes (Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max), labelled as mine

- **C1 (009 L20-38), reading consequence, confidence medium.** With h = g l₁^q, the commutation with σ∞ gives directly
  the condition "∃q tel que g l₁^q σ∞(g) ∈ L₀" (L38). On 010 L4 this is written g l₁^q σ∞(g) = l₀^r, and in the margin
  as σ∞(g⁻¹) = l₀^β g l₁^γ with β = γ (q = −r). The parametrization of the σ∞-condition is one q throughout 009-012.
  There is no hidden reparametrization by p.
- **C2 (003 L2), hypothesis, confidence medium.** The statement on 003 is about "autom. à lacets" of π̂_{0,3}
  commuting with σ₀ and commuting outerly with ρ, so (1) and (2) describe automorphisms preserving the loop structure.
  This matches 005 L31 ("en respectant la structure des lacets") and 008 L16 (NB: "u automatiquement respecte la
  structure à lacets"). The first pass's "inerts" should not be used in any companion or prompt.
- **C3 (008 L2; 010 L4; 009 L20; 011 L15-17), reading question, confidence high for the ink, open for the intent.**
  The star is in the ink in "α ∈ Ẑ*" (008) and "q,r ∈ Ẑ*" (010). Elsewhere the same parameters range over Ẑ: "q ∈ Ẑ"
  on 009 L20, and Ẑ acting by φ(α) on 011. For the gauge λ = l₀^α ∈ L₀, any α ∈ Ẑ is admissible. A companion should
  quote the star as written, and not infer that the gauge or (q, r) are restricted to units.
- **C4 (004 L13-34), unpursued implication, confidence medium.** With "compatible avec φ", the cancelled draft defines
  E_φ = {u ∈ Aut(π) | ∃ u' ∈ Aut(π') with u'φ = φu}, the stabilizer of φ, together with the map u ↦ u' (unique by
  "∃!" in L15 when φ is onto). This is the natural domain on which a filling map would be defined, if φ were the
  filling surjection. The page gives no types, so it supports IMPL P3 only as the abstract set-up (compare A04 and F01).
- **C5 (012 L17), context, confidence high.** "On a des plus jolis générateurs de ... l'extension de S₃ par π dans
  Aut_lac(π)": the εᵢ are introduced as better generators of the extension, the six generators being l₀, l₁, l∞, σ₀,
  σ₁, σ∞. The page makes no claim of "more geometric views"; geometry enters with the "Critique des notations" of 015.
- **C6 (006 L33), reading question, confidence high for the arithmetic, open for the ink.** The rotation of order 3,
  z ↦ 1/(1−z) (016 margin), has the two fixed points −j and −j̄ (roots of z² − z + 1). If the insertion reads "±j"
  (Hua batch-01), the page names a wrong pair, and that is a correction for a research note, not for the text. If it
  reads −j̄ or −j, it names one of the two. LM_140-3 (13)-(21) uses P = −j̄ (first-pass note GP).
- **C7 (013 L45-51), reading consequence, confidence medium.** The struck "? Non !" line has twelve factors: 3 + 4 +
  1 + 4, the expansion at α = 2. The "OK" line has eight factors that all cancel: the expansion at α = 1. The final
  system (L54-58) has ε∞ = l₀σ∞l∞, which is α = 1. So the margin "prends α = 2|1 ?" records a trial at α = 2, rejected
  by "Non !", followed by α = 1. This is evidence for the order of the trial only. It does not decide the overwritten
  digit in the margin, which stays unresolved.
