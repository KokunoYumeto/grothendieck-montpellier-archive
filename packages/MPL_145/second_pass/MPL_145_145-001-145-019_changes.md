  text was retyped.

  have no change.

our scans 2-21; batch 2 is scans 22-38). Where his reading settles or supports a place, the evidence column says
"Hua batch-01 reads ...", so that the credit is kept. His text is a witness and was never copied as a basis.

Independence of the readings on scans 2-4: the images of scans 1-8 dropped out of my view before my readings were
written down, and his batch-01 had been opened for scans 2-4 in between. My second reading of 2-4 was therefore made
with his text known. Those readings were checked against the ink, but where they agree with his, they are not
independent of it.

notation table):
