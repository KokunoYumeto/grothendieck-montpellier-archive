# MPL_145, scans 20–38: changes of the second pass

Second pass by Claude (Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max), 17 Nov 2026. It checks the first pass by GPT-6 Astra (ChatGPT Pro mode) against the scan images: both halves of every scan, plus enlarged and rotated crops of 58 hard places (`second_pass/work/MPL_145_145-020-145-038/`).

**Second witness.** Michel Hua's transcription of this cote, from his project (`hua_michel/commutator_grothendieck-project/transcripts/145/`):
- `batch-01.fr.tex` covers scans 20–21, which are his `\page{19}`–`\page{20}`;
- `batch-02.fr.tex` covers scans 22–38, his `\page{21}`–`\page{37}`.

His page number is the archive's lower pencil number, which is the scan number minus 1. I read the ink first and opened his text only for the places still open after that. Where his reading settles or supports a place, the evidence column says so. His conventions and wording are not used.

**Conventions.** The first pass's macros and diplomatic conventions are kept. Two features are treated differently in different places of the first pass:
- **Struck superscripts over `g_i` (scan 24).** The first occurrence, scan 24, has `\unsure{\deleted{-1}|u}`, which offers a live `u`. The image shows each of these superscripts heavily hatched, that is, struck. The live alternative is therefore excluded, and I wrote `\deleted{\unsure{-1}}`, the form the first pass uses for the same struck superscripts on scan 25. This is a reading settled by the ink, not a change of convention.
- **Hatched exponents in the cancelled (8) (scan 20).** These are the same feature as the first occurrence on scan 19, where the first pass writes `\deleted{\unsure{-1}}`. I used that form.

**Line numbers** refer to the corrected file in `second_pass/out/MPL_145/`.
