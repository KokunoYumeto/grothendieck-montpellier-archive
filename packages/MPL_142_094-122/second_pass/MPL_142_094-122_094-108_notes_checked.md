## Notes added in the second pass (Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max)

**S1. Scan 095, first paragraph; kind: unproved claim.** The corrected sentence reads "Si on veut que l'action remontée
Θ' soit l'identité sur I', on a unicité, et la condition supplémentaire que Θ̃ doit bien opérer trivialement sur I'".
Uniqueness follows from path lifting only on the components of the cover that I' meets. If X' is disconnected over K̄ and
I' misses a component, the lift is not pinned down there. That is why 101 adds connectedness ("X' étant connexe", or
"K-connexe" with a rational point). Confidence: high, for the group-theoretic point.

**S2. Scan 096, first paragraph; kind: conjecture (correction of R09).** He conjectures that the necessary conditions
suffice "même sans entrer ds la considération des Θ^ν". So he expects (a) together with the rational-point form of (b) to
force Θ to come from Γ, with the Θ^ν refinement of 095 as a consequence. A test, which is my own idea: does the
rational-point form of (b), imposed over all finite extensions K'/K, give the Θ^ν form by passing to the field over
which x' becomes rational? The power Θ^ν would then play the part of an element of Gal(K̄/K'). This is the stabilizer
reduction mentioned in R08. Confidence: medium. The step needs the conditions to be imposed for every finite K', and the
text states them over K.

**S3. Scan 098, upper lines; kind: unproved claim, corrected by the author.** The written version said that the stability
condition "que je viens d'énoncer signifie justement qu'il existe une bijection (52) … compatible avec l'action de Θ
sur le deuxième mb de (51)". As stated, the equivalence is false (R18). The interlinear addition, that at two points of
Σ̄ conjugate under Θ the degree, i.e. the cardinal of the fibre, must agree, supplies the missing multiplicity
condition. With it the equivalence holds (R17). So the page records the error and its repair. The number in the
insertion, "(52)" or "(51)", is undecided in the ink, but only (51), the map Σ̄, has fibres of varying cardinality.

**S4. Scan 101, opening; kind: unpursued implication.** With "Si" and the name Θ' restored, the paragraph reads: if
I'∩X'_K≠∅ and X' is K-connected, the lifted action Θ' is unique. Condition 3 depends on this normalization ("on
peut trouver un Θ'"), and it is consistent with the revision in 3) of "tout x'" to "un x'∈X'_K∩I'". The margin's
strengthening to all x' with Θ'^ν x'=x' then needs a lift that is unique without a rational point. Its quantifier over
the lift (cf. R06) is not settled on the page.

**S5. Scan 104, opening; kind: unproved claim.** "la formule 54 implique ``la'' condition intrinsèque": the deduction
uses (54) for every horizontal component α, as the inserted "si … la condition sur x'_α,y'_α est vraie ∀α" requires.
It also uses the fact that f̂, restricted to the vertex group of T_α, is φ̂_α. If some u breaks the T-adjacency of
u(x'_α), u(y'_α), then (53) is undefined for that α and the deduction fails. The last paragraph of 104 states exactly
this as the hypothesis ("∀α∈π₀(X'_T), ∀u∈Γ, u(x'_α) et u(y'_α) soient T-adjacents"). Confidence: high, for the logical
structure.

**S6. Scan 099, Remarque; kind: reading question.** The ink "π₀₃" (a small o before the 3, no comma) names the group whose
"autom. à lacets" carry the parameters α, β, ξ, η. The text is diplomatic, so π_{0,3} appears only as an alternative
spelling in R23. The claim that conditions a) and b) are "indéchiffrables" in these parameters therefore concerns the
three-point group of 100, π̂_{0,3}.
