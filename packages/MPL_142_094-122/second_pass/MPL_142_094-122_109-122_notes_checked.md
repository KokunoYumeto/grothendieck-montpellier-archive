## My own notes

**M01 (scan 110, reading question and unpursued implication; mine).** The struck start of the top row, "π₀(E₀) = ~~E₀/π Ssgr~~ (", shows him equating the components of E₀ with the orbit set E₀/π, striking it, and starting "Ssgr(". If E₀ is a set of subgroups on which π acts by conjugation, π₀(E₀) = E₀/π is the set of π-conjugacy classes, and the right-hand arrow π₀(E₀) → Ssgr(Π|E)/π carries exactly that identification. Confidence low: the objects are overwritten.

**M02 (scan 111, own idea; mine).** ℙ(V(J)) ≃ ℙ̌(V(J)) is the self-duality of a rank-two module: V ≅ V^∨ ⊗ det V, so a line in V is a rank-one quotient of V^∨ twisted by det V = O(ω). The ω of "F(1) ≃ O(ω)" and of "O tordu par ω = ω(J), i.e. det V(J)" is that determinant, so the opening isomorphism and the twisted formulas below it are one fact, canonical only up to the orientation line, which is what "attention signes!" warns about. Confidence high (linear algebra; it uses only that |J| = 3).

**M03 (scan 112, own idea; mine).** On the corrected ≀: Σ_1^{\*\*\*} (monogon with its vertex, edge midpoint and two face centres removed) and Σ_2^{\*\*·} (bigon with its two vertices and two face centres removed) are both four-punctured spheres, but the identification takes the monogon's edge midpoint to a vertex of the bigon. It is an isomorphism that changes the types of the punctures; that fits ≀ here, while "Σ_1^{\*\*\*} ‖ Σ_n^{\*\*\*}/D_n" above keeps the types. Confidence medium.

**M04 (scan 115, unpursued implication; mine).** His vocabulary separates "redondances" (identifications among generators: objects in (7), arrows in (10), and later (14), (19), (22)) from "relations" in the strict sense, of which these pages have one, the face relation (12) (and its eliminated forms (16)).
