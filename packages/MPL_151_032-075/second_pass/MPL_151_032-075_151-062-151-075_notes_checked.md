### C-1: scan 069 (with 063-066)

**Kind:** own idea, a test of the theorem as stated.

**The example.** Take X=ℝ², I={0<1<2}, X_0={0}, X_1=ℝ×{0}, X_2=ℝ².
- The four axioms hold (non-strict (d)).
- The open strata are nonempty and dense in their pieces.
- The successor pairs are (0,1) and (1,2).

**The diagram of type Ĩ (065 (19), 069 l.3):**
X_0^*→V_{0,1}←V^*_{0,1}→X_1^*→V_{1,2}←V^*_{1,2}→X_2^*.
- It contains no tube of the plane along the point.

**A family of sheaves over this diagram that comes from no sheaf on ℝ²:**
- the constant sheaf ℤ on every piece except V_{0,1};
- on the germ V_{0,1} of the line at 0: stalk ℤ, with specialization 1↦(1,0) into
  Γ(V^*_{0,1},ℤ)=ℤ² (two half-line germs).

**Why it comes from no sheaf on ℝ².** A sheaf G on ℝ² with G|ℝ²∖0≅ℤ specializes through Γ(D∖0,ℤ)=ℤ. The punctured
disc is connected, so the image in ℤ² is diagonal.

**Consequence.** The colimit topos (whose sheaves are such families) is not Sh(X), and "X s'identifie au topos Lim"
fails for the simplest flag point ⊂ line ⊂ plane.

**When it holds.** For index sets of height 1 the same computation gives exactly the gluing data of Sh(X).

**What the statement needs.** Tubes of non-successor pairs and the triple germs V^j_{i,k}. These are the stages his
061 programme lists, and "1ère version" (069 l.3) signals the provisional status.

**Confidence:** high for the sheaf computation; medium that it is a fair reading of his "Lim" (if his Lim is a colimit
of topoi along the displayed arrows, it applies).

**Bears on:** 069-01, STRATA C.3/C.5, BISITE D.12.

### C-3: scans 071, 072, 075

**Kind:** own idea, the reason behind "mais pas (… une imm. ouverte !)".

**Closed restriction.** For a closed X'⊂X, every neighbourhood W of Y∩X' in Z∩X' is the trace of the neighbourhood
W∪(Z∖X') of Y in Z. So tube germs commute with closed (more generally proper) base change, as 071 claims.

**Open restriction.** For an open U this fails. Take Z=ℝ², Y=ℝ×{0}, U=ℝ²∖{0}, and W={(x,y)∈U : |y|<x²}. W is a
neighbourhood of Y∩U in U, but no neighbourhood N of Y has N∩U⊂W: points (x,2x²) lie in every N∩U.

**Where the same mechanism appears:**
- in the 072 margin, in its "élémentaires" reading;
- behind the doubt on 075 l.9 whether the inclusions (17) V_{i',j',X'}↪V_{i',j'} are even homotopy equivalences. Those
  tubes are germs along the closed pieces with the lower strata removed (069 cancelled proof: "\bar V_{i,j} est un
  voisinage de X_i dans X_j").

**Confidence:** high for the example; medium for the link with 075.

**Bears on:** 071-02, 072-02, 075-01, BISITE D.4/D.12.

### C-5: scans 069-075

**Kind:** unpursued implication (own idea).

**What he announces.** 069 l.9: "un énoncé qui permet d'exprimer également les topos canoniques associés à la
stratification, en termes du système inductif de type Ĩ".

**Closed pieces.** For a crible I', the stratified space X_{I'} has as elementary pieces those of X indexed by I'
(073 (12)), and its diagram is the full subdiagram of Ĩ on I' (074 l.3: "contenu dans Ĩ"). Since a closed immersion
is proper, the 071 base change applies. So the version for the closed pieces X_{I'} reduces formally to the theorem for
X_{I'}.

**Locally closed pieces.** For U_{I',I''} the reduction breaks at exactly the point of C-3 (075 l.9).

**Consequence.** Any proof of the generalization therefore has to treat the open restriction separately. His "C) Les
V^{I'''}_{I',I''}" (075 l.13) starts there.

**Confidence:** medium.
