## Conventions followed

1. **The hooked script capital.** One letter shape (hook at top left, bar, stem curving left at the foot) is read by
   the first pass three ways: `\tau` for the E-set (019–027, fixed in its notation table), `\mathcal T` for the
   modular topoi (027–028) and `\Gamma` for the indexed groups (029–033). The Galois group Γ is drawn differently on the
   same pages (upright stem and bar, no hook: 030 L5 top row, 031 L24, 033 L8–16). For the indexed groups of 029–033 I
   use `\mathcal T`, the first pass's treatment of the first indexed occurrence of this letter (`\mathcal T_{0,\nu}`,
   027 L7). The E-set letter of 019–027 is left `\tau`, as the first pass's notation table fixes it; Hua reads it as the
   same script T ("the same script letter on the page", b2 header). That is an edition-wide decision for the editors,
   not taken here.
2. **Overwritten signs.** The first occurrence in this block (027 L9) gives a covered sign as `\deleted{\unsure{…}}`
   before the final sign. Where the covered sign cannot be read at all (the 3 of 028 L6; the equation labels of 029
   L9, L16, L22), nothing is added; the place is listed as unresolved.
3. **Insertions** go where the caret or guide line points (026 L3, 028 L3). The operator name keeps the first pass's
   `\operatorname{…}` form after the change `Autextloc` → `Autextlac`.
4. **Arrows.** The first pass's dash/arrow choices in the diagrams are kept, except where it had marked a single stroke
   doubtful (019 L10).
