## 3. Own notes (Claude second pass)

**C1 — 015, own idea (composition convention). Confidence medium.**
- Both revisions on this page go the same way:
  - the struck product λ₀λ₁λ₂λ₃ = 1 is replaced by λ₃λ₂λ₁λ₀ = 1;
  - the struck second component (γℓ′)∘ℓ of the product law is replaced by ℓ′∘γ′(ℓ).
- Both move to composing paths right to left, as functions. A reader of 015 should combine the live relation and the
  live product law under that convention.
- The confidence rests on the live status of λ₃λ₂λ₁λ₀ = 1, which is medium.

**C2 — 015, unpursued implication. Confidence medium.**
- The data are π, the loops λ₀…λ₃, and ε with ε² = 1, ελ₀ε⁻¹ = λ₀⁻¹ and ελ₁ε⁻¹ = λ₁⁻¹. The base point is i, and
  ε: i ↦ ī (−i). In the drawings 0 and 1 lie on the real axis.
- Read together, they describe the fundamental group of a sphere minus four points, extended by complex conjugation.
  Loops around real punctures are inverted by conjugation.
- For a general λ_i the page gives only the recipe ε∘λ̄_i∘ε⁻¹ along the paths i → ī → ī → i (L70-74); the action on
  λ₂, λ₃ is left as « ελ_iε⁻¹ » (L58).
- This is the Gal(C/R) = Z/2 instance of the extension 1 → π → π̃ → Z/2 → 1 and of Bitinvol(π) ≃ Invol(Ens(π)) ≃
  Ext(Z/2, π) on 014 L10-19. The two pages belong together.

**C3 — 014/016, reading consequence. Confidence medium.** The structure isomorphism is written 1 → X∘X on 014 L4 and
F² → id on 016 L33. Before 014 and 016 are combined into one coherence statement (Fμ = μF), one of the two must be
inverted.

**C4 — 013, reading consequence. Confidence high for the reading; a standard fact, no novelty claimed.**
- With the element signs, 013 states the dictionary outright: the equivalence Hom_!(Ens(π), Ens(π′)) ≃ _{π′}Ens_π
  sends F to the biset _{π′}X_π, and E is an object of Ens(π).
- The Proposition (F left exact ⇔ X a right π-torsor) and the Corollaire (F an equivalence ⇔ X a bitorsor) are stated
  inside that dictionary.
- The first pass's arrows suggested instead a functor from the category to X.

**C5 — 010, unpursued implication and support for a reading. Confidence medium; standard facts, no novelty.**
- With σ₀, σ₁, σ₂ the presentation is that of the cartographic group, whose generators are also drawn on 011. Only ρ³ = 1
  is written; Hua notes the other relations are not listed. Hua's ρ_s would be the vertex rotation.
- With the entry 2, the column 1 → Π₁ → Γ°_{∞,3} = Sl(2,Z)/(±1) → 𝔖₃ → 1 is the standard sequence
  1 → Γ(2)/{±1} → PSL₂(Z) → PSL₂(F₂) ≅ 𝔖₃ → 1.
- Γ(2)/{±1} is free of rank 2: the fundamental group of the sphere minus three points.
- This supports « 2 » and « Π₁ » (a fundamental group, rather than Γ₁) on mathematical grounds. It was not used to
  change the ink reading.

**C6 — 004, scope of the char-p sequence. Confidence medium; standard.**
- The vertical equality π₁(k) = π₁(V) on 004 L14-16 needs V henselian.
- For henselian V and n prime to p, μₙ(K̄) ≅ μₙ(k̄) by reduction; without henselianity there is no such map.
- 002 L20 drops completeness (« V pas néces. complet »). The minimal hypothesis the char-p row needs is therefore « V
  henselian ». This sharpens N48.
