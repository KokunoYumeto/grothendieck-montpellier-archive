## 5. Own notes (Claude second pass)

**C1. 029–033 · notation and dating lead (own idea).** The hooked script capital passes from the modular topoi
𝒯_{0,ν} (027–028) to the Teichmüller groups 𝒯_{g,ν}, 𝒯̂, 𝒯̃, 𝒯' (029–033) on the page where M_{g,ν} is introduced
(029 L5; Hua b2 p. 28 notes that this M is written over a first 𝒯). If LM 140-2 scan 314 is where LM renames its
Teichmüller Γ as 𝒯 (as the first-pass context note reports), these sheets follow LM's notation after that renaming:
a dating lead (contemporary with or later than that LM passage), to be checked against LM 140-2 scan 314 itself.
Confidence: high for the letter, medium for the LM comparison, which rests on the first pass's report.

**C2. 029 L11 · the decorations (reading consequence).** With "Autextlac" and "classes de lacets", the circle names
the outer automorphisms that do not permute the loop classes attached to the punctures (pure), the double circle adds
"stricts … induisant l'identité sur T = Tate(Q̄)" (029 L18), and "+" (031 L17) the oriented, marking-permuting
version. These are the standard pure and positive conditions on outer automorphisms of a surface group preserving the
set of cuspidal classes; the names "loc"/"Cartan" in the first-pass notes should not be quoted. Bears on WG2 §1.8 #5
and the convention of BISITE F.15 (as N31). Confidence: high, given the ink of 029 L11 and 031 L9.

**C3. 032 L2 · the literal question has a negative answer in general (own idea).** For the pure orientation-preserving
group of 029 L22, conjugation by a mapping class that permutes the punctures is an automorphism, and it is not inner as
soon as the centralizer of the pure group in the full group is trivial; likewise conjugation by an orientation-reversing
class. So "Autext(𝒯_{g,ν}) = 1 ?" fails in general, N37's (0,4) case being one instance; 031 L20 ("S_ν opère
fidèlement sauf g = 0, ν ≤ 4") points the same way. The question presumably means outer automorphisms modulo these,
which is the content of the NB of 031 L20 ("G_{g,ν} = Im S_ν ?"). Literature from memory, to be checked before citing:
Ivanov (1988) and McCarthy (1986), Out of the mapping class group of a closed surface of genus ≥ 3 is Z/2 (orientation
reversal); Korkmaz (1999) for punctured spheres and tori. Confidence: high for the argument under the stated
centralizer hypothesis, medium for the references.

**C4. 030 L12 · reading question.** "la flèche (1) (la flèche verticale centrale de (5))": the diagram on this page is
labelled (4) (L8); (5) labels the next diagram (031 L6), which has no vertical arrow. Either a slip for (4) or a trace of
renumbering: the equation labels on 029 are retraced, and Hua b2 p. 28 reads (*), (**), (***) under them. Cite the
labels with this caveat.

**C5. 018 L30 · reading question.** "d'où l'opération de G sur π|Π par passage au quotient": the ink has the lower-case
π form; the construction (E acting on Π̃, π acting by Π-automorphisms, G = E/π acting on Π) needs Π. A slip of the pen
or a hasty Π; the transcription keeps the doubt.

**C6. 033 L21–22 · reading question.** "M'_{g,ν}(K) ∋ ξ" and "𝒯'_{g,ν} ∋ G_ξ": G_ξ is written as an element, with
the same sign as ξ above. If G_ξ is the image of the Galois group of K at the point ξ, an inclusion ⊃ would be expected;
the ∋ may be a slip or may denote a conjugacy class. No local definition of the primes is written.
