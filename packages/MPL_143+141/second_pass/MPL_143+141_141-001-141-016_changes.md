## Conventions

The first pass's macros and diplomatic conventions are kept. Choices where a feature needed a rule:
- **Element sign written vertically.** The first pass's first occurrence (scan 012 L51) renders it with `\rotatebox`
  in a stacked position. The same device is used on 013 L6, L12 and 015 L79, where the first pass had read the sign
  as an arrow; inside tikz-cd it is put as the label of a phantom arrow, as the first pass does for `\wr` on 002 L16.
  The rotation follows the ink: the open side faces the containing set (up on 013 and 015, down on 012).
- **Maps-to arrows** (`maps to` in tikz-cd), as the first pass writes them on 013 L13, for the barred vertical
  strokes of 012 L28.
- **Uncertain struck text** stays as `\deleted{\unsure{...}}` (first pass, 009 and 015).
- **Ink colour, positions and structural alternatives** that `\unsure` cannot carry (blue-ink revisions on 008, the
  place of the sign `\otimes n` on 006, faint pencil marks on 009) are recorded in `%` comments: the first pass has no
  macro for them and itself uses a comment line on 016.
