## Own notes (Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max, second pass)

**O1 (002 L48, 003 L5; reading question with a consequence for the edition; medium).** On the first-pass reading
"Aut(X̃/X)", (5) and (6) contradict each other: π_1(X) = Aut(X̃/X) would equal ℰ_X, and the extension would be
trivial. On the corrected reading the slash in (5) is cancelled or overwritten, and the contradiction goes. An edition
note should not call (5) an error of Grothendieck's until the ink of that slash has been settled.

**O2 (007 L18, 009 L22; a hypothesis changes; medium).** "points maximaux" occurs in both places. The recapitulation
I then requires X to be smooth at the generic points of supp D, that is, regular in codimension one along D. That is the
hypothesis under which each D_i gives a discrete valuation and a Kummer inertia T(Q̄) → π_1(X') as in (17), and it is
the hypothesis already stated on 005 L22 ("régulier (i.e. lisse) en les pts gén. de chaque D_i"). The first-pass
"minimaux" would require smoothness at closed points of supp D, a much stronger hypothesis than the pages use.

**O3 (002 L19, L37; own idea; low).** With X_red in the constant-field condition, the condition "Q̄ is the integral
closure of Q in Γ(X_red, O)" ignores nilpotents. That matches the doubt he puts on "réduit": the relèvement (4)
determines X̄ only when X̄ is reduced, while 𝒢_X̄ → Γ is defined for every X̄. The condition also needs the constants
to form a field, which connectedness (② L44) gives; on this page connectedness comes after the definition of
𝒢_X̄ → Γ. Worth a sentence in a companion note; it is not a claim about his intent.

**O4 (004 L3-9; reading question; low).** If Hua's "X(Q̄) → J" is right, the cancelled draft states the injectivity
criterion through an explicit map into a commutative algebraic group J of unipotent rank 0 (a generalized Jacobian or
Albanese). 005 L5-15 then restates it with "une extension d'une VA par un tore". This bears on N05 and on the
section-conjecture leads cited there.

**O5 (pagination; documentary; medium).** On 002-016, every other scan has a numeral at the top right: 1 on 002, 2 on
004, and so on to 8 on 016. Every scan has a numeral at the bottom left: 1 to 16 on 002-017. The first pass calls the
bottom-left series the "main sequence". The top-right numerals stand on alternate faces only, and Hua batch-01 counts
them as the author's pages ("p. n de l'auteur": p. 1 is our 002, p. 8 our 016). So they are probably Grothendieck's
sheet numbers on the rectos, and the bottom-left series a later count of every face: on that reading, scans 002-017
are eight sheets written on both sides. No transcription change follows, since both series are `\mspage`, but the
first pass's "Extent and overlapping pagination" table should not call the bottom-left series his.

**O6 (012 L3, L5; documentary; medium).** The heading ("gruppentheoretisches Gleichnis") and item a)
("gruppentheoretisch") both use German; the French "groupo-théorétique" does not occur on the page.

**O7 (015 L2; reading question; low).** The heading reads "Réconstruction des I-groupoïde(s) fondamentaux
Π_1(X';J)". The "I-" might be a "1-", for 1-groupoïde, which fits the mathematics: the objects are J, and I is the
boundary index set. In the ink it is a capital I with serifs, unlike the hooked J of "Π_1(X';J)" on the same line.
Hua omits the prefix.

**O8 (003 L16; reading question with a mathematical effect; low).** With the insertion placed where its line ends,
the NB reads "Si [X est de t.f. /Q̄ et sép./si] G_X est fini, 𝒢_X est profini et 𝒢_X(ξ) est un s-groupe ouvert...". If
the last word is "si", finiteness of G_X is a hypothesis beside finite type, not a consequence of it. Finite type alone
does not make Aut(X/Q̄) finite: P^1 is an example. The first pass's placement (a margin after "est") gave a garbled
condition.
