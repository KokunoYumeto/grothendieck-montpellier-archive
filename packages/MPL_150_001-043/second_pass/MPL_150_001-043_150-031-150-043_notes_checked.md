## Own notes (Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max, second pass)

N1. 031 l. 37, reading question. The letter after "minorant" has the arched shape that writes k on this leaf (l. 4, l. 8,
the union index of (0)), while "borne inf h" in the margin (l. 33) may be a true h. The NB and the corollary then use h
for an infimum and k for a mere lower bound. This is the same distinction as in R29a/@822. Medium confidence.

N2. 033 l. 47-70, unpursued implication. The theorem on 035 (l. 18-24) cites "les conditions (20)". On the page these are
ⓐ X_β disjoint, ⓑ, ⓓ, and the late addition ⓒ on the X_i^*. In (19), Ẋ = ∐ Ẋ_β and C(p) = ∐_β C(p_β) presuppose the
margin's "les Ẋ_β disjoints". ⓐ is what keeps the cones T_β = C(p_β) from meeting inside the reconstructed X. Both
disjointness conditions are therefore used, at different steps. My reading, medium confidence.

N3. 034 l. 10, reading question. "(I ⊃ I')" carries a prime, while the next line names I^*, I_0. Either this is a slip
for I_0, or I' is a provisional name for the set of minimal elements. The transcription keeps I'. Editions should not
harmonize it.

N4. 040 l. 2-40, own idea, medium confidence. The asterisked note and the margin weaken the hypothesis of (3), "last
element of d = first element of d'". The weaker condition is that δ = d∩d' be an initial segment of d' containing max d,
and the margin then states cartesianity of the square Σ_{d''} → Σ_{d'}, Σ_d → Σ_δ for every decomposition
d'' = d ∐_δ d' that is a flag. This is a Segal-type condition for the functor Σ on Drap(I) with respect to amalgamation
along δ. The margin claims that the length-2 case suffices. The proof obligation is a pasting lemma for cartesian squares
in M, which needs the relevant fibre products to exist in M (unspecified, 039 l. 6-8). It bears on R42.

N5. 042 l. 24-28 against 040 l. 64-70, cross-reference and reading question. "(cf. p. 15', marge)" sends the reader to
condition b) in the margin of scan 040: Σ_{i_0…i_n} = Σ_{i_0i_n} ∩ Σ_{i_1i_n} ∩ … ∩ Σ_{i_{n-1}i_n} in Σ_{i_n}. The
restatement on 042 (indices "i_0i_1|i_0i_n" … "i_0i_n", uncertain) should match that pattern, all pairs ending at i_n.
That pattern agrees with the case n = 2 on the same page, Σ_{ijk} = Σ_{ik} ∩ Σ_{jk} (l. 32). For the editions: compare
042 l. 27-28 with 040 b) on the original. This is a reading question, not a correction.

N6. 043 l. 7 and l. 32-34, with 042 l. 60-64, own idea, medium confidence. "(p = long. d')" does not fit the NB "d est
l'ens des éléments maximaux des d_1, …, d_p" and the count "2^n choix possibles pour d". If p were the length of d', the
chain of strict initial segments d_1 ⊂ … ⊂ d_{p+1} = d' would contain every initial segment, and d would be determined.
If p is the number of elements of d (one d_k per element of d), the chains correspond one to one to the 2^n
non-cofinal d ⊂ d' = {i_0, …, i_n}. "long. d'" is probably a slip for a count attached to d. The transcription keeps
what the ink shows.

N7. 041 l. 9-11 and 042 l. 9-13, implication, replacing L03. With no "discrète", Σ_M → I_M is an internal category over
I_M, the object of M that also receives the partition Σ_* → I_M (041 l. 11). The margin (042 l. 16-23: "c'est un objet
ordonné de M … drapeaux x_{i_0} ≤ … ≤ x_{i_n} … au dessus des drapeaux i_0 − … − i_n de I") reads I_M as the poset I
seen as a category in M. Σ_M → I_M is then a functor of internal categories, and the fibres over flags of I give the
Σ_{i_0…i_n}.

N8. 039 l. 29-39, claim stated without proof. With the corrected margin ("Cela est trop faible, si on veut
l'équisingularité"), the strengthened axiom asks that, for fixed d and variable d' = d ∐ δ, the stratification formed by
the Σ_{d'} above Σ_d be locally trivial. A proof that this follows from a conical structure (§8) would need the
equisingular tube of 037 l. 15-17. It bears on R20 and R41.

N9. 031 l. 23-25, reading question for a reader of the original. Whether "est sûrement" is struck decides whether the NB
is an expectation or an assertion (F08). Hua reads it struck; the scan does not settle it. Recommended for the user's eye.

N10. 038 l. 13-15 and 039 l. 60-67, documentary. §9 first sets aside the cylindrical stratification of §8, remark 3
("ne se donne pas … par dessus le marché"). The cylindrical data come back on 039 in the left margin: "On va supposer de
plus données des structures cylindriques correspondantes, et formuler des conditions plus fortes que (2)". The
presentation Σ of 039-043 is first the bare case.
