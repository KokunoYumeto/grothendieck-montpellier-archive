## My own notes (Claude, second pass, Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max)

- C1. 028 l.36-37, reading question / slip. As written, "s_k ⊂ S formée de j∈S_k tels que j ≥ s_k" is circular (s_k
  on both sides). The anti-isomorphism k ↦ s_k of I_{i,j} onto 𝒫(S) (decreasing, l.38-39) needs s_k = {j∈S : j ≥ k}.
  Confidence medium that j ≥ k is meant; the ink (and Hua) read s_k.
- C2. 028 l.33, reading question with consequence. If the gloss is "minimaux" (Hua), it contradicts "prédécesseurs de j"
  (the minimal element of I_{i,j}∖{j} is i); if "maximaux", it is the correct gloss. Route to a further witness.
- C3. 021 l.19, unpursued implication. "Pour faire des récurrences dans nos constructions" is, in this folder, the first
  statement of the induction strategy: a cônification relative to a boundary Ẋ, so that the pieces cut out at one step
  carry the boundary data the next step needs. It links the cancelled 021 to 030 (§7) and 035-036, and to cote 147/190,
  where the tubular step was postponed.
- C4. 020 l.14, his slip: "Ẋ et X_i^*, parties fermées de X": X^* is meant (Ẋ and X_i^* live in X^* = X∖T̊).
- C5. 027 l.47, reading question. With Ė_J (dot) the second row, Ė_J∩Ṫ ≃ Ṫ_J×I^J, does not fit the product
  structure E_J ≃ B_J×I^J: Ė_J∩Ṫ lies over the boundary of I^J, not over I^J. Either the dot is a slip or the mark is
  the foot of the ∪ above it; E_J∩Ṫ is what the rest of the page needs.
- C6. 019 l.27, reading question. "s'il existe un voisinage conique {Ṫ|T̄|T} de Y dans X": the letter carries a dot
  (Hua notes it too); a neighbourhood is meant, so Ṫ would be a slip or an overwrite.
- C7. 025 l.7, his slip: "Y∩E} ≃ Y_0∩E" as written; Y_0×I is meant (cf. l.46 "Y∩E ≃ Y_0×I"; Hua normalises it).
- C8. 022 l.18, reading question: in "s'il est [∃λ∈]0,1[ tel qu'il soit] compatible avec λT" the sign read "est" looks
  like an insertion arrow; with "est" live the sentence does not parse. Intended: "s'il existe λ∈]0,1[ tel qu'il soit
  compatible avec λT".
- C9. 027 l.5 and l.8: whether "finie" is struck (my reading) or live (Hua), the definition on 027 is for a finite
  index set: "pour tout J⊂I, I fini". If struck, he moved the finiteness into the condition.
- C10. 018-022 and 028, systematic: the first pass wrote T̄ for a plain T at 018 l.13, 18; 019 l.5, 7, 8, 20-21, 28;
  020 l.4, 6; 022 l.8, 10, 13-14, 17, 19-20, 25-27; 028 l.5 (single top stroke in the ink). On these pages T and T̄ name
  the same closed conical neighbourhood (he writes T throughout 024-028), so no statement changes, but notes that quote
  "T̄" from these lines should quote T.
- C11. 019 l.10, consequence of the corrected reading: with "f ?" gone, the passage "Cela implique que si ∀i, X_i∩Y est
  une partie à la fois ouverte et fermée de Y, si Y est connexe, que l'on ait l'alternative (14)" is an unqualified
  claim. Proof sketch: equisingularity makes Ṫ_i→Y locally trivial, so the set of y over which X_i∩T̄ meets the
  fibre is open and closed; with X_i∩Y clopen and Y connected, each X_i∩T̄ is empty or contains Y. Confidence medium
  (it uses that X_i∩T̄ is conical, X_i∩T̄ = I.(X_i∩Ṫ) ∪ (X_i∩Y)).
