The English typescripts are generally clear enough to distinguish individual bars, subscripts, keyed digits and handwritten corrections. The two least secure stretches of continuous writing are the lower proof on scan 172 and the left-hand annotation below the pairing diagram on scan 180. The compressed Ext exponents, retraced letter starts and short horizontal connectors on scans 176 and 178 often allow more than one reading. The marginal numbers belong to the archive’s foliation. The relative darkness and uniform height of the English mathematical characters provide better evidence for keyed overstrikes than the absolute darkness of any one character.
