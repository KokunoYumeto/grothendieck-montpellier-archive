## Legibility and limits

These are rapidly written working notes, with several dense tables, heavy cancellations, overwritten cells, informal punctuation and partly detached symbols. The clearest short titles and many large table labels are more secure than the compressed prose. The pages I trust least are the cancelled cells and tiny annotations on 162 and 164, the G_a/U cell on 165, the oblique precision on 168, the parenthesis and last Ext argument on 170, the lower-left margin on 173, and the cancelled torsion definition on 176. The first brace label on 177 is still uncertain despite comparison with simple on 165.

The 15 scans were read in one stream without OCR or external texts. The second readings found and corrected omitted states, arrowhead assumptions, alphabet assumptions and several words. No mark of doubt guarantees that all ambiguities have been exhausted: faint overwriting and extremely compressed letter middles remain the main risk. Ordinary manuscript parentheses and incomplete sentences are retained even when awkward. The structural check is not a compilation or proof of visual fidelity.
