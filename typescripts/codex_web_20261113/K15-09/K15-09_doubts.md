## Legibility

The three root-system sheets have distinct type, including individual underlines and the small stars. The other two typescripts are inverted in the scans and become legible when turned. The faint mark by condition (ii) on 216 is the least secure addition to type. In the manuscripts the problems are concentrated in short inflectional endings, compressed fibres and their indices, corrections over earlier letters, and the lower functor expression on 215. The word hatched out before « c’est » on 207 is wholly unreadable. Several other discarded words retain too little ink for more than a candidate. The difference between a pen lift and a terminal comma is particularly weak on 217.
