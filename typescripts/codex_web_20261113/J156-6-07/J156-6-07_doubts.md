## Legibility

The clear primes, order signs and downward map can be checked against repetitions. The right-hand Fil on 052 and the changed subscript in the small writing on 056 remain visible at enlargement. The weakest passages are the canceled clauses beside the Top maps on 050, the bottom edge of 050 and 051, the overwritten letters in 052 and 055, and the verb under the horizontal strike on 057. Turning the margins makes their word order and placement easier to follow. The bracketed continuation on 050 and the small crossed axiom labels on 056–057 still permit more than one reading. The word count in the one solid cancellation on 050 is an estimate.
