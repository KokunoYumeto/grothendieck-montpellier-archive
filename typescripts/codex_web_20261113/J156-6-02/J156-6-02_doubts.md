## Legibility

The displayed comparisons, set brackets, and large capitals are generally clearer than the ends of words. The hardest readings are the cancelled trials on 013, the superseded wording and hatched note at the foot of 016, and the two struck lines and overwritten numerical reference on 017. In the slanted notes on 012 and 014 the main text runs across the notes at another angle. Small accents and the feet of capital F are particularly vulnerable there.
