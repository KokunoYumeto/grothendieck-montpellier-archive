## Legibility

The numbered hypotheses, the large diagrams and the uncorrected object names are generally clear. Their object and arrow labels often give a second reading of a phrase from the prose. The least secure passages are the wording under long cancellation strokes, French endings reduced to a dash, the hyphenated name on 156, and the character inside the twice-used equation label on 162. The slanted notes on 149 and 156 are readable when turned along their baselines. Two words heavily crossed out after Ch on 162 remain unreadable. A word transcribed under a cancellation elsewhere may have only one plausible candidate, as the individual rows indicate.
