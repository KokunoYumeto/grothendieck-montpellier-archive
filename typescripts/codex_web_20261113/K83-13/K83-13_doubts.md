## Legibility

The clearest numerical evidence is in the generator list on 197 and the grouped counts on 207. The darkest notes on reused paper are generally distinguishable from the paler mirrored typescript. In the handwritten algebra the smallest primes, the abbreviated letter indices, and signs written as almost horizontal strokes are less secure. Nearly solid overwrites on 195, 199 and 205 sometimes preserve the position of a letter better than its outline. The two L 17 impressions are distinct states. The earlier impression helps identify type forms, while the heavier pen on 204 changes the words and their position. L 19 on 196 and the changes to the footnotes on 208 have the greatest concentration of doubtful undertext. The last line at the right edge of 208 also has clipped type.
