# Hand and notation log

Entries are local observations. The indicated scope is the extent to which the comparison holds on these leaves.

## Scan 2

The title hand reduces the middle of « pour » and « car. » to a low stroke (002_title.jpg). The downstroke at the foot of the second p in fppf is separate from the loop of the last f. The pale 4 has a different weight from the heading. This comparison concerns the title only.

## Scan 3

A G can be an open C with only a short return at the foot; compare all three members of the filtration. Curved leaders before finis, Tout, de t. m., de type, and l’un each attach words above the line. None crosses out the words below. At the first reading the divided oblique after G″ was open. On the second reading, two separate short cross strokes distinguish ≠ from the plain quotient slash nearby.

## Scan 4

Upright reading requires 180° rotation. Crosses replacing typed characters follow the pitch of the machine. With the baseline still partly visible, « Bien entendu » and the two corollary numbers can be recovered. Darker impressions without crosses occur too. No pen correction established on this face.

## Scan 5

A leader ends in matching small loops at the foot and in the left margin; once rotated clockwise its text reads along one line. The prime on T′ is upright. End dots on some arrows make the heads look blunt. The first shaft in the last live row has a left-hand crossbar; compare the plain arrows in the middle row. The deleted bottom row has two long intersecting strokes.

## Scan 6

Rotated typescript. Paired heads of H and G at two overtypes are local corrections. The ordinary H in the corollary is clear. An underline sets off fini, Corollaire and Z. No general identification of a heavy typed letter with a new alphabet is possible on this face.

## Scan 7

The pen's n superscript is a low horizontal with a rise, whereas i has a detached dot. Central cancellations cover suffixes, then an entire rectangular block. The thin stroke joining the outside of the block is distinct from the falls of p. The first k of the right factor in the lower display has no visible prime.

## Scan 8

Three long freehand strokes cancel the four-line proposition. Its two later headings are cancelled by lower-case typewriter x. Repeated impression and crossing by a pen are separate here. An actual comma occurs between a and c in rempla,cant.

## Scan 9

The descending leg of p in the final α_p starts below the end of the Greek loop. Four separated tapering traces follow. In the pair of G_m, the m is much heavier than the body of the G. Distinguish the underline that resumes after G from a cancellation.

## Scan 10

The capital is open to the right, with a short return at the foot. The separate pale inscription is in another weight of pencil. The p in the title descends, unlike the final series of four dots.

## Scan 11

Gray mirrored printing lies behind the black manuscript. Lower-left 10 is upright and at the sheet edge. The circled sign opposite it is a lighter hand and its reading is open. The local division slashes and left-annihilator notation are distinct. Regular dimensions show different final digits.

## Scan 12

The thick handwritten last digits replace thinner typeset digits. Their numbers can be compared within this same proof. The vertical arms of the correction to P cover the first letter locally, without changing later typed R. The isolated sweeping stroke runs through several paragraphs and ends at multiplicatif. Rows for P, tildes and the bottom line belong to the correcting hand.

## Scan 13

The ascending tooth of lambda differs from the above-line dot of xi in the quotient. The author writes the denominator of the first quotient without grouping the following sum in parentheses. Pale mirror-image type descends into several spaces in the text and is excluded.

## Scan 14

The last digit of the lemma and its citation have the same new terminal curve. Pen strokes correcting Z are too dark to identify the underlying letter securely. The raised typed Alors and connexes are distinct from the heavier pen strokes. The diagonal across the whole face is similar to the mark in 12.

## Scan 15

A sharp change in the depth of the line around the second diagram separates the explanatory bracket from the following cancellations. Several overwritten attempts in front of B-linéaires survive only in fragments. A lower-case xi descends substantially and is unlike the undotted g in the linear-map sentence.

## Scan 16

The raised word quasi-compact is type, whereas Z is ink. The pen's 2 in both lemma references ends with a horizontal stroke.

## Scan 17

The manuscript is in strong ink over much lighter mirror printing. In the limit formula and the definition of M the triangular right end of the line is visible, especially under the last lim. The letter f has a sweeping descender that may reach the Q on the line below.
