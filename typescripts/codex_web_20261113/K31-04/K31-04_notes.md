# Notes on the leaves

## Steps marked for verification

| Scan | Wording on the leaf | Scope |
|---:|---|---|
| 007 | « reste à vérifier » followed by \(H^{n+1}(G)=0\) | The sufficiency argument singles out this last degree. Scan 009 continues the reduction. |
| 008 | « Il reste à prouver seulement que \(U\) est fermé. » | A local proof obligation. The same page proceeds to give the argument. |
| 009 | « il suffit de le faire pour » and « on est ramené à vérifier que \(H^{n+1}(\mu_p)=0\) » | The author points to the following exact sequence. The last sentence on the additive case ends with several traces after \(\alpha_p\). |
| 013, 015 | « Enfin, vérifions » and « Vérifions les propriétés » | These words introduce the verifications for the induction. Scan 015 starts the first, and 013 contains the later calculations. |
| 015 | Possible question mark after « surjectif » | Its outline could also belong to the end of the word. No authorial declaration of doubt can be established from it. |
