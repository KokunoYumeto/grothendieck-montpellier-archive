# H134-01 · Notes from the five scans

## Steps marked as unverified

- **Scan 2.** None.
- **Scan 4.** “qui pour l'instant reste heuristique” introduces the construction. The triangle follows “il doit y avoir un triangle distingué canonique”. In the separate sideways note the writer asks Breen “Qu'en pens-tu.” The ending of “pens” is uncertain.
- **Scan 5.** “Heuristiquement” qualifies the interpretation of E′(M,N). The writer then says “en admettant que ta théorie pour les 1-champs de Picard” and adds “stricts” above the line before “s'étend aux 2-champs de Picard stricts (ce qui pour moi ne fait guère de doute)”.
- **Scan 6.** “Je sais prouver (sauf erreur) que tout hom.” qualifies the assertion that a morphism M → ₂N “provient d'un champ de Picard convenable”. The parenthesis names an obstruction in Ext³(X;M,N) and attributes its vanishing to “un argument” whose quoted adjective reads “universel” or “universal”.
- **Scan 7.** Under “Question”, the writer asks “sont ils connu” of the triangle and sequence, naming Quillen, Breen and Illusie. The ending of “connu” is uncertain. A second question asks about “variantes” described as “supérieures”. The final parenthesis reads “Un principe” followed by the quoted adjective “géométrique” and “pour en obtenir pourrait être via des n-champs de Picard non néc. stricts...”.
