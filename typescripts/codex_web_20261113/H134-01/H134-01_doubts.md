## Legibility

The large main hand is generally readable. The horizontal strokes, small accents and word endings fade unevenly in the copies. A heavy stroke obscures the end of the date on scan 4. Fine strokes have dropped out of the sideways note. The copy edges meet the final zero on scan 5 and the punctuation of the last formula on scan 6. Scan 7 loses the upper part of its opening line and several endings at the right margin. Its underlined opening word is the only passage marked wholly illegible. The overlaid letters beneath Ext on scan 4 and the short cancelled groups on scans 5–7 are among the least secure readings.
