# Legibility

The typescript on scan 101 is substantially clearer than the manuscript. The handwriting often reduces word middles to waves, and cancellation can merge letters with formula strokes. The least reliable passages are the slanted note and overwritten fragments on 101, the long cancelled example on 102, the parenthetical word and altered comparison on 103, and the short note, long marginal paragraph and bottom reformulations on 105. Several mathematical letters, prime counts and local equality/membership differences remain surprising but were not normalized. The audit records changes actually made; it does not claim that the remaining doubtful readings have been resolved.
