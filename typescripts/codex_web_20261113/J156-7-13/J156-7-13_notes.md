# Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **101:** a marginal “?” beside the Corollaire. It is retained as an author’s question, distinct from transcription doubt marks.
- **102:** “J’ai envie de prendre…” introduces a proposed primitive relation; the later construction is called “la définition plausible”. These are tentative formulations, not explicit claims of a completed verification.
- **104:** “Vérifions At C (spac)” (spac/spec reading uncertain); “Proposition 2 (?)”; “⇒ sûrement toujours vrai, et ⇐ moyennant un axiome … supplémentaire genre At L1 (axiome de divisibilité infinie). Voyons !” The author explicitly leaves a converse dependent on another axiom.
- **105:** “Donc moralement, la validité de At C …” precedes several cancelled reformulations, then “Proposition (?)”. The question mark qualifies the proposed equivalence.
- **106:** “Il faut voir si on a ⇐.” The following text examines that direction under additional hypotheses.
- **107:** inserted “(il semble que)” qualifies the assertion that the relation cannot generally be recovered from the other structures. The final “Mag 6″ implique” is unfinished at the supplied page edge; its missing continuation is not reconstructed.
- **103:** none explicitly marked as unverified. “Il faut encore imposer…” is an additional-axiom statement, not a verification note.
