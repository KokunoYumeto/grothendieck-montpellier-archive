**Scan 191, section 6.9.** Homomorphisms of profinite groups induce the analytic, algebraic and Lie-algebra maps shown in the diagrams. The text asserts compatibility and consequences for epimorphisms and finite-index images.

**Scans 191–193, sections 6.10–6.12.** These constructions are extended to a projective system of profinite groups, denoted capital Pi. Two examples distinguish the result for this pro-object from the result for its inverse-limit group. The following statements identify representation categories and describe strictness when transition morphisms have finite-index images.

**Scans 193–195, the two paragraphs numbered 6.13.** A scheme expressed as a filtered inverse limit gives a projective system of fundamental groups. Its representations are related to categories of twisted ell-adic sheaves. Under finite-type hypotheses the system receives the name “pro-groupe fondamental arithmétique”; its inverse limit is compared with the usual fundamental group. The text stresses that the latter alone does not determine the pro-group.

**Scan 195, sections 6.14–6.15.** For an integral normal scheme, the text proposes normal integral models and applies the finite-index-transition discussion. It then asserts that, when ell is invertible, the arithmetic pro-group is not discrete in the stated Lie-theoretic sense. The argument begins with a reduction to finite type over Spec Z and stops at “Or”; the continuation is outside this package.
