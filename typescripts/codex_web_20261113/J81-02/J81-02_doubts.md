## Legibility

- Scan 11. The upper-right cancellation, the first product in the right-hand diagram and its far-right object have several layers of ink. The lowermost diagram edges are very faint.
- Scan 12. The hatching and the long cancellation-like strokes overlap in the second oval of the lower-left group. The two narrow upright marks there and above it are retained as drawings.
- Scan 13. The six inked-out, parenthesized entries and the faint displaced strokes beneath A and beside the fourth subdivision are the least legible.
- Scan 14. The heading is concentrated in the upper right. The first and penultimate words are much more contracted than the interlinear addition.
- Scan 15. The two oblique notes, the corrected family letter, the overwritten label beside the oval, and the last two lines of cancellations require particular caution.
- Scan 16. The extreme right of the first addition and of the last chain of inclusions reaches the physical edge. On the last line the lowest brace meets the bottom of the scan.
- Scan 17. The most difficult parts are the cancelled predicate, the overwritten beginning of intersection and the isolated cancelled sign after Eⱼ. The first two overbars, their signs and the dotted i at each E were read separately.
- Scan 18. The main lines and the two independent signed Σ's are comparatively clear. The tiny remaining lower mark at the lemma's Π, superimposed capitals and the two cancellations on the last baseline are much less distinct.
