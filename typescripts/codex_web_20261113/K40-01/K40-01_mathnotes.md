## What the pages do

**M1 · Scans 2–3.** The notes describe a group in the dual category of commutative unital algebras over $A$. Its algebra $B$ carries a diagonal, an augmentation and a map $x\mapsto\check x$. Equations give the inverse condition and the reversal of the tensor factors under the check. The finite-rank passage introduces a coalgebra and a compatible, possibly noncommutative multiplication on $U$.

**M2 · Scans 3–5.** An action of $B$ on $C$ is written as $q_C:C\to B\otimes_A C$. A square expresses associativity and a triangle expresses the unit condition. With a finite free dual, a map into $B\otimes_A C'$ is written as a map out of $U$. For $C=C'$ the unit and product rules are then written for a left $U$-module.

**M3 · Scans 5–7.** The comparison from $C\otimes C$ to $B\otimes C$ is required to be an isomorphism. Two diagrams express the inverse condition in terms of $g_C$. For an invariant map $h$ from $D$, the question becomes whether its values, which belong to the subalgebra $C'$ defined by $q_C x=1_B\otimes x$, must be scalars.

**M4 · Scans 7–9.** The affine quotient is described through $C'$. The example on scan 8 puts a complete homogeneous space $E/G$ beside the point obtained in the affine category. For a principal homogeneous object, the argument reduces the scalar question to $x\otimes1_C=1_C\otimes x$. Scan 9 imposes the direct-factor condition on $A\to C$ and then tests the conclusion with $C=B/JB$.

**M5 · Scans 10–11.** Two maps are compared. The first sends $C\otimes C$ to $\operatorname{Hom}_A(U,C)$, with multiplication on the target drawn as the convolution path. The second acts on $y$ by $\psi(x\otimes u).y=x\,u(y)$. The attempt using extension of scalars on scan 10 is crossed out. Scan 11 restates the first isomorphism alongside the condition on semi-primitive elements.

**M6 · Scans 12–13.** An abstract group object $G$ acts on $E$. The map $(q_E,p_2):G\times E\to E\times E$ and a division map $g_E$ are compared by two triangular identities. The diagrams are then written on algebras. Using $g_C$, the author constructs $\psi'$ from $\operatorname{Hom}(C,C)$ to $\operatorname{Hom}(B,C)$.

**M7 · Scan 14.** Each of two six-column computations changes one part of a composite at a time. In the last column the projection $p_1$ is followed by $w\otimes\mathrm{id}_C$ or $v\otimes\mathrm{id}_C$ and then by $\Delta_C$. The prose identifies the composites with $w$ and $v$, respectively.

**M8 · Scan 15.** For the converse the author assumes a finite $A$-basis of $C$, starts with an inverse to $\psi$, and rewrites it as a map from $B$ to an iterated Hom. The dual tensor factors are rearranged and the middle functional is evaluated at the unit. The last line is the contraction $\mathrm{id}_C\otimes\varepsilon\otimes\mathrm{id}_C$. The reverse isomorphism has yet to be deduced at the bottom of this sheet.
