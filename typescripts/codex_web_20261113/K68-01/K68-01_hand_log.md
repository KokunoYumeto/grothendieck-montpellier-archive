## Cross-page comparison

The joined pointed form before a variable on scans 8, 9 and 11 can represent the universal quantifier or the writer’s tt. It is marked on each of those sheets. G has a downturned inside spur; barred C in the second branch on scan 11 was retained after direct comparison with the adjacent barred G. The lower curl of i can resemble 2 (scan 13), while j descends much farther (scans 2–13). The prime, star and joined et in the later calculations were checked against both scans 15 and 16. The relative size of a prime can vary with the pen. The broad capital J that names the game on scan 12 has an inner contour absent on the player J. These are local comparisons; none decides a different occurrence without its own strokes.
