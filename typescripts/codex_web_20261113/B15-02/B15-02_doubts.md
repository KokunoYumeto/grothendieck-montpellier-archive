## Legibility and limits

The supplied images were read visually, including all whole pages and all three overlapping thirds. No OCR, text-recognition library, published transcription, or external textual source was used. Most prose is readable, but paper folds and faint type particularly affect scans 27, 29, 33, and 43. The least secure readings are the cancelled word on 27, the small insertion on 29, the directions of capital accents on 31, the mapping glyph and reference numeral on 33, the cancelled argument on 37, the damaged words and possible overstrike H on 43, and the coefficient g/e/c on 45. Small nonalphabetic marks on 35, 37, 41, and 47 may include incidental traces; uncertainty is retained rather than assigning them an unsupported meaning. The small unlabelled mark on 47 is reproduced as TikZ and is not given an invented label.

The blank after I= and the missing closing parentheses at the right edge of scan 31 are visible source features. They are not omissions or unreadable words supplied by the transcriber. Mathematical expectations were not used to repair indices, variable names, equation references, punctuation, internal word gaps, or alphabet choices. Absence of an illegibility macro does not imply that every faint glyph is certain: the explicit doubt marks carry the remaining uncertainty. The audit documents actual reading intervals and subsequent corrections; no compilation or layout verification was attempted.
