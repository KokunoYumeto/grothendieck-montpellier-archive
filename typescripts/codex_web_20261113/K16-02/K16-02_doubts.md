## Legibility

The clear type and large handwritten formulas allow a close reading of the page structure. Confidence falls sharply at the cramped addition beside (6.14) on 17, the renamed operators and slanted note on 18, the trial exponents and bars on 20, the large cancellation on 22, the oblique note on 25 and the last addition on 29. Several overtyped letters on 19, 21 and 23 are visible only as partial bowls or stems. A complete inventory records their positions even where the underlying word cannot be recovered. In the manuscript tables an isolated dash can lack a visible arrowhead. Ending a line of algebra at a bare equality or at an unmatched delimiter sometimes reflects the page itself.
