## Legibility

I could follow the main prose and the sequence of diagrams on every sheet. The least secure material is the reporter line visible through the cover, the crossed drafting on scans 3–4, the short functor labels and their raised revisions on scans 10–12, and the heavily inked signs on scans 14, 15 and 17. A little upturn sometimes does all the work of an arrowhead. French endings, abbreviation points and words added above a line deserve particular attention when checking the files against the originals. The last sheet ends in mid-argument.
