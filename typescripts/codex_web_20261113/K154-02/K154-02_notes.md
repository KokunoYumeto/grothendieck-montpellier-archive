## Title sheets, dates and page counts

The only handwritten date found is **3.1.84**, at the upper left of scan 65 beside the drawings. These fifteen scans contain no title sheet and give no manuscript page count.

Scan 69 opens a case list with “A) Recollements donnant lieu à une application naturelle”. Scan 70 continues with “B) Recollements”, an inserted “ne”, and the underlined “pas”. Scan 74 has the local heading “Cas $(a,b)$ … de prolongation des rives”. Scan 76 begins “Description combinatoire” above the graph list. These headings belong to their respective passages.

The pencil sheet numbers run from 61 on scan 62 to 75 on scan 76. They are recorded in the LaTeX comments. The reused paper on scans 62 and 73 carries job and machine identifiers. The repeated “82229.0906” on scan 62 appears on the printed START and STOP lines. No manuscript year or page count has been inferred from the machine fields.
