## Legibility

Thirteen sheets have a typescript body. Scan 004 is handwritten throughout, with most entries on the upper half of the sheet. The typewritten argument and its equation labels are generally clear. The least secure readings are the upper-right citation on 003, the covered exponent and sign in the series on 004, the overwritten tensor on 006, and isolated small strokes on 008, 013 and 014. Text on the backs of sheets sometimes shows through. The typescript itself credits J. Tate and P. Deligne; a marginal question about who made a correction does not identify the writer of all the notes. Unsigned pen additions are recorded as unknown in the inventory.
