## Legibility

The prose of 108, the bitorsor correspondences on 113 and most of the axioms on 114 are among the clearest passages. The cancellations on 104 and 109 preserve outlines more often than individual signs. The trial tables on 111–112 put a digit beside or on top of an earlier digit. Their final clean rows can be read more securely than their working cells.

Scan 118 has three writing directions. A stroke from the upside-down equation passes through the fourth group of graph labels, and the lower fork has several overwrites. On 120, ink pools and five-line ruling cover parts of two sequences and some vector labels. Connecting strokes and the paths of the freehand drawings on these two scans are approximated in TikZ. A candidate under a cancellation can therefore be much less secure than the live reading next to it.
