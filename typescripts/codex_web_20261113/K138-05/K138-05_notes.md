# Notes on the sheets · cote 138, scans 060–073

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **068, last line of the heavily cancelled draft.** The readable words are “faut vérifier” and “$X'$ de genre $0$ (facile)”. They concern the intermediate curve obtained after a degree-two extension. Several small words at and below the insertion remain uncertain. The phrase is inside the cancellation.
- **068, line beginning “groupe cyclique”.** One sign before “car.” admits the readings 2, a question mark and p. It may mark a hesitation about the characteristic. The pen stroke alone does not establish that the writer posed a question there.
