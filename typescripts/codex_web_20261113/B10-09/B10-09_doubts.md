## Legibility

The typescript is generally legible. Typed x cancellations, small marginal cursive, overwritten letters and the clipped right edge are less secure; these carry explicit doubt marks. No OCR, text recognition, external transcription or mathematical reconstruction was used.

Scan 129: the long diagonal margin was enlarged in several overlapping crops and rotated for inspection. The last word above “définie”, the first typed cancellation, and the final inserted cross/check symbol are the least secure readings.

Scan 130: the overtyped capitals and a few x-cancellations are the main uncertainties. The handwritten bottom question is substantially clearer than the diagonal margins on 127 and 129. The word “préliminaire” crosses scans 129–130 and is joined without a hyphen in the assembled fragment.
