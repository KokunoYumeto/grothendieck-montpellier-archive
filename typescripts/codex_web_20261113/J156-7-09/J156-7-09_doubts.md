# Legibility and limits

The scans show widely spaced, mostly clear large strokes, but many medial letters and endings collapse into waves or short bars. The weakest readings are the crossed NB2 block and concluding phrase on 74, the stratos/Atlas words and closing question on 75, compressed verbs near the bottom of 76, the c/C and R/P distinctions, and the isolated looped symbol and compressed comment on 79. Margin notes were read on rotated inspection crops. Script/calligraphic alphabets and blunt map endpoints remain explicit alternatives.

All seven pages were transcribed and audited, with further close reads recorded separately. The uncertainty table covers all marked doubts; it is not a guarantee that every remaining unmarked word is correct. No OCR, external edition, web source, or compilation was used. Possible omissions and slips were kept rather than repaired from mathematical expectations.
