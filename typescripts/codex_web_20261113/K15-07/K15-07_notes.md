# Notes on the sheets

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

| Scan | Place and words on the sheet | What remains open there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 185 | Left margin, “? à vérifier”, alongside “est fini” | Whether the set of polarisations under discussion is finite. The following bracket singles out the commutative case, “en tous cas !”. |
| 185–187 | “conformément aux conjectures de Tate” | The link from the data u(s′) to effectivity is presented by reference to conjectures. |
| 191 | Items (i) and (iii), “provenant sans doute de” and “provenant sans doute d’un” | The proposed origins in the two henselian rings. The reduced item is also accompanied by a large question mark. |
| 191 | After the fibre functors, “sont sans doute uniques”, with “à isom près” above the line; “de dim … 1 (?)” | Uniqueness and the extension from finite fields to a perfect field satisfying the indicated dimension condition. The abbreviation for that dimension is doubtful. |
| 191 | Last paragraph, “il faut s’attendre que”, followed by “mais il est possible que” | More than one element at finite level, possibly just one at the inverse limit, in each case up to isomorphism. |
| 191 | Lower oblique note, “relation avec les … galoisiens ??”, “Il doit être …, cf Manin” | The relation being suggested and the asserted equivalence or equivariance are both tentative. The handwriting in this note remains uncertain. |
| 193 | Middle, “il ne semble pas qu’il y ait … des isom entre les …”; lower down, “il est = gentil qu’ils veuillent être l’un tordu de l’autre” | The author doubts the availability of isomorphisms for different points valued in the same field and comments on the possible weaker relation by twisting. |

The directions to justify a construction or insert a demonstration on 192 and 194 are revision instructions. They carry no separate authorial question mark on these sheets.
