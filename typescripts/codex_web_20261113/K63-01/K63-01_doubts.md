## Legibility

The English hand is generally readable, but the copy loses fine pencil at its left and lower edges. The faint labels and overwritten coefficients on 36 and the last radical on 37 are among the least secure readings. In the French manuscript the pen frequently joins several letters into a single stroke. Dense hatching on 66 and 71 obscures old formulas even where the live ones are clear. The last raised character of the line-7 numerator on 71 runs into the scan border. Type is clearer than the handwritten operator on 70 and 72. Hand assignments for the marginal additions and individual typescript infills remain provisional.
