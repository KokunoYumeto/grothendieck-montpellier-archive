## Legibility

The two typewritten faces are generally sharp. Their handwritten operator letters require a separate alphabet decision, and the tear on 85 takes the upper limit of the first sum. The dark script on 82 and 84 is usually distinguishable from mirrored type by the direction of the letters. The least secure readings are the superimposed drafts on 73, 76 and 78, the title under the loops on 81, the last line at the edge of 77, and the tiny slanted notes on 75 and 79. A doubtful superscript on W cannot always be transferred from one occurrence to the next.
