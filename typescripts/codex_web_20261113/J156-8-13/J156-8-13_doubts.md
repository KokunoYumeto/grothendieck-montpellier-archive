## Legibility

The main text, the arrowheads in the two order diagrams and most of the numbered cases are legible at the scale of the supplied thirds. The corrections written on top of existing capitals, the raised marks and portions of the cancelled proofs are less secure. The heaviest overlaps are in the sideways definition on 100, the formulas at the right of 103, the crossed order definition on 104 and the oblique order note on 105. One crossed word in the last of these remains unreadable. The old folio on 103 and the very last punctuation on 102 are also uncertain.
