# Documentary notes

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **036, below the square.** “Question : est-il exact qu'il suffit” introduces the two conditions a) and b). The sign after “pour la validité de” resembles α. The mark at the end of the underlined “loc. fini” has been cancelled.
- **040, starred addition and sloping left margin.** “Supposons encore I artinien” is qualified by “J'ignore si cette hypothèse est nécessaire”. The following “cf commentaire p.” points to a number that looks like 39 or 35.
- **044, above Lemma 2.6.** The words “qu'il faudrait dégager en lemme” propose extracting a lemma from “diagramme 6 p. 17”. The verb and the following preposition are uncertain. Lemma 2.6 begins on this same sheet.
- **051, above remark 2.** “J'ignore si l'hypothèse artinienne dans 2.1. 3° est superflue ou non.” This is the explicit question left after the example.
