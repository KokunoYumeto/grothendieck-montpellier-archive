## Legibility

The open, widely spaced lines often make complete clauses recoverable. Clear final capitals, well-separated pairs of indices and fully formed arrowheads are among the most secure details. The least secure are the old letters beneath new K/J assignments, the small label at the end of the construction on 044, the mixed Y/Z in 046–047 and the cancelled argument across the lower half of 047. Several final vowels, plural endings and superscripts occupy only a dab of ink. The small reference in the sloping margin of 040 is particularly uncertain. A line through a group can leave enough to propose one or more readings without recovering every letter.
