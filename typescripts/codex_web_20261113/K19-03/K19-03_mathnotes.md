# Cote 19 · Mathematical notes on scans 36–49

## What the pages do

**36, 38 · Comparing cohomological functors.** The two rows of diagram (I) come from the same sequence in $A,B,C$. The vertical maps compare $T^i$ and $T'{}^i$. Parts a) and b) use a system of cogenerators to pass from an isomorphism or monomorphism there to a statement for all arguments. Parts a′) and b′) ask what can be tested on the end terms of one short exact sequence. The answer in a′) requires injectivity one degree higher on $A$.

**38 · Organizing the induction.** Part c) uses effaceability in degree $n+1$. A proposed dual statement d) is enclosed and crossed out. The author introduces $I_n(A)$ for isomorphism through degree $n$ and $J_n(A)$ for that condition together with monomorphism in degree $n+1$. The three displayed implications explain the concluding test on cogenerators. A double rule separates this from the question about vanishing.

**37, 39 · A class of morphisms.** Both sheets are cancelled drafts. Scan 39 starts with projections from projective space and writes conditions involving composition, a birational map, closed immersions and locality on the target. It argues by induction on $r$ for membership in $M$. Scan 37 considers a proper morphism, an open set where a modification is an isomorphism, and the closed complement. The argument combines a primed condition, noetherian induction and the lemma of Chow.

**38, 40 · Testing vanishing.** The end of 38 asks for a criterion for $T^i$ to vanish on every argument. Scan 40 begins with direct factors and finite filtrations. For constructible sheaves of $l$-torsion it reduces the test to sheaves built from an étale finite cover and an immersion. The square identifies the roles of $p,q,i,j$. The next paragraph asks when closure under direct factors and extensions, with specified generators, gives all the sheaves. An additional closure condition permits a further reduction to finite $X'$ over $X$.

**41 · An extraordinary inverse-image comparison.** The cancelled fragment proposes testing an isomorphism at a point of $X_0$ after applying $Rj^!$. Two diagrams give the comparison and the composite $X_0\to Y\to X$, labelled $h$ below. The upper object contains $\mu_\alpha$. The corresponding lower object, the alphabet of one $R$ and a vertical comparison sign are harder to read. The page stops at the diagrams.

**42 · Hypotheses in descent and base change.** The remark asks which directions of 6.5 use quasi-compactness and quasi-separation. It then considers 6.5 bis, 6.6 and the descent lemma 6.7. The last paragraph isolates an argument for injectivity of base change in degree $n+1$ and expresses doubt that the passage to a limit is needed.

**43 · Restriction in degree one.** In the typescript, two rows of pointed sets relate $H^1$ and $H^0$ terms. Injectivity of the last vertical map and bijectivity of the middle one enter the conclusion about surjectivity of the first. That ends (ii). In the crossed sketch of (iii), the canonical map from $F$ to $R\pi_*(\bar F)$ and its mapping-cylinder $G$ form a triangle. The final pen note directs the reader to XII 8.3 (iii) and suggests replacing $\bar X$ by $\bar X\amalg Y$.

**44–46 · Localization and adjunction.** The cover names localization functors and categories of fractions. Under 3) scan 45 compares being isomorphic to $Sy$, the unit $x\to STx$ being an isomorphism, and a Hom condition. Under 4) it compares full faithfulness of $S$, the counit and the quotient description by arrows inverted by $T$. Scan 46 works with $T\colon A\to A/C$. Item 2) gives conditions on $T$ and $C$ for a right adjoint, using largest $C$-subobjects and $C$-closed objects. Item 1) gives Hom and subobject conditions on an individual $x$. The edge cuts into the last condition.

**47–48 · Two localizing subcategories.** For $C''\subset C'$, scan 47 describes $\Gamma'$ and $\Gamma''$ by their largest subobjects, and their quotient pointwise. The note in the margin proposes writing the adjoints as $i^{\prime !}$ and $i^{\prime\prime !}$. The quotient and section functors give $\Lambda=ST$. Scan 48 uses $x''=x\cap y''$ to discuss monomorphisms, then displays a comparison between $R^0(\Gamma'/\Gamma'')$ and $\Lambda\Gamma'$. The proposed isomorphism carries a query. Its marginal qualification accompanies the ensuing closedness statements and the specialization to an injective $x$.

**49 · Preservation of injectives.** Five conditions concern the inclusion $i_*$, maximal subobjects in $C$, injective envelopes, the comparison with $ST$, and the quotient functor $T$. A brace groups the first three. The first of two short arguments uses $x'=i_*i^!(x)$ and the adjunction. The second takes an injective envelope $\bar x$ in $A$ and discusses an essential extension containing $x$. The visible implication headings read (i) ⇒ (ii) and (ii) ⇒ (i).

The struck sheets 37 and 39 share $M$, projective morphisms and lettered hypotheses. The evidence supports these two reading sequences while preserving the physical order in the transcription.

**Model, high confidence · Scans 44–46.** The cover count gives two numbers. The sheets immediately following it present four numbered items in the physical order 3), 4), 2), 1). Neither the count nor those numbers identifies how the two pencilled groups were assembled.

**Model, medium confidence · Scans 48–49.** The margin on 48 asks about injectives of $C''$ remaining injective in $C$. Condition (i) on 49 puts preservation of injectives at the start of a list for an inclusion $C\to A$. The words suggest a connection. The pages use different category labels and do not explicitly identify the two inclusions.

The notation of the preceding composite favours $g$.
