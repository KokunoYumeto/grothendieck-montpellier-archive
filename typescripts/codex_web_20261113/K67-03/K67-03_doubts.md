## Legibility

The formulas on scans 40 and 42 have clear spacing between letter bodies, indices and relation signs. Scan 36 has several crowded quotients and an abbreviated margin. Scans 37 and 39 need to be turned to read the sloping notes; the end of the note on 39 is particularly weak. Old strokes under the revisions on 41 and 48–50, including earlier exponents, account for many of the low-confidence readings. On 51 the handwriting runs in the opposite direction to the line printer. Pencil arcs can be distinguished from the printed rules over much of each sphere. The tiny mark over the last vertex and the old matrix-group label are among the least secure details. The line-printer banners are set in fixed-width characters, including repeated lines; their exact spacing and the faintest individual characters have lower confidence. No instance of the paper watermark « EXTRA STRONG » was identified as part of the writing.
