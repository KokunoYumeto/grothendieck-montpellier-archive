# Hand log

The entries distinguish the writer of a letter from the hand in the mathematical notes. Each observation is local unless the entry specifies a wider range.

## Scan 36

In the notes a small loop followed by a long horizontal is sigma. An overbar is a separate high flat stroke. The paired short horizontal lines can stand for même. Both forms occur in the opening argument, where an ordinary equality also occurs. Compare the blackboard capitals in the last two lines with the plain C denoting the circle above.

## Scan 37

In the fixed-set assertions H is above the line. The 0-shaped sign has a long rising diagonal, unlike the surrounding Greek sigma. A slash-shaped stroke running from the raised clause to its insertion point is a leader. In the margin the entire note rises toward the right. Rotating it resolves the sequence alg. linéaire, l’action.

## Scan 38

## Scan 51
