# Notes on the sheets

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

| Scan | Words on the sheet | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| 37 | “En résumé et sauf erreur”; “à vérifier”; “Il faudrait arriver à traduire en termes d'alg. linéaire, l'action d'un autom. … sur une métrique sphérique” | The opening qualification applies to the proposition about a compact subgroup of conformal automorphisms of a sphere. The separate sloping note stands by the discussion of invariant metrics and orbits. A cancelled word lies before the membership sign in that note. |
| 48 | “Mettre en évidence trois classes de conjugaison” | The closing NB assigns a task about homomorphisms from the twisted completed integer group. The inequality or equality in the following qualification is open. |
| 49 | “A examiner”; “Extension de la conjecture fondamentale”; “est-il vrai qu'on récupère”; “définir ce que c'est qu'une compactification anabélienne”; “Comportement par spécialisation ???” | The three entries propose extending the conjecture, recovering the points from group data and defining a compactification. The last ends with three question marks. The isolated sign after a) may itself be punctuation or P. |
| 50 | “Je m'attends à trouver trois types de présentations”; “et semble … commode surtout” | The three presentations are announced as an expectation. The qualification in a) concerns the usefulness of the pants or triad presentation for particular groups. |

The isolated mark after *descendre* on scan 46 can be read as the preposition *à* before $X$, as a question mark or as an exclamation. It is recorded in the transcription and supplies no secure additional authorial question.

## Title sheets, dates and page counts

There is no separate title sheet and no explicit total page count. Scans 36–44 carry the manuscript numbers 10 through 18 in sequence. The designation at the head of scan 45 reads 19, 17 or $1'$; scan 46 is transcribed as 21, with a related reading question in the mathematical notes. The number on scan 48 is 1; the separate wave on 49 could be 2. Scans 50 and 51 have no clear manuscript page number. The lower-corner archival numerals are 38–48 on scans 36–46 and 50–53 on scans 48–51.
