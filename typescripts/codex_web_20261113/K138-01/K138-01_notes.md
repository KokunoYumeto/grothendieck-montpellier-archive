# K138-01 · Notes on the sheets

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **005, after (7).** “sous réserve de conditions d’effectivité” qualifies passage from a splitting to an actual model. “de conditions” is an insertion. The parenthesis specifies the quasi-projective case.
- **009, left of (23).** “oubli à réparer ds rédaction en forme!” is the first reading of an oblique drafting note. The verb is uncertain. The note signals work for the written-up version. Its attachment to one particular equality within (23) is unclear.
- **010, lower square bracket.** “On a envie de plus … de faire varier” introduces a proposed variation of the cover, with the condition that the intersection of the kernels be 1. The writer presents this part as a wish. The next statement begins “On voit aisément”.
