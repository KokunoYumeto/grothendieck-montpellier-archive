## Legibility

The circular is consistently readable, apart from a tiny stop. Its two photographs are approximated in TikZ with outlines and shaded facets. The order form ends with pen flourishes whose letters, if any, I could not identify. The handwritten notes have easy-to-distinguish large diagrams and unusually compressed connecting prose. I place least trust in the undertext of heavy cancellations, the alphabets assigned to handwritten capitals, the right edge of scan 007, the oblique note on 009 and the lower edges of 013, 015 and 016. A symbol reconstructed under a cancellation can remain very tentative even when the live symbol is clear.
