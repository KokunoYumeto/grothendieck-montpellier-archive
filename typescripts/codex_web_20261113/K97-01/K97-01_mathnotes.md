The check ledger identifies individual calculations and the assumptions they use. The quotations record the author’s statements, including claims left open.

## What the pages do

**Scan 5 · The regular representation.** The sheet writes the total Chern class of a cyclic group's regular representation as a product over characters. For prime order it singles out $c_{p-1}$. It then restricts a multiple of the regular representation of a finite group $G$ to a subgroup $H$ of order $p$. The intended detection degree is $2Nn'(p-1)$, where $n'=[G:H]$. Two switches of multiplier matter in reading this argument (REG1–REG4 below).

**Scans 7 and 9 · Characters and bundles.** The diagrams place $\widetilde\Gamma,\Gamma$ in the covering groups and introduce the stabilizers. From their actions on $T_D(x)$ and $V$, the author constructs bundles on $X$. The two character rows have weights $1,-1$ and $2,-2$. The complex lines associated with them give the displayed relation $\widetilde c_1=2c_1$ (CHAR1).

**Scans 9 and 13 · The two structures on $E_0$.** A real rank-two representation gives $E_0$ an integrable connection. The other construction presents it as a unitary complex line. Scan 9 explicitly says that the connection does not preserve this complex structure. The sideways note identifies the real tensor square with $T_X+2\mathbb{1}_{\mathbb R}$. Scan 13 returns to this stabilized tangent bundle and breaks off in a qualification about its connection (REAL1).

**Scan 11 · The characteristic class on $X$.** The diagram puts $\gamma_1^X$ in $H^2(X,\mathbb Z)$. When a lift is available, the calculation gives $c_1=1-g$ and doubles this class. For the general case the author passes to a liftable cover of degree $d$. Pullback in degree two and the Euler characteristic both contribute a factor $d$, giving the final equality $\gamma_1^X=\mathrm{EP}(X)$ (EUL1).

**Scan 12 · Picard and Brauer groups on the reused typescript.** A cancelled comparison diagram relates exact sequences over $\bar Y$ and a fibre. The live text identifies the image of $H^1(Y,P)$ with the group it calls the Tate–Chafarévitch group. Sequence (4.17) describes $H^1(Y,P)$ as an extension by a quotient of the sum of local terms. The sheet then decomposes the fibre divisor as $X_y=\sum_i a_y^i C_y^i$ and ends as another definition begins (FIB1).

**Scan 13 · The lifting obstruction.** The universal cover supplies a central extension of $\Gamma$ by $\mathbb Z$. The author identifies its class with $\gamma_1^X$ and takes the image modulo two to obtain the obstruction for the double cover. The number $2(1-g)$ is even. The stated conclusion is that $\widetilde\Gamma$ always exists in the situation under discussion (LIFT1).

**Scan 16 · Invariant forms.** Three complexes on $G^{p+1}/G$ are compared. In the last complex the differential in the $q$ direction vanishes. The sheet identifies the $E_2$ terms, states degeneration and defines a map on transgressive elements by $\varphi(T)=T\otimes1-1\otimes T$. Its theorem presents $H^*(B_G,\mathbb R)$ as a symmetric algebra. The right edge clips part of the long cohomology display.

**Scan 17 · Cancelled letter on $C(X,A)$.** The writer says that the category detects components, their fundamental groups and cohomology with twisted coefficients. He asks how much more of the homotopy type it determines and which exact functors come from maps of spaces. Loop spaces, classifying spaces, suspensions and products are proposed tests. A handwritten addition starts with « squelettes » and includes the higher homotopy groups. The whole fragment is cancelled.

**Scan 18 · Five conditions at a prime.** Conditions (i) and (ii) concern $p$-torsion in $G$ and $B_G$. Condition (iii) concerns the characteristic map for $G/B$. The two bis conditions describe exterior and polynomial algebras over $\mathbb F_p$. The text supplies implications and the bottom diagram collects them (IMP1).

## Remarks of the model

- **Model · CHAR1 · high.** With the weight rule $e_a e_b=e_{a+b}$, squaring $e_1+e_{-1}$ and subtracting two leaves $e_2+e_{-2}$. This checks the last character line on 7 without deciding the convention behind the separately boxed superscript $(2)$.
- **Model · EUL1 and LIFT1 · high for the arithmetic.** The same integer serves two purposes on 11 and 13. Multiplication by a positive cover degree is injective on $\mathbb Z$, which justifies the last cancellation on 11. Both coefficients of $2-2g$ vanish modulo two, as needed for the parity step on 13. The geometric identifications are inputs to these checks.
- **Model · REAL1 · low for the handwritten numeral.** As real bundles the two sides of the marginal formula on 9 have ranks $2\cdot2$ and $2+2\cdot1$. Both give four. The inserted rank on 13 lies across a leader and another line of writing, so this calculation cannot settle its reading.
