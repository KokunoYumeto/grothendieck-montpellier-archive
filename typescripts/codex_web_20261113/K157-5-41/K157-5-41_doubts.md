## Legibility

The large class symbols, the numbered displays and the sequence of manuscript pages are generally clear. The short horizontal connectors vary from a plain dash to a stroke with an identifiable arrowhead. The dense diagonal note on scan 637 crosses the beginning of the theorem and several earlier strokes. Its question, the words beside the quotient condition and the tiny insertion at the foot are the least secure readings. The cancelled corollary on 634, the reworked proof on 641 and the overwritten numeral on 646 also deserve close comparison with the images. The last sentence on 647 runs to the bottom of the supplied extract and ends at « construire ».
