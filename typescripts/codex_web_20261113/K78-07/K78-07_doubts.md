## Legibility

The typescript on scan 94 gives unusually clear evidence for spellings, including its extra grave accent and the doubled d in the postscript. Its signature cannot be read letter by letter. Most main-line French on the other sheets is legible. The least secure places are the hatched calculations on scan 93, the coordinate-shift signs on scans 95–96, the ends of compressed words, and small indices pressed against a preceding letter. Rotation helps with the note on scan 98. Enlargement reveals the extent of the ink in the solid patches but cannot recover the covered strokes.
