## Legibility

The large diagrams and most vertex lists can be followed from more than one occurrence. The fast prose has long intervals where several letters have collapsed into one stroke. The least secure readings are the old term beneath cocartésien on 282, the cancelled oblique note on 286, the overlapping annotations on 290 and 293, and the last ho label on 296. On 287–288 the replaced indices are less certain than the live indices in the earlier lists. Short arrow shafts can look like dashes. Running prose and superscripts with doubt marks identify places where a new reading from the photographs would matter most.
