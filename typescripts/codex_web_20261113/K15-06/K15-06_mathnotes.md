# Mathematical notes · cote 15, K15-06

## What the pages do

**Finite fields and Tate twists (172–173).** The title on 172 names the absolute cohomology of Tate motives. On 173 the generator $f$ acts on a finite-type $\mathbb Z_\ell$-module $M$. The first table writes $H^0$ as a kernel, $H^1$ as a cokernel and the higher groups as zero. For $M=\mathbb Z_\ell(n)$ the writer separates $n=0$, divisibility of $1-q^n$ by $\ell$, and nondivisibility. A marginal note defines the order of $q$ modulo $\ell$.

**Changing coefficients and restricting to an open (173–174).** Three braces on 173 give rational $\ell$-adic, rational motivic and integral answers. The page ends with two tables for $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z$ and a restriction map for $n\ne0$. Scan 174 draws a point $x$, writes a pullback with twist $(-1)$ and shift $[-2]$, then works out the localization sequence for $X$ and $U$. The final two cases are $n\ne1$ and $n=1$. In the second, two terms are written $\mathbb Q^S$.

**A crossed calculation of $H^1$ (175).** The upper part starts from an inverse limit of finite-coefficient groups and distinguishes trivial action from action through a finite quotient. Below, a calculation with $\varphi(xy)=\varphi(x)+x\varphi(y)$ follows positive and negative powers of the generator. The right column identifies cocycles and coboundaries and writes $H^1(\mathbb Z,M)$ as a quotient by $(1-f)M$. The cancellations include the entire sheet.

**The Brauer group (176).** The page relates vanishing for the divisor sheaf to two injective maps into the cohomology of the generic point. Proposition 5.4 and Corollaire 5.5 identify the map relevant to $\mathrm{Br}(X)$; the following paragraph places it in the Brauer group of the function field $K=k(y)$. Théorème 5.6(a) then gives a local criterion in terms of annihilating a degree-two class by a finite étale surjective morphism.

**Cohomology with support and the groups on the open (177–178).** Scan 177 records the local sheaf groups at $x$, the groups with support in $S$, and the resulting localization sequences. Values are written beneath individual terms, with “somme des inv.” beneath one arrow. Scan 178 collects the groups on $\bar U$ for $\mathrm G_m$, then writes the sequence with $\mu_{\ell^n}$ and its finite-coefficient and $\ell$-adic consequences. The last brace gives four answers on $X$ in twist one, in degrees zero through three.

**Divisors and torsion (179; closing title on 180).** In the crossed block the generic-point spectral sequence leads to a torsion statement for $H^p(X,\mathrm R_X^*)$, $p>0$. The live paragraph gives the divisor sequence under an interlinear hypothesis containing “sans idéaux premiers immergés”. It identifies the connecting homomorphism modulo torsion. The sheet breaks off as the writer introduces an isomorphism of sheaves under a factoriality hypothesis. Scan 180 repeats the Tate topic on a title sheet.

**Real motivic points (182–183).** The new title introduces distribution of Frobenius in motivic Galois groups. On 183 the writer chooses a category of geometric motives with even degrees and a real motivic point. A closed point is supplemented with a map of a preferred type. A resulting pair $s'$ determines a class in $H^1$ over $\mathbb R$ and a map to a twist of $G_{\mathbb R}$. The last line specifies compatibility and the conjugation ambiguity of that map.
