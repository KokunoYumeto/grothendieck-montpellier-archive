### Cross-page reconciliation

The same plain T is used for Tate modules on scans 56–66; the angular Γ on scan 59 is kept distinct. H and R in cohomology/functor notation are upright, while coefficient Q, Z and projective-space P retain their visible double strokes. The centre Z on scan 65 remains plain. No script T or calligraphic alphabet has been supplied from the mathematics.

ξ/ζ is treated consistently on scans 53–63 and 65–66; η/y is separately unresolved on scans 63–64. The bar on I in the initial definition on scan 53 is not propagated to later unbarred occurrences. Upper/lower ab, the left torsion subscript n, E versus E_ℓ, the changing Tate-module arguments A(ξ)/A_ξ, prime counts, and unheaded connectors have been preserved locally. The compact-support readings q/c on scan 50 and !/c on scan 51 are deliberately not forced into one conventional notation. The notation table and the page audits together record these checks.

## Legibility and limits

All sixteen pages are dense handwritten working notes attributed to Grothendieck by their consistent hand and the supplied context. The main continuous writing is often readable; numerous revisions interrupt the syntax, and short reduced words are much less secure than the overall mathematical organization. The weakest places are the cancelled drafts on 51, the lower/side additions on 56 and 58–59, the boxed title on 62, the slanted note and revised hypothesis on 63, the cancelled opening and remark on 64, and the foot of 66. The large number of doubt marks is intentional. A single physically illegible span, estimated at two words, remains inside cancelled prose on scan 51.

The third images overlap; overlap was not transcribed twice. No OCR, PDF text layer, outside publication, translation, or mathematical correction was used. The source can leave sentences, references, and propositions unfinished: these are retained, not replaced by summaries. Readable struck material is present under deletion macros. The audit records actual revisions and also cases where an initial second-reading impression was rejected on closer inspection. The interruption during scan 65 is disclosed in that audit; elapsed timestamps include that downtime.
