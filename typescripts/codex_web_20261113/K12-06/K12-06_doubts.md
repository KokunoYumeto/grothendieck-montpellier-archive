## Legibility

The dark main formulas are usually clearer than the connecting prose. Reductions of French word endings, the stem through the sign for a space of forms, and the distinction between a weight written p and rho deserve attention. The least secure passages are the crossed beginning and end of the sequence on 82, the edits to the coefficient ring on 90–91 and the diagonally cancelled diagram and sloping note on 93. The cover has unlettered faint marks at its upper left. No words have been supplied inside them.
