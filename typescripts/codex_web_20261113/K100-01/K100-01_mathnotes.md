# K100-01 · Mathematical notes

## What the pages do

**Extension functor (scan 3).** For an open $U$ in $S$ with complement $T$ of residue characteristic $p$, the theorem sends an abelian scheme $A$ to its restriction to $U$, its $T_p(A)$ on $S$, and the identity on the restriction of $T_p(A)$. Meeting every connected component of $S$ is the hypothesis for full faithfulness. The revised equivalence statement puts a finite surjection from a geometrically unibranch scheme alongside the bound $\operatorname{prof\,ét}_T(S)\geq 2$. A long earlier hypothesis on strict localizations is cancelled.

**Alternative hypothesis (scan 3, keyed footnote).** The note forms the first three sets of connected components of $S'$, $S'\times_S S'$ and the triple fibre product. It picks out components whose images meet $U$. Condition b′) asks that closing $K_1$ under the “troisième face” give all of $L_1$. The last line again asserts an equivalence of categories.

**The locus of algebraicity (scan 4).** For a map between $T_\ell(A)$ and $T_\ell(B)$, the lemma specifies a closed subscheme $T$ through which a base change factors exactly when the map comes from a homomorphism of abelian schemes there. The complement discusses points of residue characteristic $\ell$. The corollary applies this to an open $U$ meeting all connected components. The surviving proof shows the locus is open and closed, and uses the component condition to conclude $T=S$.

**Full faithfulness (scan 5).** After the diagram of the functor, the argument separates faithfulness from the extension of a morphism $u_p$ known to be algebraic on $U$. A citation treats schematic density. The general argument passes to the schematic closure $X_0$ of $U$. The long cancelled route passes through the infinitesimal neighbourhoods $T_n$ and a completed ring $\widehat\Lambda$. The live ending invokes a second numbered reference. The right-hand panel considers weakening the density assumption.

**Reduction to a reduced base (scan 7).** Starting with $A_U,M,\varphi$, part a) takes an abelian scheme on the reduced base, applies Serre–Tate on the $T_n$, and obtains a formal abelian scheme over the completion. The notes call for algebraization and then use a descent datum for the cover by $U$ and $S'$. The crossed part b), continued at the top of scan 9, proposes a different reduction through a local scheme.

**The rigidified case (scans 9 and 11).** A Jacobi rigidification gives $U\to R$, where the marginal note also mentions a polarization. Taking the closure of the graph gives $S'\subset S\times R$. The valuative criterion and a result of Raynaud give properness. The remaining argument concerns discrete fibres, the normalization $S''$ and a constant $T_p$ on a fibre. The finite-normalization case is treated first. The treatment of the other case depends explicitly on extending an isomorphism from $U''$.

**Removing the rigidification (scans 11 and 13).** Part c) passes to a finite étale surjection on $U$ carrying level structure, normalizes the base, and compares the two pullbacks on $S''$. Descent of the $\ell$-torsion yields a group object $N$ on $S$. A finite étale cover splits that torsion, and the author descends the projective abelian scheme. Part d) considers a reduced $S$ with a finite surjection from a geometrically unibranch $S'$. Density on the double fibre product and full faithfulness yield a descent datum. The sheet ends while reducing the problem further.

**Categorical quotients (scans 6 and 8).** Folio −0.13− first closes a preceding argument using Zariski’s Main Theorem. Remark (5) compares universality with tests over affine schemes and over an affine open cover. Item (6) gives three sufficient conditions involving invariance of the map, the invariant subsheaf, and images and intersections of closed invariant sets. The proof on −0.14− uses complements of the $W_i$ and an affine open cover of the target of a competing invariant morphism. The oblique pencil note on scan 6 recasts the question in terms of two arrows out of $R$.

**Base change for quotients (scans 8 and 10).** Item (7) distinguishes the first three conditions of Definition 0.6 from condition (iv). The latter is expressed by an exact sequence of sheaves. The text uses flat base change to retain its exactness. Item (8) states the converse descent assertion after faithfully flat, quasi-compact base change for each of the three quotient properties listed there. The sentence divided as “pre-” and “served” joins the two typed folios.

**Kinds of actions (scan 10).** Definition 0.8 treats closed orbits, closed image of $(\sigma,p_2)$, properness of this morphism, and the condition called “free”. A pencil oval singles out “a closed immersion”. The two neighbouring pencil annotations read « monomorphisme » and « proper and free » within a comment on terminology. The following paragraph begins the construction of the stabilizer of $1_X$.

**Errata (scan 12).** The opening fragment relates depth one, homological dimension $r-1$ and vanishing of an Ext group over a regular ring. The list then amends statements about locally free modules, properness, singularities, transcendence degree and a nonfree locus on two lines. The last two instructions delete three monomials and change 12 to 4.

**An action with a geometric quotient (scan 14).** Evaluating the quartic at $(-\beta,\alpha)$ gives the displayed map associated with the pair $(\alpha X+\beta Y,F_4)$. The text claims a geometric quotient by $\mathrm{SL}(2)$. A one-parameter family with $\lambda\ne 0$ and its displayed image supply an extra point in the closure, used to show that the action morphism fails to be closed.

**Properness criterion (scan 14).** Lemma 0.5 concerns a $\mathbb G_m$-action on an algebraic scheme admitting an immersion in projective space to which the action extends. The stated conditions are separatedness and $S_1(X)=\phi$, retaining the form of the final sign. The converse begins with a valuation ring and its quotient field. The supplied folio ends mid-sentence at “point”.

**R1 · Model remark, high confidence (scan 14).** Substituting $\lambda=0$ in the two *image* coordinates gives $(Y,X^2Y^2)$ twice, in agreement with the displayed extra point. This calculation uses only the two polynomial expressions. The original matrix contains $\lambda^{-1}$ and is defined here for nonzero $\lambda$.

**R2 · Model reading question, low confidence about authorial intention (scans 3 and 5).** At the start of the theorem the frozen reading is « couples $(A_U,M,\varphi)$ ». The diagram on scan 5 uses « triples » and shows the same three entries. « triples » is a possible intended word on scan 3. The first two strokes in the actual word there give no reason to replace « couples ». In the same diagram on scan 5 the line for $M$ ends « sur $T$ », where scan 3 has « sur $S$ ». The high crossbar supports $T$; whether the author intended $S$ here remains open.

In Lemma 0.5 on scan 14 the same shape follows $S_1(X)=$. An empty-set sign is a possible interpretation, particularly where the open sets cover $X$ on scan 8. The vertical stroke resembles other inserted phi’s. This does not establish a change of notation or an error.

The capital and lowercase type are distinct and the surrounding source text is absent, so this remains a question about the intended fields.
