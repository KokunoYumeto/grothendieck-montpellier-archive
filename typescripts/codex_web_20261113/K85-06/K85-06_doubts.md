## Legibility

The most doubtful material is in the worked and reworked calculations on scans 72–73, the narrow angled qualifications on scan 76, and two heavily inked groups on scan 80, L7. Those groups contain an estimated five and two words. The diagonal cancellations across scan 72 span more than one calculation. The inventory classifies the whole leaf as a cancelled draft. The LaTeX marks local cancellations individually and does not enclose the full sheet in a single deletion. On scans 83–85, the feet on capitals, the underlined generators, and the difference between a dot and a small zero matter even when they occupy only a few pixels. The light counts on the two covers may be in a different hand.
