# Legibility and limits

The main mathematical letters and larger headings are generally clearer than the reduced French connecting words. Small suffixes, cancellations, oblique notes and retraced numerals are substantially less secure. The greatest remaining uncertainties are the compatibility glyph, pol/pré suffix, the short Axiomes margin heading on scan 030, the starred marginal label on scan 032, and the overwritten page number and footer references on scan 033. Some faint small punctuation and final letters remain explicitly doubtful. No word was declared physically unreadable: tentative readings were preferred, including for crossed words. That is not a claim that those readings are correct. The TikZ pictures preserve the visible components, points and gaps but are not facsimile pen traces. All eight scans received a fresh crop reading and comparison; actual timings, changes, and the supplemental notation review are recorded in the audit.
