## Legibility

Grothendieck’s running hand is consistent enough to distinguish the numerals, arrows and principal terms on most lines. The slanted additions on 070, 073, 074, 075, 078 and 081 need to be read along their own baselines. The least secure passages are the right-hand qualification on 073, the last lines of the cancelled argument on 074, the overwritten fibre indices on 079, the small struck word above équiv. on 080 and the overlapping note on the right of 081. The latter also has an obscure parenthesis next to the ordered model. High ticks on cohomology H and weakly pointed horizontal strokes remain uncertain even when the mathematical relation is recognizable.
