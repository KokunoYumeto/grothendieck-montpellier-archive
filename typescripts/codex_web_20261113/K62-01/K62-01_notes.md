# Notes on the sheets

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **Scan 052, two bracketed reminders.** The author writes “expliquer chaque fois …” beside the Brauer-scheme passage and “à expliquer en général, chaque fois que …” beside the barycentric section. The latter asks for the construction when the degree is invertible. Both are explicit reminders to supply an explanation. Some words within the reminders are doubtful.
- **Scan 053, proof of the corollary.** “Prop. … ?? du chap. I, § ??” leaves the proposition and section unresolved. The question marks refer to the citation.
- **Scan 061, boundary condition in theorem III.** “en supposant tjrs que sur les bords --” breaks off before giving the condition. The last paragraph of scan 060 discusses choosing sides that avoid the zeros and poles.
- **Scan 067, after (4).** “On montrerait facilement l'uniformité” states a demonstration that could be given. Later on the sheet “facile à vérifier” is followed by two suggestions for checking the displayed product identity.

The isolated question marks used as editorial readings in the TeX have no bearing on this list.
