## Legibility

The typewritten prose is generally clear enough to distinguish spelling, low punctuation and interword blanks. Several symbols were added to it by hand. Scans 52 and 54 have exceptionally compressed connecting words; the corrections and the rotated margin on 54 are the least secure prose. The crossed corner diagram, hatched filtration and altered superscripts on 57 are the least secure mathematics. The later brown-ink sheets have clear large formulas and much less distinct indices, terminal letters and signs underneath overwriting. All quoted French and mathematical readings in the comparison below are local readings. Confidence measures the surviving ink at that place.
