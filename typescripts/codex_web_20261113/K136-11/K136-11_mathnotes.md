# Mathematical notes · K136-11

## What the pages do

**U1 · Functors and cohomology (111, 113).** The manuscript asks for contravariant functors on the category written Ss, with values in bigraded differential algebras carrying divided powers. It singles out those taking the indicated direct limits to inverse limits and describes them by the objects \(C_n^{**}\). To \(C\) it associates a De Rham cohomology functor. The admissibility discussion asks that a truncation come from a \(k\)-module and displays a tensor product with \(S_k\).

**U2 · The two comparison maps (113, 115).** Regarding \(C\) as a sheaf gives an augmentation from the constant sheaf \(S_k\). A horizontal map starts with derived sections of \(S_k\). A vertical map brings ordinary sections of \(C\) into the same target. The leaf numbered 115 asks for the two maps to be isomorphisms after applying the indicated operation and translates this into a condition on kernels and cokernels.

**U3 · Evaluation on a simplex (117, 119).** On \(\Delta_n\) the upward arrow in the first diagram is labelled by an identity. The author separates the requirement on the augmentation into a degree-zero comparison and a vanishing condition in positive \(p\) for large \(i\). The next leaf considers the other comparison by asking at which \(p\) a half-simplicial group ceases to be flasque, and how this first degree, \(\pi(i)\), varies with \(i\).

**U4 · Comparing admissible theories (119, 121).** The author proposes to invert maps between the candidate theories. He asks about the groupoid this generates. Part a) seeks a third theory mapping to two given ones. Part b) asks for a morphism which “égalise” a parallel pair. The margin brings a right calculus of fractions into consideration.

**U5 · Coalgebra data (123).** A \(k\)-coalgebra \(s\), a subcoalgebra \(\bar s\), their quotient \(s^+\), a system of divided co-operations, and a \(k\)-linear datum \(t\) are set out in the left column. The right column evaluates the data on \(k'\), identifies an ideal and gives maps dual to the divided powers. The boxed formula has target \(\operatorname{Sym}_k^i(s^+)\). The page then describes a simplicial object with graded differential algebras and a divided-power ideal in its values.

**U6 · An explicit calculation (125).** The page evaluates \(A\) on \(k'\), writes a divided-power expression tensored with an exterior algebra and imposes relations on the sums of the \(X_i\) and \(dX_i\). The next displayed expression uses variables only through \(n-1\). The subsequent comparisons rewrite the result as powers indexed by multi-indices and subsets, then as a Hom functor. The lower arrows relate it to \(V(\bar s)\); the final tensor expression uses capital \(Y\)'s and lower-case \(y\)'s.

**U7 · Construction and descent of a monodromy pairing (126, 124).** In the order of the typescript's own pages, the author starts with the three-step filtration of the Tate module and two identifications of extensions. Remarque 9.7 passes to a finite Galois extension giving semistable reduction. The continued remark divides the descended pairing by \(e(S'/S)\), discusses independence of the extension and of \(\ell\), and asks about integral values. In the elliptic case it identifies the ordinary rational number in question with \(v(j)\).

**U8 · Transport and symmetry (116, 114, 112).** Section 10 announces an integrality and positivity theorem and the formal properties leading to it. After transport of structure, the biduality isomorphism lets the author express the relation between the pairing for \(A_K\) and the one for its dual by switching the two factors. A polarisation gives a symmetric form. A third formulation uses a biextension of a pair of abelian schemes and its symmetric biextension. For the Weil biextension the author identifies the resulting form with the monodromy pairing.

**U9 · Two letters (118, 120).** A German letter opens “Liebe Gertrud,” and stops in the middle of “dreiwöch”. In the French letter dated 14 September 1971 the writer tells Monsieur Poncet that the left margin stop and ribbon winding on an Hermès 3000 have failed. He cancels a further order and asks that the machine be taken back if it has already arrived. These two leaves supply no mathematical assertions.

## Remarks of the model

- **R1 · High confidence · 112.** The domains in (10.2.8) give a direct check on the order of composition. The source of \(s\) is the tensor product printed for the symmetric biextension. Its target is the source of \(\varphi_\xi^t\) in (10.2.7). Thus \(\varphi_\xi^t\circ s\) has the displayed domain. This checks the types in the equality. The equality of the two pairings is the author's assertion.
- **R2 · High confidence · 112, 114, 116, 124, 126.** The printed folios give a reading order for IX independent of scan order. Scans 126 and 124 join in the middle of the construction in Remarque 9.7. Scan 116 opens section 10, scan 114 supplies the formula announced at the foot of 116, and scan 112 continues the discussion of the biextension introduced at the foot of 114. Folios 119–123 and the lower labels 437–441 increase together in that order.
- **R3 · Low confidence · 113, L15.** A reading question concerns the raised stroke between the left \(\mathbb R\) and \(\Gamma\) in (1). The frozen formula attaches the doubtful star to \(\Gamma\). The alternative attaches it to \(\mathbb R\), as in the other upper node. The strokes at the junction of the left capitals overlap too closely to decide.
- **R4 · Low confidence · 111, L9–10.** The frozen text reads “homogènes” followed by the doubtful group “ties|tées”. An alternative construction joins the end of the first line to the second as one adjective, “augmentées”. The right end of the first line is overwritten. The uncertainty affects the list of structures in the target category.
- **R5 · Medium confidence · 117, 119.** The last line of 117 asks for independence from \(n\), whereas (4) on 119 asks for growth of \(\pi(i)\) as \(i\) grows. These are two different quantifiers in the manuscript. The surviving text does not establish either from the other.
