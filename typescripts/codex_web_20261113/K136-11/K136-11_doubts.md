## Legibility

The five leaves of IX are consistently readable. Overtyping in the Massy letter affects individual letter positions. The German fragment is clear up to its last h. In the handwritten notes the small subscript on H (111), the end of the parenthesis about uniqueness (113), the threshold in 3 a) (117), and the tightly interlined NB (121) are particularly insecure. On 123 the two columns exchange several insertions. On 125 several superscripts, the right-edge description of the ideal and the crossed letter denoting subsets deserve inspection in the original. Some tentative readings of cancelled text have only a surviving first or last stroke. Zero physically illegible-word placeholders does not imply that every word was deciphered.
