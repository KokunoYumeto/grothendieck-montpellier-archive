## Legibility

The open text on scans 23 and 24 and the large formulas can generally be followed stroke by stroke. The slanted note and crossing arrows on scan 19, the struck products on scans 20 and 21, and the overwritten letters in scan 25 carry the most uncertainty. On scan 21 one blotted word before *que* has no recoverable contour. A few lines reach the paper edge. Accents, compressed word endings and the earlier layers of corrections have their own entries above.
