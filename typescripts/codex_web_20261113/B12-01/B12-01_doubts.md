# Legibility

The live typescript is generally clear, but right-edge clipping, displaced characters, overtyping and the broad cancellation strokes prevent equal confidence everywhere. The least secure readings are the heavily x-overwritten abandoned phrases on scans 97–98, the faint handwriting and slanted marginal note on scan 69, the compressed marginal handwriting on scan 81, and the pale pencil insertion on scan 100. These places retain proposed readings and explicit alternatives rather than reconstructed wording.

The broad cancellations, local corrections and source blanks have been retained. No symbol was supplied in an unfilled operator or arrow slot. Alphabet distinctions follow the visible strokes: double underlining, a separate single underline and a double-struck letter are not treated as interchangeable. The second audit corrected several confident first-pass readings; remaining uncertainty is recorded in the text and tables. No OCR, text-recognition library, published transcription or outside source was used. There are no `\illegible` placeholders; this means a tentative reading was attempted everywhere, not that every reading is secure.
