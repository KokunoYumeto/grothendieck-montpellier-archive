## Decisions after comparison at marked places

The open, descending weight was settled as rho on 15, 17, 19 and 21 by comparing its bowl and tail to the base p on the same leaves. This reading does not extend to a hatched letter that lacks the surviving bowl. The normalized function on 15 has the two-turn head of xi. The short odd-parity subject on 23 includes the dot of the subscript i after epsilon. The full locations, including the weight positions retained in crossed expressions, are recorded in the witness table.
