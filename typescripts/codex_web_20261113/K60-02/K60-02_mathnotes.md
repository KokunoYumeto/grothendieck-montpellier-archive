# Mathematical notes on cote 60, scans 14–24

## What the pages do

**14 · Editorial decisions.** Bourbaki assigns the space of closed subgroups to the integration chapter, trims the draft on distributions, and asks Bruhat to work from Koszul’s text for the Lie groups chapter. A December seminar programme follows.

**15 · The normalized function.** Setting $\xi_E(t)=t^{\chi(E)/2}L_E(t)$ leaves a sign $\varepsilon'(E)$ in the functional equation. Pairing degrees $i$ and $2n-i$ singles out the middle-degree determinant.

**16 · Dual and bidual.** The counter-draft defines $E^*=\operatorname{Hom}(E,K/A)$ and the evaluation map $i_E:E\to E^{**}$. Proposition 1 distinguishes the generally noncanonical isomorphism with $E^*$ from the evaluation isomorphism.

**16 · Proof.** Reduction to $E=A/tA$ identifies $E^*$ with $t^{-1}A/A$. Under the resulting bidual identification, evaluation is the identity.

**17 · Parity.** An invariant alternating form gives the positive sign in odd weight. In even weight the signs are expressed through eigenvalue multiplicities. Without global twisted self-duality, the equation can relate two functions, $L_{DE}$ and $L_E$.

**18 · Programme and errata.** The table assigns reports and dates. One erratum exchanges left and right, and another supplies a new footnote about homological algebra.

**19 · Duality.** The note exchanges the two direct images, retaining the twist and shift. Passing to classes and then to $L$-functions introduces the term at infinity. The last argument runs to the edge of the sheet.

**21 · Functional equation.** The boxed identity separates $A(t)$, $\delta(E)$ and $(-t)^{\chi(E)}$. It gives bounds for the term at infinity and sets $A(t)=1$ when $X$ is complete.

**21 · Determinants.** Comparing the formulas with proper supports and with ordinary cohomology gives $\delta(E)^2=p^{(n+\rho)\chi(E)}$ for complete $X$.

**23 · Motives.** Virtual Betti numbers express $\chi(E)$ as an alternating sum. The determinant splits into a product of signs and a power of $p$ with exponent $\frac12\sum_i(-1)^i\,i\,\beta_i(E)$.

**24 · Exercise 2.** Products of ideals and primary decomposition give formulas for $\chi$. A final criterion concerns pseudo-invertibility.

**24 and 22 · Exercise 3.** The different, codifferent and conductor are related by localization. Part 3e continues at the head of 22.

**22 · Exercise 4.** A socle criterion for irreducible primary ideals leads to tests for pseudo-invertibility and equality of height-one localizations.

**22 and 20 · Exercise 5.** The proposed criterion for invertibility of the codifferent in every order is $[K:Q]\leq2$. The hint continues on 20 with $C=Z+Bp$.

**20 · Exercise 6.** Three local conditions compare the different, residue field extensions and ramification.

**20 · Exercise 7.** The parts concern the stability of a power basis, a generator satisfying a conductor condition, and monogenicity in the completely split case.

## Remarks of the model
