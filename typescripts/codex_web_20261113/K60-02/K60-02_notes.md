# Notes on the leaves

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **14.** “pourquoi pas les limites inductives et projectives ??” is an editorial question about examples. The “mythique chapitre ultérieur” is a projected reference. Neither is a proof supplied on this leaf.
- **17.** “on ne peut déterminer le signe de façon absolue” states the limit of the even-parity calculation. The manuscript specifies what it can still determine from the multiplicities.
- **18.** The handwritten question mark follows “Rapport sur Mlle Bastiani”. “Rapport sur les Probas (?)” has its own typed question mark. Both concern the programme.
- **21.** The framed marginal qualification reads “en admettant la résolution des singularités ... et les conjectures de Weil”. The last paragraph says the formula is “bien compatible” with those conjectures. Its stated role is compatibility.
- **23.** The computation invokes “conj. de Weil”. The integrality note sits next to the determinant formula.
- **20, 22, 24.** Exercise prompts and blank cross-references occur on the reused sheets. They do not have authorial question marks checking a proof.
