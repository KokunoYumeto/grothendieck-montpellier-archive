## Legibility

The typed decisions and exercises have a stable baseline. The low round subscripts on 24 and the stroke across q on 22 are much less secure than their surrounding words. Most live prose and uncorrected display lines on the handwritten leaves can be followed. The crossed quotation on 15, the eigenspace wording on 17, the factors under hatching on 19 and 21 and the first deletion on 23 carry the greatest risk. The right edge of 19 removes any reliable continuation. Repeated weights and paired bars were compared across the five handwritten leaves; at genuinely indistinguishable occurrences the table records the alternatives.
