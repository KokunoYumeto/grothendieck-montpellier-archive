# Editorial notes · cote 136, scans 002–016

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

| Scan | Mark or wording on the sheet | What it qualifies |
|---|---|---|
| 003–004 | “Poincaré ?”, “Conjecturé par É. Cartan”, “Bloch (?)”, then “(sauf erreur)” | The first three concern the historical survey and its names. The inserted reservation on 004 attaches to the precise categorical statement of Sullivan’s result. |
| 005 | “Cela serait itou”, “On aimerait avoir”, “Mais il paraît qu'il y a des ths contre !” | Grothendieck proposes extending the acyclicity argument to other forms and seeking an alternating integral model. He gives no reference for the reported obstruction. |
| 006–007 | “on ne voit pas comment envoyer”, then “Mais on ne peut dire en général […] quelle est sa cohomologie” | The first difficulty motivates replacing 1 by a parameter with divided powers. For general coefficients the second passage provides a comparison map. An earlier formulation beginning “Donc a) est acquis. Mais faute de b)” is struck. |
| 010–011 | “Mais quelle est (pour $q$ fixé) la structure”, “Ce qui donne un espoir”, “Cette […] théorie des ombres […] étant supposée acquise” | The first question receives a truncation formula. The recovery of homotopical information then rests on the proposed reconstruction from shadows. |
| 012 a | “[et plus gén. encore, si $(S,J,t)$ comme au début” and a marginal question mark, possibly doubled | The question mark stands alongside the bracketed generalization to a divided-power triple. It does not stand beside the preceding specializations. |
| 012 b–013 | “Je suis convaincu que la structure à p.d. […] est importante”, “se poser la question”, “et on peut se demander si sa restriction […] induit une équivalence” | The integral homotopy-type and equivalence questions are open here. The finiteness condition on 012 is a slanted addition. “Il n'est pas exclu que” is struck. |
| 014 c–d | “n'ai que des résultats partiels négatifs”, “Il faudrait sans doute chercher des modèles minimaux à la Sullivan”, “Je n'ai rien fait dans cette direction” | These qualifications concern cohomology operations, minimal models and homotopy groups. Grothendieck also says he has not developed a Künneth formula and questions the flatness needed for it. |
| 015 e | “Il est possible de […] de définir une cobigèbre”, “C'est lui qu'il conviendrait de considérer” | The first phrase introduces a proposed chain object. The second recommends its integral form for questions b–d without finiteness conditions. |
| 016 g | “On aimerait”, “mais y en a-t-il tjrs ?”, “le Pb est ouvert -- et posé par Deligne”, “Mais sauf erreur (si souvenirs sont exacts)” | The existence of a suitable replacement with flasque components is questioned. The last qualification attaches to a recollection about paracompact topological spaces. |
