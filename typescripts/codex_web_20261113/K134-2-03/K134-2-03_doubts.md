## Legibility

The body of the letter is generally readable. Faint right-hand edges cut individual letters on several sheets. The lower edges of 107 and 115 cut through type. Crossed-out typewriter words, letters written onto an earlier impression, and index-sized 1/l and o/0 characters are less secure than the surrounding prose. The letter forms do not settle every category subscript. The strongest pen marks are often the correction itself, with little of the earlier word left underneath.

On 128 the right-hand strip is dark, but most of the central impression is pale reverse-side printing. Character spacing and the six repeated detail lines help distinguish fields. Banner widths, the leading time and code characters, and the partly covered bottom identifier are the least secure readings. All twelve banner rows and all six data rows are represented.
