## Legibility

The ascenders and descenders on the six handwritten sheets often survive more clearly than the short strokes between them. On scan 11, the first correction and the last superscript are faint. On scans 13–14 the resolution letters and the overwritten groups in the middle display on 14 require the most caution. On scan 15 some word endings run into the right edge; on 16 the cancellation near *en principe* obscures the ending of the verb.

The typescript has recurring breaks in the same parts of the letter shapes. The date figures on 17 are legible, including the 6, but the last impression in the place name is patchy. The accents over Buu, the overwritten name initial and the short marks among the words of the postscript are especially hard to classify. The individual letters of the signature on 18 can be followed. Where only part of a word survived, the transcription could still give at least one reading.
