## Legibility

Most type can be followed from one line to the next. Some right edges cut into the last word, notably on scans 9, 14 and 16. A typewriter x often leaves a lower loop or top of a stem visible while obliterating the rest of the letter. The least secure continuous passages are the diagonal Picard note on scan 8, the struck handwritten ending on scan 11, the clipped condition on the generators on scan 14, and the note crossing the left edge of scan 17. An estimate of one unreadable word is used twice. It measures the damaged space and does not establish how many characters were there. The four directions of the base-change diamond on scan 12 are clearer than the adjacent prime and dot in type. Several other joins retain a plain stroke as the first candidate where the image does not show a distinct arrowhead.
