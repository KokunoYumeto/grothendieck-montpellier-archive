## Steps the writer marks as unverified

- **14, opening of the letter.** “I have become aware that I have been a bit rash with the conjectures I told you about Hodge structures”. He identifies the difficulty at “they contradict Tates conjecture”. The possessive mark in the name is uncertain.
- **15, continuation and questions to Mumford.** “(one hopes!)” qualifies the words before the first quoted “algebraic”. “one should try to test if one really cannot get an algebraic” precedes the family sign. “I wonder if a single Hodge-structure” introduces the question with the inserted exception about the group. “For instance, do you know an algebraic smooth projective surface” asks Mumford for an example subject to the two written cohomology conditions.
- **16.** None explicitly marked as open. The last line qualifies the asserted construction with “at least when one fixes a ‘total degree’”, continuing on scan 17.
- **17–18, conditions on the data.** “The modular varieties I expect to carry algebraic structure over” precedes the field sign. “it is possible that one will have to impose on the data some extra conditions”, followed by arithmetic and geometric possibilities. “I have no evidence so far if” introduces the question whether the quotient “has an algebro-geometric interpretation in terms of moduli for motives”, with “moduli for” inserted above the opening lines on scan 18. In scan 18 the particular positivity condition is left unstated at “which I will not write down”.
- **18–19, algebraicity of a family.** “The trouble is that,” introduces the passage that continues through “if the ‘modular family’ … was ‘algebraic’”. On 19 the words “If we admit the GAGA yoga” and the inserted “(or the above strengthening of the original assumption)” qualify the reasoning. It ends “So I really do not know what to believe!”
- **20, opening and the cancellation.** “(granting Tate's and Hodge's conjectures)” qualifies the condition on the subgroup. The crossed paragraph begins “I did not try to test directly this condition, even in the case of the usual Siegel modular space”. Its conclusion about a conjecture being wrong is also inside the cancellation.
- **20, independent questions.** The note beside the cancellation asks whether the integral points are “always Zariski-dense” for the group described there. The last paragraph asks whether the image discussed in the Boulder talk “is an algebraic variety, or at least constructible?”
