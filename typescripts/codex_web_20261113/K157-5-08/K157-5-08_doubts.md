## Legibility

The ordinary text, most section numbers and the completed square diagrams can usually be followed across the page. Fine rules, dotted diagonals, primes at two different heights and words pressed into the margins need separate examination. The cancelled names in (6.2.3) on scan 119, the last few strokes of the diagonal note on scan 117, the overwritten subscript in the calculation on scan 128 and the competing layers of numerals on the cover are the least secure readings. Some right-facing strokes end with a tiny square terminal rather than a distinguishable arrowhead. Alternative readings in the tables record that distinction.
