## Title sheets, dates and page counts

Scan 002 is an undated cover. Its handwritten heading reads “Pages 1-90 + table des matiéres” (also “matières”). It gives a page range and adds the table of contents with a plus sign. A separate pencilled 1 at the lower left is the archive sheet number.
