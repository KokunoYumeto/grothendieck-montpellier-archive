## Legibility

The handwriting is clear enough to distinguish the range, the plus and the final s of each plural. The direction of the single accent is the least secure detail. Turning and reflecting the faint paper-stock letters makes their outlines legible down to the left half of the final T. The upper inscription is unsigned, and this sheet supplies no secure basis for naming its writer. The inventory uses `typed` for the mechanically formed stock lettering.
