- **Scan 222, after (98), cancelled draft.** “Je vais faire bon poids d'hypothèses, quitte à raffiner par la suite” introduces provisional hypotheses about a connected, orientable 2-polyhedron and transitivity on its frames. The two diagonal strokes cancel this discussion, including (7) and (8).
- **Scan 229, after (37).** “qui n'est plus injective en général” is qualified by the insertion “mais qui l'est souvent.” The sheet gives no criterion for “souvent” at that point. It then explicitly assumes a free action for the ensuing calculation. The numerical criterion appears on the next sheet.
- **Scan 233, paragraph before (48).** Of the intersections of two stars, “il faudrait l'étudier systématiquement” proposes further work, with the rank-two free-module case given as an example. The small overwritten mark immediately after “systématiquement” is uncertain.

There is no explicit date or signature of a later verification on these sheets. The warning “n'est pas néc. un sous-groupe” on scan 227 is a qualification of a claim, rather than a note that a proof still needs checking.
