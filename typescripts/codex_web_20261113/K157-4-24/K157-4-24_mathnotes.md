# Mathematical notes

The references below are scan numbers. Quoted French is from the sheets. Where a word or glyph is doubtful, its alternatives are in the transcription and the readings table.

## What the pages do

**363 · A proposed smaller localizer.** Grothendieck asks whether the least localizer satisfying his list of stability axioms can differ from $\mathbb W_\infty$. He calls the candidate $\widetilde{\mathbb W}_\infty$, lists the axioms, and comments on dependencies among them.

**364–365 · A test using simple homotopy.** He proposes interpreting the corresponding Hot category in terms of Whitehead's simple homotopy types. The proposed test takes a finite simplicial model to its ordered set of simplices and asks how that object behaves under an elementary operation. The marginal comments revisit both the invariant and the sense of homotopy being used. The next sheet proposes defining the elementary operations directly in Cat or in the subcategory of ordered sets, qualified by the insertion « (petits) ».

**365–367 · Two factorization axioms.** Section c distinguishes the existence of factorizations for a category with fibrations, Loc(7F), from a condition that makes the specific canonical factorization $f=pi$ usable for computing $W$-homotopy fibres. The roles of $i$ and $p$ are given separately. The application concerns fibres over points and change of base by a morphism. An intersection question arises because the weaker axiom asserts existence.

**368–369 · Subdivision and two notions of homotopy.** The writer reconsiders the Whitehead proposal. He uses simplicial approximation to motivate a question about subdivisions. For a finite ordered set $I$ he forms the ordered set of its totally ordered parts and sends each part to its greatest element. He asks for a monotone homotopy inverse in the other direction.

**369, 371–372 · The boundary example and a new proposal.** The sentence beginning at the foot of 369 continues with the boundary of the 2-simplex on 371. Grothendieck draws the boundary and its subdivision, each in triangular and round form. He reports that the realization of every increasing map in the reverse direction is homotopic to a constant. This distinction renews his interest in defining the Whitehead invariant on a category of models. He also suggests higher invariants involving $\pi_i$.

**373–374 · A programme for derivators.** Section 7 opens with questions about Brown's fibration and cofibration formalism, existence of functors attached to a map, exactness of squares and the axioms satisfied by the predérivateur. A supplementary question asks whether $W$ belongs to a closed Quillen triple.

**374–377 · Cofibrations and the left-hand structure.** Sections 7.2–7.3 connect Loc(6a) with cofibrations, Dwyer immersions and a functorial mapping-cone factorization. They discuss the construction of $f_!$ and the integral of the fork $X_0\to X_1,\ X_0\to X_2$. Grothendieck assigns different hypotheses to existence and to exactness for cocartesian squares. He explicitly revisits the hypotheses in chapter XII.

**377–378 · Fibrations and the right-hand structure.** Section 7.4 returns to Loc(7F), the stronger condition indexed by $W$, and the factorization $f=pi$. Grothendieck compares the stronger condition with Loc(6a) and records examples he has yet to examine. The last sentence begins a qualification concerning the functor with a small crossed subscript and breaks off before naming the class of maps.

## Remarks of the model

**M1 · My finite check of S6, high confidence for the pictured example (369, 371).** I took $I$ to have the three vertices and three edges of the triangle boundary, ordered by incidence. I took $\mathrm{sd}(I)$ to have the six singleton chains and six two-element chains, ordered by inclusion. The nonempty-chain interpretation is explicit in this check. Exhaustive enumeration gave **456 monotone maps**. Their image sizes 1, 2, 3 and 4 occurred 12, 192, 216 and 36 times respectively. Following each map around the six incidences gave winding number zero on the twelve-element target cycle. A second enumeration using direct subset comparisons reproduced the image-size counts. This checks the claim about realizations for this one model.

**M2 · My reading of the scope of the questions, high confidence (373, 376).** In question 2 the phrase « de plus » separates the existence of the functors from exactness of squares. Section 7.3 returns to the distinction in the same order. This helps locate precisely what the request for an example concerns.

**M3 · My reading question about two inclusions, low confidence as an interpretation (367 line 17, 378 line 13).** The frozen left sides are $W$ on 367 and $W_\omega$ on 378. The latter sheet refers to th. 6.1. Reading these as the same assertion would require an identification of the two classes that the passages do not supply. The variable in the first expression could be used more broadly. I would keep their scopes open.
