## Legibility

The main hand is clear enough to follow across the page changes. Distinctions between a plain and a double-stemmed W, the ruled emphasis, and most large formulas are visible. Confidence drops at small word endings, at the second level of subscripts, and where a correction follows the same strokes as the word it replaces. The greatest uncertainty is in the interlaced bottom margin on scan 364, the struck last line on 375, and the cancelled slanted note on 376. On 372 a verbal phrase and the mark between Hot and Cat remain unresolved. The fork diagrams and the four boundary drawings on 371 are reconstructions from the pen lines.
