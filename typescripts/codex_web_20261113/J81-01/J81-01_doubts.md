## Legibility

**Scan 2.** The paper is nearly blank. The title is pale, particularly the middle of its first word.

**Scan 3.** The two left-margin notes, the overwritten start of the morphism line, and the cancelled formulas carry the most uncertainty.

**Scan 4.** The large cross affects both columns. Local strikes, the two orientations of writing, and the small initials at lower right require particular care.

**Scan 5.** The algebraic notation is spacious. Several compressed words in the prose and the style of the barred Q are less secure.

**Scan 6.** Two calculations cross at a half-turn. The quotient-rule cancellations and several glyphs in the older calculation are overlaid in both directions.

**Scan 7.** The upright main hand forms its letters and indices separately. The small pale marginal hand is less legible and its attribution is uncertain.

**Scan 8.** The darker text and examples are generally distinct. The overwritten groups at the bottom and the pale additions in a second hand are less secure.

**Scan 10.** The curves and labels can be followed directly. A few short lines have faint ends. The lower-right angular traces and the overdrawn lower disk are approximate.
