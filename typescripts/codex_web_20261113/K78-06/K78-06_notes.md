# K78-06 · Manuscript notes

## The steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **Scan 82, lower-right note.** “on s'attend à” introduces two expectations, β = 0 and α = ζ + ζ⁻¹ = 2 cos θ. The first also has a geometric formulation about a difference of vertices being parallel to e₁.
- **Scan 83, lines 10–11.** “On a donc envie d'imposer la condition” introduces β = 0 after the example. This is a proposed restriction on the objects under study.
- **Scan 86, lines 11–14.** “On vérifie aisément que la réalisation géom de Π₂ₚ est bel et bien fidèle” leaves the verification of faithfulness to the reader. The wording expresses confidence.
- The programme on scans 75–77 uses “il faudra pouvoir”, “On verra” and “Expliciter” for work still to do. No separate question mark or “à vérifier” accompanies a proof step on these sheets.
