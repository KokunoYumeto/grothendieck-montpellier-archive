## Legibility

The large coordinate displays, ordinary vertex indices and figure outlines are relatively clear. The hardest passages are the small insertions and the first writing beneath a cancellation. In particular, the guide on 75 crosses an exponent, the corrected equation numbers on 78–81 retain parts of earlier figures, and the false starts on 83–85 contain strokes from more than one layer. The left-hand note on 88 becomes readable when rotated. No word group had to be represented by a count of illegible words. Several heavily struck earlier readings remain tentative.
