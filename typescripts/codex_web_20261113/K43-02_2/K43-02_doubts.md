## Legibility and limits

The scans are sharp enough to show most pen strokes, but the hand often compresses a word into a wave. Crossings and repeated strokes, not image resolution alone, limit certainty. The least secure passages are the lower argument order on 17, the Ext exponents on 21, the small qualifier and Tate phrase on 22, the circled insertion on 23, the revised hypotheses on 25, the local-criterion clause on 26, the powers of the ideal glyph on 27, the heavily cancelled extension paragraph on 29, and the compressed reduction paragraph on 31. All 60 supplied images were viewed in the first pass; all three crops of each page were reread during the audit. No OCR, external transcription, web search, or parallel reader was used.

Geometric spacing, enclosing curves and cancellation strokes in the TikZ diagrams approximate the freehand layout. The structural checks do not establish successful compilation. The source deliberately preserves uncertain and surprising forms; it should not be treated as a critical edition with resolved readings.
