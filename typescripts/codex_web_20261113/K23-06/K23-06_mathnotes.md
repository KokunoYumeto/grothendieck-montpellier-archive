# Mathematical notes · Montpellier, cote 23, scans 002–016

## What the pages do

**G1 · Projective bundle and conormal sequence, scan 004.** The Euler sequence on $P=\mathrm P(E)$ gives the top relative differential sheaf with twist $-r-1$. For $X\subset P$ of relative dimension $n=r-p$, taking determinants of the conormal sequence expresses the restriction of this sheaf in terms of $\Omega^n_{X/S}$ and $\bigwedge^p(J/J^2)$. The direction of the displayed isomorphism changes between (1) and (2).

**G2 · Graded equations, scans 004–005.** The components $M_d$ of the graded module of equations give a surjection onto $J$ and an isomorphism onto $J/J^2$ after restriction. Taking exterior powers gives the twist $\sum_i\alpha_i d_i-r-1$. When this number vanishes, the author leaves a dash inside the pullback on the right.

**G3 · Plane cubic, scan 005.** The example has $r=2$, one degree-three generator of rank one, and a filtration of $E$ with line factors of weights 0, 2 and 3. The determinant has weight 5. The last displayed identification is $M\simeq\mathcal L^{\otimes6}$.

**G4 · Discriminant, scan 006.** The discriminant $\delta_{r,k}$ gives a section on the parameter space of equations. Pullback along a section $\varphi$ produces a section of a power of the dual line on the base. The sheet relates its nonvanishing to the family being “simple sur $S$” and writes the ideal of the family as a pullback twisted by $-k$. The slanting note compares a quotient line and a subsheaf.

**T1 · Integrability, scan 007.** This English typescript uses equality on $U_i\cap U_j$ to assemble a vector $(0,1)$-form on $V_0$. Its equation (3) is identified as the integrability condition for system (2). A final sentence introduces a tangent vector of $M$ at 0 and stops after a comma. Equation (2) and the rest of the sentence are outside the supplied leaf.

**E1 · Exponential and logarithm, scan 009.** In a complete separated linearly topologized rational algebra, the two series relate topologically nilpotent elements to one plus those elements. The sheet gives the commuting identities and restricts the construction to a closed left or right ideal. The last lines express compatibility with a homomorphism and refer back to the ideal paragraph in a marginal corollary on closed subalgebras.

**E2 · Multiplicative elements, scan 010.** The augmented algebra has a diagonal $\Delta$ with values in a completed tensor product. The sheet writes both $\Delta(g)=g\otimes g$ and the corresponding condition on $\xi$ for $g=1+\xi$. It calculates the diagonal of a product and an inverse. A marginal assumption says that powers of the augmentation ideal tend to zero.

**E3 · Primitive elements and the group law, scan 011.** Over a rational base the diagram restricts exp and log to primitive and multiplicative elements. Transporting multiplication back along the diagram gives a group law on the primitives. The bracket formula is named in the text and a seminar is named in the margin.

**E4 · Infinitesimal coefficients, scans 011–012.** A crossed attempt at the end of 011 continues across the page break. The live diagrams use the nilpotence of $I(\Lambda)$, first in $U\otimes I(\Lambda)$, then in $I(U)\otimes I(\Lambda)$. Elements are distinguished by reduction to 0 or 1 along $U\otimes_k\Lambda\to U$.

**E5 · Relative primitives and scalar extension, scans 012–013.** The subscript 0 records that reduction condition. Scan 013 passes to a projective limit of infinitesimal algebras and introduces a natural map $\alpha$ from $\pi(U)\otimes_k I(\Lambda)$. It gives flatness of $\Lambda$ or a pure exactness condition as sufficient for $\alpha$ to be an isomorphism. A second arrow is labelled $\exp\circ\alpha$.

**E6 · Cartier marginal argument, scan 013.** The foot of the main text says that the relative-primitive functor determines the hyperalgebra under the stated conditions. The two sideways entries give conditions on the diagonal and on a union of finite-dimensional subspaces, then relate $\mathfrak g=\pi(U)$ to its enveloping algebra. A comment on the dual and a corollary about an algebraic group follow. Several words of the corollary overlap the main writing.

**E7 · Endomorphisms, scan 014.** Three equivalences compare a nilpotent endomorphism with its exponential on fixed vectors, commuting endomorphisms and invariant submodules. A fourth uses a bilinear product on $M$ to compare a derivation with a multiplicative automorphism.

**E8 · Automorphisms reducing to the identity, scans 014–015.** If $I(\Lambda)$ has zero $n$th power, endomorphisms mapping $M$ into $I(\Lambda)M$ are nilpotent. Those reducing to the identity are unipotent. For a $\Lambda$-algebra $B$, the corollary matches the automorphisms inducing the identity after reduction with the $\Lambda$-derivations $D$ satisfying $DB\subset I(\Lambda)B$.

**E9 · A constant extension, scan 016.** For $B=A\otimes_k\Lambda$ the Hom calculation rewrites derivations over $\Lambda$ in terms of differentials of $A$ over $k$. The proposition identifies infinitesimal automorphisms with $k$-derivations of $A$ into $A\otimes_k I(\Lambda)$ equipped with the Hausdorff–Campbell group law. The last abbreviation describing $\Lambda$ remains doubtful.

## Remarks of the model

**R1 · Scan 005 · High confidence.** The three filtration weights give $0+2+3=5$. Tensoring the final isomorphism $\check{\mathcal L}\simeq\mathcal L^{\otimes5}\otimes\check M$ with $M\otimes\mathcal L$ gives $M\simeq\mathcal L^{\otimes6}$.

**R2 · Scan 009 · High confidence for the calculation.** In $\mathbb Q[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)$ take $g=1$ and $g'=1+\epsilon$. They commute. The left logarithm is $\epsilon$, the product of the two individual logarithms is zero, and their sum is $\epsilon$. This checks S4-R in the commuting case.

**R3 · Scan 013 · High confidence for the example.** Let $x$ be primitive in $\mathbb Q[x]$ and take $\Lambda=\mathbb Q[t]/(t^3)$. The reduced diagonal of $\exp(tx)-1$ has the nonzero term $t^2(x\otimes x)$. Thus the exponential of a relative primitive need not be one plus a relative primitive in the literal additive sense. This distinguishes the two readings of the target in S5.

**R4 · Scans 014–015 · High confidence.** For a $\Lambda$-linear $u$ with $uM\subset I(\Lambda)M$, iteration gives $u^jM\subset I(\Lambda)^jM$. This verifies the stated nilpotence bound in the passage to the automorphism corollary.

**R5 · Scan 006 · Low confidence about the author’s intention.** The marginal brace groups repeated $k-1$ terms, but no intervening operation is visible. It does not fix a different exponent for the displayed discriminant.

**R6 · Scan 013 · Reading question.** The frozen middle term before $\delta$ is marked $U$ or $I(U)$. An augmentation ideal is a plausible domain for the reduced diagonal, but the crossing prevents resolving that choice in the manuscript. No change to the transcription follows from this observation.
