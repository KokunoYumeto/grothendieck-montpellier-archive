## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

Scan 011, in the bracket below the two exp/log arrows, says “il suffit de vérifier que exp. applique $\pi(U)$ dans $\gamma(U)$, et log applique $\gamma(U)$ dans $\pi(U)$”. This reduces the assertion to two inclusions. The sheet moves directly to “Par transport de structure”. I found no other legible instruction to verify a step, statement of conjecture, or explicit question mark attached to a mathematical claim. The exclamation mark near the opening of 013 and the crossed-out attempt on 011–012 have different functions.
