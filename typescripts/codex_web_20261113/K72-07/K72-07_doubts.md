## Legibility

The titled sheet and most long displays are comparatively clear. The least secure passages are the sloping revisions around (16′) on 98, the cancelled argument and underletters on 101–105, the numerical endpoints on 106, and the crowded changes to indices on 109. Thin strokes can serve as a prime, a terminal letter or an insertion guide. The low line of O and the rounded E often survive better than the features that would determine their alphabet. The two overlapping letters in the biquadratic extension on 113 are especially hard to order. A further clockwise inspection of the sideways note on 98 suggests a longer indexed expression. The reading questions at the end of the mathematical notes give both that proposal and the likely alternative «sous-Modules».
