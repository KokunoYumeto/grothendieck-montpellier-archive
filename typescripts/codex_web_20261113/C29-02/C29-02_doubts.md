## Legibility

The type is largely readable, but x-overtyped cancellations and unfilled formula positions are substantially less secure than the running prose. No OCR, text layer, published transcription, or external source has been used. Local doubts remain marked in the fragment and above.

The foot of scan 24 is the least trustworthy continuous region: cancellation, partial erasure/occlusion and separated surviving marks make its syntax unrecoverable. The visible order is retained; no sentence has been repaired.

Scan 26 also has a heavily crossed-out line after “Alors”; its partly readable words are retained with local doubts; the second reading replaced its one estimated unreadable word by a tentative fragment reading. The isolated insertion s/S near “l’image” has an uncertain attachment, recorded above.
