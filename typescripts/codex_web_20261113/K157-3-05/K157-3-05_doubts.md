## Legibility

The main arguments, the sequence of headings, and the distinction between fibres and slices are generally legible. The shortest grammatical endings and arrowheads are much less even. The least secure passages are the cancellations in the factorization on 066, the interleaved rewrites on 069, the left half of the discarded diagram on 074, the tiny identity in the insertion on 078, and the two final leaves’ crossed or tilted annotations. The darker object at the left of the discarded diagram is a reading from its remaining strokes. It cannot be reconstructed completely from the later redrawing. A mark of illegibility estimates the number of overwritten words. No other transcription or edition was consulted.
