- Scan 75, last numerical scratch line: the hook after the radical and the sign after the minus may be authorial question marks; both are explicitly doubtful in the transcription. They are not treated as a settled numerical equality.
- Scan 81, upper left: the equality beginning `α′` has `?` written above the equals sign; the line ends “OK”. This records a check made on the page, not an unresolved conjecture established by the editor.

Editorial `\unsure` question marks are not evidence of authorial doubt. Incomplete or cancelled calculations are recorded as such, without inventing a request to verify them.
