## Legibility

Scans 134 and 136 are much clearer than 127 and 128. Their main uncertainty concerns the status of a tiny dot or isolated ink traces, not missing prose. In 127–128, the least trustworthy readings are the heavily x-overtyped short words, overlapped capitals, the cropped right edge, and the exact boundary between type and pen in small symbols. Hand identity cannot be established securely from these pages alone; handwritten inventory rows therefore use unknown.

The main typing is substantially legible. Multiple x-overtyping, heavy pen cancellations, the extreme right edge, and differences between typed and hand-completed mathematical symbols are less secure. These are marked in place. No OCR, text-recognition library, external text, or web source was used.
