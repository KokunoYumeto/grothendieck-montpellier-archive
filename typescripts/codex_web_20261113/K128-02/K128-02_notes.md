# Title sheets, dates and page counts

None of the fifteen sheets is a dated or numbered title sheet. No date, authorial page number or statement of a page total is visible. The numbers in the lower left are archival pencil numbers 16–29 and 31, corresponding to scans 17–30 and 32. The English sheet has no title, signature or date.

Internal headings occur on scans 20–24 and 26–27. They include « Lemme 1 » above a struck « Proposition », « Corollaire » 1, 2 and 3, « Lemme 2 », « Théorème » and « Démonstration ». The text on scan 25 also has a « Dém » heading.
