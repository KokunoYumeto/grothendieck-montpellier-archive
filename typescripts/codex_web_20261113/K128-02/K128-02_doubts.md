## Legibility

The long display hands are generally distinct enough to compare primes, the direction of a limit and the positions of subscripts. Short connective words are harder. Across 17–18 the words read « à corps résiduels / triviale »; the ending and the interrupted sentence do not support a secure grammatical emendation.
