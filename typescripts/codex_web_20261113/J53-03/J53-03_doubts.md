## Legibility

- Scan 16. The compact slanted formulas at left, the rewritten Dist line, the cancelled words in the Marche paragraph and the last three lines carry the greatest uncertainty.
- Scan 17. The English letter is relatively clear. A dot after the hooked arrow and the small angular variable inside GL are less secure. The opaque stroke above the salutation has no visible lettering.
- Scan 18. The two heads of the surjection and all nine cells of the table are visible. Tiny dots near the arrows and the case of the projection letters are less secure.
- Scan 19. The four projection calculations and the arrows are clear. The handwriting contracts the word before that and the qualifier before gps. The diagonal pointer beside the lower square refers back to the prose.
- Scan 20. The coordinates and adjacent subscripts are clear. The small raised mark after the last summand and the joined loops of the signature carry the greatest uncertainty.
- Scan 21. Only the addressee occupies a ruled line. The isolated patches below it show no letters at enlargement.
- Scan 22. The crossings at the head of the distribution paragraph, the little sign above the bilinear arrow, the diagonal subscript, and the lower slanted annotation are the least legible. The square/circle distinction on the products and the final boxed equality are clear.
