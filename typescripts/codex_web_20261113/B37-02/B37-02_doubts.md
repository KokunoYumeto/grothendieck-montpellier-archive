## Legibility and limits

The main typescript is usually legible, but overstriking creates genuinely uncertain layers, including in otherwise ordinary words. Scan25 is the least reliable: dense crossing, nested replacements and crosshatching obscure portions of the abandoned proof. The corollary in scan 24, the faint top note and appendix in18, the cancelled tails in27, and the clipped margin of30 are also weak points. Candidate lists and word counts under dense ink are necessarily provisional. The angle of a small accent or the distinction between a repeated strike and another letter cannot always be settled.

All 36 supplied images were inspected; the upside-down scan 27 was rotated for reading. The nine second readings, actual timestamps and corrections are recorded in the audit, with an additional comparison of the first pages and recurring notation. No OCR, external transcription, web source or published version was used. Mathematical correctness was not independently established. The supplied preamble was left unchanged and the files were not compiled; syntactic balance is checked separately. Handwritten authorship is left unknown where this sheet alone does not identify the writer.
