# Notes on scans 34–41

## Steps marked as unverified or left for examination

- **Scan 34.** “Reste à voir la condition At CL (spac)” is immediately followed by “mais elle est vraie par définition.” The author raises the check and gives his answer on the same line.
- **Scan 39.** “Pour la suite, il faudra voir comment les autres axiomes affectent le choix de ∥ (et du reste).” He names At supp and a second, uncertain, At ens with its variants.
- **Scan 39, at the foot.** “suffit-il que x ∥ y ⇔ x ≠ y ?? Sans doute pas”. Two question marks follow the proposed condition. He goes on to mention restrictions on the other data, injectivity of a map whose name is uncertain, and the induced relation.
- **Scan 41.** “allons voir quelles propriétés” of the crossed-chevron relation “font marcher nos raisonnements précédents”. This announces an examination before returning to ∥. The proposed last axiom below it is crossed out.
