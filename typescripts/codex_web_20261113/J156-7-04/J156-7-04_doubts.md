## Legibility

The relative heights of letters, the sides on which brackets close, and the positions of several rings and primes survive well. Connected French words can lose their middle letters almost altogether. I trust the readings least where heavy ink combines two states of a correction: the lower block on 35, the small words recast on 36–37, the name of the map at the foot of 39, the right margin and relettered comparisons on 40, and the ruled-out last axiom on 41. The alternatives preserve those uncertainties without using any text outside these eight sheets.
