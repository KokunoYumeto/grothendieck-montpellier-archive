## Legibility

The large square and interval diagrams, the page numbers and most of the continuous French are legible. Confidence is lowest where a correction occupies the same space as its predecessor, especially in the Hom subscripts on 259, the definition at the foot of 263, and the component calculation on 268. The short horizontal strokes ending in a slightly thickened point also resist a single reading. The sloping notes can be read in their own direction. On the cover the computer print is visible through the sheet in reverse. An unreadable cancellation has been counted in estimated words; the count is not a restoration of its wording.
