## Legibility

The titles and most of the typescript survive well. In the typescript, a ruled capital can usually be distinguished from a second impression. The old characters under strings of x’s are much less secure. The weakest handwritten readings are the slashed definitions on 027, the abbreviated insertion and edge labels on 029, the matrix indices on 031, and the diagonal cohomological note on 034. On 035 the edges of pasted strips and faint reverse type are visible near otherwise continuous pen strokes. The four unreadable passages retain estimated word counts.
