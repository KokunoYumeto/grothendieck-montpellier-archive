## Legibility

The main definitions and the labelled endpoints of the diagrams are generally clearer than the abandoned drafts. Confidence is lowest in the sloping notes on 44, 46, 47 and 53, the erased symbol lists on 47–50, and the overwritten starts on 55–56. A short final stroke can change a plural, a prime or a subscript. On 58 the two compact interlinear letters above Σ and the verb after peut se are particularly uncertain. Every uncertain passage has a proposed reading here. The absence of a wholly illegible word count reflects the use of partial or doubtful readings even in the cancellations.
