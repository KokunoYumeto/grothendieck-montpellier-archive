## Legibility

All sheets are handwritten. The main uncancelled lines and many displays have fairly distinct large strokes. Short links between words, reduced endings, tiny primes and low indices account for many of the doubts. The least secure passages are the overlapped upper-right note on 044, the cancelled headings and table entries on 046–047, the cancelled formula blocks on 050 and 056, and the long diagonal on 053 where it crosses the main text. The manuscript numerals at the crown were distinguished from the archive numerals in the lower corner. The cancellation in 045 covers the whole two-line text. Graphic strokes in the examples on 047 and 049 are reconstructed in TikZ, with the visible labels retained.
