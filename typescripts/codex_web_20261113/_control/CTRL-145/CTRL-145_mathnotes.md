## What the pages do

**Scans 20–22 — Generators, relations and lifts.** The notes give relations among the three sigma generators and rho, express the lacets through them, and calculate epsilon generators. They then describe lifted permutations, their conjugation actions, and three involutions tau. Canonical lifts through the isotropy of P are introduced. The scan order puts (13)–(16) before (10)–(12); the transcription preserves this order and numbering.

**Scan 23 — The tau subgroup and profinite groups.** The three tau involutions describe the cartographic group attributed to Malgoire. The lacets are products of pairs of involutions, and pi is identified as the kernel of a signature homomorphism. The text then distinguishes a profinite completion from the group of lacet automorphisms of profinite pi and displays an extension diagram.

**Scans 24–26 — Multiplier and conjugator data.** A commutant and its corresponding extension are introduced. Lacet automorphisms are described by a multiplier p and three conjugators satisfying a product relation and a topological generation condition. An enlarged S-group remembers the conjugators. The notes compute composition, discuss a cocycle, identify a kernel, and begin the problem of lifting the sigma and epsilon operations.

**Scans 27–29 — Partial splittings from fixed loci.** Fixed points and invariant components are used to describe partial splittings of the extension. The discussion includes P and its conjugate, the points 2, −1 and 1/2, three real arcs, and the unit-circle locus. It then turns toward a presentation of the whole group; the last proposal on scan 29 is cancelled.

**Scans 30–31 — A presentation with fewer generators.** Relations are written first for the quotient and then for chosen lifts. The next page reduces the generators to rho, sigma_0 and tau_0 and rewrites a relation as a product of commutators. It identifies a quotient of a free product and describes a symmetry that fails to respect the extension structure.

**Scan 32 — A further extension to make explicit.** A four-line continuation proposes describing SΓ and obtaining the corresponding S E by a fiber product. The calculation itself is not supplied on this sheet.

**Scans 33–34 — Tuples for the enlarged profinite group.** The new section describes tuples (p, sigma, g_0, g_1, g_infty), their induced automorphism, a canonical map i, multiplication, and conjugation of the image of i. It explains the remaining computational difficulty: terms such as u(g'_i) are not evaluated directly unless words or limits of words representing the inputs are given.
