## Legibility and limits

All fifteen sheets are handwritten notes in the same working hand. Scan 45 is a short cancelled fragment, not a blank page. The main mathematical displays are generally clearer than the prose, but overwriting sometimes makes an alphabet, a prime or a small index undecidable. The least secure material is the long slanted note on scan 53, followed by the cancelled opening and slanted note on scan 56, the small left notes on 44 and 47, and crowded corrections on 46 and 50. Two marks of illegibility, each estimating two words, occur in scan 53's note. They are not claims that the rest of that note is certain: its many tentative readings remain explicitly marked.

The drawings on 47 and 49 are manual TikZ redrawings: the graph parts and labels are transcribed, while handwritten curvature and spacing can only be approximated. This is a diplomatic reading with disclosed uncertainty, not a claim of an error-free critical edition. No OCR, text recognition, external transcription, literature or web source was used. No TeX compilation was attempted.
