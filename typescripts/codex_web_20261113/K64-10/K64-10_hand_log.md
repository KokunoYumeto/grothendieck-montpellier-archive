## Recurrences across the group

The doubled dotted choice on 118 (74) and 121 (87)–(89) is distinct from the short coordinate indices. A single dot can be all of a subscript i clear of its base in 117 (66) and 118 (71). The same element with a full upright is visible on the neighbouring factors. For the root, all six sheets that use it (116–118, 123, 125–126) have the capped, bent form, with differing amounts of curl. The index set on 117–121 has a variable top serif but a fairly stable squared foot. Both habits remain ambiguous. The straighter capital F with subscript t on 119–121 remains distinct from the swept F with subscript 2 on 118–119. E in the tuple on 118 is locally retraced. A single dark capital elsewhere does not justify doubling it. In 123, 125 and 126, the additional return of a small 3 often separates it from a 2, with an exception at 126 in the lower row of the box.
