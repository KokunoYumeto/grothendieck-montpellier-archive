# Mathematical notes on cote 64, scans 116–129

## What the pages do

**116–117, fixed points and their lifts.** The fractional transformation (61) has coefficients in the three roots. Rewriting its numerator by the elementary symmetric functions gives a quadratic for the fixed coordinates \(z_i\), then two forms of its radical. The sloping notes ask how to read the quotients when a root vanishes. Scan 117 continues the sentence interrupted after « un tel ». For each \(i\), first \(z_i\) and then the second coordinate \(z'_i\) are chosen. They describe a point \(x_i\) with \(2x_i=\varepsilon_i\). The sheet counts four possible lifts for each index.

**117–118, the Jacobi condition.** The relative level-four marking imposes the further equation \(\sum x_i=0\). Scan 117 gives a determinant for it. Scan 118 collects the coordinate conditions under (71) and labels the three alternating products \(\psi_1,\psi_2,\psi_3\). The note beside the display specifies which indices to take after choosing a cyclic order.

**118–119, the proposed Legendre marking.** The objective is to associate a marking to these data compatibly with a change of base. After a fourth root is chosen, the first datum is expressed by a circular order \(\omega\). The second is an isomorphism \(\kappa\). Scan 119 prescribes its square by comparing both sides with the same tangent line. It then compares two exact sequences, beginning with \(F_t\) and with the constants respectively, by tensoring the first with \(\check F_t\). Diagram (81) displays the three vertical identifications.

**120–121, coordinates for the isomorphism.** Dualizing \(\kappa\) gives a map with image \(F_t\). The sheet describes it by a family \((\delta_i)\) with zero sum. Relative to a choice of \(\delta\), the scalar coordinates \(\varphi_i^\omega(\delta)\) have weight \(-1\) under rescaling. The author writes down conditions (83 a, b) in these coordinates, then returns to the dependence on the circular order. The last word of 121 is « Posant ». Scan 123 opens with a sketch and a group of roots, without completing that sentence.

**123, three roots and three differences.** A sketch puts the labels \(1,3,2\) in that order along a transversal. Two linear equations determine the three \(\lambda_i\) up to a common invertible scalar. The exhibited solution takes pairwise differences of the roots. In the adjacent array the differences \(e_j-e_k\) are paired with vectors whose components are a root times such a difference and the difference itself.

**125–126, elimination.** The three starting relations express \(\zeta_i f_1+f_2\) in terms of \(e_j-e_k\). Scan 125 carries one coefficient into the following line by an arrow and works out \(f_2\). Scan 126 names the product of root differences \(\Delta_0\), boxes the two linear combinations, and expresses the point \(t\) with differences of \(\wp(\varepsilon_j)\). Several signs in the working remain overwritten.

**124, 127–129, other material.** Scan 124 is a thesis-defence notice, and 127 announces a talk on integers. The dated events belong to those documents. The two subsequent covers read « Relations entre \(M_{11}\) et \(M_{04}\) » and « Approche transcendante ». The latter carries a separate pale page count.

## Questions and conjectures

- **118, after (73).** « Je voudrais … associer canoniquement un épinglage de Legendre de \(E\) », « de façon fonctorielle en \(E\) ». This is the construction sought. The author also asks for compatibility « avec les changemᵗˢ de corps de base ».
- **121, after (88).** « et la condition c) s'écrit Dieu sait comment -- il faudra … y revenir. » The intervening cancellation is a scalar expression. Condition c) concerns the square of \(\kappa\).
- **121, before and after (89).** « Reste à exprimer la dépendance p.r. à \(\omega\) » introduces a « relation nécessaire ». The following description of \(\alpha\) says « (supposé déterminé) » and « associé à l'épinglage de Legendre cherché ». No subsequent construction of that isomorphism is given in this group.
