# Manuscript notes

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **Scan 121, immediately after (88).** “et la condition c) s'écrit Dieu sait comment -- il faudra … y revenir.” A cancelled formula stands between “faudra” and “y revenir”. This is the explicit postponement. The next sentence begins “Reste à exprimer la dépendance p.r. à” ω.
- **Scan 118, after (73).** “Je voudrais … associer canoniquement un épinglage de Legendre” announces the construction sought. It is a statement of intention. On scan 121 the accompanying isomorphism is twice treated as given, first with “étant supposé déterminé”, then with “(supposé déterminé)”. No question mark or promise of a completed proof accompanies the final “Posant”.
