## Legibility

The main pen text is generally readable, especially the spaced displays. Rapid joins, arrowheads and small raised signs are considerably less reliable than the large letters. The greatest losses are in the heavily revised wording of the early lemmas, the dark substitutions on the cover (268), and the water-like smears at the right of 269. The diagonal note on 270 crosses upright writing. Rotation helps with the first and last sentences, less with the words at their intersection. On 271 the original suffixes below the hatch marks are particularly insecure.

The type on 272 is mostly clear even where the large cancellation strokes cross it. The raised zigzag at *nourrir* is visible, but its value is unclear. The years in the footnote and the typed page number are much clearer than the physical origin of the small punctuation-like marks. No other text was consulted to fill these places.
