# Legibility

The ordinary typed text is mostly readable. Confidence is lowest in heavy x-overtyped deletions on 119, the cancelled labels and small margin formula on 121, the overwritten integer letter on 122, and the handwritten place/month and folded line on 125. Thin handwriting, bleed-through and occasional double impressions make several letters genuinely undecidable from these images. Enlargements were used before marking unreadable words. The doubt macros distinguish these limits from confident readings; neither grammar nor mathematical expectations were used to fill a gap. All six pages received a separate second visual reading, whose changes and actual timestamps are in the audit file.
