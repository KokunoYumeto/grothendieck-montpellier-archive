## Comparison across the eleven scans

On the three sheets with long products (65, 67, 69), the low loop of ρ, the dotted i, single vertical of L, and detached prime were compared in the first and last factors of each product. The individual exceptions retained in the table include the retraced lower indices, a dotted i beside 1 on scan 67 and a separate faint tick above the denominator on scan 69. In the typescripts (60, 62, 64, 66, 68, 70), the underlines move with the ideal letter, the functor capitals have ordinary typewriter impressions, and digit 1 has a flag. On scan 61 each R was judged independently by its vertical strokes. The heading and line-end abbreviations on the other manuscripts were checked against their own page.
