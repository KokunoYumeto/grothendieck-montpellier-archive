## Legibility

The typed sheets are generally clear down to the ideal underlines. On scans 67 and 69 the most difficult distinctions are between the thin strokes of consecutive indices, the dots of i and isolated primes. The rapid ink on scan 61 is least distinct around the corrected subscripts, the two canceled words before the lower triangle and the verb in its final sentence. The right edge of scan 63 meets the final line of the small note beside the circled condition. Several retraced stems in the middle of the equations conceal the earlier pen strokes. Scan 65 has an entire diagonal cancellation with earlier horizontal cancellations inside it. The left-side addition and the altered first indices of the long product are especially difficult.
