## Legibility

The typescript and the uncompleted official forms are readily legible. The typed le on 344 is touched by a blot. On 343–346 the small ovals, the overwritten category names and the changes from one connectivity claim to another need particular care. Scan 348 has a sheet-wide cancellation and additional hatching over two local conditions. On 346 and 355 a later addition runs obliquely across the original sentence, sometimes within a single letter. The outer objects and arrow directions are generally clearer than a buried exponent, an ending or a connecting word.
