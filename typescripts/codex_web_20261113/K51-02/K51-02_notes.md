## Title sheets, dates and page counts

There is no separate cover or title sheet among scans 13–23. Scan 23 has the manuscript heading **Schémas abéliens sur un S régulier** above a list a)–d). The upper right of scan 22 carries two strokes, possibly “11” or “II”. This is the only possible internal folio in these eleven scans. The lower-corner archive figures run from 12 on scan 13 through 22 on scan 23 and are recorded as comments. No date or written total page count appears on these sheets.
