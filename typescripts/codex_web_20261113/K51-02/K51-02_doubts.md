### Comparison across sheets

On scan 13 the angular initials repeat in the intersections and in both quotients. The two primes in the first intersection sit high and the 1 and 2 are low. On scans 14–15 the prime on B is likewise distinct from the following index on A. The Hilb underlines on 14, 15 and 20 are double. The relative indices change from A/S to P/S. Hom on 16 also has two rules. The Pic on 17–18 is generally unruled, with a little round zero above it. The ruled Pic in the discussion on 19–22 has a single line. The short raised stroke on 21 stands apart from the rule and differs visibly from the zeros on 17–19.

The rising loops of the moduli letter on 14 support script or calligraphic M. The M on 20–21 is plainer. The variable K at the right of the tensor product on 17 is full height, whereas V is written beneath the product. On 18, the insertion after the hypotheses has its K below X. The character inside Spec has a different profile. The generic and special indices in the two dimension expressions on 21 appear different. On 22 the zero on the kernel and on xi is below the line. The 1 beside H on 23 is above it. There are no indicated i/j indices on these sheets. The two strokes at the opening and closing of the quotations on 17, 21 and 23 are double quotation signs. The pages are manuscripts, with no typewritten or double-struck alphabet identified.

Soft source line endings locate the ink for the apparatus and render as spaces within paragraphs. A visibly incomplete parenthesis at the extreme right of scan 23 is left incomplete. The slash in the first pair of its display differs from the comma in the preceding inline group.
