The crossings in the lemma on 103 and the degree marks and references on 104 are among the least secure mathematical readings. The revised folios and raised sign on 105, the first digit of the diagram on 107, the inserted fibre test on 110, the arrows on 111 and the lower oblique note on 112 also deserve comparison with the images. The print on 108 is clear in both columns. Its small professional codes, the point-like mark on the first e of cette, the signature initial and the overwritten folio are much less secure. The long cancellations cross otherwise readable words and even stop within one word. Every doubt mark has a row above. No whole word was classed as physically unreadable.
