# K11-04 · Notes on the sheets

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **Scan 47, lines 14–16, and scan 45, lines 28–30.** The English proof leaves inverse composition to the reader, “leaving it to the reader to check that they are mutually inverse.” It closes with “Modulo the mechanical verifications that remain, this proves the Proposition.”
- **Scan 44, margin beside the action of a correspondence.** “il y a un choix: prendre $Cy$ ou $yC$ !” The order of the two factors is an explicit choice.
- **Scan 46, lower calculation and margins.** The note at the interchange of pullback and pushforward says “‘‘axiome cartésien’’ oublié !” The projection-formula line says “mod signe”. The closing margin reads “OK mod signe, à faire attention”.
- **Scan 48, closing three lines.** “Il faut encore définir l’associativité de l’opération $\circ$, et le fait que les $\delta_I$ jouent le rôle d’unités.” The sheet ends at “On voit que sous ces conditions,”.
- **Scan 57, left margin of the two cases.** The first “à vérifier” has an underlined “OK” below it. A second “à vérifier” accompanies the unequal case. A separate question mark is linked to the product in its second subcase, where $k=s+s'$. Its exact scope is unclear.
- **Steps the writers call evident or clear.** Scan 49 says “It is evident” of determinant invariance. Scan 43 introduces Corollary 3.2 with “The following is obvious”. Scan 47 calls equivariance obvious. Scan 54 says “The first part is clear” of Theorem 3.8. Scan 56 says “But it is easy to check” of the spanning assertion for ordered weights. Scan 57 marks stability under the external product and inverse image, and the direct-image subcase $i\in J$, “clair”.
