## Legibility

The running prose and signatures of the two letters are usually legible. The compressed editorial entries on scans 52, 53 and 77 give less evidence for word endings, accents and Roman numerals. I trust the cancellations on scans 53 and 55, the little sign between i and the generic-point letter on scan 52, the overwritten subscripts on scan 76 and the numerical range on scan 77 least. Two inked-out words have no recoverable letter sequence. Dots at the edges of several sheets are recorded with low confidence about their function.
