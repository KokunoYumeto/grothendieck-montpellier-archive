## Legibility

The main diagrams and isolated equations generally have more separation than the connective prose. The least secure continuous readings are the superposed diagonal arguments on 148–149, 151 and 159. A word can be legible while its attachment to one of two crossing sentences remains uncertain. The thick superscript on U in 156, the short repeated diagram labels on 155 and the overwritten adverbs on 159–160 need particular care. The one wholly unreadable stretch is a short cancellation on 147. No exterior witness was used to complete it.
