## Legibility

Most of the type can be read at the scale of the supplied thirds. The greatest uncertainty is in the superimposed letters on 78, the corrected numeral in 81, and small remnants that survive beside a sound letter in the bibliography. The handwritten dates, the final question mark in the table on 81, and the direction of every table arrow are clear. Fine accents in the bibliography vary from dark diagonal strokes to isolated gray points. The pens and pencils are unsigned. The inventory uses “unknown” for their writers and for overprints whose maker cannot be identified from the sheet. The paper marks at upper left on 80, 81, and 83–85 have been traced without attributing them to a writer.
