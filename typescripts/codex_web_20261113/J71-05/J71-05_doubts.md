## Legibility

On scan 36 the rounded main hand is clear. A second, much faster hand wrote across and around the proof. The oblique hatching, overwritten inequality and the two pale pencil parentheses carry the greatest uncertainty. Hand assignments distinguish the visible layers; the sheet has no signature.

On scan 37 the long left annotation can be followed after turning the sheet. Several algebraic characters are nearly solid black. Their alternative readings and the positions of the surviving strokes are recorded above.

On scan 38 most character shapes are distinct. The underlying exponent in XII, two inked-out groups, and the shallow curved accent in the section title are less secure than the surrounding writing.

On scan 39 dense overwriting affects the page number and the word preceding montre. In the two interlinear formulae the positions of the marks are clearer than the heavy second exponent and the isolated dot.

On scan 40 the high exponents and the two lines defining V and U are clear. The thickened operators near the top and middle of the proof conceal most of their earlier letters.

Scan 41 has two lines of upright dark writing when rotated through 180 degrees. The words end at de with considerable blank space beyond. The light reversed impressions elsewhere show through the sheet.

On scan 42 the subscripts sit close to their large capitals. The most difficult spots are the ink under the exponent in the middle Si argument, the blackened ending of relié and the ruled-out words under Comme.
