## Legibility

The diagrams with widely separated vertices, the consecutive manuscript folios, and much of the main prose are legible. Words end in short horizontal strokes that can conceal an ending or an accent. The least secure places are the overwritten qualifiers and names on 162–165, the angled axiom-number note on 173, and the changing subscripts and labels on 175–176. A doubt with a single candidate records a partial reading for which I could distinguish no second spelling. A bare dash in a formula records the shape of an individual stroke.
