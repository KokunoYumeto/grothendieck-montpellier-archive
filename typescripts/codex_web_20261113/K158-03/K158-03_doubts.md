## Legibility

The administrative body text can be read down to the punctuation. The last printed line under each signature is clipped at the edge. The mathematical hand is most legible in the isolated identities, where the pen lifts between symbols. On scans 36–38, superscripts and abandoned letters sometimes share strokes with corrections. The extended cancellations on scans 45, 47–49 are the least secure passages, particularly the short labels around the diamond on scan 49. On scan 50 the margin can be read after rotation, while the overwritten folio still resists a certain reading. A surviving shaft, loop, bar or stray mark has been retained even where its function is doubtful.
