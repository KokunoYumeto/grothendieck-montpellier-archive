# K93-11 · Notes on the sheets

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **17, middle.** “Pour vérifier (II) dans les cas non couverts par (III)” introduces the remaining cases. The typescript works through them on 17 and 19.
- **18, framed equation and foot.** The handwritten heading reads “question connexe”. Beside the quotient is “étude ∞ᵃˡᵉ à faire de j.” The case table and two divisibility observations are followed, much lower down, by an explicit “? semi direct de …”. A separate loop precedes the question mark. Neither the formal calculation nor an answer to the question at the foot is written out here.
- **19, beside Proposition 6.9(I).** “[vrai seulement en faisceautisant !]” qualifies the scope of the typed claim about the only automorphisms. The slightly broken compound in the annotation is marked in the transcription. The isolated mark after (V) may refer to the question on 18; its shape remains ambiguous.
- **21, bracket underneath “Cor”.** “[ou seulᵐᵗ 2 et 3 non div. de 0]” proposes a weaker hypothesis beside “plat sur ℤ”. The expression to its right ends in three written dots within brackets. The sheet gives no full corollary or verification.
- **22, right margin of the proof of 7.1.** A loop resembling σ, 6 or o is followed by a separate “?”. Its line says that the form is a polynomial of weight n in the aᵢ. The annotation has no words specifying what is questioned.
- **22, last paragraph.** “Reste à prouver que si un polynôme … est identiquement nul en caractéristique 2 (resp. 3), alors il est divisible par 2 (resp.3)”. The text takes this up on 24–25 through Proposition 7.2.
