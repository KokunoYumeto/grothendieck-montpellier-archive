## Legibility

The large capitals, the order of the diagrams and the difference between most raised and lowered operator signs can be read with some confidence. The least secure passages are the interlinear hypotheses and crossed ending on 204, the names of the comparison arrows on 205–207, the places where the sloping note crosses the horizontal text on 209, and the overwritten coefficient objects on 211. Small arrowheads often merge with the shaft. The type of the arrow and the physical trace are both recorded where they point to different readings. The times in the audit are the actual times of the crop readings and comparisons.
