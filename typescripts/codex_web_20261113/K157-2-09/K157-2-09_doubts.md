## Legibility

The main argument and the arrangement of the diagrams are generally readable. The fine distinctions are local. At the centre of the figure on 131, solid hooks, dotted arrows and cancelled heads overlap. The marginal calculations on 136–137 cross the line of the main writing. The lowest cancellation on 134 reaches the edge of the sheet. Several words in the revised condition on 146 are occluded by repeated strokes. A short upright at an arrow endpoint, a dot in an exponent or the end of a fast French plural can materially change the reading. Those places carry explicit alternatives.
