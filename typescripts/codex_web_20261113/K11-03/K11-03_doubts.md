## Legibility

The six English typescripts have regular letter spacing, with many mathematical signs and indices filled in by hand. The faint isolated mark on 30, the repeated round index and matrix tick on 39, and the typography of the hand-added capitals remain uncertain. The six sheets are classified as reused typescript from the change of subject, type and orientation. The individual who filled in their symbols is unidentified.

On the handwritten sheets, the fast French word endings and pen crossings account for much of the remaining uncertainty. The left and bottom additions on 34, the dense square on 40, the overwritten indices on 42 and the boxed sign on 29 deserve particular caution. Line ranges count written lines in reading orientation. The logged independent second readings took 38 to 122 seconds per sheet, shorter than the requested several minutes on most sheets. Subsequent notation comparisons, enlarged rereadings and witness checks are outside those intervals.
