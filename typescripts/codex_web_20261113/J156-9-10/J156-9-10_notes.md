# Manuscript notes · cote 156-9, scans 74–81

## Steps the pages mark as unverified

**Scan 75, manuscript page 71, NB below the display labelled (xxx).** Grothendieck writes “J'ignore si la validité de cette formule, pour une quasi-corr. disjonctive donnée α∗, indique que c'est une corr. disjonctive”. A first α with a low cross is struck through before “quasi-corr.”; the live α also has a low cross. The question he leaves open is whether that formula suffices for the given quasi-correspondence to be a disjunctive correspondence.
