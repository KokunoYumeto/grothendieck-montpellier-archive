## Legibility

The open lines of prose, the large unions, and the boundaries of the drawings are generally clear. The least secure readings are the two words beneath the curve on scan 49 and the very faint sideways pencil on scan 50. The latter contains one group for which no letters could be distinguished, and the hand is uncertain. On scan 51 the replacement of S by T and the indices written just above the cancelled line are difficult to separate. Scan 52 compresses several letters and places two sloping notes in the same margin. On scan 53 the second form of éléments has a different accent and fewer strokes than the first. The older terms beneath the overwriting and the small operators on scan 54 remain doubtful. Scan 55 has only drawings and the archival pencil number. Its upper-right traces correspond to the reflected drawing on scan 54. The TikZ paths retain the visible breaks, darker retracings and clipped contours.
