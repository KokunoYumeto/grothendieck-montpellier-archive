## Remarks of the model

- **R1 · Scan 32, mine, low confidence as an attribution.** The comparison of cardinalities depends on the size of I₀. In a two-point chain $a<b$, with I′ consisting of a and the new point b, I₀ is all of I while I′₀ is the singleton. Thus testing I′₀ against I or against I₀ would allow a branch with no decrease. In a two-point antichain with the same choice, I₀ has only b and the size does decrease. The two cases distinguish the proposed conditions. The reading of the crossed character remains uncertain.
- **R2 · Scans 32–33, mine, high confidence.** The right-hand path of the square on 32 accounts for both inputs to the saturation step on 33. Its upper part is the cobase change of the I′ map. Its composite is the I map, identified with the component at the final object. This explains why the conclusion applies to the lower comparison.
- **R3 · Scans 34–35, mine, high confidence.** Substituting the boxed starting open $I_0=I'$ into the three subsequent definitions yields the three identifications on the right of the last square on 35. This isolates precisely where the proof for a general starting open uses the previously established colimit assertion.
