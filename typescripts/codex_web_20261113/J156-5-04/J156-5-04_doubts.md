## Legibility

The running French and most displayed relationships are legible. On 26 the slanted note crosses the line beginning « en posant », and the last expression turns down at the cut edge. The elevated letters, close support bars and heavily revised object names on 28 need particular care. Short articles and qualifications under cancellation on 27, 29 and 31 are less secure than the surrounding paragraphs. On 30 and 32 the fine marginal writing runs between larger symbols, and on 32 it crosses the body of the page. On 33 a sideways word, a pair of elevated erasures and the small signs on I and I′ are particularly difficult. The recorded candidates express the readable alternatives. A crossed phrase can have a legible beginning and ending while the intervening letters remain uncertain.
