## Legibility

The typewriter text on scans 2–7 can usually be read down to its accents, even where it is unevenly inked. Several x-runs mask the older wording, and the exact number of overstrikes is uncertain where adjacent impressions join. The starts of successive lines in the left note on scan 2 and the tops of the first line of the sideways note on scan 9 fall outside the photographed writing area. Two words at the end of the pen cancellation on scan 2 are represented by one estimated word-count mark. The place at the head of scan 9 is faint. On scans 9–10 the parts I trust least are the contracted middles of words, the overwritten name in the two-row formula, the nearly parallel strokes between 0 and i, and the last line of the letter. The last supplied sheet ends with “près des”.
