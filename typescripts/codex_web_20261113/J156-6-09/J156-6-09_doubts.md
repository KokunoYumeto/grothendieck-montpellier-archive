## Legibility

The prose is widely spaced. In many words the middle syllables reduce to a low continuous stroke. The independent letter shapes are clearest in the hypotheses and in the live displays. The small indices of the diagonal NB on scan 68, the intersecting pen paths and subscripts on scan 70, the crossed underlying sentence on scan 72 and the last line of scan 73 carry the greatest uncertainty. On scan 72 one word-sized group remains unreadable beneath a replacement. The attempted il / ils readings and the possibility of a single contracted word are recorded in the audit. Several places retain a plausible reading alongside an alternative even where the surrounding sentence favours one of them.
