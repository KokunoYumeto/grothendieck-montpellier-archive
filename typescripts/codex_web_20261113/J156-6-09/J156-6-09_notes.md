# Notes on the eight sheets

## Steps the manuscript itself qualifies or leaves open

| Scan | Words on the sheet | What the author flags |
|---|---|---|
| 68 | “La question est si on peut se donner celle-ci arbitrairement.” | “celle-ci” refers back to the family just described. The next sentence introduces the gluing axiom as the answer. |
| 69 | “du moins, on pourra le faire, indépendamment de At 11, si on suppose … fini …” and “La difficulté est de comparer” | The parenthesis singles out finiteness when forming the supremum. The next sentence singles out the comparison of the two refinements when neither order relation holds. The verb ending in the parenthesis is doubtful. |
| 70 | “(définir ce terme)” | The author leaves a reminder at “syst compatible” in Corollaire 2. It concerns the definition used in the statement. |

Explicit “à vérifier”, “sauf erreur”, “admis” or a question mark attached to a proof step elsewhere in these sheets: none.
