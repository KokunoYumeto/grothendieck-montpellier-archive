## Legibility

The joins in the French prose often reduce a whole word to an entry stroke, a horizontal run and a final turn. The diagrams usually preserve the placement of their nodes and the distinction between a continuous shaft and a dashed one. Tiny primes, overwritten subscripts, blunt arrow ends and the words beneath cancellations are less secure. The lower block of 38, the proof labels and numerical indices on 39–43, the corrected arguments on 47, and the bottom question on 50 deserve the closest comparison with the scans. The two oblique prose notes are legible after rotation. The question mark by the retraction on 50 is separate from its lower diagonal note.
