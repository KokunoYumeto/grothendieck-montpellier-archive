## Legibility

The type on 033 resolves individual letters and the absence of accents much more clearly than the mathematical handwriting. On the manuscript sheets the positions of most stars, bars and arrowheads are clearer than the French word endings. Long cross-outs on 021, 023, 026 and 027 conceal whole groups of letters. The additions across 019, 022 and the foot of 032 have the least secure wording. On 031 the distinction between a short bar and a stroke cancelling a superscript matters to the reading of the equality. The alternatives are kept at their loci.
