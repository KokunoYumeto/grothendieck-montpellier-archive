# Cote 27 · Notes on the sheets

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **Scan 6, proof of Corollaire 1.5.** “reste à prouver que (i) implique (i bis), et , lorsque k est algébriquement clos, que (i) implique (ii).” The author proceeds with the reduction and ends the paragraph “ce qui achève la démonstration”.
- **Scan 12, beside Cor. 5.9.** The slanted “rédiger mieux” calls for a better formulation of the criterion. Beneath the cancelled 5.10 the words “On utilise un / Lemme …” leave the supporting statement open.
- **Scan 13, first line.** “On utilise un corollaire nouveau ?” The last mark can also be read as a colon. It is the possible question on this page. The remark ends “sous la forme …” within a bracket.
- There is no further explicit “à vérifier”, “sauf erreur”, “je présume” or “admis” on these sheets. The large strokes cancelling drafts on scans 9, 10 and 14 do not, by themselves, say that the author doubted the mathematical claims.
