# Cote 85 · notes on the physical text

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **Scan 44, lower NB.** “En car 0 (inutile ?)” questions the need for the characteristic-zero restriction. The question mark is clear. The word transcribed as “inutile” remains marked.
- **Scans 45–46.** “cf plus bas” and “On verra que” defer parts of the classification, including the qualification for the icosahedral case. These are announcements of later work.
- **Scans 48–49.** The crossed “La question est donc quand il est vrai que …” concerns conjugacy for opposite orientations. The live “Quand a-t-on” on 49 asks an equality question and is followed by its commutant criterion.
- **Scan 57, step 3.** “Il reste … à regarder le cas où on a un seul. él. d'ordre 4, un d'ordre 3, soit … avec” leaves that reduction unfinished at the lower edge of the last supplied scan.
