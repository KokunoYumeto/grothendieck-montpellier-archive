# Mathematical notes · cote 85, scans 44–57

## What the pages do

**W1 · Correspondences over a base, 44–45.** The opening sheet relates two subgroups of the automorphisms of a projective line. One recovers the larger group as a normalizer and the smaller as a commutator subgroup. The diagram and the three assignments pass between a four-element set, a form of $A_4$, and a form of $S_4$. The following equivalences give interpretations in terms of torsors, oriented cubes and tetrahedra. The continuation identifies the orientation-preserving subgroup of the tetrahedral group.

**W2 · Three generators, 45.** The data now include three sections with $\rho\rho'\rho''=1$ that generate a finite group $H$. Any two determine the third. The page passes from geometric objects to triples in an abstract polyhedral group and uses the centralizer to argue that the original objects have no nontrivial automorphisms.

**W3 · Orders and changes of generators, 46–47.** The first numerical invariant is the ordered list of the three orders. The author compares its possible maxima in the tetrahedral, octahedral and icosahedral cases. He then writes the three cyclic arrangements of a product-one triple and three arrangements obtained by reversing and inverting. He calls the resulting data “Systèmes de Schwartz” and specifies which automorphisms are to count as isomorphisms in each case.

**W4 · Systems from a flag, 48–49.** A vertex, edge and face of an oriented regular polyhedron give a flag $r=(s,a,f)$. Its canonical system is $\Sigma_r=(\rho_f,\rho_s,\rho_a)$. The opposite orientation leads to inverses and to the word “anticanoniques”. To decide when flags give the same triple, write $r_2=g r_1$. The test is that $g$ centralize the generated rotation group. This is stronger than conjugacy of the triples.

**W5 · The tetrahedral systems, 50–51.** Vertices and faces account for the eight rotations of order three. Opposite edges have the same associated rotation. Flags give systems of type $(3,3,2)$ and its permutations. The margins count two packets of 36. Cyclically ordered vertices of a face, and the opposite faces in reverse order, yield two packets of 12 of type $(3,3,3)$. The statement in f) totals $72+24=96$.

**W6 · Exhaustion in the tetrahedron, 51–53.** The proof starts by excluding two entries of order two. After reordering, the two order-three entries come from a face and a vertex, a vertex and a face, two vertices, or two faces. The ordered type determines the orbit under the full automorphism group. At the page break the author revises a description in terms of a flag and an orientation into one using three distinct vertices.

**W7 · The octahedron, 53–54.** Antipodal flags give the same canonical triple. Six permutations of the canonical numerical type account for the marginal $6\times24=144$. The vertex, face and edge rotations have orders four, three and two respectively. A directed edge and an opposite face give a second kind of triple of type $(4,4,3)$, with three numerical types accounting for another $24\times3=72$.

**W8 · Octahedral classification and proof, 55–57.** The corollary lists all nine ordered types and describes representatives by direct flags. The proof requires an element of order four and one of order three. Scan 56 treats the relative positions of a vertex and a face, with a triangular sketch and a cancelled calculation. Scan 57 gives the order restrictions and begins a case with a single element of order four. The supplied text ends at “avec”.

## Questions and conjectures

- **Q1 · 44.** “En car 0 (inutile ?)” asks whether the characteristic restriction is needed. The parenthetical word is a doubtful reading. There is no answer on these sheets.
- **Q2 · 45–47.** “on se propose de trouver les syst. de trois gén.” and “déterminer quels sont les transformés … qui lui sont isom.” give the classification problem and its equivalence relation. The two smaller groups receive explicit lists.
- **Q3 · 48–49.** The cancelled “La question est donc quand il est vrai que … conjugués sous $H$” concerns opposite orientations. The live “Quand a-t-on $\Sigma_{r_1}=\Sigma_{r_2}$” on 49 asks about literal equality and receives the commutant test.
- **Q4 · 47.** “On peut néanmoins se proposer de faire aussi la classification plus fine” raises the tetrahedral classification under the smaller group. The split into packets on 50–51 pertains to that distinction.

## Remarks of the model

- **M1 · Model, high confidence.** Exhaustive enumeration of ordered generating triples with product one gives 96 for $A_4$. Each of the four ordered types on 52 has 24 triples. Simultaneous conjugation by $A_4$ gives eight orbits of 12; conjugation by $S_4$ gives four orbits of 24. This accounts for the two different orbit sizes appearing in the tetrahedral discussion.
- **M2 · Model, high confidence.** The same enumeration in $S_4$ gives 216 triples. Each of the six orderings of $(4,3,2)$ and each of the three orderings of $(4,4,3)$ contributes a single conjugacy class of size 24. This checks both marginal counts independently of the geometry.
- **M3 · Model, high confidence.** The six expressions on 47 need not be six distinct triples for an arbitrary abstract group. For $(1,1,1)$ all six agree. The explicit enumeration found six distinct triples in each orbit of a generating system in the two groups treated here.
- **M4 · Model, low confidence, reading question.** On 50 d), the frozen canonical triple reads ($\rho_f$, $\rho'_s$ or $\rho_s$, $\rho'_a$). A reading without primes in the second and third slots would agree with the later pair of triples. Small high strokes beside the heads of the rhos and the very close interline correction make that comparison inconclusive.
- **M5 · Model, low confidence, reading question.** In the cancelled formulation on 46, the frozen text reads “bijection de $S$ … l'ens. des 8 éléments”. “Injection” would make the cardinalities compatible: there are four vertices. The beginning of the word is retraced and the entire theorem is cancelled. This does not support a confident claim that the author asserted a bijection.

- **M6 · Model, low confidence, reading question.** On 44, title line, the frozen reading after “corps” is “alg”. A single field letter, possibly *k*, also fits the very short group of strokes. The return line for “de car. 0” crosses this point; the mathematical meaning does not settle the letters.
