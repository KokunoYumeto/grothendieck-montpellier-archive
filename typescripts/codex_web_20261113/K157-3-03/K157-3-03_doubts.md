## Legibility

The most legible writing is in the main theorem statements and the larger display letters. Small direct-image stars, primes, weak arrowheads and the single stroke that often stands for a whole French ending require more caution. On scan 36 part of the printer text shows through backwards and fades at the edge. The printer sheet on scan 37 gives a comparison for its visible pieces. Scan 38 has lost the left edges of several printed initials.

The least secure passages are the overwritten heading on scan 40, the cancelled argument running from 41 into 42 and the slanting note at the foot of 49. Turning the last note makes “J'exagère un peu”, “Kan” and the final “peu !” easier to distinguish. Several lines still share the same physical space as the darker main text. The register gives the surviving alternatives there. Each of the two unreadable marks represents approximately one word inside an explicit cancellation.
