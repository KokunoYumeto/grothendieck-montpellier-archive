## Legibility

The clearest passages are the large displays and the equations repeated on successive sheets. The greatest uncertainties are the overinked signs on 51–54, the oblique qualification on 55, the note at the lower edge of 62, the cropped coefficient table on 63, and the layers of cancelled algebra on 65. On 60 the first words before « On vérifie » are hidden under repeated strokes. A legible formula elsewhere on the page helps identify a discrepancy but cannot recover a covered letter. Archive corner numbers 50–65 are separate from the text. Line ranges refer to manuscript bands, including writing added beside them.
