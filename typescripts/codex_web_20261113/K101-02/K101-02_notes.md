# Manuscript notes · cote 101, scans 16–29

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **16.** “Question” precedes a doubtful word, first read as “classifier”, and four short strokes. “Autres questions” introduces \(\operatorname{Ext}^i(K,A)=0\) “pour tt \(i\)”. The adjective describing the complete local ring is doubtful.
- **19–20.** A doubtful “?” stands by the cancelled opening on 19. An oblique instruction asks to “Introduire \(\Omega\) par une proposition” expressing (i) and (ii) as properties of a functor. On 20, “plus bas ?” questions where part of the cancelled argument should go.
- **25–26.** “à expliciter en lemme” is a marginal instruction on 25, with the last word doubtful. On 26, “Les conditions du cor. 2 sont elles tjrs vérifiées ?” and “est-il vrai alors que \(H^n(\Omega)\) est dualisant ?” receive a doubly underlined “Non” on the same sheet. The example ends with a bracket about “conditions valuatives” and three dashes.
- **28–29.** “Supposons qu'on sache que” on 28 makes the localization of the ambient fundamental module a hypothesis. On 29 the direct proof “semble impratiquable” (spelling doubtful). “Nous étudierons plus loin la covariance exacte” defers a question about the new modules. “loin” and the initial part of “covariance” are difficult to read.

## Title sheets, dates and page counts

Scan 17 is the title sheet. “Notes antiques” is at the left and “1958” at the right. The parenthesis below begins “Caractérisation des Coh. M., des Gorensteins etc”, with alternatives for the abbreviation and the last name. Scan 18 carries an outline in three numbered sections on the functors \(H^i\) with a doubtful lower sign, dualizing functors and modules, and the functors \(H_i\) and dualizing complexes.

No sheet gives a total page count. The lower-corner foliation runs from 15 on scan 16 through 28 on scan 29. The isolated possible “1” high on scan 19 has no certain pagination function. The date 1958 belongs to the title sheet itself.
