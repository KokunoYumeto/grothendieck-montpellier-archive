## What the pages do

**019–021 · Labelling a fibre and detecting the bijection locus.** The cancelled draft takes a finite indexing set $I$, writes $Y'=\operatorname{Hom}(I,X)=X^I$, and uses the evaluation map from $Y'\times I$. The proposed locus $Y''$ consists of the points where the map on fibres is bijective. Surjectivity, geometric injectivity and a closed-immersion interpretation figure in the revisions. Scan 020 isolates the image of a section and attempts a reduction to irreducible components. Scan 021 describes a local closed-immersion condition and then the complement of $Y''$.

**022–023 · Splitting into components and descending their set.** Successive changes of base lead to Lemma 2 and the connected pieces $X'_i$ of separable degree one. The set $I$ is now the set of connected components. The notes put $Y'\times I$ over $Y'$ and give it descent data. A further starred cover yields the comparison of connected components, the parenthetic reference to Lemma 3, and the surjective étale cover of Lemma 4.

**023–024 · The canonical factorisation.** Over the starred base there is a map $X^*\to Y^*\times I=X^*_0$. Compatibility with descent gives the map written $X\to X_0$, described as finite, radicial and surjective. For an étale target the proposed universal property is a unique factorisation through the component object. The discussion ends by loosening the hypotheses and asking which functors are represented by étale covers.

**027 · The generic descent proposition.** Here $U$ belongs to the double product and $V$ to the triple product of $T$ over $S$. An isomorphism over $U$ satisfies the displayed cocycle equality over $V$. The proposition seeks a dense open $T'$ and a descent datum on it agreeing with the given isomorphism wherever both are defined. The argument shrinks the open sets so their intersections with the projection fibres remain dense.

**027–029 · Constructing an isomorphism and descending it.** The notes form $W$ using two inverse images of $U$ and project to the outer two factors. On $W$ two local arrows can be composed. To compare the two pullbacks along $W\times_{T\times_S T}W$, a five-coordinate auxiliary point is introduced. The diagram on scan 028 and the strings of $\varphi$ on scan 029 compare the two composites through $t'$ and $t''$ by inserting $t'''$. Two surjectivity claims then enter the descent of this isomorphism.

**029–030 · Checking composition and agreement.** A six-coordinate construction tests the cocycle for $\varphi'$. Scan 030 expands one side into two parenthesised products, which are shortened using triples in $V$. The comparison with the original $\varphi$ is pulled back along $V\to U$. The corollary introduces an additional hypothesis of strict descent and concludes with a descended object over $S'$ and a compatibility isomorphism.

**032–033 · Removing all but one point of a special fibre.** For the finite cover with group $G$, $T'$ keeps the chosen point $t$ over $s$, while $T''$ removes the entire fibre. Writing the graphs as $U_g=(g\times e)\Delta_T$ gives one component isomorphic to $T'$ on the diagonal and components isomorphic to $T''$ elsewhere. The same construction with $(g,g',e)$ treats the triple product. The three projections are recorded explicitly on scan 033.

**033–035 · From a descent datum to an extension problem.** The diagonal component carries the identity. The other components give isomorphisms on $X|T''$, and transitivity is expressed by the group operation. In the quasi-projective example the notes give a description over $S''=S-(s)$. Scan 035 starts with $X'_0$ there, extends its pullback over one chosen point of the missing fibre, and asks for a model over $S$ inducing the extension.

## Remarks of the model

- **G32, model remark, high confidence for the set calculation.** In the graph written $(g\times e)\Delta_T$, the parameter $u$ has coordinates $(gu,u)$. Both coordinates belonging to $T'$ is precisely $u\in g^{-1}(T')\cap T'$. This accounts directly for the inverse on $g$ in scan 032.
- **G33, model remark, high confidence for the set calculation.** The graph of $(g,g',e)$ gives the three conditions $gu\in T'$, $g'u\in T'$ and $u\in T'$. Their intersection is the three-factor expression written on scan 033. The decomposition into one $T'$ component and otherwise $T''$ components uses the action on the points over $s$.
- **C33, model check, high confidence within the example.** For two split cyclic three-point fibres with a single point retained in the special fibre, direct enumeration gives pair-component sizes $4,3,3$. The triple product has one component of size 4 and eight of size 3. This checks the component count in one concrete instance.
- **E35, model remark, medium confidence about the distinction of bases.** Scan 035 asks for an object over $S$ after an extension has already been made over $T'$. The open problem is to descend that extension. The identity of the last written $X$ remains a reading question as noted above.
