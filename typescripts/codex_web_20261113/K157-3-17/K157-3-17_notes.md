## Title sheets, dates and page counts

There is no separate title sheet, dated entry or stated page total on these sheets. The underlined heading on scan 257 reads “Axiomatisation des ‘fibres W-homotopiques.’” Its number is uncertain and the W- was added. Scan 265 carries “Axiome de la fibre homotopique” and, beside the second circled letter, “Axiome adjoint de l’axiome de la fibre homotopique”.

The handwritten folios advance from 202 on scan 252 to 216 on scan 266. The first digit of folio 211 is overwritten. The lower-left pencil series runs from 252 through 266. The “p. 204”, “p. 108–113” and “p. 53” found in the body are references to other places in the text.
