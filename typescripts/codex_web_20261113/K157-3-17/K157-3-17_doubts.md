## Legibility

The diagrams and their object labels generally remain readable, particularly where the writer separates primes and subscripts. Word endings, hyphens, the points of short arrows, and the reduced letters high on W or Ho are less secure. The vertical cancellation on 252 hides one word. The name at the start of 256 and the overlapping last lines of the saturation note on 257 remain especially difficult. The two large oblique notes on 258 and 262 and the short counterproposal on 266 are legible after rotation. The dark substitutions in 259–261 and the cancelled composite on 262 preserve less of the earlier writing.
