## Legibility

Most of the main lines and the directions of the larger arrows can be followed continuously. The greatest uncertainty is in the miniature diagonal paragraph on scan 188, where an independent sentence crosses the upright formulas. Its verbal reconstruction should be used word by word with the individual entries above. The sloping additions on scans 183–184, the lower part of definition 3.3 on scan 189, and the text under the hatching on scan 195 are also difficult. On scan 187 the bottom edge clips the number of the displayed comparison of inductive limits. Superscript dots, overwritten primes and the final letters of compressed French words are the most vulnerable small details across the set.
