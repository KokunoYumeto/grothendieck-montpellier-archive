## What the pages do

**A presentation of the fundamental group, scans 20–21.** Proposition 1 starts with a pseudo-cofiltering category X and a least object e. The group enveloping its endomorphism monoid maps onto π₁(X,e). Pairs of arrows out of e produce bracket elements. Part c) gives the kernel by relations associated to three arrows out of e and a composable pair f,g. A marginal NB observes that a return arrow z→e makes the corresponding relation trivial [P1-NB].

**A category built from a right action, scans 21–22.** For a monoid M and an element φ the manuscript identifies u and v if φᵖu=φᵠv for some exponents. The quotient F is a right M-set [R-ACT]. The objects of X=Y_/F are the classes, and an arrow x→y is represented by u satisfying x=yu. The class a of 1 is a greatest object in the stated sense. Condition A characterises e=aψ as a least object. It requires, for every u, some u′ satisfying uu′∼ψ.

**A degree on endomorphisms, scans 23–25.** For f fixing e, the equality φᵖψf=φᵠψ is used to put δ(f)=q−p. Condition B separates the elements φʳψ for distinct r. This makes the difference independent of the exponents chosen [DEG-WELL]. Multiplication in the endomorphism monoid adds the degrees [DEG-ADD]. The map passes to its enveloping group. Condition C asks that the image be nonzero. The intervening example on scan 25 is heavily cancelled.

**Evaluating the bracket relations, scans 26–29.** A diagram gives the equivalent words ρgfu, ρgv, ρw and ψ. The manuscript first calculates a general bracket [μ,λ] with a common composite π and an auxiliary arrow ν. It then assumes ψ minimal and chooses Θ(u) with uΘ(u)=ψ. This supplies concrete factors for the three brackets in the earlier relation. The boxed result on 29 has a separate inserted factor below its main line.

**Descent to π₁(X,e), scans 30–32.** The further hypothesis ψ∼1 puts ρgfu, ρgv and ρw in M₀. On scan 31, four factors, labelled c₁ through c₄, are compared in pairs. Equal degrees within each pair give zero on the alternating product [PAIR-DEG]. The manuscript concludes that the integer-valued map descends and states the resulting surjection in Proposition 2. The assignments in the first display of scan 31 need care [PAIR-LABELS].

**Examples and the closing note, scans 33–34.** The corollary takes M isomorphic to Ep(E) or to the opposite of Mon(E) for an infinite set E. The base Y is asserted to be 1-connected. For a suitable F the category over F is 0-connected and fails to be 1-connected. The proposed choice is ψ=φ minimal. The argument rules out coincident powers by cancellation and then rules out an invertible minimal element [MIN-INV]. The final NB turns to right M-subsets of M. The sheet at scan 35 carries only the blank administrative form.

## Remarks of the model

- **[P1-NB] Model remark, high confidence.** With a return arrow from z, the three brackets can be expressed using the same three endomorphisms of e. The adjacent inverse factors then cancel in the relator on scan 21. This checks the marginal observation in that case.
- **[DEG-IMAGE] Model remark, high confidence.** Condition C alone gives a nonzero subgroup of the integers, hence a quotient isomorphic to the integers, as asserted on scan 25. Under the later condition φᵅψ=φᵝ, the element φ itself has degree 1: the defining equality is φᵅψφ=φᵅ⁺¹ψ. Its class therefore gives surjectivity of the group map. This uses the live hypotheses independently of the cancelled example.
- **[PAIR-LABELS] Model remark, low confidence as an editorial diagnosis.** Applying uΘ(u)=ψ to c₁ and c₂ makes ρgv the natural left multiplier for each. The same calculation with c₃ gives ρgfu. In c₄, the printed order of the factors on scan 29 would require Θ(ρψ) at the end. This comparison explains the proposed label changes without establishing which correction the author would have made.
- **[PAIR-DEG] Model remark, high confidence.** If the two pairs of degrees agree as written, δ(c₁)−δ(c₂)+δ(c₃)−δ(c₄)=0. This arithmetic uses just the two displayed equalities. It leaves the preceding labels as a separate question.
- **[MIN-INV] Model remark, high confidence.** An invertible minimal element, in the right-divisibility sense used here, would give every u a right inverse. In the final computation the idempotent p=vu then equals 1 by right cancellation. This checks the specific inference on scan 34 under the cancellation assumption used in the corollary.
