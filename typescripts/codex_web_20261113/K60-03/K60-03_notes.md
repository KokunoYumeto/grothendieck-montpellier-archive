# Notes on the sheets of cote 60, scans 25–35

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

**Scan 25.** Beside the first boxed shift, the writer asks “à vérifier si c'est 1 ou −1 !” The boxed formula has [1]. The question concerns the sign of this shift.

**Scan 31.** There is a small raised mark after A in the top box. A question mark is one of the two readings. The adjacent definition has plain A(t). This is a possible query, less certain than the explicit request on scan 25.
