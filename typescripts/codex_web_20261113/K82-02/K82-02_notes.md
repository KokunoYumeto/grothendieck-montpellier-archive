## Title sheets, dates and page counts they carry

None. These fifteen sheets have no title sheet, date, authorial page number or statement of a page count. The corner numbers 16–30 on scans 17–31 are archive foliation. « Construction » (18, 31) and « Remarque » or « Remarques » (22, 26) introduce passages within the notes.
