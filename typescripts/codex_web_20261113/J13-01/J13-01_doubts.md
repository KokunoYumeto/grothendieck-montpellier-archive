## Legibility

**Scan 002.** The titles occupy only the upper right. The middle of the first word is compressed. A faint pencil 1 is visible at the foot.

**Scan 004.** The handwriting is spacious. The overworked S at the head, the crossed subscripts, the map label and the compact writing in the right margin are less clear than the main formulas.

**Scan 005.** The unstruck formulas are comparatively open. The deleted first sentence, the overworked letters in (*) and (b), and the two-line cancellation around the φ arrow carry the most uncertainty.

**Scan 006.** The sideways left note can be read after rotation. The crowded additions to (iii), the small note at the right edge and the quotient crossed out against that edge carry the greatest uncertainty.

**Scan 007.** Two matching pen marks attach the left-hand continuations to the main text. The short abbreviation before de H., the deleted wording above the filtration, and the tiny equality label are the least distinct.

**Scan 008.** Most of the large crossed paragraph remains readable between its diagonal strokes. Its first internal deletion and the short struck opening above Donc are much less distinct.

**Scan 009.** The two horizontal cancellations, short marginal abbreviations, second argument of π₁, and the marks in the final fibre product are the most difficult. The separate letters under the extensive upper insertions have been retained.

**Scan 010.** The spacious upper lines give way to small interlineations below. The cancelled parenthesis after point-base and the heavily worked first fibre-functor symbol are the most difficult.
