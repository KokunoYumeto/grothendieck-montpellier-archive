## Legibility

The equations and most of the typewritten prose are legible in the overlapping strips. Individual type impressions vary in weight and a few small isolated points have doubtful status. The short pencil words above “non ramifié” on scan 27, the writing beside Proposition 6.9 on scan 30, the ending of “multiplicité” on scan 32, and the words and indexed P-shaped sign in the manuscript questions on scan 34 carry the greatest uncertainty. The pen and pencil marks are inventoried under “unknown” because none of these ten sheets identifies their writer.
