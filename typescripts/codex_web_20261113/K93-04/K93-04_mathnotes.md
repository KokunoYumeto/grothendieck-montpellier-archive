# Mathematical notes on cote 93, scans 179–192

## What the pages do

**179, residues.** A relation attributed to theorem 3 compares residues before and after the relative Cartier operation. In relative dimension one it is written with a single denominator. When the base is perfect the reformulation involves the $p$-th power of a residue.

**179, dualizing modules.** Two numbered remarks distinguish the map in arbitrary form degree obtained by projection from the top degree, and raise the extension of Cartier to the relative dualizing module. The second remark specifies compatibility with Frobenius base change.

**180–182, transposition.** For a smooth proper morphism, a base-change square gives the operation called « puissance p.ième » on cohomology. The transpose is expressed through trace and Cartier. Specializing to the structure sheaf leads to higher direct images, then to the tangent spaces of Picard schemes. The Verschiebung identification is explicitly admitted.

**182–183, the pairing.** A relative $p$-basis with one element leads to the quotient $Q=f_{X/S*}\mathcal O_X/\Lambda$. The formula $\varphi(f,g)=\widetilde C(f\,dg)$ is used to descend a pairing to $Q$. The page lists bilinearity, vanishing on constants in either slot, and antisymmetry. It argues for perfectness in the local basis $x,\ldots,x^{p-1}$ using an antidiagonal matrix.

**183–184, the determinant.** The perfect pairing is turned into an isomorphism for the tensor square of $\det Q$. The characteristic-two paragraph interprets the target as a canonical square and discusses what happens over an imperfect field. In odd characteristic the author asks for a preferred square root and examines the sign of the determinant.

**185, changing the relative basis.** Formula (***) proposes an image for the exterior product of the powers of the basis element. A polynomial calculation for a second generator is cancelled. A new argument uses the scheme of relative bases and a map to a trivial two-sheeted cover. The page ends with two possible applications or extensions.

**186, global cohomology of a curve.** A cross-shaped diagram places first de Rham cohomology between two pairs of groups. Five side notes identify the filtration, Frobenius, Cartier and two transposed maps. A cancelled block compares injectivity, surjectivity, exactness, orthogonality and dimensions.

**187, cancelled categorical construction.** Two squares specify objects by test maps and an immersion. The text introduces a category indexed by the immersion and calls it the limit of subcategories associated to successive infinitesimal neighbourhoods. Both diagrams and the prose lie within the cancellation.

**188–189, local triviality.** A title sheet opens a new group of problems. Section 1 defines a trivial relative connection by pullback from the base, and gives Zariski-local and étale-local versions. Section 2 lists four conditions in positive characteristic under a local $p$-basis hypothesis, including vanishing curvature and $p$-curvature and triviality over a Frobenius quotient.

**190–191, torsors and representations.** Section 3 twists the module attached to an étale group representation by a right torsor. It then proposes descriptions of coherent modules with integrable stratification by representations of a fundamental group, first over a field and then over a base. The more general formulation imposes a condition on each geometric fibre of a strict pro-torsor.

**192, invertible modules.** Section 4 proposes a criterion for integrability in terms of a tensor power of a stratified line bundle. It distinguishes torsion in the group of stratified line bundles from the resulting necessary condition in the ordinary Picard group. On a trivial underlying bundle, in the smooth characteristic-zero discussion, closed forms and logarithmic derivatives express the remaining condition. The sheet ends before the sentence about the lattice is finished.

## Questions and conjectures

- **181–182.** « À vérifier (et y donner un sens sous des conditions aussi générales que possible) » accompanies the Picard triangle. « est-ce dans le livre de Demazure-Gabriel ? » asks for the source of the Lie operation.
- **184.** A marginal « ? » accompanies the claim about rational curves over imperfect fields of characteristic two. In odd characteristic « On aimerait alors préciser » asks for a canonical refinement of (**). The bracket beginning « Sinon, il y aurait » mentions a cover with group $\mu_2$.
- **185.** « à prouver que $\psi$ est indépendant du choix de la $p$-base » gives the independence problem. The final brace links « Application : la dualité en cohomologie globale » with « Généralisation des relations précédentes à la dim supérieure ? ».
- **186–187.** The cancelled « Pour ceci, il suffit que » has no following condition on 186. The cancelled qualification on 187 resumes with « si » and « du moins » and never reaches a clean final formulation.
- **189–190.** « Il serait plus naturel … de travailler avec les PD-connexions » is a proposed change of setting. In section 3 « universelle dans une large mesure » is followed by the proposed comparison and « On doit pouvoir généraliser ». A separate margin note asks about « stabilité par sous-modules, quotients ».
- **191.** « $S$ loc. noeth $(?)$ » questions a base hypothesis. Another question mark stands beside the proposed fibrewise equivalence.
- **192.** « loc. de t.f. suffit ? » asks whether the finite-type assumption can be weakened. « il reste à exprimer » isolates the condition on a power with trivial underlying line bundle.

## Remarks of the model

- **M1, high confidence for the computed examples.** For the coefficient matrix in the pictured basis order, the determinants modulo $3,5,7$ are respectively $1,4,1$. In particular, all three matrices support the page’s conclusion that the pairing is perfect. This is a finite check of those examples. The ink at the final value of $\Delta$ on 184 is overwritten. Any attribution to the author of a general sign convention from that value has **low** confidence.
- **M2, high confidence for the displayed local model at $p=2$.** The sole basis element gives the one-by-one coefficient matrix $(1)$. Its form is symmetric and its diagonal coefficient is nonzero. This accounts for the separate characteristic-two entry in the list on 183.
- **M3, medium confidence.** « Je dis que » on 192 concerns order in the group of stratified invertible modules. The term « première condition nécessaire » introduces the subsequent statement in $\operatorname{Pic}(X)$. This distinction explains why the following paragraph still has to examine a stratification on the trivial bundle.
