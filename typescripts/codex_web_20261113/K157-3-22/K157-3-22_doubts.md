## Legibility

The seven typed pages are generally clear down to individual accents and italic boundaries. Dispersed ink spots occasionally resemble punctuation. The single oblique stroke on 338 hits only the last typed e. The handwritten prose is least secure in the first sheet’s crowded proof, the reworked middle of 331, the line at the bottom of 333, and the interlinear qualifications on 339. In 341 the long cancellation strokes cross letters in the calculation and run into the lower margin. The alternate readings in those places indicate what the ink still allows. The two word-count marks are compact overstruck areas on 331. Further enlargement of the rectangular erasure on 335 revealed enough structure to offer letter or sign alternatives.
