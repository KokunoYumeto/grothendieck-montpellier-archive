## Legibility

The typescript generally preserves accents and final letters. Several lines on the right edge stop in the middle of a character. The densest handwriting is the overwritten first chart column on 182, the crossed pairing and prose at the foot of 184, the dual presentation on 188, the operators and small indices on 190, and the cancelled coefficient equations on 192. Compression and cancellation often leave enough ink for competing readings, but little evidence for choosing among them. The audit records the actual duration of each pass.
