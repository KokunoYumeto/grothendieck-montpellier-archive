# Legibility and limits

The main body is generally readable, but this hand frequently reduces internal letters to waves or separated dashes. The weakest readings are scan 34’s rotated marginal definitions and cancelled bottom paragraph, the adjective after atelier on scan 35, the underlined sous-word on scan 37, and the small article on scan 39. Font assignments for decorated capitals are uncertain throughout. Scan 41’s quoted adjective, initial accent, and small range separators remain doubtful.

All 32 supplied images were viewed directly, with whole pages used for layout and overlapping thirds for reading; extra rotated/enlarged crops were used where needed. No OCR, text layer, outside transcription, or web source was used. Both passes were performed by the same transcriber, not an independent person. Zero `\illegible` marks means that an attempted reading was retained everywhere, not that every word was resolved. Low-confidence guesses are explicitly marked and should receive human review. The inventory records complete coverage, not a claim of error-free decipherment.
