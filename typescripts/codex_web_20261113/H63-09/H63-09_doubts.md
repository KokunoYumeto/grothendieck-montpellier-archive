## Legibility

Most of the typed words, numeric indices, and equation signs can be identified directly. Weak ribbon impressions remove dots, accents and portions of small circles. Scan 92's two hatched coefficient expressions are the least secure readings, particularly the signs and parentheses. The cancelled character beneath the first “courbe” on 87 and the small stroke beside the raised 2 on 88 are also uncertain. The annotations are assigned to an unidentified hand wherever the sheets give no name.
