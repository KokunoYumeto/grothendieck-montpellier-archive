## Legibility

The four Néron typescript sheets are mostly clear. Their small handwritten indices, corrected letters and left-margin notes require closer reading than the body type. The heavy cancelling strokes on 044 and the unfilled mathematical positions on the EGA typescripts limit how much of a displayed claim can be recovered from the sheet. On 041 the sloping NB and its final qualifier are less secure than the numbered conditions. The second half of 043 and the rewritten hypotheses on 045 are the hardest continuous manuscript passages. On 052–053 the variable letters are generally clearer than the cursive connective words or the heads of the shortest arrows.
