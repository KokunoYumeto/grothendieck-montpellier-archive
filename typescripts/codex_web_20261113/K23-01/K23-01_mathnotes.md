# Mathematical notes on cote 23, scans 17–32

## What the pages do

**17 · End of an earlier calculation.** If $\Lambda$ is finite free over $k$, the page identifies a tensor product involving $I(\Lambda)$ with a $\pi_0$ expression written above the line. It then gives another case in which $\Omega^1_{A/k}$ is free of finite rank. The initial letter in the tensor product and the pencilled qualification about $A/k$ remain uncertain. The preceding calculation is outside this selection.

**18–19 · Infinitesimal algebras and their thickenings.** The cover names two subjects, germs of sections of order $n$ and infinitesimal transformations. The next leaf starts with an augmented finite locally free $\underline O_S$-algebra $\Lambda$ and its augmentation ideal $I$. The quotients $\Lambda/I^{n+1}$ give the $T_n$. The author compares a $T$-scheme $X$ with $\xi=X_0\times_S T$ and identifies their underlying spaces. The ring-sheaf diagram and the $I$-adic filtration record what distinguishes the structures on this common space.

**19–20 · Automorphisms and a scheme of sections.** For an open $U$, the author takes automorphisms which induce the identity on the special fibre. These give a sheaf of groups on $X_0$. The relative Hom construction defines $\bar X$, called the “schéma des sections”; restriction along the distinguished section of $T$ gives a map $\bar X\to X_0$. The text relates passage from $X$ to $U$ to inverse image under this map.

**21 · The sheaf of germs as a homogeneous space.** An isomorphism from $\xi$ to $X$ carries the distinguished section $s_0$ to a germ of a section of $\bar X$. The proposition says that this evaluation map is an isomorphism of sheaves. The two corollaries describe the left action of the automorphisms of $X$ and the right action of the automorphisms of $\xi$.

**22–23 · The structural group and nearby points.** Corollary 3 maps the sheaf of germs into the sheaf of isomorphisms over $X_0$ and calls this map injective, citing the faithful action on $\bar\xi$. The author describes $\bar X$ using the structural group sheaf written in the box. He gives $\bar\xi$ the exponential notation $X_0^{\{\Lambda\}}$. Scan 23 continues the unfinished last sentence of 22 with two ways to interpret its sections. The last description breaks off after cancelled words.

**24 · Order-$n$ elements of sections.** The construction starts with $Y\to X\to S$. The author takes the infinitesimal neighbourhood $\Delta^{(n)}_{X/S}$ of the diagonal in $X\times_S X$. Using one projection for its structure over $X$ and the other for the pullback of $Y$, he obtains the object denoted by $\pi^{(n)}(Y/X/S)$. The diagrams exhibit a map from $Y$ and a composite back to $Y$.

**25 · Sections over a test scheme and the graph construction.** Under a finite locally free hypothesis on the diagonal neighbourhood over $X$, the boxed expression defines $P^{(n)}$ by a direct image. The Hom formula expresses its sections over $X'\to X$ in terms of the pulled-back neighbourhood. The text goes on to the neighbourhood of the graph of $\varphi$ and a section of $X'\times_S Y$ over it. Several factors in the formula for the graph are overwritten.

**26–27 · Prolongation of sections and change of order.** A map $d^n$ sends a section of $Y/X$ to a section of the order-$n$ object. The manuscript requires its composite with the map back to be the identity. It proposes defining the map on sections and checking locality on $X$. For $n'\ge n$, the diagonal neighbourhoods form a direct system with a formal limit. Maps of the section objects go from the higher order to the lower. Three identities by a brace record their compatibility with evaluation and with $d$.

**28 · Geometric and affine diagrams.** The top sketch sets the order-$n$ object alongside $Y$, $Y\times_S X$, the diagonal neighbourhood and the corresponding data with primes. Dashed arrows and an outside identity mark additional comparisons. The lower sketches replace the schemes by algebras, showing two maps $A\to A\otimes A$, tensor products with $A'$ and $B$, and an inclusion beginning with $J$. Some arrow ends are barely visible.

**29 · Connections and algebra maps.** Two sets of sections describe, respectively, infinitesimal automorphisms and order-$n$ “connections”. The page asserts a principal homogeneous action when $Y/X$ is “simple”. It then draws squares of augmented algebras. Two question marks occur in the lower square, and the adjoining line leaves the direction of one algebra map unclear.

**30 · Cancelled algebra calculation.** A draft compares the left and right placements of the module $M$ in a tensor product of principal parts. It tests $\varphi$ on an expression involving $d^n f$ and starts a second computation that ends after a tensor sign. Other sketches involve symmetric algebras and spectra. Long strokes cancel the sheet, including the tentative dashed arrow in the small $Y,P(Y),X$ diagram.

**31 · Transformations of a morphism.** An infinitesimal transformation of $f:X\to Y$ of type $S'$ is a pair $(u,v)$ of automorphisms after base change, inducing the identity downstairs and making the displayed square commute. The pairs form a group. A pair acts on a section by the written formula $\widetilde w(g)=u\,g_{S'}\,v^{-1}$. The author calls the resulting section “proche” de $g$ and begins, then strikes, an alternative description.

**32 · Action on nearby sections and compatibility of two connections.** The transformation group acts on the sections over $S'$ and induces the identity on the indicated quotient $\Gamma(X/Y)$. The N.B. distinguishes the data entering the definition of a section from the data specifying its operators. For a $Y$-morphism $h:X\to X'$, the last square expresses compatibility with the two connections using $u$, $u'$ and $h_{S'}$. The leaf ends just after “i.e.” and a few further strokes.

- **R21 · Model remark, high confidence.** The evaluation formula explains which of the two sheaves acts on each side. The source of $u$ is the model $\xi$, with its distinguished $s_0$; the evaluation lands in the section sheaf for $X$. This reads the domains in the display and the left/right labels in the adjacent corollaries.
- **R26 · Model remark, low confidence.** The identity on 26 makes an evaluation map a plausible interpretation of the angular operator returning from order $n$. Its written shape differs from the epsilon-like sign at the top of the sheet. This is a reading question, not a change to the symbol. The frozen leading reading is $g$, with $D$, $\delta$ and $\zeta$ among the alternatives; the witness check also records $\varepsilon$.

The affine sketch does not label the individual arrows, so their correspondence cannot be assigned from their positions alone.

The letters interrupt the lower edge of the frame and sit below a short downward stroke from $\Delta^{(n)}_{X/S}$. They could represent an intermediate object on that row.
