# Legibility and limits

The list on scan 136 and roster on scan 181 are most legible. The least dependable passages are the diagonal note on scan 059, cancelled handwriting on scan 063, much of the agenda on scan 170, and first quotient numerator on scan 171. Scan 064 is faint and clips several right-edge endings. Two deleted words on scan 063 remain physically unreadable, each estimated as one word. The faint upper-right trace on scan 136 may not be writing.

Every whole-page image and supplied third was viewed; additional crops and rotations were used for inspection. No OCR, PDF text extraction, published transcription, web search or compilation was used. The readings were performed by the same agent, not an independent human second reader. Actual audit times and substantive refinements are recorded. On scan 062, the saved transcription was inadvertently included in the same tool response as the original second-pass images before the image-only word list was recorded. A later full image-first comparison repeated all three thirds, recorded a fresh word list before reopening the page file, and corrected the internal hyphen in ma-ximaux while retaining two faint marks with explicit doubt. Both the initial procedural exception and the additional pass are documented.

Inventory hand identifications are visual attributions, not verified authorship. Reused_typed groups apparently unrelated analysis, administrative, root-system and English-cohomology texts; recto scans alone cannot prove physical reverse use. Explicit doubts remain and the result should not be treated as a definitive critical edition.
