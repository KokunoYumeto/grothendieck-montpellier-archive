## Legibility

The main text is generally legible at the scale of the thirds. Contrast varies across a single line, especially on 170–171. I trust the crossed phrases on 173–176 least. The lowest text on 173 is roughened by the copying edge, the last object on 176 meets the black border, and the angular source in the diagram on 177 is overtraced. Bare shafts, tiny stars, dots and isolated accents account for several other alternatives. The inventory records each cancellation even where only a tentative reading survives.
