## Legibility

The typewritten words are generally clear, even on the three inverted leaves. The type can leave the same small round impression for a zero and a letter o. The handwriting varies more. I trust the arrow directions with visible heads, the large field letters and the printed numbering more than the superposed degrees on 23, the two obliterated words and right-edge note on 25, or the compressed insertions on 27 and 29. Both occurrences of fully illegible text are separately struck words in the sentence beginning “Cet isom” on 25. The check against a second transcription often distinguished a plausible reading from a formula silently regularized by context. The marks report the remaining limits of the ink.
