## Legibility

The three typed leaves preserve most letters and punctuation sharply. The subtle distinctions are the slashed coefficient index, the separately ruled Q, and the pen-drawn sign in conjecture B. The cover is mostly blank. The manuscript is readable in continuous passages, but a cross-stroke can conceal a whole word or turn a superscript into a wedge. The least secure places are the cancelled words on 49 and 55, the small labels and blunt arrowheads on 51, the doubled round marks and class signs on 53–55, and the bottom line on 58. The page edge cuts through the last equation. Two fully hatched words could only be counted. The uncertain readings above retain the surviving letters, including those in deletions.
