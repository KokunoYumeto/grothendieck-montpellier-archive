## Legibility

The large letter forms and the printed paragraphs are more legible than the reduced transitions and endings on 114–120. On 115–116 some entire lines have been cancelled on top of earlier corrections. The common label over the upper diagram on 122, the intersecting arrows and annotations on 126, and the crossed interior of the diamond on 128 remain difficult to place exactly. The handwriting in the signature panels of 131 changes repeatedly. Some addresses and autographs, as well as the short signature on 127, cannot be recovered confidently from these reproductions.
