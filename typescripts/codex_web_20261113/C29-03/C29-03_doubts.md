## Legibility

The main typewritten text on all five pages is generally legible. The least reliable readings are the overtyped cancellations on scans 29 and 33, the clipped right-edge endings on scan 33, and the superimposed letters and subscripts on scan 32. Several insertions on scans 32 and 35 have no caret, so their precise attachment is less secure than their wording. The two phi forms on scan 32 are readable, but whether their strokes were drawn by hand or made as a typewriter composite is uncertain. Formula gaps are visibly blank and have not been completed from mathematical expectation. Doubt marks preserve these limitations; they do not indicate omitted text. No OCR, external transcription or other external source was used.
