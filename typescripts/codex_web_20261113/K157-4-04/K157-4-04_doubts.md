## Legibility

The upright displays usually distinguish an index from an exponent, and their larger capitals can be compared across sheets. More uncertainty attaches to short French endings and to the head of a horizontal arrow. The hardest passage is the oblique note across the lower half of 061. Rotation separates most baselines but several words share ink with the main text. The overwritten class letter after Ψ′ on 054, the lower K in the note on 060, and the overwritten class after F on 064 also warrant checking against the images. An unreadable-word count estimates the space occupied. It does not supply a missing word.
