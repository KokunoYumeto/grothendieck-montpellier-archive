## Legibility

I could usually distinguish the ends of the arrows, the ordinal indices, and the placement of the cardinal exponents. The exceptions include the flat map strokes on 216–218 and the overlapping compositional symbols on 214. The most difficult prose is the interlinear rewrite on 213, the cancelled hypothesis on 215, the three crossed words below the first square on 221 and the crossed proof at the foot of 225. The small glosses on 226 are less secure than the numbered list above them. The foliation and the direction of the two large slanted notes on 224 are clear. An unreadable-word estimate inside a deletion records the space under the ink; it does not restore the original wording.
