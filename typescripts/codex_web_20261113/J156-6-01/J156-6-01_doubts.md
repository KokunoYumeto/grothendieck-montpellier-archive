## Legibility

The section titles, order signs and larger display letters are generally clear. Repeated instances help with the hooked and barred arrows, whose right ends vary from full points to single ticks. The least secure readings are the crossed beginnings on scan 3, the superposed labels and capital letters on scan 5, the small replacement above d’autre part on scan 6, and the words written over one another on scan 9. The slanted notes on scan 8 can be followed after rotation. On several sheets a final s, an accent, or a mark between two letters takes up little more than a dot. The single unreadable word is in a deletion at the start of the last paragraph on scan 3.
