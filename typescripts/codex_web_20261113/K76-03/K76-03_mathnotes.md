# K76-03 · Mathematical notes

## What the pages do

**U01 · Scans 33–34. Flags and restriction.** The first sheet continues a construction in progress. A restriction written \(\sigma^\ast\) connects two levels \(\Theta_m(D)\) and \(\Theta_n(D)\). Strictly increasing maps index the operations. Replacing \(\mathbb N\) by an ordered set \(N\) gives the finite totally ordered subsets \(\mathrm{Dr}(N)\). The second sheet starts with a contravariant \(D\) on the flag poset and draws the composite from \(\Theta(D)\) through \(\mathrm{Dr}(N)\).

**U02 · Scan 36. Admissible filtrations.** Cutting \(X_i\) along \(X_{i-1}\) gives \(\widetilde X_i\), a topological sum of closed \(i\)-balls. The marked part is their boundary. The sheet specifies conditions on the induced boundary filtration and on the maps over each stratum. It works out dimensions zero and one, then describes attachment of the top balls. The stratum condition continues onto scan 37.

**U03 · Scan 37. Morphisms.** A morphism is finite, pulls each level back to the corresponding level, and induces étale maps of strata. A slanting note considers the functoriality of taking a level, cutting it, and taking its boundary.

**U04 · Scans 37–38. Cartographic groups and arcs.** The \(\sigma_i\) are involutions and non-neighbouring generators commute. In dimension one the cut segments supply arcs. Pairing their endpoints gives \(\sigma_0\). For a one-manifold there is a second pairing over the vertices, giving \(\sigma_1\). Several attempts to describe the action on coordinates are cancelled on scan 37.

**U05 · Scan 38. The recursive group-set.** The construction applies the previous-stage functor to \(\partial\widetilde X_n\). In the manifold case it uses a map to the preceding stage whose two-element fibres are meant to supply \(\sigma_n\). The word « vérifier » sits next to this map.

**U06 · Scans 39–40. Isotopy, cascades and quotients.** Th. 1 concerns the functor from the isotopy category of the manifold case to \(G_n\)-sets. For a general filtered space the author assembles \(R_i(X_n)=E_i(X_i)\) with transition maps to the preceding level. Th. 2 concerns the resulting functor to cascades. Th. 3 writes out the successive quotients in the manifold case, down to \(R_0\).

**U07 · Scan 41. Building a space back from a cascade.** The zero-stage space is discrete. The shorter cascade gives \(X_{n-1}\), and the top group-set is used to obtain a boundary over \(X_{n-1}\). An inserted « Multicône » names the proposed next space. Th 4 and the adjunction remain a programme.

**U08 · Scan 42. Subdivision.** A cellwise homeomorphism to the cone on the boundary must restrict to the identity there. The author relates this choice to recovering the filtered space. He asks for descriptions of barycentric subdivision, its iteration, the simplicial data and the induced filtration, in terms of the cascade.

**U09 · Scan 43. The essential image.** The author relates a combinatorial description of the image to detecting whether a triangulated space is a sphere. A double-underlined « Th. 7 » has no statement following it. Four further topics concern flags of ordered sets, duality, products and fibrations.

**U10 · Scan 44. Folder cover.** « Cube combinatoire » replaces a title about cellular maps. The pencil count reads « (16 p) ».

**U11 · Scan 46, upper part. Small finite sets.** Entries for cardinalities two through five compare a bare or oriented set with affine and projective geometries over small fields. The cardinal-three line includes the nonzero elements of a two-dimensional vector space over \(\mathbb F_2\). The author ends the oriented four-element entry with a question mark.

**U12 · Scan 46, lower part. Counting.** The boxed expression factors a finite-group order using \(N,r,d_\lambda\). The other formulas relate a cell count for \(G/B\) to a polynomial and record orders for linear, projective and affine groups. A four-column table evaluates the formulas at \(q=2,3,4,5\). Its affine-plane row is written separately.

**U13 · Scan 48. DEA topics.** The list proposes isotopy categories of compact one-complexes with finite automorphism group, isotopy categories of cellular maps, convexity in real projective geometry, and the geometry of the cube and homogeneous spaces. The third topic contains a specific hyperplane question.

**U14 · Scan 50, first proposition. The cube and its faces.** An involution without fixed points on a finite set supplies pairs of coordinates. Equivariant real-valued functions give \(E\), and the unit bounds give \(C\). A partial section prescribes the coordinates equal to \(1\). An insertion gives a condition on the remaining coordinates. The margin describes a facet and its opposite.

**U15 · Scan 50, second proposition. Recovering a surjection.** To a surjection \(p\) the author associates the inclusion poset of its partial sections. He recovers the source from the elements immediately above the least element. The collections of maximal extensions \(C_b\) are then used to recover the relation \(p(b)=p(b')\).

## Remarks of the model

**R01 · Scan 46. High confidence.** Direct substitution of \(q=2,3,4,5\) into \(q(q-1)(q+1)\) gives \(6,24,60,120\). Substitution into \(q(q-1)^2(q+1)\) gives \(6,48,180,480\). Multiplying this last row by \(q^2\) gives \(24,432,2880,12000\), agreeing with the products written off to the right. This checks the numerical rows independently of the overwritten labels.

**R02 · Scan 46. High confidence for the arithmetic.** With the sheet’s \(r=2\), \(d_\lambda=2,3\), and \(q=2\), the printed fraction \((1-q)^r/\prod_\lambda(1-q^{d_\lambda})\) gives \(1/21\). The factor \(\prod_\lambda(q^{d_\lambda}-1)/(q-1)^r\) gives \(21\). This isolates the reciprocal in L06 without choosing which line the author intended to amend.

**R03 · Scan 50. High confidence for the finite examples.** For one pair, an enumeration gives three partial sections, one empty and two choices of endpoint. For two pairs the counts by domain size are \(1,4,4\). These give the numbers of nonempty faces in the interval and the square. Counting alone leaves the order convention open.

**R04 · Scan 50. Low confidence about the intended face convention.** Take one pair and write \(x\) for one of its coordinates. With the inserted strict condition, the empty partial section gives \(-1<x<1\). The section choosing \(+1\) gives \(x=1\). The second set is outside the first. Ordering these pieces by inclusion of their *closures* gives the intended endpoint–interval incidence in this example. The inserted condition adjoins a heavily revised portion of the formula.

**R05 · Scan 50. High confidence for the counterexample under the stated interpretation.** If the target of S13 admits all monotone maps, apply the assertion to the identity surjection of a singleton. Its groupoid endomorphism set has one member. Its two-element chain of partial sections has three monotone self-maps, including two constants. Fullness fails for that target. The example does not depend on the overwritten source letter or the cancelled word before « morphismes ».

**R06 · Scan 50. Low confidence about authorial intent.** Restricting the target to order isomorphisms removes those two constant maps. The source diagram explicitly uses isomorphisms, and the recovery of points and fibres uses features preserved by an order isomorphism. In the singleton example both automorphism groups are trivial. The general restricted statement was not proved here.

**R07 · Scans 39 and 43. Medium confidence.** Scan 39 gives the action of individual generators as a description of the essential image. Scan 43 still calls its characterization a problem and mentions recognition of spheres. The manuscript gives no intervening argument that identifies the two criteria. Its order of exposition leaves the status of the earlier parenthesis open.

**Reading questions arising while writing the notes.** On scan 48, last line, the fixed transcription has \(G_4\). A stylized group letter such as \(\mathfrak S_4\) is another possibility. The rounded body and extended foot do not determine the precise alphabet independently of a guess about the intended group. On scan 50, middle of the turned margin, the fixed insertion has « de dim \(n-1\) » or « de codim \(1\) ». The equivalence of these descriptions for a facet gives no further evidence for the letters. Both questions concern the reading only.
