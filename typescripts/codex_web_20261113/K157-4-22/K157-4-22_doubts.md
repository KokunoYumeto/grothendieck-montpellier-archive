## Legibility

The large implication signs, many numbered references and the component calculation on 345 can be followed across successive lines. For the densest pages, the hard part is deciding which stroke belongs to which version. This affects the triangle labels on 333, the long revision on 334, the axiom and chapter citation on 338, the retraced arrows on 339, and the rotated note sharing the first line of 346. Isolated final letters, localizer subscripts written inside other subscripts, and the distinction between a blunt arrow and a plain stroke merit the most caution. Crossed passages are included even where their proposed words are provisional.
