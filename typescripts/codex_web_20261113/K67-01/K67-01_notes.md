# Notes on the sheets

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

| Scan | Place and wording | What remains open there |
|---:|---|---|
| 5 | “on pourrait exiger” and “[Éventuelt on pourrait exiger l'équidistance …]” | Candidate restrictions in the search for finitely many moduli. The difficulty with pieces that are discs follows immediately. |
| 7 | The display for genus at least two ends with a question mark over the inequality before zero. | The sign of the proposed difference in parameter counts is queried. |
| 16 | “the specific problem on hyperbolic …” and “it looks suitable to consider both modular spaces above as imbedded in a larger third one” | The letter asks Bers about the construction; it describes the proposed enlargement. |
| 17 | “There appears to be a geometrically meaningful retraction”; “I expect the semi-group … to act”; “it is not clear that this operation is intrinsic”; “an intrinsic geometric description would still be … desired” | The intrinsic action and the interpretation of its fibres are sought. |
| 18 | “this should give the desired retraction”; “But then we'll have to define what we mean by these !” | The first concerns the descent to isomorphism classes; the second concerns the radii of admissible discs. |
