# Annotations and pagination

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

**Scan 330, manuscript p. 246, upper left margin.** Beside the claim that Theorem 4.10.1 follows “de façon essentiellement verbale”, the oblique note begins “un peu court ! Il faudrait expliciter l'argument”. It continues “ou en faire” and ends “trois pages”, with several doubtful intervening words. The author singles out the deduction from Corollary 4.10.2 to the theorem.

**Scans 332 and 335, manuscript pp. 248 and 251.** The author inserts a qualification into two different assertions, “Il est ± immédiat” on 332 and “L'implication ⇐ est ± immédiate” on 335. These are the author’s marks about the ease of the steps. Neither passage explicitly labels its result unverified.

## Title sheets, dates and page counts

The sixteen sheets carry a continuous run of manuscript numbers, 241 on scan 325 through 256 on scan 340. The lower-left pencil numbers run from 324 through 339. The count of sixteen comes from these supplied leaves. No sheet bears a written total or date.

There is no separate title sheet. Scan 327, manuscript p. 243, carries the section heading “4.10. Le théorème de représentation indicielle : démonstration.” Scan 326, manuscript p. 242, has a struck heading beginning “4.9.7 Démonstration de 4.9.” with a doubtful final digit. Scan 338, manuscript p. 254, begins numbered subsection 4.10.6 within the text.
