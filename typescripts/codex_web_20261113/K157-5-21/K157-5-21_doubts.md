## Legibility

The changes of line, pagination, main hypotheses and the larger diagram labels are generally distinct. The least secure continuous passage is the end of the slanting marginal note on 330, where the orientations cross. A different difficulty arises on 335, where the last word and cardinal of the slanted note reach the upright column. The status of individual erased primes on 339–340 is less certain than the distinction between the three object rows on 340. The cropped right edge on 333, the digits in several overwritten references, and fine horizontal links throughout account for further low-confidence readings. A candidate could be proposed for every location marked here, including heavily cancelled words. The absence of an illegibility mark does not give those candidates additional evidential weight.
