## Legibility

The English type is generally clear. The typewriter underletters in the two letters are much less secure than the words typed over them. On 31, the lower left note is crossed by large loops. Several putative words there depend on disconnected pieces of strokes. On 37 the repeated coefficient in the two cohomology groups has a long descending stroke, and the right edge clips the first formula. The rank subscripts on 37 and 39 are heavily retraced. Different handwritten alphabets and printed typefaces can describe the same mathematical object without deciding which strokes this writer actually made.
