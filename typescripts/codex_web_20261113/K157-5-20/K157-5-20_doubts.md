## Legibility

These pages can be read continuously. The little source and target indices in the two-column sketches are less dependable than the larger objects. The old layer in the scratches on 309–310, 315, 317 and 324 is especially difficult. The strokes can support a candidate even when they do not decide its identity. The oblique notes on 309, 310, 312 and 323 were rotated. Their argument is often clearer than their small connecting words. A high, detached stroke can belong to a prime, an abbreviated ending or an overwritten letter. The tables distinguish the ink-based reading from an explanation suggested by the proof.
