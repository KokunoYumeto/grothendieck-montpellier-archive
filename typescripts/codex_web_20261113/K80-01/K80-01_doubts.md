## Legibility

The larger displays on scans 006–008 and the boundary diagram on 005 can be followed even where the prose runs together. The least secure passages are the nested corrections in the branching diagram on 003, the three oblique notes on 017, the heavily cancelled upper-left note on 018, the example crossed through on 019 and the reworked first line on 021. Scans 010 and 012 contain superposed lines whose individual paths are hard to recover at the intersections. Their TikZ figures are approximations. The most vulnerable details are the small interior labels on 010 and the faint structural edges and small edge numerals on 012. The layout, overwritten strokes and labels are supplied as editable drawing code.
