# Legibility and limits

The pages are legible enough for a continuous best-effort transcription, but not for a claim of certainty in every word. The least reliable portions are the dense cancelled formulas on 94, the slanted and revised NB on 95, the interrupted parenthesis and cancelled minimum line on 97, and the overwritten or crowded letters and indices on 100. Several quick words reduce to long strokes; their plausible completions remain marked rather than asserted.

The graphs, arrow directions, ordinary live formulas and most capitals are clearer than the cancelled material. The ring exponent, phi form, C/complement shape and several isolated alphabet choices remain intrinsically ambiguous at the available resolution. No word was classified as physically unreadable: tentative readings were retained with `\unsure` instead. Zero `\illegible` marks therefore does not mean zero reading uncertainty. The pointing-hand TikZ is a shape approximation, not a facsimile. Only the supplied scans and attachments were used; no OCR, external transcription, or web source was consulted. No LaTeX compilation was attempted.
