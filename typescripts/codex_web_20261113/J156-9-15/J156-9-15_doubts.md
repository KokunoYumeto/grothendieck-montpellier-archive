# Legibility

Most intact typescript is clear. The x-overtyped passages on 112 and 114, the clipped last deletion on 114, the cancelled labels and uncertain arrowheads on 113, and the overwritten variables on 118 are the least reliable parts. The handwriting on 115–118 has many reduced middle letters and very small indices. It has been read twice, but the tentative words and alphabet choices remain tentative.

Scan 112 is transcribed upright after rotation and its diagonal body cancellation is represented block by block, with local deletions nested. Scan 114 is also rotated. Faint reversed type visible through scan 113 is show-through, not live writing on this face. The small black blot below 112’s final line has no discernible character and is recorded as a comment, not guessed into a word. Diagrams and the unlabelled drawing are reconstructed in TikZ, with uncertain heads/labels marked in place. No images are embedded in the LaTeX. No compilation was attempted.
