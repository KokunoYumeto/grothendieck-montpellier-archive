# Legibility

Most live type is clear, but dense x-overtyping, overlapping handwritten revisions and clipped right edges materially reduce certainty. The least trustworthy zones are scan 7's cancelled indices and overwritten capital, scan 8's cancelled or edge-clipped formula endings, scan 13's right-hand addition to (4.5) and cancelled starts of formulas, scan 14's overwritten (4.8), and scan 15's altered letters and lower diagram. Several ordinary-looking words have therefore been marked uncertain after close inspection. No wholly unreadable run was assigned an illegible-word count; attempted readings remain visible in the doubt macros instead. This is not a claim that those attempted readings are correct.

The second reading was a separate pass by the same transcriber, not an independent human collation. No OCR, text-recognition layer, external transcription or published mathematical source was used. The attached image thirds and additional enlarged crops were the only reading evidence. Geometric editorial marks are reproduced in TikZ; their proportions are approximate, and no semantic content is attributed to the faint upper-left mark on scan 15.
