## Steps marked as unverified by the writer

- **Scan 27, between the two propositions.** A detached question-shaped stroke stands below the first proposition’s type II₄ and just above the next proposition’s “2-dim”. The ink supplies no definite attachment. It is set above the 2 with a doubt mark. No surrounding words say which assertion is being questioned.
- **Scans 22 and 24, end of the first left-hand paragraph.** “auf Französisch?” asks about the language of the projected writing. On scans 26 and 30 the phrases “Doch könnte ich” and “Es könnte” also describe possibilities. These are questions of method in the autobiographical passage.
- No express “à vérifier”, “admis” or comparable reservation was found in the mathematical formulas. The heavily crossed lemma on 27 is preserved as a cancelled draft. The marginal calculation “cf (9)” on 36 is a reference back to an equation.
