# Mathematical notes · scans 14–24

## What the pages do

**Scan 14.** A quasi-coherent module over the ring sheaf on $C$ gives sheaves on the sites over $T$. The diagrams retain the intervening $T_0$. The text then calls the resulting object a “faisceau formel” on the completion of $Z$ along $X$. It compares two cohomologies and specifies a category of adic projective systems for the right-hand side. Both stages carry marginal requests for verification.

**Scan 15.** Three calculations share the sheet. The first places $F_U\to G_U$ beside a factorization through $G$. The second moves between modules over quotients of $A$, localizations, and a map $A\to B$. The third follows an associated point $x_\alpha$ and the fibre of its closure. A birational example gives dimensions zero and one on different fibres. At the foot, the condition on $(T_\alpha)_{y'}\cap U$ is density in $(T_\alpha)_{y'}$.

**Scan 16.** The remarks on 1.4 separate what is tested by Artinian base changes from an implication obtained by testing traits. The proposed corollary combines the two tests for the existence of a solution. Its right margin qualifies the trait case by a reducedness condition. A crossed-out proof attempt and a short argument about dimensions of fibre components follow.

**Scan 17.** Lemme 1.4.1 assembles four conditions, lettered d, a, b, c on the page. They concern geometrically normal formal fibres, density in the fibres of a closure and after étale base change, a family of local maps detecting elements of a completed ring, and a finiteness alternative. The conclusion asserts flatness on the fibre, containment of its associated points in $U_y$, and existence of a coherent solution if the conditions hold at every indicated point. The thickly written subject of the flatness assertion reads $X$.

**Scan 18.** The diagrams compare a category over $\delta$ with a category of Cartesian diagrams. A first display identifies cohomology groups. A boxed sequence then places $H^q(X^{(i)},F)$ between a first derived inverse limit and an ordinary inverse limit. The condition below the box concerns membership of the $X_n^{(i)}$ in the indicated category. The degree in the left-hand term and the letters naming the topology are uncertain.

**Scan 19.** A sketch places $Z_U=Z\cap U$ over $Y$. The note asks for universal schematic density over $Y$ and relates the condition to the inclusion of $\operatorname{Ass}Z_y$ in $Z_y\cap U$ for each $y\in Y$. The finite-presentation or noetherian qualification is part of the same note.

**Scan 20.** The upper diagram records maps over $Y',Y'',Y'''$. A second diagram carries an fpqc label. A finite set of indices occurs in a kernel condition for the map from a completed local ring to a product of local rings, compared with a power of the maximal ideal. Two tests involving $Z'_i$ stand beside an arrow into the product. Six small sketches show planes or sections, curves, and marked intersections. The sheet gives no written explanation assigning a specific claim to each drawing.

**Scan 21.** The cancelled draft restricts an injective resolution from $S_\infty$ to $S_i$ and writes its sections as an inverse limit. It states a surjectivity conclusion and a bijectivity condition involving ML in the preceding degree. The tentative general formula introduces a spectral sequence with derived inverse limits, followed by a short exact sequence. The final sentence stops at “il vient :”.

**Scans 22–23.** A cover on unramified and étale functors precedes a lemma about a contravariant set-valued functor on finite preschemes over $S$. Condition (i) concerns passage to the reduction. Condition (ii) gives an equalizer for an effective epimorphism. The lemma extends the equalizer assertion to every epimorphism. Its argument factors the map into strict epimorphisms and follows an element and its two pullbacks by induction on the number of factors.

**Scan 24.** The first corollary uses reduction and the preceding lemma to get injectivity of $F(Y)\to F(X)$ for a surjective map. The special case takes $F(S')$ to be the set of abelian subpreschemes of the base change of $A$. Rigidity is invoked for (i) and descent for (ii). The final replacement clause states an analogous result for homomorphisms of abelian schemes.

The first proposed correction has low confidence. The arrow directions in the accompanying diagram support the second one.
