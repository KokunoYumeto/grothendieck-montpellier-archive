$\unsure{\mathscr G|\mathcal G}$ un faisceau abélien

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathscr P&\xrightarrow{u}\deleted{\unsure{\mathbb R}}\unsure{\mathscr Z|Z}^0\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}^\bullet(K',K'')\\
