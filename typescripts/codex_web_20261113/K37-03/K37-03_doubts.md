## Legibility

The page and line lists are easier to read than the comments alongside them. The least reliable readings are the small subscript on 078, the Serre titles touching the rule on 079, the low strokes in the verification on 082, the cancellation on 084, the parenthesis on 095, and the words inside and under the brace on 100. The two unreadable spans are each estimated at one word, one on 079 and one on 084. The pencilled corner numbers are recorded in TeX comments. The inventory attributes the longer notes by comparison of their hands within this group. The very short covers on 073 and 099 do not identify their writer.
