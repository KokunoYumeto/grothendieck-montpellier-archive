## Legibility

All sixteen leaves are handwritten. The numbered displays usually separate the class letters, low zeros, primes and waves well enough to compare neighbouring occurrences. The most difficult readings are the contracted noun after « les deux » on 101, the direction under lim on 103, the rewritten conclusion and solid cancellation on 106, and the cancelled argument across 107–108. The slanted notes on 110, 111 and 114 needed rotation. The short insertion at the right edge of 114 is particularly uncertain. The one entirely covered word is at the foot of 106. The audit locates the individual word and sign decisions.
