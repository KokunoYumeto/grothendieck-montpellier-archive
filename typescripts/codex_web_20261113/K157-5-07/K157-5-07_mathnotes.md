# Mathematical notes on scans 101–116

## What the pages do

**U1 · End of a proof, 101.** The factorization for the pair $(TC,F)$ completes the criterion for a Quillen triple. The author links $TC=C\cap W$, $TF=F\cap W$ and $W=TF\circ TC$. With the other displayed identities in hand, closedness comes down to stability of $C$ under direct factors.

**U2 · Remark 5.4, 101–102.** The author identifies which hypotheses suffice for the preceding results. He lists the first three Cof axioms, $TF=C_*\subset W$, and factorization for $(C,TF)$. The latter implies the next Cof axiom.

**U3 · Corollary 5.5, 102.** A small part $TC_0$ whose closure contains $TC$ gives a Quillen triple with $F=(TC_0)_*$. It is closed precisely when $C$ is stable under direct factors.

**U4 · Definition 5.6, 103.** The author calls these Quillen triples *special*. He specifies the conditions on $(W,C)$ from 4.4, a small part generating $TC$ in the stated closure sense, and $F=(TC)_*$.

**U5 · Corollary 5.7, 103.** For a closed triple he gives a criterion using accessibility, stability of $M$ under small limits of the indicated kind, and small classes $C_0,TC_0$ with the two closure equalities (5.7.1). The direction of the stroke under “lim” is uncertain on this sheet.

**U6 · Proposition 5.8, 104.** An adjunction between $M$ and $M'$ transfers a closed special Quillen triple under (5.8.2). The target admits the indicated small inductive limits and has accessible objects. The right adjoint preserves those limits, and the composite $u^*u_!$ takes $C$ into $C$ and $TC$ into $TC$. The author defines $W'$ by inverse image under $u^*$, $C'$ from the image of $C$ under $u_!$ and closure, and $F'=(TC')_*$ with $TC'=C'\cap W'$. He also identifies the inverse images of $F$ and $TF$, and the closure giving $TC'$.

**U7 · Choice of generators, 105–107.** The proof takes $TC'_0=u_!(TC_0)$. The image inclusion and the adjunction give one inclusion between the right classes. The author recasts the remaining problem as (5.8.12 bis), now expressed using $u_!(TC)$.

**U8 · Factorization and lifting, 107–110.** For $f'\in TC'$ the author factors $f'=p'i'$ and uses the saturation argument on 108 to put $i'$ in $TC'$. He obtains $p'\in TF'$. The return map $q'$ in the triangle makes $f'$ a direct factor of $i'$. The resulting containment and the reverse one establish (5.8.5).

**U9 · Remark 5.9, 110.** If the structure on $M$ is not assumed closed, the author says the argument still produces a special structure on $M'$ with the choice of $C'$ from 4.6. He then considers a modification of the initial triple when $W$ is stable under small filtered inductive limits.

**U10 · Theorem 6.1(a,b), 111–112.** In the diagram category $M(I)$ the author defines $W(I)$ and $F(I)$ object by object. He states existence of a closed special Quillen structure and characterizes $C(I)$ by lifting. For the discrete category $I_0$ on the same objects he describes the cofibrations and trivial cofibrations objectwise, then expresses $C(I)$ and $TC(I)$ using the functor from $I_0$ and closure.

**U11 · Theorem 6.1(c,d), 112–114.** An indexing map $I\to I'$ yields an adjunction on diagram categories and, after localization, the adjunction (6.1.9). The following formulas concern cofibrations, fibrations, their trivial counterparts and weak equivalences on the indicated subcategories. For an ordered set, (6.1.15) compares the two cofibration classes with earlier classes from §2. The author states equality in the Artinian case.

**U12 · Proof of Theorem 6.1 in these sheets, 114–116.** Parts a) and b) use Proposition 5.8 applied to the functor from the discrete indexing category. For c) the author uses the commutative square on 115 and the action of $u_!$ on closures on 116. He reduces (6.1.10) to (6.1.16) and the corresponding trivial-cofibration assertion (6.1.17) for the discrete categories. Direct sums supply the calculation. The extract ends at (6.1.17).
