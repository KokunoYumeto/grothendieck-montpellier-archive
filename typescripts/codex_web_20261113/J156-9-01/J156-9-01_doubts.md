# Legibility and limits

The main prose and large formulas are often readable, but this is compressed handwriting with many retraced, cancelled and minute interlinear passages. The weakest readings are scan 2's bottom prose and tiny parenthesis, scan 3's last verb in the Question and the cover print quantity, scan 4's cancelled words and bottom margin, scan 5's slanted support definitions and tiny bracketed note, scan 6's proof cancellations, and scan 7's abandoned upper argument. The cancelled dual formula on scan 9 is also uncertain. One blackened two-word span is explicitly illegible; this does not mean all other uncertain spans have been solved.

The diagrams reproduce the observed labels, connectors, directions and decorations in TikZ. Their pen contours, text spacing and marginal enclosures are approximations, not facsimile typography. They should be reviewed against the scans, especially the heavily retraced classifications. No LaTeX engine was run, as requested: the delivered checks establish delimiter/environment balance and certain static invariants, not a verified rendered layout. The audit records actual reading times and changes; its completion is not a claim that every provisional reading is correct. No pages are intentionally omitted.
