# Notes on the sheets

## Steps the manuscript marks as unverified

None expressly marked. On scan 291, manuscript p. 32, Grothendieck postpones discussion of the amalgamated sum with “on reviendra sur ce point”. The next word looks like “plus” and carries a doubt mark.

## Title sheets, dates and page counts

Scans 291–306 carry the consecutive manuscript page numbers 32–47. Each scan advances the handwritten number by one. The separate pencilled sheet numbers run from 290 to 305. On scan 304 the pencilled 303 appears at the upper left. No sheet in the sequence carries a title page, a calendar date or an explicit total page count.
