## Legibility

The larger displays, page numbers and successive proposition headings are generally clear. At word endings a single pen stroke can carry an inflection, a dot or the join to the next word. The least secure readings are the opening cancellations on scan 291, the angled lemma on 292, the struck block spanning 296–297, the additions that meet the lower-left text on 304, and the overwritten endpoints at the foot of 306. In the numerical portée passage on 295–296 the surrounding uses of the word help more than its middle letters do. The table retains tentative letters even in cancellations.
