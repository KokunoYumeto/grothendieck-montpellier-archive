# Mathematical notes on cote 12 · K12-05

## What the pages do

**064–065 · Cycle classes and coefficients.** The first lines continue a sentence about isomorphisms and finite generation. The author crosses out two proposed cases. He then relates $A^i(X)$ to an image from rational to $\ell$-adic cohomology for a smooth projective $X$. On 065 he distinguishes a canonical from a noncanonical identification and singles out the degree in which the image consists of algebraic cycles with rational coefficients.

**067 · Absolute duality.** For two complexes of motifs $M,M^*$ on $X$, the note compares ordinary cohomology of one with cohomology with compact support of the other. The bracketed regular case introduces $\mathbb Q(n)[2n+1]$. In the box, the low exclamation mark appears on Ext in the first row and on H in the second. The closing sentence invokes relative duality and breaks off after « au ».

**071 · A finite field and its extensions.** An exact sequence uses coinvariants of the preceding degree on the left and invariants of the current degree on the right. The note separates $i=2j$ and $i=2j+1$, then takes a direct limit over the fields $k_n$. A marginal note discusses the change in the isomorphism when $k$ is replaced by $k_n$. The multiplier remains doubtful.

**072 · A cancelled lemma and the start of its proof.** This typescript concerns a weakened projectivity hypothesis on $A'$. It imposes a local freeness condition after passing to an integral quotient. To prove that a section vanishes, the proof chooses a maximal point of its support and localises at an ideal. It breaks off after « l'on peut trouver … tel que ». The substitutions of « libre » for « projectif » fall within the cancelled block.

**073 · Weight ranges.** The page places a pair of weight intervals beneath the outside terms of a cohomology sequence. It writes two inequalities for a possible nonzero group, then gives separate lines for $i\ge2$, $i=1$ and $i=0$. The three small number-line drawings include a struck trial between the other two.

**074–075 · Rational points and a motivic expression.** Starting with an abelian variety over a finitely generated field $K$, the author chooses a regular base $S$ and writes $A_K(K)=A(S)$. He proposes to express $A(S)\otimes\mathbb Q$ as $H^1(S,T_{\mathrm{mot}}(A))$. Sequences in finite powers of $\ell$ and a passage to the limit lead him to formulate finiteness of the $\ell$-primary part of $H^1(S,A)$.

**076–077 · Kummer, the fixed part and descent.** Over $\mathbb F_p$, the author places a geometric cohomology group below the arithmetic one. He uses a Kummer sequence and a sequence involving the fixed part, the direct image and a module $M$. The two-row diagram on 077 compares groups over $S$ and $\bar S$. The discussion then uses the Leray spectral sequence of the projection to $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb F_p$.

**078 · A degree-two example.** For a smooth projective geometrically connected curve $S$ with Jacobian $J$, he takes $A=J\times S$. An expression with two Tate modules leads to $\operatorname{End}(J)\otimes\mathbb Q_\ell$ and to a description using correspondences. He gives this as a nonvanishing example. Some of the words specifying the correspondences remain doubtful.

**079–080 · Finiteness and a comparison of ranks.** The argument returns to the last vertical map of the diagram. It restates finiteness in terms of invariants, asks about a degree-two pullback, and proposes finiteness in higher degrees. The final page compares a $\mathbb Q_\ell$-rank involving geometric cohomology and $\pi$ with the $\mathbb Z$-rank of $A(K)$. The author connects this to Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer.
