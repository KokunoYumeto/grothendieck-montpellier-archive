## Extent of the observed habits

The sloping-p versus dotted-i distinction holds in the coordinate families on scans 3, 10, 12, 15 and 19. The last j in the Jacobian on scan 12 still has an ambiguous preceding x. Doubled Z in the coefficient rings is consistent on scans 3 and 11. The double stroke in the time-dependent tangent diagram on scan 5 is less conclusive. Distinct local choices for blunt and headed maps occur on scans 4, 7, 11 and 14. Short dark bars may denote cancellation, a repeated word or a descender, depending on the actual crossing: compare the horizontal replacement on scan 8, de = on scan 15 and intégration on scan 17. Hatching separately cancels indices on scan 19 but the final N+1 on scan 20 is beside a much shorter mark. The field names, dotted reductions and archive numerals have been checked at their other listed occurrences. These observations apply only to the cited forms. None licenses extrapolating a reading into an obscured letter.
