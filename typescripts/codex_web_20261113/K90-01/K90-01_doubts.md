## Legibility

The six handwriting sheets differ markedly in density. The black corrections inside lemma 1 on 002, the rapid interlines on 004, and the cancelled foot of 008 are the least recoverable. Most typescript letters are distinct once turned. Several runs of x’s and pen hatching hide entire words. The two marginal notes on 009 required a separate rotation. The pencilled digits after IV on 013 are much fainter than the body. An unprinted relation on 003 or 005 is represented by horizontal space. A plain dash on 008 records visible ink.
