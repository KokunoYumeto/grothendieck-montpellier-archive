## Legibility

The body of the arguments, the three permutation rows on each of the last two sheets and most of the diagram outlines are readable. The hardest passages are the overwritten edge names on scan 19, the two layers of cancellations at the foot of 29, the small square and shortened words on 30, and the overwritten family names on 32. The faint triangle on 28 may be an impression from another sheet. On 23 the heavy hatching interferes with adjacent vertex names. Fine differences between a bar and the top of a handwritten omega, between u and w, and between a small prime and the end of a line are less secure than the larger objects.
