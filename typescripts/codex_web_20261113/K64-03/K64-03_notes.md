# Sheet notes

## Steps the pages mark as unverified

None is explicitly labelled « à vérifier » or marked with a question mark. On scans 37–38 the author does identify work still to be understood. He writes « Il importe maintenant de bien comprendre la structure d'extension » before (47), and asks what additional datum allows one to recover the rank-two module from its octahedron. The discussion ends with the problem of describing a square from its two given sets of diagonals and codiagonals.
