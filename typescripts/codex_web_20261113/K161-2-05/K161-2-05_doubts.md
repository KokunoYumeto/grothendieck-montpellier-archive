Across the sheets the manuscript distinguishes rings (opposite categories), triangular roofs (presheaves), waves and horizontal bars (different envelopes or functors). Compare 068 L11, 071 L7–11 and 075 L6–16. The C and J in 066 L15–32 have substantial retracing, while the nearby R and C are single pen forms. The short stroke under lim is read for each occurrence; the horizontal ruling sometimes conceals its direction. The two horizontal inclusions in 066 L36–39 and the inclusion in 074 L2 have hooks. In 077 L7 the open shape is next to a long stroke and remains doubtful. S in 078 L10 and 079 L15 has two rounded parts, unlike the broad zigzag of Σ on both sheets. For pF and Fp the actual order on 079 L15 prevails.
