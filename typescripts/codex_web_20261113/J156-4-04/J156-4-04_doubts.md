## Legibility

**026.** The facing text and the distinction between strates and support are comparatively clear. The overwritten F, the heading after Définition, and the first word of the slanting note have much less surviving detail.

**027.** The nonemptiness condition and the two mappings are clear. The limits of the second union, the overwritten heading of the corollary, and the cancelled compatibility draft at the edge have the greatest uncertainty.

**028.** The two orders on figures and the example can be followed stroke by stroke. The bottom definition was first reworded and then cancelled with obliques. Some of the prior wording there is only tentatively legible.

**029.** Three diagonal notes cross the main text. The last lines of the lowest note, the changes in b′ and the sign beside somme amalgamée are the least secure. Both orientations were viewed separately.

**030.** The family indices can be differentiated by their descenders except for the rewritten first one. The first abandoned corollary and the cancelled NB are especially compressed.

**031.** The main lines are large. Two independent notes cross them diagonally and a set of nested braces has been overwritten. The readings of the earlier symbols in these two cancellations are particularly weak.

**032.** The later entry has generous spacing. The earlier state of the crossed algebra in the opening and the single erased glyph before the pair are less secure than the surviving text.

**033.** The two arrow annotations can be followed from their entries. The deleted beginnings at the left edge of the first oblique note and immediately beneath the replacement about the relations are the hardest passages. Loss of contrast affects the last line.
