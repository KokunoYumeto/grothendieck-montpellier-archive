## Legibility

The large equations, the field labels under the first two gamma signs, and the arrows in the main diagram on scan 6 are distinct. The hardest readings are where two pencil states overlap on scan 2, the low product near the edge of scan 3, the argument written into an arrow on scan 4, and the lightly written right-hand argument and two slanting jottings on scan 6. A few two-point marks have the same dark-and-faint appearance as isolated specks elsewhere on the paper. The transcription marks their possible punctuation at the places where they occur.
