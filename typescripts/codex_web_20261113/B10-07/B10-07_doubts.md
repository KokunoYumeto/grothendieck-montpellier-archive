# Legibility

All eight pages were read directly from the attached whole-page and third-page images, then reread separately. No OCR, text layer, external transcription or web source was used. The body type is mostly recoverable, but the copy is faint at several left and right edges. The least secure readings are the quoted functor name on 102, the local overprinted capital on 102, the added F on 104, the earlier cancellations inside the cancelled NB on 105, the first cohomology exponent and cancelled celle-ci, on 107, the retraced marginal sign and overwritten bidegree on 107, and the clipped/overprinted punctuation and word endings identified in the table. A tentative reading replaces the initially unreadable deleted word on 107; this does not make that reading certain. The recurring tensor glyph is marked at its first occurrence on each page where it appears. Visible grammatical and mathematical surprises were retained without correction.
