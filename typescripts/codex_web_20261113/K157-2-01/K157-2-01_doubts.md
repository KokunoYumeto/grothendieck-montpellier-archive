## Legibility

The cover and the isolated large formulas are comparatively clear. On the mathematical sheets the middle of a word can be a level stroke and a final letter can be no more than a dot. The most fragile readings are the raised and slanted additions on scans 3 and 5, the old arrow labels on scans 4 and 8, and the endpoint names under the diagonals on scans 15–16. Only two cancelled passages seemed too densely inked for even a tentative word. Other uncertain passages have readings in place. On scan 17 the last word is visible, but its sentence continues beyond this set of scans.
