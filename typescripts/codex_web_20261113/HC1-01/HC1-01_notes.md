# Notes on the eight sheets

## Steps the manuscript marks as unverified

- **Scan 39, upper third.** A pencilled question mark and a vertical mark are beside the paragraph beginning « Si la dim de E est finie ». The interlines include « soit n », « d'attacher » and « Si le rang est fini ». The mark brackets the passage; it has no line identifying one particular formula.
- **Scan 39, foot.** A separate question mark stands beside « les inégalités de Ky-Fan » and the framed estimates for products and sums.
- **Scan 35, left edge.** Beside the revised inequality in phi is a word tentatively read « preuve » or « prouver ». The second reading would mark a proof still to supply. The last letters do not establish which reading the writer intended.

## Title sheets, dates and page counts

Title sheets — none. Dates — none. Page counts and authorial sheet numbers — none. Scan 12 carries the internal heading « 2. Fonctions de Weyl ». The isolated pencilled numerals in the lower corners (5, 13, 16, 25, 28, 36, 40, 50) are recorded as archive sheet numbers in the TeX comments.
