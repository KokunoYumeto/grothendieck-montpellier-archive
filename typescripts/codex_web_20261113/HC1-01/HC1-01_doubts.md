### Comparisons between sheets

- **4, 15, 24, 27, 35.** Short free strokes below C, b and R are recorded with a mathematical underline. The folded or rounded Greek letter in the distribution formulas has a loop with a descending stem. In scans 27 and 35 a separate raised horizontal stroke gives a minus on particular occurrences. It is absent from several adjacent occurrences.
- **12 and 15.** The space written with a forked K near the definition in scan 12 also appears with a lower compact-set index. The later L spaces have one upright and one foot. Both sheets use a prime on the revised third condition. The angular letter in the convexity formula is lambda, and the running index under Sup on scan 15 is a short i.
- **24, 27 and 35.** Round brackets, single modulus bars and double norm strokes have been recorded separately. Parentheses after the trace vary from one formula to the next, as do T and Tr. The two sideways inequalities about 1+A on scans 27 and 35 put the bars at corresponding places in the subscripts. The longer version on scan 35 also has an equality.
- **35, 39 and 49.** The script X, Y and H in scan 35 have crossed or returning strokes; E and F in the Hilbert-space bases of scan 49 have separate horizontal arms. On scan 39 the looped letter with the long vertical is transcribed as rho after its first marked occurrence. The hooked mark of the second basis on scan 49 was checked between the statement and proof; both candidate readings are retained.
- **24 and 49.** The quotation of A in the oblique note has paired strokes, represented by the TeX opening and closing quotes. In scan 49 the two strokes on either side of u or x in the norm formulas have mathematical scope.
