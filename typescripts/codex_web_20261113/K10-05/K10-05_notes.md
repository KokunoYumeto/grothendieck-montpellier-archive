# Notes on the manuscript

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **155, L14–20.** “Mais c'est sans doute une bijection s'il n'y a pas de torsion.” The words “sans doute” are inserted above the line. The following sentence extends the discussion to a coefficient field.
- **158, L4 and L10–12; 159, L5–9.** “conditions pour que ce soit … un isomorphisme” introduces an investigation. On 159, “On veut que cet hom. soit bijectif” and “on veut que les … soient identiques” name its two requirements. The long attempt on the lower part of 158 is crossed.
- **164, L2.** “Montrons qu'il existe un …” introduces a finite-module argument. The whole passage from this word through the proposed sum and the next fragment has cancellation strokes.
- **165, L10–16.** “pourvu qu'on fasse la supposition” introduces the condition on the operations on the tensor product. The oblique note restricts its assertion “cette condition est vérifiée d'elle-même” to the case written “1/2 simple”.
- **168, L12–16.** The crossed wording about the product starts “défini, il faudrait que pour tout”. The live conclusion reads “il est bien défini par passage au quotient”.
- **169, L19–22.** “Si G est total dans U” is the express hypothesis of the uniqueness statement. These lines do not establish totality.
