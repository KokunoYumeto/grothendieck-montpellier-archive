## Legibility

The large display signs and the two foliation sequences are usually distinct. Confidence is lowest in the hatch-covered draft on 514, the lower corollary on 513, the rewritten hypotheses on 515, the two oblique layers on 517 and the small diagrams and marginal references on 523. At 527 the auxiliary Hom formula has pen marks at three different heights within a cramped space. A doubt on a cancelled word can concern its position and boundaries as well as its letters. A single short terminal flick can stand for a head on an arrow, and a reduced accented preposition can resemble a colon. The candidate order and the individual crop notes retain those distinctions locally.
