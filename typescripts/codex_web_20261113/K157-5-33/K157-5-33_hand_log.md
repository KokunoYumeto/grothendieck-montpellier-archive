Positions follow the blocks in the inventory. Each observation applies to the cited forms.

### Scan 516

### Scan 520

### Scan 523

The rho above the arrows and the Latin p at the loop have different stems. The lower diagram has its own added prose, partly at two angles. The 5 in the count of arrows has a flat top. These observations apply locally.

### Scan 524

### Scan 526

### Scan 527
