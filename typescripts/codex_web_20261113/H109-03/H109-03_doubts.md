## Legibility

Most of the dark English prose, repeated short entries, page references and low asterisks can be followed directly. The ruled edges sometimes run through a final letter or quotation mark. The line cancelled below “N.B.” on 033 has repeated crossings across the bodies of nearly every word. Its proposed words, especially the word before “is correct”, have much less support than the prose immediately above. On 034 several cancelled letters in the long comment remain covered, and on 037 the lower character on the second T and the barely visible point by the following parenthesis are especially uncertain. On 038 the line numbers rewritten at the top are harder to recover than the replacement numerals. The inventory describes these unsigned sheets as notes in another hand; the pages alone do not establish a personal name.
