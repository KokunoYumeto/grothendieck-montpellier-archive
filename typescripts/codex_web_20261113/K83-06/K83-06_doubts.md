The capital T of the twists has a cap on an upright, clearly visible on scans 140–142. It is distinct from both the small t of the coordinate and the letter Δ of the quadratic form. On scans 137–138 and 144 a negative exponent, a prime and a small vee occupy different places. These distinctions explain why similar occurrences do not always have identical LaTeX.
