# Notes on the sheets · cote 62, scans 68–82

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

None could be identified with confidence. On scan 77, “il faudrait que ces zéros fussent zéros de …” belongs to a conditional argument in a cancelled proof. The small question-like strokes in the exponents on scans 79 and 80 are doubtful characters. Neither supplies a legible authorial query about a step. Scan 81 gives the period relation at the foot of the sheet, and scan 82 opens with a parenthesis indicating the proposed contour calculation.

## Title sheets, dates and page counts

There is no separate title sheet, dated entry or written total page count in scans 68–82. The principal heading “Fonctions elliptiques” opens scan 75, with an added subtitle and “1) Généralités”. Other headings include the expression in terms of sigma on scan 68, “formules de dérivation” on scan 70, “Compléments sur” the two Weierstrass functions on scan 79, the differential-equation heading on scan 80, “3) Fonction ζ(u)” on scan 81, and “Formule d'Hermite” and “4) Fonction σz” on scan 82. These pages all carry working mathematics. The corner pencil numbers run from 67 on scan 68 to 81 on scan 82. They are archive sheet numbers; the author’s handwritten item and equation labels occur separately.
