## Legibility

The abbreviated grd, grde and grdes are particularly easy to expand by expectation. Their short stem was rechecked in each occurrence and the visible endings were retained. The main writing usually distinguishes the open i from the descending j. The horizontal rules under lim and the minuscule heads on transition arrows are appreciably harder to distinguish. The weakest continuous passage is the starred qualification on 394, where two directions of writing overlap. The lower note on 399 is cut by the image boundary. The overwritten addition to the colimit on 401, the crossed prescription on 402, and the earlier words under the last correction on 404 remain less secure than the main exposition. Neither the visible fragment of the note nor an opaque cancellation establishes the missing text.
