# Notes on the manuscript

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

No explicit “à vérifier”, “sauf erreur” or “admis” qualifies a live assertion on these leaves. Scan 16, in a cancelled clause of §3.15, has « on ne sait pas » before a remark about the extension of residue fields. The following qualification of « galoisienne » is itself partly obscured. On the last line a small hooked mark after π may be a question mark. Its attachment to the group symbol is uncertain. Neither occurrence establishes that the author questioned the entire proposition. The future « nous verrons » and « Deligne montrera plus tard » on scan 5 announce results for later. The empty parentheses after « Hironaka » on scan 3 and the empty reference brackets on scan 5 show places for references.

## Title sheets, dates and page counts

Scan 3 begins the notes on the same face as §0.1. Its heading reads « Exposé I », then the cancelled « Action du groupe de monodromie », followed by « Généralités » and « le th. de monodromie par voie arithmétique » with an insertion. On scan 6 the next major heading is « 2. Rappels sur la structure de π ». On scan 12 the heading is « 3. Comportement pour S variable », with an earlier wording struck through. There is no separate title sheet or written date and no statement of a total number of pages. Fifteen manuscript faces are present. The time range in the description of the attached archive supplies no date on any individual leaf.
