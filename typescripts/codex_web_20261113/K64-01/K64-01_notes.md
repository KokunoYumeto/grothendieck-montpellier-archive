# Cote 64 · Scans 2–14

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **Scan 4, after the second functor.** “suffirait bien sûr de l’énoncer pour” the group on four letters is followed by a question mark. The question concerns how generally to state full faithfulness. The next paragraph constructs a quasi-inverse.
- **Scan 7, above the first margin note.** “en fait ce cube est canoniquement orienté, cf plus bas.” This sends the justification of the cube’s orientation forward. The paragraph below specifies a convention for the comparison of two orientation sets.
- **Scans 13–14.** Scan 13 asks “Pour vérifier … si cette description … correspond à $(*)$” and says “il faut expliciter d’abord la convention”. Scan 14 continues “On vérifie que avec ces conventions” and addresses the apparent reversal for the dual square, “on semble obtenir le résultat opposé”. The question is followed through on the next sheet.
