## Legibility

All sixteen sheets are handwritten. The large formula bodies, the comparison of cell counts on scan 89 and the sequence of sheet numbers after the first are comparatively clear. On scan 82 the long diagonal marginal note and the letters at the gluing locus are much harder. On scan 83 the shapes closing shifts vary within a single display. The crossed block on scan 84 and the cancelled signs above the arrows on scan 89 retain very little uncrossed ink. The short shafts, raised ticks and revised axiom labels on scans 90–97, especially the three marginal observations on scan 93, deserve comparison at full size. One cancelled word on scan 82 has no defensible reading. The last line of scan 97 stops at “en” at the end of the supplied material.

The transcription contains 185 doubtful readings and one physically unreadable word estimate. Its 96 cancellation marks count marked passages, which can include a single glyph, a nested change or several words.
