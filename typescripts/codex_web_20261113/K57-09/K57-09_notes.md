# K57-09 — notes on the leaves

## The steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **150, English typescript p. 40.** “A proof of this fact based on our results in Section 1 will be given in Appendix below.” The deferred fact is the finite algebraic extension described in the preceding sentence.
- **156, English typescript p. 41.** “Now we show that m = 1.” The argument begins here. The supplied leaf ends after “another general member … and”.
- **158, upper right.** “inutile ?” is beside the first hypothesis, with a pen stroke pointing down towards it. The exact word carries a doubt mark. The oblique left note also asks the writer to “faire attention” to the property under “changement de base”.
- **159, diagonal pencil note.** “définition insuffisante il faut aussi que les … engendrent … !” The note questions the generator requirement in the printed proof. The short character attached to the pencilled c remains doubtful. A separate “!” stands beside the underlined surjectivity assertion.
- **160, lower-left corner.** A heavily cancelled and circled note ends with a possible “quoi ?”. Its full question is unreadable.
- **162, last two lines.** “Donc si … sans pts fixes … et si … existe” makes the last correspondence conditional. The left margin restricts a conclusion to “très amples spéciales”; the wording of the restriction is uncertain. The supplied text stops at the dots.
