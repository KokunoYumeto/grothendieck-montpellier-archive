## Legibility

The large headings and the uncancelled displays generally have separated strokes. The hardest passages are the revised contents and appendix titles on scan 51, the oblique qualification near the corollary on 52, the crowded pencil- or pen-sized marginal additions on 58–64, and the earlier implication scheme and bottom callout on 65. Several horizontal connectors end in a slight thickening that could be a barb. The difference matters most where two maps with unlike endings appear on the same line. Low-confidence words inside cancellations and their division into words are especially tentative. Each supplied whole sheet and each of its three overlapping crops was inspected. The readings above use only these images.
