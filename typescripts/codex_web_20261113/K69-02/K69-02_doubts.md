## Legibility

The main definitions, large root formulas, manuscript pagination and the printed address on 63 are generally legible. The staff lines sometimes run through small signs. Raised numerals, primes, abbreviated endings and the difference between a light cancellation and the end of a letter are less secure. The punched and slanted note on 51 and the cancelled lower half of 61 are the hardest prose. Three gaps on 61 cover about four words in total. The narrow table cells on 62, the inverted sketches on 63 and the rotated margin and retraced six-ray figure on 64 need particular care. The dedicated second-pass times are itemized in the audit; many were shorter than the several minutes requested per sheet.
