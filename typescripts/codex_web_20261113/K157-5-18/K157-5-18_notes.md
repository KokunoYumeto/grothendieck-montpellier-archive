## Title sheets, dates and page counts

The sixteen sheets are a continuous run of handwritten mathematical notes. The first opens in the middle of a numbered result. The headings within the run identify corollaries, lemmas, a definition and a proposition. There is no separate title sheet, inscribed date, typescript pagination or statement of the total number of pages. The manuscript numerals and the archive's pencilled numerals belong to different sequences.
