## Legibility

The separate equations on 78, 84 and 91 are generally easier to follow than the densely worked charts on 81–82. Fine staff rulings touch the ink on several sheets. On 80 the writing must be read in two orientations. The hardest passages are the crossed equivalence and divisor on 81, arrow labels and corrected coefficients along the perforated edge of 82, item b) on 86, the cancelled f) and rotated notes on 87, and the division between old and new wording in the top diagram on 88. The small weights and the right-hand marginal note on 90 also deserve caution. The absence of an illegible-word macro means a tentative reading was possible at each recorded place. It does not imply that every letter survived.
