# Mathematical notes on K93-09

The scan numbers are those of the archive PDF. Quoted French and expressions retain the manuscript's notation. A question mark described as being on the sheet belongs to the manuscript. Editorial readings that remain open are recorded in the doubts file.

## What the pages do

**U01 · Scans 76–77. From a local basis to a connection.** The author replaces the additive difference $dg-df$ by $dg/df$, passes to the bases of an invertible sheaf $L$, and associates a connection $D_f$ to a basis $f$. He writes $B(f,g)=d(g/f)/(g/f)$. For the trivial sheaf the map is $g\mapsto dg/g$. Scan 77 also considers $g(dg)^{-1}-f(df)^{-1}$, asks which conditions on an invariant survive, and restricts the domain.

**U02 · Scans 77–78 and 84. Affine and multiplicative actions.** The quotient by the action of the affine group is built up from translations and homotheties. The base $df$ singles out a connection on $\underline\omega_X$. In a local coordinate the difference is $(g''/g'-f''/f')dt$. For inversion and multiplication on $G_m$ the diagram ends in the square of a logarithmic differential. Scan 84 writes the resulting ratio of squares explicitly.

**U03 · Scan 80. Weights and changes of basis.** Starting with a basis $\omega=f(t)dt^n$, the author takes a local $n$-th root and primitive, records its ambiguity, and writes $c_n(\omega)$. He defines $G_{m,n}$ by a difference and lists identities for a third basis, taking powers and changing the weights. A second normalization has $\delta=m\alpha=n\beta$. The inverted calculations at the foot compare equal differentials and equal squared logarithmic differentials.

**U04 · Scan 81. Two working diagrams and a separate calculation.** The diagrams connect $U(x),B(x),V(x)$ with $\Sigma(x)$, a hatted $T_X$ and real- or complex-analytic labels. The crossed work considers a quotient of pairs. Lower down, the sheet lays out two-by-two arrays of powers of $\Omega$, then starts separate calculations with $f+1/f$, $g/g'$ and an integral. The last line of the quadratic expression ends with an equals sign.

**U05 · Scan 82. Comparison of the difference constructions.** Two charts set the additive, multiplicative, affine and projective constructions beside one another. The six formulas between them keep track of their values. The lower chart names the maps $\alpha_n,\beta_n,\gamma,\delta$. The final lines evaluate them locally and revisit the quadratic expression attached to $D+\omega_1$.

**U06 · Scan 83. Successive cohomology groups.** Under a possible $X=\Omega Y$, the sheet places $k_1,k'_1,k''_1$ beside groups in degrees 3, 4 and 5. A further degree and a suspension map appear below. Two diagrams on the right give maps labelled $k'_1$ and $k_1$. The evidence for the first letter and the final degree is recorded separately.

**U07 · Scans 86–87. A projective-line bundle and its associated objects.** Section 1.1 lists a projective-line bundle, rank-two module data, an Azumaya algebra, Lie algebras and group forms. Starting from $P$, the author writes $E=f_*L$, relates $M$ to endomorphisms of $E$, and writes the commutator $xy-yx$. Ideals, Borel subalgebras and subgroups give descriptions of $P$. Marginal qualifications pay particular attention to 2.

**U08 · Scan 87. Adding a marked section.** Section 1.2 puts a marked section beside an extension $0\to\Omega\to\mathscr L\to\underline O_X\to0$, a special ideal and Borel data. With a rigidified line bundle, the calculations give the three graded pieces of a filtration. A bracketed passage asks for a relative connection on $P$ with a section $\sigma$, without assuming that the section is horizontal.

**U09 · Scan 88. Two disjoint sections.** The top diagram brings two disjoint sections into correspondence with an invertible $\Omega$ and a split description of the associated objects. The middle of the sheet gives three bracket values. The corresponding primed construction has a second list of coefficients. At the foot, vertical arrows return to $G$ and $G'$.

**U10 · Scans 89–90. A split calculation of the connection.** A local generator $\delta$ gives the basis $\delta,1,\delta^{-1}$. The connection $c_0$ kills those basis elements. A new connection is expressed with three components $\varpi_2,\varpi_1,\varpi_0$. Scan 90 expands the brackets, gives the weights, and reads off the induced maps on successive graded pieces.

**U11 · Scan 90. Fixing the induced map.** The boxed equation relates $c_{1,2}$ and $c_{2,3}$. The page characterizes preservation of the filtration and discusses horizontal sections. After fixing $i$, it reduces the structural group of a torsor of connections and describes the restricted first-order operator $c_\Omega$ by its composite and its rule on $fw$.

**U12 · Scan 91. Principal parts.** The last page restates the restriction of structural group and considers the case where the composite labelled $-2i$ is an isomorphism. It then identifies $\mathscr L$ with $\mathcal P^1_{X/S}(\underline\omega)\otimes\underline\omega^{-1}$ and identifies $c_\Omega$ with the first principal-parts operator.

## Slips

**S77 · Scan 77, middle display after « Donc ici ».** The sheet gives (S77a) $(dg/g):(df/f)=(dg/df)(g/f)$. The corrected factor (S77b) is $f/g$ for this equality. Dividing by $df/f$ puts $f$ in the numerator. The ornament above $C$ is doubtful but plays no role in the computation. High confidence for the factor.

**S80 · Scan 80, second parenthesis of $G_{m,n}$.** The squared quotient (S80a) is again $(g'/g)^2$. The definition immediately to the left subtracts $c_m(\omega_m)$, with $\omega_m=f(dt)^m$, giving $(f'/f)^2$ at this occurrence (S80b). The numerator of a *different* term in the same parenthesis looks like a raised 4 or two primes and is left open. High confidence for the repeated squared quotient alone.

## Remarks of the model

**R77 · Model, high confidence.** Scan 84 independently writes $E(f,g)=(dg/df)^2/(g/f)^2$. On nonzero local values it agrees with the square of the ratio on the left of S77. As a check, for $f=2,g=3,f'=5,g'=7$ both squares are $196/225$. The unsquared final expression written on scan 77 gives $21/10$, whereas the ratio on its left gives $14/15$.

**R80 · Model, high confidence for the squared quotient.** Set $f=t+1,\ g=3t+1,\ m=1,\ n=2$ at $t=1$. Both second derivatives vanish. The difference from the two individual $c$-formulas is $-3/64$. Repeating the $g$-quotient in the second term gives $-63/64$ under the double-prime reading of the adjacent ambiguous mark. The variable dependence can also be tested without interpreting the mark. Fix $f=1$, let $g$ be either 1 or $1+t$, and compare at $t=0$. The contribution from the repeated square inside the second parenthesis changes by 3, although every term depending only on $f$ stays fixed.

**Q82 · Model, low confidence as a reading question.** On scan 82, last display, the frozen first line reads $2((1/n)f'/f)'+((1/n)f'/f)^2$. The next line has $-2nf''/f+(2n+1)(f'/f)^2$, divided by $n^2$, beside a crossed coefficient. A negative sign on the differentiated term would reconcile the two local expressions. For $f=t+1,\ n=2,\ t=1$ the displayed forms instead give $-3/16$ and $5/16$. This concerns the sign of that term or of the preceding local connection form. The ink does not assign the discrepancy to a particular line.

**Q83 · Model, low confidence as a reading question.** Scan 83, central horizontal suspension arrow, target. The frozen reading is `K(\pi_1(X,2)`. Another reading is `K(\pi_1(X),2)`. On enlargement a stroke immediately to the right of X could be its last pen stroke or a narrow closing parenthesis before the comma. The neighbouring diagrams use the latter grouping.

**R90 · Model, high confidence.** The boxed $c_{1,2}=-2c_{2,3}$ on scan 90 follows directly from the two coefficients on the immediately preceding line, $c_{12}=-2\varpi_0$ and $c_{2,3}=\varpi_0$. This particular substitution requires no division by 2. The separate question about characteristic two stays open on the page.
