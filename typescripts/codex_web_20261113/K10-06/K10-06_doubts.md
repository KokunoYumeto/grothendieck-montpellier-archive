## Legibility

The principal arguments can generally be followed in the baseline writing. The two sides of a pairing, the height of a prime, the presence of an arrowhead and the accent in « coéfficients » often require a closer look than the adjoining sentence. The least dependable passages are the dense cancellations on 171–176, the three diagonal additions on 179, the lower indices overwritten on 182 and the first three lines on 183. The angle of the pencil equations on 174 also makes a difference to the interpretation of their small indices. A candidate inside a cancellation represents surviving strokes, even when the live wording next to it would make a different reading more natural.
