## Legibility

The two typescripts are readable down to their punctuation. The shapes underneath a few handwritten repairs are less certain than their positions. The ordered prose on scans 143–149 is clearer than the abbreviated labels and multiple layers of ink on scans 134, 137 and 140. The sloping notes on 143 and 147 pass close to the edge or into the main text. Candidate readings are particularly sensitive to that overlap and to the distinction between an arrowhead, a terminal flick and a bare stroke.
