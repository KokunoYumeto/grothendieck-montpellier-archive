# Mathematical notes on scans 44–56

## What the pages do

**44–45 · A letter to Coates on the index conjecture.** Grothendieck asks Coates to place the conjecture immediately after the results of Hodge theory. Coupling it with condition \(A\) gives the proposed implication “\(A+\text{index}\) implies \(B\)”. He distinguishes the independence statements available in characteristic zero from those for which he wants a proof in positive characteristic.

**45–46 · Equivalent conditions.** A proposition compares \(C_\ell(X)\) with conditions on a hyperplane section \(Y\), on \(X\times X\) and \(X\times Y\), and on algebraic correspondences inducing particular left or right inverses. The corollary iterates hyperplane sections. The proof sketch identifies the two outer maps in a three-map composite and supplies a formula involving \(L_X,L_Y\) and \(\Lambda_X\). A second formula is added in pen.

**46–47 · Known cases and the first open cases.** The letter lists the algebraicity of certain projectors and a correspondence from \(H^{2n-1}(X)\) to \(H^1(X)\). It singles out 1-cycles on threefolds. A proposal using specialization to nonsingular hypersurfaces runs up against Serre’s simple-connectivity objection.

**48–49 · A second letter to Coates.** The proposition relates \(C\) to the existence of algebraic isomorphisms \(H^{2n-i}(X)\to H^i(X)\) for \(i<n\). One version assumes \(D_\ell(X)\), another asks for rational coefficients of characteristic polynomials of algebraic endomorphisms. The proof forms \(w=uv\) and expresses its inverse using its characteristic polynomial.

**49 · Inverses and algebraic classes.** The corollary assumes \(C_\ell\) for both \(X\) and \(X'\). Duality and transposition supply the return map \(u'\) for an algebraic isomorphism \(u\). The letter then considers a monomorphism and gives a left inverse in a hyperplane-section example.

**50 · Variants of the Lefschetz conditions.** The letter introduces \(A'_\ell\) and \(A''_\ell\) by asking when an algebraic class can be recovered by restriction or lifted through the map from a section. It restricts attention to critical dimensions and gives separate factorizations in even and odd dimension. The indices in the heavily revised second definition remain as written.

**51, 53, 55 · A lemma on projections.** An endomorphism ring is assumed stable under homogeneous components and transposition. Given the stated left inverses to powers of the degree-raising operator, the lemma puts \(L\) and the projections onto the factors written \(\xi^jP^i\) in the ring. The corollary formulates an algebraicity criterion. The proof inducts on \(k=i+2j\), forms \(T_k\) by subtracting projections already obtained, then constructs the individual factor projection “par différence.” Several signs and superscripts in the intermediate maps are marked in the transcription.

**52 · “Différente”, typescript page (4).** The outline defines the Kähler different from the first ideal called “Filting” on the page and a presentation of \(\Omega_A(B)\). It identifies the ramification locus and asks for accounts of change of ring, transitivity and the relation to the trace different. It ends with tame ramification, extraction of a root of a uniformizer, and an example from Riemann surfaces.

**54 · Automorphisms and a nonramification criterion, typescript page (3).** After finishing a local-structure statement, the outline revises material on automorphism groups and the discriminant. It adds two finite-étale conditions to a theorem, compares local and residue-algebra criteria, and proposes an appendix about a nilpotent ideal.

**56 · Étale and unramified algebras, typescript page (2).** The outline defines the two terms and étaleness at a prime. It treats base change, faithfully flat descent, completions and the splitting criterion over a connected base. The last line begins a local-structure criterion and breaks off at “de polynôme minimal \(F\)”. The grammatical continuation is at the top of typescript page (3), scan 54.

## Remarks of the model

- **Model remark R1a · High confidence, scans 48–49.** With the order \(w=uv\) used in the letter, \(w^{-1}u=v^{-1}\). Formal cancellation of invertible symbols verifies the order of the factors.
- **Model remark R1b · High confidence, scan 49.** The corollary puts \(u'\) on the other side when it defines \(w=u'u\). Formal cancellation gives its displayed conclusion \(w^{-1}u'=u^{-1}\).
- **Model remark R2 · High confidence, scan 50.** The displayed even-dimensional chain changes cohomological degree by \(0,2,2\). The odd-dimensional chain changes it by \(0,2\). Subtracting successive degrees verifies this, including the endpoints written \(2m+1-1\) and \(2m+1+1\).
- **Model remark R3 · High confidence, scan 53.** The parenthesis “\(j'\geq j\) (i.e. \(i'\leq i\))” uses a fixed \(k\). Substitution into \(i+2j=i'+2j'=k\) gives \(i'-i=-2(j'-j)\), which explains the direction of the second inequality.
