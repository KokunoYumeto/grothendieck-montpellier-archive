## Legibility

The main runs of English type are relatively clear. Corrections made with a row of x’s can leave both the original letters and the cancellation visible, but the strike order is often unrecoverable. The right edge of the formulas on 45 and 46 cuts through characters. The smaller Tribu type remains readable under enlargement.
