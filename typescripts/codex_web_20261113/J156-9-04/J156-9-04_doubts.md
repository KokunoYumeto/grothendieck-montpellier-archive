# Legibility and trust

The large formulas and most ordinary prose are readable, but many word middles are reduced to horizontal strokes. The least trustworthy passages are the underlined noun and short abbreviation on 26, the very low edge note on 27, the compressed words on 30, the concluding slanted margin sentence on 31, and the upper restriction sentence on 33. The cancelled block on 31 is retained, but some underforms and its small subscript remain doubtful. Some candidate phrases are provisional readings, not secure restorations.

This is an internally reread transcription, not an independent human verification. LaTeX was checked structurally and deliberately not compiled.
