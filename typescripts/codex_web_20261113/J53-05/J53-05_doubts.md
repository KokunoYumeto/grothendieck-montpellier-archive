## Legibility

- **Scan 29.** The open lines of the main argument read more readily than the interlinear criterion and the two marginal cancellations. The crossings at the right obscure parts of several words. The small arrow tips are especially shallow.
- **Scan 30.** The angular exponent and both limits can be compared at several sites. The successive, crossed starts before the third corollary obscure the lower parts of the letters.
- **Scan 31.** Additions above the proposition and the reversals of gauche and droite survive at different heights. The target E and the erased starts of the last argument have far less distinct contours than the equation g ↦ ε(g.φ).
- **Scan 32.** This sheet was also used sideways. Turning its two marginal diagrams makes the positions of the objects and arrows visible. The final pair of ε expressions and the direction of the heavy group-diagram strokes are the least distinct details.
- **Scan 33.** The heading, functor and tensor-product formulas are well spaced. The small sign before k′, the letters under the strike near libre, and the vertical sign below the diagonal groups have the least distinct strokes.
- **Scan 34.** The lower half is blank except for the archive number. Reading the second argument of each Hom is substantially harder than reading the surrounding formula. Several layers of ink intersect and the lowest Hom line reaches the cut edge.
