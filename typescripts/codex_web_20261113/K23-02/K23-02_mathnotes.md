# K23-02 · Mathematical notes

These notes follow the scans in their order. Quotations from scans 44–47 belong to the correspondent Lê Dũng Tráng. The identifiers below link the two computations and the cross-page observation to the check log.

## What the pages do

**33 · Connections and products.** The sheet first gives an identity between the operators attached to two connections and composition by h. Under the heading numbered 3°), it forms a connection on the fibre product over Y. The displayed rule evaluates the tilde operator for the product on a pair of sections. A corollary mentions compatible composition laws.

**35–36 · Two examples of regular rings.** In (i), a quotient of k[X,Y] is placed beside assertions about regularity, its fraction field, and failure of integrality after extending the ground field. The first polynomial has been revised; a pencil substitution and the « non » above a circled assertion are integral to reading the example. In (ii) the equation is F = Y²−Xᵖ+a, with p≠2. After the change of variables X−λ=Z the writer looks at Y²−Zᵖ at the origin. He presents the module of differentials, identifies a candidate exceptional prime (Y,Xᵖ−a), and calculates a quotient of the ideal by its square. Scan 36 calls the point « simple, mais non absolument simple ». It then explicitly returns to (i).

**37 · Differential criteria.** A landscape sheet puts criteria involving Ω¹ and Δ next to short labels, including non-ramification, étalement and a finite morphism. A second group takes B/A with a p-basis and compares vanishing of differentials of several orders with membership in subrings generated by powers. At the foot, the author sketches implications for a field extension. The middle exponent and parts of the captions remain uncertain.

**38 · Transporting a structure by frames.** This reused typescript specifies a condition C for a family attached to a map into G/Γ. It argues that a frame transports a structure to F′ and that the result is independent of the frame because changes of frame act by automorphisms. It then introduces H with a larger triviality subgroup and starts condition C′ on the quotient map. The page ends after « est une ».

**39–41 · A differential functor of bounded order.** A title leaf precedes a construction using categories fibred over schemes over S. A Cartesian section associates an object to X′/S′ compatibly with change of base. The examples include the underlined P with upper n and the associated principal bundle. An action by automorphisms of a truncated polynomial algebra supplies data for a functor. Scan 41 asks how to characterize the resulting functors. Material about the schemes with labels E and S and the augmentation is heavily cancelled.

**44 and 45–46 · A letter and corrections on regular sections.** The letter explains why the errata arrived late and why their effect on proofs is uncertain. In (20.1.3), Lê proposes defining the subsheaf using regularity after restriction to every open subset. He distinguishes its stalk from the set of all regular elements of the local ring. Other replacements concern the multiplicative set of denominators, invertibility after restriction, and the sets of sections in (20.1.11), (21.1.5) and (21.1.6).

**46–47 · Divisors, local identifications and cycles.** The two additions at (21.2.9) describe how a local generator f enters the map on sections and identify the divisor of the section labelled s with subscript D. The third enclosure has a cancelled proposal about a locally noetherian hypothesis and a separate cancelled reference. Its surviving correction at (21.6.2) distinguishes two forms of the cycle letter Z.

**49 · Gluing and descent for sections.** First the sheet gives compatible sections over a schematically dense open U and a closed subscheme Y₀ with the same underlying space as Y. It asserts existence and uniqueness over Y. The second diagram introduces Y′ and Y″, with a surjectivity condition over the fibre product, and says that the corresponding sequence of sets of sections is exact. A marginal note suggests weaker hypotheses. The first corollary proposes replacing Y₀ by a finite surjective Y′; the next begins and stops.

**50 · Regular sequences.** The heading « Suites régulières » is followed by a reference to a theorem of Raynaud. The sheet carries no statement of that theorem.

## Questions and conjectures

- **35–36.** The word « non » on the circled line of (i) and « il reste à vérifier » on the following sheet leave the status of the field assertion open in these notes.
- **37.** A « ? » belongs to the crossed differential equality at the left. The reading of the equality is itself uncertain.
- **40–41.** « Peut-être faut-il plutôt prendre comme base C » is struck. « On veut caractériser les foncteurs obtenus de cette façon » describes the continuing problem.
- **44.** « Je ne sais pas s'ils entravent des démonstrations » is Lê’s question about the implications of his errata.
- **49.** « On doit pouvoir éliminer l'hyp. loc. noeth. et remplacer fini par entier ». The last corollary on the sheet has no written conclusion.
