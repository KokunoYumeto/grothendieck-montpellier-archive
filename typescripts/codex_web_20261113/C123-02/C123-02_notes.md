# C123-02: notes from these four scans

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **Scan 22:** “I wonder if you know the answer to some of them.” The letter asks whether, “for some, or for all” of the described quadruples, B and B' are “in general position”, with their intersection a torus; whether there is a “distinguished conjugacy class” invariant under exterior automorphisms; and “What are the groups of automorphisms of such quadruples”. The last question continues on scan 23. These are questions, not established conclusions on these sheets.
- **Scan 23:** “Do you know for instance exactly how varies the rank” of the indicated map. “There are some reasons to believe” introduces the proposed ranks on the orbits of dimension n-r-2 (with n- inserted). “I would expect that there is just one orbit” and “maybe this property will be characteristic” mark the subsequent claims as expectations.
- **Scan 23:** “what can be said of the dimension” of the variety of Borel subgroups containing an element. For an orbit of dimension 2N-2i, “I would expect the dimension of the previous variety to be equal to i”. The following “the inequality … would imply” states a conditional consequence concerning rational singularities; this sheet does not supply a proof of the proposed inequality. The author closes by asking for comments on the questions.
- **Scan 28, cancelled draft:** “Plutôt que d'entrer dans la tâche peu engageante de donner un sens précis à cet énoncé et de le justifier de façon formelle” expressly declines the formal justification here. The reader is advised “de l'expliciter et de se convaincre de sa validité” for the listed structures; l'expliciter et de is an insertion. In the footnote, the doubtful cancelled words “vérifier que” are replaced by the reference “(1.5 et 1.8)”. Their cancellation is important: they are not a surviving instruction to verify.
- **Scan 40:** none explicitly marked as an unverified step. “s'il s'avère que” is a condition in the discussion, not an independent assertion that a proof is missing.
