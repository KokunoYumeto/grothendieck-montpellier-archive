## Legibility

The arrowheads on the larger diagrams, the contrast between j and i, and the exponents on the two spheres are particularly clear. Short trial phrases under a strike are harder to separate from their replacements. The least secure areas are the trial diagram on 387, the join between 388 and 389, the overwritten folios on 391–392, and the heavily retraced letters in the degree argument and the last Hom calculation. The three oblique marginal notes were read after rotation. Their doubtful words are marked where they occur.
