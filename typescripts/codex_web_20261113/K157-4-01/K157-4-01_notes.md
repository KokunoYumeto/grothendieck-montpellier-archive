| Scan | Wording on the sheet | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| 003 | “donner définition en forme, ou renvoi à Quillen” | A marginal reminder to supply either a formal definition of the lifting terminology or a reference. |
| 004 | “Donner définition … en forme.” | A further marginal reminder beside the discussion of closed parts. The intervening word is uncertain. |

| 012–013 | “Il y aurait lieu de développer la notion …” / “qui impliquerait l’existence …” / “serait donc un couple de Quillen.” | The proposed notion of accessible parts and the claims conditional on it are prospective. |
| 013 | “conjectural.” | Qualifies the subtitle of section 2. The intervening word after “Théorème-” is doubtful. |
| 015–016 | “Reste à prouver M′” / “Donc il reste à prouver le Corollaire … 1” | Identifies the remaining part of the proof. The following pages take up one equality and refer the other to duality. |
