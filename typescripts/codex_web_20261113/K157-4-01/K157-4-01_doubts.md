The type on 426 and 427 is mostly clear. The whitened final line on 427, the small references on the cover and the foot of handwritten pages 3 and 9 carry the greatest uncertainty. Across the mathematical sheets a pen stroke may be both an earlier letter and the main stroke of a replacement. The stars of the two lifting operations are often clear in isolation, but the extra dark marks beside them on scans 005 and 013 resist a firm reading. The angle and the overlap of the two directions of writing at the foot of scan 007 make its chapter reference particularly insecure.
