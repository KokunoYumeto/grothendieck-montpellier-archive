## Comparisons across the folder

Scans 48 (ll. 11, 16, 19), 51 (l. 20), 52 (ll. 5, 8, 12), and 55 (last two lines) use distinguishable R forms. A parallel inner stem can end in a short connecting foot. The cross of a low bowl or a diagonal cancellation across the entire line does not supply such a stem. Scan 52 shows the two forms within four lines. Scans 49 (l. 17) and 57 (l. 5) have a two-arm raised cross: the boxed stars on 57 (l. 13) have additional arms. The high dot on α on 59 (l. 15) is much darker than the hollow degree on Q on 58 (l. 11). The second exponent on Δ on 57 (l. 9) is much flatter than the parenthesised letter over Δ on 60 (l. 19). The plain shaft, hooked tail and wavy arrow were compared separately on 54, 58 and 60. These shapes do not form a uniform convention throughout the folder.

## Final checks of recurring shapes

On scan 48, the three upper signs do not all permit the same conclusion. Parallel tips and a connecting diagonal on the last X support a struck capital sigma, while the two earlier signs admit either g or sigma. On scan 51 the tall j in the compressed « tjrs » descends below its neighbours. On 53 the high i and the following short rises support « invariance ». The final z in the proper name on scan 59 has its own low return. These decisions apply at the stated locations. The marking of the short superior on alpha on 59 as a dot does not decide the hollow mark above Q on 58.
