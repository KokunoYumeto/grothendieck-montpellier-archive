# Mathematical notes · cote 7, scans 47–60

Grothendieck, A. (1966–1967). *Notes laïus novembre-décembre 1967 (Cristaux)* [Manuscript]. Montpellier archive, cote 7, scans 47–60. Scan numbers below refer to the supplied facsimiles.

## What the pages do

**Traces (47).** The opening fragment reports a Lefschetz–Weil expression for the zeta function of a smooth projective scheme over a finite field. The proposed input is the trace of Frobenius on $H^i(X)$ despite the missing duality and Künneth theorems.

**Lifts and descent (47–50).** Sections of $M$ over $S$ through a marked point give different lifts of $X_0$. Grothendieck compares the pulled-back de Rham complexes and obtains a comparison modulo torsion on their cohomology. Effective descent illustrates a transitive system of isomorphisms. Relative stratification gives another source for such a system. Scan 50 sets out the complete local case and the connection as topics.

**Lifting derivations (51).** After recalling Manin’s degree-one result, Grothendieck invokes Cartan’s identity. A local lift of a base derivation acts on the relative de Rham complex. Independence up to homotopy gives an action on cohomology in the formally smooth affine case.

**Derived connection (52–53).** The absolute connection is placed on the derived direct image. Grothendieck gives further conditions for connections on its individual cohomology objects. The example of elliptic curves limits the proposed explanation of invariance by stratifications. A three-part programme brings together complexes of differential operators, stratified structures and Washnitzer–Monsky theory.

**Infinitesimal extension (54–55).** For $Y\to X$ and an infinitesimal enlargement $Y'$ admitting local prolongations to $X$, the pullbacks along these prolongations have compatible isomorphisms. This motivates extension of the module to $Y'$ and the two sites. The base-change isomorphism condition defines special sheaves.

**Comparison statements (55–56).** The theorem concerns de Rham cohomology of a smooth $X$ in characteristic zero. The conjecture over $\mathbb C$ concerns cohomology of $X^{\mathrm{an}}$ with complex coefficients. The further direct-image formula at the foot of 55 is crossed out.

**Two cohomologies (57–58).** A stratified quasi-coherent $F$ gives a cosimplicial Zariski sheaf $C(F)$ by taking inverse limits over infinitesimal neighbourhoods. Its hypercohomology computes stratifying cohomology. In the other direction, the proposed stratified pro-module $Q^\circ(F)$ starts with ordinary $F$ and admits relative differential operators as morphisms.

**Restriction of scalars (59).** For $i\geq1$, restriction along $\alpha^{i-1}$ makes $P^i[M]$ a $P^{i-1}$-module. The circled sign pairs the name $Q^{i-1}[M]$ with the boxed tensor formula. A sideways addition discusses pro-objects and operators of bounded degree.

**Outline and calculation (60).** An eight-part outline precedes §6.1. The cover by $\widetilde X$ leads to a Leray spectral sequence. Its iterated powers are described by infinitesimal neighbourhoods of diagonals. The next sentence about $(U,X')$ ends at « on aura ».

## Questions and conjectures

- **47.** « Ce formalisme semble alors proche de celui de Dwork, que je ne connais pas ». Grothendieck qualifies the analogy.
- **52–53.** « espérer que ces connexions canoniques proviennent en fait de stratifications canoniques ». The next leaf limits the proposed explanation by characteristic and calls the relation « heuristique et sentimentale ».
- **53.** « un puzzle, où il s'agit de reconstituer une théorie d'ensemble ». The three listed ingredients still await a common treatment.
- **56.** « Conj. Si $X$ loc. de […] f. sur $\mathbb C$ ». The analytic comparison is conjectural in the stated generality. The abbreviation in the hypothesis admits v. and t. The author singles out the smooth case as proved.
- **57–58.** « l'inverse ne semble pas vrai. On va voir pourtant que, dans un certain sens, il en est bien ainsi ». The tentative judgment motivates the construction on 58.
- **60, §2.** « Motivations pour le désir d'une coh. $p$-adique en car. $p$ ». This is a stated aim.

## Slips

**S49, scan 49, last line.** The phrase around « $\underline E_0$ un Module sur $S$ » opens with a round parenthesis and closes with a square bracket. The crop shows a left arc and a right vertical with a foot. A round close would match the opener. Whether the writer instead intended square brackets is uncertain.

## Remarks of the model

- **M1, model observation, high confidence (54–55, 60).** The body numbers the sites discussion §4 and 4.2. The outline gives it §5. Both numerals and their headings are legible. Their chronological relationship is undated.
- **M2, model observation, high confidence (57, 60).** The arrow under « lim » points left in the definition of $C^n(F)$ and right in the description of $\widetilde X^{\nu+1}$. Both arrowheads are visible.
- **R49, model reading question, low confidence (49, lines 9–10 and 18).** As transcribed, « La donnée … peut être considéré ». The exit of the accented terminal might contain the second e of « considérée ». The last article, « un » or « une », is separately uncertain before « tel système » on 50.
- **R58, model reading question, low confidence (58, lines 5–6).** First candidates give « les différentielles n'est pas linéaire ». A singular noun, « ne sont » and a plural adjective remain possible. The two-level noun ending and the adjective’s compressed exit leave the apparent disagreement unresolved.
- **R59, model reading question, low confidence (59, lines 16–17).** The resulting phrase reads « algèbre … cosimplicial ». A terminal e in « cosimpliciale » would change the apparent agreement. The ascender has a short exit and the passage contains overwritten lettering.
