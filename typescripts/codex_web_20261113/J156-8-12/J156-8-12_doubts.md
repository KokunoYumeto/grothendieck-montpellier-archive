## Legibility

The open intervals, endpoint primes and large display layout are generally clear. The method of cancelling matters here. A horizontal strike often covers just the middle of a word. On scan 93 a zigzag also obscures a primed interval and the words below it. The two long diagonals on 97 show exactly where the last draft is cancelled, but the short overwrites inside that draft leave the old capitals and the third support subscript particularly uncertain. Other vulnerable readings are the last words of the sloping note on 90 and the end of the note on 96. For those places the register gives the visible candidates and the local parallel separately.
