## Legibility

The foliation, large displayed identities and most of the ordinary prose are readable. The horizontal staves can supply a false crossbar, erase the contrast of an accent, or make a real cancellation look like part of the paper. The openings on 34 and 36, the letters under the heavy corrections on 37 and 45, and the little indices below the graph symbol on 46 are particularly vulnerable. Rotation makes the marginal questions on 40 and most of the longer note on 43 much easier to follow. The lower edge of 40 cuts the final line, and the right edge of 47 removes part of a condition and a marginal inscription. The illegible-word count there is an estimate of the surviving space. The drawings preserve their visible incidences and labels; their coordinates approximate the pencil or pen contours.
