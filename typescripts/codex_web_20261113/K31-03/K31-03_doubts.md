## Legibility

The separation of the typewriter characters makes most of scans 100 and 102 readable down to the indices. On scan 88 two long pen strokes run through an entire typed draft, and some of its rightmost characters meet the image edge. The heavily revised passages on scans 93–99 are less secure. I used alternatives even inside cancellations when their surviving strokes allowed a reading. No run of words was marked physically illegible. That count includes tentative attempts to read ink under crossings.
