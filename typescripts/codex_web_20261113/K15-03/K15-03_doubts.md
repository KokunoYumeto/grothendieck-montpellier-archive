## Legibility

The printed words on the eight typescript pages are generally distinct, including the endings of the listed slips. Single-struck and overtyped characters can look alike on the descent sheets. The stand-alone signs under f, the retouched Y subscripts, and the typewriter’s l/1 deserve particular care.

On the manuscripts the long strokes often remove the middle of a word. Scan 90 has several superposed revisions around the degree map and three large crosses through an argument. The bottom of 94 has two conditions written between the same pair of baselines; turning the region separates them. The lower edge of 96 and the oblique margin of 102 remain difficult even when enlarged and reoriented. On 100 the upper sign on the live λ is substantially less secure than the boxed radical or the word poids. The doubt register keeps these differences visible.
