## Legibility

The large diagrams, endpoint primes and alternation of the long zigzag are generally easier to distinguish than word endings in the prose. I trust the distinction between an underlined Ch and the double underline on the ind-objects more than I trust the letters under the repeated superscript hatching. The most difficult passages are the two overwritten category names on 402, the tiny bounds and transition exponent on 406, the rewritten comparison on 408, and the dated remark on 411 where two directions of writing intersect. One cancelled word before «Les faits principaux» on 399 remains unreadable. The final «mais» on 412 is the end of the supplied material.
