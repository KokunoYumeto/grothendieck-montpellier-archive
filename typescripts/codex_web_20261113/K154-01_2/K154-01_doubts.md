## Legibility

The main ink of the two-column notes is usually legible. The cancelled drafts, the thin pencil between columns, the indices on curves, and long sequences of pen strokes through words are substantially harder to read. The printer characters on scan 053 can often be followed cell by cell, except under the heavy drawings. TikZ retains editable outlines and labels. Its strokes are approximations of the originals.
