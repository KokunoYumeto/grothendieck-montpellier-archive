# Mathematical notes · 161-3

These notes refer to the scan numbers. The identifiers in the last three sections also locate the mathematical checks in CHECK_EVENTS.tsv. A request to prove something, or a transcription of a claim, in itself contributes no check event.

## What the pages do

**22 · Bicovering maps.** The surviving end of an argument asks to show that the image of a bicovering $F\to G$ under a hatted functor is an isomorphism. After « On a » only dots and a struck line remain.

**24–25 · Topology and preorder.** The maps $\alpha_X$ and $\beta_X$ go from a topology to a relation defined using closure of a singleton, and back from a preorder to a topology. Minimal opens $O_x$, continuity and increasing maps lead to the two adjunction maps and the Hom bijection at the head of 25. The author characterizes the essential image of $\beta$ by the existence of smallest open neighbourhoods. The sloping margin treats finite and arbitrary products separately.

**25–27 · Irreducibility, points and essential spaces.** A cancelled first treatment of the category attached to a preorder on 25 gives way to a new g) on 26. Here irreducible closed sets are described by a closure condition and cofiltering. Evaluation on $O_x$ relates sheaves to presheaves on the order category. Pro-objects, ordered by reverse inclusion in the description on the sheet, give a model for points and for the map into the associated sober space. Entries i) and j) turn this into two lists of equivalent conditions, first on a space, then on a topos.

**29 · Opens in a product.** Maps from lattices of opens that preserve the stated joins and meets are compared with pairs of such maps from the two factors. Values on rectangles suggest a formula for $\gamma(W)$ as the supremum of the values of rectangles inside $W$. The upper calculation tests preservation of finite infima. The lower argument asks about the bijection and checks restriction to a factor.

**29 · Trigonometric working.** A separate computation on the same sheet begins with $z=e^{i\theta}$ and $u=a+bi+cj$. A labelled system of rays and a small curved sketch accompany the equations for $a,b,c$, the squared sum and the triple angle. The long cancellations make the intended endpoint uncertain.

**31, 33 · A lemma on a ringed topos.** A pair of locally free modules gives an algebra $\underline{\mathrm{End}}(G)$, two modules and a comparison isomorphism. The proof passes to fixed ranks and to local groupoids, then studies automorphisms. On 33 the compatibility square turns the remaining question into one about conjugation on endomorphisms. An alternative argument is crossed out. The corollary starts with $A\otimes\underline{\mathrm{End}}(E)\simeq\underline{\mathrm{End}}(F)$ and asks for $A$ itself as an endomorphism algebra.

**32 · Torsor and cohomology diagrams.** The sheet puts a cochain sequence, cycles, a Hom complex and expressions involving TORS and cohomology beside several towers of arrows. A comparison using $\pi_0(T^n)$ is explicit. The labels on the two comparison arrows and the degree or subscript in several towers are less legible than the adjacent objects. No explanatory prose connects the diagrams.

**35, 37, 39 · Spaces recovered from their topoi.** The English notes list information recoverable from $T(X)$, introduce generalized fiber functors and identify the associated sober space using irreducible closed subsets. They pass from maps of spaces to morphisms of topoi. Scan 39 returns specifically to specialization, stalks, and full faithfulness. The French note in its left margin is too uncertain to carry a further conclusion.

**37, 39 · The notion of a topos.** The author lists reasons to work with topoi, from classifying structures through quotients and cohomology, and gives examples including $\widehat C$ and $B_G$. Scan 39 sets an exactness-and-generators definition beside a fully faithful embedding into a presheaf category with left exact left adjoint.

**36, 38 · Species of structure.** The French leaves numbered 1 and 3 treat structures defined by limits on a family of base objects. The underlying-family functor into $C^I$ is natural in a functor preserving the limits. The author passes to cofibered categories and compatible morphisms. On 38 a representing object $\beta$ is assumed and morphism categories are evaluated there. Two versions, one with suffix 0, are written alongside one another. The return to the category $\Gamma^T$ is explicit at the foot.

**40 · Fields as a full subtype.** Starting from commutative unital rings, the author asks for a subtype whose set-valued models are the fields. The product theory, representing categories, categories of fractions and maps tested against every field occur in the argument. The last part recasts this test in terms of affine schemes and residue-field extensions.

## Remarks of the model

- **R33 · Model remark, medium confidence.** The uniqueness of $(G,\psi)$ in the corollary on 33 depends on what an isomorphism must preserve. For a rank-one module over $\mathbf F_3$, both invertible scalars act identically on its endomorphism algebra. An isomorphism of the larger tuple in the lemma has additional compatibility to satisfy. The corollary's final lines leave little room to spell out that distinction.
- **R40 · Model remark, high confidence for the example.** The finite product of the field $\mathbf F_2$ with itself in rings has a nonzero element $(1,0)$ with no inverse. This illustrates the finite-product difficulty in the question on 40. It does not decide the more general question about categories of fractions.
- **Reading question, 29, first product identity · Model, low confidence.** The final transcription has $1_O$. The small suffix could be $\sigma$ rather than roman $O$; its small loop and rightward stroke resemble both on this sheet. This question was noticed after the hash was fixed.
- **Reading question, 40, after the underlined corps · Model, low confidence.** The frozen candidate is a xi-shaped mark. An opening brace is another possibility: it sits at the right edge and has two sharp bends. Its function in the sentence remains uncertain.
