## Legibility

The main lines are usually legible, especially the repeated constructions and the numbered formulae. A word can narrow abruptly to a horizontal stroke, and several short false starts survive only above or below a crossing line. The least secure passages are the first two marginal notes on scans 39–40, the small qualifications on 41 and 44, and the oblique notes on 49, 52 and 54. Rotation separates some letters from the main lines but cannot restore letters beneath an overstroke. The alphabets of the order symbols and the very small raised and lowered signs in (24), (27), (34) and the lower paragraph on scan 52 deserve particular care. The drawings are typeset tracings of their lines and labels. A doubtful mark in a drawing stays at the corresponding location.

The first reading had 168 marked places. The comparison resolved 17 and added candidates at 34. The remaining 151 marks all offer a reading. None required a count of physically unreadable whole words. The timed second readings lasted 46–95 seconds apiece. Additional comparison of recurrent notation, rotated margins and marked crops followed. The timing limits the confidence warranted by an unmarked short word or punctuation stroke.
