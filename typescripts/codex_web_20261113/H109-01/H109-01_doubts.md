## Legibility

The typed corrections on 013–015 are the clearest material. The last typed lines on 013 and 014 have patchy impressions, but their words and endings can be read. On 011 the internal uprights and the detached bars of the Qs are visible even though the letters are small. The least secure readings are on 009, particularly the overwritten fragments, the endings reduced to short waves, the raised sign by 29, and the joining stroke in the weak-equivalence entry. The shapes of the two boxed pen figures are reproduced; the underlying signs are difficult to distinguish from the diagonal overmarking. On 017–019, the small fused quotation strokes, the connector between the centuries, and the scored numeral before 41 deserve particular caution. The text is unsigned. The identification of the hand of 009 rests on the folder description and the appearance of that list; the fine annotation on 015 has no secure author.
