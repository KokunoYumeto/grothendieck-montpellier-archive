## Legibility

The running English text is generally clear. Uneven type and copy loss affect small letters and accents. I trust the covered heads, type beneath cancellations, GF on 51, the two detached strokes before log on 54, the exponent cut by the rule on 54 and the overstruck letter in “aff…cted” on 55 least. Different hands cannot be identified from these sheets with confidence. The inventory records unassigned ink as “unknown”.
