## Legibility

The definitions, large formulas and underlined headings on scans 19–28 generally have clearer strokes than the arguments they replace. The ends of connecting lines vary from clear arrowheads to plain horizontal strokes, sometimes in the same formula. The most difficult passages are the multiply crossed paragraph on 19, the large rewriting on 22, the altered labels on 24–25, and the left-hand and interlinear material on 31. On 32 and 34 long cancellation lines cross most of the sheet, but substantial text survives beneath them. Pale lettering at the edges of scans 23–28 belongs to the paper. The faint count and pencil traces on 29 are materially different from its dark ink title. The covers, marginal marks and pencilled entries do not establish a separate writer’s identity.
