## Legibility

The section headings, manuscript folios, larger displays and most uncorrected words are legible. In the faster passages a short horizontal stroke may stand for several letters. A tick beside X, a flattened arrow tip and the small digits in display labels are especially easy to overread. The least secure passages are the sideways notes on 181–183, the deleted arrow caption and hatched words on 190, the lower margin of 193, the second question on 194 and the cancelled sentence on 196. All five word-count estimates concern crossed prose. The isolated sign alongside 192 and 193 resembles a large 2 or Z. The last sheet ends in the middle of a conditional clause.
