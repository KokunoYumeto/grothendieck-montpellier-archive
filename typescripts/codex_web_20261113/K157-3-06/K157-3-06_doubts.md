The endpoints, the tall named categories and the arrangement of the squares can usually be read. The most difficult continuous text lies inside the stepped cancellation on 82 and the oblique cancelled note on 92. The transition from one functor name to another on 84–86 obscures whole indices. The oblique cross-references on 90 have handwriting from the main lines running across them. Small terminal strokes are less trustworthy than the positions of the objects in the diagrams. Several heads, French word endings and the stars or primes on 97 admit more than one reading.
