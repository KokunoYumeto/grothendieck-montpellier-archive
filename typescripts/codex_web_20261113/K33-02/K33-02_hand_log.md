## Scope of the comparisons

The distinction between an underlined sheaf H and a plain group H holds where the lower stroke is separately visible on 022–030. A raised parenthesised n can disappear in the abbreviated class and can also be cancelled. Each occurrence was checked before treating it as live. The two Greek-class subscripts X/Y and Y/X on 028 are visibly different. There is no uniform rule that a connecting line in the handwriting has an arrowhead (018, 020, 022, 026, 030, 032). The single versus doubled stem of R changes even between two cells of 030. In the typescript a lower o-shaped index remains undecided at the first relevant instance on 019, 021 and 023; neither the keyed type form nor the section’s enumeration decides all three. The defective parity type repeats at both positions on each of 027 and 031. The small n and taller X in the coefficient μ occupy overlapping levels on several leaves; the steps are most pronounced at the end of the first display on 024. The two dots of the double accent sequence in général and générales on 024 survive even though the intermediate letters are reduced almost to a line.
