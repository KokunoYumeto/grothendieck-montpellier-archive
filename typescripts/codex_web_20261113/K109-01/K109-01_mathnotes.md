# Cote 109 · Mathematical notes on scans 2–8

## What the pages do

**Scan 2.** “Notations” groups symbols beside references. The page distinguishes \(A_{/x}\) (case doubtful), \(A^\circ\), \(A^\wedge\) and \(\hat A\). It places \(K\) and \(|K|\) with Ord, Preord, Hot and triangles in the group beside 22. The last two lines have squares and tilded shapes.

**Scan 3.** Under “Introduction”, two editorial reminders precede a short English line relating stacks and chain complexes. It points the reader to p. 4.

**Scan 4.** “Imprimeur” supplies two pairs of written and desired signs, the first using two forms of N. The step-shaped drawing and the hatched oval below it each stand alone.

**Scan 5.** The “Index des notations” places examples side by side. The Hom brace gives two lines for A and B. Later lines distinguish upper and lower markings on X, L, D, Ch and K. The sheet ends with words for kinds of functor and with “localization (two meanings) colocalization.”

**Scan 6, no. 130.** The first heading proposes non-connected “bundles” as components for semisimplicial models. The mark after bundles is faint.

**Scan 6, no. 131.** The revised heading names a tentative construction of the spherical functor \(\widetilde S\) and an “infinitesimal” extension of a basic notion. The replacement name for that notion reads bungles or bundles. Several formulations are crossed.

**Scan 6, no. 132.** The abandoned heading concerns a formula for homotopy groups of spheres. Its replacement reads “A crazy tentative wrong-quadrant (bi)complex for the homotopy groups of a sphere.” The sheet gives neither terms nor differentials of the proposed complex.

**Scan 6, no. 133.** “Birth of Saleyman” follows. The vowels in the name remain uncertain. No construction accompanies the title.

**Scan 7.** A plan orders front matter, chapters, an appendix, notes and indexes. Two sketches connect selected Roman-numbered chapters. The left uses double arrows and the right uses plain strokes.

**Scan 8.** “Questions” is a register of four entries keyed to pages and sections. It gives a short expression or reminder at each location. It supplies no answers.

## Statements left without proof

**Scan 3, last line.** “Stacks = non commutative chain complexes (cf. p. 4)”. The word commutative is partly reduced to waves. The line gives no definitions or argument from which to determine the force of the equals sign.

## Remarks of the model

**M1 · High confidence · Scans 2 and 5.** The same pen feature distinguishes all three large shapes on both lists. The triangle has an extra left slope, the square an extra left upright, and the oval an extra left arc. A side-by-side comparison of the two rows supports preserving each doubled side in the typesetting.

**M2 · High confidence · Scan 7.** Ignoring arrowheads, the two sketches share the five links II–III, III–IV, IV–VII, V–VII and IV–VIII. The right sketch also joins VII and VIII separately to IX. I and VI stand free in both. This comparison follows the endpoints on the sheet and uses only the live numerals.
