## Legibility

The long prose lines on 117 are light but their spacing is usually recoverable. On 121 the tabular rules and the loops that move from one entry to another must be followed across columns. Scan 122 is the least reliable sheet. Two long cancellations cross earlier tensors and coefficients, and notes at the left and bottom require different rotations. The time order of strokes within its lower matrix is uncertain. Scans 123–125 repeatedly write one letter over another. In the lower fragments of 127–129 the relative level of a subscript, a prime or a short stroke can determine the entire reading. On 130 some fork arms are plain strokes and the labels beside the lower projections are exceptionally compressed. An empty braced alternative in a superscript denotes a possible absence of an exponent.
