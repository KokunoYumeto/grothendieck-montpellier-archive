## Legibility

The running text is usually legible even when a word ends in a single rising stroke. The two horizontal rules over the polynomial on scan 50, the overwritten subscripts on scans 54 and 58, and the intersecting cancellations on scans 59–63 are much less secure. The rotated margin notes are short enough to compare letter by letter, but their opening words on scans 50 and 56 remain ambiguous. The page count on the cover is clearer than its punctuation. An illegible word inside the abandoned proof on scan 59 has no surviving letter on which to base a reading. A manuscript specialist should recheck the two recurrent mathematical glyphs identified in the final section of the mathematical notes.
