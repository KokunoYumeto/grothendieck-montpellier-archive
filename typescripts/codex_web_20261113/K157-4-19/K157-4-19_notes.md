## Title sheets, dates and page counts

All fifteen sheets are handwritten mathematical notes. There is no separate title sheet, manuscript date or explicit total page count. Scan 289 has the internal heading *Commentaire final*. Scan 290 begins a new heading naming the axiom of cocartesian squares, “Mayer-Vietoris” and W-cofibrations. The preliminary heading character is doubtful.
