## Legibility

The larger formulas and the live labels of the squares are easier to distinguish than the small words between them. I trust the three-way implication at the foot of 290 and the direction of the queried implication on 301 more than the tiny signs in the slanted note next to the cube on 290. The heavily crossed third cube on 294 is the least secure diagram. Single subscripts there can look like the intersection of hatching with an arrow. The strikethrough in Proposition 3.3 on 298, the end of the slanted integral note on 299, the upper folio corrections, and the name following Brown on 302 also merit comparison with the images. Short French endings and punctuation are a greater source of uncertainty than the repeated category names.
