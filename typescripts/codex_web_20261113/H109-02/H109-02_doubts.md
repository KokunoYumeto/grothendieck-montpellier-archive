## Legibility

The ordinary English entries and most page and line references are clear. The upper edge of 021 cuts through a sentence, and the upper and right edges of 027 remove ink from a reference and a marginal question. Two long oblique pen lines cross the upper entries on 021; their purpose is unclear. On 024 the letters at the rule run together in the formula at 224. On 025 the lower wording at 247 is crossed and the exact attachment of “also” remains uncertain. On 026 the tail of the deleted sentence at 293 is difficult to separate from its cancellation. The symbols under the zigzag at 029, 357 and the short lower-right cancellations on 028 and 029 are among the least secure readings. Colour and superscript question marks in the LaTeX indicate transcription doubt; punctuation already in the handwriting is retained separately.
