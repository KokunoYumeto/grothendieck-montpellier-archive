# Cote 137, scans 31–44

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **Scan 34, in the slanting margin.** The underlined “à vérifier” stands beside “est pl. fidèle” for restriction to \(\mathcal A\). The following attempt to strengthen the statement begins “(et même une équival. de cat. si” and is crossed out. Two words in the condition remain unreadable.
- **Scans 38–39, across the page break.** “Il faudrait examiner si pour” opens the question whether the cochain complex with its additional operations is determined by the “complexe de De Rham-Sullivan” in characteristic zero and by the “complexe de De Rham à p. d.” in the other setting.
- **Scan 39, lower paragraph.** “il faut voir dans quelle mesure” concerns reconstruction of the “type d’homotopie”. The last parenthesis also proposes “faisceautiser” (alternatively “faisceauter”) “et relativiser”.
- **Scan 42, to the right of the two columns.** The description of the essential image assumes “en admettant que tt facteur direct d’un libre de t. f. est itou”. The assumption belongs to the discussion of the finite free terms.
- **Scan 43, oblique upper-left note.** “Ce n’est pas bien bon à cause de l’irrégularité” comments on the nearby crossed model. The end of the objection is difficult to read.
- **Scan 44, definition following Prop. 3.** The object is introduced with “s’il existe”. The author then writes “la question est si ce foncteur est représentable”, gives a special case and begins a more general criterion. The sheet ends in the middle of that criterion.

## Title sheets, dates and page counts

None. Scan 43 has the internal heading “Passage de l’exemple 1 à l’exemple 2”. The lower-corner numbers run from 30 on scan 31 to 43 on scan 44 and are recorded as archive numbers. The sheets supply no authorial page number, date or total page count.
