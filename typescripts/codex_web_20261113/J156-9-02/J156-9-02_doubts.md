# Legibility and limits

The main formulas are generally more legible than the fast prose, but thin subscripts, tiny superscripts and crossings-out remain difficult. The least secure passages are the oblique notes and cancelled bottom of 010, the final endpoints in the 011 branching implication, the abandoned lemma on 012, the small inner mark and changing terminal indices on 015, and the heavily cancelled hypotheses on 016. The terminology paragraph and special I notation on 017 also retain several doubts.

All eight pages were viewed as whole pages and overlapping thirds, then read again. No OCR, outside transcription, web text or mathematical reconstruction was used. A tentative reading was preferred to an illegibility token where even partial strokes allowed one; the absence of `\illegible` tokens therefore does not mean that the cancelled passages are certain. No compilation was attempted.
