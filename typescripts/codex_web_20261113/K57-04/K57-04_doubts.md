## Legibility

The three covers and the surviving typewritten letters can be read more readily than the revised plan on 66. Its old numerals overlap the arrows that reorder the list. Some words on 68 and in the left-hand note on 81 survive only as ascenders or descenders beyond an ink bar. At 71 the added reference to Abhyankar is written below a much longer insertion. The tiny right-hand phrases and cancelled indices on 86 are particularly uncertain. Its three long diagonal strokes have been drawn across the formulas at their approximate positions. Their status as a cancellation of the whole sheet is uncertain.

The numbers in the lower corners match the PDF scans minus one. They appear as archive comments. The faint reversed traces on the two typed sheets have not been counted as recto writing. The pen strokes cancelling the correspondence do not identify the hand that made them.
