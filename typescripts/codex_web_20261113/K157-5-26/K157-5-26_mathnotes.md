# Mathematical notes on scans 405–420

Grothendieck, A. (s.d.). *Dérivateurs* [Manuscript, cote 157-5, scans 405–420]. Archives de l’Université de Montpellier. Scan numbers below refer to the supplied images. Page numbers inside French quotations are the author’s.

## What the pages do

**Successor stage, scans 405–408.** For $\alpha=\alpha_0+1$, the construction asks for two objects $i_\alpha,j_\alpha$ and four arrows $\tau_{\alpha,\alpha_0},\tau'_{\alpha,\alpha_0},v_{\alpha_0},u_\alpha$. The diagram on 407 specifies the first two compatibilities. Starting with $j_{\alpha_0}\to\varphi(i)$, the author passes to $i'$ so that the composite out of $\varphi(i_{\alpha_0})$ comes from a transition in $I$. Cofinality then supplies $j_\alpha$ in $J'$. Full faithfulness supplies the unique transition in $J'$ corresponding to the composite through $\varphi(i_\alpha)$. Scan 406 preserves a cancelled version of the choices.

**Two remaining compatibilities, scans 409–413, opening.** For $3_\alpha(\beta)$, scan 409 uses the commutative upper square and asks to equalize the two routes around the lower one. This changes $j_\alpha$. For $4_\alpha(\beta)$, the pair starts at $j_\beta$ and ends at $\varphi(i_\alpha)$. Scan 411 invokes the bound $\operatorname{Card}\alpha<\pi$ to equalize the whole family and replaces $i_\alpha$ using $\sigma$. The recipe on 412–413 fixes the corrected $i$-triple first. It then constructs and corrects the $j$-triple.

**Limit stage, scans 413–415.** The heading “Cas 2 B” opens the construction for a limit ordinal $\alpha$. Here the author asks for data 1), 2), 3) and the single family $3_\alpha(\beta)$. He chooses $i_\alpha$, $j_\alpha$ and their transition maps using bounds on the earlier stages. Cofinality and filteredness give a common target admitting $u_\alpha$. Equalization of the remaining parallel arrows gives the last replacement of $j_\alpha$.

**Lemma 4.13.17, scans 415–417.** In the cartesian square with $I'=I\times_J J'$, the version on 417 assumes that $\varphi,\psi$ are cofinal, that $\psi$ includes a strictly full subcategory, and that $I$ is filtered. For a limit ordinal $\alpha_0$, $I$ and $J'$ admit the indicated colimits of type $I_{\alpha_0}=\{\beta\mid\beta\text{ ordinal}<\alpha_0\}$, and $\varphi,\psi$ commute with them. For each $\alpha<\alpha_0$, $I$ is “grd devant” $\operatorname{Card}\alpha$. The conclusion is that $I'$ is cofinal in $I$ and has this same size property. The closing sentence identifies the limit-ordinal property as what the preceding proof used. Scan 416 preserves a cancelled list of hypotheses under the same folio number, 330.

**Cancelled transition, scan 418.** A sheet numbered 321 constructs $\tau'_{\alpha,\alpha_0}$ after allowing a common majorant. It ends by leaving all four constraints to be checked.

**Return to theorem 4.13.15, scans 419–420.** The lemma gives $b\Rightarrow d$. A new diagram and the line beneath it introduce the remaining implications, apparently from $b)$ or $c')$ to $a')$. Several primed letters have been rewritten. The goal at the foot of 419 is to characterize the objects of $\underline S$ as the essential image of $\operatorname{Ind}_\pi(\underline S(\pi))$. Under $b)$, scan 420 gives the forward direction. Cofinality allows the presentation of $x$ over $M_\pi/x$ to be restricted to $\underline S(\pi)_{/x}$. That index category is large in the stated sense, which yields the desired presentation. The final sentence starts the reverse direction and continues beyond scan 420.

## Remarks of the model

**M1. Model remark, medium confidence about the author’s intended shortcut.** On 409 the parallel arrows have source $\varphi(i_{\alpha_0})$ in $J$, and the text seeks a map in $J'$ that makes the two composites equal. Membership of that source in $J'$ is unstated. Equalization in filtered $J$, followed by an arrow into cofinal $J'$, gives a composite between objects of $J'$. Fullness identifies that composite with an arrow of $J'$. This accounts for the ambient categories using the hypotheses visible on 408 and 417.

**M2. Model remark, high confidence.** The order of the two corrections changes between the exploratory argument and the closing recipe. Scan 409 first modifies $j_\alpha$ for condition 3. On 412 the author secures condition 4 and writes “On ne touchera plus à ces choix” about the $i$-triple. The correction to $j_\alpha$ for condition 3 then follows at the top of 413.

**M3. Model remark, low confidence about the original sequence of the drafts.** The three small repeated-sign labels on cancelled scan 406, numbered 320, are followed in form by the four-sign label on cancelled scan 418, numbered 321. The latter sheet supplies the primed transition missing at the end of 406. These features suggest a continuation. The two sheets are separated in the supplied sequence, and the inner label signs themselves remain doubtful.
