## Legibility

The type in document 392 is clear enough to distinguish small i and j, the printed accents and the empty references. Its low marks under ideal letters are much fainter than the type. The open-ended notation for the adèle ring is easier to see than these marks. On the handwritten sheets, a heavy line often leaves the first or last stroke of a discarded word visible while removing most of its interior. The least secure passages are the upper cancellation on 53, the revisions of exponents on 55 and 57, the successive cancellations on 61 and the long formula running into the right edge of 62. On 60 the three pale verbal labels can be read, but the small ring at the centre cannot securely be classified by shape alone.
