## Legibility and limits

The broad structure and most large formulas are legible. Dense working handwriting compresses many word endings into waves; small subscripts, accents, and crossed layers are appreciably less secure. The least reliable areas are matrix (16) on scan 33, the cancelled cases on 35–36, the rewritten lower paragraph on 40, the bottom cancellations on 42–43, the clipped edge and tiny insertion on 44, and the connector and small decorations on 47. Scan 45 is a wholly cancelled upside-down draft, read after rotation. Scan 39’s pencil count has unknown hand. Source slips, unfinished parentheses/phrases, and awkward correction states are deliberately retained.

All 60 supplied whole-page/third images were inspected during transcription, and all three thirds of each page were revisited for the audit. No OCR, text recognition, external edition, web search, or parallel transcription worker was used. Audit timestamps report actual working intervals; some sparse pages and several later passes were shorter than the requested several minutes per page. They are not a claim of an independently human-verified edition. The surviving uncertainty marks are part of the delivered transcription, not omitted text. LaTeX is checked structurally only, without compilation.
