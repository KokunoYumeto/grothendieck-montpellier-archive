## Legibility

The six typescript leaves can be read after turning them through 180 degrees. The hardest typed passages are the old words under rows of x’s, especially on scans 19, 21 and 25. In the manuscript, short terminal strokes frequently stand for a whole ending. The tentative clauses at the foot of 14, the first lines of 20 and the break out of the cancelled box on 24 deserve particular care. The entries below give one comparison for every mark in the frozen text, including separate marks within a single cancellation. Hua’s numbering is one below the scan number. His two batches cover every scan in this packet, but give summaries of the typescript leaves. An omission in such a summary cannot recover erased letters. “Changed” includes one revised reading that still carries a doubt mark. Confidence in the register refers to the local reading, including the under-reading when the text is crossed.
