## Legibility

The continuous typescript is usually clear enough to preserve spelling and accent direction. Its overtyped corrections are harder to distinguish than the handwritten additions. Ink shows through from the memorandum on scan 108. The closely joined words and overwritten indices on 96, the marginal definition on 100, the corrections and long cancellation on 103–104, and the heavy marker signs on 105 and 111 are the least secure. The drawings were reconstructed in TikZ; the composite drawing on 111 is an approximation. Scan 109 has no unresolved transcription mark. The memorandum carries no visible signature or date.
