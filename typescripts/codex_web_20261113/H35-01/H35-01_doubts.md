## Legibility

The typescript on scan 2 and the three functor names on scan 5 can be read letter by letter. Two groups in the last cancelled item on scan 2 remain unreadable; each is estimated at two words. The weakest manuscript readings are the middles and ends of the pale titles on scan 3. On scan 4 the entries are legible apart from a few tightly joined names, the dotted ending of Inventiones, and the overwritten year. The two long hooked strokes are reproduced across the list; the sheets give no explicit indication of their function. On scan 5 most of the upper type is clear, but several earlier impressions under x's are too fragmentary to reconstruct with confidence. The upper-right folio 11bis is enclosed by a curved line.
