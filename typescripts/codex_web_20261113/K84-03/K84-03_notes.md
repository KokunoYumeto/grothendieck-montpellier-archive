# Notes on the leaves

## Steps the author marks for checking

On scan 47, immediately to the right of the boxed formula for \(Q(\varphi_N(x))\), he writes “Vérification à partir du cas décomposé,”. This identifies a way to check the formula. There is a cancellation at the sign-sensitive place in the box.

On scan 44, “attention au signe” accompanies “\(\delta\) est le discr. de \(N\) changé de signe”. A second “Attention au signe !” points to the added plus in \(T^2-4N=+\delta\). Both are reminders to watch the convention.

On scan 46, the claim that the form takes values in \(L^{\otimes 2}\) is followed by the parenthesis (argument “universel”…). He indicates the argument without developing it. On scan 41 an attempted “Dém.” for the preceding proposition is crossed out. On scan 36 the NB beginning “Cette dernière condition” is too heavily overwritten to establish what qualification it makes.

On scan 50, “Quelle condition à mettre sur \(z\) ?” introduces a question he answers on the same sheet. He writes “OK” beside the restriction condition and “Ok” beside the verification of \(\psi(x)=1\).

## Title sheets, dates and page counts

Scan 43 is a title sheet written on ruled music paper. It opens with “\(\chi\)-scindages, produits contractés d'extensions \(\chi\)-scindées” and ends with “d'Arf-Stiefel-Whitney généralisés, etc.” The intervening line about quadratic covers contains a cancelled word and uncertain endings. A light “(79)” at upper left is distinct from the small pencilled 42 at the foot. There is no wording that identifies 79 as a page count. The right-edge printer’s imprint reads “MidiGraf Montpellier” followed by digits transcribed in the comment as “2 1999”. They may form the single number 21999. No date of composition is identifiable.

Scan 38 carries the section heading “4.2. Relation avec les symétries.” Scan 44 has the underlined heading “Revêtements quadratiques”. Scan 45 is a blank verso with faint reversed show-through. The lower-left pencilled numbers run from 34 to 43 on scans 35–44 and from 45 to 49 on scans 46–50. There is no explicit “n pages” statement, date of composition or typed page sequence on these leaves.
