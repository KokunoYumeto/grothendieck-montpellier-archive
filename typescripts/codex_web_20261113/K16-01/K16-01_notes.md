# K16-01 · Manuscript notes

## Steps the pages mark as unverified

**Scan 013, after (6.13), manuscript page 10.** Grothendieck writes “Il suffit de prouver en fait que l'on a alors $\pi_i=\pi'_i$.” The underlined words immediately after it are **“Démonstration à fournir”**. This is the step from (iv) to (ii).

**Scan 010, (6.5), manuscript page 7.** “On a aussi l'hom $\Lambda$ de degré $-2$, défini par” leads to a brace with dots. The definition is left unwritten at that point. Subsequent sheets do give identities for the operator.

**Scan 014, manuscript page 11.** The underlined “Question” asks for relations involving $\Lambda,L$ alone and for relations describing the $\pi_i$ or determining $\Lambda$. It is posed as a question. No other step carries an explicit “à vérifier”, “sauf erreur” or comparable reservation in the surviving text.
