## Legibility

The running French and the larger displays are usually clear enough to follow. The pen leaves less evidence in short word endings, under horizontal cancellations and at the tip of a short arrow. The slanted note on 648 and the turned note on 655 are readable in their own orientation. On 651 the paper edge cuts into a subscript and a closing sign. The narrow loop in the module ring on 657, the digit and overwritten o on 658, and the small amendments on 661–662 are among the least secure readings.
