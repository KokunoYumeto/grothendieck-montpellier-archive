## Legibility

The ordinary body text, the larger object labels, and most primes and tildes can be followed across the seven sheets. The least secure readings are the older forms under retraced letters, the low writing among the diagonals of 74–75, the two junctions between insertions and the enumeration on 78, the oblique note and crossed Scholie on 79, and the qualifications close to the right edge of 80. The typeset diagrams retain the visible objects, strokes and directions; the spacing and the exact paths of the cancellation lines are approximate. The times and changes from the independent second reading are in the audit.
