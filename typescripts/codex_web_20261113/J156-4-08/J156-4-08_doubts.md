## Abbreviations

- 58. **ss-fig**, **ss fig**, **ss. fig** appear to mean sous-figure(s). **ens** stands for ensemble. **NB** stands for nota bene, **bij.** for bijections, **resp** probably for respectivement and **sup** for supremum. **ds**, **Particulièrt**, **néct**, **automatiqt** probably abbreviate dans, particulièrement, nécessairement and automatiquement.
- The alternate reading “fais.t” may abbreviate *faisant*. The upper “Item”/“Idem” has both ends of its horizontal middle stroke faint; “pas néc.t” would mean *pas nécessairement*.
- 59. **i.e.** means id est. **Cor** abbreviates corollaire. **il f et s** reads as il faut et suffit. **p. ex.** probably stands for par exemple. **él.** may stand for élémentaire; the alternative **ir.** would suggest irréductible. **gén** abbreviates généralement here.
- 60. **cf** means confer, **tjrs** means toujours, **sup.** and **supr.** abbreviate supérieure after borne. **t.f.** indicates type fini. **nb** probably means nombre.
- 61. **rel.**, **sym.**, **refl.** stand for relation, symétrique and réflexive. **tte** appears to abbreviate toute. **Cond** in the margin possibly abbreviates condition.
- 62. **i.e.** is used before two explanations. **ds** means dans. **ssi** (possibly **ss**) in the small right note probably means si et seulement si.
- Scan 63. “q.e.d.” probably *quod erat demonstrandum*, “comp. conn.” *composante(s) connexe(s)*, “p. ex.” *par exemple*, “tte” *toute*, “t.f.” *type fini*, “il f et s” *il faut et suffit*, “corr.” *correspondance*, “l'ens” *l'ensemble*.

- Scan 64. “nb” probably *nombre*, “disj.” *disjointe*, “suiv.” *suivant*, “quelc.” *quelconques*, “Sup” *supremum*, “Inf” *infimum*, “t.f.” *type fini*.
