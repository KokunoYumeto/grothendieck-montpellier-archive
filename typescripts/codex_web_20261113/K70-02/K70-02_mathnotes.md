# What the pages do

These notes were written after the transcription freeze recorded in `progress.log`. They use only the supplied pages. Quotations retain the transcribed wording; proposed corrections below do not alter the frozen text. Confidence concerns the proposed interpretation or correction, not the historical authorship of a statement.

**Scans 17–18 — fixed loci and coefficients.** Starting with a cancelled coordinate formula and fixed-locus calculation, the notes examine when the locus called `H_i` can be a hyperplane. A field argument counts independent equations. The continuation eliminates several coefficients, uses the involution relation, introduces `alpha_i`, and records a large matrix with corrections and dimension annotations.

**Scans 19–20, upper portion — coefficient relations over rings.** The coordinate action and the equation of `H_i` are restated. The text then considers reduced and more general rings, a nilpotent parameter whose glyph is ambiguous between nu and v, and relations involving `2+nu`. Scan 20 returns to the hyperplane condition and records the coefficient conditions said to be necessary. Several intermediate attempts remain cancelled.

**Scans 20, lower portion, and 23 — products and iterates.** A coordinate observation is used to state commutation for sufficiently separated indices. The product `sigma_0 sigma_1` is expanded, and functions `F_p` and `G_p` are introduced for its powers; the last equivalence on scan 20 is unfinished. Scan 23 introduces a pi-shaped abbreviation for the product, computes its second and third iterates, and writes scalar conditions alongside `alpha_0=-1`. The page does not finish a general treatment of the powers.

**Scans 21–22 — restrictions, flags and an unresolved stabilizer question.** Scan 21 lists restrictions to subspaces and quotients, leaving two product restrictions with question marks. Scan 22 considers an affine group image and a regular combinatorial object. It connects flags and affine subspaces, then asks whether an element fixing `f_i` belongs to the displayed subgroup generated by the other generators. The text explicitly expresses doubt about the implication for orders of adjacent-generator products.

**Scans 24–26 — coordinate and tensor descriptions.** These pages contain cancelled two-dimensional trials, coordinate formulas for the generators, a cancelled quadratic expression and a cancelled incidence/invariant block. They then pass to homogeneous coordinates, fixed hyperplanes, points at infinity and tensor expressions. Scan 26 gives actions on the points `s_j`, a boxed case formula, and a tensor expression for `sigma_i-id`. Differences in bounds and coefficients are retained in the transcription.

**Scan 27 — nonmathematical sheet.** This is a typed political leaflet, including an announcement for a demonstration on 10 DECEMBRE 1977. It contains no mathematical construction or proof.

**Scans 29–30 — cover and affine-frame construction.** The cover bears “n-quasi-polyèdres”, a doubtful singular/plural description of an old draft, and “(8 p)”. Scan 30 begins with affine transformations, commutation conditions and a flag. Points are defined recursively. The proposition asserts that these points form an affine basis and describes the flag and certain fixed points. An induction and a contradiction argument are written out on the page.

**Scans 31–32 — parameters, matrices and a reflection condition.** Scan 31 chooses an origin and translation basis, introduces `u_i` and a v/nu-shaped parameter, and expresses later images using these data and commuting generators. Coordinate formulas follow, with two displays both labelled (10). Scan 32 develops the action on translation vectors, writes a matrix for `sigma_0`, computes its fixed locus and states a projective-reflection criterion. It then names two remaining parameters `lambda_0` and `mu_0` and gives the resulting coordinate formula.
