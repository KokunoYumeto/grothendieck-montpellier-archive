# K157-5-01 · Mathematical notes

## What the pages do

**U1 · Scans 2–3.** The cover names chapter XVII of « Catégories de Modèles ». Its six-entry contents sheet places the preliminaries first, then the indexed class $C_I$, model categories with cofibrations, special cofibrations, special Quillen categories and the construction of derivators. « 98– » leaves the last range open. Several connecting words and overwritten range ends are uncertain.

**U2 · Scans 5 and 7.** Starting with a Quillen triple $(W,C,F)$, Grothendieck asks when an internal Hom can be computed after passage to homotopy. He puts the two comparisons with $S$ and $T$ on consecutive lines, including the products with $X$. The next sheet draws factorizations using $I_S$ and writes
$$
 \mathrm{Hom}_W(S,\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,Y))\simeq\mathrm{Hom}_W(S\times X,Y).
$$
It explicitly calls $S\times X$ « le produit dans M ». The proposition below imposes conditions on the Hom and on the action of $S\mapsto S\times X$ on $C$ and $TC$.

**U3 · Scan 9.** The corollary considers a $Y'$ isomorphic to $Y$ in $\mathrm{Ho}_W$, with $Y'$ fibrant. The next statements concern the existence of internal Homs in $\mathrm{Hot}_W$ under numbered Loc conditions, and passage from $(M,W)$ to $(M(I),W(I))$. The coefficients and some of the inserted qualifications in « Corr. 2 » are difficult to read.

**U4 · Scan 11.** An objectwise description in $M(I)$ uses restrictions to $I_{/i}$ and an expression under a product indexed by objects of that category. A separate NB considers an internal Hom as a morphism of derivators and writes a pullback comparison. « Non ! » stands beside that passage. After « Plutôt », the arguments have independent indexing categories $I,J$, and the indicated value lies in $\mathbb D(I^\circ\times J)$.

**U5 · Scan 14.** Proposition 1 defines $C_I$ by the arrow $\widetilde u_{i_0}$ out of a pushout built from the earlier $X_i,Y_i$. With $I$ well ordered, clauses a) and b) concern the induced colimit map and the component maps $u_i$. A marginal b′) compares two indices. The corollary introduces $TC=C\cap W$ and relates componentwise membership in $W$ to the analogous condition using $TC$.

**U6 · Scans 16–17.** The proof factors the component $u_{i_0}$ through $\beta_{i_0}$ and $\widetilde u_{i_0}$. It reduces the reverse direction to a condition in $W$ on initial-segment colimits and proposes a simultaneous ordinal induction for a), b), c). The revised zero case takes $I=\varnothing$. In the successor case the text uses the largest element of $I_{\alpha+1}$ and applies the preceding hypothesis to $I_\alpha$.

**U7 · Scans 17–19.** At the limit ordinal, b) is inherited and the proof turns to $u_\alpha\in C$. A pushout square defines $Z_i$ from $X_i,Y_i,X_\alpha$. The text identifies the colimit of the $Z_i$ with $Y_\alpha$, then treats successor transitions, the initial transition at $i=-1$, and transitions at limit ordinals. The final calculation gives $Z'_{i_0}$ as an amalgamated sum and refers to diagram $(\alpha)$. The supplied text ends in the ensuing cancelled diagram.

**U8 · Scans 6, 8, 10 and 12.** Read in their own folio sequence P 49–52, these typed pages compare the builder and the explorer, then reconsider their names and the roles of « yang » and « yin » in discovery. In scan order their folios descend. P 52 begins section 18. A detached rule on P 51 separates a continuation from the main text.

**U9 · Scan 15.** The cancelled typescript PU 37 extends an alternating diagram of paired words. The upper row runs from « discipline » to « savoir » and the lower from « imagination » to « mystère ». The dated footnote explains the replacement of « le particulier » by « rigueur » next to « généralité ». The printed job notice on scan 3 is separate from both typescripts.

## Slips

**E1 · Scan 16, before the reduction to (c). Medium confidence.** The sheet writes « $\alpha_{i_0}\in TC$, ou encore $\alpha_{i_0}\in C$ ». The following implication has target $W$, and $TC$ has just been written $C\cap W$. The second $C$ may stand for $W$. The frozen reading is $C$.

**E2 · Scan 19, (*) and the two diagrams below it. Medium confidence.** For $Z_i$ to $Z_{i+1}$, (*) writes $v_{i+1}$ above the stroke. The diagram writes $v_i$, and the special case writes $v_{-1}$ from $Z_{-1}$ to $Z_0$. Writing $v_i$ in (*) would reconcile these labels. The direction in which Grothendieck would have changed them is uncertain.

**E3 · Reused typescripts.** Scan 3 prints « CONNECTIONS » in the French printer notice, where « CONNEXIONS » is the ordinary spelling. Scan 10 prints « ce qui nous a abrité et nourri » after « nous-mêmes issus »; a plural reading of *nous* would give « abrités et nourris » (medium confidence). Scan 15 prints « de hier » with both words intact, where one would ordinarily write « d'hier » (low confidence in calling this a slip within a cancelled draft).
