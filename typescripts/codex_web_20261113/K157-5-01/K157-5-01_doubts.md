## Legibility

The ordinary type on P 49–52 can usually be read letter by letter, including its accent directions and spaced words. The printer notice is pale but legible when rotated. The handwritten mathematical nouns and diagram objects generally have better defined outlines than the ends of sentences. Scan 11 distinguishes *i* and *j* well in the live formula. Confidence is lowest for the intersecting lines of handwriting on scan 14, the tightly inserted conditions on scan 9, and the beginnings and ends of cancelled lines on scans 17–19. The marks in these locations preserve the difference between a possible reading and a word recoverable from the paper. The cancellations on scan 15 cover a whole page of otherwise legible print.
