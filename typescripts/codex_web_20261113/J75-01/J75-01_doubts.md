## Legibility

The course label and page count are clear after a quarter turn. The second accent in the title is faint. The small count has a lighter hand whose authorship is uncertain.

On scan 3 the slanted class correspondence reads more clearly after rotation. The ink in the boxed composition, and the small crossed sign below the calculation, make those readings the least secure.

Scan 4 mixes light pencil with retouched letters. The crossed headings, a few joins beside the drawing, and the insertion above “rectiligne” deserve particular caution.

On scan 5 intensive revision affects the names for contours. The underlying symbol at the embedding into E, the cancelled sign before the segment, and one word before “prolonge” are the most difficult readings.

On scan 6 the cancellation across the first three lines of c) remains readable in patches. The short erased words in a) are the least secure on this sheet.

Scan 7 is relatively open. Endings in the two short summaries and ink directly under the horizontal cancellation in 2°) are less clear.

On scan 8 the slanted definition and the overstruck names have clear final strokes but poor undertext. The small gloss above the four entries near the foot is harder to read than the entries themselves.

The indices of the crossed-out last entry of the right-hand table, the extra ink beside the splitting identity, and the writing in both bottom corners are the least secure parts of scan 9.
