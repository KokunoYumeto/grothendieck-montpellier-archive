## Legibility and limits

The main writing on 195–196 is comparatively clear. The reduced cursive, dense interlinear replacements, cancelled computations and overwritten symbols on 197 and 199–202 are less secure. The Tate and local-symbol pages 204–208 have clear large formulas but several highly compressed prose words and small changes of symbol. Least trusted are cancelled letters, the title's final glyph on 203, the lower annotation and squeezed notation on 206, and the top corrections on 208. Printed imprints are clear; pale watermark wording is not.

All 14 pages were viewed as whole images and in their three supplied thirds; a second-reading audit and further targeted enlargements are recorded. No OCR, text layer, external transcription, mathematical source or web search was used. Uncertainty remains openly marked; this is not a claim that the handwriting has been resolved beyond those marks. Diagram topology and text are transcribed; TikZ placement is schematic and has not been compiled or visually typeset in this environment.
