## Legibility

The main right-running text and the larger class symbols are generally readable. Small endings, the ends of arrows and weak primes are less secure. Where the hand is hurried, cofibration and cofibrations, or an abbreviated limit sign and an underline, can look almost identical. The slanting NB on scan 92 crosses another layer of writing. The altered limit index on scan 96, the interleaved corrections near the bottom of scan 97 and the bottom edge of scan 98 are the places I would recheck first.
