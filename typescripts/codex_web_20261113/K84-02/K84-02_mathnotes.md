# Cote 84 · Mathematical notes on scans 19–34

## What the pages do

**Discriminants and the value line (19).** The boxed identity makes the sign explicit: the discriminant $(\operatorname{Tr}u)^2-4N(u)$ is the negative of the discriminant of $Q_u$. For a form with values in $\Delta'$, the author takes determinants of the map from $E$ to its twisted dual. The braces name the two determinant lines. The case $\Delta'\simeq L$ gives a scalar discriminant.

**The algebra attached to a binary form (19–21, §2.4).** A form $Q:E\to\Delta'$ supplies a map $u_Q$ from $\Delta\otimes L$ to endomorphisms modulo scalars. Pulling the scalar extension back along $u_Q$ gives $\mathcal A_Q$ and a lift $u'_Q$ to the endomorphisms. Pulling the norm back along the lift produces $N_Q$. Its value at the distinguished unit is 1. The text gives the associated algebra structure and writes its trace and conjugation identities.

**Functoriality, faithfulness and the zero form (22–23).** The numbered NB records functoriality, base change, invariance under invertible scalar multiples and four tests for strict faithfulness. One test is fibrewise injectivity of the algebra map. Another asks that $Q$ vanish identically on no reduced fibre. In §2.5 the zero form gives the scalar algebra with a square-zero summand $\Lambda$. The map to endomorphisms kills that summand.

**A polynomial and its matrix (23–24, first §2.6).** After choices of bases the algebra has generator $u$ with $u^2-\beta u+\alpha\gamma=0$. Its matrix has rows $(0,-\gamma)$ and $(\alpha,\beta)$. The author asks for the isomorphism between two such presentations under a change of bases and declines to write it out on the next page. The hard-to-read exponent and change-of-basis index are separate from the displayed matrix. [C23]

**Arf invariant and the recapitulating theorem (24–25).** The quadratic cover associated to $\mathcal A_Q$ receives the name of the binary form’s Arf invariant. The theorem lists descriptions by a line of quadratic forms, a form valued in a line, a quadratic algebra with an invertible module, and a quadratic cover $p:X'\to X$ with an invertible module $\Lambda'$. For the last description, the three-line brace gives the pushforward module, its target norm line and the norm form.

**Two descriptions by torsors (26–27).** Corollary 1 describes $P$ as the local sections $x$ for which $Q(x)$ generates the value line. The norm homomorphism from $W(\mathcal A)^*$ gives the associated bundle and has norm-one group $U_{\mathcal A}$ as kernel. Corollary 2 takes $Q(x)=1$ for the torsor under that group. The two slanted notes qualify the topologies under discussion. Some of the actual abbreviations and connecting words are doubtful.

**The cover as a zero locus (27–28).** The NB identifies the quadratic cover with the locus $Q(x)=0$ in the projective line of $E$. For a section of the cover, an algebra homomorphism gives a kernel $J$ and hence the isotropic line $D=J E$. The sheet states the converse for line subbundles and reduces it to $E=\mathcal A$, with $Q$ the norm.

**Passage to an Azumaya algebra (28–29, §4).** Twisting $E$ by a line on the base preserves the similarity data under discussion. The new object is $\mathcal M=\operatorname{End}(E)$, with a submodule of $\Sigma(\mathcal M)=\mathcal M/\underline O\,1_{\mathcal M}$. In the strictly faithful case the author describes the cover by intersecting a projective subspace with the reduced-norm quadric. He then asks what survives of the invertible module on $X'$.

**The line and its conjugate (29–30).** The ratio of $\Lambda$ to its conjugate survives a change by a pullback from $X$. Its norm is identified with the trivial line. This suggests a reduction of the unit group to its norm-one subgroup. The author considers $\mathcal M/\mathcal A$ as an $\mathcal A$-line and asks for an isomorphism with $E$ up to scalars. A projective reformulation and a trace-zero note at the foot are cancelled.

**The determinant calculation and the abandoned proposal (31–32).** The left and right algebra actions on $\operatorname{End}(E)$ are exchanged by the anti-involution. Using $E\otimes E^\vee$, the two determinant calculations lead to expressions for the quotient in terms of tensor squares. The proposed isomorphism would require a distinguished generator up to base scalars, or a distinguished line in $E$. The author says there is no canonical choice and redirects the question towards a square root.

**A cokernel and a desired norm (33).** The revised construction introduces the map $\varphi_u(\theta)=u\theta-\theta\bar u$. Its image is $\mathcal M^\natural$. The author writes the quotient as a cokernel and gives an exact sequence ending in $\Delta$. The next desired datum is a trivialization of $N_{\mathcal A/\underline O}(\Delta)$. A trace pairing on $\mathcal M$ supplies adjoints for multiplication on the left and right.

**Transpose, kernel and conjugators (34).** The calculation gives $\varphi_{\bar u}=-\varphi_u$, hence a description of the base dual of the cokernel by a kernel. The text also relates the $\mathcal A$-dual to the cokernel up to a base twist. The invertible elements intertwining $u$ and $\bar u$ are described as a pseudotorsor under a centralizer. After local choices, the last line asserts the existence of $\theta$ carrying $u$ to $\bar u$ by conjugation. That is the end of this extract.

## Questions and conjectures

- **23–24.** “Pour achever de déterminer … comme foncteur” introduces a coordinate-change question. The following sheet explicitly leaves it aside.
- **24.** “On définira un inv. d’Arf … en distinguant les cas du rang pair et du rang impair.” The construction in arbitrary rank is prospective.
- **29–30.** “Que devient le … Module inversible Λ … ?” leads to the conjugate ratio, then to “on s’attend à trouver” a line with reduced structure group.
- **30–32.** “Il faudrait voir qu’on a un ‘iso. à scalaire près’ canonique” is the proposal concerning $E$ and $\operatorname{End}(E)/\mathcal A$. On 32, “la supposition du début est erronée” expressly withdraws it. The author then asks how to grasp “la racine carrée”. [Q32]
- **33–34.** “Il faudrait ensuite définir sur Δ une forme … quadratique canonique, plus précisément un iso.” What is wanted is $N_{\mathcal A/\underline O}(\Delta)\simeq\underline O$. The argument that follows studies the kernel and conjugators. A final construction of the desired isomorphism is not identified in this extract.

## Remarks of the model

- **C23 · High.** The matrix on scan 23 has trace $\beta$, determinant $\alpha\gamma$ and characteristic polynomial $T^2-\beta T+\alpha\gamma$. Symbolic multiplication also gives zero on substituting the matrix into this polynomial. This checks the local display independently of the ambiguous exponent in $Q$.
- **R29 · High, conditional on the indicated pullback.** A base line pulled back to $X'$ contributes the same factor to a line and its conjugate. Cancelling that common factor explains the invariant ratio discussed on 29. This observation does not decide where the small superscript is written.
- **Q32 · High as a reading of the argument.** Scan 32 explicitly withdraws the isomorphism sought on 30. The new cokernel on 33 belongs to the revised question. Combining the two quotients into one claimed identification would hide the author’s change of mind.
- **Reading question · 33, oblique NB, third line. Low.** The frozen reading attaches a doubtful second “NB” to the diagonal note. The capitals could instead start an “NB” followed by a small index in the horizontal parenthesis. The two baselines intersect at this group. The transcription retains its frozen placement.
