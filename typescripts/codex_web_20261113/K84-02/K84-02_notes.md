## Title sheets, dates and page counts

All 16 sheets are handwritten mathematical notes. There is no separate title leaf, date, stated total page count, or typescript pagination. The small lower-left numbers run from 18 on scan 19 through 33 on scan 34. They are recorded as archive sheet numbers in TeX comments.

Section markers visible in the text are 2.4 on scan 19, 2.5 on scan 22, 2.6 on both scans 23 and 24, “3. Théorème récapitulatif” on scan 24, “Cor. 1” and “Cor. 2” on scans 26 and 27, and “4.” on scan 28. The circled mark at the upper right of scan 26 is transcribed as 3°. A line connects it to “celle” in the enumeration. Its interpretation as an item number is editorial.
