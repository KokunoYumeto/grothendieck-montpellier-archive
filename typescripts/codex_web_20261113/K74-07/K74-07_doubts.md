## Legibility

The printed fields of 123 and most of the later running text are clear. In 121 several values occupy the same small cell as an earlier value. Black ink at the head of a prime or a tiny subscript can obliterate the whole character. The scratch diagrams of 122 and 126, the crowded oval labels on 125, and the cancellations around the diagonal map on 128 remain the least secure. The TikZ figures retain the visible solid, broken, doubled and crossed paths; a dark junction does not always disclose the original order of the pen strokes. Two cancellations remain unreadable as words.
