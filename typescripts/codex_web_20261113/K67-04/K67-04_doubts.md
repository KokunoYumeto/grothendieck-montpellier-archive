## Legibility

The central writing on 54–56 and the live interval formulas on 58 are generally clear. Comparison becomes hardest where a diagonal crosses a second pen stroke at the same angle. Examples include the first margin and the equivalence chain on 53, two abandoned conditions on 57–58, the apparent folios on 60–61, and the parenthesis alongside the disc construction on 68. A mathematical expectation often chooses a different reading from the visible ink, especially at the dotted tangent-space coordinates and the date on 54. The register retains that distinction. A count of zero wholly illegible passages means that a candidate could be offered everywhere. Several individual candidates have low confidence.
