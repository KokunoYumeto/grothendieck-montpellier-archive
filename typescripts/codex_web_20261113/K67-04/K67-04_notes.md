# Notes on the sheets

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

| Scan | Words on the page | What the qualification applies to |
|---|---|---|
| 56–57 | “rectifiabilité suffit ?”, then “rectifiable suffit ??” | The regularity needed for cutting along a topological 1-complex and for the local lemma on a segment. Scan 59 uses “rectifiable suffit” without the question mark. |
| 58 | “reflètent sans doute”, “Ceci devrait …”, “On doit avoir un théorème dans le style du” | The regularity of the map between the two banks and the proposed sewing theorem that starts on 59. |
| 60 | “ou plus régulier que ça ?” | The deleted first form of Lemme 3 asks what regularity is needed of the interval homeomorphism. |
| 63 | “rectifiable (?) pour sa structure analytique réelle” | The condition imposed on the chosen boundary circle in part a) of the proposition. |
| 64 | “impliquant (qui sait?) qu'il est déterminé par le système des ombres” | Whether the shadows determine a system of disjoint discs subject to a suitable restriction. |
| 65–66 | “Il est tentant de restreindre”, “Comment cette condition d'admissibilité se reflète-t-elle … ?”, “quelle est la relation … ?” | Equality of two toric structures is proposed. Its intrinsic meaning and relation to Bergman geodesic discs are questions. |
| 67 | “je présume qu'elle est fibrante”, “c'est la projection canonique”, “cela doit provenir du fait que” | The hole-capping map, and its proposed explanation using the sphere of type (0,3). |
| 68 | “Il faudrait, pour le voir clairement”, “de centre … ?”, and a freestanding “?” | The relation of the punctured sphere to the bordered sphere and the independent dependence of each removed disc on its own length. |
| 68 | “Ce point étant supposé acquis, ou quelque variante”, “il semble donc bien que”, “très tentant de faire la Conjecture” | The description of the family of discs by its shadows. The leaf ends with the displayed assignment. |
