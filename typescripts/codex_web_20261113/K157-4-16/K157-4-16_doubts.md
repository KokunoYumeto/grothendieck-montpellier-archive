## Legibility

The large diagrams, isolated equation labels and uninterrupted prose are generally easier to read than the changes made inside a sentence. A small mark at the end of a shaft can be a pen lift or an arrowhead. In 243 the crossed axiom and the note on limits are particularly difficult. In 250 and 252 the slanting marginal arguments overlap the main writing. In 254 the manuscript page number, the primed faces and the older labels in the diagram carry several layers of ink. In 256 the numerals in the marginal references are small and partly retouched. The last sheet ends in the middle of a live sentence. Its continuation is outside this packet.
