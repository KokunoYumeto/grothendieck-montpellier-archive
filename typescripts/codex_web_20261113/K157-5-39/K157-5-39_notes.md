## Steps the manuscript marks as unverified

- **605, Proposition 4(b).** An oblique note at the left reads “Pas prouvé ?”. The ending of *prouvé* is compressed. The question mark stands below the words, beside (b).
- **607–610, return to (b).** On 607 the author writes “Prouvons maintenant le b)” and introduces a lemma. On 610 he says “on ne peut donc conclure de cette inaccessibilité de S, celle de S′”. He formulates a criterion in a corollary, then asks “Cela est-il vérifié, en prenant simplement π ≥ Card k ?” The ending of *simplement* is uncertain. The next sentence ends “me paraît délicate, et j’ignore la réponse”. The indicated products and the construction involving kΦ are suggestions for a counterexample.
- **616–617, the equality involving direct factors.** After “On a envie de poser”, the author gives an inclusion and adds “mais il n’est pas clair en général qu’on ait égalité, faute de pouvoir montrer que … est stable”. He names change of base and transfinite composition on 617. A theorem and corollary with explicit hypotheses follow.
- **609, corollary.** A separate “!” stands next to the condition beginning with underlined *pourvu*. It calls attention to a hypothesis. There is no question mark beside this corollary.
