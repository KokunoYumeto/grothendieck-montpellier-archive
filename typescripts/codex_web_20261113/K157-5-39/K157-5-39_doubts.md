On 606 the opening power has an elevated J, while the dark final J/D is beside the lower half of k. The next page has a clear low J. On 608–609 the dot or descending stroke on the small letter after x distinguishes i from possible j.

On 613–617 the bar at the foot of Φ is part of its letterform. The open fork of Ψ usually has no comparable foot. The straight underline of the class S on 603–610 is separate from the letter. The left closure is drawn with a high wave next to Φ. The right closure is drawn with a short horizontal above or beside Ψ. One, two and three primes have been counted separately. The terminal mark after Φ″ on 616–617 has two offset uprights and a connecting stroke.

The relation glyphs have independently drawn ends. In the formula register, a description within ⟨ ⟩ identifies the two tips and the count of vertical crossing strokes. The page files draw these details in TikZ. The note in the left margin of 617 is the only instance in these sheets with two distinct crossings.
