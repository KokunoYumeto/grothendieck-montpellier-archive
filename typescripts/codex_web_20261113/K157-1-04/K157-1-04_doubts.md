## Legibility

The main propositions and the large diagrams can generally be followed. The smallest hands are much less secure. On 050 the last line of Cor 2 sits in the space above the next heading. On 054 primes disappear in the hatching around the product of slices. The lower Hom on 060 reaches the dark edge. On 061 and 063 different directions of writing intersect; on 064 and 065 several words in the sideways notes occupy the same space as upright lines. The three illegibility marks are in cancelled prose. Some other cancellations retain enough of an outline for tentative readings. Approximating a long cancelling stroke with several local strikeouts and reproducing a very flat arrowhead in typeset form both lose details of the original ink.
