# Editorial notes · cote 23, scans 51–65

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

| Scan | Words on the sheet | What they qualify |
|---|---|---|
| 51–52 | “À signaler … remarques ?”; the cancelled “est-elle … ?? Vérifier § 16 !!” | The first is a decision about adding a remark. The second questions regular immersion outside the noetherian case. The adjective in that question is uncertain. |
| 53–54 | “n'a pas l'air de devoir …”; “semblent marcher encore”; “Berthelot en a déjà vérifié une, en tous cas”; “je prévois”; “S'il en est bien ainsi” | The author distinguishes his expectation for localization and transitivity from the one verification credited to Berthelot, and makes the terminology change conditional. |
| 56–57 | “encore une fois de lire 19.7”; “J'ignore si la proposition est vraie”; “même en admettant 16.1.5”; “j'ignore si le noyau …”; “C'est sans doute faux” | The postscript questions 16.1.5 and then 16.1.6. The finiteness of the kernel is the specific unresolved point. |
| 58–59 | “?” beside item (i) and the example; “Sinon, pour coiffer … on pourrait prendre”; “Je n'ai pas encore compris 19.7”; “Ne faudrait-il pas parler de topologie … ?”; “Je ne suis pas capable de rédiger une théorie naïve utilisable …” | A substitute separation condition is proposed. The following page distinguishes a result asserted for 19.5.5 from difficulties with 19.7, the exposition of topology, and intersections. |
| 60 | “J'espère donc que tu trouves quelqu'un qui le fasse”; “il faudrait savoir …”; “Je crois qu'il n'y a pas d'espoir” | The first concerns the Lefschetz theorem mentioned in the letter. The latter two concern the compactness argument in 19.3.12. |
| 61–63 | “je ne connais pas de référence”; the marginal “dans réédition du Chap I ?”; “que je n'ai pas su bien dégager”; the question beside the boxed sum; “Il reste à voir … plate”; the marginal “à compléter …” and the “(?)” on the slip | The Tor and completion assertion is given without a known reference; the following construction has an explicit flatness obligation, taken up on scan 63. The question marks on 62–63 belong to particular written corrections or additions. |
| 64 | “je crois” in the crossed-out NB | An alternative dévissage is proposed in a cancelled sentence. The last description of its hypothesis is uncertain. |
| 65 | Marginal “?” at 19.4.7; “Il suffirait … de traiter directement le cas N=A (et M=B) … mais j'en suis incapable” | The question sits beside the proposed simplification. The letter closes with a precise reduction Deligne does not know how to treat directly. |
