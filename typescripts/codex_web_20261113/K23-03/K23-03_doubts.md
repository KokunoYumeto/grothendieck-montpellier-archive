## Legibility

The two printed headings and most of the type are clear. Spacing, accents and the earlier impressions beneath overtypes are less secure. In the Deligne letters the main hand is generally readable, including most of the small degree and index marks. The faint recipient annotations on 58, 62, 63 and 65, the hatched addition on the pasted slip on 63, and the cancelled sentence on 64 are the least reliable passages. Some pencil-only interventions are attributed by resemblance to the reference sheets; the inventory uses “unknown” where the comparison does not identify a hand. Audit times are recorded per page. The timed second readings were shorter than the several-minute pace requested. The witness-stage inspections added a further reading of the marked places.
