## Legibility

The six pages are handwritten in English. Broad strokes, a consistent set of upright mathematical capitals and the shape of the little indices recur across the sheets. The inventory records “other hand” throughout. The supplied archival description attributes the hand to J. Tate; there is no signature on these six pages. The weakest readings are under the crosshatching and heavy overwriting on scans 10 and 11, in tiny exponents and multiplicity labels, and at the ends of the longer formulas on scans 13 and 14. The ink is very pale at the foot of scan 11. Several reduced letters have nearly flat tildes, distinct from the clearly horizontal closure bar over k. Each doubt is located above.
