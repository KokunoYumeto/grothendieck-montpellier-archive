Scan 31 says «On veut déterminer» before (22), then «Pour avoir une description intrinsèque … on va …». These announce work in progress. The last proposal trails off in a cancelled passage. No other surviving passage explicitly says «à vérifier», «admis», «sauf erreur» or «je présume». The coloured doubt marks in the transcription belong to the reader. The small overwritten marks near formulas are recorded locally and are not all authorial question marks.
