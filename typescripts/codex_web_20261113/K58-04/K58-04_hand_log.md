## Cross-page reconciliation before freezing

On 36 and 40 two short parallel bars in running French can stand for *même*. The barred angle in a depth comparison on 46 and 50 is a different sign. Across 36–43 and 46–50, a slash before Y at the foot of X or F is a restriction index. A high tick belongs to the adjacent letter, while a low slanted turn can belong to punctuation. The last pair on 43 remains genuinely ambiguous. The two feet of Λ in 40–41 recur in the negative powers. No upper bar is added to those letters. The sharply turned Γ in the small diagram of 48 differs from the script F of 41, 43 and 50 and the broad G drawn alongside it. The wave above F in the first sequence of 48 differs from its pointed roof lower down. The 47 and 49 typescripts use isolated underlines for the ideals I and P. The matrix letters on 51 are also individually underlined. All those capitals have one type impression. Reversed type on the backs of 46, 48 and 50 was distinguished by its mirror orientation. It did not supply readings in the manuscript hand.
