# Mathematical notes on cote 12, scans 36–50

## What the pages do

**The filtration and twists, scan 36.** The opening continues an earlier argument. It tests membership in $D_i$ after a sufficiently large twist, gives a tensor-product inclusion in $D_{i+j}$ and writes an Ext inclusion. A proposed comparison of two successive quotients is crossed out.

**Constant twisted motives, scans 36–38.** Section 10 singles out the effective constant twisted motives and their twisted counterparts. The notes pass from finite length, tensor products and simplicity to a λ-ring. Its effective version has a basis indexed by simple objects. Inverting the class ξ gives the larger ring. The transition on simple classes and the two vertical inclusions on scan 38 express this passage.

**Degrees and primitive classes, scans 38–40.** The successive subsets of simple classes give a grading. Scan 39 describes even and odd parts using $M_0$, $M_1$ and ξ, with the odd multiplication specified on a pair $u,v$. Scan 40 labels the primitive classes of weight $i$ by $\sigma_i$, separates the two parity cases and writes a quotient whose basis involves their twists. The last line records additional maps needed for multiplication.

**Duality and λ operations, scan 41.** Duality followed by a power of ξ defines an involution on the primitive classes. Its proposed identification and the determination of the λ operations are deferred to other arguments.

**A topological interpretation, scans 42–43.** Section 11 places the terms of a codimension spectral sequence beside a drawing of the $(p,q)$ plane. The author asks for a spectral sequence of motives and for an identification of pieces of its filtration by dimension type. A cancelled continuation considers a twisted constant coefficient and flags the shift by δ.

**The fundamental homomorphism, scans 43–44.** Section 12 forms $L(K)$ from finite-type preschemes and cutting relations. The map σ sends a class to an alternating sum of classes of $H^i_!$. The homogeneous components give virtual motives and virtual Betti numbers. A first set of questions about the dimension filtration is crossed out; the oblique note gives a revised target.

**Birational invariants, scans 45–47.** The dimension filtration gives a map on associated graded groups. The notes extract separate weights γ from the image of a class, relate them to Serre’s β and describe the smooth projective case by cohomology on dense opens. The restriction map is called injective and bijective modulo two different levels of the filtration. Its resulting class is denoted $\gamma_i(X)$.

**An object and a functor, scans 48–49.** Scan 48 specifies the largest bipure submotive of the indicated type in the limit over dense opens. It considers the map under a dominant morphism and then asks how much information the entire system contains about the function field. The curve example uses a map between Jacobians with finite kernel.

**Ruled varieties, scan 50.** The last page proposes passing to a quotient by ruled classes. It asks what the remaining invariant detects, first for $X$ and then for a surface with algebraic $H^2$. The note beside the surface question gives a negative answer in positive characteristic.

Its zero-index term is $\xi^0\Sigma^+_{i-2\cdot0}(X)=\Sigma_i^+(X)$, the very set from which the union is subtracted. **High confidence** in that consequence of the printed formula. Starting the union at $\alpha>0$ would remove the difficulty. **Medium confidence** in that proposed correction.
- **Scan 36 L12.** The crop shows two successive small groups *des des*. A single *des* gives the ordinary phrase. The repetition is retained.
- **Scan 38 L5.** The second row of the diagram closes $(X)$, leaving the outer parenthesis of the exponent open. Adding a closing parenthesis would parallel the first row.

- **Scan 48 L2.** After *des*, *invariant* ends at a t-shaped stroke. *Invariants* would supply the expected plural. The crop shows no distinct final s. **Low confidence** in attributing the missing letter to the author.
- **Scan 50 L2–3.** The square opening before *i.e.* ends with a round parenthesis after *indéterminée*. A matched pair appears intended.

## Remarks of the model

- **My reading, high confidence.** The margin warning on scan 42 distinguishes an initial $E_1$ from $E_2$. The body separately places the desired motivic interpretation at $r\geq2$. Keeping these qualifications apart preserves the order in which the author presents the two assertions.
- **My reading, medium confidence.** The change from a class in the ring on scans 45–47 to an object of a category on scan 48 is explicit in « on a mieux » and in the underlining of γ. It helps explain why a question about *systems of homomorphisms* follows.
- **Reading question, scan 42 L17, low confidence.** The independent reading is *en degré h*, and the final text confirms the letter $h$. The small word before it may instead be *dim*. The final letter is easier to see than the low strokes of the preceding word.
- **Reading question, scan 48 L9, low confidence.** The frozen reading begins *où on s’amène aux fonctions*. The final text has *où on l’anneau des fonctions*. The two small groups before the noun could read *ou en*. Their connecting strokes and the accent are indistinct. Those groups were outside the frozen doubt mark.
- **Reading question, scan 48 L11, low confidence.** The frozen and final readings are *NB*. The first capital’s high, separate left stroke could give *TMB*. It is clearer that the adjacent little vertical diagram reads $X'$ above $X$.
