The assembled fragment contains 58 `\unsure`, 0 `\illegible`, and 55 `\deleted` macro occurrences. Counts include deletion macros inside mutually exclusive alternatives; they are not counts of distinct physical cancellations. No OCR, outside transcription, or published mathematical text was used. All 12 scans were read from the supplied images and received a separately logged second reading.
