# K136-16 · Mathematical notes

## What the pages do

**Scans 235, 237, 239 · A filtered De Rham complex.** The first sheet presents the algebra on the $X_i$ and their differentials, with relations on their sums. A filtration by closed divided-power ideals puts the $X_i$ and $t$ in filtration at least one. The exterior-power decomposition reduces the homotopy calculation to the degree-zero part. The computation of its associated graded is conditional on the displayed epimorphism $k\{T\}\to\operatorname{Gr}^*(S)$ being an isomorphism. Scan 237 extends the filtration to differential forms and marks the cohomology comparison with $\operatorname{Fil}^p S$ as an isomorphism for $i\le p$. Scan 239 places that comparison in a square for $q\le p$.

**Scans 239, 241 · The divided powers of a prime.** After the example $t=0$, the notes ask which ideal in $\mathbb Z_p$ is generated by the $p^j/j!$ for $j\ge i$. Scan 241 expresses their valuations using the sum of the base-$p$ digits and takes an infimum over the tail. It distinguishes the constant positive-threshold value at $p=2$ from the growing lower bound for other primes. The attempted comparison between consecutive valuations is examined below.

**Scans 242, 240 · Relative algebraic and analytic groups.** In typescript order, §13.5 sets up a smooth separated relative group scheme, its analytification, its localization at $s$, and its formal completion. The later sheet turns to effective descent, poses an algebraization question, and chooses roots of unity to identify two $\ell$-adic sheaves. It begins an application of an exact Ext sequence.

**Scans 238, 236 · Fixed and toric parts, then the rigid-analytic case.** The inverse system of torsion subgroups leads to a comparison involving $T_\ell$. The text identifies the fixed and toric parts separately and introduces pairings supplied by a biextension. The next supplied typescript leaf starts §14. In the complete-trait case it recalls the extension of an abelian scheme by a torus and its counterpart for the dual, then identifies the two lattices using the opposite tori.

**Scans 244, 246 · Inclusion of categories of graded modules.** For a nonnegatively graded ring, $\mathcal M_N$ consists of modules supported in degrees at least $N$. For $N'\ge N$, the inclusion from $\mathcal M_{N'}$ into $\mathcal M_N$ has two visibly different constructions beside it. Truncation $\tau$ keeps the submodule in degrees at least $N'$. The quotient $q$ kills the submodule generated by all the lower components. The Hom diagrams distinguish their adjunctions.

**Scans 248, 250 · A further right adjoint.** Scan 248 describes the kernel of $\tau$, marks the proposed Hom comparison with a question mark, and tries to express $\rho(P)_i$ by graded Hom out of a truncation of $S$. Scan 250 collects the four functors in the order $q,i,\tau,\rho$. Its last chain of Hom identifications concludes with the boxed formula $\rho_{N,N'}=\tau_N\rho_{N'}$.

**Scans 247, 249 · Divided powers on an alternating graded algebra.** The outline on 247 distinguishes the product, differential, ideal $J$, and divided powers. It requires odd homogeneous squares to vanish. Below, writing $z=x+y$ separates the even and odd terms. Comparing expansions of $(z+z')^{(\alpha)}$ exposes a term proportional to $yy'$. Scan 249 tests scalar multiplication, iteration and multiplication of divided powers, keeping the factorial coefficients in view.

**Scan 251 · Change of coefficient data.** The page gives polynomial and exterior-algebra presentations, then records contravariance in $X$ and covariance in $(A,\pi,\gamma)$. A pair $(\varphi,\lambda)$ satisfies the displayed compatibility with $\pi$ and its divided powers. The generator diagram sends $X_i$ and $dX_i$ to $\lambda X_i$ and $\lambda dX_i$. The sheet ends with the algebra, differential and sign rules written beneath the tuple $(A^*,\bullet,d,J,\gamma)$.

**Scan 245 · Letter.** On 20 May 1975 Pierre, Robert Lafon asks a university director to invite colleagues to teach at the Université du 3ème Age. He reports attendance after three months, asks for proposals for 1975–76, describes possible teaching formats and discusses payment.

## Remarks of the model

**R1 · Scan 241 · High, exact arithmetic.** At $p=3$, $i=2$, both $3^2/2!$ and $3^3/3!$ have valuation $2$. Thus the weak inequality holds even though $i+1$ is divisible by $3$. More generally the difference of consecutive valuations is $1-v_p(i+1)$. The digit-sum formulas matched direct factorial valuations in all 384 tested pairs, with $p=2,3,5,7$ and $1\le n\le96$. This check concerns the numerical statement. The attribution to the pen strokes is recorded separately in L5.

**R2 · Scans 244–246 · High, explicit graded-module example.** Take $S=k[t]$ with $t$ in degree one, $k$ a field, $N=0$ and $N'=1$. The inclusion $(t)\hookrightarrow S$ becomes $(t)\to0$ under $q$. The degree-zero part of $(t)$ vanishes, whereas the degree-zero part of $S$ generates $S$. This gives the failure of left exactness anticipated on 246.

**R3 · Scans 247 and 249 · High for the coefficient calculation.** The mixed coefficient in the product formula satisfies Pascal’s identity. Exact integers gave agreement for all 144 pairs $1\le p,q\le12$. The coefficient of $x^{(\alpha\beta-1)}y$ in the iteration calculation agreed with $(\alpha\beta)!/(\beta!(\alpha!)^\beta)$ for all 64 pairs $1\le\alpha,\beta\le8$. These checks concern coefficients only. The page itself singles out the odd products by « OK si ».

**R4 · Scan 247, last differential rule at right · Reading question, low.** The transcription gives $x^{(\alpha+1)}dx$ with minus as an alternative to plus. Subtracting one would give the expected degree for differentiation. The extra upright in the exponent prevents a confident choice or attribution of a sign error.

**R5 · Scans 247, 249, 251 · Reading question, low.** The transcription marks parts of the outline, factorial work and coefficient calculations as deleted where long diagonals run through them. The other possible reading is that the diagonals cross a page of calculations without cancelling all the text they traverse. Unlike the local horizontal deletions, these strokes sometimes continue through substantial blank space. Both interpretations leave the transcribed formulas available.

**R6 · Scan 235, l. 2 · Reading question, low.** The transcription has a deleted, doubtful sign at the defining equality, with the alternative “dfn”. Its position just above the equality also allows an uncancelled definition label. The horizontal strokes there cannot be assigned securely.

**R7 · Scan 237, case display, ll. 12–17 · Reading question, low.** The transcription places the interlinear “p≤i, et ℓ=i” between the zero and k cases. A line of the k case itself also reads p≤i=ℓ. The added condition might belong to that case, or the first comparison might have been intended as ℓ≠i. An enlarged view shows two horizontal strokes without a clear diagonal through them. The position and intended scope of the insertion remain uncertain.
