## Legibility

The type is clearest on scans 236, 238, 240 and 242. Raised round signs, symbols supplied in pen, and faint numbers at the edges of the typescript copies are less secure. The letter on 245 is readable after rotation, except for the strokes of the signature. Its flourish in the TeX is a drawn approximation.

The small conditions on 235–237, the carry indices on 241 and the cancelled Hom formula on 248 are among the least secure readings. On 247, 249 and 251 long diagonals pass over many otherwise legible calculations. The extent of cancellation assigned to those diagonals is an editorial judgment. Separate local erasures have been represented as well. Faint letters can also disappear where the paper folds.

Each page received a separate crop-first audit. The timestamps in the audit are the actual ones. Its page intervals were shorter than the several minutes requested. The longest passages of nearly flat cursive and overwritten indices merit particular caution.
