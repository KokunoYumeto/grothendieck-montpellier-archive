## Legibility

The long sentences are easier to follow than their small qualifiers. The ink usually distinguishes a prime above X from an index below it, and distinguishes the angular M for a subset from the broader M after Ob on scan 176. A faint stroke under lim can be hard to distinguish from a rightward arrow. The worst overlap occurs in the margins of scans 168 and 172, in the cancelled passage of Proposition 5.3 on scan 172, and around the last two insertion lines of scan 179. Three words in the cancelled passage on scan 172 remain unreadable in two groups. The positions of the insertion strokes can sometimes be established more securely than the words they carry.
