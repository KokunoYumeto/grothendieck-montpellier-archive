## Comparisons across leaves

The detached horizontal below I, J, V, Γ or a is significant at the particular places marked in scans 40, 48, 51 and 52. Plain examples also occur. A subscript may itself be underlined, as on scan 40. The curled permutation-group capital in 42, 48, 49 and 52 differs from the angular Γ. In 42 the older reading G remains possible. In 48 and 49 there are clearer repeated forms on the same leaf. The double G before mS in 46 and the double V at the zero scheme in 47 each have a local second stroke. These do not justify doubling the free V elsewhere. The 4s under D in 47 and 52 are distinct from the 2 in the heading on 45. The marked basis-field subscript on 45 has a short foot and the alternatives 1 and 2.

Quotation ticks, added leaders and crossbars have been compared individually. In 49 the long internal strokes in « faces étant » belong to the letters. The separate bars crossing the old clause at the top of that leaf extend beyond word boundaries. In 50 the diagonals represent two notes on the calculation and a third on the group. In 51 the upper and lower diagonal paragraphs each have their own baselines; they do not form extra lines in the surrounding horizontal prose.
