## Legibility

The four typewritten leaves are generally clear after a half-turn. A few small additions call for enlargement, especially the generator subscripts, the patches of ink after *bijectif* on scan 6, and the pale lines under N and Z. Scan 3 has the most difficult continuous passage. A straight rule and a wavy cancellation pass through different versions of the hypothesis of Lemme 2. The cancelled draft under Lemme 3 on scan 5 contains a short stretch whose words could not be recovered. On scan 9 the oblique note becomes readable when turned into its own direction, but the first underlined word remains particularly insecure. These doubts concern the ink or the correction layer. They are separate from the author's mathematical qualifications.
