## Legibility

The first seven leaves have two fairly regular columns. The clean Hom formulas on 312–315 and the paired formulas on 315 can often be read stroke by stroke. The large deletion on 313 and the oblique warning at the foot of 317 are less secure. On the ruled leaves, horizontals in arrows, equality signs and cancellations run close to the ruling. The lower draft on 321 is crossed by a single diagonal. I trust the overwritten exponents on 320–323, the coefficients of the differential equation on 326, and the smallest point and arrow labels on 327 least. A group of letters or a label can often be proposed there even where its ending or index stays open.
