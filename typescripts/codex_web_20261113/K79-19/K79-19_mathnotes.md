# Mathematical notes on cote 79, scans 283–297

## What the pages do

**Moving an edge and counting faces (283–284).** A pair $(s,t)$ for $A_0$ is compared with $(s,\lambda t)$ for $A_\lambda$. Two facing fans on 283 give two possible neighbours, labelled $t'$ and $t''$. A count of pairs $(a,f)$ leads, on the assumption $\nu=1$, to $N/6$ triangular faces. The sequence of sketches on 284 asks for a vertex $s'$ with the same star in a second edge relation. The subscript of the second star there is simply $A$.

**Two local coordinates (285).** A coset calculation at the top tests what happens to $gU=g'U$ after inserting $a$. Subdivided polygons and labelled edges run alongside equations for stabilizers. In the middle, the map $y\mapsto y'=\lambda y$ carries the equation $x\wedge y=e$ to one with $\lambda^{-1}x$. Comparing $y\mapsto y+x$ with the translation in the second star produces a factor $\lambda^2$. The coset and edge calculations at the foot have several unfinished lines.

**Matrices and generators (286).** Conjugates of a subgroup, two assignments on $e_1,e_2$, and a matrix denoted $\rho$ share the sheet. The first matrix product puts $\mu,\mu'$ in opposite off-diagonal positions. The four-factor product below it has lower-left entry $\lambda\lambda'-\lambda^2$ and ends with $1-\lambda^2$. The sideways drawings have their own labels, including a test for a nonempty intersection of stars. See M286-a and M286-b in the check register.

**A parameter for incidence (287, 289).** Sheet 287 distinguishes $\mu(\lambda)$, written in the group of units, from $\nu(\lambda)$, written modulo sign. The two arrow diagrams relate vertices and stars. The notes try to express adjacency after changing the edge relation and say that $\nu$ depends on $A_0$ and $\lambda$. On 289 the diagonal action takes $s$ to $\lambda^{-1}s$ and $t$ to $\lambda t$. The successive adjacency tests use $s$ and $s'$ beneath their signs. Several lines stop after an implication, an equality, or “adj. à”.

**Counting incidences with a star (291).** There are $N/n$ stars or vertices and $N/2$ edges. Counting the pairs $(a,E)$ gives $(N/2)\alpha=(N/n)\beta$, where $\beta$ counts edges in a star. The cases $\alpha=1,2$ are written separately. The first has a parity condition on $n$ and mentions its diagonals. Two equations with question marks connect the value of $\nu$ at $\lambda$ with its value at $\lambda^{-1}$.

**What the star action determines (293).** The diagonal matrix induces a map from $\mathrm{Et}(s)$ to $\mathrm{Et}(\lambda^{-1}s)$. In the star coordinate the scaling factor is $\lambda^2$. Incidence supplies two possible displacements, expressed as $\zeta'=\lambda^2\zeta\pm\lambda$. Taking the sum and difference of the two candidate coordinates gives $2\lambda^2\zeta$ and $2\lambda$ respectively.

**The torsor calculation (294).** The two stars are written as sets of vectors with prescribed exterior product. A translation in the first uses $x$ and one in the second uses $\lambda^{-1}x$. The calculation headed “NB” compares the translations. The lower part takes $y'_0=\lambda y_0$ and obtains $y\wedge y'=(\lambda\zeta-\lambda^{-1}\zeta')e$. A scalar function of the two coordinates and a partly written invariance condition follow.

**Changing origins (295).** A new constant, written as a barred oval read $\theta$, enters the relation $\lambda\zeta-\lambda^{-1}\zeta'+\theta\in\pm1$. The words “dépend des origines choisies” point to the term $\lambda\theta$ in the expression for $\zeta'$. Underneath are trials involving translations of the two coordinates, with both struck and surviving versions.

**Attempted composition (296).** The sheet opens with $T_\lambda(A_{\lambda'})=A_{\lambda\lambda'}$. It compares two descriptions of $\mathrm{Et}_0(\lambda^{-1}t)$ and starts to characterize $\nu(A_0,\lambda)$. The line following “défini par la condition” contains only the start of a pair.

**University notices (288, 290, 292, 297).** Scan 288 announces a teaching committee meeting. Scan 290 announces P. Molino’s talk on transversely Riemannian foliations. Scan 292 gives dates for two talks by C.-C. Chou on residues and the Bochner-Martinelli integral. Scan 297 announces André Touboul’s defence, summarizes his study of noise in a charge-transfer device, and lists referees and the proposed jury. None supplies a premise for the calculations in the handwritten notes.

## Questions and conjectures

- **283.** “Si $\nu=1$, ça fait $N/6$ faces”. This singles out a case. The quantifier immediately above the face count has crossing strokes.
- **287.** A question mark is placed over the implication after the $A_\lambda$-adjacency formula. A further “?” accompanies the vertical comparison at the foot.
- **291.** $\nu(\lambda)\nu(\lambda^{-1})\overset{?}{=}1$ and $\lambda^{-2}\nu(\lambda)=\nu(\lambda^{-1})\overset{?}{=}\nu(\lambda)^{-1}$. The first equality on the second line carries no question mark. The box about units follows the question.
- **293.** “Donc on ne récupère là que $\lambda^2$, pas $\lambda$ !” motivates the use of the incidence relation to recover the pair of possible displacements.
- **294.** “à prouver” stands below the boxed law $\lambda(y\dotplus\zeta)=\lambda y\dotplus\lambda^2\zeta$. The later NB line calculates both sides. The second line in the brace following the definition of $\varphi$ is unfinished.

## Remarks of the model

- **M283-a and M291-a · model · high confidence.** The two counts divide by different incidence multiplicities. On 283, three edges per triangular face give $\nu N/6$ from $\nu(N/2)$ pairs. On 291 the displayed count instead gives $\beta=n\alpha/2$. Both arithmetic reductions were checked. This leaves the geometric hypotheses as the author states them.
- **M286-a and M286-b · model · high confidence.** Multiplication gives the displayed entries of the two unipotent factors. Multiplication of the four displayed factors gives diagonal entries $\lambda\lambda'$ and lower-left entry $\lambda\lambda'-\lambda^2$. Specializing $\lambda\lambda'=1$ yields the final matrix. This observation uses the surviving entries of the matrices; it makes no claim about the older ink in the retraced entry.
- **R291-b · model · high confidence.** Equality of squares alone need not recover a unit up to sign over the rings named on the sheets. Modulo 8, $3^2=1^2$, while $3$ differs from both $1$ and $-1$. This exact check concerns the scalar inference only. It leaves the sheet’s geometric incidence questions open.
- **M294-a and M294-b · model · high confidence.** Scaling a displacement $\zeta x$ by $\lambda$ gives coefficient $\lambda^2\zeta$ in direction $\lambda^{-1}x$. With the chosen origins on 294, a determinant calculation gives the printed scalar $\lambda\zeta-\lambda^{-1}\zeta'$. Both checks use the surviving NB calculation and coordinate displays.
- **M294-c · model · low confidence as a reading.** The invariant vector for the printed scalar is consistent with adding $(\alpha,\lambda^2\alpha)$ to $(\zeta,\zeta')$. The calculation is exact, but the small raised glyph in the manuscript can also be read $\alpha$. The ambiguity remains in the transcription.
