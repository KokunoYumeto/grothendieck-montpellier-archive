## Legibility

The typed notices are generally clear. Type collisions on 288 and 290, the faded honorific on 297, and the handwritten signature beneath the notice need the original image. In the manuscript, fine graphite disappears beside much darker retracing. The crossed labels on 285–287, the small superscripts on 294–295, and the lower index of A on 296 carry the greatest uncertainty. The drawings are vector reconstructions. Their strokes, curves and spacing are approximate. The numbered leaves have been represented throughout; 67 readings still bear an explicit doubt mark.
