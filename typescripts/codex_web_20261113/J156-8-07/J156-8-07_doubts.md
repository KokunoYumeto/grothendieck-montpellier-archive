## Legibility and limits

The larger formulas and most uncancelled prose are readable. The least secure material is the crowded revision below condition c) on scan 51, the cancelled lower arguments on scans 51–52, the small parenthesis beside the covering argument on 52, and the slanted notes and decorated L’s on 55. Reduced internal word strokes and overwritten symbols account for many explicit doubts. One cancelled passage contains two estimated unreadable words. The masks and alternatives record reading uncertainty; they do not resolve the mathematics. Missing punctuation and unfinished expressions remain missing. The scan 53 index set’s double-stroked N was distinguished from the plain N naming the intersection. No other bold or double-struck alphabet was silently inferred.
