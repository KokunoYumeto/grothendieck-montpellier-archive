## Legibility

Main lines and displayed capitals generally survive clearly. Endings and pen lifts within French words are much harder to distinguish than the overall word shapes. The least secure passages are the oblique corollary and cancelled C6 on 020, the very small additions on 021 and 023, and the sentence beside “vérifier plus tard” on 025. On 019 and 020 a solid strike completely covers one word apiece. The register distinguishes those two instances from readings for which fragments or plausible alternatives survive.
