# Mathematical notes on cote 72, scans 51–66

## What the pages do

**51–52 · Products and parity.** Scan 51 takes powers of the $B_i$ outside $\delta_n$ and writes congruences for the residual polynomials at even and odd indices. Scan 52 expands the determinant recurrence and boxes the recursion for $L_n$. The first argument multiplies the term two degrees lower.

**52–53 · The distinguished vector.** Vanishing pairings and the normalisation $\zeta_n=1$ give a backwards recurrence for its coordinates. The sheet identifies $\zeta_{n-i}$ with $L_i$ on the last $i-1$ parameters in reverse order. The endpoint coordinates are called $\rho$ and $\sigma$. The small loop under the tilde in the vector’s name has more than one possible reading.

**53 · Smoothness.** The author gives separate criteria for the smoothness of $f_E$ and $f_V$. Both involve invertibility of the $\beta_i$. The extra coefficient is $\sigma'$ in the first criterion and $\rho'$ in the second. The parity annotation beneath the first double arrow differs from the one beneath the second.

**54–55 · Antipodisme.** From $\underline a=-\mathrm{id}+\pi\otimes o'$ the notes discuss fixed points in the ambient space and its projectivisation. They restrict the quadratic form to the plane generated by $s_0$ and the distinguished vector. The resulting binary form $g(\lambda,\mu)$ is treated by parity, then with 2 invertible and in characteristic two. Scan 55 starts a further restriction to $(C,Q_C,\tau_*^C)$ and crosses the preceding discussion.

**56–57 · Calculs autoduaux.** The title leaf precedes a diagram between $V$, $V'$ and a dual space. Scan 57 writes coefficient products for the images of basis vectors and ends with a determinant with diagonal $-2$, upper diagonal 1 and lower entries labelled by the $\beta_i$.

**58 · Invariant subvarieties.** A sketch places $h_0,h_1$ on intersecting lines. The crossed draft considers which $H_i$ contain a putative invariant subspace and which $h_j$ it must contain. One case gives the boxed equation $\beta_0=0$. A dimension count is squeezed into the bottom left corner.

**59 · A pairing table.** Four cases specify $\langle a_i,a'_j\rangle$. The nonzero entries shown lie on three neighbouring diagonals. Two drawings distribute the $1$, $\lambda_i-1$ and $\beta_j$ labels among the corresponding pairs of vertices.

**61 · Dual bases.** The writer names $\rho_{ij}$, the four boundary coefficients $\rho,\sigma,\sigma',\gamma$, and two values for indices just outside the triangular range. He expands $a_i^*$ and then $a_j^{\prime *}$. The sheet boxes $\langle\pi,\pi'\rangle=\rho$ and evaluates $\psi$ and $f$ on basis vectors.

**63 · Compositions.** The primed calculation leads to expressions for $\psi'\psi$ and $\psi\psi'$ as a scalar identity plus a rank-one term. The boxed scalar relation is $\sigma\sigma'=\beta+\gamma\rho$. A crossed calculation tests a vector $t_0$. Below it, multiples of inverse maps are named with $\Theta$ and $\Theta'$.

**65 · Vertices and orthogonality.** Partial sums define $s_i$ and $s'_j$. Their pairings are tabulated for $i<j$ and $i=j$. The author sets $t_i=\Theta(s'_i)$, expands $t_{n+1}$ and relates successive $t_i$ by $\tau_i$. The last lines evaluate the polar form and $f$ on $t_{n+1}$.

**60, 62, 64, 66 · Other material on the sheets.** Three university notices announce doctoral defences in biochemistry, solid-state physics and ecology. The last sheet lists university council meetings. The dates and addressee are recorded in the companion notes.

## Remarks of the model

- **R1 · Model observation, high confidence for the numerical example.** On scans 52, 61 and 63 take $n=2$ and $(\beta_0,\beta_1)=(2,3)$. The entries on these sheets give $\rho=-2$, $\sigma=1$, $\sigma'=2$, $\gamma=2$ and $\beta=6$. Each side of the boxed identity on 63 is 2.
- **R2 · Model observation, high confidence for the three-by-three determinant.** The same parameters give the rows $(-2,1,0),(2,-2,1),(0,3,-2)$ in the lower pattern on 57. Direct evaluation gives 2. This tests the sign in S57 independently of the $L$ recurrence.
- **R3 · Model observation, low confidence about an intended identification.** In the crossed list on 63, $s'_0=a'_{n+1}$. The live first primed row on 65 begins $s'_{n+1}=a'_{n+1}$. The endpoint labels differ. The cancellation leaves open how the author meant the two lists to be used together.
- **Reading question, 63, foot.** The frozen abbreviation is “Dén.|Dém.”. A denominator would fit a scalar multiple of an inverse. The last low arch of the abbreviation does not distinguish n from m well enough to decide the word.
