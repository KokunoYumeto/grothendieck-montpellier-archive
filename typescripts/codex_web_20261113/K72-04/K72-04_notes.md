# Cote 72, scans 51–66

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **54, upper right margin.** The underlined “Attention” introduces a possibility, “il se peut que” the doubly or singly primed $c$ “n’existe pas” “à dist. finie”. The next line says that $c'$ exists. A second marginal note qualifies the argument at points of characteristic two.
- **63, middle of the crossed block.** “$t_0$ [orthog] à $s_0\cdots s_n$ i.e. $a_{-1},a_0\cdots a_n$ ?” The abbreviation before the first list is doubtful. The question mark is visible at the right of the second list. The subsequent computation is also crossed.
- **65, middle and lower part.** An exclamation mark singles out the coefficient $0\cdot a_0$. “On peut définir” ends in dots after the proposed point in $X$. The punctuation shows emphasis and an unfinished continuation. Neither passage explicitly asks for verification.
