## What the pages do

**Scans 032–033 — Exact sequences and an extension class.** The notes begin with a descent construction of a map from H¹(X,G) to a group of homomorphisms from Gₘ. They display a short exact-sequence candidate involving a looped P-like object, propose a longer sequence, and describe a fundamental class in Ext². Two extensions on 033 give classes ξ and η, whose product is written ζ = η ∘ ξ. A final conditional discussion leads to a boxed family of exact sequences. The precise script alphabet and several words in that conditional passage remain uncertain.

**Scans 034–035 — A limiting construction and comparisons.** Passing through larger S is used to propose a pro-object, a rational map from X and a universal property for commutative group schemes. Hom, H⁰ and H¹ comparisons are followed by a proposed higher-degree comparison. The right half of 034 relates P and P⁰ through an extension by Z. Scan 035 revisits the relative Picard object, Jacobian autoduality and comparisons between cohomology and Ext. It then experiments with spectral sequences; a boxed sequence and a five-term-sequence sentence are cancelled.

**Scan 036 — Title sheet.** The title is transcribed “Adèles des proschémas,” with the last word uncertain. It has no mathematical argument, date or author-written page count.

**Scans 037–038 — Power-series operations and a Witt-ring map.** The notes describe an S-like ring scheme through augmented formal series, with multiplication of series used for addition and a convolution product denoted by *. In characteristic p they write operations F and V, the identity FV = VF = p, and a calculation involving Fᶦ. They then propose a map ψ to a Witt ring, specified on {1+at} by ε(a) = (a,0,0,…). The continuation on 038 links this description to a functorial multiplicative representative. The argument of S∞ in the first formula and some of the accompanying words remain uncertain.

**Scans 040–042 — Cohomology, resolutions and inverse limits.** A quotient of a local product of Kₓ* by K* is related to a double-stem J-like object. Vanishing claims lead to cochain resolutions and spectral-sequence expressions involving Γ and π. The pages explicitly flag passage to the limit and admissibility as problems. Scan 041 rearranges related formulas, includes abandoned fragments, and writes a differential expression. Scan 042 adds inverse limits and an M.L. annotation, followed by Ext/Hom/cohomology diagrams. Several of these displays are visibly unfinished, so the pages do not supply a single completed argument.

**Scan 043 — Extensions and rational-point pairings.** An Ext¹ expression is directed toward Hom(J(k),G(k)). A pairing with J(k), a boxed H¹(k,G) ≃ G(k) labelled “corps fini,” and an extension diagram are written below. The lower two-row diagram compares Ext¹ expressions with Hom expressions. Some comparison marks and the distinction between ordinary and double-stem J remain reading questions.

**Scans 045–048 — Multidimensional adelic working formulas.** Under the heading “Adèles pour plusieurs dimensions” on 045, the notes arrange products of completed local rings, localisation steps, chains of points and A-like indexed objects. Scan 046 has two short product/limit expressions, the second ending in a bare quotient slash. Scan 047 expands product and inverse-limit expressions and writes recursive formulas for Aⁿ and Aⁿ⁺¹, with a doubtful flatness annotation. Scan 048 tests related completions and localisations using k[s,t], k[[s,t]], k((t))[s]/(sⁿ) and other series expressions. It includes a polynomial factorisation, loose tensors and small drawings; it is a scratch calculation, not a finished presentation.
