## Legibility

The typescripts preserve enough detail to distinguish missing or reversed accents and most pen additions. The heavily hatched parenthesis on 4 still conceals approximately one word. On 3 and 5 the most fragile readings are the amended numbers and the small pencil writing beside the outline. On 7, “en pièces détachées” became clearer on the second reading. On 9 the lowest Hom formulas meet two scan edges, and the pen has obscured several earlier indices. The slanted surnames and final verb beside f on 11 remain uncertain. In those places the marked readings preserve the surviving ink without supplying an unseen continuation.
