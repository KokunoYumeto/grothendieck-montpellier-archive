## Legibility

Scan 18 is dense enough that the short labels above and below Hom, the product diagram and the last line reward comparison at full size. The English course outline on scan 23 has clearly formed headings and formulas, while small connective words can collapse into a single stroke. The weakest passages are the right-hand and slanted notes on scan 25, the pale pencil beneath the cancellations on scan 26, and the left margin on scan 32. Elsewhere, the darkened indices, prepositions trapped under insertions and words beneath horizontal cancellations carry most of the residual uncertainty. A proposed reading of visible traces can have low confidence even when no word has been replaced by an illegibility count.
