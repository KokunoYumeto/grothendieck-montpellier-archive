## Legibility

The editorial page references and most typescript sentences can be read independently of their mathematical setting. Cross-typing on 30, 32 and 34 destroys some of the original sorts. The hand on 29, 31 and 33 compresses syllables and sometimes leaves arrows headless. The stair-shaped cancellation on 29, the slanted pencil replies on 6–7 and the bottom question on 37 are particularly uncertain. The central band on 36 has several overwritten coefficients that a cleaner parallel cannot identify.
