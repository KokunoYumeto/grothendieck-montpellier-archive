# Blind reading and visual collation

The register contains independent notes made from the images before reopening each page. They use abbreviated descriptions for drawn arrows and superposition. The first table for each leaf lists the revised passages and the crop decisions, including readings deliberately retained against the blind observation. The alignment gives the adopted reading and locates its historical transcription for every independent entry. Multiple punctuation signs in a blind note sometimes just separate the objects observed. “Verbal or graphical comparison” means there is a literal difference or a difference of notation. The block register retains the two complete readings for inspection. The current page column detects any later witness change.
