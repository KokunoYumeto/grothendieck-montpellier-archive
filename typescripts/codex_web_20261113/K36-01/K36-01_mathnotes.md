## What the pages do

**An editorial reading of SGA 7 IX (scans 3–8).** The six sheets address a “tu”. The reader asks where hypotheses enter and how assertions are justified. Scan 3 distinguishes a reference for “réduction stable” from the requested definition of “réduction semi-stable”. Scan 4 compares pairings, then formulates strict compatibility with the weight filtration for a map between extensions of abelian varieties by tori and an isogeny criterion using \(T_\ell(\varphi)\). Scan 5 proposes a description of a torsion kernel, asks about specialization and inertia, and sketches a morphism of étale topoi in the discussion of a “voisinage tubulaire”.

**Normalization, reduction and missing references (scans 6–8).** Scan 6 puts two possible values for a Dirac function at the identity, \(1\) and \(\#G\), beside the same value \(0\) elsewhere. The reader asks what becomes of the discrete part and suggests deforming a product of two elliptic curves, one ordinary and one nonordinary. Scan 7 proposes finite and toric parts up to isogeny, asks about a sign, and requests the location of several verifications. Scan 8 asks for a reference about completion and paraphrases a claim about extension from \(\ell\)-primary torsion. Short marginal replies sometimes dispute the reader. None of these exchanges signs the responding hand.

**A pair of extensions (scan 28).** The sketch places \(M\) above a row containing \(T,G,B\), and \(M'\) below a row containing \(T',G',B'\). It identifies \(T=D(M')\) and \(T'=D(M)\). At the right of the upper row the arrowhead points towards \(B\) from the zero. Several other connections have only a shaft.

**Splittings and lifts (scan 29).** Maps out of \(B'\) and \(B\) lead to two extension rows. An example compares a Hom expression, a Biext group and an Ext group, with vanishing conditions written underneath. The writer then pulls a biextension back to \((M,M')\) and discusses lifts \(j,j'\). After a long cancelled explanation, the live text gives the criterion “ssi les deux splittings … coïncident”. One splitting comes from \(\rho\) and \(j'\), the other from \(\rho'\) and \(j\).

**From complexes to torsion (scan 31).** In the injective case the page uses the quotients \(G/M,G'/M'\). It then places \(M\to G\) in degrees \(-1,0\), gives the primed complex alongside it, and announces a class \(\xi\in\mathrm{Ext}^1(A\otimes^{\mathrm L}A',G_m)\). The definition of \({}_nA\) uses \(\underline{\mathrm{Ext}}^0(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z,A)\), with a second definition for \(A'\). A finite sequence joins \({}_nG,{}_nA,M_n\). The page puts a primed sequence below it and passes to \(n=\ell^\nu\). The final primed line repeats \(T_\ell(A')\).

**Gluing, orthogonality and perfect duality (scan 33).** A triangle leads to a local Hom group. The maps “se recollent”. The finite pairing \(\varphi_n^\xi:{}_nA\otimes{}_nA'\to G_m\) and the limit pairing with target \(\mathbb Z_\ell(1)\) follow. The proposition pairs two orthogonality statements with conditions on lattice torsion, then names the induced pairings. Its first lattice has been overwritten. The corollary assumes perfect duality for \({}_nB,{}_nB'\), vanishing of \({}_nM,{}_nM'\) and surjectivity \(nB=B,\ nB'=B'\) to conclude perfect duality for \({}_nA,{}_nA'\).

**The finite pairing in the typescript (scan 34, §§2.1–2.2).** For \(n>0\) and \(nP=P\), restriction to \((P,{}_nQ)\) and the isomorphism cited as (1.3.6) give a pairing on \({}_nP\) and \({}_nQ\). The next paragraph takes \(n'=mn\), assumes \(n'P=P\), and compares the two pairings. The handwritten sentence beneath (2.2.2) specifies multiplication by \(m\) for each vertical map.

**Projective systems and stronger compatibility (scan 32, §§2.2–2.3).** Taking \(n=\ell^\nu\) gives (2.2.4)–(2.2.6). Here \(T_\ell(M)\) is explicitly the projective system \(({}_{\ell^\nu}M)_{\nu\geq0}\). The second part treats passage to a quotient under an epimorphism hypothesis on \(Q\), then states \(\phi_\xi^n(mx,y)=\phi_\xi^{n'}(x,y)\) with \(y\in{}_nQ(S)\), without that hypothesis on \(Q\).

**The symmetric compatibility (scan 30, §2.3).** The square and (2.3.5) move multiplication to the second variable. The argument invokes bilinearity and reduces a remaining compatibility to a triangle comparing \(\underline{\mathrm{Tor}}_1(P,Q)\) with two tensor products. It breaks off after “Or cette compatibilité résulte de”. The section numbers place these three typescript leaves in the order 34, 32, 30.

**“Picard et dualité” (scans 35–36).** A title leaf precedes a landscape table. Across three columns the table compares \(H^0,H^1,H^2\) for several coefficients. It names the Jacobian \(P\), its elements killed by \(\ell\) or of order \(p\), self-duality and “dualité de Serre - Cartier”. The first coefficient has competing readings \(\zeta,\xi,\mathfrak z\). In the \(i_p\) band the two occurrences of \(F\) attached to \(H^1(X,\underline O_X)\) have different positions, left below in the middle column and right below in the last.

**Cohomology, Ext and a list of topics (scan 37).** An \(H^1\) expression precedes a comparison of two Ext expressions, with an upward arrow from \(H^p(X,\underline{\mathrm{Ext}}_X^q(A,G_{m\,X}))\). A brace collects several topics, including Cartier duality, rigidity and linear, symplectic and orthogonal bundles. Below is a question about representations, a cancelled Hom expression and two double-struck Zs separated by an uncertain mark.

## Remarks of the model

- **Model remark 1 · high confidence.** The short “c'est pourtant trivial.” on scan 7 answers the line doubting “il est immédiat”. Their placement and their opposed wording make the exchange legible. Neither line supplies the missing argument or identifies the respondent.
- **Model remark 2 · medium confidence.** On scan 30, the reduction introduces \({}_{mn'}P\) above an arrow labelled \(m\), then takes \(x'\in{}_{n'}P(S)\). Scan 34 has \(n'=mn\). The subscripts warrant comparison. The leaves do not single out a unique repair to the wording of the proof.
- **Model remark 3 · high confidence about the repetition.** The left cell in the middle band of scan 36 reads \(H^2(X,\mathbb Z/p)\simeq H^2(X,\mathbb Z/p)=0\). Neither an alternative coefficient nor a replacement exponent is specified on this sheet.
- **Model remark 4 · low confidence.** The arrangement on scan 37 recalls a relation between local and global Ext. The arrow, the unresolved upper-left notation and the absence of a stated convergence law leave its precise interpretation open.
- **Model remark 5 · high confidence about the strokes, low about their function.** Three layout questions emerged on returning to the whole sheets. On scan 29, beside the extension rows, the frozen reading gives the two marginal splitting statements separately. Three fine horizontal strokes divide and bound them. The small vanishing conditions below the example also have partial enclosing strokes. On scan 31 the frozen reading places both slanted notes in the margin. The upper note follows a long curved brace and the lower one a straight vertical stroke. On scan 33 the frozen reading underlines “Prop” and “Corollaire”. A vertical stroke runs beside the first lines of the proposition, and another beside the final lines of the corollary. These strokes are visible in the scan. The page files record the neighbouring words and their positions without reproducing these outlines individually. The final section of the audit supplies separate TikZ tracings.
