# Mathematical notes on K157-2-05

## What the pages do

**Scans 67–69, manuscript pp. 11–13.** Grothendieck wants to compare the six composites of three pairs of functors with identity functors. His lemma allows a natural comparison in either direction, provided its components belong to the appropriate localizer. He extends the conclusion to a full subcategory through which $p$ factors, with the induced localizer. The triangle through $A$ and the proof beginning $f'=gq$ belong to a cancelled formulation.

**Scans 69–71, pp. 13–15.** A diagram sets out five comparisons with $\mathrm{Cat}_{/S}$. Duality leaves three cases to consider. Grothendieck revises his plan when he sees that $\Phi$ and $\Psi$ need not send a total $W$-equivalence to a fibrewise one. He proposes primed functors, then asks which arrows one should invert in each category. Statement ① goes from the strict fibres to the total arrow under smoothness or properness assumptions.

**Scans 72–73, pp. 16–17.** The arrow category $\mathrm{Fl}(X)$ factors the diagonal through the assignment $x\mapsto\mathrm{id}_x$. The fibre of the pair of endpoint functors at $(a,b)$ is the discrete category on $\mathrm{Hom}(a,b)$. Grothendieck then considers the source and target functors separately and describes their contractible fibres.

**Scans 73–75, pp. 17–19.** An object of the factorization of $f:X'\to X$ consists of $x'$ and an arrow $u:f(x')\to y$. The new projection returns $y$ and the section uses the identity at $f(x')$. Applied over $S$, this gives $\Psi(X)$ with fibres $X_{/s}$. The square on scan 75 expresses compatibility with change of base along $u:s\to t$. The comparison $i_X$ is a total Hot-equivalence. Its map on the fibre at $s$ is the inclusion $X_s\to X_{/s}$. Some formulas for a return arrow on scan 75 are struck out.

**Scans 76–77, pp. 20–21.** Grothendieck distinguishes the total arrow $\Psi(f)$ from the maps $f_{/s}$ on its fibres. For $X,Y$ proper over $S$, or in $\mathrm{Fib\,hot}\,S$, a commutative square compares $f_{/s}$ with $f_s$. A sideways corollary discusses the transition arrows between the slash categories. Exchanging source and target in the construction gives $\Phi$.

**Scans 77–78, pp. 21–22.** Replacing $\mathrm{Fl}(S)$ by $\mathrm{Ch}_\infty(S)$ gives a factorization through an object perfect over $S$, hence a functor $C$ to compare with the inclusion $r$. The square with sides $j_X,j_Y$ relates the total arrows $f$ and $Cf$. Statement ② asks for passage from a total equivalence to equivalences on the strict fibres for perfect objects, or more generally for the indicated Hot-fibred ones.

**Scans 79–80, pp. 23–24.** The proposed argument for ② invokes a criterion on connected components, changes base to a Hot-aspherical $S'$, and compares a fibre square with the total arrows over $S'$. A marginal note permits working over the component containing the chosen $s$. The result is conditional on a base-change property, numbered ② with subscript 0.

**Scans 80–82, pp. 24–26.** Natural arrows from the identities to $rC$ and $Cr$ bring the argument back to the opening lemma. For full subcategories containing $\mathrm{Parf}\,S$, scan 81 takes the localizer induced from $\mathrm{Cat}_{/S}$. Grothendieck revisits the choices for smooth, proper, fibred, cofibred and split objects. The last page asks how restricting the morphisms to cartesian or cocartesian functors interacts with restricting the arrows inverted.

## Remarks of the model

**R1 — model, high confidence.** On scans 68–69, $iF$ and $(p\varphi)i$ both go from $C'_0$ to $C'$. Their componentwise comparison with $i$ therefore has the source and target required by the displayed Hom line. I checked the domains using $p=ip_0$, $\varphi_0=\varphi i$ and $F=p_0\varphi_0$. This checks the typing of that step only.

**R2 — model, high confidence.** The square on scan 77 compares $f_s$ and $f_{/s}$, with a hypothesis on its vertical maps. The square on scan 78 compares $f$ and $Cf$ on the total categories. This explains why scan 80 still calls for ② when discussing the fibrewise localizer, while scan 81 can apply the full-subcategory lemma with the induced coarse localizer.

**R3 — model, low confidence.** The late “donc aussi” on scan 79 immediately follows “des Hot-équivalences”. In the square below, the bottom arrow $f'$ was only assumed to be a $W$-equivalence. The comparison passes membership in $W$ to the upper arrow, which is the conclusion on scan 80. Its wording leaves open whether Grothendieck also meant to assert a Hot-equivalence there.

**R4 — model, low confidence, reading question.** Scan 77, sideways Cor. 3, gives $\Psi(X)$ in $\mathrm{Fib}\,\mathrm{sc}_0\,S$ in the frozen text. The convention on scan 74 suggests the alternative $\mathrm{Cofib}\,\mathrm{sc}_0\,S$. The note speaks of the forward transition $X_{/s}\to X_{/t}$, but the visible category name was read as Fib. The possible naming slip and the doubtful small subscript should be considered separately.
