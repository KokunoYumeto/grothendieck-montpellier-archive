## Legibility

The large diagrams, folio numbers and most object names have clear outlines. In the prose, accents and plural endings sometimes consist of a single detached dot or a trailing stroke. The crossed-out ordered pairs at the foot of scan 67, the opening of scan 70, the intersection of two writing directions on scan 73, and the rewritten labels on scans 74–77 are especially uncertain. On scan 82 the final word has too few distinct strokes to choose confidently among the readings. The uncertainty marks include tentative reconstructions of cancelled notation.
