## Legibility

The main paragraphs usually have space between words. The short handwritten labels, small terminal strokes and mixtures of adjacent writing directions carry much less evidence. The crossed definition and slanted note on 89, the nested braces on 90, the superposed numbers and letters on 91, and the long slanted note on 93 are the readings I trust least. Two estimated words under the cancellation before « le premier » on 93 remain unreadable. The alternatives in the tables preserve those limits.
