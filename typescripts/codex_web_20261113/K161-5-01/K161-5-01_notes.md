# Cote 161-5 · scans 003–016

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

Scan 015 introduces a temporary assumption in the proof of proposition 8. Serre writes “Admettons provisoirement ce lemme, et démontrons la prop. 8.” Once the orbit argument is finished he returns to it on the same page with “Reste à démontrer le lemme 3.” The valuation argument on scan 016 ends with “ce qui démontre le lemme.” Thus the one expressly deferred step is discharged within these scans.

## Title sheets, dates and page counts

Scan 003 is the cover of J.-P. Serre’s *Sur les groupes de Galois attachés aux groupes p-divisibles*. The English lines identify it as a reprint of *Proceedings of a Conference on Local Fields*, “NUFFIC Summer School held at Driebergen (The Netherlands) in 1966”. The imprint reads “Springer-Verlag Berlin Heidelberg New York 1967” and “(Printed in The Netherlands). Not for Sale”. A raised 1 after the French title refers to the footnote on scan 004.

Scan 004 dates the text itself with “Le texte ci-dessous a été rédigé en décembre 1966.” The cover and introduction carry no printed folio. On scan 007 the additional footer reads “9    Local Fields”. Its position and wording suggest a printer’s signature. The sheets give the following individual numbers. They state no total page count.
