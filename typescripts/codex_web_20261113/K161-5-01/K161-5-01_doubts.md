## Legibility

The sheets are a typeset reprint. Body text, running heads, printer’s footnotes and folios are generally clear. The inventory’s controlled value `typed` denotes these printed elements. The heavy roman letters are typographic bold. Two slanted additions, on scans 010 and 016, are inventoried as `unknown`; the paper supplies no signature identifying the writer. Pencil numbers in the lower corners appear as archive comments. Faint reverse-side printing is visible on several sheets. The least secure readings are the isolated trace on 014 and the joined letters following “voir Sen” on 016. Two opposed ink specks above and below the first «résulte» in the proof of proposition 7 leave the word intact. The last sheet ends mid-sentence.
