# Mathematical notes on cote 37, scans 008–038

## What the pages do

**008, 014 — cover and publishing correspondence.** The cover reads “SGA 4”. The Brandeis correspondent assigns “Image inverse d'un plat” to exposé V and “Assez de points” to VI, promises another version of 4.10.6, and asks for IV §§9–13. The postscript says the writer will ask Mike about the exposés after VI.

**017 — a proposed extension of IV 4.10.6.** Number 5°) starts with a full subcategory $S_0$ of $S$. Condition (i) concerns the source of an arrow in the underlined class $M$ whose target belongs to $S_0$. The live version of (ii) asks that the sieve generated by the objects of $S_0$ be covering. The writer gives a covering-family formulation with arrows $X_i\to Z_i$. The first version of (ii) is struck out.

**017 — induced topology and functors.** The reader is asked to identify the covering families of $S_0$ for the induced topology and to check the earlier conditions a)–d) for the indicated class $M_0$. A final proposal introduces Res and Prol. The second functor is circled, and the description of its essential image breaks off.

**019 — reference list for XVII.** Six page-and-line entries concern a reference to V, the use of $f_!$ for pointed sets and group-valued sheaves, and references to VI with subscript B, EGA II and Deligne. The last two entries share a brace.

**020 — a plan for three fascicules.** A contents list, three slanted subject headings and two tallies lay out the proposed volumes. The first tally has a deleted figure above the live 42. The third heading crosses a cancelled “Cat. Dérivées” entry; a connected oval asks “Cat. dér. ? 40”.

**021 — placement of missing text.** A cancelled instruction proposes a page 83 bis and the transfer of lines 7–18 from stencil 84. For p.85 the writer sets several forms of a double-stroked L followed by g side by side. The primes and the small signs change from row to row. A separate pencil calculation ends with a circled 536.

**022 — revisions to VI.** The page proposes a new orientation for the slash-and-phi notation adjoining E, a change to an exercise on finite topoi, and removal of a reference to the noetherian case. The §1 entry asks for verification of 1.25 and replaces an altered phrase about sums by an expression for finite direct limits. A final exercise note contains a rightward arrow under “Lim top”.

**026 — prospective additions and credits.** A pair of entries on flat modules and a functor for pointed sets or groups is followed by a reference check and a finiteness-theorem heading. For a second-edition preface the writer names two contributions by Deligne, integration and trace of torsors. A separate question concerns Verdier’s “C.D.”. The final IV §8 list names local topoi, pseudopunctual topoi and a topos written with a caret on I, for an ordered set I.

**028, 033 — short revision instructions.** The slip on 028 asks that XVI 1 be completed “comme dans Giraud”. The separate list on 033 names Artin, gives missing or incomplete references, and asks for a noetherian hypothesis in XIII 3.1. Its last line is a pencilled list of pages for XIX.

**036, 037 — cancelled production notes and a letterhead.** On 036 the type size, summaries and a footnote are dealt with in cancelled instructions. The live last block sends the reader from VIII, on fibre functors, to IV. Scan 037 carries the inverted institutional letterhead alone.

**038 — cohomology and limit functors.** After two references for SGA 5 VII, the writer asks for a discussion of the relation with inverse limits and their higher functors. Item a) treats a topos of the form $\widehat C$. Item b) turns to a general topos and sketches a display with a direct limit followed by an inverse limit. An adjacent “si ça marche ...” qualifies the display. The last sentence describes a covering of the final object of $\widehat C$ by objects of $C$.
