## Legibility

The Brandeis letter has a steady hand. Its Paris sentence, the signature and the edge-written postscript required separate examination. The pencil leaf numbers and the printed letterheads are usually distinct. The prospectus on 020 mixes several directions of writing with overwritten numbers. Large cancellations leave most of 021 and 036 readable, but two blackened groups at the opening of 021 have lost their letter shapes. The most consequential remaining uncertainties are the arrow strokes and cancelled subscript on 017, the altered phrases on 022 and 026, and especially the alphabets and overwritten last term of the display on 038. The final formula should be quoted together with its uncertainty marks. No independent author attribution can be read from the Brandeis signature beyond the name transcribed there.
