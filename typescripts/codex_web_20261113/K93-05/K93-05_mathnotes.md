# K93-05 · Mathematical notes

## What the pages do

**Scan 193.** A sentence already in progress compares connections on $\mathcal O_X$ with logarithmic differentials. It gives $L\otimes\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z$ as a group of isomorphism classes. Returning to arbitrary characteristic, Grothendieck compares a horizontal section $\sigma$ of a tensor power with $\sigma_0=1$. Under the hypothesis on global units, he concludes that the usual stratification is the only integrable one on $\mathcal O_X$. This identifies the line bundles in question with those whose suitable prime-to-$p$ tensor power is trivial.

**Scans 194–195.** Over an integral finite-type base dominating $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z$, the author defines « de niveau zéro » for the generic fibre of a module with connection. The definition uses a finite étale cover. He restates it in terms of representations of fundamental groups and mentions torsors. After giving a necessary condition on the $p$-curvatures of closed fibres, he asks for its converse. In rank one he separates the underlying line bundle from its connection.

**Scans 195–198.** The rank-one problem leads to questions about the dual abelian scheme, its universal vector extension, sections of group schemes, and lifting points under Verschiebung. The order of the sheets matters for reading the argument. Scan 197 ends d) with « qui est propre ». Scan 196 opens with its parenthetical alternatives, then develops the unipotent-rank-zero case and a proposed reduction. Scan 198 returns explicitly to part a) of c).

**Scan 199.** The author forms a map from relative one-forms to their Frobenius twist using $\widetilde C$, then composes its successive twists. When the $p^\nu$-power operation acts as the identity on the indicated scalars, the composite becomes an endomorphism. The stated induction for a locally logarithmic form ends in the boxed equation $F_*^{(\nu)}\omega=\omega$. The indices on the line immediately preceding the box are overwritten.

**Scans 200–202.** Two discarded drafts surround the construction on scan 201. Scan 200 experiments with hypotheses on relative Frobenius and differentials. Scan 201 iterates relative Frobenius and identifies the trace in smooth relative dimension one, subject to a base-change formula. Its square places the twist of $X$ over the source of $F_S^\nu$. Scan 202 cancels a short finite-field version of the resulting endomorphism statement.

**Scans 203–204.** The trace is compared with Gysin on degree-one de Rham cohomology. The exact sequence places the space of forms inside that cohomology. The author says the map on the quotient is zero in the indicated proper case and relates the remaining eigenvalues to $p$-adic units among Frobenius eigenvalues. He goes on to compare logarithmic forms, eigenvalues congruent to 1 modulo $p$, and the group written ${}_pA(k)$ for the Jacobian.

**Scans 205–206.** A cover introduces variants of the relative Picard functor. Scan 206 defines the sheaf $P^c$ of invertible modules with relative connection. The first sequence exhibits the term of differentials modulo logarithmic differentials, the forgetful map to Picard, and its obstruction term. The author pulls back along $u:B\to\underline{\mathrm{Pic}}_{X/S}$, then specializes to its abelian identity component. He begins the definition of $P^{ci}$ by imposing zero curvature.
