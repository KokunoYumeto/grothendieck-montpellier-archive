Gr-categories

Summary

The purpose of these notes is to study the Gr-categories and give some applications of them. Below is a brief description of the organization of the work.

Chapter I gives some definitions and results, which are used continually in the sequel, on $\otimes$-categories one can find in [2], [6], [11], [14], [15], the terminology employed in this chapter being of Neantro Saavedra Rivano [14]. A \uline{$\otimes$-category} is a category $\underline C$ together with a \uline{law $\otimes$}, i.e a covariant bifunctor
\[
\begin{aligned}
\otimes:\ \underline C\times\underline C&\longrightarrow\underline C\\
(X,Y)&\longmapsto X\otimes Y
\end{aligned}
\]

An \uline{associativity constraint} for a $\otimes$-category $\underline C$ is an isomorphism of trifunctors
\[
a_{X,Y,Z}:\ X\otimes(Y\otimes Z)\xrightarrow{\sim}(X\otimes Y)\otimes Z
\qquad X,Y,Z\in\operatorname{Ob}\underline C
\]
satisfying the \uline{pentagon axiom}, i.e all the pentagonal diagrams
\diagram{\resizebox{\linewidth}{!}{\begin{tikzcd}[ampersand replacement=\&,column sep=.5em,row sep=3.8em]
\& (X\otimes Y)\otimes(Z\otimes T)\arrow[dr,"{a_{X\otimes Y,Z,T}}"] \& \\
X\otimes(Y\otimes(Z\otimes T))\arrow[ur,"{a_{X,Y,Z\otimes T}}"]\arrow[d,"{\mathrm{id}_X\otimes a_{Y,Z,T}}"'] \& \& ((X\otimes Y)\otimes Z)\otimes T\\
X\otimes((Y\otimes Z)\otimes T)\arrow[rr,"{a_{X,Y\otimes Z,T}}","{\unsure{\cdot^{\cdot}|:}}"{pos=.98,anchor=west,inner sep=0pt}] \& \& (X\otimes(Y\otimes Z))\otimes T\arrow[u,"{a_{X,Y,Z}\otimes\mathrm{id}_T}"']
\end{tikzcd}}}
are commutative. A $\otimes$-category together with an associativity constraint is called a \uline{$\otimes$-associativite category}.
