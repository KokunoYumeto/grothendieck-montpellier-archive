# Reading notes · Montpellier, cote 57

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

| Scan | Words on the sheet | What is left open |
|---|---|---|
| 89 | “que nous voudrions calculer” (the verb can also read “voulons”) | The opening poses the calculation of the pullback class. The ensuing identification treats the case of a section coming from an invertible sheaf. |
| 91 | “sous réserve d'existence de \(B^*\) et de possibilité de descente (p.ex. si \(B/S\) est projectif)” | The margin qualifies the description of \(u\) by means of a torsor. |
| 91 | “fonctorialité doit être explicitée avec un peu de soin !” | The author calls for the functoriality behind the diagram to be made explicit. |
| 91–92 | “Je vou-” / “drais l'interpréter comme l'extension universelle (de Serre)” | The identification with a universal extension is proposed. The next paragraphs begin its calculation. |
| 94 | “Il reste à calculer sa classe dans le deuxième” | The paragraph identifies two vanishing components. Its last two verbs are difficult to read and it ends in dots. |
| 94, cancelled | “canoniquement ??” and “Question équivalente” | The crossed passage asks about a canonical isomorphism and the correspondence between two prolongations with connection. The question marks belong to the cancelled draft. |
| 95, English typescript | “We shall not try to give a precise form to this statement.” | The assertion about “most of the groups in the commensurability class” is expressly left imprecise. |
| 97 | “Question Surjectivité (donc bijectivité) … sans conditions cohomologiques” and “Démonstration algébrique ???” | The first asks for surjectivity of Picard restriction in dimension at least four without the listed cohomological assumptions. The second follows the appeal to Kähler theory in characteristic zero. |
| 99–100 | “N.B. Généralisation au cas d'un morphisme donné” and “On utilisera un argument de spécialisation.” | The first suggests a generalization to a given map to projective space. The second announces a specialization argument after the incomplete characteristic-zero clause on 99. |
| 100 | “La vérification est presque triviale …” | The verification of injectivity on global differential forms is abbreviated to these words. |
| 101 | “Certainement inutile, en procédant de façon convenable” | The annotation queries a restriction beside the Laurent-polynomial lemma and its added regular-open hypothesis. Its precise scope is unstated. |
| 102 | “Inutile de se borner à des faisceaux loc libres” and “tels que \(\operatorname{prof} E_x\geq2\) pour \(x\in X-U\)” | The slanted note proposes a wider class of sheaves and ends in dots. The verb after “On peut” is uncertain. |
