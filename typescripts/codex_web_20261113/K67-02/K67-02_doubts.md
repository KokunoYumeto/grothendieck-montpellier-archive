## Legibility

The English type is usually clear, including some unexpected spellings. The letters under overtyping, and the boundary between key and pen inside a word, are less secure. On the French leaves the mathematical displays give better models for the alphabets than the fast connective prose. The weakest readings are the backtracked words in scans 29–35, the labels below the two coefficients on scan 26, and the raised signs on the family in scan 28. The strokes behind the black writing of scan 26 and the reverse-side columns on 24 and 28 were distinguished from the writing face. The notation table records local observations. In particular the flat-topped action sign σ and the descending group sign γ on scans 26 and 34 are different; the first occurrence on each sheet is less decisive.
