# Legibility and limits

The formulas are generally more separable than the running prose, but several small superscripts, primes and overwritten letters required additional crops. The quick French hand often reduces a whole middle syllable to a joining stroke. The least secure passages are the bottom paragraph of 18 and its continuation at the top of 19, the hatched covering formula on 19, the retraced family symbol on 21, and the cancelled NB block on 24. The right edge of 24 physically clips the Oubl argument. The raised mark on the first induced Y on 25 is also low-confidence.

All whole-page images and all supplied thirds were viewed directly. Further enlargements and rotations were used for inspection only; no OCR or text-recognition software was used. Every page has been transcribed and audited, but “complete” means that all visible material has a transcription or an explicit doubt/unreadability mark, not that all readings are certain. There is one physically unreadable cancelled word estimate. No mathematical diagram or freehand picture was identified: boxed text, braces, cancellation lines and annotation connectors are represented by the corresponding text/state macros rather than invented diagrams.
