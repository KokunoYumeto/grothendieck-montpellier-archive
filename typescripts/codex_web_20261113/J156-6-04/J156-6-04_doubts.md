## Legibility

The separated main lines, large comparison signs and numbered pages are generally legible. The tall tildes and the direction of most arrows can be followed directly. The weakest passages are the successive F/G and Φ/family corrections, the erased references on 26, 29 and 31, the tiny three-level revision below the diagram on 31, and the cancelled wording near the foot of 33. The two diagonal notes on 31 become much clearer when rotated. One small cancelled group at the bottom edge of 29 remains unreadable even after enlargement. The register keeps reduced word endings, the state of individual strokes and possible pen slips open wherever the crop did not decide them.
