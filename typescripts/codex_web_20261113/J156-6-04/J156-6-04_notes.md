# Notes on scans 26–33 of cote 156-6

## Steps the manuscript marks for verification or leaves open

| Scan and place | Words on the page | What the author marks |
|---|---|---|
| 27, below the three-line comparison | « et il faut vérifier la commutativité de (1,42), ce qui » | The square's commutativity is explicitly left to verify here. The reference also admits (1.42). The following words, read tentatively as « se voit comme à l'instant », are crossed out. « signifie » stands above them. |
| 27, after the corollary | « Il semble qu'il faille le poser en axiome, sous forme en apparence affaiblie, mais en fait équivalente. » | The writer qualifies the need for an axiom with « Il semble ». Scan 28 resumes « On doit donc poser » and gives the circled-chevron relation. |
| 28, reverse implication | « tautologique » after the equality with the projection indexed by G | A cancelled mark beside the closing parenthesis may be a question mark. Its cancelled state and doubtful shape are retained in the transcription. |
| 28, slanted note beside the corollary | « Ce ne sera sans doute plus vrai, en passant aux petites multifigures » | The author qualifies the extension to « petites multifigures ». The verb « passant » is a doubtful reading. |
| 29, addition after « certaines conditions » and next paragraph | « qui doivent traduire C1 ; C7 » and « Je voudrais expliciter les conditions sur cet ens de figures ensemblistes dans M. » | The correspondence with the numbered conditions is set out as work to be done. The separator between C1 and C7 is unclear. |
| 33, final parenthesis | « La stabilité par sup équivaudra, moyennant la condition suivante » | The equivalence is expressed in the future and referred to the following condition. The verb ending is doubtful and there are cancellations in the wording. |

Scans 26, 30, 31 and 32 carry no separate explicit request to check a step. The numbered requirements introduced on those pages are transcribed in their stated form.
