## Legibility

The formula letters added to the typescript are often darker than the type. The four typed sides are legible after rotation, including the abrupt beginnings and endings of exercises. The most fragile readings are under the strikes on 131, 135 and 136, in the unfinished calculations on 140, and in the crowded coefficient subscripts at the foot of 142. The diagonal note on 131 and the ending after «langage» in the margin on 134 have enough continuous ink for partial readings, but several strokes run together. The type visible backwards through 138, 140 and 142 belongs to the opposite face. Its dark patches sometimes coincide with a manuscript prime or dot.
