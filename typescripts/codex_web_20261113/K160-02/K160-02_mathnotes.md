## What the pages do

**The determinant and its invariants, scan 13.** The opening identity describes how $\Delta_n$ changes under a linear substitution. The corollary gives generators $A_1,\ldots,A_{n-1},\Delta_n$ for the invariants under $S\Gamma_n$. The proof compares two quotient maps in a square and invokes normality and the « Main Theorem » in its last line.

**Special linear structures, scan 14.** For a finite étale subgroup $M$ of the additive group, of rank $p^n$, the corollary relates special linear structures on $M$ to the $(p-1)$-st roots of its invertible coefficient $a_0$. The reformulation beginning « Ou mieux » describes the roots as a torsor obtained from the power map on $G_m$.

**The bundle and its sections, scans 14–15.** The NB passes from the group to a locally free $E$ with a map $E^{(p)}\simeq E$. It writes $M$ as a kernel and interprets a map from $M$ to the additive group in terms of a section of the dual. The next page iterates the operation on a section, comparing it with Frobenius on the additive group.

**The exterior product and its open set, scans 15–16.** Wedging a section with its first $n-1$ iterates gives $\Delta^E$. The sheet assigns it degree $1+p+\cdots+p^{n-1}$ and takes the inverse image of the zero section. On the complementary open set the iterates form a basis, so the next iterate supplies the relation defining the $A_i^E$. Scan 16 says that these coefficients extend across the divisor.

**The matrix and the table, scans 16–17.** The cyclic basis gives the successive images $e_1,\ldots,e_{n-1}$ and expresses the last image in terms of the $a_i$. Scan 17 draws the matrix and sets out two columns. The left has an invertible $a_0$, finite étale subgroups, and an isomorphism on the module. The right has a coefficient tuple without that condition, finite locally free subgroups, and a map on the module that need not be an isomorphism. A question mark qualifies the correspondence on the right.

**Pinching two points, scan 19.** The left diagram obtains $X$ from $X'$ by « pinçant » $a$ and $b$. The calculation puts equal fibres of $H'$ beside unequal fibres of a subgroup $N'$, while the indicated completions agree. Two rows of additive groups, the maps labelled by $\alpha'$ and $\beta'$, and a homothety enter the closing descent sketch.

**Pencil and ink calculations, scan 20.** The pencil relates additive-polynomial coefficients to a list of powers and to basis vectors. A darker list gives their successive images and records $a_n\ne0$. In the sideways block the quotients involving $H_0,G_0,K_0$ and the two Hom expressions lead into conditions on $V,F,p$. At the foot, coefficients $\lambda_{ij}$ describe a map on a locally free module.

**Alternating coefficients and a determinant, scan 21.** Three root equations for $f,g,h$ share coefficients. Beside the determinant of powers of the $X_i$ is the sum $1+p+\cdots+p^{n-1}$. Two products of linear forms and a rank-two calculation in $x,y$ follow. The final quotient calculation breaks off.

**Eliminating two coefficients, scan 22.** The two roots $\alpha,\beta$ give equations for $a,b$. The left column eliminates a coefficient, and the right uses powers of $\alpha-\beta$. Below them the sheet rearranges the terms, tabulates coefficient values, and labels three expressions $r,s,t$. A map of additive groups and the quotient by $H$ appear immediately beneath it.

**Values and exceptional fibres, scan 23.** The upright block prescribes complementary zero and unit values for $f,g$ at $a,b$. It writes the two fibres in opposite factor orders. The turned block considers a product $H_1\times H_2\times K$, singles out the exceptional fibres, and replaces the nonzero values by $\alpha,\beta$. The choices $h(a)=\alpha$, $h(b)=\beta$ accompany inclusions, a quotient diagram and two formulas in $\lambda$.
