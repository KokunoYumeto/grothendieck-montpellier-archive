## Across the leaves

Scans 17 and 22 both have a 2 to the left and below G. Scans 19–22 repeat the upright E of a level set, the small subscript α or star, the check over a function, and the two forms of abbreviated « même ». A single upright bar either side of f,g on scan 19 differs from the paired norm bars on scans 20–22. In scans 24–30 the italic stemmed R alternates with a looped R in lists of game data. The loop alone does not establish a different relation. Compare the terminal sets on scans 24–26 and 30–31: G has a short returning foot in the clearer instances, while the shallow capital in the reminder on scan 26 remains queried. In scans 28–29 the broad E for a set and the two-lobed ε for its minimum have different sizes. On 31–32 a horizontal bar and a two-stroke hat are distinct. A C with no detached bar is common, so a thick upper edge on C by itself does not decide a bar. Quotation marks on scan 24 are paired high strokes around configurations, positions, joueurs and terminées.
