# Notes on the sheets

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **Scan 20, after the definition of a prétassé pair.** “Il n'est pas clair que la relation soit symétrique” is followed by a cancellation and “mais OK si G commutatif”. This is the explicit expression of doubt. Scan 21 returns to symmetry under a hypothesis on the two functions.
- **Scan 22, below the two expansions.** The double-underlined “Supposons” introduces an ordering hypothesis on the union of the level sets. The next line introduces an alternative with “ou seul” and a raised t. The sheet leaves these as hypotheses.

## Title sheets, dates and page counts

- **Scan 18** — reused sheet: personal list. The page has the archive foliation 17. No added text in the author's hand is discernible on this face.
- **Scan 24** opens with the underlined title “Jeux de position”. Definitions a)–e) follow on the same sheet.
- **Scans 17–22 and 24–32** bear the archive foliation 16–21 and 23–31 respectively. The numerals 1 and 2 on scan 19, 3 on scan 20 and 4 on scan 21 number mathematical sections. The cancelled 4 at the head of scan 21 is recorded separately. No manuscript date, statement of page count or independent title leaf appears in these scans.
