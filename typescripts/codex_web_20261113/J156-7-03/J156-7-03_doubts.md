## Legibility

The separate open circles, most primes, and the shapes and directions of the comparison diagrams can be distinguished across the eight sheets. The lowest-confidence readings are in the slashed ML 2 and ML 3 on 26, the heavily crossed openings on 29 and 32, and the superposed letters attached to Inf on 33. The sideways notes are generally clearer when turned. Low words crossed along their own baseline can retain only their initial loop and terminal stroke. The doubt table gives those positions individually.
