## Legibility

The larger axiom labels, the directions of most arrows, and the numbered formulas are the clearest material. The open C/G, the raised ring, the tiny family character under the empty set, and the unusually small arguments of omb or Omb required comparison at more than one place. The narrowest readings are the crossed qualification in the left margin of 019, the parenthesis near the foot of 020, the cancellations in the large diagram on 023, the superposed numerals on 024, and the two intersecting directions of writing and clipped right edge on 025. A proposed word in a cancellation can be much less secure than either of its neighbours. The marks in the transcription identify those individual readings.
