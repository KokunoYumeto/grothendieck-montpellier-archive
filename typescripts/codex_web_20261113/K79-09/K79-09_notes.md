# Notes on the manuscript, cote 79, scans 129–144

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

| Scan | Author's words | What is left open |
|---|---|---|
| 131, beside (6) | «laisser […] tomber le […] finalement !» | He considers dropping a numbered condition. The words before «tomber» and the number itself are doubtful. |
| 132, upper-left note | «Il faudrait réexpliciter l'opération “astucieuse” de Z sur Π» and «il vaudrait donc mieux ne pas noter» | He asks for the action to be spelled out again and for notation distinguishing it from transport of structure. |
| 133, beside (18) | «on pourrait élargir la définition au cas des structures ne satisfaisant pas (To 4)» | He proposes an extension beyond the centrality axiom. |
| 134, NB | «cette axiomatique n'est pas encore réduite vraiment à l'os» | He considers moving the earlier axioms into the setting of graphs. |
| 135, after (20) | «on se propose d'expliciter les conditions sur F» | The recovery of a structure from the aggregate face family introduces a programme of conditions. |
| 136–137, before (26) | «Il faut en arriver enfin, progressivement, à des conditions plus strictes encore» and «Je voudrais en arriver à définir un isomorphisme» | He aims to reach the module examples and exhibit the group of units modulo sign. |
| 141–142, after (36) | «pour tout» ν and «une propriété remarquable et très forte, qui mérite d'être examinée pour son propre mérite» | Whether each transformed edge set supports a triangulation merits investigation. |
| 142, last paragraph | «Mais l'icosaèdre gauche ?» and «on ne s'attendra pas à ce que […] soit surjectif» | He distinguishes the availability of the operation from his expectation about the group map. |
| 143, middle | «est-il vrai que pour tout 2-polyèdre […] triangulé […]» and «Même question pour n = 8», with n = 10 added | He asks whether the transformed graph again comes from such a polyhedron. The author’s deletion before «triangulé» matters to the scope. |
| 144, note to (39) and following paragraph | «Il vaut mieux séparer en deux axiomes distincts»; «il semble que dans la plupart des arguments»; «on doit pouvoir traduire notre axiomatique» | He considers separating regularity from orientability, weakening what is needed to a transitivity condition, and expressing the axioms in group terms. The set at the end of the marginal condition is doubtful. |
