## Doubt ledger

The last column distinguishes comparison with related passages from what the strokes themselves establish. A dash in the alternatives column means that only one reading could be proposed. In the context snippets, ‹…› marks the location of the entry.

## Legibility

The object names, the large arrows and the changes of paragraph are generally clear. The weak spots are the numeral and word endings, the heads of short horizontal links and the overwritten components on scan 60. The local condition and two intersecting marginal notes on scan 64 are the least secure extended passage. The last reference in the slanted note on 68 and the last parenthesis at its right edge are also difficult. Those readings and their alternatives remain visible in the fragment.

The cancelled label before Cof₀ on scan 52 is the one place where no word could be recovered. Labels and subscripts in formulas carry proposed readings even where an overwritten loop prevents a firm choice.
