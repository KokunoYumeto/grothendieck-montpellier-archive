# K83-09 · Mathematical notes

## What the pages do

**Powers and traces, scan 3.** The sheet computes successive powers of $u$, identifies $u^4=u'$ and $u^5=\alpha\,1$, and sets $u$ beside elements of orders three and five. A second group of calculations compares $\operatorname{Tr}(uv)$ with $\operatorname{Tr}(uv')$. Replacing $v$ by $v+1$ adds $\operatorname{Tr}u=1$.

**Two-by-two endomorphisms, scan 4.** An inverse matrix and the first row of a matrix product precede the identities $uu'=u'u=\det u$ and $u+u'=\operatorname{Tr}u.\mathrm{id}$. The sheet links the characteristic equation to the pairing $u(x)\wedge y=x\wedge u'(y)$. Both a cancelled formulation and the later boxed formulation survive.

**Trace-one table, scan 6.** For $\det u=0,1,\alpha,\alpha'$ the four rows give counts $20,20,12,12$. The sheet displays a companion matrix, lists four possible trace values and uses $\lambda u+\mu$ to calculate a trace and a determinant. A small diagram places $1,u,v,uv$ at four corners. The map $u\mapsto u'$ is labelled “antipodisme”.

**The two orbits, scan 8.** The text removes $u,u'$ from $S$, leaving ten elements. It identifies an order-five cyclic quotient of $k[u]^*$, discusses the fixed points and concludes that the other elements form two orbits of five. The trace can consequently take at most two values there, one on each orbit. The upper and lower parts of the sheet phrase the trace question differently.

**Choice of a conjugate, scan 10.** Matrices for $u,u'$ and $u^{-1}$ lead to conjugation by a diagonal matrix involving $\lambda$. A copy labelled $v_0$ carries $\lambda\ne1$. The lower working expands products involving $v$, $u$ and the inverse and ends with an expression in $\sigma$.

**The two determinant conditions, scan 12.** Starting from $v=\lambda u+\mu$, the trace condition gives $\lambda=1$. The determinant conditions lead respectively to $\mu^2+\mu=0$ and $\mu^2+\mu+1=0$. Later the boxed trace of $v_0\,u(v_0)$ is $\alpha$. The sheet extends the value to five conjugates and distinguishes an edge, a coedge and two exceptional cases.

**Four directions, scan 14.** Vectors $e_1,\dots,e_4$ are drawn in a plane, with $e_3=(1,1)$ and $e_4=(1,\lambda)$ in column form. Prescribed images of $e_3,e_4$ yield equations in $a,b,c,d$. The sheet gives a boxed matrix for $v$, computes its determinant and adds the two matrix products $uv$ and $vu$. The final result is the scalar matrix written $2\zeta a.1$, using the preferred reading of the Greek letter.

**Pairs, a proposition and triangles, scan 16.** A table compares $\det(u-v)$ and $\operatorname{Tr}(uv)$ in four cases. The proposition assigns the traces of $v\,u^i(v)$ to three residue classes up to sign modulo five. Matrices for conjugates, three labelled drawings and counts of pairs and triples accompany the statement. The final line groups the determinant of $u+v+w$ into three single determinants and three pair terms for a triangle.

**The reused sheets, scans 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13 and 15.** The L folios retain parts of a letter about *Récoltes et Semailles*, mathematical work, former pupils and colleagues. The other typescript leaves discuss images, dreams, bodily expression and the relation of these forms of communication to words. Their folio numbers explain their textual order better than the order of the scans.

## Remarks of the model

**M1 · Scan 6, determinant counts. High confidence in the computation.** I enumerated the $4^4$ two-by-two matrices over the field specified by $\alpha^2+\alpha+1=0$. Filtering by trace one gives 64 matrices. Their determinant frequencies, in the order $0,1,\alpha,\alpha'$, are exactly $20,20,12,12$.

**M2 · Scans 8, 10 and 16, range of the trace.** For the $u$ displayed on scan 10 and the twelve matrices of trace one and determinant $\alpha$, the frequencies of $\operatorname{Tr}(uv)=0,1,\alpha,\alpha'$ are $1,1,5,5$. The unique zero is at $v=u'$ and the unique one at $v=u$. Confidence is high for those finite computations and low for attributing an error to the universal $\{0,1\}$ line on scan 8. That attribution would require identifying its $S$ with the later $S_\alpha$ and deciding the status of a line of working.

**M3 · Scan 16, two uses of the label $v_0$.** The displayed $u$ of scan 10 times the displayed $v_0$ matrix of scan 16 has diagonal $1,\alpha'$, hence trace $\alpha$. A line above the matrix gives $\operatorname{Tr}(uv_0)=\alpha'$. Confidence is high in the matrix multiplication and low in any claim of a slip. The earlier calculation involves a choice of $\lambda$, and the pages do not say whether the same choice is in force.

**M4 · Scan 16, counts.** The two integer calculations give $\binom{12}{2}=66=6+30+30$ and $\binom{12}{3}=220=20+20+60+60+6\cdot10$. Confidence is high in the arithmetic. The second result on the manuscript has an overwritten leading digit, so the transcription retains both 220 and 320. The arithmetic cannot distinguish the earlier ink.

**Reading questions arising in the notes.** On scan 5, line 2, the frozen “ma” in “que ma recueillir mon témoignage” could be an overtyped “va”. On scan 10, lower right, the frozen equals sign in $\lambda+\lambda^{-1}=1$ has a small peaked stroke above it that might be a question mark. On scan 16, fine pencil strokes around the left and lower sides of the middle polygon sometimes approach a vertex without meeting it. The TikZ perimeter joins those points. The widths of the gaps are uncertain. All three observations concern the reading or reproduction of the marks.
