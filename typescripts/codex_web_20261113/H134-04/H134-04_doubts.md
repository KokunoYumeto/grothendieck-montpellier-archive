## Legibility

The large formulas and the disposition of the diagrams are generally clearer than the compressed handwriting. In the opening of 162 the word starts outside the supplied leaves. I have least confidence in the tiny ends of the horizontal strokes, the former digit in the shift on 164, the under-writing of *formalisme* on 165, and the abbreviation and the middle of *mentionnée* in the rotated note on 166. On 167 the article before *anneau* and the crossed adjective are weak. On 169 the phrase before *de zinc*, the words crossed beneath *ce*, the ticks around *Objets*, and the abbreviated ending at the lower-left vertex are especially uncertain. There are candidate readings at each of these places. The English attachment cannot determine the French pen strokes.
