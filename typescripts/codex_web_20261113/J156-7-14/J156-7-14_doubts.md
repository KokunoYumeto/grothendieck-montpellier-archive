# Legibility and placement limits

The main large formulas are relatively legible, but the hand reduces many French words to short waves and isolated strokes. The least secure parts are the slanted notes and dense cancelled draft on 110, the long marginal note and crossed lower block on 112, and the compressed final clause of 109. The precise ordering of the interlaced comparison remains uncertain throughout. The differing varphi/Phi glyph on 113 and the apparent étalier on 114 are intentionally not regularized.

Every whole image and each of its three thirds was personally viewed, and all seven pages were independently reread before reopening their transcription files. Rotated/enlarged crops were used for the margins and cancellations; no OCR, external transcription, web source, or parallel worker was used. At 4 on 113 is placed before “+ les axiomes” because the long curved arrow terminates there; a source-position comment records that the block physically stands at the top. This relocation is an interpretation of the visible leader, not duplicated text. See the audit for the actual checks and changes. The result is a complete attempted diplomatic transcription with explicit doubts, not a claim of certainty in the difficult passages.
