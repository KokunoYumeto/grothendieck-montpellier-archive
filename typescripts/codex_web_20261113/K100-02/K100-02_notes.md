# Notes on the sheets

## Steps the pages mark as unverified

| Scan | Words on the sheet | What remains open there |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | “montrer que la donnée de descente envisagée est effective”; marginal “?!”; “Supposons qu’on ait prouvé que” | Effectiveness of the datum and membership of the local family in the essential image. The slanting marginal lemma gives a proposed test. Several words of the test remain doubtful. |
| 16 | “the example shattered over-optimistic conjectures that the author had entertained!”; “One checks that” | The typescript does not state those earlier conjectures. It leaves the checks on the example to the reader. |
| 17 | “Il reste à prouver la pleine fidélité précédente (on la laisse au lecteur)”; “Douteux, sans doute faux”; “[ ] inutile?” | The first remark assigns a proof to the reader. The second is next to the lemma asserting that a local system comes from an abelian scheme. The last questions the bracketed restriction. |
| 18 | “18.10.8?”; “Est-il encore possible de changer le texte en conséquence ?”; “une référence pour le fait (effectivement vrai)” | Questions about a proof reference, an editorial revision and a reference for the filtered-limit assertion. The writer calls the latter fact true. The long pen stroke crosses all three entries. |
| 19 | “L’unicité sera garantie dès que”; last word “Alors” | A sufficient condition is discussed and a reduction is begun. Its conclusion falls beyond the sheet. |
| 21 | “Question: quand cela provient-il d’un hom”; interlinear “(si S noeth)” | The algebraicity question is explicit. Part a) has an added hypothesis. Parts a), b) and the final corollary have no proof here. |
| 22 | “I only know one fact in this direction”; “I omit the proof.” | The writer states a field case of the noetherianity discussion and expressly omits its proof. |
| 23 | “[ ] inutile si X loc de t.f.?”; “à montrer que g′ se factorise par S” | The margin asks whether the bracketed hypothesis can be dropped. The proof is organized around this factorization. |
| 25 | “Il faut prouver que X est formellement net.” | This is the remaining point in the argument for groups that are p-divisible. |
| 27 | “On veut prouver que f est un morphisme de descente effective”; “Mais il faut savoir que”; “on ne peut directement appliquer le … cor. 2, car S₂ n’est plus loc. noeth !” | The problem is effectiveness. Part b) identifies a missing justification after base change and proposes a kernel argument. The lower case analysis and formal construction lie inside a crossed block. |
