Concerning restrictive conditions on the action of $G$, a notion intermediate between the closedness of one orbit and the properness of the whole action is sometimes convenient:

\uline{lemma} \uline{0.3}: Let $S=\operatorname{Spec}(k)$, $k$ an algebraically closed field, and assume $X$ and $G$ are algebraic pre-schemes over $k$. Let $\handwritten{\sigma}$ be an action of $G$ on $X$, and let $x\in X_k$. Then $\handwritten{\psi}_x$ is proper if and only if $O(x)$ is closed in $X$ and $S(x)$ is proper over $k$.

\uline{Proof}: If $\handwritten{\psi}_x$ is proper, then its image $O(x)$ must be closed, and its fibre over $x$ - which is $S(x)$ - must be proper over $k$.
