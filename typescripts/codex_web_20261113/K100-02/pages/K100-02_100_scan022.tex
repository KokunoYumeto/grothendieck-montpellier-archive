(3) Suppose $(Y,\phi)$ is a universal categorical quotient. Applying this assumption to the base extensions given by the inclusion of open subsets $\handwritten{\unsure{\mathcal U|U}}$ in $Y$, one deduces condition (iv) of definition 0.6. Moreover, applying it to the inclusion of a single point $y$ in $Y$, and noting that the categorical quotient of an empty scheme by any group is an empty scheme, one deduces that $\phi$ is surjective. Therefore, for $(Y,\phi)$ to be a universal geometric quotient, it is necessary and sufficient that (1) $\phi$ is universally submersive, and (2) the image of $\unsure{\Psi|\psi}$ is $X\times_Y X$.
