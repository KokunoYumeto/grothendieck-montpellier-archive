## Legibility

The chapter IX type is generally crisp. Several small circles, handwritten mathematical fills and overwritten typewriter keys are harder to separate than the prose. On the handwritten pages, the least secure passages are the interleaved multiplication and struck words on 206, the retouched diagram objects on 208, the pale transitions on 212, and the crossed formulations on 219. The two systems of page numbers are visibly separate. A numbered archive label and a repeated pencilled number at the bottom of a typescript are recorded in comments.

The manuscript hand follows the recurring forms on the mathematical notes. The inventory labels distinct penned additions on the Stoia typescript “other hand”, without assigning them to its named author. Unattributed fills on the chapter IX typescript and the marginal hands on 215 remain unidentified.
