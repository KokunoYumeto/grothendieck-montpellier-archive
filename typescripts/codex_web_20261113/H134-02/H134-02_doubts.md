## Legibility

The personal opening and most of the continuous text can be followed across the overlapping crops. Short words, accent directions and final minims often reduce to one stroke. The riskiest readings are the two cancellations on 146, the overwritten words on 147–148, the lost opening of the sideways note on 150 and both lower page edges on 152–153. The small letters of the additions are generally clearer than the words underneath them. Right-pointing strokes vary from a shaft with a nick to a complete arrowhead. No French wording has been recovered from the English for the two clipped passages.
