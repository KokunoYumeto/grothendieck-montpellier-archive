## Legibility

The main hand is continuous across the fifteen sheets. The large diagrams and repeated letters give strong local comparisons. Short articles, word endings that trail into a line, the hatching in the small sketch on 375, and the older arguments beneath the limits on 382 are less secure. The long diagonal note on 374 crosses several body lines and contains the weakest continuous passage. The marks of uncertainty give readings of the available ink, including overwritten ink. Several tentative readings could change on inspection of the physical sheets.
