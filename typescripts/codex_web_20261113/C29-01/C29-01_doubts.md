## Legibility

The main typescript is generally readable. Confidence is lowest in the layers of typed cancellation, additions between lines, clipped line endings and overprinted letters. These have been read from enlarged image crops and marked in place. Large spaces in formulas are visibly blank on the supplied scans; the transcription preserves those spaces without supplying missing relation signs. The capital and font readings are local visual readings, recorded above. The least secure passages are the overlapping cancellations on scans 19–20 and the clipped right edges on scans 13, 15 and 17. Faint offset background impressions, including the repeated opening of scan 14 above scan 13’s heading, are documented in the audit; they were not promoted into the foreground text.
