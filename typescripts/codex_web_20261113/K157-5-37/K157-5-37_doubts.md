## Legibility

The folios 482–491 usually let the reader distinguish an underlined category name, a Greek index and an inequality. Small words, article endings, and numerals crossed by descenders are less secure. The additions above the line on 573–574 and 577 run into the surrounding text. The left margin on 579 becomes clearer on rotation.

The lower title line on 584 is in light pencil. On 585 the large crosses, the rewritten numbers and the interline above the personal name make the outline especially difficult. The outer diagonal on 586 obscures several operators. On 587 the two lists are legible as lists, but a low index, the heavily retraced capitals and the conjecture heading are uncertain. A proposed reading remains in the text even for each of the heavily cancelled words.
