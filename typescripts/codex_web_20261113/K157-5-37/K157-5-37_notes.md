# Notes on the sheets

## Steps the manuscript marks as unverified

- **575–576, the transfinite sequence.** “Je n’ai pas été capable de construire une suite … ni de prouver qu’une telle suite n’existe pas” continues with the case of a sequence of fields “ayant les propriétés dites”. The star on “capable” leads to “Mais si finalement, cf 4.16.7.10 plus bas”. The example attached to that later corollary is on 579.
- **576, stability.** “Je n’ai pu tirer au clair la question si une sous-catégorie L stable d’une catégorie M accessible est tjrs accessible dans M.” He considers both possibilities and the case of a closed subcategory. The side note reads “C’est faux cf plus bas”. On 578 he introduces the new proposition with “J’ai finalement tiré au clair la question”.
- **585, first outline.** The cancelled group on the letterhead has “le pb d’existence” beside the two operations on u and a question mark. The adjacent balloon reads “Perplexités !”. This is a question in a cancelled plan.
- **586, cancelled draft.** After the boxed map comes “il faudrait que ce soit contenu dans” the indicated product, followed by “est-ce raisonnable ?”. The whole page is crossed.
- **587, outlines.** “Question pas clarifiée (question d’univers)” follows the heading on open or closed subcategories. Item 6 says “Questions ouvertes.” In the second outline “Conjectures sur … des dites parties” is a heading; the intervening word is doubtful. These headings supply no formulation of an individual conjecture.
