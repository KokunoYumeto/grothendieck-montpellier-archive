## Legibility

The five typescript sheets are readable almost throughout. Their chief traps are the tiny coordinate subscripts, overstrikes, and the boundary between type and pen. The last sheet has to be turned through 180 degrees. The most difficult manuscript is 121, especially the oblique encircled objection and the words before replacements. Scans 125, 135 and 137 also have fast, nearly horizontal joins. The loops through the corollary on 132 obscure text in two layers. On 131 the outer edge clips the last character of the first criterion. The number of visible doubt marks measures recorded uncertainty, not the probability that an unmarked reading is correct.
