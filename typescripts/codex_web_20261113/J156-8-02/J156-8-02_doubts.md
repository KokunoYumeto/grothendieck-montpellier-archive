# Legibility and limits

The main proposition statements, most formulas, and ordinary open relation signs are reasonably legible. Reduced cursive, cramped superscripts and corrections are less secure. The least trustworthy readings are the cancelled opening and lower argument of scan 10; the short cross-reference, cancelled symbols and lower arrow endpoint on scan 13; the wrapped/oblique annotations on scan 12; and the two-line cancelled fragment on scan 17. The formal alphabet of the crossed figure-collection glyph cannot be established securely from these images. Doubt marks are substantive and should not be read as certified alternatives to an otherwise verified text.

Every supplied whole-page image and third was viewed; the second audit reread all thirds. Additional enlarged or rotated crops were used where needed. No OCR, recognition library, PDF text layer, published transcription, or web source was used. No unreadable mathematical sign was replaced by a word-count placeholder. No `\illegible` mark proved preferable to a tentative reading; this does not make the low-confidence cancelled passages reliable. The final colon on scan 17 is the visible end of the supplied material, not an omitted completion.
