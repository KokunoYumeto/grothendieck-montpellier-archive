# Legibility

Scan 89 is clear in its main typescript. The page-number suffix and a few overtyped letters are substantially less secure than the surrounding text. Their alternatives are explicitly retained.

Scan 90: the live index entries are clear. The first cancelled reference is the least legible element. The X-overtyped words after Valeur can be read as d’une section. The top has two dashes with a blank between them, not a recoverable page number.

Scan 91: typed text is clear; the pale boxed pencil addition is uncertain. Its box and curved arrow are reproduced in TikZ, anchored alongside 10.12, with the pointer ending after 10.15. Attribution of that pencil hand remains unknown.

Scan 92: seven cancellation/overtyping sites are retained. The masked word before pleine is physically unreadable and is the first explicit illegibility mark. The s of S-sections is crossed by a diagonal stroke whose authorship cannot be determined.

Scan 93: the typescript is unevenly inked, with hand-filled delimiters and relation signs. The right edge is cropped. Small indices, the initial paired delimiters, and overtyped characters are less secure than the prose. The writer is not named on this sheet itself; the salutation is to Dieudonné.

Scan 94: most prose is clear. Missing mathematical glyphs in otherwise blank positions have not been reconstructed. The bottom paragraph is preserved in full despite the diagonal cancellation strokes; it continues beyond the supplied sheet, ending here at mot.

Scan 96: the typed prose is generally clear. Pencil underlining and the marginal reference are much fainter than the hand-filled arrows. The reference and densely X-overstruck short words are least secure. A q overwritten by a in pas is retained as a deletion; the C in Cela is crossed out without an independently visible replacement C.

Scan 97: most type is clear, but many mathematical relation positions are genuinely blank. Those spaces are preserved; they are not illegibility marks. The faint pencil tildes and the tight parenthesis/subscript placement in the last sequence required enlargement. The g/h and other overprint layer orders remain less secure than the visible letters themselves.

Scan 98: most prose is clear, with conspicuous typing slips and local overprints. The symbols before après, the E/S superposition, the numéro abbreviation, and several overtyped letters are the least secure elements. The cancelled premature quotation mark is preserved.

Across all nine pages, the most reliable material is the unobscured live typescript. Confidence is lower for layered overprints, edge-clipped print, pale pencil strokes, and the interpretation of the two diagonal cancellation strokes on scan 94. An uncertainty mark does not mean omitted text. One short word on scan 92 remains physically unreadable. No OCR, external edition, or mathematical completion was used.
