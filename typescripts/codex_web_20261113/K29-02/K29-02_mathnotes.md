# K29-02 · Mathematical notes

## What the pages do

**Scan 188 · Cancelled homotopy calculation.** The sheet juxtaposes a formula for barred \(W_n(G)\) and a projection with a polynomial \(\varphi_n\), two integral operators \(U_j,T_j\), and an operator on forms. The hatted differential is inside a wedge product. The bottom line cancels a term and writes \(P d'' f=f-U_1\cdots U_n f\). The surrounding draft is crossed out.

**Scans 189, 191 · A datum of ramification.** The cover pairs ramification data with multigaloisian categories. The numbered text introduces a category fibred over \(Et(S)\) and its geometric realization \(r\). R1 treats finite sums and quotients. R2 compares direct summands with open-and-closed parts of the realization. R3 concerns effective descent for covering subobjects. R4 asks that the realizations of the pieces of fixed rank cover the target.

**Scans 191–192 · Covering families and the associated toposes.** A family covers when its realizations are jointly surjective. The notes discuss the axioms separately. Two diagrams accompany the reduction to monomorphisms and the local test for covering. The corollary passes to associated toposes and identifies the original objects with locally constant finite objects. A crossed paragraph asks again about the covering of \(Y\) by its rank pieces.

**Scan 193 · Tests for emptiness and connectedness.** Prop 2 gives two equivalences comparing \(X\) with \(r(X)\). A marginal note names R1 and R2. The next definition concerns subordination to a fixed object \(P\), tested on the pieces of \(P\) where the rank is fixed. The text describes the resulting full subcategory and then the fibred subcategory \(\mathscr R^P\) defined by local subordination.

**Scan 194 · Cancelled faithfulness criterion.** Under R1, R3 and R4 the statement equates R2 with faithfulness of \(r\). The attempted argument takes two subobjects with the same realization, intersects them, and reduces to an inclusion and its complementary summand. The final equation sets the realization of the complement equal to the empty object. The statement and argument share the cancellation.

**Scans 193, 195 · Galois objects.** For \(P\) Galois with group \(G\), the comparison uses objects over \(P\) that are constant by pieces with a compatible \(G\)-action. Prop 3 gives the realization over \(r(P)\). Th. 1 uses descent to pass to objects locally subordinate to \(P\) and to étale coverings of \(Z=r(P)\), with a covering condition on the schemes \(S'\) where the pulled-back object is piecewise constant.

**Scans 196–197 · The converse construction and a limitation.** Prop 4 starts from an admissible finite group action on \(Z\) over \(S\). The quotient supplies the realization. After a bounded-rank hypothesis the notes describe a filtered union of subcategories and an inverse system of objects with operators. In the connected case they discuss connected \(P_i\), surjective transition homomorphisms and quotients by subgroups. Prop 5 ends in dots. The author then explains why global sections need not generate the whole fibred category.

**Scans 197–198 · Maps of ramification data.** A homomorphism includes a cartesian functor and an isomorphism of the two realizations. These data form a fibred 2-category as the base varies. Prop 6 evaluates a homomorphism on a distinguished Galois object. The next statements concern full faithfulness and a description by principal bundles with compatible group action. The sideways qualification beside the last corollary reads “\(P'\) constant par morceaux loc. (ét) sur \(S\)”.

**Scans 199–200 · Gluing and a gerbe construction.** Pairs \((Z_i,G_i)\) over a cover have subordination data on double overlaps, transitivity isomorphisms on triples, and a compatibility condition on quadruples. A second construction starts from a category fibred in groupoids and its associated gerbe. The notes attach to its representation in \(\mathrm{Fl}_S\) a category of liftings and describe \(r_S\) by a direct limit.

**Scan 200 · Kummer data from sections.** Ex 1 specifies sections \(a_i\) and integers \(n_i\), with a qualification on residue characteristics and finitely many entries different from 1. It describes a groupoid with a single object and the action on \(Z_{\underline n}^{\underline a}\). Ex 1 bis varies the indices while fixing the sections.

**Scans 200–201 · Kummer data from divisors.** Ex 2 replaces the sections by a family of divisors. Objects are local equations. The next display specifies the arrows between two tuples of equations. The associated abelian gerbe and the obstruction to a section are described using the classes of the divisors. Ex 2 bis again varies the indices. The author wants a condition on the characteristics along each divisor. The sentence returning to Ex 2 ends at the bottom of scan 201.

**Scan 202 · Generators.** A family \(X_i/S_i\) generates if the indicated schemes on which \(X\) becomes subordinate give a covering. An interlinear addition attaches a further covering condition. The author considers how local Galois generators could reconstruct the datum, then uses a cartesian functor \(\varphi:C\to\mathscr R\) whose image generates it. After setting \(s=r\varphi\), the sheet ends during the next assertion.
