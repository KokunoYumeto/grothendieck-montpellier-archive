## Legibility

The keyed English prose generally has distinct letter endings. Detached points, small upper indices and handwritten signs in spaces left by the typist are less certain. The handwritten French is uneven. The exchange heading on 98, the additions and cancellations on 100 and 102, the oblique qualifications and overwritten symbols on 104 and 106, and the transition into the operating conditions on 108–110 are the least secure. A confidently identified final letter does not establish the form under a cancellation. Bounds on complexes and signs beside functor initials have been checked at their separate occurrences. Crop names refer to working enlargements made from the supplied facsimiles.
