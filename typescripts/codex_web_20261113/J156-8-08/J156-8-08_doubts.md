## Legibility and limits

The large spacing helps separate formulas, but many French words have internal letters reduced to waves. Insertions and deletions are substantially less secure than the main text. The least trusted passages are the amended paragraph on 58, the deleted phrase and cramped final addition on 59, the opening parenthesis on 61, the compressed terminology/insertion on 63, and the cancelled block and bottom note on 65. The precise conventional identity of the crossed relation and several alphabet choices also remain unresolved.

All eight pages and their supplied thirds were directly viewed. No OCR, text layer, external transcription, web source, or parallel reader was used. All pages were transcribed and reread; doubtful content is included in place. There are no illegible-word placeholders, which does not mean every reading is secure: tentative recoveries are explicitly marked with `\unsure`. LaTeX was checked for delimiter/environment balance, not compiled or visually typeset. Historical revisions and actual UTC audit times are recorded in the audit file.
