The TikZ sketches preserve outlines, labels and crossings approximately. On 75 the loops around the two expressions are recorded in a source comment. Single source newlines within a prose paragraph are TeX spaces. They allow the reading register to identify a short passage without forcing a manuscript line break in the typeset text.
