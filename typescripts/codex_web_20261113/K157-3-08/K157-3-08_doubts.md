## Legibility

The main statements and the large diagrams generally separate well. The least secure passages are the run after “devrait” on scan 118, the crossed evaluation on scan 119, the second functor in scan 120, and the corrected words at the outer edge of Proposition 5 on scan 126. The crossed cube on scan 127 is fainter than its replacement. Short arrowheads, small primes, final plural strokes and the overwritten numerals 7 and 8 are recurrent limits. In several places a plausible reading can be given under a cancellation even though the individual letters cannot all be separated. Those readings carry doubt marks.
