# K11-02 · Notes on the sheets

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

| Scan | Words on the sheet | What the qualification applies to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 016 | “according to a conjecture of Tits” | Finding the parabolic subgroup “most” responsible for failure of semi-stability by taking the “center” of the convex set. |
| 017 | “Cas $u$ central ?!” and the boxed “mod. signe!” | The hypothesis on the correspondence in the transposition calculation, and the sign in its last equality. Earlier, the slanted addition by “égal à” could also read “à vérifier”. That reading remains uncertain. |
| 019 | Cancelled “Il faudrait être … soigneux pour les signes”, followed later by the live “Il faudrait alors remplacer les monoïdes par des ‘monoïdes jaugés’” | The former is an abandoned caution about signs. The latter proposes how to handle the two projection formulas when signs intervene. |
| 021 | “il faudrait donner un énoncé d’équivalence plus général” | A slanted note, linked to the circled occurrences of *tout*, asks for a more general criterion. In condition c), “[il suffit de prendre les …]” leaves the proposed restriction unfinished. |
| 023 | “du moins si on admet la résolution des singularités” | An explicit assumption in the example involving smooth projective schemes. |
| 024 | “If $G$ is actually semi-simple, it can be shown that” | The assertion that the rational points lie on a spherical simplex is left without its argument here. |
| 025 | “reste à prouver qu’ils sont stables par multiplication. OK.” | The last line singles out multiplication as the outstanding closure operation and then adds “OK.” No intervening argument is written. |
