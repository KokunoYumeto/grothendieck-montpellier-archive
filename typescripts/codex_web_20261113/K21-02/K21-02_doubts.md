## Legibility and limits

The numbered headings, directions of the larger arrows, and differences between low and high indices are generally clearer than the small connective words. At several points the last letters of a French word reduce to a horizontal stroke. The broad cancellation on scan 18 and the crossed, slanted note on scan 20 contain the two passages for which there is no defensible word reading. The dark superpositions on scans 19 and 25, the marginal insertions on 22 and 24 and the right edge of the formula on 18 deserve particular caution. The small stroke with a dot in the left margin on 18 is reproduced as a drawing because the ink shows its shape more clearly than its purpose.
