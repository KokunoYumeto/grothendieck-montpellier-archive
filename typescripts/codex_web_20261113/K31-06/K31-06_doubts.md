## Legibility

The ENS letterheads and most of the rounded handwriting on 35–39 can be followed line by line. The short abandoned phrases on these sheets are substantially less secure. The attributions in the inventory distinguish the ENS hand and the much flatter hand on 42–51; the name on 35 is evidence of an addressee or attribution, not a signature establishing authorship. The cover pencil, the long oblique notes on 42–44 and 48, the crossed draft on 45, and the overwritten coefficients on 51 are the least secure.
