# What the sheets themselves mark

## Steps marked as unverified or provisional

| Scan | Words on the sheet | What remains open there |
|---|---|---|
| 002 | “?” beside the model chapters | Pencil queries accompany XIII, XV, XVII and possibly XIX. Their purpose is unspecified. |
| 003 | “existe-t-il … ? (pour les dérivateurs habituels.)” | Existence of the exceptional inverse image for the map to e. |
| 004 | “Notations provisoires” | The five names for successive adjoints are provisional. |
| 006 | “Il semble qu’elle n’ait pas servi en pratique”; “Il semblerait”; “Ça a l’air vrai du moins si … (et je l’ai vérifié pour …)” | The proposed common strings of adjoints and their general length. The only indices he names as verified are i=0 and i=1 in the case j=i+1. |
| 007 | “une cat. au topos qu’elle définit ?”; “en fait, nettement plus…”; “(pour simplifier)” | He questions an identification with a topos, leaves further stability requirements unspecified and adopts strictness as a simplifying assumption. |
| 009 | “du moins provisoirement”; “Ce sont sans doute les dér. à g. qui sont les plus importants.” | Provisional names for the adjoints and a tentative assessment of which variant matters most. |
| 010 | “Il faut de plus … l’image ess.”; “Je voudrais formuler des axiomes astucieux”; “On veut … le carré (1) page 2” | Characterizing the essential images and choosing axioms for two complementary immersions. The oblique note qualifies the biadjoints by pointedness. |
| 011–012 | “On cherche donc un carré … mais mieux que ça”; “se demander s’il ne provient pas ‘canoniquement’”; “On voudrait”; “Question analogue” | A functorial lift of an ordinary square and an intrinsic expression of its cartesian or bicartesian character. |
| 013 | “sauf qu’on est embarrassé”; “devraient être canoniquement isomorphes” | Comparing the constructions obtained by completing different partial diagrams. A proposed preference below is cancelled. |
| 016 | “il faut vérifier les axiomes”; “Ce n’est pas clair pour moi qu’alors …” | Whether the indicated categories of diagrams themselves arise from a model-category structure of the proposed kind. The existence of the two adjoints is attributed separately. |
| 017 | “avec commutativité (stricte ?)” and “Sans doute … une commutation aux … et (?) aux …” | Strictness for a morphism of derivators and the desired commutation with direct images, particularly the second operation. |

The question mark used as a functor superscript in many formulas is notation. It is distinct from a question raised in prose.
