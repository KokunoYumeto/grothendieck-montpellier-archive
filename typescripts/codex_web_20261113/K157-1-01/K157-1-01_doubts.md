## Legibility

The dark body writing generally preserves the order of the argument and the layout of its diagrams. The small signs change considerably in clarity. A hooked j is usually distinguishable from a short i, while a star, prime, or opposite sign can shrink to a nick. I trust the faint sideways pencil on scan 002, the compact interlinear writing on 004, the overwritten vertices on 005 and 011, the physically clipped last line of 013, and the two overlapping notes on 017 least. Scan 010 also required rotation to distinguish its notes from the crossing body lines. A reading in a cancellation records what is visible under the pen. It does not endorse the statement or identify a superseded symbol with certainty.
