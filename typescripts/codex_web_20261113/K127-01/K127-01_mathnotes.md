# Mathematical notes on cote 127, scans 2–13

## What the pages do

**Scans 2–3, problem 1.1.** The cover names two subjects, flatness of a schematic closure and the lemma of Hironaka. Given an open immersion $i:U\hookrightarrow X$ and a quotient $G_U$ of $F|U$ flat over $Y$, 1.1 asks for a flat quotient on $X$ prolonging it. The map $F\to i_*G_U$ singles out its image as the only possible answer. Existence now depends on properties of this particular image.

**Scan 3, Lemma 1.2.** Uniqueness leads to a locality assertion for the base $Y$ in the fpqc topology. The next lines distinguish descent on $Y$ from descent along a faithfully flat cover of $X$, and ask the same question with finite presentation imposed.

**Scans 3–4, Lemma 1.3.** For an Artinian base, the notes start with a solution after an epimorphism $Y'\to Y$. The continuation gives finiteness of that map as one alternative. In the other it puts finite presentation hypotheses on $X$ and the solution upstairs and asserts finite presentation downstairs.

**Scan 4, Theorem 1.4.** Condition (a) relates a point outside the open to a generization and an intermediate specialization lying inside the open fibre. The small diagram places these three points. Condition (b) introduces local test schemes mapping to the spectrum of a completed local ring and a zero intersection of kernels. Under the additional finiteness alternatives in (c), the conclusion is a coherent solution flat over $Y$.

**Scans 4–5, proof of 1.4.** The argument localizes at a point and reduces to $U=X-\{x\}$. It passes through a henselian local scheme and completion, following the associated cycles $Z_\alpha$. Solutions over the $Y_n$ lead to a quotient $G\to H$. The last paragraph uses injectivity to identify $G=H$ and exclude $x$ from $\operatorname{Ass}G_y$.

**Scans 6–7, Corollaries 1.5–1.7.** The crossed 1.5 combines $(S_1)$, a common dimension and fibrewise density. Under “Critère valuatif”, 1.6 and 1.7 test existence on traits finite over specified local bases. The margin beside 1.7 singles out the containment of associated points as the remaining issue on a trait. Scan 7 concludes the existence statement.

**Scans 7–8, Theorem 1.8.** The open problem about a quotient gives way to a local normality statement. Conditions (a)–(e) examine reducedness of the base, universal openness, dimensions at generizations, generic reducedness of the fibre and geometric normality of its reduction. A bracket relates the last two to base change to $\overline{k(y)}$. Successive versions of a corollary compare a fibrewise dense separable open with normality on the reduced spaces. Much of that corollary is cancelled.

**Scan 9, end of a typescript argument.** An invertible module pulls back to an ample module. A neighbourhood of the closed point is the whole base in the situation on the sheet. The typescript invokes numbered references to conclude ampleness and closes the preceding argument.

**Scan 9, typescript §21.10.** The proof of factoriality for a regular Noetherian local ring proceeds by induction on dimension. Writing $U$ for the punctured spectrum, it reduces to $\operatorname{Pic}(U)=0$. An invertible $L$ on $U$ is extended to a coherent $F$ on $X$. Restricting a finite resolution of $F$ gives the displayed resolution of $L$. The typescript breaks off after introducing a locally free module on a ringed space.

**Scan 10, proof headed “Dém”.** A subscheme $U_0$ and a relatively schematically dense open $V$ express the local conclusion. Constructibility, a passage to a limit or stage, and the lemma of Hironaka enter the argument. The proof identifies a separable open using reducedness on the fibre and states that it is dense fibre by fibre. A cancelled lower argument considers its schematic closure.

**Scan 11, local statement and proof (a).** Braces collect the local-ring, dimension, openness and fibre hypotheses. A double arrow restates the last conditions on the geometric fibre. The downward implication leads to a neighbourhood on which the morphism is flat with geometrically normal fibres. The proof under excellence considers persistence of the hypotheses in a neighbourhood. The diagrams record the points under change of base, and the lower paragraph returns to the dense separable open.

**Scan 12, sheaves and change of scalars.** A cancelled note introduces categories of schemes over a field, then describes sheaves by functors of finite algebras $\Lambda$. The formula for a quasi-coherent example is $\Lambda'\mapsto M\otimes_\Lambda\Lambda'$. The note compares extension of $\ker u$ with the functor taking the kernel after extension of scalars. The turned sketches at the foot separately compare the density of intersections with $T_y$ and $T'_y$.

**Scan 13, proof (b)–(c).** Assuming the hypotheses throughout $X$, (b) starts from the fibrewise dense separable open. It states the schematic-closure and flatness goal, invokes a maximal generization, and compares primed fibres. Part (c) invokes the valuative criterion of flatness for the schematic-closure step.
