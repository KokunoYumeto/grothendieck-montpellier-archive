## Legibility

The initial definitions and the typewritten sentences are substantially clearer than the fast prose in the middle of 044. The top of 046 has successive versions in the same space. The oblique note on 038 was read after rotation. The cancelled superscript on 043, arrowheads at the edge of 039 and the short strokes on the right of the grid on 045 are particularly uncertain. Several old words have enough surviving ink for a tentative reading but too little to choose confidently between endings. The angular strokes on 050 are reproduced as a figure. Attribution of its individually penned symbols to a particular writer is uncertain.
