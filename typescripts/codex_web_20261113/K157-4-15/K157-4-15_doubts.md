## Legibility

The ingredient that causes the most uncertainty is the superposition of two directions of handwriting. On scan 231 the outer NB crosses the theorem, and the stroke leading to the right-hand box runs across the middle of the sheet. The less legible parts of scan 235 are the underlying text of the cancelled proof and the adverb opening the new paragraph. The short amendment before *des choses* on scan 241 also resists a single reading. The heads of short arrows, especially in Loc (1c) on scan 242, are less reliable than the objects at their ends. The page files preserve these differences in confidence.
