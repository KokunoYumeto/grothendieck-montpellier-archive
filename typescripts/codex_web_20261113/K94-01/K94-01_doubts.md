## Legibility

The headings, statement numbers, principal displayed maps and uncancelled typescript were generally legible. The small gaps between two repeated strokes matter most in the ideal and module letters, the final indices of the Hom comparison on scan 10, and the prime and subscript on the cancelled functors on scan 13. The hardest continuous prose is at the foot of scan 7. The least recoverable layers are the hatching in scan 9(a), the old heading on scan 11, the x-overtyped expression at the foot of scan 12 and the two local cancellations inside the crossed-out draft on scan 13. Changes in direction within a single handwritten letter sometimes resemble an additional pen stroke. The register records that distinction where it affects a reading.
