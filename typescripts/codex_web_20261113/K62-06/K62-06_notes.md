## Title sheets, dates and page counts

The even-numbered scans contain undated handwritten working notes. The odd-numbered scans are reused typescript sheets. The catalogue title supplied with the packet is *Courbes elliptiques (généralités - vieille rédaction) : notes manuscrites (s.d.)*, Montpellier archive of Alexander Grothendieck, cote 62. No leaf in this run is a dated title sheet or gives the total length of the work. The typescript leaves themselves carry document numbers but no author credit. The faint paper mark “EXTRA STRONG” is distinct from the text.
