## Legibility

The dark, uncorrected equations can generally be followed through their individual strokes. The least secure readings are the old fibre labels on 36, the cancelled factors on 38, the note carried from 44 to 46, and the overwritten prose on 50. In the typed text the main difficulties are displaced accents, the direction of hats and primes, and distinguishing a marginal pencil mark from the type. The bottom line of 49 is physically cut. The ages and authorship of the light pencil additions cannot be established from these leaves alone.
