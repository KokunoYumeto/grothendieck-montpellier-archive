## Title sheets, dates and page counts

There is no title sheet, date, statement of the number of pages, or author’s page number on scans 32–46. Two internal section headings occur. Scan 34 has VI, « Opérations ψᵣ d’étoilage d’un polygône. » Scan 40 has VII, « Systèmes réguliers de n sommets », with replacements in the heading and « (i.e. structures polygonales modulo étoilage …) » underneath. The lower-corner archive numbers run consecutively from 31 to 45. Each appears as a comment in its page file.
