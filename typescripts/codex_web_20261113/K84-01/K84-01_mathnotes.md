## What the pages do

**Scan 2 · A product with two parities.** Two monoids \(R_0,R_1\) come with maps \(\alpha:R_0\to R_1\) and \(\beta:R_1\to R_0\). The author defines \(x\wedge y\) on their disjoint union, separately for even, odd and mixed inputs. The mixed rule uses \(\alpha\); the odd–odd rule applies \(\beta\) to the product in \(R_1\). The page warns that \(\beta(xy)\) need not equal \(\beta(x)\beta(y)\).

**Scan 2 · Associativity.** The author sorts the eight parity patterns for three factors. Two boxed relations carry the remaining work: \(\beta(\alpha(x)u)=x\beta(u)\) and \(\alpha\beta(x)=x\eta\). The arrows from the box give \(\beta\alpha(x)=x\zeta\) and \(\beta(x)\beta(y)=\zeta\beta(xy)\), with \(\zeta=\beta(1)\). A short calculation then uses the concrete pairs \((L,\delta)\) and triples \((L,\delta,T_0)\).

**Scan 2 · Integer degrees and invariants.** The even formula for \(w_n\) uses \(x\zeta^\nu\). The odd formula uses \(\beta(x)\zeta^\nu\). The lower half compares \(w_1(E)\), \(\operatorname{Arf}(E)\) and \(c_1(E)\) for a sum of lines. A marginal observation makes the half-exponents integral, and the boxed formula relates \(c_1\) to Arf by a power of \(\zeta\). The last words, “Mais \(c_1(E)\)”, stop mid-thought.

**Scan 3 · Products written in coordinates.** A parity map separates pairs and triples. Four boxed rules give their products explicitly. The two-odd product introduces \(4\delta\delta'\) and a zero in the third position. The final calculation tracks the outputs for three factors. Above the box the author stresses that the map \(R_1\to R_0\) “n’est pas un hom de monoïdes !”.

**Scan 3 · A personal note.** The sheet carries an isolated addressed line, a multilingual label for offset paper, and a note thanking the recipient for help. The writer offers to pay for the figs and to be reimbursed for the portion taken. A separate request concerns garlic and planting. The underlined word after “Demain ?” is uncertain.

**Scan 4 · Quadratic subalgebras and involutions.** The tensor product of three quadratic algebras with generators \(u_i\) has a table of monomials and sign-changing involutions. One table lists \(k\) and seven two-dimensional entries. The next lists seven four-dimensional entries and their involutions. The author then reverses the lookup and lists which primed entries contain each unprimed one. The margin relates these lists to the lines and hyperplanes of \(\mathbb F_2^3\).

**Scan 5 · Reused typescript, page 449.** The writer quotes an account of Deligne, mixed Hodge structures and the Weil conjectures. His bracketed interventions question its description of his own contribution. The dated footnote explains why he chose mathematical names carefully and points to another note on the subject.

**Scan 6 · Trace, norm and a diagram.** Comparing a conjugate generator with its translate gives two conditions on the translation, including \(2\alpha=0\). The page then expands the norm of \(\alpha+\beta u\), specializes to \(N(u)=0\), and writes \(\lambda(\lambda-\mu b)\) for the norm of \(\lambda1+\mu u\). The lower diagram relates \(SW\), \(W^\ast\), \(G_m\), \(\mu_2\), and two quotients. Two earlier entries are crossed out.

**Scan 7 · Reused typescript, page 444.** The “chasseur de papillons” brings along the specimen he intends to find. The child entering the sea discovers its actual conditions. The writer recognizes both attitudes in himself and describes correcting errors as part of discovery in mathematics and in personal experience. The page ends inside a sentence on fear.

**Scans 9–10 · Forms up to multiplication by a unit.** On a scheme or a locally ringed topos, §1.1 forms the quotient sheaf \(\mathrm{Sim}(E)\) of quadratic forms by units. The distinction between local and global factors matters: injectivity of \(a\mapsto aQ\) makes a factor relating two representatives unique. §1.2 replaces an orbit by the locally monogenic submodule it generates. Faithful structures correspond to invertible submodules.

**Scans 10–12 · Strict faithfulness and evaluation.** §1.3 requires injectivity on each residue fibre and gives a description by line subbundles or projective-bundle sections. In §1.4 evaluation at \(x\) defines \(Q_E(x):f\mapsto f(x)\). Its polar expression evaluates the polarization of \(f\) at \(x,y\). Transposing \(i:\Delta\to\mathrm{Quad}(E)\) gives the composite \(Q_i:E\to\Delta^\vee\). Choosing a basis \(e\) of \(\Delta\) recovers \(f=i(e)\). The sideways §1.5 formulates strict faithfulness through generation of the value line by the image of \(Q\).

**Scans 12–13 · The determinant pairing in rank two.** With \(L=\bigwedge^2E\), the exterior product identifies \(E\) with \(E^\vee\otimes L\). The induced comparison of spaces of forms involves \(L^{\otimes(-2)}\). The author uses it to carry similarity structures between \(E\) and \(E^\vee\) and says the two comparisons are inverse.

**Scans 13–14 · Endomorphisms modulo scalars.** §2.2 places the alternating tensor map next to the symmetric-square quotient. Tensoring with \(L^\vee\) identifies the image of the first term with the scalar identity inside \(\mathrm{End}(E)\). The vertical diagram exhibits the quotient \(\mathrm{End}(E)/\mathcal O\) as the module of \(L\)-valued quadratic forms.

**Scans 15–16 · The algebra attached to a similarity structure.** The inverse image in \(\mathrm{End}(E)\) of a locally monogenic submodule of the quotient contains the identity. Lemma 1 describes it locally by two generators, which may be chosen as \(1_E\) and \(u\). The marginal characteristic equation accounts for closure under multiplication. The term on the next page is “l’anneau des homothéties”, with “directes” underlined.

**Scans 16–17 · Strict faithfulness and cyclic vectors.** Lemma 2 lists equivalent fibre, algebra and module conditions. An algebra generator is nonscalar on every residue fibre. The module \(E\) is locally free of rank one over the algebra. Corollary 1 identifies \(\Delta\) and then \(\Delta^\vee\). Scan 17 tests whether a section \(x\) generates \(E\), using the value \(Q(x)\) and whether \(x(s)\) is an eigenvector of the algebra generator. The heading “Cor. 2” was cancelled.

**Scan 18 · Coordinates.** §2.3 proposes \(Q_u(x)=u(x)\wedge x\), explicitly leaving its sign to check. The calculation writes a binary polynomial for a general two-by-two matrix, identifies the scalar kernel, and gives a family \(u_a\) for a fixed form. At the foot is the representative with \(a=0\).
