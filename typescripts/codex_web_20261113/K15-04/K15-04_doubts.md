## Legibility

The English type usually preserves individual letters, even where a typewriter used nearly identical ovals for 0 and O. Hand-entered primes and indices require closer inspection. The manuscript diagrams on 108, 110 and 112 have short shafts whose tips can disappear into the ruling or the next symbol. The oblique pencil note on 117 is much lighter than its surrounding text.

The least dependable passages are the abandoned argument at the foot of 104, the deletions across the conjecture on 106, the first valuation construction on 110, the corrected clauses on 117 and the first paragraph of 119. A candidate inside a cancellation sometimes rests on two or three surviving strokes. In the main text those candidates remain visible at their locations. In particular, the absence of a complete-word illegibility mark does not make the marked conjectural readings secure.
