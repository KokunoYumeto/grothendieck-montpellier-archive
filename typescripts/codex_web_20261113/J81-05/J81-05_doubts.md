## Legibility

**35.** The large set expressions are clear. The subscript of the arrow target, the altered digits, and the small signs squeezed below the first pair carry the greatest uncertainty.

**36.** The short lines squeezed above the Proposition, the altered subscript in (****), and the last line meeting the bottom of the scan are the least secure.

**37.** The diagonal note can be read after rotation. Its compressed connective and interlinear abbreviation, the middle cancellation and the extra strokes in the inclusion formula are the least secure.

**38.** Both margin notes run across the direction of the body text. The bracketed inner symbol beside (23), the cancelled label below the diagram, and the overwritten word beneath transposition are the least clear.

**39.** The long descender and paired t strokes of the underlined term can be compared on 40. The P subscript, and especially the crossed alternatives above and within (28), remain difficult. The lower diagonal note overlaps the equation label.

**40.** The two sets and the lettered nodes are clear. Ink has pooled in the earlier words of the proof. The little word before peut and the single stroke at the end of the argument are especially uncertain.

**41.** Only the upper-right heading is written. The pencil gives incomplete contours in its first line. The title hand is left unattributed.
