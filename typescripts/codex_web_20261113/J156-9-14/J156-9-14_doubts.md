# Legibility and limits

Six sheets are handwritten, with many middles and endings reduced to waves or strokes. Scan 109 is especially dense and repeatedly corrected. The weakest readings are the opening of 105, the slanted notes on 105/107/109, the parenthetical reference on 107, and the minute added condition/cancelled cases on 109. The reused typescript on 110 is generally more legible, but worn or doubled impressions, a clipped right edge, and dense x-overtyping make the cancelled sentence and the run after cas particularly uncertain. Four illegible spans remain (estimated eight words in total); no formula uses an illegible-word placeholder. The long cancellation stroke on 110 is an interpretation of page state, explicitly distinguished from secure local x-cancellation.
