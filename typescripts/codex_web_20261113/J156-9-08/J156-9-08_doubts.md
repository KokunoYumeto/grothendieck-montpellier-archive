# Legibility

The writing is spacious but often reduces whole word interiors to waves. Crossed formulas and very small notation labels are the least secure. Doubt marks express visual uncertainty, including where context suggests a plausible reading. The full second reading added or refined doubts and corrected several readings. The overlap of the NB note and definition on scan 61, the abandoned formulas on scans 58 and 63, and the clipped lower annotations are trusted least. No external text or OCR was used, and no independent human reader or LaTeX compilation is claimed.
