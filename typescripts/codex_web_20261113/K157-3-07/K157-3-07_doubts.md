## Legibility

The body hand alternates between clearly separated formula signs and French words joined into a single low stroke. The primes on 112 are especially uneven, even when the letters themselves are clear. The least secure passages are the cancelled lines on 098–099, the sideways argument on 103, the lower-left citation on 105 and the inkblots on 110. Printed typefaces for the manuscript’s swashed category letters are editorial approximations. The opening of 098 and the last words on 113 continue across the boundaries of this set.
