## Legibility

The typescript letters and most of the degree tables are easier to check than the running manuscript hand. The heaviest uncertainty is in the cancelled cutoff conditions on 299, the overwritten symbols on 303–305, the very short connecting words on 306–307, and the hatched right-hand entries in the list on 309.
