## Title sheets, dates and page counts

The manuscript numbers at the top run consecutively from 79 on scan 130 to 94 on scan 145. Each is 51 less than the archive number in pencil at lower left. All sixteen sheets are handwritten mathematical notes. There is no title sheet, date line or statement of the total length in these scans.

Scan 131, manuscript p. 80, begins §2.2, « Application aux carrés cocartésiens déduits d'une immersion ouverte ou fermée ». Scan 138, manuscript p. 87, begins §2.3, « Objets totalement W-asphériques de Cat, et morphismes totalement W-asphériques ». The 1983 on scan 132 occurs in « en 1983 en écrivant P.S. » and dates a recollection.
