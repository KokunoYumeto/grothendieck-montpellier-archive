## Legibility

The diagram objects, point names, bars and tildes are generally more distinct than the ends of French words. The densest revisions are the conditional on 130, the three versions of b′/b″ on 135, the slashed corollary on 136, and the rewritten map names on 133, 143 and 144. The pencil sheet numbers are lighter than the manuscript folios. In the cancellations the first reading sometimes rests on only the initial or final strokes. The most consequential uncertain letters are the last category letter in Definition 1(a), the X/V in the first display on 140, and the small v′/V′ on 142.
