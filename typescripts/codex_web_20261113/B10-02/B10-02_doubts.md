# Legibility

Most live typescript is clear. Handwritten formulas are usually readable, but several original letters have been retraced or overwritten; the A/a forms and small o/0 subscripts remain systematic ambiguities. The least reliable readings are the cancelled group formula on scan 42, local black hatching on 44–46, the faint replacement citation on 48, the two heavily blacked-out lines at the foot of 48, and fragments beneath x overprinting on 49–50. The clipped cancelled word at the upper right of 48 remains physically unreadable and is represented by one illegible-word mark. The remaining partial readings are retained with explicit doubts. Hand attribution in the inventory is cautious where the image alone does not settle it.

The handwriting and annotations were read directly from the supplied images, including enlarged crops. All nine pages received a separate crop-first second reading. No OCR, outside transcription, bibliography search, or compilation was used. The audit records corrections and unresolved readings; it is not a claim that the uncertain ink has become recoverable.
