# K12-09 · Mathematical notes

## What the pages do

**M1 · Scan 137.** A spectral sequence with rows $q=0,1,2$ is used to study admissibility. The bottom and top rows are related by the twist $\mathbb Q_\ell(-1)$. For the middle row the author describes $E_2^{p1}$ as an extension of three sheaves, with middle term $E_3^{p1}=\cdots=E_\infty^{p1}$. He reduces the remaining assertion to a direct-image case. Several exponents in that reduction are difficult to read.

**M2 · Scans 139–140.** Over a normal integral base $S$ with generic field $K$, smoothness of a semisimple isomotive is expressed by non-ramification of its $T_\ell(M)$ on $S[1/\ell]$. In characteristic zero the next definition adds sheaves $E_\ell$ and comparison maps. The square for morphisms shows both rationalized sheaves and the induced $T_\ell(u)$. The axiom asks for integral comparison for almost all primes.

**M3 · Scans 140–141.** The notes describe morphisms, kernels and cokernels of smooth motives, identify a quotient by certain finite commutative groups with smooth isomotives, and introduce the tensor product, Hom and isogenies. The cohomological example is a proper smooth $X$ over $S$, with the notation $R^i f_*(\mathbb Z_X)$.

**M4 · Scans 141–142.** A contravariant functor associates a motive to a proper smooth commutative group scheme $A$. Its three entries involve $R^1$ of $A^0$, the underlined Hom out of $\,{}_{\ell^\infty}A$, and their comparison. The next sheet characterizes the essential image through the quotient modulo torsion and an abelian scheme. It then asks how far this characterization can be tested at the generic point.

**M5 · Scans 143–146.** The proposed universal functor $h$ starts from a category with abelian groups of arrows and bilinear composition. The construction uses pairs $(X,\pi)$ with $\pi^2=\pi$. Arrows commute with the projectors and are taken modulo arrows killed by them. An additive functor $H$ extends to a pair by $\psi(H)(X,\pi)=\operatorname{Im}H(\pi)$. The slanted note on 146 allows an additive target in which projectors split.

**M6 · Scans 146–148.** Under semisimplicity and finiteness hypotheses on the endomorphism algebras, the author investigates when the resulting category is abelian semisimple. Scan 148 sets out chain conditions on direct factors and a normal form $A\times B\longrightarrow B\times C$ for arrows. Scan 147 works in an endomorphism ring with $u,v$, two projectors and the identities in the oval. See R1 for the two joins between these sheets.

**M7 · Scan 149.** The construction extends to a biadditive functor on two categories. Applied to a symmetric, associative and unital product, it gives the tensor product in the completed category. In characteristic zero the author lists covariant tensor operations, with tensor, exterior and symmetric powers as examples. The small exponent over the tensor sign remains uncertain.

The top of 148 reads “cette catégorie est semi-simple).” The bottom of 148 in turn ends “Cela nous”, and the top of 147 reads “permet de … ramener à un objet”.
