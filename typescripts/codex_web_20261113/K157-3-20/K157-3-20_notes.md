## Title sheets, dates and page counts

All fifteen sheets are handwritten mathematical notes. The manuscript folios run consecutively from 247 on scan 297 through 261 on scan 311. The separate lower-left archive numerals run from 297 through 311. There is no title sheet, explicit calendar date, or written total page count. “Je le ferai demain” on 307 and “Aujourd’hui” on 308 are relative time expressions.

The internal headings include the theorem on manuscript p. 253 (scan 303), “Proposition 19” on p. 258 (scan 308), and “Définition” on p. 259 (scan 309). “Corollaire 2” occurs on both pp. 259 and 260. The last manuscript line on p. 261 ends after “de”.
