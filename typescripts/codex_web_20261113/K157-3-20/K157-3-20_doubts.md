## Legibility

The manuscript folios, objects in the larger squares, and most of the named assertions are legible. Small terminal pen strokes frequently do duty for an entire French ending. The least secure readings are the superscript on Ho on 297 and 300, the short gloss in the addition on 297, the words under diagonal hatching, and the subscripts next to arrows that end against another stroke. The first loop of the initial-object sign on 304 and the final words on 305 and 311 also deserve checking against the source. For each worked-over passage a candidate could be offered. The black wedges above *adjoints* on 298, beside *plus bas* on 299, and at the very bottom left of 311 have the granular edges of reproduction marks. They cover no discernible sequence of words.
