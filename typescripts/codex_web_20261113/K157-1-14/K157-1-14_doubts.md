## Legibility

The main French prose, large display letters and directions of the long arrows are usually clear. The most fragile readings are the cancelled low indices on 212, the oblique reference on 213, the repeatedly compressed polar signs, the two-line rewrite on 222, the small writing beside the orthogonality examples on 223, the class letters and accents on 225, and the two labels around the diagonal on 226. The copies retain enough ink to suggest candidates in those places. A proposed reading of a crossed passage carries less weight than the live text beside it.
