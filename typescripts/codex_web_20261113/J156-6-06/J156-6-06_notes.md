# J156-6-06 · Manuscript notes

## Steps the pages mark as unverified

| Scan | Words on the sheet | What the author leaves open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 42 | “Il faut voir, … quand ℱ est un atelier, si ℱ′ est un sous-atelier de ℱ” | The question introduces the check of the atelier axioms for the proposed sub-atelier. The later Sousat conditions continue that discussion. |
| 44 | “mais ça l’est peut-être dans le cas ensembliste, à voir” | The example leaves open whether the displayed inclusion gives a *sous-atelier spacieux* in the set-theoretic case. |
| 46 | “il y en a qui vont éliminer, je pense, cet exemple trivial d’atelier” | “Je pense” qualifies the prediction about the axioms still to come. |
| 47 | “Il n’y a aucune raison que At 8 soit stable par passage à un sous-atelier” | The remark questions passage of At 8 to a sub-atelier, even one called *spacieux*. It then introduces a further condition. |
| 48 | “pas clair, il … faudrait que … on ait” and “ce qui n’est pas clair, cf plus bas” | The lower margin questions the equality with a tilde over the whole expression on the left and over the argument of f on the right. It adds the membership of F′ in ℱ′. |
| 48–49 | “Il faudrait arriver à imposer aux quasi-morph. des conditions …”; “soit bijective” | The author is looking for conditions giving the desired behaviour of the map (1.54). Its sentence crosses the sheet break. |
| 49 | “dans tous les cas à ma connaissance” | The author limits the claim about the canonical map (1.55) to the cases known to him. |

Scans 43 and 45 carry no separate qualification of this kind. The upper margin of scan 48 also leaves a choice of *terminology* open with “il vaudrait peut-être mieux de parler de comorphismes”.
