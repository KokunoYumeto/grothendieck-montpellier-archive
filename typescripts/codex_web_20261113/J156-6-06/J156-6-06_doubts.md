## Legibility

The main lines, the long arrows and the page numbers are generally clear. The least secure passage is the old opening of At 1 on scan 42. One word under the zigzag is unreadable. On scan 43 the ruling cuts straight through a repeated relation, and on scans 48–49 the earlier words under corrections are harder to distinguish than the live text. In the sloping notes the syntax is often clearer than the individual low strokes. Subscripts beside tall f’s, tiny marks beside F, the right ends of some shafts and differences between a smooth C-shaped bowl and the author’s fuller G remain specifically marked. The prose and formulae preserve those local differences.
