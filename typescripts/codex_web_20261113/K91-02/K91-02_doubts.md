## Legibility

The numerals, diagrams and large symbols are generally easier to follow than the smaller letter endings. On scan 19 the left-hand correction is crossed by near-vertical strokes. The top equation of scan 20 runs into the right edge. The slanting lines across the cancelled draft on scan 22 sometimes coincide with the strokes needed to read the words beneath. On scan 27, turning each note to its own writing angle recovers substantially more text than the witness supplies, but the first and last notes still contain low-confidence readings.

On scans 31–32 the aligned dark type is readily distinguished from most of the paler, reversed text from the other side. Individual superimposed letters, a few possible accent strokes and punctuation near dark specks are less secure. A reader checking only a few places should start with the scan 19 exponent and margin, the lower wording on 22, the scan 24 condition at the foot, and scan 27’s crossed marginal additions.
