**U11 · Scan 278, typescript p. 108.** A choice of a blended extension X gives a class associated with Y′. On replacing X by H∧X the representative changes by a class coming from the original category. Formula (9.3.13) gives the resulting quotient class and states that its vanishing characterizes descent of the blended extension along r.

**U12 · Scan 276, typescript p. 127.** Section 10.5 reduces assertion 10.4 to convenient choices of base trait and to direct factors. It distinguishes an integral homomorphism from its rationalization, displays the denominator e(S′/S) after a base change, and uses functoriality of the lattices and monodromy pairings. The last sentence continues beyond this scan.
