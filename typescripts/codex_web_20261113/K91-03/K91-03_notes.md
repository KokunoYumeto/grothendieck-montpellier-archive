## Title sheets, dates and page counts

Scan 34 opens with the handwritten heading « Schémas Hom », an added « etc. » and the cancelled continuation « et calcul différentiel. » It is a numbered plan. A small stroke at the upper left may be a handwritten 1 or Roman I. Scan 37 carries a second plan numbered §1–§10. On scan 45 « A) Détermination de la grassmannienne » is followed on scan 46 by « B) Étude de la situation suivante ». These are internal headings.

Manuscript dates and explicit page counts: none. Archive sheet numbers 33–45 are visible on scans 34–46 respectively and appear only in comments in the transcription. The dates attached to the digital task do not date the manuscript.
