## Legibility

The typed notices and the body of Mumford’s letter are generally clear. The signatures and the marks beneath corrections are less secure. The heaviest losses are in the crossed ramification labels on 47, the central network on 53, and the A/B and sign changes on 55. On 58 the lower product reaches the lower and right cut edges. Only the surviving characters are set. On 51 the edge also interrupts the coordinate triple. A source file can have balanced TeX delimiters while a parenthesis written on the sheet has no visible partner.
