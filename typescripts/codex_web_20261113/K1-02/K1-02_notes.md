# Notes on the manuscript

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

| Scan and approximate line | Words or mark on the sheet | What is left open |
|---|---|---|
| 67, short sideways direction | “à développer en rédigeant le cas de la dimension finie” | A revision instruction singles out the finite-dimensional case for fuller treatment. The verb is doubtful. |
| 68, last line | “matrice triangulaire” followed by a disputed hook and small word | The hook may be punctuation. Its status as a question about the triangular-matrix argument is uncertain. |
| 69, lines 4–8 | “dans un sens évident à préciser” | The continuity of the whole system of eigenvalues is invoked in passing from finite rank to compact operators. The precise sense of continuity is deferred. |
| 71, after (5), by the rejected pair of norm formulas | “(deux références)” and a marginal “?” | The justification needs references. The question mark stands beside a cancelled corollary, so its exact target is uncertain. |
| 71, after (10) | “Pour la preuve, on … ramènera au cas où $u$ est hermitien positif, où c'est facile” | The reduction for the determinant theorem is announced. These sheets do not carry it out. |
| 72, lower left at (16)–(17) | “notation ? à réviser” | A slanted pencil direction calls for another look at the notation. The mark following “notation” is doubtful. |
| 73, first argument below Proposition 3 | “pour tout $i$ (---)” | There are strokes where a justification or reference for the singular-value comparison might belong. Their meaning is uncertain. |
| 74, lines 1–6 | “semblent les inégalités les plus fortes possibles” and “on pourrait déduire … directement l'inégalité (15)” | Optimality is tentative. A direct deduction from (12) is suggested. |

| 76, middle and last lines | “Question … Trouver la forme générale des fonctions $f$ d'une variable positive” and “Généralisation à des fonctions de $n$ arguments.” | The classification and its generalization are posed here. |
| 78, top insertion | “(vérifier d'après cas décomposable)” | The insertion points to verification of the finite-rank case of (14), before the appeal to continuity. |

## Title sheets, dates and page counts

Scan 71 begins a section headed “Puissances extérieures d'op. de Fredholm, inégalités diverses.” The section number is damaged at the torn edge. The transcription favours “5.)” and gives alternatives. This is a heading on a sheet of formulas.

Separate title sheets, dates, authorial page numbers and statements of total page count — none. The lower-corner numbers run consecutively from 66 on scan 65 to 79 on scan 78. They are recorded as archive comments in the LaTeX files. The numbers beside the propositions, corollaries and formulas belong to the text.
