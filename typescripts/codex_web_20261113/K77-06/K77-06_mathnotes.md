The proposition compares writing \(\Delta=b^2-4c\) with two extension conditions. Scan 085 continues the étaleness assertion of 084. **088, a cancelled enumeration.** Five rows associate monomials in the \(Y\)'s with small graphs and counts. A weighted column ends in \(24=4!\). Below it, sums indexed by \(\Gamma_0\) and \(\Gamma_1\) are rearranged in an expression involving \(\Delta-B^2\). The crossings extend over both the table and the algebra.

**089–091, from a ternary form to an affine quadric.** The cover on 089 gives the subject. On 090 the data \((E,q,\omega)\) are related to the trace-zero elements of a twisted quaternion algebra. The manuscript expresses \(q\), its polar form and the bracket in determinant, trace and exterior-product notation. Scan 091 rewrites the orientation identity, gives the affine quadric \(q(x)=-1\) and its coordinate ring, and introduces the projective conic \(q(x)=0\).

**092–093, sections after adjoining a square root of −1.** The author introduces \(S[i]\) by \(T^2+1\) and announces an open immersion in the functor of \(S[i]\)-points of \(X\). For \(x+iy\) the equations become \(q(x)=q(y)\) and \(\varphi(x,y)=0\). Units \(a+ib\) act on the pair. Requiring the common value of \(q\) to be a unit gives a stable open and then an open \(U\) in the quotient.

**093–095, the bracket map.** Formula (20) sends the pair to a multiple of \([x,y]\). The sheet that follows compares the norm of the bracket with \(q(x)q(y)\) and \(\varphi(x,y)^2\). The formula used in (22) and on 095 is \([x,y]/(2q(x))\). The numerator and denominator acquire the same factor on changing the representative. This gives the morphism \(f:U\to\Sigma\) in (23).

**095–097, comparison of two representatives.** To prove the stated isomorphism when 2 is invertible, the author first takes two representatives with the same image. He expresses their coordinates in a common perpendicular plane, obtains a scalar \(\lambda\), and uses the exterior-product equation to set it equal to 1. On 097 he turns to the epimorphism and chooses an étale-local orthonormal basis of a rank-two complement. The last displayed conditions are \(q(x)=q(y)=1\) and \(\varphi(x,y)=0\).
