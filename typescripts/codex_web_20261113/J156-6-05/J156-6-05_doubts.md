## Legibility

The standalone letters, large comparisons and main lines are often clear. The least secure passages are the oblique notes on scans 34, 37 and 38, the locally crossed argument under the long cancellation on scan 39, and the additions above the example on scan 41. At those points the candidates name the strokes that remain visible, including traces below the final version. The smaller distinctions between script alphabets, a blunt shaft and an arrowhead, and the endings of several cancelled words remain open.
