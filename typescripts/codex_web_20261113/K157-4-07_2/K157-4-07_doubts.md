## Legibility

The type is generally clear, including the double diaeresis on 109 and the handwritten addition to 113. On 98–105 the hand often joins the middle and ending of a word into one shallow wave. On 99 the two slanted notes share space with the cancelled argument. The category names there and on 98 and 100, and the small signs at the foot of 104–105, are among the least secure readings. Scan 108 loses portions at the left and bottom edges. On 110 the crowded indices and the overwriting below the fork are especially difficult. The geometry on 114 follows the visible strokes. At a few crossings the ink cannot distinguish an intersection from a vertex.
