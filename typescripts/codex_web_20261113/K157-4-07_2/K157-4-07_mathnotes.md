# Mathematical notes on cote 157-4, scans 98–106 and 108–114

## What the pages do

**98–99.** Grothendieck defines a category of triples with a map from Z to Xᵢ. A morphism consists of two maps satisfying a square. The next square gives a fibre-product description of the category C. He expresses Xᵢ as a colimit and describes taking colimits first on the fibres of C → I and then on I. The lower part of 99 contains a crossed attempt to pass to small filtered subcategories.

**100–102.** For each i he orders the filtered subcategories of Cᵢ with at most π arrows. He assembles them into a single ordered set using transition functors. Taking a colimit over one such subcategory gives an object Z; he singles out those Z in M and invokes b) for cofinality. By 102 he has represented X by objects in <code>M^\pi</code>. He then identifies what he still needs in order to apply b).

**103–104.** He indexes a new family by filtered subsets I′ of I of cardinal at most π. To obtain cofinality he uses the subsets with a greatest element. The next leaf finishes the argument with the “il suffit” of b), “qed.” and the assertion that b implies a. The NB distinguishes the place where he used accessibility.

**104–105.** For the reverse implication he proposes changing the cardinal in the conclusion. Lemme 3 compares presentations indexed by I and J. It asks for subfamilies containing a prescribed i, an isomorphism between the indexing subsets and a strictly increasing map back. The supplied leaf ends in the middle of the statement.

**106, 108.** The cover opens chapter XIV on cartesian and cocartesian squares. The next sheet pairs a rightward suspension sequence with a leftward loop sequence. A staircase and two small tables arrange expressions of the form πᵢ(A,X). The NB compares the degrees after suspension and looping; its final subscript and punctuation are difficult.

**110, 112, 114.** A diagram carrying the caption “diagramme des 18 flèches” relates the objects labelled X with two indices. Grothendieck identifies fibres of r₀ and r₁ with a fibre of π and ends with two arrows labelled as isomorphisms. Scan 112 asks how the maps labelled α and q₁ are related and draws several comparisons. Scan 114 contains an unlabelled cubical configuration; its strokes supply no names for its vertices.

**109, 111, 113.** The three typewritten faces contain prose. A 2 and A 3 discuss creative work, ambition and the writing of Récoltes et Semailles. The sheet numbered 422 describes the effect of work and persistent reflection on sleep.

## Statements left without proof

- **99–100.** The crossed attempt says “J’admets (cf plus bas)” of a filteredness assertion. The live text on 100 begins “J’admets pour l’instant le Lemme” and asserts that the category written <code>\underline M^{\pi}/X</code> is filtered. The addition gives b), or the condition on the form of every object of M stated in a).
- **101–102.** He calls the identification of the colimit over a fibre “immédiat” and introduces the colimit over the whole system by “On vérifie aussi immédiatement que”. The calculations on the sheets are the displayed identifications themselves.
- **104–105.** “Ceci va résulter du” introduces Lemme 3 after the weakened implication. Its last words here are “et des hom”. The supplied leaves contain neither the rest of that sentence nor its proof.
- **108, 110.** The NB gives a degree comparison without an accompanying argument. On 110 “on trouve que ... sont des fibrations” and “induisent des iso.” introduce the two fibre claims. The diagrams and final display give his stated identifications.

## Questions and conjectures

- **99, 101.** The marginal conditional on 99 begins “Si on admet la validité de a)”. The cancelled question on 101 asks “Alors à quoi ça a servi de ...” in connection with choosing the categories Cᵢ rather than the larger quotients involving Filt.
- **102.** “Mais on ne sait pas que ...” and “et encore moins que” distinguish having suprema from identifying the objects at those suprema with colimits.
- **104.** He puts a question mark over a⇒b, says “Admettons que” when imposing accessibility, and writes “Je pense que je n’arriverai pas à prouver” the same-cardinal implication. His proposed replacement is <code>a_\pi\Longrightarrow b_{\pi'}</code> with π′ greater than π.
- **110, 112.** The cube on 110 has a question at the right descent. “ou des 12 flèches ?” revisits the count in its caption. The two question marks on 112 sit above the arrows from Z(α) toward X₁₂ and from α toward q₁.
- **109, 111, 113.** In the prose he writes “j’ignore si je verrai jamais le fond de ce mystère” (109), “Ce ne serait pas un luxe que j’examine la situation de près” (111) and “Je souhaite que les pages de Récoltes et Semailles puissent contribuer à te le faire sentir” (113).
