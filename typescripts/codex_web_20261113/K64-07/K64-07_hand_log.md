# Hand and notation, cote 64, scans 78–90

## Scan 78

The bottom-left foliation 77 is much finer than the central manuscript 3. In (16)–(19) the subscript 4 has an open top, the i has a detached dot and its prime is a separate high stroke. The star has crossing strokes in (16) and (19). The mark above the 4 in (17) has been filled in. The underscore belongs to the small omega and to the U, not to the following index. A long straight line cancels « satisfaisant » and « les extrémités ». The long curve over the tensor product attaches words written above the line. These tests concern the indicated places; the crossed material is less distinct.

## Scan 79

J has a horizontal upper bar and a lower hook stretching left. Both parts recur in the list and under omega. The flat middle on that subscript distinguishes it from the 0 on scan 78. Sigma has two long horizontals and a diagonal middle. In (21b) the top of the author's t is open and the following membership sign has a shorter middle bar. The pair of strokes before the second mu on line 8 look joined at their foot. The tiny raised stroke over the first 4 is much less distinct than the second star. The large P in the projective line has two uprights. The lower-case k in « sur k » resembles h; the original bottom index on P is less clear.

## Scan 80

Here the group-scheme mu has a second upright meeting the first at the foot, especially clear in (22)–(24). The ordinary mu in the parameter line uses one upright. This comparison bears on 79 (21c). The raised sign on M bends down at its right end and has two cross-strokes connected at the left; the left stroke extends up, the right down. The companion superscript in (23) is looped like a flat. One hatched earlier expression retains only the upper 1 and low curl. The base S and the subscript 0 must be read separately.

## Scan 81

An underline on J distinguishes the cubic cover in the first line, the exponent in the right-hand sequence and (28)–(29); it is not everywhere present. Short connectors in the side sequence have blunt terminals. The vertical relation under the third term consists of two distinct uprights. The last arrow reaches the cut edge and no terminal object can be read there. The two extra instances of « étale » sit over different words with separate leaders. In (28) the 2 stands to the left of E within the parentheses.

## Scan 82

The thick zigzags over single signs in (31) are cancellations. A T is still visible on either side of the arrow. The pair of fine horizontals joining the Δ to its description are a brace; the thin mark above the arrow is a separate tilde. The small k-shaped label shows two right branches, unlike the field k in (21b). In (30) a short line under J survives. The text has the same long, low ending on « d'un »/« d'une » as elsewhere; the last letter before « section » is unresolved. A single long underline covers the two lines of « épinglage de Legendre ».

## Scan 83

In the slanted NB the lines rise toward the right. Rotating the crop brings « Il faudrait », « expliciter la construction » and « en termes de ces données » onto their own baselines. The display number is horizontal and belongs to the brace; it crosses a line of that note. The letter after « fonctions » has a large bowl and a long descending stem. A top-left foliation 82 has the same fine strokes as the foliation previously at the foot. The raised « groupoïde » has both a separate insertion leader and a cancellation stroke.

## Scan 84

On this leaf the group capital above the index J has an open top flourish, a broad curved left side, and a narrow horizontal loop at the foot. Its shape resembles a calligraphic G; the same glyph, with a plus, names a subgroup and occurs above the fixed locus. A double vertical relation and a round enclosure survive at the foot. The word « signifie » has a long lower loop on g that approaches the line below and is not an underline. The abbreviation tte has two upright strokes joined by a single long crossbar.

## Scan 85

The arrow of (35) has a small but legible head at the left. In (36) the left head is clear; at the right end several short dark strokes obscure what looks like an earlier head. The right end has not been read as a live second direction. The field of dots at the end of the second line consists of three distinct points. The two interlinear additions in line 1 attach on opposite sides of « courbe ». The short top stroke in κ separates it from the open u of the next entry.

## Scan 86

The capital T has a doubled downstroke in (38) and (39). One line of explanatory prose sits above the two cancelled lines at the foot. The two strokes read as a natural in the raised sign in (38), with a sharp also possible.

## Scan 87

In the first k formula the surviving T stroke goes through a separately drawn triangular delta. The cross-hatching after sigma is confined to its index. The descending curl above the word ‘des’ attaches the script L there. The underline runs through ‘de’ before E/S. In (41) a wedge sits below the obliteration and the ±1 is under the wedge.

## Scan 88

In (42) the Q has an underline absent on the Q in (43). In the second formulation the script L is written over Q. The little line crossing the wedge in the lower prose turns under omega and over the starred mu. The right end of (44) has a brace underneath and a left parenthesis.

## Scan 89

The group initial resembles a G, but also a hand-formed fraktur S, here followed by 3. The straight line through the word following ‘groupe’ ends with that word. In (47) the two tall braces each govern two lines; ‘sur’ under the second character belongs to the lower right entry. The text says ‘en = temps’ with an actual equals sign.

## Scan 90

The D of (48)–(50) and the Z of the cyclic groups have separate close uprights or diagonals. In (50) the first surviving term after 1 is flanked by braces, with older slanted strokes underneath. The sequence uses short horizontal rules with blunt or retraced ends. On the last line the radical covers the minus sign and the 1 together.

## Comparison across leaves

The ruled round letter on scan 78 has the same inward upper hook as O on scans 82 and 86. It is transcribed as O with U kept as an alternative. The tilted cross following it is t or +. The broad loop on 84 and the repeated group sign on 89–90 share a flat internal turn. The same fraktur S / calligraphic G choice is recorded on each page.

## Readings after the witness comparison

The inward hook and descending cross-stroke on scan 78 settle the two symbols at the first tensor product as underlined O with subscript t. The raised angular two-stroke sign at the marked positions on scans 80, 81, 86 and 87 is a natural. The doubled left stroke of the right-hand mu in (21c) is distinct from the ordinary mu at the left. The old angular superscript of the inverse in (43) remains open and is carried as a deletion. The three occurrences of the curled group initial on 84, 89 and 90 retain the same S/G alternatives. The geometry and coordinates of all 96 enlarged places are indexed at the end of the audit.
