## Remarks of the model

- **M164 · Model, high confidence.** In a commutative polynomial ring, the residual of the large boxed equation on 164 equals (α+β₁χ)+χ(β₂+γχ−1). Thus the two smaller boxed conditions imply the large one. Expansion of their difference gave zero. This observation uses the displayed coefficients without dividing by χ.
- **M172 · Model, high confidence.** Substituting the written values of b and c into b²−4c gives (b₁²−4c₁)(b₂²−4c₂). A polynomial expansion gave zero for the difference. This checks the underbrace expressions directly.
- **Model, low confidence, scan 176.** The boxed product and the unfinished line containing Tr₁ and Tr₂ may record two stages of the same coefficient calculation. The strong cancellation near u and the open end after “2·” leave their intended relationship unresolved.
