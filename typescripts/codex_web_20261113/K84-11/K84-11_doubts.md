## Legibility

Most of the type is distinct enough to distinguish an absent accent or a missing consonant. A pale band at the right trims several lines, particularly on 165, 167, 169 and 171. The small pen repairs interrupt the regular spacing. Several leaves carry a detached marginal dash. Its relation to the text is unclear.

Among the handwritten pages, the darkest strokes are often the least legible. The right-hand identities on 170, the interlinear arrow labels on 172, and the expanded and cancelled pairings on 176 merit particular caution. In the diagrams a short horizontal can be a plain line, an arrow with a nearly lost head or part of a cancellation. The archive number is a pencil annotation distinct from any numbering of the text.
