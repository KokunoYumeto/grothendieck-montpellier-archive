# Notes on the manuscript

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

Scan 74 labels a problem “Question”. Given the small hexagon *h*, its associated triple also determines a large hexagon. The continuation on scans 75–76 describes how the two are related. Earlier on 74, “facile à voir directement” and “évident directement également” refer to steps for which the author gives no separate argument.

Scan 78 has a short parenthesis after the subgroup count. The mark after “3 de” may be a question mark; both the words and the mark are uncertain. Scan 79 qualifies the count of subgroups in the lower margin with “Sauf qu’il y en a 4 d’ordre 2 qui se groupent en paires de Sgpes conjugués”. The qualification concerns how the subgroups are counted.

Scan 84 explicitly asks “Quels sont les graphes finis qui ont … une ‘enveloppe’ … ?” and continues with “Exemples” and “Regarder p.ex.”. Among the sketches are two crossed examples. No later passage in this batch claims to settle this general question. No explicit “à vérifier”, “sauf erreur” or “admis” was identified.
