The open prose, the larger polygon labels and the paired coordinates on scan 88 are clearer than the short endings of words. The numbers and crossed names at the top of 73, the landscape chart on 78, the turned chart on 83 and the crowded combinations of labels and sketches on 84 are the least secure.
