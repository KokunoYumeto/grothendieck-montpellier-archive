# Legibility

The ordinary typescript is generally readable. The least reliable readings are cancelled underlayers, the altered numbers on 51, the dark index on 52, the vector-functor/character overwriting on 53, compressed handwritten words and the extremely faint rotated pencil note on 55, the cancelled BIBLE reference and N.B. endings on 56, and old layers of the diagrams on 57. Scan 52 ends at a clipped partial character. Broad black cancellations on 55 and 58 remain partly unreadable; the word counts inside illegible macros are estimates.

All eight whole-page images and all twenty-four supplied thirds were viewed directly. Further local crops were enlarged or rotated for inspection. No OCR, text-recognition software, PDF text layer, web source or published transcription was used. The second reading and subsequent consistency review are documented in the audit. Repeated viewing does not make the faint or overwritten readings certain.

On 56, faint mirrored type is visible through the paper beneath the forward-facing hand. It was treated as reverse-side show-through, not reconstructed as an additional text of this face. Archive sheet numbers are comments only. The drawings reproduce annotation shapes in TikZ; they are schematic tracings rather than pixel-level facsimiles. No image is embedded. The requested structural checks are supplied; compilation was not attempted.
