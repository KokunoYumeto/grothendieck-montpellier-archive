# Notes on the manuscripts

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

| Scan | Manuscript words | Step at issue |
|---|---|---|
| 19 | “suffisance claire, pour la nécessité il faut travailler un peu …” and a separate marginal “?” | The author proposes fibrewise irreducibility as the reformulation b′) of linear independence in b). He singles out necessity for further work. |
| 21 | “C'est sans doute ce qui exclut le cas” followed by the quoted word read *diédral* or *dièdral* | The slanted note tentatively connects the absence of an invariant divisor of degree two with the exclusion of the dihedral case. |
| 34 | “?? Prop” and “sera bijective. (?)” | The proposed restriction from representations of the three-generator group to representations of its subgroup ends with an explicit question. The accompanying low-rank conditions have also been revised at the foot of the page. |

## Title sheets, dates and page counts

| Scan | Title as written | Count and date |
|---|---|---|
| 27 | *forme hermitienne invariante pour un hyperpolyèdre régulier* | A separate faint “(4p)” stands to the left of the title. The hand of the count cannot be identified with confidence. No date appears. |
| 32 | “détermination de $(\tau_{\alpha})$ par $(\rho_\alpha)$, i.e. d'la bonne repr. de $G_n$ via celle de $G_n^+$” | No count or date. The first subscript could be $i$. The compressed group after “i.e.” also permits “d'la” and “d'une”. |

The other sheets carry no explicit date or authorial page count. The small lower-corner figures are archive folio numbers. They are recorded as comments in the page files.
