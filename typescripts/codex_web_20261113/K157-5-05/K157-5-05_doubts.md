## Legibility

The distinction between a baseline letter, a prime and a lower index is usually visible. The greatest risks are the inner letters of short words, vowel accents and grammatical endings that disappear into an exit stroke. A faint tick can turn a plain connector into an arrow. Compare the two shafts on 070 and 071 and the separate β expressions on 074.

The proof reference on 069, the old wording behind the example on 072, the phrase above the two cases on 076 and several cancellations on 080–082 are especially uncertain. On 084 the rotated marginal note can be followed through Cof 3. In the central cancellation one estimated word remains unreadable, and the possible stable or stables is precarious. The repeated live (4.1) is clearer than the overwritten circled letters below it.
