## Legibility

The path diagrams on 124 and the two separate numerals dix and six are comparatively legible. The most difficult ink occurs under cancellations on 115 and 127, in the adjusted triangle labels on 126, in the raised object and arrow labels of the localization formulas, and at the intersections of rotated marginalia and running text on 125 and 129–130. The two unreadable spans are confined to struck text. Near-horizontal pen strokes sometimes stop with a vertical nib and sometimes form an arrowhead. The register records the distinction instead of supplying direction from the argument.
