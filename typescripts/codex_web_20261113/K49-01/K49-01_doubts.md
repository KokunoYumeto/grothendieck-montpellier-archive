## Legibility

The theorem statements, displayed maps and the last sentence can mostly be followed through continuous ink. Their subscripts, the compressed ends of words, and the direction of detached accents are less secure. The least recoverable writing is under the crossings in the theorem on scan 7, inside Hom on scan 5, and in the abandoned pullback calculation on scan 9. The long rotated note on scan 3 is more legible in its middle than at its foot. A candidate beneath a strike represents the readable traces of an abandoned form.
