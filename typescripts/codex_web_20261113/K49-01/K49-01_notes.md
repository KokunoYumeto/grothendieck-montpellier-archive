# Manuscript notes

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

| Scan | Words on the sheet | What is left open |
|---|---|---|
| 003, bracket after the translation formula | “c’est un hom de schémas en groupes (réf)” | The parenthesis appears to call for a reference. Its abbreviation is doubtful. |
| 004, left margin of the lemma | “mettre aussi le cas où l’hypo. noeth. … peut-être sera remplacée par l’hypothèse que Y … est local à radical nilpotent” | The marginal note proposes replacing a hypothesis in the flatness lemma. The crossings and the word read as *peut-être* are marked in the transcription. |
| 006, Remarque | “J’ignore si le corollaire reste valable sans supposer A projectif sur S”; “j’ignore si … existe dans ce cas” | He leaves open the preceding corollary without projectivity, the existence of the Picard scheme in that case, and its simplicity along the unit section. The word immediately before *simple* is doubtful. |
| 007, theorem | “un hom de S-Groupes” followed by a small curled sign | One reading of the sign is a question mark. Its position is clear, but its identity is too uncertain to count as a definite reservation. |
| 008, after the lemma | “C’est connu, et sera prouvé plus bas” | The proof that a translation-invariant divisor class is algebraically equivalent to zero is deferred. |

## Title sheets, dates and page counts

Scan 002 is the cover. It bears the underlined title *Picard des schèmas abèliens*, with the acute spellings *schémas* and *abéliens* also possible. There is no date or stated page count on the cover or the eight following leaves. The small corner numerals 1–9 run from scan 002 to scan 010. They are recorded in LaTeX comments as archive sheet numbers. No separate manuscript pagination is visible.
