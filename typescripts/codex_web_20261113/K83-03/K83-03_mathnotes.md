# K83-03 · Mathematical notes

## What the pages do

**079.** A cancelled diagram links the six named terms Totalité, simplicité, Unité, Structure, Causalité and Ordre. Each has a separate IV′ number. The strokes have no arrowheads. The diagram supplies no proposition to read into the links.

**081–082.** For a rank-four Azumaya algebra the text records trace, determinant, a Hamilton-Cayley identity and the formula for the determinant of a sum. A remark compares the latter two identities by polarization. The next sheet takes trace and determinant of 1, writes a relation for \(uv+vu\), and relates it in the sloping NB to conjugation \(u\mapsto\operatorname{Tr}(u)1-u\).

**082–083.** The proposition says that \(1,u,v,uv\) span a subalgebra. After a cancelled product-by-product attempt, the new argument tests stability under left multiplication by \(u,v\). The two square relations dispose of several terms. The anticommutator rewrites \(vu\); right multiplication by \(v\) then handles \(vuv\).

**084–086.** The five parameters are the two traces \(b,b'\), the two determinants \(c,c'\), and \(t=\operatorname{Tr}(uv)\). Underlined generators name a formal rank-four algebra \(M\). The notes describe extending trace and determinant, then reduce the verification of identities to invertible discriminants. A lemma varies a conjugate of \(v\) and records dominance of the resulting trace map.

**086–088.** Two degree-two divisors on the projective scheme associated with the algebra give an anharmonic parameter \(\lambda\), locally defined up to inversion. Its symmetric expression \(\rho=\lambda+\lambda^{-1}\) descends. Scan 088 gives first the calculation for zero traces, then (11), its characteristic-two specialization, and the shifted invariant \(\rho_0=\rho-2\).

**089–091.** To realize a prescribed mixed trace, the author introduces the roots of the equation in \(t\). Replacing \(u\) by \(b1-u\) exchanges \(\tau\) with \(bb'-\tau\). Scan 090, physically between the start and end of this calculation, records (11′) and the sufficient Azumaya condition that \(\delta\delta' Q(t)\) be a unit. Scan 091 asks for the converse under the unit hypothesis on the two discriminants.

**091–092.** The discriminant of the quadratic \(Q\) factors as \(\delta\delta'\). The notes pass from an abandoned implication involving \(2t-bb'\) to the quadratic étale cover of solutions of \(Q=0\). The tentative corollary concerns two generators lying in a Borel subalgebra.

**093.** A multiplication table precedes two versions of a four-by-four trace-pairing matrix, the earlier one crossed out. A separate three-by-three array is labelled with a product of traces. Its lower-left entry is less legible than its upper-right counterpart.

**094.** The reused typescript discusses how words and concepts become familiar and concrete. The relative abstraction of “nombre”, “deux” and “trois” leads into the example of the mathematician, the pantry and the world market in grain. Local revisions and the diagonal cancellation both remain visible. The argument breaks off mid-sentence.

**095.** The author seeks an everywhere nonzero element orthogonal to \(1,u,v\). He places the trace discriminant \(-2Q\) alongside a reduced discriminant \(-Q\). The final lines pass to an isotropic trace-zero line and its association with a Borel.

## Remarks of the model

- **M01 · high.** Taking the ordinary polynomial discriminant of the displayed monic \(Q\) in \(t\) gives exactly \((b^2-4c)(b'^2-4c')\), as in (13). The check is a polynomial expansion with independent indeterminates.
- **M02 · high.** In (11), subtracting twice the denominator from the expanded numerator yields \(\delta\delta'\). This independently recovers the numerator in (11′), including the sign of the \(16cc'\) term.
- **M03 · high.** The determinant of the upper-left \(3\times3\) trace block on 093 is \(-2Q\) in the order \(1,u,v\). This is the unbracketed formula on 095.
- **M04 · low for the convention.** For the quadratic norm on \(x1+yu+zv\), the diagonal coefficients are \(1,c,c'\) and the mixed coefficients are \(b,b',bb'-t\). Half the determinant of its polar matrix, computed first over the integers, is \(-Q\). Identifying this precise convention with the manuscript's “réduit” is less certain because the convention is not defined there.
- **M05 · high.** In the root algebra of \(Q\), \((2t-bb')^2=\delta\delta'\). This gives a precise restriction under which the expression in the cancelled (14) is a unit.
- **M06 · high for the two computed determinants.** With the (4,4) entry exactly as in the new matrix on 093, its determinant is \(-Q^2-2cc'Q\). With the entry calculated from the multiplication table it is \(-Q^2\). The cofactor at (4,4) is the trace discriminant in M03.
- **M07 · high.** The relation on 087 gives \(\rho-2=(\lambda-1)^2/\lambda\) for invertible \(\lambda\). It relates the excluded cross-ratio value directly to the shift used on 088.
- **M08 · low for the intended identification.** The additive gloss beside \(M=k[u]\oplus k[v]\) on 085 is compressed. For the natural sum of the two displayed *subalgebras* in a nonzero unital algebra, \((1,-1)\) goes to zero. An abstract identification of free modules could be intended.
- **Reading question · scan 082, above L7.** The fixed text has “on trouve” and records the reference to \(6''\) inside the sloping NB. A separate small brace and what looks like \((6'')\) stand above “trouve”, near the end of a marginal line. Their exact attachment is unresolved. That separate occurrence is not encoded in the fragment.
- **Reading question · scan 094, around “Ainsi”.** The fixed text uses a doubtful paragraph sign in the margin. The ink itself is a double angular pen mark, with a similar mark immediately before the typed “Ainsi”. Its exact shape and the relation between the two strokes are less certain than the position of the paragraph break.
