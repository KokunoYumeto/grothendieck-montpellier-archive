## Legibility

The cover and most of the typescript can be read stroke by stroke. The poorest evidence is the wording underneath the zigzag in the chapter title on 003, the middle and ending of its §9, and the order of the superimposed division letters on 005. The right-margin query after corps on 005 and several earlier characters beneath pen corrections on 004 and 006 also have low confidence. Mirrored type shows through the manuscript faces. The facing, right-reading type is transcribed on 004 and 006. Each proposed reading under a cancellation remains marked in place when its strokes are uncertain.
