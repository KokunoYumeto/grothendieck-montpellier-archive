# Notes on the manuscript sheets

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **Scan 158, between M′ and Proposition 4.3.** After listing the axioms obtained so far, the author writes « il manque les axiomes de stabilité M3, M4 » and also names the M″ axioms. This marks what is still missing at that point.
- **Scan 163, below (4.4.2.).** The author changes “deux” to “un” in « Il y a … un problème… seulement ». He identifies the outstanding step in the words « Il faut la factorisation pour le couple » followed by `(TC,F=(TC)_*)`. The argument that follows states consequences once that factorization is available. Its construction is still open at the last line of this selection.

On scan 148, « Il faut prouver l’inégalité similaire » introduces the calculation immediately below. On 156, « Reste à prouver » introduces the last implication, completed on 157. The possible question mark in the number following “Lemme 3.” on 149 belongs to a label. I could identify no further explicit reservation such as « à vérifier » or « sauf erreur ».
