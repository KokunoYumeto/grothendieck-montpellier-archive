# Mathematical notes on scans 59–72

## What the pages do

**The cancelled kernel argument (59).** A map out of a Hom complex into cycles is followed by a diagram of Hom categories, a vanishing condition in degree one and a restriction map involving P₀. A diagonal cancels the whole attempt. Its labels distinguish several kinds of exactness. Their small suffixes remain doubtful.

**Building an exact category from complexes (60–62).** The successive short sequences link Ψⁱ, ℂⁱ and Ψⁱ⁺¹. The functor sending L to its chain complex is described first on finite sums. Axiom d) calls for sums amalgamated and fiber products of short sequences. Axiom e) calls for successive extensions by the Ψⁱ. The remark on 62 separates the inclusion of a kernel from the induced map A′→B. This leads to a revised universal property with a left exactness condition on morphisms.

**Relative resolutions and evaluation (63–65, opening).** In P₁ the text identifies generators for the relative projectives and injectives. It chooses a right resolution of Ψ⁰, discusses the homotopy class of a comparison between resolutions, and applies the resolution to a functor F. The notation Hⁱ(F) is proposed for RⁱF(Ψ⁰). The last line of 64 and first line of 65 identify the complex F(ℂ•) used to compute this value.

**A product on the categories (65).** The half-simplicial realization gives a tensor product taken component by component. One formula compares the tensor square of the unit with the unit. A second expresses the product indexed by i and j in terms of i+j and an object of K. The text transports the product to the other realizations and specifies its notation there.

**Multiplicative functors (66–67).** A map F(X)⊗F(Y)→F(X⊗Y) and a map from the target unit into F of the source unit are required to satisfy three diagrams. The page treats associativity, the unit, and symmetry separately. The following page draws the consequence for ring objects and describes composition of such functors. Specializing the source to K gives a proposed meaning for a multiplicative structure on a cochain complex.

**The maps on components (68–69, upper part).** The product datum is written using the generating objects, then as a map into a Hom complex and as a paired tensor map. The variants labelled (1*) and (2*) involve exterior powers. The diagram on 69 displays the differential in one index. The subsequent condition mentions unit, associativity and commutativity. The upper-right exponent and several overwritten terms in the diagram are unresolved.

**Recovering the unit (69–71).** Under “Remords” the author observes that K has no unit for *. He passes to P₀ or Q, and later to P for targets with kernels. The short sequences at the foot of 70 lead to a kernel formula. A square then relates a proposed product at X,Y to one known at I,J. At the foot of 71 the remaining unit datum lands in degree-zero cycles. The sideways note formulates it as a map into C⁰ whose composite into C¹ vanishes.

**Application up to homotopy (72).** A totalized product resolution gives a comparison map up to homotopy. Composing the maps in the diagram gives a product on C• with associativity, commutativity and a unit up to homotopy. The page ends with the resulting graded algebra on H*(C•) and an explicit reminder about signs.

## Slips

- **SL1, scan 65, paragraph on the ideal.** The text says “l’autre aussi”. In the stated ideal condition, the expected conclusion is *le produit aussi*. On its face the written clause asserts membership of the other factor. Confidence medium. The two words are legible, whereas the immediately preceding correction makes the author’s intended syntax less certain.
- **SL2, scan 71, first display.** The left argument is X in F(X), followed by L in R⁰F(L). If these are evaluations on the object L introduced on 70, the left side would be F(L). Confidence low. The X is heavily inked, and the following square introduces X and Y.

## Remarks of the model

- **M1. Model observation, high confidence, scan 66.** Both routes around the associativity diagram begin at (F(X)⊗F(Y))⊗F(Z) and end at F(X⊗(Y⊗Z)). The position of the right-hand associator can be checked by following the parentheses at those two vertices. This checks the types of the displayed routes.
- **M2. Model observation, high confidence, scan 71.** In the square, the monomorphism on the right accounts for uniqueness of a filling arrow once its composite with that side is prescribed. Existence also requires the composite along the left and bottom to factor through the right side. That distinction matters for the author’s “détermination unique de ?”.
- **M3. Model observation, medium confidence, scans 71–72.** The sideways note specifies the degree-zero unit by a map to C⁰ with zero differential. The application on the next page asks for a unit up to homotopy before passing to cohomology. These are two separate uses of the unit in the text. The precise ending of the marginal explanation remains unclear.
