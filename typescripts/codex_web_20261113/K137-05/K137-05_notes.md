# Notes on the manuscript

## Steps the pages mark as unverified

| Scan | Author’s words or mark | Step at issue |
|---:|---|---|
| 59 | “Il faut prouver que …”, in the cancelled opening | The existence of a Σ-kernel for the displayed Hom map is a proof obligation in the abandoned draft. |
| 60 | “On peut voir que le … est universel” | The universality of the tuple is asserted in parentheses. The short slanted note ending in “pas a)” is too uncertain to attach a definite objection to it. |
| 61 | “Il doit y avoir assez maintenant pour conclure que le foncteur …” | Whether the hypotheses established so far suffice for the claimed description of the chain-complex functor. |
| 64 | “[on doit pouvoir montrer que ce sont les dérivés …” | The proposed identification of the right derived functors of evaluation. Near the foot the ink also admits “sauf erreur”. The corresponding reading question is recorded in the mathematical notes. |
| 69 | “Remords … n’a pas d’unité pour *! … Comment s’en tirer?” | The proposed definition within K alone has no unit object at which to impose its unit condition. |
| 71 | “?” above the upper arrow, then “d’où la détermination unique de ?” | The map on the general pair is initially unspecified. The text argues for its uniqueness and then says “Il reste donc à examiner l’axiome d’unité”. |
