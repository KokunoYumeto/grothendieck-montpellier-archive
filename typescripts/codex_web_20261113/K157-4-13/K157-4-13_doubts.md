## Legibility

The headings, most displayed equations and the two sets of implication and pushout diagrams are easier to follow than the short prose between them. The cancelled start of the recursion on 196–197 and the qualification after the exactness condition on 201 are especially compressed. On 202 the two marginal notes are legible only in their own orientation. On 209 the diagonal objection and the horizontal conclusion share strokes. The objection about the $C_i$ is substantially clearer than its last words. Terminal pen strokes in prose and arrowheads at the ends of short shafts remain recurrent sources of uncertainty.
