# Mathematical notes · cote 157-4, scans 196–211

## What the pages do

**196–198 · Intersection of cofinal parts.** Lemma 5.15 concerns a π-adapted filtered ordered set $I$ and a family of π-cofinal subsets $I_\alpha$ with $\operatorname{card}A\leq\pi$. To prove their intersection cofinal he passes to $I/i_0$, well-orders the index set, and forms $J_\alpha=\bigcap_{\beta<\alpha}I_\beta$. The successor step uses 5.5. At a limit he constructs an increasing sequence of elements and takes its supremum. The cancelled induction at the beginning of 197 precedes this version.

**198–199 · Finite unions and small intersections.** For $M=\bigcup_\alpha M_\alpha$ with finitely many terms, he relates membership to the cofinality of $\bigcup_\alpha I_{M_\alpha}$. Lemma 5.14 supplies a cofinal member of the finite union. Corollary 5.16 gives finite unions and small intersections of L-accessible parts of $M$.

**199–201 · The filtration on a functor category.** Section 6 puts $M^\pi(S)=\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(S,M^\pi)$ inside $M(S)$. He takes $\pi\geq c=2^{c_0}$, where $c_0$ is the supremum of $\pi_1$ and the cardinality of the arrows of $S$. With $S=\Delta^1$ the category is the arrow category and the displayed bound becomes $\pi\geq 2^{\pi_1}$. He then lists the accessibility notions for classes of arrows and singles out the intersection $C\cap W$.

**202–204 · Proposed generation theorem and corollaries.** Under a)–e), the theorem as amended considers an L-accessible class $C$ of monomorphisms containing the isomorphisms. It proposes a small $C_0\subset C$ with $C\subset\widetilde{C^\pi}=\widetilde C_0$. Corollaries 6.2 and 6.3 concern the associated classes, closure under cobase change and composition or amalgamated sums, and the resulting Quillen pair. The small implication diagram leaves $c'\Rightarrow a$ to be established. The margin of 202 already doubts the theorem.

**204–206 · Transfinite reduction to Lemma 6.4.** Starting at $A=A_0\subset B$, he seeks strictly increasing $A_\alpha$, with a filtered inductive limit at a limit ordinal. For a nonisomorphism $A\hookrightarrow B$ in $C$, Lemma 6.4 asks for a strictly larger $A_1\subset B$ such that $A\to A_1$ is in $\widetilde{C^\pi}$ and $A_1\to B$ is in $C$. He argues conditionally that the set of subobjects of $B$ bounds the possible stages.

**206–208 · Small approximations and an extra exactness condition.** Taking small $B_i\subset B$, he writes $A_i=B_i\cap A$ and $f_i:A_i\to B_i$. The supremum calculation for $A_{i_J}$ leads to condition e′ on filtered inductive limits and base change. With this condition he selects $B'$ outside $A$, with $A'=B'\cap A$ and $A'\to B'$ in $C^\pi$. He then argues that the family $J$ of such choices is π-cofinal. The last sentence continues on 209.

**209–210 · Pushout calculation and the marginal objection.** The first square forms $C'=A\amalg_{A'}B'$. Its map from $A$ has the desired closure property. For the map from $C'$ to $B$, he returns to an indexed square whose right-hand map is $\gamma_i:C_i\to B$. The displayed calculation ends at $A\amalg_A B=B$. He tries to extract a cofinal family with $\gamma_i\in C$. The diagonal objection on 209 and its reference on 210 qualify the apparent conclusion of the proof.

**210–211 · Closed Quillen triples and the remaining question.** Section 7 opens with $C=\mathrm{Mon}\subset\mathrm{Cof}_W$. He proposes to test accessibility and generation for $\mathrm{Cof}_W$ and $W$ in $\mathrm{Cat}$, especially for $W=W_\infty$. He asks if $C\cap W$ again has a small generating family and asks for a counterexample. The packet ends as he announces a positive result.

## Remarks of the model

- **M1 · Scan 209. Model check, high confidence.** The displayed last identity $A\amalg_A B\cong B$ uses the identity map on the $A$-leg. The universal property of the pushout gives the canonical identification referred to alongside $\mathrm{id}_B$. This checks that last identity alone. The background property used is B2.
- **M2 · Scans 202 and 206. Model check, high confidence about the two wordings.** On 206 the membership of $B_i\cap A$ in $\mathrm{Filt}^\pi$ is attributed to « sous-objets d’objets de » that class and « condition d) ». The d) written on 202 specifies colimits of bounded size and « tout quotient » of an object in the class. Subobject closure would need a further justification from the hypotheses.
- **M3 · Scans 209–210. Model check, high confidence about the location of the objection.** The starred sentence asserts adaptedness for $(\gamma_i)$. The next leaf uses accessibility to infer cofinality. The marginal sentence about $C_i\in\mathrm{Filt}^\pi$ identifies missing information about the individual stages. The calculation of their limit in M1 leaves that question open.
- **Reading question · Scan 197, last line, continued on 198. Low confidence in any change.** The frozen reading is « chaque $I_{\beta_0}$ ». The next leaf uses $J_{\beta_0}$, which suggests comparing the two capitals. The last line of 197 has a straight cap-and-foot letter. The lower curl of $J$ is more distinct in the next line on 198.
- **Reading question · Scan 207, last two lines. Low confidence in any change.** The frozen reading of (e₀) is « dans $I$ ». One might expect $M$ from « sur $M$ » above, but the letter after « dans » has the two horizontal terminals of $I$.

B1 is the cardinal bound from an injection into a set. B2 is the universal property of a pushout. The event register identifies where each was used. The account of the author’s attempted theorem records his argument and his marginal reservations.
