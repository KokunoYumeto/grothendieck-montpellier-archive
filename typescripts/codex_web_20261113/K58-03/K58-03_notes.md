# Editorial and physical notes

## Steps the pages mark as unverified

- **23.** A separate pencilled “?” is aligned with the hypotheses of Théorème 2, beside (a). Its scope may include the block marked by the adjacent vertical rule.
- **25.** “Le lemme 4 nous ramène alors à vérifier le / Lemme 5” introduces the local task that the next leaves take up. “une réduction facile (?)” accompanies the reduction to the formally equidimensional case. “expliciter” stands to the left of the revised quotation. “à énoncer … avant le th” is an editorial instruction with an uncertain opening.
- **27.** “Généraliser au cas d'un morphisme” is followed by a second note asking “Peut-on généraliser de façon analogue le th. local ?” The following justification is partly indistinct.
- **33.** The marginal “Question” asks whether the named Lefschetz condition implies the depth bound for points whose closure misses Y. The writer gives the bound three question marks. The abbreviation for the condition is difficult to distinguish. Higher on the leaf, “inutile” stands beside the inserted degree-\(n+1\) clause.
- **34.** “Question : Si … est un isom pour … est-il vrai que … est un mono … ?” is followed on the same leaf by “Oui, prendre …” and a compressed argument. The separate N.B. below the main display is crossed through. The side note beginning “Le même argument marche” remains live.
