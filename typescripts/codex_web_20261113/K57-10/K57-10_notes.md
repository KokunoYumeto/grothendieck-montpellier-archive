# Documentary notes on cote 57

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **Scan 173, beside Proposition 1 and Corollaire 1.** The left margin asks to “expliciter les objets” and to “mettre … sous forme d’une suite exacte plutôt”. Lower down, a question mark precedes “ou une proposition préliminaire”. The intervening words are difficult. These are questions about arranging the argument.
- **Scan 175, after the gluing argument.** “Question : donner une formulation cohomologique des raisonnements.” The two slanted notes also ask for “terminologie à introduire” and say “il faut harmoniser avec la démonstratⁱᵒⁿ de … (langage de la descente)”. The reference between those phrases is uncertain.
- **Scan 178, bracket following the definition of Pic.** “Il y a un ennui” introduces the difficulty of comparing an open cover of a non-quasi-compact prescheme with a single faithfully flat quasi-compact morphism. The underlined replacement is “morphismes de localisation fidèlement plats”.
- **Scan 179, example and last paragraph.** “On verra” postpones an obstruction assertion. The cases of representability “seront déterminés par la suite”. Neither discussion is supplied on these sheets.
