## What the pages do

**Cube edges and face sums (209, 211).** The small and large perspective drawings distinguish four upper edge labels and their displaced partners. The large drawing relates the side labels $u,v$ to $b,d$. Scan 211 chooses $a,b,c,d,a',u$, writes $\alpha=a'-a$, and expresses six face sums in those variables. A six-column matrix collects their coefficients. Four five-by-five determinants complete the expansion at the foot. [M209, M211]

**Three edge differences (213, 215).** A second drawing labels vertices $A,B,C,A',B',C'$, with Latin and Greek edge labels. On 213 two chains of differences introduce $\delta'$ and $\delta''$. Scan 215 supplies a third chain, compares pairs of expressions, and gives three half-sum formulas for $\delta,\delta',\delta''$. Its last line labels the individual differences beneath horizontal strokes. [M213, M215, S215]

**Two triples of edges and two triples of face sums (217).** Six $\sigma$ expressions have ordered endpoint subscripts. The arrows beside each triple record a cycle. Beneath them, a line ending « sur les arêtes d'une face » introduces six $\varphi$ expressions. The unprimed rows are at left. The primed rows and the heavily cancelled two-line comment are at right. [M217]

**Block determinant (219).** The six columns are headed $a,b,c,a',b',c'$. Rules separate the matrix into four square blocks. The sheet passes to a two-by-two determinant, calculates $27^2-1$, and factors the result. A separate three-by-three determinant calculation and a long multiplication appear below. [M219a, M219b]

**Opposite edges (221).** The page sets $A=a+a'=\lambda$, $B=b+b'=\mu$, $C=c+c'=\nu$. The adjoining note refers to three directions of edges. A list for $A',B',C'$ and a small unlabelled drawing follow. The bottom line compares the sum of the two $\varphi$'s indexed by $a,a'$ with $2(\mu+\nu)$. Several joins in the expanded expression have ambiguous cross-strokes. [M221]

**The SGA correspondence (216, 214, 210, typescript pp. 450–452).** On p. 450 the author recounts a letter about the authorship of SGA 5 and describes the two-page letter he wrote to K.F. Springer about two *Lecture Notes* volumes. Page 451 reproduces Springer's brief reply in French, marked « (traduite) », and considers a lawsuit. On p. 452 he asks how readers, pupils and friends regard his part in the SGA. [T450]

**The reflective typescript (222, 220, 218, 212, typescript L 19–L 22).** L 19 and L 20 describe what the author sees as a loss of respect for parts of mathematical discovery and explain how he came to write *Récoltes et Semailles*. L 21 turns that assessment into a question about his own responsibility. L 22 describes how asking it changed the character of his testimony. [TL19]

## Remarks of the model

- **Model remark, high confidence (209, M209).** Moving all terms to the left gives the same linear expression for the boxed relation and each of the next two equations.
- **Model remark, high confidence (211, M211).** Collecting the six face sums in the stated order of variables reproduces every matrix row. The four displayed minors have determinants $256,-256,256,-256$ in page order. With the written alternating signs their sum is $1024$, also the determinant of the full matrix.
- **Model remark, high confidence (213, 215, M213, M215).** The two expressions for $\alpha$ and the two for $\beta'$ on 213 give the same difference $a'-a$. Substituting the three live half-sum formulas on 215 returns the three pairwise differences in order. Their total gives $\delta+\delta'+\delta''=(\varepsilon_1+\varepsilon_2+\varepsilon_3)/2$.
- **Model remark, high confidence for the transcribed entries (217, 219, M217).** Taking the coefficients of the three unprimed face formulas and the three simplified primed formulas at the right gives exactly the six rows written on 219, in the column order $a,b,c,a',b',c'$. This comparison uses the live numeral in the first cell.
- **Model remark, low confidence as an attribution (219, R219).** With the entries as transcribed, the diagonal three-by-three block $A$ has $\det A=27$ and $A^2=9I$. Direct integer evaluations give $\det(A+I)=16$, $\det(A-I)=32$, and the full determinant $512$. Both 512 and 728 are relatively prime to 3. The uncertainty concerns the overwritten entry and the intended reduction.
- **Model remark, low confidence as a sign-slip claim (217, 221, M221).** Adding the two $a$-indexed expressions from 217 gives $2(b+b'+c+c')$, hence $2(\mu+\nu)$ with the definitions at the top of 221. The first visible horizontal before $c$ in the middle expression on 221 is a minus. The join before $c'$ is marked doubtful. The formulas on 217 support a plus at both places, but the strokes on 221 do not justify changing the text.
