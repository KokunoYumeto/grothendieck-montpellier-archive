## Legibility

The ruled geometry pages have well-separated main lines. The finer work is in their interlines, particularly a deliberately transposed pair of factors, the subscripts in the second face table and the right edge of the principal-bundle note. On the cover the loan description and surname are very faint. The inverted pencil work on 14 and the pale proof on 16 have long cancellations, and V/W and the tiny indices are hard to separate. The sketch leaf on 17 is least secure as continuous prose: several words physically intersect much darker lines. The TikZ curves approximate the intersecting layers. The colour, order of the strokes and exact pencil pressure cannot be recovered from those traces.
