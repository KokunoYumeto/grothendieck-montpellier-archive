### Comparison across the eight sheets

The looped family L has a baseline foot in the definitions on scans 2–5, in the raised qualification on scan 6, and in the hypotheses on scans 7–9. The closed oval on a staff before the equivalence on scan 6, after *dans* on scan 8, and inside the support bars on scan 9 is recorded separately at those locations. The fork of Ψ can be seen next to the family L on scans 7–9. The angular, long-stemmed P before a parenthesis recurs on scans 2, 6, 7 and 8. The written headings on these sheets use their own ordinary text capitals. The retraced B on scan 3 and the overwritten capitals on scans 5, 6 and 9 have been checked individually.

The small raised rings on A and B continue from scans 2–8. The family prime on scans 2–5 is a short right-shoulder tick; the dark multiarmed exponent in scan 2 is recorded separately. The last condition label on scans 2–4 retains η as its first reading. The map on scans 5–6 has a long stem; the single ended stroke in its introduction on scan 5 contrasts with the visible heads in the adjacent point-map and on scan 6. The comparative indices in the last display of scan 5, the order criterion on scan 6 and the scratched line on scan 8 have each been read locally. The round and squared union at the bottom of scan 5 share one index. The ink resembling an equals sign in running French was checked at each appearance from scans 5–9; in the slanted note on scan 2 and the lower note on scan 8 the manuscript has paired quotation strokes.
