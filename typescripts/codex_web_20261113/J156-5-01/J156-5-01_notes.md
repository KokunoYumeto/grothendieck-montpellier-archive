# Notes on the manuscript

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- Scan 4. Two visible question marks follow the cancelled marginal condition “si A ∈ ℒ ⇒ A ≠ ∅ ??”. The oblique note on the two crossed examples begins “Exemple idiot” (second word uncertain) and refers to GF VIII, pp. 44, 45.
- Scan 8. The paragraph ending “Reste à prouver b ⇒ c, c ⇒ a” identifies two outstanding proof steps at that point. The proof of the first begins immediately below.
- Scan 9. “Il reste à prouver que si …” introduces the remaining union condition. The last line runs on beyond this sheet.

No explicit reservation was found on scans 2, 3, 5, 6 or 7.
