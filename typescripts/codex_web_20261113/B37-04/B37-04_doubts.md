# Legibility

Most live typescript is clear. The least secure readings are letters hidden by overtyping, the clipped right-edge word in the letter, small handwritten quote/separator/index forms, the Q on scan 69, and the joined x/se on scan 72. Marked alternatives represent unresolved readings. The two small non-text shapes are approximated in TikZ; the origin of the lower-right curve on scan 74 remains uncertain.

All 36 supplied images were personally viewed. No OCR, text layer, text-recognition library, external transcription or web source was used. No compilation was attempted. The audit records actual timestamps and changes, including correction of my initial misreading sobré to the image's sobre.
