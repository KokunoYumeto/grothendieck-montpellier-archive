## Legibility

The underlined category names, the three-way comparison at the end and the page references in the index are generally distinguishable. Thin connectors often stop in a tiny upstroke that could be part of an arrowhead. The most fragile readings are the two end objects written over in the first Ch expression, the slanted notes on 058 and 064, the cancelled category labels on 061, and the earlier version of the corollary on 066. In that corollary the newer lines occupy gaps in a cancelled paragraph. Its earlier wording and newer wording are kept in separate runs at the same textual location. One blacked-out word under “cor.” is physically unreadable. The light, reversed index entries on 055 are show-through from the sheet read on 056.
