## Legibility and limits

Most running type is readable. The least secure parts are typed-over cells, words cancelled with x, very small cursive insertions, the determinant filling on 061, the crossed interline's anchor on 066, and the displaced marks in the middle category name on 068. Several right-edge strokes are clipped. Every recovered word or symbol is transcribed, with uncertainty in place where the ink does not decide; there are no illegible-word placeholders. This is a visual diplomatic reading with unresolved alternatives, not a claim of error-free reconstruction. No OCR, external transcription, or LaTeX compilation was used. Blank source spaces and sentences continuing beyond the supplied sheets have not been filled.
