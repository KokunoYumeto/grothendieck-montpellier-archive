# Notes on scans 28–34

## Steps marked as unverified

None explicitly marked. On scan 33, in the proof under the second *Lemme VII*, the writer uses tentative wording for the existence of an open set, “on doit pouvoir trouver un ouvert”. The next insertion reads “auquel t appartient”.

## Title sheets, dates and page counts

Title sheets, dates and stated page totals: none. The manuscript numbers in the upper-right corners are V on scan 29, VI on scan 31, VII on scan 33 and VIII on scan 34. Scans 28, 30 and 32 have no upper-right page number. Both lemma headings on scan 33 read VII. The separate pencilled archive numbers run from 27 on scan 28 through 33 on scan 34.
