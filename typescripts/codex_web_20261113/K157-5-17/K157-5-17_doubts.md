## Legibility

The main argument, page numbers, section headings and directions of most arrows are readable. The least secure continuous passage is the long slanting comment on scan 261, especially the clause before the first Filt and the sentence through the cancellation after the second Filt. On 268 and 269 the margins can be turned into reading position, but rulings and neighboring lines still reach into the writing. Several overwritten indices retain little more than the cardinal’s roof. Small word endings, the citation on 270, the old labels on 271 and the cancelled construction on 276 are also weak. The doubt counts describe editorial marks, not an estimate of the number of errors. The recorded second-reading intervals were shorter than the several minutes per sheet envisaged in the brief. The enlarged details and subsequent collation are identified separately in the audit.
