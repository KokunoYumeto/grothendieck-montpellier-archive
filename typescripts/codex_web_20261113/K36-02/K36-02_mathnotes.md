# Mathematical notes on cote 36, scans 38–52

The scan numbers below refer to the archive sequence. Formula and proposition numbers belong to the sheets. A description of a claim reports its status on the sheet. Identifiers in square brackets refer to the limited checks recorded in CHECK_EVENTS.tsv.

## What the pages do

**Scan 38 · Exponentiating a derivation.** The reused typescript begins mid-sentence. For a nilpotent derivation \(u\) of a possibly nonassociative algebra \(E\), the expanded Leibniz formula reorganises the terms of \(e^u(xy)\) into \(e^u(x)e^u(y)\). The first sentence refers back to an automorphism of the underlying \(E\).

**Scan 38 · Definitions 5 and 6.** A special automorphism is \(e^{\mathrm{ad}\,x}\) for \(x\) in the nilpotent radical. A Levi subalgebra is a subalgebra supplementary to the radical. The last paragraph identifies such a complement with the quotient, then discusses semisimple subalgebras and images under surjections.

**Scans 39–40 · Two presentations of \(H^1\).** With \(X\) “propre et simple sur \(k\)” and \(A\) an abelian variety, the first chain rewrites an exact sequence using \(\mathrm{Alb}_X\), the dual of \({}_nA\), and \(\mathrm{Pic}_X\). The second applies Hom to a sequence on \(A'\). Two boxed limit sequences precede the canonical isomorphism \(H^1(X,A)\leftarrow\mathrm{Ext}^1(A',\underline{\mathrm{Pic}}_X)\) on 40. A second box gives the Hom comparison for “\(G\) commutatif affine”. The lower diagram relates the Albanese extension group to \(H^1(X,A)\) and its dual descriptions.

**Scan 41 · Relative comparisons.** Two boxes ask how \(\mathscr H^1(X/S;-)\) interacts with sheaf Hom and sheaf \(\mathrm{Ext}^1\) out of \(\gamma\). The first has a right-pointing comparison. The second is followed by a question about hypotheses. A small table over \(X\) and \(S\), an unfinished calculation and a separate vertical comparison occupy the lower half.

**Scan 42 · Cancelled birational argument.** A proper birational map \(f:X\to Y\) and an open \(U\) on which the inverse is defined give the square and the two identities \(gf=i,\ fg=j\). The draft discusses codimension under regularity in codimension one. It starts to associate a divisorial cycle on \(U\) to \(g^{-1}(\xi)\), for \(\xi\in\mathrm{Pic}(X)\), and ends mid-argument. The diagonal cancellation crosses the whole unit.

**Scan 43 · Higher-degree calculations.** A point marked “pt singulier” and two sketches accompany a return to the \(H^1\)/Pic comparison. Relative Ext expressions for \(f:X\to Y\) are followed by the case \(H=G_m\), with a higher Pic term circled. The rows indexed by \(0,1,2\) list possible terms. The degree-two row shows a four-stroke \(\#\) and a separate “!”. A final calculation for “\(G\) abélien” breaks off after an equals sign.

**Scan 44 · Adjunction.** “Foncteurs adjoints” opens a definition for a pair \(f,g\) between categories \(X,Y\). It stops at “s'il existe un”. Lower on the sheet are separate Hom and Ext calculations, a map \(X\times\gamma\to G\), the partly cancelled sketch below it and a line of maps beginning with \(e\to\gamma\).

**Scan 45 · The \(p\)-part.** The Artin–Schreier–Witt formulas describe classes fixed by \(F\), written \(Fx=x\). Several attempts to express a pairing are struck. The live lower boxes identify \(\pi_1^p(A)\) with \(T_p(A)\), pair \(T_p(A)\) with \(H^1(A,W)^{(p)}\) into \(\mathbb Z_p\), and give a Hom expression involving \(W_\infty(k)\). The sheet also uses a low \(s\) on \(H^1(A,W)\).

**Scan 46 · The part prime to \(p\).** The kernels \(A^{(n)}\) form an inverse system, with transition from \(mn\) to \(n\) by multiplication by \(m\). For \(\ell\ne p\) the sheet identifies \(T_\ell(A)\) with the corresponding fundamental group and a Hom group. The Kummer comparison and an explicit admitted hypothesis lead to a pairing of \(T_\ell(A)\) and \(T_\ell(A^*)\), with value group written \(T_\ell(U_\ell)\). Quotients and a finite-level pairing follow.

**Scans 47–48 · Restricting a biextension.** The two-line cover on 47 introduces biextensions of sheaves of groups. For \(P'\to P\to P''\) and \(Q\), scan 48 records a biextension \(E'\) on \(P'\times Q\) and a form \(\varphi\) on \({}_nP\times{}_nQ\). Their compatibility is the displayed restriction to \({}_nP'\times{}_nQ\). The author asserts an equivalence with pairs \((E',\varphi)\). A marginal hypothesis c) and substantial parts of the attempted verification are crossed out.

**Scans 48 and 50 · The corollaries.** The corollary at the foot of 48 begins “Supposons, pour un \(\mathbb P\) de nbs premiers”. Its sentence resumes at the head of 50 [M48-50]. A second corollary treats exact sequences in both variables and relates biextensions to their restrictions and a pairing on \(T_{\mathbb P}\). The proof applies the one-variable corollary successively. Two marginal notes specify divisibility conditions and the finite-level system to retain.

**Scan 49 · Sheafification, injectives and the spectral sequence.** Typescript page 15 compares the construction with a cited theorem, with a condition \(P(\emptyset)=0\). Formulas (21)–(23) concern the adjunction with sheafification \(a\), its evaluation and the case of zero associated sheaf. Proposition 2.2 and its proof concern injectives and the associated sheaves of the positive-degree cohomology presheaves. Proposition 2.3 displays a spectral sequence functorial in \(S,F\). Its total degree \(p+q\) is on the left.

**Scan 51 · Cohomology presheaves.** Typescript page 13 introduces \(\mathscr H^0\) from sheaves to presheaves and its right derived functors. Formula (12) evaluates them on \(X\). A second construction starts with \(L\) and gives the Čech functors. In (14) the left side was rewritten by hand. The final parenthesis indexes the direct limit by covering sieves of \(X\).

**Scan 52 · Two vanishing hypotheses.** Part a) compares global extensions with sections of the sheaf \(\underline{\mathrm{Ext}}^1(A,G)\) under the vanishing of a sheaf of group homomorphisms. Part b) uses morphisms preserving the unit section, glossed in the margin. A decomposition of \(H^1\) is followed by two descriptions of torsor classes in terms of rigidification along \(e_A\).

**Scan 52 · From extensions to torsors.** The forgetful functor is asserted to be fully faithful. An injection into \(H^1(A,G_A)\) and an essential-image condition follow. At the foot, three maps from \(A\times A\) to \(A\) accompany the diagram involving \(\underline{\mathrm{Ext}}^1\), a derived direct image, \(\underline{\mathrm{NS}}(A)\) and birigidified symmetric torsors. The superscript on the diagram's \(R\) admits both \(1\) and \(q\).

## Remarks of the model

- **[M38] High, finite coefficient check.** Iterating the displayed product rule in formal ordered factors gave 45 coefficients for \(0\le p\le8\). In every tested term the coefficient of \(u^r(x)u^s(y)\), divided by \(p!\), equalled \(1/(r!s!)\). The calculation checks the transition between the two sums over this finite range.
- **[M48] High, domains and target.** Pulling the first input of the displayed \(\varphi\) back along \({}_nP'\to{}_nP\) gives precisely the source \({}_nP'\times{}_nQ\) and target \(G\) of \(E'(n)\). The condition can therefore be read before settling whether the first \(n\) in hypothesis a) is a scalar or a left subscript. This check concerns the displayed condition only.
- **[M49] High, direction of the adjunction.** Formula (10) on 51 takes a sheaf to a presheaf. The line defining \(a\) on 49 sends a presheaf back to a sheaf. In (21) the composite \(a\mathscr H^0\) and the displayed identity both act on sheaves. This cross-check survives the reversed order of the two numbered sheets.
- **[M52] Medium, missing or implicit pullback.** In the first essential-image formula the fragment reads \(\operatorname{pr}_2(L)\). The next expression has \(\operatorname{pr}_2^*(L)^{-1}\). The comparison of those two occurrences suggests that pullback may be understood in the first. Treating the missing star as an authorial error would go beyond the evidence.
- **Reading question, scan 46, L31 [R46]. Medium.** The frozen alternatives are “et” and “cet” before “accouplement qui est une dualité”. On the individual crop, the small initial loop leads directly into the tall t. I favour “et”. The broader contextual crop ends on the preceding line.
- **Reading question, scan 52, L3 [R52]. Low.** The frozen title reads “Biextensions sous hyp. de rigidité”. An alternative is “sans hyp. de rigidité”. The underline passes through the middle of the short word. Its initial s and low final trace survive, but the interior does not securely distinguish ou from an on reinspection. The title in the transcription remains the independent reading.
- **Reading question, scan 52, L35 [R52p]. Low.** At the second $\pi$ the frozen superscript reads $*$ or $\alpha$. The individual crop shows a loop with a rising tail, whereas the adjacent pullback marks have small crossing strokes. The loop favours the alpha-shaped reading. Its mathematical meaning is open.
