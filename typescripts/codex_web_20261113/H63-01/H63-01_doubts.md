## Legibility and hand

Most of the English prose and the main coefficient columns are readable. The lowest-confidence passages are the pale exponents and differential in scan 4, the small cancellation and right edge in scan 5, the two solidly inked cancellations in scan 6, the formula overwritten by the last theorem in scan 7, and the mixed powers, overwritten parameters and small reference beside the difference quotients in scan 8. A doubtful mathematical fragment gives the visible traces and a proposed reading. The handwritten uncertainty “sauf erreur” is part of the source.

The notes form a continuous hand across the six sheets. The inventory records “other hand”. The supplied file description attributes the material to J. Tate. There is no signature on the six sheets. Lower-corner archival numerals are kept in comments.
