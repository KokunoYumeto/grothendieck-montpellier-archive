## Legibility

The body of the typescript is generally clear. The faint understrikes on ideals and number systems and the small circular indices need enlargement. The manuscript distinguishes ordinary E, the large upright I and most x subscripts well. I trust the overwritten factors of the trace formula on scan 45 least. The upper note on 39, the erased verb on 41, and the crossed final paragraph on 43 retain substantial uncertainty. The bent sign is drawn from the visible strokes in the LaTeX. No name for it is assumed. The register documents observations in reading order. Later entries refine earlier choices, and the table below gives the decision at every place still marked when the text was frozen.
