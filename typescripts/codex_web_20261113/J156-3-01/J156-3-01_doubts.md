## Legibility

The spaced writing usually leaves signs, word boundaries and corrections visible. The very short strokes within the words are less secure than the spacing suggests. On 002 the older phrase under the left heading, the short note under the pair condition and the overwritten verbs are particularly uncertain. On 003 the diagonal marginal argument intersects horizontal text in a different orientation, and several crossed words survive only in fragments. One word in the doubly cancelled sentence on 004 has no reliable reading. The rewritten letters in the displays on 005–006, the first enumeration index and outermost bracket on 007, the unfinished marginal sentence on 008 and the competing a/x shapes and struck words on 009 are the other places I trust least. The pale background lettering was read as visible fragments; its clipping and the reverse impressions reduce confidence at the edges.
