## Legibility

The large displays usually separate the object letters, and repeated drawings often settle which way an arrow points. The slanted note on scan 18 crosses a second sentence and a second set of strokes. The rotated notes on scan 19 are much clearer, although several letters still meet the main text. The tiny future-page reference on scan 25, the hatched passage on scan 29 and the scratched arrows and exponents on scan 33 are the least secure mathematical readings. A surviving underline, a crossing-out stroke and the edge of a bracket can be only a pen-width apart. The estimated word counts for the two unreadable cancellations are estimates of space, not recovered words.
