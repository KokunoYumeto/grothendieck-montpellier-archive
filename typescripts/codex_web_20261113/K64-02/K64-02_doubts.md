## Legibility

All thirteen sheets are manuscript notes in French. The formal displays, large capitals and the later counting columns are relatively distinct. The least secure readings are the words concealed by horizontal rules, the interlinear rewrites on scans 16, 21 and 23, the slanted margin on scan 18 and the crowded subscripts and addition signs on scans 26–27. A continuous nib stroke can stand for several letters. The marks identify specific uncertainties and do not measure the accuracy of the surrounding text.
