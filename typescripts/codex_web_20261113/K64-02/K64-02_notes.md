# Notes on the manuscript sheets

## Steps marked as unverified

- **Scan 16, final remark.** “qui mériterait d'être comprise” refers to the equivalence between squares and groups isomorphic to the dihedral group. Scan 17 takes up the construction from a group to a square.
- **Scan 22, lower right.** “Pour bien faire, il faudrait les … ds un diagramme d'inclusions, ou diagr. de morphismes d'espaces homogènes.” The missing verb in this short quotation has two candidate readings in the transcription. The proposed diagram would organize the subgroup list of scans 21–22.
- **Scans 23–24, across the page turn.** “Il y aurait lieu d'expliciter” introduces three correspondences for the oriented cube. The list continues with “et de décrire également une orientation canonique du cube”. The formulas on scans 24–25 develop the orientation.
- **Scan 25, main paragraph.** “Il y aurait lieu aussi d'interpréter les 10 classes d'isomorphie d'espaces homogènes” precedes the geometric inventory. The author ends it with “Le compte y est !”

## Title sheets, dates and page counts

There is no separate title sheet, date or statement of the total page count. The author’s folios 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 and 13 appear on scans 15, 17, 19, 21, 23, 25 and 27 respectively. The final 13 is encircled. The archive’s lower-corner numbers run from 14 to 26.

Scan 20 has the internal heading “3. Complément sur les …-ss-groupes” followed by the group on a four-element set. The numeral before “-ss-groupes” is doubtful. Scan 23 begins “4. Relations avec le cube orienté”. Scan 26 begins “5. Modules libres de rang 2 sur Λ = ℤ/4ℤ”.
