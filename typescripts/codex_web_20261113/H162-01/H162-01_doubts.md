## Legibility

The cover title, printed letterhead, the longer uncancelled entries and most of the barred numerals are comparatively clear. The least secure are the small formulae below Berthelot on scan 003, the overwritten Picard entries on scan 005, and the names under multiple crossing strokes on scan 006. The lower Saint Donat parenthesis on scan 007 and the retouched numbers and dark pencil crossings on scans 008–009 also admit competing readings. Four individual word positions remain unreadable, one each on scans 003, 005, 006 and 008. Each is marked at its position.
