# K8-01 · Mathematical notes

## What the pages do

**U1 · Removing the last derived tensor, scan 79.** The opening example takes coefficients in a field. In (1.4′) the small L remains over the tensor with base ℤ. The last tensor has base 𝔽ₚ and is written without L. The distinction also appears in the sentence following (1.8).

**U2 · Descent from a representation, scan 79.** A principal covering S′ of S with group π and a representation on M₀ give the setting for (1.6). The diagram places f from S to Bπ. Formula (1.7) writes M as the inverse image of M₀. After equipping the two topoi with the constant ring Λ, (1.8) uses pullback and a derived tensor with the structure sheaf on S.

**U3 · The tensor and Tor terms, scan 80.** The first brace expresses $\omega_G$ by a tensor product and $\underline{n}_G$ by a first Tor. The next brace treats characteristic p and distinguishes $M_p$ from ${}_pM$. When p annihilates M, (2.2′) gives the same expression for both terms.

**U4 · The degree-two classes, scans 80–81.** Scan 80 writes a class ξ in an Ext group over the structure sheaf and a class $\xi_\Lambda$ over the coefficient ring. For Λ = ℤ/nℤ and M locally free of finite type, the page places the classes in degree-two cohomology with endomorphism coefficients and sets $E=M\otimes_\Lambda\underline{O}_S$. The display at the head of 81 continues the coefficient comparison. The first connector and the exponent over the final H are both doubtful in the manuscript.

**U5 · The universal lifting problem, scan 81.** With the hypotheses of 1.5 and M₀ also flat over Λ, the text places a class ξ₀ on Bπ whose image is $\xi_\Lambda$. It turns to the universal automorphism group of M₀, attributes a lifting interpretation to Illusie, and compares rank at most one with larger rank. The final paragraph connects this question to cohomology on S via the universal covering.

## Questions and conjectures

**Q1 · Scan 81.** “mais sans doute pas nulle si M₀ de rang d ≥ 2, dans le cas universel”. The group at the end of the sentence is Gl(d, 𝔽ₚ). Grothendieck expects nonvanishing here. The page gives neither an example of a nonzero obstruction nor a calculation of this class.

## Remarks of the model

**R1 · Scan 80, S6. Model remark, high confidence for this finite example.** For M = (ℤ/4ℤ)², enumeration of its 16 elements gives kernel of multiplication by 4 equal to M and image equal to zero. The quotient by that image has 16 singleton cosets. These are the kernel and cokernel giving the first Tor and tensor with ℤ/4ℤ in this example.

**R2 · Scan 81, S7, S10 and Q1. Model remark, high confidence about the implication.** If ξ₀ is nonzero and the asserted map is injective, its image $\xi_\Lambda$ is nonzero. Injectivity supplies this implication. The outstanding premise for the proposed higher-rank example is the nonvanishing expressed by “sans doute”.
