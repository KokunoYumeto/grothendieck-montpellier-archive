## Legibility

The large letters and the main lifting and adjunction signs are comparatively clear. Small changes of level matter: the stars in the adjoint and orthogonality notations can occupy different sides of the same parenthesis. The arrows drawn almost as dashes on 630 and 632, the page reference and contracted words written diagonally on 624, the repeated cancellations in 626–627 and 630–631, and the overwritten upper mark in (2.3.5) have the least secure readings. A strike sometimes leaves an entire word recoverable but hides its ending. No outside text has been used to expand those strokes.
