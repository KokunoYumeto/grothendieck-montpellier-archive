## Steps the pages mark as unverified

- **Scans 620–621, proof of Corollaire 1.8.** “Il reste à prouver que” introduces the specialness of the transferred left class. The next sheet derives the needed inclusion, labelled (*), before stating the more general Corollaire 1.9.
- **Scan 624, Question-conjecture 1.11.** Grothendieck asks whether the dual pair \((\Phi(I),\Psi[I])\) has a factorization theorem under suitable hypotheses on \((\Phi,\Psi)\). He writes “Je peux seulement hasarder la” before the heading. After the reformulation in terms of the specialness of \(\Phi(I)\), he adds “Je conjecture que oui”. The insertion continues “en faisant au besoin des hypothèses”, with *besoin* marked doubtful. The slanted continuation suggests additional hypotheses on \(M\) and asks about a filtration. Its qualifier is difficult to decipher.
- **Scan 624, example immediately below.** The further question begins “P. ex. si” and asks whether \(\Phi_0(I)\) “est-elle \(Q\)-génératrice” in \(\Phi(I)\). The sheet crosses out *petite* after the premise about \(\Phi_0\), and the final connective is doubtful. The question is open within these pages.
- **Scans 629–631, proofs of 2.2 and 2.1.** The words “il reste à vérifier” on 629 introduce the corrected reference to condition (2.1.4a). The very next sentence appeals to 1.8. Lower on the same sheet “Il reste à prouver” introduces the three cases for condition b). Case C begins “On va vérifier b) sous la forme équivalente b′)” on 630 and closes with “qed” on 631.
- **Scan 632, last line.** The proof of Théorème 2.3 has begun. The final words, “Il reste”, continue beyond the supplied material without naming the remaining step on this page.
