## What the pages do

**207–208, the fibre criterion.** The first line continues a discussion of $\varphi_Q\colon\mathcal X\to Y$. For a point $x_0$ and $u\colon y'\to y$, the author draws the fibre as an integral over a fork. The two outer components are $W$-aspherical under the smoothness hypotheses on $f_1$ and $f_2$. He compares the central component with the fibre of $\mathcal X$ in a second diagram. A diagonal note questions the commutativity used in this description. Corollaire 6 gives a test for $W$-smoothness at a point in $X_i$ and singles out the $X_0$ case, where $W(7\ \mathrm{bis})$ appears.

**209, Proposition 15.** The question is when a commutative square in Cat is a pushout with an open immersion along its upper edge. Four conditions involve the pullback square, the open immersion on the lower edge, the comparison of closed complements, and the functor on slices over each point of a complement. The last condition is given both by a shriek image of the terminal object and by 0-connectedness of the “fibres colocales”.

**210–211, Corollaire 1.** Starting with a pullback, an open immersion, a $W$-smooth vertical functor and an isomorphism of complements, the author obtains a pushout under $W(5)$. A cube identifies the pullback square on slices. Smoothness and change of base are used twice. The displayed fibre-to-comma-category inclusion lies in $W$, and $W(5)$ is invoked to compare components. This addresses condition d) of the proposition.

**212, Corollaire 2.** When $q$ is a local isomorphism, the author gives a version with no explicit choice of localizer. He first allows $W_\infty$ or $W_0$, then gives a direct argument. The slice maps are isomorphisms and the relevant comma category is identified with one having an initial object.

**212–213, evaluation of a square of presheaves.** A pushout along a monomorphism in $X^\wedge$ is evaluated at each $x$. The resulting square of sets is also a pullback. The author concludes that the original square of presheaves is a pullback.

**213–215, the functor of elements.** The assignment $F\mapsto X/F$ is first displayed with target Cat, then with target $\mathrm{Cat}_{/X}$. The author recalls full faithfulness and identifies the essential image as the categories fibred over $X$ with discrete fibres. He uses the triangle of slices to discuss local isomorphisms and the span over $X_0$ to discuss fibre products. Finite products are addressed on the next sheet. Lemme 1 states the resulting closure under finite inverse limits and the left exactness of $\varphi_X$.

**216, Lemme 2.** A monomorphism that is a local isomorphism is identified with an open immersion. The author applies this to the full subcategory of discrete fibrations, then to the effect of $F\mapsto X/F$ on a monomorphism. He returns to the earlier square and adds the closed complements $T,T'$ to its image in Cat.

**217–218, identifying the complements.** An arrow from $x$ to a presheaf that fails to factor through the indicated subobject describes an object of the complement. Composition induces the map to the primed complement. For each $x$ the pointwise pushout gives the bijection on the two complements of sets. This discharges the remaining comparison in the local-isomorphism criterion. The unprimed condition has an apparent slip on 217, recorded below.

**218–219, Théorème 5.** For a pushout square $Q$ in $X^\wedge$ along a monomorphism $i$, the author concludes that $i'$ is a monomorphism, that $Q$ is a pullback, and that the corresponding square of categories over $X$ is both a pullback and a pushout. He then asserts that $X/Q$ is $W$-cocartesian for the fundamental localizer under the stated axioms, including $W(7\ \mathrm{bis})$.

**219–220, the induced class on presheaves.** The class $W_X$ is specified by testing $X/u\colon X/F\to X/G$ in $W$. The corollary equates membership of $i$ with membership of $i'$, and membership of $p$ with membership of $q$. A marginal NB treats the two forward implications under a weaker condition. The conclusion calls the monomorphisms in $X^\wedge$ “$W_X$-cofibrations” and points back to a definition.

**221, degree zero.** A new section announces a criterion of $W_\infty$-asphericity. The first numbered item begins defining $W_n$ among the arrows of Cat. The $W_0$ clauses compare bijectivity on components, full faithfulness on constant set-valued coefficients and bijectivity on $H^0$. Two Hom formulas and an annotated cycle of implications accompany them. The curved marginal pointer reorders the lettered clauses.

**221, degree one.** The $W_1$ clauses concern components and pointed $\pi_1$, equivalence of groupoids, $H^0$ for locally constant set coefficients, $H^1$ for group coefficients and equivalence on locally constant sheaves of sets. The interlinear variant of b) restricts the $H^1$ test to constant coefficients and asks only for surjectivity. The supplied pages end with the categorical formulation in c).
