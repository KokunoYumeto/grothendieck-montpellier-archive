## Legibility

The upright mathematical prose is usually readable. The strokes that distinguish a blunt line from a very small arrowhead, a plain from a primed letter, and the end of a French word from a pen lift are much less dependable. The two oblique paragraphs on 207 compete for space with the square and the final assertion. The lower paragraph is struck through. On 219 the oblique NB crosses the corollary heading. On 221 the cancelled title and the alternative cohomological condition are especially compressed. The faint sideways capitals on 220 can be seen most clearly after rotating the sheet. The register gives the remaining unreadable words in place, including their estimated number.
