## Legibility

The long argument can usually be followed through its headings, repeated formulas and page references. I place less trust in the comment after “obscure” on 312, the overlapping oblique notes on 313 and 316, and the two overwritten top-face indices. On 319 some arrows end in open space and the scan clips the foot of a small square. The Roman reference and first sentence of the sideways note on 322 are clearer than the clause between the implication and Brown. The listing on 326 is visible through the paper. Only a few fields and the terminal number column can be separated from other impressions.
