# Notes on the manuscript

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

| Scan | Manuscript words | What the reservation concerns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 315, upper-left margin | “Réflexions provisoires, supplantées par XVI.” | The annotation qualifies the appendix as a whole. The two adjectives are uncertain in the sloping hand. |
| 316, left margin | “C'est un peu vif, car il n'est pas clair que … soit ∈ W, sans utiliser déjà …” | The map in question is from the amalgamated sum with the two primed outside terms to the sum with the two unprimed terms. Both are written over X₀. He asks whether the reduction already uses an ingredient of W(7), then refers to the “lemme de Brown”. |
| 318, lower half | “une théorie des cofibres W-homotopiques, qu'il me faudra bien écrire !” | The theory underlying the cubical construction still needs to be written up. This is a commitment about the exposition. |
| 322, asterisked note | “Mais on ne sait vérifier l'existence d'une telle théorie dans (Cat,W) que si on admet la forme faible de W(7)” | The note writes the weak form as the implication from f₀, f₁, f₂ ∈ W to f₃ ∈ W. The words between this formula and the pointer to Brown are less certain. |
| 322, slanted note | “à vérifier que la factorisation suffit, = si tous les objets ne sont pas fibrants sur e.” | He queries the sufficiency of the factorisation and suggests replacing the category by a subcategory written M with a low f-like letter. The two horizontal strokes after “suffit” are in the original. |
| 326, item II | “comme W∞-sommes amalgamées (?)” | A parenthesized question mark closes the proposed description of recollement. The number of “somme(s)” and the ending of “amalgamée(s)” are uncertain. |
