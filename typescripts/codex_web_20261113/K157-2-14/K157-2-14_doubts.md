## Legibility

The large squares, their object names, and most of the main column can be followed continuously. The weakest readings are the left NB and hatched right note on 214, the cancelled calculation and marginal NB on 215, the overlapping lines in Proposition 5 on 219, and the long oblique addition on 225. On 226 the margin meets several lines of the main paragraph. Low ink density at the end of a word makes its number and accent less dependable than the first syllable. A mark under a deletion has been registered even when only its shape could be recovered.
