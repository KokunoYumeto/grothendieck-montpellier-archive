## Legibility

The announcement, booking form and envelope address are the clearest leaves. The crossed and overwritten labels on the working sheets pose the greatest difficulty. A dashed path can disappear into a deletion, and the tail of ξ can resemble ζ or η. The repeated office heading helps distinguish printing from pen strokes where they overlap. On scan 71 the far-right end of the upper line reaches the image boundary. The slanted NB on scan 73, the crossed diagrams on scan 76, and the coefficient qualification on scan 80 deserve particular scrutiny. The return postcode is a separate numeric uncertainty.
