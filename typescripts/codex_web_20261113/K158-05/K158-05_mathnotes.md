# K158-05 · Mathematical notes

## What the pages do

**Asphericity and a converse, scans 71 and 73.** Scan 71 compares the slices written $X_{/e}$ and $X_{/F}$, then uses a cocofinal map $X\to Y$ to relate the $W$-asphericity of $X$ to that of the $0$-connected objects of $X^\wedge$. The proposition makes necessity conditional on $\mathrm{Loc}(6)$. A corollary recalls the filtered case under $\mathrm{Loc}(8)$. Scan 73 opens by asking for a converse.

**A diagram for local cofilteredness, scan 73.** The lemma tests irreducibility of $X_{/F}$ by nonemptiness and by the ability to map a common $c$ into $a,b$ over $F$. The corollary asks for a completion of a diamond in $X$. An oblique NB narrows a case to $\xi=\xi'$, although its introductory verb remains uncertain. The leaf ends by introducing an ordered category $S$.

**Exploratory diagrams, scans 72, 74 and 76.** The scratch sheets compare a pair of relations through $B,C$, track possible common targets, and try out parallel maps between slice objects. On 74 the indices 1 and 2 exchange places between the two displayed generators. On 76 several older diagrams remain visible under cancellations.

**G75 · Adjoining a final object, scan 75.** The author constructs $Z$ by adjoining $e$, calls it « (préfabriqué) », and draws the comparison square (D). He calculates $\mathrm{Hom}_{X'}(x,e)$ through connected components and a colimit. The calculation ends with a disjoint union $E(x)$ modulo the equivalence relation generated by $\lambda v\sim\lambda'v$ and $\mu w\sim\mu'w$.

**A parallel-arrow calculation, scan 78.** Another sheet marked 6 draws $e\to a\rightrightarrows b$ with braces distinguishing $D$ and $Z$. Here the calculation runs the other way, starting at $\mathrm{Hom}(e,x)$. The identity at $a$ is used to describe an object mapping to all the others and to conclude that $X\to X'$ is cofinal.

**A cover and its intersection, scans 77 and 79.** Scan 77 writes a union for $\widetilde X$ and another for the intersection of its two displayed pieces. Scan 79 considers representatives $u,u'$ equivalent modulo $R(x)$. In a chain $f_0,\ldots,f_n$ connecting them, the first generating step yields a factorisation through $(a,\lambda)$ or $(b,\mu)$. There is a reading question about the subject in the last line.

**The suspension argument, scans 72 and 80.** The second working diagram on 72 proposes suspension comparisons and marks a slice condition for checking. The fuller diagram on 80 stacks the squares, starting at $T=\widetilde X_{//(\tilde a,\tilde b)}$. The lower paragraph tests $T=\varnothing$ and arrives at a comparison with $S^2$. Under the stated assumptions involving $W$ and $H^2$, the writer concludes $T\ne\varnothing$. The coefficient qualification and several overwritten subscripts are uncertain.

**C84 · Interpreting the common object, scans 82 and 84.** Scan 82 groups four representatives by their targets and reduces the remaining comparison to the boxed $\lambda v\sim\mu w$. Scan 84 distinguishes this congruence from equality of the underlying arrows. It closes with the difficulty of making both squares commute simultaneously.

**Printed matter and envelope, scans 81, 83 and 85–86.** A seminar programme and an unsigned registration form give the Toulouse meeting dates. The final two faces bear the address to Luc Illusie and Grothendieck's return address. These leaves contain no mathematical argument.

## Slips

Each proposed correction here is **low confidence**. The manuscript readings are retained in the TeX.

- **Scan 73, final diamond.** If $S$ is meant to reproduce the corollary's diamond, its two right-hand arrows would start at $b$. Their visible heads point towards $b$ in the last drawing.
- **Scan 77, lemma.** The η-like indices might be intended as $\tilde\xi'$, the name of the corresponding vertex in the upper drawing. A second possible slip is the extra α-like trace before the double slash preceding $\tilde a$. The neighbouring term begins directly with the slashes.
- **Scan 78, lower paragraph.** « un objet de $x$ » might be intended as « un objet $x$ de $X$ ». The morphism in the same sentence ends at $x$.
- **Scan 79, second inclusion and final sentence.** $E(a)$ might be intended as $E(x)$, as in the preceding line. In « $\tilde\xi$ est dans », $\tilde x$ would name the shared source that the preceding factorisation concerns.
- **Scan 80, coefficient condition.** If « non vide » is indeed written, « non nul » might be intended as a condition on the coefficient group. The word's initial strokes are inconclusive.
- **Scan 82, superscript on $w$.** The first occurrence looks like a double tick, while the repetition looks like $w^*$. The latter is a possible intended form for both.

## Remarks of the model

**M1 · Scans 75, 82 and 84 · High confidence.** I checked the implication from $\lambda'v=\mu'w$ to $\lambda v\sim\mu w$ using the two displayed generators. The primed equality identifies the middle representatives, and symmetry and transitivity give the stated congruence. This check applies only to that implication. The manuscript's question about simultaneous strict commutation remains open.

**M2 · Scan 79 · Reading question, low confidence.** In the sentence with $f_i$ and $f_{i+1}$, the fixed reading gives $f_i$ once. There is a thick loop and a short stroke at the left of that occurrence. A cancelled first attempt at the subscript or a retracing of $f_i$ could account for them. The strokes overlap too closely to distinguish the possibilities.
