## What the pages do

**Scans 33–34 · Choosing an auxiliary index.** Lemme 2 asks for $l\geq0$ such that both $C_{i+j,l}$ and $C_{i,j+l}$ are nonzero modulo $p$. A cancelled proof writes out the digits, carries and complementary digits. The shorter proof on 34 takes $p^r>i+j$ and $l=p^r-1-(i+j)$. The following NB isolates the common observation about two nonnegative summands whose sum is $p^r-1$. [L1]

**Scan 35 · Factorials and digit sums.** The boxed valuation of $n!$ is expressed through $n$ and the sum of its base-$p$ digits. The sheet applies it to $p^n/n!$, expands the digit sum, starts a separate line on $(n+1)!$, and computes zero as the valuation of $(p^{r+1})!/(((p^r)!)^p\,p!)$. [V1]

**Scan 36 · A typescript fragment about a torus.** Following $T\to A^\natural\to A$, (13.4.10) identifies $\underline F(A^\natural)$ with the $f$-part and gives $\underline F(T)=0$. It appeals to the exact sequence for $T$. The sheet breaks off after « induit un isomorphisme », before naming that isomorphism’s target. [H1]

**Scan 37 · Changing the truncation in a Hom group.** The displayed chain passes from a degree-$d$ Hom into $\varphi_N N^*$ to a degree-$d$ Hom out of $\tau_{N-d}M^*$. Under the box it applies the comparison when $M^j=0$ for $j<-d$, then specializes the target to a truncated scalar-extension module. The right end of that calculation is cut off. [T1]

**Scan 37 · The inverted drawing.** A separate cancelled block labels a Cayley diagram and includes an origin, generators, directed edges and a note on a word in the generators. The handwriting supplies different single and double arrow marks in its small legend. No identification with the Hom calculation is written.

**Scan 38 · The Hodge filtration on the abelian quotient.** The typed page places two pieces in degrees $-1$ and $0$, respectively, and considers the filtration induced on $\mathcal E^{\mathrm{ab}}=\mathcal E^f/\mathcal E^t$. It describes $\underline F^{\mathrm{ab}}$ as an image. The remark interprets the locally constant situation on $U$ as a family of mixed Hodge structures and points to Deligne. [H2]

**Scans 39 and 41 · Degree ranges for maps between truncations.** Scan 39 separates $d\geq\beta-\alpha$ from $0\leq d<\beta-\alpha$. It writes the image of $mT^{(j)}$ using $C_{d,j}$. Scan 41 asks which maps have image in $\tau_\beta N_{S_k}$ and gives vanishing conditions on $C_{d,j}u$. Part c starts $d<0$ and shifts the source by $[-d]$. [T2]

**Scan 43 · Generators of the truncated divided-power algebra.** The sheet puts $T_r=T^{(p^r)}$, computes $T_r^p$, and identifies a factorial quotient as a $p$-adic unit. It gives generators $t_s$ for the truncation and the adjacent relations for $s\ge r$. Writing the image of $t_s$ in degree $p^s-d$ leads to a digit expansion of that degree and a monomial for its graded piece. [G1]

**Scan 45 · The recurrence and factorisation statement.** Extracting the monomial leaves a coefficient $\xi_s$. The boxed recurrence relates it to $p$ times a unit times $\xi_{s+1}$. Iteration puts it in every $p^h M$. The proposition states that a map of degree $-d$ factors through the part indexed by $N_0$. The corollary gives vanishing when $N_0=0$. [D1]

**Scan 46 · Kernel and filtration of the canonical map.** The earlier numbered typescript fragment calls $\underline F$ the kernel of the map (13.4.2) from $\mathcal E$ to $\underline E$. The next displays distinguish the sequence ending at $\underline E$ from the short exact sequence when the map is onto. The page gives $\underline F\cap\underline R=0$ and writes $\mathrm{Fil}^{1}\mathcal E=\mathcal E$, $\mathrm{Fil}^{0}\mathcal E=\underline F$, $\mathrm{Fil}^{-1}\mathcal E=0$. [H3]
