## Legibility

The typed paragraphs are mostly sharp enough to distinguish accents, terminal letters, plain capitals and many doubled impressions. Mirror traces from the manuscript on the other side obscure a few typewriter impressions. The handwritten carry proof on 33 is readable through the diagonal cancellation, but the local hatching beneath it hides individual signs. The right edges of 37, 39 and 43 and the compressed indices at the foot of 45 are the least secure portions. The Cayley block on 37 was read after rotating the image through 180 degrees.
