# Documentary notes · K136-06

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

None of the mathematical sheets explicitly labels a step « à vérifier », « admis » or « sauf erreur », or puts a question mark on a step. Scan 35 breaks off at the equals sign following the valuation of $(n+1)!$. Several lines on scans 37, 39 and 41 also break off. Scan 38 refers to « conjectures de DELIGNE » without expressing a doubt about a step of its own. The question marks produced by the LaTeX doubt macros belong to the transcription.

Two reused sheets request further evidence on administrative matters. On scan 42 the council « surseoit à l'examen de cette demande » and asks for « Un rapport plus détaillé ». On scan 44 the commission asks that Mme MALEVILLE « fasse la preuve de son activité scientifique (publications) ».
