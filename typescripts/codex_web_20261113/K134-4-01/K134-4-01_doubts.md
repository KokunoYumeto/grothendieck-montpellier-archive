## Legibility

Most of the type is clear. The fold through B11 lightens parts of the letters across a whole line, but the words and the colon are recoverable from the surviving strokes. The least secure reading is the canceled ending of the age in F1.1. The word `par` in T07 is legible despite a small, uneven impression. The pen work at B08 and F1.1 is too brief to identify its writer.
