# Mathematical notes on scan 84

## What the pages do

**Scan 84, T01–T13.** The author uses the topos as an example of what Bourbaki’s account of structures leaves outside its scope. He credits Bourbaki with establishing a language and a style of writing. He traces that style to Hilbert and describes what he thinks it became within his lifetime.

**Scan 84, B01–B21.** The author identifies Galois as the first of his “frères de tempérament”. He sees the beginning of a union of number and magnitude in Galois’s work and recalls the moment when he felt his own research was taking up that inheritance. The paragraph breaks off at “s'il s'était trouvé dans”.

**Scan 84, F1–F3.** The first note gives Galois’s dates and mentions a biography by Infeld. The second directs the reader to an account of the author's intuition. The third considers how far Galois might have gone. Its last words on this sheet are “la majeure part de son énergie”.

## Questions and conjectures

- **Scan 84, B01–B06.** “Il semblerait bien” qualifies the claim to be “unique en mon genre”. The possibility of a predecessor appears in “je crois pourtant discerner une sorte de f r è r e potentiel (et providentiel !)”.
- **Scan 84, B07–B09.** Of Galois he writes “je crois discerner l'amorce d'une grande vision”, which he calls the “épousailles du nombre et de la grandeur”.
- **Scan 84, B13–B17.** He thinks his recent reflection “y aura sûrement encore contribué” and describes a filiation “que je crois reconnaître à présent avec un mathématicien du passé”.
- **Scan 84, B17–B21 and F3.1–F3.4.** “A tort ou à raison, il me semble” introduces the thought that Galois “n'aurait pu s'empêcher de développer” the same vision. The third note strengthens the counterfactual with “Je suis persuadé d'ailleurs qu'un Galois serait allé bien plus loin encore que je n'ai été.” His second reason begins “parce qu'il est probable qu'il n'aurait pas, comme moi, laissé se distraire la majeure part de son énergie”.
- **Scan 84, F1.2.** The bibliographical qualification is “Il y a, je crois, plusieurs biographies de lui.”

## Remarks of the model

**M1. Model’s remark, high confidence. Scan 84, F1.1–F1.2.** The source data (A1) are the printed years `1811-1832` and the live age `vingt ans`. The difference between the years is 21. A death before the birthday in 1832 would give age 20, and a death on or after it would give age 21. The live age is therefore compatible with the years printed on the sheet. This calculation uses only the legible dates and live words.
