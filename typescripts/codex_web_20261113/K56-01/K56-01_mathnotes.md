# K56-01 · Mathematical notes

## What the pages do

**Specialization of a quadric (scans 2–3).** The first cover leads into a projective flat family over the spectrum of a strictly local discrete valuation ring. Its special fibre is a quadric with a singular point; the geometric general fibre is nonsingular. The question about the map on cohomology is followed by a sequence with terms $\Phi^{i+1}$. Vanishing in odd dimensions gives a shorter sequence. A class $\xi$ comes from the section at infinity.

**Two parities (scan 5).** The lists for the two fibres show where an additional middle class occurs. For even $n$, specialization in degree $2\nu=n$ is surjective and the kernel has generator $\lambda'_\nu$. For odd $n$, the indicated map in degree $2\nu=n-1$ is injective and the generator of the cokernel has been overwritten. Each case is followed by statements about $\Phi$, the direct images and the action of $\pi$.

**Affine calculation (scan 7).** Removing $Z$ gives $U=X-Z$. The page uses a vanishing assertion for $U_0$ to identify $H^i(\bar U_1)$ with $\Phi^{i+1}$. It places the surviving group in degree $n$ and calls its generator the “vanishing cycle”.

**The projective cone (scans 9, 11).** A second cover introduces the cone calculation. Scan 11 compares a diagram involving $C,\hat C,X$ and their primed counterparts. It sets $E=C-a$ and $\hat E=\hat C-a$. A boxed formula relates $H^i(\hat C)$ to $H^{i-2}(X)(-1)$. A product formula for $j$ follows. The lower sequence is annotated with multiplication by $\xi$ and a parenthetic alternative sign.

**Local cohomology and the quadric case (scan 13).** Two rows distinguish degrees up to $n$ and from $n+1$ on, modulo torsion. The capital in the second row is overwritten. The local cohomology sequence is written out again in low degrees. Its boxed conclusion gives vanishing in degrees 0 and 1 and an identification with $H^{i-1}(E)$ for $i\geq2$. The page finishes with two lists for $Q'^{\,n+1}$ and relations involving $\lambda'_{\nu+1}$.

**Ranks and maximal tori (scan 4).** The cancelled typescript states semicontinuity results for three rank functions, lists equivalent local-existence conditions for a maximal torus, and asserts local conjugacy. Its proof begins by examining base change and the quotient topology. It breaks off at “sont locales pour”.

**Definitions and scope of the exposé (scans 6, 8).** The cancelled draft on 6 defines a maximal torus and reductive rank, invokes conjugacy over an algebraically closed field, then begins a definition using the centralizer. The title sheet on 8 explains how results about groups over algebraically closed fields will be used over a general base. Its handwritten addition describes the affineness assumption in the oral exposés and how the written version will treat it.

**Representing maximal tori (scans 12, 14).** The remarks on 12 name the prescheme of maximal tori and indicate a route to affineness in a special case. Scan 14 finishes the local-constancy argument, deduces a neighbourhood where the unipotent rank is zero, then states the representability of the maximal-torus functor. For finite type, the proof passes to a base with a maximal torus $T$, identifies the quotient by its normalizer and uses the surjection from $G$.

## Remarks of the model

- **M3 · High confidence.** In the short exact row on scan 3, $\Phi^{2\nu}$ occupies the kernel position and $\Phi^{2\nu+1}$ the cokernel position for the same specialization map. This follows from the zeros at the two ends and the direction of the maps. The check concerns the displayed row.
- **M5 · High confidence.** The parity headings on scan 5 use $n$ for the parameter and $n-1$ for the fibre dimension. Expanding their expressions gives $n-1=2(\nu-1)+1$ in the first case and $n-1=2\nu$ in the second.
- **M7 · Low confidence as an interpretation.** In the sequence on scan 7 the first two terms use $U$ and the two terms after $\Phi$ use $X$. This may carry over wording from scan 3. The letters on 7 distinguish the two pairs.
- **M13 · Reading question, low confidence.** In the upper box on scan 13 the frozen exponent after the overwritten capital is $i-1$. A very tightly written $i-2$ is another possibility at the opening parenthesis. Neither the upper row nor the later formula for the quadric decides the shape of that exponent.
