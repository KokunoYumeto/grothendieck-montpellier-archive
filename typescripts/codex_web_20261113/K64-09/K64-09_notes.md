# Notes on the twelve sheets

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

Scan 113 qualifies the third intersection with « sous réserve d'existence d'une solution » distinct from the first two and « sous réserve que » immediately before (50). The page gives the conditions and then invokes « Par principe de prolongement » for the larger domain. No step on these sheets is labelled « à vérifier », « admis » or « sauf erreur ». The « il reste a voir » on 106 leads directly into the corollary and its calculation.

## Title sheets, dates and page counts

None. There is no title sheet, date or total page count in this excerpt. The author's folios are 2 on scan 104, 3 on 106, 4 on 108, 5 on 110, and 6 on 112. The other scans, including 114 and 115, have no visible author folio. The small archive sheet numbers run from 103 to 114. Proposition, corollary and remark headings belong to the argument.
