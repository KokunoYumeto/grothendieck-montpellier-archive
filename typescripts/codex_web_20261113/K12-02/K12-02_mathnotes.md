# Mathematical notes on cote 12, scans 018–035

## What the pages do

**018–019. The group of a Hodge structure.** Two homomorphisms act by the two indices of a bigrading. The author takes the smallest algebraic subgroup whose complexification contains their images, calls it the Mumford–Tate group and tests its invariants in tensor constructions by bidegree (0,0). He then takes a motive giving rise to the structure and expresses the Hodge conjecture as an equality of groups. The heading on positivity has no following argument on these sheets.

**021. A plan for “Motifs”.** Nineteen entries range from an effective category and its dependence on the base to realization, twisting, filtrations and Galois invariants. The last entries mention absolute cohomology, rational points, positive forms, L-functions and Hodge cohomology. Only the first nine numbered subjects begin in the subsequent sheets of this package.

**023–024. The effective category and change of base.** To a noetherian prescheme X the author assigns an abelian rational category of effective motives, with a commutative tensor product and a unit. He passes to a bounded derived category, gives an exact inverse image, and introduces a derived direct image under stated hypotheses. The projection formula has an uncertain subscript on its right. A margin adds extraordinary functors, adjunction and duality.

**024–025. Limits.** A filtered projective presentation of the base gives a direct-limit description of the effective category by inverse image. The reduction to schemes of finite type over the integers is followed across the page break by a direct-image formula for an object and a map descending to a stage of the system.

**025–026. Realization at an invertible prime.** The functor Tℓ takes effective motives to a category described using constructible ℓ-adic sheaves and torsion. The text specifies exactness, faithfulness and several compatibilities. A cancelled paragraph tries an infinity-labelled functor with coherent modules as its target. Beside it a separate note tests the choice of the zero category against direct images of the unit. A live reminder names the Künneth isomorphism.

**026–028. The twist and purity.** A distinguished object with twist −1 is characterized using the realization of the multiplicative group and the cohomology of the projective line. Tensor powers give negative twists. For smooth projective fibres the manuscript gives a top direct image, then a compact-support version under a quasi-projective hypothesis. The next page asks for a compatible formula for an inclusion of codimension d.

**028–029. Passing from effective motives to all motives.** Tensoring by the negative twist is said to be fully faithful. A system of three displayed categories illustrates its iteration. The author takes a limit in which the twist has an inverse, writes an object in the form Mₙ(n), and relates successive choices of n. The marginal note calls the effective category a thick abelian subcategory and makes membership a fibrewise condition.

**029. Internal Hom.** Two Hom–tensor adjunction formulas exchange P and Q. The text then introduces a derived Hom and claims compatibility with Tℓ. A closing NB returns to the construction of the extraordinary inverse image.

**030–031. Comparing primes.** The author asks for the same twisted-constant property and ranks for two realizations. He reduces the rank question to a field, states a rational trace and characteristic-polynomial comparison, and invokes exterior powers. Contracting the tensor of a dual with its object sends the endomorphism u to an endomorphism c(u) of the unit. A boxed claim about a connected base is presented as sufficient to finish this step.

**031–032. The increasing weight filtration.** The filtration begins in degrees 0 and 1 and is called exhaustive. Membership is tested on fibres, with a closed-point criterion indicated in the finite-type case over the integers. The text gives inverse- and direct-image assertions, generators over an algebraically closed field, and inclusions for tensor products and twists. Small marginal instructions say “indices en bas” even where the displayed indices stand high.

**033–034. Extending the filtration and taking quotients.** The author uses a sufficiently large twist to define the filtration on the enlarged category. He compares it with the effective part by intersection, gives equivalences shifting weight, and distinguishes the effective inclusion, which he calls strict. He forms the graded quotients and describes an effective graded part as a full thick subcategory.

**035. Filtration by dimension type.** The author defines Dᵢ through fibrewise or locally étale dévissage by negative twists of effective objects in the indicated weight piece. He describes the least thick filtration with the stated stability, writes two direct-image bounds and questions the starred one. In the bottom twist formula he circles the corrected index and points to the warning “pas … Dᵢ₊ⱼ !!”.

- **021 L15.** “À généraliser à des coefficients quelconques” is attached to the birational-invariants item.
- **024 L2a, 026 L13a and 031 L5a.** “Considérer aussi la formule de dualité”, “Signaler aussi la compatibilité” and “à inclure dans le formalisme tensoriel” are instructions for the development.

## Slips

- **025 L11.** The page reads “déduite des la catégorie”. The short word ends in a hooked s before a separate la. The construction “déduite de la catégorie” appears intended. Confidence is medium after reinspection of this line.
- **030 L17.** In the boxed trace equality a small stroke follows the second u, while the second ℓ has no equally clear prime. The adjacent polynomial comparison suggests the intended second term is the trace of Tℓ′(u). This is a possible misplaced prime, with low confidence because the frozen argument was doubtful.
- **023 L3, reading question.** The transcription has “est associé une … catégorie”. The expected agreement would be “associée”. The accented final strokes run together. I do not identify a secure agreement error from this word.
- **028 L9, reading question.** The first-pass sign inside the right-hand twist reads +1 or −1. The left side M(−1) and both later diagram labels favour −1. The ambiguous stroke prevents assigning a sign error to the author.

- **032 L7, reading question (low confidence).** The frozen target is $\mathcal M^{i+j}(Y)$. The source has an explicit leading plus, so $\mathcal M^{+i+j}(Y)$ is a possible reading of the target. The first raised stroke touches the right leg of the target’s M. I can distinguish the plus before j, but cannot separate another plus before i.

## Remarks of the model

- **R01. Model remark, high confidence.** The integer change of index displayed on 034 L12 preserves parity. For integer j, i+2j and i have the same remainder modulo two. I checked just this arithmetic, including the odd-index case.
- **R02. Model remark, low confidence.** The shift on 034 L12 connects the even indices to zero. On L13, “en particulier tous” and “à G₀” are live, while the qualifier “équiv. can.” is an insertion above a cancellation. Extending that inference to an odd index would require an additional identification. I cannot tell from these lines whether the author intended one.
- **R03. Model remark, low confidence.** On 035 L16–20 the large question mark and the marginal “pas … Dᵢ₊ⱼ” have different attachments. The former bears on a starred direct image and the latter points to a twist index. The lower index was heavily overwritten, so its earlier value remains a reading question.
