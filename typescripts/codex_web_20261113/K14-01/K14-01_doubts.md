## Legibility

The English prose is generally distinct when each sheet is rotated. The pen-filled ring and module names, the typewriter’s small oval exponents, and a faint reference numeral are harder. On the French sheets the words frequently reduce to one connecting stroke. The left and right annotations on 003, the crossed derivation at the foot of 013 and the change-of-field wording at the foot of 015 carry the greatest uncertainty. The numbers in the outer corners have been recorded as archive sheet numbers. Attribution of the manuscript hand follows the continuity of the French notes. The repeated pen fills on the typescripts are distinguished by hand type; the sheets do not name their writer.
