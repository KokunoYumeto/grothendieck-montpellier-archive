# Editorial notes on scans 513–527

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **517, B04, B06–B07.** Against lemma 4.15.3 the author directs the reader to “Cf démonstration page 452.” The lower margin begins “Sous réserve de la validité du … de 4.8.13” and adds “Mais c'est à présent établi”; the wording of the qualification and its reference are difficult. The main line says “4.8.13 (pages 212–216), que je n'ai tjrs pas prouvé !” The different assessments occupy different parts of the leaf.
- **523, B05–B07.** He asks for “des conditions néc. et suff.” for a small Karoubian base. After proposing two test categories he writes “Mais je doute qu'il en soit ainsi.” The lower addition beside the two three-object diagrams says “devrait marcher”, with its opening word uncertain.
- **524, B01, B04.** Beside proposition 4.15.6 he writes “faux ?” and “Le cas fibrant n'est pas prouvé, peut-être faux. -- et de = pour le cas cofibrant !”
- **526, B03–B05.** “Prouver que E satisfait à Lπ” introduces a construction. Its diagram places a question mark after the proposed receiving object x.
- **527, B07–B08.** “je ne vois comment faire pour construire une limite” leads to “Il faudrait prendre C=P pour essayer de comprendre -- je ne vais pas insister.” In the margin he says “J'ai vérifié” a narrower assertion about E when f is fibrant. The abbreviation after “E est” is uncertain.

## Title sheets, dates and page counts

Scan 513, manuscript p. 425, begins “4.15. Catégorie accessible sur une autre”. It is a section heading. These fifteen handwritten leaves carry manuscript numbers 425, 426, 426, then 427 through 438. The two sheets numbered 426 are the cancelled draft on 514 and the new version on 515. The pencilled archive numbers run from 512 to 526. There is no separate title sheet, visible document date, or stated page total in these scans. The paper watermark is identified in the instructions as “EXTRA STRONG”.
