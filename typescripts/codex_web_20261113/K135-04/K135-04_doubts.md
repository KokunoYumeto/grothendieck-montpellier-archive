## Legibility

The regular ink hand writes most of the English text and the French bibliography with well-separated lines. Its small repeated letters sometimes merge, especially e before a tall stroke. The lower-right corners of wide diagrams are cramped. The least secure readings are the pencil question beside “unity object” on 53, the older layer of the product sign and the slanted instruction on 57, and several bibliography letterforms on 59. The sheets are unsigned. The regular main hand is recorded as unknown. The identifiable lighter annotations are recorded as a different hand without assigning them a name.
