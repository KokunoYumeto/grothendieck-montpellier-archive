# Legibility and limits

The spaced body handwriting is often readable, but short grammatical words and word middles repeatedly collapse into strokes. The strongest readings are the larger formulas and the ordinary body prose away from cancellations. The least trustworthy passages are the diagonal note on 134, the bottom continuation on 136, the overwritten diagonal note on 138, and the old numbering and lower marginal question on 139. Those remain candidate transcriptions even after enlargement and rotation. The cancellation extent of the lower marginal question on 139 is uncertain; its text is retained and the intersecting strokes are documented in the audit.

All 28 supplied images were viewed directly, whole page before thirds on the first pass; all thirds were read again during the audit. No OCR, text-recognition software, outside transcription, external text or web search was used. No illegible placeholder was necessary: uncertain recoverable ink is represented by explicit candidates. This does not mean the difficult marginal passages have been solved.
