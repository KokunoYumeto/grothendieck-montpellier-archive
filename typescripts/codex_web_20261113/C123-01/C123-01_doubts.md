# Legibility and editorial handling

The typescript is mostly legible. Multiple typewriter overstrikes, handwritten corrections and small marginal wording are substantially less secure. Explicit uncertainty marks record readings rather than mathematical expectations. Underlining beneath typed field/ring letters is preserved as visible underlining. Pure marginal rules and the oval insertion mark are reproduced in TikZ; textual insertions are attached to their insertion point. A word continued across two supplied scan pages is joined by an end-of-line `%` and an intervening scan marker; the word is not duplicated. All seven pages received a second visual reading. No physically unreadable run was left as an illegible-word count: difficult traces have attempted readings with explicit uncertainty. The least secure passages are the multiply cancelled words on scans 16–19, the small marginal opening on scan 19, the lower-right object of its subsidiary diagram, the hand-drawn Lie-algebra/filtration alphabets, and the displaced marks in Ces on scan 16. Bracket and delimiter checks are syntactic only; the files have not been compiled. Blank spaces in unfinished formulas are visible source blanks, not omitted readings. The hand attribution in the inventory follows the supplied folder identification; there is no external handwriting authentication.
