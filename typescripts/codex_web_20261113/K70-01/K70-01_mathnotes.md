## What the pages do

**004–005: combinatorial data.** The notes present the group `G_n` using involutions `sigma_0,...,sigma_n` and commutation for separated indices. They describe a regular homogeneous `G_n`-space through a common stabilizer `H` and quotient `Gamma`, impose a nontriviality condition on products indexed by nonconsecutive subsets, and introduce the orders `p_i` of adjacent products. The tuple `p_*` determines a further presentation `Gamma_{p_*}`. Quotients by subgroups generated by selected involutions give the sets `D_H`, including the facet sets `D_i`. Some quotient and facet indices in this passage are apparent slips or doubtful readings.

**006–007: affine realizations.** A realization consists of an affine torsor `E` under a locally free module of rank `n+1`, maps from combinatorial facets to affine subspaces of the corresponding dimensions, and a compatibility/uniqueness requirement for affine actions. The text then reformulates the data using maximal affine flags and a group homomorphism, with a spanning condition on vertices and a stabilizer condition on flag components. The page carries “à vérifier”; the long insertion is difficult to read.

**008–009: successive points and an affine basis.** Starting from `s_0=f_0`, the author sets `s_{i+1}=sigma_i s_i` and claims that the resulting `n+2` points form an affine basis of `E` on every fibre, with the facets given by the corresponding affine spans. A heavily amended induction argues through nonmembership in the preceding facet and invariance under generators. The continuation checks a commutation calculation and identifies another facet-stabilizer assertion as still requiring verification. These paragraphs are a proof attempt, not a clean statement separated from its revisions.

**010–011: coordinate parameters.** With `e_i=s_i-s_0`, the generator actions are tabulated. The next page singles out the values `sigma_i s_{i+2}` and records their coefficients in a triangular array. It counts `2+3+...+(n+1)=n(n+3)/2` parameters before further conditions and gives a calculation intended to recover the other images. Coefficient indices are superscript pairs on 010 and subscript pairs on 011; the transcription preserves that visible change. Several factors in the recovering product remain doubtful.

**012–014: involution and reflection conditions.** The affine action is converted into formulas on the `e_j`, with determinant expressions and equations intended to express `sigma_i^2=id`. Scan013 puts a question mark over one proposed reduction of the involution check. Scan014 collects the formulas and starts expressing the additional requirement that a generator be a reflection. A more specialized formula using a single `lambda_i` also appears on 012; its transition from the preceding parameters is not fully explained in these sheets.

**015–016: fixed points of `sigma_0` and the remaining matrices.** The fixed-point equations are examined over the coefficient ring, with special attention to invertibility, nilpotents and characteristic 2. A slanted note introduces a homogeneous-coordinate candidate `z/2`, and a substantial argument is cancelled. The live continuation requires `lambda_02=1`, writes `lambda_01=1+alpha_0`, and gives the resulting affine matrix and fixed-point equation. It calls `alpha_0` a modular invariant of the regular polygons induced in `f_2`. The final block matrix and clipped coordinate formula begin the corresponding analysis for `sigma_i`, `1<=i<=n-1`.
