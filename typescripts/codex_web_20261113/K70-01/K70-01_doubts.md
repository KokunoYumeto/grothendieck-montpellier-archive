## Legibility and limits

The two covers are brief but their accents differ. The main formulas are generally more legible than the prose, although individual indices, signs and overwritten digits remain uncertain. The least reliable areas are the long insertion on 007, the revised proof on 008, the first product on 011, the interlinear and cancelled prose on 015, and the crossed opening and cropped bottom formula on 016. The block matrices preserve the visible entries and grouping, with typeset spacing rather than a facsimile of pen placement.

All whole images and thirds were viewed directly in the initial pass; all thirds were read again for the audit. No OCR, web text or external transcription was used. No TeX compilation was attempted. All unresolved groups were given tentative `\unsure` readings rather than `\illegible` placeholders; zero illegible macros therefore does not mean the ink was uniformly readable. Particularly doubtful cancelled readings should be checked against the scans before quotation.

Final page-file counts: **132 unsure**, **0 illegible**, **94 deleted**. Counts include nested marks and each deleted matrix cell; they count macro occurrences, not words or unique glyphs. The assembled duplicate is not counted.
