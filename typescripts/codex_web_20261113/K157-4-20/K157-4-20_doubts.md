## Legibility

The section headings, the displayed containments and most changes of base can be read continuously. The weakest stretches are the low, blackened indices on 304–305 and 317, the crossing of two writing directions at the foot of 312, and the earlier sentence underneath the addition F) on 315. Several terminal letters, including plural endings, reduce to a single pen stroke. Two entirely cancelled groups are recorded by estimated word count. The detailed audit identifies connectors whose shafts are visible without a certain head.
