## Title sheets, dates and page counts they carry

Scan 133 is a handwritten contents sheet on reused printer paper. The left column runs from the reminders on Quillen through item 4.15. Items 4.16 and 4.17 continue at upper right. Section 4 begins at manuscript p. 50; the last entry has the open range “p. 492-”. The ranges are references in the contents. No total number of pages is written. The light printed notice beneath the contents mentions “LUNDI 3 NOVEMBRE” and a doubtful “MARDI 4 NOVEMBRE”. It gives no year. These are dates on the underlying form. The pencil number at lower left is 132, with a faint first digit.
