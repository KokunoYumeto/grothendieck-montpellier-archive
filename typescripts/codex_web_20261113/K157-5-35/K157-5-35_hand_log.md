# Hand log · cote 157-5 · scans 543–557

Locations refer to the manuscript from top to bottom. Each observation applies to the cited forms.

## Scan 543

The long upper hook of J and the barred I are distinct in lines 1–4. At the end of line 4 the first designation is overdrawn. Small v has a cupped return, u a second shoulder. This distinction holds in the two bracket labels and the products in the last two paragraphs. The last subscript of the cofinality argument is less clear.

## Scan 544

Low tails on j in αji contrast with the short dotted i. In this leaf the induced set has an upright crossed by three horizontal strokes and an underlying diagonal. Its name is repeatedly overwritten. The long stroke below « injective » is an underline. The bowed stroke at « de stabilité » instead carries an insertion.

## Scan 545

Lines 7–11 have two different operations above the baseline. The raised « limite » replaces struck « système ». A smaller right-hand change also alters the end of « inductif ». Two tiers in the next cancellation survive. The underlining ends with « Lemme »; the bent stroke beside « -clef » attaches it to the heading.

## Scan 546

An uncrossed high 7 ends the foliation. The small i and terminal zero are individually discernible in the first transition. Do not transpose the indices. A small curved stroke after E in the cardinal expression has virtually the height of E. The long bent line on the lower right belongs to « aisément ».

## Scan 547

On this leaf j has a long horizontal foot; the baseline foot of capital I in the opening index is less decisive than in the added I. The small upright after the line between E’s has a downward tip. The reference in the corollary is heavily inked. The subscript of the Greek letter in the ordinal bound must be read afresh at each occurrence.

## Scan 548

The letter under E′ in the first inequality is a single alpha-like curl, distinct from the two loops following E′ in the next line.

## Scan 549

The long vertical line to the left of the corollary borders the passage; it does not cross the words. The two arrow shafts in the assignment have much blunter right ends than those on scan 548. The clearly written relation i < j is preceded by a cancelled descending letter. Lower indices α and α₀ differ between the injection and its stated bound.

## Scan 550

The two instances of M in (a) have extended entries, shorter in the monoid M below. This distinction is local to the leaf. Small x and alpha can resemble one another; the indexed tuple immediately following the underlined x repeats x. « petite », « ens ordonné » and the j-tuple each have a separate insertion stroke.

## Scan 551

The two occurrences of 1 ending the first line have a baseline. The primed M is plain and single-stroked. Left cancellation appears in the prose as an equation with 1, even though scan 546 said « un iso. ». Marking strokes in the paragraph on the successor hit several aborted starts but do not erase the inequalities.

## Scan 552

The capital for the category has a long entry and the monoid M is plainer. The conspicuous stacked, struck headings before 4.15.11 are separate from its live heading. A pointed initial below the strike on « corollaire précédent » resembles an aborted abbreviation; the same shape recurs before « résultat litigieux ».

## Scan 553

In the double Hom display an ordinary arrow follows E, but the power-set example has an initial bar on its arrow. The two powerset letters are flourished. The long extension atop the t in the first « tout » joins the word itself. The added N on the left has an attachment stroke pointing into the second paragraph.

## Scan 554

A brace-shaped insertion pointer gathers several words over the line at once; it ends immediately before de. The same mechanism appears above k farther down. A short flat terminal tick can stand where another mapping arrow has an unmistakable head. This contrast belongs to the two separate arrows on this leaf.

## Scan 555

A horizontal line under the first fermée is distinct from the line crossing a later fermée; dark joining strokes make the first form harder to classify.

## Scan 556

## Scan 557
