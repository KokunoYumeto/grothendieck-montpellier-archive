## Legibility

The indices in the displays are often easier to separate than the ends of French words. The principal difficulty is layering. On 543 the older number begins with 4 but its remaining digits run into one mass. On 545 the two tiers of a canceled reformulation use both the space above the line and the original baseline. On 555 the rotated block retains « sous-catégorie fermée » and parts of a reference, while two other groups remain unreadable under the hatching. On 556 the page edge cuts into a possible inequality. On 557 the tiny exponent after the final Ens has no secure counterpart on this leaf. Those readings, and the middle digits of the forward references on 555, deserve particular caution.
