## Legibility

Scan 26. The main prose and the three arrows in the lower diagram are clear. Dense cancellations in the first three displays, the nested indices in the right note and the writing against the bottom edge give the least secure readings. The last line of the note under the final formula meets the black edge and its word divisions are uncertain.

Scan 27. Points of attachment in the middle of the sheet are traceable on the wide image. The indices in the long basis row, the obliterated determinant exponent and several compressed phrases require caution.

Scan 28. The upper addition is light, the right-hand traces below the orientation display are severely broken, and the opening of the filtration is overwritten. The final quotient's two E's and their indices are clearer than their separator.

Scan 30. Most of the typescript is sharp, including the unspaced salutation and ce cette. Overinking obscures the postal digit and the final letter of opportun. Two long curves cross both body paragraphs and the close. The transcription marks the extent of the crossing and reproduces its path; the reason for the marks is uncertain.

Scan 31. The typescript is clear, including both grave accents in arètes. The alphabet of the handwritten capital before the typed 5 and a lighter trace inside a c are less certain.

Scan 32. Both formula lines are clear. The extra strokes on the seven field letters vary; only their manner of production is uncertain. The bottom of the second row repeats at the top of the middle crop. The remaining space contains the archive number 31.

Scan 33 is sharply typed. The pale character under the comma and the small typed-over letter in avec are less legible. The ink of en commun is separate from the handwritten underline. The short s in fondements is written at reduced size just above the baseline. Comparison with the other opening parentheses also shows a heavier stroke after points.
