# Notes on the leaves

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **Scan 40, final projector calculation.** A separate “?” follows a tall oblique sign in the left margin. A matching sign appears between the factors in the displayed calculation ending in the projector with indices i,j+1. Its identity and the extent of the question are uncertain. The concluding “Mais” breaks off.
- **Scan 41, Corollary 2.2.** The English typescript explicitly leaves the proof open for the reader. “The proof is a good exercise in the use of way-out functors, and will be left to the reader.” The next sentence begins to explain the hypothesis in case b) and continues beyond the sheet.
- **Scan 43, margin beside (ii).** The editorial instruction appears to read “Vider (ii) sauf raison sérieuse !” The first word and parts of the last two words remain doubtful. The question here is whether to retain the case in the exposition.
