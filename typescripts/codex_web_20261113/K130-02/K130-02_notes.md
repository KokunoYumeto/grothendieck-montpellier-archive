# Notes on the sheets

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **Scan 30, between the two noncontainments and the formula for D.** A square-bracketed reminder begins « Lemme » and appears to continue « dégager un » or « dégager en ». Its position flags a lemma still to be formulated. The manuscript does not give its statement.
- **Scan 31, comment on §5.** The writer says that « des notes prises en Congrès semblent dire que ça marche aussi sur un anneau convenable » and adds that he « s'est d'abord échiné en vain, puis dégonflé ». The unsettled step is the extension of the polynomial-invariant assertion from the stated field case to a suitable ring.

There is no further explicit qualification of this kind that I can read with confidence. The small hook under M on scan 35 may be a question mark or a 2. Its position does not securely attach a question to an assertion.
