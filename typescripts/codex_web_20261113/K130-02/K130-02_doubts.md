## Legibility

The typescript usually allows an individual letter, prime or underline to be checked against another impression. Its smallest round subscripts, a few worn type faces and the source of some filled-in symbols are less secure. The landscape sheets change pen density sharply. I trust the larger labelled formulas more than the ends of the administrative words and the prose hidden by strokes. The weakest places are the raised addition and the cancelled footnote on 22, the isolated diagram labels on 24, the last lines of 26, the interlinear additions on 28, and the bracketed reminder on 30. On 35 the dotted marks beneath the crossed adjective may reinstate it. On 36 the crossed sign at the shoulder of L is particularly hard to separate from its cancelling stroke.
