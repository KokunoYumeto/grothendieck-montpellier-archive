# Legibility and limits

All sixteen sheets are handwritten notes in the same apparent hand. The main prose and larger formulas are mostly legible, but many middle letters and endings collapse into strokes. The most fragile readings are the slanted notes on 165–166 and 172, the crowded revisions on 168–169 and 173, overwritten reference numbers, and the final cancellation/insertion clusters on 179–180. Two estimated single-word gaps remain physically unreadable; doubtful mathematical signs are represented by candidates instead of word-gap markers.

The supplied whole images and all three thirds of each page were viewed directly. Enlargements and rotations were used for the small notes and revisions; no OCR or outside text was used. A second source-first pass is recorded in the audit, followed by specific reconciliation passes. Some initial audit passes were short; their actual times, and the supplemental passes, are retained rather than retroactively lengthened. The audit records corrections and discarded provisional overreadings as well as unchanged readings. The uncertainty register is an honest reading record, not a claim that all remaining unmarked handwriting is error-free. No LaTeX compilation was attempted.
