# Notes on the sheets

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

| Scan | Words on the sheet | What is left open |
|---|---|---|
| 87, last two lines | « Que peut-on dire des hom λ, μ de K·· dans K′·· ? » | The maps agree on the scalar map and have the same effect on the marked section. The page ends with the question about the induced pair. |
| 91, below the two examples | « Question Est-ce vrai pour … (topos des ens. cosimpliciaux) ? Non » | The author asks the question and writes **Non**, underlined three times. He gives no justification alongside the answer. |

On 91 the last assertion ends « qui est un quasi-iso. … En effet ». The addition begins a justification whose continuation is absent from these scans. The supplied leaves contain no express « à vérifier » or « admis ». Question marks introduced by the transcription denote editorial doubts.
