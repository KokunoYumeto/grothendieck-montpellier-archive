## Legibility

The numbered headings, large formulas and separate objects of the diagrams are generally clear. The hand often reduces the middle of a word to a horizontal stroke. Short arrow ends, primes, the tiny letters above f and the relative heights of localization labels can change a reading. The cancelled reference and the end of the admitted step on 207 contain the only two places marked physically unreadable. They account for an estimated three words. The horizontal sentence and diagonal exception at the foot of 210 remain the least secure sustained reading. The register also retains genuine uncertainty in formulas whose intended mathematical sense is comparatively clear.
