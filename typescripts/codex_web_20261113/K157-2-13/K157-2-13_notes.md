# Manuscript notes · 157-2, scans 195–210

## Steps the pages mark as unverified

- **195–196.** The slanted note opens the general case with “non néc. lisse” and “doit trouver”. On 196 the author asks for a “description générale” valid for “tout représentant”. The diagram on 196 still gives the lower β a dashed arrow.
- **197–198.** “Il faut définir, si on peut, une flèche” leads to “est-elle aussi dans” the indicated class and “Si oui”. The margin answers “Non, elle ne l'est pas”. Scan 198 resumes with “Mais ça ne marche pas” and proposes the other composite. Its note reserves the fuller account “à expliciter dans rédaction en forme”.
- **199–201.** “Et vérifier que le composé est l'identité” introduces the second compatibility check. The triangle on 200 carries a question mark. “Essayons de prouver que c'est comm.” asks whether commutativity holds before localization. On 201 the author calls the resulting square “évidente par fonctorialité”.
- **202–205.** “Je présume que la même démonstration … donnerait” concerns the coarser localizers. He prefers to “déduire” the result. The “?” over the Hom equivalence on 204 marks a goal, and on 205 he ends with “ce qui est la bijection annoncée”.
- **206–207.** “On voudrait expliciter la formule” introduces the boxed identity and “il faut expliciter le deuxième membre”. The first corollary then has “(Faux!)” above it and “Cet énoncé est sûrement faux !” in the margin. “Ce point étant admis” on 207 is struck. The replacement reads “Mais c'est faux (prendre X={s})”.
- **208–210.** “Il reste à prouver séparément le résultat suivant” and its proposed proposition belong to a cancelled block. The live restart is “Montrons que le corollaire est faux”. The example continues on 209 before “Corollaire 1 rectifié”. On 210 “ne doit pas être confondue avec” limits the product comparison; the overlapping footnote adds an exception whose last words are uncertain.

## Title sheets, dates and page counts

None of the sixteen scans is a title sheet or carries a date or an explicit total page count. The author's page numbers run from 140 on scan 195 to 155 on scan 210, one per sheet. Scan 201 has “146.” with a point. On 202 the final digit of 147 overwrites a possible 6. The separate pencil numbers run from 194 to 209; the last one is at the lower right on scan 210. “Théorème 2”, “Proposition 4”, “Corollaire 1” and “Corollaire 1 rectifié” are internal headings.
