# Mathematical notes · cote 157-2, scans 195–210

## What the pages do

**U1 · 195–198. Construction of the counit.** For a smooth representative over $S$, the author describes $f^*$ by taking a fibre product with $S'$. He wants a map from the result of $f_!f^*$ back to the original object. For a general representative $X$, his first proposal uses the base change of $i_X$. The marginal objection on 197 interrupts that proposal. On 198 he projects from $\Phi_S(X)\times_S S'$ to $\Phi_S(X)$ and uses the inverse of the comparison from $X$ in the localized category.

**U2 · 195–198. First compatibility.** Starting from $\xi'$ over $S'$, the author writes out the two arrows of the composite on $f_!(\xi')$. The map $\alpha_{X'}$ is specified by the diagram labelled (**). At the end of the check the required equality is the projection relation defining that map. He concludes “on gagne !”

**U3 · 198–202. Second compatibility.** Starting from $\xi$ over $S$, he chooses a representative in $\underline{\mathrm{Fib}}\,S$ and writes $X'=X\times_S S'$. The proof separates the two components of a map to a fibre product. The $S'$ component comes from being an $S'$-morphism. The remaining square on 201 comes from functoriality of the comparison with $\Phi_S$. Scan 202 concludes that $f_!$ and $f^*$ are adjoint.

**U4 · 202–206. Descent of the adjunction.** For the coarser localizations the author introduces $A,A',A_0,A'_0$. The left functor descends through localization and the right functor preserves the essential images of the inclusions. In the Hom calculation he writes the primed object as $\rho'(\xi')$. The five successive comparisons on 205 invoke square (I), the unprimed localization adjunction, the original adjunction, square (II), and the primed localization adjunction. Théorème 2 collects the two pairs of functors and refers back for the adjunction morphisms.

**U5 · 206–207. Multiplication over the base.** The boxed expression for $f_!f^*(\xi)$ motivates Proposition 4. It specifies multiplication in $\mathrm{HOT}_W(S)$ by its value on two smooth representatives. Compatibility of the ordinary fibre product with the indicated localizers supplies the argument. For a map between smooth $X,X'$, the author observes that the compatibility calculation allows arbitrary $Y$. The middle of the written formula (+) is overwritten.

**U6 · 207–209. A proposed corollary and its counterexample.** The author initially extends (+) to the case where just one input is smooth. He then takes $Y=\{s\}$ and computes $\Phi(\{s\})\simeq S_{/s}$. For a terminal $e$ this leads to a comparison of $X_e$ with $X$. His example over $\Delta^1$ takes $X=\{0\}$. Its fibre at 0 contains the single object, and its fibre at 1 is empty. This is the obstruction he uses against the proposed corollary.

**U7 · 209–210. The rectified corollary.** Fixing any $X$ over $S$, the author restricts the variable $Y$ of ordinary multiplication to smooth objects and passes to localization. The resulting endofunctor can be evaluated on a smooth $Y$ with $X\times_S Y$ and on an arbitrary $Y$ using $X\times_S\Phi_S(Y)$. When $X$ is smooth he identifies it with multiplication by $\mathrm{hot}_{S,W}(X)$. He closes by circling $\mathrm{hot}_{S,W}(X\times_S Y)$ and annotating the case in which neither input is assumed smooth.

## Remarks of the model

- **R1 · Model, medium confidence.** The two triangles on 197–198 differ in the arrow whose inverse the construction needs. In the first it is the vertical base-changed comparison, which the margin challenges. In the second it is the comparison at $X$ itself. The geometry is clear even though a letter in the second written composite can look like v or i.
- **R2 · Model, high confidence.** Scan 210 distinguishes a condition on the fixed input from a condition on the variable input. Smoothness of $Y$ controls the use of the ordinary product in evaluating the functor. Smoothness of $X$ is the stated condition for identifying the functor with multiplication by $\mathrm{hot}_{S,W}(X)$. The two conditions appear on different lines beside different equations.
- **Reading question · Model, low confidence.** Scan 210, final horizontal paragraph, has the frozen reading “ce dernier” or “ça” immediately before the opening quotation. The oval favours a reference to the last object displayed, but several strokes belong to the diagonal note. The precise syntax of the last assertion and the end of its marginal exception remain uncertain.
