## Legibility

The large clause labels, most displayed objects, and the direction of most arrows are considerably clearer than the ends of words. The least secure passages are the old system description on 421, the nested corrections in the cancelled corollary on 425, the conditions touching the bottom edge on 426, the rising and boxed notes on 431, the hatched equality on 433, and the abandoned cardinal bounds on 435. A proposed word inside a cancellation reports what can still be traced there. Where a formula is obscured, its uncertainty is attached to the proposed symbols themselves.

All sixteen leaves are handwritten. Scans 425 and 430 each retain live text as well as a cancelled draft on the same sheet. The manuscript numbers and the lower pencil numbers form separate sequences. The changing S underlines, the \(\Phi\)/script-letter distinction, the contracted word before *devant*, and the different sizes or positions of primes were compared across sheets. The duration of each second reading is recorded in the audit. Several were shorter than the several minutes requested; a further ordered collation of vulnerable passages is identified there separately.
