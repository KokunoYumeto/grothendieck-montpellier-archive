## Legibility and limits

The live typewriting is generally clear. Confidence is lowest in x-overtyped words, handwritten numeral clusters, heavily cancelled references, and the obliterated two-word passage on scan 39. Some apparent extra characters are remnants of overtyping, and their exact order cannot always be recovered; these are explicitly doubtful rather than silently discarded. The short hook/bar on scan 68 is visually retained without supplying an arrowhead from the mathematical context. Thin ovals, insertion guides and long curves are reproduced schematically in TikZ; their semantic attachments and labels were checked against the images.

Scan 42 contains only four dark authored rows, upside down relative to the archive number; the other visible lettering is mirrored show-through from scan 41. It is not counted as a second authored inscription. Scan 45 is mostly blank below its two handwritten additions. Archive corner numbers occur only in comments. Handwriting is recorded as unknown in the inventory because these pages alone do not independently establish authorship. No OCR, external edition, search, translation, or mathematical correction was used. No compilation or rendered-page verification was attempted, as requested; the structural check is reported separately.
