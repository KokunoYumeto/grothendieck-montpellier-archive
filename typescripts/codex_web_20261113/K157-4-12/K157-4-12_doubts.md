The large headings, most object letters, the underlying order of the proofs, the long cancellation strokes and the author’s page sequence are comparatively clear. I have the least confidence in the bottom indices of the zigzag on 182, the crossed oblique note on 183, the upper-right correction and small marginal arrows on 184, the crowded introductions to the lemma on 188, and the overwritten qualification and display labels on 195.
