## Legibility and coverage

The eight typewritten faces are generally clear. The sheets headed “Problème MP 2” and its continuation have white spaces at many mathematical operators and dense rows of typewriter x's through earlier versions. The algebraic-geometry typescript has much smaller pen-written mathematical signs than the surrounding type. The seven handwritten sides vary from isolated, heavily inked calculations to faint, quickly joined prose. The lower right of 291 and 295 is trimmed. The group beneath the last display on 296 and the arrow at the foot of 290 may be transferred ink. The lowest-confidence passages are the lower half of 283, the overwritten diagrams on 285, the cancellations on 287 and 291, and the last equalities on 295.

Hua's batch 15 skips its odd-numbered typescript and exercise versos. Its numbered page 282 has the tensor calculations on our scan 283, and its page 294 has the abelian-category notes on our scan 295. Its page number plus one gives our scan. No witness reading was available on our scans 282, 284, 286, 288, 290, 292, 294 or 296. Their outstanding readings appear above. The comparison below is limited to marked places on the seven manuscript sides.
