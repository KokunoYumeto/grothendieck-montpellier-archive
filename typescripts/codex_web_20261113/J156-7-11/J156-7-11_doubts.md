## Legibility and limits

The large body formulas and most ordinary prose are readable, but the hand frequently reduces word middles to a wave. The least reliable parts are the overlapping dated note on 87, the dense slanted NB and cancelled proof line on 90, the overwritten restriction forms on 91, and the pale last paragraph on 92. Crossed text can retain a plausible outline without enough ink for a secure word; those readings remain low-confidence. One overwritten prose word on 90 is estimated as unreadable. No formula or diagram is replaced by an illegibility label.

All 28 supplied images were personally viewed; readings were made in one stream without OCR, external texts, or parallel readers. The audit includes the separate crop-first pass and later close comparisons, with actual UTC timestamps and changes. This is a completed diplomatic attempt with explicit unresolved readings, not a claim that every uncertain word has been deciphered. A second human palaeographic check of the low-confidence passages would be the most valuable next verification. No LaTeX compilation was attempted, as requested; the delivered balance report checks syntax structure only.
