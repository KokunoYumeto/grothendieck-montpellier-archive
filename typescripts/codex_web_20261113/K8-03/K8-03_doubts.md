## Legibility

The reused typewritten sheets generally preserve individual letters and stops. The overwritten keys on 19 and 32 are much less secure than the surrounding type. On the manuscripts, scale and position often distinguish an exponent from a subscript. Those cues become unreliable under a cancellation. The diagonal note on 17, the diagram labels and cramped prose on 20, and the tiny labels inside the drawing on 30 carry the greatest uncertainty. A missing arrowhead at the edge of scan 20 cannot be recovered from the surviving sheet. The drawing on 30 is traced schematically from its visible contours, labels, open regions and penned points. The letter parts hidden by a strike are recorded only to the extent that a separate stroke can be identified.
