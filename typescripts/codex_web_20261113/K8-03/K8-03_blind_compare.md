# Blind readings compared with the page files

178 blind crop readings. 49 differences from the first reading in characters, placement or certainty. 178 of 178 final anchors occur literally in their distributed page files.

The blind TSV preserves the independent readings. The collation records the first reading, the exact audit anchor before the freeze, the final anchor and the image-based decision. Short anchors can recur. The scan, named crop and position identify their intended occurrence. The comparison accepts equivalent descriptions of the same ink, including an isolated label read without its surrounding layout commands.
