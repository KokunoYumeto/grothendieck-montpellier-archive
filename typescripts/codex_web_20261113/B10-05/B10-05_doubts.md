# Legibility

The ordinary typescript is mostly readable. The least secure readings are the blackened formulas and small overwritten characters on scans 69–70, the vertical edge endings and erased marks on 73, and the handwritten marginal notes on 74–75. Scan 74 retains one cancelled span estimated at two unreadable words, followed by a doubtful Q_p-shaped symbol. Several right-edge letters on 69–71 are clipped. Font distinctions at local overprints remain explicit where the strokes do not decide them.

All eight pages were read from the whole images and thirds, checked in a separate image-first audit, and compared again for recurring notation. The audit records actual reading intervals and changes; residual doubt remains rather than a claim of error-free recovery. No OCR, web source, external transcription, or mathematical reconstruction was used. Handwritten elements are attributed as unknown in the inventory because these images alone do not establish their writer.
