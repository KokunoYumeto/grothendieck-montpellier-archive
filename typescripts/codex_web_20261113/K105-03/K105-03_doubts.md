## Legibility

The printer fields and most of the continuous prose can be read directly. On the English pages, striped stationery runs through labels and small subscripts. In the French notes a cropped image can distinguish shafts, arrowheads and primes while leaving a whole cancelled word indeterminate. The least certain readings are the crossings in 38 and 43, the original type beneath the pen on 44, and the oblique qualifications on 49, 50 and 52. A hole punch touches both writing and a diagram label. Strikes, underlines and the stationery ruling were compared to neighbouring strokes before assigning them a function.

The working crops can be recreated from the supplied scan ZIP with `python K105-03_make_crops.py /path/to/K105-03_scans.zip`. The companion TSV gives the original coordinates and angle for each named crop. A shared rectangle may show two writing layers on the same physical letter.
