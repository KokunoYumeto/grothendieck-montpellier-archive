## Title sheets, dates and page counts carried by the sheets

The thirteen sheets are numbered consecutively by hand. Archive scans 002–014 carry the manuscript numbers 1–13, respectively. The handwritten 8 on scan 009 is followed by a dot. A separate title sheet, a date and an explicit statement of a total page count are absent from this run.

The manuscript has the internal headings « Ex. 1 » on 008, « Ex. 2 » on 010, « Exemple » and « Remarque » on 012, and « Remarque » on 013. On 014 the heading « g) Généralisation des constructions précédentes » is crossed out. A short underlined heading beneath it is read as « Remarque » or « Remarques ». The catalogue wording and approximate catalogue dates have not been attributed to any sheet.
