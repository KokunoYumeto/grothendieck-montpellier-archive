## Legibility

The central prose and the larger displayed formulas are generally legible. In scan 3 the long marginal note crosses several lines of the main paragraph. The last word of the lower-left note on scan 4 and the old digits on scans 5–7 are especially difficult. Two words under the cancellation in the lower slanting note on scan 6 could not be read. The operators with tiny subscripts on scan 8, the terminal marks on scan 9, and the lower counterexample where two directions of writing meet deserve the most caution.
