## Legibility

The main lines of the proofs and most cardinal subscripts can be followed through the three overlapping crops. The first subscript on 357 is unusually small. Successive rewrites crowd the hypotheses on 358, the abandoned paragraph on 366, and the lower-right and diagonal formulas on 368–369.
