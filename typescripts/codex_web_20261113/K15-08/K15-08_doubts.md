## Legibility

The sharpest handwriting occurs in the large displays and the two short « à vérifier » notes. The riskiest readings are the cancelled lines and sideways exponents on 195, the large crossed passage on 199, the overwritten subscript on 203 and the last quotation on 205. On 197, 199 and 205 some strokes approach the paper’s edge. The typewriting is generally distinct when turned upright. The worn Tribu numeral on 200 and the last Greek letter on 198 remain open. These uncertainties affect mathematical symbols as well as prose.
