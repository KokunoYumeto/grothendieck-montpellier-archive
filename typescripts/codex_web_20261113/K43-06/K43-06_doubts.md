## Legibility and limits

The two bibliography sheets and the regular English hand on 143–144 are substantially clearer than the fast French working notes. The least secure passages are the top-right notes on 80, the tiny conditions under the Hom symbols and the edge note on 81, the category-diagram labels on 84 and 86, the cancelled line on 89, overlapping indices/arguments on 90, and the slanted note on 92. Long tentative prose readings there are explicitly marked; their apparent mathematical plausibility does not make them secure. The few `illegible` counts estimate physically unrecovered word blocks rather than conceal omitted formulas.

Diagram content, arrow direction and cancellation were reread from the images. TikZ spacing, framing and freehand cancellation geometry are approximations, particularly the complex category frames and the cancelled long implication on 89; the audit records that limitation. No TeX compilation or rendered-layout check was performed, as requested. The recorded second-pass intervals are actual times and vary with page density; several are shorter than the requested several minutes, especially sparse sheets. No longer duration is claimed. All 60 supplied images were personally viewed, with additional enlarged/rotated crops, without OCR, external texts or parallel readers.
