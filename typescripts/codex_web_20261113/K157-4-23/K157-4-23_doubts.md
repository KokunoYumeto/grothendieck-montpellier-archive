### Readings that span several sheets

The small two-lobed sign in the perfect-map label and in the axiom name recurs on 348–350, 356 and 359–362. The capital to its left is ordinary W. In the usual weak-equivalence class, the first stem is doubled and the infinity sign is separate and low (355, lines 12–18, and 359, heading). The right-leaning angular cardinal in 351–356 and the flatter pi-like one are identified by their local strokes. Both occur where the role is a size bound. On 357–358 there is also a distinct small zero. The difference between a joining stroke and an arrowhead is most visible in the three chains on 348 and in the first and second edges on 360.
