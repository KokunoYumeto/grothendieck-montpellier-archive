# K57-18 · Hand and notation log

## Scan 287

The hand can reduce an entire internal syllable to a level stroke. This is especially visible in « comparant » and « voulons ». The long crossbar on the g of rang does not consistently cancel the word. In a′) the convex mark over the equality bar is distinct from the straight equality in the line above. Invariants are written high, the last Γ low. The drawing of Ω does not always carry the detached underline.

## Scan 288

The superscript τ curves at the foot, unlike the Γ on A′, at both occurrences on this sheet. In « enfin » a single long downstroke supplies much of the f. Distinguish the short underline below « et » from the detached bar belonging to the preceding coefficient sheaf.

## Scan 289

The low folio number belongs to the archive. In the three conditions a diagonal through ⊂ is a negation, while the slash of X/F is taller than the F subscript. The last reference to the target of Hom has a clear F, with two horizontal strokes. A narrow lower loop and bent upper return give the repeated δ its shape. Small i strokes in the hypothesis labels are difficult to count where overwritten.

## Scan 290

The straight horizontal stroke over the upper-line « fini » crosses the word; the one over u in the main paragraph sits clear of the letter. The underline below each functor name ends before its subscript. The inclusion after F₁ is open on the right and has no diagonal.

## Scan 291

Uppercase M often covers an older, rounder capital on 291. It is a single open zigzag in the last display. The p preceding M is often near the baseline although it is a left subscript in the displayed Hom identity. The handwritten ρ has a loop at its head; compare it in the power and as the subject of « est défini ». Both labels in the lower left margin give the variance explicitly. Read the other marginal note after rotating it 41° clockwise.

## Scan 292

The two long uprights over a detached upward-curving stroke in the upper right are reproduced without assigning them a manuscript page number. In the criteria a drawn arrowhead can be as small as a wedge. Compare the blunt opening M–N with the clear fork in (ii). The raised h stands directly over the Z side of the quotient. The leader to the prefix on « équiv. » bends down to the left edge of the word.

## Scan 294

The coefficient on the first line has a long tail below its underline. This shape also occurs where a later O was drawn over it. The letter under the second horizontal arrow is the same short u written after Im. A curved arrowhead and the diagonal d in the lower triangle are distinct. The low note reads « divisible par p » when levelled 36° clockwise. The loops around x₀ carry the insertion to « pts ».

## Scan 295

This is the upright face of a typescript with hand-finished mathematics. Attribution of the individual writing in the typescript is unknown. ξ and δ have repeatable typewriter outlines; η and τ have variable hooks. The small o under G is rounder than the numeral zero. Compare the independently struck X with the doubled N in Proposition 2. The accents of déterminé, caractéristique, vérification and immédiate have separate type impressions.

## Scan 296

On this typewritten sheet ink is much darker than the copy. Handwritten Λ, α, arrows, inequalities and the cross-stroke on Z are independently visible. The fine cursive marginal hand differs in pressure from the formula filling. The prime before the second E faces down to the right. Compare the two distinct equal signs at the last line break.

## Scan 297

The peak over A or B is separate from a 1 below the baseline. The cramped high sign over the first B resembles both a square gamma and the lowered form of tau elsewhere. A thin looping leader towards the circled H group crosses the page above the denser horizontal arrows. There is no clear arrowhead on the line to the final mu. The numeral in each mu subscript has a right-going foot.

## Scan 298

The diagrams have two separate upward arrows in the central column. A low disconnected stroke may be a whole word where the ink is pale. The horizontal fold bisects a largely cancelled computation. Several lines there are deliberately swept out. The little cross on the left has a retraced horizontal arm.

## Scan 299

This reused typewritten page is upside down relative to archive foliation. A diagonal dark streak touches the g of algébriquement; it forms no replacement character. Field letters are single-struck. The open-top typed 4 is the same in the serial number and the text. Vertical machine quotation marks enclose the initial quotation.

## Scan 300

Very light horizontal pen trails connect what are otherwise isolated downstrokes. Ascenders such as the two f strokes in affine and the downstroke in quotient are more dependable than internal vowels. The slanted marginal words were rotated to lie on a horizontal baseline; their four separated underlining dashes are clear.

## Scan 301

This leaf is again inverted against the archival pencil number. The three levels of F with raised p and raised k are distinct. The typed single-outline capitals of the headings and the underlining are separate impressions. Wide spacing before Weil remains visible. No manuscript revision can be distinguished in the body.
