| Scan | Words on the page | What is left open |
|---|---|---|
| 005 | “Descriptions conjecturales” in both headings. “Par quoi remplacer 1′,4′ ??” | The lists are proposals. Four primed entries are supplied, and the question asks what should take the two other places. |
| 007 | “il semble qu'on sera obligé de travailler avec l'anneau de Chow”; “on n'a pas développé pour le moment une théorie de la spécialisation pour l'anneau de Chow” | The suggested way to keep torsion depends on a theory the writer says has yet to be developed. | 012 | “Conjecture de transcendance”; “et cas général ?” | The whole draft is cancelled. A marginal question asks about the general case. Another couples elliptic curves “à invariant transcendant” with “τ algébrique” and ends in doubtful punctuation. |
| 013 | “de degré de transcendance égal à … (4, ou 2) ?”; “A-t-on H=G”; “Peut-on avoir … ou … tjrs transcendant” | The author asks about the comparison point, the group generated by its locus, and the splitting parameter. He distinguishes the case with complex multiplication in the margin. |
| 017 | “faire le raisonnement”; “Provient de la comm. des” | The slanting note calls for the argument to be worked out. The written justification for global commutativity breaks off after des. |

The phrases “To Appear” and “In Preparation” on 003 are publication statuses. The relation to earlier propositions on 016 is an explicit reference in a proof. Neither is labelled a doubtful mathematical step on its leaf.
