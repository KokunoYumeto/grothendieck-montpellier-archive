# K157-3-18 · Notes on the manuscript

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

| Scan | Manuscript wording | What is left open |
|---|---|---|
| 267–268 | “J’ignore si les axiomes a) b) ci-dessus … impliquent déjà la variante de ⓐ”; “Mais je présume que pratiquement …” | Whether the axioms imply the version expressed with the whole category of fibrations, and the practical expectation concerning the circled conditions. The second circled letter of the prediction is difficult. |
| 268, slanted note | “Il faudrait ici développer un peu plus le formalisme … et des fibres homotopiques” | Further development of that formalism is called for. The adjacent enclosure refers to page 240 and following. |
| 269 | “que je voudrais axiomatiser”; “Il semble bien que …” | Axiomatise local isomorphisms. Availability of a natural corresponding notion in the principal categories of models is presented tentatively. |
| 271 | “Il faut visiblement un axiome …” | A further axiom is wanted in order to handle inverse images for arbitrary base arrows. The verb relating it to the preceding axiom is doubtful. |
| 272 | “Il n’est pas clair … qu’il soit stable par chgt de base”; “J’ignore si elle est égale.” | Stability of the initial saturated class is unclear. He strengthens the definition; equality with the class of W-smooth arrows remains open. |
| 274–275 | “me vient la perplexité si cette complémentarité, voire cette dualité …”; “il faudra que j’examine la chose”; “(qui sait)” | Scope of smooth/proper duality in other categories of models, particularly the two presheaf examples and the example of topological spaces. |
| 275–276 | “N’en ayant pas le cœur net, je suis un peu indécis”; “Dois-je me donner d’emblée”; “(peut-être)” | Whether both classes should be given from the start and whether their intersection should be required to consist of fibrations. The requirements for their localisers and adjunctions are proposed on 276. |
| 276 | “la question subsidiaire de simplifier les conditions de validité de la formule d’adjonction”; “la question de faire la théorie des lois covariantes” | Simpler adjunction hypotheses and the construction of covariant laws are left to develop. |
| 277 | “J’ai bon espoir que …”; “je n’ai pas envie de m’y arrêter à présent”; “sous quelles conditions … il existe … une théorie duale”; “peut motiver l’espoir” | General construction of the covariant functor and its adjunction is deferred. A dual fibre theory and an adequate supply of cofibrations are being sought. |
| 277–278 | “Je suppose dans … cette section que W satisfait W(7 bis)”; “Je vais essayer de le voir directement” | The cocartesian-square axiom is assumed. A direct construction of a factorisation is then undertaken on 278–280. |
| 279 | “Comment l’utiliser pour construire la factorisation d’une flèche … ?” | This is a working question answered on the same sheet by a pushout and on 280 by the expanded diagram. |
| 281 | “D’autre part, comme … est” | The supplied sheets end during the proof of Proposition 16. The end of the line belongs to the manuscript. |
