## Legibility

The large object names, source and target of most diagrams, and the successive manuscript folios are often clear. Small raised letters over W or Ho, the last letters of French words, and headless line ends are appreciably harder. The most fragile readings are the two crossed-out starts on 278, the heavy deletion at the foot of 279, and the tiny labels in the turned margin of 277. Confidence in a cancelled word concerns only the suggested surviving letters. The mathematical context is recorded separately wherever it favours a different reading.
