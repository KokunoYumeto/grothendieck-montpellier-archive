## Legibility

The large arrows, table outlines, numerals and most lines of prose can be read directly. The least secure readings are the black loops inside the lens on scan 2, the clipped F-line on scan 3, the overwritten Fl and dotted capitals on scan 4, the small boundary indices and right-column rewrites on scan 5, and the labels on the sideways diagram on scan 6. Several cancelled word endings and the order of strokes in overwritten terms on scans 7–9 also remain uncertain. Fine ticks in these photocopies can resemble paper specks. The TikZ drawings trace the visible contours and shaft ends; individual irregularities of pen pressure and paper texture are not recoverable as text.
