# K45-03 · Mathematical notes

All scan citations refer to the attached leaves.

Grothendieck, A. (n.d.). *Groupes semi-simples : notes manuscrites (s.d.), tapuscrit annoté (1961)* [Manuscript and typescript leaves, cote 45, scans 33–42 and 45–47]. Montpellier archive.

The four reused typescript faces have no author attribution on the selected leaves.

## What the pages do

**033 · Order on rays.** The typescript parametrises rays other than a distinguished ray by the tangent of a half-angle, after choosing an auxiliary quadratic form for which \(-\delta\) is a square. It claims an increasing bijection with \(k\). In the basis \((a,Ja)\) it gives coordinates for three rays and a criterion for their orientation from the signs of three expressions. It factors the middle expression at the foot of the page.

**034–035 · The hyperalgebra and its pairing.** For an affine group scheme over \(A\) the notes put its affine algebra beside its hyperalgebra. They identify elements of the latter with \(A\)-linear forms on the former annihilating some power of the augmentation ideal. They record the two actions, their compatibility with the scalar pairing, and the formula evaluating a contraction against the augmentation.

**035 · Descent of an element of the hyperalgebra.** After extending scalars along \(A\subset B\), Proposition 1 gives four equivalent tests for \(X\in U_B(G)\). They involve membership in \(U_A(G)\), the two actions on \(\operatorname{Aff}_A(G)\), and \(A\)-valued pairings. The branching implication diagram disposes of the forward implications. The converse identifies the functional defined by \(X\) with an element of the smaller hyperalgebra.

**036 · Descent of an affine function.** Proposition 2 exchanges the roles of \(f\) and \(X\), with an inserted connectedness hypothesis. For \(f\in\operatorname{Aff}_B(G)\), it compares membership in the smaller affine algebra, preservation of the smaller hyperalgebra under contraction, and integrality of the values of the pairing over \(A\). It invokes Proposition 1 for one direction and introduces a square of completions at the augmentation ideal for the other.

**037–038 · Lie algebra cohomology and automorphisms.** A title leaf precedes conditions on \(H^0\) and \(H^1\) of the special-fibre Lie algebra with adjoint coefficients. The notes relate them to the map from the group to the automorphisms of its Lie algebra. They discuss an unramified map, an étale map, and an identification with the identity component under additional conditions. They next start with the Lie algebra alone, form its automorphism group \(H\), and identify the Lie algebra of the special fibre of \(H\) with derivations. Degree two enters in connection with infinitesimal changes of the bracket.

**041, then 039 · Local rings of an integral prescheme.** The typescript defines the domain \(D(f)\) of a rational function and gives a separatedness criterion in terms of the local rings at two points. Its argument uses affine neighbourhoods and prime ideals. Corollary 1 separates distinct points by a rational function defined at one of them. Corollary 2 starts at the foot of 041 and continues on 039. Corollary 3 obtains a basis of sets \(D(f)\) in the Noetherian case. The ensuing paragraph recovers topology and sections from local subrings of the function field. The typescript leaves spaces in several of these formulas.

**039 · Morphisms.** Proposition 3 characterises the local ring at the image of a point of a dominant morphism by domination. Proposition 4 treats a separated morphism that is locally an immersion, and also the case of a local isomorphism, with \(X\) irreducible. The proof reduces to the dominant integral situation and breaks off while applying Proposition 3.

**040 · Lifting and comparison.** The notes connect lifting the group with lifting its Lie bracket and place an obstruction in \(H^3(\mathfrak g,\mathfrak g)\). They then ask for a map from group cohomology to Lie algebra cohomology, suggest a spectral sequence with more general coefficients, and ask about vanishing in the semisimple adjoint case.

**042 · Bracket tables and normalizers.** Three tables use \(X,Y,H\) or \(H'\). The first uses the weight \(\lambda(H)\). The table explicitly labelled characteristic two has \(H\) commuting with \(X,Y\) and has \([X,Y]=H\). The last table gives \(X,-Y,0\) as the three bracket values. Normalizers of particular lines are written alongside them. The last « Normalisateur de » has no object or answer on this leaf.

**045 · Outer forms and a fixed identification.** Starting from a model \(G_0\), its automorphisms and \(T_0\subset B_0\), the notes attach an outer torsor to a form \(G\). They twist the model by a chosen torsor \(P\). Fixing an isomorphism from \(P\) to the torsor attached to \(G\) enters the definition of an interior isomorphism between two such objects. A map of pointed cohomology sets compares classes with the chosen identification and classes of forms. Several coefficient symbols in that map are overwritten.

**046 · Sections of an associated sheaf.** Proposition 1 asserts that a section on a paracompact open subspace has a representative from the presheaf, under the displayed condition \(\mathrm{PF}_{\mathrm{II}}(U)\). The auxiliary lemma takes a locally finite open covering of a normal space \(Y\) and prescribed neighbourhoods in pairwise intersections. It chooses \(V(y)\) inside each prescribed neighbourhood that applies at \(y\), so that two meeting choices have union inside some member of the cover. Only the beginning of the proof is on the leaf.

**047 · Fibres of the map on forms.** The exact sequence and its cohomology sequence lead first to classes over the neutral element. The notes then twist again by \(\xi\) and write the sequence involving \(\operatorname{Ad}(G),\operatorname{Aut}(G),\operatorname{Ext}(G)\). They ask about lifting outer automorphisms for the twisted form. The separate real example invokes opposite Hermitian forms and their unitary group.

## Remarks of the model

**M33 · High confidence.** In \(\mathbb Z[t_1,t_2]\), the difference between the middle determinant on scan 033 and the factorised expression printed below it is the zero polynomial. Both expressions in this check are unambiguous on the sheet. This check concerns the factorisation alone.

**M42 · High confidence for the indicated finite-field calculation.** Using the live bracket values on 042 and taking \(k=\mathbb F_2\), exhaustive enumeration gives four vectors normalizing \(kY\) in the first characteristic-two table, precisely those in \(kY+kH\). All eight vectors normalize \(kH\) there, and all eight normalize \(kY\) in the last table. The computation uses no reading of the erased coefficients or of the overwritten group name.

**M47 · High confidence about the progression of the argument.** Scan 047 examines the neutral fibre before introducing a general \(\xi\). Its own phrase « pour tordre encore la situation » explains why the lower diagram reintroduces the automorphism sequence. The marginal real example concerns what the group can forget about the form that defines it. The exact identities of several overwritten group symbols remain doubtful.
