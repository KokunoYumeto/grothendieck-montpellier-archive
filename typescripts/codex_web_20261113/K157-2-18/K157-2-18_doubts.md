## Legibility

The contrasting forms of the two forks, the labelled arrows over the interval functors and the numbered words of signs can generally be followed at full size. I trust the material under the diagonals on 278 least. In the sloping notes on 277, 284, 285 and 288, body strokes cross the reading line; the end of the reference on 285 and the small character after Ch on 277 remain particularly uncertain. Several final letters and the little heads of horizontal arrows are no more than a hook. The archive pencil number on 280 ends in a shape resembling 3 as well as 9. Scan order is retained even though the manuscript numbers 26 and 25 occur in that order on 284 and 285.
