## Legibility and limits

Most live typed prose is readable. The main limitations are the clipped right edges of scans 36 and 38, overlapping correction strikes in the English pages, and the original ring capitals beneath the handwritten replacements on scan 83. The underlined slashed-oval symbol on scan 83 is especially uncertain as a mathematical letter even though its drawn shape is visible. Cancelled fragments generally deserve less confidence than live prose. No unreadable word block was replaced by `\illegible`; each doubtful fragment has a tentative reading in place, which is not a claim of complete certainty.

The long diagonal on scan 83 is represented by three outer `\deleted` wrappers, one per paragraph, with local cancellations nested inside; it is one physical page cancellation. The isolated mark beneath the scan-39 closing is reproduced as a small TikZ approximation, without claiming it is a signature. Handwriting is not assigned to a named writer merely from the archive attribution. The two typed insertions on scan 38 lack explicit carets, so their attachment remains a placement judgment. No OCR, published transcription, web source, or reconstruction from expected mathematics was used.
