# Hand log — cote 68, scans 45–56

The locations refer to the written lines and headings on each leaf. The lower-corner numerals are archival foliation.

## Scan 45

The squared hook of (J) has a top stroke and a returning foot. The two long parallel cross-strokes on (I) distinguish the larger ideal in (iii). This distinction holds in the powers and in the inclusion after the universal quantifier. The crossed circle of capital theta occurs in the derivation and exponential throughout. The final map has a much fainter tip than the map in 2). The short horizontal in the last letter of `diff` can extend through the whole pair of ascenders. The return after `nilp.` can be mistaken for another letter. The signs beside (ii) and (iii) have independent strokes of cancellation.

## Scan 46

The nilradical has two separate letters at the top, without parentheses. Lower down the same hand encloses A and raises N+1. Keeled loops in the two occurrences of Q carry a second internal stroke. An upright beside a darker rolled-up mark is especially hard to distinguish from an i in the last parenthesis. Compare the clearly dotted superscripts on its right. The three linked s strokes between the two assertions do not recur elsewhere on this leaf.

## Scan 48

The player function has a long cross-stroke low on the descending j in the play list, particularly in the equations for the successive players. The player itself has an uncrossed descending j. The bar is absent in parts of the circular-game definition. The open C has an inward curl at the top. The low, hooked σ and the angular capital Σ are distinct at all occurrences in their definitions. The loop and long rising slash in the empty-set sign recur after both = and ≠. At the head of the page a shaft ends bluntly, while σ's map has a two-sided head.

## Scan 50

A small a with its own short underline designates the involution. The little bar is distinct from the long upper stroke of the next capital. The category's V has a loop curling back across its top, unlike the unlooped object V. Several maps have only a shaft. Others have a clear head, or a tilde over it, and the y-map has ink at both ends. The pair of uprights under GL runs vertically to G. The Ø-like omega in the proof has a base on either side of its loop. The opposing halves in axiom 2 have one and two pen ticks; the rightmost St there has no visible tick.

## Scan 51

The typewriter gives an l-shaped numeral 1, identifiable by its place in the numbered list. The deliberate spaces in mon, plaisir, désir and te recur in section headings and pairs. Pen corrections have variable stroke widths over individual regularly spaced letters. In trace and désir the new letters share the old baseline. The leader from révolu ends between jeu and d'un. A separate diagonal divides the two columns at the foot.

## Scan 52

There are two kinds of crossing-out. Slanting hatching destroys the start of the first matrix and the last word, while single long strokes cross whole lines of readable prose. The near-horizontal border of the cancellation under the box spares the formula inside the box. A little hook leads the half-space in the upper left to the brace marked 1. In the matrix ordinary lower-case u has two downstrokes, like the first stroke of omega. The latter has two rounded bowls in the map underneath the opening display.

## Scan 53

The paired loops above the title give 82. A black pen makes a near-horizontal wave through type. The t of trace shares a typed stroke in collier. The caret for révolu lands after jeu. Two square marginal strokes stand beside whole paragraphs. The two occurrences of désir have different underlying type and different placements. The uncancelled mm of assemmble and the unaccented a of plait are visible. The long stroke dividing the bottom columns does not cancel words.

## Scan 54

A straight horizontal stroke without an identifiable head occurs between orientation spaces. Other arrows on this sheet have both a distinct head and the same shaft. The plotted quadrant carries a downward head at the origin as well as one at the top. The handwritten wedge has two inclined strokes with no crossing. In the flag the dimensions are minute and n sometimes contracts to a short level wave. The dark line through the two St expressions is a cancellation.

## Scan 55

The oblique bar through the extra character of assemble cuts a different type impression from scan 53. The first désir after toi is clean letter-spaced type here. The second has new pen loops interleaved with type serifs. The hook from révolu ends in the interval after jeu. The pale horizontal paper fold traverses the list, while the dark stroke through A quoi bon is a cancellation. The bottom of the last line touches the frame.

## Scan 56

The category letter has a curled tip on V. The triangular Δ closes along the baseline. This differs from the open wedge in scan 54. Compare the unattached prime after a complement index with the spot at the very end of the quotient clause. Vertical strokes in the diagram include a rotated tilde. The bottom left object has a visibly pointed V with index 1. The prompt for the diagram starts with W in other positions. The ending of the underlined heading and the cancelled category labels remain difficult.
