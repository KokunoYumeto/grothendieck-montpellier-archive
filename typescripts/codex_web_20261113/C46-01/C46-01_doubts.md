## Legibility and limits

The live typescript is mostly clear at the supplied resolution. The least secure readings are letters under repeated x cancellation, the dense H/h/F/G overprints, the faint accent-like mark on 026, and the insertion clipped by the right edge on 035. The one wholly unreadable cancelled word on 024 was not replaced by a contextually plausible word. Several formula spaces are genuinely unfilled in these images; the package preserves that incompleteness. Handwritten mathematical additions on 024–026 are generally much clearer than the typed-over letters. Hand attribution is left unknown where the pages themselves do not establish it.

Only the supplied images were used as textual evidence. The transcription contains no imported reconstruction, and no claim is made that all doubtful alternatives can be resolved from these scans.
