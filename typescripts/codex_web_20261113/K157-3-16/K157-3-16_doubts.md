## Legibility

The main argument, the page numbers and most diagram vertices are clear. I trust the overwritten coefficients on 237–240, the first arrow label on 241, the ends of the lines at the right of 240 and 250, and the nested product at the foot of 251 least. Even large versions of the ink leave strokes superimposed. Several short words have only an initial and a horizontal stroke for their ending. A reading in the table can therefore be weak even where the mathematics suggests an obvious continuation.
