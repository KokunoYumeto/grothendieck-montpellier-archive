# Mathematical notes · cote 157-3, scans 237–251

## What the pages do

**237 · A degree lost in a chain of implications.** The opening takes \(M=\pi_{n-1}(Z,x)\) in a vanishing Hom group to conclude that this homotopy group is zero. For \(n=2\) the author recalls that the group in question is abelian. The chain beginning at \(a_n\) returns only to \(a_{n-1}\). He then turns to the case where \(Y\) is a point. [S237.1]

**238–240 · A criterion for asphericity.** “Théorème 2 (?)” pairs a connectedness condition expressed through homotopy groups and iterated loop objects with a condition on cohomology with constant coefficients. Degree one is stated for a group \(G\); higher cohomology uses an abelian group. The induction takes \(E=\underline{\mathrm{Ch}}(X,x,-)\) over \(X\), with fibre \(Z=\Omega(X,x)\), and uses the Leray spectral sequence in both directions. In the reverse direction the exact sequence gives the vanishing of \(H^0(X,H^{n-1}(Z,M))\). The words under the brace identify the coefficient system as constant because \(X\) is \(1\)-connected. The corollary ranges over all positive degrees. [S238.1, S239.1, S240.1]

**241–244 · Factoring an arrow.** The diagonal of \(X\) and the inclusion of a point in \(X\) supply two examples. With target \(S\) fixed, the proposed construction takes an object \(X\to S\) to \(X\to C(X)\to S\), with a natural first arrow in \(W\). The variant on 243 allows both ends of the original arrow to vary. Returning to the fixed target, 244 uses the naturality square to relate membership in \(W\) for an arrow and its image. [S241.1, S244.1]

**245–247 · Comparing two choices.** The comparison passes through \(C(C'(X))\). With the stated fibre condition on \(W\), the two arrows induce equivalences on fibres and yield isomorphic homotopy-fibre functors. Reversing the order of \(C\) and \(C'\) raises the uniqueness question. The diamond on 247 compares the two maps from \(X\) into \(C(C'(X))\) by naturality. [S245.1, S246.1, S247.3]

**248–250 · A category of choices.** An object is a pair \((C,i)\). A morphism \(u:(C,i)\to(C',i')\) makes the displayed triangle commute. The notes claim that the resulting comparison sends every such \(u\) to an isomorphism and hence factors through \(\Pi\mathcal C\). Scan 250 also writes the fibre assignment in the two variables \(X,s\), then proposes a condition on \(\Pi\mathcal C\) for canonicity. [S248.2, S249.1, S250.1]

**251 · A law on the choices.** The common target for \((C,i)\) and \((C',i')\) has underlying functor \(C\circ C'\). The sheet writes two expressions for its structural map and draws arrows from each input to their product. It ends while discussing what these maps might imply for iterated products. The notation for the operations in the last line is uncertain. [S251.1]

## Questions and conjectures

- **237–238.** “Le th. rectifié … a bien l’air vrai” and “il est ± clair que le ‘bon’ localiseur …” qualify the preceding conclusions. “Je vais essayer de faire mieux” introduces the point-target version. A question mark follows the new theorem number.

## Remarks of the model

- **Model remark [S247.3], high confidence.** Substituting \(X'=C'(X)\) and the arrow \(i'_X\) in the naturality relation gives the paths labelled in the upper diamond. This explains why the missing prime in the prose can be localized to one subscript.
- **Model remark [S250.2], high confidence, conditional.** If the proposed groupoid is equivalent to the point, it has a unique arrow between any two objects. Its image under the functor of 249 would then specify a comparison between the corresponding fibre functors. Uniqueness among all possible natural isomorphisms of those functors would require a further argument.
- **Model remark [S251.1], high confidence for the displayed property.** The arrows in the last diagram witness a common receiving object for each pair. That particular diagram does not address what happens to a pair of parallel arrows, the other condition relevant to the usual categorical meaning of filtered. The statement here concerns the evidence on this sheet.
