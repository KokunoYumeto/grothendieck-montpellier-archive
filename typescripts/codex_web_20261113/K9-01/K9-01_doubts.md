## Legibility

The print on scans 11–14 is generally clear enough to distinguish typewriter letters and handwritten additions. Its greatest losses are the x-overs and the clipped right edge. On the manuscripts the ink is often dark enough, but a rapid connective word may consist of a single return stroke. The discarded calculations on scans 3, 5 and 8, the script sheaf name on the cancelled scan 9, and the crossed tail of the marginal note on scan 13 carry the most uncertainty. The Italian exercise has a freer joined hand on one line, small corrections in the vocabulary list, and two labels written in different directions on the plan. The inventory leaves the hand of the joined farewell unidentified. Other manuscript attributions describe the working hand visible through the folder and do not establish authorship by themselves.
