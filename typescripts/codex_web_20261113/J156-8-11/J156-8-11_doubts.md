# Legibility

The main handwritten lines and most formulas are reasonably legible, but many words are reduced to joining strokes. The least trustworthy readings are the struck opening on scan 82; the slanted note and cosupp exponent on 83; the retouched symbols and physical lower edge on 84; the opening qualification and several corrections on 85; the cancelled complement-like expression on 88; and the overwritten phrase/insertion in item 2 on 89. One physically obscured word in that last deletion is marked illegible; mathematical symbols there and elsewhere use candidate readings instead. The lighter correction on 86 is recorded with unknown hand.

All eight scans received a second image reading and a formula/state review. The audit records both substantive corrections and remaining uncertainties. Recurring ambiguous alphabets are marked at the first occurrence on each page and tracked above. No external transcription, OCR, linguistic normalization, or mathematical correction was used. Balance checking verifies delimiters and environments, not the truth of a reading or the rendered layout; the files were not compiled.
