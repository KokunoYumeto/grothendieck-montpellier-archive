## Legibility

The indices on the large diamonds and the wording of the numbered definitions are generally legible. The most fragile readings are the overwritten names of the two pieces on 177–178, the long note on 182 where two directions of writing cross, and the successive versions of “ouvertes” and “fermées” on 184–185. Small arrowheads sometimes reduce to dots. The sheet edge cuts close to the final equivalence on 191. A single cancellation on 188 yielded no recoverable word.
