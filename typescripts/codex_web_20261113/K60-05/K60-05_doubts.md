## Legibility

The typescript is generally clear, including the separate rules below the prime ideals. Punctuation beside the margin and the projections of overstruck keys are less certain. The six manuscript leaves range from broad, distinct equation lettering to cursive words whose middle strokes almost disappear. The hardest passages are the cancellation through the local-duality calculation on 49, the stacked rewritings on 53, and the two tiny labels and the proper name on 55. The number of doubt marks records editorial decisions; it is not an error-rate estimate.
