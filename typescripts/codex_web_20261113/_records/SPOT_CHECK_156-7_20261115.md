## Method

The method is that of `SPOT_CHECK_J_20261114.md` (and its addendum for 156-6):
- **Pages chosen by script:** the two hardest-looking of the 48, by the number of `\unsure` marks plus the size of
  the page image, which stands for ink density. The second is the hardest page outside the first page's folder.
  - J156-7-01 #11: 36 marks, 666 KB.
  - J156-7-03 #26: 33 marks, 659 KB.
- **Every line compared with the ink:** the three thirds `_s1`–`_s3` in `done\<ID>\<ID>_scans.zip`, the crops the
  model saw, enlarged where needed. I read them myself, with no OCR.
- **What is scored:** only discrepancies of high or medium confidence. Low items are counted apart.
- **Words:** prose words plus about one per short formula.
- **Kinds:** MW misread word, MS misread symbol, OM omission, PL placement, DI deletion or insertion state, OT other.
- **The places neither the model nor I can decide from the scan** are neither scored nor counted as right, so the
  rates are lower bounds.
