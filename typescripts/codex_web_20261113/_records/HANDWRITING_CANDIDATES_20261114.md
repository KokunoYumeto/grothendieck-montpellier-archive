## The scale

It is fixed by the samples above:
- **A:** a careful hand or fair copy (Tate's formulaire), or typed pages.
- **B:** a legible running hand: spaced lines, few crossings-out, the letters readable. Examples are Murre's letter,
  Brown's corrections, the Mumford and Serre letters, the plans and lists. Expected near the 1 per 100 words measured.
- **B-C:** his spaced hand with letters reduced to strokes: 10 #56, the 1986 series 156-x, 150, 151, 81, 103 and 104.
  Codex web read 10 #56 well, but this is one page; hence the pilot.
- **C:** his fast, dense working hand: crowded lines, strike-outs, formulas run into the text. Examples are cote 1 and
  142–149. Pro mode's 1.9 per 100 words applies; Codex web is untested. Stays in Pro.
- **D:** very hard: faint pencil (68, 91, parts of 72 and 73), computer-listing printouts (106, 153), colour drawings
  (154), crossed-out diagrams.
