# Grade of the Codex-web test HC1-01: grade C working notes, cote 1

**What was graded.** The 8 scans 4, 12, 15, 24, 27, 35, 39 and 49 of cote 1 (*Analyse fonctionnelle*, his notes of
the early 1950s, grade C).
- The pages were transcribed by GPT-6 Astra on Codex web. **Its effort for this session is not recorded in the
  return.** `README.md` of this folder tells the user to open the Codex sessions at reasoning effort high, while the
  routing plan assumes effort max; which one HC1-01 ran at should be checked in the session.
- The run took 50 minutes from start to frozen transcription: the first reading took about 1.5 minutes a page,
  followed by a second reading (`HC1-01_audit.md`).

**Grader and files.** Graded on 14 Nov 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort high (a sub-agent of the main session, effort
set at launch). The return was not changed. The table is in `GRADE_HC1-01_20261114.tsv`.

## Method

**These 8 scans are exactly the 8 pages of the earlier sample check of our Pro first pass** of cote 1
(`returns/MPL_1_002-050_check/SAMPLE_CHECK.md`, Claude, 13 Nov), so the same pages have three readings:
- our Pro first pass: GPT-6 Astra, ChatGPT Pro mode;
- the corrected text of that check (`pages_checked/`);
- the Codex-web text.

**How each page was graded:**
1. Every one of the 47 places of that sample check whose ink it had settled was looked up in the Codex text.
2. The Codex text was compared with the checked text, line by line. Where they differ in a word or a symbol, the ink
   decided on the page halves (`returns/MPL_1_002-050_check/img/`). Eleven of the 16 halves were looked at.
3. For the halves not looked at, the score rests on the sample-check places and on a word diff against the checked
   text. These are 012 b, 024 b, 035 a and b, 039 a and 049 a. Their counts are lower bounds.

**Same rules as the Pro sample check, so the rates compare directly:**
- **Counted:** errors of high and medium confidence. Low-confidence marks (bars, arcs, strokes) are not counted.
- **Words:** that check's word counts, 1,526 in all. They leave out the 82 struck words on scan 024 that cannot be read.
- **Kinds:**
  - MW: misread word;
  - MS: misread symbol or formula;
  - OM: omission;
  - DI: deletion or insertion marked wrongly;
  - OT: other (abbreviation expanded).

**Witnesses not used.** Hua's text covers all 8 scans but was not used as a witness here; the budget went to the
images.

**The errors, in short** (the full list is in the TSV):
- **Deletion state, 5 errors.** This is its main weakness here.
  - Two struck *tout* given as live text (004).
  - A live *Soit* given as struck (012).
  - A live *tout* inside an insertion given as struck (015).
  - An insertion above the line not marked as one (004).
- **Misread words, 6:**
  - *restriction de T* for *corollaire 1* (024), marked, but both readings wrong;
  - *qui sont* for *pris* (027);
  - *on pose* for *pour* (027);
  - *Définition* for a word beginning with R (024);
  - *Pour|Soit* for *Sur* (012);
  - one insertion on 039.
- **Symbols, 2:** *u|x* for *x* (004) and the Ky-Fan subscript *m+n−1* for *m+n+1* (039). Both are marked, with the
  alternative right.
- **Omissions and expansions, 4:**
  - *vt.* left out (035);
  - a raised token after *d'où* left out (039);
  - *T.* written out as *Tout*, marked (004);
  - *l'espace* for *l'ens* (012).

**Where it was right and our checked text was wrong.** On a dozen places Codex web reads the ink correctly where the
Claude check had kept or made a wrong reading. This matters for how far the checked text of cote 1 can be trusted.
- 004:
  - *son bitransposé T''*, where the check has *on notera que T''*;
  - *u* throughout, in *x→ux*, *u∈C''* and *u hermitien*, where the check has *a*;
  - the insertion *bien déterminé de norme ‖T''‖*.
- 012: the subscript *K* of *L^∞(K)* in the margin.
- 015:
  - the rotated margin note ending *a fini*;
  - the struck *tout* in *pour ~~tout~~ g*.
- 027: the subscript *E mesurable ⊂ M*.
- 049: *équivalente en effet à*, where the check has *résulte*.

**Doubt marks.**
- Codex web set 265 `\unsure` and 8 `\illegible` on about 1,900 words: about 14 marks per 100 words, more than Pro's
  226 on the same pages.
- The marks are dense and mostly fall on genuinely hard words and on struck text.
- They do not single out the errors: 11 of the 17 errors have no mark, and those are mostly the deletion-state
  errors, where a mark would not help anyway.

## Verdict for the routing plan

1. **Light C: yes.** Codex web can take his working notes of the cote-1 kind: the 1950s–60s working hand, spaced and
   fast, with struck runs and insertions. On the same pages it beat our Pro first pass, 1.1 against 1.9 errors per 100
   words and 0.7 against 1.25 unmarked. Its second pass should look hardest at the deletion state: struck and live
   words, and insertions.
2. **Dense C: not shown.** Neither the 1980s Teichmüller hand (142/147/149, where Hua had 3–8 errors per 100 words)
   nor the densest SGA-period drafts were tested.
3. **D (faint pencil, drawings): not shown.** D stays in Pro.
4. **The test is small.** It is 8 pages, 1,526 words and 17 errors. The difference from Pro is in the same direction
   on 5 of the 8 pages and equal on 2.

**What this changes in `ROUTING_THREE_TIERS_20261114`.** The plan is not edited here.
- **The base case no longer holds for light C.** Light-C packages can move from Pro to Codex web.
- **The "HC1-01 good" scenario applies in part.** That scenario moves all 166 C-majority packages: 6,737 pages, 38.2
  weeks of Codex web in all, against 17 Pro runs left for D. The test supports that move only for the light C cotes.
  - **Middle course, recommended:**
    1. Move the C packages in the order of `PRIORITY_FOR_PRO_20261114_PACKAGES.tsv`, light cotes first. Most remaining
       C packages are 1950s–70s working notes like cote 1, so this is the bulk of the 6,737 pages.
    2. Before the bulk, run one more 8-page Codex-web test on a dense C cote: 33 or 136 from the SGA period, or one of
       the dense 1980s cotes. Hold the cotes of that kind in Pro until it is graded.
  - **The real limit is the weekly allowance.** At 0.5 % of a week a page the move is weeks of work. Pro keeps the D
    packages and whatever C the allowance cannot reach.
- **A second pass is still needed on everything Codex web does on grade C.** The two-witness second passes
  (`second_pass_pro_20261114/`) remain the route, with Hua's text as the second witness.
- **A side result for our own text:** the Claude-checked text of cote 1 has wrong readings that Codex web got right
  (listed above). The cote-1 second pass (S017 of the two-witness packages) should take Codex web's HC1-01 pages as a
  third witness for these 8 scans.
