## What the pages do

**U01 · End of the argument for 4.15.6 (528–529).** To establish $E(\pi)\subset E_\pi$, Grothendieck asks whether arrows $x\to y$ into an inductive limit factor essentially uniquely through a $y_i$. In the fibrant case he passes to $\lambda_i^*(y_i)$ in the fibre $E_s$. He marks the identification of their limit with $\lambda^*(y)$ for verification. The cofibrant case leaves open whether the essentially surjective functor $\mathrm{Ind}_\pi(E(\pi))\to E$ is an equivalence.

**U02 · Lemma 4.15.7.1, statement (529–530).** An Ind-object is essentially constant if and only if it admits a filtered cofinal reindexing with projectors $p_j$ on the $x_j$. The four conditions require commutation with transitions, an outgoing transition at each $j$ fixed by the projectors, existence of their images, and isomorphisms on those images. The canonical functor from $i_0\backslash I$ is specified in the margin. A cancelled strengthening begins in b) on 530.

**U03 · Corollary 4.15.7.2 (531).** When $C$ is « sans projecteurs », the argument makes the $p_j$ identities and the transition maps isomorphisms. The underlined conclusion says the reindexed system is « constant ». A longer formulation involving an isomorphism of systems is crossed out. The cancelled discussion at the foot invokes a 1-connected indexing category. The distinction between the two formulations matters for R01.

**U04 · Corollary 4.15.7.3, statement and first reduction (532–533).** Under the infinite-cardinal hypothesis on $I$, the proposed reindexing has a single object value $x_0$, a single projector $p$ with image, and transition $p$ on every strict comparison. A possible greatest element is treated separately as a final object. In the converse direction the author takes a cofinal full subcategory on which $x_i=x_0$, then a cofinal part on which $p_j=p$. He restates the four conditions for these data. The long marginal addition also bounds the size of the reindexing category in terms of $I$.

**U05 · Lemma 4.15.7.4 (534).** For a projector $p$, an endomorphism satisfying $\tau p=p\tau=\tau$ and inducing the identity on the image of $p$ is equal to $p$. The proof reduces to sets, writes the two maps between the endomorphisms, and draws the inclusion and retraction of $pE$. The concluding displayed calculation uses the letter $u$ at the place discussed in S02.

**U06 · Finishing 4.15.7.3 (534–537).** Fixing $j_0$, the argument passes to a cofinal set on which the induced isomorphisms out of $x_0^\natural$ agree. Conditions $4^\circ\mathrm{bis}$ and $2^\circ\mathrm{bis}$ result. The new ordered set $K$ has the objects of $J$ and retains a strict comparison when its transition is $p$. The text checks the upper-bound condition for $K$, considers the case with no final object, and concludes by applying 4.15.7.4 to the transitions.

**U07 · Corollary 4.15.7.5 (537).** The added corollary combines essential constancy, the cardinal hypothesis and the absence of nonidentity projectors. It gives an ordered cofinal reindexing, still large with respect to the same cardinal, on which the system is constant. The qualification « ess. constant » is itself a circled insertion.

**U08 · Proof of 4.15.7.1 (537–541).** The proof represents inverse morphisms $\alpha,\beta$ by maps out of a fixed $z$ and compatible maps into it. It forms $p_i=\alpha_i\beta_i$, identifies its image with $z$, and checks the reindexed conditions. The converse chooses a common value for the system of images and constructs the maps back. The clean identities at the end of 541 give $\beta\alpha=\mathrm{id}_z$ and $\alpha\beta=\mathrm{id}_{\underline{x}}$.

**U09 · Return to 4.15.3 (541).** Grothendieck combines 4.15.7.5 with the assertion of essential constancy attributed to 4.8.13 for the stated Karoubi and cardinal hypotheses. He closes this paragraph with « Qed. ». The proof of the cited input is the subject of the next leaf.

**U10 · Section 4.15.8, opening (542).** He resumes the cardinal estimate on $F(y)$ (the argument also permits $g$ or $G$) and announces reduction to a monoid with left cancellation. For each $i$, $N_i$ consists of the $u\in M$ for which $\alpha_{ji}=u$ on a cofinal set of $j$. He asserts $N_i\ne\varnothing$ and $N_i=N_{i'}\alpha_{i'i}$ for $i'\geq i$. The reverse inclusion is in progress at the last line, « tels que $\alpha_{ji'}=v$ ».

## Remarks of the model

**R01 · Model, high confidence (531, 537).** The literal reindexing assertion in U03 needs qualification. Take the category with distinct objects $x_n$ for $n\in\mathbb N$ and exactly one arrow between each pair, and index $x_n$ by $\mathbb N$. Its transitions are isomorphisms and its only endomorphisms are identities. A cofinal reindexing still takes more than one object value. The isomorphic-to-constant formulation visible under the strike covers this example. The extra upper-bound hypothesis of U07 is absent on 531.
