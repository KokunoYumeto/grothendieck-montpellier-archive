# Notes on the sheets · cote 19

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **59, lower left.** A line leads from “Question Si $j^*$ existe est-il vrai que $i_!$ existe ?” back towards part (i). Part (i) had assumed the existence of $i_!$.
- **63, last two lines.** The question returns in English, now with the qualification “the existence of $j^*$ (exact or not)” and the ending “imply the existence of $i_!$ ?”. The openings “does” and “it” were reworked. The N.B. in the cancelled upper corollary separately says that $j^*$ “may not be exact”.
- **64, cancelled closing paragraph.** “Under these conditions, is it true that the existence of $i_*$ is essentially equivalent to the existence of” a $j$-functor whose mark is unclear. The draft immediately answers “Of course not, it only implies the one of $j^!$”. The question and the reply both lie under the cancellation strokes.

On **54**, the slanted “Il n’est [pas] possible de donner ceci comme critère pour l’existence de $STx$, $x$ fixé” limits the proposed criterion. On **61**, “ne seront pas vérifiées dans les applications en vue” says that the dual hypotheses will fail in the intended applications. Neither passage labels a proof step as awaiting verification.
