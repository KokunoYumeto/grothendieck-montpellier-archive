# Mathematical notes · cote 19, scans 50–55 and 57–64

## What the pages do

**U51 — Injective envelopes (51).** For $x$ in $\underline C$, take an injective envelope $\bar x$ in $\underline A$. The lemma describes $i^!(\bar x)$ as an injective envelope in $\underline C$. The first corollary characterizes injectives there up to isomorphism. The next one links (i), (ii), (iii) and relates a further implication to a complement of $i_*i^!(x)$ in $x$. The definitions of the numbered conditions are outside this selection.

**U53 — Freeness over a quotient (53).** The diagram gives $T:A\to B$ and the proposed adjoint $S:B\to A$. With $y=T(x)$, the proposition compares five formulations of “$x$ est libre au dessus de $y$”. They use maps into $x$, the effect on Hom of morphisms inverted by $T$, a subobject and splitting condition for $B=A/C$, the map $x\to STx$, and the essential image of $S$. The last two formulations assume $S$ exists.

**U54 — Existence of the adjoint (54).** The first live corollary asks for each $x$ to admit a map to an $x'$ free over $Tx'$ inducing an isomorphism under $T$. The second, attributed to Gabriel, asks for a largest subobject $x''$ in $C$ and, when $x''=0$, an embedding into a $C$-closed object. The last paragraph connects the first condition with stability under direct limits and asserts preservation of injectives by $T$. A cancelled opening gives an earlier formulation.

**U57 — The product adjunction and the gluing datum (55, 57).** The cover names Artin and Cartier. On the manuscript’s first numbered page, $f^*=(i^*,j^*)$ has values in $C'\times C''$. A three-line Hom calculation identifies $f_*(G,H)$ with $i_*G\times j_*H$. Under the full-faithfulness and final-object assumptions, the page describes the datum on $(G,H)$ by a map $u:H\to\varphi(G)$, where the margin sets $\varphi=j^*i_*$.

**U58 — Equivalence, example and squares (57–58).** Conditions c) and d) require equalizers and their preservation, and conservativity of $f^*$. The natural functor $C\to K$ targets triples $(G,H,u)$. The sheaf example takes an open $U\subset X$ and its complement $Y=X-U$. A further formulation asks that a square with corners $F$, $i_*i^*F$, $j_*j^*F$ and $j_*j^*i_*i^*F$ be cartesian. The second square rewrites the corners in terms of $G,H,\varphi$.

**U59 — Adjoint pairs and partial exact sequences (59).** The diagram, four Hom formulas and two columns of identities set up adjunctions between $C''$, $C$ and $C'$. Proposition 1 starts with $j_*$ fully faithful, $i^*$ an exact quotient functor, and $j_*$ identifying its kernel. Part (i) gives the existence of $j^*$ from that of $i_!$ and the sequence ending in $j_*j^*F\to0$. Part (i bis) gives the other sequence from the existence of $i_*$. The note at the foot warns against adjoining zeros in general.

**U60 — Proof of Corollaire 1 (60–61).** Assuming $i_!$ exists, the corollary connects exactness of $j^*$, identification of its kernel, thickness of the essential image of $i_!$, and injectivity of $i_!i^*F\to F$. The proof uses a kernel $N$, applies $i^*$ or $j^*$ to it, and compares the rows for $F,F',F''$. The two end terms in the short exact sequence receive the names $F^U$ and $F^Y$.

**U61 — The dual and the symmetry (61).** The dual corollary is followed by three failures expected in the intended applications. A remark invokes the dual of module localization to separate exactness of $i_!$ from the conditions just proved. A second remark interchanges the sides using the opposite categories and announces that this symmetry will be lost.

**U62 — Restatement (62).** The next corollary compares existence and exactness of $j^*$ with existence of $i_!$ having thick essential image, and with the condition that $i_!i^*F\to F$ is monic for every $F$. It names the kernel and cokernel and singles out which implications the previous proposition and corollary supply.

**U63 — Construction in English (63).** After a cancelled restatement the writer sets $\tau(F)=\operatorname{Ker}(F\to j_*j^*F)$. He identifies it with $i_!i^*F$ and reduces the proposed adjunction to Hom groups involving $F'$ and $F$. The last paragraph invokes the construction of the quotient category $C/C''$. The final question removes the exactness assumption on $j^*$.

**U64 — Further English draft (64).** Six identities and three short rows of maps precede a cancelled diagram and list of conditions. The draft compares thickness, the injection condition and a kernel description. It returns to the short exact sequence, calls the situation “essentially autodual”, then narrows an equivalence about adjoints to a one-way implication. The last box describes a kernel using an unusually placed bang.

## Remarks of the model

- **M57, scans 57–58. Model remark, high confidence.** The map written in the opposite direction to the usual reading order, $(G,\varphi(G)\times H)\leftarrow(G,H)$, has second component $(u,\mathrm{id}_H)$. Projection to its first factor has precisely the displayed type $H\to\varphi(G)$. This accounts for the direction of $u$ in the definition of $K$.
- **M62, scans 62–63. Model remark, low confidence.** The exactness condition on $j^*$ is a) on 62 and b) in the cancelled English list on 63. The labels in “We have seen already a) ⇒ b)” therefore need the list on their own sheet. The English heading and list are cancelled.
- **M63, scan 63, last argument. Model remark, low confidence.** The live words assign the role of “biggest … quotient” to $j^*F$. If the quotient is taken inside $C$ and is to lie in $\operatorname{Im}j_*$, then $j_*j^*F$ has the required target category. The expression immediately before the image notation was struck and remains doubtful. This observation concerns the types of the terms and supplies no proof of the claimed converse.

- **Reading question, scan 63, L13. Model remark, low confidence.** The frozen identification of τ(F) with i!i*F uses an equivalence sign. An equals sign is also possible: the upper stroke is thick and only slightly wavy. This question concerns the relation sign alone.
