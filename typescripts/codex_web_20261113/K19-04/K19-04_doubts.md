## Legibility

The printed headings and the larger equations are the clearest parts. Confidence falls sharply where a stroke has been written over an earlier index, where a horizontal line doubles as a cancellation, and where short words run into a leader. The small proof on 60 and the tightly written English at the right and left margins of 63 are especially uncertain. On 50 and 52 even rotation of the watermark leaves the ends clipped. The manuscript sometimes distinguishes a dash from an arrowhead only by a slight thickening. A zero count of illegible-word placeholders means that a tentative reading was entered in each such position, not that every word has been established.
