## Legibility

The four typescript leaves are substantially legible, down to the blank exercise reference on scan 18. The small indices o/0, the pen accents on u and the y-shaped variable in the total degree need particular care. The five manuscript leaves combine broad, distinct mathematical strokes with words reduced to short horizontal runs. The densely corrected middle and clipped foot of scan 15, the opening cancellations and final two lines of scan 17, and the target subscript and corrections on scan 19 are the least secure passages. The reversed pale type behind the handwriting is show-through; the right-reading typescript faces have their own page files. In those difficult passages a proposed word in a doubt mark may preserve only a few surviving strokes.
