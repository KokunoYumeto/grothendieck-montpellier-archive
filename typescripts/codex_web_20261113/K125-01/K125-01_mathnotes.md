## Remarks of the model

- **M1 · scans 3 and 5 · high confidence for the arithmetic.** Setting $q=r-p$ as suggested by the degree on scan 5 takes $p>m$ to $q<r-m$ and $p=m$ to $q=r-m$. The numerical switch of sides in the two boxes can therefore be read directly. The assertion of duality itself is carried by the arrow on scan 3.
- **M2 · scan 7, (14) · high confidence as a conditional statement.** If the outer cohomology groups $H^0((f),M)$ and $H^1((f),M)$ vanish, exactness of (14) makes the middle map $M\to H^0(U,F)$ bijective. This explains why the corollary can cite (14) for its degree-zero claim. The printed bound at the clipped right margin in the corollary's argument remains uncertain.
- **M3 · scans 3 and 8 · medium confidence.** Both sheets put a degree-zero restriction to a completion next to a range of higher vanishing. The coefficient on 8 is written with Ω; the rectangular box next to the oval on 3 has a pen correction at the analogous coefficient. That correction limits a more precise identification of the two statements.
