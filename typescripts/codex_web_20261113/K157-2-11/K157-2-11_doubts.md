## Legibility

The display lettering and the two foliation systems can usually be separated. The least secure readings are the obsolete words under the factorization statement on 163, the short parenthetical phrase on 172, and several citations and connecting words in the margins of 177–178. In the lower corner of 178 the diagonal sentence crosses the square and resumes in the space above its first row. The horizontal and slanted words were read at different rotations. The three solid cancellations recorded as illegible each conceal an estimated single word.
