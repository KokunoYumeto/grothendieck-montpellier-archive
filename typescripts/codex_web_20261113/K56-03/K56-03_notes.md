# Cote 56 · Notes on the sheets

## Steps the pages themselves mark as unverified

- **Scan 33, lines 13–24.** The typescript says “reste à prouver l'implication inverse”, with (i) ⇒ (ii) inserted. It then introduces the functor of maximal tori, gives the étale local section argument, and concludes “ce qui prouve (i) ⇒ (ii)”. This is a task announced and discharged on the same sheet.
- **Scan 38, lines 19–22.** Delzant's printed *Remarque* gives two questions. After “On ne sait pas si l'application”, the display is $\operatorname{grad}^{2}(\mathrm Q(\mathrm A))\to\mathrm H^{2}(\mathrm A,\mathbf Z/2\,\mathbf Z)$. He continues “est toujours injective, et l'on ne sait pas non plus si tout élément d'ordre 2 du groupe de Brauer de A est somme d'éléments de la forme a.b.”
- No other explicit question about the validity of a step appears on these sheets. “Nous nous dispensons de reproduire ici le raisonnement” on scan 31 refers to an omitted argument. The *N.B.* on scan 30 introduces a definition.
