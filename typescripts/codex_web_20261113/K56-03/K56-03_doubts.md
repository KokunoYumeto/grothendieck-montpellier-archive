## Legibility

The type in Delzant’s offprint is sharp enough to separate the two levels of the exponents on scan 38. The correction layers on scans 29 and 33 are much harder. Some runs were struck at the same height as the letters or typed over more than once. On scan 28 the long pen stroke crosses the sideways wording and rises above several groups. On scan 30 the pale pencil at the top, the cancelled lower indices and the small connective before des classes have the least contrast. On scan 35 the first ending after est and the initial of the word before the last two fractions remain doubtful.
