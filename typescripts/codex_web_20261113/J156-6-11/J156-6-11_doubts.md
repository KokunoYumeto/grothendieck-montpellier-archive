## Legibility

The clearest evidence is usually a separate prime, tilde, overbar or numeral on an uncancelled line. The least secure text is the old line under the first insertion on 81, the overwritten middle of the abandoned proof on 83, the support comparison and its following verb in the rotated left margin of 84, and the overlapping cancellations on 85. On 86 the second figure of the page reference is uncertain even after enlargement. On 87 the vertical subdivision sign could be compared directly with a horizontal instance on the same sheet. Every retained reconstruction of difficult crossed strokes is given as a candidate in context.
