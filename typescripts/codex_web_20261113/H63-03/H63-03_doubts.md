## Legibility

Most of the type is distinct. Numerals above or below the line, freehand arrows, the sheaf letters and long fraction bars can generally be followed even where strokes vary in thickness. The least secure material is the small right-hand writing on 17, the case table reaching the cut edge on 21, the damaged word and pencilled words on 22, and the possible transferred traces and overtype on 24. The handwriting on these additions is unsigned. Their inventory entries therefore leave the writer unidentified.
