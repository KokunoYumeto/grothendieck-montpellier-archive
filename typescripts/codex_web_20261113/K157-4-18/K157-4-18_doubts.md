## Legibility

The typescript on scan 274 is clear under its long cancel strokes. Its overwritten letter and isolated specks are less certain than its words. In the handwritten pages the main lines and displayed nodes are generally more legible than the endings of verbs, old numbers, and writing underneath revisions. The hardest passages are the small interleaved additions on scans 275–276, the block crossed out on 277, the last line of the oblique NB on 282, the rubbed-out triangle on 283, and the repeatedly reversed arrows on 284. The ink determines the existence of these passages more securely than it determines all their letters or directions. A dash without a discernible head, an auxiliary stroke on a triangle, or a word ending in a single undulation has been left open where appropriate.
