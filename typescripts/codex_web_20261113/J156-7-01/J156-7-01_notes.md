# Notes on scans 010–017

## Steps the manuscript marks as unverified

- **012, middle and lower text.** The words “ai admis que” introduce the identification of the relation between simplexes with the relation between “figures ensemblistes”. The writer continues “j’ai accepté l’inclusion” beside the sketches, then describes the proposed subdivision as one “que personne ne regardera comme une subdivision simpliciale !”. These are the explicit admission and its stated consequence.
- **014, lemma, approximately lines 13–16.** Immediately after “sont inductifs.” there is a small dotted, question-like mark and a second short mark. The first may qualify the inverse assertion just before it. The ink does not settle the punctuation.
- **015, oblique left margin.** “La formuler tout de suite sous forme de la prop ?” questions the form of presentation. On **017**, “lemme ?” in the small marginal cross-reference is likewise uncertain. Both are retained at their locations. Neither supplies a further explicit admission about a proof.
