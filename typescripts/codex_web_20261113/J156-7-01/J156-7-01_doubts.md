The weakest passages are the parenthesis in the Attention note at the foot of 011, the thoroughly cancelled heading on 012, the tiny overlapping reference at the foot of 013, and the longer left-hand note on 017. The figures and diagram strokes are transcribed as pen shapes and connections. The cancellation marks record surviving earlier readings even where later writing covers them. No second manuscript, earlier transcription or external edition was used to fill a gap.
