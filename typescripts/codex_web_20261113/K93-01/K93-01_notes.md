## Title sheets, dates and page counts

There is no separate title sheet, written date or statement of a page count. Scan 139 opens with the label “Prop”. Scan 142 begins **“1) Connexions sur torseurs affines”**. Scan 148 writes **“2) Stratifications sur torseurs affines”** twice, first struck through inside the preceding paragraph and then as the heading of the new section.

Every sheet is handwritten. No separate authorial pagination was identified. The corner numbers 134–139 and 141–149 are archive pencil numbers. They are recorded in comments in the corresponding page files. Formula and section numbers are preserved in the text.
