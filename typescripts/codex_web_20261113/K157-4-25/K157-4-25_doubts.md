## Legibility

The large formulas and the boundaries of the two braced tables are generally legible. Word endings and tiny words written across the body text cost more readings than entire sentences. The least secure passages are the diagonal addition on 379, the parenthesis and overwritten closing question on 382, the diagonal note and fine table additions on 384, the overwritten Ho name on 385 and the cancelled expressions surrounding the two formulas on 391–392. The lower stroke beneath W in the 7D axiom is open even in the clearer copies. No coefficient, word group or crossed-out passage has been replaced by a placeholder.
