## Legibility

The large diagram letters help distinguish f, i and j, and the small vertical displacement often separates a star from a prime or an exclamation. The shape of a star by itself is much less reliable where the pen has gone back over it. The most fragile passages are the sloping box on scan 34, the middle cancellation on scan 39, the first marginal judgement on scan 41, the lower-right note on scan 43, the cancelled alternatives on scan 44, and the cramped corrections on scans 48–49. The two unreadable-word marks occur in the cancellation before *un iso* on scan 34 and at the end of the marginal reference on scan 49. The archive sheet numbers were recorded only as comments.
