## Legibility

The larger variables, most relation signs and the sequence of the manuscript numbers are clear. In the oblique note beside the first NB on scan 010, the middle of each word often consists of only one stroke. On scan 012, the long NB and the main proof cross one another in different directions. Rotation separates the marginal lines from the proof. Some earlier strokes are hidden beneath the cancellations. The proof struck as a block on 014 can be followed across most lines. The three heavily ruled and hatched lines on 015 are the least legible continuous passage. On 016, the small letters discarded from the X subscripts are particularly uncertain. On 017, endings in the last remark meet the physical edge of the sheet. The doubtful transcriptions give the remaining strokes in their positions.
