# Notes on scans 98–104

## Steps the manuscript leaves open

| Scan | Wording on the page | What is left open there |
|---:|---|---|
| 98 | None. | The leaf gives definitions and correspondences, without an explicit reservation. |
| 99 | “Il reste seulement à prouver” and “Ainsi, il reste à prouver (iv b) sur α” | The two statements identify the outstanding part of the verification at those places. The calculation follows and ends with a short proof-ending mark, read with doubt as “qed.” |
| 100 | “ou ‘espaceurs’ ?”; “Questions ouvertes, que je ne vois pas moyen de tirer au clair” | The first question concerns the proposed name. The latter heading introduces the mathematical questions that follow it. |
| 101 | “Il serait tentant alors”, “il ne semble pas que”, “l’inverse soit vrai”, “la question demeure si” | The product interpretation is proposed, its converse is doubted, and the question about a spatios of correspondences remains open. |
| 102 | “Mais la structure C, ou la structure de disjonction, n’est pas claire.” | This is the reservation after the calculation with Sup. |
| 103 | “la question de définir”, “semble lié à la question”, “Une question sans doute étroitement apparentée est la suivante.” | The author links the first question to elements described as minimaux or objects taking their place, then starts the question continued on 104. |
| 104 | “Y-a-t-il une décomposition similaire”, “entre disjoncteurs, ou ce qui revient au même, entre spatios ?” | The closing question asks about the decomposition beyond the case described above it. |
