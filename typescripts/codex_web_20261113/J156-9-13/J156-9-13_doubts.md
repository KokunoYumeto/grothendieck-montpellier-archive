## Legibility

The spacing usually separates clauses, and the larger letters and labelled arrows provide comparison forms. The right edge of the ψ formula on 98 cuts off ink. The successive canceled comparisons on 99 leave several mutually crossing strokes. The added mark over the E′ subscript on 101 and the tiny marks beside both f’s on 102 have little internal detail. On 104 a blot touches the end of commutation. These places carry the greatest reading uncertainty. Alternatives inside cancellations belong to the earlier wording as far as it is visible.
