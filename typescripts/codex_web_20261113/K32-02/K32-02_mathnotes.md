Line numbers refer to the individual TeX files. The notes distinguish statements made in the manuscript from checks performed while writing these notes. Words in quotation marks retain the page’s language.

## What the pages do

**Scan 24 · Correction slip.** The writer changes a reference near the foot of a page and strikes a question relating the Lie algebra of $\mathrm{Pic}_X$ to $H^1$ on $X$. The question mark still matters: the slip does not assert the equality as a correction.

**Scan 25 · A functor and two conditions.** Condition I puts the map $G\to p_*p^*G$ beside a comparison from $\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(G_m,G)$ to $R^1p_*G$. Condition II calls the functor (1.2.1.1) an equivalence. Three remarks introduce an exact sequence with a map $\theta$, locality on the base, and an injectivity statement. The forward argument separates full faithfulness from essential surjectivity. The reverse argument returns to I.a and I.b.

**Scans 26–27 · Pullback of torsors.** Over a local Artinian base, the lemma gives conditions on $H^0$, $\mathrm{Pic}^{\tau}$ and the absence of a multiplicative subgroup in the geometric fibre of $G$. Its conclusion compares $G$-torsors on $S$ with $f^*G$-torsors on $X$. The bottom insertion on scan 26 belongs before b: it passes to the strictly henselian case and invokes a section for full faithfulness. Part b uses torsion for an abelian variety and inverse images of its torsion subgroups. Part c studies a length-one square-zero thickening and identifies a deformation group using $L=e^*\Omega^1_{G/S}$. Its final $H^1$ is set equal to zero.

**Scan 28 · Cancelled arguments for two cases.** In the Artinian case, the writer uses the Cartier dual and the invertible elements of its affine algebra to introduce a short exact sequence with smooth groups $G_1,G_2$. A cohomology sequence yields a comparison involving $\mathrm{Hom}(\mathbb G_m,G)$ and $R^1p_*G$. In case (iii), an extension of the ground field precedes a sequence with kernel $\mathbb G_m^n$ and quotient $H$ containing no torus. Both arguments lie under the crossing strokes.

**Scan 29 · Complex structures and lifting.** The upper box collects a real vector space, a connection and the sequence $0\to\omega\to V\to\omega^{-1}\to0$. The page proceeds from the “disque orienté” to a flat $\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb R)$-torsor and asks about lifting to $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$. Beside the lifting question it places obstruction, choices and automorphisms against $H^2,H^1,H^0$ with coefficients $\mathbb Z/2$. The bottom line asks the question for $\Gamma$ and records a product relation for lifted generators. The material is cancelled.

**Scan 30 · Corrections to XVII.** The writer asks for the reference behind a condition on residue characteristics, sketches a pointwise restriction over $\mathrm{Spec}(O_{S,s})$, and proposes an addition concerning “plat/lisse”.

**Scans 31–32 · Letter and working list.** P. Deligne separates what he feels obliged to supply for identifying $Rf^!$ from the remaining compatibilities. He names the pieces enclosed. The following nine-item list spells out comparisons involving $Rf_!\to Rf_*$, $f^*(d)[2d]\to Rf^!$, local fundamental classes, correspondences, complex comparison, curve duality and Gysin. Its last item replaces a crossed base-change formula by a leftward comparison for smooth $f$.

**Scan 33 · Low-degree cohomology and a pairing.** One orientation has an $X,S$ diagram with $H^1(H^0)$, $H^1$, $H^0H^1$ and $H^2H^0$. In the other orientation, a pairing of two script letters is specialized to $L=O(E)$. Norm-like expressions, the sequence ending in $O_F$, and two determinant formulae appear alongside a small diagram with $f$ and $f^{-1}$. Some arguments of the determinants and norms are left empty.

**Scan 34 · Valuations and descent.** The writer juxtaposes pairs of $X_i,Y_i$, a signed quotient involving $v_xf,v_xg$, and expressions with $T,T',T''$. A second group of sketches connects the $A_i$ by parallel arrows. The foot contrasts $A_0\leftarrow A$ with $A_0=A$ through a questioned implication.

**Scan 35 · Cover.** “ETAT PRIMITIF.” labels material entered under a question about compatibility. The much fainter lower strokes supply no securely readable mathematical statement.

**Scan 36 · Cancelled adjunction diagrams.** Two boxed pairs of categories precede alternative squares labelled by Hom expressions. In the large frame the left square carries $g^*,f_!$ and the right square $g_*,f^!$; each contains a small curved comparison arrow. A lower pair of Hom comparisons is placed over $X$ and $V$. The last line starts a relative RHom comparison and stops at $\sim$.

## Slips

- **Scan 25, lines 37–39 · S25.1, low confidence.** After writing $\theta(k)=\varphi(O(1))$, the author places $\theta(k)-\varphi(O(1))$ in $H^1(S,G)$. In the preceding exact sequence $\theta$ lands in the $\Gamma$ term. A kernel argument would subtract from $k$ a class in the middle group with the same image. This is a type comparison; the sheet does not specify such a lift separately. The O and some nearby letters carry doubt marks.
- **Scan 27, line 7 · S27.1.** “affins (non nécessairement lisse)” has singular *lisse*. The grammatical agreement with “Les groupes” would be *lisses*.
- **Scan 32, line 23 · S32.1, low confidence.** The left side begins $R'f^!g^*$. If the prime designates the changed morphism, $Rf'^!g^*$ would be expected. The surrounding first attempt is cancelled; a corrected prime placement cannot be established here.
- **Scan 33, line 30 · S33.1.** The closing sign of $\langle O(E),O(-F))$ is round. A matched angle would give $\langle O(E),O(-F)\rangle$. Both lower $N_{E/}$ also stop at the slash. Completing them as $N_{E/S}$ is plausible from the upper line, with low confidence about the writer’s intention.
