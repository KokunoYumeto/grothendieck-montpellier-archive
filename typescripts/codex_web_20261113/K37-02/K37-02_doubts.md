## Legibility

The two inverted typescripts, 48 and 50, preserve the running prose well. The earlier keystrokes under x’s, repeated letters and revised numbers are much less reliable. The short SGA memoranda have large areas of clear paper around a few lines of very compressed handwriting. Their punctuation, short connecting words and volume or reference corrections carry most of the uncertainty.

The least secure reconstructions are the canceled upper grid on 47, the small captions beside its arrows, and the crossed words and physically torn last line on 59. On 49 a densely retraced I and the words before the diagram resist a unique reading. Faint mirror-image type visible through the two manuscript sheets was distinguished from ink on their written face. Repeated stationery, source underlines, and archive numerals were checked independently of the mathematical lettering.
