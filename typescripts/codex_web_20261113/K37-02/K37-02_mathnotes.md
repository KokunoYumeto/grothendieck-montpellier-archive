# K37-02 · Mathematical notes

## What the pages do

**47 · Finite spaces, orders and topoi.** A pair of arrows between E′ and E″ and two canceled comparison maps precede a crossed grid. A second diagram relates finite spaces to preorders, then finite sober spaces to finite orders. The boxed “Dictionnaire” connects X, I, a lattice-like object (read first as σ) and E by arrows labelled with opens, sieves, points and other constructions. Arrows for passing to an opposite object extend out of the dictionary.

**48 · Four questions on subtoposes.** The canceled typescript “9.1.13. Questions ouvertes” asks what happens to intersections, finite suprema and points of subtoposes of E. It gives implications between the proposed conditions, cites special cases, and breaks off when it turns to a converse.

**49 · Sieves as opens.** The writer starts with an ordered set I and asks which subsets are its cribles. **[49.lower]** The condition is explicit: whenever x belongs to the subset, the y with y ≤ x belong to it as well. These subsets provide the opens of the topology. A display identifies Ouv(I), Crib(I) and Ouv(I with a wedge), then identifies I with a wedge with Top(I).

**49 · Criteria linked by arrows.** A separate four-line diagram relates generation by opens and enough essential points to generation by certain subobjects of the final object. Two more lines have hypotheses involving E as a presheaf topos or E as Top(X) for X sober. Their conclusions concern monomorphisms in C and neighbourhoods of points in X.

**50 · Biextensions.** The canceled proof reduces to a separably closed base and then to A=P. The module gives a map from Q to the underlined Pic with subscript P/k. The writer pulls back the Weil sheaf along id on P times f, compares the two birigidified modules, and invokes a result on divisorial correspondences. For the general extension of A by L, he uses the sequence 0 → L → P → A → 0 and two displayed Biext vanishings. A separate Corollaire 4.8 starts to specify an extension Q and stops after “par un”.

**51 and 57–58 · Editorial material.** Scan 51 is the SGA 5 cover. Scan 57 asks for an interpretation of the relation between the quoted polynomial sequences and formal series by means of f*g. Its second note asks whom to mention on the title page. Scan 58 attaches a reference to a particular place in Exp II.

**59 · A proposed group action.** The strip sets the product of barred i and barred j equal to τ. For an object E of C it announces an application from τ(E) × E to E which will make E “un objet formellement principal” under τ(E). A sequence leading from J through E to B and a partly lost formula at the foot appear to belong to the construction. The labels and the subscript at the torn edge remain uncertain.

**60–61 · Two requests about Gr.** Scan 60 concerns the self-intersection formula for Gr(X) and Gr(Y), with a tentative location in the exposé. Scan 61 calls for the explicit additive structure of the ring written Gr of X with a tilde, tensored with the handwritten blackboard Q, for a blown-up scheme. Neither sheet supplies the requested calculation.

**62–63 · Exposé edits.** Scan 62 requests review of Exp. X. Scan 63 asks for a footnote in SGA 6 III about a flat morphism locally of finite presentation, its perfection and the resulting pseudo-coherence.

## Questions and conjectures

- **47.** “topos opposé ?” is circled. Question marks also appear at the ends of the outgoing construction involving E and “opp”.
- **48 a).** “le Inf de deux éléments est-il distributif par rapport aux Sup finis ?” The elements are subtoposes.
- **48 b).** “Existe-t-il un sous-topos E″” with E″ ∩ E′ equal to Top(∅) and E″ different from Top(∅), for the given proper E′?
- **48 c).** “Les conditions suivantes sont-elles équivalentes”. The three conditions concern E as supremum, E″ as a greatest disjoint candidate and E″ as a maximal candidate. The intersection in the text of (ii) is written using Top(E′) and Top(E″₁).
- **48 d).** “tout point de E est-il isomorphe à un point provenant de E′ ou de E″ ?” The hypothesis is that E is their supremum.
- **49.** “quels sont les cribles de I” is answered by the condition on y ≤ x later in the same sentence.
- **57.** “Donner interprétation de la relation” is a request for a mathematical explanation. “Mentionner Serre et Jouanolou sur page de garde ?” is an editorial question.
- **60.** “Peut-être fin de l’Exp ?” asks where to put the formula; the word read first as fin is doubtful.

## Remarks of the model

- **[R48] Model, high confidence.** The formal implication from “greatest” to “maximal” used in [48.c] is valid for the same class of candidates ordered by inclusion. This check bears on that one implication; the existence and distributivity questions remain open in the draft.
- **[R49] Model, high confidence.** With the opens specified in [49.lower], the set of y ≤ x is the least open containing x. The NB calls these y the générisations of x. This gives a specific instance of the neighbourhood condition in the last line of the diagram. Both the inequality and the word générisation are legible.
- **[R59] Model, low confidence.** The fragment appears to connect the proposed action to the arrow ending at B. The connecting prose is crossed and the formula after it reaches the torn edge. Those surviving lines do not establish how the action is obtained.
