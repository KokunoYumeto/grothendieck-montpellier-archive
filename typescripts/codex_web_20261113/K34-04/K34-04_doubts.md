## Legibility and limits

All sixteen whole-page images and all forty-eight overlapping thirds were viewed directly. No OCR, text layer, recognition library, published transcription, internet source, or second worker was used. The second-reading audit records actual UTC times and the observations made before reopening each page file. These are model readings, not an independent human certification.

The large live handwriting is mostly legible. Trust is lowest in the heavily cancelled block on scan 69; the discarded opening and oblique draft on scan 70; the tiny editorial note on scan 72; the small circled note and lower replacement on scan 74; the repeated top-right overwriting in scan 77; and the dense formula cancellations on scan 80. The bibliography is clearer, except for its last item number. The two one-word illegibilities on scan 70 and the two-word illegibility on scan 74 are all inside cancellations.

The first candidates in marked formulas are readings of ink, not mathematical repairs. In particular, headless strokes, unexpected H-degrees, the numeral 1 in (2.5.5), local-support arguments without a prime, and the positive twist on scan 73 remain as written. The very short publication entry “S. Lang,” and unfinished sentences/references remain unfinished. Faint reversed show-through on blank areas was not treated as new writing on the front of the sheet.
