## Legibility

The large path diagrams, most endpoint letters and the changes in the direction of arrows can be followed across the leaves. Confidence is lower for French word endings that collapse into a horizontal stroke, small signs added to an already written symbol, and letters beneath dense cancellation. The slanted note on 325 intersects the main text, the edge of that leaf cuts into the last subscript, and the old right-hand side of (1) on 330 is extensively hatched. The two parts of the note on 329 and the slanted reminder on 334 have been read in their own orientation. A proposed reading beneath a strike is especially provisional, even where the surrounding argument would give an obvious completion. No passage was assigned a whole-word illegibility mark; tentative readings of obscured passages are marked in place.
