# K10-02 · Mathematical notes

Scan numbers refer to the archive PDF. Line references count lines in each delivered page fragment. The French excerpts retain the manuscript's wording and abbreviations.

## What the pages do

**17 · Twisting a polarization.** The continuation opens with the rule $\varphi^z(x,y)=\varphi(x,zy)$. Item 19 applies it in a tensor category. A marginal note connects $z^2=1$ with preservation of $\epsilon$-symmetry. The adjoint of a map between two objects is compared before and after twisting, followed by equivalences for Weil forms, a tensor-product formula and the action on polarizations. Some of the later raised letters look like $\epsilon$ where the definition has $z$.

**19 and 21 · Forms and adjoints.** The heading *Notes anciennes* introduces a form with values in $Q(p)$ on a motive of weight $p$. The two drawings put $\widetilde\varphi$ and $\widetilde\psi$ on parallel arrows. Scan 21 writes down $\Theta_\varphi$, its inverse and the effect of exchanging the two arguments. The final lines distinguish the two parities of $p$ when writing the adjoint identity. Scan 20 between these sheets contains a cancelled fragment of prose.

**23–24 · Changing a form.** Four identities specify how the adjoint acts on a sum, a scalar multiple, an adjoint and a product. Replacing $\varphi$ by the form associated with $a$ gives $u^\psi=a^{-1}u^\varphi a$, with $a^\varphi=a$. Scan 24 observes that a form contains information lost in its involution, using commuting endomorphisms and scalar multiples as examples. Scan 22 is a cancelled line of prose.

**24–26 · Positivity and a cone of forms.** The symmetric trace pairing on endomorphisms is related to the tensor product of the dual form with the original form. Under a positivity assumption the endomorphism algebra is called an *algèbre de Riemann*. The closed cone generated by $u^\varphi u$ corresponds to the cone generated by the forms $\varphi^u$. The notes relate interior points to units and nondegenerate forms, transfer the positivity condition to the new involution and say that every interior form generates the same cone. Changing the sign of the form gives the opposite cone.

**26–28 · Polarity and admissibility.** The claim that $A^+$ is its own polar in $A^s$ leads to two descriptions of $A$, one in terms of forms on $M$, the other in terms of forms on its dual. The trace pairing becomes contraction. Scan 27 pairs two admissible cones. Scan 28 gives three conditions on a candidate pair $P,P^*$ — stability under automorphisms, a nonnegative pairing, and the presence of an admissible pair of polar forms — and states what follows for the cones and their interiors.

**28–29 · An abstract algebra and its module of forms.** Under *Abstraitement*, the ingredients are a finite-type algebra $A$ over $Q$, written $\frac12$ simple, a trace, a right $A\otimes A$-module $\Phi$ free of rank one over each factor, and its $Q$-dual. Using a basis over either factor and the trace produces $\check\varphi$. A symmetry interchanges the two module structures. For a fixed symmetric element the page recovers an involution on $A$, describes what happens on $A^\circ$, and gives the bimodule operation in terms of $aub^\varphi$. The margin revisits the change-of-form terminology.

**30–31 · Riemannian forms and the number of cones.** The trace inequality defines the Riemannian condition. The cone generated by a form under $u\otimes u$ is compared with positive elements of $A$ and with a dual cone. The interiors are described as disjoint sets of Riemannian forms. The page distinguishes the cone of $\varphi$ from that of $-\varphi$ and asks what the number of such cones measures.

**88 · Title sheet.** The sheet names a filtration of fibre functors. The title is its only substantive text.

## Remarks of the model

- **Model remark, high confidence (17 and 31).** The two final questions concern different objects. The first asks about the action on polarizations of a tensor category. The second asks about the number of cones in a space of forms. The nouns in the two question lines identify those objects explicitly.
- **Model remark, medium confidence (26, 28–30).** The marginal list on scan 26 anticipates the section headed *Abstraitement*. Both name an algebra, a trace, a module free on either side and a cone. The later passage introduces the additional symmetry explicitly. The pages do not state an equivalence between the two formulations.
- **Model remark, low confidence (29, L23–27).** The relative force of *composition* in the insertion and *conjugaison* in the circled note is unsettled. A cancelled word, a reference sign and the two different terms intervene. Reading the marginal comment as a final convention would give it more force than the sheet establishes.
