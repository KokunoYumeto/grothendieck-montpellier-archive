# Legibility and limits

Typed bodies of 005/007 are relatively clear; overtyping and hand leaders make their correction layers less secure. Scans 004/006 have faint reversed show-through, excluded as the other face and inventoried as reused typed sheets. Least trusted: slanted notes of 003/009, two struck lines of 004, the altered name on 007, tiny superscripts and interlaced relation signs. Three words in the deleted 004 line remain unreadable. All eight scans were viewed whole and in all three supplied thirds, with extra rotated/enlarged crops. No OCR, web source, external transcription or sub-agent was used. Structural checks cannot establish textual accuracy or guarantee compilation; compilation was deliberately not attempted.
